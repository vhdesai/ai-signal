AlphaProof Code applies machine-checked proofs to a bounded Signal build
deepmind.google
November 10, 2026
  • DeepMind says AlphaProof Code machine-checked security properties of a compiled AArch64 build of Signal’s libsignal-cli, using multiple agents with human goal-setting and review.
  • The company reports producing 6.5 million lines of Lean proofs over four weeks, supported by 26,000 lines of trusted specifications and semantics.
  • The result is bounded to a fresh-process build and does not cover key generation or group messaging, so it is not a blanket assurance that all of Signal is secure.
  • For technology leaders, the important distinction is between checking a defined property against trusted assumptions and certifying an entire product.

November 10, 2026
  • Gemini-powered agents generated about 6.5 million lines of Lean proofs over four weeks to show that a compiled build of Signal's library meets specified cryptographic properties, with humans reviewing every specification and roughly 1% of proof steps.
  • When previously fixed bugs were reintroduced into a test copy, the system correctly failed to verify the flawed versions.
