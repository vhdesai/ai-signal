November 1, 2026
  • DeepSeek's Harness team released dsh-superagent v0.1.0, an MIT-licensed prototype.
  • A long-running router takes requests from GitHub or Feishu and assigns each authorized task to its own worker session.
  • In DeepSeek's simulation, the 95th-percentile task completion time fell to 15.7 minutes from 29.5 minutes for a single-session agent.

  • The design treats human attention as the bottleneck, which mirrors how Western agent tools are developing.
