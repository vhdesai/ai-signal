thehackernews.com
August 11, 2026

Unreleased Anthropic Model Raises the Riemann Zeta Lower Bound from 41.6% to 67.2%
anthropic.com
August 11, 2026
  • An unreleased Claude research model, working over multi-day sessions with roughly 60 coordinated subagents, raised the proven lower bound for the share of nontrivial Riemann zeta zeros known to lie on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2%.
  • The result does not resolve the Riemann Hypothesis but extends a 160-year-old boundary, and Anthropic reports extensive numerical checking plus a Lean formalization so the argument is machine-verifiable.
  • Reported search cost was on the order of 650 failed approaches.
  • This is the strongest current evidence that frontier models can produce novel, formally verified mathematics rather than recombination.
