September 28, 2026
  • EAGLE (Esophageal AI-Guided malignant Lesion Evaluation) was trained on 6,813 patients at two Chinese centres and validated across 12 centres in China, the Czech Republic and Australia covering 80,612 patients.

Sakana AI and the University of Tokyo raise robot task success from 25% to 73% with test-time search
sakana.ai
September 28, 2026
  • SAIL (Scaling In-Context Imitation Learning) treats robot control as Monte Carlo tree search over whole trajectories: a frozen vision-language model generates candidate motions from retrieved demonstrations, a simulator executes them, and an evaluator VLM returns step-level feedback for refinement.
  • Across six simulated manipulation tasks, raising the search budget from one node to 45 lifted average success from 25% to 73%, peaking at 95% on one task, using Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 with no weight updates.
