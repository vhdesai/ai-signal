September 17, 2026
  • Z.ai published a technical account describing how an "Infra Agent" powered by GLM-5.3 did most of the work to bring GLM-5.3-Flash (320B total / 18B active, 1M context) into production on a cluster of more than 100,000 Chinese-made accelerators — a scale the company says had not previously been operated on domestic silicon.
