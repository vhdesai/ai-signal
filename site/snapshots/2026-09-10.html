Google DeepMind proposes Autoregressive Ranking to replace dual/cross-encoder search stacks
searchenginejournal.com
September 10, 2026
  • Google DeepMind, UMass Amherst, and UT Austin researchers published Autoregressive Ranking (ARR), a proposed replacement for the classic dual-encoder plus cross-encoder two-stage search architecture.
  • A new SToICaL training loss makes the LLM assign more probability to tokens that lead to higher-ranked documents.
