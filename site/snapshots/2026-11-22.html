November 22, 2026

MIT's SLIC trains small, laptop-scale models to design analog chips as well as frontier LLMs
November 22, 2026
  • MIT researchers, working with IBM Research and Analog Devices, developed SLIC (Small LM Iterating on Circuits).
  • It trains small language models that run locally to design analog integrated circuits, matching or beating open-weight frontier LLMs.
  • Because training and use stay on-premises, chipmakers can use proprietary design and foundry data without sending it to cloud models.
  • The work will be presented at NeurIPS 2026.
  • Small specialized models are a practical option for chip-design work where intellectual property is sensitive.
