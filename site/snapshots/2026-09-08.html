OpenAI publishes agent-generated proof of finite-time singularity in 3D Navier–Stokes
September 8, 2026
  • OpenAI released an analytical proof and a Lean formalization showing that an initially smooth, at-rest fluid under a smooth applied force can develop a finite-time singularity in the 3D Navier–Stokes equations.
  • The work used an unreleased internal model more capable than GPT-6 Astra, running on the order of 10,000 concurrent agents; the search reached its result in about 88 hours from a September 1 start, followed by roughly 17 hours of Lean formalization, consuming ~2.7 million agent messages and ~130 billion output tokens.
  • OpenAI says it will not pursue the $1M Clay prize; the Clay Mathematics Institute has not commented.
  • Regardless of the prize question, this is the first credible demonstration of frontier-scale agent orchestration producing a machine-checkable result at research-frontier difficulty.

US closes $1.9B loan to restart the Iowa reactor already contracted to Google
techcrunch.com
September 8, 2026
  • The Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing closed a loan of up to $1.9 billion to NextEra Energy to fund the restart of the 615 MW Duane Arnold Energy Center, shuttered since 2020.
  • Google signed a 25-year PPA for the output in October 2025 to serve its Iowa cloud and AI operations; restart is targeted for 2029 pending NRC approval.
  • It is the third federally financed nuclear restart and the second such loan tied to hyperscaler demand, after Three Mile Island.
  • Restarting mothballed reactors is now the fastest firm-power path available to AI data centers.
