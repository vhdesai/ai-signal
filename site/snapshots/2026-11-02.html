Zyphra's hierarchical attention method aims to cut the cost of long-context inference
marktechpost.com
November 2, 2026
  • To save compute, many models attend to only some earlier tokens, but they still score every earlier token to decide which ones to use.
  • Near 1 million tokens, that selection step can take 60-90% of the computing time.
  • Zyphra's ZHA method uses a tree search over compressed summaries of the context instead, and it can be added to an existing model with about 4B tokens of extra training.
  • On Qwen3.6-35B-A3B it scores within a few points of standard attention; its SWE-bench Verified score matches at 67.4.
  • However, the speedups are estimated rather than measured, no optimized code is released, and one model dropped 25 points on a long-context retrieval benchmark.
