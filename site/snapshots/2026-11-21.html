Columbia and CertiK's TensorLean formally verifies the GPU kernels behind production AI
November 21, 2026
  • Researchers from Columbia University and CertiK introduced TensorLean, a Lean-based framework that proves compiled GPU kernels match a tensor-level math specification at the PTX level, checking for data races, termination, numerical correctness and untouched memory.
  • Applied to 149 kernel instances, including every FlashAttention-2, -3 and -4 kernel plus DeepGEMM, it proved 83 correct, and the remaining 66 led to 38 correctness bugs across six projects.
  • One find was a FlashAttention-4 data race that never surfaced in 17,760 test runs, a strong case for formal methods in the low-level code that training and inference depend on.
