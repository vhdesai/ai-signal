Tsinghua's PACMAN group publishes Puro-2B, a full pretraining recipe for consumer GPUs
September 1, 2026
  • The Puro-2B report describes training 2-billion-parameter models from scratch on up to 1.4T tokens using FP8 precision on consumer RTX 5090 hardware, with the team publishing training code, checkpoints, and data.
  • Reported runs in the roughly $5,000-7,000 range are claimed to beat or approach Qwen2-class 1.5B baselines.
