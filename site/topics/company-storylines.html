Nous Research releases Arete-27B, a Qwen3.8-27B version trained to drop Chinese state framing
November 7, 2026
  • Nous further trained Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B on about 35,000 prompts, using answers from Thinking Machines' open Inkling model as examples; it used no reinforcement learning.
  • The weights, training data, and evaluation are all public under Apache 2.0 and MIT licenses.
  • On Nous's own 803-prompt test, the share of answers that adopted state propaganda fell from 22% to 0.9%.
  • Its factual-accuracy score rose from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5, and Hermes Bench stayed flat.
  • These results are self-reported and use another AI model as the grader, and 216 answers still score zero.
  • Companies that want Chinese open-weight models for their capability but not their political alignment now have a reproducible way to retrain them.
