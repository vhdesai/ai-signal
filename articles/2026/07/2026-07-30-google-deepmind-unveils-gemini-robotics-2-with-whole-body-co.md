# Google DeepMind unveils Gemini Robotics 2 with whole-body control and multi-robot collaboration

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2, a family of physical-AI models adding whole-body control, improved dexterity, and multi-robot coordination, demonstrated on Apptronik's Apollo humanoid and paired with a new “ASIMOV” agentic-safety benchmark. It marks a step toward general-purpose robot behavior driven by frontier multimodal models rather than task-specific programming. For enterprises eyeing warehouse and manufacturing automation, it signals the accelerating convergence of foundation models and robotics.
