# CyrusOne prepares IPO groundwork as AI data-center demand keeps valuations high

The Information reported that CyrusOne, the data-center operator owned by KKR and BlackRock's Global Infrastructure Partners, is preparing to hire banks for a possible IPO. The move would test public-market appetite for AI-infrastructure exposure as private owners look to monetize data-center assets amid sustained demand for compute capacity.
