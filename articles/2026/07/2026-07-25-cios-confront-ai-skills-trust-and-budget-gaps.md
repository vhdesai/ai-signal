# CIOs confront AI skills, trust, and budget gaps

CIO Dive's Weekender collected several enterprise AI adoption stories: end-user AI spending is expected to soar, AI skills gaps persist despite widening personal use, and employee distrust is limiting AI scale. The coverage points to a common bottleneck: model access is no longer the hard part; training, governance, cost controls, and organizational trust are. This is the operating layer that will decide whether AI pilots become durable productivity improvements.
