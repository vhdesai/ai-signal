# One fallen power line trips 3.1 GW of AI data centers, stressing the PJM grid

A power-line failure outside Washington, DC caused more than 3.1 GW of data-center load to disconnect in roughly 30 seconds, sending a ~3.49 GW surplus and voltage spike across PJM from Northern Virginia to Chicago and taking ~11 minutes to stabilize. PJM projects data centers will grow from 6% of its load in 2024 to 24% by 2040, making simultaneous defensive disconnects a systemic stability risk. Signal: compute build-out is now colliding with grid physics — a siting and resilience risk for every AI operator.
