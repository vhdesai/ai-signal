# OpenAI Unveils Project Camellia, a $30B+, 3.2-GW Data-Center Campus in Georgia

1,400-acre campus in Effingham County near Savannah. OpenAI will fully fund grid infrastructure (no ratepayer subsidies) and use closed-loop cooling. Power phasing in 2028–2032. A bet on the next decade of compute demand. Electricity, not chips, is now the binding constraint on AI scale.
