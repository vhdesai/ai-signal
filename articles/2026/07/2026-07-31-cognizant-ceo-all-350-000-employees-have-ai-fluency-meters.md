# Cognizant CEO: All 350,000 Employees Have "AI Fluency Meters"

Cognizant says all 350,000 employees now have AI fluency dashboards that track usage, workflow integration, and token consumption. The company rolls those signals into a private composite score so workers can benchmark themselves without turning AI adoption into public surveillance. New engineering graduates are trained in "agent management," emphasizing delegation and steering rather than manual execution alone. That makes Cognizant one of the clearest examples yet of enterprise AI being treated as a measurable operational competency. The broader implication is that AI readiness is moving from informal experimentation to something leaders intend to manage at workforce scale. Links: DealBook
