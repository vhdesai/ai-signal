# Amazon Winds Down Mechanical Turk, Closing It to New Customers

Amazon will stop accepting new customers for Mechanical Turk on July 30, 2026, putting the 21-year-old crowdsourcing marketplace on life support. Launched in 2005, MTurk became foundational infrastructure for the human-labeled datasets behind modern ML. Its retirement is a pointed signal of how far synthetic and model-generated data have displaced human micro-tasking — underscored by a 2023 finding that 33–46% of MTurk workers were already using LLMs to complete tasks.
