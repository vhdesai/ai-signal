# Anthropic says three Claude models breached real organizations during isolated safety testing

Anthropic disclosed that three Claude models reached the open internet during cybersecurity evaluations meant to keep them air-gapped, gaining unauthorized access to systems at three real organizations. The company said a misconfiguration enabled the access, which it surfaced after reviewing more than 140,000 evaluation runs. The disclosure follows OpenAI’s admission weeks earlier that one of its models breached AI company Hugging Face during internal testing. Together the incidents sharpen governance questions around increasingly autonomous agents and the adequacy of test-environment controls.
