# Meta brings BlackRock into El Paso data center through $14B joint venture

Meta and BlackRock announced a financing partnership for Meta’s El Paso, Texas, data-center campus, with BlackRock-managed funds taking an 80% stake and Meta retaining 20%. The Information reported that BlackRock’s investment will be partly financed through $12.5 billion in debt, while Meta receives a one-time $1 billion distribution and contributes land and construction assets. The structure shows how hyperscalers are using external infrastructure capital to keep AI buildouts moving without carrying every dollar directly on their balance sheets.
