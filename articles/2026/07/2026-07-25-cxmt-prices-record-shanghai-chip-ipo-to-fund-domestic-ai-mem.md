# CXMT prices record Shanghai chip IPO to fund domestic AI memory

China’s ChangXin Memory Technologies, a flagship DRAM maker, is reportedly pricing an approximately $8.6 billion STAR Market IPO at a valuation near $85 billion. The capital raise shows China is funding memory independence alongside model development.
