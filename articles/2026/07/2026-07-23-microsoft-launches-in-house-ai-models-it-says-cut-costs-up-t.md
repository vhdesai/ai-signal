# Microsoft launches in-house AI models it says cut costs up to 89% versus OpenAI

VentureBeat reports that Microsoft launched new in-house AI models that it says can reduce costs by up to 89% versus OpenAI for some workloads. The move is strategically important because Microsoft is both a major OpenAI partner and one of the largest enterprise AI distributors. If the models perform well in targeted workloads, Microsoft can use model routing and in-house models to improve margins while preserving access to frontier models when needed.
