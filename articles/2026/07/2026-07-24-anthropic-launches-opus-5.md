# Anthropic launches Opus 5

TechCrunch reports that Anthropic launched Opus 5, positioning it as cheaper and less restrictive than Fable for many use cases. The release suggests Anthropic is segmenting its model portfolio across capability, cost, and safety constraints rather than treating a single flagship model as the default for every workload. For enterprise buyers, the practical question is whether Opus 5 offers a better operating balance for agentic and knowledge-work deployments than Fable.
