# Meta in Talks to Lease Excess AI Compute to Anthropic in Deal Worth Up to $10 Billion

Mark Zuckerberg revealed during Meta's earnings call that the company has received "a lot of offers for compute at a significant premium over what we paid for it" and confirmed Meta is in talks with Anthropic about a potential deal worth up to $10 billion to lease excess computing capacity. However, Zuckerberg appeared indecisive about committing, alternating between noting strong internal demand for the capacity and suggesting Meta would earn "significantly higher margins from selling intelligence rather than selling compute directly." The Information characterizes this as Zuckerberg not wanting Meta to become a cloud firm while being reluctant to say so directly for fear of further upsetting Wall Street. If consummated, a Meta-Anthropic compute deal would represent an unprecedented cross-pollination between a social media platform and a frontier AI lab.
