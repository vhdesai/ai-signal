# &lsquo;Open Dreamer&rsquo;: a JAX/Flax reproduction of DeepMind&rsquo;s Dreamer 4 world-model pipeline, full recipe published

An independent group published Open Dreamer, a JAX/Flax reproduction of DeepMind’s Dreamer 4 world-model pipeline — including a causal video tokenizer, action-conditioned latent dynamics, and FVD scoring — with the full training recipe released openly. It ships with a real-time browser Minecraft demo featuring a Game-to-Dream toggle. It is the most academically oriented, genuinely new open-science release confirmed in the 24-hour window.
