# Zuckerberg frames Meta's enterprise-AI opportunity beyond agents — APIs and selling compute

On Meta's Q2 earnings call, Mark Zuckerberg described a "large enterprise opportunity" that extends past its June business-agent launch to include APIs, business agents, and potentially selling compute directly to large customers. The framing points to new revenue lines beyond advertising and subscriptions. It positions Meta as an emerging enterprise-AI vendor competing more directly with Microsoft, Google, and AWS, even as capex continues to climb on AI infrastructure. FUNDING
