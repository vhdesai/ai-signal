# “ChatGPT for doctors” reportedly weighs financing at $20 billion valuation

The Information reports that a “ChatGPT for doctors” company is considering new financing at a $20 billion valuation. The signal is that vertical AI in regulated, high-value workflows continues to command premium private-market pricing. Health care remains especially attractive because workflow bottlenecks, documentation burden, and decision-support needs are large, but defensibility will depend on trust, clinical validation, distribution, and liability management.
