# Google Search Traffic to Publishers Drops 34% as AI Answers Resolve Queries On-Platform

Chartbeat data cited by Axios shows Google Search referrals to publishers fell 34% over the last year as AI answers kept users on-platform. Smaller publishers were hit hardest, losing about 60% of search traffic over two years versus roughly 22% for large outlets. That makes AI answer boxes not just a product feature, but a structural redistribution of value away from content creators and toward platforms. At the same time, LinkedIn, Reddit, and other networks are filling with AI-generated material, compounding pressure on original journalism. Any company dependent on organic search should treat this as a durable change in distribution economics rather than a temporary algorithm tweak. Links: Yahoo Finance
