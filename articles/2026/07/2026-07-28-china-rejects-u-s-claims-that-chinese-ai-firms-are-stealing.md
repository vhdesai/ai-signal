# China Rejects U.S. Claims That Chinese AI Firms Are Stealing IP Through Model Distillation

China's Ministry of Commerce issued a formal rebuttal to recent U.S. accusations that Chinese AI firms have been appropriating American intellectual property by distilling proprietary U.S. AI models, calling the claim devoid of “factual basis or legal support.” The pushback comes amid escalating tensions over AI competitiveness, with Washington increasingly framing Chinese model development — particularly breakthroughs from labs like DeepSeek — as dependent on illicitly acquired Western technology. The dispute highlights a fundamental disagreement over whether techniques like knowledge distillation, which uses outputs from one model to train another, constitute IP theft or standard research methodology. For enterprises evaluating Chinese AI models for deployment, the regulatory uncertainty adds another dimension of geopolitical risk to procurement decisions.
