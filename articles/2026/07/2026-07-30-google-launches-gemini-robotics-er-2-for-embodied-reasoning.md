# Google launches Gemini Robotics ER 2 for embodied reasoning and multi-robot collaboration

Google DeepMind launched Gemini Robotics ER 2, an embodied-reasoning model that can process video, plan multi-step physical tasks, call tools, and coordinate with lower-level robot control systems. The model adds progress tracking, self-correction, and multi-robot collaboration, and is available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and private preview on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. The release signals that frontier-model competition is moving further into physical AI, where orchestration, timing, and execution reliability matter as much as language capability.
