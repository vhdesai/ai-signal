# Gartner: Agentic AI to Disrupt $234B in SaaS Spending

Gartner projects agentic AI will disrupt $234 billion of SaaS spend as software use shifts from clicking through interfaces to delegating outcomes. That marks a move from additive AI budgets toward substitutive AI spending, where agents begin displacing traditional application usage. For incumbents, the risk is that expensive UI-heavy suites become harder to justify when APIs and natural-language automation deliver the same result. The pressure is likely to hit margins, packaging, and pricing models across enterprise software over the next budget cycle. CIOs planning 2027 spend should assume part of today's SaaS line items may migrate into agent infrastructure and orchestration layers. Links: CIO Dive
