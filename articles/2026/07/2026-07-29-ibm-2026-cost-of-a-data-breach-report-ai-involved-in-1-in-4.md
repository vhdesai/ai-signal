# IBM 2026 Cost of a Data Breach Report: AI involved in ~1 in 4 malicious breaches

IBM's annual report finds that attackers used AI in roughly 25% of malicious breaches, which averaged about $6 million each. The data quantifies how quickly AI is being absorbed into the offensive-security toolkit.
