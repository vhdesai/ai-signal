# Google Earth Gets "Nano Banana" AI Image Generation Grounded in Satellite Imagery

Google Earth now includes the Nano Banana image model, letting users generate visuals grounded in real satellite, aerial, and 3D mapping data. People can redesign places, reconstruct historical scenes, and create geographic infographics from natural-language prompts tied to real terrain. That makes this one of the clearest consumer examples of generative AI moving from abstract imagery into geospatially anchored synthesis. Urban planning, real estate, and architecture workflows could benefit because the tool reduces reliance on specialist GIS and manual rendering steps. The launch also fits Google's broader strategy of embedding AI inside large existing products instead of forcing users into standalone apps. Links: Engadget
