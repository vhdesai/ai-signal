# Patreon blocks AI training bots instead of relying on robots.txt

Patreon said it is working with Cloudflare to actively block AI training bots that scrape creator content without permission. The company says its testing reduced individual AI training crawler attempts from thousands per week to zero. This reflects a broader shift from voluntary crawler norms toward technical enforcement, paid access models, and stronger creator control over AI training use.
