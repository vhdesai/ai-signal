# Launch Open Source

A research consortium coordinated by Germany's KI Bundesverband released Soofi S 30B-A3B, a Mixture-of-Experts model that activates only 3.2B of 31.6B parameters per token and, per its pretraining report, tops all fully open models on aggregate German and English benchmarks — ahead of OLMo 3 32B and Apertus 70B. It was trained entirely on Deutsche Telekom's Industrial AI Cloud in Munich (~512 Nvidia B200 GPUs, ~253k GPU-hours), a deliberate European sovereignty play. The notable enterprise claim is throughput: a hybrid Mamba-Transformer design that holds roughly 8× the tokens/sec of comparable dense models at 40k-token context — though the benchmarks are consortium-reported, and the underlying paper posted to arXiv on July 10. 🔗 the-decoder.com/german-ai-consortium-releases-soofi-s
