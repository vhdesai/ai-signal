# As Token Costs Mount, Enterprise Leaders Revise AI Plans

An EY survey shows rising token costs are forcing enterprises to revisit how broadly and how quickly they deploy AI in production. Some organizations are pushing ahead on the assumption productivity gains will outrun spend, while others are narrowing to only the highest-ROI use cases. That shift marks a transition from experimentation to scaled operations, where inference bills become visible and politically important budget items. It also raises the importance of model routing, workload optimization, and matching tasks to the cheapest model that can do them reliably. AI cost management is quickly becoming as strategic as model selection, helping explain the urgency behind recent pricing cuts from major labs. Links: CIO Dive
