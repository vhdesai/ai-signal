# Nvidia-led “Open Secure AI Alliance” forms in the wake of the OpenAI breach

The OpenAI breach is intensifying the open-source AI policy debate, and dozens of firms led by Nvidia have formed an “Open Secure AI Alliance” to build open-source defensive-cybersecurity tools. The coalition positions open collaboration as a response to AI-enabled attacks rather than a liability. It sets up a fresh front in the open-versus-closed AI argument.
