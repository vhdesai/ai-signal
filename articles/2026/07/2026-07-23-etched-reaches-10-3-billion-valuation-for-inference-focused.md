# Etched reaches $10.3 billion valuation for inference-focused AI systems

Etched raised a $300 million Series C at a $10.3 billion valuation, led by Sequoia, after previously disclosing $1 billion in orders and successful silicon manufacturing. The company says its systems accelerate both the prefill and decode phases of inference using low-voltage inference and cluster-scale memory. The broader signal is that investors are willing to back non-GPU AI compute architectures if they can materially improve inference economics.
