# Major publishers sue Google over Gemini AI training

Hachette, Cengage, Elsevier, Scott Turow, and S.C.R.I.B.E. filed a class action lawsuit alleging Google used copyrighted works to train Gemini without permission. The complaint also alleges Google removed or altered copyright information to conceal training use. The case adds another major front to the unresolved copyright battle over AI training data.
