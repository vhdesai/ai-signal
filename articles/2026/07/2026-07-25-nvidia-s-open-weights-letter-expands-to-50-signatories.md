# Nvidia’s open-weights letter expands to 50 signatories

A letter urging Washington not to impose broad restrictions on open-weight AI models reportedly grew from roughly 25 to 50 signatories. The expanded list includes OpenAI, Google, AMD, Cisco, and GitHub alongside earlier supporters such as Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, Palantir, Dell, Andreessen Horowitz, and Mistral.
