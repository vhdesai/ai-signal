# WSJ argues AI job-loss policy should tax capital, not labor

A WSJ analysis argued that one response to AI-driven job displacement is shifting taxes away from labor and toward capital. The argument is that current payroll-heavy tax structures can make automation artificially attractive by taxing workers more directly than machines, software, or compute. The policy tradeoff is sharp: such a shift could slow displacement but may also reduce incentives to invest in productivity-enhancing AI. URL: A Simple Answer to AI Job Loss: Tax Capital, Not Labor
