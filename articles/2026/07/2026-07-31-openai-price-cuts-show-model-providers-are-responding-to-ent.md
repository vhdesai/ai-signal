# OpenAI price cuts show model providers are responding to enterprise cost pressure

The Information reported that OpenAI cut API prices for two GPT-5.6 models shortly after release, including a steep reduction for the Luna tier and a smaller reduction for Terra while keeping the highest-end Sol model unchanged. The move fits the broader pattern in enterprise AI this week: customers are pushing back on inference bills, and providers are using pricing, model tiers, and efficiency claims to defend adoption.
