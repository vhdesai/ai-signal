# Indian AI coding startup Emergent reaches unicorn status

Emergent raised a $130 million Series C at a $1.5 billion post-money valuation - a five-fold jump in six months - led by Creaegis, with Khosla Ventures, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Lightspeed, and Y Combinator participating. The company reports a $120 million annualized revenue run rate and more than 200,000 paying customers, targeting non-technical small businesses rather than professional developers. It underscores how quickly capital in the AI-coding market is consolidating around a handful of fast-growing platforms.
