# NVIDIA DGX GB300 supercomputer comes online at Naval Postgraduate School

NVIDIA says a DGX GB300 system is now online at the Naval Postgraduate School, giving more than 1,500 students and 600 faculty on-premises AI training and inference capability. The system will support applications including weather prediction, cybersecurity, disaster resilience, digital twins, and military education. The deployment highlights how advanced AI infrastructure is moving into defense education and applied public-sector research, not only commercial cloud environments.
