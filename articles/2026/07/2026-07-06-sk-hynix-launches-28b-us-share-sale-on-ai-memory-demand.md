# SK Hynix Launches ~$28B US Share Sale on AI-Memory Demand

South Korean chipmaker SK Hynix launched a US share sale to raise approximately $28 billion — one of the world's largest new equity offerings — drawing up to $7B in indications of interest from investors including Baillie Gifford and Coatue Management. The raise capitalizes on surging demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) that pairs with AI accelerators. SK Hynix, a dominant HBM supplier to Nvidia and Google, has more than tripled in 2026 and recently overtook Samsung as South Korea's most valuable company.
