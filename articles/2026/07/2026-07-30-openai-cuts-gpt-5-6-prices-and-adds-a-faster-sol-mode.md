# OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 prices and adds a faster Sol mode

OpenAI lowered API pricing across its GPT-5.6 line, cutting the low-cost Luna model by 80% (to $0.20/$1.20 per million input/output tokens) and mid-tier Terra by 20%, while adding a “Sol Fast” mode that runs up to 2.5x faster at twice the price. The reductions also flow through to ChatGPT Work and Codex. OpenAI attributed the savings partly to production-kernel and speculative-decoding improvements that the Sol model itself helped identify.
