# Aschenbrenner’s Situational Awareness AI fund forced to sell public portfolio to Citadel

Leopold Aschenbrenner’s AI-thesis hedge fund, Situational Awareness, sold most of its public equity portfolio to Ken Griffin’s Citadel after steep losses on leveraged AI infrastructure and software bets, per Wall Street Journal reporting relayed by TechCrunch. The fund had grown from roughly $1.5B to a reported $20B+ using leverage of up to four times, with a concentrated book spanning names like SK Hynix, Micron, Nebius, and CoreWeave, while retaining private stakes including Anthropic. AI shares rebounded after the block sale, suggesting a fund-specific unwind rather than a broad repricing — but the episode is a cautionary tale on leverage and concentration in the AI trade.
