# Amazon shuts AI agent research lab during AGI layoffs

The Information reports that Amazon shut an AI agent research lab as part of broader AGI-related layoffs. The story suggests that even the largest AI investors are reallocating resources within AI, not simply expanding every research effort. For executives, it is a reminder that AI investment is becoming more selective: teams must connect research direction to defensible product, platform, or infrastructure outcomes.
