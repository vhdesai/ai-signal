# Deezer says more than half of daily uploads are AI-generated

TechCrunch reports that Deezer says AI-generated tracks now account for more than 50% of daily uploads, peaking at an average of 90,000 tracks per day in June. Deezer plans to remove AI-generated tracks that have not been streamed in six months or are tied to fraudulent streams. The story highlights a platform-economics issue: generative AI can overwhelm discovery, royalties, and fraud controls even when individual outputs are low cost.
