# AI-Driven Memory Crunch Jolts India's Smartphone Market

Surging AI demand for memory is tightening global supply and lifting component prices, with India's smartphone market showing early strain. The AI buildout carries second-order effects across the broader consumer electronics supply chain — a planning signal for hardware procurement teams.
