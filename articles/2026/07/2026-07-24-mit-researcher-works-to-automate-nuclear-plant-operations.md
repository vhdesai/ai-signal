# MIT researcher works to automate nuclear plant operations

MIT News profiles doctoral work on supervised autonomous control systems for nuclear plants, focused on microreactors and remote operations. The approach uses transparent finite-state automation rather than data-driven machine learning, reflecting the validation constraints of safety-critical nuclear systems. The research is relevant to AI strategy because it distinguishes where autonomy can be useful from where conventional, explainable automation remains more appropriate.
