# SAP Pins Future on Business AI Platform After Strong Q2 Cloud Earnings

SAP reported strong second-quarter results driven by its cloud business and announced that the company is staking its strategic future on its new Business AI Platform, which it describes as the centerpiece of its ambition to “build AI at scale” for the enterprise. The platform integrates generative AI capabilities directly into SAP's core enterprise workflows — finance, supply chain, HR, and procurement — positioning the company to compete with Microsoft, Salesforce, and ServiceNow for the enterprise AI middleware layer. The announcement comes as CIOs face growing pressure to demonstrate measurable ROI from AI investments beyond pilot programs. SAP's approach of embedding AI natively into existing ERP workflows, rather than offering standalone AI tools, reflects a broader industry recognition that enterprise adoption scales fastest when AI is woven into processes employees already use.
