# Meta says it will alert parents when teens show distress in Meta AI conversations

Meta announced parental notifications for cases where teens show signs of distress in conversations with Meta AI. The move reflects rising scrutiny of AI companions and chatbots used by minors, especially around self-harm and emotional dependency risks. For platform companies, safety obligations are expanding from content moderation into proactive detection and intervention in AI conversations.
