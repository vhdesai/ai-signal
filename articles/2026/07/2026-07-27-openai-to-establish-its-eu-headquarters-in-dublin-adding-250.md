# OpenAI to establish its EU headquarters in Dublin, adding 250 roles

OpenAI announced it has leased Dublin's Tropical Fruit Warehouse as its new EU headquarters and will create 250 additional roles, per an IDA Ireland statement. The expansion deepens OpenAI's European regulatory and commercial base as the EU AI Act rolls out, and roughly triples its Dublin footprint (reported toward ~350 staff). For enterprise buyers, a larger in-region presence signals investment in data-residency and compliance capacity.
