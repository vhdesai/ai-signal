# Corporate America starts rationing AI spend as costs balloon

The Wall Street Journal reports that some enterprises exhausted annual AI budgets in only a few months and are now becoming more selective about model spend. Companies are mixing lower-priced models, including Chinese models, with OpenAI and Anthropic products instead of relying on one provider.
