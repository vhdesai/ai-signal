# China Clears Apple Intelligence to Launch on Alibaba's Qwen

China's Cyberspace Administration approved Apple's generative AI services powered by Alibaba's Qwen model. Alibaba's US-listed shares rose ~4–5% and Apple gained ~1% premarket. The approval unblocks Apple's AI roadmap in one of its largest markets while cementing Qwen as the default on-device model for Chinese iOS, iPadOS, and macOS users.
