# Meta's free cash flow craters as it doubles down on AI capex

Meta raised the low end of its annual capital-expenditure forecast to fund its AI buildout, and its free cash flow took a sharp hit. The pattern echoes Alphabet recently going free-cash-flow-negative. It underscores investor concern about how quickly the hyperscalers' AI spending will translate into returns.
