# Visa Cuts 2,600 Jobs — 7% of Workforce — Citing AI Efficiency

Visa announced it is eliminating approximately 2,600 positions, primarily in its technology and product divisions, as the payments giant restructures operations around AI-driven automation. The cuts represent roughly 7% of the company's global workforce and follow a pattern of major enterprise players reducing headcount while citing efficiency gains from artificial intelligence. Visa joins a growing list of blue-chip companies — including Intuit, Cisco, and Meta — that have announced significant layoffs in recent months with AI as a stated rationale. The announcement underscores a shift in the AI narrative from speculative investment to tangible operational restructuring, even as questions remain about whether AI is the true driver or a convenient justification for cost-reduction programs.
