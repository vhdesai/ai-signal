# Massive AI supply deals face execution risk

WSJ reports that the large supply deals feeding the AI boom are “no sure thing.” The story reflects growing concern that AI infrastructure commitments depend on difficult assumptions about power, chips, construction timelines, buyer demand, and financing. For technology leaders, it is a reminder to stress-test capacity roadmaps and vendor commitments rather than treating announced supply as guaranteed usable capacity. URL behind paywall.
