# Zuckerberg says Meta's enterprise-AI opportunity extends beyond agents

On Meta's Q2 earnings call, Mark Zuckerberg cited a 'large enterprise opportunity' spanning agents, APIs, compute, and internal software tooling. The comments signal Meta's intent to monetize AI beyond its consumer surfaces. It positions the company more directly against enterprise-focused rivals.
