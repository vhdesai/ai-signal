# NVIDIA and Japan launch a national AI infrastructure project

NVIDIA is working with Noetra and Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry to build a Vera Rubin AI factory with 13,750 Vera CPUs and 27,500 Rubin GPUs delivering 140 MW of capacity. The system will anchor Japan's FRONTia project, producing open multimodal foundation models for agents, digital twins, robotics, and physical AI across manufacturing, logistics, and healthcare. The deal is a marker of how fast sovereign-AI buildouts are moving from strategy to deployed infrastructure.
