# Samsung and SK hynix post record profits as AI memory demand surges

Samsung reported a record operating profit on strong DRAM, HBM and NAND demand for AI servers and shipped early HBM4E samples, while SK hynix also posted robust results. Shares of both memory makers jumped sharply. The results show AI-cycle profits concentrating among the leading memory suppliers.
