# Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-Encoder-230M and -350M, fast at 8K context on CPU

Liquid AI, the MIT CSAIL-spun-out lab, released two open-weight bidirectional encoders built on its LFM2 hybrid backbone. Both models are engineered to stay fast at 8K context length while running on CPU, targeting efficient embedding and retrieval workloads. The release continues the industry's shift toward small, deployment-friendly models over ever-larger frontier systems.
