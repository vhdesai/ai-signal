# Wall Street develops new financing structures for the AI buildout

The Information reports that Wall Street is creating more ways to fund AI infrastructure, including structures such as CLOs and ATMs. That broadens AI financing beyond hyperscaler capex, venture rounds, and corporate debt into more complex capital-market instruments. More financing may expand capacity, but it also introduces refinancing, utilization, and counterparty risk if AI demand or pricing assumptions weaken.
