# Employee distrust and skills gaps hinder enterprise AI scaling

CIO Dive reports that employee distrust and skills deficits are limiting AI scale inside enterprises. The issue matters because model access alone does not drive adoption: organizations need training, workflow redesign, governance, and trust-building to convert AI pilots into durable productivity. The operational bottleneck is shifting from model availability to organizational readiness.
