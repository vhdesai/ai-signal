# New Microsoft Copilot flaws reportedly could leak customer secrets

The Information reported that separate cybersecurity firms found previously unreported vulnerabilities in Microsoft Copilot for Office 365 that could have let attackers trick the AI into exposing private customer data. The story reinforces that prompt injection, tool access, and data exfiltration remain live enterprise risks even for major productivity-AI deployments.
