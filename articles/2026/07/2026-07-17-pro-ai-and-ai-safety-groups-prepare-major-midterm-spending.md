# Pro-AI and AI-safety groups prepare major midterm spending

Axios reports that a pro-AI super PAC has $31 million ready for the midterms, while AI-safeguards groups are also directing funds toward candidates. AI policy is becoming a funded political contest rather than a technocratic debate among labs and regulators. Executives should expect AI legislation to be shaped by electoral incentives, industry coalitions, and safety advocacy groups, not only by technical risk assessments.
