# Glow emerges from stealth at $1.2 billion valuation to secure AI-era endpoints

TechCrunch reports that Glow, founded by former Meta and Snowflake executives, emerged from stealth with $180 million in Series A funding at a $1.2 billion valuation. Glow is building endpoint security for risks created by AI agents, developer tools, and software running on employee devices. The deal shows investors expect AI to reshape enterprise security architecture, not just increase attack volume.
