# Nvidia weighs roughly $250B financing backstop for OpenAI’s Ohio data center

Nvidia is reportedly in talks to guarantee about $250 billion in financing for OpenAI’s Ohio data-center project, one of the most ambitious financing structures yet in the AI buildout. DealBook framed Nvidia as acting like a “central bank of the A.I. economy,” underwriting credit that traditional lenders may not provide. The structure would accelerate OpenAI’s compute expansion while raising familiar circular-financing concerns: Nvidia would be helping fund demand for its own chips.
