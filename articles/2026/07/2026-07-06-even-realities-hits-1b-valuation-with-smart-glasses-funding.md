# Even Realities hits $1B valuation with smart-glasses funding led by Meituan and Tencent

Even Realities raised $150 million at a $1 billion valuation for camera-free AI smart glasses. The round signals continued conviction that ambient AI will extend beyond phones and PCs, while the privacy-first hardware design differentiates the company from camera-centric smart-glasses strategies.
