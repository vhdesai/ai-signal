# EU orders Google to open Android and share search data with AI rivals

The European Commission issued two binding Digital Markets Act decisions requiring Google to open 11 Android features to rival AI assistants by July 2027 and to share anonymized search data with competing search engines and AI chatbots starting January 2027. The changes pry open distribution and data layers that have kept Gemini's on-device advantage intact. For anyone building assistants or agents, this is a structural shift in the European AI-assistant market.
