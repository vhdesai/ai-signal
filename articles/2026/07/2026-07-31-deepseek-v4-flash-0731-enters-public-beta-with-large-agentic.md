# DeepSeek V4-Flash-0731 Enters Public Beta with Large Agentic Gains

DeepSeek put V4-Flash-0731 into public beta, positioning it as a lightweight model with outsized gains on agentic benchmarks. The company says it beats its own V4-Pro preview on Terminal Bench and DeepSWE while keeping the architecture but fully retraining the model. Support for the OpenAI Responses API, Codex compatibility, and DeepSeek's own Harness runtime lowers friction for existing agent developers. The release continues DeepSeek's unusually rapid cadence, keeping latency and pricing pressure high across coding and tool-using workloads. Its emphasis on agent benchmarks over chat scores shows how evaluation priorities are shifting toward autonomous execution rather than conversation alone. Links: TechNode · The News International
