# Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3 Weights — The Largest Open Model Ever at ~2.8T Parameters

China's Moonshot AI published full weights for Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model (~104B active parameters per token) with a 1M-token context window and native vision — the largest open-weight model released to date. On reported benchmarks it is competitive with U.S. and Chinese frontier systems across reasoning, coding, and knowledge tasks. The weights ship under a revenue-tiered license (free for commercial use up to $20M in annual revenue), though the ~1.5 TB VRAM footprint keeps self-hosting at the edge of an 8×B200 cluster, limiting immediate accessibility to large teams and inference providers rather than individual developers.
