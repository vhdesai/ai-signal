# Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 reportedly challenges top U.S. systems

The Information reports that Moonshot AI's new Kimi K3 challenges U.S. frontier models. This follows rapid progress from Kimi K2 and other Chinese open models, and it reinforces the thesis that frontier-like capability is diffusing faster than many buyers expected. For senior technology leaders, the question becomes less “which lab has the best model?” and more “which mix of models, controls, and infrastructure delivers acceptable quality at sustainable cost?” URL: https://www.theinformation.com/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=Moonshot%20Kimi%20K3
