# AI shifts mainframe modernization strategy

CIO Dive reported that AI is changing how enterprises think about mainframe modernization. Rather than forcing a binary choice between staying on legacy systems or ripping them out, AI tools may help organizations analyze, refactor, document, and selectively modernize mainframe workloads. The story reflects a broader enterprise pattern: AI is becoming an orchestration layer for difficult modernization work, not just a standalone productivity tool.
