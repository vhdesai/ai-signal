# Data centers may face temporary power cuts on the largest US grid

Data centers on PJM Interconnection — the largest U.S. grid — may face temporary, mandatory curtailments to prevent blackouts, as construction outpaces available generation. The prospect of interruptible service for AI facilities departs from the long-held assumption of firm, always-on power. Executives planning AI capacity should treat grid access, siting, and on-site generation as first-order constraints rather than afterthoughts.
