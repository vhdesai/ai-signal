# Fish Audio raises $52 million seed for expressive voice models

Fish Audio raised a $52 million seed round to expand AI voice models for creators and enterprises. The company says more than 8 million users use its open-source or hosted models, with $21 million in annual recurring revenue, and it is building speech-to-speech and audio-understanding models. The key issue will be trust: voice licensing, consent, takedown, and revenue-sharing models will determine whether community-driven voice platforms can scale responsibly.
