# Dario Amodei says Anthropic does not oppose open weights but fears Chinese AI

TechCrunch reports that Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei clarified his position on open-weight models, saying he does not oppose them categorically but remains concerned about Chinese AI capabilities and governance. The distinction matters because the policy debate has been drifting toward a binary view of open versus closed models.
