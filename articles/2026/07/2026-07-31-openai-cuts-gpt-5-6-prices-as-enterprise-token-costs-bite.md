# OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 prices as enterprise token costs bite

OpenAI cut API prices on two of the three GPT-5.6 models it released broadly on July 9. Luna input pricing fell 80% to $0.20 per million tokens and output to $1.20 per million tokens, while Terra input and output pricing fell 20%; OpenAI left its top Sol model unchanged. The move directly addresses enterprise concerns that production AI workloads are becoming constrained by inference costs. URL: The Information search: OpenAI GPT-5.6 price cuts
