# NVIDIA Cosmos-H-Dreams: a real-time surgical world model

NVIDIA published Cosmos-H-Dreams, described as a real-time, action-conditioned generative simulator for surgical robotics. It reaches roughly 160 frames per second on a single NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 — fast enough for closed-loop robotic control rather than offline rendering. The release shows how quickly generative world models are moving from research demos toward real-time embodied applications. Products & Tools Trending Physical AI
