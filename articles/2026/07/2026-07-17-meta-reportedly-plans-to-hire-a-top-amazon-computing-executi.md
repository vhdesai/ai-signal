# Meta reportedly plans to hire a top Amazon computing executive

WSJ reports that Meta plans to hire a top Amazon computing executive as it weighs a broader cloud push. If accurate, the move would indicate Meta is looking beyond internal AI infrastructure toward a more externalized compute or cloud strategy. It also highlights the premium now placed on cloud-scale operating expertise as AI labs and hyperscalers compete for infrastructure leadership. URL behind paywall.
