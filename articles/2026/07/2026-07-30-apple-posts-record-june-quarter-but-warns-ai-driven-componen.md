# Apple Posts Record June Quarter but Warns AI-Driven Component Shortages Will Bite

Apple reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $109.4B (up 16%) and net income of $29.8B, with iPhone revenue up 22% to $54.3B, in Tim Cook’s final earnings call as CEO. Guidance of 9–11% growth landed below the ~12% Street expectation, and the company warned that constrained availability of memory and advanced nodes could limit shipments; shares fell more than 7% pre-market. The read-through is that the AI data-center buildout is now diverting components away from consumer electronics, pressuring even the industry’s largest buyer. S FUNDINGDEFENSE
