# BlackRock and MGX commit $5B to Aligned Data Centers

A BlackRock- and MGX-backed group committed $5 billion of growth capital to Aligned Data Centers after completing a roughly $40 billion acquisition. Aligned has 51 campuses and more than 6.4 GW of planned capacity, with total investment capacity potentially reaching $100 billion including debt. The transaction shows AI data centers becoming an institutional asset class with infrastructure-style financing.
