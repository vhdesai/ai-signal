# China’s CXMT debuts on Shanghai’s STAR Market with an ~$8.6B raise near an $85B valuation

ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), China’s flagship DRAM maker, priced its STAR Market IPO to raise roughly $8.6B at an implied ~$85B valuation — among the largest chip listings by a Chinese firm — positioning it as a domestic alternative to Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron. Signal: state-backed capital continues to underwrite China’s memory self-sufficiency, with direct implications for HBM supply and AI-hardware competition. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH SECURITY
