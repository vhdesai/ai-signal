# Nvidia in talks to help fund a ~$500B, 10-gigawatt OpenAI data center in Ohio

Nvidia has discussed providing up to $250 billion to help OpenAI lease a proposed 10-gigawatt site south of Columbus, Ohio — developed on federal land by SoftBank energy subsidiary SBEnergy — in a project that could exceed $500 billion once chips are included, with a chip purchase reportedly valued up to $350 billion, per the Wall Street Journal as cited by Forbes. Japan agreed to fund $33 billion of the power build in exchange for a share of proceeds, with a first phase (~800 MW) targeted for 2028. The talks land alongside Nvidia's separate ~$500 billion SK hynix memory-supply agreement and Meta's planned 5-gigawatt Louisiana expansion, underscoring how concentrated and capital-intensive the frontier-compute race has become.
