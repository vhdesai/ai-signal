# China's Moonshot AI closes $3.5B round at ~$35B valuation, eyes Hong Kong IPO

Moonshot AI has closed a $3.5B financing round valuing the Beijing lab at about $35B — far above its original $1–2B target — as first reported by Bloomberg. The raise, struck at a fixed price, reads as a demand signal, and the company is already sounding out investors on a follow-on near a $50B pre-money valuation ahead of a possible Hong Kong listing this year. Momentum is driven by its Kimi K3 open model. It underscores how well-capitalized Chinese frontier labs remain despite GPU-access constraints. NOTABLE
