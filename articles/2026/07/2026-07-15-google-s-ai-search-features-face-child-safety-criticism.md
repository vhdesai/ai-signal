# Google's AI search features face child-safety criticism

Axios reports that Google's AI search features flunked a child-safety test, with related coverage describing unacceptable risks for children. The story is part of a broader scrutiny cycle around AI-generated answers in consumer search, especially when minors may rely on summaries rather than source material. For platform operators, the issue reinforces that AI search safety will be judged not only on factual accuracy but on age-appropriate risk handling and escalation paths.
