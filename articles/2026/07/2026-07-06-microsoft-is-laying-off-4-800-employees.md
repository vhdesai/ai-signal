# Microsoft is laying off 4,800 employees

Microsoft is cutting roughly 4,800 roles, with Xbox and commercial sales among the affected areas. The company framed the move as organizational restructuring while acknowledging that AI is changing how work gets done; for executives, the signal is that AI productivity programs are now being paired with large-scale workforce redesign rather than treated as isolated tooling initiatives.
