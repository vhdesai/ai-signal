# Google Dissolves Nobel Prize-Winning AlphaFold Team

Google DeepMind has reportedly dissolved the AlphaFold team, shifting key researchers toward Isomorphic Labs and other efforts. The move suggests structural biology is being commercialized through dedicated business entities rather than sustained as a standalone research program. That is a notable turn for one of AI's most celebrated scientific successes and a Nobel Prize-winning achievement. It also signals that Google now sees drug discovery commercialization as the more direct path to capturing value from this body of work. For the research community, the change raises questions about stewardship when breakthrough scientific tools become absorbed into commercial priorities. Links: Engadget
