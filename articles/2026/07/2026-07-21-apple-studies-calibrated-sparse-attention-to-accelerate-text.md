# Apple studies calibrated sparse attention to accelerate text-to-video generation

Apple published research on calibrated sparse attention for accelerating text-to-video diffusion models. The work identifies attention connections that consistently contribute little and can be skipped with limited impact on output quality. The practical relevance is clear: video generation is compute-intensive, and efficiency improvements could materially affect cost, latency, and on-device feasibility.
