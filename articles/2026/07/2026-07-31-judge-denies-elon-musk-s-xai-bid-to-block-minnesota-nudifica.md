# Judge denies Elon Musk's xAI bid to block Minnesota “nudification” ban

A federal judge denied xAI's request for a temporary restraining order to stop Minnesota's first-in-the-nation ban on AI “nudification” technology, which took effect Saturday, August 1. The ruling is an early test of state-level limits on generative-AI misuse. It sets up a broader legal fight over how far states can go in regulating AI-generated imagery.
