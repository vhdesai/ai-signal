# Meta’s AI spending pressures free cash flow while it explores compute monetization

Meta reported record revenue, but profit fell 14% to $18.3 billion as operating expenses rose sharply and free cash flow fell to $784 million from more than $12 billion in the prior quarter. The company raised full-year capex guidance to $130 billion and disclosed interest from third parties in leasing excess AI compute, including talks with Anthropic reportedly worth up to $10 billion. Meta’s challenge is increasingly clear: it is spending at hyperscaler scale before proving hyperscaler-style infrastructure monetization.
