# Apple launches 'Apple Upgrade' leasing program amid AI-driven component inflation

Apple introduced a Klarna-partnered U.S. leasing program that replaces the iPhone Upgrade Program. The move is framed partly as a response to rising memory and storage costs driven by AI data-center demand. It signals how AI-fueled supply pressure is beginning to reshape consumer hardware financing.
