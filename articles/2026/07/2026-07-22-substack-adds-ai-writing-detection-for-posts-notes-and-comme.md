# Substack adds AI-writing detection for posts, notes, and comments

TechCrunch reports that Substack integrated Pangram's AI-writing detection into its app, allowing users to estimate how much of a post, note, comment, or reply was written by AI. Substack says the feature is meant to encourage disclosure rather than penalize AI-assisted writing. The move is strategically notable because platforms are shifting from broad AI-content labels toward reader-facing transparency tools that can affect trust, creator reputation, and platform quality.
