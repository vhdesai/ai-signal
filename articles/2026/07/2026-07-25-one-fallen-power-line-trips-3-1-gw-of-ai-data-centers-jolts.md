# One Fallen Power Line Trips 3.1 GW of AI Data Centers, Jolts the Grid

Single failure outside DC caused 3.1+ GW to disconnect in ~30 seconds, sending a voltage spike from NoVA to Chicago. PJM projects data centers growing from 6% to 24% of load by 2040. Simultaneous defensive disconnects are a systemic grid risk. Strengthens the case for new power-control requirements.
