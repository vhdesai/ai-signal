# DOE Genesis Mission launches broad AI-for-science funding push

The U.S. Department of Energy's Genesis Mission awarded roughly $293 million across 278 projects to apply AI to scientific discovery, with coordinated announcements from MIT, Stanford/SLAC, Princeton, Cornell, and UT Austin, plus a $40 million in-kind commitment from Google DeepMind. Projects span fusion, materials, rare-earth extraction, digital twins, quantum sensors, and generative design. The program marks a major federal push to make AI an operating layer for science and energy innovation.
