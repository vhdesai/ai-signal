# Thinking Machines Releases Inkling-Small: 276B Open-Weights Model Rivaling Its 975B Predecessor

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling-Small, a 276B-parameter multimodal reasoning model under an Apache 2.0 license. Despite being about a quarter the size of the original 975B Inkling, it lands within a point of the larger model on the Artificial Analysis index. Its Mixture-of-Experts design activates only about 12B parameters per request while supporting a one-million-token context window. The permissive license makes it immediately attractive for enterprises that want self-hosting, fine-tuning, and tight control over data residency. By rapidly shrinking the size gap without losing much capability, Thinking Machines is pressuring closed-model vendors to justify premium pricing. Links: VentureBeat
