# Amazon's cloud growth helps justify another step-up in AI infrastructure spending

Amazon reported stronger-than-expected results, with AWS revenue up 37% year over year to $42 billion, while raising its 2026 capex forecast to $220 billion. Investors responded positively because AI spending is connected to an established cloud revenue engine rather than an uncertain standalone bet. The broader market signal is that AI capex remains acceptable when customers are already absorbing capacity through cloud demand.
