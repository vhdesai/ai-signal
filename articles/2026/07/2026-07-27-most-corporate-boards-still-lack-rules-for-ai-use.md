# Most corporate boards still lack rules for AI use

A Deloitte survey cited by CIO Dive found that many corporate boards still lack formal rules for AI use, even as directors begin using AI for board-level tasks. The deployment of AI in governance settings remains uneven and immature. That gap matters because boards are expected to oversee AI risk while many have not yet defined how they themselves should use AI tools.
