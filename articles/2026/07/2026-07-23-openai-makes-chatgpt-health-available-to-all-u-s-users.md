# OpenAI Makes ChatGPT Health Available to All U.S. Users

ChatGPT Health — assisting health queries with connected health records and Apple Health data — now available to all U.S. users 18+ across every plan. Pushes a consumer AI assistant directly into a regulated, high-liability domain. Expect heightened scrutiny on accuracy, privacy, and medical-advice boundaries.
