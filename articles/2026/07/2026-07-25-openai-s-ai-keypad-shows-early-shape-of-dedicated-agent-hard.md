# OpenAI's AI keypad shows early shape of dedicated agent hardware

TechCrunch tested OpenAI's new AI keypad, a physical interface aimed at users working with Codex and related agent workflows. The product appears most useful for developers and power users who want dedicated controls for agentic coding or desktop AI tasks, while remaining less obvious for mainstream users. The broader point is that AI interaction design is moving beyond chat windows into specialized hardware and workflow controls.
