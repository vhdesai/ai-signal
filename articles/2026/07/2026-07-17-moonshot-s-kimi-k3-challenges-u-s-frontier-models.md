# Moonshot's Kimi K3 challenges U.S. frontier models

The Information reports that Moonshot AI's new Kimi K3 challenges top U.S. frontier models, while related coverage says Kimi K3 may close the gap with Anthropic's Opus 4.8. The model is expected to be one of the largest open-weight models from China, with a parameter count reportedly between 2 trillion and 3 trillion. The executive takeaway is that Chinese open-weight models are rapidly becoming credible substitutes for expensive closed models in coding and enterprise evaluation settings.
