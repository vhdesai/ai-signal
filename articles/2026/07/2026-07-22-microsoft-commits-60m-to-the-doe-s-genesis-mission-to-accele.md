# Microsoft Commits $60M to the DOE's Genesis Mission to Accelerate AI for Science

Microsoft announced a $60 million commitment to the U.S. Department of Energy's Genesis Mission, aimed at using AI to accelerate scientific discovery across climate, materials science, and energy research. The investment deepens Microsoft's positioning in AI-for-science and ties its compute resources to national research priorities.
