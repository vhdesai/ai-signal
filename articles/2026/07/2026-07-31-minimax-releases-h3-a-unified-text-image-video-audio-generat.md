# MiniMax Releases H3, a Unified Text-Image-Video-Audio Generation Model

Chinese AI firm MiniMax released H3, a video-generation model that can process text, images, video and audio in a single system, intensifying competition in a market led by ByteDance and other Chinese labs. The launch continues the rapid multimodal cadence out of China and adds another credible option for enterprises evaluating generative video pipelines outside U.S. providers.
