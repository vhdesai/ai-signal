# Amazon says AWS growth accelerated to 37% as AI demand lifts cloud

Amazon reported that AWS revenue grew 37% in Q2, nine points faster than Q1, while AWS operating margin improved to roughly 39%. The company raised its 2026 capex outlook to $220 billion.
