# Inference Chips Pledged as Loan Collateral in a $400M Financing

General Compute secured a $400M loan from Upper90 using inference-specific chips (SambaNova SN50) as collateral — possibly the first such deal. The structure extends asset-backed lending from training GPUs to inference silicon, a sign that AI-infrastructure finance is maturing. Lenders increasingly treat AI compute as a financeable hard asset.
