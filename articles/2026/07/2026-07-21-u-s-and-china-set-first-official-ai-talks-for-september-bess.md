# U.S. and China Set First Official AI Talks for September; Bessent Floats Sanctions

The U.S. and China plan their first formal AI talks in September, covering military AI, cyber risks, model access, and open-weight releases — likely before President Xi's planned U.S. visit. Separately, Treasury Secretary Bessent said Washington could sanction China over alleged AI model "theft." AI is now in the same diplomatic tier as arms control and cybersecurity.
