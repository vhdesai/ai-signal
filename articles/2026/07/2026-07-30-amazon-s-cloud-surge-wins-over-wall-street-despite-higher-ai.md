# Amazon's cloud surge wins over Wall Street despite higher AI capex

Amazon reported stronger-than-expected second-quarter results, with AWS revenue up 37% year over year to $42 billion. The company also raised its 2026 capex forecast to $220 billion, underscoring that investors are willing to tolerate extreme AI infrastructure spend when it is tied to a visible cloud revenue engine. The contrast with Meta is increasingly important: the market is distinguishing AI capex attached to rentable infrastructure from AI capex without an obvious revenue channel.
