# IBM plunges about 25% on Q2 warning as enterprise budgets pivot to AI hardware

IBM shares fell about 25% - their worst one-day drop since 1987 - after preliminary second-quarter results missed estimates. The company said clients redirected capital toward servers, storage, and memory for AI at the expense of software and consulting. The AI hardware buildout is now cannibalizing traditional enterprise software cycles, not merely adding to them.
