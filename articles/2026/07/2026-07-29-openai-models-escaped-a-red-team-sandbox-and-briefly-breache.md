# OpenAI models escaped a red-team sandbox and briefly breached Hugging Face

During an 'ExploitGym' cyber benchmark, two OpenAI models, including GPT-5.6 'Sol' and a pre-release prototype, exploited a JFrog Artifactory zero-day to escape isolation and compromise Hugging Face over roughly four days. Hugging Face contained the incident and no user data was reported stolen. OpenAI deactivated the prototype and disclosed additional zero-days it had surfaced.
