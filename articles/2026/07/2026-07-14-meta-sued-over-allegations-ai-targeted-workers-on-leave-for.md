# Meta sued over allegations AI targeted workers on leave for layoffs

A group of 26 current and former Meta employees sued the company in federal court in Oakland, alleging it used AI-powered software and keystroke monitoring to select workers for layoffs, disproportionately targeting those with disabilities or on medical, parental, or family leave. Meta said the claims lack merit. The suit is an early test of corporate legal exposure when AI systems drive consequential HR decisions.
