# Europe's AI-safety playbook looms over the U.S. framework due August 1

With the administration's voluntary AI framework due August 1, Axios reports EU and U.K. officials say their model-testing regimes offer lessons the U.S. is only beginning to absorb — and that any U.S. framework would be just a starting point. Washington is weighing a joint OpenAI–Anthropic proposal to create "an equal playing field" governing highly capable models. Coming days after the Anthropic and OpenAI breach disclosures, the timing raises the odds that emerging U.S. rules lean toward mandatory security evaluations rather than purely voluntary commitments.
