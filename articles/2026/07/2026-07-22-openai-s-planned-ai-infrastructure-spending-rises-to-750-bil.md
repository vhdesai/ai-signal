# OpenAI's planned AI infrastructure spending rises to $750 billion

TechCrunch reports that OpenAI now plans to spend $750 billion on infrastructure through 2030, about 25% above an earlier estimate. The first major project cited is Project Camellia, a $20 billion Georgia data-center campus expected to draw at least 3.2 gigawatts from Georgia Power. The key executive implication is that frontier AI now depends on multi-year power procurement, local tax incentives, grid flexibility, and construction execution at a scale comparable to national industrial projects.
