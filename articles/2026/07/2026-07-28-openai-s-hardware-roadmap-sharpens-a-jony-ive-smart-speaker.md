# OpenAI’s hardware roadmap sharpens: a Jony Ive smart speaker and an AI phone

A supply-chain roundup detailed OpenAI’s device plans: a screenless smart speaker co-designed with Jony Ive (early 2027, around $200–$300, running ChatGPT/GPT-Live) and an “AI agent phone” targeting mass production in the first half of 2027 (MediaTek Dimensity 9600, TSMC N2P, Luxshare). The effort stems from OpenAI’s $6.5B acquisition of io and 400-plus ex-Apple hires — the same hiring that underpins Apple’s July 10 trade-secrets lawsuit. The roadmap positions OpenAI to compete directly in consumer AI hardware. MILESTONE
