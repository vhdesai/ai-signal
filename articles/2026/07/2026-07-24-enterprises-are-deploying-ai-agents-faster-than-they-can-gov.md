# Enterprises are deploying AI agents faster than they can govern them

A VentureBeat study of 573 enterprise leaders found that companies are moving agents into production before access controls, monitoring, and accountability processes have caught up. CIO Dive's weekender echoed the operational strain, highlighting banks changing workflows because of AI adoption and CIOs grappling with cost, architecture, and governance. The practical takeaway is that agent governance is becoming a prerequisite for scaling, not a cleanup step after deployment.
