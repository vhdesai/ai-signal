# AWS signs a ~$410M compute deal with Recursive Superintelligence

Richard Socher’s startup Recursive Superintelligence signed a multiyear AWS compute contract worth about $410 million — most of the $650 million it raised in May. The company builds “open-ended, self-improving systems” and is targeting its first products by October 2026, using AWS co-developed infrastructure. The agreement underscores how hyperscalers are locking in frontier-lab compute commitments.
