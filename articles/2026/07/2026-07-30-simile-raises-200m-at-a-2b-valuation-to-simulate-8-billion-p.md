# Simile raises $200M at a $2B valuation to “simulate 8 billion people”

Synthetic-user startup Simile raised a $200M+ Series B at roughly a $2B post-money valuation, led by Greenoaks, just five months after a $100M Series A. The company is building a foundation model that simulates about 8 billion people to test products, messaging, and policy, with customers including CVS and Gallup.
