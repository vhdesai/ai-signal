# Situational Awareness sells most of its public portfolio after AI-infrastructure losses

Situational Awareness, the hedge fund founded by former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner, reportedly sold most of its public stock portfolio to Citadel after steep losses in AI infrastructure equities. The fund had built a concentrated thesis around semiconductors, memory, compute, and energy infrastructure before a sharp pullback in several holdings. The episode is a cautionary signal that even a correct long-term AI thesis can be highly vulnerable to timing, valuation, and liquidity pressure.
