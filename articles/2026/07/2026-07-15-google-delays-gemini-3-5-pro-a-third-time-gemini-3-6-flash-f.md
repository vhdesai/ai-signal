# Google Delays Gemini 3.5 Pro a Third Time; Gemini 3.6 Flash Floated as Stopgap

Google DeepMind has postponed Gemini 3.5 Pro again — the third slip since its original June target — citing hallucinations and inconsistent outputs against GPT-5.6's bar. A lighter Gemini 3.6 Flash is floated as an interim release. GA reportedly targeted for July 17. The repeated delays underscore how difficult reliability at the frontier has become.
