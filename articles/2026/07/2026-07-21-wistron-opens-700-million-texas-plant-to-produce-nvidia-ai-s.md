# Wistron opens $700 million Texas plant to produce NVIDIA AI systems

NVIDIA says Wistron opened a 324,000-square-foot Fort Worth manufacturing plant producing NVIDIA GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra and future Vera Rubin systems. The facility represents a $700 million commitment and is expected to scale to tens of thousands of boards per month while creating up to 1,000 jobs. The strategic signal is that AI infrastructure supply chains are being localized and industrialized, not just scaled through chip orders.
