# Google Ships Three Lower-Cost Gemini Models; Flagship 3.5 Pro Stays Delayed

Google released Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber (vulnerability detection, initially limited to governments), Gemini 3.6 Flash (17% fewer tokens on coding tasks), and 3.5 Flash-Lite. The continued absence of flagship 3.5 Pro raises roadmap questions even as compute is directed to Gemini 4. The push toward lower price-per-token is a direct response to cheap Chinese open-weight models like Kimi K3.
