# AMD locks up 529 MW of data-center capacity from Core Scientific in $14B, 15-year deal

AMD secured more than 529 megawatts of U.S. AI data-center capacity from former bitcoin miner Core Scientific under 15-year leases worth more than $14B in base contracted revenue — AMD's largest infrastructure commitment to date — with an option to reserve up to ~1,925 MW more through 2028 and warrants for up to 30M Core Scientific shares. Customer deployments begin in 2027. The move signals AMD competing with Nvidia on the physical layer (power, land, grid), not just silicon, as power availability becomes the binding constraint on AI compute.
