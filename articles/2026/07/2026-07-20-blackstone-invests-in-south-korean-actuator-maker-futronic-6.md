# Blackstone Invests in South Korean Actuator Maker Futronic (~$676M Valuation)

Blackstone invested in Futronic, a maker of high-precision actuators for automotive and industrial robotics, at ~$676M. Pushes institutional capital past AI software into the physical robotics component supply chain — the actuation and motion-control hardware that humanoid and industrial robots depend on.
