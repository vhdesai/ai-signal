# Multiverse Computing raises ~$570M Series C at a ~$1.7B valuation

Spain's Multiverse Computing raised a $570M (€500M) Series C to compress AI models for edge-to-cloud deployment, reaching unicorn status at roughly $1.7B. The round highlights strong investor appetite for "efficient AI" that cuts inference cost and hardware footprint. It positions model-compression as a distinct, fundable layer of the stack.
