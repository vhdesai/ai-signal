# xAI sues Minnesota over first-in-nation AI 'nudify' ban

xAI filed a federal suit challenging Minnesota's HF 1606, which carries penalties up to $500,000 per violation, as an overbroad restriction on speech. The suit lands amid separate class-action complaints over Grok-generated deepfakes. It sets up an early court fight over state-level AI content regulation.
