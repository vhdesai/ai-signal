# Fireworks AI Raises $1.5B Series D at $17.5B Valuation

The largest U.S. venture round of the week. Fireworks, which helps enterprises turn general-purpose models into specialized systems on their own data, raised $1.505B led by Atreides, Index, and TCV. Reflects investor conviction that the customization and inference-tooling layer — not just foundation models — is where durable enterprise value accrues.
