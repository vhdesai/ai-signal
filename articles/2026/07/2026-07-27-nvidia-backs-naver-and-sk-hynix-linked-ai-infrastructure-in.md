# Nvidia backs Naver and SK Hynix-linked AI infrastructure in South Korea

Nvidia said it would invest $1 billion in South Korea’s Naver to help expand AI data-center infrastructure, while Brookfield plans up to $9 billion more. The Information also reported that Nvidia and SK Group engineers plan to collaborate on high-bandwidth memory and AI cloud capacity. Nvidia is increasingly acting as a systems integrator for sovereign AI, helping customers secure land, power, memory, and financing rather than simply selling GPUs.
