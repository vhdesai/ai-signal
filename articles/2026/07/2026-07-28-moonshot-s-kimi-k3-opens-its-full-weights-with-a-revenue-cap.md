# Moonshot's Kimi K3 opens its full weights — with a revenue-capped license

Moonshot AI published the full weights of Kimi K3, a ~2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model, making it the largest open-weight model released to date. VentureBeat flags an important caveat for enterprises: the license permits commercial use only up to roughly $20 million in annual revenue, above which a negotiated agreement with Moonshot is required — a meaningful constraint for larger adopters. Practical deployment is also demanding, with the model needing on the order of 1.5 TB of VRAM, and it has drawn U.S. government scrutiny over how it was trained.
