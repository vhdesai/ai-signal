# Indonesia copyright rewrite puts Google and AI platforms on notice

Reuters reports that Indonesia's copyright rewrite is putting Google and AI platforms on notice. The issue reflects a global shift from litigation-led copyright fights toward statutory updates that may define how AI systems can use protected content. For companies deploying or training models internationally, country-specific copyright regimes are becoming a growing compliance and product-design risk.
