# Thinking Machines releases Inkling, a 975B-parameter open-weight model

Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling, a 975B-parameter mixture-of-experts open-weight model with 41B active parameters, a 1M-token context window, and a Tinker customization toolkit. The model is positioned as a leading U.S. open-weight enterprise alternative, giving regulated buyers an inspectable and self-hostable option. While it reportedly still trails the strongest Chinese open models on some benchmarks, it gives the U.S. a serious new entrant in the open-weight race.
