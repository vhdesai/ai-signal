# Microsoft reportedly trains sales teams to compete more directly against OpenAI and Anthropic

Microsoft is reportedly coaching salespeople to position its in-house AI models as more efficient and cost-effective than offerings from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The shift is notable because Microsoft has long depended on OpenAI and Anthropic models across parts of its AI portfolio. It signals a more explicit move from partner-led model access toward Microsoft-owned model economics and full-stack enterprise positioning.
