# SpaceX won't remove xAI's unpermitted Colossus turbines for another year

SpaceX is building a new power plant to feed xAI's Colossus data centers but told regulators it won't remove the existing, unpermitted gas turbines currently powering the site for many more months. The disclosure highlights how power availability — and permitting — has become the binding constraint on frontier-scale compute, with AI operators improvising on-site generation ahead of grid interconnection.
