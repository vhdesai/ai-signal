# Pentagon official blasts OpenAI's Dean Ball over regulation stance

A senior Trump-administration official and a key adviser publicly criticized OpenAI's head of strategic futures, Dean Ball, over his views on AI regulation. The rift could complicate OpenAI's government relationships and defense-related work. It underscores the political sensitivity of AI-policy positioning for frontier labs seeking federal contracts and public-market credibility.
