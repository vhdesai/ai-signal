# Moonshot's Kimi K3 Becomes the Largest Open-Weight Model Ever Released

Moonshot made Kimi K3 weights available at 00:00 UTC July 27 — 2.8 trillion parameters, ~1.4 TB download. Independent assessments: ahead of other open models, strong on coding/agentic tasks, still trailing top closed models on general capability. The size means immediate beneficiaries are large teams and inference providers. U.S. officials have alleged the model was "distilled" from a rival — a claim outside experts have disputed.
