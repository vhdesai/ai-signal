# DoorDash opens CLI beta for ordering through developers and AI agents

DoorDash introduced a limited beta of dd-cli, a command-line tool that lets developers and AI agents search stores, find deals, build carts, and check out. While the interface is deliberately playful, the product is a real example of commerce infrastructure being exposed for agentic use. The broader signal is that consumer marketplaces are preparing for AI agents as transaction initiators, not just recommendation interfaces.
