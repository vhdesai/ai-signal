# Intel Commits €5B ($5.7B) to Expand Its Leixlip, Ireland Fab for AI Chips

Intel announced €5 billion to expand Fab 34 at Leixlip, upgrading fabrication for Xeon 6 and next-gen Xeon processors on its Intel 3 node. It follows Intel's $14.2 billion April buyback of the Apollo stake in the same plant. Intel has now invested more than €30 billion in Ireland since 1989. The investment is a foundry-strategy statement as much as a capacity one.
