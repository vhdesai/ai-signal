# Thinking Machines cofounder Lilian Weng returns to OpenAI for recursive self-improvement work

Lilian Weng, a cofounder of Thinking Machines Lab and former OpenAI safety research leader, is returning to OpenAI, The Information reports. She will reportedly work on using AI models to develop new models, also known as recursive self-improvement research. Her return is notable because it follows departures from Thinking Machines and because recursive improvement sits near the frontier of both capability acceleration and safety concern.
