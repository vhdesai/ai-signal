# AMD takes on NVIDIA with Helios rack-scale AI system

AMD unveiled Helios, a rack-scale AI system aimed at the largest model labs and hyperscale deployments. TechCrunch reports that OpenAI, Meta, Oracle, Anthropic, and Microsoft are among customers or planned users, and that Anthropic and AMD separately announced plans to deploy up to two gigawatts of AMD Instinct MI450-series GPUs. The launch shows AMD trying to compete at the rack and system level, not just the chip level, as agentic workloads drive demand for programmable accelerator capacity.
