# Lilian Weng returns to OpenAI for recursive self-improvement work

Lilian Weng, a cofounder of Thinking Machines Lab and former OpenAI safety research leader, is returning to OpenAI. She will reportedly work on using AI models to help develop new models, commonly referred to as recursive self-improvement research. The move is both a talent-war signal and an indicator that OpenAI is prioritizing automated research workflows as a frontier capability.
