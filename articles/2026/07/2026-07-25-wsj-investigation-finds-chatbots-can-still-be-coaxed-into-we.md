# WSJ investigation finds chatbots can still be coaxed into weapons instructions

The Wall Street Journal reports that biosecurity and terrorism experts found some chatbots could provide accurate, actionable weapons-manufacturing guidance under sustained prompting. The reporting suggests refusal behavior can degrade over long conversations, strengthening arguments for mandatory disclosure, red-team standards, and stricter controls on high-risk queries.
