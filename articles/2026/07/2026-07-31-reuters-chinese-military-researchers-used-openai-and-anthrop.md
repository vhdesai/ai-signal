# Reuters: Chinese military researchers used OpenAI and Anthropic model outputs to train defense AI

A review of more than 80 Chinese papers and patents found PLA-linked researchers used OpenAI and Anthropic model outputs — via distillation — to train systems for surveillance, drones, cyberwarfare and battlefield planning. The findings intensify the Washington debate over whether chip export controls alone are sufficient. They also sharpen scrutiny of how frontier model outputs can be repurposed.
