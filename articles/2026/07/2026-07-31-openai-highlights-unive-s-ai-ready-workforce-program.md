# OpenAI Highlights Unive's AI-Ready Workforce Program

OpenAI spotlighted Italian insurer Unive as an example of an enterprise focusing on workforce readiness rather than tool access alone. The case emphasizes training, governance, and role-based adoption at a moment when many companies are moving from pilots into scaled programs. That matters because productivity gains increasingly depend on process integration, new job expectations, and accountable measurement. The story aligns closely with the Uber and Cognizant examples, where organizational redesign appears to be driving more value than raw model capability. For CIOs, the implication is that enablement and change-management budgets may need to rival or exceed model-licensing spend. Links: OpenAI
