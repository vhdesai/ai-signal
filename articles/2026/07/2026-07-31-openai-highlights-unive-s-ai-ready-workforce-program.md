# OpenAI highlights Unive's AI-ready workforce program

OpenAI published a customer story on Unive building an AI-ready workforce, pointing to the continued enterprise focus on training, change management, and governed adoption rather than tool deployment alone. The timing is notable because many companies are moving from AI pilots to role-based usage programs that require operating-model change. The executive takeaway is that productivity gains depend less on access to models than on workflow integration, employee enablement, and measurable business outcomes.
