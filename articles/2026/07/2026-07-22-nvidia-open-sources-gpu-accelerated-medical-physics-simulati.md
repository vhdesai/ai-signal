# NVIDIA open-sources GPU-accelerated medical physics simulation framework

NVIDIA announced Medical Physics Simulation, an open-source GPU-accelerated framework within Isaac for Healthcare for modeling anatomy-device interactions, sensor inputs, and healthcare robot policies. The framework is designed to generate hard-to-capture scenarios and evaluate medical robotics systems before hardware-heavy testing. This is a concrete example of physical AI infrastructure expanding into regulated, evidence-heavy domains where simulation, transparency, and synthetic data can shorten development cycles.
