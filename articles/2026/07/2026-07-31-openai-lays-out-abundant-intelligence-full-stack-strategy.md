# OpenAI lays out 'abundant intelligence' full-stack strategy

OpenAI published a strategy note outlining a 'full-stack' approach to what it calls abundant intelligence — spanning models, infrastructure, and pricing to make advanced AI cheaper and more widely useful. The framing pairs directly with its price cuts and user milestone, casting cost reduction as deliberate platform strategy rather than a reaction to competition. Leaders should read it as OpenAI signaling a long-term commitment to commoditizing capability while owning the distribution layer.
