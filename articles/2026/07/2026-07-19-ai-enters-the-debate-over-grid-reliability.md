# AI enters the debate over grid reliability

WSJ published an opinion article arguing that AI can be a fast track to grid reliability. The timing is notable because AI is simultaneously increasing data-center power demand and being positioned as a tool for grid optimization, forecasting, and resilience. Policy teams should expect AI infrastructure debates to include both sides of that ledger: AI as a grid load and AI as a grid-management technology. URL behind paywall.
