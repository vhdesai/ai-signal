# Moonshot plans a $30B Hong Kong IPO within six months

Moonshot AI, maker of the Kimi models, is preparing to list in Hong Kong within roughly six months and is wrapping a funding round that could value the three-year-old startup above $30 billion. The move follows a recent model breakthrough and reflects a reopening of AI capital markets in Asia. A successful listing would set an early public-market benchmark for Chinese frontier-model valuations.
