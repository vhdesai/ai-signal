# Moonshot AI adds pressure to the chip selloff

WSJ reports that Moonshot AI's model release added fuel to Wall Street's chip selloff. The market reaction reflects investor concern that more efficient open-weight models could reduce the need for ever-larger frontier training clusters or weaken the pricing power of AI infrastructure suppliers. While that conclusion may be premature, the episode shows how model architecture and cost curves can now move semiconductor equities. URL behind paywall.
