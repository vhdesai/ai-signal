# Big Tech earnings diverge on AI: Amazon rallies ~11%, Apple falls ~8%

Quarterly results split Big Tech along AI lines: Amazon jumped roughly 11% on accelerating AWS growth and heavy AI investment, while Apple fell about 8% on a forecast that underwhelmed investors.
