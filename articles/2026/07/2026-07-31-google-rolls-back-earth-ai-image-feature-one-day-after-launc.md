# Google rolls back Earth AI image feature one day after launch

Google rolled back a Google Earth feature that let users use Nano Banana 2 to generate fabricated images inside its mapping interface after criticism that it could spread misinformation. The incident is notable because geospatial imagery is often treated as evidentiary by journalists, researchers, and policymakers. It highlights the need for stronger provenance and guardrails when generative tools are embedded into trusted information products.
