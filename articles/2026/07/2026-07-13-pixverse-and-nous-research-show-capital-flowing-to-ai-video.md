# PixVerse and Nous Research show capital flowing to AI video and open agents

Singapore-based AI video startup PixVerse raised $439M, pushing valuation above $2B on roughly 15M monthly active users. Separately, Nous Research, maker of the Hermes agent, is reportedly finalizing at least $75M at a $1.5B valuation. The paired stories show funding moving into application-layer AI and open-agent infrastructure rather than only foundation-model labs.
