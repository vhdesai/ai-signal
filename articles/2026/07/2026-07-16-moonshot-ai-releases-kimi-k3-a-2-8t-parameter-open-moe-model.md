# Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3, a 2.8T-Parameter Open MoE Model

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open MoE model that activates 16 of 896 experts per token and introduces "Kimi Delta Attention" with a 1M-token context window. Early leaderboards place it at or near the top for frontend coding, positioning it as a direct open-weight alternative to GPT-5.6 and Claude. The launch extends China's open-weight momentum as Moonshot's valuation reportedly reaches ~$31.5B after a $2B raise.
