# Google delays Gemini 3.5 Pro again as reliability pressure rises

Google DeepMind reportedly delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro for the third time since its original June target, with reports citing hallucinations and inconsistent outputs against the bar set by OpenAI's GPT-5.6. A lighter Gemini 3.6 Flash has been floated as a stopgap, while general availability is reportedly targeted for July 17. The delay underscores that reliability at the frontier is becoming harder, not easier, even for the largest labs.
