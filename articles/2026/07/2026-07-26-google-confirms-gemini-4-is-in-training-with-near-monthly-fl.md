# Google confirms Gemini 4 is in training, with near-monthly Flash cadence

On Alphabet's Q2 earnings call, Sundar Pichai said Google is "now training Gemini 4," calling it the company's "most ambitious pre-training run yet." Google is also targeting Gemini Flash updates at "almost a monthly cadence," with Gemini 4 expected around November–December 2026. The comments signal an accelerated release tempo as Google presses its frontier roadmap.
