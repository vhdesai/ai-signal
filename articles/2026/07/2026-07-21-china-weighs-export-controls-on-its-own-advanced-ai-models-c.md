# China weighs export controls on its own advanced AI models, chips, and data

China is reportedly consulting Alibaba, ByteDance, Zhipu, and others about restricting foreign access to advanced models, semiconductor technology, and training data. The move mirrors U.S. tactics by treating Chinese AI IP as a strategic national asset. It could affect developers and enterprises building on Chinese open-weight models, especially if Beijing narrows cross-border access.
