# Google's Gemini nears one billion monthly users

TechCrunch reports that Gemini now has more than 950 million monthly users, up from 750 million in February. Google said the app has benefited from agentic features such as Daily Brief and Gemini Spark, while AI Mode in Search has crossed one billion users. The adoption data strengthens Google's position against ChatGPT and shows that distribution through existing consumer platforms remains a major AI advantage.
