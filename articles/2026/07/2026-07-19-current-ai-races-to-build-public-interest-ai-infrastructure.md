# Current AI races to build public-interest AI infrastructure

TechCrunch profiles Current AI, a nonprofit building open public AI infrastructure intended to serve languages and communities underserved by commercial models. The organization has $400 million in committed funding and is supporting projects across India, Kenya, Lebanon, Brazil, and Japan, including offline multilingual devices and open-source chatbot infrastructure. The story is notable because it frames AI infrastructure as a public good, not only a hyperscaler or frontier-lab business.
