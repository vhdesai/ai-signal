# Nvidia positions Nemotron open models for enterprise control and customization

Nvidia published a Nemotron Labs post arguing that enterprises and nations need open models they can inspect, customize, and evaluate against their own business outcomes. The post highlights examples from Abridge, Glean, Harvey, Heidi Health, and others using Nemotron or related open-stack approaches to reduce cost and tune for specialized domains. Nvidia is framing enterprise AI ownership as a platform strategy spanning models, evaluation, customization, and inference infrastructure.
