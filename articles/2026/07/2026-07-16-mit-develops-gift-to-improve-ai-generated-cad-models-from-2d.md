# MIT develops GIFT to improve AI-generated CAD models from 2D designs

MIT researchers introduced GIFT, a framework that helps vision-language models convert 2D designs into executable CAD programs more accurately and with less computation. The system learns from a model's near-misses and successful outputs, generating targeted training data without human correction. The work could reduce prototyping costs and make AI-assisted engineering design more reliable for physical products.
