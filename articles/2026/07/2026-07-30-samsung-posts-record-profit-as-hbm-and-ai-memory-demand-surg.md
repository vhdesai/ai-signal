# Samsung Posts Record Profit as HBM and AI Memory Demand Surges

Samsung reported record quarterly results, with its Device Solutions semiconductor division delivering 89.2 trillion won in operating profit on 127.5 trillion won in sales, driven by DRAM, HBM and NAND for AI servers. The company shipped initial HBM4E samples and expanded HBM4 volumes, and expects tight supply and strong server demand to persist into 2027. The results underline how memory has become the binding constraint of the AI buildout — and how profits are concentrating among a handful of suppliers. M INFRACHIPS
