# DeepSeek moves V4-Flash-0731 into public beta with major agentic and coding gains

DeepSeek published the official build of its efficiency model, DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, on Hugging Face and moved the API into public beta with the call method unchanged. It reports notable agentic and coding gains — about 82.7 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 54.4 on DeepSWE — paired with aggressive pricing observers peg near a $0.28 agentic-output floor. Landing within 48 hours of OpenAI's cuts, it intensifies price and capability pressure from open-weight Chinese labs. (TechNode corroborated the beta launch.)
