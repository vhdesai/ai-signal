# BlackRock–MGX Consortium Commits Another $5B to Aligned Data Centers

BlackRock and Abu Dhabi's MGX committed $5B in growth capital to Aligned Data Centers after closing its ~$40B acquisition. Aligned runs 51 campuses with 6.4+ GW of planned capacity. AI data centers becoming an institutional asset class — total investment capacity could reach $100B including debt.
