# Chai Discovery raises $400M as WSJ questions AI drug discovery timelines

OpenAI-backed Chai Discovery raised $400M at a $3.8B valuation, with Novartis joining Pfizer and Lilly engagements. At the same time, WSJ asked whether AI can make better drugs on Wall Street's timeline, highlighting the gap between speeding known workflows and discovering what humans do not yet know. The combined signal: AI drug discovery is attracting serious capital, but investors are starting to scrutinize proof points and time-to-return.
