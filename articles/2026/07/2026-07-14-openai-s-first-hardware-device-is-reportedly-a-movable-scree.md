# OpenAI's first hardware device is reportedly a movable, screenless speaker

TechCrunch, citing Bloomberg, reports that OpenAI's first hardware device is being developed as a screenless, movable smart speaker intended to act as a home AI companion. The device reportedly draws on former Apple hardware talent and is designed to learn from a user's digital life, including email and other personal context. It points to a broader industry bet that consumer AI hardware will be ambient, personalized, and companion-like rather than another conventional screen.
