# Anthropic Launches Claude Opus 5, a Cheaper Agent-Focused Flagship

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, aimed at long-running agents, coding, and professional work. Positioned as both cheaper and less restrictive than Fable 5, with roughly half the cost of comparable premium models. Lands as Anthropic reportedly prepares a 2026 public listing — a clear play to win enterprise agent workloads on price-performance.
