# Netflix paid $587M for Ben Affleck's AI filmmaking startup

A new Netflix regulatory filing revealed it paid $587 million in cash for InterPositive, an AI filmmaking startup co-founded by actor-director Ben Affleck. The disclosure puts a price on a previously announced deal and ranks among the larger acquisitions targeting generative tools for film production. It signals continued consolidation of AI creative tooling inside major studios and streamers.
