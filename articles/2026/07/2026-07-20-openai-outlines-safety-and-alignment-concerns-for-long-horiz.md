# OpenAI outlines safety and alignment concerns for long-horizon models

OpenAI published guidance on safety and alignment in an era of long-horizon models, focused on risks observed when systems run for longer periods and take more complex actions. The official blog frames iterative deployment, failure analysis, and improved safeguards as necessary for models that can sustain work over time. The executive implication is that agentic AI risk management must cover persistence, tool use, and accumulated decision chains—not just single-turn model outputs.
