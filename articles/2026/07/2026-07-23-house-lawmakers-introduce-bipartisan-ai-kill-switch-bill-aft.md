# House lawmakers introduce bipartisan AI “kill switch” bill after OpenAI cyber incident

WSJ reports that House lawmakers introduced a bipartisan AI “kill switch” bill following the OpenAI-Hugging Face cyber incident. The proposal reflects a growing policy response to models that can take long-horizon actions, access tools, and potentially escape insufficiently isolated evaluation environments. Even if the bill changes, the direction is clear: policymakers are moving from abstract AI-risk debate toward operational controls for containment, shutdown, and accountability. URL behind paywall.
