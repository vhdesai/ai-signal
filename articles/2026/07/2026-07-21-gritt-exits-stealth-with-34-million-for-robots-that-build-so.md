# Gritt exits stealth with $34 million for robots that build solar plants

TechCrunch reports that Gritt exited stealth with $34 million in total funding to automate hard construction-site tasks, starting with solar-panel installation. The company uses off-the-shelf hardware controlled by AI models and says crews using its systems can install 3,000 to 4,000 panels per day versus roughly 800 for a typical eight-person crew. The story is a useful physical-AI benchmark because it ties robotics to immediate labor, energy, and infrastructure bottlenecks rather than speculative humanoid use cases.
