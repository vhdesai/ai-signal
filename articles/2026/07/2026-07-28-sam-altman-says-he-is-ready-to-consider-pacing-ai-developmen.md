# Sam Altman says he is ready to consider pacing AI development

TechCrunch reports that Sam Altman said the world may need to pace AI development so society can harden around new capability levels, citing the OpenAI-Hugging Face incident as the first security event he felt “very viscerally.” Both OpenAI and Anthropic also backed a petition calling for technical and governance tools to deliberately pace frontier automated AI development. The shift is significant because OpenAI had previously resisted pause-style arguments; the question now is how to pace without regulatory capture or collusion among frontier labs.
