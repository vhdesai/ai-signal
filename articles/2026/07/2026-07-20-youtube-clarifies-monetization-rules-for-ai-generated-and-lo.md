# YouTube clarifies monetization rules for AI-generated and low-quality content

TechCrunch reports that YouTube clarified its policies around AI-generated and low-quality videos that cannot earn ad revenue. The new guidance targets repetitive template content, distressing or manipulative videos, and AI personas discussing sensitive topics such as finance, legal issues, and health. The shift shows major platforms tightening the economics of low-cost AI content farming rather than only labeling AI-generated media.
