# SpaceX will not remove all xAI unpermitted turbines until 2027

TechCrunch reports that SpaceX will not remove all unpermitted gas turbines supporting xAI's Memphis-area Colossus data-center operations until 2027 while permanent power infrastructure is built. The story underscores that AI infrastructure constraints are not limited to GPUs; power procurement, permitting, emissions, and local opposition are becoming strategic bottlenecks. For enterprise buyers and investors, energy compliance is now part of AI vendor diligence.
