# AI's wider availability puts pressure on OpenAI and Anthropic

WSJ reports that AI's broader availability is good for China but creates new pressure for OpenAI and Anthropic. The core issue is commoditization: if high-quality open models become widely accessible, premium closed-model providers must justify higher pricing through reliability, distribution, ecosystem lock-in, safety, or workflow integration. For enterprise buyers, the story strengthens the case for multi-model architectures and cost benchmarking. URL behind paywall.
