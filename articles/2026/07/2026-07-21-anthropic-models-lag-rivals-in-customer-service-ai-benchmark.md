# Anthropic models lag rivals in customer-service AI benchmarks

The Information reports that Anthropic's models, while strong at coding, are less capable than some rivals at powering AI voice and chat applications for customer service, based on data from NiCE. The finding matters because enterprise AI adoption is splitting by workflow: coding leadership does not automatically translate into contact-center leadership. Buyers should evaluate model families against task-specific benchmarks rather than assuming one frontier model dominates every domain.
