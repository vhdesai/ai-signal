# ByteDance launches Seedance 2.5 video-generation model

ByteDance released Seedance 2.5, capable of generating 30-second high-quality clips with new multi-input capabilities. It builds on Seedance 2.0's strong text-to-video benchmark results and lands the same day as MiniMax's H3, underscoring an intense Chinese race in AI video.
