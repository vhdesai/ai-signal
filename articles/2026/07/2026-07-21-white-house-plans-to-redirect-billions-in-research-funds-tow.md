# White House plans to redirect billions in research funds toward AI

WSJ reports that the White House plans to redirect billions in research funds toward AI and away from colleges. The move reflects a broader federal effort to treat AI as strategic infrastructure and a national-competitiveness priority. It could accelerate applied AI, defense, and compute-related programs, but may also deepen friction with universities if traditional research channels are bypassed or deprioritized.
