# IBM Plunges ~25% on Q2 Warning as Enterprise Budgets Pivot to AI Hardware

IBM shares fell ~25% — its worst single-day drop since 1987 — after preliminary Q2 results missed estimates. CEO Krishna attributed the miss to clients redirecting capital toward servers, storage, and memory for AI, at the expense of software and consulting. The read-through: the AI hardware build-out is now cannibalizing traditional enterprise software cycles, not merely adding to them.
