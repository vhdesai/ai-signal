# Advocates tell Google to fix AI Mode or leave classrooms

Mashable reports that advocates are calling on Google to fix AI Mode or get out of classrooms. The concern follows broader criticism that AI search features can expose children to unsafe or inappropriate answers. For education and platform leaders, the issue is that classroom AI products must be evaluated against child-safety standards, not only general search or assistant benchmarks.
