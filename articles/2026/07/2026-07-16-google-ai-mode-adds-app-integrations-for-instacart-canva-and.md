# Google AI Mode adds app integrations for Instacart, Canva, and YouTube

Google AI Mode now lets U.S. users link and interact with select apps including Instacart, Canva, and YouTube. The shift moves AI Mode from conversational answers toward task completion, such as adding groceries to an Instacart cart or creating Canva templates. This is a direct response to the same app-integration logic driving ChatGPT and Claude, and it reinforces search as an agentic workflow surface.
