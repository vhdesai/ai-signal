# OpenAI expands European footprint with new Dublin HQ and ~250 jobs

OpenAI announced a new EU headquarters in Dublin and 250 additional jobs, deepening its European operational and regulatory presence. The expansion strengthens OpenAI's ability to engage with EU AI Act implementation and localize enterprise sales, safety, and policy functions. Ireland continues to serve as the European base for major US AI and cloud firms. The move signals OpenAI's intent to institutionalize its footprint in the bloc rather than manage Europe remotely.
