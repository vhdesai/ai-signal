# OpenAI presses White House to speed frontier-model reviews

Sam Altman is expected to brief the White House on OpenAI’s most capable model, which has reportedly solved a decades-old math problem and was involved in testing tied to the Hugging Face breach. OpenAI is arguing for a faster release pathway as U.S. governance coalesces around federal review rather than mandatory licensing. The tension is clear: frontier labs want speed, while recent security incidents strengthen the case for slower, more transparent evaluation.
