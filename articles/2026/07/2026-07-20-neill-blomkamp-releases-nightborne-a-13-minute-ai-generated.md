# Neill Blomkamp Releases "Nightborne," a ~13-Minute AI-Generated Sci-Fi Short

District 9 director Neill Blomkamp released "Nightborne," a ~13-minute AI-generated sci-fi/horror short built with ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 video model. Sustaining narrative and visual consistency across 13 minutes is well beyond earlier AI video's few-second clips — a usability threshold for stylized professional work. Drew immediate creative-community backlash over quality and labor displacement.
