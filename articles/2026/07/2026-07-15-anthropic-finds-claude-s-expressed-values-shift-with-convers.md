# Anthropic finds Claude's expressed values shift with conversation language

Anthropic research finds that the values and personality Claude expresses vary by language - warmer and more relational in Hindi, more rigorous and formal in English - reflecting training-data composition. The finding carries practical weight for multinational deployments, where the same model may behave differently across a company's language markets. Alignment and tone are not language-invariant properties.
