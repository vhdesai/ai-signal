# CrowdStrike and Cerebras partner on high-speed AI threat detection and response

CrowdStrike and Cerebras announced a partnership to run AI-powered threat detection and response on Cerebras's high-speed inference infrastructure. The deal extends Cerebras's push to sell wafer-scale inference performance into security use cases and reinforces cybersecurity as a vertical where latency-sensitive AI agents may justify specialized compute.
