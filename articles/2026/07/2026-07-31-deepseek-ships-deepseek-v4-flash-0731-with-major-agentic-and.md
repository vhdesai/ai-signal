# DeepSeek ships DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 with major agentic and coding gains

DeepSeek published DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 — a 284B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model (13B active, 1M-token context) — on Hugging Face and moved the official V4-Flash API into public beta. The gains come from re-post-training on an unchanged architecture, yet the model reportedly beats the earlier V4-Pro preview on every published agentic benchmark at roughly a third of the output price ($0.28 per 1M output tokens). The release is widely read as a pricing countermove landing two days after OpenAI's 80% cut on GPT-5.6. Benchmarks are vendor-reported, so independent evaluation is advised.
