# MIT Students Connect Research Priorities to Capitol Hill Policy

MIT said 25 students and postdocs met with 62 congressional offices to discuss research funding, higher education, and science policy priorities. Although not limited to AI, the effort is directly relevant because compute access, chip controls, and technical immigration now shape AI competitiveness. The story reflects a growing recognition that researchers need to engage policymakers rather than leaving technical questions to non-specialists. It also shows how academic institutions increasingly view Washington decisions as central to the future of AI infrastructure and talent development. For industry leaders, that advocacy is a reminder that the AI race is being decided by budgets and rules as much as by labs and models. Links: MIT News
