# Samsung Pulls First Yongin Fab Forward to 2029 to Chase AI Memory Demand

Samsung will begin operations at the first of six planned fabs in its Yongin cluster one to two years ahead of the prior 2030–2031 schedule. The acceleration sits inside Korea's ~$880B national commitment to chips, data centers, and robotics. Land, power, and water provisioning — not fab tooling — remain the pacing constraints.
