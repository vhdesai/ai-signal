# Anduril reportedly seeks funding at a roughly $100B valuation

Anduril is reportedly in talks to raise capital at a valuation around $100 billion, more than triple its mark from a year earlier. The round would show that autonomy and defense AI are attracting frontier-lab-scale capital, not just traditional defense financing. It also suggests AI-enabled defense capabilities are consolidating around a small number of well-funded platforms.
