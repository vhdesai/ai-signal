# Apple beats but guides soft in Tim Cook's final quarter as CEO

Apple posted fiscal Q3 revenue of $109.4B and EPS of $2.02, topping estimates on a 22% jump in iPhone sales, but issued weak current-quarter guidance citing supply constraints — sending the stock down more than 6% after hours. The report was Tim Cook's last as chief executive, layering a leadership-transition overhang onto an already cautious outlook. Set against Amazon's blowout, the divergent reactions underscored how sharply investors are now discriminating between clear AI beneficiaries and companies with murkier AI monetization.
