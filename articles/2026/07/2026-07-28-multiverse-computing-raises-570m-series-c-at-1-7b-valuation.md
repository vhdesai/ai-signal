# Multiverse Computing raises $570M Series C at $1.7B valuation for model compression

Spain's Multiverse Computing raised a $570M Series C — co-led by Forgepoint Capital International, BNPP SIVF and Bullhound Capital — at a $1.7B pre-money valuation, nearly a 5x step-up from its June 2025 round. Its CompactifAI technology uses tensor networks drawn from quantum physics to shrink LLMs by up to 80–95% with minimal accuracy loss, enabling on-device and on-prem inference; customers include Allianz, Bosch, PwC and Telefónica. The raise is a bet that model efficiency, not just scale, is where enterprise AI economics will be won. FA FUNDING
