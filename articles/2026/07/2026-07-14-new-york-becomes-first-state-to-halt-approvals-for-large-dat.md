# New York becomes first state to halt approvals for large data centers

York Gov. Kathy Hochul signed an executive order temporarily halting approvals for new data centers of 50 megawatts or larger while the state develops an environmental review process. The order reflects concerns over electricity costs, water use, noise, local zoning, and public backlash tied to AI infrastructure growth. The decision turns data-center siting into a state-level policy precedent and may complicate AI capacity planning for hyperscalers and cloud customers.
