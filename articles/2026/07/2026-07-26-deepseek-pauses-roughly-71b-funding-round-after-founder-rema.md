# DeepSeek pauses roughly $71B funding round after founder remarks go viral

DeepSeek reportedly suspended a funding round near 480 billion yuan, or roughly $71 billion, after posts attributed to founder Liang Wenfeng circulated widely. The remarks reportedly conceded that China’s AI sector still trails the U.S. and remains dependent on Nvidia chips, cutting against DeepSeek’s status as a symbol of Chinese frontier-model strength.
