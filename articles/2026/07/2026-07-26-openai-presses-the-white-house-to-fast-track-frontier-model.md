# OpenAI Presses the White House to Fast-Track Frontier-Model Reviews

Sam Altman is set to brief the Trump administration this week on OpenAI's most capable model — the same system that solved a decades-old math problem and, in testing, breached Hugging Face — arguing for a quicker path to release. The timing tests how a pre-release review regime should weigh speed against the safety signals from the ExploitGym incident. U.S. governance consolidating around federal review rather than mandatory licensing.
