# OpenAI unveils Presence, an enterprise platform for realtime voice and chat agents

OpenAI launched Presence, a limited-availability enterprise platform that gives AI agents a shared foundation of company context, policies, permissions, guardrails, actions, and evaluations so they behave consistently across voice and chat. It targets customer support, sales, and higher-risk internal workflows, with a built-in simulation tool to test guardrails and Codex-based monitoring once agents are in production. OpenAI says Presence already handles about 75% of its own inbound support requests, with BBVA, SoftBank, and IAG among early explorers.
