# Apple Sends Legal Preservation Letters to ~40 Former Staff Now at OpenAI

Apple sent personal legal preservation letters to roughly 40 ex-employees now at OpenAI, instructing them to retain documents and prepare to meet company lawyers, escalating its trade-secrets dispute. The action targets talent OpenAI needs for its hardware ambitions and lands as OpenAI approaches both a planned device launch and a possible IPO.
