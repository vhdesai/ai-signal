# Nscale buys Anyscale to own more of the AI compute stack

British AI neocloud Nscale is buying Anyscale, the company behind commercial tooling around the Ray distributed computing framework, in a deal Bloomberg reported at $1.65 billion. The acquisition moves Nscale further up the stack from energy and data centers into orchestration, workload management, and developer tools. This is an important infrastructure signal: AI compute providers are trying to capture more software-layer value rather than remain pure capacity suppliers.
