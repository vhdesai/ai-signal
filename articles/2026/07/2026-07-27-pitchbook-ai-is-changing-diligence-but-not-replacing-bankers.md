# PitchBook: AI is changing diligence but not replacing bankers yet

PitchBook reports that AI is increasing the volume of diligence questions in private-equity deals but not necessarily improving their depth. Some firms are using AI for sourcing, diligence, filtering, and seller Q&A, yet dealmakers said human trust and interpretation still matter, especially in founder-led and family-owned businesses. The piece also warns that deals can break down when counterparties rely on AI-generated answers they do not fully understand.
