# Apple Publishes MoMo for Controllable Robot Manipulation Styles

Apple published MoMo, a two-stage imitation-learning framework that separates task success from motion style in robot manipulation. That lets the same robot execute a job steadily, dynamically, or somewhere in between without losing reliability. Across six real-robot tasks, the system transferred unseen motion modes while preserving successful execution. The work matters because practical robots in human spaces need to adapt not only what they do, but how gently or quickly they do it. Apple's continued robotics publishing suggests deeper physical-AI ambitions even as its commercial AI strategy stays focused on devices and Siri. Links: Apple ML Research
