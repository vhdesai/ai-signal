# Alphabet Leans on "Neoclouds," Conceding It Can't Build Fast Enough

Alphabet raised 2026 AI capex guidance to ~$205B and acknowledged it needs third-party capacity (Nebius, CoreWeave, IREN) to meet demand — sending neocloud stocks sharply higher. Even the best-capitalized hyperscaler is compute-constrained. A second tier of GPU landlords is becoming structurally important to the AI supply chain.
