# Meta Expands Louisiana "Hyperion" Supercluster to $50B and 5 Gigawatts

Meta will scale its Hyperion data center in Richland Parish, Louisiana, to 5 GW of compute at more than $50 billion — up from an initial ~$10B / 2 GW plan. Partner Entergy will build ~7 GW of new generation (primarily natural gas) to serve the site. Aggregate AI-infrastructure commitments in Louisiana now exceed $250 billion, underscoring that the physical layer — not model architecture — remains the binding constraint.
