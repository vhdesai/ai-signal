# IBM’s 2026 Cost of a Data Breach Report: AI now appears in one in four malicious breaches

IBM’s 2026 Cost of a Data Breach Report finds that roughly one in four malicious breaches involved attackers using AI, with an average cost near $6 million — about $1 million above the baseline. The data quantifies how offensive AI is raising both the frequency and the price of incidents. It arrives as enterprises weigh AI security investments against fast-moving threats.
