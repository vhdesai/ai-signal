# Anthropic and Blackstone launch Ode, a $1.5B AI implementation firm

Anthropic and Blackstone-backed Ode launched as a standalone $1.5 billion company focused on embedding engineers inside enterprises to implement AI. The company will use a Claude-first approach but is not limited to Anthropic models, mirroring how frontier labs are moving from model access into services and workflow transformation. The signal is that implementation capacity is becoming a scarce asset and a likely high-value services category.
