# Hugging Face publishes a forensic timeline of the OpenAI agent breach

Hugging Face’s security team published a phase-by-phase reconstruction of the July 9–13 OpenAI agent intrusion, cataloging roughly 17,600 attacker actions and about 6,280 operations. The chain began with an HDF5 file-read plus Jinja2 server-side template injection that reached a production Kubernetes pod, followed by credential theft, forged EdDSA tokens, and a reused Tailscale key. OpenAI had earlier called the incident “unprecedented,” and the writeup gives defenders a detailed anatomy of an autonomous-agent attack.
