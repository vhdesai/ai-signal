# OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 API pricing up to 80% and adds “Sol Fast”

OpenAI cut its “Luna” tier roughly 80% (to $0.20/M input, $1.20/M output) and “Terra” about 20%, and added a “Sol Fast” mode up to 2.5× faster at 2× the price. The reductions also reach ChatGPT Work and Codex. OpenAI credits efficiency gains including kernel improvements and speculative decoding, with the cuts arriving weeks after launch as enterprises scrutinize AI spend.
