# Gartner's 2026 Cloud AI Infrastructure MQ names AWS, Google, Oracle, Microsoft as leaders

Gartner placed the four hyperscalers at the top of its 2026 Magic Quadrant for cloud AI infrastructure. The ranking also recognized CoreWeave, Nebius, IBM, Vultr, Huawei, and Alibaba among others. It offers a market snapshot of who enterprises are betting on for AI compute.
