# Google DeepMind unveils Gemini Robotics 2

A family of “physical AI” models featuring intelligent whole-body control, fine dexterity via 22-DoF hands, multi-robot teamwork, and on-device adaptation to new robot bodies within hours. The release includes a humanoid vision-language-action model, Gemini Robotics ER 2 for embodied reasoning and orchestration, and an on-device VLA. It already powers robots including Apptronik's Apollo 2, with ER 2 becoming publicly available first.
