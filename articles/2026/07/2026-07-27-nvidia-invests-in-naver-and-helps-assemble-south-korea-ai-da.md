# Nvidia invests in Naver and helps assemble South Korea AI data-center capacity

Nvidia said it would invest $1 billion in South Korea’s Naver to help expand AI data-center infrastructure, with Brookfield planning up to $9 billion more. The Information reports that Naver’s planned capacity would rise from 55 MW to 200 MW, starting with Nvidia Grace Blackwell racks and later adding Vera Rubin systems. The deal shows Nvidia increasingly using its balance sheet and infrastructure coordination to accelerate sovereign AI deployments.
