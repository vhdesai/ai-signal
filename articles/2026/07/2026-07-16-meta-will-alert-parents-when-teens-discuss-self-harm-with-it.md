# Meta will alert parents when teens discuss self-harm with its AI

Meta announced it will notify parents when a teen discusses suicide or self-harm with the Meta AI chatbot, using a dedicated detection system. The company said it is also working toward contacting emergency services when conversations suggest imminent risk. Crisis detection and guardian-notification features are likely to become table stakes and a compliance expectation across consumer AI products.
