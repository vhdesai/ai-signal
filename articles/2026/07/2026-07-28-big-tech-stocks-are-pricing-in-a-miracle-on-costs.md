# Big Tech stocks are pricing in a “miracle on costs”

The Wall Street Journal reported that the bullish earnings case for Big Tech assumes companies can spend trillions on AI infrastructure while also delivering major new operating efficiencies. That combination looks increasingly hard to sustain as investors scrutinize Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, and others for evidence that AI spending is translating into revenue and margin expansion. The piece captures the market’s shift from excitement about AI spend to skepticism about AI payback.
