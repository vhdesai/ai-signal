# BlackRock takes 80% stake in Meta's El Paso AI campus

Meta and BlackRock formed a joint venture to own the gigawatt-scale data-center campus Meta is building in El Paso, Texas, with BlackRock-managed funds holding an 80% interest and Meta retaining 20%. The structure is the latest example of hyperscalers moving multibillion-dollar AI infrastructure off their own balance sheets and into external-capital vehicles. For enterprises, it signals that the financing of AI compute — not just the compute itself — is becoming a distinct, investable layer.
