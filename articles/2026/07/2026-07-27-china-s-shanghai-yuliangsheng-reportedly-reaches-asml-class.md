# China's Shanghai Yuliangsheng reportedly reaches ASML-class lithography in mass production

The Information reported that China's Shanghai Yuliangsheng has begun mass production of an advanced chipmaking (lithography) technology long dominated by Dutch supplier ASML — a claim that, if borne out, would mark a significant crack in the export-control regime built around EUV tooling. The report was the proximate trigger for a region-wide chip selloff and carries direct implications for U.S. and allied semiconductor strategy. It remains a single-sourced report, unconfirmed by the companies involved, and was corroborated in wire coverage by AFP.
