# DealBook highlights U.S. warning on Chinese AI risks

DealBook flagged Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's warning on Chinese AI as part of a broader U.S. trade and technology conflict. The item reinforces that AI is now central to U.S.-China economic policy, not simply a technology-sector issue. For enterprises, the risk is a more fragmented AI stack, with model access, training data, and silicon availability increasingly shaped by national-security policy.
