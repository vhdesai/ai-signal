# OpenAI advances the price-performance frontier with GPT-5.6

OpenAI published its GPT-5.6 price-performance update, emphasizing efficiency across model tiers, inference, and agentic harness design. The release continues the market shift from pure capability competition toward cost-normalized performance, where enterprise buyers compare quality per dollar and latency rather than headline benchmark scores alone. For procurement leaders, the practical implication is that model selection will increasingly depend on workload routing and service-level economics.
