# Inference-Chip Startup Etched Doubles Valuation to $10.3B in $300M Round

$300M Series C led by Sequoia at $10.3B — 2×+ prior mark. SK Hynix, a16z, Jane Street participated. Drawing the world's largest AI-memory supplier into inference silicon. Credible non-GPU challengers to Nvidia being funded at scale on the bet that purpose-built chips undercut GPUs on cost and latency.
