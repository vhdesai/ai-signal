# Samsung posts record profit, up 1,814% year-over-year, on AI memory demand

Samsung reported an all-time-high quarterly operating profit (~89.5 trillion won) as DRAM, HBM, and NAND demand for AI servers drove its semiconductor division; the company shipped initial HBM4E samples and expanded HBM4 volumes. Management expects tight supply and strong server demand to persist into 2027, with more customers seeking long-term contracts. The results confirm AI infrastructure is concentrating profit among a handful of memory suppliers — and tightening component availability for everyone else. ________________________________ INFRASTRUCTURESILICON
