# Unionized workers are bargaining with the bots

Axios reports that unionized workers are increasingly negotiating around AI deployment, monitoring, task automation, and job protections. The story points to a maturing labor response: AI is becoming a collective-bargaining topic rather than a management-only technology decision. Enterprises should expect AI workforce strategy to involve negotiated guardrails, retraining commitments, transparency rules, and limits on automated decision-making.
