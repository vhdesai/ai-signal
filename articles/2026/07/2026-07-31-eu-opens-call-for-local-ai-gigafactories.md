# EU opens call for local AI gigafactories

The Wall Street Journal reported that the EU opened a call for the creation of local AI gigafactories. The effort reflects growing policy interest in sovereign AI infrastructure, where compute capacity, local data-control requirements, and industrial strategy increasingly overlap.
