# Anthropic Extends Free Claude Fable 5 Access to July 19; Lifts Claude Code Limits 50%

Anthropic pushed its free-access window for Claude Fable 5 from July 12 to July 19 for Pro, Max, and Team subscribers, alongside a 50% increase to Claude Code usage limits. The repeated extensions track directly against OpenAI's GPT-5.6 rollout and its aggressive pricing. The signal: frontier vendors are now competing on access and price, not just capability.
