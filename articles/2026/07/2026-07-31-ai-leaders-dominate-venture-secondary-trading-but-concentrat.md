# AI leaders dominate venture secondary trading, but concentration risk is rising

PitchBook said SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic have dominated venture secondary volume, with the top five startups accounting for half of all secondary trading value on Hiive in Q2 2026 and the top 20 accounting for 86%. The analysis argues that exits or reduced availability among the largest AI-linked names should eventually broaden secondary demand, but only after investors unwind concentrated positions and redeploy into the next cohort.
