# MICROSOFT ANTHROPIC STRATEGY

Business Insider reports that Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella criticized, indirectly, AI model makers whose value is concentrated in foundation models rather than products, distribution, and workflow integration. The comments matter because Microsoft is positioning enterprise AI advantage around application surfaces, cloud infrastructure, and customer workflows, not only model access. It also reflects intensifying competition and partner tension as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft converge on enterprise agents. URL not verified.
