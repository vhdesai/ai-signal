# New Microsoft Copilot Vulnerabilities Could Leak Customer Secrets

Researchers from two cybersecurity firms found previously unreported flaws in Microsoft Copilot features for Office 365. The bugs could allegedly let attackers manipulate the assistant into exposing sensitive customer data, raising concerns about prompt-injection defenses. That is particularly awkward because Microsoft has positioned Copilot as a safer enterprise option than rival assistants. The report also highlights the contradiction of AI vendors selling security automation while their own assistants remain exploitable. For millions of paid Copilot users, the story raises hard questions about whether embedded AI is being deployed faster than it can be secured. Links: The Information search
