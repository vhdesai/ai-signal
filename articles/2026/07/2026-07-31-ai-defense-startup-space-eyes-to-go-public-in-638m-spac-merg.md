# AI Defense Startup Space-Eyes to Go Public in $638M SPAC Merger

Space-Eyes agreed to merge with McKinley Acquisition Corp. at roughly a $638M valuation, with up to $251.7M in gross proceeds and plans to trade on Nasdaq under ticker CUAS. The Miami-based firm builds AI geospatial intelligence, maritime surveillance (SeaWatch) and counter-drone systems (Morpheus), and reports about $1M in current revenue against roughly $35M in contracts over five years. The deal — with Eric Trump as investor and adviser — illustrates rising investor appetite for AI-plus-sensors defense-tech, alongside the political scrutiny that comes with it.
