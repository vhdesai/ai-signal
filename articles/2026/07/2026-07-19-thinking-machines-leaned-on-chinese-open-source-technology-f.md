# Thinking Machines leaned on Chinese open-source technology for its debut model

The Financial Times reported that Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab built its first general-purpose model partly on inexpensive Chinese open-source technology. The report adds important context to Inkling's debut: even well-funded U.S. startups are using Chinese open-model infrastructure as a cost and capability accelerant. It signals that Chinese open-weight models are becoming foundational inputs across the global AI ecosystem, not just regional competitors.
