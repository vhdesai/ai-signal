# Nscale Acquires Anyscale for $1.65B to Own More of the AI Compute Stack

Nscale agreed to buy Anyscale for about $1.65 billion, adding the company behind Ray to its AI cloud infrastructure business. The deal gives Nscale software for orchestration, training, and inference on top of its existing GPU and data-center footprint. That kind of vertical integration is becoming common as neoclouds try to capture more of the economics above raw compute rental. Anyscale will keep its brand for now, but users will watch closely to see whether Ray remains truly cloud-agnostic over time. The acquisition shows that infrastructure competition is moving quickly from owning hardware to owning the full production stack. Links: TechCrunch · Digital Market Reports
