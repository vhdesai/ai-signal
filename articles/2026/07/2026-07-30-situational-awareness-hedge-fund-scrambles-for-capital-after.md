# Situational Awareness Hedge Fund Scrambles for Capital After Tech Rout

Situational Awareness, the $20 billion hedge fund founded by Leopold Aschenbrenner — a former OpenAI employee known for his influential AI forecasting essays — is seeking emergency capital from investors after suffering heavy losses in the recent technology stock rout, according to The Financial Times. The fund, which built large concentrated positions in AI and semiconductor stocks based on Aschenbrenner's thesis that artificial general intelligence is imminent, appears to have been caught on the wrong side of the AI capex backlash that has driven the Nasdaq into correction territory. The episode serves as a cautionary tale about conviction-driven AI investing: even sophisticated operators with insider knowledge of frontier AI capabilities can be overwhelmed by short-term market rotations away from the trade.
