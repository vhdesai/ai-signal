# Multi-turn AI attacks reportedly break models 88% of the time

VentureBeat reports that multi-turn attacks can bypass model defenses 88% of the time, highlighting how attackers can accumulate context, reframe intent, and gradually steer models past safeguards. The finding is important because many safety systems are evaluated on single-turn prompts or static policy checks. Enterprise model evaluation needs to test persistent adversarial behavior, tool use, and multi-step escalation paths.
