# Nvidia, Microsoft, SpaceX, Palantir and IBM launch an “Open Secure AI Alliance” — without OpenAI, Google or Anthropic

Nvidia, joined by Microsoft, SpaceX, Palantir, IBM and roughly three dozen companies and open-source foundations, launched the Open Secure AI Alliance to give cyber defenders access to frontier models and shared tooling, per CNBC. Notably absent are OpenAI, Google and Anthropic — an omission CSO Online highlighted and that underscores competitive fault lines in AI security. The alliance is explicitly framed as a response to the fallout from the first documented autonomous-agent cyber incident.
