# IBM says AI hardware cost pressure delayed mainframe purchases

TechCrunch reports that IBM's quarterly results missed expectations after mainframe sales fell sharply, with CEO Arvind Krishna arguing that customers shifted budgets toward other data-center and PC hardware facing AI-driven component price increases. IBM says the issue is temporary and that customers have not moved off mainframes, but the episode shows how AI infrastructure demand is distorting adjacent enterprise hardware budgets. For CIOs, AI capex is no longer isolated; it can crowd out refresh cycles across the broader technology estate.
