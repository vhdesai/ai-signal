# Retailers prepare for bots as buyers

Axios reports that retailers are adapting as AI bots increasingly become buyers, while related coverage says AI is breaking existing shopping measurement and affiliate-commerce models. The practical shift is that product discovery, comparison, and purchasing may move from human-facing pages to agent-mediated flows. Retailers and publishers will need new instrumentation, attribution models, and commerce APIs to remain visible in AI-mediated shopping journeys.
