# Google's AI search is rapidly becoming the default

TechCrunch reports that Google's AI Overviews now appear in 43% of searches, up from 15% a year earlier, according to Similarweb data. AI Mode visits also rose sharply, indicating that conversational search is becoming a normal part of the search journey. The strategic issue is distribution: Google is turning search from a web-navigation layer into an answer destination, increasing pressure on publishers and brands that rely on referral traffic.
