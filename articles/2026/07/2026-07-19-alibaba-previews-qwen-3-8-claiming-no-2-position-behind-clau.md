# Alibaba previews Qwen 3.8, claiming No. 2 position behind Claude Fable 5

Alibaba unveiled a preview of Qwen 3.8, branded Qwen3.8 Max, positioning it as its new flagship and claiming it trails only Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 among frontier systems. Alibaba's Hong Kong shares rose about 5% on the disclosure, a reminder that model launches now move megacap valuations. The release sharpens competition at the top of the open-weight and API market, especially from Chinese labs.
