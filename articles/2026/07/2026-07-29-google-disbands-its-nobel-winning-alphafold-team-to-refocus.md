# Google disbands its Nobel-winning AlphaFold team to refocus on Gemini

Google has disbanded the DeepMind team behind AlphaFold — the Nobel Prize–winning protein-structure model — reassigning key members and original paper authors to Gemini-related projects and other scientific efforts, with some having already left, per the Financial Times. AlphaFold remains widely used in drug discovery, vaccine development, and disease research, so the reorganization signals DeepMind consolidating around its Gemini roadmap rather than standalone science programs. The move raises questions about how frontier labs will sustain long-horizon scientific research amid the race to ship general-purpose models. K FUNDING
