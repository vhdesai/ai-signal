# OpenAI's Infrastructure Budget Balloons to $750B Through 2030

OpenAI will spend $750 billion on infrastructure through 2030 — roughly 25% more than its estimate earlier this year — even as its flagship Stargate project appears stalled. The first project, a $20B campus in Georgia ("Project Camellia"), spans 1,400 acres near Savannah drawing at least 3.2 GW from Georgia Power, phasing in 2028–2032. Compute commitments are now the central competitive lever among frontier labs.
