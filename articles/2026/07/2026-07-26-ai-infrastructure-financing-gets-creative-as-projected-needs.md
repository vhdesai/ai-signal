# AI infrastructure financing gets creative as projected needs hit $7.5T

The Information reports that bankers and investors are searching “every nook and cranny” for capital to fund an expected $7.5 trillion in chips, data centers, and power over the next five years. Traditional data-center financing is no longer sufficient, pushing dealmakers toward new equity partnerships, hyperscaler-backed structures, and debt markets that are already showing digestion issues. The story reframes AI infrastructure as a capital-markets problem as much as a technology problem.
