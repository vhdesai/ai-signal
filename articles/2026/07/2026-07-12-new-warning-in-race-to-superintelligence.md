# New warning in race to superintelligence

Axios AI+ continued to feature the AI Futures Project’s “AI 2040” proposal calling for an internationally verified slowdown in superintelligence development. The proposal is strategically relevant because it outlines a concrete policy architecture — lab transparency, U.S.-China verification, and broader distribution of AI power — rather than treating superintelligence governance as an abstract risk debate.
