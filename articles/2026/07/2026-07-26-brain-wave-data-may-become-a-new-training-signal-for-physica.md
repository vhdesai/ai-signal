# Brain-wave data may become a new training signal for physical AI

TechCrunch reports that Encord is testing brain-wave-tagged robotics training data with Zander Labs to evaluate whether mental-state signals such as error, intent, and surprise can improve physical-AI models. The story underscores that robotics foundation models face a data bottleneck unlike language models: high-quality embodied data must be manufactured, densely annotated, and often collected from specialized environments. If these signals prove useful, robotics data pipelines may become a defensible layer in physical AI. URL: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/are-brain-waves-the-next-unlock-for-physical-ai/
