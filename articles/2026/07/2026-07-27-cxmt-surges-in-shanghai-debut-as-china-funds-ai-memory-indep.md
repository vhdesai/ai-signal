# CXMT surges in Shanghai debut as China funds AI memory independence

ChangXin Memory Technologies, China’s leading memory-chip maker, jumped more than 470% in its Shanghai trading debut after raising up to 66.6 billion yuan. The opening price reportedly valued CXMT at about 3.3 trillion yuan, making it one of mainland China’s most valuable listed companies. The surge matters for AI because DRAM and high-bandwidth memory are core bottlenecks in the compute stack.
