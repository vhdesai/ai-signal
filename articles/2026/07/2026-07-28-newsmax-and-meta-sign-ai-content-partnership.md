# Newsmax and Meta sign AI content partnership

Newsmax (NYSE: NMAX) said it entered an AI content partnership giving Meta access to its current and archived digital news content to support AI queries across Meta's apps and devices. Financial terms beyond content access were not disclosed. The deal extends the run of publisher–AI licensing agreements as platforms seek authoritative, rights-cleared sources for their assistants.
