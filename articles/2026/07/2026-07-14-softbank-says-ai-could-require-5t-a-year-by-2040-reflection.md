# SoftBank says AI could require $5T a year by 2040; Reflection signs $1B-plus compute deal

SoftBank's Masayoshi Son projected that AI buildout will require roughly $5 trillion of annual investment by 2040, while open-model startup Reflection signed a more-than-$1 billion agreement to secure Nvidia GPU capacity from Nebius through 2029. Together the items show that AI economics are increasingly governed by long-term compute access, financing, and power constraints rather than model release cadence alone.
