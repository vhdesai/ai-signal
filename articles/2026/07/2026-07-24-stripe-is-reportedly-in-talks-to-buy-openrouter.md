# Stripe is reportedly in talks to buy OpenRouter

The Information reports that Stripe is in talks to buy OpenRouter, a startup that helps developers access hundreds of AI models, for close to $10 billion. A deal would extend Stripe's usage-based billing and token-consumption tools into model routing, letting businesses direct workloads to cheaper or task-appropriate models. The story underscores how AI infrastructure is converging with payments, metering, and enterprise software procurement.
