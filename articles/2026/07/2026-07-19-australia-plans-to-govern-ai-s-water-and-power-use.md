# Australia plans to govern AI's water and power use

WSJ reports that Australia is planning to govern the use of water and power for AI. The proposal reflects a broader global shift in which data-center development is being treated as an environmental and utility-policy issue. AI infrastructure leaders should expect more jurisdictions to attach conditions to water access, grid impact, local permitting, and community benefit. URL behind paywall.
