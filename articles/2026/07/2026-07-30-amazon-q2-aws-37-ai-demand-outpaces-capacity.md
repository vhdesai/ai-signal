# Amazon Q2: AWS +37%, AI demand “outpaces capacity”

Amazon posted its first $200B+ revenue quarter (+20%), with AWS up 37% to a roughly $169B annualized run rate. CEO Andy Jassy said AI demand outpaces capacity into 2027, and the AI and custom-chip businesses each topped $25B run rates with triple-digit growth. Net income was boosted by an Anthropic-investment gain, though free cash flow turned negative on about $200B of planned capex.
