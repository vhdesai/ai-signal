# OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 prices to defend the price-performance frontier

OpenAI reduced pricing on two GPT-5.6 models — cutting GPT-5.6 Luna by roughly 80% and GPT-5.6 Terra by 20% — as enterprise buyers grow more cost-sensitive and Chinese labs undercut Western pricing. The move, published on OpenAI's blog and confirmed by CNBC, is a defensive repricing rather than a new-capability release. It immediately set up a same-week price war: DeepSeek's new V4-Flash landed within 48 hours at a fraction of the cost.
