# CXMT prices record ~$8.6B Shanghai chip IPO at a ~$85B valuation

ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), China's flagship DRAM maker, is set to debut on Shanghai's STAR Market in Asia's largest capital raise of 2026, pricing at ~8.66 yuan/share to raise roughly $8.55B at a ~$85B implied valuation. CXMT is positioned as a domestic alternative to Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron, though it still lacks EUV access and carries a US military-designation overhang. The listing signals Beijing's determination to fund a homegrown memory supply chain for AI.
