# MediaTek approves up to $5B to build custom AI data-center chips

MediaTek approved up to $5B to push into custom AI accelerators (ASICs), targeting 15–20% of the market and more than $2B in data-center AI-chip revenue this year, with first production in Q4. The move positions MediaTek as another Nvidia alternative for hyperscalers. It adds to a widening field of in-house and merchant silicon efforts.
