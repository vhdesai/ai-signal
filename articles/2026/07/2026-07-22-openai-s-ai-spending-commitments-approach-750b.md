# OpenAI's AI spending commitments approach $750B

TechCrunch reports that OpenAI's cumulative compute and infrastructure commitments have swelled to roughly $750 billion across data-center, chip, and cloud deals. The figure sharpens questions about the capital intensity and financing structure behind the current AI buildout as OpenAI approaches a potential IPO. The core question for investors and customers is whether new enterprise agent products can generate enough durable revenue to support this obligation stack.
