# Amazon shuts an AI agent research lab during AGI layoffs

Amazon closed its San Francisco AGI Lab, a research and product team focused on making AI agents more useful, as part of layoffs in its AGI unit. The Information reports that Nova Act, the browser-use agent model and service launched by the lab, remains available on AWS, while broader frontier-model research continues under Pieter Abbeel. The move suggests large labs are pruning experimental agent teams while keeping commercially promising infrastructure alive.
