# Meta Q2: revenue +28% but AI capex raised to $130–145B

Meta reported $60.8B in revenue (+28%), but profit fell on legal charges, severance, and R&D, and it raised its 2026 capex floor to $130–145B. Zuckerberg touted AI-accelerated app development and “always-on” agents, noting 1M+ businesses use Meta agents weekly, but shares slid on concerns about returns. Filings disclosed $279B in future AI data-center lease commitments.
