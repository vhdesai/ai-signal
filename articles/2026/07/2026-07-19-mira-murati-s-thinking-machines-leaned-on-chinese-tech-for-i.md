# Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Leaned on Chinese Tech for Its Debut Model

The FT reported that Thinking Machines Lab built its first general-purpose model partly on inexpensive Chinese open-source technology. The approach signals a cost-driven strategy and shows how open Chinese models are becoming foundational inputs even for well-funded U.S. startups. The model debuted days earlier; the FT reporting adds detail on its technical lineage.
