# NVIDIA and Japan Launch "World's First National AI Infrastructure"

NVIDIA and Japan's METI-backed Noetra Corp. will build a Vera Rubin AI factory with 13,750 Vera CPUs and 27,500 Rubin GPUs delivering 140 MW. It will anchor Japan's "FRONTia" project, producing open multimodal foundation models for agents, digital twins, robotics, and physical AI. A marker of how fast sovereign-AI buildouts and Rubin-generation demand are materializing.
