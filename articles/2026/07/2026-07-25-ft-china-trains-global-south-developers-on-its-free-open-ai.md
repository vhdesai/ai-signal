# FT: China trains Global South developers on its free, open AI models

The Financial Times reports China is pairing wide release of open models (from DeepSeek, Qwen and Kimi) with active training programs for developers in developing countries, framing capacity-building — not just weight releases — as the mechanism for an alternative global AI bloc. Signal: AI soft power is becoming an instrument of geopolitical alignment; enterprises with Global South operations should watch the resulting standard-setting dynamics.
