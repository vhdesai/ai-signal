# OpenAI's Codex now encrypts instructions between AI agents

Codex now encrypts parent-to-sub-agent instructions, and the change is mandatory for the larger GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra variants. Developers report broken audit trails and failed handoffs, while OpenAI has not publicly explained whether the change is privacy hardening or an anti-distillation measure. The result is a governance gap for regulated enterprises that must log what agents are instructed to do.
