# Index Ventures co-founder warns AI wealth may be redistributed voluntarily or involuntarily

Index Ventures co-founder Neil Rimer told TechCrunch he expects some form of redistribution from the wealth accumulating around AI, either voluntary or forced. The article connects AI-driven wealth concentration with weakening philanthropic commitments, possible wealth taxes, and emerging proposals such as public stakes in AI companies. For executives, the issue is not just social commentary: extraordinary AI wealth creation is becoming a policy, tax, and reputational risk.
