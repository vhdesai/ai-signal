# Whatnot acquires Shaped to improve real-time live shopping recommendations

Livestream shopping platform Whatnot acquired Shaped, an AI startup focused on real-time recommendations and search. The deal is aimed at improving personalization and discovery as Whatnot expands across product categories. It highlights continued consolidation around applied recommendation systems, where low-latency personalization remains commercially valuable even outside the frontier-model race.
