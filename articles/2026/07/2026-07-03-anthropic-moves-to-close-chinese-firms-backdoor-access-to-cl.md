# Anthropic moves to close Chinese firms' backdoor access to Claude

Anthropic is working to shut down "transfer station" relay services and cloud workarounds that let Chinese companies — including Ant Group — access Claude while obscuring the origin of API requests. The enforcement is part of formal commitments Anthropic made to the US government during the Fable 5 export-control episode and is tied to concerns about model distillation. Engineers at Chinese firms are reportedly still finding new routes, signaling that access control at the frontier is now an ongoing operational and geopolitical problem, not a one-time fix.
