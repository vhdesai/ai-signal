# Anthropic moves closer to a mega-IPO as banks line up investor meetings

Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan are reportedly scheduling investor meetings for a potential October Anthropic IPO. The company was last valued at roughly $965 billion after a $65 billion Series H, above OpenAI's most recent private valuation. If completed, the offering would make Anthropic the first frontier AI lab to reach public markets and would test whether public investors will underwrite frontier-model economics at near-trillion-dollar scale.
