# FAIRChem v2 + UMA: a universal ML potential for multidomain atomistic simulation

MarkTechPost published a hands-on tutorial for FAIRChem v2 and Meta FAIR’s UMA (Universal Model for Atoms), a universal machine-learning interatomic potential that unifies atomistic simulation across molecules, catalysts, and materials (spanning the omol, oc20, and omat domains). It illustrates applications from vibrational analysis to molecular dynamics. The write-up is educational and builds on prior FAIR research rather than announcing a new paper.
