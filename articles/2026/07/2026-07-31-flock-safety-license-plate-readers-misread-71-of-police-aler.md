# Flock Safety license-plate readers misread 71% of police alerts in Roseville

Business Insider reported that Flock Safety's AI-powered license-plate readers misread plates in 71% of alerts sent to the Roseville Police Department in 2023 and 2024. Roseville's configuration, older hardware, and camera placement contributed to the errors, but the case highlights a broader governance question: AI accuracy thresholds are context-dependent, and public-safety deployments require human verification before action.
