# Nvidia weighs a ~$250B financing backstop for OpenAI's 10-gigawatt Ohio data center

The Wall Street Journal reported that Nvidia is in talks to guarantee roughly $250 billion of financing to help OpenAI lease a 10-gigawatt data center that SoftBank's SB Energy is developing in Piketon, Ohio.
