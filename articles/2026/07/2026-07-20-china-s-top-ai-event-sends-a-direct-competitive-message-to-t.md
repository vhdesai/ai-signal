# China's top AI event sends a direct competitive message to the U.S.

WSJ reports that China's top AI event delivered a message to the U.S.: Chinese firms are coming for AI leadership. The timing follows heightened attention to Moonshot's Kimi K3 and new Alibaba model claims, reinforcing that China is competing through open models, domestic chips, and national AI mobilization. Executives should treat Chinese AI progress as both a market-pricing force and a geopolitical compliance issue. URL behind paywall.
