# Amazon and Microsoft's combined ~$400B AI spend tests investor patience

Ahead of both companies' earnings (Amazon reports July 30), coverage highlighted that the two cloud leaders are on track to spend a combined roughly $400B on AI infrastructure — days after Wall Street punished Alphabet for aggressive AI capex. The framing captures a growing market question: how long investors will fund hyperscaler buildouts before demanding clearer returns. Cloud AI capacity remains a strategic advantage, but the cost of that lead is now a board-level scrutiny item. Expect AI ROI narratives to dominate the upcoming earnings calls.
