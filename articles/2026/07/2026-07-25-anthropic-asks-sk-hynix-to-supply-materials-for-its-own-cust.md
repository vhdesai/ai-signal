# Anthropic Asks SK Hynix to Supply Materials for Its Own Custom Chips

SK Group Chairman said Anthropic has formally requested semiconductor materials from SK Hynix to build its own silicon — spanning both ASICs and GPUs. Would follow Google's TPU and Amazon's Trainium in vertically integrating away from Nvidia. Lands alongside a data-center letter of intent with SK Telecom and the Samsung SDS enterprise deployment.
