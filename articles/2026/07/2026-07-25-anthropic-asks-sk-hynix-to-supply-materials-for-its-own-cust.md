# Anthropic asks SK Hynix to supply materials for its own custom chips

SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won said on stage in San Francisco — seated beside Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei — that Anthropic has formally requested semiconductor materials from SK Hynix to build its own silicon, spanning both ASICs and GPUs. The move would follow Google's TPU and Amazon's Trainium in vertically integrating away from Nvidia dependence. It lands alongside Anthropic's broader Korean infrastructure push, including a data-center letter of intent with SK Telecom.
