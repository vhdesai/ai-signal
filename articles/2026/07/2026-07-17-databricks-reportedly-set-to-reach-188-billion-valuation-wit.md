# Databricks reportedly set to reach $188 billion valuation with Coatue investment

WSJ reports that Databricks is set to reach a $188 billion valuation with new investment from Coatue. The valuation underscores investor conviction that data platforms remain central to enterprise AI deployment, especially as companies try to operationalize proprietary data, governance, and model choice. It also suggests private-market appetite for AI infrastructure software remains strong despite public-market volatility around the AI trade. URL behind paywall.
