# Situational Awareness collapse becomes the cautionary tale for leveraged AI investing

WSJ reported that Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness fund peaked around $45 billion before leverage and concentrated AI bets triggered a rapid unwind. The fund's collapse, including a distressed sale to Citadel, shows that even a directionally correct AI thesis can fail if portfolio construction, liquidity, and risk controls lag the volatility of the trade. It is the clearest market warning yet that AI conviction and leverage are a dangerous mix.
