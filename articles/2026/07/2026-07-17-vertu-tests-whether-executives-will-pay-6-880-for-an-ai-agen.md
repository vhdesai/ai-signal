# Vertu tests whether executives will pay $6,880 for an AI-agent phone

TechCrunch reviewed Vertu's Alphafold, a luxury foldable phone centered on Hermes Agent, an AI assistant meant to automate executive workflows. The review found that Hermes was more willing than Google's Gemini to act across apps and files, but that reliability and judgment remained uneven. The article is a useful reminder that agentic autonomy is a product differentiator only when the agent can decide when to act and when to ask for clarification.
