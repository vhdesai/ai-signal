# Alphabet leans on neoclouds as it struggles to build fast enough

Alphabet is reportedly leaning on third-party GPU capacity from providers such as Nebius, CoreWeave, and IREN while raising 2026 AI capex expectations to roughly $205 billion. The story highlights a structural shift in AI infrastructure: even the largest hyperscalers cannot always build enough powered capacity on their own schedule. Neoclouds are becoming a second layer of AI supply, not just a short-term overflow market.
