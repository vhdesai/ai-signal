# DeepSeek ships V4-Flash-0731 with major agentic and coding gains at ~$0.14/$0.28 per million tokens

DeepSeek released an upgraded V4-Flash-0731, claiming frontier-class agentic and coding performance (including a reported ~82.7% on Terminal-Bench) at input/output pricing of roughly $0.14/$0.28 per million tokens, with open weights published on Hugging Face. The release establishes an aggressive new price floor for agentic automation just 48 hours after OpenAI's cuts. For buyers, it intensifies cost-per-capability pressure on U.S. incumbents and strengthens the case for multi-model sourcing. ________________________________ MODELSVIDEO
