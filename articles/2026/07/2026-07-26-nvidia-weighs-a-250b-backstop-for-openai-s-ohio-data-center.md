# Nvidia weighs a ~$250B backstop for OpenAI's Ohio data center

Per WSJ reporting, Nvidia is in talks to guarantee roughly $250B in lease-and-debt financing for OpenAI's 10-GW Ohio campus, on top of separately financing up to $350B in chips — pushing the total campus commitment above $500B. Reuters could not independently verify the figures, so this is best treated as credible reporting rather than a closed deal. Either way, it underscores how vendor balance sheets are now underwriting the AI build-out.
