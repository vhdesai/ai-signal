# The Enterprise "Context Gap": A Trust Problem, Not a Retrieval Problem

VentureBeat analysis argues that the dominant barrier to enterprise AI adoption is not RAG retrieval quality but trust — organizations lack confidence that AI systems have the right context and can be relied on to act on it. The piece frames the gap as an orchestration and governance challenge rather than a data-engineering one, with implications for how enterprises should architect their AI stacks.
