# Snapchat stops rewarding fully AI-generated Spotlight videos

Snapchat adjusted its recommendation systems so that fully AI-generated videos are no longer eligible for Spotlight rewards, reserving promotion for content made by real people. The move is an explicit platform stance against 'AI slop' and a signal that distribution — not generation — is becoming the scarce resource as synthetic media proliferates. It previews how consumer platforms may gate monetization on human authorship. ANALYSISHOT
