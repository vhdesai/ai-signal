# Scale AI names Francis deSouza as CEO

Scale AI named Francis deSouza — former Google Cloud COO and security-products president, and ex-Illumina CEO — as chief executive effective August 10. The appointment signals Scale's pivot from training-data infrastructure toward enterprise AI applications.
