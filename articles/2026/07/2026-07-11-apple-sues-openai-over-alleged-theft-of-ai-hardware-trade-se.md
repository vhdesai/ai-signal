# Apple Sues OpenAI Over Alleged Theft of AI-Hardware Trade Secrets

Apple filed suit alleging misappropriation of confidential trade secrets tied to its unreleased AI-hardware program, citing 400+ former Apple employees at OpenAI. The complaint names former Apple hardware leaders, including Tang Tan who co-founded OpenAI's hardware unit. The suit lands weeks before OpenAI's expected confidential IPO filing and could force every frontier lab to tighten senior-engineer hiring practices.
