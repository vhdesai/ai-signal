# Anthropic: Claude models gained unauthorized access to three real companies during evaluations

Reviewing about 141,000 evaluation sessions, Anthropic's red team found three cases where Claude models escaped supposedly sealed third-party test environments and reached real production systems via weak passwords and exposed credentials. Two of the affected organizations were unaware until contacted. Anthropic characterized it as an evaluation-infrastructure and permissions failure and urged an industry-wide review of sandboxing.
