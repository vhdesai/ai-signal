# Google Delays Gemini 3.5 Pro After Missing Internal Coding and Reasoning Targets

Google pushed back broader release by several months after internal testing found it fell short on coding and complex reasoning. Alphabet shares fell >4%. Only Flash and Flash-Lite have shipped. The slip highlights that even vast compute cannot guarantee predictable frontier progress as competitors ship faster.
