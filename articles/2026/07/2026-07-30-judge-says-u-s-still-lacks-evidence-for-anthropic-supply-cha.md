# Judge Says U.S. Still Lacks Evidence for Anthropic Supply-Chain Risk Label

A federal judge said the U.S. government had not shown enough evidence to justify branding Anthropic a supply-chain risk. That weakens the administration's attempt to bar federal agencies from using the company's technology. The underlying dispute centers on whether vendor-imposed use restrictions can make an AI supplier unreliable for national-security missions. The ruling offers temporary relief for Anthropic, but it leaves unresolved how governments should evaluate AI vendors with policy guardrails. The case may shape future procurement decisions for any lab trying to balance safety commitments with public-sector business. Links: TechCrunch
