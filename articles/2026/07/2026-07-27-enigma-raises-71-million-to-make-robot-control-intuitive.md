# Enigma raises $71 million to make robot control intuitive

Enigma emerged from stealth with a $71 million seed round to study how people naturally want to control robots and build interfaces for embodied AI. The company is opening online access to more than 100 proprietary robots so users can test different interaction modes such as text, audio, video examples, and direct manipulation. The signal is that physical AI needs better human-robot interaction models, not only better manipulation models. PHYSICAL AIROBOTICS DATABRAIN SIGNALS
