# Dili raises $15 million to automate compliance for AI infrastructure projects

Dili raised a $15 million Series A to apply AI to compliance workflows for U.S. infrastructure and data-center construction projects. The company targets permitting, prevailing wage, OSHA, EPA, and clean-energy funding rules that can delay physical buildouts. The funding is a clear example of “AI for the AI buildout”: software aimed at accelerating the regulatory and construction workflows needed for compute expansion.
