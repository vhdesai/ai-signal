# NVIDIA deploys Vera CPU to accelerate chip-design workflows

NVIDIA said it is using its Vera CPU across electronic design automation workflows for future CPUs and GPUs, while working with Cadence and Synopsys to optimize EDA applications. Early testing reportedly showed up to 1.5x higher performance on selected Cadence Jasper and Synopsys VCS workloads. The point is strategically important: the AI infrastructure race is now also about accelerating the chip-design cycle that produces the next generation of accelerators. SSINVIDIAAI SAFETY
