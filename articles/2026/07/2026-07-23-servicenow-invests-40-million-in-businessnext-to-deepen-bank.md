# ServiceNow invests $40 million in BusinessNext to deepen banking AI push

TechCrunch reports that ServiceNow invested $40 million in Indian banking software specialist BusinessNext at a $700 million valuation. BusinessNext serves more than 70 banks and is building an “autonomous banking” platform that applies AI agents to regulated financial workflows while keeping sensitive data on private infrastructure. The move shows large enterprise software vendors using strategic investments to add vertical AI depth, especially in sectors where workflow specificity and compliance matter.
