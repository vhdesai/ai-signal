# Zoom “do not record me” hack highlights friction from AI transcription ubiquity

TechCrunch covered a growing backlash to always-on AI note-taking and transcription, including a Zoom-name workaround that explicitly says the participant does not consent to recording. The issue illustrates a workplace UX and legal challenge: AI meeting capture may improve recall and summarization, but it can also suppress candid conversation and create sprawling records few people actually review. Enterprises need clearer consent norms, retention policies, and meeting-recording defaults as AI transcription becomes ambient.
