# Small Firms Use Anthropic's Claude to Replace Salesforce CRM

Greenleaf Management, an Atlanta-based real estate property and investment manager with about 55 employees, saved approximately $100,000 annually by replacing Salesforce's CRM software with a custom application developed using Replit and Claude Code. The new app costs around $300 per month to maintain. While the world's largest companies are not yet hurriedly replacing enterprise applications with vibe-coded alternatives, smaller firms are proving out the economics — a signal worth watching as AI coding tools mature.
