# Meta signs the EU AI Act Code of Practice on AI-content transparency

Meta announced it is signing the EU AI Act Code of Practice on transparency of AI-generated content, committing to labeling and tools that help identify photorealistic AI media. The move aligns Meta with the EU’s emerging disclosure regime ahead of broader enforcement. It adds momentum to industry adoption of content-provenance standards.
