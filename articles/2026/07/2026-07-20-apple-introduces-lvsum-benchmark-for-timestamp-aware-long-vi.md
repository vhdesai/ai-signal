# Apple introduces LVSum benchmark for timestamp-aware long video summarization

Apple published LVSum, a human-annotated benchmark for evaluating long-form video summarization with fine-grained temporal references. The benchmark covers 72 diverse videos across 13 domains and focuses on whether multimodal models can summarize content while preserving temporal fidelity. This is a practical research direction because enterprise video, meetings, training, and media workflows require summaries that are not just fluent, but traceable to time-coded evidence.
