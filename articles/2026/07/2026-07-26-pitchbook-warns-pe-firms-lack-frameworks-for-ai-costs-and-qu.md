# PitchBook warns PE firms lack frameworks for AI costs and quality control

PitchBook reports that private-equity firms are moving AI from pilot projects into daily deal work without robust budgeting or review frameworks. The story highlights token-pricing confusion, agentic-coding cost overruns, and diligence outputs that can look visibly AI-generated, eroding trust with investors.
