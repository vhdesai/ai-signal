# Monday.com cites AI as a factor in fresh layoffs

Monday.com became the latest company — roughly the twentieth tracked by TechCrunch — to cite AI as a factor in workforce reductions. The trend underscores how enterprise AI adoption is increasingly being tied, explicitly, to headcount decisions.
