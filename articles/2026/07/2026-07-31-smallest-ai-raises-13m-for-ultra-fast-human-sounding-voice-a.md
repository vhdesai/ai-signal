# Smallest.ai raises $13M for ultra-fast, human-sounding voice AI

Smallest.ai raised $13 million to build low-latency voice AI aimed at closing the perceptibility gap in customer-support agents. The round reflects continued investor appetite for real-time voice as a wedge into enterprise contact-center automation. It is a smaller data point in an otherwise consolidation-heavy market, but signals where applied-AI value is migrating.
