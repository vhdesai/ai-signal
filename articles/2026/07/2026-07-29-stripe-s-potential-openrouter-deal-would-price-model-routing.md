# Stripe’s potential OpenRouter deal would price model routing at a steep multiple

The Information reports that if Stripe buys OpenRouter for close to $10 billion, it would pay roughly 70 times OpenRouter’s recent annualized revenue. OpenRouter, which helps developers access hundreds of AI models, is reportedly generating about $140 million in annualized revenue, up nearly threefold since April. The story highlights how model-routing and AI metering are becoming strategic control points for payments, developer platforms, and enterprise AI procurement.
