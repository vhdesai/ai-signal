# CIOs grapple with AI costs as tools sprawl across the enterprise

CIO Dive reports that enterprises are trying to balance AI adoption with budget discipline as vendors and tools proliferate. Contract management, architecture, governance, and vendor evaluation are becoming core CIO responsibilities as AI shifts from experiments to operational spend. The same cost-pressure story now appears at two levels: hyperscaler capex and enterprise IT budgets.
