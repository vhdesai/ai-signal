# Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3, an Open-Weight Model That Tops Coding Benchmarks

Alibaba-backed Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8T-parameter open MoE model built for long-horizon agentic coding. It reached #1 on Arena.ai's Frontend Code Arena with a ~76% pairwise win rate, ahead of Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, and scored 88.3 on Terminal Bench 2.1 (just behind Sol's 88.8). Priced below top U.S. systems and freely self-hostable, the launch sharpens the case that Chinese labs are closing the frontier gap.
