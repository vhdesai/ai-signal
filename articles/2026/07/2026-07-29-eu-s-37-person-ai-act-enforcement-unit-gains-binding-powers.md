# EU's 37-person AI Act enforcement unit gains binding powers over frontier models

Beginning Sunday, August 2, the European Commission's “A3” unit inside the AI Office acquires authority to demand model access, order restrictions or recalls, and levy fines of up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover on providers of the most capable models. The 37-member team — which operated without a permanent head for roughly 18 months — is charged with policing four systemic risks, including AI-enabled cyberattacks and loss of control. Politico updated the article Friday with news of a further AI Office hiring push running through 2027.
