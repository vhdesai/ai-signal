# Stanford SIEPR: “What is really happening to jobs? Separating AI hype from reality”

A widely circulated SIEPR policy brief by Neale Mahoney, Erika McEntarfer, and Karsen Wahal argues AI's effect on aggregate U.S. employment is likely small so far, while a tougher market for recent graduates may be partly AI-related. It finds productivity effects “mixed but generally positive” (with outsized gains for less-experienced workers) and firm adoption accelerating unevenly — cautioning that, as with past general-purpose technologies, measurable impacts may lag. The brief reached the top of Hacker News on July 25, sharpening the executive debate over AI's labor-market timeline.
