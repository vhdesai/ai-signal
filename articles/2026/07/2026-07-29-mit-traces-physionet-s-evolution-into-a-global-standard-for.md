# MIT traces PhysioNet’s evolution into a global standard for biomedical data sharing

MIT News marked the 25th anniversary of PhysioNet, an open biomedical and clinical data repository rooted in earlier MIT-linked physiological datasets. The platform now supports a large global research community and is heavily cited in health AI and clinical machine-learning work. Its durability is a reminder that high-quality, governed datasets remain a core AI asset alongside models and compute.
