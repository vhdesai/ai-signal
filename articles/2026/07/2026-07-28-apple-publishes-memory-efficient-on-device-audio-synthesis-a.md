# Apple publishes memory-efficient on-device audio synthesis architecture

Apple published research on the memory-efficient audio synthesis architecture behind Siri Expressive Voices, powered by AFM 3 Core Advanced on device. The work describes a detokenizer that converts semantic audio tokens into high-fidelity audio under the compute and memory limits of Apple's Matrix Coprocessor. This points to a broader product trend: expressive voice AI is moving toward on-device execution for latency, privacy, and reliability.
