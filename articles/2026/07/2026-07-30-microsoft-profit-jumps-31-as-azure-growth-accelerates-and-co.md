# Microsoft profit jumps 31% as Azure growth accelerates and Copilot subscriptions double

Microsoft reported quarterly profit of $35.8 billion, up 31%, while Azure revenue growth accelerated to 43%, its fastest pace in four years. Paid Microsoft 365 Copilot subscriptions doubled to 30 million, and Satya Nadella said Microsoft is moving toward a consolidated Copilot “super app” spanning chat, coworking, agents, and GitHub Copilot. The result strengthens Microsoft’s case that AI capex can translate into cloud growth and operating leverage, rather than simply depressing free cash flow.
