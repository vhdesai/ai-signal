# Nvidia Uses Vera CPUs and AI Agents to Speed Up Its Own Chip Design

Nvidia detailed how it is using current-gen Vera CPUs alongside AI agents to accelerate design of future chips — a feedback loop where today's silicon engineers tomorrow's. A concrete example of AI compressing semiconductor design cycles. Reinforces Nvidia's argument that its platform, not just GPUs, is the durable moat.
