# 1,100+ AI-lab employees petition the U.S. government for tools to slow frontier AI

More than 1,100 employees across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Meta signed a letter urging governance and technical 'off-ramps' to slow progress if capability outpaces control. Reported signatories include Dario Amodei, Jakub Pachocki, and Shengjia Zhao. The petition adds insider weight to the debate over pacing frontier development.
