# Microsoft beats on FQ4 as Azure grows 43%; shares jump 8%

Microsoft posted fiscal Q4 revenue of roughly $90 billion, up about 18% year over year, with Azure growing 43% in constant currency — ahead of the ~40% analysts expected. Adjusted EPS of $4.81 beat consensus and shares rose about 8% in extended trading.
