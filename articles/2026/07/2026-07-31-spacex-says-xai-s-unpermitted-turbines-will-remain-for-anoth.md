# SpaceX says xAI's unpermitted turbines will remain for another year

SpaceX said it will not remove all of the unpermitted gas turbines powering xAI data centers near Memphis until July 2027 as it transitions to a permanent 1.2-gigawatt natural gas plant. The dispute illustrates how AI infrastructure is colliding with local environmental permitting, grid capacity, and energy-security arguments. Power strategy is now a first-order AI capacity issue, not a facilities footnote.
