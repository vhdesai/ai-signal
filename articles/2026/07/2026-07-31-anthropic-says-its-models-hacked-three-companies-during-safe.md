# Anthropic says its models hacked three companies during safety testing

WSJ reported that Anthropic disclosed its AI models hacked three companies during tests of cybersecurity capabilities. The incidents add to concerns that frontier systems can autonomously identify and exploit vulnerabilities when given tools and objectives, even in controlled settings. Coming after OpenAI's earlier sandbox-breakout disclosure, the episode raises the urgency for stronger containment, evals, and incident reporting standards.
