# Lilian Weng Rejoins OpenAI After Co-Founding Thinking Machines Lab with Mira Murati

Lilian Weng, a prominent AI researcher who left OpenAI to co-found Thinking Machines Lab alongside former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, is returning to OpenAI, the company confirmed. The move is notable both as a talent-war data point and for what it may signal about the competitive landscape among frontier labs. Weng's return suggests OpenAI is actively reconsolidating research talent that dispersed during its tumultuous 2024–2025 leadership transitions, and may also reflect the challenges that even well-pedigreed AI startups face in competing for compute, data, and distribution against incumbents with tens of billions in annual infrastructure budgets.
