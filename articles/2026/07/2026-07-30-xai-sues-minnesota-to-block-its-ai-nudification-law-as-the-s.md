# xAI sues Minnesota to block its AI "nudification" law as the statute nears effect

xAI has filed a federal lawsuit to block Minnesota's first-in-the-nation law banning AI "nudification" tools, arguing it is an overbroad, content-based restriction on protected speech; the statute takes effect Aug. 1 and carries penalties up to $500,000 per violation. The suit comes as xAI's Grok faces a proposed class action over exactly the imagery the law targets. The case is an early test of whether states can hold AI toolmakers — not just users — liable for foreseeable misuse.
