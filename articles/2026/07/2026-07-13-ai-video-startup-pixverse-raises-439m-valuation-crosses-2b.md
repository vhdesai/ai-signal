# AI Video Startup PixVerse Raises $439M; Valuation Crosses $2B

Singapore-based PixVerse closed a Series C extension totaling $439 million, pushing valuation past $2 billion on ~15 million MAUs. New investors include Alibaba. The company plans to expand its "world model" offering across more geographies. AI video generation remains a well-capitalized, hotly contested category.
