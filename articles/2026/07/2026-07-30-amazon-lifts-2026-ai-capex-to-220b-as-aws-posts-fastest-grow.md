# Amazon Lifts 2026 AI Capex to $220B as AWS Posts Fastest Growth in Five Years

Amazon reported Q2 revenue up 20% to $200.6B, with AWS growing 37% to $42.2B — its fastest pace in nearly five years — and AWS operating income up 64% to $16.6B. CEO Andy Jassy raised full-year capital spending guidance to roughly $220B, largely for data centers, chips and power, and said the company still lacks enough capacity to meet demand. Amazon added that its custom Trainium/Inferentia silicon has crossed a $25B annual revenue run-rate. For executives, this is among the clearest signals yet that hyperscale AI spending is translating into real cloud revenue — and into a credible challenge to Nvidia’s economics.
