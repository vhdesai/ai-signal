# Apple's lawsuit could complicate OpenAI's hardware plans

TechCrunch examined whether Apple's trade-secret lawsuit against OpenAI could jeopardize OpenAI's planned move into consumer hardware and its longer-term IPO ambitions. The dispute adds legal and strategic risk to OpenAI's device roadmap, reportedly tied to its Jony Ive collaboration. For enterprises, it raises questions about platform dependencies, IP diligence, and talent movement across the AI hardware stack.
