# Vertu tests demand for a $6,880 executive AI-agent phone

TechCrunch reviewed Vertu's Alphafold, a $6,880 luxury foldable phone centered on Hermes Agent, an AI assistant designed to analyze files, automate tasks, and support executive workflows. The review found Hermes more willing than Gemini to act across apps and files, but also exposed tradeoffs around autonomy, follow-up questions, and execution reliability. The product is a useful signal that “AI agent” is becoming a premium device differentiator, though actual workflow trust remains uneven.
