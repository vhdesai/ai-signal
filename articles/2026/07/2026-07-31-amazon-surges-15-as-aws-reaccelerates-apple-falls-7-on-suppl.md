# Amazon Surges 15% as AWS Reaccelerates; Apple Falls 7% on Supply Constraints

The AI infrastructure investment thesis received its clearest validation yet as Amazon shares surged 15% after reporting 37% AWS revenue growth — the fastest rate in 18 quarters — while Apple fell roughly 7% on a weak September-quarter forecast citing "supply constraints" on components. Amazon simultaneously raised its 2026 capital spending plan to approximately $220 billion, citing higher memory costs. The market's divergent reaction encapsulates Wall Street's current framework: AI capex is rewarded only when paired with visible, accelerating cloud revenue; Apple's capex-light, license-based AI strategy (spending ~$11B vs. peers' $100B+) drew less enthusiasm even with a 22% iPhone revenue jump.
