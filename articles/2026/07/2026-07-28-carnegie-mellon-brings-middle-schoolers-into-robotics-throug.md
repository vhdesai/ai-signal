# Carnegie Mellon brings middle-schoolers into robotics through a STEM partnership

Carnegie Mellon’s Robotics Institute hosted a week-long STEM camp bringing 20 Pittsburgh middle-schoolers into its Robotics Innovation Center, supported by Professor Sneha Narra’s NSF CAREER Award. The program focuses on advanced manufacturing and robotics education. It is a community-outreach effort rather than a new research result.
