# Are brain waves the next unlock for physical AI?

TechCrunch reports that frontier “physical AI” models — those trained for robotics and embodied tasks — are pushing beyond video toward multi-camera capture, dense annotation, and, increasingly, brain-wave (neural) recordings as a richer training signal. The thesis is that human neural data could help models learn intent and motor control that video alone cannot convey. It is an early-stage direction rather than a shipped result, but it points to where robotics-oriented training data is heading. Worth tracking for anyone evaluating the embodied-AI and robotics roadmap.
