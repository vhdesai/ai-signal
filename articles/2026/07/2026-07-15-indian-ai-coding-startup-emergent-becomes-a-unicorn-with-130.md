# Indian AI coding startup Emergent becomes a unicorn with $130 million Series C

Emergent raised $130 million at a $1.5 billion post-money valuation, led by Creaegis with participation from MNI Ventures-Claypond, Sentinel Global, Khosla Ventures, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Lightspeed, and Y Combinator. The company says it has reached a $120 million annualized revenue run rate and more than 200,000 paying customers, targeting entrepreneurs and SMBs that need application development without traditional engineering teams. Its growth highlights the broadening of AI coding beyond developers into business software creation.
