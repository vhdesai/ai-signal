# Apple opens redesigned Siri AI through the iOS 27 public beta

Apple released the iOS 27 public beta, giving non-developers access to its redesigned AI-powered Siri ahead of the fall launch. The assistant can access on-device context such as emails, photos, messages, and screen content, with Apple Intelligence and Private Cloud Compute underpinning the experience. This is Apple's largest real-world test of whether deeply integrated, privacy-positioned AI can compete with standalone assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.
