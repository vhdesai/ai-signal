# Google DeepMind debuts Gemini Robotics 2 for humanoid robots

DeepMind released the Gemini Robotics 2 family, pairing a high-level “embodied reasoning” planner (Gemini Robotics ER 2) with a vision-language-action model that translates plans into low-level motor commands. The system adds whole-body control, fine dexterity via 22-degree-of-freedom hands, multi-robot coordination, and on-device adaptation to new robot bodies within hours. DeepMind says it can chain hundreds of steps to complete complex tasks such as clearing cluttered rooms. The launch intensifies competition to supply the software “brains” for the emerging humanoid-robot market.
