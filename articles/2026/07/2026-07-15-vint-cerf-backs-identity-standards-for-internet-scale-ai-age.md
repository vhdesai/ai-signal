# Vint Cerf backs identity standards for internet-scale AI agents

Vint Cerf is advising Innovation Labs, an Identity Digital subsidiary proposing DNSid, a registry approach for identifying AI agents through domain-name infrastructure and cryptographic proofs. The effort targets a core gap in agentic computing: how to identify, audit, and assign accountability to autonomous agents operating across the open internet. For enterprises, agent identity and authorization standards may become as foundational as API keys and service accounts are today.
