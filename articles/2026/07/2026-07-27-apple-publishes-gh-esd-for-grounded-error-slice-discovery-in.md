# Apple publishes GH-ESD for grounded error-slice discovery in vision models

Apple published GH-ESD, a method for discovering systematic failure slices in instance-level vision tasks such as object detection and segmentation. The work addresses failures that arise from contextual, relational, and spatially grounded visual patterns, not just image-level attributes. For enterprise AI, this is a reminder that evaluation has to find coherent failure modes in deployed tasks, not only aggregate benchmark scores.
