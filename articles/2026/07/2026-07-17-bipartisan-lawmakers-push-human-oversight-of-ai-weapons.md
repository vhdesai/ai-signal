# Bipartisan lawmakers push human oversight of AI weapons

Axios reports that bipartisan lawmakers are pushing for human oversight of AI weapons. The proposal reflects growing concern that military autonomy may outpace accountability, command responsibility, and escalation safeguards. Even for commercial AI leaders, the debate matters because defense AI policy often shapes broader norms around human-in-the-loop requirements for high-risk systems.
