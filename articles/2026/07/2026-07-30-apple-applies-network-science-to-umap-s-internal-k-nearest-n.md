# Apple Applies Network Science to UMAP's Internal k-Nearest-Neighbor Graph

Apple researchers showed how to mine the internal k-nearest-neighbor graph built by UMAP instead of relying only on the final low-dimensional plot. By applying graph techniques like PageRank, k-core decomposition, and clustering coefficients, they extract richer structural insights from data. The approach helps identify representative points, dense regions, and tight neighborhoods without throwing away high-dimensional information. For enterprise data science teams, that could improve anomaly detection, data quality assessment, and exploratory analysis with minimal extra compute. It is also a useful reminder that better ML insight sometimes comes from understanding intermediates more deeply, not just building larger models. Links: Apple ML Research
