# Nature Health: 1.7M Copilot Health Conversations Mapped Across 109 Countries

A Microsoft AI study analyzed 1.7M health-related Copilot conversations across 109 countries, finding lower public confidence in hospitals predicted more intense health querying, and wealthier, older nations skewed toward clinical navigation questions. Co-authored by Mustafa Suleyman. Observational and shows associations, not causation — but an early, large-scale look at how populations actually use chatbots for health.
