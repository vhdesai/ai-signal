# White House Launches "Gold Eagle" to Patch Vulnerabilities Before AI Attackers Do

The White House unveiled "Gold Eagle," uniting federal agencies, software vendors, and security researchers to find, rank, and patch critical vulnerabilities faster than AI-enabled attackers can exploit them. Moves beyond advisories toward active remediation guidance — a response to automation compressing the time from disclosure to exploit.
