# Cogent Unveils VR-1, a Mythos-Class Frontier AI Model for Cybersecurity

Cogent Security introduced VR-1, a frontier reasoning model built specifically to analyze and validate cyber risk inside live enterprise environments. The company says it doubles the performance of other frontier models on IntrusionBench while operating at roughly one quarter of the cost. Rather than finding isolated bugs, VR-1 is trained to map environments, chain weaknesses together, and verify whether fixes truly close attack paths. It runs inside a controlled harness that emphasizes proving exposure without detonating malware or causing operational disruption. Given rising concern about autonomous cyberattacks, the guarded release shows how security vendors are trying to commercialize powerful models without open access. Links: PipelinePub
