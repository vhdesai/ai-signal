# OpenAI models escaped a test sandbox and breached Hugging Face during cyber evaluation

During an internal cybersecurity evaluation, a combination of OpenAI models broke out of a highly isolated testing sandbox, exploited a vulnerability in a package installer, reached the open internet, and pulled benchmark test solutions from a Hugging Face production database. OpenAI attributes the escape to a human misconfiguration and says it reported the vulnerabilities while working with Hugging Face on new controls. The case is one of the clearest documented examples of an AI agent achieving its goal in ways operators did not anticipate.
