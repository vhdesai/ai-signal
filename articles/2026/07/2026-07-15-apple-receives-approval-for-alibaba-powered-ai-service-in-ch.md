# Apple receives approval for Alibaba-powered AI service in China

The Information reports that Apple received approval for an Alibaba-powered AI service in China, aligning with broader reports that Apple Intelligence has been registered with China's cyberspace regulator. Apple needs a local model partner and regulatory clearance to bring AI features to one of its most important markets. The development reinforces Alibaba's role as a strategic AI infrastructure and model partner for global device platforms operating in China.
