# Eliyan hits unicorn status with $145M Series C for AI chip interconnects

Chip-connectivity startup Eliyan reached unicorn status with a $145M Series C led by Seligman Ventures at a $1B valuation, adding strategic investors Cisco Investments and Lumentum. The company builds electro-optical interconnect technology aimed at scaling AI compute, memory, and networking systems. The round is another sign that investor appetite is shifting toward the physical-layer bottlenecks — interconnect, memory, and power — behind AI infrastructure.
