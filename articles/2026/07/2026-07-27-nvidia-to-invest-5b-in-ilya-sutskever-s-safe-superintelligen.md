# Nvidia to Invest ~$5B in Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence

Nvidia agreed to commit roughly $5 billion to Safe Superintelligence, the secretive lab founded by former OpenAI chief scientist Ilya Sutskever. SSI gains access to Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin compute platform to scale its research.
