# TSMC reportedly plans another $100B U.S. investment as AI demand surges

The Information reports that TSMC is planning another $100 billion U.S. investment as AI demand surges. Combined with record Q2 results and continued advanced-packaging expansion, the report suggests the AI chip supply chain is entering another phase of geographic and capital expansion. For customers, the implication is more U.S.-based capacity over time, but also a much larger long-duration capex commitment tied to AI demand durability.
