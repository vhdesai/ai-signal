# Google Pulls Google Earth’s AI Image Tool One Day After Launch Over Fake-Satellite Risk

Google launched a feature — powered by its Nano Banana 2 image model — that let Google Earth users generate AI edits on top of real satellite imagery, then rolled it back within a day after researchers showed it could fabricate convincing fake events such as accidents and military activity. Generated images carried SynthID watermarks, but the episode showed how embedding generative editing into a trusted mapping product can erode the evidentiary value of satellite imagery.
