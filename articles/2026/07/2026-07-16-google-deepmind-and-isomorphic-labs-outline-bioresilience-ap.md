# Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs outline bioresilience approach

Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs published a joint approach to bioresilience, focused on preventing model misuse, detecting outbreaks faster, and accelerating response through trusted partnerships. The companies say they have advanced more than 15 partnerships with government bodies, biosecurity organizations, and research groups over the past year. The announcement places biosecurity more centrally in frontier AI safety, especially as biological design tools become more powerful. URL: https://deepmind.google/blog/our-approach-to-bioresilience/
