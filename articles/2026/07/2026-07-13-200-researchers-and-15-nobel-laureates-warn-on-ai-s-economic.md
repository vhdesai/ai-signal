# 200+ Researchers and 15 Nobel Laureates Warn on AI's Economic and Labor Impact

More than 200 researchers — including 15 Nobel laureates (Spence, Acemoglu, Johnson) plus signatories from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google — issued a joint statement urging urgent policy action on AI's economic effects. They warned AI could drive a transformation larger than the Industrial Revolution but "vastly shorter" in time frame. The unusual breadth — frontier-lab executives signing alongside independent academics — reframes labor displacement from a research topic into an active policy demand.
