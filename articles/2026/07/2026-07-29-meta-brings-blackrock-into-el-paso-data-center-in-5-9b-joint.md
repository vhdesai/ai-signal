# Meta Brings BlackRock Into El Paso Data Center in $5.9B Joint Venture

Meta Platforms has structured its $10 billion El Paso, Texas, data center campus as a joint venture with BlackRock, which will own 80% of the project. BlackRock is putting in $4.9 billion in cash, partially funded by a $12.5 billion debt financing that priced at higher interest rates than a comparable deal struck last year, signaling rising costs for AI infrastructure capital. Meta will recoup $1 billion after contributing the land and buildings under construction, and will lease the campus back under a four-year agreement with extension options spanning 20 years. This is Meta's second such asset-light data center structure, following a nearly identical arrangement with Blue Owl for its Louisiana Hyperion project, suggesting a broader industry shift toward off-balance-sheet financing to manage the staggering costs of the AI infrastructure buildout.
