# Nscale buys Anyscale for $1.65B to own more of the AI compute stack

British "neocloud" Nscale agreed to acquire Anyscale — the company behind the open-source Ray framework for scaling AI workloads — for a reported $1.65B, per Bloomberg. The deal pairs Anyscale's orchestration, observability, and training/inference tooling with Nscale's data centers, GPUs, and power, letting Nscale capture more of customers' end-to-end AI spend. It is another sign that AI-infrastructure providers are integrating vertically — from silicon and power up through the software layer — as they race to lock in capacity.
