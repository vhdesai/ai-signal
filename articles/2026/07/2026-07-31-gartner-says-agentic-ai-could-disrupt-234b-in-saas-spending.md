# Gartner says agentic AI could disrupt $234B in SaaS spending

CIO Dive highlighted Gartner's projection that agentic AI will disrupt $234 billion in SaaS spending as AI agents reduce dependence on traditional software interfaces. The shift reframes AI from an additive software category to a potential substitution layer that can alter dashboards, workflows, seats, and pricing models.
