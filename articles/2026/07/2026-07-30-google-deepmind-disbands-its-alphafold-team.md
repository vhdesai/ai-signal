# Google DeepMind disbands its AlphaFold team

According to the Financial Times, DeepMind dissolved the Nobel Prize–winning AlphaFold team, reassigning key members as protein-structure work shifts toward drug-discovery spinout Isomorphic Labs. The move reflects a broader push to commercialize DeepMind's scientific breakthroughs.
