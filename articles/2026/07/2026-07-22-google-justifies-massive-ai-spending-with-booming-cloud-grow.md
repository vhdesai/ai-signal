# Google justifies massive AI spending with booming cloud growth

TechCrunch reports that Google Cloud revenue grew 82% year over year to $24.8 billion, driven largely by enterprise AI infrastructure and AI solution adoption. Alphabet's cloud backlog reportedly reached $514 billion, while the company still expects $180 billion to $190 billion in capital expenditures this year. The result gives Google a stronger investor narrative for AI spending: cloud AI demand is converting into revenue, not only speculative capacity buildout.
