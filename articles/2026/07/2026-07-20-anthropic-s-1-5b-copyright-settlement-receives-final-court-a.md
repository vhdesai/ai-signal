# Anthropic's $1.5B copyright settlement receives final court approval

Anthropic's landmark $1.5 billion copyright settlement received final court approval, with more than 91% of rightsholders filing claims and more than $101 million in attorney fees. The court distinguished lawfully obtained works, which may support fair-use arguments, from piracy-sourced material. The settlement resolves one major case while similar suits against OpenAI, Meta, and others remain active.
