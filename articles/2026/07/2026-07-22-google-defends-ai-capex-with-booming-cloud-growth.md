# Google defends AI capex with booming cloud growth

Alphabet's cloud unit is thriving as enterprises adopt AI and AI-infrastructure services, helping the company post strong results and defend heavy capital expenditure. The framing positions Google Cloud growth as the commercial justification for aggressive AI spend — a notable contrast to broader investor anxiety about AI capex elsewhere in the sector. For cloud buyers, it also confirms AI workloads are increasingly central to hyperscaler economics.
