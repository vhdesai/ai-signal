# Google uses Wall Street financing techniques to expand AI chip sales

The Information reports that Google is using more sophisticated financing structures as AI spending changes Big Tech balance sheets. The approach helps expand chip sales and AI infrastructure capacity without relying only on conventional cash-funded capex. It is another sign that hyperscalers are adapting capital-markets tools to keep up with compute demand.
