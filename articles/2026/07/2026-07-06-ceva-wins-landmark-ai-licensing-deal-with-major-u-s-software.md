# Ceva wins landmark AI licensing deal with major U.S. software and AI platform company

Ceva disclosed a strategic licensing deal for its NeuPro-M neural processing unit IP with an unnamed major U.S. software and AI platform company. The announcement reinforces a broader pattern: software platform owners are seeking tighter control over AI compute stacks, especially for edge and device-side inference.
