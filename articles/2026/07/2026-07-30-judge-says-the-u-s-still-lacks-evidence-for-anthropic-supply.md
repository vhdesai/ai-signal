# Judge says the U.S. still lacks evidence for Anthropic supply-chain risk label

A federal judge said the Trump administration had not presented enough evidence to justify labeling Anthropic a supply-chain risk and banning federal use of its technology. The dispute centers on Anthropic's refusal to allow certain military uses and the government's claim that the company could alter or disable AI models during operations. The case is an important precedent for how governments evaluate AI vendors when safety policies, procurement rules, and national-security claims collide.
