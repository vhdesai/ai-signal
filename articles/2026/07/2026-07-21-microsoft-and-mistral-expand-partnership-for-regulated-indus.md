# Microsoft and Mistral Expand Partnership for Regulated-Industry Sovereign AI

Microsoft and Mistral announced a significant expansion emphasizing data residency, governance, and deployment flexibility. Positions Mistral's models within Microsoft's enterprise stack as an alternative for customers wary of single-vendor lock-in across cloud, cloud-connected, and fully disconnected environments. Extends Microsoft's multi-model strategy beyond OpenAI.
