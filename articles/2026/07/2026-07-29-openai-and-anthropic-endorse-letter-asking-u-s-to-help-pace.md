# OpenAI and Anthropic endorse letter asking U.S. to help pace AI development

More than 1,100 executives and employees across leading AI companies signed a letter asking the U.S. government to support technical and governance tools that could deliberately pace frontier AI development. OpenAI and Anthropic endorsed the effort, a rare point of public alignment between commercial rivals. The letter indicates that pacing, evaluation, and controlled deployment are moving from activist arguments into mainstream frontier-lab policy engagement.
