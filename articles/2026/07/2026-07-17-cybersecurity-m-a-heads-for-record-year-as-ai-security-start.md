# Cybersecurity M&A heads for record year as AI security startups reshape the sector

WSJ Wealth Adviser reports that cybersecurity dealmaking surged in the first half and is headed for a record year, powered by demand for AI-security startups. The story reflects a second-order effect of AI adoption: as agents, copilots, and model-connected workflows proliferate, security buyers are consolidating around tools that can govern AI-era attack surfaces. It also reinforces that AI is driving not only software budgets but M&A activity in adjacent sectors.
