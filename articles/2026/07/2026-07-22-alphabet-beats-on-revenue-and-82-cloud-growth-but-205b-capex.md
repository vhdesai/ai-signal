# Alphabet Beats on Revenue and 82% Cloud Growth, but $205B Capex Hike Rattles Investors

Alphabet reported Q2 revenue of $119.8B (up 24%), with Google Cloud surging 82% to $24.8B and cloud backlog reaching $514B. Shares fell after-hours as 2026 capex guidance rose to $195–205B (from $180–190B), with Q2 capex alone doubling YoY to $44.9B. A ~$99B gain on Anthropic and SpaceX stakes lifted net income 298%. Management said it will lean on third-party cloud as a Q3 bridge and is committing compute to Gemini 4.
