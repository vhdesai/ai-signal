# Sam Altman signals willingness to 'pace' AI as 1,100+ lab employees petition Washington

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said it may be time to "pace" AI development so society has time to adapt, echoing a public statement signed by more than 1,100 employees across leading labs urging Washington to build tools to deliberately slow frontier progress. OpenAI and Anthropic signaled support within hours. The episode matters less for concrete commitments than for reframing "deceleration" as an insider position — a possible precursor to voluntary or regulated pacing mechanisms.
