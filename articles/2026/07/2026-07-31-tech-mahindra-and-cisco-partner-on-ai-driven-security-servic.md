# Tech Mahindra and Cisco Partner on AI-Driven Security Service Edge

Tech Mahindra and Cisco announced an AI-driven Security Service Edge offering that combines managed services with Cisco's Secure Access platform. The stack brings together secure web gateway, CASB, ZTNA, firewall-as-a-service, and DLP under a single cloud-native architecture. Notably, the partners are explicitly framing the product around protecting generative AI tools and autonomous agent workflows. That positioning reflects how quickly agentic AI has become a first-class security design problem rather than a future roadmap item. For distributed enterprises, the pitch is consolidated policy enforcement across users, devices, applications, and now AI agents themselves. Links: PipelinePub
