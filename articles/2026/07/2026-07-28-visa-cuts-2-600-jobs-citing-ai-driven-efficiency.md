# Visa cuts 2,600 jobs, citing AI-driven efficiency

Visa said it would cut about 2,600 jobs, primarily in technology and product teams, as it seeks greater efficiency from AI. The move is notable because AI’s impact is moving from product roadmaps into operating models, organizational design, and headcount decisions. For executives, the signal is that AI productivity claims are increasingly being converted into explicit labor-cost actions.
