# Treasury threatens sanctions after White House claims Moonshot distilled Anthropic's Fable

White House OSTP Director Michael Kratsios publicly accused China's Moonshot AI of large-scale, covert industrial distillation of Anthropic's Fable model to build Kimi K3, and of accessing export-restricted Nvidia GB300 chips through Thailand. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent warned that the U.S. could sanction Chinese AI companies in response. These are contested allegations, not proven findings, but the episode moves U.S.-China AI rivalry from benchmark competition into allegations of model theft and export-control circumvention.
