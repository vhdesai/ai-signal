# Oracle and Databricks add AI-native enterprise workflow building blocks

Oracle introduced a builder experience that lets developers create and deploy agentic AI applications directly within Oracle Fusion Applications, targeting workflows tied to existing Fusion data and processes. Databricks made sample datasets available through OpenSharing in the samples catalog, easing access to reference datasets for analytics and AI workloads. These are incremental but practical examples of AI moving into enterprise platform plumbing rather than standalone chat interfaces.
