# Claude autonomously finds new attacks on post-quantum (HAWK) and reduced-round AES

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team reported that Claude 'Mythos Preview' improved the best-known attack on HAWK — a NIST third-round post-quantum signature candidate — in roughly 60 hours, effectively halving its key strength, and found a 200–800x faster attack on a 7-round (reduced) variant of AES. Neither result affects production systems (HAWK is not deployed and the AES break is on a weakened variant), and findings were responsibly disclosed to HAWK's authors, NIST, and government and industry partners. Each result cost roughly $100K in API compute — an early signal that frontier models can materially accelerate cryptanalysis. AI Safety & Policy OpNVHF
