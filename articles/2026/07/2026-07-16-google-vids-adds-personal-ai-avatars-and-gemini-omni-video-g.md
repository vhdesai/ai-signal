# Google Vids adds personal AI avatars and Gemini Omni video generation

Google updated Vids with personal AI avatars that can look and sound like the account holder, plus Gemini Omni-powered video generation and step-by-step editing. Google says avatars are tied to user accounts, age-restricted, and invisibly watermarked with SynthID. The move pushes Workspace deeper into AI-generated business video and puts Google into closer competition with avatar-video companies such as Synthesia, HeyGen, Captions, and D-ID.
