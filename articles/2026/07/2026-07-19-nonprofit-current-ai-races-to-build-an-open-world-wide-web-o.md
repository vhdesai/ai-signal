# Nonprofit Current AI races to build an open World Wide Web of AI

TechCrunch profiled Current AI, a nonprofit building freely available AI infrastructure spanning on-device models, AI chat, and other open components designed to leave no culture behind. The effort positions public-interest AI as an alternative to closed commercial platforms. For enterprises, it points to a growing pool of permissively available components for internal tools and localized AI systems.
