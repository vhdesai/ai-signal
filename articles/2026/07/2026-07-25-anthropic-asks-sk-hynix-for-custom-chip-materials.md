# Anthropic asks SK Hynix for custom-chip materials

Anthropic reportedly asked SK Hynix for semiconductor materials tied to custom ASIC and GPU development. If the effort advances, Anthropic would be moving in the direction of Google’s TPU and Amazon’s Trainium strategy: reducing dependence on Nvidia by vertically integrating parts of the AI compute stack.
