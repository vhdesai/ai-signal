# Microsoft's fiscal-Q4 beat lifts shares ~8% as it raises AI-driven capital spending

Microsoft reported fiscal Q4 revenue of roughly $90.0B, ahead of the ~$87.7B consensus, and shares rose about 8% after hours as management again lifted capital-spending plans, citing continued AI demand. Results were helped by Azure strength and a gain tied to its Anthropic stake. The signal to investors: the hyperscaler capex cycle is still accelerating, not peaking. For enterprise buyers, sustained Azure/AI investment points to more capacity — and pricing pressure to fund it. EARNINGS
