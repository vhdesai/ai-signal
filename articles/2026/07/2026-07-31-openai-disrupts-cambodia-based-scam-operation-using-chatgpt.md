# OpenAI disrupts Cambodia-based scam operation using ChatGPT

OpenAI said it disrupted a Cambodia-based scam operation that used ChatGPT to support investment, romance, gambling, and impersonation schemes. The disclosure adds to evidence that generative AI is being adopted by organized fraud groups for scale, localization, and social engineering. For enterprises and regulators, the operational challenge is detecting misuse patterns quickly without degrading legitimate business use.
