# Microsoft Q4 Blowout: Profit Jumps 31%, Cloud Posts Fastest Growth in Four Years

Microsoft reported quarterly profit of $35.8 billion, a 31% surge that beat consensus estimates and validated the company's disciplined approach to AI capital expenditure. Azure cloud revenue growth accelerated three percentage points to 43%, the fastest pace in four years, with CFO Amy Hood predicting even faster growth in the current quarter as "customer demand continues to exceed available capacity." Crucially, Microsoft held its annual capex plan at $175 billion (lowered from ~$190B via an accounting reclassification), calming investor fears about runaway spending. Operating expenses rose just 10% while operating income climbed 18% — demonstrating that the company can absorb $35.8 billion in quarterly capex without sacrificing profitability. Shares jumped 9% in pre-market trading, helping the Nasdaq recover from closing in correction territory the prior session.
