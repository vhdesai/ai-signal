# Google plans new “Frozen” chip to run AI models more efficiently

The Information reports that Google plans a new “Frozen” chip designed to run its AI models much more efficiently. The item points to a continuing shift from generic accelerator procurement toward vertically optimized silicon for large-scale inference economics. If successful, custom chips could strengthen hyperscalers' cost advantage and make model serving efficiency a key competitive lever.
