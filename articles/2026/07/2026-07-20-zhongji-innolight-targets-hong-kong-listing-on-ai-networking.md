# Zhongji Innolight targets Hong Kong listing on AI networking demand

Chinese optical transceiver maker Zhongji Innolight won approval for a Hong Kong listing that could become the city's largest IPO of 2026. Optical interconnect is a critical bottleneck as AI clusters scale, making networking components a major beneficiary of the AI infrastructure boom. The listing underscores that AI supply-chain value is broadening beyond GPUs and memory into the networking fabric that links accelerators together.
