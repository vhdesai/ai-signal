# Moody's warns AI capex is straining Big Tech credit quality

Moody's warned that the AI infrastructure race is pushing even cash-rich technology companies toward debt and off-balance-sheet financing structures. The report reframes AI capex as a credit-quality issue for Amazon, Meta, Alphabet, and peers, not just a growth investment. Investors should watch financing structure, lease obligations, and utilization — not only headline AI spending numbers.
