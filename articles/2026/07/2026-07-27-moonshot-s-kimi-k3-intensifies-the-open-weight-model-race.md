# Moonshot’s Kimi K3 intensifies the open-weight model race

Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3 is live as a massive open-weight model, reportedly 2.8 trillion parameters and about a 1.4 TB download. It appears especially relevant for coding and agentic tasks, even if top closed models still lead on general capability. Its release intensified U.S.-China AI tensions because some U.S. officials have alleged model distillation while outside experts have questioned how conclusive the evidence is.
