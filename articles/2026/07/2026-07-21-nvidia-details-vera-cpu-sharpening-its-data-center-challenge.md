# Nvidia Details Vera CPU, Sharpening Its Data-Center Challenge to AMD and Intel

Nvidia released new technical specs for Vera, its next-gen data-center CPU that pairs with Rubin GPUs in forthcoming AI "factory" systems. Deepens Nvidia's push to control more of the server stack and sets up a more direct competitive comparison with AMD and Intel in the data center.
