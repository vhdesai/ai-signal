# Intel's data-center and AI unit jumps on AI customer demand

Intel shares rose after the company reported strong second-quarter results, including 59% growth in its data-center and AI hardware unit to $6.3 billion. The Information notes that AI agent workloads rely heavily on CPUs, memory, and orchestration in addition to GPUs, giving Intel exposure to a broader AI infrastructure stack. The foundry business also improved, reinforcing the national-security dimension of U.S. chip capacity.
