# ICON to deploy Anthropic's Claude across the clinical-trial lifecycle

Contract research organization ICON plc (NASDAQ: ICLR) announced a multi-year collaboration with Anthropic to bring Claude's capabilities across the clinical-trial lifecycle, advancing its broader digital and AI strategy. It is a concrete enterprise deployment of frontier AI into regulated drug-development workflows, where accuracy, traceability and auditability are paramount.
