# Top 30 VC Firms' AI Portfolios Hit $7 Trillion Combined Valuation

PitchBook's Q2 2026 Venture Monitor reveals that the 30 leading venture capital firms have seen their collective portfolio valuations double to $7 trillion since 2023, with valuation step-ups now matching the 2021–2022 peaks. These firms have raised over one-third of all global venture capital this year and used it to acquire dominant positions across the AI market, from earliest-stage seed deals to the largest late-stage companies. The concentration is unprecedented: a small number of AI companies have absorbed vast amounts of capital while the overall number of VC firms has shrunk. PitchBook frames the central question as whether AI is "the generational technology its backers expect, or have they paid the highest prices in history for a bubble."
