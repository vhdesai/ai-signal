# Big Tech pressed to justify AI spending as investors dump stocks

A sharp selloff in chip and technology stocks put the largest AI spenders under pressure to show clearer returns heading into earnings season. The scrutiny tests the multi-trillion-dollar capex thesis behind data-center and accelerator buildouts. Expect investors to focus more closely on utilization, monetization, depreciation schedules, and whether AI products can generate enough revenue to justify the infrastructure bill.
