# Bristol Myers Squibb to Build One of the Largest Private NVIDIA Clusters in Life Sciences

BMS will deploy an NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD on next-gen Vera Rubin NVL72 systems — what it calls the most powerful single-owned NVIDIA infrastructure in life sciences. Cites up to 10× greater performance per megawatt vs predecessor, powering a "Predict First" approach where AI generates predictions before the bench. Concrete case of frontier compute moving into regulated enterprise R&D.
