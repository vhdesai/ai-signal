# Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 as a cheaper, agent-focused flagship

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, positioning it for long-running agents, coding, and professional workflows at a lower price point than earlier premium Opus-tier models. The launch comes as Anthropic prepares for a possible 2026 IPO and seeks to win enterprise agent workloads on price-performance, not only benchmark leadership. The model's timing also reinforces the market shift from chat assistants toward durable, tool-using agents.
