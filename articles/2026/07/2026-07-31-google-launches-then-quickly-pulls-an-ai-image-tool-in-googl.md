# Google launches — then quickly pulls — an AI image tool in Google Earth

One day after adding a “Nano Banana 2” AI image feature to Google Earth, Google rolled it back after researchers showed it could fabricate convincing satellite imagery of war zones and disasters on real coordinates. SynthID watermarking was judged insufficient to contain the risk. The rare, fast reversal on a flagship generative feature highlights mounting concern over geospatial deepfakes.
