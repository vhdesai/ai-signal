# Thinking Machines Lab Releases Inkling, a 975B-Parameter Open-Weight Model

Mira Murati's Thinking Machines released Inkling — a 975B MoE model (41B active) with 1M-token context and the lab's Tinker customization toolkit. Positioned as the leading U.S. open-weight alternative for enterprises that want to inspect, tune, and self-host. Early third-party testing ranked it among the strongest U.S.-based open-weight entrants, though Chinese models still lead several benchmarks.
