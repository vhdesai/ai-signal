# Databricks reaches $188 billion valuation as AI infrastructure demand continues

Databricks announced a new strategic funding round led by Coatue that values the company at $188 billion. The company has successfully repositioned itself from a big-data platform into an AI infrastructure and governance platform, helped by products such as Lakebase, Unity, and Omnigent, and by its advocacy for cost-efficient open-weight coding models. The valuation signals that investors still see enterprise data platforms as core AI infrastructure, even as public markets question parts of the AI trade.
