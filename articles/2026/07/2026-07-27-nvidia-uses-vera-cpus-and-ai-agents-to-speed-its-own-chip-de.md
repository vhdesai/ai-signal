# Nvidia uses Vera CPUs and AI agents to speed its own chip design

Nvidia is putting Vera CPUs to work alongside AI agents to accelerate chip design. The story shows a concrete feedback loop: today’s AI infrastructure is helping design the next generation of AI infrastructure. It also reinforces Nvidia’s argument that its moat is the full platform and design ecosystem, not only GPU accelerators.
