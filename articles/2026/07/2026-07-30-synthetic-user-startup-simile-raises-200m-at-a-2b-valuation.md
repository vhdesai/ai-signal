# Synthetic-user startup Simile raises $200M at a $2B valuation

Simile, which builds AI “synthetic users” to simulate customer and product research, closed a $200M Series B led by Greenoaks at a $2B valuation — just five months after a $100M Series A. Founded by Stanford PhD Joon Sung Park (known for the “Smallville” generative-agents work), the company counts CVS Health among marquee customers and investors. The round underscores investor appetite for application-layer AI that stands in for human research subjects, and adds to a run of rapid up-rounds in the AI startup market. A EARNINGS
