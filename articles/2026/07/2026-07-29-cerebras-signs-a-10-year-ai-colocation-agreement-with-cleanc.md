# Cerebras signs a 10-year AI colocation agreement with CleanCore in Minnesota

CleanCore Solutions (NYSE American: ZONE) signed a 10-year colocation agreement with Cerebras Systems for a Minnesota data-center campus. The deal expands Cerebras’s compute footprint as it scales its wafer-scale AI systems. It adds to a run of long-dated infrastructure commitments across the sector.
