# Thinking Machines Lab debuts Inkling-Small with open weights

Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab released a 276B-total / 12B-active mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token context window and native text, image, and audio support. It scores 31.6% on Humanity's Last Exam and 80.2% on SWE-Bench Verified — roughly matching its larger sibling at about a quarter of the size and fitting on small systems such as an NVIDIA DGX Spark. Full and NVFP4 weights are available via the lab's Tinker platform.
