# Corporate America starts rationing AI as costs balloon

The WSJ reports enterprises are curbing AI usage after some exhausted annual budgets within three months or watched costs double and triple, pushing teams toward cheaper models — including Chinese ones — and multi-model “routing” to control spend. The piece cites examples such as Microsoft paring back internal coding-assistant licenses on cost and one company spending $500M on AI in a single month. It marks an inflection from open-ended experimentation toward unit-economics discipline, reshaping vendor leverage and model selection.
