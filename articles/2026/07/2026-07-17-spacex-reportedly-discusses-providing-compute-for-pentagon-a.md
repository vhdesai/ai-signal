# SpaceX reportedly discusses providing compute for Pentagon AI push

WSJ reports that SpaceX is in talks to provide computing power for the Pentagon's AI effort. If completed, the deal would place SpaceX more directly into AI infrastructure for national-security workloads, not just launch, satellite, or connectivity services. The item underscores how AI compute is becoming a defense-industrial asset and how nontraditional infrastructure providers may enter government AI supply chains. URL behind paywall.
