# Amazon Q2: AWS Revenue Growth Accelerates to 37%, Stock Jumps 9%

Amazon Web Services posted 37% revenue growth in the June quarter, while operating margin improved to 39.4%. CEO Andy Jassy said customers were reserving compute capacity years in advance, supporting a $220 billion capex projection.
