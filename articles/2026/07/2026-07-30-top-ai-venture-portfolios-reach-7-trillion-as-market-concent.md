# Top AI venture portfolios reach $7 trillion as market concentration intensifies

PitchBook reported that portfolio valuations across the top 30 VC firms have doubled to roughly $7 trillion since 2023, with valuation step-ups returning to 2021-2022 levels. The same period has seen fewer firms control a larger share of global venture capital, particularly in AI. The strategic question is whether AI will justify historically high entry prices or expose investors to a concentrated bubble if monetization lags infrastructure spending.
