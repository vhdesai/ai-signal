# Google ships Gemini Flash models and cuts long-horizon agent costs

Google made Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite available, with Gemini 3.6 Flash reportedly cutting agent token costs by up to 65% on long-horizon engineering tasks. The release shows Google leaning into inference economics where its custom silicon advantage is strongest, even as Gemini 3.5 Pro remains in testing. For enterprise buyers, cheaper long-context agent execution may matter more than marginal frontier benchmark gains.
