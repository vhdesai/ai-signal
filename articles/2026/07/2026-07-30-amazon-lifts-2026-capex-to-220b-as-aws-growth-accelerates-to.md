# Amazon lifts 2026 capex to ~$220B as AWS growth accelerates to 37%

Amazon reported Q2 revenue up 20% to $200.6B, with AWS up 37% to $42.2B — its fastest growth in nearly five years — and AWS operating income up 64% to $16.6B. CEO Andy Jassy said the company will spend about $220B this year, much of it on AI data centers, and still lacks enough capacity to meet demand; its custom-silicon (Trainium/Inferentia) run-rate topped $25B. The print is among the clearest signs that AI infrastructure spend is converting into cloud revenue, easing “capex outrunning demand” fears and pressuring Azure and Google Cloud. ________________________________ EARNINGSMEMORY
