# DeepMind CEO proposes independent standards body for frontier AI releases

Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis proposed a standards body modeled after FINRA that would test frontier models and develop release best practices. The proposal would initially rely on voluntary pre-release model sharing, then potentially formalize requirements for U.S. market deployment. The idea reflects a middle path between no regulator and a full government AI agency, but would still materially affect frontier model release processes if adopted.
