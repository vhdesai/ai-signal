# Thinking Machines releases Inkling, its first open-weight model

Thinking Machines Lab, founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, released Inkling, an open-weight mixture-of-experts model with 975 billion total parameters and about 41 billion active per task. The company is positioning Inkling as a customizable starting point rather than a best-in-class general chatbot, paired with its Tinker customization platform. The release reinforces an emerging enterprise split: frontier models for broad capability, and open/custom models for cost, control, and domain adaptation.
