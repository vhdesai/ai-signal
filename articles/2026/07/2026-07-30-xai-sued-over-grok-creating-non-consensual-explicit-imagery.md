# xAI Sued Over Grok Creating Non-Consensual Explicit Imagery of a Minor

An Arkansas family sued xAI, alleging Grok was used to generate non-consensual explicit imagery of a minor. A second related lawsuit surfaced the same day, intensifying legal focus on AI nudification and abuse-enabling image generation. The cases spotlight the widening gap between model capability and the safeguards needed to stop especially harmful misuse. They also arrive as the EU moves to ban precisely this category of synthetic content, suggesting tighter international convergence. For AI companies, this is becoming not just a reputational issue but a material source of litigation and regulatory exposure. Links: The Christian Post
