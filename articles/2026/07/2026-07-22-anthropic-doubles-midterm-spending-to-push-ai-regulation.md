# Anthropic doubles midterm spending to push AI regulation

Anthropic added another $20 million to Public First Action, a bipartisan pro-oversight nonprofit, bringing its total to $40 million ahead of the U.S. midterms. The move sharpens a Silicon Valley proxy war, positioning Anthropic against groups backed by OpenAI president Greg Brockman, Marc Andreessen, and Ben Horowitz. CEO Dario Amodei has argued for an FAA-style AI regulator, and the spending shows labs are now competing directly over the regulatory architecture that will govern them.
