# Anthropic and OpenAI split on how AI should be regulated

Anthropic is pursuing a state-by-state strategy that encourages legislatures to adopt progressively tougher safety requirements for the most capable systems, rejecting the idea of a single harmonized ceiling. OpenAI, by contrast, is lobbying to streamline rules toward common ground and favors federal pre-emption of state law. With both labs eyeing public markets, the two leaders of the field are now openly divided on the regulatory architecture that will govern them.
