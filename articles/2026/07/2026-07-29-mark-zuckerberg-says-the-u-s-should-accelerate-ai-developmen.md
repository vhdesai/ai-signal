# Mark Zuckerberg says the U.S. should accelerate AI development, not restrict it

WSJ reports that Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg argued the U.S. should accelerate AI development rather than restrict it. The position aligns with other industry efforts to resist broad restrictions on open models and to frame AI leadership as a national competitiveness issue. The debate is increasingly about the policy balance between speed, openness, and controls after recent model-security incidents. URL behind paywall.
