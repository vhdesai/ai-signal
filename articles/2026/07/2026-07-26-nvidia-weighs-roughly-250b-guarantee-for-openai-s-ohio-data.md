# Nvidia weighs roughly $250B guarantee for OpenAI’s Ohio data center

Nvidia is reportedly in talks to guarantee about $250 billion in financing for OpenAI’s Ohio data-center project, one of the most ambitious transactions of the current AI buildout. DealBook described Nvidia as acting like a “central bank of the A.I. economy,” providing the kind of credit support traditional markets may be reluctant to extend. The structure could accelerate OpenAI’s 10-GW campus while raising circular-financing concerns because Nvidia would be helping fund demand for its own chips.
