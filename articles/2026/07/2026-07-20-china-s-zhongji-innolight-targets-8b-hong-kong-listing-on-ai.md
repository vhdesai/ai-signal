# China's Zhongji Innolight Targets ~$8B Hong Kong Listing on AI-Networking Demand

Optical transceiver maker Zhongji Innolight won Hong Kong listing approval for what could be the city's largest IPO of 2026. Optical interconnect is a critical bottleneck as training clusters scale to tens of thousands of chips. The AI infrastructure boom is minting large opportunities in networking components, not just processors.
