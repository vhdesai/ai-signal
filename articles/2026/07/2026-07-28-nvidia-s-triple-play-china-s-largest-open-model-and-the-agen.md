# Nvidia's Triple Play, China's Largest Open Model, and the Agentic-Security Land Grab

The past 24 hours were dominated by Nvidia, which moved on three fronts at once: a reported ~$250B financing backstop for OpenAI's planned 10 GW Ohio data-center campus, a ~$5B stake in Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence, and a 37-member Open Secure AI Alliance. In parallel, China's open-weight push escalated with Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 — the largest open model released to date — while Microsoft pushed deeper into agentic cyber-defense and Anthropic clarified its stance on open weights amid a widening U.S.–China policy debate.
