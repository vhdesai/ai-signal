# Waymo: an AI project isn't 'ready' until its evals are

Waymo detailed how it treats AI evaluation as continuous engineering rather than a one-time pre-launch gate. The readiness philosophy, tying deployment to the maturity of eval suites rather than raw model performance, offers a template directly applicable to enterprise agent rollouts. It reflects a growing emphasis on measurement discipline in production AI.
