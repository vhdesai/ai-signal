# MiniMax releases H3 video model, with open weights to follow

China's MiniMax released H3, a multimodal video-generation model that accepts text, image, video, and audio inputs and produces up to 15-second clips at 2K resolution with native stereo sound, plus editing and motion-transfer. The Shanghai-based "AI tiger" said it will publish H3's model weights within days, extending China's open-weight strategy into video — a segment where most leading models remain proprietary. The move intensifies competition with ByteDance and Kuaishou and pressures Western incumbents on both capability and openness.
