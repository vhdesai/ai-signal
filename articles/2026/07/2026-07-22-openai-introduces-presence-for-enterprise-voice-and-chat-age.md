# OpenAI introduces Presence for enterprise voice and chat agents

OpenAI introduced Presence, an enterprise AI agent platform for deploying voice and chat agents in customer and internal workflows. The product targets a crowded but high-value segment: contact centers, internal help desks, and process automation where AI agents must integrate with existing business systems and be trusted at scale. The launch shows OpenAI moving further from model access toward packaged enterprise workflow platforms. URL: https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/
