# Jump Capital raises $350 million fund for AI investments

The Information reports that Jump Capital raised a $350 million fund focused on AI investments. The fundraise is another sign that AI venture capital remains active even as investors scrutinize infrastructure costs, model margins, and crowded application categories. The stronger opportunities are likely to sit where AI creates durable workflow control, proprietary data loops, or infrastructure leverage rather than model-wrapper applications.
