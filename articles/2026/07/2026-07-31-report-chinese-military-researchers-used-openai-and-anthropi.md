# Report: Chinese Military Researchers Used OpenAI and Anthropic Outputs to Train Defense AI

A review of more than 80 Chinese academic papers and patents found military-affiliated researchers used outputs from OpenAI and Anthropic models — via distillation — to help train domestic systems for defense, surveillance, drones, cyberwarfare and battlefield planning, despite usage policies barring military use. The findings highlight that model capabilities can cross borders through APIs even when weights and chips are restricted, sharpening the export-control debate and likely pushing labs toward tighter customer verification and extraction monitoring. by Microsoft Copilot.
