# Amazon raises 2026 capital spending to ~$220B as AWS growth hits 37%

On its Q2 call, Amazon lifted 2026 capital-spending guidance to about $220 billion, citing AI demand and higher memory costs, while AWS revenue grew roughly 37% year over year — well ahead of expectations. The scale of the buildout reaffirms that hyperscaler capex remains both the primary constraint and the primary enabler of the AI cycle. It also raises the bar for the free-cash-flow scrutiny investors are now applying to AI infrastructure.
