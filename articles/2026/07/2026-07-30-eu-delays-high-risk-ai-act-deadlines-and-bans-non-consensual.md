# EU Delays High-Risk AI Act Deadlines and Bans Non-Consensual Deepfake Imagery

An EU AI Act amendment pushes back high-risk system obligations by 18 months to two years because key standards and authorities are still incomplete. The same package also bans AI systems that generate non-consensual intimate imagery, with that prohibition taking effect in December 2026. Other changes soften some AI-literacy obligations while adding new supply-chain cooperation rules backed by potentially large fines. Transparency labeling for synthetic media gets a brief extension as regulators try to align enforcement with technical reality. For multinationals, the message is mixed: there is more compliance runway, but still plenty of planning uncertainty as the framework keeps shifting. Links: JD Supra
