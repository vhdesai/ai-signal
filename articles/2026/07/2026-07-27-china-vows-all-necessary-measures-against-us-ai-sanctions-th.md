# China vows 'all necessary measures' against US AI-sanctions threat

China's Commerce Ministry warned it would take "all necessary measures" if the US sanctions Chinese AI firms over model "distillation," calling the threat a "typical act of AI hegemony." The statement responds to Treasury Secretary Bessent's warning and to IP-theft claims from OpenAI and Anthropic. It marks a sharp escalation in the US–China AI trade conflict.
