# Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 open weights go live — the largest open-weight model to date

Moonshot AI released the open weights for Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model billed as the largest open-weight release yet, at 00:00 UTC on July 27. The full weights run roughly 1.4 terabytes even with MXFP4 4-bit quantization, so practical self-hosting is limited to large teams and inference providers rather than individual developers. Independent assessments place K3 as a strong coding and agent specialist that still trails the leading closed models (Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.6 Sol) on general capability. The strategic draw is data control — self-hosting keeps prompts in-house and sidesteps residency questions tied to Chinese API access.
