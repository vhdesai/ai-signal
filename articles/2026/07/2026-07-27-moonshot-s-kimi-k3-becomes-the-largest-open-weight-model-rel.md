# Moonshot’s Kimi K3 becomes the largest open-weight model released

Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3 is now live as an enormous open-weight model, reportedly 2.8 trillion parameters and roughly a 1.4 TB download. It appears strong on coding and agentic tasks while still trailing the top closed models on general capability. The release intensifies the U.S.-China open-model debate because U.S. officials have alleged distillation while outside experts have questioned how conclusive the evidence is.
