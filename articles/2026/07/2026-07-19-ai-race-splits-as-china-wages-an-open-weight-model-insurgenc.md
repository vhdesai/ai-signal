# AI race splits as China wages an open-weight model insurgency

Axios reports that the AI race is splitting in two as Chinese open-weight models rapidly narrow the perceived gap with U.S. frontier systems. Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 is the focal point, with coverage describing it as a shock to investors, U.S. policymakers, and model providers. The strategic implication is that model buyers may increasingly treat open-weight systems as credible production options, pressuring closed-model pricing and weakening the assumption that U.S. frontier labs retain a durable lead.
