# OpenAI announces Project Camellia, a $30B Georgia data-center buildout

OpenAI announced Project Camellia, a roughly $30 billion, 3.2-gigawatt data-center project in Georgia. The announcement makes the infrastructure dimension of OpenAI's frontier strategy explicit: model competition now requires massive power, land, and capital commitments. It also lands amid growing scrutiny of whether frontier labs can finance and monetize the compute footprint they are locking in.
