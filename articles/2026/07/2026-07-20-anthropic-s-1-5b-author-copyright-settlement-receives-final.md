# Anthropic's $1.5B Author Copyright Settlement Receives Final Court Approval

A federal judge granted final approval to Anthropic's $1.5B settlement with authors who alleged it acquired pirated books to train Claude. More than 91% of eligible rightsholders filed claims; over $101M in attorneys' fees. The court distinguished between training on lawfully obtained works (potentially fair use) and acquiring copyrighted material from piracy sources. Resolves one landmark case; active suits against Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and others remain.
