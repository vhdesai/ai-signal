# DeepSeek nears $500M revenue and prepares for public-market path

DeepSeek disclosed roughly $400–500 million of annualized revenue through its V4 API, reportedly at 70–80% gross margins, while raising about $7.4 billion at a roughly $74 billion valuation and preparing for a potential STAR Market listing in 2027. The numbers show that leading Chinese open-model labs are converting model momentum into commercial scale despite chip controls. They also underline why compute costs are pushing open-source labs toward public capital.
