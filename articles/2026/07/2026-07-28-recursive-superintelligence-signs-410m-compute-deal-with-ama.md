# Recursive Superintelligence signs $410M compute deal with Amazon

Recursive Superintelligence, led by Richard Socher, signed a $410 million compute agreement with Amazon — the bulk of the company's fundraising to date. Socher framed it as the first of many such commitments, reflecting how frontier labs increasingly lock in multi-year cloud capacity upfront. It reinforces the pattern of compute contracts becoming both a primary competitive moat and a primary cash-burn line for AI developers. ⚡
