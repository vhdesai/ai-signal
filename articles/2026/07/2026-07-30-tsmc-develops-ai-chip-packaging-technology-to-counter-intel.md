The Information reported that TSMC is developing advanced chip-packaging technology similar to Intel's offering. The effort matters because packaging has become a strategic bottleneck for AI accelerators, where memory, interconnect, and manufacturing capacity increasingly determine who can deliver frontier-scale compute.
