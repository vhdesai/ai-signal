# Banks discuss $15 billion loan for Anthropic data center backed by Google

Banks are reportedly in talks to lend $15 billion for an Anthropic data center backed by Google. The scale of the financing underscores how frontier-model competition is now inseparable from project finance, energy access, and long-term infrastructure commitments. It also shows hyperscalers and model labs using more complex partnership structures to secure capacity.
