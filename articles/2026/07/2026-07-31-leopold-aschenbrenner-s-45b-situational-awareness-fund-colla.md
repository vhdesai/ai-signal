# Leopold Aschenbrenner's $45B Situational Awareness Fund Collapses Under Leverage

The Wall Street Journal publishes a detailed postmortem on the collapse of Situational Awareness, the AI-focused hedge fund founded by Leopold Aschenbrenner that peaked at $45 billion before excessive leverage triggered a catastrophic unwind. The fund, which had been hailed as the "Nostradamus of AI" play, reportedly suffered a 67% drawdown in its final weeks as concentrated positions in AI infrastructure names unwound during a broader tech rotation. Citadel is now acquiring what remains at distressed prices. The story serves as a cautionary tale about leverage in AI-thesis investing — even correct directional bets can fail when position sizing and risk management lag.
