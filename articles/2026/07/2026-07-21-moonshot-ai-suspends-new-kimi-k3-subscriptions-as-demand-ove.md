# Moonshot AI Suspends New Kimi K3 Subscriptions as Demand Overwhelms GPUs

Moonshot suspended new K3 subscriptions ~48 hours after launch as demand exceeded serving capacity. Highlights how compute scarcity — sharpened by U.S. export controls — constrains even a leading Chinese lab. Plans to release open weights on July 27, resolving the capacity wall through openness rather than more hardware.
