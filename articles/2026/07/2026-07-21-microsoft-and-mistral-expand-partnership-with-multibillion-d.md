Mistral Medium 3.5 and OCR 4 available in Microsoft Foundry; Mistral Medium 3.5 in Copilot Studio. Extends sovereign cloud approach, letting regulated European customers run Mistral models across cloud, cloud-connected, and fully disconnected environments.
