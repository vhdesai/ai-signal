# Encore AI raises $30 million for agents that learn from customer calls

Encore AI raised a $30 million Series A to build AI agents trained on customer calls, messages, and CRM data. The platform mines successful customer interactions and turns them into playbooks that agents can use directly or recommend to human employees. The approach shows how enterprise agents are being built from proprietary workflow history, not just generic model capability. AI HOMECONSUMER TOOLSSTARTUPS
