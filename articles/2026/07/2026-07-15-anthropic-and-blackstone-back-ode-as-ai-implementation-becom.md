# Anthropic and Blackstone back Ode as AI implementation becomes the next enterprise battleground

Anthropic and Blackstone-backed Ode launched as a $1.5 billion AI implementation company built from the Anthropic joint venture with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, Goldman Sachs, and others. Ode will use a Claude-first approach but is not limited to Anthropic technology, with its core business focused on embedding applied AI engineers into enterprises to rework products and business processes. The strategic takeaway is that frontier labs increasingly view implementation capacity as a scarce asset and a potential trillion-dollar services category.
