# Fallen power line exposes AI data-center grid stability problem

TechCrunch reports that a fallen power line near Washington, D.C., caused more than 3 gigawatts of data-center load to drop nearly simultaneously, creating voltage spikes across the PJM grid and taking more than 10 minutes to stabilize. The incident did not cause a blackout, but experts called it a warning sign as data centers become a larger share of grid load. The key infrastructure lesson is that AI data centers need better ride-through behavior, coordinated load shedding, and campus-scale battery systems so they do not amplify grid disturbances.
