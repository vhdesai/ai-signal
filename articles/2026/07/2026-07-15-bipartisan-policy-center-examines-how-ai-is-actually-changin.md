# Bipartisan Policy Center examines how AI is actually changing U.S. firms

The Bipartisan Policy Center published new work on AI and the workforce, asking what is actually happening inside American firms as AI adoption accelerates. The item adds to a growing body of evidence that executives need operational data, not broad automation narratives, to understand where AI changes work, productivity, hiring, and organizational design. It is relevant for senior leaders because workforce impact is becoming a board-level measurement and governance issue.
