# Oak exits stealth with $60M to govern identity for AI agents

Israeli startup Oak emerged from stealth with $60 million in seed funding and a unified control plane for managing identity across humans, machines, and AI agents. The product is already generally available and deployed by enterprise clients. Oak targets a fast-emerging gap: legacy cloud-era identity tools were never designed for autonomous agents acting on users' behalf.
