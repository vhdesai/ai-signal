# Nokia and NVIDIA Unveil the First Commercial AI-RAN Platform

Nokia and NVIDIA unveiled a commercial AI-RAN platform running radio-access networks on AI chips rather than fixed hardware, with sales to operators from 2027. They claim spectral-efficiency gains above 100% by 2028 — effectively doubling data capacity from existing spectrum. Extends Nvidia's compute footprint into telecom infrastructure.
