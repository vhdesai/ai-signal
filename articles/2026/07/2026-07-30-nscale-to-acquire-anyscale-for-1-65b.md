# Nscale to acquire Anyscale for ~$1.65B

London-based “neocloud” Nscale agreed to buy Anyscale (about 200 staff), whose software scales AI workloads across GPU clusters, extending Nscale's stack from power and GPUs into production orchestration. The deal is expected to close in the second half of 2026, intensifying competition with CoreWeave.
