# Moonshot's Kimi cluster runs on ~20,000 Nvidia chips leased from Alibaba

Bloomberg reported that Moonshot AI built its flagship Kimi model on a cluster of roughly 20,000 Nvidia chips leased through backer Alibaba. The report lifted Alibaba stock to two-month highs. It illustrates how Chinese labs are securing Nvidia compute via cloud intermediaries amid export constraints.
