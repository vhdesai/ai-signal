# Enterprises contend with mounting AI costs as tools sprawl

CIO Dive reports that enterprises are trying to balance AI adoption with budget discipline as vendors and tools proliferate. Contract management, AI architecture, governance, and vendor evaluation are becoming core CIO tasks as AI shifts from pilot programs to operational spend. The issue connects directly to broader capex pressure: the same cost discipline now shaping Big Tech infrastructure is arriving inside enterprise IT budgets.
