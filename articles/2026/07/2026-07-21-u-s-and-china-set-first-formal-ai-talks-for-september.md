# U.S. and China set first formal AI talks for September

The U.S. and China are preparing their first formal AI talks, likely before Xi Jinping's U.S. visit, covering military AI, cyber risks, model access, and open weights. Treasury Secretary Bessent also floated possible sanctions over AI model “theft.” The move places AI diplomacy in the same strategic category as arms control, cybersecurity, and export controls.
