# Agility Robotics opens new training center near Tesla's factory

Agility Robotics is opening a 60,000-square-foot facility in Fremont, California, to train its Digit humanoid robots in environments similar to customer deployments. The company says Digit is already generating revenue in manufacturing and warehouse workflows with customers such as Amazon, GXO, Schaeffler, and Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada. The story reinforces the near-term robotics thesis: industrial and logistics settings are commercializing before consumer humanoids.
