# Global Chip Sell-Off Intensifies as AI Spending Doubts Grip Markets

A cascading sell-off in chip and memory stocks has gone global, driven by growing investor anxiety over Big Tech's ballooning AI capital expenditure and whether the returns will ever justify the scale of investment. South Korea's Kospi index plunged more than 10% in a single session, shaving nearly $600 billion off SK Hynix's market capitalization despite the memory-chip maker reporting record quarterly operating profit. In the U.S., Nvidia fell 5% and lost its crown as the world's most valuable listed company to Apple, while the PHLX Semiconductor Index declined 4.5%. The sell-off reflects three converging fears: runaway AI spending by hyperscalers, circular financing structures between chipmakers and AI startups, and the competitive threat from increasingly capable Chinese rivals.
