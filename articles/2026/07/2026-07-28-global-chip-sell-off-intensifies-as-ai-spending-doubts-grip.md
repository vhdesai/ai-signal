# Global chip sell-off intensifies as AI spending doubts grip markets

Chip and memory stocks sold off globally as investors worried about Big Tech’s ballooning AI capex and signs that Chinese competitors are catching up. The Wall Street Journal reported that Asian chip stocks plunged, with South Korea’s Kospi down 10%, Japan’s Nikkei down 4%, and U.S. memory names including Sandisk and Micron falling sharply; PitchBook noted the sell-off could delay VC-backed chip startup IPOs. The move reframes AI infrastructure from a consensus growth trade into a question about returns, financing durability, and exit timing.
