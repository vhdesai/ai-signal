# Xsight raises $300 million as server networking becomes an AI bottleneck

The Information reported that the server-networking boom drove a $300 million funding round for Xsight. Networking is becoming a core AI infrastructure constraint as clusters grow larger and accelerators require faster communication among chips, memory, and servers. The funding reflects investor focus shifting beyond GPUs toward the less visible bottlenecks that determine real-world AI cluster utilization.
