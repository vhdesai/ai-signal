# How an MIT database evolved into a global standard for data-sharing (PhysioNet)

MIT News profiles PhysioNet, an open biomedical and clinical data repository that launched 25 years ago on a system MIT first developed in the 1970s. It has grown into one of the most comprehensive physiological and clinical data repositories in existence. The datasets have become a de facto standard for training and benchmarking reproducible clinical AI.
