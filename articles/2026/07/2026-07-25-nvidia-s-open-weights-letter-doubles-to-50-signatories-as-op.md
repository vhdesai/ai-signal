# Nvidia's “Open Weights” letter doubles to 50 signatories as OpenAI and Google sign on

Jensen Huang's “Open Weights and American AI Leadership” letter — launched July 24 with 25 signers including Meta, Microsoft, and Palantir — roughly doubled to ~50 companies within a day, adding OpenAI, Google, AMD, Cisco, Cloudflare, GitHub, and others. The letter argues against premature restrictions on open-weight models as Chinese open releases gain share. Notably, Anthropic and Amazon remain off the list, underscoring a widening split among leading labs over open-model policy. POLICY
