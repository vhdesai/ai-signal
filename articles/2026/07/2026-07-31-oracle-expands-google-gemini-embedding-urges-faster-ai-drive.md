# Oracle expands Google Gemini embedding; urges faster, AI-driven patching

Analysts detailed Oracle's expanded deal to embed Google's Gemini across Fusion Apps, NetSuite and its AI Agent Studio. Separately, Oracle urged customers to move from quarterly to monthly security patching, citing AI-accelerated vulnerability discovery. Together the items show AI reshaping both Oracle's product stack and its security posture.
