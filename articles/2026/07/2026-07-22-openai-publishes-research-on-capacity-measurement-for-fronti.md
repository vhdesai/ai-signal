# OpenAI Publishes Research on Capacity Measurement for Frontier Models

OpenAI published new work on measuring the "capacity" of frontier models — an effort to develop more rigorous, standardized methods for evaluating what models can and cannot do. The research arrives as the SWE-Bench controversy (30% broken tasks) highlighted how unreliable existing benchmarks have become for procurement and governance decisions.
