# Apple posts record $109.4B June quarter but warns AI-driven component shortages will bite

Apple reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $109.4B, up 16%, with iPhone up 22% to $54.3B and Services at a record, in Tim Cook's final earnings call before stepping down. But it guided current-quarter growth to just 9–11%, below expectations, and warned that constrained advanced-node and memory supply — absorbed by the AI data-center buildout — could limit shipments; shares fell sharply premarket. The signal for the sector: hyperscaler component demand is now crimping even the largest consumer-hardware maker.
