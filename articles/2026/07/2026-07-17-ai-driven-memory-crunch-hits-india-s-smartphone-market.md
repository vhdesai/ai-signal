# AI-driven memory crunch hits India's smartphone market

TechCrunch reports that AI-driven demand for memory chips is now affecting India's smartphone market, with shipments down 10% year over year in the June quarter and low-end models hit hardest. Memory suppliers are shifting capacity toward high-bandwidth memory for AI accelerators, constraining cheaper memory used in phones and laptops. This is a clear example of AI infrastructure demand spilling into consumer electronics pricing and replacement cycles.
