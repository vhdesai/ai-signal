# OpenAI argues teens deserve access to safe AI

OpenAI published a safety-focused post arguing that teens should have access to AI with age-appropriate protections, learning tools, parental controls, and expert partnerships. The piece comes amid broader concern over AI companions, child safety, and age-appropriate behavior in consumer AI products. For platform leaders, the issue is moving from whether minors can use AI to how providers design defaults, controls, and escalation paths.
