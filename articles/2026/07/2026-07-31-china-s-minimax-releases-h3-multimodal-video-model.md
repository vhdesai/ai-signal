# China's MiniMax releases H3 multimodal video model

MiniMax released H3, a video-generation model that jointly processes text, images, video and audio, stepping up competition with ByteDance and Google in generative video. The company also launched the model on Product Hunt the same day. It is the latest signal of China's accelerating open-model cadence.
