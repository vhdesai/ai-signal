# Google DeepMind disbands its Nobel-winning AlphaFold team to focus on Gemini

Google DeepMind has reportedly broken up the team behind AlphaFold, the Nobel Prize-winning protein-structure model, reassigning key members to Gemini and other efforts while several researchers have departed. AlphaFold remains widely used in drug discovery, vaccine development, and disease research, making the reorganization strategically significant. The move raises a broader question for AI research leaders: whether long-horizon science programs can remain durable inside labs increasingly organized around general-purpose frontier-model franchises.
