# Apple's Capex-Light AI Strategy Wins Investors Even as Guidance Disappoints

Apple beat on quarterly revenue and EPS, helped by a 22% iPhone jump, but softer supply-constrained guidance still pushed shares lower after hours. Tim Cook's final earnings call as CEO highlighted Apple's refusal to match hyperscaler-style AI infrastructure spending, with capex still near $11 billion. Instead, Apple is leaning on efficiency, on-device inference, and a licensed Google-powered Siri redesign due in September. Cook also warned that a memory "hundred-year flood" is inflating costs across the industry as AI demand tightens HBM and DRAM supply. The quarter reinforced a widening split in AI strategy: Apple is betting restraint and product integration can beat brute-force compute expansion. Links: CNBC
