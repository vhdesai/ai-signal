# Databricks valued at $188 billion as AI demand surges

The Information reports that Databricks has been valued at $188 billion as AI demand continues to drive investor appetite. The valuation reflects the strategic role of governed enterprise data platforms in AI deployments, particularly as companies balance proprietary data, model choice, and cost control. It also shows that while some public AI trades are volatile, private-market demand remains strong for data infrastructure that can operationalize AI at scale.
