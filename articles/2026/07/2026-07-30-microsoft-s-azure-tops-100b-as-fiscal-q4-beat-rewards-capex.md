# Microsoft's Azure tops $100B as fiscal Q4 beat rewards capex discipline

Microsoft reported fiscal Q4 2026 revenue of $90B (+18%) and diluted EPS of $4.81 (+32%), both ahead of consensus, with Azure growth accelerating to 43% and crossing $100B in annual revenue for the first time. Microsoft Cloud reached $59.3B (+27%), commercial remaining performance obligations jumped 84% to $678B, and the company disclosed more than 30 million paid Copilot seats. Capital expenditures landed at $41B — below the ~$42B analysts feared — and CFO Amy Hood signaled continued capex growth in FY2027 while staying free-cash-flow positive. Shares rose ~9%, a pointed contrast to Alphabet's 6% drop last week after it lifted its 2026 capex outlook toward $205B. M EARNINGS
