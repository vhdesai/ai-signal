# Google Reportedly Building "Frozen v2," a Gemini-Specific Chip Claiming 6–10× TPU Efficiency

Google is reportedly developing a custom inference chip that hardwires the Gemini architecture into silicon, with internal claims of 6–10× the inference efficiency of current TPUs. Targeted for 2028, it would complement rather than replace TPUs. Treat efficiency figures as unverified pre-silicon claims — but the direction (model-specific hardware to cut cost-per-token) is where every hyperscaler is racing.
