# UK Startups Raise $17B in H1 2026; AI Captures 74% of Venture Capital

UK startups raised approximately $17 billion in the first half of 2026 — roughly double the prior year — with AI ventures capturing an estimated 74% of VC. The UK's share of European deep-tech funding nearly doubled to about 41%. The concentration underscores how sharply capital is indexing to AI and raises concern that non-AI sectors are being crowded out of funding.
