# Microsoft unveils Project Perception and an in-house MAI-Cyber-1-Flash cyber model

Microsoft escalated the AI-security race with Project Perception, a system of security "agent teams," alongside a new in-house model, MAI-Cyber-1-Flash. The model — reported at roughly 5B active parameters — is positioned to deliver top-tier security performance at about half the cost, and independent coverage cites a 95.95% score on the CyberGym benchmark within Microsoft's MDASH stack. The launch signals that specialized, security-specific models are becoming a distinct front in enterprise cybersecurity rather than a feature of general-purpose assistants.
