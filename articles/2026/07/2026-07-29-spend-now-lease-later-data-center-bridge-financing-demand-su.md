# "Spend Now, Lease Later": Data Center Bridge Financing Demand Surges

Data center developers are racing to bring AI infrastructure projects online so quickly that traditional financing structures cannot keep pace, creating surging demand for a new class of "bridge financing" instruments, Goldman Sachs' global head of infrastructure finance told The Information. Developers need capital to level land, conduct power studies, and secure increasingly scarce gas turbines before tenants have signed leases — a financing gap that Goldman describes as "probably one of the largest areas of increased inquiries from our clients." New structures are emerging, including "cost reimbursement agreements" (CRAs) where prospective tenants tell developers to begin early purchases before formal leases are signed. The trend reflects the extraordinary speed at which the AI infrastructure buildout is outpacing the financial and legal frameworks designed to govern it.
