# Black Forest Labs launches FLUX 3 for image and short video generation

VentureBeat reports that Black Forest Labs launched FLUX 3, a limited-release model capable of generating images and 20-second video with audio. The release points to continued convergence across image, video, and audio generation, with model providers racing to support more complete media workflows. For creative and marketing teams, the practical question is shifting from single-asset generation to controllability, rights, workflow integration, and cost.
