# CXMT soars in Shanghai debut as China funds AI memory independence

ChangXin Memory Technologies, China’s leading memory-chip maker, jumped more than 470% in its Shanghai trading debut. The opening price valued CXMT around 3.3 trillion yuan, or roughly $487 billion, after the company raised up to 66.6 billion yuan in China’s largest onshore IPO. CXMT’s rise matters because DRAM is a core bottleneck for AI systems, and China is trying to build a domestic alternative to Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron.
