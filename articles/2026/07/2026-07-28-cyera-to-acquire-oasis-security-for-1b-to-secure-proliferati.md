# Cyera to acquire Oasis Security for ~$1B to secure proliferating AI agents

Data-security company Cyera — recently valued near $12 billion — signed a letter of intent to buy identity-security startup Oasis Security for roughly $1 billion. The stated rationale is securing the surging population of autonomous AI agents and the machine identities they create, an emerging attack surface. The deal signals that non-human identity and agent governance are consolidating into a distinct security category now attracting nine-figure M&A.
