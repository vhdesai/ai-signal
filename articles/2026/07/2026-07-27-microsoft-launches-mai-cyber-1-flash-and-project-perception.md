# Microsoft Launches MAI-Cyber-1-Flash and Project Perception Agentic-Defense Platform

Microsoft unveiled MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, a cost-efficient in-house security model, embedded within Project Perception, a new agentic defense platform aimed at cutting enterprise security operations costs by roughly half. It is Microsoft's first purpose-built cyber model, positioned to run detection and response workflows autonomously at machine speed. The launch signals Microsoft's intent to commoditize AI-driven SecOps as agentic threats — and agentic defenses — scale across the enterprise. Combined with the Open Secure AI Alliance and Hush Security's agent-governance platform, it marks the emergence of agentic cybersecurity as a distinct, heavily funded market segment.
