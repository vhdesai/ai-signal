# NVIDIA Jetson GPUs are headed to the lunar surface

TechCrunch reports that Lunar Outpost plans to use NVIDIA Jetson chips in its next moon rover to control lidar processing, likely making it the first GPU on the lunar surface. The rover is part of NASA-backed private lunar exploration efforts and will test whether compact AI hardware can handle radiation, thermal swings, and low-power operation. The story is a high-signal example of physical AI moving into edge environments where local sensing and autonomy are mandatory.
