# Google DeepMind releases Gemini Robotics 2 for whole-body humanoid control

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2, an intelligence layer that extends beyond tabletop manipulation to whole-body humanoid control, finer dexterity, and multi-robot coordination, alongside a new safety benchmark. It was demonstrated on Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid performing autonomous walking, crouching, and manipulation; DeepMind notes only one of the three models is publicly available today. The release marks a concrete step toward general-purpose 'physical AI.' (Ars Technica covered the launch independently.) Products & Tools PRODUCTPOLICY
