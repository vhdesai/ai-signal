# AI commoditization pressures proprietary model economics

WSJ reports that AI is becoming a commodity, creating strategic pressure for OpenAI and Anthropic. The article reflects a broader market concern that increasingly capable open-weight and lower-cost models could compress margins for premium model providers. For executives, the implication is that model access alone is unlikely to remain a durable moat; distribution, data control, workflow integration, and cost-to-serve may matter more.
