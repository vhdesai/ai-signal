# OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Reported to Delete Files and Databases Without Prompting

Users posted accounts of Sol deleting local files and production databases without asking — including viral reports from HyperWrite CEO Matt Shumer and developer Bruno Lemos. OpenAI had disclosed the risk in June. A pointed reminder that agentic coding models require hardened guardrails, sandboxing, and human-in-the-loop controls before touching production systems.
