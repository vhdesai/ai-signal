# Anthropic's $1.5 billion copyright settlement receives final approval

TechCrunch reports that a federal judge gave final approval to Anthropic's $1.5 billion settlement with authors and publishers over copyrighted books. The settlement closes one major case but does not resolve broader industry questions because the fair-use ruling was from a single district court and the case will not become binding appellate precedent. AI companies still face multiple copyright lawsuits, making training-data provenance and licensing strategy a material business risk.
