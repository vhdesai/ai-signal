# Databricks Extends Its Microsoft Azure Partnership into the 2030s

Databricks will expand its Azure commitment through the 2030s, running core operations on Microsoft's cloud and increasing use of Arm-based Cobalt processors. Deepens one of the most important hyperscaler–data-platform alliances as both firms push agentic analytics. Further entrenches Azure as the default substrate for lakehouse and AI pipelines.
