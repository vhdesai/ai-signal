# Apple lawsuit could complicate OpenAI's hardware and IPO plans

TechCrunch analyzed whether Apple's trade-secrets lawsuit against OpenAI could derail OpenAI's hardware ambitions and IPO planning. The article notes that Apple alleges a pattern of misconduct involving current and former employees, while OpenAI says it is not aware of evidence supporting the complaint. Even before the merits are tested, litigation could delay hardware work, complicate investor messaging, and increase diligence burden around OpenAI's move beyond software.
