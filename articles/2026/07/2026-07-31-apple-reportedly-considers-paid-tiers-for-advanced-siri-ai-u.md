# Apple reportedly considers paid tiers for advanced Siri AI use

TechCrunch reports that Apple may monetize advanced Siri AI capabilities through iCloud+ or related paid tiers for power users. The move would frame premium AI assistance as a metered or subscription-adjacent service inside Apple's device ecosystem rather than a fully bundled software feature. That would have implications for consumer AI pricing norms and for how hardware platforms recover cloud inference costs.
