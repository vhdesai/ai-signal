# Australia to curb government use of automated AI decision-making

Australia will require agencies such as Centrelink and Services Australia to follow a framework ensuring fair and accurate outcomes when they use AI in decision-making. The rules tighten transparency and accountability obligations for automated decisions affecting citizens. They add to a widening set of national AI-governance regimes that multinational companies must track.
