# Internal US Treasury Draft Warns the AI Market Echoes the Dotcom Bubble

A draft report circulating inside the US Treasury warns that the AI market poses systemic economic risks, likening key aspects to the dotcom bust. Career analysts prepared it for Secretary Scott Bessent, Fed Chair Kevin Warsh, and federal financial regulators, concluding AI firms are "more deeply entrenched in the U.S. economy than their dotcom predecessors" and that a downturn would ripple through equities, private credit, data-center financing, chipmakers, and utilities. A Treasury spokesperson dismissed it as unvetted; it has been complete for weeks and awaits formal approval.
