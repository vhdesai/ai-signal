# EU Commits €10B to Seven AI Gigafactories in Race with U.S. and China

The European Commission unveiled a €10 billion plan to fund up to seven AI gigafactories and attract another €20 billion in private capital. Major chip vendors including AMD, Nvidia, and Qualcomm have already signaled support for the effort. The initiative is Europe's clearest attempt yet to correct its deficit in sovereign AI compute capacity versus the U.S. and China. Applications close in November, with site selection expected in early 2027, giving the program a relatively fast policy timetable. For global infrastructure players, the plan creates both new competition and partnership opportunities across Europe's emerging compute buildout. Links: U.S. News / Reuters
