# Chai Discovery Raises $400M at $3.8B Valuation for AI Drug Design

OpenAI-backed Chai Discovery closed $400 million led by Index Ventures at $3.8 billion — roughly tripling its December 2025 mark in seven months. The company claims ~20% antibody-design hit rates versus <0.1% for traditional methods, and added Novartis to engagements with Pfizer and Eli Lilly. Another vote of investor conviction in vertical, applied AI rather than general-purpose models.
