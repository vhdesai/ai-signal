# Hack suggests Suno scraped YouTube and other sources for AI music training data

TechCrunch reports that a hacker accessed Suno source code and said it showed the AI music company scraped audio from YouTube Music, Deezer, Genius, stock music libraries, and podcast RSS feeds. The report also says customer data was accessed, while Suno characterized the breach as limited and contained. The story increases legal and security pressure on generative media companies already defending training-data practices in court.
