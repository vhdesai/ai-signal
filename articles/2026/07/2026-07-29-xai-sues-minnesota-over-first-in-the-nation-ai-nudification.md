# xAI sues Minnesota over first-in-the-nation AI “nudification” ban

xAI filed a federal lawsuit challenging Minnesota’s first-in-the-nation law banning AI “nudification” tools, arguing it is an overbroad restriction on speech. The case lands amid separate litigation over Grok-generated deepfakes and will test whether states can hold AI toolmakers liable for foreseeable misuse. It is an early legal signal for any company deploying generative image systems into consumer or enterprise workflows.
