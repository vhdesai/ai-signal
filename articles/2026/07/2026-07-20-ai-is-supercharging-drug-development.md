# AI is supercharging drug development

Axios reports that AI is accelerating drug development as pharmaceutical companies apply models to discovery, prediction, and R&D workflow automation. The theme aligns with the BMS-NVIDIA infrastructure announcement: life-sciences AI is shifting from narrow computational chemistry tools toward broader AI factories and agentic research systems. The executive question is no longer whether AI can assist discovery, but how quickly organizations can connect models, data, compute, and scientists into repeatable development pipelines.
