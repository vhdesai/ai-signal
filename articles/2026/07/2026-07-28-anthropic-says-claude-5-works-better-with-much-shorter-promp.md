# Anthropic says Claude 5 works better with much shorter prompts — developers debate the tradeoffs

Anthropic reported cutting its Claude Code system prompt by more than 80% (from ~800 to ~164 tokens) for Claude 5 models with no measurable drop in internal coding evals, arguing that richer context, better tool design, and clear goals now outperform long lists of explicit rules. Early developer feedback is mixed, with some noting that simpler instructions can shift the burden of safety onto surrounding systems and external guardrails. The shift — from prompt engineering toward context and tool design — has practical implications for how teams govern and safeguard AI coding assistants. AI Safety & Security SECURITY ADOPTION
