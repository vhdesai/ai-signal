# TSMC Breaks Ground on Three More Advanced-Packaging Fabs at Chiayi; Q2 Revenue Hits Record

TSMC will add three advanced-packaging (CoWoS) facilities in Phase II of the Chiayi Science Park — the step that fuses logic and stacked memory and has gated AI-accelerator supply for two years. Once both phases run, annual output is projected above NT$300B ($9.35B). The groundbreaking coincided with TSMC reporting record Q2 revenue of ~$39.62B (up 36% YoY).
