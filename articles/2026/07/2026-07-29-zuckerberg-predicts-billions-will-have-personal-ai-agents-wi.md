# Zuckerberg predicts billions will have personal AI agents within five years

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg told investors he expects billions of people to have personal AI agents within five years. He framed the agents as systems that understand goals and work continuously across domains such as finance, health, relationships, and household management. The forecast is ambitious, but Meta's distribution across WhatsApp, Messenger, Threads, and future glasses gives it one of the strongest paths to consumer-agent adoption if trust and utility improve.
