# OpenAI CFO proposes a scorecard for measuring AI value

OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar published a scorecard for measuring AI ROI through useful work, cost per successful task, dependability, and return on compute. The framework is meant to move enterprise AI evaluation beyond token burn and demo quality toward measurable operational outcomes. The post is notable because it acknowledges buyers' growing pressure to justify AI spend with unit economics and reliability metrics. URL: https://openai.com/index/a-scorecard-for-the-ai-age
