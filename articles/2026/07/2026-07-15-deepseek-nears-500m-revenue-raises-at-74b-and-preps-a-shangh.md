# DeepSeek Nears $500M Revenue, Raises at ~$74B, and Preps a Shanghai IPO

DeepSeek's annualized revenue has reached ~$400–500M — its first disclosed figure — driven by API access to V4 at 70–80% gross margins. The lab is simultaneously raising up to 50B yuan (~$7.4B) at ~$74B and preparing a Shanghai STAR Market IPO for a 2027 debut. Compute costs are pushing even open-source-first labs toward public capital.
