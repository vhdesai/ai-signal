# Nano Banana image generation comes to Google Earth

Google added its “Nano Banana” image model to Google Earth, grounded in real satellite, aerial, and 3D imagery. The feature lets users redesign locations, reconstruct historical scenes, and generate place-based infographics directly from a prompt.
