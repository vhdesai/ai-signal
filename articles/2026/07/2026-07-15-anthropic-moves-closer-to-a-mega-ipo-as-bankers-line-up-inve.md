# Anthropic Moves Closer to a Mega-IPO as Bankers Line Up Investor Meetings

Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, and JPMorgan have begun scheduling investor meetings ahead of a potential Anthropic IPO as soon as October. Anthropic filed confidentially with the SEC in June and was last valued at $965B after its $65B Series H — above OpenAI's $852B. A listing would make it the first frontier lab to reach public markets; its eventual S-1 would give enterprises their first hard look at frontier-lab economics.
