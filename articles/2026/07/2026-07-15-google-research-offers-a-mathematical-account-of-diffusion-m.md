# Google Research Offers a Mathematical Account of Diffusion-Model "Creativity"

Google Research published a theoretical account (presented at ICLR 2026) arguing that diffusion models' "creativity" is a mathematical consequence of learning a smoothed score function that interpolates between training points rather than memorizing them. The framing helps explain why generative models produce novel outputs and offers a handle for steering novelty — relevant beyond images to molecular discovery and scientific generation tasks.
