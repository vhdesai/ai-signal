# Survey says 54% of enterprises have already had an AI agent incident

VentureBeat reports that 54% of enterprises surveyed have already experienced an AI-agent incident, while many still allow agents to share credentials. The finding aligns with recent security incidents showing that agentic systems introduce new operational risk around identity, tool access, and delegated authority. For executives, agent governance should be treated as an identity and access-management problem, not only as model-risk management.
