# Situational Awareness fire sale turns leveraged AI conviction into market stress

DealBook reported that Situational Awareness, Leopold Aschenbrenner's AI-focused hedge fund, was forced to sell public-equity holdings to Citadel after margin calls and an AI-stock selloff. The fund's public AI portfolio reportedly fell about 67% in July, crystallizing how quickly concentrated, leveraged AI exposure can move from thesis to liquidity event.
