# Microsoft and Mistral expand sovereign AI partnership with European compute deal

Microsoft expanded its strategic partnership with Mistral, tapping Mistral's Europe-based GPU capacity and bringing Mistral Medium 3.5 into Copilot Studio. The deal emphasizes sovereign cloud options for regulated European customers, including cloud, cloud-connected, and fully disconnected environments. It shows Microsoft hedging model dependence while deepening deployment choices for customers that need regional control.
