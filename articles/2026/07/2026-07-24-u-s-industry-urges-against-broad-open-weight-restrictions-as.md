# U.S. industry urges against broad open-weight restrictions as Washington weighs China response

TechCrunch reports that AI companies including NVIDIA, Mistral, and Hugging Face are urging policymakers not to impose broad restrictions on open-weight AI models as Washington debates how to respond to Chinese systems such as Kimi K3. The pushback reflects a split between national-security concerns and the economic value of open models for research, startups, and enterprise deployment. The policy outcome could shape model access, compliance obligations, and procurement risk for U.S. companies.
