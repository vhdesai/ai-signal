# Jack Dorsey's Buzz puts humans and AI agents in the same workplace chat

TechCrunch reports that Jack Dorsey launched Buzz, an open-source workplace group-chat platform designed for teams and their AI agents. Buzz resembles Slack but adds native agent participation and GitHub project management in the same workspace. It is early, but strategically notable because collaboration tools are becoming the operating layer where agents, developers, and business teams coordinate work.
