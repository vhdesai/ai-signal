# Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro Slips Again Over Coding Performance

Google's flagship Gemini 3.5 Pro is reportedly months behind schedule as retraining on updated data has produced disappointing coding results, even as OpenAI and Meta ship models that outperform on code. Only Flash and Flash-Lite have shipped so far. Google says it is testing 3.5 Pro with partners and "shipping quickly," but the delay increases monetization pressure heading into H2.
