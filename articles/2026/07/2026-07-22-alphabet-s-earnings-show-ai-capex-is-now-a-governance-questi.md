# Alphabet's Earnings Show AI Capex Is Now a Governance Question, Not Just a Strategy One

Alphabet's earnings crystallize a tension that now faces every major AI investor: cloud revenue is surging but the capital required to serve it is rising faster. The $205B guidance and the after-hours sell-off signal that shareholders are demanding a clearer line from capex to revenue. For boards, AI infrastructure spend has crossed from a strategic bet into a governance and capital-allocation accountability question.
