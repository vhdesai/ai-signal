# China begins mass production of homegrown DUV chipmaking tools

A Shanghai-based state-backed company has begun manufacturing immersion deep ultraviolet lithography machines, according to The Information. DUV tools are key to semiconductor production, and the reported rollout marks a concrete step in Beijing’s effort to reduce reliance on foreign chipmaking equipment under U.S. export controls. The first output targets are modest, but the direction matters for AI supply chains because equipment independence supports long-term domestic chip capacity.
