# Apple publishes RayRoPE for multi-view transformer attention

Apple published RayRoPE, a projective ray positional encoding method for multi-view transformers that process posed input images. The research targets a core computer-vision problem: representing geometry so models can attend across views while respecting scene structure. It is relevant to spatial computing, robotics, 3D reconstruction, and any workflow where AI must reason from multiple camera perspectives.
