# DeepSeek plans 1-gigawatt data center in Inner Mongolia

DeepSeek is reportedly planning a gigawatt-scale AI data center in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia — a major infrastructure step for a lab best known for efficiency-first models. The plan is a notable marker of China's broader sovereign-compute push.
