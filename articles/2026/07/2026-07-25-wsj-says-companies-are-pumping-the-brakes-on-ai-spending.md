# WSJ says companies are pumping the brakes on AI spending

The Wall Street Journal reports that companies are becoming more selective about AI spending, mixing lower-priced models — including some from China — with products from OpenAI and Anthropic. The shift changes industry economics by pushing customers toward model choice, routing, and task-level procurement rather than one-vendor commitments. It aligns with the broader move toward a la carte AI infrastructure and price-performance scrutiny.
