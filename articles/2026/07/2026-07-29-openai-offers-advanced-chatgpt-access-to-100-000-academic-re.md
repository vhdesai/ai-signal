# OpenAI offers advanced ChatGPT access to 100,000 academic researchers

OpenAI announced free access to advanced ChatGPT models for 100,000 academic researchers. The program is positioned as a way to accelerate research, collaboration, and discovery across scientific domains. The move also deepens OpenAI's relationship with academia at a time when AI labs are competing for research mindshare, data, and scientific legitimacy. MITHEALTH AIDATA SHARING
