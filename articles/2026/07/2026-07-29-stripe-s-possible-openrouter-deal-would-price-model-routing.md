# Stripe’s possible OpenRouter deal would price model routing at a steep multiple

A potential Stripe acquisition of OpenRouter near $10 billion would value the model-routing startup at roughly 70 times recent annualized revenue. OpenRouter gives developers access to hundreds of AI models and is reportedly generating about $140 million in annualized revenue. The valuation suggests model access, routing, and cost-control layers are becoming strategic infrastructure in the AI application stack.
