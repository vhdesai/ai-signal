# Nvidia and 30+ tech firms launch open-source AI security alliance after attack

Nvidia and more than 30 technology companies launched an alliance to build open-source AI tools for cyber defense, following a high-profile security incident involving AI systems. The coalition enters an active debate over whether freely available AI models help or hinder defenders. It frames open-source security tooling as an industry-coordinated response and a counterweight to arguments for restricting open models on safety grounds.
