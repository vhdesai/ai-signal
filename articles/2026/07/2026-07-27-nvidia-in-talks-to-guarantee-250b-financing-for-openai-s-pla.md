# Nvidia in Talks to Guarantee ~$250B Financing for OpenAI's Planned 10-GW Ohio Data Center

The Wall Street Journal reported that Nvidia is in talks to provide roughly $250 billion in financing guarantees backing OpenAI's planned 10-gigawatt data-center campus in Ohio — a site being developed by SoftBank's SB Energy on a former uranium-enrichment facility in Piketon that could cost roughly $500 billion to build. The structure would have Nvidia backstop the debt used to construct the campus, deepening the circular capital-and-compute ties between the two firms. Terms are not final, and the arrangement's unprecedented scale has drawn early scrutiny over concentration risk and vendor-financing dynamics across the AI buildout.
