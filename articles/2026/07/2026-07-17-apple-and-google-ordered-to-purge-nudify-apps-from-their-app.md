# Apple and Google Ordered to Purge "Nudify" Apps from Their App Stores

San Francisco City Attorney David Chiu sent letters demanding removal of AI "nudify" apps generating nonconsensual explicit imagery. Raises platform-liability pressure on app-store gatekeepers over generative-AI abuse. Adds to a widening regulatory front targeting harmful AI image generation at the distribution layer.
