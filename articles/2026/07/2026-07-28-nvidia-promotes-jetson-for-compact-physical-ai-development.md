# NVIDIA promotes Jetson for compact physical-AI development

NVIDIA highlighted Jetson as a compact edge-AI and robotics platform for students, researchers, and developers building local physical-AI systems. The post emphasizes on-device voice and vision assistants, robotics projects, and open models running locally without cloud dependence. The strategic relevance is that physical AI needs edge compute that can run perception and action loops close to devices, not only centralized cloud inference.
