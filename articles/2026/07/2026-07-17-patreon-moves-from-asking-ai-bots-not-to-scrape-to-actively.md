# Patreon moves from asking AI bots not to scrape to actively blocking them

Patreon said it is working with Cloudflare to block AI training bots that scrape creator content without permission, moving beyond reliance on robots.txt. The company said testing reduced individual AI training crawler attempts from thousands per week to zero. The move reflects a broader shift from voluntary web norms to technical enforcement and monetization systems for AI crawler access.
