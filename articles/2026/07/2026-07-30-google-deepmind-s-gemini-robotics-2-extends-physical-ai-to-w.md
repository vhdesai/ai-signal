# Google DeepMind’s Gemini Robotics 2 Extends Physical AI to Whole-Body Control

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2 as three models — a vision-language-action (VLA) model, an embodied-reasoning VLM (ER 2, built on Gemini 3.5 Flash), and an on-device VLA — moving beyond tabletop manipulation to whole-body humanoid control, five-finger dexterity and multi-robot collaboration, demonstrated on Apptronik’s Apollo 2. Reported benchmarks include whole-body pick success of 45.7–76.3%, dexterity ranging 32–92%, and 91.3% accuracy on ‘moment finding.’ DeepMind also published an ASIMOV-Agentic safety benchmark; dexterity across hands remains the weakest axis and the clearest gap to close.
