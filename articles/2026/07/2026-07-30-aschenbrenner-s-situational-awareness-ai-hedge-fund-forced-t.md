# Aschenbrenner's “Situational Awareness” AI hedge fund forced to unwind

The AI-thesis fund — which had grown from about $1.5B to $20B+ — was forced to unwind its public equities after leveraged AI-infrastructure bets fell, with Citadel reportedly buying the book at a discount. The fund retains private holdings including Anthropic. AI shares rebounded after the block sale and Microsoft's earnings.
