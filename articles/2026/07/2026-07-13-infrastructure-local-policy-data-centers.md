# INFRASTRUCTURE LOCAL POLICY DATA CENTERS

Axios reports that New Orleans has paused data-center activity amid rising local concern over AI-driven infrastructure demand. The story fits a broader pattern: local zoning, water and power availability, and ratepayer exposure are becoming practical choke points for national AI buildout. For enterprises depending on cloud AI capacity, permitting friction is now part of infrastructure risk. URL not verified.
