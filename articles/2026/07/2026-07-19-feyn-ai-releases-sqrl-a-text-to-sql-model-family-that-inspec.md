# Feyn AI releases SQRL, a text-to-SQL model family that inspects databases before querying

MarkTechPost reports that Feyn AI released SQRL, a text-to-SQL model family designed to inspect the database before writing a query. The approach reflects a practical enterprise trend: models that ground themselves in schema, data distributions, and execution context before generating actions. If effective, this class of model could reduce brittle analytics workflows and support safer agentic data access.
