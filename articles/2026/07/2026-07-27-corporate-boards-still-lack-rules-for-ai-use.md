# Corporate boards still lack rules for AI use

A Deloitte survey cited by CIO Dive found that many corporate boards still lack formal rules for AI use, even as directors begin using AI for board-level work. The finding matters because boards are expected to oversee enterprise AI risk while their own use of AI remains uneven and immature. Governance is moving from an abstract policy question into the boardroom’s own workflow.
