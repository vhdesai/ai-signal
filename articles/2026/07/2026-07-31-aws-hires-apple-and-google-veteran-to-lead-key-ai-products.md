# AWS hires Apple and Google veteran to lead key AI products

AWS confirmed that Ori Herrnstadt, an engineering veteran of Apple and Google, joined as vice president of compute AI services. His remit includes AgentCore, AWS's service for building and running AI agents, and SageMaker, signaling that AWS is tightening product leadership around enterprise agent infrastructure and model operations.
