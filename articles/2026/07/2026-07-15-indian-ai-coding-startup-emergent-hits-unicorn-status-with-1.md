# Indian AI Coding Startup Emergent Hits Unicorn Status with $130M Series C

Emergent raised $130M at $1.5B — a 5× jump in six months — led by Creaegis with Khosla, SoftBank, Lightspeed, and YC. Reports $120M ARR and 200K+ paying customers, targeting non-technical small businesses rather than professional developers. Capital in AI coding is consolidating around a handful of fast-growing platforms.
