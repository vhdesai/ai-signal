# Smallest.ai raises $13 million to build ultra-fast humanlike voice AI

Smallest.ai raised a $13 million Series A to build specialized voice models designed for real-time, low-latency customer conversations. The company argues that voice agents need smaller models that can listen, reason, and speak simultaneously, while routing harder problems to larger foundation models. The approach is a useful architecture signal: production agents may increasingly combine small real-time models with larger offline reasoning systems.
