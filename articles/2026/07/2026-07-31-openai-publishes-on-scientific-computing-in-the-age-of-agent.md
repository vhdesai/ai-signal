# OpenAI publishes on scientific computing in the age of agentic AI

OpenAI published a research item on applying agentic AI to scientific computing. The topic is strategically important because scientific computing workflows combine tool use, simulation, data analysis, and expert interpretation -- a natural proving ground for agents that can plan and execute multi-step tasks. This is relevant to government, industrial R&D, and vertical AI opportunities where the value of automation is tied to scarce technical expertise.
