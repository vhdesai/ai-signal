# AI policy groups and researchers converge on regulation efforts

Axios reports renewed AI-regulation activity, including AI researchers converging on policy discussions, Obama-Biden alumni launching an AI policy initiative, and AI safeguards groups funding candidates. The story shows AI policy becoming more organized, better funded, and more partisan as the 2026 midterm cycle approaches. Executives should expect AI regulation to become a sustained political campaign issue rather than a narrow technical debate.
