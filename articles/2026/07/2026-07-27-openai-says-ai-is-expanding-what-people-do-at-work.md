# OpenAI says AI is expanding what people do at work

OpenAI published research arguing that AI is expanding the range of tasks workers take on, with ChatGPT users crossing traditional role boundaries. The framing is important because it moves the discussion beyond job displacement toward task recomposition: employees may absorb adjacent analytical, creative, and operational work as AI lowers capability barriers. Executives should treat this as an operating-model question, not just a tool-adoption question.
