# MIT projects selected for DOE Genesis Mission AI-science funding

MIT News reports that MIT researchers will contribute to 15 collaborative projects selected for Phase I funding under the U.S. Department of Energy's Genesis Mission. The initiative aims to build an integrated scientific discovery platform using AI, supercomputing, quantum systems, and advanced instruments across areas such as rare earth extraction, fusion, quantum sensing, generative design, and digital twins. The program reflects a major shift toward AI-native scientific workflows with universities, national labs, and industry working as integrated teams.
