# Fish Audio raises $52M seed for AI voice models

Fish Audio raised a $52 million seed round to build AI voice models for creators and enterprises. The company says more than 8 million people use its open-source or hosted models, generating about $21 million in annual recurring revenue — unusually strong traction for a seed-stage voice startup. Voice continues to commercialize quickly as an AI-stack layer, with implications for contact centers, media production, and accessibility.
