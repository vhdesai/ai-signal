# Apple Intelligence cleared for China with Alibaba's Qwen and Baidu

China approved Apple Intelligence for launch using Alibaba's Qwen and Baidu services, unblocking Apple's AI roadmap in one of its largest markets. The approval follows reports that Apple Intelligence was registered with China's cyberspace regulator, and comes as Apple's Greater China sales rose sharply. The development cements Alibaba and Baidu as strategic model and infrastructure partners for global device platforms operating in China.
