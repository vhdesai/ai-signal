# Nvidia and Japan launch national AI infrastructure for physical AI

Nvidia announced it is working with Noetra and Japanese government and industry leaders to build an Nvidia Vera Rubin AI factory with 13,750 Vera CPUs and 27,500 Rubin GPUs, delivering 140 megawatts of data-center capacity. The system will support Japan's FRONTia Project and the development of open multimodal foundation models for AI agents, digital twins, robotics, and physical AI. This is one of the clearest examples yet of AI infrastructure being framed as national industrial policy, not just cloud capacity.
