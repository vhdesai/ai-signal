# DeepSeek pauses its ~$1.4B funding round after founder’s leaked remarks go viral

DeepSeek told prospective backers it would not sign investment agreements as expected, pausing a second round targeting at least 10 billion yuan (~$1.4B) at a reported ~480 billion yuan (~$71B) pre-money valuation. The suspension follows founder Liang Wenfeng’s frustration after viral posts — drawn from an investor-meeting transcript — quoted him saying China’s AI still trails the U.S. and depends on Nvidia chips. The round would have followed a $7B first financing closed in June. Signal: capital markets are acutely sensitive to founder messaging, and China–U.S. compute dependency remains an open question.
