# Synthetic-User Startup Simile Raises $200M Series B at $2B Valuation

Simile raised a $200 million Series B at a $2 billion valuation only five months after its $100 million Series A. The startup, founded by Smallville researcher Joon Sung Park, sells simulated users for research, product testing, and policy analysis. Its pitch is that synthetic populations can help enterprises test decisions across massive scenario sets more quickly than human panels alone. The up-round shows that application-layer AI companies with real enterprise traction can still command premium multiples despite market volatility. It also reflects investor appetite for agent-simulation tools, even as debate continues over how faithfully AI can model human behavior. Links: TechCrunch
