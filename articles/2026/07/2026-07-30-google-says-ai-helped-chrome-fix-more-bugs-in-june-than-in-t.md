# Google says AI helped Chrome fix more bugs in June than in the prior two years

Google said it fixed 1,072 Chrome security bugs across two June releases, more than the prior 23 releases combined, citing internal AI tools and Gemini-assisted vulnerability remediation. The data point supports a broader cybersecurity shift: AI is increasing both the pace of vulnerability discovery and the pressure on defenders to automate patching. For enterprise security teams, the operational issue is not only attacker use of AI, but also whether internal remediation workflows can absorb a much higher volume of findings.
