# Alibaba Launches Qwen-Image-3.0 — Without Benchmarks or Open Weights

Alibaba released its newest image-generation model but departed from its open-weights positioning by publishing no benchmarks or downloadable weights. The lack of evaluations makes comparison difficult. The release underscores how quickly Chinese labs are iterating across modalities as competition broadens beyond text.
