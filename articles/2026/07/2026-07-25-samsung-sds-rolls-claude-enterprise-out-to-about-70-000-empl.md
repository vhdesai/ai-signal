# Samsung SDS rolls Claude Enterprise out to about 70,000 employees

Samsung SDS is deploying Claude Enterprise, including Claude Code, to roughly 70,000 employees across about 20 Samsung affiliates. It is one of the larger disclosed single-enterprise frontier-model rollouts and gives Anthropic a large enterprise reference account in Korea.
