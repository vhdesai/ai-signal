# Meta reportedly in talks for a $10B Anthropic compute deal

Meta is reportedly negotiating a roughly $10 billion arrangement with Anthropic that would deepen Meta's role in AI cloud and compute. Details remain unconfirmed by either company, but the report fits a broader pattern of hyperscalers and labs cross-contracting for scarce compute. It also highlights how AI partnerships increasingly blend model access, cloud capacity, and strategic investment.
