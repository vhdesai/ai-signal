# Dili raises $21.7M to apply AI to the data-center construction boom

Dili raised a $15M Series A (led by Khosla Ventures, with participation from Y Combinator's Garry Tan and others), bringing total funding to $21.7M, to apply AI to compliance for US infrastructure and data-center construction projects. The pitch targets a specific bottleneck: the tangle of permitting and regulatory rules slowing the buildout of power and data-center capacity that AI demand requires. It's a clear example of "AI for the AI buildout" — tooling aimed at accelerating the physical expansion underpinning the model race. Academic Research M ACADEMIC
