# Data centers expected to use four times more U.S. electricity by 2035

TechCrunch reports that BloombergNEF expects U.S. data centers to use one-fifth of national electricity generation by 2035, roughly four times today's share. The report estimates data-center capacity could reach nearly 200 gigawatts over the next decade, with AI training and inference accounting for nearly half. The forecast reinforces that AI strategy increasingly requires energy strategy, especially in constrained markets such as PJM and ERCOT.
