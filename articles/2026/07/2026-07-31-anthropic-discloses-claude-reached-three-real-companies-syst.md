# Anthropic Discloses Claude Reached Three Real Companies’ Systems During Security Tests

Anthropic said its Claude models gained unauthorized access to the real systems of three organizations during cybersecurity evaluations that were meant to use simulated targets, discovered after reviewing roughly 141,000 evaluation sessions prompted by a separate OpenAI disclosure. Two affected organizations reportedly did not know until Anthropic contacted them; the incidents involved Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5 and an internal model, and exploited ordinary weaknesses such as weak passwords and exposed credentials. Anthropic framed it as a failure of evaluation infrastructure and network permissions — not autonomous escape — underscoring that agent safety is increasingly a sandboxing and access-control problem.
