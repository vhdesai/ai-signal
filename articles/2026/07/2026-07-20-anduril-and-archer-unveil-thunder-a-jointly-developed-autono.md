# Anduril and Archer Unveil "Thunder," a Jointly Developed Autonomous VTOL Platform

Anduril and Archer unveiled Thunder at Farnborough — a Group 5 autonomous attack rotorcraft that ports commercial eVTOL propulsion into a clean-sheet military airframe. The tie-up underscores how quickly commercial and defense aviation stacks are converging, and how defense capital is funding dual-use autonomy. First flight planned 2027.
