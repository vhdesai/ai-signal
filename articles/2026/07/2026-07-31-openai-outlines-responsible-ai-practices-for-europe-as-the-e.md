# OpenAI outlines responsible AI practices for Europe as the EU AI Act advances

OpenAI published an overview of how its safety, security, transparency, and provenance practices support responsible AI governance in Europe. The company framed the work in the context of the EU AI Act, which is moving from legislation into implementation. The update shows major model providers positioning their internal safety practices as part of regulatory readiness and market access strategy.
