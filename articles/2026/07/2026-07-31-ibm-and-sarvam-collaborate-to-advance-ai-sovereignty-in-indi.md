# IBM and Sarvam collaborate to advance AI sovereignty in India

IBM and Sarvam, India's full-stack sovereign AI company, announced a collaboration to advance sovereign AI development and adoption in India. The partnership aligns with IBM's enterprise focus on data control, governance and trust. It reflects growing demand for nationally controlled AI infrastructure.
