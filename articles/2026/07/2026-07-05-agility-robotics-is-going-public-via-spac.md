# Agility Robotics is going public via SPAC

Agility Robotics, maker of the Digit humanoid robot, is merging with Churchill Capital Corp XI at a reported valuation near $2.5 billion. The company is positioning around industrial and logistics deployments rather than consumer robots, making the transaction a useful benchmark for investor appetite in revenue-backed physical AI businesses.
