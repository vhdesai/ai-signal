# Apple posts record June quarter but warns of AI-driven component shortages

Apple reported revenue of about $109.4B (+16%) with iPhone up 22%, but shares fell roughly 7% on softer guidance and warnings that AI-data-center-driven memory and advanced-node shortages will pressure supply. It was Tim Cook's final earnings call as CEO. The commentary underscored how the AI build-out is now reshaping the broader electronics supply chain.
