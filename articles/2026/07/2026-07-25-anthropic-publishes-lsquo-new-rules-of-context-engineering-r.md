# Anthropic publishes &lsquo;new rules of context engineering&rsquo; for its Claude 5-generation models

Anthropic published new context-engineering guidance for its Claude 5-generation models (Claude Opus 5 and Claude Fable 5), reporting that developers can remove “over 80% of Claude Code’s system prompt … with no measurable loss” on internal coding evaluations. The best practices favor model judgment over hard-coded rules, interface design over few-shot examples, and “progressive disclosure,” and introduce a claude doctor / /doctor diagnostic command.
