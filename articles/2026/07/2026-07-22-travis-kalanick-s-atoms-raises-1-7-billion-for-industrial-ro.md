# Travis Kalanick's Atoms raises $1.7 billion for industrial robotics

TechCrunch reports that Travis Kalanick's robotics company Atoms raised $1.7 billion in a round led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Uber also participating. Atoms is described as a physical-world automation company built on top of Kalanick's CloudKitchens work and a prior acquisition of industrial automation company Pronto. The size of the round shows investor appetite for physical AI and industrial productivity plays, even when product specifics remain broad.
