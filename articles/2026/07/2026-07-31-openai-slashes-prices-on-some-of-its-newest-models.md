# OpenAI Slashes Prices on Some of Its Newest Models

OpenAI has cut prices on several of its newest models, a move that intensifies the ongoing price war in the foundation model market and directly addresses enterprise complaints about mounting inference costs. The pricing reductions come as competition from open-weight alternatives and rival providers like Anthropic and Google continues to put downward pressure on API margins across the industry. For enterprise customers already grappling with rising token costs, the cuts provide near-term relief but also raise strategic questions about vendor lock-in and the sustainability of building production workflows on proprietary APIs whose pricing can shift rapidly.
