# OpenAI publishes guidance on building an AI-ready workforce

OpenAI highlighted how Unive built an AI-ready workforce with ChatGPT Enterprise by combining leadership, responsible governance, and employee-led innovation. The case study reflects a broader enterprise pattern: adoption depends on training, operating model changes, and governed experimentation, not merely access to a chatbot. For senior leaders, AI productivity programs need ownership, enablement, and measurement if they are to move beyond pilot usage.
