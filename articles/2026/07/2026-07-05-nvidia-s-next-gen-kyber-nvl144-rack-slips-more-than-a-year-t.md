# Nvidia's Next-Gen "Kyber" NVL144 Rack Slips More Than a Year to 2028

Research firm SemiAnalysis reports that Nvidia's Kyber NVL144 rack — designed to house its 2027 Rubin Ultra chips — has slipped more than 12 months to 2028 because the specialized PCB "midplane" board remains difficult to manufacture at volume. The NVL72x2 backup was scrapped after cloud-provider pushback. Asian PCB-supplier stocks fell 8–14%. The slip leaves Nvidia without a proven path to scale its most powerful training clusters and opens a rare high-end window for AMD and Google's TPUs.
