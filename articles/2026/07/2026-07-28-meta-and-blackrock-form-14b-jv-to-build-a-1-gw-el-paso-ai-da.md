# Meta and BlackRock form ~$14B JV to build a 1 GW El Paso AI data center

Meta and a BlackRock-led group (with GIP and HPS) formed a joint venture to build a 1 gigawatt AI data-center campus in El Paso, Texas, valued at roughly $14 billion and slated to come online in 2028. BlackRock’s group owns about 80% (~$4.9B cash plus ~$12.5B debt), while Meta retains 20%, operates the site, and leases it back under a four-year lease with four additional four-year extensions. The deal is a template for off-balance-sheet financing of the AI compute buildout.
