# Stripe reportedly in talks to buy AI-model marketplace OpenRouter

WSJ reports that Stripe is in talks to buy OpenRouter, an AI-model marketplace that routes developers to multiple model providers. The potential deal would connect payments infrastructure with AI usage routing and metering, a strategic position as developers increasingly want model choice, cost comparison, and unified billing. It also suggests that model marketplaces may become core commerce infrastructure for AI applications. URL behind paywall.
