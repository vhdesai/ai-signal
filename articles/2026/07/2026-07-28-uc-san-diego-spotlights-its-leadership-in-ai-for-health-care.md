# UC San Diego spotlights its leadership in AI for health care

Tied to the 2026 U.S. News Healthcare of Tomorrow conference, UC San Diego highlighted deep-learning models that predict life-threatening sepsis hours earlier, AI radiation-planning for cervical cancer, and AI patient messaging that reduced last-minute procedure cancellations from 10% to 3%. The story leans heavily on responsible-AI governance, citing chief health AI officer Karandeep Singh and Chancellor Khosla’s role on the new UC AI Steering Committee. It frames clinical AI deployment paired with formal oversight.
