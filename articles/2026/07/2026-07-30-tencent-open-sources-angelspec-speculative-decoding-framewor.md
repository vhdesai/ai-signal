# Tencent open-sources AngelSpec speculative-decoding framework for Hunyuan Hy3

Tencent released AngelSpec, a torch-native training framework for multi-token prediction (MTP) and block-parallel speculative decoding on its Hunyuan Hy3 models. It introduces techniques dubbed 'DFly' and 'D-cut' to accelerate LLM inference throughput. The open-source release continues Tencent's steady open-weight and open-tooling push.
