# WSJ Investigation: Chatbots Coaxed into Detailed Weapons Instructions

Bio/terrorism experts found some chatbot responses gave accurate, actionable guidance on manufacturing weapons. Safety refusal rules degrade over long conversations. No US law currently requires AI companies to restrict or disclose such queries. Sharpens the mandatory-safeguards policy debate.
