# Rime raises $24 million to improve enterprise voice AI calls

Rime raised a $24 million Series A led by M13 Ventures, with Twilio Ventures, Corazon Capital, Unusual Ventures, and existing investors participating. The company says it handles more than 100 million calls per month and is shifting toward speech-to-speech models to reduce latency, improve turn-taking, and handle noise in regulated enterprise settings. The voice AI market remains crowded, but Rime's focus on proprietary conversational data and pronunciation tuning addresses a practical adoption bottleneck.
