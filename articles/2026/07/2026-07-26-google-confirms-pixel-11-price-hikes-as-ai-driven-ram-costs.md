# Google confirms Pixel 11 price hikes as AI-driven RAM costs surge ~6&times;

Google VP Shakil Barkat confirmed that Pixel 11 prices will rise because memory makers are redirecting wafer capacity toward the high-bandwidth memory used in AI data centers. Cited Morgan Stanley figures show RAM climbing from about $2.80/GB in 2025 to roughly $12/GB — a ~6× jump — with leaks pointing to a ~$100 increase (base around $899), to be confirmed at the August 12 Made by Google event.
