# EU commits €10B to build up to seven AI “gigafactories”

The European Commission unveiled a €10B initiative to finance up to seven large-scale AI gigafactories, up from five, targeting an additional €20B in private investment. Chipmakers including AMD, Nvidia, and Qualcomm submitted letters of support. Applications are due November 12, with selections expected in early 2027.
