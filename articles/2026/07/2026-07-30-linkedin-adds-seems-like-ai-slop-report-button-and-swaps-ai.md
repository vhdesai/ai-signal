# LinkedIn Adds "Seems Like AI Slop" Report Button and Swaps AI Writer for Proofreader

LinkedIn added a control for users to flag posts that appear machine-generated, internally framing the problem as "AI slop." At the same time, it is replacing its AI writing assistant with a proofreader that improves human-written drafts instead of generating posts from scratch. That reversal implies LinkedIn concluded synthetic post generation was degrading feed quality and reducing the value of professional conversation. The change also aligns LinkedIn with a broader platform trend toward limiting or labeling low-quality AI content rather than amplifying it. Brands and professionals should read this as a signal that authenticity is becoming a competitive advantage on major social platforms again. Links: TechCrunch
