# Bot-detection startup Spur raises $200M from Insight Partners

Spur Intelligence raised $200 million from Insight Partners for technology that distinguishes legitimate human web traffic from bots. The raise lands as AI agents proliferate and blur the boundary between sanctioned automation and adversarial bots, driving demand for traffic-authentication tooling. For enterprises, it underscores that bot management is being repriced as an AI-era security problem rather than a commodity.
