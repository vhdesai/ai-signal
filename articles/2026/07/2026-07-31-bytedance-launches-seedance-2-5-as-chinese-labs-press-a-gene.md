# ByteDance launches Seedance 2.5 as Chinese labs press a generative-video offensive

ByteDance released Seedance 2.5, which can generate a 30-second, single-take high-quality clip from multiple inputs, extending its Seed video-model line. Separately the same day, MiniMax launched H3, an open omni-modal model that generates 2K clips with native stereo audio. Together they signal a fast-maturing Chinese challenge in generative video, a segment where U.S. labs have led.
