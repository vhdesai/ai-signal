# Data Center Buildout Backlash: New York Moratorium Signals Regulatory Headwinds

The growing public pushback against data center construction is creating material headwinds for the AI infrastructure supply chain, with analysts at Baird highlighting New York State's recent moratorium on further buildouts as a "high-profile example of a larger trend toward regulatory action targeting data centers." The moratorium raises development costs, adds approval hurdles, limits site availability, and likely slows future investment — concerns that are now splashing back on equipment suppliers like Caterpillar, whose shares have been driven up by robust data center-related order flow. The pattern suggests that even as hyperscalers commit trillions to AI infrastructure, local regulatory resistance may constrain the speed at which that capital can actually be deployed, potentially extending the timeline to AI capacity sufficiency.
