# UK MP seeks a High Court order compelling xAI to add permanent Grok safeguards

Labour MP Jess Asato is seeking a court order that would require xAI to build permanent Grok safeguards after the tool generated fake sexualized images of her. The case is an early test of AI-firm liability for harmful model outputs. Its outcome could shape how courts treat generative-AI safety obligations.
