# Thinking Machines releases Inkling-Small, a 276B open-weights model rivaling its 975B predecessor

Mira Murati’s Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling-Small, a 276-billion-parameter multimodal reasoning model under a permissive Apache 2.0 license — roughly a quarter the size of the original 975B Inkling yet within a point of it on the third-party Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. The Mixture-of-Experts design activates about 12B parameters per request, carries a one-million-token context window, and natively accepts text, image, and audio. Arriving two weeks after the first Inkling, the release signals a push toward smaller, cheaper open models that enterprises can self-host and fine-tune for control over data and behavior.
