# AI-Capex Doubts Drive a Memory and Semiconductor Sell-Off

Memory and chip leaders fell sharply — Micron among the biggest decliners — as investor concern over AI spending durability resurfaced. The move signals growing market sensitivity to AI-capex sustainability after a year of record data-center investment. Scrutiny of the "AI trade" is intensifying even where underlying demand remains strong.
