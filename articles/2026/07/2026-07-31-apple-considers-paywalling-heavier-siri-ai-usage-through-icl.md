# Apple considers paywalling heavier Siri AI usage through iCloud+

Tim Cook said Apple's long-awaited Siri AI upgrade could include paid usage tiers through iCloud+, allowing heavy users to buy more compute. The comments suggest Apple is converging toward the freemium consumption model already used by OpenAI and Anthropic, but inside its existing services bundle. The strategic question is whether Apple can turn assistant usage into recurring services revenue while controlling inference costs and preserving user experience.
