# DeepSeek puts funding round on hold after leaked founder call

DeepSeek told investors it is pausing fundraising talks that valued the company at about 500 billion yuan, or roughly $74 billion. The pause followed a leaked transcript of CEO Liang Wenfeng’s investor call and comes as Moonshot’s Kimi K3 has become a global open-model talking point. The episode shows how quickly narrative risk can become financing risk for frontier AI labs, especially in China’s politically charged AI market.
