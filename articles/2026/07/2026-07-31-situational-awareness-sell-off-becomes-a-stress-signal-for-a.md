# Situational Awareness sell-off becomes a stress signal for AI-infrastructure trades

Business Insider reported that the sell-off in Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness portfolio helped relieve pressure on some AI-linked stocks, but likely came too late to protect other funds' July returns. The hedge fund had become emblematic of a concentrated AI infrastructure thesis spanning chips, memory, power, and compute. The episode shows how quickly crowded AI-capex trades can become liquidity events even when the long-term thesis remains intact.
