# Microsoft ships a July 2026 Excel Copilot feature wave

Microsoft delivered another wave of Excel Copilot upgrades in its July 2026 update, including a long-requested change that removes a prior limitation on how Copilot can be used in the spreadsheet. The additions continue Microsoft’s steady expansion of embedded AI across the Office apps. The improvements target everyday analyst and finance workflows.
