# Token costs force enterprises to revise AI deployment plans

CIO Dive reported that rising token costs are pushing enterprise leaders to revisit AI deployment strategies, citing EY survey findings that some companies are charging ahead while others scale back. The update underscores a practical production-AI constraint: model routing, workload triage, and cost governance are becoming core CIO responsibilities rather than after-the-fact optimization.
