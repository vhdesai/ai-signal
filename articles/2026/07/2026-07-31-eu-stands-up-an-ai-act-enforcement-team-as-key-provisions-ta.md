# EU stands up an AI Act enforcement team as key provisions take effect

The European Commission formed a Brussels enforcement team — roughly 38 added staff plus compliance and whistleblower tools — to police advanced AI from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Chinese providers. Priorities include deepfakes, unlabeled synthetic content, illicit imagery and automated cyberattacks. The Article 50 deepfake-labeling obligation takes effect August 2.
