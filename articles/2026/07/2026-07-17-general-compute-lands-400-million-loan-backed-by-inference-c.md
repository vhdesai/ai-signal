# General Compute lands $400 million loan backed by inference chips

General Compute secured a $400 million loan from Upper90 to build an AI inference cloud around SambaNova inference chips. The deal may be one of the first to use inference-specific chips as collateral, extending chip-backed financing beyond Nvidia GPUs. The financing reflects a broader shift toward cheaper inference capacity for open models, as buyers try to reduce the cost of running AI at scale.
