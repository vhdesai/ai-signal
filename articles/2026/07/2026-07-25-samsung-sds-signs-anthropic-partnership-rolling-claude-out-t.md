# Samsung SDS signs Anthropic partnership, rolling Claude out to ~70,000 employees

Samsung SDS and Anthropic signed a strategic partnership (made public July 25) to deploy Claude Enterprise across roughly 70,000 employees at about 20 Samsung affiliates under a “Client Zero” strategy, adding Claude Code and joint enterprise offerings. Signal: a marquee non-U.S. enterprise deployment that deepens Anthropic’s commercial footprint against OpenAI and Google in Asia.
