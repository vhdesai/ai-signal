# Satya Nadella warns companies not to trust one AI for everything

TechCrunch reports that Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella warned companies that fully outsourcing AI work to proprietary model providers may leave them exposed. He argued enterprises should retain prompts, metadata, memory, and context so they can train or fine-tune their own models and use AI gateways to separate harnesses from underlying models. The message aligns with a multi-model enterprise architecture: control the orchestration layer, not just the model subscription. CURSORINDIADEVELOPER TOOLS
