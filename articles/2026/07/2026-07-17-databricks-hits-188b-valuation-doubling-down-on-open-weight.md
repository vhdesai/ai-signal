# Databricks Hits $188B Valuation, Doubling Down on Open-Weight Economics

Databricks reached $188 billion, cementing its shift from data-lakehouse vendor to full-stack AI company. It published research arguing open-weight models deliver material cost savings for AI-assisted coding versus closed APIs. A maturing platform vendor positioning open models as a deliberate cost lever rather than a compromise.
