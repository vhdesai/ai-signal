# Google and KDDI launch an AI startup support program for Japanese founders

The joint program offers equity investment, Google Cloud credits, and access to Gemini, the Nano Banana image model, and the Lyria music model. It also provides 'sovereign' Gemini and GPU capacity through KDDI's Osaka-Sakai data center. The effort is aimed at accelerating Japan's domestic AI startup ecosystem.
