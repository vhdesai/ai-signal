# Nscale acquires Anyscale for a reported $1.65B to own more of the AI compute stack

British AI cloud provider Nscale agreed to acquire Anyscale — the company behind the open-source Ray framework for distributed AI workloads — in a deal Bloomberg pegged at about $1.65 billion. The purchase adds an orchestration, training, and inference software layer atop Nscale’s GPU and data-center infrastructure. Anyscale will keep its brand and customers, with the transaction expected to close in the second half of 2026 pending regulatory approval. The deal reflects continued vertical consolidation as “neoclouds” move up the stack to capture more AI infrastructure spend.
