# Nvidia helps customers finance GPU purchases to expand AI chip demand

Nvidia has built a financing structure in which it can absorb potential customer defaults in exchange for a share of a cloud provider's revenue, making banks more willing to fund expensive GPU purchases by smaller neocloud operators. GMI Cloud has reportedly committed about $500 million through the model. Supporters say it broadens access to Nvidia hardware; critics warn it risks circular financing that indirectly props up Nvidia's own sales.
