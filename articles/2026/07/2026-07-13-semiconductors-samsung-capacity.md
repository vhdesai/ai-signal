# SEMICONDUCTORS SAMSUNG CAPACITY

The Information reports that Samsung is moving up plant capacity plans to 2029, amid continued pressure to expand advanced chip supply. While details remain behind the publication's paywall, the timing points to persistent demand from AI infrastructure customers and competitive pressure from SK Hynix and other memory suppliers. The key takeaway is that capacity planning is extending the AI cycle into late-decade manufacturing commitments.
