# ChatGPT nears 1 billion weekly active users

OpenAI’s ChatGPT is nearing 1 billion weekly active users, according to internal information reported by The Information. OpenAI also reportedly expects roughly half of revenue to come from business customers this year. The adoption scale underscores ChatGPT’s position as both a consumer distribution channel and an enterprise conversion funnel.
