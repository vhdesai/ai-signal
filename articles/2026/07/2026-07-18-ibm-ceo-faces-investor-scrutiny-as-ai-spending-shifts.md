# IBM CEO faces investor scrutiny as AI spending shifts

WSJ reports that IBM CEO Arvind Krishna has “nowhere to hide” from AI after investor concerns over IBM's positioning. The story follows broader market skepticism about whether legacy enterprise vendors can translate AI demand into growth fast enough. The executive lesson is that AI narratives are no longer sufficient; investors are scrutinizing whether AI budgets are actually flowing to incumbent product lines or to newer infrastructure and platform providers.
