# China's Moonshot Plans a $30B Hong Kong IPO Within Six Months

Moonshot AI (Kimi models) is preparing to list in Hong Kong within ~6 months, wrapping a round that could value the three-year-old startup above $30 billion. Reflects a reopening of AI capital markets in Asia. A successful listing would set an early public-market benchmark for Chinese frontier-model valuations.
