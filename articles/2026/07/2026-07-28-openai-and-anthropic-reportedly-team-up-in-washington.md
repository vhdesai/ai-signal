# OpenAI and Anthropic reportedly team up in Washington

The Information reports that OpenAI and Anthropic are quietly teaming up in Washington as policymakers debate how to pace AI development and respond to safety risks. The collaboration is notable because the labs compete commercially but share an interest in shaping AI governance, model evaluation, and frontier safety policy. Enterprises should expect model providers to have growing influence over regulatory frameworks that affect deployment and procurement.
