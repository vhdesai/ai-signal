# Alibaba previews Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4 trillion-parameter multimodal model

MarkTechPost reports that Alibaba previewed Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4 trillion-parameter multimodal model, shortly after Moonshot's Kimi K3 launch. The report frames the release as part of a broader wave of Chinese model competition aimed at narrowing the gap with leading U.S. systems. The headline reinforces that scale, multimodality, and rapid iteration remain central to the competitive narrative, even as buyers focus more heavily on cost, latency, and deployability.
