# Monday.com cuts 20% of staff to refocus around AI Work Platform

TechCrunch reports that Monday.com is cutting about 630 employees, or 20% of its workforce, as it restructures around its AI Work Platform. The company says it is pursuing a leaner operating model while redesigning its product around AI agents, workflow automation, no-code app building, and task-oriented chat. The broader signal is that even successful SaaS vendors are changing cost structures and product roadmaps in anticipation of AI-native competition.
