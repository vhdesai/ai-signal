# OpenAI lays out “Building abundant intelligence” full-stack strategy

In a post from CFO Sarah Friar, OpenAI argued that infrastructure matters chiefly for the cheaper, more capable intelligence it enables. It cited GPT-5.6 Sol optimizations that cut serving costs 20% and lifted an ARC-AGI-3 score from 13.3% to 38.3% using six times fewer tokens, alongside more than 1B users and 2M businesses. The piece frames the economics behind OpenAI's large capacity commitments.
