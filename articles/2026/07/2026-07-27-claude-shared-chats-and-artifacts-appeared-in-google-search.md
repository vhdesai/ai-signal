# Claude shared chats and Artifacts appeared in Google search

TechCrunch reports that some Claude shared chats and Artifacts appeared in Google search results, apparently tied to Claude's share-chat feature. Even if shared links are user-created, search indexing of AI conversations creates a data-governance risk when users treat generated workspaces as semi-private. The incident is a practical reminder that AI collaboration features need clear visibility controls, indexing defaults, and enterprise policy enforcement.
