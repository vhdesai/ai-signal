# Enterprises Deploying AI Agents Faster Than They Can Govern Them

A study of 573 enterprise leaders found organizations are putting AI agents into production ahead of access, monitoring, and accountability controls. The gap concentrates operational and security risk where autonomy is highest. Agent governance — identity, permissions, audit — must be treated as a prerequisite to scaling, not an afterthought.
