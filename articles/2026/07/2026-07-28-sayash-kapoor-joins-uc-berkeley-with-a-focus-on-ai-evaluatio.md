# Sayash Kapoor joins UC Berkeley with a focus on AI evaluation

UC Berkeley appointed Dr. Sayash Kapoor (from Princeton) as an assistant professor starting July 2027. His research “develops AI evaluation methods with application to security, science, and policy,” including assessing the risk of open-weights models, large-scale evaluation of AI agents, and building benchmarks for AI-based science. Kapoor co-authored AI Snake Oil and was named to Time’s 100 Most Influential People in AI.
