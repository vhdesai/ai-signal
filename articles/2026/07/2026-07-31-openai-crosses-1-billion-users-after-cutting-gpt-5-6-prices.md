# OpenAI crosses 1 billion users after cutting GPT-5.6 prices up to 80%

OpenAI said it has surpassed 1 billion users, roughly coinciding with an aggressive repricing that cut GPT-5.6 Luna by 80% and Terra by 20% and made the Sol tier about 2.5x faster in the API. The company frames the cuts as internal efficiency gains rather than discounting, positioning scale and unit economics as its moat. For enterprise buyers, the milestone signals both deepening platform lock-in and a downward trajectory on inference costs. (WSJ reported the user milestone in parallel.) EARNINGSHOT a
