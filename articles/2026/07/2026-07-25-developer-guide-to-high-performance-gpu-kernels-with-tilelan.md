# developer guide to high-performance GPU kernels with TileLang

MarkTechPost published a hands-on guide to writing high-performance GPU kernels with TileLang (built on TVM), covering tensor-core GEMM, fused softmax, FlashAttention, and autotuning. It targets developers who want to optimize inference and training kernels without dropping all the way down to raw CUDA.
