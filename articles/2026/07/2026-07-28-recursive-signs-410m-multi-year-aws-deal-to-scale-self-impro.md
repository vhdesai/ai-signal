# Recursive signs $410M multi-year AWS deal to scale self-improving AI

Recursive — the Richard Socher-led startup building recursive self-improving 'superintelligence' for automated research, which emerged from stealth in May at a $4.65B valuation — announced a multi-year, $410M agreement to run its automated AI-research system on AWS. The two will co-develop infrastructure purpose-built for large-scale automated research; Recursive says its system has already beaten a two-year human leaderboard record across three benchmarks. The deal underscores how self-improving 'AI-scientist' loops are becoming a new, compounding source of compute demand. AMDCS INFRASTRUCTUREHOT
