# Amazon's Zoox wins first U.S. approval for paid robotaxis with no steering wheel

NHTSA granted Zoox a landmark exemption — the first vehicle with no manual controls cleared to charge for rides — with service beginning in Las Vegas. Regulators signaled that brake-pedal-free designs could follow. It is a significant regulatory milestone for purpose-built autonomous vehicles and for Amazon's mobility ambitions.
