# EU tells Google to open Android and search data to AI rivals

Reuters reports that EU-mandated changes require Google to open Android and search-engine data access to AI and search rivals. The intervention targets platform control points that could determine distribution and training advantages for AI assistants and search competitors. If implemented broadly, it could alter competitive dynamics for AI products on mobile devices and search surfaces.
