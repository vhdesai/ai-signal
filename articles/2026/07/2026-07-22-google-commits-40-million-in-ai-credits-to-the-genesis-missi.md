# Google commits $40 million in AI credits to the Genesis Mission

Google DeepMind says Google is committing $40 million in AI tokens and credits to support the DOE Genesis Mission. The commitment complements federal and university efforts to build AI-enabled scientific discovery workflows. The strategic takeaway is that hyperscalers are positioning cloud AI credits and model access as core infrastructure for national science, not just commercial developer adoption.
