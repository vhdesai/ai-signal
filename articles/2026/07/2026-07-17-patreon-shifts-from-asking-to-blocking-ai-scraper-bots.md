# Patreon Shifts from Asking to Blocking AI Scraper Bots

Patreon is working with Cloudflare to actively block AI crawlers that scrape creators' content for model training without consent, moving beyond voluntary robots.txt requests. Platforms are increasingly willing to technically enforce data rights rather than rely on opt-out norms — another marker in the escalating standoff between content platforms and model developers over training data.
