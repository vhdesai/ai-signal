# Adobe's Project Indigo adds AI photo critique and editing guidance

TechCrunch reports that Adobe is adding AI-powered critique, editing suggestions, object removal, depth-of-field generation, and style transfer to its experimental Project Indigo iOS camera app. The implementation is notable because several features use structured controls rather than relying only on open-ended prompts. That points to a broader product-design trend: AI features that guide expert workflows and reduce prompt burden may be more useful than generic generation tools.
