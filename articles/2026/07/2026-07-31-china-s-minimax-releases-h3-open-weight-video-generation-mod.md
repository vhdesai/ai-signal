# China's MiniMax Releases H3 Open-Weight Video Generation Model

MiniMax released H3, a multimodal video model that can take text, image, video, and audio inputs and output 2K clips with stereo sound. The model also supports editing and motion transfer, and the company says it will publish weights within days. That makes H3 part of a broader Chinese push toward open-weight generative media systems while many Western rivals still keep comparable models closed. H3 already ranks near the top of independent leaderboards, reinforcing China's growing strength not just in chat models but in creative AI infrastructure. With ByteDance, Kuaishou, Alibaba, and MiniMax all active, the center of gravity in AI video is increasingly shifting toward Chinese firms. Links: AOL / Reuters
