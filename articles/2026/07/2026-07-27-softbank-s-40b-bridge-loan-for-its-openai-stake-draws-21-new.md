# SoftBank's $40B bridge loan for its OpenAI stake draws 21 new lenders

SoftBank's $40 billion bridge loan financing its OpenAI investment has attracted a syndicate of 21 additional lenders in a broader distribution phase, according to Bloomberg. The expanded lender group signals continued institutional appetite to fund OpenAI's capital needs despite mounting questions about AI-sector returns. It also underscores how OpenAI's expansion increasingly rests on layered debt across many counterparties rather than equity alone.
