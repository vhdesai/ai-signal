# Apple proposes LEAD to address long-horizon reasoning failures

Apple published LEAD, or Lookahead-Enhanced Atomic Decomposition, a method aimed at reducing failures in long-horizon reasoning. The research argues that extreme decomposition can create a no-recovery bottleneck, where errors on a few hard steps become irreversible. The work is relevant for agentic systems because it targets a core reliability problem: how to break work into steps without making early mistakes unrecoverable.
