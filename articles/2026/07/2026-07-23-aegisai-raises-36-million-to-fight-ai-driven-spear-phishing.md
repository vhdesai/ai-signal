# AegisAI raises $36 million to fight AI-driven spear phishing

AegisAI, founded by former Google security executives, raised a $36 million Series A led by Battery Ventures to stop AI-generated spear phishing. The company uses AI agents to analyze email context and detect subtle anomalies that rule-based systems may miss. The funding reflects enterprise concern that attackers are using generative AI to personalize attacks faster than legacy defenses can adapt.
