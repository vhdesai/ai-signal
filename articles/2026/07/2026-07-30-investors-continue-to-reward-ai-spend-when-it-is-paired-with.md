# Investors continue to reward AI spend when it is paired with cloud demand

TechCrunch framed the post-earnings market response as a clear pattern: investors are more comfortable with massive AI capex at Amazon, Microsoft, and Google because cloud revenue is growing alongside capacity. Amazon spent $173 billion on property and equipment over the fiscal year ended June 30 and still drew a positive reaction because AWS demand remains strong. The market is effectively asking every AI infrastructure spender the same question: where is the revenue engine that absorbs the buildout?
