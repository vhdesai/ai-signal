# Pro-AI and AI-safety groups prepare for midterm policy fight

Axios reports that a pro-AI super PAC has $31 million ready for the midterms, while AI-safeguards groups are also directing funds toward candidates. The story indicates that AI regulation is becoming a funded political contest rather than a narrow technical-policy discussion. Executives should expect AI legislation to be shaped by electoral coalitions, advocacy groups, and industry spending as much as by safety benchmarks.
