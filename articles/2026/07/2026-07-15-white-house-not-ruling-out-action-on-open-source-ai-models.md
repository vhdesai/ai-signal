# White House not ruling out action on open-source AI models

A senior White House official signaled possible executive action on open-source AI for China-related concerns. National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross said the June executive order covers open-source scanning and deconfliction, framed as strengthening a U.S. open-source ecosystem worried about losing ground to China. Firms such as Reflection AI have pitched the administration on a new open-source framework.
