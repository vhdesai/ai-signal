# Applied Computing raises $20 million for an oil-and-gas foundation model

Applied Computing raised a $20 million Series A led by KBR, with Databricks Ventures participating, to build Orbital, a foundation model for oil, gas, refining, and petrochemical operations. The model combines time-series data, physics-based modeling, and language models to predict facility state, flag anomalies, and simulate operational changes. The story is a concrete example of AI moving into high-value industrial systems where proprietary operational data and domain constraints matter more than generic chatbot capability.
