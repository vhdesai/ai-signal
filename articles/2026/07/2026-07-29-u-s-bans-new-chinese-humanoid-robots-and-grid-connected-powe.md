# U.S. bans new Chinese humanoid robots and grid-connected power inverters

The U.S. announced a ban on imports of new foreign-made humanoid robots, most of which come from China, citing national-security risks. The FCC also added foreign-produced power inverters to its covered list, widening the hardware-security perimeter around AI, robotics, and grid infrastructure. China’s Ministry of Commerce warned that the move could undermine U.S.-China trade relations, suggesting rapid escalation risk for companies sourcing robotics systems from China.
