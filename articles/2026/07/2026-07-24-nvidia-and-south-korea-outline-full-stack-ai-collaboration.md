# NVIDIA and South Korea outline full-stack AI collaboration

NVIDIA says South Korean President Jae Myung Lee and senior Korean business and research leaders are meeting with NVIDIA and ecosystem partners at an AI Summit in San Francisco. NVIDIA and KAIST announced a joint AI research lab focused on agentic AI, and NVIDIA highlighted expanded work with SK around memory, AI infrastructure, physical AI, and robotics. The announcement reinforces the sovereign-AI pattern: countries are pairing national talent and industrial champions with U.S. accelerator ecosystems.
