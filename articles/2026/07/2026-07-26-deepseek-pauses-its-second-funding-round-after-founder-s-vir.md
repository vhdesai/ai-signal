# DeepSeek pauses its second funding round after founder's viral remarks

DeepSeek suspended an ongoing funding round after viral posts attributed to founder Liang Wenfeng — touching on reliance on Nvidia chips and China's AI gap — circulated widely. The reputational stumble clouds the company's reported IPO ambitions. It's a reminder that founder messaging now carries direct financing consequences.
