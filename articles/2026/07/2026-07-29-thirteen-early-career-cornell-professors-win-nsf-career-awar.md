# Thirteen early-career Cornell professors win NSF CAREER awards

Thirteen early-career Cornell faculty received NSF Faculty Early Career Development (CAREER) awards, with research spanning artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and ribosome biology. The five-year grants fund foundational research and education. A subset of the awardees focus specifically on AI and machine learning.
