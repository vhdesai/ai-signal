# New York school district pauses humanoid "robot teacher" after state pushback

The Salamanca City Central School District paused plans to place a ~$57,590 Realbotix humanoid ("Sally") in high-school STEAM classes after parents, teachers, and the New York State Education Commissioner raised objections. The district says it will negotiate stronger student-data-privacy terms before any 2026–27 rollout. It is one of the first US cases of a state education regulator formally intervening to halt a live classroom AI deployment.
