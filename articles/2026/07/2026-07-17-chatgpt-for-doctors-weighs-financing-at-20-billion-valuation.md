# “ChatGPT for doctors” weighs financing at $20 billion valuation

The Information reports that a “ChatGPT for doctors” company is considering new financing at a $20 billion valuation. The item signals continued investor willingness to pay premium multiples for vertical AI in regulated, high-value workflows. Healthcare AI remains attractive because documentation, care navigation, and clinical decision support are large pain points, but the durability of these valuations will depend on outcomes, trust, distribution, and liability controls.
