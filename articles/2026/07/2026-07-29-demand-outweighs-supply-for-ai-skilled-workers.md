# Demand outweighs supply for AI-skilled workers

CIO Dive reported that demand for AI-skilled workers continues to outstrip supply, citing Indeed research. Enterprises are investing heavily in AI but need stronger upskilling, role-based training, and business metrics to convert pilots into measurable impact. The talent bottleneck is becoming as important as the model or platform choice.
