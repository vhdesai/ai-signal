# Google Ships Three New Gemini Flash Models; Flagship 3.5 Pro Slips Again

Google released Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and a third Flash variant — available immediately through the API. Gemini 3.6 Flash cuts AI-agent token costs by up to 65% on long-horizon engineering tasks. Google is competing hardest on inference economics, where its custom-silicon advantage is strongest, even as flagship 3.5 Pro remains in testing.
