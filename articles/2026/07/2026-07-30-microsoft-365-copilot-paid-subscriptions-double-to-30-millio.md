# Microsoft 365 Copilot Paid Subscriptions Double to 30 Million

Microsoft disclosed that paid subscriptions for its Microsoft 365 Copilot AI assistant doubled to 30 million in the June quarter, one of the clearest demand signals yet that enterprise customers are willing to pay for embedded generative AI productivity tools at scale. The milestone validates Microsoft's strategy of bundling AI capabilities into existing Office workflows rather than offering standalone AI products, and represents meaningful incremental revenue given Copilot's $30/user/month pricing tier. CFO Amy Hood emphasized "efficiencies" throughout the earnings call, describing how Microsoft manages its chip fleets to "get more out of everything" — a philosophy that extends beyond hardware to enterprise AI monetization. The 30 million figure makes Copilot one of the fastest enterprise SaaS products to reach this scale in Microsoft's history.
