# Meta Hires Senior AWS Compute Chief Dave Brown to Accelerate AI Infrastructure

Meta is hiring Dave Brown, one of AWS's most senior compute and AI executives, to help oversee its data-center expansion as it plans $125–145B in capital spending this year. The move, first reported by the WSJ, comes as Meta weighs whether to offer AI computing to outside customers — a commercial cloud push that would put it in direct competition with AWS, Microsoft, Google, and Oracle.
