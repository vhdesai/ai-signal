# CyrusOne (KKR/BlackRock) Lays IPO Groundwork in Potential Multi-Billion-Dollar Listing

CyrusOne is reportedly preparing to hire banks for what could become one of next year's largest IPOs in the data center sector. KKR and BlackRock's infrastructure arm took the company private for $15 billion in 2022, and a new listing could value it much more richly. The move would extend the recent parade of AI infrastructure plays seeking public-market liquidity despite rising concern about overheating. For the owners, an IPO offers both debt relief after heavy expansion and a structured path to monetizing their stake.
