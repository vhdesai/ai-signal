# More than 20 tech companies urge Washington not to restrict open-weight AI

Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, Palantir, Dell, Andreessen Horowitz, Mistral, and others signed a letter urging U.S. policymakers not to impose broad restrictions on open-weight AI models. OpenAI and Anthropic did not sign, highlighting a real industry split between open-model distribution and closed frontier-model strategies. The debate has direct implications for export controls, model release rules, and the competitive position of U.S. labs against Chinese open models.
