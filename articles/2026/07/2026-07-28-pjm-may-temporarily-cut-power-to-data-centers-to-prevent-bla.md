# PJM may temporarily cut power to data centers to prevent blackouts

PJM Interconnection, the largest U.S. grid operator, plans to curtail power to data centers and other large users during shortages beginning in June 2027. The policy follows rapid data-center growth and an auction that fell short of desired generation capacity. For AI infrastructure leaders, grid access is becoming a strategic constraint requiring demand response, on-site power, storage, and local regulatory planning.
