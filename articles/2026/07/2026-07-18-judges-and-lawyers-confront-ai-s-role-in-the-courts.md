# Judges and lawyers confront AI's role in the courts

Axios reports that judges and lawyers are grappling with AI's role in the legal system. The judiciary is becoming a critical test bed for practical AI governance because errors can affect rights, remedies, evidence, and public trust. Legal-sector AI adoption will likely require auditable workflows, clear disclosure rules, human accountability, and stronger professional norms around AI-generated research and filings.
