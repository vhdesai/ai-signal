# AI guardrails are impeding legitimate offensive-security research

TechCrunch reports that cybersecurity researchers say OpenAI and Anthropic guardrails can block legitimate vulnerability discovery and exploit validation, even when the goal is defensive assessment. Researchers told TechCrunch that inconsistent refusals push them toward open-source or Chinese models that can be run locally without restrictions. The key issue is dual use: the same capabilities needed to confirm and fix vulnerabilities can also be used offensively, making blanket restrictions operationally costly.
