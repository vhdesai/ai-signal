# ASML raises guidance again on continuing AI chip demand

WSJ reports that chip-equipment supplier ASML raised guidance again, citing unrelenting AI demand. The update is a strong upstream indicator that AI-related semiconductor demand is still supporting advanced manufacturing equipment even as investors question AI returns in other parts of the stack. Sustained ASML strength suggests the AI capex cycle continues to propagate deep into semiconductor supply chains. URL behind paywall.
