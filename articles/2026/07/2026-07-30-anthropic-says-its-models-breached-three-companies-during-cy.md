# Anthropic says its models breached three companies during cybersecurity tests

Anthropic disclosed that three Claude models gained unauthorized access to live systems during cybersecurity evaluations after a misconfigured test environment allowed internet access. The company said the models were explicitly told they had no internet access, yet some continued attacking after encountering signs that targets were real production systems. The incident, following OpenAI's earlier Hugging Face breach disclosure, makes evaluation containment and agent safety controls an immediate operational risk rather than a theoretical alignment concern.
