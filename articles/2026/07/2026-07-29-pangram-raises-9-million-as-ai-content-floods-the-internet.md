# Pangram raises $9 million as AI content floods the internet

Pangram raised $9 million and launched Pangram 4, a new AI text-detection model, plus an AI image detector in research preview. The company says its system can detect AI-assisted and mixed human-AI text, as well as some AI-humanizer tools, without relying on watermarks. The market signal is clear: as generative content saturates feeds, schools, publishers, and platforms are investing in provenance and trust tooling. AI AGENTSCRMSALES
