# Meta's free cash flow craters 91% as Zuckerberg doubles down on AI spend

Meta's Q2 2026 free cash flow collapsed 91% year over year to $784M (from $8.55B), sending shares down ~10% after hours as its AI infrastructure buildout outpaced returns. Management narrowed its annual capex forecast upward, echoing Alphabet's first-ever cash-flow-negative quarter a week earlier. On the call, Mark Zuckerberg doubled down — predicting billions of people will use personal AI agents within five years and framing enterprise AI as an opportunity that extends beyond agents.
