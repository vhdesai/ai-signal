# Nvidia briefly cedes largest-US-company crown to Apple in an AI-chip sell-off

Nvidia fell roughly 5%, allowing Apple to reclaim the top US market-cap spot amid a broad AI-semiconductor sell-off. The decline was driven by concerns over AI-capex financing and competition from cheaper Chinese open-weight models. The swing highlights how sensitive megacap valuations have become to the economics of the AI buildout.
