# Meta says enterprise AI opportunity extends beyond agents

Zuckerberg also told investors that Meta sees a broader enterprise AI opportunity across APIs, business agents, compute sales, and internal productivity tools it may expose to customers. Meta initially plans to serve advertisers and small businesses through AI agents in messaging apps, but it also sees opportunities in larger enterprise customers.
