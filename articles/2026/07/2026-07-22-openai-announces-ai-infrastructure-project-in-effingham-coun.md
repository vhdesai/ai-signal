# OpenAI announces AI infrastructure project in Effingham County, Georgia

OpenAI announced Project Camellia in Effingham County, Georgia, emphasizing responsible energy, community investment, jobs, and access to Codex. The post signals OpenAI's continued move into physical infrastructure and community-facing data-center development. For executives, the issue is that AI capacity is now a local permitting, grid, labor, and community-relations challenge as much as a cloud procurement challenge.
