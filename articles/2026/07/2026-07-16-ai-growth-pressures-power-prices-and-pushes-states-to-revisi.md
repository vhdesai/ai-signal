# AI growth pressures power prices and pushes states to revisit water use

Axios reports that AI continues to pressure power prices and is driving states to rethink data-center water use. The stories follow New York's data-center moratorium and show infrastructure concerns broadening from grid interconnection to consumer power costs, water access, and local control. AI capacity planning is now inseparable from energy economics and state-level permitting politics.
