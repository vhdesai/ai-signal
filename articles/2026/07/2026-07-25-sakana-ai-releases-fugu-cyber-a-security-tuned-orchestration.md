# Sakana AI releases Fugu-Cyber, a security-tuned orchestration model

Sakana AI released Fugu-Cyber, a security-tuned orchestration model built on its Fugu system. It reports 86.9% on UC Berkeley’s CyberGym benchmark (1,507 vulnerabilities across 188 OSS-Fuzz projects) and 72.1% on Microsoft’s CTI-REALM, edging past GPT-5.5-Cyber and Claude’s “Mythos Preview.” Access is gated behind a defensive-use policy, and the scores are self-reported and not yet independently verified.
