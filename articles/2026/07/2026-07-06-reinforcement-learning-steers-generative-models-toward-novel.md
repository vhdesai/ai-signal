# Reinforcement Learning Steers Generative Models Toward Novel, Stable Crystals

Hyunsoo Park and Aron Walsh (Imperial College London) introduce a reinforcement-learning framework that guides latent diffusion models toward crystalline materials that are simultaneously thermodynamically stable, diverse, and genuinely novel. The method pairs group-relative policy optimization with verifiable multi-objective rewards. It is a concrete example of RL-for-science advancing inverse materials design toward targets like better battery cathodes and thermoelectrics.
