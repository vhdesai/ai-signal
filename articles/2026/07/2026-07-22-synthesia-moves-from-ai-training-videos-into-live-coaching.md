# Synthesia moves from AI training videos into live coaching

TechCrunch reports that Synthesia launched Roleplay Sessions, an enterprise training product where employees practice conversations with AI avatars that respond, push back, score performance, and generate analytics. The shift is important because Synthesia is moving from content generation toward measurable behavioral training outcomes. This is a broader enterprise AI pattern: vendors are trying to prove ROI with performance data rather than relying on faster content creation alone.
