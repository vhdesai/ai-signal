# AI-Chip Startup Etched Reportedly in Talks for a ~$20B Valuation

Etched, which builds transformer-specific inference silicon, is reportedly in talks at ~$20B alongside a separate ~$10B Sequoia-led round — up from ~$5B six months ago and still ahead of meaningful commercial revenue. Emblematic of how far ahead of fundamentals AI-infrastructure valuations have run. The bet: hardwiring the dominant architecture into chips can beat general-purpose GPUs on cost-per-token.
