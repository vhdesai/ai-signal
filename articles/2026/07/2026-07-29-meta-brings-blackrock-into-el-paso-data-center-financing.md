# Meta brings BlackRock into El Paso data-center financing

Meta and BlackRock announced a financing partnership for Meta’s El Paso data-center campus, with BlackRock-managed funds taking an 80% stake and Meta retaining 20%. The deal shows hyperscalers experimenting with asset-light structures to fund AI capacity without carrying every dollar of infrastructure spend on their own balance sheets. It also points to a growing role for private capital in AI infrastructure buildouts.
