# OpenAI Cuts GPT-5.6 Prices: Luna Down 80%, Adds Sol Fast Mode

OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 API pricing only weeks after launch, slashing Luna by 80% and Terra by 20% while adding a faster Sol tier. The company said kernel optimizations and speculative-decoding improvements helped lower operating costs enough to pass savings through. Those price drops also flow into ChatGPT Work and Codex, directly affecting how businesses experience AI coding and productivity bills. The move sharpens the foundation-model price war just as enterprises become more vocal about usage-based cost escalation. Anthropic, Google, and Meta now face more pressure to compete on price-per-intelligence, not just benchmark performance. Links: OpenAI · CNBC
