# Palantir faces growing European backlash over dependence

European governments are reportedly shifting toward domestic intelligence platforms amid unease over reliance on Palantir. The trend reflects a sovereignty-driven reassessment of foreign AI and data infrastructure. It represents a procurement risk for US vendors serving public-sector customers in Europe.
