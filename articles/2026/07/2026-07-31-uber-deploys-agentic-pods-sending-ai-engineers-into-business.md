# Uber Deploys "Agentic Pods" — Sending AI Engineers Into Business Units to Redesign Workflows

Uber is embedding top AI engineers inside functions like finance, legal, HR, and support to study work directly before building tools. These "Agentic Pods" shadow employees, map each workflow, and then prototype software changes in tight two-week rotations. The results are dramatic, with some finance tasks falling from 15 hours to 30 minutes and reporting cycles dropping from days to minutes. Uber's leaders say the biggest wins come not from speeding up one task, but from redesigning entire processes around AI-native execution. The approach echoes forward-deployed engineering, except Uber is using it internally to rewire the company rather than to serve customers. Links: Business Insider
