# SEMICONDUCTORS EARNINGS AI SUPPLY CHAIN

Axios reports that memory-chip companies are expected to post large sales gains as earnings season begins. The relevance is the continued pull-through from AI servers into high-bandwidth memory and adjacent semiconductor supply chains. Strong memory results would support the view that AI infrastructure spending is broadening beyond GPUs into the full compute stack.
