# Could Apple's Lawsuit Derail OpenAI's Hardware Plans?

TechCrunch's Equity examined whether Apple's trade-secret suit could jeopardize OpenAI's planned move into consumer hardware and its longer-term IPO ambitions. The dispute adds legal and strategic risk to OpenAI's device roadmap, reportedly tied to its Jony Ive collaboration. Raises questions about platform dependencies and IP exposure across the AI-hardware stack.
