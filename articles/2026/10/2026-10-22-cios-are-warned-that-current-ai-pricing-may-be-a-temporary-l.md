# CIOs are warned that current AI pricing may be a temporary low

CIO Dive reported Gartner's forecast of rising 2027 IT spending and highlighted warnings that subsidized AI pricing conditions may not last. The article points to infrastructure expansion and memory cost pressure as likely drivers of future cost normalization. The implication is to make architecture and procurement decisions before pricing resets erode optionality.
