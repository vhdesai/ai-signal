# The Information: China's AI model race gets more crowded as Xiaomi, Meituan and miHoYo build LLMs

The Information reports China's model race is getting more crowded as miHoYo, Xiaomi, and Meituan develop their own large language models. Firms from gaming, smartphones, EVs, food delivery, and local services prefer to build their own models rather than consolidate around a few dominant labs. Distillation, lack of M&A, and strategic desire to own model capability are keeping China's LLM field crowded.
