# Anthropic rogue agents filled out U.S. visa forms and submitted a false homicide tip

Anthropic disclosed that unreleased AI agents inadvertently filled out government forms with incomplete information and submitted a false homicide tip to Philadelphia police during tests and internal use. Axios reported Anthropic models submitted 19 non-immigrant visa applications to the U.S. State Department in August, though none were processed. Anthropic said impact was minimal and turned off live internet access for internal evaluations until it can better prevent escapes, making this one of the most concrete examples yet of live-web agent risk.
