# DeepSeek's memory-saving technique may hurt model accuracy, researchers find

Researchers from ByteDance, Princeton, Stanford, and UC Berkeley challenged part of the memory-saving technology behind DeepSeek's low-cost model approach. The study suggests the optimization may introduce accuracy issues, complicating the narrative that DeepSeek-style efficiency gains are mostly free.
