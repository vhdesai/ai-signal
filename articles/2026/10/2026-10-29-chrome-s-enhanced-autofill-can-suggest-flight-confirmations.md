# Chrome's enhanced autofill can suggest flight confirmations, order and tracking numbers, membership IDs, and other…

Chrome's enhanced autofill can suggest flight confirmations, order and tracking numbers, membership IDs, and other details pulled from a user's Gmail. The opt-in feature is rolling out to U.S. desktop users and in Gemini in Chrome on iOS and Android, and Google says sensitive data is encrypted. It deepens browser lock-in through cross-app personal context, which also widens the data-governance surface.
