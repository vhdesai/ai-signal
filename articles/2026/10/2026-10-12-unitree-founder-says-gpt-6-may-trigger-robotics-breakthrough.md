# Unitree founder says GPT-6 may trigger robotics breakthrough as soon as next year

Unitree founder Wang Xingxing said OpenAI's GPT-6 has raised expectations that robotics could see a major breakthrough within one to two years, potentially with GPT-7 directly accelerating humanoid capabilities. The claim reflects a broader shift: frontier models are increasingly seen as planning and reasoning engines that can plug into modular control stacks. Academic Research RESEARCH
