# Automation Anywhere acquires Boost.ai, connecting conversational AI to workflow execution

Automation Anywhere agreed to acquire Boost.ai, connecting enterprise conversational AI directly to robotic process automation and workflow execution. The premise is that chat interfaces alone are insufficient; assistants need toolchains, governance and operational integrations. The deal is part of a broader consolidation wave around agentic process automation. NEW AGENT COMMERCE
