# GPU access becomes a fragmented market across hyperscalers, neoclouds and direct suppliers

CNBC reported that companies seeking Nvidia GPUs now navigate a growing supplier field spanning hyperscalers, neoclouds, marketplaces and direct hardware purchases. The report cites SemiAnalysis data showing 323 Nvidia GPU providers as of September, up from 209 less than 11 months earlier.
