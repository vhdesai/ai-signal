# Microsoft reported record revenue of $92.3 billion (+19%) and net income of $36.6 billion, with Azure at $33.2 billion,…

Microsoft reported record revenue of $92.3 billion (+19%) and net income of $36.6 billion, with Azure at $33.2 billion, about $850 million above consensus and ahead of Google Cloud's $30.7 billion. Commercial remaining performance obligation rose 73% to $677 billion, and quarterly capex hit a record $45.2 billion. The dollar disclosure enables direct comparison with AWS, which reports Thursday, as hyperscaler capex and backlog continue to scale together.
