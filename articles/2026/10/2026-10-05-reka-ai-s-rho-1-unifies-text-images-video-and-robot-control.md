# Reka AI's Rho-1 unifies text, images, video, and robot control in one 19B model

Reka introduced Rho-1, a 19B-parameter omni-model that processes and generates text, images, video, and robot-control actions through one shared token stream. It was trained on 320 H100s over roughly three months, well below frontier-lab training footprints. https://the-decoder.com/reka-ais-omni-model-rho-1-handles-text-images-video-and-robot-control-in-a-single-model/
