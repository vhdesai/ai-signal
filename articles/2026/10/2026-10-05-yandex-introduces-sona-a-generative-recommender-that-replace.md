# Yandex introduces Sona, a generative recommender that replaces the recommendation cascade

Yandex introduced Sona, a generative recommender that replaces candidate generation, pre-ranking, and ranking with a single served transformer. In a seven-day production A/B test on Yandex smart speakers, the model reportedly improved active users by 4.53%, listening time by 6.30%, likes by 11.42%, and repeat commands by 17.99%. The release shows generative architectures entering core recommender infrastructure, not just content creation. Products & Tools ADVERTISINGMONETIZATION
