# Anthropic's financing stack shows strain: an expected junk rating and an Nvidia backstop

Citing the Financial Times, TechCrunch reports that Blackstone, anchor investor in a $63 billion Anthropic chip-financing deal, now expects a BB-range rating after the IPO rather than investment grade before 2027; Anthropic and Blackstone declined to comment. Separately, Nvidia is discussing a residual value guarantee on construction financing for Nscale's West Virginia campus, where Anthropic has a $44.6 billion contract for the first 460-megawatt building. Higher borrowing costs and vendor backstops concentrate counterparty risk around a few frontier labs and their suppliers.
