# Meta built a humanoid prototype with China's Fourier as its robotics push accelerates

The Information reports that Meta and Chinese robotics company Fourier built a roughly 106-pound humanoid prototype, known internally as Max, in three months, and are already working on a second version. Meta sees humanoids as the physical embodiment of its Muse AI agent for household tasks, though no launch target has been set. The partnership adds supply-chain and policy risk to Meta's embodied-AI ambitions.
