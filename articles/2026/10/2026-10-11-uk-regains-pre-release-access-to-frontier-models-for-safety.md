# UK regains pre-release access to frontier models for safety testing

The Telegraph reported that the UK regained pre-release access to models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google DeepMind after a White House reversal. This strengthens external testing access but should not be mistaken for a safety endorsement. The development matters because governments are trying to regain visibility before frontier capabilities reach broad deployment. URL(s): The Telegraph
