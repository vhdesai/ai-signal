# China struggles to recruit foreign AI researchers in race with the US

The New York Times reports China is struggling to recruit foreign AI researchers despite heavy investment in frontier labs, chips and university pipelines. The finding complicates recent narratives that China’s AI talent pipeline may eclipse the US: domestic scale is strong, but international recruitment remains difficult. Talent remains one of the few AI inputs that cannot be solved purely with capital or compute.
