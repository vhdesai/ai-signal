# Alibaba Launches the Zhenwu V900 AI Chip; Huawei Debuts Grid-Interactive AIDC 1.0; CoreWeave Adds the Nvidia Vera CPU

Three concurrent capacity moves: Alibaba formally launched the Zhenwu V900 — its most advanced in-house AI chip — for internal cloud workloads and external customers, slotting next to the Amazon–Synopsys and Broadcom–Anthropic financings as further diversification beyond Nvidia. Huawei introduced Grid-Interactive AIDC 1.0, a reference stack integrating AI data-center power, cooling, and Ascend compute into one coordinated plane (demand response as a competitive feature). CoreWeave added the standalone Nvidia Vera CPU to its portfolio a day after announcing Vera Rubin NVL72 GA for Cognition — a full-stack Nvidia-aligned compute menu against hyperscaler alternatives. The silicon customer base is diversifying structurally in a single news cycle.
