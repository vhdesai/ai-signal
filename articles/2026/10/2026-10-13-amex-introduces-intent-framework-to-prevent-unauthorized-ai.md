# Amex introduces intent framework to prevent unauthorized AI-agent purchases

American Express introduced an Intent Intelligence Framework that captures and authenticates a customer's purchase intent before an AI agent completes a transaction. The system creates an Intent ID and proof-of-intent token so merchants and payment systems can verify constraints such as merchant, budget and preferences before checkout. It is one of the first serious payment-infrastructure answers to agentic commerce risk.
