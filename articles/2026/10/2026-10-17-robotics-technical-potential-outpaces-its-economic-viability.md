# Robotics’ technical potential outpaces its economic viability

The Next Web covered Anthropic research suggesting robots and AI could technically reach a large share of work, but rarely cheaply enough to be economically viable today. The distinction matters: automation capability and automation ROI remain separate executive decisions. Robotics deployments still need careful labor-cost, safety, maintenance and integration modeling before they justify broad substitution claims.
