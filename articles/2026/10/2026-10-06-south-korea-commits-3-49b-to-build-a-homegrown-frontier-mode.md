# South Korea commits $3.49B to build a homegrown frontier model

South Korea plans government-backed equity investments of 4.7 trillion won ($3.49B) to develop a domestic frontier model capable of competing with China's best open-weight systems. The move follows Beijing's 2030 intelligent-compute targets and comes as US export restrictions make sovereign capability a first-order policy goal across Asia. https://the-decoder.com/south-korea-bets-3-49-billion-on-building-a-homegrown-frontier-ai-model-to-rival-chinas-best/
