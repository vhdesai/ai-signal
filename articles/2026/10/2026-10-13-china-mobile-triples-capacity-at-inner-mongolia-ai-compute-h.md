# China Mobile triples capacity at Inner Mongolia AI compute hub

China Mobile tripled capacity at its Hohhot data center in Inner Mongolia to 20,100 petaflops, making it a central asset in Beijing's AI compute build-out. National AI capacity is increasingly measured in regional compute campuses, not only chip access.
