# Microsoft reports AI diagnostic gains on complex cases in Science-backed evaluation

TechCrunch reports Microsoft's multi-agent diagnostic orchestration outperformed generalist physicians in a simulation benchmark published in Science, while noting gains varied by case type and base model. The reported improvements were strongest on complex cases, with more mixed results in emergency-department scenarios. The work adds to a growing pattern of frontier labs moving from benchmark claims toward clinical evaluation pathways.
