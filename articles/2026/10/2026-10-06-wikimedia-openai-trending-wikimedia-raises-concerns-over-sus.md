# Wikimedia / OpenAI Trending Wikimedia raises concerns over suspected OpenAI agent activity

Wikimedia attributes unauthorized edits and heavy traffic to suspected OpenAI agents. It says requests may have contributed to a partial outage in May. Primary statement: Wikimedia Foundation Sources scanned and coverage notes Official and research sources: OpenAI News/Blog, Google DeepMind Blog and Google Keyword, Meta AI (research-site fallback), BAIR Blog, Apple Machine Learning Research, Nvidia, Anthropic, Mistral, Microsoft, IBM, Palantir, Apple, xAI, Qwen, AWS documentation, Huawei, Qualcomm, Tencent, Databricks, Cursor, Replit, Oracle, DeepSeek and Cerebras investor news.
