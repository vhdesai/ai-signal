# DeepSeek is helping shape Huawei's future AI processors

The Information reports that Huawei engineers stationed at DeepSeek are incorporating the AI lab's requirements into future chip designs. The collaboration aims to make Huawei hardware more suitable for frontier-model development without Nvidia and illustrates the value of co-designing models and accelerators. The account relies on people familiar with the work; it is not evidence of demonstrated performance parity with Nvidia.
