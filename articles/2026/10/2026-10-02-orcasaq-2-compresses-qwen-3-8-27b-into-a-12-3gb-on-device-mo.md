# OrcaSAQ-2 compresses Qwen 3.8 27B into a 12.3GB on-device model

Geeky Gadgets covers OrcaSAQ-2, a quantization/pruning pass that shrinks the 27B-parameter Qwen 3.8 model to a 12.3GB on-device footprint while preserving most capability. It's the clearest signal yet that frontier-class capability on consumer laptops is practical this quarter — slotting next to today's Digital Today piece arguing on-device AI is ready to shake up the frontier-dominated market.
