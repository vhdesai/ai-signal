# CrowdStrike details AI-driven espionage scaled across 200-plus organizations

CrowdStrike reports the VAPOR PANDA group used an AI-driven framework, ARTEX, reportedly powered by DeepSeek, to target more than 200 organizations, with nine confirmed post-exploitation cases. Human operators still directed the campaign, so AI amplified reach rather than replacing tradecraft. AI is lowering the marginal cost of targeting and shrinking the window defenders have to respond.
