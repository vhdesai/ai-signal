# Anthropic's internal Claude usage now exceeds its payroll at retail prices

Business Insider highlights an S-1 disclosure that, since April, the retail value of Claude used internally at Anthropic has exceeded monthly employee compensation excluding stock awards, with internal usage up more than 250-fold between January and September. The figure reflects list pricing rather than cash spend. It frames AI tokens as a fourth component of workforce cost alongside salary, bonus and equity.
