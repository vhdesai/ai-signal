# Yandex introduces Sona, a generative recommender replacing multi-stage recommendation stacks

Yandex introduced Sona, a generative recommender that replaces candidate generation, pre-ranking, and ranking with a single served transformer. In a seven-day smart-speaker production test, Yandex reported gains across active users, listening time, likes, repeat commands, and deeply engaged users. The release shows generative architectures entering core recommender infrastructure, not just content creation. Products & Tools ADVERTISINGMONETIZATION
