# Tenstorrent raises $1.5 billion at a $6.2 billion valuation

The AI-chip company raised $1.5 billion to continue scaling its open-weight AI-chip business, reaching a reported $6.2 billion valuation. The financing gives another Nvidia challenger capital to pursue an alternative hardware and software stack, but valuation and funding alone do not establish customer adoption or production-scale competitiveness.
