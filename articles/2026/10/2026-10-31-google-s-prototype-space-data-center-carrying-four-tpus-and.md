# Google's prototype space data center, carrying four TPUs and generating up to 1 kW, completed commissioning and ran…

Google's prototype space data center, carrying four TPUs and generating up to 1 kW, completed commissioning and ran Gemma 4 inference from orbit. Power limits allow a TPU to run only about 20 minutes at a time, and the campaign will test thermal stress and radiation-induced bit flips. Two follow-on satellites with inter-satellite optical links are planned for 2027, but economic viability depends on very high Starship launch cadence.
