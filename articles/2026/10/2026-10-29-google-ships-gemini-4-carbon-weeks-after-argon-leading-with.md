# Google ships Gemini 4 Carbon weeks after Argon, leading with cyber-defense capability

Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon less than a month after Gemini 4 Argon, at the same introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, which The Information reports will double after the teaser period. Google says Carbon ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 and leads SEC-bench Pro (88.2%), ExploitGym (54.8%) and CWE-bench (71.0%). Access starts with a subset of vetted cyber defenders before widening through the Fairwind program, alongside voluntary U.S. pre-release testing. The cadence puts Google back in the frontier race, while the gated rollout shows offensive-security capability now shapes launch policy.
