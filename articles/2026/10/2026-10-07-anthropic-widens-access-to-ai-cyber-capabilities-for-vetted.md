# Anthropic widens access to AI cyber capabilities for vetted security teams

Anthropic introduced a three-tier cyber verification program that expands Claude access for vetted security teams. The move is meant to give defenders more capability without opening unrestricted offensive access, and it lands one day after Google's cyber-gated Gemini 4 Argon narrative. The pattern is clear: frontier cyber models are moving toward credentialed access regimes rather than broad public release. HOT CONSUMER AI
