# Amazon reportedly discusses a roughly $7B Decart acquisition

Reuters relayed WSJ reporting that Amazon is in talks to acquire AI startup Decart for around $7 billion. The report remains unconfirmed, but the strategic direction is plausible: Amazon would be adding more application-layer and generation capability around its infrastructure business. If completed, the deal would signal that hyperscalers are still willing to buy scarce AI product and model talent at premium prices. URL(s): Reuters via MarketScreener
