# DeepSeek releases V4.1-Flash-1017 without raising API prices

DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash-1017 with stronger benchmark scores while keeping API prices flat. The free MIT-licensed update strengthens DeepSeek's position as the low-cost benchmark for coding, agentic retrieval and general assistant use. It also arrives as Washington escalates restrictions on DeepSeek in government systems, highlighting the split between technical adoption and policy risk.
