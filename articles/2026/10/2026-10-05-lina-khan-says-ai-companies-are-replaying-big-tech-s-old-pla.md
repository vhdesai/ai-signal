# Lina Khan says AI companies are replaying Big Tech's old platform playbook

Business Insider reported that former FTC Chair Lina Khan warned AI companies are following the same playbook that previously got Big Tech into trouble. The critique centers on market power, vertical integration, and efforts to shape the rules while platforms are still forming. For executives, the regulatory risk is that AI market structure could attract antitrust scrutiny before business models fully stabilize.
