# OpenAI and Microsoft give publishers separate controls for AI training and grounding

Microsoft is adopting the shared IETF-drafted AI Training preference in robots.txt, extending it to the page level, and adding a separate AI Grounding preference, including a new DisallowExtended option that limits grounding to Microsoft-operated experiences such as Copilot and Microsoft 365. The new preferences replace Microsoft's NoArchive and NoCache tags for these uses, and OpenAI separately outlined plans to honor shared training opt-outs. Splitting training from grounding gives content owners finer leverage over AI use of their work and will shape content-licensing negotiations.
