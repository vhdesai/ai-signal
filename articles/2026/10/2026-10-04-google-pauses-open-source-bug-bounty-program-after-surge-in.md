# Google pauses open-source bug bounty program after surge in AI-generated submissions

Google paused its Open Source Software Vulnerability Rewards Program until next year, citing a significant rise in automated AI submissions, most of which were invalid. TechCrunch noted prior warnings that AI-generated vulnerability reports could overwhelm bug bounty programs with hallucinated or low-quality findings. The lesson for security teams is that AI can increase defensive surface area by flooding triage pipelines, not just by improving exploit discovery.
