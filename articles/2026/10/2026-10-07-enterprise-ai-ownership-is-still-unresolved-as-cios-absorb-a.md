# Enterprise AI ownership is still unresolved as CIOs absorb accountability

CIO Dive reported that there is no dominant model for who controls enterprise AI, even as CIOs increasingly feel responsible for mistakes. A companion CIO Dive item noted that banks are accelerating responsible-AI guardrails as adoption expands. Together, the stories point to a maturing governance pattern: enterprise AI ownership is moving from innovation teams into accountable operating functions, especially where model decisions touch customers, risk, or compliance.
