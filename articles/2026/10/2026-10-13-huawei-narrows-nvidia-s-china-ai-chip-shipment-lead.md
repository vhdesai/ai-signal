# Huawei narrows Nvidia’s China AI-chip shipment lead

Reuters reported IDC estimates showing Nvidia with 34% of China accelerator shipments in the first half of 2026 versus Huawei at 28%. The numbers point to a more competitive domestic chip market, but shipment share is not the same as performance parity or software ecosystem maturity. AI VALUATION CAPITAL
