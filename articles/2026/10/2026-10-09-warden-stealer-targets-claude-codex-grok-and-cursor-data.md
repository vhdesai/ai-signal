# Warden Stealer targets Claude, Codex, Grok and Cursor data

GBHackers reports Warden Stealer malware is targeting Claude, Codex, Grok, and Cursor to steal AI-agent data. Attackers are now focusing on credentials, local context, prompts, and tool permissions inside agentic developer environments. As AI coding agents become default engineering infrastructure, their local state is becoming a high-value target.
