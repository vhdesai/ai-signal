# INDUSTRY Anthropic commits 5% of shares to public-benefit fund

Anthropic said it will commit 5% of its shares to a public-benefit fund as it heads toward a planned IPO. The fund may study direct payments to workers at risk from AI-driven displacement, but remains under company control. https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/anthropic-says-its-committing-5-of-its-shares-to-a-public-benefit-fund/
