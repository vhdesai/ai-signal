# CIOs are being warned that AI may never be cheaper than it is now

CIO Dive reports enterprise leaders are preparing for memory-cost pressure in 2027 even as global IT spending rises. The strategic message is that organizations should treat today's cost curve as a temporary window and accelerate architecture, procurement, and governance decisions before infrastructure prices tighten further.
