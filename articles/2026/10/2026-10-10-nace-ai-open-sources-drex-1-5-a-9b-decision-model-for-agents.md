# Nace.AI open-sources Drex 1.5, a 9B decision model for agents and backend workflows

Nace.AI released Drex 1.5 as an open-weight 9B decision model that scores fixed answer options rather than generating text. The model targets agent control and backend decision workflows, with MarkTechPost reporting support for CUDA, llama.cpp, Ollama, and hosted OpenRouter deployment. The important competitive angle is that open-weight decision models are moving quickly toward the latency and calibration territory that closed providers are also targeting. A MODEL ALIBABA
