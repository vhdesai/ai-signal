# Mathematicians call for OpenAI boycott after AI-generated proof flood

The Association for Human Mathematics is calling for an OpenAI boycott after the company released hundreds of AI-generated math manuscripts and later retracted three over a sign error. The dispute has shifted from whether AI can produce formal artifacts to whether bulk release practices respect scholarly norms.
