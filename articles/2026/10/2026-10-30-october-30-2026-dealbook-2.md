# [October 30, 2026] · [DealBook]

Nvidia is in talks to give Nscale billions of dollars in credit guarantees for a 460-megawatt data center Anthropic plans to use, part of a $45 billion deal; terms are unsigned. Microsoft priced $25 billion of bonds, while DealBook tallies SpaceX's $40 billion GPU debt, a $63 billion Broadcom-backed chip purchase for Anthropic and up to $97.5 billion of Meta equity in the same week. Credit is flowing to the largest, most proven names, concentrating exposure around a few frontier labs and suppliers.
