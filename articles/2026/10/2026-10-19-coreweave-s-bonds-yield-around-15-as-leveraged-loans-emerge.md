# CoreWeave's bonds yield around 15% as leveraged loans emerge for GPU-rich AI borrowers

PitchBook reports CoreWeave bonds are yielding around 15% as investors question how neoclouds will service debt. GPU-rich borrowers are increasingly turning to leveraged loans, using chips as collateral to reassure lenders. The shift marks another phase in AI infrastructure financing, where GPUs are treated less like normal servers and more like financeable hard assets.
