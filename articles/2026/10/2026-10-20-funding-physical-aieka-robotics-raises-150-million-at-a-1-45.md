# FUNDING PHYSICAL AIEka Robotics raises $150 million at a $1.45 billion valuation

Eka Robotics raised a $150 million Series A at a $1.45 billion post-money valuation for a manipulation platform pairing a force-sensitive gripper with a deep-learning model. TechCrunch reports the system can adapt to different objects without retraining, a significant bet on generalizable physical AI rather than proof of broad deployment.
