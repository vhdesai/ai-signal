# GOVERNANCEREPORTED PROPOSALSAltman outlines competing endgames for governing frontier AI

The Information reports that Sam Altman described three industry proposals for safe AI development: an international safety body, consolidation of AI labs, or one firm dominating available compute. Sources said he appeared to support an international organization modeled on the International Atomic Energy Agency. These were reported discussions, not an adopted governance framework or an announcement that the labs will merge.
