# OpenAI acquires Orchestra Bio to deepen life-sciences operations

TechCrunch reports OpenAI acquired Orchestra Bio, a startup focused on AI agents for biotech operations such as trial logistics and research workflows. OpenAI said the team and technology will be integrated into Rosalind and Rosalind Workbench, extending from foundation models into domain software execution. The deal reinforces a verticalization trend in which frontier-model providers move up-stack into regulated industry workflows.
