# AI cost governance becomes a product as Anthropic ships spend reporting and banks right-size models

Anthropic launched Claude Enterprise reports, employee surveys and pooled budgets that show what each team produced, what it cost and where workflows broke down; it says the data will not train models by default. TechCrunch frames the launch against Anthropic's disclosed 900% net revenue retention. CIO Dive reports smaller models are helping Bank of America and Capital One manage AI costs, with executives arguing that limiting excessive frontier-model use makes financial sense. As AI budgets scale, the buyer's question is shifting from capability to cost per outcome.
