# China's open-weight models are winning global users — but third parties may capture the value

SCMP reports that Chinese open-weight models from DeepSeek, Moonshot, Alibaba, and peers are gaining global adoption, but much of the revenue accrues to third-party hosting, orchestration, and API providers rather than the model creators. The implication is that AI value is moving toward deployment infrastructure, routing, and vertical packaging. https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3370012/chinas-open-weight-ai-models-are-winning-global-users-who-capturing-value INDUSTRY
