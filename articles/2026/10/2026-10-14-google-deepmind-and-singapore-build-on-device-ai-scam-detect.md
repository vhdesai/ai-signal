# Google DeepMind and Singapore build on-device AI scam detection for private chats

Google DeepMind and GovTech Singapore announced a fine-tuned, lightweight Gemma-based model that detects conversational scams in private chats on-device, with no cloud data egress. If it works at scale, it could become a template for privacy-preserving safety filters in banking, marketplaces, and consumer messaging apps. PRODUCT
