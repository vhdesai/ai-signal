# Cyber chiefs say autonomous AI defenders are not ready for prime time

WSJ Pro CyberSecurity reported that cyber leaders remain skeptical that autonomous AI defenders are ready for broad deployment. The report fits the broader pattern from Anthropic’s incident disclosure: AI can accelerate security workflows, but unsupervised autonomy remains risky where systems can touch real infrastructure. The practical near-term architecture is likely human-supervised triage, evidence collection and remediation recommendation, not fully autonomous defense. GOVERNANCE PRODUCTIVITY
