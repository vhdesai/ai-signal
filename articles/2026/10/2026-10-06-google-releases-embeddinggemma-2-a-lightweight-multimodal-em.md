# Google releases EmbeddingGemma 2, a lightweight multimodal embedding model

Google released EmbeddingGemma 2, an open 740M-parameter model that converts text, images, video, audio, and code into vectors. It runs on-device, needs about 191MB of RAM, and enables offline RAG applications that do not send enterprise data to external servers. https://deepmind.google/blog/embeddinggemma-2-an-open-lightweight-multimodal-embedding-model/
