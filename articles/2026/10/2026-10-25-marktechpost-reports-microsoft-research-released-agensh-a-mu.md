# MarkTechPost reports Microsoft Research released Agensh, a multi-agent harness designed to scale coding teams up to…

MarkTechPost reports Microsoft Research released Agensh, a multi-agent harness designed to scale coding teams up to 1,024 agents without a central orchestrator. The framework emphasizes shared state, peer coordination, and asynchronous task ownership over top-down control. This architecture is strategically relevant for organizations planning high-throughput agent engineering at repository scale.
