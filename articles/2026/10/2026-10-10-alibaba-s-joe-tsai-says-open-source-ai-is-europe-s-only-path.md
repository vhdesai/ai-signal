# Alibaba's Joe Tsai says open-source AI is Europe's only path to tech independence

Alibaba chairman Joe Tsai argued that Europe should embrace open-source AI as its only realistic path to technological independence, pointing to China's own open-source playbook under constraints. The message lands as European enterprises weigh Mistral, Gemma, Beam, Qwen, and DeepSeek against US proprietary systems.
