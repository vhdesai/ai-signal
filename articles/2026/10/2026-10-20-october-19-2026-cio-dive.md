# [October 19, 2026] · CIO Dive

CIO Dive's Gartner conference report says 86% of CIOs see AI risk growing faster than value, while AI spending is 18 times its level two years earlier. A related item in the October 20 newsletter reports that just 4% of technology leaders have plans to redesign roles for an agentic-human workforce. These survey findings point to an execution gap between buying AI and establishing the workflows, accountability and outcome measures needed to use it effectively.
