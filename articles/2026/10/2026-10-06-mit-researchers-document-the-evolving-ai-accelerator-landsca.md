# MIT researchers document the evolving AI accelerator landscape

MIT Lincoln Laboratory researchers published an update on the Lincoln AI Computing Survey, which now tracks more than 120 commercial AI accelerators across chips, cards, and systems. The work compares performance and power across CPUs, GPUs, ASICs, FPGAs, and dataflow accelerators using public sources. For technology leaders, this is useful because accelerator selection is becoming more fragmented as AI workloads diversify beyond a single GPU-centric pattern. DATA CENTERS SUPPLY CONSTRAINTS
