# Google brings agentic AI to Gemini for businesses

Google is turning Gemini into an enterprise agent that can plan, execute tasks, work across business systems, delegate to subagents, and operate with its own workplace identity and email address. The key CIO question becomes which agent identity and permission model can safely operate across enterprise systems. PRODUCT
