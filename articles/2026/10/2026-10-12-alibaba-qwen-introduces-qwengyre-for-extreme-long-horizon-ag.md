# Alibaba Qwen introduces QwenGyre for extreme-long-horizon agent training

Alibaba's Qwen team introduced QwenGyre, an elastic reinforcement-learning framework for training coding agents through long-running black-box harnesses. The framework targets rollouts lasting hours and approaching 1 million tokens, moving GPUs dynamically between rollout and training while converting branching agent histories into bounded training samples. For executives tracking agent capability, this is a concrete example of training infrastructure evolving specifically for autonomous, multi-step software work rather than short prompt-response tasks.
