# MIT researchers call for answerability behind AI-generated civic speech

MIT News interviewed Deb Roy of the MIT Center for Constructive Communication about accountability for AI-generated text in civic processes. Roy argues that AI-generated public comments and other civic speech can appear human while leaving no person meaningfully answerable for the words, weakening feedback channels that democratic institutions rely on. The executive takeaway is that provenance and human accountability are not abstract ethics issues; they are operational requirements for any AI system used in public participation, policy, or institutional communication.
