# Microsoft president says UK is best placed to drive AI regulation

Microsoft president Brad Smith called for urgent AI regulation and said the UK is well positioned to lead, arguing that milk and washing machines currently have more oversight than AI companies. The comments follow the White House reversal restoring UK AISI access to frontier models.
