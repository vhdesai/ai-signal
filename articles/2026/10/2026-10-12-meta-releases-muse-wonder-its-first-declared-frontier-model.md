# Meta releases Muse Wonder, its first declared frontier-model push

Meta's Superintelligence Lab released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, positioning them as Meta's first frontier-class models. TechCrunch reports that Meta claims benchmark comparability with Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra, with particular emphasis on coding and active memory for long-running personal-assistant use cases. The release matters because Meta is shifting from lightweight assistants and open-model ecosystem strategy toward direct competition in premium frontier inference, including API pricing comparable to high-end commercial models.
