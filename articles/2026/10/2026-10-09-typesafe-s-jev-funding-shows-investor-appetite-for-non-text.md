# TypeSafe’s Jev funding shows investor appetite for non-text decision models

TechCrunch reported that TypeSafe AI, maker of the non-text decision model Jev, raised $870 million at a $7.5 billion valuation weeks after launch. Jev is positioned to return calibrated decision scores rather than prose, making it part of a broader move toward models that can sit directly inside software control loops. The funding round suggests investors are underwriting decision infrastructure as a category adjacent to, and potentially more operationally valuable than, general chat. TALENT APPLE
