# House proposal would clarify liability rules for AI agents

CIO Dive reported on a House proposal intended to clarify liability rules for AI agents. The development reflects a shift from abstract AI ethics toward concrete responsibility for agent actions, errors and downstream harm. Agent deployments need explicit ownership, audit logs and escalation paths before regulation forces those controls externally. CYBER READINESS ENTERPRISE
