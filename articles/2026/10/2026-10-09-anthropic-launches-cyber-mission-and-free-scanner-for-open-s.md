# Anthropic launches Cyber Mission and free scanner for open-source projects

Anthropic launched Cyber Mission to protect critical infrastructure and open-source software, with partners including CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks. The initiative includes a free AI scanner for open-source projects that Anthropic expects to reach above 90% accuracy.
