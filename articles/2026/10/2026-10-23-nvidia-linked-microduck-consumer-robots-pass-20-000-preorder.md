# Nvidia-linked Microduck consumer robots pass 20,000 preorders

TechCrunch reports Microduck robot preorders exceeded 20,000 after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang featured the devices at GTC Berlin. The open-source robots are positioned as consumer-friendly entry points into programmable physical AI. It is an early signal that embodied-AI demand may be broadening beyond enterprise pilots.
