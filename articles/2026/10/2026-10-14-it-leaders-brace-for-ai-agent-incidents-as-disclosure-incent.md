# IT leaders brace for AI-agent incidents as disclosure incentives remain weak

CIO Dive reports IT executives are bracing for AI-agent security incidents, and a Workato report found 36% of leaders would try to quietly handle an agent-driven security incident without disclosing it. The finding lands amid broader debates over mandatory incident reporting, independent review and agent liability. It suggests governance failure may come not only from unsafe agents, but from organizations choosing opacity after incidents occur.
