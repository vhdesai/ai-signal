# AI-enabled attacks are accelerating and increasing pressure on internet defenses

The Wall Street Journal's cyber coverage highlights a rising tempo of AI-assisted attack activity and resulting strain on defensive operations. The shift is from isolated incidents to persistent operational pressure, which elevates readiness requirements for both product and infrastructure teams.
