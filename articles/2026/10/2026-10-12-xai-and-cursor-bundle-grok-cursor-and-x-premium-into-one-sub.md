# xAI and Cursor bundle Grok, Cursor and X Premium into one subscription

xAI introduced The X Subscription, bundling Grok, Cursor and X Premium, with shared Grok usage and separate billing for some third-party models. The move is less about subscription packaging than distribution: agent tools, coding assistants and consumer identity are converging into one commercial surface. BUSINESS CONTINUITY CHATBOTS
