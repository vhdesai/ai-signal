# ChatGPT adds visual advertising tests and expanded measurement

OpenAI will test labeled visual ads beside ChatGPT image generation in the US this month. Ads remain separate from generated images; measurement partnerships are expanding. Verification note: Official announcement; same-day TechCrunch coverage was published at 8:14 a.m. PDT. US testing is planned for later October.
