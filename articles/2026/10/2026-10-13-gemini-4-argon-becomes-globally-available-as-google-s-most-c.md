# Gemini 4 Argon becomes globally available as Google's most capable frontier model

Google made Gemini 4 Argon available globally through its API, Gemini app, and Google Antigravity. The model is priced aggressively and positioned for hard coding, cybersecurity, and reasoning workloads, though independent benchmarks still place it behind Claude Opus 5.5 and Meta's Muse Wonder Full in several areas.
