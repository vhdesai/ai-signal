# Inception launches Mercury 3 diffusion LLM, reporting over 1,000 tokens per second

Inception released Mercury 3, a diffusion-based language model that refines output in parallel rather than generating strictly token by token. The company reports 1,068 tokens per second, a one-million-token context window, and API pricing of $0.20 per million input tokens and $0.70 per million output tokens at standard rates. It is positioned for latency-sensitive agents, search, and voice applications; benchmark comparisons in the launch post are company-run and should be validated against workload-specific evaluations.
