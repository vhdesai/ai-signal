# Robotics and physical-AI funding accelerates sharply

Business Insider reports global funding momentum for robotics and physical AI has expanded significantly versus 2020 baselines. Capital intensity is rising fastest where hardware, dexterity, and deployment scale intersect, suggesting a bifurcation between software-only AI winners and operators that can execute in the physical world.
