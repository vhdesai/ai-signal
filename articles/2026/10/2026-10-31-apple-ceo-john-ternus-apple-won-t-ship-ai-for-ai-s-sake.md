# Apple CEO John Ternus: Apple won't "ship AI for AI's sake"

In a Wired interview summarized by TechCrunch, Apple CEO John Ternus said Apple will "absolutely" use AI in new products but wants it to feel seamless rather than marketed as a feature, and pushed back on the idea that AI agents will make apps unnecessary. The remarks confirm Apple's integrate-quietly strategy rather than a bid to compete on frontier models, even as agentic interfaces put pressure on app-store economics.
