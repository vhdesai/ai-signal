# Character.AI files for Chapter 11 after legal, regulatory, and monetization pressure

Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy only five years after launch, listing assets and liabilities between $50 million and $100 million. The company also agreed to sell some assets to Disney for $20 million and provide a non-exclusive perpetual technology license, while warning that the platform could wind down if no viable buyer emerges. The bankruptcy is a cautionary signal for consumer AI: high engagement can create high inference cost, legal exposure, and child-safety scrutiny faster than monetization can mature.
