# DHS order removes DeepSeek technology from federal civilian systems

TechCrunch reports the Department of Homeland Security order requiring federal civilian agencies to stop purchasing and remove DeepSeek products, including covered third-party offerings. Agencies can request exceptions in defined cases, but the baseline posture is explicit exclusion. The policy signal is that model provenance and jurisdictional exposure are now procurement-critical in government AI supply chains.
