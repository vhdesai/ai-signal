# Grok goes live in XChat; Tesla pushes staff toward Grok 4.5 under new AI-spending cap

Grok is now integrated directly into XChat ("Ask it anything"), and Tesla is reportedly nudging staff toward Grok 4.5 as a new AI-spending cap takes effect, per Stocktwits. Together the two moves deepen Musk-ecosystem lock-in — and make Grok the first frontier model that an auto-OEM is standardizing on company-wide, strengthening the enterprise/consumer-bundle thesis behind the $100/month Ultra plan.
