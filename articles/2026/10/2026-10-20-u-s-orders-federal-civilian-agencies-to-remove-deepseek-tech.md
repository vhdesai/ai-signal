# U.S. orders federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek technology

The U.S. ordered federal civilian agencies to stop purchasing DeepSeek products and remove them from government IT systems, including third-party products built using DeepSeek technology; agencies may seek exceptions. The order cites national-security and data-access concerns and extends restrictions beyond the intelligence systems covered by earlier action. ENTERPRISE SECURITY AGENTS
