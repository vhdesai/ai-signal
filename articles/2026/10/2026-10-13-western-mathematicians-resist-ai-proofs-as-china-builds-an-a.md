# Western mathematicians resist AI proofs as China builds an AI-for-math alliance

While many Western mathematicians warn that AI-generated proofs could damage the discipline's creative process, China is moving in the opposite direction. Fields Medalist Shing-Tung Yau has launched an alliance of eight institutions and more than 1,000 scientists to push AI into mathematical research.
