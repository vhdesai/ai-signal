# ChatGPT adds virtual try-on and a Favorites library for apparel shopping

OpenAI rolled out shopping features that let ChatGPT users virtually try on clothes and accessories using their own photos, with saved items landing in a Favorites library. Combined with the Sponsored Agents + Shopify integration and the ChatGPT Ads expansion to Southeast Asia and Taiwan, OpenAI is moving quickly to monetize agentic commerce — a move that reads as a direct response to Meta Muse's App-Store traction and Google's Flipkart Gemini pilot.
