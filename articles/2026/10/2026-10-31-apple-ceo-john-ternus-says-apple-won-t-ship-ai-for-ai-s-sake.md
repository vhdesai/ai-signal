# Apple CEO John Ternus says Apple won't ship AI for AI's sake

In a Wired interview summarized by TechCrunch, Apple CEO John Ternus said Apple will absolutely use AI in new products but wants it to feel seamless rather than marketed as a feature. He also pushed back on the idea that AI agents will make apps unnecessary. The remarks confirm Apple's integrate-quietly strategy rather than a bid to compete on frontier models.
