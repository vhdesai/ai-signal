# Anthropic and AWS formalize the forward-deployed-engineer push

Anthropic and AWS are expanding certification efforts for forward-deployed engineers, reflecting demand for talent that can translate frontier models into deployed enterprise systems. The move underscores a practical bottleneck: AI adoption is increasingly constrained by architecture, change management, and production integration rather than model access alone. For executives, this is a signal that the AI services layer around models may become as strategically important as the models themselves. URL(s): CIO Dive
