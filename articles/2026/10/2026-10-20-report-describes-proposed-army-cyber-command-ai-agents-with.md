# Report describes proposed Army Cyber Command AI agents with self-propagating behavior

The Decoder, citing internal documents, reports that the Army Cyber Command is developing ECHOTRIBBLE, a program involving agents designed to move across networks and “neutralize” targets with limited human oversight. The report raises risks of unintended spread and collateral effects, particularly where systems are isolated; it does not establish that the capability is operational. LEGAL AI SEARCH
