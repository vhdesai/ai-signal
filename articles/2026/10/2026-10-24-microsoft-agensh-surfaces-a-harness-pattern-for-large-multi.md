# Microsoft Agensh surfaces a harness pattern for large multi-agent orchestration

MarkTechPost reports on Microsoft Agensh as a harness-focused approach for coordinating large numbers of agents with clearer control boundaries. The design attention is on orchestration reliability and manageability when agent count scales sharply. This is consistent with the broader shift from single-agent demos to production multi-agent systems.
