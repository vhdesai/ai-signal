# Atlassian and OpenAI expand partnership to turn enterprise knowledge into action

Atlassian and OpenAI expanded their partnership around turning enterprise knowledge into actions. The announcement points toward tighter integration of frontier models with collaboration, project, and knowledge-management systems where enterprise context already lives. For executives, this reinforces the platform race: AI agents become more valuable when they can reason over company knowledge and then execute inside existing workflows.
