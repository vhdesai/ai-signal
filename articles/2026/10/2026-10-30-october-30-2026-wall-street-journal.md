# [October 30, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

Intel lost 29% after warning of disappointing fourth-quarter revenue and saying PC sales are tanking on soaring component costs, upending the AI trade, the Wall Street Journal reports. TechCrunch attributes the slump partly to AI build-outs absorbing memory and other components even as Intel's data-center revenue grew, and says the drop cut about $12 billion from the US government's stake. AI's claim on components is now visibly depressing adjacent hardware markets.
