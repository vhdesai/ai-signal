# OpenAI revenue confusion hits Oracle, CoreWeave and the AI chip stack

The Information and PitchBook report OpenAI's widely circulated annualized revenue number was overstated by about $20B: closer to $50B than $70B at the end of September. The revision rattled Oracle, CoreWeave, Nebius, Nvidia, and chip-stack names before some rebounded. The episode shows how dependent the AI-infrastructure trade has become on private-company revenue assumptions, accounting methodology, and investor communications.
