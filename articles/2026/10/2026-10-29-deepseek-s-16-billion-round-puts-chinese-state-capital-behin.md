# DeepSeek's $16 billion round puts Chinese state capital behind a national AI champion

DeepSeek's nearly $16 billion round, China's largest AI venture raise, kept Tencent and CATL as top investors and added memory maker ChangXin Memory Technologies, with state-owned investors contributing 27% of the capital. The company avoided outside funding until April and reportedly doubled this round because of soaring Nvidia chip prices. Compute costs, not ambition, now set funding requirements even for efficiency-focused labs.
