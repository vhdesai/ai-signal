# Voice AI still lacks a clear ChatGPT-style adoption moment, executives say

TechCrunch reports that voice AI executives see strong investment momentum but not yet a breakout user experience comparable to ChatGPT. PolyAI and Otter executives point to low-latency reasoning, transcription accuracy, speaker identification and trustworthy automation as remaining hurdles. For enterprises, the question is not whether voice gets better, but whether voice agents can safely take downstream actions while preserving user trust. URL(s): TechCrunch
