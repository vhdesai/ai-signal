# U.S. moves to ban DeepSeek from government agency IT systems

Reuters reports the U.S. is banning Chinese AI model DeepSeek from government agency IT systems. The move reflects growing concern that powerful foreign open-weight systems present data, security and influence risks in public-sector environments. It also collides with DeepSeek's rapid model improvements and extremely low pricing, forcing agencies to choose security policy over cost-performance.
