# OpenAI expands enterprise distribution through Google Cloud channels

OpenAI announced that its models are coming to Google Cloud pathways including Model Garden and Marketplace surfaces. The move broadens procurement and deployment options for enterprises and signals stronger multi-cloud distribution behavior among frontier providers. It changes route-to-market reach more than core model capability.
