# Meta and Microsoft pull back from Claude as Anthropic becomes a competitor

Meta and Microsoft are sharply cutting internal Claude usage after Anthropic's product expansion turned it from supplier into competitor. Microsoft reportedly reduced some cloud-division budgets from $100,000 to $10,000 per employee per month, while Meta halved Claude Code users to about 30,000. https://the-decoder.com/meta-and-microsoft-pull-back-from-claude-as-anthropic-transforms-from-partner-into-competitor/ INDUSTRY
