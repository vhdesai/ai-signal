# NYC Council puts Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta under oath on AI risks

Unite.AI reported that New York City's Committee of the Whole is holding under-oath testimony from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta on AI risks, with SpaceXAI subpoenaed. The agenda includes oversight of AI risks and legislation covering third-party validation, shutdown capabilities, whistleblower-style enforcement, and liability for foreseeable misuse. This is one of the most concrete local-government moves to turn frontier AI safety concerns into enforceable obligations. SAFETY PACTSELF-REGULATION
