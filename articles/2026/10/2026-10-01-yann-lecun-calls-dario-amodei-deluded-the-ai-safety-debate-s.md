# Yann LeCun Calls Dario Amodei "Deluded" — the AI-Safety Debate Spills Into Open Lab-Chief Combat

Meta’s chief AI scientist Yann LeCun called Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei "deluded" and "crazy," arguing Amodei fundamentally misunderstands cybersecurity in his framing of frontier AI as existentially risky. The salvo comes a day after Anthropic’s S-1 explicitly named existential risk, and lands amid internal uncertainty around Meta’s AI leadership trajectory. It is the sharpest public lab-chief exchange on safety governance of the cycle and will shape the external narrative at least through Anthropic’s expected roadshow — expect it to be referenced in every AI-safety committee meeting this quarter.
