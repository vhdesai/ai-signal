# OpenAI will watermark ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU

OpenAI said it will roll out invisible watermarking for ChatGPT and Codex text in the European Union to comply with EU AI Act transparency obligations. The technique subtly shapes word choices so a detector with the key can identify likely AI-generated text, though OpenAI cautioned that editing, short passages, math answers, and translation reduce reliability. The move shows provenance policy becoming a production requirement, but also highlights the technical limits of watermark-based accountability. BUG BOUNTYAI SLOP
