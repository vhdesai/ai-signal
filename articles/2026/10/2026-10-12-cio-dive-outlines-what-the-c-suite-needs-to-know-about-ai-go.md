# CIO Dive outlines what the C-suite needs to know about AI governance

CIO Dive highlighted AI governance as a C-suite issue tied to security, strategy and accountability. The framing reflects a maturation point: governance is no longer a post-deployment compliance exercise; it is part of operating-model design for AI systems that make, route or recommend decisions. The practical agenda includes ownership, risk classification, security review, audit trails and escalation paths. URL(s): CIO Dive
