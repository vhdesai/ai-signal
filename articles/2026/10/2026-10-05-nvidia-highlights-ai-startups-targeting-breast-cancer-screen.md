# Nvidia highlights AI startups targeting breast-cancer screening and treatment gaps

Nvidia profiled startups using its AI infrastructure to address breast-cancer workflow bottlenecks, including iSono Health's automated 3D ultrasound platform, Whiterabbit.ai's breast-density and risk tools, and Ataraxis AI's digital-pathology models for treatment-response and recurrence prediction. The examples span access, radiology capacity, false positives, genomic-test delays, and personalized treatment planning. The larger signal is that healthcare AI is shifting toward specific workflow bottlenecks where time-to-diagnosis and capacity constraints are measurable. APP SECURITYVIBE CODING
