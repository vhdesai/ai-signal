# AI meets living cells in a $28.6M bid to predict protein misfolding

Bioengineer.org reports a $28.6M research effort using AI and living cells to predict protein misfolding before disease strikes. The project sits squarely in the AI-for-biology surge led by AlphaFold, Isomorphic Labs, and Biohub-style virtual-cell efforts. Predicting misfolding earlier could materially improve disease modeling, drug targeting, and preventive therapeutics.
