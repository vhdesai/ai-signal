# Taste-Bench shows agents still make poor long-term strategic choices

Taste-Bench evaluates how well AI agents make strategic decisions during long-running tasks. Even the best model, GPT-5.6 Sol, selected the right path on just under 60% of simple binary decisions, and longer thinking did not reliably improve outcomes. Academic Research RESEARCH POLICY
