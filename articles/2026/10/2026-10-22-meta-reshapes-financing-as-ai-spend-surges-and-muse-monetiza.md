# Meta reshapes financing as AI spend surges and Muse monetization accelerates

DealBook reported Meta's governance and capital-structure moves against the backdrop of much higher AI spending, while TechCrunch reported Muse passing 10 million users with shopping becoming a top use case. Together, the updates point to a shift from pure user growth narratives toward financing durability and transaction economics. The key operating question is whether revenue expansion can keep pace with infrastructure-heavy cost growth.
