# Broadcom to Lend Anthropic Up to $42B to Lease Its Chips (From the S-1)

Reuters’s exclusive from Anthropic’s leaked S-1 reveals Broadcom will extend up to $42B in financing specifically for Anthropic to lease Broadcom chips. Barron’s notes Broadcom shares reacted on the newly disclosed depth of the relationship. It is one of the largest customer-vendor financing arrangements in semiconductor history, and it lands as the Bank of England’s Andrew Bailey warned that AI could spark financial shocks ("everybody is currently priced to be a winner"), and as PitchBook’s analysis flags $518B in Anthropic’s contracted computing commitments against a $2T IPO price tag. Vendor financing — not operating cash flow — is now visibly the AI buildout’s swing variable.
