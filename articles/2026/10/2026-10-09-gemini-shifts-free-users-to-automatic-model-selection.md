# Gemini shifts free users to automatic model selection

Google is moving free Gemini users to automatic model selection, generally routing them to Flash-Lite while reserving more granular controls for subscribers. The shift is a consumer-facing version of the same model-routing economics now appearing in enterprise platforms: users increasingly buy outcomes while providers optimize latency, cost and capability behind the scenes. Expect this abstraction layer to spread as model portfolios become too complex for users to manage manually. Industry News CAPITAL MODEL ECONOMICS
