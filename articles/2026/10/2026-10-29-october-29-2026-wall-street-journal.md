# [October 29, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings agreed to buy ADT for $10 a share, about $7.6 billion in equity and $15.3 billion in enterprise value, extending its model of retrofitting mature companies with frontier-lab tooling. Separately, legal AI startup Crosby submitted a letter of intent for Weil, Gotshal & Manges' internal data, trademark and brand after the firm moved to wind down, and hopes to hire some of its 1,200 lawyers. Proprietary operating data and domain experts are becoming acquisition targets in their own right.
