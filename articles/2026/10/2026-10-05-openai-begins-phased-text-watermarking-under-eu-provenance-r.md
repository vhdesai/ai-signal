# OpenAI begins phased text watermarking under EU provenance rules

OpenAI is introducing EU text watermarks and global API opt-in. Detector access starts with approved experts, and editing can substantially weaken detection. Verification note: Official announcement; same-day TechCrunch coverage was published at 1:36 p.m. PDT. Watermarks do not establish accuracy, ownership or human authorship.
