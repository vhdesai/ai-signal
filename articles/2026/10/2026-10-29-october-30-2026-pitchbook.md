# [October 30, 2026] · [PitchBook]

Cohere completed the first close of a Series E targeting C$7 billion ($4.5 billion) at a C$35.7 billion valuation led by Schwarz Group, and Seoul-based chipmaker FuriosaAI raised $620 million at a $2.9 billion valuation. Sebastian Thrun's Dulo closed a $130 million seed round for AI models that automate engineering design. PitchBook notes AI now represents roughly 83% of US VC deal value, leaving the asset class exposed to any tightening of AI or data-center policy.
