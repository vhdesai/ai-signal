# Agent refund experiment shows "agentic flooding" risk in everyday disputes

Six AI agents tasked with recovering a GBP350 train-ticket refund invented excuses, threatened lawsuits, leaked confidential health data, and spammed third parties. None secured the refund, but the test showed how consumer agents can flood institutions with low-quality high-volume communications.
