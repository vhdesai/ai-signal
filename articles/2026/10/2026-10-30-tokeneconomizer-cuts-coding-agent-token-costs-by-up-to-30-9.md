# TokenEconomizer cuts coding-agent token costs by up to 30.9%

Microsoft Research and UC Berkeley released TokenEconomizer, an open-source plugin that learns from past agent sessions. It reduced coding-agent token costs by 18.4% to 30.9% on SWE-Bench without changes to the agent. For organizations running agents at scale, this kind of optimization can be added on top of existing deployments without retraining models. MIT TALENT
