# Apple clears China AI hurdle as Alibaba models are approved for iPhone Duo and other handsets

China approved Alibaba's AI models for integration into several Apple handsets, including the iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro, clearing a key regulatory hurdle for Apple Intelligence in the Chinese market. The decision gives Apple a practical route into Chinese on-device AI while reinforcing Alibaba's role as the domestic AI partner of choice for foreign device makers. INDUSTRY
