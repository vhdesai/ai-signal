# Trump orders US government to cut ties with Anthropic; Hegseth labels company a supply-chain risk

ABC News reports President Trump directed the US government to cut ties with Anthropic, and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth formally labeled the company a supply-chain risk. The move comes four days after the Pentagon-security-risk ruling was upheld on appeal and follows Dario Amodei's dinner with Trump and the smear memo circulating in the White House. Anthropic's own IPO prospectus had already flagged US-government sentiment as a risk factor — Bloomberg now reports the pre-IPO investor event is scheduled, positioning Anthropic for a mid-November listing after its October 14 investor day.
