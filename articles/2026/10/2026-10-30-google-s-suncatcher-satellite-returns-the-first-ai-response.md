# Google's Suncatcher satellite returns the first AI response from a TPU in orbit

Google's Project Suncatcher prototype, carrying four TPUs and up to 1 kW of power, completed commissioning and ran Gemma 4 inference in orbit. Power limits allow only one TPU to run at a time for about 20 minutes, and the campaign will now test thermal stress and radiation-induced bit flips, with two follow-on satellites planned for 2027. It is a long-horizon bet on power-unconstrained compute rather than near-term capacity.
