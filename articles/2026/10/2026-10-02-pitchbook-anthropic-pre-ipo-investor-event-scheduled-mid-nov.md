# PitchBook: Anthropic pre-IPO investor event scheduled — mid-November listing in sight

Anthropic's pre-IPO investor event has been scheduled per Bloomberg, positioning the startup for an early-November listing following its October 14 investor day — a critical litmus test for AI-infrastructure debt pricing. PitchBook also flags Gulf-state AI data-center positioning (Qatar's cheap-LNG pitch, Humain's $2.5B Saudi raise, UAE's 5 GW Abu Dhabi campus) as patient capital increasingly bids where power is cheapest. And LPs that wanted out of software after the "SaaSpocalypse" are finally finding buyers as secondaries appetite shifts from 20% software exposure caps toward 35–40%.
