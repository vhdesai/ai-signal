# Sam Altman says society should accept some AI harms to preserve broad access

Sam Altman told POLITICO's Decoded that OpenAI differs from Anthropic in believing society should accept some bad outcomes in exchange for broad access to AI's benefits. He rejected a model in which one lab tightly controls powerful AI and said OpenAI supports a lighter-touch stance, even as it has recently endorsed stricter safety requirements and outside evaluator proposals. The comments clarify the emerging split among frontier labs: how much control is acceptable before safety policy becomes market control. Model Releases OPEN WEIGHTSU.S. MODEL LAB
