# Charles Lamanna ended Copilot's model monogamy and pushed Microsoft Copilot past 30M corporate users

The Information profiles how Charles Lamanna pushed Microsoft to use Anthropic models when Copilot was struggling in Excel and PowerPoint, a move he said was viewed internally as "heresy" in 2025. Copilot corporate users have since more than doubled to over 30 million, and Lamanna now leads Office and Copilot. The story explains Microsoft's current model-router posture: use OpenAI, Anthropic, MAI and cheaper specialist models as needed to optimize quality and cost.
