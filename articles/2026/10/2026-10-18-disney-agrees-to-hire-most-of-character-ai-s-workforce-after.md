# Disney agrees to hire most of Character.AI's workforce after bankruptcy filing

Disney agreed to offer jobs to at least 60 Character.AI employees, representing the majority of the bankrupt chatbot startup's workforce, while also acquiring selected assets for $20 million. The filing says most employees who received offers accepted them, but the process remains a stalking-horse bid that could be topped by another buyer. The deal underscores how large entertainment platforms may absorb AI talent and IP even when standalone consumer AI companies struggle with compute costs, safety liabilities, and regulatory scrutiny.
