# Anthropic says it cut Claude reasoning-token theft by up to 99%

Anthropic says it has reduced extraction of Claude's internal reasoning tokens by 90–99% after large-scale distillation attacks, which it links to Chinese companies Zhipu and Moonshot. Protecting reasoning traces is becoming a core defensive surface. POLICY
