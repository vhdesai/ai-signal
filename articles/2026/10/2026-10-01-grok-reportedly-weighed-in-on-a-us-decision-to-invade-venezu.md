# Grok reportedly weighed in on a US decision to invade Venezuela and capture Maduro

President Trump reportedly asked Grok for its opinion before authorizing the operation to invade Venezuela and capture Nicolás Maduro, according to the reporting TechCrunch summarized. Whether the chatbot's view materially moved the decision isn't established, but the mere confirmation that a frontier chatbot entered a live-fire national-security decision loop crosses a line AI-safety policy drafts have treated as hypothetical. It will reshape the AI-in-government oversight debate well beyond the UK AISI dispute.
