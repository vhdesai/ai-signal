# Apple discloses deal to hire Huxe AI team and license personalized-audio technology

Apple disclosed in a European Commission filing that it made employment offers to selected Huxe employees and licensed Huxe intellectual property. Huxe was founded by former NotebookLM developers and focused on personalized AI audio. The deal reinforces a pattern of targeted AI talent absorption by large platforms without necessarily acquiring entire startups outright. URL(s): TechCrunch
