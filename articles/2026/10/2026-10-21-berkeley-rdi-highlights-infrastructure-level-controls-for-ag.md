# Berkeley RDI highlights infrastructure-level controls for agentic AI safety

Berkeley RDI's weekly briefing summarized a security meetup focused on securing autonomous AI systems at machine speed. A central theme was that model alignment alone is insufficient without hardened execution infrastructure, sandboxing, and access boundaries. The discussion reflects growing consensus that agent safety requires end-to-end system controls, not only model behavior tuning.
