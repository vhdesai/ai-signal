# AI infrastructure financing accelerates: SpaceX seeks $40B, DeepSeek eyes $15B, Groq backer targets $10B

SpaceX is reportedly seeking roughly $40B in loans and debt financing to buy Nvidia chips, putting xAI, SpaceX and X into a shared compute-financing story. PitchBook reports DeepSeek is raising roughly 80B yuan, or about $12B, while other coverage says its pre-IPO round could swell toward $15B; Moonshot AI is preparing for a Hong Kong IPO at a reported $50B valuation. WSJ also reports Groq's venture backer is targeting a $10B megafund, underscoring continued investor demand for inference and AI-chip infrastructure. Industry News TRENDING ENTERPRISE
