# Amazon opens Trainium AI chips to PyTorch developers with no rewrite

Amazon opened its AWS Trainium AI chips to every PyTorch developer without requiring code rewrites, directly challenging Nvidia's software advantage. GeekWire and Reuters frame the toolchain as Amazon's most serious attempt yet to make Trainium accessible to mainstream AI teams. The strategic target is clear: reduce CUDA lock-in that keeps developers and workloads glued to Nvidia hardware.
