# Sam Altman says society may need to accept some AI harms; TechCrunch questions voluntary safety pact

Sam Altman told POLITICO's Decoded that OpenAI differs from Anthropic in believing society should accept some bad outcomes in exchange for broad access to AI's benefits. TechCrunch separately analyzed the White House-backed "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities" and was skeptical that a voluntary, non-binding pact will materially change frontier-lab incentives or safety behavior. Together, the stories clarify the live policy split: broad access and voluntary governance versus enforceable controls and incident transparency.
