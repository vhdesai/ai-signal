# OpenAI shares new AI progress in mathematics

OpenAI published an update on AI progress in mathematics, while The Information reported the company published more than 700 new math papers with AI-made solutions. The development signals that frontier systems are moving beyond solving benchmark-style math tasks toward producing larger volumes of formal or semi-formal mathematical work. The key question is not only raw output volume, but verification quality, attribution, and how human mathematicians integrate AI-generated results into trusted research workflows.
