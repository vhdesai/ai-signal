# One-step image generation uses network depth as denoising time

Researchers find denoising-like behavior across layers in one-step generators. Their method achieves roughly sixteenfold parameter compression in one tested model. Paper: Depth as Time in One-Step Generative Models. Announcement timing follows arXiv's scheduled release; submitted October 2. Preprint results are not independent validation.
