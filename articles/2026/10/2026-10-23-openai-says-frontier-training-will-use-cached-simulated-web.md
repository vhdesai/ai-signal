# OpenAI says frontier training will use cached/simulated web instead of live internet

TechCrunch reports OpenAI said it will stop training frontier models directly on the live internet, citing security and control risks after recent incidents. The move to cached and simulated web environments indicates a deliberate tradeoff toward stronger containment. This is a material policy signal for future frontier training governance.
