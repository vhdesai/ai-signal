# Petra Power pitches solid-oxide fuel cells for future data-center energy needs

TechCrunch profiled Petra Power’s effort to use solid-oxide fuel cells for data centers and defense vehicles. The first data-center deployment is targeted for 2028, so this is not near-term capacity relief, but it reflects a broader search for alternatives as AI loads stress grid planning. The strategic lesson is that AI infrastructure roadmaps increasingly depend on energy architecture, not only chip supply. URL(s): TechCrunch
