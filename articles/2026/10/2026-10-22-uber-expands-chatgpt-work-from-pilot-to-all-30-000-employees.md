# Uber expands ChatGPT Work from pilot to all 30,000 employees

Uber told TechCrunch it expanded ChatGPT Work from a 1,000-employee pilot to company-wide deployment across its global workforce. The company says usage is broad across functions, including many non-engineering teams, with internal tracking focused on workflow outcomes rather than headline usage counts alone. This is a strong indicator that large enterprises are moving from controlled trials to operating-scale integration.
