# Nvidia highlights AI startups closing breast-cancer screening and treatment gaps

Nvidia profiled startups using its AI infrastructure across breast-cancer workflows, including iSono Health's automated 3D ultrasound platform, Whiterabbit.ai's breast-density and risk tools, and Ataraxis AI's digital-pathology models for treatment-response and recurrence prediction. The examples address access, radiologist capacity, false positives, genomic-test delays, and therapy selection. The larger signal is that healthcare AI is being framed around specific bottlenecks with measurable clinical and operational impact. APP SECURITYVIBE CODING
