# Underdog launches as an on-device, privacy-focused AI assistant

Silicon Valley founder Sigil Wen launched an invite-only beta of Underdog, an AI assistant that runs entirely on a user's own devices rather than in a cloud service. The product uses a 27B reasoning model fine-tuned from Qwen3.8-27B, and its early business model is transaction-fee based rather than advertising or subscription inference. The launch is a clear counter-position to cloud-hosted assistants: keep sensitive personal context local, then monetize only when the agent completes paid transactions. PERSONAL AI COMPUTER USE
