# Apple’s reported HomeView hub would put Siri back at the center of the smart home

TechCrunch, citing Bloomberg reporting, says Apple plans a Siri-powered smart-home hub called HomeView, with display-based countertop and wall-mounted versions. The story is still a reported product plan, not an Apple-confirmed launch. Strategically, it suggests Apple may re-enter ambient-home AI through a controlled device surface rather than a general-purpose chatbot. URL(s): TechCrunch
