# NVIDIA highlights France's agentic-computing stack from sovereign infrastructure to industry deployments

NVIDIA profiled France's AI ecosystem across sovereign infrastructure, open models, and production agent deployments. The examples span Bull's planned European production of Vera Rubin Ultra systems, Mistral models running on orbital edge AI, Nemotron-based sovereign digital workplace deployments, and sector-specific agentic systems in finance, beauty, public services, ports, telecom, and scientific research. The broader readout is that sovereign AI is becoming a full-stack deployment pattern, not just a data-residency slogan. Research Breakthroughs A MODEL ARCHITECTURE EFFICIENCY
