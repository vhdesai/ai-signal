# Carnegie Mellon researchers analyzed 14,581 Facebook ad posts from 310 pages linking to 383 domains and found spending…

Carnegie Mellon researchers analyzed 14,581 Facebook ad posts from 310 pages linking to 383 domains and found spending on partisan sites posing as local news rose from hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars around the 2020 and 2024 elections. Campaigns targeted swing states and specific demographics and used local Facebook groups to exploit community trust. The researchers note improving AI-generated content makes such sites harder to distinguish from real journalism.
