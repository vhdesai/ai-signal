# Ramp AI Index: US businesses are using more AI and paying less for it

Ramp economist Ara Kharazian's monthly Ramp AI Index shows US company spend on AI vendors declined on a per-active-user basis for the first time in a year even as adoption breadth kept rising. The pattern is the near-term consequence of the GPT-6 Sol/Luna, Claude Opus 5.5, and Qwen Audio 3.1 pricing cuts flowing through to enterprise contracts. For CFOs modeling 2027 SG&A, the index argues the AI unit-cost deflation story is now empirically visible, not just forecast.
