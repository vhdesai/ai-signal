# MIT Technology Review: "Don't be fooled — LLMs don't reason"

MIT Technology Review published a sharp, high-profile piece arguing that despite marketing framing of "reasoning models," LLMs do not reason in any philosophically rigorous sense. The piece lands as Anthropic claims novel scientific discovery and Google ships Gemini 4 Argon — framing the industry debate about capability vs. interpolation. Expect it to be heavily cited by both sides over the next cycle.
