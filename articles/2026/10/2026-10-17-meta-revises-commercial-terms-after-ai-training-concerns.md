# Meta revises commercial terms after AI-training concerns

Meta removed language from its commercial terms that raised scraping and AI-training concerns, while saying its web-crawling behavior has not changed. The episode highlights how AI data-use risk is increasingly fought through contract language, platform policies and customer trust signals. For enterprise buyers, the key question is whether vendor commitments are operationally enforceable, not simply whether terms sound reassuring. URL(s): TechCrunch
