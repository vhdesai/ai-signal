# Chinese optical-chip stocks sell off on fears of possible US curbs

Chinese optical-chip and optical-module suppliers extended losses after renewed fears that the US could target next-generation optical transceivers for data centers. Potential curbs here would reach beyond GPUs into the networking fabric that makes large AI clusters practical.
