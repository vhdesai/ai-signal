# [October 28, 2026] · [The Information]

Incident-response providers report record demand, leaving hacked companies waiting in line for help, according to WSJ Pro CyberSecurity. CIO Dive reports CISA has confirmed exploitation of the AI-discovered PHP flaws, and The Information describes companies such as Atomicwork tripling security hiring after back-to-back incidents including a LiteLLM compromise. Discovery now runs at machine speed while patching still runs at human speed.
