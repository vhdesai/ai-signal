# MIT researchers Adam Berinsky, Chara Podimata, and Charles Stewart III built a dashboard that repeatedly queries…

MIT researchers Adam Berinsky, Chara Podimata, and Charles Stewart III built a dashboard that repeatedly queries popular chatbots with a fixed set of election questions. It tracks how answers change over time and by simulated user location, party, and identity. The project creates a historical record of the AI information environment voters see, which is otherwise hard to reconstruct after model updates.
