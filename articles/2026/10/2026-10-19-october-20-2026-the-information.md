# [October 20, 2026] · The Information

The Information's new report traces how CoreWeave's expansion depended on debt backed by cloud contracts and GPU purchases, including early disagreements inside Blackstone about the risk. PitchBook's newsletter reports CoreWeave bond yields around 15% and growing use of GPU collateral, while also presenting an investment thesis that rental pricing and Nvidia support could cushion stress. Together, the reports highlight financing and counterparty exposure without establishing that the wider AI infrastructure market will fail.
