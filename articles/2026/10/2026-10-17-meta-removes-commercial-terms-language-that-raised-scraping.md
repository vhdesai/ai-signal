# Meta removes commercial-terms language that raised scraping and AI training concerns

Meta removed language from its commercial terms that legal experts said could have allowed the company to use content from a business account's domains, web pages, or URLs to train and improve Meta products. Meta said the provision was intended to clarify crawler terms for ads and business products and did not change how Meta uses web crawlers. The episode shows how AI-training rights are increasingly being contested through product terms, publisher relationships, and business-account contracts rather than only courtroom litigation.
