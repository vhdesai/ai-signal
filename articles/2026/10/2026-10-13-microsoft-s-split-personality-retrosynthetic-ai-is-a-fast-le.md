# Microsoft's split-personality retrosynthetic AI is a fast learner

Chemistry World covered Microsoft's split-personality retrosynthetic AI, a model architecture designed to learn chemical synthesis planning quickly by balancing complementary reasoning styles. The system targets retrosynthesis, a core bottleneck in drug discovery and materials chemistry. It extends Microsoft's AI-for-science portfolio beyond general foundation models into practical lab-planning workflows.
