# Anthropic expands verified access to advanced cyber capabilities

Anthropic expanded its Cyber Verification Program, creating access paths for vetted security researchers and teams to use higher-capability cyber functions with fewer restrictions. The move reflects a tension every frontier lab faces: defenders need stronger tools, but the same capabilities can increase misuse risk if access control fails. For CISOs, the practical question becomes how model vendors verify users, audit activity, and preserve useful defensive capability without normalizing dangerous autonomy. URL(s): Anthropic
