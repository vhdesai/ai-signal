# Amazon's AI buildout is draining cash

Internal reporting indicates Amazon's AI expansion is under significant financial pressure from power constraints, expensive Nvidia server capacity, and data-center construction demands. The takeaway for executive planning is that frontier AI economics are increasingly governed by physical infrastructure bottlenecks rather than model costs alone. URL: Business Insider article
