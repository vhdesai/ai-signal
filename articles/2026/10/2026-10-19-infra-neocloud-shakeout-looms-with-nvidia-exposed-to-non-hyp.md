# INFRA Neocloud shakeout looms, with Nvidia exposed to non-hyperscaler demand

Industry executives expect only 7 to 10 of hundreds of AI cloud providers to survive after stock sell-offs, Firmus scrapping its IPO, CoreWeave losing a quarter of its market value, and Microsoft reportedly pulling out of an Nscale Nvidia-chip deal. Nvidia has meaningful exposure: customers outside hyperscalers represent about 45% of data-center revenue. https://the-decoder.com/the-neocloud-shakeout-is-coming-and-nvidia-has-the-most-to-lose/
