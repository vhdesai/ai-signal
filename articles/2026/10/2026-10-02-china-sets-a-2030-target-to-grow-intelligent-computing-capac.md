# China sets a 2030 target to grow intelligent computing capacity more than 4×

Beijing formally set a five-year goal to expand intelligent-computing capacity by more than 4× over the end-of-June-2026 baseline, framing it as the AI-infra anchor of the broader industrial plan. Capacity grew 177% in the prior year, so the trajectory is plausible but requires sustained innovation in domestic chips given US export curbs. The target gives a concrete scoreboard against which to measure Huawei, Hygon, and Alibaba's Pingtouge chip roadmaps over the next 24 months.
