# Google joins Amazon in reducing PyTorch lock-in to Nvidia

Google open-sourced TorchTPU while Amazon promoted TorchNeuron, both aimed at running PyTorch workloads on non-Nvidia accelerators with minimal code changes. The immediate impact is lower migration friction rather than an instant hardware-share reversal. Strategically, it pushes control of developer defaults from chip-specific tooling toward portability layers.
