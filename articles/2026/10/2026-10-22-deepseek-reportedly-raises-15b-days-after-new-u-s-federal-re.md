# DeepSeek reportedly raises $15B days after new U.S. federal restrictions

TechCrunch, citing Bloomberg, reports DeepSeek raised about $15 billion in one of China's largest AI rounds, shortly after US federal actions to remove DeepSeek technology from civilian agency systems. The timing highlights a widening policy-commercial split between US security posture and Chinese domestic capital support for AI scaling. Reported investor participation and terms remain externally sourced and not company-confirmed.
