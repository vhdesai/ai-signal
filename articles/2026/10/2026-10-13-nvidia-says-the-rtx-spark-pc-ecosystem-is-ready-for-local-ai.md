# NVIDIA says the RTX Spark PC ecosystem is ready for local AI agents

NVIDIA detailed the software ecosystem around RTX Spark ahead of laptop availability, positioning it as a personal AI supercomputer with up to 128GB of unified memory and 1 petaflop of AI compute. Updates include optimized local-agent support for GitHub Copilot, Hermes, OpenClaw, Perplexity, Pi, and WorkBuddy; MXC and OpenShell sandboxing; NVIDIA Switchyard routing between local and cloud models; and upcoming Nemotron 3.5 Bolt models. The practical signal is that local AI is becoming a managed product stack, not just hobbyist model downloads. Infrastructure N AI FACTORIES OPEN STANDARDS
