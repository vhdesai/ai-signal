# OPPO partners with Manus to bring AI agents to devices

OPPO is partnering with Manus to bring AI agents into its device ecosystem. The move fits China’s broader trend of phone and device makers embedding personal agents directly into hardware and OS layers. For Manus, it adds distribution; for OPPO, it helps keep pace with Alibaba Qwen glasses, Tencent Yuanbao, Huawei AI phones and Meta/Apple smart-device moves.
