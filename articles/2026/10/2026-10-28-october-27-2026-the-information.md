# [October 27, 2026] · [The Information]

The Information first reported that OpenAI, a longtime Nvidia customer, plans to buy Google's tensor processing units, and Google then confirmed TPU sales to OpenAI and Oracle. Oracle said TPUs will be available to its cloud customers by 2027, the first time the chips will be offered on another hyperscale cloud. The move diversifies frontier compute supply and strengthens Google's position as a chip vendor. ANALYSIS CAPEX
