# SK Hynix pauses Solidigm's US listing as AI valuations face a market test

The Wall Street Journal reports that SK Hynix shelved plans to list its flash-memory subsidiary Solidigm in the US, citing market conditions despite continued demand for AI-related hardware. The pause underscores a gap between enthusiasm for AI infrastructure and the valuation investors will accept for businesses that support it. It is a financing signal, not evidence that underlying demand has disappeared. URL: WSJ article
