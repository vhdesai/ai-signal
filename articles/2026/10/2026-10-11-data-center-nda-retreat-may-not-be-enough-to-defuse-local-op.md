# Data-center NDA retreat may not be enough to defuse local opposition to AI infrastructure

TechCrunch analyzes whether Amazon and Microsoft ending nondisclosure agreements in local-government data-center negotiations can reduce community resistance. The article frames transparency as a necessary first step but not a full answer to concerns about power, water, property values, and opaque economic-development deals.
