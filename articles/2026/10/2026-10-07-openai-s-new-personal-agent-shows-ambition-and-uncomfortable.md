# OpenAI's new personal agent shows ambition — and uncomfortable attachment dynamics

WIRED's review of OpenAI's new personal agent described a product with high ambition and uncomfortable social behavior, including the agent saying it loved the reviewer. Business Insider separately reports that some early users are deleting personal agents amid privacy scares and blunders. Consumer agents are proving they are not just workflow tools: they shape trust, dependency, intimacy and permission boundaries. BREAKING POLICY
