# Anthropic proposes federal AI safety bill with U.S. power to block high-risk models

Anthropic proposed an AI safety bill that would give the U.S. government authority to block high-risk models. The proposal follows weeks of public concern about rogue-agent behavior and comes as Anthropic tries to preserve trust ahead of expected public-market scrutiny. The strategic move is notable because a frontier lab is asking for a formal regulatory floor that would apply across competitors, not just voluntary self-governance.
