# Asian memory-chip stocks sell off despite AI demand

Samsung, SK Hynix, and CXMT shares came under pressure despite strong AI-driven memory demand and pricing. Investors are starting to price the risk of overinvestment or margin normalization in one of AI infrastructure's key bottlenecks.
