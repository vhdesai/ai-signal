# Oracle, Broadcom and SpaceX seek large debt packages to fund AI chips

WSJ reported that Oracle, Broadcom and SpaceX are seeking blockbuster debt deals to pay for AI chips. The story reinforces a widening split in AI infrastructure: demand remains enormous, but financing structures are becoming part of the risk model.
