# Nvidia plans to invest in chip rival d-Matrix as challengers choose partnership

The Information reports Nvidia plans to invest in d-Matrix, a seven-year-old developer of server chips for AI that aims to compete with Nvidia. The investment is part of Nvidia's effort to make its technology compatible with rival chip designers and bring them into its dominant hardware ecosystem. The story underscores Nvidia's shift from pure supplier to ecosystem orchestrator, even around firms that nominally compete with it.
