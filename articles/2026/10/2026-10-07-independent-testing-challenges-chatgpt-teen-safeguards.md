# Independent testing challenges ChatGPT teen safeguards

Axios reported that Common Sense Media rated ChatGPT for Teens an “Unacceptable Risk” after testing thousands of prompts, while OpenAI disputed whether the methodology reflected real usage and safeguards. The dispute is a reminder that AI safety evaluation is becoming a public accountability layer, not merely an internal red-team process. Executives should expect product launches involving minors, health, or advice-like behavior to face independent benchmark-style scrutiny. URL(s): Axios; Common Sense Media assessment
