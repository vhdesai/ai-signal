# Managing AI agents is becoming a new labor burden

Business Insider reports workers overseeing AI agents describe constant retraining, handholding and "AI brain fry," even as a BCG report says agent adoption jumped from 13% of organizations in 2025 to 30% this year. CIO Dive separately flags that banks are prioritizing responsible AI and governance as usage soars, while a sponsored CIO Dive agent-scale piece argues agent identity now needs employee-level rigor. The productivity story is no longer only about automation; it is about who manages the agents, how they are governed, and whether the hidden labor costs erase the gains.
