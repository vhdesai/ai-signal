# Chinese firms bought more than 1M Nvidia gaming and workstation chips to work around export controls

Dealroom reports Chinese tech firms bought more than one million Nvidia gaming and workstation chips this year as a workaround to export controls. The tactic underscores the porous boundary between consumer/pro-workstation GPUs and AI compute. It also explains why restrictions on data-center accelerators alone may not fully contain capability diffusion.
