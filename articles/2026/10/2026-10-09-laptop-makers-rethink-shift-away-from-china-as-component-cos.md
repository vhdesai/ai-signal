# Laptop makers rethink shift away from China as component costs surge

TrendForce projects global notebook production outside mainland China will fall from about 24% in 2025 to 21% this year and below 20% in 2027, as soaring component costs pressure manufacturers to reconsider relocation plans. The finding matters for AI PCs and edge-AI hardware supply chains.
