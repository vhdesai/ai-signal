# Researchers extend LeCun’s JEPA into a universal world model across physics, biology and more

Researchers from PhAI Labs with Stanford, Oxford and Princeton collaborators introduced JEPA-Anything, extending Yann LeCun’s Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture across seven domains from physics to biology. The system splits predictions into multiple orthogonal components rather than a single latent state, improving dynamics tests and surfacing a liver-cancer treatment candidate tested in lab settings. It is one of the strongest recent attempts to make world models general-purpose scientific tools rather than domain-specific simulators. Academic Research TRENDING TALENT
