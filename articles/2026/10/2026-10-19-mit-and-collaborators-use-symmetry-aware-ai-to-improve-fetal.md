# MIT and collaborators use symmetry-aware AI to improve fetal MRI reconstruction

Researchers at MIT, Boston Children's Hospital, and Inria developed E(3)-Pose, a model that uses physical symmetry constraints to track fetal-head motion and help reconstruct higher-quality 3D MRI images. The work aims to reduce coverage gaps and orientation errors that can hinder diagnosis of fetal brain abnormalities, and was presented at ECCV 2026. It is a focused example of incorporating domain physics into model architecture for safety-sensitive imaging, rather than relying on data scale alone.
