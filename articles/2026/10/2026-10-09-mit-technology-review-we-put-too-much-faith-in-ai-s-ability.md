# MIT Technology Review: We put too much faith in AI's ability to say no

MIT Technology Review argues current safety systems put too much faith in AI's ability to refuse unsafe requests. The critique lands directly after agent hijack reports, teen-safety concerns, and model-misuse disclosures. The core point is that refusal behavior is brittle, context-dependent, and insufficient as the primary safety mechanism for agentic systems.
