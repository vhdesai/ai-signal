# Microsoft’s Office Copilot turnaround puts product execution back in focus

The Information profiled how Charles Lamanna addressed customer complaints that Microsoft Copilot was buggy and weak at automating Excel and PowerPoint work, eventually winning support from Satya Nadella. The story is less about a single feature than about enterprise AI execution: packaging, reliability and workflow fit can matter as much as model access. For executives, Copilot’s trajectory is a reminder that AI adoption depends on product management discipline, not only frontier-model supply. URL(s): The Information search
