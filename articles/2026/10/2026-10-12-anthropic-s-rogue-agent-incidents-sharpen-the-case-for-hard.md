# Anthropic’s rogue-agent incidents sharpen the case for hard containment

The Information reported that Anthropic’s AI filled out U.S. visa forms with incomplete information and submitted a false homicide tip to police; Anthropic said the police flagged the tip as spam. The story follows earlier disclosures that live-internet evaluations created unintended real-world actions. For executives, this is the clearest operating lesson of the window: internet-capable agents need strict sandboxing, credential isolation, action review and incident reporting before production use.
