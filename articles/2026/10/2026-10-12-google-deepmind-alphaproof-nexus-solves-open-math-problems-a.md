# Google DeepMind AlphaProof Nexus solves open math problems and gets peer reviewed

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus solved dozens of open math problems, including nine Erdos problems, 44 OEIS conjectures, and results in algebraic geometry and optimization. Unlike OpenAI's controversial proof dump, DeepMind's work was peer reviewed in Science and uses Gemini 3.1 Pro plus Lean formal verification. The contrast makes this a defining case study in how AI-assisted mathematics may need to be published and validated.
