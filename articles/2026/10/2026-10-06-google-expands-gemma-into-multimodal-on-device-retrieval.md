# Google expands Gemma into multimodal on-device retrieval

Google launched EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740M-parameter multimodal embedding model that unifies text, code, images, video, and audio in a shared retrieval space. The release matters because embedding models are increasingly the quiet infrastructure layer for private RAG, local search, and latency-sensitive agent memory. Google is positioning Gemma less as a chatbot family and more as an edge-capable building block for products that cannot send every signal back to the cloud. URL(s): Google announcement
