# ChatGPT adds Locked Chats with PIN and biometrics

OpenAI launched Locked Chats so users can protect selected conversations behind a PIN, Face ID, or Touch ID. The feature improves local access control and shared-device privacy but does not represent end-to-end encryption or an automatic retention-policy change. For enterprise users, it is a practical interface safeguard rather than a full governance control.
