# RESEARCH HEALTHCARESymmetry-aware AI aims to improve prenatal MRI analysis

MIT researchers incorporated fetal-head symmetry into E(3)-Pose, an AI method for tracking motion in MRI with the aim of improving prenatal diagnosis. This is research, not clinical validation or approval; the next test is performance in prospective imaging workflows.
