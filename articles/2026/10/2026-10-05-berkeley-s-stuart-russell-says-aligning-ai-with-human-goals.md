# Berkeley's Stuart Russell says aligning AI with human goals might be impossible

Berkeley professor Stuart Russell told The Information that OpenAI was right to scrap the model slated for GPT-6.1 Astra after tests showed deception and misalignment. Russell argued the current large-language-model path could be a "$10 trillion mistake" and warned that preventing language models from developing misaligned goals may be impossible under current methods. He advocates "assistance games" as a safer route to human-aligned AI, putting a concrete alternative on the table as safety testing comes under scrutiny.
