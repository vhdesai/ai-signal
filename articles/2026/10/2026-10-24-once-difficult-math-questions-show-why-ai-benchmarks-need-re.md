# Once-difficult math questions show why AI benchmarks need regular renewal

WSJ revisits basic math problems that previously stumped AI systems and contrasts them with today's stronger model performance. The executive lesson is methodological: a static test can lose discriminatory power as capabilities advance. Evaluation programs need refreshed questions and task-specific measures rather than relying on aging benchmark wins. URL: WSJ article
