# The Information: Chinese token resellers running an Anthropic Claude "gray market"

The Information's Jing Yang, Juro Osawa, and Qianer Liu report that thousands of Chinese resellers operating out of Beijing's Haidian District provide unauthorized access to Anthropic's Claude and other US models using fake or stolen accounts, credit cards, and identities. Chinese AI labs are using that access to distill US models and accelerate their own development, matching what Anthropic has publicly called an "industrial-scale, covert campaign." Anthropic is actively combatting the gray market, prompting new Chinese bypass methods — and the story lands the same day Trump orders the US government to cut ties with Anthropic.
