# OpenAI starts EU text watermarking for ChatGPT and Codex

OpenAI will add invisible textGrain watermarks to ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU to comply with AI Act provenance requirements, while making watermarking optional for API users globally. Detection can reach 95% in clean text but falls to 17% if a quarter of words are replaced. https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance POLICY
