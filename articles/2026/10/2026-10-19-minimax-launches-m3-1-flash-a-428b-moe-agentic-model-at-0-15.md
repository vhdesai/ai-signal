# MiniMax launches M3.1 Flash, a 428B MoE agentic model at $0.15 per million input tokens

MiniMax launched M3.1 Flash, a 428B mixture-of-experts agentic model priced at $0.15 per million input tokens. The launch intensifies the open and low-cost model race led by Chinese labs and puts further price pressure on OpenAI, Anthropic and other closed frontier providers. For enterprise buyers, it reinforces a central October theme: model quality is rising while inference prices collapse.
