# DoorDash's agent orders are a reality check on agentic commerce

DoorDash said third-party AI agents have placed only several hundred orders in total, against roughly 3 million deliveries a day. It remains open to agent partners and may eventually advertise inside them. Agentic commerce is drawing heavy investment in payments and infrastructure, but consumer volume is still negligible.
