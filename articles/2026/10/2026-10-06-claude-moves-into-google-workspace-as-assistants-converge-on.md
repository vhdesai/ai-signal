# Claude moves into Google Workspace as assistants converge on the work surface

Anthropic brought Claude into Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides, letting paid users open Workspace files inside Claude and use a sidebar while working in Google apps. The important shift is that model vendors are no longer competing only in chat windows; they are competing for the document, spreadsheet, and presentation surfaces where enterprise work actually happens. Approval-gated edits also show how vendors are trying to reconcile autonomy with user control. URL(s): VentureBeat
