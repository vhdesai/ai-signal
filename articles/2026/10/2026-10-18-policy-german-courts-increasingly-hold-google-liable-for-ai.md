# POLICY German courts increasingly hold Google liable for AI Overview outputs

Two German courts have now treated Google's AI Overview outputs as Google's own content. A European liability pattern is forming: generated answers are not neutral links or third-party snippets, and platforms may be responsible for their claims. https://the-decoder.com/two-german-courts-now-hold-google-liable-for-what-its-ai-overviews-say/
