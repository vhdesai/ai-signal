# Amazon drops data-center NDAs as community backlash grows

Amazon says it will stop using NDAs when negotiating data-center deals with local governments, following Microsoft. Secrecy around land, water, and power commitments has fueled moratoriums; communities increasingly want binding transparency on power, grid, water, and tax impacts.
