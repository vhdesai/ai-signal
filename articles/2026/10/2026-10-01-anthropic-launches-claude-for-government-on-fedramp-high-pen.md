# Anthropic launches Claude for Government on FedRAMP High; Pentagon still excluded

Anthropic rolled out Claude for Government for US federal and state civilian agencies in a FedRAMP High environment, with usage-based billing and per-department spending caps. The move routes around the Pentagon, which still classifies Anthropic as a supply-chain risk and whose designation was upheld on appeal last week. The product directly contests OpenAI's Daybreak-anchored civilian-cyber positioning and signals Anthropic is willing to concede defense in exchange for the much larger civilian federal and state footprint.
