# On its Q3 earnings call, DoorDash CEO Tony Xu said agents such as Muse and Instinct have generated only "several…

On its Q3 earnings call, DoorDash CEO Tony Xu said agents such as Muse and Instinct have generated only "several hundred" orders in total, compared with roughly 3 million daily deliveries. DoorDash remains open to agent partners and sees possible future advertising inside agents, while running its own text, CLI, chatbot, and MCP ordering channels. It is a useful reality check on the near-term revenue impact of agentic commerce.
