# Mistral releases Mistral Large 4, a 1.05T-parameter multimodal MoE

Mistral released Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk," as a public preview with 1.05T total parameters, 49B active parameters per token, native image input, and a 1M-token context window. TechCrunch reported that the model was trained on Mistral's own European compute using about 4,000 Nvidia GPUs, and that weights are expected after additional safety testing. The release is strategically important for sovereign and open-weight AI: Mistral is trying to offer a European alternative to both closed U.S. systems and Chinese open models. OMNI MODEL ROBOTICS
