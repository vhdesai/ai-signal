# Stanford HAI: humans and AI engage in meaningful collaborations

Stanford HAI published new work on meaningful human-AI collaboration. The research focuses on collaboration quality rather than pure automation, a useful counterweight to claims that agents will simply replace knowledge workers. It fits a growing academic theme: the frontier is not only model capability, but how humans and models share judgment, context and accountability.
