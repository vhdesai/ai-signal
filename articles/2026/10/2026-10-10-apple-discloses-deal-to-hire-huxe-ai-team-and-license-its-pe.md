# Apple discloses deal to hire Huxe AI team and license its personalized-audio technology

Apple disclosed in a European Commission filing that it reached an agreement to make employment offers to certain Huxe AI employees and receive a non-exclusive license to Huxe intellectual property. Huxe was founded by developers who had worked on NotebookLM's AI-generated podcast features and shut down its app earlier this year. The deal fits the broader pattern of large platforms using reverse acqui-hires to absorb AI talent and capabilities without acquiring startups outright. C N CEREBRAS MARKET
