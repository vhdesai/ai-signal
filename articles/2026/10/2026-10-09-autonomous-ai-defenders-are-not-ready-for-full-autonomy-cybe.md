# Autonomous AI defenders are not ready for full autonomy, cyber chiefs say

Corporate security chiefs remain wary of unleashing fully autonomous AI defenders even as they face a rise in robot attackers. PwC found fewer than a quarter of surveyed security, tech, and business leaders feel comfortable deploying agents without human controls. The emerging pattern is earned autonomy: let agents work through part of an incident, but hand over decisions one at a time after the system proves reliability.
