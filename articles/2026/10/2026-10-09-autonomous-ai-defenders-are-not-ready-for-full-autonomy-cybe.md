# Autonomous AI defenders are not ready for full autonomy, cyber chiefs say

Corporate security chiefs remain wary of unleashing fully autonomous AI defenders even as they face robot attackers. PwC found fewer than a quarter of surveyed security, tech, and business leaders feel comfortable deploying agents without human controls. The practical pattern is incremental autonomy: agents can work through much of an incident, but decisions are handed over one at a time only after the system earns trust.
