# Bain's Bridge Data Centres seeks upsized $6.6B loan for Malaysia operations

Bain Capital-owned Bridge Data Centres is reportedly in talks to expand a loan from $2.8B to about $6.6B for its Malaysia operations. If completed, it would be one of Asia's largest data-center debt financings. The deal adds another marker to the global AI-infrastructure debt wave, alongside chip-backed financing, hyperscaler capex and regional power constraints.
