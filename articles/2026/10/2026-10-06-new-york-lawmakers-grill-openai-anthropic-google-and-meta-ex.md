# New York lawmakers grill OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta executives over AI safety

Top executives and safety officials from major AI labs appeared before New York City lawmakers and faced repeated questions about guardrails, worst-case scenarios, whistleblower protections, and a possible city-run AI “kill switch.” WSJ Pro Cybersecurity notes New York is trying to position itself as both a leading AI hub and a bulwark against the technology’s fastest-moving risks. The hearing also included testimony from former frontier-lab employee Jacob Coxon, who warned that reckless development could create catastrophic risk. BREAKING POLICY
