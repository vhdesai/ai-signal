# University of Washington researchers presented micro language models with 8 to 30 million parameters at EMNLP 2026

University of Washington researchers presented micro language models with 8 to 30 million parameters at EMNLP 2026. The tiny model starts answering on-device immediately, and a cloud LLM takes over after four to eight words, correcting the 4% to 8% of starts that went wrong. In a user study, nine of 10 participants preferred the paired system to cloud-only responses, a practical pattern for hiding latency on constrained devices.
