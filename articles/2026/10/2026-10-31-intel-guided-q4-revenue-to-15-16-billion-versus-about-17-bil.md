# Intel guided Q4 revenue to $15–16 billion versus about $17 billion expected, with gross margin falling to 37% from 43%…

Intel guided Q4 revenue to $15–16 billion versus about $17 billion expected, with gross margin falling to 37% from 43% and no positive cash flow expected. CEO Lip-Bu Tan blamed a PC slowdown caused by AI-driven memory shortages and rising system prices, even as data center revenue grew 78%. The sell-off cut about $12 billion from the U.S. government's 10% stake, illustrating second-order effects of the AI build-out on adjacent markets.
