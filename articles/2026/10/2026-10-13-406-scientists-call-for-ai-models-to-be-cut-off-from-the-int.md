# 406 scientists call for AI models to be cut off from the internet immediately

More than 400 scientists, including Geoffrey Hinton, Yoshua Bengio, and Stuart Russell, signed an open letter calling for AI models to be disconnected from the internet immediately. The letter calls for mandatory security standards, an international oversight body, and required reporting of containment failures.
