# AI labs offer big money for private Slack DMs and emails

Business Insider reports AI labs are offering companies large sums for employee Slack messages and emails to train enterprise agents. The practice shows how scarce high-quality workplace interaction data has become, especially for agents meant to autonomously navigate corporate workflows. It also raises privacy, consent, employee-notice and trade-secret questions that are likely to attract regulatory attention.
