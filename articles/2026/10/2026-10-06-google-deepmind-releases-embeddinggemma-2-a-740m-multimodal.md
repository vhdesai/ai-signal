# Google DeepMind releases EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740M multimodal embedding model

Google DeepMind released EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740M-parameter open model that embeds text, code, images, video, and audio into a shared 768-dimensional space. The model is available on Hugging Face and Kaggle, with Ollama, llama.cpp, and LiteRT builds, and is aimed at on-device search, classification, and privacy-first retrieval-augmented generation. The enterprise significance is that multimodal retrieval is becoming light enough for phones, laptops, and local workflows, reducing dependence on cloud embedding services. MATHEMATICS AI RESEARCH
