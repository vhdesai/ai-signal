# Z.ai releases GLM-Legal for agentic legal work

Z.ai released GLM-Legal, a legal-domain AI model built on GLM-5.3-Flash and targeted at contract review, evidence analysis, and compliance research. MarkTechPost reports a 19.17% all-pass rate on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark, nearly three times its base model's 6.67%, with application-only API access for now. The strategic signal is that domain-specialized models are using practice-specific benchmarks to challenge frontier generalists in high-value enterprise workflows. Q AGENTS REINFORCEMENT LEARNING
