# Former Plaud executive raises $26M for Ambi, an always-listening AI assistant

Former Plaud executive Peter Mo raised $26 million for Ambi, an AI app designed to listen continuously and provide real-time context during meetings and work. TechCrunch reports Ambi is launching iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch apps, with future hardware planned. The product sits at the center of the convenience-versus-privacy debate: continuous context can make agents more useful, but it creates major consent and data-security questions.
