# Study suggests heavy LLM use may reduce some measures of brain function

IAI TV covered a new study suggesting certain measures of brain function may be reduced during heavy LLM use. The finding will likely feed education and workplace debates over whether AI assistance augments cognition or substitutes for it. It pairs with recent MIT reporting about AI eroding office hours, study groups and faculty-student trust.
