# Google tests orbital AI data centers as Amazon warns blocked US projects threaten AI leadership

Tech Wire Asia reports Google is sending AI chips into space under Project Suncatcher, an exploratory orbital data-center effort aimed at long-term power, cooling and land constraints. Amazon is making the terrestrial version of the same argument: AWS CEO Matt Garman warned that $68B in blocked data-center projects and roughly 100 proposed bans threaten US AI leadership. The combined message from hyperscalers is that AI infrastructure politics now spans land use, grid access, water concerns and even off-planet compute concepts.
