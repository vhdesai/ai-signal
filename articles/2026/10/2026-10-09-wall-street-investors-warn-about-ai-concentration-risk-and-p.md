# Wall Street investors warn about AI concentration risk and private-equity exit pressure

WSJ coverage points to growing investor concern that AI exposure is becoming concentrated across a narrow set of infrastructure, platform, and capital-market assumptions. For senior buyers, the practical implication is that vendor diligence now needs to include financing durability, customer concentration, and exit-market assumptions, not just technical capability. The timing is notable because the article landed inside the same window as fresh coverage of GPU capacity fragmentation and data-center transparency pressure.
