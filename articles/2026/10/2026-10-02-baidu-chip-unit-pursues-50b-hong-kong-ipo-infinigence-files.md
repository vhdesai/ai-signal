# Baidu chip unit pursues $50B Hong Kong IPO; Infinigence files confidentially

Baidu's chip unit is reportedly pursuing a $50B Hong Kong IPO ("Neocloud backed by Chinese tech giants"), potentially one of the largest AI-adjacent listings of the cycle. Separately, Tencent-backed Infinigence filed confidentially for a Hong Kong IPO (PitchBook / Bloomberg). It's a direct response to the DeepSeek + Huawei + Alibaba stack push, giving mainland capital markets a concentrated bet on sovereign AI compute.
