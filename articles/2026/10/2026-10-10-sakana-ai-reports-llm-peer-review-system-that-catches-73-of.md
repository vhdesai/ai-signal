# Sakana AI reports LLM peer-review system that catches 73% of core-claim errors

MarkTechPost covered Sakana AI research on a multi-layered review system for AI-assisted scientific peer review. The system reportedly caught 73.43% of planted core-claim contradictions with four reviews, though performance was weaker on real retracted arXiv papers and prompt-injection risk remains. The significance is methodological: AI review systems are being evaluated on error detection, not just imitation of human reviewer prose.
