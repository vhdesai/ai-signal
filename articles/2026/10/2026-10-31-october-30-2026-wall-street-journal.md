# [October 30, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

Alphabet, Amazon, Meta and Microsoft have outlined more than $740 billion of capital spending this year, and Alphabet reported negative third-quarter free cash flow of $9.1 billion while Amazon's trailing free cash flow reached negative $42.4 billion. Microsoft was the exception, generating $18.6 billion. On Friday the Nasdaq fell 2.9% and the S&P 500 1.8%, with Nvidia and Broadcom also sliding, as the market shifted from rewarding AI spending to demanding evidence of returns.
