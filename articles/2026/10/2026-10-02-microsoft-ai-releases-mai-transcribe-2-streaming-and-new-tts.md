# Microsoft AI releases MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming and new TTS models for voice agents

Microsoft AI launched MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming for real-time transcription plus a companion family of TTS models explicitly aimed at voice-agent builders. The release positions MAI as a competitor to Nvidia Nemotron 3 Diarization, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, Qwen-Audio 3.1, and ElevenLabs on the inbound-and-outbound voice-agent stack. It also quietly reinforces Microsoft's post-OpenClaw Copilot repositioning as a voice-and-agent platform rather than a text-chat vendor. Products & Tools PRODUCT
