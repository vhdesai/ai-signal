# Disney to hire majority of Character.AI workforce

Disney agreed to hire the majority of Character.AI’s workforce, giving the entertainment company deeper conversational-AI talent while Character.AI’s independent business struggles with safety, monetization and platform competition. The move shows entertainment companies treating AI character and agent talent as core IP infrastructure rather than experimental R&D.
