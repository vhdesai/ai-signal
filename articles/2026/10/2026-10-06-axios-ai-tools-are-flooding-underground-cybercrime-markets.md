# Axios: AI tools are flooding underground cybercrime markets

Axios reported that AI tools are flooding underground cybercrime markets, adding to concern that agentic and generative systems are lowering the skill threshold for malicious activity. The story sits alongside recent disclosures of frontier-agent security incidents and bug-bounty overload. The practical takeaway is that enterprise defensive programs need to assume adversaries will increasingly use AI for reconnaissance, social engineering, exploit adaptation, and scale.
