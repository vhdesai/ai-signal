# AI wearables run into wiretap-law limits; Anthropic asks outsiders to weigh in on Claude's values

Tech-Insider reports AI wearables may run into wiretap-law limits in more than a dozen U.S. states because always-listening glasses, pins and assistants can record third parties without proper consent. NBC News separately reports Anthropic wants outside input on Claude's values, raising a different governance question: who gets final say over increasingly agentic assistants. Together, the stories show personal AI governance moving from abstract values into consent, recording and everyday social boundaries.
