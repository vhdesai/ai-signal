# Nvidia’s AI dealmaking expands from chips into the model and application ecosystem

The Information reported that Nvidia explored an OpenRouter bid before Stripe prevailed, and that the chipmaker has moved through more than $140B in deals and commitments, including large credit support. The reported target map - open-source AI, local AI, robotics, and autonomous systems - suggests Nvidia is using capital to defend and expand the demand stack around its GPUs. The broader implication is that AI infrastructure leaders are increasingly acting like platform consolidators, not just component suppliers. URL(s): The Information search
