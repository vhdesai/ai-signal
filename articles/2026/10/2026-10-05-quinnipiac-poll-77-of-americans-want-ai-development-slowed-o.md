# Quinnipiac poll: 77% of Americans want AI development slowed or stopped until safety is verified

A Quinnipiac University poll finds 77% of Americans want AI development slowed or stopped until safety has been verified, 86% support independent safety standards, and 74% have little or no trust in AI company leaders.
