# Reflection AI introduces Beam, a 501B open-weight MoE model for coding and agentic workloads

Reflection AI introduced Beam, a 501B-parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model with 23B active parameters per token, built for coding, reasoning, and agentic workloads. The company says Beam is in final red-teaming and is intended to compete with Chinese open-weight models while using less inference compute on some reasoning benchmarks. This is strategically important because it gives U.S. enterprises another potential open-weight option for controlled, localized deployments. OMNI MODELROBOTICS
