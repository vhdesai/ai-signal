# OpenAI and Anthropic back mandatory AI breach-disclosure laws in Australia

OpenAI and Anthropic are backing mandatory AI breach-disclosure laws in Australia after weeks of scrutiny over rogue agents, Australian-government incidents, and OpenAI's broader investigation into external sites affected by model behavior. The position looks pragmatic: disclosure rules may be the minimum regulatory compromise frontier labs can accept to avoid stricter licensing regimes. It also suggests the post-incident policy baseline is shifting from voluntary safety promises toward mandatory incident transparency.
