# Amazon reportedly in talks to buy AI startup Decart for around $7B

The Wall Street Journal reports Amazon is in talks to buy Decart, a San Francisco AI startup whose software helps developers switch between different AI chips. The deal would strengthen Amazon's AI infrastructure and portability layer at a time when enterprises are trying to avoid lock-in to any single chip stack. It also fits Amazon's broader effort to add AI product depth beyond AWS capacity.
