# NVIDIA expands Nemotron with enterprise and edge models for always-on agents

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, an open 120-billion-parameter model for enterprise agent workflows, alongside two smaller Bolt models aimed at local and edge deployment. The company says Super VL is optimized for instruction following, tool use, and multimodal workflows, while Bolt targets RTX/DGX Spark and other local systems. The release advances NVIDIA's strategy of pairing its hardware with open, post-trainable models across data-center and PC deployments; comparative performance claims are vendor-reported.
