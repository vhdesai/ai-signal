# Blackstone reportedly expects Anthropic to get a high junk rating after its IPO

Citing the Financial Times, TechCrunch reports that Blackstone, the anchor investor in a $63 billion Anthropic chip-financing deal, now expects a BB-range rating rather than the investment grade it had forecast before 2027. A speculative-grade rating would make debt costlier as Anthropic keeps signing large compute contracts. Anthropic and Blackstone declined to comment. ANTHROPIC LIFE SCIENCES
