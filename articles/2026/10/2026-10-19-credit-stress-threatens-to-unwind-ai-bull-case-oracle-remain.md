# Credit stress threatens to unwind AI bull case; Oracle remains in focus

CNBC reports credit stress is threatening to unwind the AI bull case, with all eyes on Oracle to hold the line. Oracle also faces downgrade risk, debt concerns and continued questions about Project Jupiter and OpenAI-linked infrastructure exposure. This remains the key macro risk for AI equities: demand is huge, but financing structures are increasingly strained.
