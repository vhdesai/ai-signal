# OpenAI Disrupts a Reasoning-Extraction Campaign and Attributes It to Moonshot AI Associates

OpenAI disclosed it disrupted a reasoning-extraction campaign targeting its models, with The Hacker News reporting attribution to associates of Chinese lab Moonshot AI (behind Kimi K3). It is the first time OpenAI has publicly attributed a model-IP-theft attempt to a specific competitor’s associates. DealBook separately reported Dario Amodei is fielding pressure from more aggressive AI CEOs asking why he has been so blunt about AI risks publicly. The attribution sharpens the US–China model-theft discourse ahead of next week’s export-control reviews — and makes "distillation attack" a formal procurement-risk category.
