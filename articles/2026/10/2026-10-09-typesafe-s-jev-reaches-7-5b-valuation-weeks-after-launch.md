# TypeSafe's Jev reaches $7.5B valuation weeks after launch

TypeSafe, maker of the non-text decision model Jev, has reached a $7.5B valuation only weeks after launch. Investor excitement centers on Jev working faster and using fewer tokens than LLMs, suggesting cheap decision models may become the control plane for moderation, routing, security, and agent governance.
