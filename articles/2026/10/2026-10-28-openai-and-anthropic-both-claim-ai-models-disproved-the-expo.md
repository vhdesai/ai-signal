# OpenAI and Anthropic both claim AI models disproved the exponential time hypothesis

OpenAI said its models found an algorithm faster than the exponential time hypothesis allows, contradicting a widely held assumption in theoretical computer science, alongside new physics and math results. Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge then claimed Anthropic's models reached the same result on the same day, using 14 million tokens. The result is not expected to affect encryption immediately, but it marks a step change in AI-driven mathematical discovery.
