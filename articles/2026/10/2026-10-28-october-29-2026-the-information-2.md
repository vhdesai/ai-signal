# [October 29, 2026] · [The Information]

Shopify added Visa, Mastercard and American Express agentic payment tokens to Shop Pay, letting authorized agents spend from a user's card without seeing card details. Berkeley RDI notes that major banks are already detecting unvetted personal agents inside customer accounts, with JPMorgan and Schwab restricting access. Agent identity and scoped delegation are becoming core payments infrastructure.
