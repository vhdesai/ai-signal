# Microsoft and Alphabet earnings split the market's AI scorecard

Microsoft grew revenue 19% to $92.3 billion, Azure rose 48% to $33 billion, and OpenAI-related revenue reached nearly 11% of the total; $18.6 billion of free cash flow beat estimates despite $45.2 billion of quarterly capex. Alphabet grew revenue 25% to $127.6 billion and more than doubled Google Cloud to $30.7 billion on TPU sales, but search grew only about 12% as AI Mode monetizes below classic ads, free cash flow was negative by more than $9 billion, and shares fell roughly 10%. Both reported capex below expectations, with Microsoft trimming its annual forecast to $170 billion and citing third-party GPU capacity delays into the first half of 2027. Investors are now rewarding AI spending that converts to cash and penalizing AI that dilutes a core franchise.
