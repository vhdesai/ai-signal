# SpaceXSI bundles X Premium, Grok, and Cursor into one subscription

SpaceXSI launched a subscription bundle that combines X Premium features with AI tools including Grok and Cursor, with plans from $8 to $200 per month. The bundle shows AI tools being packaged into broader productivity and media subscriptions rather than sold only as standalone model access.
