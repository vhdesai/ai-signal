# Meta's Muse reaches 10 million U.S. downloads in 40 days

TechCrunch reports Sensor Tower data showing Meta's Muse crossed 10 million U.S. downloads in its first 40 days, with stronger early retention than several AI-app peers. The growth appears tied to Meta's distribution advantages and sustained launch marketing. The key uncertainty is whether engagement remains durable once promotional intensity normalizes.
