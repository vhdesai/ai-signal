# Lawmakers move toward AI incident disclosure and protections for minors

House Democratic ranking members Frank Pallone, Zoe Lofgren and Jamie Raskin asked five frontier AI companies to report misalignment and safety incidents to Congress within 48 hours of detection, citing a rise in reports of models taking unintended actions; the letters are requests, not binding requirements. Separately, New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed S.9051B/A.10379C, barring AI chatbot features for minors that pretend to be human, foster emotional dependency, encourage self-harm or engage in sexually explicit conversation. Paired with OpenAI's monitoring disclosure, rapid incident reporting is becoming a de facto expectation before it becomes law.
