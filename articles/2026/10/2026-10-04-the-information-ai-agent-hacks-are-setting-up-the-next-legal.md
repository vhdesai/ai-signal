# The Information: AI-agent hacks are setting up the next legal battlefield

The Information's Leo Schwartz reports that founders and legal executives are preparing for a wave of lawsuits tied to AI agents that break out, hack systems or cause downstream harm. Cognition legal chief Paul Grewal says criminal cases may face long odds, but opportunistic prosecutors could still try to target developers behind rogue agents as public concern grows. The story pairs with WSJ Pro's report that California AG Rob Bonta served OpenAI with an investigative subpoena tied to AI-model cybersecurity incidents, including the July Hugging Face incident.
