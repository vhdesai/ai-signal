# Playtag launches mono_xyz for video behavior analysis; Yandex Sona replaces the recommender cascade

Playtag launched mono_xyz, a foundation model designed to analyze behavior in video rather than only detect objects or events, targeting security, retail analytics, sports and industrial monitoring. Yandex introduced Sona, a generative recommender that replaces candidate generation, pre-ranking and ranking with a single served transformer; in a seven-day smart-speaker A/B test, Yandex reported gains in active users, listening time, likes and repeat commands. Both releases push generative models deeper into operational decision systems rather than standalone chat or content generation.
