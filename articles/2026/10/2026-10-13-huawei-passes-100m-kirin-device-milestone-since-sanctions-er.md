# Huawei passes 100M Kirin-device milestone since sanctions-era comeback

Huawei says it has shipped more than 100M Kirin-powered devices since returning to premium smartphones in 2023, with Kirin 2027 testing progressing as expected. Huawei's domestic silicon comeback is now measured at consumer scale, not only benchmarks.
