# CoreWeave selloff highlights the financing risk of AI expansion

CoreWeave shares fell 15.5% as bond yields approached 15%, exposing concern over debt-funded AI expansion. Oracle also declined, while CoreWeave’s chief executive defended its backlog and financing. Source / freshness note: Published 9:24 AM PDT; updated 1:46 PM PDT. The initial 15% yield touch occurred October 13; the October 14 selloff and executive response are the qualifying developments.
