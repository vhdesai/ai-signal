# NVIDIA releases open Nemotron transcription weights with speaker labels and translation

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe weights under the OpenMDW 1.1 license, supporting 40 languages, speaker labels and translation. Its fastest serving optimizations are not yet open source. The release extends NVIDIA's push to anchor open model ecosystems around its own hardware.
