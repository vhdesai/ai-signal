# Google turns Gemini Enterprise into a single hub for workplace agents

Google is consolidating fragmented workplace agents into a Gemini Enterprise hub aimed at proactive task completion rather than one-off assistance. CIO Dive frames the move as a sign that agentic work is shifting from copilots in individual applications toward shared orchestration layers for enterprise workflows. For senior technology leaders, the governance question becomes who owns policy, identity, auditability and exception handling across that agent hub. API DECISIONING
