# [October 30, 2026] · [Microsoft Bing Blog]

Microsoft is adopting the shared IETF-drafted AI training preference in robots.txt, extending it to the page level, and adding a separate AI grounding preference that can limit grounding to Microsoft-operated experiences such as Copilot. OpenAI separately outlined plans to honor shared training opt-outs in coming months. Splitting training from grounding gives content owners finer leverage in licensing negotiations.
