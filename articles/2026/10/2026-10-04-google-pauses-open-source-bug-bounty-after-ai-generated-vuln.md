# Google pauses open-source bug bounty after AI-generated vulnerability slop overwhelms maintainers

Google paused its Open Source Software Vulnerability Rewards Program until early 2027 after a surge in automated AI-generated vulnerability reports overwhelmed engineers and maintainers. Most submissions were invalid, underscoring that AI can create defensive surface area by flooding triage pipelines, not just by improving exploit discovery. For security leaders, the story is a practical warning that AI-generated reports need intake controls, reputation systems and automated deduplication before they hit human queues.
