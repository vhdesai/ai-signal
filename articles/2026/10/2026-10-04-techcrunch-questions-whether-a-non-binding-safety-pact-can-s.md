# TechCrunch questions whether a non-binding safety pact can solve AI's image problem

TechCrunch analyzed the White House-backed "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities" and the broader attempt to rebrand AI as "super intelligence." The discussion was skeptical that a voluntary, non-binding pact will materially change frontier-lab incentives or safety behavior. The key takeaway is that symbolic alignment among AI leaders may help public messaging, but durable trust likely requires enforceable controls, measurable standards, and incident transparency.
