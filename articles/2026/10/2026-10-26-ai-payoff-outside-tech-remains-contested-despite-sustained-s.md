# AI payoff outside tech remains contested despite sustained spending

Business Insider highlights an Apollo Global Management view that AI has yet to produce broad financial payoff outside core technology sectors. This sharpens executive pressure to translate AI deployment into measurable unit economics and productivity outcomes. The near-term implication is tighter budget scrutiny for AI initiatives without hard business impact evidence.
