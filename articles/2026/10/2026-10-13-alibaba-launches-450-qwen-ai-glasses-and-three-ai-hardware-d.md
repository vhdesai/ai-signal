# Alibaba launches $450 Qwen AI glasses and three AI hardware devices

Alibaba launched Qwen N1 AI glasses in China starting around $450 and introduced three AI hardware devices tied to its Qwen personal-agent ecosystem. The move expands Alibaba's consumer AI footprint beyond apps and cloud APIs into hardware. It puts Alibaba directly into competition with Meta's smart glasses, Apple's home devices and China's growing AI-phone ecosystem.
