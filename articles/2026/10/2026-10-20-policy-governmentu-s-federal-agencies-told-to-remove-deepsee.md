# POLICY GOVERNMENTU.S. federal agencies told to remove DeepSeek from their systems

TechCrunch reports that U.S. federal civilian agencies must stop purchasing DeepSeek products and remove them from government systems, including some third-party offerings, subject to exceptions. The restrictions apply to federal agencies—not as a general private-sector ban—and sharpen the need to document model provenance.
