# Canva releases its first in-house image generation model

TechCrunch reports Canva launched Canva Craft, a two-stage generation model designed for layout-aware outputs, text handling, and more controllable edits. The model is available to Canva users and via API, with planned integration paths into third-party chatbot workflows through MCP. Strategically, Canva is shifting from external-model dependence toward tighter cost and quality control in its core product loop.
