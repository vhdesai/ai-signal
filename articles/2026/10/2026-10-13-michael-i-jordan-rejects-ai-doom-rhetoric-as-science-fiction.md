# Michael I. Jordan rejects AI doom rhetoric as "science fiction"

Machine-learning pioneer Michael I. Jordan criticized superintelligence warnings as science fiction and called parts of the current AI-risk discourse a "grand moralizing performance." The comments highlight a widening split among senior AI researchers on risk framing.
