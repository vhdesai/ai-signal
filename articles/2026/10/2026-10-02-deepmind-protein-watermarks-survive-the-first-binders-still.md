# DeepMind protein watermarks survive — the first binders still bind

Mixed Reality News documents the critical validation step for DeepMind's AI-protein watermarking system: the first generation of watermarked binders still bind their intended targets. That answers the critical "does watermarking damage function" question — essential for biosecurity utility. Expect this to accelerate regulator comfort with mandatory biowatermarking in NIH-adjacent AI protein pipelines.
