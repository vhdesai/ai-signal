# China chip exports jump 136% to $45B as AI demand lifts prices

China's integrated-circuit exports surged 136% year over year in September to $45B, outpacing the first nine months. Export volume rose only 1%, meaning higher prices drove the value jump amid memory-chip tightness and AI demand.
