# Amazon Science reports priming method to alter model architecture without full retraining

Amazon Science published research on priming, a method for adapting pretrained Transformer weights into hybrid state-space architectures without retraining from scratch. The approach is intended to reduce the pretraining cost of exploring hybrid architectures by more than 99%, enabling comparisons across attention, sparse-attention, sliding-window, and state-space designs at larger scales. If the results generalize, priming could reduce the cost of designing long-context and agent-optimized models with better memory and latency tradeoffs. Academic Research M CIVIC AI ACCOUNTABILITY
