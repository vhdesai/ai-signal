# On TechCrunch's Equity podcast, Wiz's Tomer Schwartz said the team could not have found last week's critical PHP bugs…

On TechCrunch's Equity podcast, Wiz's Tomer Schwartz said the team could not have found last week's critical PHP bugs without AI, and described custom tooling that tested dozens of exploit ideas over a weekend. He argued that patching by hand will not scale as AI accelerates discovery of long-dormant flaws in aging code. The takeaway for security leaders is to plan for higher vulnerability volume and faster disclosure cycles.
