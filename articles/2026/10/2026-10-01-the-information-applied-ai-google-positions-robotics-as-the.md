# The Information Applied AI: Google positions robotics as the "Android" play to Tesla's "Apple"

In his first sit-down since taking over as CEO of Google DeepMind, Koray Kavukcuoglu told The Information's Jessica Lessin that Google will not chase a single flagship humanoid; instead it is pursuing a software/intelligence play with Gemini Robotics running across many partner platforms like Boston Dynamics. Google separately launched Gemini 4 Argon to a select group of cyber-defense customers this week — a leading indicator for how quickly it closes the general-model gap with Anthropic and OpenAI. Lessin notes a historical echo: eventually, Google may verticalize into its own humanoid, mirroring the Android→Pixel playbook.
