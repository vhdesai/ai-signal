# Isomorphic Labs reportedly seeks funding at a $40B valuation

Dealroom reports DeepMind spinout Isomorphic Labs is seeking funding at a $40B valuation, up sharply from its $2.1B round in May. The valuation reflects investor conviction that AI drug discovery is moving from research promise into a platform business.
