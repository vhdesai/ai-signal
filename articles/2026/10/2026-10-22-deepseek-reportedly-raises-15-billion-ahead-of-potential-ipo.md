# DeepSeek reportedly raises $15 billion ahead of potential IPO

A Wall Street Journal report distributed via Morningstar said DeepSeek raised $15 billion with strategic participation from major Chinese investors. The report, if sustained, would signal continued concentration of capital around frontier model labs despite tighter policy and infrastructure constraints.
