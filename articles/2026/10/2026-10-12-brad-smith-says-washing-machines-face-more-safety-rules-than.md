# Brad Smith says washing machines face more safety rules than AI

Microsoft President Brad Smith argued AI is currently less regulated than washing machines and called for more AI kill switches and safety obligations. His comments extend Satya Nadella's "emergency brake" theme and align with Microsoft's new agent execution-container and Windows guardrail efforts. The framing is designed to make AI regulation sound like ordinary product safety rather than exotic frontier governance.
