# TechCrunch reports on a Convovo study led by a Cornell-affiliated researcher examining forced-choice election…

The study found pronounced directional divergence across models under constrained prompting conditions. For enterprise governance teams, the practical implication is to harden sensitive-domain policy controls and continuously test behavioral drift.
