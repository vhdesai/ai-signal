# OpenAI seeks $30B in fresh capital at $1.4T valuation after revenue correction

OpenAI is reportedly negotiating to raise at least $30B at a $1.4T valuation, even after its annualized revenue run rate was corrected from $70B to roughly $50B. The fundraising would give OpenAI a larger capital cushion, but raises the bar on monetizing ChatGPT ads, enterprise workflows, and API usage.
