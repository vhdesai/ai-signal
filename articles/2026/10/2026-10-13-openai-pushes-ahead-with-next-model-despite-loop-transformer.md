# OpenAI pushes ahead with next model despite loop-transformer safety concerns and eyes Trail of Bits

The Information reports OpenAI is trying to fix safety issues before shipping its next flagship model after using loop transformers, a design that can make model reasoning harder to monitor. Microsoft accidentally confirmed OpenAI's use of the technique in GPT-6.1 Sol before editing the disclosure out of a blog post. Separately, OpenAI is in early talks to acquire Trail of Bits, which would deepen its cybersecurity business and open-source vulnerability capabilities.
