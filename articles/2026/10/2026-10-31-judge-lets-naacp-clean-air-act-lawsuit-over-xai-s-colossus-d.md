# Judge lets NAACP Clean Air Act lawsuit over xAI's Colossus data center proceed

A federal judge denied xAI's motion to dismiss claims that it ran at least 35 gas turbines at its Colossus site near Memphis without the required air permits. The judge rejected xAI's arguments that the turbines are exempt "mobile sources," that the plaintiffs lack standing, and that citizen suits are unconstitutional. A similar Justice Department motion was denied earlier in October.
