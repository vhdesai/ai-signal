# Otter.ai secures $320 million from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund

Meeting assistant provider Otter.ai secured $320 million from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, a non-dilutive financing vehicle tied to customer growth outcomes rather than a conventional equity round. Otter says it will use the capital for enterprise growth, international expansion, and product development; it reports 40 million lifetime users but did not disclose current active-user or churn figures. The investment reinforces the push to turn meeting capture into actionable enterprise workflows, while retention and differentiation remain key tests in a crowded market.
