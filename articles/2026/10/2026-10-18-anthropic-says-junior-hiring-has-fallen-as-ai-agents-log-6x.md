# Anthropic says junior hiring has fallen as AI agents log 6x human work hours

Anthropic says junior roles among new technical hires fell from 27% to 9% since early 2025, while AI agents logged six times as many hours as humans by August 2026. Researchers warn of a "tragedy of the cognitive commons" if apprenticeship paths disappear. INDUSTRY
