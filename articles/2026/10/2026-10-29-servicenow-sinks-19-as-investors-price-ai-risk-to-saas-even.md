# ServiceNow sinks 19% as investors price AI risk to SaaS, even as it deepens OpenAI ties

ServiceNow shares fell 19% after earnings despite 20.5% revenue growth to just over $4.1 billion, an 80% jump in AI deal volume and a higher AI annual-contract-value target of $1.7 billion by end-2026. Investors focused instead on current remaining performance obligations, which ServiceNow expects to slow to 18.5% growth. The same day, OpenAI and ServiceNow expanded their partnership, making ServiceNow's MCP plugin available in OpenAI's marketplace. For enterprise software, AI attach is no longer enough; markets want proof that agents expand rather than erode seat-based economics.
