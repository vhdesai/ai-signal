# MIT advances collision-free drone planning under uncertainty

MIT described SANDO, a planning system designed to give drones mathematical guarantees against collisions with unknown moving obstacles. The significance is the combination of learning-adjacent autonomy with formal safety constraints, a requirement for real-world robotics deployment in crowded or dynamic settings. Results span simulations and real flights, making this a useful marker for where autonomy research is becoming operationally grounded. URL(s): MIT News
