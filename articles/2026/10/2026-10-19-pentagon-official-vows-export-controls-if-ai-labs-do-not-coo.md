# Pentagon official vows export controls if AI labs do not cooperate with government

The Information reports Defense Under Secretary Emil Michael warned that export controls could follow if AI labs do not cooperate with the U.S. government. Michael, part of the Trump administration's Super Intelligence Force, is charged with liaising with AI companies to develop protocols for cyber breaches, hacks and other critical incidents. The remarks show the U.S. may use export-control leverage not only against foreign rivals, but to shape domestic lab behavior.
