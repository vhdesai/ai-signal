# Voxel51 3.0 targets multimodal data operations for robotics, vehicles, and factories

Voxel51 released version 3.0 of its multimodal data platform, adding time-series multimodal support, unified annotation, agentic labeling, data provenance, and a Voxel51 Agent with MCP tools and open-source skills. The platform targets teams training physical AI systems on images, video, 3D point clouds, audio, geospatial data, and other sensor streams. The practical significance is that physical AI programs are increasingly constrained by data search, curation, annotation, and reproducibility rather than model architecture alone.
