# Citing The Information and Bloomberg, TechCrunch reports Nvidia is discussing a residual value guarantee for…

Citing The Information and Bloomberg, TechCrunch reports Nvidia is discussing a residual value guarantee for construction financing on Nscale's West Virginia campus, where Anthropic has signed a $44.6 billion contract for the first 460 MW building. Nvidia's earlier support for cloud providers mostly covered leases and chips, not construction. The move shows vendor financing extending deeper into the AI capital stack as lenders grow cautious and Nscale has struggled with deployment pace and site issues.
