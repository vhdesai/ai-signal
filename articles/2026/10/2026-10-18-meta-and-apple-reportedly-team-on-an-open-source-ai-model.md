# Meta and Apple reportedly team on an open-source AI model

Android Headlines reports Meta and Apple are teaming up on a powerful open-source AI model. If confirmed, the partnership would be unusual given Apple's privacy-first posture and Meta's aggressive consumer-agent strategy. It would also signal that even the largest consumer-tech companies may need shared open infrastructure to compete with OpenAI, Google and Chinese open models.
