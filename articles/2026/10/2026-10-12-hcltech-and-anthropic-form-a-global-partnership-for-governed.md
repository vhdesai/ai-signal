# HCLTech and Anthropic form a global partnership for governed agentic AI

HCLTech announced a Global Premier Partnership with Anthropic to scale Claude, Claude Code and related agentic AI deployments, including a dedicated Anthropic Business Unit and Center of Excellence. The partnership expands Anthropic’s enterprise channel even as the company faces scrutiny over agent safety incidents. It also shows systems integrators becoming a primary route for converting frontier models into governed enterprise programs. URL(s): Yahoo Finance
