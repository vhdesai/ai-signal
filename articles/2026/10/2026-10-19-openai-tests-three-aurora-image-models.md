# OpenAI tests three Aurora image models

OpenAI is testing three Aurora models focused on image generation, according to Geeky Gadgets and TestingCatalog follow-up coverage. The tests suggest OpenAI is continuing to harden its multimodal pipeline after rolling out GPT-6, Intelligent UI and interactive ChatGPT graphics. Image generation remains strategically important because it drives consumer engagement, creative workflows and advertising products.
