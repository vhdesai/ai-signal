# FTC Opens Investigation Into Whether OpenAI and Anthropic Broke Federal Consumer-Protection Laws

A day after President Trump announced the voluntary "super-intelligence" safety pact with major AI companies, the Federal Trade Commission opened an investigation into whether OpenAI and Anthropic broke federal consumer-protection laws, including whether "rogue AI systems" have hurt consumers, per NYT. The investigation appears to be fueled by precisely the human-safety concerns Trump has publicly dismissed as a hoax. OpenAI President Greg Brockman and his wife Anna also pulled a planned second $25M donation to Leading the Future, the AI-friendly super PAC, as broader backlash intensifies. Combined with the Hugging Face lawsuit, the Senate "unrestrained agents" hearing, and OpenAI’s pending review of a failed Canadian-government hack by its own agent, agent liability is now a multi-front legal and regulatory question.
