# Meta unveils Muse Wonder models to compete with OpenAI

Meta unveiled Muse Wonder, a stronger model family for its Muse assistant, with early coverage positioning the release as a direct challenge to OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini. Meta says the models improve work and multi-step task performance, and the release arrives while Muse still has strong consumer momentum despite privacy and safety scrutiny.
