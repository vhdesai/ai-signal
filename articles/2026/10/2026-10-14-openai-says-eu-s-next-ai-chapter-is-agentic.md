# OpenAI says EU's next AI chapter is agentic

OpenAI published new analysis arguing that agentic AI adoption is growing across EU enterprises and small businesses, but deeper use could unlock more value. The post is both market analysis and policy positioning: OpenAI is trying to frame agents as Europe's productivity tool rather than only a regulatory risk.
