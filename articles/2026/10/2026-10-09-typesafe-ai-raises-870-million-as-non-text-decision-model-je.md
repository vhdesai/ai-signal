# TypeSafe AI raises $870 million as non-text decision model Jev gains enterprise traction

TechCrunch reports that TypeSafe AI, developer of the non-text model Jev, raised $870 million at a $7.5 billion valuation led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Sequoia and DCVC participating. Jev does not generate prose; it returns calibrated probabilities for decisions, positioning the company in a fast-emerging category aimed at automation rather than content generation. The claimed early adoption by Fortune 500 companies suggests investor interest is moving toward models that can be directly embedded in software control loops. W MARKET RISK
