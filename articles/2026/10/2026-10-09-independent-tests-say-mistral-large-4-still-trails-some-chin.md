# Independent tests say Mistral Large 4 still trails some Chinese open models

Tom's Hardware reports Mistral's new Large 4 trails some Chinese open models in independent testing, despite Mistral's claim that "Le Chonk" leads outside China. BankInfoSecurity separately framed the model as a capable European sovereign offering. The practical takeaway is nuanced: Mistral may have Europe's strongest open-weight model, but Chinese open systems remain the benchmark to beat.
