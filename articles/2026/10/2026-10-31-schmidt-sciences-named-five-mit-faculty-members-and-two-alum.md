# Schmidt Sciences named five MIT faculty members and two alumni to its 2026 AI2050 cohort, part of a $12 million program…

Schmidt Sciences named five MIT faculty members and two alumni to its 2026 AI2050 cohort, part of a $12 million program for research on beneficial AI. Senior Fellow Sendhil Mullainathan is focused on human-centered AI through MIT's new Bike Shop research center. The fellowship continues philanthropic funding for long-horizon AI problems outside commercial lab priorities.
