# Lambda seeks up to $4B ahead of planned 2027 IPO

Nvidia-backed AI compute provider Lambda is raising up to $4B at a $14.5B pre-money valuation in a round led by Coatue and Blackstone, ahead of a planned IPO in 2027. The financing would put Lambda into the same late-stage neocloud capital lane as CoreWeave and Nscale, but with a more explicit enterprise-compute orientation. https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/ai-computing-startup-lambda-to-raise-4b-ahead-of-planned-ipo/ INDUSTRY
