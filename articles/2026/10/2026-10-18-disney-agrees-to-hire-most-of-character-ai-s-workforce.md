# Disney agrees to hire most of Character.AI's workforce

Disney agreed to hire the majority of Character.AI's workforce as part of the bankrupt chatbot company's asset sale process. The move turns Character.AI's remaining value into talent and select assets rather than a standalone consumer chatbot platform.
