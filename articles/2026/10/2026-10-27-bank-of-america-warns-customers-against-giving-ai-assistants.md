# Bank of America warns customers against giving AI assistants banking passwords

TechCrunch reports Bank of America publicly reminded customers not to share credentials with AI assistants and emphasized user responsibility for resulting activity. This is a practical policy marker for regulated sectors: assistant convenience is now colliding with established identity and account-control rules.
