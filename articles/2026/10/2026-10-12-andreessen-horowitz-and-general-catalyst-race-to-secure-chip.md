# Andreessen Horowitz and General Catalyst race to secure chips for startups

The Information reports Andreessen Horowitz is exploring ways to expand compute access for its startups, potentially through backed data-center companies, while General Catalyst is building a data center to supply portfolio companies. Several other VC funds are using brokers to locate scarce GPUs. Compute access is becoming a platform service and competitive differentiator for venture firms, not just a cloud procurement problem.
