# VentureBeat warns vibe-coded apps are a business security risk

VentureBeat argued that vibe-coded applications can become ticking time bombs for businesses when prototypes move into production without dependency review, identity boundaries, secure secrets handling, or ownership. The warning reflects a broader enterprise pattern: AI coding tools reduce build friction faster than governance processes evolve. Teams adopting vibe coding should add review gates for architecture, data access, security scanning, and operational support before deployment. Infrastructure ORBITAL COMPUTEGOOGLE
