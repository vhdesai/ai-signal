# Morgan Stanley says Nvidia and Broadcom are shielded as AI power crunch hits chip supply chain

Reuters reported, via Morgan Stanley analysis, that Nvidia and Broadcom are relatively shielded as AI power constraints pressure parts of the chip supply chain. The story reinforces that the AI bottleneck is no longer only silicon availability but power delivery, data-center readiness, and associated supply-chain exposure. For technology leaders, compute plans increasingly need power-risk analysis alongside GPU procurement. URL behind paywall.
