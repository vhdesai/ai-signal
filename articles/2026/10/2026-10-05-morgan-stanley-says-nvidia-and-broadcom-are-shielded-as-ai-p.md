# Morgan Stanley says Nvidia and Broadcom are shielded as AI power crunch hits chip supply chain

Reuters reported, citing Morgan Stanley, that Nvidia and Broadcom are relatively shielded as AI-related power constraints put pressure on parts of the chip supply chain. The report reinforces that the AI bottleneck is no longer only silicon availability but power delivery, data-center readiness, and deployment timing.
