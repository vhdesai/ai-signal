# OpenAI makes progress reducing ChatGPT delusions

The Wall Street Journal reports OpenAI has made progress reducing AI-driven ChatGPT delusions, a core safety and product-quality issue for consumer assistants. The timing matters because ChatGPT is adding more interactive and agentic features, which raises the cost of confused or false outputs. Progress on delusions could help OpenAI rebuild trust after recent agent and teen-safety controversies.
