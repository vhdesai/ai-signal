# Anthropic introduces Claude Duet for professional agent workflows

TechCrunch reports Anthropic launched Claude Duet in research preview for Pro and Max users via waitlist, positioning it for business and productivity use cases. The app emphasizes background task execution with approval gates for sensitive actions and broad connector support. The launch reflects intensifying competition among personal and work-focused agent products.
