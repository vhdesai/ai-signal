# NYSE's Lynn Martin says Anthropic's listing is holding back the IPO pipeline

Anthropic publicly filed its IPO paperwork on Monday for a Nasdaq listing, and NYSE Group President Lynn Martin said the pipeline is strong but has been held back while portfolio managers focus on the megadeal. Prior reports put Anthropic's potential raise as high as $100 billion. Companies such as Nscale, which filed publicly last month, are waiting for Anthropic to go first.
