# Meta's Muse passes 10 million users, putting monetization in focus

WSJ reports that Muse reached roughly 10 million users in just over a month; Meta says it onboarded more than 10 million in the US and Canada in the early weeks of launch. The adoption signal is strong, and Meta says shopping leads use cases, but durable revenue per user and inference costs remain the more important economic tests. Its separate equity-raising plan makes that distinction especially relevant. URLs: WSJ article · Meta announcement
