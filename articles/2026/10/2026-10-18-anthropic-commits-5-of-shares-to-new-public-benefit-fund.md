# Anthropic commits 5% of shares to new public benefit fund

Anthropic announced that, subject to stockholder approval, it will commit 5% of company shares to a new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, with potential additional allocations tied to future valuation milestones. The company says the fund will be structured as a wholly owned LLC and will not use Anthropic equity for lobbying or political giving. The move is strategically notable because it links AI company valuation growth directly to a public-benefit vehicle at a time when labor-market disruption and safety governance are becoming central legitimacy questions for frontier labs.
