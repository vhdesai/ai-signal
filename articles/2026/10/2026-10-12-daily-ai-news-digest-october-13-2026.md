# Daily AI News Digest – October 13, 2026

The last 24 hours sharpened three themes: agentic commerce needs explicit authorization rails, frontier competition is broadening beyond OpenAI and Anthropic, and AI infrastructure is being standardized for factory-scale deployment. The strongest enterprise signal is a shift from model novelty to production control surfaces: purchase intent tokens, local-agent sandboxes, legal-domain benchmarks, and open AI-factory reference designs. Safety and accountability remain intertwined with deployment, especially where AI-generated speech, civic feedback, and agent actions need a responsible human or institution behind them.
