# American Express introduces an intent framework for agentic commerce

American Express introduced an Intent Intelligence Framework intended to prevent AI agents from making unauthorized purchases. The framework captures customer purchase instructions, authenticates the user, creates an Intent ID, and issues a Proof of Intent Token that can be used to obtain a secure digital payment credential instead of exposing the card number. The move is important because payments networks are beginning to define control layers for autonomous agents before agentic commerce scales into a dispute and fraud problem.
