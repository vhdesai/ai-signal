# Shopify debuts Canvas, letting merchants build storefronts by chatting with Sidekick

Shopify's new Canvas site builder lets merchants describe the storefront they want and have Sidekick build and re-arrange it in real time. The release packages Sidekick (Shopify's agent) as a direct competitor to Wix's AI builder, Framer's AI tools, and Lovable — all of which have been climbing the vibe-coding usage curve. For Shopify it also doubles as a defensive moat against ChatGPT's apparel shopping and Google's Flipkart Gemini pilot moving down into merchant-side tooling.
