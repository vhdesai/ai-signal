# Nvidia releases Nemotron models for enterprise agents and local AI PCs

Nvidia introduced the 120-billion-parameter Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for enterprise agents, alongside smaller Bolt models intended for local use on AI PCs and workstations. The releases extend Nvidia’s strategy from supplying compute to offering open-weight models that customers can post-train and deploy on their own infrastructure; the company’s comparative performance claims are vendor-reported.
