# Danu Robotics pushes AI waste-sorting toward commercial reliability

Danu Robotics is working on AI-powered recycling robots for mixed recyclable waste, one of the messiest real-world robotics domains. The story is a useful reminder that robotics value often comes from narrow perception, grasping, throughput, and reliability gains rather than deploying the largest general model.
