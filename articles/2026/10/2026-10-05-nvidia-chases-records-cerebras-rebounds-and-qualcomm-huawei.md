# Nvidia chases records, Cerebras rebounds and Qualcomm-Huawei patent deal keeps AI hardware geopolitics tangled

Barron's and CNBC report Nvidia shares are chasing records, with options traders watching a potential $6T market-cap milestone. Cerebras also rebounded after Sam Altman called it a close OpenAI partner, reviving the specialized-inference-cloud thesis. Meanwhile, Huawei and Qualcomm signed a multiyear patent license covering 5G, compute, AI and networking; Bloomberg adds Qualcomm is licensing patents tied to Huawei's LogicFolding chip technology, showing semiconductor IP remains intertwined despite US-China AI hardware separation.
