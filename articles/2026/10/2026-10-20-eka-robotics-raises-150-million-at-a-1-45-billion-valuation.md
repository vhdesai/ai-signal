# Eka Robotics raises $150 million at a $1.45 billion valuation

Eka Robotics closed a $150 million Series A at a $1.45 billion post-money valuation, based on a manipulation system combining a force-sensitive gripper and a vision-force-action model. The company says its robot can handle about 1,500 objects per hour and succeeds on more than 95% of picks; if those results generalize beyond demonstrations, tactile feedback and reinforcement learning may be meaningful differentiators for industrial robotics.
