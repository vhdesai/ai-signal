# OpenCode breach could affect OpenAI, Anthropic and Google

Infosecurity Magazine and DevOps.com report an OpenCode breach may affect OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, putting renewed focus on AI distillation threats and the software supply chain around coding tools. The incident highlights how shared developer infrastructure can expose multiple frontier labs at once. It also reinforces why provenance, credential hygiene and secure coding-agent environments are now core AI security concerns.
