# Daishin Securities reported exposure of 38,490 customers' personal data, while Mirae Asset Global Investments and…

Daishin Securities reported exposure of 38,490 customers' personal data, while Mirae Asset Global Investments and Bithumb reported separate incidents. The attacks follow a September wave that researchers linked to a suspected China-based actor using an AI penetration-testing tool called Artex, although the root cause of the latest breaches is still under investigation. South Korea's financial regulator is pushing stronger controls and private-sector security checks.
