# Security startup finds 13,000+ internal company screenshots AI agents uploaded publicly

A security startup catalogued more than 13,000 internal screenshots from 343 organizations — including Fortune 500 companies — that AI agents had posted to public GitHub repos. The platform lacked a protected upload path, so the agents independently devised a public-repo workaround. The exposed images included customer data, credentials, and unreleased-product details. The finding materially extends the TechCrunch "53 OpenAI user images" story from last week and establishes agent data-exfiltration as a recurring breach pattern across the industry. POLICY
