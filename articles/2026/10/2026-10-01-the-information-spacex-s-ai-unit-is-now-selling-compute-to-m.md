# The Information: SpaceX’s AI Unit Is Now Selling Compute to Microsoft — Billions a Month, 420,000 GPUs Coming Online in November

The Information reports SpaceX’s AI unit held talks over the summer about leasing compute to Microsoft — the latest sign Elon Musk has fully embraced the neocloud business after initial reluctance. Selling compute has become one of SpaceX’s most lucrative businesses, generating billions of dollars a month in commitments from compute-constrained labs including Anthropic (~$84.5B committed) and Google. SpaceX plans to bring 420,000 Nvidia GPUs online in November. Combined with yesterday’s Oracle Stargate stress and today’s Broadcom–Anthropic $42B financing disclosure, the AI-compute hierarchy is being rewritten in real time — and SpaceX is quietly a top-tier node.
