# Alibaba launches $450 Qwen AI glasses and always-on vision hardware

Alibaba launched Qwen N1 AI glasses in China starting around $450 and introduced three AI hardware devices tied to its Qwen personal-agent ecosystem. Himax's WiseEye ultralow-power AI processor is being integrated into the Qwen Glasses N1 series to support always-on sensing without keeping the main system active. That gives Alibaba a hardware differentiator as it competes with Meta's smart glasses, Apple's home devices and China's AI-phone ecosystem.
