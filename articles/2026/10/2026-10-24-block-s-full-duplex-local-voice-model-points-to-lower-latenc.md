# Block's full-duplex local voice model points to lower-latency assistant economics

TechCrunch reports Block introduced Dessa, a voice model designed to speak and listen concurrently while running on consumer-grade hardware. If validated at scale, local-first voice architectures could reduce inference costs and improve responsiveness for high-frequency workflows. This is a practical step toward less cloud-dependent voice-agent deployment. URL: TechCrunch
