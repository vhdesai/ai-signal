# Microsoft warns enterprises to treat AI agents like insider threats

Microsoft argues that organizations should closely monitor agents employees use, particularly systems outside the control of established AI labs, because frontier models cannot be assumed trustworthy. The framing puts agent governance alongside identity and insider-risk controls: organizations need to account for what an agent can access and do, not only how well it answers prompts.
