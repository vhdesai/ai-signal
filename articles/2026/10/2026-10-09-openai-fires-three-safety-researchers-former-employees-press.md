# OpenAI fires three safety researchers; former employees press for outside auditors

OpenAI fired three safety researchers amid a dispute over AI risks, extending a turbulent two-week stretch for its safety organization. WSJ Pro Cybersecurity reports the fired employees are now calling on OpenAI to work with outside safety auditors and preserve the ability to monitor increasingly sophisticated models' chain-of-thought. The story keeps OpenAI's internal safety governance in the foreground at the same time the company is facing teen-safety pressure, agent incidents, and revenue-expectation confusion.
