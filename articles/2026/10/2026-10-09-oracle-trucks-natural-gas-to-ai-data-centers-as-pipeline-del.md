# Oracle trucks natural gas to AI data centers as pipeline delays threaten projects

Quartz reports Oracle is trucking natural gas to AI data centers because pipeline delays threaten key projects. The workaround underlines how AI infrastructure constraints are no longer just about chips: power, fuel, interconnection, permitting, and construction timing are now critical path items. It gives tangible color to concerns about Oracle's AI data-center financing and execution risk.
