# Mistral previews Large 4, adding pressure to the open-weight frontier race

Mistral opened an API preview of Large 4, a trillion-parameter model, with open weights planned later in October. The strategic signal is less about one benchmark table than about Europe trying to keep a credible open-weight alternative in the frontier conversation as U.S. and Chinese labs compress release cycles. For enterprise buyers, the interesting question is whether open-weight economics can materially pressure closed-model pricing without sacrificing governance, support, and reliability.
