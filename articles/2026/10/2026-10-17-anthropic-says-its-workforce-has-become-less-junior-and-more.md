# Anthropic says its workforce has become less junior and more meeting-heavy

TechCrunch reported on Anthropic’s workforce study showing junior technical hiring share falling from 27% to 9% while meeting time rose 20%. The pattern is a useful early signal for AI-era organization design: firms may hire fewer junior contributors while coordination overhead rises around complex AI systems. Leaders should watch whether productivity gains are offset by review, alignment and cross-functional governance costs. URL(s): TechCrunch
