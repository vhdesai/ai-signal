# Runware plans an assembly line for prefabricated AI data center pods

Runware says its Romania factory will scale to 30 MW of modular data center capacity per day by 2028, with pods online within 48 hours of delivery. A second factory is due early next year, and customers can rent compute or bring their own GPUs. The plan is unproven at scale but reflects pressure to compress deployment timelines to match GPU lead times.
