# Baseten's joey-1 targets cheap, calibrated routing decisions

Baseten released joey-1, an MIT-licensed 2-billion-parameter model that returns calibrated probabilities over typed options instead of free text, priced at about $0.01 per million tokens with roughly 20 ms median latency. It targets routing, classification and guardrail checks, with 9B and 27B siblings sharing one API. Small decision models let teams route routine requests cheaply and escalate only uncertain cases.
