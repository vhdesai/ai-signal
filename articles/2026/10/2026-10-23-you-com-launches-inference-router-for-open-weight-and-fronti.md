# You.com launches Inference Router for open-weight and frontier-model access

AiThority reports You.com launched an OpenAI-compatible inference router with SLA-backed reliability claims. The product pitch is operational control across mixed model backends rather than single-model primacy. If adopted, this could lower provider-switching friction while centralizing production governance.
