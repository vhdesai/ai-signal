# PARTNERSHIP HEALTHCAREAstraZeneca and Oracle partner on AI-enabled clinical research

AstraZeneca and Oracle announced a collaboration spanning clinical-trial matching, care pathways, and safety monitoring, with an initial hypertension program in Abu Dhabi alongside M42. The companies say projects will use de-identified data under governance safeguards; clinical outcomes and execution will determine the partnership's value.
