# Claude Haiku 5.5 launches with up to 90% token-price cuts

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, lifting OSWorld computer-use performance from 15.7% to 72.4% and cutting token prices by up to 90%. The strategic signal is clear: low-cost computer use is becoming a default expectation, not a premium tier.
