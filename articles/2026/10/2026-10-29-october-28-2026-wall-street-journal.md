# [October 28, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

Microsoft reported accelerating cloud growth and strong free cash flow despite heavy capex, while Alphabet's shares fell as AI-heavy search monetized below classic ads even as Google Cloud surged on TPU sales. Both companies reported capex below expectations, with Microsoft trimming its annual forecast to $170 billion and citing third-party GPU delivery delays. Investors are rewarding AI spending that converts to cash and penalizing AI that dilutes a core franchise.
