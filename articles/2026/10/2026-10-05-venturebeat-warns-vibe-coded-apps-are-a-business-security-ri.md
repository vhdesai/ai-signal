# VentureBeat warns vibe-coded apps are a business security risk

VentureBeat warned that vibe-coded apps can become ticking time bombs for businesses when prototypes move into production without dependency review, secure secret handling, identity boundaries, and operational ownership. The issue is not simply that AI writes code, but that it reduces build friction faster than control processes adapt. Enterprises should treat AI-generated apps as software supply-chain artifacts requiring the same architecture, security, and lifecycle discipline as hand-built systems.
