# Iluvatar CoreX unveils 144-GPU AI supernode in one cabinet

Chinese AI chipmaker Iluvatar CoreX debuted the Tiangai 300 IXL144, packing 144 GPUs and 18 CPUs into a single cabinet and claiming about 10.1 petaflops of AI compute. Chinese AI infrastructure vendors are focusing on system-level packaging and interconnect density, not only GPU-by-GPU substitution for Nvidia.
