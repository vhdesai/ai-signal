# Drug discovery becomes a frontier-lab growth story for Anthropic and Isomorphic Labs

Business Insider reports that Anthropic's S-1 positions Claude as core infrastructure for biological and medicinal research ahead of its expected November IPO. It also profiles Isomorphic Labs, the Google DeepMind spinout led by Demis Hassabis, and its push to design cancer drugs with AI.
