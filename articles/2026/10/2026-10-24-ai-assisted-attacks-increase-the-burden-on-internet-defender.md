# AI-assisted attacks increase the burden on internet defenders

WSJ reports growing use of AI in cyberattacks and mounting pressure on defenders. Faster offense increases the value of timely patching, incident visibility, and resilient response processes. The reporting signals operational risk, not proof that every observed attack was fully automated. URL: WSJ article
