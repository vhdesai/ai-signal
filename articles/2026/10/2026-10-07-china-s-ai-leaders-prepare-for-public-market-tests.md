# China’s AI leaders prepare for public-market tests

PitchBook highlighted reports that Moonshot AI and DeepSeek may pursue IPOs next year, with Moonshot reportedly valued around $50B and AI representing roughly half of 2026 Greater China VC deal value. The financing signal is important even without a near-term listing: China’s AI ecosystem is moving from private funding momentum toward public-market validation. That transition will test whether open-model adoption can convert into durable revenue capture.
