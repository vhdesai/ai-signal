# Anthropic filing details growing cloud-capacity strain

Reporting on Anthropic's leaked S-1 indicates the company is balancing strong growth with very large infrastructure commitments and dependence on hyperscaler capacity. The filing discussion highlights concentration and reliability risk even for top-tier model labs. Compute access and contract structure are increasingly material to valuation and execution, not just benchmark performance.
