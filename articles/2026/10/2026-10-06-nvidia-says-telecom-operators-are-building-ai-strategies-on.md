# Nvidia says telecom operators are building AI strategies on open models

Nvidia reported that telecom operators are increasingly using open models for AI strategies across autonomous networks, customer care, and sovereign/local AI services. Its State of AI in Telecommunications report found 89% of respondents say open-source models and software are important to their AI strategy. The implication is that telcos are positioning themselves not just as AI users, but as trusted local AI infrastructure providers with control over data, deployment, and regulatory fit.
