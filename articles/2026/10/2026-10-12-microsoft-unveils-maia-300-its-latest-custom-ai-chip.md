# Microsoft unveils Maia 300, its latest custom AI chip

Microsoft revealed Maia 300, a TSMC N2P-based custom AI accelerator it plans to deploy at multi-gigawatt scale. Reuters, CNBC and Microsoft's Azure architecture deep dive frame the chip as a cost and supply-chain hedge against Nvidia and AMD. The strategic message is that Microsoft wants to own more of the token-production stack, not simply rent scarce GPUs forever.
