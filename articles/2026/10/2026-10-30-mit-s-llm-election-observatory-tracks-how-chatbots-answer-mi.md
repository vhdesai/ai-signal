# MIT's LLM Election Observatory tracks how chatbots answer midterm questions

MIT researchers built a dashboard that repeatedly queries popular chatbots with fixed election questions and tracks how answers vary over time and by simulated user location, party and identity. Days before the US midterms, it creates an auditable record of the AI information environment voters see. Preliminary, non-peer-reviewed findings suggest framing differences across users.
