# Brad Smith calls for binding AI safety controls

Microsoft President Brad Smith argued that AI is less regulated than everyday products and called for stronger controls, including incident reporting, emergency brakes and independent evaluation. The comments extend Satya Nadella’s recent “emergency brake” framing from architecture into public policy. The common theme is that industry self-regulation is unlikely to satisfy governments as agent autonomy increases. URL(s): BBC via Yahoo News
