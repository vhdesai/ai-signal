# Microsoft moves to reduce Nvidia dependence with Maia 300 at multi-gigawatt scale

Microsoft's Maia 300 announcement is also a major infrastructure signal: hyperscalers are accelerating in-house silicon to reduce Nvidia dependence and control AI infrastructure costs. CNBC and The Verge emphasize the Nvidia angle, while Barron's highlights token efficiency. Even if Nvidia remains dominant, Microsoft is telling investors it has a credible path to greater vertical integration.
