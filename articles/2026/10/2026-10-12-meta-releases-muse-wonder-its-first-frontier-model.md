# Meta releases Muse Wonder, its first frontier model

Meta released Muse Wonder, its first frontier model from Meta Superintelligence Lab. The Wonder models are larger and more capable than Muse Spark, with higher benchmark scores and better complex-task results such as coding. Meta now has both a fast-growing consumer agent surface and an in-house frontier tier. Products & Tools PRODUCT
