# Google says Starship must launch 1,800 times before orbital AI data centers pencil out

Google launched its first Suncatcher TPU satellite yesterday and simultaneously disclosed internal math: SpaceX's Starship would need roughly 1,800 launches before orbital AI data centers hit a cost-competitive total-cost-of-ownership vs. terrestrial. The admission scales back the "orbit this year" framing some analysts applied last week, but Google is clearly still committed to running the engineering experiment.
