# Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta testify under oath at New York City AI risk hearing

Senior leaders from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Meta were scheduled to testify under oath before the New York City Council on AI risks, with SpaceXAI subpoenaed separately. The hearing follows model-containment and autonomous-agent incidents and includes proposed legislation on third-party validation, shutdown capabilities, and liability for foreseeable misuse. This is a concrete sign that AI safety oversight is moving from voluntary federal principles into local legislative action. RISK TRADEOFFOPENAI
