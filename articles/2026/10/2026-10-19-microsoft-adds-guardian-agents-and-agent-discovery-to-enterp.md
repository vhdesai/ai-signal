# Microsoft adds Guardian Agents and agent discovery to enterprise security

Microsoft introduced Guardian Agents within Project Perception and agent discovery in Defender to monitor AI systems operating inside enterprises. The accompanying controls can restrict access, adjust permissions and require human approval for risky actions. The architectural principle is to separate an agent's capabilities from authority over enterprise systems, rather than relying solely on model-provider assurances.
