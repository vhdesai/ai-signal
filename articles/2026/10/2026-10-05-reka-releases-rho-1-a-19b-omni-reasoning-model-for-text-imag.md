# Reka releases Rho-1, a 19B omni-reasoning model for text, images, video, and robot actions

Reka released a research preview of Rho-1, a 19B model that understands and generates text, images, and video while also outputting robot actions. The model uses one context window for discrete and continuous tokens, replacing multi-model pipelines with a unified multimodal architecture. If the approach proves robust, it could reduce latency and complexity in agentic systems that need to reason across perception, generation, and embodied action. ENTERPRISE AGENTSMEMORY
