# Sam Altman says society should accept some AI harms to preserve broad access

Sam Altman said OpenAI differs from Anthropic by accepting that some bad outcomes may occur in exchange for broad public access to AI benefits. He rejected a model in which one lab tightly controls powerful AI and described a lighter-touch regulatory posture, even as OpenAI has recently supported stronger safety assessments. The comment frames the central executive debate: whether AI risk management should optimize for broad diffusion or tight containment. Model Releases OPEN WEIGHTSFRONTIER COMPETITION
