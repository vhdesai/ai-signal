# The Information: Workday customers use external AI agents, pressuring SaaS growth

The Information reports some Workday customers are using agents from Anthropic, Microsoft and other providers to pull and analyze data from Workday rather than visiting Workday's own app. Consultants and partners say many companies paying for Workday's native AI tools are not regularly using them, forcing Workday to offer free access to proprietary AI features. The SaaSpocalypse thesis is evolving: enterprise apps may not disappear, but they risk becoming "dumb" systems of record beneath external AI agents.
