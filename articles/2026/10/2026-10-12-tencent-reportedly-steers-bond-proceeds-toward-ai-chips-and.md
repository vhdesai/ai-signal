# Tencent reportedly steers bond proceeds toward AI chips and capital spending

Bloomberg reported that Tencent told investors proceeds from a roughly $5 billion bond offering would primarily support AI expenditure, especially chips. The report is about use of funding rather than a new bond announcement, but it underscores the same pattern across the sector: AI strategy is becoming balance-sheet strategy. Chip procurement now competes directly with other corporate capital priorities. URL(s): Bloomberg via Moneyweb
