# China proposes export controls on AI agents and other AI technologies

China proposed export controls on 23 AI technologies, including AI agents. The proposal shows Beijing building its own AI software and agent-platform control regime, not only reacting to U.S. chip restrictions. If enacted, the policy could reshape global availability of Chinese open-weight systems and agent tools just as enterprise interest in lower-cost Chinese models accelerates.
