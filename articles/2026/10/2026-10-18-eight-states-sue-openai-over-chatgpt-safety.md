# Eight states sue OpenAI over ChatGPT safety

Eight states sued OpenAI over ChatGPT safety, adding state-level consumer-protection pressure on top of federal oversight and product-safety scrutiny. The litigation pushes ChatGPT governance from voluntary controls into multi-jurisdiction legal exposure. It also raises the cost of rapid expansion into teen-facing, multimodal and agentic use cases where harm narratives can move faster than product-policy remediation.
