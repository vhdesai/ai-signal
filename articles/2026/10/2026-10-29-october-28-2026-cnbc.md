# [October 28, 2026] · [CNBC]

The Justice Department is asking how Nvidia's agreement to buy Hugging Face, a platform hosting AI models from many companies, would affect competition, according to Reuters reporting cited by CNBC and DealBook. DealBook adds that the department has also examined other Nvidia deals, including Groq. Control of open-model distribution is becoming an antitrust question.
