# AI buildout financing shifts toward discipline as Oracle caps capex and lenders lean in

Oracle shares rose more than 10% after it framed fiscal 2028 and 2029 as "peak capex," projected positive free cash flow from fiscal 2028 and more than $30 billion by fiscal 2029, and capped capex-related capital raises at $20 billion through fiscal 2030. PitchBook reports that 41% of polled private-credit lenders see digital infrastructure as their biggest 2027 opportunity, with Ares estimating roughly $1.3 trillion of data-center development spending next year. WSJ notes that banks' commercial lending jumped in the third quarter on the data-center build-out, even as bankers remain cautious. Capital remains abundant, but markets now reward a visible path to self-funding.
