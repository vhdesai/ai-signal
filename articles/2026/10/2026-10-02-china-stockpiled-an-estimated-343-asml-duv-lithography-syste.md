# China stockpiled an estimated 343 ASML DUV lithography systems, former US officials say

A late-September report by the Centre for Technology & Statecraft, co-authored by former US officials, says Chinese fabs amassed roughly 343 immersion-DUV lithography systems — worth billions — before export controls tightened. The group is calling on Washington and allies to close remaining DUV loopholes. For AI capex planning the signal is that domestic Chinese advanced-node capacity has a longer runway than the US restriction regime assumed, which bears on 7–10nm production forecasts underpinning Huawei's Tau Scaling Law roadmap.
