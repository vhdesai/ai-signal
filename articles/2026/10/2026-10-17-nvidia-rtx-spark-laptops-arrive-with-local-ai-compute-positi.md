# NVIDIA RTX Spark laptops arrive with local AI compute positioned for agents and creators

NVIDIA said RTX Spark laptops are now available from major Windows PC makers, combining Blackwell RTX graphics, Grace CPU, up to 128GB of unified memory, and up to 1 petaflop of AI compute in premium portable systems. The company is positioning the launch around local AI, creation workflows, and gaming, with developer resources for running local AI on RTX Spark. For executives, this is another sign that inference and agent workloads are moving toward hybrid local-cloud deployment patterns, not only centralized cloud endpoints.
