# Microsoft publishes Daron Acemoglu's bearish AI forecast: 1.5% GDP lift over a decade

Microsoft published Nobel economist Daron Acemoglu's view that AI may add only about 1.5% to GDP over ten years and replace at most 5% of jobs unless applications meaningfully change how work is done. The piece reads as a call to shift from capability demos to workflow redesign. https://the-decoder.com/microsoft-publishes-nobel-economists-bearish-ai-forecast-of-just-1-5-gdp-growth-over-a-decade/
