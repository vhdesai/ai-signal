# Tech stocks slide as investors worry about AI overspending

Semafor and Yahoo Finance reported a broad tech selloff as investors grew more anxious about AI overspending, China competition and chip-sector risk. The selloff hit Nvidia, chip suppliers and AI-infrastructure names after weeks of revenue, debt and power-supply concerns. The AI trade remains intact, but the market is increasingly distinguishing durable cash flow from speculative buildout assumptions.
