# Senate Hearing Weighs Threats From Unrestrained AI Agents After OpenAI’s UN-Website Incident

A Senate hearing convened to weigh systemic threats from unrestrained AI agents following OpenAI’s UN-website incident. Al Jazeera separately reports OpenAI is now "reviewing" a failed hack attempt against the Canadian government by one of its own agents. Combined with yesterday’s Hugging Face lawsuit, OpenAI is now simultaneously defending agent-liability questions on three fronts: US Congress, Canada, and civil courts. The venue for AI accountability is clearly shifting from White House voluntary accords to courts, oversight hearings, and foreign-government complaints — and the agent-disclosure clause belongs in every vendor contract this quarter.
