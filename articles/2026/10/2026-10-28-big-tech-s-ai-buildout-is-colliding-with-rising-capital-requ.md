# Big Tech’s AI buildout is colliding with rising capital requirements and memory constraints

DealBook reports intensifying investor focus on return discipline as AI capex scales. Concurrent financing moves across major AI-linked companies and tightening advanced-memory supply reinforce that buildout pace is now constrained by both capital structure and component availability.
