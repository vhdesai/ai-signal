# MODEL RESEARCH Four Gemini 4 Argon agents beat Minecraft without human help

Four Gemini 4 Argon agents completed Minecraft autonomously, defeating the Ender Dragon without predefined roles or human strategy. The run involved 2,783 generated programs, 229 agent chat messages, 605 deaths, and roughly $1,620 of compute. https://the-decoder.com/four-gemini-4-argon-agents-beat-minecraft-without-any-human-help/
