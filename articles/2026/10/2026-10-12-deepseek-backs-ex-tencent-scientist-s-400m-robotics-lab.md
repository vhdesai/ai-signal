# DeepSeek backs ex-Tencent scientist's $400M robotics lab

DeepSeek is leading a $400M seed round for Winverse AI, a robotics startup founded by an ex-Tencent scientist. The move links DeepSeek's model ambitions to embodied AI and robotics, not just chat and code. It also shows Chinese frontier labs moving aggressively into physical AI before the category is mature.
