# Data-center permitting becomes a binding constraint as xAI's Colossus suit proceeds

A federal judge denied xAI's motion to dismiss an NAACP Clean Air Act suit alleging it ran at least 35 gas turbines without required permits at its Colossus data center near Memphis. Separately, TechCrunch reports data centers dominated a pre-midterm event in Brooklyn and cites a Data Center Watch estimate that nearly $200 billion of projects were delayed, stalled or blocked in the first half of 2026 by local opposition.
