# Google open-sources Harness² for recursive agent harness optimization

Google Cloud AI Research open-sourced Harness², a recursive agent harnessing framework that improves prompts, tools, memory, skills, scripts, subagents, and orchestration around a frozen model. MarkTechPost reports benchmark gains across WorkBuddy-Bench, JobBench, and Harvey LAB without changing model weights, including an 11.5-point WorkBuddy improvement for GLM-5 under OpenCode. The release matters because it frames agent performance as a systems-engineering problem, not only a model-scaling problem. V PHYSICAL AI DATA PLATFORM
