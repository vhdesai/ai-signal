# Cohere launches North 2 with agent memory, orchestration, and cost controls

Cohere launched North 2, adding cross-session memory, reusable skills, shared libraries, app generation, automations, enterprise connectors, and granular administrative cost controls. The platform supports cloud, on-premises, hybrid, and air-gapped deployments and emphasizes governance with roles, quotas, token limits, guardrails, and autonomy policies. North 2 is a direct response to enterprise concerns that agents need persistent context and workflow power without losing budget control or sovereignty. RECOMMENDERSGENERATIVE AI
