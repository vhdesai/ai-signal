# Intel and Broadcom warn capital costs could become an AI bottleneck

CNBC reported that Intel and Broadcom leaders flagged financing costs and investment returns as constraints on continued AI expansion. The warning matters because AI infrastructure debates often focus on chips and power, while the cost of capital can be just as constraining. Enterprises should watch whether vendor financing, debt loads and utilization assumptions start shaping capacity access and pricing.
