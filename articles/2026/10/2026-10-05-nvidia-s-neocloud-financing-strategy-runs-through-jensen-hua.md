# Nvidia's neocloud financing strategy runs through Jensen Huang's son-in-law

The Information's Phoebe Liu reports Nico Caprez, Jensen Huang's son-in-law, has become Nvidia's key point of contact for young cloud providers and data-center firms. Caprez manages multibillion-dollar relationships, provides financial assistance and sales leads, and has been instrumental in recent investment deals with firms securing power and land for data centers. The story adds governance texture to Nvidia's AI-infrastructure moat: supplier financing and customer-channel orchestration are now central to how the GPU ecosystem scales.
