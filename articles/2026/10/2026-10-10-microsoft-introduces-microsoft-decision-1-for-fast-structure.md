# Microsoft introduces Microsoft-Decision-1 for fast structured decision-scoring

Microsoft introduced Microsoft-Decision-1, a decision-scoring model available through Microsoft Foundry and OpenRouter. The model is designed for routing, classification, verification, prioritization, and agent workflow control; Microsoft says it post-trained Qwen3.5-9B and reports leading accuracy across a 36-benchmark comparison with p50 latency positioned as materially faster than GPT-6 Sol. The release is strategically aligned with a broader market move toward models that produce auditable probabilities rather than natural-language answers. N OPEN-WEIGHTS MODEL
