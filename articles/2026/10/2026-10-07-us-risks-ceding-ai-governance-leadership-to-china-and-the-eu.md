# US risks ceding AI governance leadership to China and the EU, SCMP opinion argues

Denis Simon argues that the Trump administration's "morally binding" voluntary AI pact lacks the legal enforcement needed to compete with the EU AI Act or China's centralized approach. It reframes AI governance as strategic competition, not only consumer protection. https://www.scmp.com/opinion/world-opinion/article/3369823/us-risks-ceding-ai-governance-leadership-china-and-eu
