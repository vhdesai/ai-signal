# [October 30, 2026] · [DealBook]

Alphabet lost roughly $500 billion in market value as search revenue fell short, partly because less-profitable AI Mode searches are gaining popularity, and the company burned cash faster than expected. Amazon then fell more than 6% after hours despite AWS revenue rising 39% to $45.98 billion, as it held 2026 capex at $220 billion. DealBook tallies more than $720 billion of planned 2026 spending across Amazon, Google, Meta and Microsoft, with Microsoft the relative outperformer after holding the line on spending.
