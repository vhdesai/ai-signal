# Nvidia faces DOJ questions on Hugging Face as a counterintelligence report adds pressure

The Justice Department is asking how Nvidia's nearly $13 billion acquisition of Hugging Face could affect AI competition, according to Reuters reporting confirmed by CNBC; Hugging Face is used by more than 200,000 companies to share at least 3 million models. DealBook adds that the department has also examined other Nvidia deals, including Groq, and highlights a New York Times report that U.S. officials concluded a Singapore data-center executive with ties to Jensen Huang was a Chinese intelligence asset; Nvidia called the relationship normal business. Control of open-model distribution is becoming an antitrust question, and Nvidia's political insulation looks less absolute. Sources: CNBC: DOJ questions Nvidia Hugging Face deal<https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/doj-questions-nvidias-12point9-billion-hugging-face-deal.html>
