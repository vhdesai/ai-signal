# Ambi raises $26M for an always-listening AI assistant

Former Plaud executive Peter Mo raised $26 million for Ambi, an AI assistant designed to listen continuously and surface real-time context during meetings and work. The product is launching across iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch, with future hardware planned. The executive signal is clear: ambient capture is moving from meeting notes into workflow assistance, raising familiar questions about consent, retention, and enterprise data boundaries.
