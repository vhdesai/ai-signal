# PitchBook flags a valuation surge across inference-chip startups

PitchBook described inference-chip startups as entering a funding and valuation upswing tied to agentic and production-serving demand. The core thesis is that specialized inference silicon can win on cost and latency against more general-purpose accelerators in selected workloads.
