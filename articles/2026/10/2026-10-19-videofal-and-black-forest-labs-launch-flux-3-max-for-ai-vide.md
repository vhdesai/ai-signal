The companies say FLUX 3 Max generated a 10-second clip in 12.45 seconds, versus 277.8 seconds for Seedance 2.5 in fal's comparison. The benchmark is vendor-reported and quality scores reportedly overlap, so the latency claim needs independent reproduction before it informs production economics.
