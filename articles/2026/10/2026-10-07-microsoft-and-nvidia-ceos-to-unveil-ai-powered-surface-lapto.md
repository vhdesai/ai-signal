# Microsoft and Nvidia CEOs to unveil AI-powered Surface Laptop Ultra

Microsoft and Nvidia are reportedly teaming up for an AI-powered Surface Laptop Ultra launch in San Francisco. The device is expected to make on-device AI and Nvidia acceleration central to the Surface story after Microsoft moved away from the Copilot+ PC branding. The joint stage appearance signals Microsoft's client-AI roadmap is now tied as closely to Nvidia's stack as to its own Copilot software. NEW ENTERPRISE
