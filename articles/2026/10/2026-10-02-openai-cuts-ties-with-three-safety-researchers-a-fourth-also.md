# OpenAI cuts ties with three safety researchers; a fourth also departs

The Wall Street Journal reports OpenAI fired three safety researchers after an internal investigation concluded they had leaked confidential information to an outside AI safety organization, with a fourth researcher leaving separately in the same window. The departures sharpen the leadership tension that began with the Super-alignment team's dissolution in 2024 and now spans multiple research-pipeline teams. For enterprise buyers evaluating OpenAI's third-party-assessment commitments from last week, the firings make the question of who inside OpenAI is actually empowered to escalate safety concerns much harder to answer.
