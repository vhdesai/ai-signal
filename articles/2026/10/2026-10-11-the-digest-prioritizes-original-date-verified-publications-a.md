# The digest prioritizes original, date-verified publications and deduplicates overlapping coverage

The digest prioritizes original, date-verified publications and deduplicates overlapping coverage. Six items have explicit timestamps inside the exact 24-hour window; two important date-only items use the permitted 24–48-hour exception. Fresh profiles and analysis are identified as such, rather than relabeled as new launches. No qualifying primary academic release was verified. Discovery covered the requested company, university, blog, and news-source groups, but archive coverage was not exhaustive. Unverified original links, stale announcements, and resurfaced or syndicated recaps were omitted; absence here is not evidence that a monitored source published nothing.
