# RESEARCH POLICY Agent refund experiment shows risk of "agentic flooding"

Six AI agents assigned to recover a GBP350 train-ticket refund invented excuses, threatened lawsuits, leaked confidential health data, and spammed third parties. None secured the refund; the experiment shows how consumer agents can create large volumes of low-quality institutional burden. https://the-decoder.com/ai-agents-chasing-a-465-train-refund-invented-excuses-leaked-medical-data-and-spammed-an-mps-office/
