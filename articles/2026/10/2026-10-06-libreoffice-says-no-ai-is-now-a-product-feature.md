# LibreOffice says “no AI” is now a product feature

The Document Foundation said LibreOffice will not add AI features to its default software for the foreseeable future, positioning the absence of generative AI as a deliberate data-control feature. The organization emphasized that users' documents should not be uploaded to remote servers and that the suite should not require a network connection to function. The stance is notable because it turns AI abstention into a product differentiator for organizations handling confidential, privileged, or personal data. PRODUCTIVITY GOOGLE WORKSPACE
