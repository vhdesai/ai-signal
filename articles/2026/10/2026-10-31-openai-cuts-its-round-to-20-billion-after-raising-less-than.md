# OpenAI cuts its round to $20 billion after raising less than half its original goal

The Wall Street Journal reports OpenAI has raised less than half of the $30 billion it originally sought in its latest round, with about $11 billion committed; the Financial Times reports the target has been cut to $20 billion after a tepid response from some large investors, with UAE investors leading. The round had been discussed at valuations of $1.25 trillion to $1.4 trillion ahead of a planned IPO. Coming days after Anthropic's S-1 and amid a broad tech sell-off, the shortfall shows private capital for frontier labs now carrying an explicit risk discount.
