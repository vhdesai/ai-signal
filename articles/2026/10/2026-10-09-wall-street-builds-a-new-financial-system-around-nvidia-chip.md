# Wall Street builds a new financial system around Nvidia chips

Business Insider reports Wall Street is building new financing structures around Nvidia's AI chips, treating compute hardware as collateral-like assets. PitchBook adds that Nvidia-backed data center operator Firmus postponed its $5.5B IPO and is considering another private funding round. AI finance is becoming its own asset class, with real systemic-risk implications across chip leases, neocloud debt, and hyperscaler capacity commitments.
