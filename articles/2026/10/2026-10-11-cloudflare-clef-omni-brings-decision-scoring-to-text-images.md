# Cloudflare Clef-omni brings decision scoring to text, images, audio and video

Cloudflare’s Clef-omni was covered as a multimodal decision-scoring model for text, image, audio and video inputs. The model fits a broader pattern: vendors are packaging bounded decision models for routing, moderation and workflow control instead of relying solely on open-ended generation. Decision models are becoming an important middle layer between raw foundation models and production automation. URL(s): MarkTechPost
