# Anthropic opens Claude Duet waitlist preview for proactive task execution

Anthropic announced a U.S. preview waitlist for Claude Duet focused on longer-running, app-connected workflow automation. The design emphasizes background task continuity and user approval on consequential actions, positioning the product as supervised autonomy rather than unrestricted delegation. Near-term impact is likely strongest in professional and SMB productivity use cases.
