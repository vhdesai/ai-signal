# Anthropic and Nscale reportedly discuss another $45 billion compute deal

Bloomberg reports that Anthropic and infrastructure provider Nscale are in talks for a potential additional $45 billion cloud-computing agreement. The negotiations are not final and terms may change; if completed, the deal would underscore both the scale of frontier-model compute commitments and the capital concentration risk attached to them.
