# OpenAI publishes AI-generated mathematics for external scrutiny

OpenAI released a large collection of AI-generated mathematical results and proof artifacts from an unreleased model. The publication is notable because it shifts the claim from “the model can reason” toward artifacts that outside mathematicians and formal-methods researchers can inspect. It is still not equivalent to peer review, but it raises the bar for how frontier labs may need to substantiate scientific-capability claims. URL(s): OpenAI
