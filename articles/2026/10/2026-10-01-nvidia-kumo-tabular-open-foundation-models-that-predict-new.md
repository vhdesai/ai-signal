# NVIDIA Kumo Tabular: Open Foundation Models That Predict New Rows in One Forward Pass

NVIDIA Research released Kumo Tabular, an open family of tabular foundation models that generate new rows in a single forward pass without row-by-row sampling. MarkTechPost’s deep dive flags it as a step-change for enterprise data augmentation, synthetic-data pipelines, and relational-DB ML workloads. It materially simplifies the tabular foundation-model story that Prior Labs and Together AI have been building and gives enterprise data teams a credible open substrate to evaluate against commercial synthetic-data vendors — a direct cost-and-governance input for anyone running privacy-constrained analytics or ML training.
