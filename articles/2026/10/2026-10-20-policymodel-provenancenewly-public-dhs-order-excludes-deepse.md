# POLICYMODEL PROVENANCENewly public DHS order excludes DeepSeek from federal civilian systems

WSJ Pro Cybersecurity reports that a June DHS memo, made public Monday, requires removal of DeepSeek tools from federal civilian agencies; TechCrunch details the restriction on future purchases and covered third-party products. Agencies can request exceptions, including for mission-critical functions. This is disclosure and coverage of an earlier order, not a newly issued blanket ban on private-sector use.
