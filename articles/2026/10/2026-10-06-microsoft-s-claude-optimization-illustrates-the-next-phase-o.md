# Microsoft’s Claude optimization illustrates the next phase of model routing economics

The Information reported that Microsoft reduced internal Claude spending from a peak annualized run rate above $1B while customer-driven Claude usage through Microsoft products has offset some of the decline. Microsoft is now signaling that Copilot is becoming a “tapestry” of models optimized for quality and cost, including substitutions with cheaper alternatives where output quality holds. This is the enterprise model-routing thesis in practice: the winning platform owns the outcome layer, not loyalty to a single frontier provider. URL(s): The Information search
