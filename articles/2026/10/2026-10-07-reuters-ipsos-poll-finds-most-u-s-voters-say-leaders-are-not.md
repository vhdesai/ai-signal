# Reuters/Ipsos poll finds most U.S. voters say leaders are not taking AI risks seriously

Reuters reported that most U.S. voters say President Trump and Congress are not taking AI risks seriously, according to a Reuters/Ipsos poll. The finding matters because public trust is becoming a gating factor for AI deployment, data-center expansion, and policy legitimacy.
