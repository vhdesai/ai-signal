# AI beats Stratego's greatest player on an $8K training budget

Ataraxos, a reinforcement-learning system built by researchers from CMU, NYU, Stanford, and MIT, decisively beat the top-ranked human Stratego player — the last major imperfect-information board game where humans still held the edge. Google DeepMind's 2023 attempt reportedly cost millions; Ataraxos cost under $8,000. The cost delta is the headline, but the methodological contribution is a value-function-reshaping trick that makes partial-observation RL much cheaper to train. RESEARCH
