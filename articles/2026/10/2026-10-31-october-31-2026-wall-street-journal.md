# [October 31, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

The Wall Street Journal reports OpenAI has raised less than half of the $30 billion it originally sought, with about $11 billion committed, and the Financial Times reports the target has been cut to $20 billion after a tepid response from some large investors. UAE investors are leading the round, which had been discussed at valuations of $1.25 trillion to $1.4 trillion ahead of a planned IPO. The shortfall shows private capital for frontier labs now carries an explicit risk discount.
