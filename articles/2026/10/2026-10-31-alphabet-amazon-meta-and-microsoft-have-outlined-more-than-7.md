# Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft have outlined more than $740 billion in capex this year, and investors are now…

Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft have outlined more than $740 billion in capex this year, and investors are now focused on negative free cash flow and rising debt. On Friday, the Nasdaq fell 2.9%, Nvidia fell 4%, and Broadcom fell more than 5%, after Alphabet's worst day on record on Thursday. Alphabet reported -$9.1 billion of Q3 free cash flow, Amazon -$42.4 billion over the trailing 12 months, and Meta -$2.1 billion; Microsoft was the exception at +$18.6 billion. The market is shifting from rewarding AI spend to demanding evidence of returns.
