# Amazon expands Shop Direct to defend against cross-retailer AI shopping agents

Amazon expanded Shop Direct to approximately 250 million listings connecting shoppers to external retailer sites. The move reflects competitive pressure from AI-native shopping assistants and appears designed to preserve customer discovery inside Amazon’s demand surface.
