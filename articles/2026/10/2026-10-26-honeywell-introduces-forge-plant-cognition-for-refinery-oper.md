# Honeywell introduces Forge Plant Cognition for refinery operations

Honeywell's announcement describes a Forge Plant Cognition deployment at Dangote's refinery built with NVIDIA AI and Dell infrastructure. The product is positioned to assist plant operations, but vendor-projected benefits should be treated as targets rather than realized net savings. Actual value will depend on operational integration, reliability, and the cost of deployment. URL: Business Wire announcement
