# The Information Briefing: Microsoft's exodus — Ryan Roslansky and Peter Lee out, following Rajesh Jha

Martin Peers reports a wave of senior Microsoft departures: Office apps EVP Ryan Roslansky said Thursday he'll leave at the end of the year, a day after Microsoft Science President Peter Lee's exit — both following former Office and Windows chief Rajesh Jha. The pattern: 15+ year veterans "with lots of money on the stock" cashing out as Microsoft rethinks operations around AI. Nadella is simultaneously promoting younger execs (Asha Sharma for gaming; Charles Lamanna and Jacob Andreou on Copilot).
