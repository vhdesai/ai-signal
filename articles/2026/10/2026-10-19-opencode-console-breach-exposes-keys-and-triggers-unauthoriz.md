# OpenCode Console breach exposes keys and triggers unauthorized AI charges

TechCrunch's update reports that a breach of an older OpenCode Console system affected 1.4% of Console users whose provider API keys were stored there; the open-source app and CLI were not affected. Model providers disabled exposed keys and refunds were underway after users reported unauthorized charges. OpenCode described a distillation attack, but the report notes that this purpose remains unproven; the confirmed issue is the credential breach, whose initial disclosure preceded this reporting window.
