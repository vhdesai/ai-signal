# Anthropic files for an IPO, turning a prospective disclosure into a public-market test

The Information previewed an imminent filing; Anthropic now says it has filed an S-1 for a proposed Nasdaq listing under ANT. The filing makes the economics and governance of a frontier-model developer more consequential to customers and investors, though the offering's price and share count are not yet set. Its registration statement is not yet effective, so the filing should not be mistaken for a completed IPO. URLs: Anthropic announcement · The Information search
