# Microsoft releases Decision-1 as decision-scoring models become an agent-routing category

Microsoft AI released Microsoft-Decision-1, a Qwen3.5-9B decision-scoring model that ranks options rather than generating long prose. Neowin reports it is 35x faster than GPT-6 Sol for decision-making tasks, and The Register notes Microsoft is leaning on an open-weight Chinese model to challenge Jev-style decision systems. Nace AI's Drex 1.5 and AWS Strands Decider show the same pattern: small, fast specialist models that choose actions may become core agent infrastructure.
