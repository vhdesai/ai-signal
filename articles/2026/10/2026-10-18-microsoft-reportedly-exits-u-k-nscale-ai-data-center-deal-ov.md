# Microsoft reportedly exits U.K. Nscale AI data-center deal over power constraints

Microsoft reportedly pulled out of a major U.K. AI data-center deal with Nscale, with power-supply concerns cited across reports. The move is a setback for Britain’s AI infrastructure ambitions and another sign that power availability is now a gating factor for AI buildouts. It reinforces why hyperscalers are signing long-term energy deals and scrutinizing grid risk before committing capital.
