# Reflection AI prepares open-weight model to challenge DeepSeek and Qwen

Axios reported that Reflection AI is preparing an open-weight foundation model intended to compete with leading Chinese open models such as DeepSeek and Alibaba's Qwen. The company, founded by former Google DeepMind researchers, is reportedly pairing open weights with customer data and large Nvidia compute under an "AI factory" deployment strategy. If credible, the release would give U.S. enterprises another controllable model option outside closed frontier APIs and Chinese open-weight systems.
