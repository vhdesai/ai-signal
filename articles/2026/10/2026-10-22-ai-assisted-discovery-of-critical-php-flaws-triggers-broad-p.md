# AI-assisted discovery of critical PHP flaws triggers broad patch urgency

Coverage described high-severity PHP vulnerabilities discovered with AI-assisted workflows, followed by urgent patching guidance from security authorities and maintainers. The incident shows how AI can compress both defensive detection cycles and attacker adaptation windows once vulnerability details are public. Operationally, organizations with legacy web estates face tighter remediation timelines.
