# AMD's $8.2B World Labs deal puts a price on world-model talent

PitchBook reports AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B in stock, a 52% premium to the startup's February valuation. The deal signals a buy-over-build era for world models: incumbents that lack specialized 3D simulation, rendering and physics talent may pay up rather than assemble teams from scratch. World models are increasingly tied to autonomous vehicles, robotics and factory-floor systems, so AMD's move is both a talent acquisition and a bet on embodied-AI infrastructure.
