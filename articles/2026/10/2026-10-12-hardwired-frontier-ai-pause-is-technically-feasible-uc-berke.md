# Hardwired frontier-AI pause is technically feasible, UC Berkeley-led report says

A 26-researcher working group including UC Berkeley, Princeton, Stanford, Harvard and Oxford researchers released a 200-page report arguing a mutually verified global pause in frontier-AI training is technically feasible. The plan centers on halting production of training-capable chips and replacing them over time with inference-only chips, while using verification measures to detect defections. It is one of the most detailed policy-engineering proposals yet for controlling frontier-model escalation.
