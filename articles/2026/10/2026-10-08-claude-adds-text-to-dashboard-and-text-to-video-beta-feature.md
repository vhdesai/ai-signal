# Claude adds text-to-dashboard and text-to-video beta features

Anthropic launched Claude Dashboards, which turns BigQuery and Snowflake data into live dashboards from text prompts, and Claude Motion, which creates animated explainer videos from text and images. Claude is moving from assistant/chat into lightweight BI, presentation, and communication tooling. PRODUCT
