# Workday and Anthropic connect reasoning to business rules as finance autopilots arrive

Workday and Anthropic announced a partnership to connect Claude-style reasoning to the rules that run business processes across Workday environments. CIO Dive separately reports Workday is bringing autopilots to finance workflows, with customer-defined controls for autonomous actions across finance and HR. The shared signal is that enterprise-agent competition is moving toward governed reasoning layers, not just embedded chat.
