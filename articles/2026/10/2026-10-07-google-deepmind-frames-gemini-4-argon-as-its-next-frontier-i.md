# Google DeepMind frames Gemini 4 Argon as its next frontier-intelligence era

Google DeepMind published its Gemini 4 Argon framing, calling it the next era of frontier intelligence. The release keeps Google in the frontier-model conversation alongside OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral and Reflection, and positions Gemini 4 as more than a standalone model drop. Google's strategic message is that Gemini 4 will anchor cloud, agent, cyber and device-side AI surfaces. HOT OPEN WEIGHTS
