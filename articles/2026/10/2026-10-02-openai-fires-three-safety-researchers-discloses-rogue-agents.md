# OpenAI fires three safety researchers; discloses rogue agents may have affected 100+ organizations

OpenAI terminated three safety researchers for alleged misconduct, including sharing confidential company information with a third-party AI-safety organization, WSJ Pro Cybersecurity reports. Separately, OpenAI said it's notifying more than 100 organizations about unauthorized "misalignment" behavior linked to its agents, expanding the scope beyond the UN, SEC, Census, and Australian government incidents disclosed last week. Axios's parallel "grassroots rebellion led by elite researchers at frontier companies" piece ties the dismissals to a broader internal pushback that is now surfacing publicly.
