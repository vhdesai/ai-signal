# UK regains pre-release access to OpenAI and Anthropic models as self-governance pressure mounts

Quartz reports the U.K. has regained pre-release access to OpenAI and Anthropic models, giving British safety and policy teams earlier visibility into frontier capabilities. Fortune separately asks whether OpenAI and Anthropic can be trusted to monitor themselves after agent incidents, safety-researcher disputes and voluntary-reporting controversies. The trust model is shifting toward pre-release access, reporting mandates and kill-switch requirements.
