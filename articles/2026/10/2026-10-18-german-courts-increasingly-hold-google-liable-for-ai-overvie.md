# German courts increasingly hold Google liable for AI Overview outputs

Two German courts have treated Google's AI Overview outputs as Google's own content. A consistent European legal view is emerging: AI search answers are not merely neutral links or third-party snippets; platforms may be liable for generated claims. POLICY
