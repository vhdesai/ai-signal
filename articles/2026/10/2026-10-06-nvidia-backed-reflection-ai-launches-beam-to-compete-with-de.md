# Nvidia-backed Reflection AI launches Beam to compete with DeepSeek and Kimi

Reflection AI launched Beam, its first major open-weight model, with coverage calling it the "DeepSeek of the West." The model is backed by Nvidia-linked capital and positioned as a low-cost, high-performance alternative to Chinese leaders like DeepSeek and Kimi. Together with Mistral Large 4, Beam makes the day feel like an open-weight counteroffensive rather than another closed-frontier-lab cycle.
