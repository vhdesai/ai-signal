# Symmetry-aware AI targets motion errors in fetal MRI

Researchers at MIT, Boston Children's Hospital and Inria developed E(3)-Pose to track fetal-head motion and support reconstruction of higher-resolution MRI images. The model embeds left-right symmetry in its architecture instead of relying on training data alone to learn that constraint. The work was presented at ECCV 2026 and is primarily trained on third-trimester fetuses; it is research, not evidence of clinical approval or proven diagnostic benefit.
