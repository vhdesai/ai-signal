# FUNDINGAI CHIPSTenstorrent raises $1.5 billion to scale inference hardware

Tenstorrent raised $1.5 billion at a $6.2 billion valuation in a round led by Trace Capital and Innovation X, according to PitchBook and TechCrunch. The company plans to scale chips for serving open-weight models and video generation, after demonstrating DeepSeek models on its Galaxy servers. The financing expands an alternative to Nvidia, but does not by itself establish production-scale competitiveness or customer adoption.
