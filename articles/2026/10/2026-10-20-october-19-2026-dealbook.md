# [October 19, 2026] · DealBook

DealBook reports that Town raised $108 million at a $1 billion valuation in a round co-led by Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures, a financing also listed by PitchBook. Its Townie assistant connects workplace productivity tools and is adding agent-to-agent collaboration. The company says paying customers have quadrupled since July, but exact revenue was not disclosed.
