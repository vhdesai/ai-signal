# SCMP: asking whether China would slow AI development is the wrong question

Xiao Qian argues Beijing is unlikely to accept a generic slowdown frame because it views AI as economic modernization, not simply frontier-risk management. The piece reframes the pause debate around practical risk controls, misuse prevention, and human-control guarantees. https://www.scmp.com/opinion/world-opinion/article/3369561/would-china-agree-slow-ai-development-thats-wrong-question
