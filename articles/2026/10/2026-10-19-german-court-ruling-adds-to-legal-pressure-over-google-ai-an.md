# German court ruling adds to legal pressure over Google AI answers

The Decoder reports that the Aachen Regional Court barred Google from spreading false claims generated by its AI search feature, applying reasoning from an earlier Munich case that treats AI answers as Google’s own content. The injunction is not final and can be challenged, but the reasoning could increase legal exposure for AI-generated search summaries that make false factual claims.
