# Hyperscalers' growth hinges on how fast they can ship their own chips

PitchBook argues the shift from chatbots to autonomous agents is increasing compute demand so quickly that hyperscaler growth now depends on resolving their own silicon bottlenecks. Amazon's chip business reportedly has a $25B annual run rate and over $225B in Trainium revenue commitments, Google has begun recognizing TPU system-sales revenue, and over 60% of Alibaba's T-Head chips serve external customers. Proprietary silicon is moving from cost-saving tool to strategic product line.
