# Amazon drops local-government data-center NDAs as AI infrastructure faces community scrutiny

TechCrunch's Equity coverage notes that Amazon will stop using NDAs in data-center negotiations with local governments, following a similar Microsoft move earlier this year. The change responds to mounting community backlash around AI infrastructure projects, including proposed and enacted moratoriums. For AI leaders, the implication is that compute expansion is becoming a public-policy and community-trust problem, not just a capital-expenditure problem. AI Safety & Policy A SAFETY AGENTS
