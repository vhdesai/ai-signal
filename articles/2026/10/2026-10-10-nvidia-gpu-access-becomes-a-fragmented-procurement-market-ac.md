# Nvidia GPU access becomes a fragmented procurement market across hyperscalers and neoclouds

CNBC reports that companies seeking Nvidia GPUs now face a widening set of options across Amazon, Microsoft, Google, CoreWeave, neoclouds, marketplaces, and direct hardware purchases. The article notes that Nvidia expects $108 billion in October-quarter revenue, while SemiAnalysis counted 323 Nvidia GPU providers as of September, up from 209 less than 11 months earlier. The core executive takeaway is that GPU capacity is no longer a simple cloud-procurement line item; it is becoming a supplier-risk, latency, geography, and financing decision.
