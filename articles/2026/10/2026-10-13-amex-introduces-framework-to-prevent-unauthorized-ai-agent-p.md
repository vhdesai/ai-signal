# Amex introduces framework to prevent unauthorized AI-agent purchases

American Express introduced a framework designed to ensure AI agents only make purchases users have explicitly authorized. Payment networks are now entering the same governance layer as browsers and marketplaces: identity, authorization, auditability, and dispute handling for non-human buyers. INDUSTRY
