# Microsoft Research and UC Berkeley's TokenEconomizer cuts coding-agent costs by up to 31%

TokenEconomizer is an open-source plugin for Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot CLI and Pi that learns from past sessions in a repository to trim waste in later ones, without touching model weights or agent code. On 277 SWE-Bench tasks it reduced mean cost by 18.4% to 30.9% across five agent-model settings, with small accuracy trade-offs in some configurations. Meaningful agent savings are available from orchestration alone. MarkTechPost AI Safety & Policy POLICY
