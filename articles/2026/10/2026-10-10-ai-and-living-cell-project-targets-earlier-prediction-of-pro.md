# AI-and-living-cell project targets earlier prediction of protein misfolding

Bioengineer.org covered a $28.6 million research effort that combines AI and living-cell systems to predict protein misfolding before disease manifests. The project sits within the broader AI-for-biology surge, where models are being pushed from retrospective structure prediction toward prospective intervention and measurement. The key execution question is whether biological feedback loops can make these systems more clinically reliable than purely computational predictions.
