# Every major Nvidia customer is becoming a competitor

Axios framed Microsoft's Maia reveal as evidence that every major Nvidia customer is now also a competitor. Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta and OpenAI are all pushing custom silicon or chip-financing structures to reduce dependence on Nvidia. The result is not immediate displacement, but long-term margin and bargaining pressure on the AI chip leader.
