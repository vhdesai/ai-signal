# Character.AI files for bankruptcy five years after launch

Character.AI filed for bankruptcy and plans to sell some assets to Disney for $20M. The collapse is a sharp reversal for one of the earliest breakout consumer chatbot platforms and highlights the hard economics of high-engagement consumer AI: inference costs, safety pressure, weak subscription conversion, and platform competition.
