# Nvidia enters correction as AI bubble index jumps

Benzinga reported that Nvidia entered correction territory after a technical breakdown while an AI stock bubble index jumped. The correction follows weeks of credit-stress headlines, infrastructure-financing concerns and neocloud volatility. Wall Street still broadly views the AI trade as strong, but investors are becoming more selective about balance-sheet risk and revenue quality.
