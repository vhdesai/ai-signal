# Google puts AI chips in space to test orbital data centers

Tech Wire Asia reported that Google is sending AI chips into space as part of Project Suncatcher, an experiment in orbital data-center infrastructure. The concept targets a long-term AI bottleneck: terrestrial power, cooling, and land constraints. It remains exploratory, but the direction is important because AI infrastructure demand is pushing providers to consider radically different siting models, including space-based compute.
