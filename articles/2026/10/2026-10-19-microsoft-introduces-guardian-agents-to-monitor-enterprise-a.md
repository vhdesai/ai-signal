# Microsoft introduces Guardian Agents to monitor enterprise AI agents as potential insider risks

Microsoft outlined Guardian Agents, a planned supervisory layer within Project Perception to monitor agent actions, assess risk in context, and adjust permissions or contain behavior. The company argues that agents can be unpredictable even without being compromised because they optimize for task completion rather than organizational norms. The design principle is to separate AI capability from authority: independent controls, identity, least privilege, and real-time human oversight should govern actions with material impact.
