# AI-driven dynamic pricing raises consumer trust and data-use questions

CNBC reported on how AI could shape pricing in restaurants and grocery settings, while noting that McDonald’s denies assessing individual willingness to pay and that Amazon individualized-pricing claims remain contested. The story is less about a single vendor policy and more about the governance of consumer data, personalization and perceived fairness. As AI pricing systems mature, disclosure and constraints may become as important as optimization. URL(s): CNBC
