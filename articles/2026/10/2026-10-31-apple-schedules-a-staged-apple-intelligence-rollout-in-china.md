# Apple schedules a staged Apple Intelligence rollout in China with Alibaba Qwen support

Apple said developer-beta testing of Apple Intelligence features in China begins November 4, with broad availability in iOS 27.3 in December. Selected experiences will be supported by Alibaba's Qwen models. The rollout opens Apple's largest overseas market to its AI features through a local model partner.
