# XPeng + Banma Run a 30B-Parameter Cockpit AI Model Entirely On-Device on a 750-TOPS Turing Chip

XPeng and Banma demonstrated a 30-billion-parameter cockpit AI model running fully on-device on XPeng’s 750-TOPS Turing chip — no cloud roundtrip, per Pandaily. It is one of the largest edge-LLM deployments in a shipping vehicle to date and reinforces XPeng’s claim to leading autonomous-plus-assistant integration. Combined with China’s Maxwell embodied-AI model topping a global physical-task benchmark the same day, the edge/physical AI thread is being carried — publicly — by Chinese automotive and robotics teams. Expect Western automakers to accelerate on-device cockpit benchmarks on their next CES schedule.
