# PitchBook: Anthropic Revenue Jumped From $4.73B (Q1 2026) to $11.5B (Q2 2026) — Against $518B in Contracted Compute Costs

PitchBook’s Jacob Robbins broke down the leaked Anthropic S-1 draft: 2025 revenue was $4.6B; Q1 2026 alone hit $4.73B; Q2 2026 reached $11.5B. The counterweight is $518B in contracted computing commitments to partners including Google and AMD. Senior analyst Harrison Rolfes argues the reported ~$2T IPO target is the key open question. PitchBook separately shows global PE exits of $462.9B in Q3 (highest since Q2 2021 peak), driven by SpaceX’s $60B Cursor acquisition (second-largest VC M&A exit ever). The IPO window is open; the math gets harder as compute commitments compound against still-vast losses.
