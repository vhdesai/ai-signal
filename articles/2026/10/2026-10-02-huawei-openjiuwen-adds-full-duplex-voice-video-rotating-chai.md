# Huawei openJiuwen adds full-duplex voice + video; Rotating Chairman claims Ascend has surpassed Nvidia in China

Huawei's openJiuwen project added full-duplex voice and video to its WorkSwarm agent framework, with ready-to-deploy support on Ascend NPUs — closing a key gap versus OpenAI's real-time voice stack and GPT-6.1 Sol capabilities, per Pandaily. Separately, Geopolitechs captured Huawei's Rotating Chairman declaring Ascend has overtaken Nvidia in Chinese AI accelerator market share. Combined with the DeepSeek-Huawei TileLang launch and Baidu chip-unit $50B HK IPO reports, Huawei is converging on a full-stack sovereign-AI narrative.
