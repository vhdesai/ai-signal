# The AI graveyard narrative highlights rapid product churn across AI startups and incumbents

TechCrunch compiled a running record of AI products and startups that were shut down, rolled back, or materially re-scoped after launch. The pattern reinforces that distribution and retention, not model novelty alone, are deciding factors in product survival. For executives, this is a reminder to prioritize durable workflow fit and unit economics over launch velocity.
