# Publishers begin serving ads that only AI agents can see

Time began running agent-targeted ads in August with ad-tech startup Mobian, feeding AI agents product information that human readers never see. Several new advertisers are now launching, while agent developers worry marketers could steer agents without users' knowledge. Agent-directed persuasion will need disclosure norms and agent-side defenses similar to prompt-injection controls.
