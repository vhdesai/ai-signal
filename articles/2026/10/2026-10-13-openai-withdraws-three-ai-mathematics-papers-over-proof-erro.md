# OpenAI withdraws three AI mathematics papers over proof error

OpenAI withdrew three AI-generated mathematics papers because of a sign error in a key proof, according to academic and technical coverage. Tom's Hardware separately reported mathematicians warning against sharing research with LLMs after OpenAI's 722-manuscript proof dump. The episode strengthens the argument that AI-generated mathematics needs formal verification and careful peer review before public release.
