# Google pauses open-source bug bounty program after surge in AI-generated reports

Google paused its Open Source Software Vulnerability Rewards Program until next year, citing a significant rise in automated AI submissions, most of which were invalid. Security teams had previously warned that AI-generated vulnerability reports could overwhelm bug bounty triage with hallucinated or low-value submissions. The operational lesson is that AI can increase defensive workload by flooding review pipelines, not only by improving discovery of real vulnerabilities. ANTITRUSTAI PLATFORMS
