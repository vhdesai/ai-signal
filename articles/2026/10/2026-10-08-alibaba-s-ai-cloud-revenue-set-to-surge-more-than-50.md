# Alibaba's AI cloud revenue set to surge more than 50%

Analysts expect Alibaba's AI Cloud and Compute Services unit to report more than 50% revenue growth for the September quarter, accelerating from 45% in the prior quarter. That would validate Alibaba's full-stack AI monetization push across chips, cloud, models, and agentic commerce.
