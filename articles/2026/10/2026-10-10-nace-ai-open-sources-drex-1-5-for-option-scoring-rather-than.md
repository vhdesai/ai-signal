# Nace AI open-sources Drex 1.5 for option scoring rather than prose generation

Nace AI open-sourced Drex 1.5, a 9B model focused on scoring options instead of producing open-ended text. Its positioning overlaps with Microsoft Decision-1 and OpenAI’s decisioning layer, but with an open-weight deployment story that may appeal to teams requiring local control. This is an early marker that the decision-model category will have both proprietary and open infrastructure lanes. Research Breakthroughs AI BIOLOGY RESEARCH
