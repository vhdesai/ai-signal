# National Compute Grid proposal targets idle AI capacity

Axios reported on a National Compute Grid concept aimed at connecting unused AI compute capacity with demand. The idea responds to a structural mismatch: AI capacity is scarce and expensive at peaks, yet underutilized in pockets across geography, ownership, and time. If credible, grid-style coordination could become a policy and market-design complement to brute-force data-center buildout. URL(s): Axios
