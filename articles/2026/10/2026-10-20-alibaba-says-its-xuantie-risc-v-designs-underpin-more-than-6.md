# Alibaba says its XuanTie RISC-V designs underpin more than 6 billion shipped chips

Alibaba’s Damo Academy reported that more than 6 billion chips based on its XuanTie RISC-V architecture have shipped, with use cases spanning AI and automotive systems. The milestone points to the scale of an open instruction-set ecosystem that could broaden processor design options and reduce reliance on foreign chip architectures.
