# Sui and Alibaba let AI agents spend stablecoins without human approval

Sui and Alibaba are enabling AI agents to pay for Alibaba Cloud tasks in stablecoins. The feature moves agentic commerce from shopping demos to machine-to-machine cloud consumption. It also raises a governance question that will matter for every enterprise-agent deployment: how much spend authority should autonomous agents have before a human must sign?
