# Google DeepMind Releases Gemini 4 Argon — Only Vetted Cybersecurity Defenders Can Use It

Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Argon — which it calls its most powerful frontier model to date — but access is restricted to vetted cybersecurity defenders. Axios notes the sharp departure from DeepMind’s prior "widely available first" posture; BBN Times frames the gating as a direct response to Anthropic’s GLM-5.3-hacking warning yesterday. It is the first time a Western frontier lab has explicitly launched a frontier model into controlled distribution for offensive-cyber capability reasons — a Western precedent for "gated by default" frontier releases. Google’s shares jumped on the news; some employees reportedly expressed doubts about Argon’s performance on coding, per DealBook.
