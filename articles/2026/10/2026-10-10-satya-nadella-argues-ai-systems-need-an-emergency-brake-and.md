# Satya Nadella argues AI systems need an emergency brake and tamper-proof action evidence

TechCrunch reported that Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella called for stronger AI containment, including separation between models and orchestration harnesses, externalized safeguards, human-readable action evidence and authorized shutdown controls. His framing treats models as potentially compromised components that need containment from the start. The comments land at a moment when agent incidents are making abstract safety concerns operationally concrete. URL(s): TechCrunch
