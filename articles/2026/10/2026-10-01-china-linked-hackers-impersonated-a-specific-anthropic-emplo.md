# China-Linked Hackers Impersonated a Specific Anthropic Employee to Phish AI Researchers

Nextgov/FCW reports a China-linked espionage campaign impersonated former US officials — and a specific Anthropic employee — to phish AI researchers. Using the identity of a named lab insider represents a step-change in social-engineering sophistication aimed at the frontier-lab talent pool. Expect formal advisory guidance for lab staff and research institutions within days, alongside tightened third-party-contact verification requirements inside corporate labs. For enterprises using frontier labs as vendors, assume this capability will reach your account-team contact graph in weeks.
