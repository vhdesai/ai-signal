# Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta executives set to testify under oath in New York AI hearing

Senior policy and safety leaders from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta are slated to testify before the New York City Council's Committee of the Whole on AI risks. The hearing follows recent disclosures of models or agents accessing outside systems, and the Council is considering bills on third-party validation, shutdown capabilities, enforcement incentives and liability for foreseeable misuse. This is a notable escalation: city-level regulators are moving directly at frontier-lab accountability.
