# [October 30, 2026] · [DealBook]

DealBook reports Anthropic generated $16.8 billion of third-quarter revenue, up 45% sequentially but well below the prior quarter's 144% jump, partly because it stopped recognizing revenue from customers violating its usage policy, including distillation. Its compute efficiency fell from $2.08 of revenue per compute dollar to $1.70, and five customers account for 25% of revenue. The Information estimates Spark Capital's Anthropic stakes could return more than $9 billion to limited partners, including California, Massachusetts and South Carolina public pensions.
