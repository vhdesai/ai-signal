# NVIDIA contributes Vera Rubin AI-factory designs to OCP and expands DSX open components

At the Open Compute Project Global Summit, NVIDIA announced open architecture contributions for Vera Rubin rack and compute tray designs through its MGX architecture, plus new open-source DSX AI Factory components. The announcements cover rack-scale liquid cooling, higher-density power and interconnect designs, AI-factory grid resilience, and open software for network configuration, cluster readiness, rack management, and bare-metal provisioning. This is a material infrastructure signal: the AI buildout is moving from bespoke megawatt clusters toward repeatable, interoperable gigawatt-scale factory designs. N F SOVEREIGN AI AGENTIC COMPUTING
