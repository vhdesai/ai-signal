# AI valuation concerns hit the IPO market as SK Hynix pauses Solidigm listing

The Wall Street Journal reports that SK Hynix shelved plans for a major US IPO of its flash-memory unit Solidigm, citing market conditions despite continued AI hardware demand. The pause suggests investors are widening the gap between AI narrative strength and acceptable valuation levels. It also signals that infrastructure-adjacent companies may face a stricter pricing regime through 2027. URL: WSJ
