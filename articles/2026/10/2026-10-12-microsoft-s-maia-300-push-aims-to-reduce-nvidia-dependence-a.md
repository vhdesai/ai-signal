# Microsoft’s Maia 300 push aims to reduce Nvidia dependence at scale

Microsoft revealed Maia 300, a custom AI accelerator it plans to deploy at multi-gigawatt scale. Coverage frames the chip as a cost and supply-chain hedge against Nvidia and AMD, not merely another internal silicon project. The strategic implication is that hyperscalers are treating custom accelerators as control points for capacity, margins and bargaining leverage across the AI stack.
