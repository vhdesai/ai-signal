# MIT project tracks how chatbots answer 2026 midterm election questions

MIT professors Adam Berinsky and Chara Podimata describe a project that monitors how AI chatbots respond to questions about the US midterm elections. With election day days away, independent monitoring of chatbot answers about voting is becoming a reputational and possibly regulatory issue for consumer AI providers.
