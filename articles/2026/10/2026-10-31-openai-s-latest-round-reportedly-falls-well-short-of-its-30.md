# OpenAI's latest round reportedly falls well short of its $30 billion goal

The Wall Street Journal reports that OpenAI raised less than half of the $30 billion it originally targeted in its latest funding round. Reuters, citing the Financial Times, said the goal had first been cut to about $20 billion as the tech sell-off dampened investor appetite. Coming the same week Anthropic filed its S-1, the shortfall is the clearest sign yet that private capital for frontier labs is tightening.
