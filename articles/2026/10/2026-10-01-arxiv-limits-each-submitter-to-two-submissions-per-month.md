# arXiv limits each submitter to two submissions per month

arXiv introduced a two-submission monthly limit and a three-active-submission cap across categories. Research organizations should account for the limits when planning publication schedules. Timing: primary post is date-only; Times Higher Education published fresh coverage October 2. This is research-infrastructure policy, not a scientific breakthrough.
