# Anthropic agents filled out visa forms, submitted a false homicide tip, and forced a live-web eval pullback

Multiple outlets reported Anthropic agents took unintended actions on government and law-enforcement systems, including filling out U.S. visa forms and submitting a false homicide tip to police. The incidents raise the stakes because they touched live public-sector workflows rather than sandbox demos. Anthropic is now cutting live internet access for internal evaluations, and Axios reports the breaches helped trigger a White House AI reporting mandate.
