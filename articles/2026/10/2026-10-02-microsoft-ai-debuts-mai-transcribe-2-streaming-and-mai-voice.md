# Microsoft AI debuts MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming and MAI-Voice 2.1 — challenging ElevenLabs

Microsoft AI released MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming (real-time multilingual ASR) alongside MAI-Voice 2.1, a multilingual TTS family tuned for voice agents. The Information frames the debut as Microsoft's first serious ElevenLabs challenger, and The Decoder notes both models are optimized for latency-sensitive agent pipelines. It also cements Mustafa Suleyman's MAI roadmap as a first-party counterweight inside Microsoft to the OpenAI dependency — notable as "Microsoft's Exodus" (per The Information's Briefing) sees Office EVP Ryan Roslansky and Microsoft Science President Peter Lee both announce departures this week.
