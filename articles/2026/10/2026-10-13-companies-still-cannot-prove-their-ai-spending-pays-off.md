# Companies still cannot prove their AI spending pays off

A Wharton / GBK Collective study cited by The Information found most managers still measure AI ROI through productivity proxies such as hours saved and error reduction rather than revenue or output growth. Forty percent of managers measured hours saved per employee, 37% tracked error reduction, and only 27% tracked revenue from AI features, apps or tools. The finding explains why vendors and CIOs are both still searching for harder evidence that AI budgets translate into growth.
