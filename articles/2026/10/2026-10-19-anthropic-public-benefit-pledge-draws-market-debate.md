# Anthropic public-benefit pledge draws market debate

Anthropic's plan to commit 5% of shares to a public benefit fund continued drawing investor and policy reaction. Coverage framed the move as both a governance signal and a pre-IPO positioning tool. The question for investors is whether it improves trust or complicates valuation at a time when rogue-agent risk remains a headline issue.
