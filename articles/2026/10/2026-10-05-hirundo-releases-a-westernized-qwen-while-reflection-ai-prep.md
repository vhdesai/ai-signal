# Hirundo releases a "westernized Qwen" while Reflection AI prepares a US open-weight challenger

Hirundo released a modified Qwen variant tuned to reduce censored or CCP-aligned responses from 89.8% to 2.8%, positioning ideology-removal as a deployable governance product rather than a research exercise. Axios separately reports Reflection AI is preparing an open-weight foundation model to compete with Chinese open models such as DeepSeek and Alibaba's Qwen. Together, the stories show the open-model contest shifting from raw benchmark parity to controllability, ideology, sovereignty and deployment trust.
