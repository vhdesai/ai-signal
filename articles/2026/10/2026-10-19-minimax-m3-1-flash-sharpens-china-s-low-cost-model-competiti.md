# MiniMax M3.1 Flash sharpens China’s low-cost model competition

The Decoder reports that MiniMax M3.1 Flash scored 42 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 13 points above its predecessor, at half the cost. It also reports a 73.7% score on DeepSWE v1.1 for less than $0.30 per task, illustrating how price and usable coding performance are becoming central dimensions of competition; these are reported benchmark results, not independent validation in this digest.
