# Amazon seeks to offload $8B of Nvidia chips; Broadcom amasses $60B for Anthropic chip supply

Reuters and the Financial Times report Amazon is seeking to offload approximately $8 billion of Nvidia chips to outside investors — a sale-leaseback-style structure that reduces balance-sheet exposure to AI capex. Bloomberg separately reports Broadcom amassing $60B to fund chips for Anthropic. Combined with Amazon's $1B community-investment pledge, Nvidia + SoftBank each closing final $10B tranches of OpenAI's $122B round, and Sharon AI's $356M GPU-backed loan at 9.95%, AI-infrastructure financing is openly fracturing across new instruments.
