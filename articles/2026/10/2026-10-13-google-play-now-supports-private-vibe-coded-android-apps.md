# Google Play now supports private, vibe-coded Android apps

Google Play is adding support for private Android apps built with AI, letting users create apps for themselves without publishing them publicly in the Play Store. The feature lowers distribution friction for personal software and legitimizes vibe-coded apps as a consumer workflow. PRODUCT
