# OpenAI models are coming to Google Cloud Marketplace

OpenAI said its frontier models and Codex workflows are becoming available through Google Cloud surfaces, including Gemini Model Garden and Google Cloud Marketplace. The move adds another major enterprise distribution path beyond Azure-centered assumptions and signals increasingly multi-cloud go-to-market patterns among frontier labs. The announcement changes procurement and deployment options more than underlying model capability.
