# Mistral Large 4 is Europe's trillion-parameter answer to US and Chinese models

Mistral released Large 4, a 1T-parameter multimodal model trained on European infrastructure and positioned against both closed US models and Chinese open-weight rivals. Independent tests show a major improvement, though still short of top Claude, GPT-6, and Chinese systems; Mistral's differentiator is cybersecurity work that some closed US models refuse. https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/mistrals-new-1t-model-aims-to-leapfrog-closed-and-open-rivals/
