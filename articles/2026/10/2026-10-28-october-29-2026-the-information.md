# [October 29, 2026] · [The Information]

Google unveiled Gemini 4 Carbon less than a month after Gemini 4 Argon, at the same introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, which The Information reports will double after the teaser period. Google calls Carbon a significant leap in cybersecurity capability and says it is nearly twice as good as Argon at generating secure code. Access starts with vetted cyber defenders and Fairwind partners, alongside participation in government pre-release testing.
