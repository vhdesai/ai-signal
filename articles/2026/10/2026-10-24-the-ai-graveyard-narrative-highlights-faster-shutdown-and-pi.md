# The AI graveyard narrative highlights faster shutdown and pivot cycles

TechCrunch published a running record of AI products and startups that were shut down, rolled back, or materially re-scoped after launch. The pattern reinforces that retention and distribution fit are increasingly decisive, even when model quality is strong. For operators, this raises the importance of portability and disciplined unit-economics planning.
