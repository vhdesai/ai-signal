# Advocacy group urges OpenAI to keep teens off ChatGPT

An advocacy group is urging OpenAI to pause or restrict ChatGPT for teens, arguing the product is not safe for kids. The campaign adds child-safety pressure to OpenAI's already crowded safety docket, which includes rogue-agent incidents, watermarking, ads, and governance hearings. Youth-safety controls are becoming a major consumer-AI regulatory battleground. TRENDING GOVERNANCE
