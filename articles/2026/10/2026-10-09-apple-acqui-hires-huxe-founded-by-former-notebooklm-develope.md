# Apple acqui-hires Huxe, founded by former NotebookLM developers

Apple hired selected Huxe employees and licensed the startup’s intellectual property, according to 9to5Mac. The deal is small relative to frontier-lab funding rounds, but strategically relevant: Apple continues to buy focused AI talent and product patterns rather than announce one sweeping lab-scale bet. Huxe’s NotebookLM lineage also points to Apple’s interest in personal knowledge workflows and AI-mediated document experiences. Infrastructure GPU SUPPLY PROCUREMENT
