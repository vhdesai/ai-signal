# China proposes export controls on 23 AI technologies, including AI agents

China proposed export controls on 23 AI technologies, including AI agents. The proposal shows Beijing is not only responding to U.S. chip controls but building its own regime around AI software, models and agent platforms. If enacted, it could reshape the global market for Chinese open-weight systems and agent tools at the same time those systems are gaining enterprise traction.
