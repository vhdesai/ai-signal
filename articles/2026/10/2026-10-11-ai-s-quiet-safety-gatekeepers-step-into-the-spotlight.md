# AI’s quiet safety gatekeepers step into the spotlight

CNBC examined external AI evaluators, their funding models, access to labs and independence as frontier systems become more consequential. The piece highlights a governance tension: labs need credible outside assessment, but evaluators often depend on the same firms for data access or funding. Independent evaluation is becoming part of AI market infrastructure, not merely a research-side function. URL(s): CNBC
