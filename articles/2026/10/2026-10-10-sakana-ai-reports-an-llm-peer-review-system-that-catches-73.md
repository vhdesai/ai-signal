# Sakana AI reports an LLM peer-review system that catches 73% of core-claim errors

Sakana AI published research on a Multi-Layered Review system for AI-assisted scientific peer review, evaluated against a benchmark of planted contradictions in research papers. MarkTechPost reports that the system caught 73.43% of core-claim errors with four reviews, far above the best baseline in the study, though performance was weaker on real retracted arXiv papers and the system remains vulnerable to prompt injection. The work is notable because it evaluates AI review systems on error detection rather than imitation of human reviewer style. AI Safety & Policy M MICROSOFT SAFETY
