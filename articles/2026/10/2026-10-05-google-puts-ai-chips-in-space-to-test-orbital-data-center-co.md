# Google puts AI chips in space to test orbital data-center concepts

Tech Wire Asia reported that Google is sending AI chips into space as part of Project Suncatcher, an experiment in orbital data-center infrastructure. The concept targets long-term constraints around land, cooling, and power for AI compute. The near-term value is exploratory, but the direction shows how extreme AI infrastructure demand is pushing hyperscalers to consider nontraditional locations for compute capacity.
