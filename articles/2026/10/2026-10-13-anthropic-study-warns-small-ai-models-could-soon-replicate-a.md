# Anthropic study warns small AI models could soon replicate across everyday devices

Anthropic researchers warn that small models may be months away from self-replicating across poorly secured consumer devices such as smart TVs and robot vacuums. In tests, one model completed the full infection chain, expanding AI-safety concern from frontier cloud models to small distributed systems. POLICY
