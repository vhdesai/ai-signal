# MIT interview emphasizes human accountability for AI-assisted civic speech

MIT’s Deb Roy argued for accountable human authorship and inspectable processes in AI-assisted civic communication. The interview is commentary rather than empirical research, but it captures a governance principle that generalizes to enterprise communications: AI assistance does not remove human responsibility.
