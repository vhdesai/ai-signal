# Mistral releases trillion-parameter Large 4 weights for self-hosted cyber defense

Mistral published downloadable weights for Mistral Large 4, letting enterprises and governments run the trillion-parameter model on their own infrastructure. Mistral positions it for coding and cyber defense and says it matches GLM-5.3 on cyber tasks with 47% fewer tokens, a vendor claim. One day after Google gated Gemini 4 Carbon to vetted defenders, the release sharpens the choice between controlled frontier access and self-hosted capability.
