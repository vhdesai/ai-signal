# Zuckerberg Biohub, US partners and Google build a virtual cell

Axios reports Zuckerberg's Biohub, US partners and Google are building a virtual cell, framing it as the beginning of a new scientific paradigm. The project aims to simulate cellular behavior with enough fidelity to accelerate biomedical discovery and drug development.
