# According to the Financial Times, Blackstone, the anchor investor in a $63 billion chip-financing deal for Anthropic,…

According to the Financial Times, Blackstone, the anchor investor in a $63 billion chip-financing deal for Anthropic, now tells investors it expects a double-B rating after the IPO, reversing an earlier expectation of investment grade before 2027. Anthropic and Blackstone declined to comment. A speculative-grade rating would raise Anthropic's cost of debt just as it funds large multi-year compute commitments.
