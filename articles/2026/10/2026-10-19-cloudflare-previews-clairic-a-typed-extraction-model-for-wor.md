# Cloudflare previews Clairic, a typed extraction model for Workers AI

Cloudflare introduced Clairic, a System One-style model that extracts or computes typed values from text and images rather than generating prose. It is designed for tasks such as invoice fields, dates, estimates, and forecasts, and is available in preview on Workers AI, with open weights planned. Cloudflare's demonstration reported faster and lower-cost processing than an LLM pipeline, but it has not yet published a standard independent accuracy benchmark.
