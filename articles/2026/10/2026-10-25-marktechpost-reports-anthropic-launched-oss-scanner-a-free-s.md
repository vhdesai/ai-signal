# MarkTechPost reports Anthropic launched OSS Scanner, a free service that runs frontier models against enrolled…

MarkTechPost reports Anthropic launched OSS Scanner, a free service that runs frontier models against enrolled open-source repositories and sends findings directly to maintainers. Reported early metrics indicate high finding volume, with maintainers retaining remediation control and triage responsibility. The policy significance is a faster AI-assisted disclosure loop that still depends on strong downstream verification.
