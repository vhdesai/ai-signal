# vLLM Semantic Router releases Decision 3.0 open decision-model family

vLLM Semantic Router released the Decision 3.0 open model family for structured choice and routing tasks. The release adds open infrastructure to the same decision-model trend seen in OpenAI, Microsoft and Cloudflare tooling. For enterprises, the open-model angle may matter where local deployment, auditability or cost control are stronger requirements than frontier reasoning. URL(s): MarkTechPost
