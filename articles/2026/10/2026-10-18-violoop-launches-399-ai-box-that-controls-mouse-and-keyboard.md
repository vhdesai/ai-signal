# Violoop launches $399 AI box that controls mouse and keyboard; Grok gets an email identity

Violoop launched a $399 AI box that uses a person's mouse and keyboard to automate desktop workflows, a physical answer to browser and screen-control agents. Grok Bot also added a dedicated email address that lets it sign up for services, coordinate schedules and interact with third-party workflows. These products push agents toward durable identities and cross-app control, but they also sharpen audit, permission and abuse questions.
