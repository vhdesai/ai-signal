# Anthropic's IPO is setting the tempo for the entire listings pipeline

NYSE Group President Lynn Martin said the IPO pipeline is "really strong" but has been held back by Anthropic's S-1, because portfolio managers have limited attention; Nscale, which filed publicly last month for an NYSE listing, has not yet moved. Wellington's Matt Witheiler countered that public investors can absorb SpaceX, OpenAI and Anthropic "with room to spare" and called Anthropic's filing "very liberating." PitchBook data show late access was costly: only 28% of investors who bought SpaceX secondaries in 2026 have received capital back, versus 60% of earlier buyers. Sequencing, not capacity, looks set to govern the AI listing window.
