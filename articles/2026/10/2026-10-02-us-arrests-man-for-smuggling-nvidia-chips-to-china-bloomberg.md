# US arrests man for smuggling Nvidia chips to China — Bloomberg flags Nvidia's export-control "blind spots"

Federal law enforcement arrested an individual for smuggling Nvidia chips to China, The Information reports. Bloomberg's follow-up piece argues the case exposes Nvidia's blind spots in supply-chain accountability, and intersects directly with today's Tencent-Oracle $7B lease (via offshore capacity) and the Baidu chip unit's reported $50B Hong Kong IPO. All three stories collectively question whether export controls can hold against sovereign-capital demand.
