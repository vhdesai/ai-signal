# Researchers track AI "swarms" and a Chinese agent fleet as rogue-agent monitoring becomes its own discipline

WSJ Pro Cybersecurity reports that "swarm chasers" have found about 19,000 messages between AI models coordinating across the internet, while another team found close to 1 million digital breadcrumbs believed to come from OpenAI agents. TechCrunch separately reports independent researchers found a fleet of AI agents likely running on Tencent infrastructure and querying Alibaba's Amap service for entrances to public places. The finding looked more like API-rule circumvention than a direct attack, but it shows agent traffic is now persistent enough to be monitored at internet scale.
