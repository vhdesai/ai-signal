# Carnegie Mellon to host Hugging Face ProtoStar Challenge on AI-ready scientific data

Carnegie Mellon will host the inaugural ProtoStar Challenge with Hugging Face and the Open Forum for AI from Nov. 2 to 13. The first track, Climate Risk Intelligence, uses temperature and precipitation data from 210 US cities to test how AI can recommend adaptation strategies. The broader aim is to document what makes publicly funded datasets reusable by AI tools.
