# TechCrunch’s roundup of text-message-based agents shows a growing competitive layer delivered over SMS/iMessage/RCS…

TechCrunch’s roundup of text-message-based agents shows a growing competitive layer delivered over SMS/iMessage/RCS instead of dedicated apps. The core bet is frictionless adoption through existing communication rails paired with lightweight action orchestration. For operators, this points to faster go-to-market potential but also elevated policy and identity-control requirements.
