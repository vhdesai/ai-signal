# Reuters: independent AI review fight turns on what gets made public

Reuters reports the AI industry's call for independent reviews is colliding with a fight over how much review data should be disclosed publicly. Labs want credibility without exposing sensitive model details, while policymakers and civil society groups want enough transparency to make reviews meaningful. The dispute may decide whether third-party audits become real accountability or another trust-us mechanism.
