# Microsoft president says the UK is best positioned to lead global AI safety regulation

Microsoft president Brad Smith argued that AI now needs urgent regulation and that the U.K. is better positioned than the U.S. or EU to drive global AI safety rules. Smith pointed to Britain's AI Security Institute, outside evaluator access, incident notification, and potential pause or shutdown mechanisms as pieces of a more credible oversight model. His comments reinforce a growing industry view that voluntary commitments are insufficient for systems that can act autonomously and create cybersecurity or public-safety risks.
