# OpenAI is reportedly preparing a new $30B private raise ahead of IPO

TechCrunch reports OpenAI is working on a funding round of up to $30 billion at a reported $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, with terms still in negotiation. If completed near that level, it would reset private-market valuation benchmarks and materially raise expectations for a later public listing. The report also indicates possible links between financing and future compute-capacity arrangements.
