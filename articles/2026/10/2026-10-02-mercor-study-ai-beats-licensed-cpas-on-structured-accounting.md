# Mercor study: AI beats licensed CPAs on structured accounting tasks, still needs supervision

A new Mercor study shows frontier AI models now outperform licensed CPAs on structured accounting tasks for both speed and accuracy — a reversal from 18 months ago. On the harder APEX Benchmark no single model closes the books end-to-end, so human review remains necessary. The result is the cleanest profession-level reversal published in 2026 and will intensify the Big-Four audit-automation conversation into Q4.
