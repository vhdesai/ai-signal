# Tencent Leases 100,000 AI Chips From Oracle in a $7B Deal — Reopens the "China Buys US Compute via Lease" Workaround

Benzinga, Reuters, and Quartz confirm Tencent has signed a $7B lease with Oracle for 100,000 advanced AI chips, giving the Chinese tech giant access to US AI infrastructure through Southeast Asian capacity. The deal rescued Oracle stock from a four-day losing streak and reopens the "China buys US compute via lease" workaround that Washington attempted to close via export controls on direct sales. Expect immediate Treasury and Commerce scrutiny. The structure — routing capacity rather than selling silicon — will be the template for Chinese labs wanting Nvidia-tier training compute while US silicon stays on US soil.
