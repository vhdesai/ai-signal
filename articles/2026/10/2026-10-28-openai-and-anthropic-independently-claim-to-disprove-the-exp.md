# OpenAI and Anthropic independently claim to disprove the Exponential Time Hypothesis

OpenAI says its models found an algorithm on October 16 that disproves the Exponential Time Hypothesis, while Anthropic researcher Levent Alpoge says Anthropic models reached the same result independently on the same day. OpenAI says there is no immediate encryption impact, but the claim challenges long-standing assumptions about computational hardness.
