# INDUSTRY Replit makes OpenAI its default over Anthropic

Replit CEO Amjad Masad said OpenAI will replace Anthropic as Replit's default model provider, though Claude will remain available to customers. It is another warning that model vendors' biggest customers may become distribution rivals or re-optimize toward cheaper and more controllable defaults. https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/17/replit-is-no-longer-working-with-anthropic-ceo-says/
