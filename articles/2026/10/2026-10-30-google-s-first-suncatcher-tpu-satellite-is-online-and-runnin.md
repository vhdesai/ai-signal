# Google's first Suncatcher TPU satellite is online and running Gemma 4

Google's Suncatcher prototype carries four TPUs on up to 1 kW of power and is now running Gemma 4 inference in orbit. Power limits mean a TPU can run for only about 20 minutes at a time. The test will measure radiation and thermal effects on the chips, and two more satellites with optical inter-satellite links are planned for 2027. This is a long-term bet aimed at the 2030s, not near-term capacity. SEMICONDUCTORS SUPPLY CHAIN
