# Workday and Anthropic partner to connect reasoning to business rules

Workday and Anthropic announced a partnership to connect Claude-style reasoning to the rules that run business processes across Workday environments. The premise is that enterprise agents need not only language capability but explicit policy, HR, finance and workflow constraints. The partnership is a strong signal that business-app vendors will compete on governed reasoning layers, not just embedded chat.
