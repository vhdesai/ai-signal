# Anthropic circulates draft U.S. bill with catastrophic-risk intervention thresholds

TechCrunch reports Anthropic circulated draft federal language that could allow courts to halt model release under defined imminent catastrophic-risk conditions. The proposal frames intervention around explicit harm thresholds and a legal process rather than purely voluntary commitments. If advanced, it would push U.S. AI governance toward threshold-based enforceability.
