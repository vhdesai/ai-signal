# Palantir and Ondas launch Regna AI for sovereign defense operations

Ondas and Palantir launched Regna AI, a venture focused on sovereign artificial intelligence for defense operations and secure model deployment. The launch extends Palantir's sovereign-AI strategy into a dedicated defense-AI structure. It also gives investors another reason to treat Palantir as the public-market proxy for government-controlled AI deployments.
