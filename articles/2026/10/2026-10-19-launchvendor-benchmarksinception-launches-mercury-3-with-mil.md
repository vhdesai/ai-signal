# LAUNCHVENDOR BENCHMARKSInception launches Mercury 3 with million-token context

Inception released Mercury 3, a diffusion-based language model that refines text in parallel rather than generating strictly token by token. The company reports 1,068 tokens per second on Nvidia GPUs and a one-million-token context window, positioning it for latency-sensitive agents, search and voice applications. Its speed and quality comparisons are vendor-reported and should be evaluated on representative workloads.
