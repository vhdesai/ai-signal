# [October 29, 2026] · [PitchBook]

Oracle shares surged more than 10% after it capped capex-related capital raises and projected positive free cash flow. PitchBook reports private-credit lenders see digital infrastructure as their biggest 2027 opportunity, and the Wall Street Journal notes bank lending jumped on the data-center build-out even as bankers remain cautious. Capital remains abundant, but markets now reward a visible path to self-funding.
