# Anthropic commits 5% stake to public-benefit fund before IPO

Anthropic plans to set aside 5% of shares for a new public-benefit fund before its IPO. The fund is designed to support broader public-interest work as Anthropic heads toward public markets under intense safety scrutiny. It is also a governance signal to investors and policymakers that Anthropic wants its public-benefit status to have real economic weight.
