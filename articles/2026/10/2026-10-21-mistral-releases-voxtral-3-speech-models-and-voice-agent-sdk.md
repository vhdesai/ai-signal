# Mistral releases Voxtral 3 speech models and voice-agent SDK

MarkTechPost reports Mistral launched Voxtral 3 with transcription, text-to-speech, and LiveKit-based voice-agent tooling in public preview. The release targets multilingual speech workflows and lower integration overhead for real-time conversational systems. Performance and ranking claims are vendor-attributed and should be interpreted as directional until independently replicated.
