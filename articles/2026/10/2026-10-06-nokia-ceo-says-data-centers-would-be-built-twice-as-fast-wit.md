# Nokia CEO says data centers would be built twice as fast without supply constraints

CNBC reported that Nokia's CEO said data centers would be built roughly twice as fast without supply constraints. The comment reflects continuing bottlenecks in AI infrastructure buildouts, where demand is constrained by equipment availability, power delivery, networking, and site readiness. For AI capacity planning, this reinforces that procurement and supply-chain risk remain strategic variables, not back-office details.
