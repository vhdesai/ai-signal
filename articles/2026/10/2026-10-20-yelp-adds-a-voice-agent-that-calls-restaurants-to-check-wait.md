# Yelp adds a voice agent that calls restaurants to check wait times

Yelp's new Check for Me feature lets users submit party size and a phone number so an AI voice agent can call a restaurant and text back its wait time. Yelp also added OpenTable booking, chat-history access, preference memory, and custom reminders to its assistant. The feature illustrates the practical consumer value of agents that act across business phone systems, while raising the prospect of agent-to-agent interactions and the need for reliable disclosure and escalation.
