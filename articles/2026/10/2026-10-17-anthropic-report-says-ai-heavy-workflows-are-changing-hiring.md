# Anthropic report says AI-heavy workflows are changing hiring mix and increasing coordination load

Anthropic published internal workforce analysis showing fewer junior technical hires, a smaller technical share of the workforce, and more time spent in meetings as AI tools diffuse through the company. TechCrunch reports that junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9%, while meeting time per employee rose 20% year over year. The executive implication is that AI adoption may shift bottlenecks from task execution to human judgment, review, coordination, and accountability. Products & Tools G GOOGLE AGENTS
