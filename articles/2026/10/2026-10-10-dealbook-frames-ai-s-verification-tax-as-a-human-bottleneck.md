# DealBook frames AI’s “verification tax” as a human bottleneck on efficiency

DealBook argued that AI gains are increasingly constrained by the time humans spend checking AI output. The “verification tax” is a useful executive lens: even if AI accelerates drafting or analysis, labor can shift into review, exception handling and accountability. Enterprise ROI will depend on designing workflows where the cost of verification does not erase the benefit of automation.
