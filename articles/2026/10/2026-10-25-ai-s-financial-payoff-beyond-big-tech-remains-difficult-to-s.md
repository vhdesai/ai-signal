# AI's financial payoff beyond big tech remains difficult to see in aggregate data

TechCrunch reports Apollo chief economist Torsten Slok's assessment that margins and revenue per worker for the S&P 500 excluding seven large technology companies have not yet shown an AI payoff; Business Insider also highlighted that view. The aggregate finding challenges vendors to demonstrate economic outcomes rather than adoption counts. It does not establish that every individual enterprise AI deployment lacks value.
