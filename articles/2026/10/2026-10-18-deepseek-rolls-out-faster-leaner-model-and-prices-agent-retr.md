# DeepSeek rolls out faster, leaner model and prices agent retrieval below rivals

DeepSeek unveiled a faster, cheaper model with improved performance while Forkast reported the company is monetizing agent retrieval at $0.75 per 1,000 calls, far below rivals charging $5 or more. The paired model and retrieval-pricing strategy reinforces DeepSeek’s role as a global cost disruptor. It also gives enterprises a new benchmark for what agent memory and retrieval should cost at scale.
