# Enterprise data is emerging as a primary AI training asset

Business Insider reports that Micro1 is offering six- and seven-figure payments for corporate workplace data and planning to spend up to $1 billion on acquisitions. The move reinforces a structural shift: model quality at work increasingly depends on proprietary operational context, not just public web corpora. The strategic trade-off for enterprises is whether to sell data now or treat it as a long-term competitive moat.
