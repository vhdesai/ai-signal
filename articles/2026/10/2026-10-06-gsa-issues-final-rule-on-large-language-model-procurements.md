# GSA issues final rule on large-language-model procurements

The Government Contracts Legal Forum reported that the U.S. General Services Administration issued a final rule on large-language-model procurements. The rule signals that public-sector AI buying is moving toward more formal requirements for contracting, risk allocation, and model governance. Vendors selling LLM-based systems to government buyers should expect procurement rules to become a major channel for AI safety and accountability requirements.
