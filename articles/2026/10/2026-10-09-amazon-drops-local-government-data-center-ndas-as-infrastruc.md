# Amazon drops local-government data-center NDAs as infrastructure scrutiny rises

TechCrunch reported that Amazon will stop using NDAs in data-center negotiations with local governments, following a similar Microsoft move. The change reflects mounting community and policy pressure around AI infrastructure, power usage and local transparency.
