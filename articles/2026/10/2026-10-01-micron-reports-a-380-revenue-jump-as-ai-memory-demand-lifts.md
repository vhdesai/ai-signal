# Micron Reports a 380% Revenue Jump as AI-Memory Demand Lifts Chip Peers

Micron reported a 380% revenue jump for the quarter ending September 3 as AI-driven DRAM demand lifts memory-chip peers. DealBook contextualizes with SK Hynix’s $26.5B US share sale in July and ~170% year-to-date move; Apple called the memory crunch a "hundred-year flood." Combined with CoreWeave adding the Nvidia Vera CPU to its portfolio and GMI Cloud’s $668M raise, the memory/compute capital-flow story is now visibly broadening beyond foundry and GPU names into the full chip stack — and consumer electronics pricing is already being hit.
