# OpenAI adds invisible watermarking to AI-generated text

OpenAI is adding an invisible watermark to AI-generated text, according to Mashable. The move arrives as regulators, educators and publishers demand provenance for AI-written content and as OpenAI faces mounting pressure from rogue-agent incidents. If robust against paraphrasing and editing, text watermarking could become the first mainstream provenance control used by millions of ChatGPT users.
