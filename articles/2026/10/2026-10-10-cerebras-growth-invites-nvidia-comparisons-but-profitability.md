# Cerebras growth invites Nvidia comparisons, but profitability and customer concentration remain key gaps

Yahoo Finance/Motley Fool analysis compares Cerebras' expected 2026 core revenue with Nvidia's data-center business nearly a decade ago, while emphasizing important differences. Cerebras is growing quickly and selling differentiated wafer-scale inference capacity, but it remains unprofitable on an adjusted operating basis and had three customers representing roughly three-quarters of second-quarter revenue. The analysis is a useful reminder that AI-chip challengers need not only technical differentiation but also a broader customer base and a credible path to sustained margins.
