# Claude can now open and edit Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides

VentureBeat reported that Claude can now open directly in Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides, and conversely open and edit those files inside Claude. The integration is a practical sign of where enterprise assistants are heading: cross-application context, file editing, and bidirectional workflows rather than isolated chat. The adoption question is whether organizations can pair these integrations with permissions, auditability, and data-boundary controls. Infrastructure TELECOM AI OPEN MODELS
