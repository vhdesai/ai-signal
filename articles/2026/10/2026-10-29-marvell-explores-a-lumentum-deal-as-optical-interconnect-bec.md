# Marvell explores a Lumentum deal as optical interconnect becomes an AI bottleneck

Marvell has been in talks to buy data-center optics maker Lumentum, which has a market capitalization above $80 billion, according to The Information, citing Bloomberg; a deal could be announced as soon as next week, though its status is unclear. Lumentum shares rose 8% on the report while Marvell fell 8% after hours. A transaction would rank among the largest semiconductor deals and underscores how optics and interconnect have become strategic constraints on AI cluster scaling.
