# Nvidia reportedly in talks to guarantee debt for Nscale's Anthropic data center

The Information and Bloomberg report that Nvidia may provide a residual value guarantee for Nscale's West Virginia campus. Anthropic has a $44.6 billion contract for the campus's first 460 MW building, which is expected to use Vera Rubin chips. Under the guarantee, Nvidia would take over the facility if Nscale defaulted on construction payments. That would extend Nvidia's support, reported at about $170 billion across neoclouds and large customers, from leases and chips into construction risk. GOOGLE SPACE COMPUTE
