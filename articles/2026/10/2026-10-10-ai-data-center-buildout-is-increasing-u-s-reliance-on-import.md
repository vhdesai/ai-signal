# AI data-center buildout is increasing U.S. reliance on imported semiconductors

Yahoo Finance reported that U.S. semiconductor imports reached nearly $90.5 billion from January through August, up from $49.2 billion a year earlier. The AI data-center boom is therefore intensifying dependence on overseas chip supply even as policy rhetoric emphasizes domestic resilience. For executives, supply-chain risk now sits alongside power, real estate and financing in AI infrastructure planning. URL(s): Yahoo Finance
