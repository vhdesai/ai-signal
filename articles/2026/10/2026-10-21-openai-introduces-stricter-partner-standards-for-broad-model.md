# OpenAI introduces stricter partner standards for broad model access

OpenAI published new standards for partners that distribute model access, including stronger consistency expectations around policy and safeguards. The update indicates distribution expansion is now coupled with tighter governance requirements at access gateways and routing layers. For enterprise buyers, partner-control posture is becoming part of model risk due diligence.
