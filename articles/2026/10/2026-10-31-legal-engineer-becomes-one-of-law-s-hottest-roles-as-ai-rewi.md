# Legal engineer becomes one of law's hottest roles as AI rewires the profession

DealBook reports that Legora has built a team of about 200 legal engineers since 2024 and Harvey employs about 180, while Latham & Watkins advertises legal engineering attorney roles at $220,000 to $250,000. Almost 50 lawyers left the 200 largest US firms for AI companies in the first half of the year, and Weil, Gotshal & Manges' collapse this week underscored pressure on traditional firm economics. Hybrid domain-plus-AI roles are emerging as the practical bridge for deploying agents in expert professions.
