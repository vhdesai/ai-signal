# Mathematicians react with shock and grief to OpenAI's proof flood

After OpenAI published more than 700 AI-generated manuscripts claiming solutions to open math problems, a math blog collected over 100 researcher responses ranging from fascination to grief. The controversy is now about whether mass proof generation changes the social process by which mathematics finds and values insight.
