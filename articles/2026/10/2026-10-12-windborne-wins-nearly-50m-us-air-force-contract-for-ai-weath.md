# WindBorne wins nearly $50M US Air Force contract for AI weather-balloon data

WindBorne Systems won a US Air Force contract worth nearly $50M for weather-balloon data used to improve AI-driven forecasting. The award shows defense buyers continuing to fund AI-adjacent sensing infrastructure, not only models and drones.
