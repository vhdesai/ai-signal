# Meta launches approximately $35 billion in equity offerings for its AI expansion

Meta announced concurrent offerings of roughly $22.5 billion in common stock via forward-sale agreements and $12.5 billion in preferred-stock depositary shares, alongside a possible future $60 billion at-the-market program. The company connects the plan to its AI infrastructure investment and forecasts $135 billion–$145 billion in 2026 capital spending. These are launches and conditional plans, not $95 billion of completed financing; Meta says it does not expect initial proceeds from the common-stock forward sales. URL: Meta announcement
