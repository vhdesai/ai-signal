# Nvidia's China AI-chip share falls as Huawei gains

Multiple outlets report Nvidia's China AI-chip share has fallen sharply as Huawei gains, with some coverage saying Huawei has captured around 28% market share and Nvidia shipments have plunged. The shift reflects U.S. export controls, Chinese substitution and Huawei's Ascend ecosystem push. It is one of the clearest signs that Nvidia's global dominance has a China-specific ceiling.
