# Nvidia's $100B dealmaking engine moves toward robots, self-driving cars and local AI

The Information reports Nvidia held nearly $100B of equity investments and $25B in future investment commitments as of late July, after inking more than $140B of deals over two months, including a $105B credit guarantee. Nvidia explored but missed an OpenRouter acquisition before Stripe's $8B bid, then moved on to other targets; the report says future targets could include robots, self-driving cars and local AI.
