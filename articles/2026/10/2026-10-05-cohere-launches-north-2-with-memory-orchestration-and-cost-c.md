# Cohere launches North 2 with memory, orchestration and cost controls for enterprise agents

Cohere launched North 2, adding cross-session memory, reusable skills, shared libraries, app generation, automations, enterprise connectors and granular administrative cost controls. The platform supports cloud, on-premises, hybrid and air-gapped deployments, emphasizing governance through roles, quotas, token limits, guardrails and autonomy policies. North 2 directly targets enterprises that want persistent agent workflows without unpredictable frontier-model spend or sovereignty tradeoffs.
