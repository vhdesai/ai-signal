# Cloudflare previews Clairic, a model for structured document extraction

Cloudflare’s Clairic lets developers specify fields to extract from unstructured documents and returns structured values with confidence scores; it is available in preview on Workers AI, with open weights planned. Its single-pass extraction approach could make document workflows faster and less expensive than using a general-purpose LLM for every step, though Cloudflare’s comparisons are not independently validated here.
