# Black Forest Labs ships FLUX 3 Image with multi-step localized editing, 4K output

BFL released FLUX 3 Image, the image-generation side of its FLUX 3 model family. It supports multi-step edits confined to selected regions without perturbing the rest of the frame, bounding-box composition with up to 10 reference images, and native output up to 4K. Open weights are promised "in the next few weeks," which would make it the strongest permissively-licensed image model vs. Flux.1 Pro and the previous Stable-Diffusion-family entrants.
