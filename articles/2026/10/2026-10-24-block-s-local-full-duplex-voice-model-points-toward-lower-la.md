# Block's local, full-duplex voice model points toward lower-latency assistants

TechCrunch reports Block introduced Dessa, a model designed to listen and speak concurrently while running on consumer-grade hardware. The architecture may reduce latency and cloud dependence for voice-first applications, though broader access and independent performance evaluation are still pending. It is a deployment-oriented advance rather than evidence of commercial-scale adoption. URL: TechCrunch article
