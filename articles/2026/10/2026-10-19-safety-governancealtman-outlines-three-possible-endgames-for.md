# SAFETY GOVERNANCEAltman outlines three possible endgames for AI development

The Information reports that Sam Altman described three scenarios for developing AI safely, including one in which a single firm consumes the industry's GPU capacity. The framing puts compute concentration alongside technical safety: governance mechanisms must address access and systemic dependency, not only model behavior.
