# Hark releases an AI personal assistant focused on privacy and computer use

Hark released Hark Pro, a full-screen AI assistant built around computer use rather than general-purpose chat. The product connects to email, calendar, files, and payment accounts, shows users how it navigates the web, and positions itself as more privacy-conscious than ad-driven assistants. The launch reinforces a fast-forming category: personal AI operating systems competing on task completion, trust, and how much of a user's digital life they can safely access.
