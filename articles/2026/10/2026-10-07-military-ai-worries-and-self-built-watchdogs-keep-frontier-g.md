# Military AI worries and self-built watchdogs keep frontier governance under pressure

Fortune reports an OpenAI executive said what militaries could do with AI “keeps me up at night,” highlighting the tension between frontier labs pursuing government business and warning about military misuse. NBC News separately reports Meta's Oversight Board warned AI companies about relying on self-built watchdog systems. The combined signal: AI governance is increasingly contested at the same time labs are expanding into defense, public-sector and consumer-agent deployments.
