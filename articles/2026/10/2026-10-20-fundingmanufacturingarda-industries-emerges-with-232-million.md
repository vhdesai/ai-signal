# FUNDINGMANUFACTURINGArda Industries emerges with $232 million for manufacturing AI

DealBook reports that Arda Industries is emerging from stealth with a $232 million Series A at a $1.5 billion valuation, led by Altimeter, Accel and Khosla Ventures. Led by former OpenAI research chief Bob McGrew, the company aims to consolidate manufacturing-engineering work in one platform and eventually use AI agents to accelerate assembly-line reconfiguration. Its ambitions concern physical production workflows; the newsletter does not establish that the proposed time savings have been achieved broadly.
