# Microsoft pulls out of Nscale U.K. data-center deal over power-supply issues

Microsoft's exit from Nscale's U.K. data-center project continued to reverberate, with several outlets highlighting power-supply delays stretching into the 2030s. The withdrawal is a major setback for the U.K.'s AI infrastructure ambitions and a reminder that grid readiness is now a core AI strategy variable. Compute deals increasingly fail not on model demand, but on electricity.
