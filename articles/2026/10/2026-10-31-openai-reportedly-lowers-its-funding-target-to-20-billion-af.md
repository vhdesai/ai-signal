# OpenAI reportedly lowers its funding target to $20 billion after a lukewarm investor response

The Financial Times reports that OpenAI cut its target for the current round from $30 billion to $20 billion. A group of UAE investors is leading the round, and the first close is expected to be lower. The Wall Street Journal says OpenAI has secured about $11 billion so far. Talks had previously been reported at a $1.4 trillion valuation, then $1.25 trillion. The pullback comes alongside the tech sell-off over AI capex and weaker credit expectations for Anthropic, which suggests private investors are now pricing financing risk into frontier-lab rounds.
