# Yelp adds AI calls to check restaurant wait times

Yelp is adding AI features that can call restaurants on a user’s behalf to check wait times, alongside personalized recommendations that retain user preferences. The feature brings agentic actions into a consumer service, making consent, call accuracy, and clear disclosure part of the product experience—not just model-quality questions.
