# Reported Nvidia–Reflection AI talks remain unconfirmed but strategically notable

Reuters relayed Financial Times reporting that Nvidia is in early discussions to invest further in or acquire Reflection AI, while noting it could not independently verify the report. Even unconfirmed, the story fits Nvidia’s broader pattern of defending the AI demand stack through stakes in model, infrastructure and application-layer assets. Treat this as deal watch, not a closed transaction. URL(s): MarketScreener
