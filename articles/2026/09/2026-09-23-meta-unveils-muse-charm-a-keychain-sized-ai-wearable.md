# Meta unveils "Muse Charm," a keychain-sized AI wearable

Muse Charm is a small pendant-style device designed to keep users continuously connected to the Muse agent, and marks Meta's entry into the crowded AI-gadget category. Zuckerberg said it would be available to order around the holidays. It complements rather than replaces the glasses line.
