# Qualcomm launches two new smartphone chips with an AI emphasis

Qualcomm's new top mobile SoC can run a 30B mixture-of-experts model entirely on-device, materially widening the ceiling for what phones can execute without a cloud round-trip. The second chip targets the tier just below, extending on-device inference deeper into the mid-range. It shortens the runway for handset OEMs betting on local-agent experiences and pressures cloud-only assistant strategies.
