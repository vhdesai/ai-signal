# AI-Assisted Team Closes the Last Open Case of the Inverse Galois Problem

A six-person team — Bjorn Poonen (MIT), Rachel Pries (Colorado State), Kyu-Hwan Lee (UConn), Xiaoyu Huang (Temple), Blake Jackson (ICARM) and Shaowu Zhang (Caltech) — realized the Mathieu group M23 as a Galois group over the rationals, the last of the 26 sporadic simple groups to resist since the 1984–1989 program. The collaboration formed at an American Institute of Mathematics workshop in May and closed the problem in under three months. Participants are explicit that AI supplied computational scale while humans set direction: an early AI recommendation to push numerical precision past 90 digits was abandoned for a human-chosen change of coordinates that resolved the equations at 40 digits.
