# Amazon Blocks Meta's Muse Agent From Shopping on Amazon.com

Amazon began serving Muse users a blocking notice on the night of September 20, stating that "continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use." Amazon says Meta never disclosed that Muse would access its store, that the agent does not identify itself while browsing, and that it appears to capture and store customer credentials. Notably, Amazon framed the action around its customer contract rather than anti-hacking law — a direct consequence of losing its Ninth Circuit injunction against Perplexity's Comet browser on August 4. The lesson for enterprise agent workflows: capability is not access, and destination platforms retain the veto.
