# xAI releases Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0 for lower-cost speech-to-text

xAI released Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0, claiming twice the accuracy of version 1.0 while keeping batch transcription pricing at $0.10 per hour and streaming at $0.20 per hour. The model supports batch and real-time transcription, word-level timestamps, speaker diarization, multichannel audio, key-term biasing, and multilingual transcription. xAI also said Atlassian is using the model for Loom transcripts, positioning speech recognition as a workflow input for downstream coding and collaboration agents.
