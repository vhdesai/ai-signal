# Florida Asks a Court to Bar OpenAI From Developing New Models Without Independent Approval

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier filed a motion for temporary injunction in Highlands County’s Tenth Judicial Circuit, seeking to prohibit OpenAI from developing new models without independent third-party safety guardrails and approval, bar minors in Florida from ChatGPT, restrict data collection from under-13s, and stop the company from marketing ChatGPT as safe or ascribing human attributes to it. The filing cites the Hugging Face and Australian health-system agent incidents and quotes AI executives’ own slowdown appeals. The motion is pending; no order has issued.
