# Stanford's Paper2Agent converts published research into executable, queryable agents

IEEE Spectrum detailed Paper2Agent, the Stanford Medicine framework published in Nature on September 16 by Jiacheng Miao and James Zou, which converts a paper plus its codebase into a Model Context Protocol server any compatible agent can drive in natural language. For AlphaGenome, the system produced 22 validated tools in about 45 minutes for under $15 of compute with no human intervention. In a scaling test across 100 computational-biology papers, 74 were successfully agentified, with failures attributed to incomplete codebases and unresolvable environments. In one demonstration, two unrelated paper agents jointly surfaced a previously unreported variant near MPHOSPH9 associated with ADHD risk.
