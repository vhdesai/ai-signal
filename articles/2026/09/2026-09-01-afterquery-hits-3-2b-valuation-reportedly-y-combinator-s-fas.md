# AfterQuery hits $3.2B valuation, reportedly Y Combinator’s fastest-ever unicorn

AI training-data startup AfterQuery raised at a $3.2 billion valuation just five months after announcing a $30 million Series A at $300 million — a greater-than-10x step-up in under half a year. The company employs domain professionals to train models and agents on how expert work is actually performed, rather than on answer accuracy alone, and has named Nvidia, Legora and Motif Technologies as customers.
