# IBM and NASA release an open-source AI foundation model for lunar science

IBM and NASA released an open-source foundation model targeting lunar science and exploration, positioned as a complement to the earlier Prithvi Earth-observation models. The release extends the growing catalog of open scientific foundation models for domain-specific research and gives the NASA–IBM partnership a fresh flagship. It is also a notable public-sector counterpoint to the wave of closed frontier launches this week.
