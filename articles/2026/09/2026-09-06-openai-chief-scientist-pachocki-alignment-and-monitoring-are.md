# OpenAI chief scientist Pachocki: alignment and monitoring are not keeping pace with scaling

In a companion essay titled "An Alien Mind," OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki writes that current AI systems are increasingly capable and increasingly hard to interpret, and warns that no frontier lab — including OpenAI — has alignment and monitoring adequate to safely scale at the current maximum rate. He calls for stronger safeguards and explicit international coordination. Publishing this the same weekend as OpenAI's research-acceleration post is a deliberate, notable acknowledgment from inside the lab that its own pace outstrips its safety tooling, and it hands regulators and enterprise buyers a direct citation to raise in procurement and policy discussions.
