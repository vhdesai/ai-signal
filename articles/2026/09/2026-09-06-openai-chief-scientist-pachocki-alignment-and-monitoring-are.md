# OpenAI chief scientist Pachocki: alignment and monitoring are not keeping pace with scaling

In a companion essay titled "An Alien Mind" published alongside the research-acceleration post, OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki writes that current AI systems are increasingly capable and increasingly hard to interpret, and warns that no frontier lab — OpenAI included — has alignment and monitoring adequate to safely scale at the current maximum rate. He calls for stronger safeguards and explicit international coordination. Publishing this the same weekend as the productivity claim is a deliberate acknowledgment from inside the lab that its own pace outstrips its safety tooling, and it hands regulators and enterprise buyers a direct citation to raise in procurement and policy discussions.
