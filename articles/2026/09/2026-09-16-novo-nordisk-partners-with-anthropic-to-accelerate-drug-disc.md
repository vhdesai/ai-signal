# Novo Nordisk partners with Anthropic to accelerate drug discovery using Claude

*The Wall Street Journal* reports Novo Nordisk has partnered with Anthropic to use Claude for drug discovery workflows — one of the most consequential pharma-AI deals to date. The partnership joins a wave of scientific-AI wins for Anthropic, including its Fermat's Last Theorem formalization and the reported Neptune/Poseidon protein-design programs. For enterprise buyers, it's another signal that model providers' most valuable near-term wins are landing in scientific R&D rather than generic productivity. - https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/novo-partners-with-anthropic-to-speed-up-drug-discovery-using-claude
