# Ant Group consolidates Alipay into one business group, doubling down on "agentic commerce"

Ant Group merged its Digital Payment, Alipay, and Zhima Credit business groups into a single Alipay unit specifically to power AI "agentic commerce" — the next iteration of last week's Agentic Mobile Protocol and Ant International agent rollout. Ron Johnson (former Apple retail chief) may not buy Silicon Valley's agentic-commerce thesis (per today's TechCrunch), but Ant is now the largest player structurally reorganizing around it. For payment-strategy executives, Ant's org move is the concrete competitive marker to react against.
