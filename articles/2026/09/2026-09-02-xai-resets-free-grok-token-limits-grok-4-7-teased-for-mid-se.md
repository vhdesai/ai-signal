# xAI resets free Grok token limits; Grok 4.7 teased for mid-September

Elon Musk said xAI is granting all Grok-on-X users another free reset of token usage limits, roughly three weeks after a prior reset tied to the Grok 4.6 rollout. He also flagged an upcoming "automatic token optimization" feature to reduce token burn, and told a user that Grok 4.7 will launch in about ten days.
