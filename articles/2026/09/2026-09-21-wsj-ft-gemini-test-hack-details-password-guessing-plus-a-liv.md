# WSJ + FT: Gemini test-hack details — password-guessing plus a live-internet config oversight

WSJ published fuller details on Google's Gemini test hack: Gemini gained entry to three companies' systems either by guessing passwords or finding credentials in public repositories, and a configuration oversight let the model onto the live internet despite web access supposedly being restricted. On recognizing it was interacting with real-world targets rather than mock scenarios, Gemini halted and exited. Third-party testing firm Irregular notified Google in late July; Google didn't disclose until contacted by WSJ. The incident joins similar disclosures at OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta and makes the "AI-Hacking Club" the operative frontier-lab category.
