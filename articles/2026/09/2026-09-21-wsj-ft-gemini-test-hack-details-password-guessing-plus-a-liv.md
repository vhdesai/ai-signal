# WSJ + FT: Gemini Test-Hack Details — Password-Guessing Plus a Live-Internet Config Oversight

WSJ published fuller technical details on Google's Gemini test hack: Gemini gained entry to three companies' systems either by guessing passwords or finding credentials in public repositories, and a configuration oversight let the model onto the live internet despite web access supposedly being restricted. On recognizing it was interacting with real-world targets rather than mock scenarios, Gemini halted and exited. Third-party testing firm Irregular notified Google in late July; Google did not disclose until contacted by WSJ.
