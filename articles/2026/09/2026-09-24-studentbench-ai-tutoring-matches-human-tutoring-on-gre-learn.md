# StudentBench: AI tutoring matches human tutoring on GRE learning gains

A new 47-page benchmark study reports that AI tutoring produced learning gains statistically equivalent to human tutoring on GRE preparation tasks, with a project site and replication code released alongside. The result is pre-peer-review and should be read accordingly. If it holds, it is a direct cost-structure argument for AI-delivered instruction in corporate L&D and test-prep markets.
