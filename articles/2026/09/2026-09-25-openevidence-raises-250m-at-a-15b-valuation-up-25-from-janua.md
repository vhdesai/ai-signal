# OpenEvidence raises $250M at a $15B valuation, up 25% from January

OpenEvidence, the clinical search engine used daily by more than 40% of US physicians across over 10,000 hospitals and medical centers, raised $250 million from hospital systems and Andreessen Horowitz at a $15 billion valuation — up from $12 billion in January. The company has raised more than $1 billion over the past year from backers including Nvidia, Thrive Capital, GV, Kleiner Perkins and Sequoia. Its differentiator remains training restricted to licensed medical literature via partnerships with the New England Journal of Medicine, the AMA and others. VENTURE
