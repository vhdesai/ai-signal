# NVIDIA Expands CUDA-Q to Fault-Tolerant Quantum Processors

NVIDIA has expanded its CUDA-Q hybrid classical–quantum platform to support fault-tolerant quantum processors — a meaningful step for hybrid QC/HPC workloads. The update lands as BlueQubit separately launched a Quantum Flywheel Grant program using AWS, IBM, and NVIDIA hardware. It deepens NVIDIA's positioning across accelerated-computing categories beyond LLM training and gives its neocloud partners a differentiated workload story for post-2028 procurement conversations.
