# Google Launches Gemini 3.8 Flash, Its Third Flash Release in Six Weeks

Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash across the Gemini app, AI Studio and Antigravity, with introductory API pricing reported at $0.75 / $3.75 per million tokens through December 31. The release lands as Alphabet shares exit a four-month losing streak, the longest since 2015, making the launch as much an investor-facing proof point as a product one.
