# Report: China's AI giants stretch each compute dollar further than US rivals

A Moody's Ratings report cited by SCMP finds that while US hyperscalers vastly outspend Chinese AI companies in absolute AI capital expenditure, the resulting gap in usable compute capacity is far narrower than the spending disparity implies. Lower domestic costs and heavy state support let Chinese firms secure more effective compute per dollar spent. For executives tracking the US-China AI compute race, this suggests capex totals alone overstate the American infrastructure advantage.
