# Google reframes CC as an AI agent for household coordination

Google refocused its CC AI agent from a general assistant into a household-coordination product that lets families share emails, schedules, and tasks so the agent can manage calendars, fill out forms, build shopping lists, and plan meals. It's the clearest positioning move from Google against Meta's Muse (which just launched on Mac) and Amazon's Alexa+ India rollout. For consumer-AI executives, the industry has now converged on "household agent" as the durable consumer AI product surface for 2026–2027. PRODUCT
