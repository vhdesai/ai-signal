# Anthropic Threat Report Names Five Distinct Misuse Patterns

A new Anthropic threat report details five categories where Claude was exploited: war operations, spying, bioweapon research, government repression, and — separately — Claude-distillation attacks by seven named Chinese labs that pulled 151 million conversations. Anthropic specifically calls out a UAE-linked operation targeting UN experts on Sudan. The disclosure is unusually specific in naming state and semi-state actors and lands as Anthropic prepares for its IPO under intense safety scrutiny — turning misuse transparency itself into an investor-facing narrative.
