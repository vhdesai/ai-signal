# Google introduces agentic video understanding for Gemini

Google moved Gemini from passive video description to agentic video understanding, letting the model navigate, reason over and act within long-form video rather than simply labeling it. Coverage on September 2 reported substantially higher accuracy at lower token consumption, which is the operative change for cost-sensitive deployments. The capability is surfacing through Gemini APIs and AI Studio. Video-heavy workflows — compliance review, field inspection, media archives — are the near-term enterprise targets. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-agentic-video-in-gemini/
