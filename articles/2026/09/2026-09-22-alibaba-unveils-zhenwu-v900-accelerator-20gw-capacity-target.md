# Alibaba unveils Zhenwu V900 accelerator, 20GW capacity target and a 10-trillion-parameter Qwen roadmap

At its Apsara Conference in Hangzhou, Alibaba introduced the Zhenwu V900, claimed at three times the performance of the M890 released in May, with 216GB of memory, 1,200 GB/s inter-chip bandwidth and native FP8/FP4 support; mass production is slated for Q1 2027. CEO Eddie Wu set a target of more than 20 gigawatts of global data-center capacity by 2032 and said Qwen 4 is in training, with Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 projected at 5–10 trillion parameters against the current Qwen 3.8‑Max at 2.4 trillion. Alibaba says existing Zhenwu chips already serve more than 650 customers and that clusters can scale to 500,000 accelerators. Hong Kong-listed shares rose on the announcements.
