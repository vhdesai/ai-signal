# Former Apple researchers raise $50M for AI models with more natural conversation

Business Insider reported that Seattle startup Nuance, founded by three former Apple researchers, raised a $50 million Series A led by Lightspeed with participation from Accel and Nvidia's NVentures. The company is building a single-model audio-video conversational system intended to reduce the lag and awkwardness of stitched-together voice-to-text, LLM, and text-to-voice pipelines. The bet highlights continued investor interest in AI interfaces that feel more human, but the company remains pre-product and still has to prove monetization.
