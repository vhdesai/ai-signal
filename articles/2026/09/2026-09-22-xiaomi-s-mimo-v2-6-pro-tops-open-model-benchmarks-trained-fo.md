# Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro tops open-model benchmarks — trained for $2.62M, allegedly with Claude distillation

Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro moved to the top of the open-model leaderboards, priced well below competitors, powered by a $2.62M reinforcement-learning training run. Anthropic simultaneously accused Xiaomi of siphoning training data from Claude, joining the ongoing distillation dispute with Alibaba, Moonshot, and DeepSeek. For enterprise buyers, MiMo is now a credible open-weight option in the same size class as Qwen and DeepSeek — but the distillation allegation adds procurement and regulatory risk that should factor into vendor choice.
