# Meta Launches an Enterprise Platform and Hires MongoDB's CEO to Run It

Mark Zuckerberg announced Meta Enterprise Platform as a new pillar of the business, bundling the Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code for businesses and developers. Chirantan "CJ" Desai joins as Chief Enterprise Platform Officer reporting directly to Zuckerberg, arriving from MongoDB where he was CEO and President, with prior senior roles at Cloudflare and ServiceNow. MongoDB shares fell sharply on the departure, and Microsoft and Oracle traded lower on the announcement. This is Meta's first credible move into the enterprise software stack that Microsoft, Salesforce and ServiceNow currently own. N CAPITAL
