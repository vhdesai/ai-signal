# British Columbia sues OpenAI, alleging ChatGPT aided mass school shooting

The Wall Street Journal reported that British Columbia sued OpenAI, alleging ChatGPT aided a mass school shooting. Details were behind the Journal's paywall, but the headline underscores rising legal exposure around how general-purpose AI systems may contribute to real-world harm. For executives deploying AI assistants, this is another reminder that safety, auditability, and escalation behavior will become litigation issues, not just product-quality concerns.
