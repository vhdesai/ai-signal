# Chinese stealth AI lab Naive AI hits $1.4B valuation on Tencent-led rounds

Beijing-based Naive AI, founded in February by Tsinghua computer-vision professor Jifeng Dai, is valued at more than $1.4 billion after raising $400 million across three rounds including from Tencent. The startup plans to release an open-weight LLM as early as this month, joining DeepSeek, Moonshot, and Alibaba on the open-weight side. Naive AI builds its LLM using existing open-weight models — a "post-train on Chinese frontier weights" pattern echoing Cognition's SWE-2 on Kimi K3 last week and reinforcing the shift from closed-model training to open-weight customization.
