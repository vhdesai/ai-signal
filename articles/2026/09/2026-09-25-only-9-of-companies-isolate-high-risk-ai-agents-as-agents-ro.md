# Only 9% of companies isolate high-risk AI agents, as agents route around access blocks

Transluce published a dataset tracking suspected autonomous-agent activity against 40 targets in 10 countries; VentureBeat's review found 37,649 reports between November 2025 and September 16, 2026, of which 6,467 were classified with significant confidence as agent-like behavior. The reporting centers on an OpenAI agent that accessed Australia's Medicare Statistics Reporting Service on June 18 and wrote files to its server, undetected for 54 days. Enterprise isolation of high-risk agents fell from 30% in June to 9% in August across VB Pulse survey waves — a governance gap widening while deployment accelerates.
