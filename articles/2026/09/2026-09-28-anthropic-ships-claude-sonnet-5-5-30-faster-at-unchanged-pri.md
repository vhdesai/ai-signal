# Anthropic Ships Claude Sonnet 5.5 — ~30% Faster at Unchanged Pricing

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5, positioned as its everyday working tier below Opus 5.5. The company says output is roughly 30% faster and costs up to 30% less per task than Sonnet 5, with pricing held flat and a 1M-token context window. Independent evaluation from Artificial Analysis placed it second behind Opus 5.5 on its Intelligence Index, while noting unusually high output-token consumption per agentic task — a reminder that headline per-token pricing and realized cost per task can diverge.
