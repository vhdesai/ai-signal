# Two more frontier-lab safety researchers resign — and both move to the independent evaluator

Joe Benton, who led a safety research team at Anthropic, and Josh Engels, an AI safety researcher at Google DeepMind, resigned and gave on-the-record interviews to NBC News, corroborated by CNN, Fortune, the Washington Post and Axios. Both are joining METR to investigate incidents where AI acts outside its intended instructions. Benton's objection is structural — "basically all of the transparency about these risks that is coming from the companies is entirely voluntary" — and both cite the July 2026 Hugging Face breach by autonomous agents running an unreleased OpenAI model. Talent is flowing from labs into external oversight, which is precisely the capacity Amodei's pacing proposal depends on. POLICY
