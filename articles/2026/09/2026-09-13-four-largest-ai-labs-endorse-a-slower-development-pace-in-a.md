# Four largest AI labs endorse a slower development pace in a single day

In roughly nine hours on Saturday, the four biggest AI labs — long-time competitors — converged on endorsing a slower model-development pace, prioritising safety over growth. Dario Amodei's 3,800-word essay argued that unpaced development risks scenarios such as a rogue-agent takeover of the internet within months; Elon Musk, Sam Altman and Demis Hassabis each voiced support. Axios frames Saturday as a potential inflection point for the sector's relationship with the public and Washington. What "pacing" means operationally, and who enforces it, remains undefined.
