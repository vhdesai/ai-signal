# NVIDIA Adds CUDA-Q Logical for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Development

NVIDIA expanded its open-source CUDA-Q platform with CUDA-Q Logical, an orchestration layer for designing and verifying applications on fault-tolerant quantum systems. Fermilab reported compressing fault-tolerant architecture development from five months to three weeks — a 7x speedup — and Iceberg Quantum modeled 1,000 logical qubits using 150,000 physical qubits, roughly 10x fewer than prior estimates for Diraq's qubits. Sandia National Laboratories' QUOPS benchmark, which measures hardware readiness for utility-scale applications, is now available in CUDA-Q. Early adopters include Fermilab, Infleqtion, IQM, Quantum Motion and Sandia. RESEARCH
