# The Information: Why Wall Street's big, bad bears aren't ready to short AI yet

The Information profiles Big Short's Steve Eisman and other short-sellers hesitating to place a big short-AI trade despite growing skepticism. Eisman is watching Nvidia's dependency on Anthropic and OpenAI ("the entire chain is dependent upon two companies that lose a ton of money") and treating a further OpenAI IPO delay as the potential trigger for a big short call. Combined with today's Oracle rent-delay and AWS CPU-conservation memo, bearish signals now include real corporate-behavior evidence.
