# For all their talk about AI, few companies are quantifying gains

Yahoo Finance reports that relatively few companies are quantifying concrete financial or productivity gains from AI despite broad public messaging. The signal is that AI programs are entering a more disciplined measurement phase. Senior leaders should expect investor and board scrutiny to move from pilot count and model access toward baseline metrics, realized productivity, margin impact, and repeatable operating leverage.
