# Meta Superintelligence Labs releases Muse Voice Transcribe, its first real-time audio perception model

Muse Voice Transcribe collapses three normally separate production components — streaming speech recognition, speaker diarization, and endpointing — into a single autoregressive model, removing the latency and failure modes introduced at each hand-off. Meta reports multilingual coverage of roughly 70 languages and API pricing near $0.18 per hour, with a Mac dictation surface available at launch. It is Meta's first shipped real-time audio perception model rather than a headline generative release. https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-voice-transcribe ________________________________ Products & Tools PRODUCT
