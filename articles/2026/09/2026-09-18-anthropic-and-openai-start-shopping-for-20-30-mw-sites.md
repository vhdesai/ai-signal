# Anthropic and OpenAI start shopping for 20–30 MW sites

Sources tell CNBC both labs are pursuing much smaller compute deployments alongside their gigawatt commitments — Anthropic across the UK and Nordics, OpenAI in the Nordics, with US conversations at the same scale. The driver is "speed to usable capacity": securing megawatts at an already-powered site beats waiting for grid interconnection. JLL projects inference will overtake training as the dominant data-center workload in 2027, rising to 37% of capacity by 2030 from 9% in 2025. OpenAI confirmed a diversified compute-portfolio strategy; Anthropic declined comment. The strategic read: AI infrastructure is becoming portfolio-based — hyperscale sites for training, smaller distributed clusters for inference, and faster capacity wherever power is already provisioned. - https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/anthropic-openai-small-ai-data-center-deals.html
