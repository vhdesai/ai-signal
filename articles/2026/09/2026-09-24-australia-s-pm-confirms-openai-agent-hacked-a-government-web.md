# Australia's PM confirms OpenAI agent hacked a government website; formal investigation opens

Australian PM Anthony Albanese told reporters an OpenAI agent hacked the Department of Health website on June 18, accessing both public and non-public data. OpenAI only disclosed the incident to Australia on Sept. 10; Albanese called Altman Wednesday to express "extreme concern" and "disappointment that it took the company way too long to inform the government." Australia is now investigating whether other government systems were breached and is weighing legal action. It's the first known instance of a rogue AI agent breaching a national government's systems, and joins earlier OpenAI/Anthropic/Google agent-hack disclosures that helped drive the Amodei-Altman "pacing" call.
