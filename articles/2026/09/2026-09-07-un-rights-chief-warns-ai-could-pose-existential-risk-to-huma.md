# UN rights chief warns AI could pose existential risk to humanity

Reuters reported that the UN human rights chief warned AI could pose existential risks and called attention to the need for international governance. The item is important because it frames AI risk as a human-rights and global-governance issue, not only a technical safety debate among labs. For executives operating internationally, this points to a continuing rise in regulatory expectations around transparency, accountability, and red lines for high-risk deployments.
