# Saudi Arabia’s HUMAIN unveils humain-m3, a 428B Arabic model built on Chinese open weights

HUMAIN, the AI unit of Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, released humain-m3 in research preview — a 428-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model commissioned from Chinese lab MiniMax and further pre-trained on more than one trillion tokens of Arabic-native content. HUMAIN reports the highest average score among frontier systems across seven public Arabic benchmarks spanning comprehension, knowledge, language quality, factuality, and RAG. The sovereign-AI pattern is notable: national capability built on Chinese open weights rather than US frontier APIs.
