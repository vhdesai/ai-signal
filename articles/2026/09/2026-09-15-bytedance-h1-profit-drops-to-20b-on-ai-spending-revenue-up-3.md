# ByteDance H1 profit drops to $20B on AI spending, revenue up 30% to $120B

The Information reports ByteDance's H1 2026 net profit declined by a single-digit percentage to $20 billion as it ramped AI investments, while revenue rose ~30% year-over-year to $120 billion driven partly by TikTok international advertising and e-commerce. That top-line growth is marginally accelerating from prior years (2025 revenue $200B / +29%, net profit $42B / +27%), and puts ByteDance's revenue pace on par with Meta's. It's the clearest hard-dollar look yet at what full-scale AI capex is doing to a hyperscaler-adjacent Chinese platform.
