# Jensen Huang Rejects AI Extinction Warnings, Opposes New AI-Specific Regulation

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told CBS News there is "0% chance" AI ends the world by 2030, calling recent researcher warnings "doomsday narratives" not grounded in science. He argued existing product-liability and unauthorized-access law is sufficient and that no new AI-specific guardrails are needed. Asked about Nvidia's incentives given a ~$5.3T market value, Huang said the company's success is "directly connected to the safe deployment of products and services."
