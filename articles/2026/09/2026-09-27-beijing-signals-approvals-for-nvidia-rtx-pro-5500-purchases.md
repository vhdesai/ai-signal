# Beijing Signals Approvals for Nvidia RTX Pro 5500 Purchases; ByteDance Weighs ~1M Units

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology has contacted domestic technology companies — including ByteDance and Alibaba — asking how many RTX Pro 5500 chips they want and for what purposes, with officials indicating approvals are coming. ByteDance is reportedly considering an order of roughly 1 million units of the 84GB Blackwell workstation part.
