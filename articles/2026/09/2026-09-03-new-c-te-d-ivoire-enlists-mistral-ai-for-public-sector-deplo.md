# New Côte d’Ivoire enlists Mistral AI for public-sector deployments

Côte d’Ivoire has partnered with Mistral AI to identify practical applications across public administration, healthcare, education, agriculture and public services. The agreement extends Mistral’s sovereign-AI positioning into Francophone Africa. It is a useful datapoint on how European model providers are competing for government accounts outside the EU.
