# DeepSeek's Annualized Revenue Hits $1B; Chinese Lab Targets a $7.5B Round at a $75B Valuation Ahead of Shanghai IPO

DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate reached $1B, more than double the ~$500M level a few months ago, driven by a 2.3×–4.5× price hike on model access that did not materially affect user demand, per CEO Liang Wenfeng in a recent investor meeting. The company is finalizing a $7.5B (50B yuan) fundraise at a ~$75B (500B yuan) valuation targeted to close by end of October, ahead of a planned Shanghai Stock Exchange listing. Revenue comes almost entirely from API model access; Liang told investors DeepSeek allocates more than 70% of its compute to training and less than 30% to inference — a strategy consistent with the company's stated priority of pushing model quality over near-term profitability.
