# Apple accuses OpenAI of destroying evidence in trade-secrets case

Apple moved for expedited discovery after forensic analysis of a former employee's MacBook, which Apple says contains internal discussion of destroying material relevant to its trade-secrets claim. The device was produced only recently, and Apple argues delay risks permanent loss of evidence. The case is now the most adversarial legal front between two firms that remain commercial partners in consumer AI.
