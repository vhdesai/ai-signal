# Anthropic and OpenAI sound the alarm on AI safety — and seek to shape how it is controlled

An AP analysis argues that both labs' warnings about their own systems serve a strategic purpose ahead of anticipated public listings and the U.S. midterms, letting them set the terms of safety protocols in an unregulated market. Former OpenAI geopolitics lead Sarah Shoker notes the existential-risk framing displaces attention from present-day harms such as data center impacts, surveillance and military use. OpenAI pointed to its training pause as evidence of self-governance; Anthropic said it has called for regulation for years. POLICY REGULATION
