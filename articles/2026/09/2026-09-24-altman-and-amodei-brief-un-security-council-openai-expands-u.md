# Altman and Amodei brief UN Security Council; OpenAI expands Ukraine cyber-defense partnership

Sam Altman and Dario Amodei — Altman in person, Amodei virtually — briefed the UN Security Council on AI risks Wednesday, both endorsing global cooperation, external evaluators, and a biological-weapons AI ban. Contrary to earlier reporting, DeepSeek did not participate. Separately, WSJ Pro and CyberScoop confirm OpenAI agreed to give Ukrainian cyber teams access to advanced AI models plus more than $1B in subsidized tokens to defend critical infrastructure from state-sponsored hackers. Trump also bought cybersecurity stocks per Barron's — even as he publicly plays down AI risks.
