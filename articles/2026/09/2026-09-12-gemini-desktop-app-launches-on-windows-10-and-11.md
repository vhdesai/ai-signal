# Gemini desktop app launches on Windows 10 and 11

Google's Gemini desktop app is now available on Windows 10 and 11, giving Windows users a native always-available Gemini surface instead of the browser-only experience. The launch lands directly on Microsoft's home turf just as Microsoft is pushing AI-native app generation flows in WinUI 3 and doubling down on Copilot as a default. Expect a parallel Windows Copilot vs. Gemini desktop battle for consumer AI defaults.
