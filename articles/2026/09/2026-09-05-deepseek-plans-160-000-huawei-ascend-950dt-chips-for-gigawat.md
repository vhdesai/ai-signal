# DeepSeek plans 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT chips for gigawatt-scale site

DeepSeek has placed an order for at least 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT accelerators to fill a gigawatt-scale data center in Inner Mongolia, with training reportedly staying on existing Nvidia capacity for now. Huawei plans to launch the 950DT in Q4 2026 with its own high-bandwidth memory, and HBM shortages are expected to cap output in the low hundreds of thousands of units this year. The move is the clearest signal yet that Chinese inference capacity is being rebuilt on domestic silicon while Nvidia remains locked out.
