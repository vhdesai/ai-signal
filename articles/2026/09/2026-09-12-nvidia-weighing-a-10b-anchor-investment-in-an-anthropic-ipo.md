# Nvidia Weighing a $10B Anchor Investment in an Anthropic IPO at a ~$2 Trillion Valuation

Reuters reports Anthropic is in discussions for an IPO raising up to $100 billion at a valuation near $2 trillion, with Nvidia considering up to $10 billion as anchor investor. No filing or timeline has been reported, and Amazon retains the larger existing stake. If it proceeds it would be the largest IPO in history by a wide margin, and would further entwine the dominant compute supplier with a frontier lab’s multi-year purchase obligations.
