# MIT's "HardFlow" Forces Generative Models to Satisfy Non-Negotiable Constraints Without Retraining

MIT researchers published HardFlow, a deployment-time algorithm that enforces hard constraints — collision-free robot paths, physical boundary conditions — on the final output of pretrained flow-matching models rather than at every intermediate sampling step. The method met constraints in every reported test across robotic manipulation, maze navigation, PDE boundary control, and text-guided image editing.
