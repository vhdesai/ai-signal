# Broadcom's AI revenue surge reinforces the non-GPU infrastructure trade

24/7 Wall St. reported that Broadcom's Q3 AI revenue reached $16.7 billion, up 221%, with management guiding to substantially higher AI semiconductor revenue in the following quarter. The coverage reinforces that the AI infrastructure cycle is not only about Nvidia GPUs; custom silicon, networking, memory, and packaging are becoming major profit pools.
