# Citigroup warns AI slowdown could pressure stock-market gains built on earnings optimism

Crypto Briefing reported that Citigroup analysts warned a slowdown in AI model development could trigger downward earnings revisions across companies whose valuations depend on AI productivity gains. The concern is that the market has priced in rapid translation from AI investment to earnings leverage, particularly in software, cloud, and semiconductor names. This makes the pace of frontier AI development a financial variable, not only a technical or policy issue. STARTUP FUNDING VOICE AI
