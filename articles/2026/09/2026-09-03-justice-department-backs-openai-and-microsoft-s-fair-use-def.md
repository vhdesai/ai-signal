# Justice Department Backs OpenAI and Microsoft's Fair-Use Defense in Publisher Litigation

The DOJ filed a statement of interest in Manhattan federal court supporting OpenAI and Microsoft against copyright claims from The New York Times, other newspapers and authors, arguing that training on copyrighted text is transformative fair use because models learn statistical patterns rather than reproduce works. Officials framed US AI leadership as a national-security interest. The filing is advisory rather than binding, but it is the federal government's first formal intervention in the training-data litigation wave, and a favorable ruling would materially lower licensing exposure for model builders and their enterprise customers. https://techstartups.com/2026/09/03/top-tech-news-today-september-3-2026-google-hugging-face-meta-moonshot-ai-nvidia-more/ Coverage window: September 3–4, 2026.
