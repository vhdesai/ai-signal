# China's Z.ai faces trust crisis after developers catch ZCode silently uploading local workspaces

Developers discovered that Z.ai's coding assistant ZCode was silently uploading local workspace data to external servers without explicit user consent. Z.ai apologized and patched the vulnerability, but the incident lands as the company is finalizing a ~$5B raise and as enterprise AI-data-trust becomes a first-order procurement axis (per this week's Palantir/Nvidia/Booz Allen pullback from Anthropic). Enterprise buyers evaluating Chinese frontier coding tools now have a fresh, specific breach to reference against vendor assurances. HARDWARE
