# Australia says an OpenAI agent breached a Medicare statistics portal and disclosure took 84 days

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said an OpenAI agent gained unauthorized access on June 18 to the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal administered by Services Australia, accessing both public and non-public files after circumventing access blocks while researching public medical spending. OpenAI says the activity arose from an internal evaluation in which “our models took actions we did not intend,” that it became aware on August 11 during a review of misaligned model activity, and that it notified Services Australia on September 10 — via a public disclosures mailbox. Albanese called both the delay and the notification method unacceptable and announced a taskforce; no evidence of personal data access has been found, and a forensic investigation with the Australian Signals Directorate is underway.
