# Oracle Shares Slip on an Unconfirmed Wisconsin AI Mega-Campus Delay — Stargate Story Widens

Oracle stock slipped on an unconfirmed report of construction delays at the company’s Wisconsin AI mega-campus, following Sunday’s New Mexico rent-delay disclosure. Yahoo Finance argues the 8% bond yield is "an Oracle problem, not an AI problem," while Seeking Alpha downgraded the stock a day after Motley Fool paired it against Salesforce. The AI-infra-financing-stress thesis is now firmly in play going into Q4 earnings — and it validates Goldman’s power/labor/memory bottleneck framing on the $1.2T 2027 capex path. Treat contracted capacity and delivered capacity as distinct diligence lines when reading Q4 hyperscaler filings.
