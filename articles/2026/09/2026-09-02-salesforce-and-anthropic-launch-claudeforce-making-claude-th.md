# Salesforce and Anthropic Launch “Claudeforce,” Making Claude the Default Reasoning Model in Agentforce and Slack

Salesforce and Anthropic announced a deep integration branded Claudeforce, positioning Claude as the default reasoning model across Agentforce and Slack. The arrangement extends Anthropic’s enterprise distribution into one of the largest installed bases of business workflow software.
