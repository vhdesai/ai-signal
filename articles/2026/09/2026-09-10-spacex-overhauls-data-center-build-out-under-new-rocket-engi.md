# SpaceX overhauls data-center build-out under new rocket-engineer leadership

SpaceX is reworking how it builds the giant facilities that house xAI's servers. A new management team of rocket engineers recently installed by Elon Musk is prioritizing more backup power and cooling, more thorough pre-launch testing, and less reliance on temporary utility hookups — a break from the earlier "move fast" build-out that produced frequent outages at xAI's first sites. The shift is expected to slow new campus construction and displaces veteran staff, and it signals xAI is putting reliability ahead of the record-time expansion Musk previously bragged about. For executives modeling AI-infrastructure supply through 2027, treat SpaceX/xAI capacity as a moving variable rather than a locked commitment.
