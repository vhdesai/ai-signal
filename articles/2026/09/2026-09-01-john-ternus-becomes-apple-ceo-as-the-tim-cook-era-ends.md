# John Ternus Becomes Apple CEO as the Tim Cook Era Ends

Apple’s long-planned leadership transition took effect, with hardware chief John Ternus becoming CEO and Tim Cook moving to executive chairman after 15 years. Changes are already underway: Phil Schiller is stepping down as head of the App Store and product events, with the App Store moving under services lead Eddy Cue. Ternus inherits an AI problem — a delayed Siri overhaul, reliance on Google’s Gemini for the next Apple Intelligence generation, and a talent exodus that sent foundation-models lead Ruoming Pang, engineering head Frank Chu and design VP Alan Dye to Meta. Apple’s iPhone event is September 9. 9to5Mac · Business Insider
