# Anthropic says Claude helped identify a CRISPR-like enzyme system

Anthropic said its Bay Area biology lab found a previously unknown enzyme system in bacteriophage DNA with properties reminiscent of CRISPR, including operations such as cutting, copying, and pasting DNA. TechCrunch reported that Anthropic says Claude drove much of the discovery process, using roughly 950 agents and 210 million tokens over 21 hours, while human scientists performed the physical lab work. The claim still requires outside validation, but it is an important example of frontier models being used for scientific discovery in a domain with both high upside and biosecurity risk.
