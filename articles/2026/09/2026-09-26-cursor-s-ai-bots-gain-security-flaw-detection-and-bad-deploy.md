# Cursor's AI bots gain security-flaw detection and bad-deploy halts

Martin Cid Magazine reports Cursor's AI bots now automatically catch security flaws and halt bad deploys — a substantial hardening of what had been a productivity-focused coding assistant. Combined with Replit's acquisition of Atta for analytics and Replit's expansion of AI Agent Models with Meta ecosystem integration (TipRanks), the AI coding-agent stack is executing the same governance-plus-productivity playbook that Microsoft demonstrated Thursday with the Copilot Home/Code/Autopilot launch. Safe agents are now a shipping product category — not a research topic.
