# ScrollEd wants to turn textbooks into TikTok

Palo Alto startup ScrollEd, pitching in TechCrunch Disrupt’s Startup Battlefield 200, converts any text file or PDF into a vertical feed of AI-generated video, audio, text and quizzes — swipe up for a new topic, sideways to go deeper. Bootstrapped this year by Stanford’s Utsav Gupta and UPenn’s Rebecca Neff, it targets schools, corporate training and consumers on a freemium model with a Pro tier and annual institutional licenses. “The ambition is a social network that’s better for you,” Gupta said.
