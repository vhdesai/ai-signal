# Altman, Huang, Cook and Musk Reported as Confirmed for the Trump–Xi White House AI Summit

Eight senior technology executives — reportedly including Sam Altman, Jensen Huang, Tim Cook, Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos and Sundar Pichai — are described as confirmed for the Trump–Xi state dinner on September 24, with a White House meeting of AI executives planned around it. CNBC independently reports Huang’s attendance and the parallel executives meeting. Treat the full attendee list as provisional pending confirmation from a first-tier outlet; the corroborated elements are the dinner, Huang, and the adjacent AI meeting.
