# ServiceNow Acquires Israeli Agentic-CRM Startup Sweep

ServiceNow acquired Sweep in a deal reported in the hundreds of millions of dollars, with terms undisclosed. Sweep's software integrates with ServiceNow, Salesforce and HubSpot to monitor CRM metadata, surface configuration misalignments and auto-generate documentation. The purchase extends ServiceNow's agentic workflow ambitions into revenue-operations tooling, an area where governance of system-of-record data is the practical constraint on agent deployment. https://insight.tmcnet.com/insight/servicenow-buys-sweep-to-expand-agentic-crm-workflows-mtmx35tz
