# Both frontier labs cut flagship prices on the same day, even as safety concerns grow

Analysis of the simultaneous September 22 price cuts frames it as the first time Anthropic and OpenAI moved top-tier pricing down within the same day. Axios separately argued that cheaper inference could help the labs slow down safely by relieving the compute-margin pressure that drives release cadence. The cuts follow three mid-frontier launches — Grok 4.7, MiMo-V2.6, and Step 5 — in the preceding 48 hours. Enterprise buyers should expect renegotiation leverage on multi-year inference commitments.
