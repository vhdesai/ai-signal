# Google tests letting Gemini call businesses for users

TechCrunch reported that Google is testing a Gemini feature that can call businesses on a user’s behalf. The feature pushes voice agents from passive assistant roles into real-world task execution, such as calling, asking questions, or potentially booking services. As with other agentic commerce and assistant workflows, the risk surface shifts to consent, call disclosure, misrepresentation, and error recovery.
