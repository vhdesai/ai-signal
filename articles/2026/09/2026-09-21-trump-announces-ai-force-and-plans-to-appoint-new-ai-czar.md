# Trump announces "AI Force" and plans to appoint new AI czar

President Trump announced Saturday on Truth Social he is creating a new "AI Force," modeled on Space Force, and plans to appoint a new AI czar to lead it. Trump reiterated that AI-safety concerns are a "hoax" and defended data-center expansion — "we will not in any way hinder or stifle the Growth of this incredible Industry." A draft executive order to create such a body has been circulating for weeks but stalled; former crypto/AI czar David Sacks (who continues as external adviser) opposes creating a self-regulatory body. It's unclear how AI Force would differ from existing bodies like the Commerce Department's Center for AI Standards and Innovation.
