# Crusoe raises $3.9B Series F at $30.9B valuation

Crusoe closed an oversubscribed $3.9 billion Series F at a $30.9 billion post-money valuation, co-led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital, and Valor Equity Partners, with Nvidia, Founders Fund, GIC, QIA, Salesforce Ventures, Robinhood Ventures, and TPG participating. The company reports more than $140 billion in total contracted value, 6+ GW of gross contracted capacity (1 GW operational), and 20× year-over-year growth in Crusoe Cloud bookings — roughly triple the ~$10 billion valuation set in October 2025. Flagship sites include a 1.2 GW Abilene, Texas campus Oracle will use to host OpenAI workloads plus a 900 MW site nearby for Microsoft. Proceeds fund both hyperscale campuses and truck-transportable modular "Spark" units.
