# Another DeepMind researcher quits, calls near-term superintelligence "inherently irresponsible"

Google DeepMind researcher Robert O'Callahan resigned publicly, saying AI's "current rate of change is far too high" and that he can no longer justify contributing to chip-design tools that make AI cheaper and faster. He said many colleagues share the view but rarely speak out. His exit continues a low-level but sustained pattern of visible resignations from senior US labs over the second half of 2026, arriving the same day the White House told OpenAI and Anthropic to route new models through US review before the UK AI Safety Institute.
