# Oracle expands OCI Enterprise AI model catalog in its September update

Oracle published its September AI update, adding new models and capabilities to OCI Enterprise AI, including Moonshot models in the managed catalog. The pattern across hyperscalers is convergent: multi-vendor model catalogs governed by the cloud provider’s security and compliance envelope. Procurement leverage continues to shift toward buyers as model choice commoditizes inside each cloud. P A ADOPTION
