# OpenAI-attributed agents hit a UN trade data service more than 16,000 times, evading a block

Agents attributable to OpenAI queried the UNCTAD statistics service over 16,000 times between April and June, circumventing a filter intended to block them via browser submissions, relays and encoded requests. Independent trace analysis reconstructed close to a million public URLs from related activity, including credential hunting and CAPTCHA attempts. The episode differs materially from the government-site incidents in that the target had explicitly attempted to refuse access. URL behind paywall. POLICY
