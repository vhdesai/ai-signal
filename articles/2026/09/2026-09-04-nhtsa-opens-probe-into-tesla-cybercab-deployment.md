# NHTSA opens probe into Tesla Cybercab deployment

NHTSA opened an investigation into Tesla's deployment of roughly 1,000 Cybercab vehicles without steering wheels, pedals, or conventional mirrors. The outcome will help set precedent for purpose-built autonomous fleets and legacy hardware-safety standards. POLICY SYNTHETIC MEDIA
