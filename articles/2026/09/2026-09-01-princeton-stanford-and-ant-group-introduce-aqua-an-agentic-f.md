# Princeton, Stanford and Ant Group Introduce AQuA, an Agentic Framework for Autonomous Factor Discovery

AQuA pairs two LLM-driven quantitative research agents and makes data leakage structurally impossible by sealing splits, features, labels, and evaluators before iteration begins. Reported results include a crypto factor information coefficient of about 0.190, a per-stock equity IC of +0.0843 against +0.0613 for a GRU baseline, and +2.50 Sharpe with positive performance across 2021–2025. Note the caveat: the coverage is an Ant Research-sponsored placement, so results should be verified against the underlying paper before being treated as replicated.
