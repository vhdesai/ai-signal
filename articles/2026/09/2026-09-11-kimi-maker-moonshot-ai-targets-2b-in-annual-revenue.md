# Kimi-maker Moonshot AI targets $2B in annual revenue

Moonshot is guiding to roughly $2 billion in annualized sales for 2026 on the back of Kimi K3, its 2.8-trillion-parameter base model, which undercuts US frontier pricing. Combined with DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash launch the same day, the pattern is that Chinese labs are now competing on commercial traction rather than benchmarks alone. Revenue guidance is company-stated and not independently audited.
