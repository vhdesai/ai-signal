# Google nears Gemini 4 release; DeepMind chief says "much earlier" than year-end

At The Information's AI Agenda Live Summit — in his first media appearance as the new DeepMind chief — Koray Kavukcuoglu said Google is nearing release of Gemini 4, which is now in post-training and is hoped to ship "much earlier" than year-end. Google engineers already use it internally on the Antigravity coding tool. Gemini 3.5 Pro reportedly missed three deadlines and never shipped, prompting Google to "take a step back" to maximize learning speed. Kavukcuoglu also signaled DeepMind is shifting away from an AGI-centric identity toward "trustworthy intelligent agents" — as Anthropic Mythos/Fable and OpenAI's GPT-6 line have opened a lead in the frontier.
