# ICLR 2027 pulls ~50,000 abstract submissions — up from 19,500 at ICLR 2026

ICLR 2027 attracted roughly 50,000 abstract submissions before the deadline, up from 19,500 at ICLR 2026 — a 2.5x increase in a single year. The Decoder attributes it to AI-hype, corporate publication-linked compensation, and AI making paper drafting materially faster. For R&D leaders, the practical implication is that peer-reviewed AI research is now nearly impossible to consume comprehensively and reviewer signal quality is likely to degrade further, raising the value of curated in-house paper filters.
