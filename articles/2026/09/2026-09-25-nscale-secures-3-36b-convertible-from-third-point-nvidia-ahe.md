# Nscale secures $3.36B convertible from Third Point, Nvidia ahead of US IPO

British AI-neocloud Nscale raised $3.36B in convertible financing led by Third Point with Nvidia participating, in what looks like a bridge to its US IPO. The round arrives days after Nscale's prospectus was filed with Microsoft and Anthropic as disclosed anchor customers and ByteDance kept off the main filing. Convertibles this large usually price the deal implicitly ahead of the roadshow; the Third Point involvement is the most interesting datapoint. INDUSTRY
