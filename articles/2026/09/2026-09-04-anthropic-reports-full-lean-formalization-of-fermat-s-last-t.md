# Anthropic reports full Lean formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem by collaborating Claude agents

Anthropic said collaborating Claude agents produced an end-to-end, computer-checked Lean formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem in 11 days. The result formalizes an existing proof rather than discovering one, but points toward AI-assisted formal methods for higher-confidence math, software, and security proofs.
