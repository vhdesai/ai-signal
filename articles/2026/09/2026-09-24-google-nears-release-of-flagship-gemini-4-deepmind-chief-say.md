# Google Nears Release of Flagship Gemini 4; DeepMind Chief Says It Could Ship "Much Earlier" Than Year-End

In his first media appearance as Google DeepMind's new top leader at The Information's AI Agenda Live Summit, Koray Kavukcuoglu said Gemini 4 is in the early days of post-training and could ship "much earlier" than the end of 2026. Google engineers are already using the model internally for the Antigravity coding tool. Kavukcuoglu also signaled DeepMind is shifting away from its AGI-centric identity toward "trustworthy intelligent agents," a reset that follows Gemini 3.5 Pro reportedly missing three deadlines and never shipping. Gemini 4 enters a frontier landscape where Anthropic (Mythos/Fable) and OpenAI (GPT-6 line) have opened a clear capability lead — the pacing and messaging changes at DeepMind are both real.
