# The Information: Google Tests Paying ~100 Digital Publishers for Content That Improves AI Answers

Google has been paying roughly 100 digital publishers for how much their content contributes to AI-powered answers across Search and other products, in a pilot that reverses Google’s long-standing position that it should not pay publishers whose content answers user queries. AI Overviews has drastically cut referral traffic and generated lawsuits. Some participants say payouts amount to less than 1% of revenue; anime and gaming content pays better. It is the first commercial admission from a Big Tech platform that the training-and-attribution economics of generative search need real dollars, not just link credit — and it will pressure OpenAI, Perplexity, and Meta to respond.
