# Cursor Ships Projects: One Coordinator Agent, Thousands of Subagents

Cursor launched Projects in beta, a workspace where a coordinator agent plans and delegates rather than writing code, maintaining shared context across months of work and running on cloud machines independent of the developer's laptop. Coordinators can subscribe to Slack channels, schedules or pull-request streams and act without prompting. Cursor reports new users merge 30% more pull requests and Projects-primary users six times as many — vendor figures measuring volume, not review quality.
