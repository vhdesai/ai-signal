# The Hacker News published a detailed technical writeup: Hacktron used a libheif CVE (CVE-2026-32882) in an unpatched Debian package on OpenAI's Discourse-based help forum to gain RCE, then abused OpenAI's SSO — the same "Sign in with OpenAI" that staff use — to take over ChatGPT and Codex accounts. The forum's exposure came from OpenAI's own identity system, not the forum software. Claude Opus 4.8 failed at the exploit; Opus 5 produced a working chain within hours of release. OpenAI paid a $6,500 bounty but described it as recognizing only the OpenAI-side finding.
