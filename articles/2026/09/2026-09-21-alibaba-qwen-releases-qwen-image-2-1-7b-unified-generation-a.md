# Alibaba Qwen Releases Qwen-Image-2.1 — 7B Unified Generation and Editing, Non-Commercial License

Alibaba's Qwen team folded text-to-image generation and image editing into a single 7B-parameter diffusion transformer, down from the 20B original, paired with a Qwen3-VL 8B text encoder. It supports native RGBA transparency, multi-reference editing with up to ten reference images, native 2K output, and prefix KV-cache reuse for speed. It scores 60.28 on Qwen's in-house benchmark — top among listed open-weight models, ahead of Nano Banana 2.0 at 59.82. The commercially material detail is licensing: the Qwen Research License bars commercial use absent a separate agreement, continuing the move away from permissive defaults among Chinese open-weight releases.
