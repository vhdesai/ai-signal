# Google moves Project Suncatcher to its first orbital TPU test

Google confirmed that a prototype satellite named MVP, built with Planet, will launch on SpaceX’s Transporter-18 rideshare to evaluate whether Tensor Processing Units can survive and operate in low Earth orbit. The craft carries four TPUs on roughly 1 kW of solar power. Google reports Trillium TPUs withstood a total ionizing dose greater than a five-year mission in proton-beam testing at UC Davis’s Crocker Nuclear Laboratory, and that vibration testing across all three axes held. Cooling by radiator in vacuum remains the binding constraint; a two-satellite test of high-bandwidth laser interconnects is targeted for 2027. REGULATORY ENERGY
