# Snowflake defies outcome-pricing pressure as AI coding adoption accelerates

Snowflake's Applied AI newsletter item highlighted that CoCo, its coding assistant formerly known as Cortex Code, reached more than 9,100 customer accounts and helped lift product-revenue growth. The company is arguing that enterprises want a trusted intermediary that can route across Anthropic, OpenAI, and open-weight models rather than locking into one model vendor. Snowflake's position is a counterpoint to the market's push toward outcome-based AI pricing: strong data-platform pull can still support consumption pricing when AI expands core usage.
