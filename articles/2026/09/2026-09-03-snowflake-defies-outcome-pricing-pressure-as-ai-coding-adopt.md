# Snowflake defies outcome-pricing pressure as AI coding adoption accelerates

Snowflake's Applied AI newsletter item highlighted that CoCo, its coding assistant formerly known as Cortex Code, reached more than 9,100 customer accounts and helped lift product-revenue growth. Snowflake is arguing that enterprises want a trusted intermediary that can route across Anthropic, OpenAI, and open-weight models rather than locking into one model vendor.
