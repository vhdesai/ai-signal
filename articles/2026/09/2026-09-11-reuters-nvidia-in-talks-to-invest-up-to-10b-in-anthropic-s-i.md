# Reuters: Nvidia in talks to invest up to $10B in Anthropic's IPO

Reuters and The Information report Nvidia is in talks to invest up to $10 billion in Anthropic's upcoming IPO — a scale that would rank among the largest strategic anchor commitments in tech IPO history. The move would deepen Nvidia's ownership of the AI supply chain following the Hugging Face acquisition and its financing of Nscale, Mistral, and Nebius, and it entwines Nvidia more tightly with Anthropic's ~$517B compute obligations disclosed earlier this month. It also lands with Anthropic's early backer Anjney Midha profiled by The Information the same morning as a rising VC power broker on the back of an Anthropic position that could be worth up to $2T at IPO.
