# Pentagon requests $30M for an AI-enhanced polygraph program

A Department of Defense budget request seeks $30.3M over five years for "Polygraph+," a program to modernize federal credibility assessment using machine-learning scoring algorithms and "standoff sensing" that takes physiological readings without attaching devices. Run by the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency, it would be used for pre-employment vetting and insider-threat detection. Researchers quoted call AI-enabled lie detection "the worst of both worlds," noting the 2003 National Research Council finding that polygraph evidence is "weak at best" — and that at DoD's 2.8M-person scale, even 90% accuracy misclassifies tens of thousands.
