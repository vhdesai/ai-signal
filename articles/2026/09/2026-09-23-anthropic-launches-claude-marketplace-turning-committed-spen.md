# Anthropic launches Claude Marketplace, turning committed spend into a procurement channel

Anthropic opened the Claude Marketplace as a single destination for connectors and plugins (more than 2,000 at launch, including Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, Salesforce and Snowflake), purchasable Claude-powered products from partners such as Cursor, CrowdStrike, Harvey, Legora and Lovable, and service partners including Accenture, BCG and Deloitte. The commercially significant detail is that customers can apply a portion of their existing committed Anthropic spend to third-party software, collapsing procurement cycles. Listings are built on Anthropic's open standards (Model Context Protocol and Agent Skills), and enterprise purchases route through account teams rather than a public checkout. This positions Claude against Microsoft's Agent Store as an enterprise distribution layer, not just a model.
