# Hierarchical NeRF with JAX3D for Volumetric Rendering and Novel-View Synthesis

An end-to-end hierarchical Neural Radiance Field implementation built “using JAX, Flax, Optax, and the volume-rendering primitives provided by jax3d,” covering coarse-to-fine sampling, volumetric rendering and 3D reconstruction. This is a methods walkthrough rather than a new research result, but it is a usable reference for teams evaluating JAX-based 3D reconstruction pipelines against PyTorch equivalents. Worth noting for spatial-computing and digital-twin workloads where reproducible rendering stacks matter more than novelty.
