# Alibaba Unveils Zhenwu V900 and Targets 20GW of Data Center Capacity by 2032

At its Apsara Conference in Hangzhou, Alibaba introduced the Zhenwu V900 accelerator from its T-Head unit, claiming three times the performance of the M890 released in May and cluster scaling to 500,000 chips, with mass production slated for Q1 2027. CEO Eddie Wu set a target of more than 20 gigawatts of global Alibaba Cloud data-center capacity by 2032, backed by a committed $53B+ three-year AI spend, and said Qwen 4 is in training with successor models planned at 5–10 trillion parameters. Shares rose about 3% in Hong Kong. The strategic read is vertical integration: with Nvidia procurement politically unstable, Chinese players are internalizing chips, cloud and models together — though Alibaba has not disclosed foundry arrangements for the V900.
