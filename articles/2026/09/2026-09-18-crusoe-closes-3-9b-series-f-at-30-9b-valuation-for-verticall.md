# Crusoe Closes $3.9B Series F at $30.9B Valuation for Vertically Integrated AI Infrastructure

The Denver-based AI infrastructure company announced an initial close of an oversubscribed round co-led by Atreides, Mubadala, and Valor, with Nvidia, Founders Fund, GIC, QIA, Radical, and TPG participating. Crusoe reports more than $140B in total contracted value, over 6GW of gross contracted capacity with 1GW operational, and more than 20x YoY growth in Crusoe Cloud bookings. Capital will fund existing campuses — including the Abilene, Texas site used by OpenAI — plus truck-deliverable modular "Spark" units.
