# Credit dispute over the Navier–Stokes result exposes an academia–lab attribution gap

NYU professor Tristan Buckmaster alleges OpenAI accelerated its own Navier–Stokes effort after learning of his and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge's progress, and proposed a co-authored rewrite that excluded Alpöge because of his Anthropic affiliation. OpenAI's Sébastien Bubeck said the effort began after rumors that Anthropic was close, denied that OpenAI researchers or agents used Buckmaster's Codex prompts or proof, and called the allegations false and inflammatory. OpenAI has not stated whether product-usage data from those sessions could have entered later training. For research institutions, the unresolved question is provenance governance when the same vendor supplies the tooling and competes for the result.
