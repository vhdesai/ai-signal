# Trump Announces an "AI Force" and Forthcoming AI Czar, Calls Safety Concerns a Hoax

Posting on Truth Social Saturday, President Trump dismissed AI safety concerns as a Democratic hoax, said he would "cherish" and "watch over" the AI industry, and announced he is forming an "AI Force" modeled on the Space Force plus a forthcoming AI czar. He also polled followers on renaming the technology. No duties for either the AI Force or the czar were specified.
