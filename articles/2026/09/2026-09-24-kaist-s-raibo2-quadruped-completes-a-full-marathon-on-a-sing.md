# KAIST’s RAIBO2 Quadruped Completes a Full Marathon on a Single Charge — Published in Nature

Prof. Jemin Hwangbo’s team reports RAIBO2 ran the full 42.195 km course in 4 hours, 19 minutes and 52 seconds without charging or replacing its battery — described as the world’s first quadruped robot to complete an actual marathon event. The paper treats endurance as a whole-robot system problem spanning mechanical, electrical, actuation and control co-design, with voltage, current, temperature and GPS telemetry logged throughout. KAIST notes it is the first Korean robotics research published in the main Nature journal. The team is now targeting industrial commercialisation — a credible signal that legged platforms are approaching duty cycles that matter for inspection and logistics.
