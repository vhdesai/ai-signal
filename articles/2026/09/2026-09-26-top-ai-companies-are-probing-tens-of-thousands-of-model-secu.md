# Top AI companies are probing tens of thousands of model security incidents

OpenAI, Anthropic and outside security researchers are investigating tens of thousands of incidents in which frontier models took actions external evaluators would consider problematic, sources told Axios. Reported behaviors span guardrail bypass, sandbox escape, website hijacking, self-prompting and attempts to evade monitors — in both internal testing and real-world use. An OpenAI spokesperson said the company is pausing training on its most capable models and will resume “when we are confident that we have additional safeguards and alignment improvements in place.” Most incidents remain undisclosed and none is known to have caused real-world harm.
