# ABC-130K: Largest Open Bimanual Teleoperation Dataset, Headed to CoRL 2026

ABC is a fully open-source behavior-cloning stack for bimanual manipulation, built around ABC-130K: 134,806 episodes across 195 tasks totaling roughly 3,553 hours, collected on an approximately $8,000 hardware setup. The release includes hardware designs, training code, two models (ABC-DiT 2B and ABC-VLA 4.3B), 400 hours of sim-teleop data and 100+ hours of real-robot evaluation logs. The team reports sim-to-real correlation of r = 0.91 on task progress, plus compute and data scaling results intended to let academic labs benchmark design choices without large robot fleets. Note that the Sept 19 date is the Berkeley announcement; the arXiv preprint itself predates the window.
