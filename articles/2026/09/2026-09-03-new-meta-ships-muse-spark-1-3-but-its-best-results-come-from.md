# New Meta ships Muse Spark 1.3, but its best results come from a config developers can’t use yet

Meta rolled out Muse Spark 1.3 across Muse Code and the Meta Model API, claiming frontier-level coding and agentic performance. VentureBeat notes the headline numbers come from a “max reasoning” configuration that remains in limited preview pending safety testing. The gap between benchmarked and generally available configurations is becoming a recurring pattern across frontier launches and warrants scrutiny in vendor comparisons.
