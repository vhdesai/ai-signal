# Nvidia joins iPronics' $125M round for programmable optical networking

Valencia-based iPronics raised a $125 million Series B co-led by Maverick Silicon and Light Street Capital, with Nvidia participating, to scale programmable photonic switching for AI infrastructure. As clusters scale toward hundreds of thousands of accelerators, optical switching and interconnect efficiency are becoming core constraints alongside power and cooling. Nvidia's participation shows infrastructure capital moving beyond GPUs into the networking layers that determine cluster utilization.
