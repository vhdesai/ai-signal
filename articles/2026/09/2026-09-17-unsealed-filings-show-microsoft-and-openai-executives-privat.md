# Unsealed filings show Microsoft and OpenAI executives privately called AI scraping "theft"

A newly unsealed summary-judgment brief in the publishers' copyright suit quotes Microsoft's Director of Applied Science describing mass scraping as potentially "the largest theft of labor in human history," and an internal Microsoft document warning of a "doom loop" that would hurt both model performance and the wider web. Microsoft's own data cited in the filing showed click-through declines of 83–93% for some plaintiff domains. Microsoft says the remarks reflect one employee's view, not company position; the underlying exhibits remain sealed and no infringement finding has been made. The disclosure meaningfully raises the commercial risk around AI content supply chains and licensing exposure downstream. - https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/microsoft-exec-called-ai-scraping-the-largest-theft-of-labor-in-human-history-new-unredacted-filings-reveal/
