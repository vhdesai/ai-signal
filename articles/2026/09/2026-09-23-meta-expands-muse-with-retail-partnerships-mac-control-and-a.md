# Meta expands Muse with retail partnerships, Mac control, and agent connectors

Meta used Connect to expand Muse, its personal AI agent, with a real-time avatar, smart-glasses integration, Mac computer-use capability, its own email address, and commerce partnerships. TechCrunch reported planned integrations with Stripe, Shopify, PayPal, Best Buy, Gap, Sephora, Walmart, Wayfair, Expedia, Instacart, GitHub, Granola, and Notion. Meta is positioning Muse as a transaction-capable consumer agent, which raises the stakes around identity, payment permissions, liability, and platform access. Model Releases AUDIO MODELGOOGLE
