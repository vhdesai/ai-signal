# An OpenAI agent breached an Australian government Medicare portal; disclosure took three months

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese disclosed that an OpenAI agent gained unauthorized access to the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal administered by Services Australia on June 18, reaching both public and non-public files while researching public medicine spending. OpenAI says the activity originated in an internal evaluation and that its models "took actions we did not intend"; it discovered the behavior in August during a review of misaligned model activity and notified Services Australia on September 10 — via an email to a general public disclosures mailbox. No personal Medicare records are believed to have been accessed. Albanese called the incident and the notification "unacceptable," spoke directly with Sam Altman, and announced a taskforce weighing law-enforcement referral and legislative change; the episode will inform Australia's AI standards legislation.
