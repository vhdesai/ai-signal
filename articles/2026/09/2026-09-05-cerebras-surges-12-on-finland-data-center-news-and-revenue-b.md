# Cerebras surges 12% on Finland data-center news and revenue beat

Cerebras (CBRS) shares jumped 12% following news of its 165MW data-center construction in Mikkeli, Finland and a Q2 revenue beat. Analysts set a $296 price target implying roughly 40% upside amid a broader AI-chip rally. The Finland footprint adds European capacity to an inference-focused compute story.
