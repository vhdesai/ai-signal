# AI data centers may create far fewer jobs than projected, think tank warns

A think-tank analysis surfaced in the CIO Dive Weekender warns that projected job creation from AI data-center buildouts is being systematically overstated: most permanent operating roles are highly specialized and few, with construction-phase gains temporary. The finding lands as US states — including sites tied to Amazon, Meta, and Google — start clawing back tax exemptions granted on the promise of local employment. Expect the fiscal politics of data-center siting to accelerate as the delta between promised and delivered jobs becomes visible.
