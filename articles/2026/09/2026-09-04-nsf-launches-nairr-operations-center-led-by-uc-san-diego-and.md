# NSF launches NAIRR operations center led by UC San Diego and UT Austin

MeriTalk reported that NSF is creating a National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource operations center led by UC San Diego and UT Austin. The center will coordinate compute, data, models, tools, support, and portal operations as NAIRR moves from pilot toward durable national research infrastructure. BIOLOGY AI DESIGN
