# What we still don't know about Google's Flipkart shopping test

MediaNama's India-focused read on the same Gemini/Flipkart test is markedly more skeptical, noting that Amazon listings appear alongside Flipkart's without a direct-purchase option and that the feature may go live at or before Big Billion Days starting October 9. Open questions it flags: which protocol actually underpins the flow (UCP vs. ACP is undisclosed), what the commercial terms are, and whether selective inclusion in AI answers invites competition scrutiny — echoing IndiaMART's 2025 Calcutta High Court case against OpenAI. It also contrasts the move with OpenAI scaling back ChatGPT checkout in March.
