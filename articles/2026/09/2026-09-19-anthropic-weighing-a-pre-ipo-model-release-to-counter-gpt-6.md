# Anthropic Weighing a Pre-IPO Model Release to Counter GPT-6 Astra

Reuters reported that Anthropic is considering shipping a new model ahead of its expected listing, to counter enterprise momentum from OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra. Safety evaluation is reportedly under way and no launch has been announced. The deliberation comes roughly a week after Dario Amodei’s essay calling for an industry-wide slowdown. Caveat: the original Reuters piece was not directly retrievable; this is carried here via two independent secondary outlets within the window.
