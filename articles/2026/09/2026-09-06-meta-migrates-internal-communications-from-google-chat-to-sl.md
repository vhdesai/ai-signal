# Meta migrates internal communications from Google Chat to Slack, citing AI agents

Meta is moving internal communications from Google Chat to Slack, saying Slack is better suited for using AI agents. The migration is a win for Salesforce, which owns Slack and has been investing heavily in AI features and products. For enterprise buyers, the story is a concrete data point on how AI-agent readiness is now driving major internal-tooling procurement decisions.
