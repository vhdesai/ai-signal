# AI-assisted team closes the last sporadic case of the inverse Galois problem

A six-person team assembled at an American Institute of Mathematics workshop at Caltech — including Rachel Pries (Colorado State), Bjorn Poonen (MIT), Xiaoyu Huang (Temple), Blake Jackson, Kyu-Hwan Lee (UConn) and Caltech PhD student Shaowu Zhang — used AI search to find the polynomial realizing M23, the one sporadic group among 26 that had resisted resolution since the 1980s. The approach paired human choice of coordinates and problem framing with machine-scale search over symmetry combinations and numerical approximation of candidate equations. “That wasn't really possible five years ago,” Kyu-Hwan Lee said of the three-month turnaround. It is a useful template for enterprise R&D: AI as a scale multiplier on a human-specified search space, not an autonomous prover.
