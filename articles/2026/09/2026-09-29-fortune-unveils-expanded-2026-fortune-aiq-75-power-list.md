# Fortune Unveils Expanded 2026 “Fortune AIQ 75” Power List

The second annual ranking of Fortune 500 companies generating measurable business impact from AI expanded from 50 to 75 names across 18 sectors, with 36 debutants. JPMorgan Chase took the top spot, followed by Alphabet (2), Coca-Cola (3), Amazon (4) and Nvidia (5), with Microsoft at 10.
