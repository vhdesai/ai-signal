# Figure committed $3.5B for AI compute after raising $1.9B

An analysis of Figure's compute commitments found the robotics company has contracted for roughly $3.5 billion of AI compute despite raising only $1.9 billion to date. The gap illustrates how multi-year compute contracts are being signed against expected future funding rather than cash on hand. Boards evaluating AI capex should treat these commitments as debt-like obligations in scenario planning. Products & Tools G M
