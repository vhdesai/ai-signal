# Anthropic Publishes Its Most Detailed Misuse Casebook — Weapons, Espionage, Distillation

The report covers operations disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026 across seven harm areas: cyber, influence, surveillance, conventional weapons, biological misuse, scams and fraud, and illicit distillation. Cases include a Russian-linked espionage operation consistent with public reporting on Midnight Blizzard; a Hunan-based operation running agent swarms against roughly 50 organizations; an effort in northern Yemen to use Claude in place of human engineers for missile-guidance software; and five biological cases including gain-of-function grant drafting tied to a military institute. Anthropic's central claim is that AI has collapsed the labor gap between state-sponsored operators and individuals, with breaches now completed in two to three hours.
