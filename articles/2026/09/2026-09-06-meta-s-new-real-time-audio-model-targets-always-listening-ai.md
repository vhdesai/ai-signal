# Meta's new real-time audio model targets always-listening AI assistants

Meta's Superintelligence Labs unveiled Muse Voice Transcribe, a real-time audio perception model that transcribes speech, separates 20+ speakers, and detects sentence boundaries in a single pass, processing 80ms audio chunks with reinforcement-learning-tuned latency. Independent Artificial Analysis testing puts it at a 3.1% word-error rate on English, with Meta undercutting rivals on price at $3 per 1,000 audio minutes; model weights are not released. The release is positioned as infrastructure for always-on assistants rather than a standalone consumer product, an area where OpenAI and Google are also investing.
