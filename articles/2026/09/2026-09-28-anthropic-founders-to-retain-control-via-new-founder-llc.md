# Anthropic Founders to Retain Control via New “Founder LLC”

The IPO filing reveals a Founder LLC comprising Anthropic’s seven co-founders, which will direct a single Class F share carrying 50.1% of voting power over key corporate matters. Anthropic will remain a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation, with four of seven board seats elected by its Long-Term Benefit Trust, whose trustees include Ben Bernanke.
