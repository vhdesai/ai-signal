# Snorkel AI triples valuation to $3.5B as demand for AI training data booms

Snorkel raised a $350M Series E led by demand for its data-as-a-service approach, tripling its valuation to $3.5B. The round underlines how curated, labeled data has become the scarce input as frontier labs saturate on public web text and pivot to RL and post-training regimes. For CIOs, it signals that vendor pricing for high-quality domain data will rise before it falls. INDUSTRY
