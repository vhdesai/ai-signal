# At Meta Connect, smart glasses were everywhere

TechCrunch's Connect wrap concludes Meta's strategic center of gravity has shifted decisively to smart glasses — camera-free, Ray-Ban style, and higher-end Orion-adjacent variants all appeared on stage and were pushed as the surface Muse will live in. Combined with PrismML shipping tiny LLMs onto Qualcomm smart-glasses silicon this week, the "glasses-first assistant" thesis moved from speculation to product roadmap for at least two of the top platform vendors.
