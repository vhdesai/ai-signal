# AfterQuery becomes Y Combinator's fastest-ever unicorn at a $3.2B valuation

AI model-training startup AfterQuery has reportedly raised at a $3.2 billion valuation, roughly five months after announcing a $30 million Series A at $300 million in April — a better than 10x step-up within a single fiscal half. The company sits in the training-data and evaluation layer, which continues to command premium multiples as frontier labs compete for scarce high-quality supervision.
