# Thales unveils HexaForce as European-sovereign alternative to Palantir NATO battlefield stack

Cybernews reports Thales unveiled HexaForce, a European-sovereign alternative to Palantir's NATO-facing battlefield analytics platform, capitalizing on the weekend Palantir/Iran-strike Pentagon report and complementing Thales's earlier Palantir-alternative announcement. Palantir CEO Alex Karp's simultaneous "these businesses have to be nationalized" quote adds unusually direct fuel to the sovereign-AI-defense category — now distinct from sovereign enterprise AI and moving fast under transatlantic tension over US frontier-model use in military contexts.
