# More Than 100 Researchers, Including Hinton, Demand Independence from Embedded AI Evaluators

Hours after Anthropic named Accenture its first embedded evaluator, over 100 AI researchers signed a public letter arguing that evaluators embedded in AI companies must be "meaningfully independent" — at minimum, not owned or governed by frontier AI companies, without other significant commercial business with them, and not accepting payment contingent on findings. The letter lands directly on Anthropic's decision to fund Accenture's work itself.
