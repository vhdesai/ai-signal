# New Jersey issues record $1.07M data center fine over 62 unpermitted gas generators

Jersey’s Department of Environmental Protection fined DataOne $1.07 million — the state’s largest enforcement action against a data center — for installing and operating 62 natural-gas generators at its Vineland AI campus without preconstruction permits or operating certificates. Each unit is rated 1,982 kW, roughly 54 times the 37 kW threshold requiring a permit, for combined capacity near 123 MW. The enforcement followed a Guardian and Floodlight investigation using thermal drone footage showing at least 45 units running in August. DataOne has 45 days to obtain permits or cease operating the generators, and disputes the determination while continuing a transition to Bloom Energy fuel cells. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
