# Jensen Huang says AI CEOs have ‘ulterior reasons’ for warning about doomsday

In a CBS News interview aired Sunday, Nvidia’s CEO accused frontier-lab leaders of disingenuousness: “They’re actually not asking for more laws. They’re asking to be relieved of the laws we do have… They must be doing it for ulterior reasons.” He called safety scaremongering “irresponsible” and “unnecessary,” arguing existing regulation is sufficient. The remarks echo Trump’s Saturday post pledging not to “hinder or stifle” AI growth and land a week after Dario Amodei’s “We Must Pace the Frontier” essay — fracturing the coordinated-pacing consensus.
