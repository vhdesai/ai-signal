# Alibaba's Amap "Street Stars" uses AI to challenge Meituan in local commerce

Alibaba's Amap mapping app has quietly rolled out Street Stars, an AI-driven local ranking layer that surfaces small merchants — with SCMP reporting real-world 50–60% order-volume lifts for restaurants ranked highly. The mechanism is a direct challenge to Meituan's local-services grip and is one of the more concrete monetization proof-points behind analyst arguments this week that Alibaba's Apsara AI push is a monetization pivot, not just an ASI declaration. Watch this space for the antitrust implications when merchant fees follow the AI-directed traffic. Academic Research RESEARCH
