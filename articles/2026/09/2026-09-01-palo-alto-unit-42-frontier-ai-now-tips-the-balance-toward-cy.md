# Palo Alto Unit 42: Frontier AI Now Tips the Balance Toward Cyber Adversaries

Researchers at Palo Alto Networks’ Unit 42 reported that threat actors are already using AI to accelerate attacks beyond the pace at which modern defenses can respond. The same CIO Dive edition reported that roughly two-thirds of companies have suffered a business-critical application outage caused by misconfigured security policies — a reminder that AI-accelerated offense is meeting an operational baseline that is already fragile. CIO Dive Daily Dive — Sept. 1
