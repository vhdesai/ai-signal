# Google DeepMind proposes Autoregressive Ranking to replace dual/cross-encoder search stacks

Google DeepMind, UMass Amherst, and UT Austin researchers published Autoregressive Ranking (ARR), a proposed replacement for the classic dual-encoder plus cross-encoder two-stage search architecture. A new SToICaL training loss makes the LLM assign more probability to tokens that lead to higher-ranked documents. In WordNet testing, ARR matched cross-encoder quality while significantly outperforming dual encoders, with theoretical proofs suggesting a fixed embedding dimension can rank arbitrarily many documents — a structural limitation of dual encoders and, if it holds at scale, a material change to retrieval economics.
