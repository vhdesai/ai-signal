# Mistral and Cloudera partner on sovereign enterprise AI

Cloudera and Mistral announced a partnership to bring Mistral models directly to customer data on Cloudera's hybrid platform, positioning them as a sovereign alternative to hyperscaler-hosted AI. WSJ frames it as Mistral's key move to grow its enterprise base beyond model API sales. Alongside the Nvidia–Palantir sovereign supply-chain stack and Nvidia's Australia 2GW buildout, this is the second major sovereign-AI enterprise announcement inside 24 hours — the operative geography of AI infrastructure is now regional and jurisdictional.
