# Clay Mathematics Institute: Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem "apparently been settled" — formal review underway

The Clay Mathematics Institute stated that the Navier-Stokes existence-and-smoothness problem — one of the seven $1M Millennium Prize problems — "has apparently been settled" and that a formal review is now underway. This is Clay's first public acknowledgment of the AI-generated proof OpenAI published on Sept 8 and that a 25-Fields-Medalist consortium sharply criticized last week. Whether the proof ultimately survives review or not, Clay's acknowledgment materially strengthens the credibility of AI-assisted mathematics as a research method and reopens the question of academic-credit norms in AI-assisted proof work.
