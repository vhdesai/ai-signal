# DeepSeek crosses $1B annualized revenue after raising API prices 2.3x–4.5x, targets ~$7.5B Shanghai raise

DeepSeek's annualized run rate has more than doubled from roughly $500M to over $1B, following API price increases of 2.3x to 4.5x implemented last month. Demand reportedly held through the increase, with the API business reaching an 82.9% gross margin through July. The company is simultaneously closing a raise of approximately $7.5B (50B yuan) tied to a planned Shanghai STAR Market listing. The strategic read for buyers: the vendor that anchored the low-cost tier has now re-priced after acquiring the customer base — anyone with DeepSeek in their inference mix should re-baseline unit costs.
