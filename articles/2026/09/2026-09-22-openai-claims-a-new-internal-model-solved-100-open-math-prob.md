# OpenAI claims a new internal model solved 100+ open math problems in one month of training

OpenAI announced that a new internal model — whose training began August 28 — solved more than 100 long-standing open math problems across most areas of mathematics, on top of the Navier-Stokes Millennium Problem. Simultaneously, OpenAI launched an independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI at the Institute for Advanced Study, with Fields Medalist Timothy Gowers as a founding member; critically, OpenAI has stated the group cannot advise on the pace of internal progress. Skeptical mathematicians see the announcement as a response to the 25-Fields-Medalist "Severe Misalignment" open letter and question whether "solving problems" and "understanding math" are the same goal.
