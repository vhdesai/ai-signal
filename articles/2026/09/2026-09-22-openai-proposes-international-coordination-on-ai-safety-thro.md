# OpenAI proposes international coordination on AI safety through national safety institutes

OpenAI published a proposal calling for national AI safety institutes — such as the US Commerce Department's Center for AI Standards and Innovation — to coordinate internationally on model evaluation, risk assessment, and incident reporting. It's OpenAI's most concrete governance proposal to date and lands the same weekend Trump announced the "AI Force" and Bessent confirmed US-China AI-safety notification talks. The framework leans on existing institutions rather than new supranational bodies — a pragmatic answer to concerns about lab self-regulation and the fractured slowdown coalition.
