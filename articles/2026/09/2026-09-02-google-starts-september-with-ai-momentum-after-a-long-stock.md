# Google starts September with AI momentum after a long stock losing streak

CNBC reported that Alphabet entered September with renewed AI momentum after a four-month losing streak, helped by the Gemini 3.8 Flash launch, antitrust relief, and investor commentary from Berkshire Hathaway's Greg Abel. CNBC noted that Google is positioning Flash around lower-cost coding and agentic workloads while adding pay-as-you-go pricing, token discounts, agent spending caps, and a zero-dollar base subscription option for Gemini Enterprise. The business question is whether lower-cost breadth and cloud distribution can offset concerns that Google trails OpenAI and Anthropic in enterprise mindshare.
