# Google rolls out Lyria 3.5 music generation to the Gemini app and API

Google made Lyria 3.5, its newest DeepMind music generation model, available in the Gemini consumer app and API. The model supports genre and vocal/instrumental selection, adjustable track length, multimodal image input, and structural tags for verses and choruses. Every generated track carries an inaudible SynthID marker identifying it as machine-made, and safety filters block requests for specific artist voices or copyrighted lyrics. Music generation is currently single-turn; iterative refinement across prompts is not supported. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
