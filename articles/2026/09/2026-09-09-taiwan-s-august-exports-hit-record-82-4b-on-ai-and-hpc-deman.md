# Taiwan's August exports hit record $82.4B on AI and HPC demand

Taiwan's Ministry of Finance reported August exports of $82.4 billion, up 41.0% year-over-year and the highest monthly total on record, marking a 34th consecutive month of growth. Electronic components rose 58.0% to a record $32.22 billion; information and communications products rose 41.8% to $33.10 billion on AI server, storage, and networking demand. Together the two categories were 79.3% of total exports — a concentration that is now as much a risk as a strength, and a real-time indicator of where AI capacity is actually being built.
