# Researchers Used Anthropic's Claude to Breach OpenAI

A three-person team at Hacktron AI chained two vulnerabilities — a libheif memory bug reached through OpenAI's Discourse community forum, then an account-takeover flaw — to access multiple OpenAI employee ChatGPT and Codex accounts and reach the company's GitHub organization. OpenAI paid a $6,500 bug-bounty award and says the issues are fixed. The capability detail is the one that matters: Claude Opus 4.8 repeatedly failed to build a working exploit, while Opus 5 succeeded within hours of its release.
