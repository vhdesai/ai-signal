# MIT uses small-data machine learning to make RNA vaccines survive room temperature for a year

MIT engineers working with MIT CSAIL applied a machine-learning algorithm purpose-built for very small datasets to find excipient formulations that stabilize the lipid nanoparticles delivering mRNA vaccines. The algorithm screened roughly 50 FDA-approved excipients and converged “in just a handful of iterations, rather than the exhaustive search that would normally be required,” per CSAIL assistant professor Mina Konaković Luković. The resulting vaccines remained stable at room temperature for a year, or at ~98°F for two months, and produced immune responses in mice equivalent to a Moderna-like control. The work appears in Nature Biotechnology, with Ana Jaklenec and Robert Langer as senior authors.
