# Abuse survivor sues xAI over allegedly Grok-generated illegal imagery

A survivor of child sexual abuse has filed suit against xAI, alleging its Grok chatbot used images of her abuse to generate new illegal sexual imagery depicting her. The case adds to mounting legal and safety scrutiny of xAI's image-generation capabilities.
