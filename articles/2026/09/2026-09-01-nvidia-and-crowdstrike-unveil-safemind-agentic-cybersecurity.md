# NVIDIA and CrowdStrike unveil SafeMind agentic cybersecurity system at Fal.Con 2026

Jensen Huang appeared with CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz at Fal.Con 2026 in Las Vegas to introduce SafeMind, an agentic cybersecurity system built on NVIDIA Nemotron open models combined with Falcon telemetry and specialized agent harnesses. The stated design is validation-first, pairing offensive and defensive agents, with NVIDIA claiming both improved protection and cost reduction for defenders.
