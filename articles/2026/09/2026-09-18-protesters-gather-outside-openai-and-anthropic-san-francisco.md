# Protesters gather outside OpenAI and Anthropic San Francisco offices over frontier AI pace

Demonstrators gathered outside the San Francisco offices of OpenAI and Anthropic to protest the pace of frontier AI development and perceived safety shortfalls. The protests coincide with the same week's disclosure wave from both labs and Amodei's slowdown essay, turning an intra-industry debate into a visible public one. Scale and organizer details were not independently confirmed. - https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/protesters-target-openai-anthropic-san-070257146.html
