# Anthropic Names Accenture as Its First Embedded Evaluator — $1B Each Over Five Years

Anthropic will embed evaluators from Accenture's Faculty unit inside the company to red-team new models, run alignment assessments, and test safeguards, with access "comparable to an employee's." Each firm expects to invest at least $1B over five years. Anthropic will fund the work directly for now while arguing long-term funding should ultimately come from pooled or government sources that do not yet exist. The choice of a consultancy over METR or Apollo surprised the field; Accenture shares rose roughly 8% after hours.
