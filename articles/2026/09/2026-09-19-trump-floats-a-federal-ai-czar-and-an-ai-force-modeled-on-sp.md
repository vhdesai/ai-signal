# Trump floats a federal AI czar and an “AI Force” modeled on Space Force

Axios reported that President Trump wants a new AI czar and an “AI Force” modeled on Space Force, while Business Insider separately reported that he said the government should not “stifle” AI. The proposal is still early-stage, but it signals that AI governance is being framed as national competitiveness and command structure, not just consumer protection. For executives, this points to a likely increase in federal coordination around AI infrastructure, defense applications, and regulation-by-agency even if comprehensive legislation remains stalled.
