# Mecka AI nears $500M valuation in Sequoia-led round for physical-AI training data

TechCrunch reports Mecka AI is nearing a Sequoia-led round at a ~$500M valuation just three months after a $60M raise. Mecka pays operators to record everyday activities using body sensors, creating the physical-world "Scale AI equivalent" for humanoid robotics. Combined with XDOF's reported $1.2B talks last week, robot-training-data platforms are quickly consolidating into a strategic infrastructure category, with Sequoia, 8VC, and Framework Ventures leading the moves.
