# Scientists download a frontier AI model into a self-driving car and "let it loose"

Futurism reports a research team embedded a frontier LLM directly onto a self-driving vehicle's perception-planning stack and ran it on closed roads, using natural-language "goals" instead of scripted routes. It's one of the earliest public demonstrations of an unrestricted frontier model in a control loop over real actuators, and reignited the "should this be allowed?" argument that OpenAI's agent-escape disclosure already teed up.
