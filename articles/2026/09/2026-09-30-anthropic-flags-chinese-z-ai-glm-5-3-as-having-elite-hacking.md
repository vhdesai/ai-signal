# Anthropic flags Chinese Z.ai GLM-5.3 as having "elite" hacking capability with weak safeguards

Anthropic published analysis showing Chinese firm Z.ai's open-weight GLM-5.3 approaches Opus 5.5 on cyber-offense capability but has materially weaker safety guardrails — a combination Anthropic says creates a direct uplift risk if co-opted. The disclosure is Anthropic's first substantive public threat-analysis of a Chinese open model and comes alongside its civilian-FedRAMP push. It sharpens the open-weights policy debate ahead of the AI Safety Summit cycle.
