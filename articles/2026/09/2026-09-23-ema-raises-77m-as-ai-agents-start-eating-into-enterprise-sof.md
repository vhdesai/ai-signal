# Ema raises $77M as AI agents start eating into enterprise software and services

Ema, which deploys teams of AI agents to automate corporate processes across HR, IT, and finance, raised $77 million led by Bengaluru-based Creaegis with existing investors Accel and Section 32 participating. The company has now raised $140 million and reports more than 50 enterprise customers, including Google and Microsoft. The round's thesis is explicit: agents displacing both enterprise software seats and IT services labor, which puts the pressure on per-seat pricing models across the category.
