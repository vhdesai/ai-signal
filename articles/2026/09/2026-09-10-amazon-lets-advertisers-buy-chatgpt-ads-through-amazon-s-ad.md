# Amazon lets advertisers buy ChatGPT ads through Amazon's ad-tech platform

Amazon is letting advertisers test buying ads on ChatGPT via Amazon's advertising-technology platform, initially as a US-only pilot. ChatGPT's ad business is at roughly $1B in annualized recurring revenue — well below the earlier ~$2.4B FY target. The partnership marks a warming in the previously frosty Amazon–OpenAI relationship following Amazon's $35B OpenAI investment earlier this year, and it plugs OpenAI into a direct-response advertiser pool Amazon has been aggressively building as Google retreats in certain categories.
