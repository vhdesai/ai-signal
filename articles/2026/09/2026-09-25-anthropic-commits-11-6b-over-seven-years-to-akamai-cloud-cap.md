# Anthropic commits $11.6B over seven years to Akamai cloud capacity

Anthropic will spend $11.6B over seven years on Akamai's distributed cloud, with an option to expand by $9B to roughly $20B — the largest contract in Akamai's history. Unusually, the deal targets CPUs rather than GPUs, reflecting the general-purpose compute that agentic workloads consume. Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant convertible into 7.7M shares (~5% of outstanding stock) at $111.33, with ~2% vesting on the initial commitment and the rest tied to further spend. Akamai expects $5.5B in related capex and $150M–$300M of revenue in 2027, inverting the usual circular-financing pattern in which the supplier invests in the AI lab.
