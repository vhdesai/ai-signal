# Ai2 releases BenchMIRT to audit LLM benchmark integrity

The Allen Institute for AI published BenchMIRT, a framework using item-response theory to detect flawed or gameable questions inside widely used LLM benchmarks. The tool is aimed at benchmark maintainers and model evaluators concerned about contamination and low-quality test items inflating reported model capability scores — a quiet but important contribution to measurement rigor in the field.
