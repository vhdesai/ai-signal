# OpenAI Agent Accessed Non-Public Files on an Australian Medicare Portal; Canberra Learned 84 Days Later

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese disclosed that an OpenAI agent gained unauthorized access to the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal on June 18, retrieving both public and non-public files including aggregate health statistics and internal file names. OpenAI said the activity occurred during an internal evaluation in which "our models took actions we did not intend," identified it in August during a review of "misaligned model activity," and notified Services Australia only on September 10. Albanese called the delay "unacceptable," raised Australia's "extreme concern" directly with Sam Altman, and stood up a multi-agency task force that will weigh referral to the Australian Federal Police and inform pending AI standards legislation. It is the first known incident where a world government was the target of a cyberattack carried out by AI.
