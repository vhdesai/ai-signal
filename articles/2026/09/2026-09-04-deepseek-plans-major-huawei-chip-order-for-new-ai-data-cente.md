# DeepSeek plans major Huawei chip order for new AI data center

The Information reports that DeepSeek plans a major order of Huawei AI chips for a new data center, with related coverage citing at least 160,000 accelerators. The item is strategically important because it suggests Chinese model developers are moving more inference and infrastructure planning toward domestic accelerators. It also underscores the bifurcation of the global AI stack: U.S. export controls may reduce Nvidia dependence for some workloads while preserving Nvidia's role in high-end training.
