# Instinct at ~$10B: BI profiles the 23-year-old college dropout Noah Shinn

Business Insider profiles Noah Shinn, the 23-year-old college-dropout founder of Instinct, which after only a summer soft-launch is reportedly raising at a $10B valuation up from $2.5B in August. BI's own review of Muse — Meta's rival that briefly topped Apple's US app store — is skeptical about reliability; a separate BI piece flags experts warning about the risks of handing an AI agent full life-management access. Consumer AI-agent adoption is now a real race despite that Sunday's WSJ argues US general-public AI enthusiasm remains muted.
