# Meta launches "Meta One" AI subscriptions from $2.99 to $499/month

Meta rolled out Meta One subscription tiers across Instagram, WhatsApp, and Facebook, offering higher Meta AI usage limits plus 50+ features spanning content creation, audience engagement, and business management. Plans start at $2.99/mo (WhatsApp Plus) and climb through $7.99 individual bundles, $14.99 creator/business bundles, $149 "Expert," and $499 "Max" — the top tier unlocking the highest levels of Meta Business Agent. It is Meta's clearest attempt yet to convert AI usage into recurring revenue that offsets the hundreds of billions being spent on AI capex; the service already has 15M subscribers globally. - https://www.theinformation.com/articles/the-briefings-metas-ai-subscriptions - https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/meta-expands-subscription-push-with-new-ai-focused-plans/
