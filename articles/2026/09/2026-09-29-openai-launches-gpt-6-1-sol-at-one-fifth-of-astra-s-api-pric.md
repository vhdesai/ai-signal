# OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Sol at one-fifth of Astra's API price

OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol lands with "near-Astra intelligence for coding, computer use, and professional work" at one-fifth of GPT-6 Astra's input and output token prices. The release collapses the standard premium-vs-mid-tier spread the industry has been pricing against for a year and pairs with the DevDay 2026 announcements — "dots" proactive assistants, GPT-6 Astra updates, and the Codex Originals program. The combined effect is to put sustained unit-cost pressure on Anthropic's Opus 5.5 price position.
