# Anthropic's Amodei takes a one-on-one White House dinner with President Trump

Dario Amodei dined privately with Trump at the White House — their first such meeting, and one that puts the industry's most prominent advocate for slowing development across the table from its most vocal accelerationist. CNBC reports the invitation followed Amodei's absence from the September 24 state dinner for Xi Jinping, which Altman, Musk, Huang and Zuckerberg attended. The timing matters commercially: Anthropic is preparing for a widely expected IPO, and the Pentagon's supply-chain-risk designation is still being contested in court. A White House official said “America will lead the world in Super Intelligence, while protecting American consumers.”
