# Eligible ASUS laptops unlock bundled Google AI experiences

BusinessMirror reported that ASUS Philippines and Google are bundling three months of Google AI Pro or Google AI Plus with selected ASUS and ROG laptops. The offer includes access to Gemini-related services, creative and productivity tools, developer experiences such as AI Studio and Antigravity where available, and substantial cloud storage. The strategic signal is that AI subscriptions are being pushed through hardware channels, making distribution and bundling a competitive lever alongside model quality. WEATHER AI GLOBAL APP
