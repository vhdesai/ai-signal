# Microsoft Research finds offloading robot inference to edge and cloud improves success and battery life

Microsoft Research reported that moving physical-AI inference off onboard robot GPUs to edge or cloud infrastructure improved task success, efficiency, and battery life. The team added a Kubernetes-based capability to its open-source Physical AI Toolchain for containerizing and distributing robotics AI workloads across robots, edge devices, and the cloud. For enterprises piloting embodied AI, the finding pushes against the assumption that autonomy requires full on-device compute.
