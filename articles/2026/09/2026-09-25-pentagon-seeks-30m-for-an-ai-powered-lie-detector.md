# Pentagon seeks $30M for an AI-powered lie detector

A DoD budget request seeks $30.3M over five years for “Polygraph+” / “Polygraph Next,” centered on AI/ML scoring algorithms and “standoff sensing” that captures physiological readings without attached devices. The Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency would run it for employee vetting and insider-threat detection. Academic experts note the polygraph has been repeatedly debunked — a 2003 National Research Council review called the evidence “weak at best” — and warn that applying such error rates to 2.8M DoD staff could falsely implicate tens of thousands.
