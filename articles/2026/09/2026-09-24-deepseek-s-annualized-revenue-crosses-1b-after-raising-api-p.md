# DeepSeek's annualized revenue crosses $1B after raising API prices, targets ~$7.5B Shanghai raise

DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate reached approximately $1 billion, more than double its level a few months ago, following API price increases of roughly 2.3x to 4.5x depending on model. Founder Liang Wenfeng reportedly disclosed the figure to investors. The company is working to close a roughly $7.5 billion financing round by late October tied to a planned Shanghai STAR Market listing, at a valuation reported near $74 billion. The pricing move matters strategically: the lab that built its reputation on undercutting Western frontier pricing has demonstrated pricing power without material demand loss. MARKETS
