# Former Ukrainian Defense Minister Fedorov pitches a private-sector "Army of Robots"

Ex-Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov unveiled "Army of Robots," a private combat-robotics initiative he says would handle casualty evacuation, mine clearance, and combat operations. Fedorov says drones already account for 95% of target engagements on the current line; the initiative would extend the pattern to ground robotics under private-sector funding and coordination. It's the sharpest public articulation yet of the AI-plus-autonomous-hardware model of national defense that Ukraine has run as a live experiment since 2022.
