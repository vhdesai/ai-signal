# WSJ: an AI slowdown "has to happen regardless of safety"

WSJ's Markets A.M. argues Monday's software-vs-chip market split — hyperscalers Microsoft, Alphabet, and Meta up, chip/power/infrastructure names down — reads as an overdue reality check on data-center-buildout math rather than pure safety pricing. Data centers are set to hit 12% of US electricity demand by 2030 (Rystad), copper supply would be ~25% short by 2040 without new mines (S&P Global), and hyperscalers' share of the leading investment-grade bond index grew 78% in one year (Capital Group). Rising open-source competition and falling token prices raise the "how does this get monetized?" question just ahead of Anthropic and OpenAI IPOs. - https://www.wsj.com/finance/investing/markets-a-m-newsletter-ai-slowdown-reality-check
