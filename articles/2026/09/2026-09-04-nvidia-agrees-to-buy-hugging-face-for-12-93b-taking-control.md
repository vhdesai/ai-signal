# Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for $12.93B, taking control of the open-model layer

Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.93B, giving the dominant AI accelerator supplier ownership of a platform used by more than 18 million developers and 200,000 companies to discover, evaluate, customize, and deploy models. Nvidia says Hugging Face will remain open and that Nvidia compute will not be required. Even so, the deal moves Nvidia from hardware into developer distribution, making neutrality and regulatory review the central questions.
