# Bank of America to double its AI budget; McDonald's commits $8.5B to an AI-enabled tech roadmap

Bank of America's co-president pointed to "clearly identifiable" gains in justifying a doubling of next year's AI budget, a rare concrete ROI claim from a major financial institution. Separately, McDonald's laid out a 10-year, $8.5B investment roadmap that includes scaling its AI-enabled ordering system, ArchIQ. The same edition reports a Visier study finding widespread "performative" AI use, as employees face pressure to adopt the technology amid job-loss anxiety and unclear advancement paths.
