# Shanghai AI Lab Ships Intern W0, a Physical World Model for Force-Tactile Robotics

Shanghai AI Lab released Intern Physical World Model W0, a foundation model specifically designed for force-tactile robotics. Unlike pure vision-language grounding, W0 incorporates contact-force feedback loops important for dexterous manipulation, tool use, and industrial assembly. It is a notable Chinese entry into the physical-AI category currently dominated by NVIDIA Cosmos, Perceptron Isaac 0.5, and Meta's world-model research — and a reminder that Chinese labs are pushing hard on embodied-AI foundations even as the US regulatory conversation focuses on frontier language models.
