# Apple releases LensVLM-9B open weights for document understanding

LensVLM-9B renders long documents as compressed page images and uses learned tools to expand only the pages required to answer a question. Apple reports accuracy comparable to a full-text upper bound at 4.3× effective compression, and superiority over retrieval and text-compression baselines up to 10.1× across seven text-QA benchmarks. For document-heavy enterprise workloads this is a materially different long-context cost curve than conventional RAG.
