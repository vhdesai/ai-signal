# Anthropic Signs $13.7B, Six-Year Compute Deal With Trump-Linked Rum Group

Anthropic signed a $13.7B, six-year computing deal with Rum Group — a firm with social-media roots and long-standing ties to the Trump administration — to add capacity via a Georgia data center, and took an option to buy 51 million Rum shares for one cent apiece. The agreement extends Anthropic's disclosed ~$517B, decade-long compute obligations across Google, SpaceX, and neoclouds including Nscale. The deal draws Anthropic further into US political geography just as Beijing is formally rebuking Amodei and Washington debates federal lab inspectors.
