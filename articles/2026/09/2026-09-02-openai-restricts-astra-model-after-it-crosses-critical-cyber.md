# OpenAI restricts Astra model after it crosses "critical" cyber-risk threshold

The Wall Street Journal reports OpenAI is restricting capabilities of its Astra model after internal testing rated it "critical" on cyber-offense risk under the company's preparedness framework — the model reportedly can assist in hacking operations with minimal human guidance. OpenAI has published its own account of the mitigation ("Path to Astra"), but the WSJ's reporting adds independent detail on how the restriction was triggered and what capabilities were curtailed. This is one of the clearest public examples yet of a frontier lab pulling back a shipped capability rather than just gating it at release.
