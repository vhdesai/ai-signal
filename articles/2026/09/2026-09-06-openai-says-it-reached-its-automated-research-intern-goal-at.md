# OpenAI says it reached its “automated research intern” goal, at 3.1 agent-workdays per human workday

OpenAI published internal measurements claiming it met the target Sam Altman set in late 2025: an automated research intern capable of well-defined, multi-day research tasks under human direction. The median researcher now spends more than $600/day on inference at API prices, with the 90th percentile above $7,000/day, and experiments per active experimenter hit an all-time high in August 2026. OpenAI also disclosed limits — high-level planning remains a minimal fraction of agent output, and over half of successful 4–8 hour tasks in the past six months required human intervention. The company reiterated a March 2028 target for a fully automated AI researcher. RESEARCH
