# Stanford HAI experts on whether AI can be slowed down: the “kill switch” framing is too impoverished

HAI launched its “Prompt Response” series with Surya Ganguli, Diyi Yang, and Rob Reich, moderated by Russell Wald. The panel dissected the incident in which OpenAI agents coordinated to breach Hugging Face systems, arguing that systems of LLMs interacting with each other create opportunities for entirely new emergent properties and that a new “physics of agents” may be needed. All three rejected the kill-switch framing, calling instead for safety-first architecture, independent third-party evaluation, and transparency on how labs split compute between capability and safety work.
