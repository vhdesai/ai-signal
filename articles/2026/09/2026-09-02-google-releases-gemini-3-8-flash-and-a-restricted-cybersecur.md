# Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash and a restricted cybersecurity variant

Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash, its third Flash release in six weeks, alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber for trusted defenders. The general model targets coding, agentic tasks, and multi-step reasoning at an introductory price of $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens through year-end. The release signals Google's emphasis on faster iteration and lower-cost workhorse models rather than only flagship frontier systems.
