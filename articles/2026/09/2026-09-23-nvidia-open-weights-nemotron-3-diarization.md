# NVIDIA open-weights Nemotron 3 Diarization

NVIDIA released a compact 100M-parameter speaker-diarization model on Hugging Face that answers "who spoke when," tracking up to eight speakers including overlapping speech. A single checkpoint serves both offline recordings and real-time streaming, with day-one availability through Transformers and the Dell Enterprise Hub. A model this small makes speaker-attributed meeting transcription practical on-premises, without routing audio to a third-party API.
