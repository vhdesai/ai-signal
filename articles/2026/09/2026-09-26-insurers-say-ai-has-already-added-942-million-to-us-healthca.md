# Insurers say AI has already added $942 million to US healthcare costs

A Blue Cross Blue Shield Association analysis attributes an additional $942 million in healthcare spending over two years to hospitals’ use of AI tools in claims submission. BCBSA reports a sharp rise in patients documented with complex conditions but “no evidence of corresponding change in care delivered.” Abridge founder Dr. Shiv Rao conceded the dynamic could produce “bots fighting bots, agents fighting agents,” while BCBSA SVP Luke Chalker called it “a completely one-sided blood bath” against insurers. An early, concrete case of AI shifting economics between counterparties rather than reducing system cost.
