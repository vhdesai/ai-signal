# Palo Alto Networks launches Unit 42 Continuous Frontier AI Defense

Palo Alto Networks introduced a service that continuously tests deployed AI systems for vulnerabilities and returns remediation guidance, rather than treating red-teaming as a point-in-time exercise. It runs as a multi-model harness reported to combine Anthropic's Claude Mythos 5, OpenAI's GPT-5.6-Cyber, and open-weight models. The launch landed the same day as Cisco Talos' autonomous-malware disclosure, underscoring how quickly AI-system assurance is becoming a standalone security category.
