# Trump announces an "AI Force" and a forthcoming AI czar, calling safety concerns a hoax

Posting on Truth Social Saturday, President Trump dismissed AI safety concerns as a Democratic hoax, said he would "cherish" and "watch over" the AI industry, and announced he is forming an AI Force modeled on the Space Force plus a forthcoming AI czar. He also polled followers on renaming the technology. No duties for either the AI Force or the czar were specified, and the post follows his comments last weekend that he is open to "guardrails" while criticizing those raising concerns.
