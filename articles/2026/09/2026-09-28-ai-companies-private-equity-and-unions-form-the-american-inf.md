# AI Companies, Private Equity and Unions Form the American Infrastructure Alliance

A new multimillion-dollar coalition — backed by Blackstone, OpenAI, QTS, SoftBank and the IBEW among others — launched to set data centre standards and guardrails with state and local officials across seven states in 2027. The group's explicit aim is to head off construction moratoriums, with initial campaigns planned in Texas, Georgia, Ohio, Iowa, Pennsylvania, Indiana and South Carolina.
