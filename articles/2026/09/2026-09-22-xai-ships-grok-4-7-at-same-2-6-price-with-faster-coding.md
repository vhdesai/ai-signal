# xAI ships Grok 4.7 at same $2/$6 price with faster coding

xAI launched Grok 4.7 at the same $2/$6 per-million-token price as v4.5, claiming coding-speed improvements but with benchmark results that trail Claude Fable and GPT-6 Astra. Musk has teased Grok 5 as a potential AGI breakthrough with rumored ~3-trillion parameters, so 4.7 reads as a pricing/latency polish ahead of the next-generation leap. It also coincides with the GrokBot enterprise rollouts as xAI executes a full commercial push against OpenAI and Anthropic.
