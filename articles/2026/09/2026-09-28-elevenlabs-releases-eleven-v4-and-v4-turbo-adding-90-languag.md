# ElevenLabs Releases Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo, Adding 90+ Languages and ~100ms Latency

ElevenLabs launched two speech models on a new architecture: Eleven v4 for expressive creative work, and Eleven v4 Turbo for real-time voice agents with a median inference latency of roughly 100ms and time-to-first-speech near 150ms. Language coverage rises from 70 to more than 90, and instant voice cloning is claimed from 10 seconds of audio — a figure worth validating against the company’s own documentation, which cites longer samples. More than 55% of ElevenLabs’ business now comes from large enterprises, making latency and consistency the commercially load-bearing claims here.
