# Anthropic introduces Enterprise Frontier Safeguards with customer-held monitoring data

Anthropic announced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, an architecture that stores misuse-monitoring data in the customer's own cloud account rather than Anthropic's. Detection remains automated and Anthropic-operated, but custody, encryption keys, and flag review sit with the customer — combining zero data retention with cross-session misuse detection. The announcement accompanied Anthropic's account of its response to recent security incidents.
