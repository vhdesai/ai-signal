# Fireworks AI releases Ember-1, a post-trained Kimi K3 that uses ~40% fewer tokens

Fireworks Research post-trained Moonshot's open-weight Kimi K3 to emit shorter reasoning traces while holding accuracy — a different mechanism from simply dialing down inference-time reasoning effort. In a production A/B test, output tokens fell from 49.3K to 29.9K per task with scores essentially flat (0.753 vs 0.751). Ember-1 leads K3 Max on Terminal Bench 2.1 (82.0%) and DeepSWE 1.1 (75.2%) but trails on SWE-bench Verified (92.2%). It ships API-only as a Research Preview — weights, training code and algorithms are not released, which limits how far enterprise buyers can verify the cost claim independently.
