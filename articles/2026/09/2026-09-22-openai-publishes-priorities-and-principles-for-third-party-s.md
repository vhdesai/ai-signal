# OpenAI publishes priorities and principles for third-party safety assessments

OpenAI set out four priority areas for independent assessors: safety cases spanning training through deployment, critical safeguards under realistic conditions, capability evaluations tied to its Preparedness Framework, and independent investigation of misalignment incidents. Accompanying principles cover pre-registered scope and claims, proportionate access, disclosed conflicts of interest, remediation time before publication, and redaction rights — while preserving assessor editorial independence. OpenAI says it is in discussions with multiple independent assessors to expand the pool of organizations with frontier-safety expertise. The structure is closer to a commissioned audit than to statutory oversight, since the lab helps set scope and sees findings first.
