# Meta Opens an Enterprise Front, Hiring MongoDB CEO to Run It

Meta announced Meta Enterprise Platform, a new business line packaging its AI stack — the Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code — for companies and developers. Chirantan “CJ” Desai leaves the MongoDB CEO role to lead it as chief enterprise platform officer, reporting directly to Mark Zuckerberg. Meta is entering a market long held by Microsoft, Salesforce and ServiceNow, and is doing so with distribution rather than model leadership as its opening argument. MongoDB shares fell sharply on the executive departure.
