# Software stocks rally, chip stocks sell off as market prices in AI slowdown

Enterprise-software firms rallied Monday on the coordinated slowdown call: ServiceNow +7%, Salesforce ~+5%, Shopify and Figma also up — the market's read on who wins if AI R&D pace slows. Neoclouds and chip firms fell by similar proportions. PitchBook's parallel analysis says a genuine slowdown would lengthen release cycles and push competition from raw performance toward distribution, safety, and cost — a shift that favors Anthropic and OpenAI (which can absorb delayed launches and compliance costs) and hurts smaller labs and open-source developers. Business Insider notes cybersecurity stocks also caught a bid.
