# Anthropic: Claude now "leads" 26% of its own AI R&D, up from under 1% in February

Anthropic published the first results of a prototype R&D Automation Index, reporting that as of August 2026 Claude "leads" 26% of the company's AI research and development work — completing most of a task end-to-end from a high-level prompt under human supervision — up from under 1% in February 2026, scored on Epoch AI's AL0–AL5 automation scale. Work at "collaborates" level or above now exceeds 90%, and Anthropic states Claude is not operating fully autonomously on any measured subset. The index was built from roughly 15,000 tasks sampled across 20% of staff into a frozen 542-node hierarchy; a Claude judge assigned the ratings, matching human raters exactly 59% of the time versus 35% human-to-human agreement. Anthropic also disclosed ~30,000 concurrent internal agents, with online monitors blocking about 1 in 47,000 actions, and roughly 6% of AI R&D compute (12% of AI-driven R&D compute) allocated to safety in a one-week July snapshot. - https://www.engadget.com/2261909/anthropic-says-claude-leads-26-percent-of-its-ai-research-and-development/
