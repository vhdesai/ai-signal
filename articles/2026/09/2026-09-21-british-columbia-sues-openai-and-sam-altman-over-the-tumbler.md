# British Columbia Sues OpenAI and Sam Altman Over the Tumbler Ridge Shooting

The province filed in San Francisco federal court, jointly with school district SD59, alleging OpenAI designed an unsafe product and was negligent in failing to alert the RCMP after its safety team flagged the shooter's ChatGPT activity. Eight people, primarily children, were killed on February 10. The claim cites Altman's April apology — "I am deeply sorry that we did not alert law enforcement to the account that was banned in June" — as an admission that OpenAI identified the risk and failed to act. BC seeks recovery and rebuilding costs including a replacement school, plus injunctive relief. Filing in California rather than Canada positions the claim for materially larger damages.
