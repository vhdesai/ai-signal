# Google Launches Gemini 3.8 Flash and a Restricted Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber Variant

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash, targeting coding and agentic workloads, alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a defense-oriented variant for vulnerability discovery and patching. The Cyber model is not generally available: access is gated to vetted defenders through Google's Fairwind Program. Google reports a substantial lead over commercial rivals on its Chrome security evaluations, with third-party coverage citing roughly 2.6x faster patching. The gating decision mirrors an emerging norm — frontier cyber capability shipped under allowlist rather than open API. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/
