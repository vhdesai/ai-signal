# Nscale secures $3.36B in pre-IPO convertible financing ahead of NYSE listing

The British neocloud closed a $3.36B convertible loan note round led by Third Point, with $2.36B available immediately and a further $1B from Nvidia expected in mid-November; Apollo, Citadel, Hudson Bay Capital and Abu Dhabi Investment Council also participated. Notes convert automatically on IPO completion. Nscale filed its IPO paperwork a week earlier, is expected to be valued near $35B, and reports over $103B in total contracted value — roughly 85% of which sits with just two customers, Microsoft ($43.8B through 2033) and Anthropic ($44.6B). The concentration risk is the story: Anthropic's agreement is contingent on Nscale hitting financing and build milestones the filing itself calls "stringent."
