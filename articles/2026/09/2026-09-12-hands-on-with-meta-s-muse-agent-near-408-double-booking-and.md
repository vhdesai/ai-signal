# Hands-on with Meta's Muse agent: near-$408 double-booking and login snarls

The Information's Abram Brown reviewed Meta's new Muse consumer agent and reported a near-$408 accidental Marriott double-booking, a broken login-code flow that forced creation of a second account, and slower-than-manual completion of even simple Resy and Uber tasks. Instinct, the invite-only rival, was contrasted as similarly hyped but currently rate-limited by compute — Instinct is now raising at a rumored ~$10B valuation, up from $2.5B a month ago. Brown's takeaway: agents will succeed as embedded features inside existing apps and OSes, not as stand-alone consumer surfaces, until reliability improves dramatically.
