# SpaceX Overhauls xAI Data-Center Build-Out — Reliability Over Speed

A new team of rocket engineers Elon Musk installed to run xAI's data-center program is shifting away from his "move fast" build cadence toward more redundant power and cooling, pre-commissioning testing, and less reliance on temporary systems. The change may slow the pace of new campus openings after outages plagued the first fast-built sites. The pivot is a concrete signal that the merchant-scale AI infrastructure race is entering a reliability phase — a shift enterprise buyers of neocloud capacity should price into procurement.
