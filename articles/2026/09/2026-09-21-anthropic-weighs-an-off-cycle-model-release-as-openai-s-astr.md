# Anthropic Weighs an Off-Cycle Model Release as OpenAI's Astra Gains Enterprise Share

Anthropic is discussing a new model release ahead of its expected IPO, according to three people familiar with the matter, as OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra picks up enterprise spend and developer traffic. Ramp data puts Astra at roughly 13% of tracked enterprise AI spending versus about 8% for Claude Fable, and OpenAI surpassed Anthropic in OpenRouter spending last week for the first time in more than two and a half years. Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate had passed $65 billion by end of July against roughly $40 billion for OpenAI, and investors argue enterprise switching costs will slow any displacement. Meta, one of Anthropic's largest customers, is reportedly looking to reduce its use of Anthropic models. FUNDING
