# Trending French finance minister warns Europe cannot rest sovereignty on Mistral alone

France’s Finance Minister Roland Lescure said European AI sovereignty cannot depend on a single startup such as Mistral. The remarks surface an uncomfortable tension in EU industrial policy, where national-champion strategy collides with the scale of US and Chinese incumbents. Expect this framing to shape upcoming EU funding and procurement debates.
