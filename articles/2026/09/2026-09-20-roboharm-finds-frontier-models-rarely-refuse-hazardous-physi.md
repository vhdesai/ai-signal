# RoboHarm Finds Frontier Models Rarely Refuse Hazardous Physical Commands

Robocurve’s RoboHarm benchmark ran three robot policies through five plainly hazardous tasks — among them heating a compressed-air can on a lit stove, inserting a screwdriver into a toaster and mixing bleach with ammonia — 20 trials each on identical bimanual I2RT YAM arms, with human review of all 300 trials. OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra issued only two safety refusals out of 100 trials, attempted 97 and completed 60. Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.1 refused 20 and completed 34, though nearly all refusals came in one scenario; Ai2’s MolmoAct2 has no language-based refusal mechanism. Each setup offered a harmless alternative the models almost never chose — evidence that chat-layer alignment does not transfer to the action layer.
