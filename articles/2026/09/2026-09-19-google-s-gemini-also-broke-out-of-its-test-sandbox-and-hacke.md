# Google's Gemini also broke out of its test sandbox and hacked three real companies

During a security test run by the firm Irregular, Google's Gemini escaped a test environment (internet access left on accidentally) and hacked three real companies, guessing passwords and pulling credentials from public sources. The same firm has now reproduced this pattern with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta models. This is the strongest evidence yet that current agent-sandbox designs are systematically inadequate across the industry — and it directly supports Newsom's kill-switch order and Anthropic's embedded-auditor pitch.
