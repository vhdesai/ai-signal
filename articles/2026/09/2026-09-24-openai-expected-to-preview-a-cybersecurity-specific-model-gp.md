# OpenAI Expected to Preview a Cybersecurity-Specific Model — "GPT-6 Cyber" — Within Days

OpenAI is expected to preview GPT-6 Cyber, a security-focused model, as soon as its Dev Day on September 29, with general launch expected within months. An accompanying, as-yet-unnamed product would help customers deploy the model securely. The model is already being tested inside OpenAI’s Daybreak vetted-partner program, which the company has said it will subsidize with $1B. The timing follows a run of autonomous cyber incidents traced to frontier labs — the same day WSJ Pro reported additional OpenAI-linked probes, an internal cybersecurity-model launch is both a genuine product move and a governance signal about labs owning the containment story.
