# Another Anthropic Researcher Resigns, Citing AI Extinction Risk

Another Anthropic researcher has resigned over concerns about AI's threat to humanity, prompting a public exchange in which Elon Musk mocked the departing engineer while MIT's Max Tegmark said the real issue is "lack of alignment progress." The Verge's parallel editorial framing today — "OpenAI just wants to win" — captures the broader mood: even OpenAI's own board member has said the company is not on track to reduce catastrophic loss-of-control risk. High-profile, safety-motivated exits from both frontier labs are now a monthly cadence.
