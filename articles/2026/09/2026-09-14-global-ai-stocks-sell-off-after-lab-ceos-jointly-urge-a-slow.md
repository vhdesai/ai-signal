# Global AI Stocks Sell Off After Lab CEOs Jointly Urge a Slowdown

AI-linked equities fell worldwide on Monday after Amodei's call to slow frontier development drew same-day endorsements from Altman and Musk. In Asia, SK Hynix closed down more than 6%, Samsung Electronics more than 4%, and SoftBank — a major OpenAI backer — fell 10%; in Europe ASML dropped more than 5% and Infineon more than 7%. In US premarket trading, “Memory chipmaker Micron was down around 5%, Intel dropped nearly 6%, while Nvidia was nearly 3% lower,” with Microsoft, Amazon and Alphabet only slightly lower and Nasdaq-100 futures off more than 1.5%. Reuters framed it as the starkest threat yet to the AI trade; the asymmetry — memory and semicap hit hardest, hyperscalers largely spared — indicates markets priced this as a capex risk to suppliers, not an earnings risk to AI consumers.
