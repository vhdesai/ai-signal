# Anthropic’s $518B Buildout Rests Largely on Non-Cancelable Deals, IPO Filing Shows

Anthropic’s confidential IPO prospectus discloses at least $518 billion of decade-long compute commitments across six partners, roughly 80% of it non-cancelable: $111.1B with Google, $110B with Amazon, $31.4B with Microsoft and about $161.2B in Broadcom equipment leases. It further discloses up to $84.5B of xAI-supplied Nvidia capacity through 2029 and an AMD arrangement including up to $5B of Anthropic stock purchases.
