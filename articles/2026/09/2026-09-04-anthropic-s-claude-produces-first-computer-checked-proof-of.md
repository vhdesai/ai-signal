# Anthropic's Claude produces first computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem

Anthropic said Claude, working largely autonomously over 11 days, formalized a proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in the Lean language — roughly 13 million lines of code and about 29,500 intermediate theorems — using the Prove2Me collaborative platform. The result was shared with the mathematics community on September 4 and described as an extraordinary autoformalization achievement. It is positioned as a step toward automatically verifying AI-generated mathematics at scale.
