# Anthropic Warns China’s GLM-5.3 Has "Elite Hacking Ability" — FBI and CISA Reportedly Review

SCMP reports Anthropic formally warned that Zhipu’s GLM-5.3 demonstrates elite-tier offensive-cyber capability comparable to top red-team performers. FBI and CISA are reportedly reviewing. The claim adds urgency to today’s DeepSeek-Huawei CUDA-alternative stack announcement and materially reframes the export-control debate: the binding concern is now capability parity, not just chip access. Combined with Anthropic’s Zero Trust for agents paper (excerpted in WSJ’s Zscaler placement today), it is the sharpest current articulation of the "offense outrunning defense" thesis in commercial-grade AI.
