# Alibaba unveils Zhenwu V900 accelerator and targets 20GW of cloud capacity by 2032

At its Apsara Conference in Hangzhou, Alibaba introduced the Zhenwu V900 from its T-Head unit, claiming three times the performance of the M890 released in May, 216GB of memory, and clustering up to 500,000 units for frontier-model training. CEO Eddie Wu set a target of more than 20 gigawatts of global Alibaba Cloud data-center capacity by 2032 and confirmed Qwen 4 is in training, with later series planned at 5–10 trillion parameters. Mass production is slated for Q1 2027, and none of the performance claims have been independently verified. Wu also conceded supply constraints: "the industry's mid-to-long-term demand far outpaces our supply capabilities." MARKETS
