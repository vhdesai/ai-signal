# How reliable are AI writing detectors? The same text scored “very likely human” and “100% AI”

Prompted by allegations that novelist Thelyson Orelien used AI tools, AFP ran identical text through multiple detectors and got verdicts ranging from “very likely human” to “100% AI-generated.” The piece draws on the University of Chicago paper “Artificial Writing and Automated Detection,” which found the detector Pangram achieved near-zero error rates on medium-to-long excerpts, and on CNRS researcher Thierry Poibeau, who explains detectors hunt linguistic tics such as triadic phrasing and em dashes. Poibeau flags a “moving target problem” — models evolve faster than detectors — and warns the repetitive patterns a detector flags may simply be an author’s style.
