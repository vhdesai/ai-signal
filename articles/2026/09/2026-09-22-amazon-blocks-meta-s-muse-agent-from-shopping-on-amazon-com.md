# Amazon blocks Meta's Muse agent from shopping on Amazon.com

Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent from making purchases on its storefront, citing unauthorized bot access. The block follows Shopify wiring Muse into Shop Pay checkout across its merchant base, setting up a direct contrast between platforms that welcome third-party agents and those that do not. It is an early flashpoint in what will likely become a multi-year fight over whether browser agents may transact on major retail platforms and who captures the economics when they do.
