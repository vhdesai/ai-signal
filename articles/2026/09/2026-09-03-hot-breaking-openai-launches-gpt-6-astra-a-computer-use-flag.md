# Hot Breaking OpenAI launches GPT-6 “Astra,” a computer-use flagship with ~1M-token context

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra positioned as an agentic “computer-use” model rather than a conversational upgrade, citing roughly a 1.05M-token context window and 72.6% on OSWorld V2. The company said the model is rolling out first to a limited set of organizations before reaching ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users. The launch ships with additional cybersecurity safeguards, consistent with earlier disclosures that Astra crossed OpenAI’s critical cyber capability threshold. For enterprises, the strategic shift is that the frontier benchmark is now task completion on real software, not chat quality.
