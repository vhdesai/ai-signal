# Meta's Iris AI Accelerator (MTIA Gen 3) Enters Mass Production at TSMC 3nm

Iris, the third-generation part in Meta's MTIA program, has entered mass production at TSMC's 3nm node after clearing a six-week bug-testing phase without significant defects. Co-designed with Broadcom under a multi-generation roadmap extending through 2029, Iris is Meta's first MTIA silicon built for both high-frequency inference and large training runs. It does not displace Meta's H100/B200 fleet but reshapes the compute mix as Meta targets 7GW of compute online this year and 14GW by 2027, with 2026 AI-infrastructure spending projected as high as $145B.
