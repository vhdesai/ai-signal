# Nscale seeks about $3.5 billion in pre-IPO financing

British AI infrastructure provider Nscale is reportedly seeking $3.5 billion in pre-IPO financing, including $1.5 billion in convertible notes and $2 billion from Nvidia. The company has recently signed a large compute deal with Anthropic and has been discussing an IPO as soon as this month. The story reinforces how quickly AI compute demand is being converted into debt, equity, and supplier-linked financing structures. EDGE AI INFERENCE
