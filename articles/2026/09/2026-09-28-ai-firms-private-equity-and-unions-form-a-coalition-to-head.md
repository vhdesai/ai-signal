# AI firms, private equity and unions form a coalition to head off data-center moratoriums

A new multimillion-dollar group, the American Infrastructure Alliance, unites Blackstone, OpenAI, QTS, SoftBank and unions including the IBEW to set standards and guardrails for data centers with state and local officials in 2027. Initial campaigns target Texas, Georgia, Ohio, Iowa, Pennsylvania, Indiana and South Carolina — a pre-emptive move against construction moratoriums after data centers became a flashpoint in the 2026 midterms. Ohio polling cited by Axios shows roughly two-thirds view data centers unfavorably, with support rising where they deliver low electricity costs, jobs and tax revenue.
