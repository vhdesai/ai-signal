# Cerebras and Gimlet Labs plan 100 MW of inference capacity targeting 3,000 tokens per second

Cerebras (NASDAQ: CBRS) and Gimlet Labs announced a collaboration bringing Cerebras wafer-scale compute into Gimlet Cloud as a disaggregated inference platform, with Gimlet stating a plan to deploy 100 megawatts of Cerebras-powered capacity. Gimlet’s software splits inference into phases — prefill, decode, attention, feed-forward — and schedules each on the silicon best suited to it. The stated ceiling of up to 3,000 tokens per second is a company target, not a published benchmark, with no model, batch size or measurement method disclosed. The first datacenter is expected later in 2026, with CS-4 customer access planned for 2027. Academic Research RESEARCH
