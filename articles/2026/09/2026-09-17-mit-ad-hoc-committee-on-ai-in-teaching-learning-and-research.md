# MIT Ad Hoc Committee on AI in Teaching, Learning, and Research Training delivers final report

MIT's Ad Hoc Committee on AI Use in Teaching, Learning, and Research Training, co-chaired by Eric Klopfer and Sam Madden, delivered its final report. Community surveys found nearly 80% believe AI skills will be necessary for their careers, while 48% worried about the cognitive consequences of relying on AI and only 25% of undergraduates felt MIT was preparing them to use AI professionally. The committee recommends a standardized menu of four course AI-use policies and cautions that investing in automated AI-cheating detection risks an arms race. - https://thetech.com/2026/09/17/ai-committee-report
