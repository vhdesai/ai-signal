# OpenAI says 80–90 percent of its research already targets GPT-7 and beyond

Boris Power, OpenAI's Head of Applied Research, said 80 to 90 percent of the company's research effort is directed at GPT-7, GPT-8 and later generations, because that is where "most of the value" sits. Within-generation updates draw on specialized training data and are treated internally as deliberate short-term bets — useful for fast iteration, but described as "extremely shortsighted" as strategy. Power also argued the binding constraint on AI assistants today is onboarding rather than model quality: most users do not know what the tools can do. ENTERPRISE
