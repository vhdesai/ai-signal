# DeepSeek turns to Huawei for 160,000 Ascend AI chips as Nvidia stays locked out of China

DeepSeek plans to install at least 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT chips at a new ~1GW data center in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, according to people familiar with the plan. Training reportedly remains on Nvidia hardware, with the Ascend fleet aimed at inference capacity. The commitment underscores China's accelerating shift toward domestic accelerators under US export restrictions.
