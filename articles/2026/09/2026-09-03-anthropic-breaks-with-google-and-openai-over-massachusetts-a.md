# Anthropic breaks with Google and OpenAI over Massachusetts AI safety bill

Anthropic is supporting a Massachusetts Senate proposal requiring major AI developers to hire independent evaluators to assess catastrophic risks every four months, while Google and OpenAI oppose the measure. The split shows frontier labs no longer aligned on state-level AI safety regulation.
