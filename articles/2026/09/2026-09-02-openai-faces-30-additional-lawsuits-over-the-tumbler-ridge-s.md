# OpenAI faces 30 additional lawsuits over the Tumbler Ridge shooting

Law firm Edelson PC is filing 30 new complaints against OpenAI this week, adding to seven filed in April on behalf of victims and families connected to the Tumbler Ridge mass shooting. New plaintiffs include teachers, a principal and students present during the attack but not physically shot. For the first time the filings allege aiding and abetting rather than negligent failure to prevent — a claim requiring proof of intent that is likely to face early dismissal challenges, but which materially raises the liability ceiling for model providers. finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-faces-30-more-lawsuits-120942562.html
