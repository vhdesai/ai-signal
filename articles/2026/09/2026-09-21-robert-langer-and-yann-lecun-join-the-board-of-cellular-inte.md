# Robert Langer and Yann LeCun join the board of Cellular Intelligence

MIT’s Robert Langer, the Moderna co-founder, and Yann LeCun — formerly Meta’s chief AI scientist, now at AMI Labs and NYU — have joined the board of Cellular Intelligence, which is applying machine learning to a Parkinson’s therapeutic program. It is a marker of senior academic AI talent moving into AI-for-biology translation, the commercialization counterpart to the UC San Diego and Adelaide work above.
