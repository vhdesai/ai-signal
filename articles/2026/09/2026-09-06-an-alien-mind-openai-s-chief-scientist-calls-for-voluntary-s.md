# “An Alien Mind”: OpenAI’s Chief Scientist calls for voluntary slowdowns and third-party safety audits

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published an essay arguing that current progress could sustain into recursive self-improvement, and that “no lab has solved alignment and monitoring to a sufficient degree to continue responsibly scaling at maximum speed for much longer.” He states that chain-of-thought monitoring — OpenAI’s primary safety bet — is becoming less reliable as models grow more capable. The essay calls for voluntary slowdowns, shared safety bars enforced by third-party auditors and governments, and international coordination on frontier systems. A frontier lab’s own chief scientist arguing for external enforcement is a material shift in the governance debate.
