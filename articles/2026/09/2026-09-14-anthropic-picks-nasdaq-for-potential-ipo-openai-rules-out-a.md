# Anthropic Picks Nasdaq for Potential IPO; OpenAI Rules Out a 2026 Listing

Anthropic selected Nasdaq as its listing venue, targeting a reported mid-October IPO at a ~$2T valuation with NVIDIA reportedly in talks to invest up to $10B as an anchor. Sam Altman separately called an OpenAI 2026 listing "ill-advised," pushing the most anticipated tech listing of the year into at least 2027. Anthropic told investors it will book a second consecutive adjusted-profitable quarter — a metric that excludes stock-based comp and is not GAAP profitability.
