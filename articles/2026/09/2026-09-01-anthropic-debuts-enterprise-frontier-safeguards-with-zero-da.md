# Anthropic debuts Enterprise Frontier Safeguards with zero data retention

Alongside the model release, Anthropic published Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, a framework developed with customers that lets organizations keep prompt and output data inside their own cloud infrastructure with no retention by Anthropic. It pairs with recalibrated safeguards intended to permit legitimate vulnerability discovery without enabling exploit development. The framework is a direct response to enterprise objections over data residency and over-refusal. It sharpens the contract-level comparison against OpenAI and Google enterprise tiers. https://www.anthropic.com/news
