# Google Opens Gemini Skills to All Free Account Users

Following confusion over the Gems sunset, Google dropped the AI Pro/Ultra paywall on Gemini Skills, letting free-tier users create and run their own Skills. The change answers the open question of what free Gems users receive after the November 17 migration.
