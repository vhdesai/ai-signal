# Huawei ships Kirin 9050 Pro built on its Tau Scaling Law architecture

Huawei unveiled the Kirin 9050 Pro, its first mobile processor built on the company's proprietary Tau Scaling Law architecture, which vertically stacks logic circuits to boost performance without advanced foreign lithography. The chip debuts in a new trifold smartphone announced Monday in Guangzhou. It follows a Huawei research paper from the prior day arguing the same architecture solves the thermal ceiling that analysts had flagged as its primary risk — putting Huawei materially closer to a domestic-only advanced-chip supply chain.
