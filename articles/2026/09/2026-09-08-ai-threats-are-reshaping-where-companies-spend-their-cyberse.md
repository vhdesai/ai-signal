# AI threats are reshaping where companies spend their cybersecurity budgets

Corporate cybersecurity buyers have significantly raised planned spending on employee-AI-use monitoring, AI models that detect vulnerabilities before hackers, and AI-powered patching tools, security executives told The Information. Spend on models from Anthropic and OpenAI for defensive workflows is up, while traditional penetration testing and legacy software face reduced budget allocation. The story quantifies how the AI hacking threat is remaking security procurement priorities, not just growing them. URL: The Information search
