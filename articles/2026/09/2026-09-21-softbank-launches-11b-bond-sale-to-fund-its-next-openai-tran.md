# SoftBank Launches $11B Bond Sale to Fund Its Next OpenAI Tranche

SoftBank Group launched $10 billion of dollar-denominated senior unsecured notes across 3.5-, 5.5- and 7.5-year tenors plus €1 billion of euro notes, with proceeds earmarked for its $10 billion third-tranche payment into OpenAI closing October 1. The offering would be the largest non-financial corporate bond deal on record from Asia-Pacific and Japan if completed at size, surpassing 7-Eleven's $10.93 billion 2021 issuance. Fitch assigned the notes BB+ and expects SoftBank leverage to rise as it funds committed investments. The deal also cancels a $10 billion bridge facility raised for the same purpose, taking cumulative OpenAI investment toward roughly $64.6 billion for about a 13% stake. FUNDING
