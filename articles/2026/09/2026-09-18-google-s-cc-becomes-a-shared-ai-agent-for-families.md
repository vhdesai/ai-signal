# Google's “CC” Becomes a Shared AI Agent for Families

Google Labs refocused its CC agent on household coordination for up to six family members, giving the agent its own verified Google account and permissions model. CC produces a shared “Your Day Ahead” brief, updates Calendar and Tasks, fills out permission-slip PDFs, builds shopping lists and plans meals. Each instance runs in an isolated cloud environment on Google's Antigravity agent harness and current Gemini models; availability is US-only, 18+, via waitlist. The design precedent that matters is the agent holding its own identity rather than borrowing a user's credentials.
