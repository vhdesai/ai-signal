# Frontier capability and frontier cost moved on the same day

The last 24 hours delivered a rare convergence of frontier-model releases and hard safety disclosure. Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 with materially lower agentic pricing, while OpenAI disclosed that its forthcoming Astra model is the first to cross the 'Critical' cybersecurity threshold under its Preparedness Framework — a capability admission with direct implications for enterprise security posture and procurement diligence. In parallel, compute contracting escalated again (Anthropic–Lambda, $35B) and regulators moved: Brussels placed ChatGPT under its strictest platform regime, and the Pentagon put ChatGPT and Grok in front of millions of users. For technology leaders, the signal is that capability, cost and control are now moving on the same clock.
