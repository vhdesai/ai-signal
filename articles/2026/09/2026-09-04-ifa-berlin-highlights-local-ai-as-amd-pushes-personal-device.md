# IFA Berlin highlights local AI as AMD pushes personal-device inference

Tech Times reported that IFA Berlin opened with AI-centered product messaging, including AMD's push for chips capable of running very large models locally. The broader signal is that edge AI is moving from assistant branding into hardware differentiation, with vendors arguing that latency, privacy, and cost can improve when some inference shifts from cloud to device. The business question is whether consumer and enterprise buyers will value local AI enough to reshape refresh cycles.
