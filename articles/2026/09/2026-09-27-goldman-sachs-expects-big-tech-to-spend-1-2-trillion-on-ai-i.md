# Goldman Sachs expects Big Tech to spend $1.2 trillion on AI infrastructure in 2027

Goldman Sachs projects that Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft, Oracle and Meta will spend a combined $1.2 trillion on AI infrastructure in 2027 — more than 50 percent above the roughly $800 billion projected for this year, and above Wall Street's $1.1 trillion consensus. Growth decelerates after that, from roughly 100 percent in 2026 to 54 percent in 2027 and 12 percent in 2028. Goldman estimates the group needs about $300 billion in annual AI revenue simply to break even, and notes spending now exceeds operating cash generation, pushing more of the buildout onto debt.
