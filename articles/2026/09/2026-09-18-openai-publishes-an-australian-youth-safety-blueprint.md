# OpenAI Publishes an Australian Youth Safety Blueprint

OpenAI released a six-pillar policy roadmap for youth AI safety in Australia, covering AI literacy, age-appropriate safeguards, privacy-protective age assurance, crisis-support connections and parental controls. It references Australia's draft Online Safety Amendment (Digital Duty of Care) Bill 2026 and builds on ChatGPT for Teens, which began rolling out in Australia in August. This is a policymaker-facing document, not a product launch.
