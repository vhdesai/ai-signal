# NVIDIA and Palantir Ship a Sovereign AI Stack — With NVIDIA as Customer Zero

The companies announced a joint stack bringing NVIDIA Nemotron open models and cuOpt optimization into Palantir Foundry and AIP, grounded in the Palantir Ontology and deployed first inside NVIDIA's own supply chain. NVIDIA is using it for weekly materials allocation across the 1.3 million parts in each Vera Rubin rack, framed as minimizing "time of ownership" from wafer-out to first token. The pitch to regulated industries is data sovereignty: customers post-train open models on proprietary data and retain ownership of models and deployment environment.
