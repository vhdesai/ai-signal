# Agentic AI is shifting the pricing models CIOs rely on

CIO Dive reports that agentic AI is pushing vendors and buyers toward outcome-based billing, forcing technology leaders to revisit vendor-management strategies. The shift matters because agent value is increasingly measured by completed workflows rather than seats, tokens, or static software licenses. It also raises governance questions around measuring outcomes, allocating accountability, and preventing runaway costs.
