# xAI adds enterprise access, network, and audit controls to Grok

xAI is adding enterprise-grade access, network, and audit controls to Grok Bot, targeting business deployments where governance and traceability are prerequisites. The rollout includes a two-week free-access window for Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers. It signals xAI moving from consumer visibility toward commercial procurement readiness.
