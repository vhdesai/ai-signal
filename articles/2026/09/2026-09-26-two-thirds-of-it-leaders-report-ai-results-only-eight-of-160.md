# Two-thirds of IT leaders report AI results; only eight of 160 would call the CEO

Speaking with about 160 IT vice presidents in Las Vegas, Exponential View founder Azeem Azhar asked who could point to measurable AI results; roughly two-thirds indicated they could. When he narrowed the question to results worth interrupting the CEO's summer vacation, about eight remained. Azhar calls the overall investment picture "finely balanced," and flags a countervailing incentive: a Boston Consulting Group survey found roughly 70 percent of CEOs say AI success affects how they are perceived in their roles.
