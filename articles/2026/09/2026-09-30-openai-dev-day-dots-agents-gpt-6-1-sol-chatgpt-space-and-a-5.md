# OpenAI Dev Day: "Dots" Agents, GPT-6.1 Sol, ChatGPT Space, and a $500/Month Top-Tier Plan

OpenAI’s Dev Day headlined GPT-6.1 Sol, a new "always-on" agent runtime called Dots that lets developers stitch together modular skills (marketplace launch partner: DevRev), ChatGPT Space for coworker collaboration, and a new $500/month top-tier consumer plan. CEO Sam Altman outlined a three-part strategy focused on models, building, and distribution. Dots is initially gated to Enterprise, Business Premium, and ChatGPT Pro subscribers — explicitly not free like Meta’s Muse or Instinct. Axios called it OpenAI’s most ambitious release since GPT-5, and DealBook framed it as OpenAI’s formal entry into the consumer-agent market where Meta, SpaceXAI, and Instinct are already competing.
