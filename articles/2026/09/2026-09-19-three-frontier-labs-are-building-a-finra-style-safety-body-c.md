# Three Frontier Labs Are Building a FINRA-Style Safety Body — Cohere Calls It a Cartel

OpenAI’s Chris Lehane confirmed that OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind have coordinated on safety protocols aimed at an industry-funded, federally overseen body that would review models up to 30 days before release — an idea first floated by Demis Hassabis. Cohere CEO Aidan Gomez called the arrangement “a cartel by any other name,” and Meta, xAI and Nvidia publicly opposed new government-led regulation at Dreamforce. A competing legislative path, the FRONTIER Act (H.R.9925), would instead license independent verification organizations through NIST/CAISI. Read this alongside yesterday’s Anthropic–Accenture embedded-evaluator deal: the assurance layer is being built commercially before it is built statutorily.
