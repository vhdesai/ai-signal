# Meta Launches Muse for Small Business

Meta extended its Muse personal agent to small businesses with connectors for Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe and Zoom, plus Facebook and Instagram business accounts and Meta ad accounts. Pricing mirrors the consumer app — free with usage limits, subscription beyond. Meta states “nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval,” a notable framing given ongoing scrutiny of Muse’s permission scope.
