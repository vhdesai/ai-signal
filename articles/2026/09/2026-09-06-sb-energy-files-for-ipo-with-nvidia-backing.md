# SB Energy files for IPO with Nvidia backing

SB Energy has filed for an initial public offering with Nvidia as a backer, part of a broader pattern of Nvidia extending its balance sheet into adjacent energy and data-center infrastructure plays that support AI compute demand. Details on valuation and use of proceeds were not immediately available in open reporting.
