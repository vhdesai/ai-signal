# John Ternus becomes Apple CEO today as Tim Cook moves to executive chairman

The succession announced in April took effect today: hardware engineering chief John Ternus assumes the CEO role after Cook's roughly 15-year tenure, with Cook staying on as executive chairman. Ternus inherits a company widely viewed as behind peers in generative AI while dependent on partners for frontier model capability. His first public test is the September product event, where a foldable iPhone is expected. https://www.pocketgamer.biz/tim-cook-steps-down-as-apple-ceo-after-15-years/
