# iPronics raises $125M with NVIDIA participation for optical AI networking

Valencia-based iPronics raised $125M co-led by Maverick Silicon and Light Street Capital, with participation from NVIDIA, to scale its programmable photonic optical circuit switching for AI data centers. The round targets networking bottlenecks as clusters scale toward hundreds of thousands of accelerators and interconnect bandwidth becomes the binding constraint. The raise was corroborated by Unite.AI and MarketScreener on the same date.
