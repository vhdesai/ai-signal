# Google DeepMind's Dream-RSI lets agents self-improve by "dreaming" over past searches

DeepMind published Dream-RSI, which lets AI agents improve their search strategies by replaying past attempts against a stored search tree rather than running expensive new live searches. Tested with Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3.7 Flash on statistics, math optimization, and GPU-kernel generation, Dream-RSI matched performance with up to 2.43× fewer generations or delivered 2.09× higher performance within the same budget. It's a striking practical result for recursive self-improvement without changing the underlying model — and directly relevant to Noam Brown's stated OpenAI "top priority" of automating AI research.
