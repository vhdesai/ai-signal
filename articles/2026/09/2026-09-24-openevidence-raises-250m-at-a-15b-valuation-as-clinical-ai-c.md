# OpenEvidence Raises $250M at a $15B Valuation as Clinical AI Consolidates

OpenEvidence — the clinical search platform used daily by roughly 40% of US physicians — closed a $250M round led by hospital systems and Andreessen Horowitz, lifting its valuation 25% above the $12B mark set in January. Reporting indicates the founders are fielding acquisition interest and would consider deals that include access to compute. The company has raised more than $1B in twelve months, and this week announced a partnership with Anthropic covering nearly 100 low- and middle-income countries.
