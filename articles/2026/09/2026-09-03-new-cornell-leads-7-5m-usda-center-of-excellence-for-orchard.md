# New Cornell leads $7.5M USDA Center of Excellence for Orchard Robotics

Cornell received a four-year, $7.5 million USDA grant to establish a Center of Excellence for Orchard Robotics, with Carnegie Mellon and Stanford participating. The center will develop perception and manipulation systems for automated fruit production. It reflects continued federal investment in applied embodied AI where labor constraints create clear economic pull.
