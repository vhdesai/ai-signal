# Data-center backlash becomes a midterm political issue

NPR reported that voter frustration with data centers is becoming a political issue for both parties ahead of the midterms. The relevance to AI is direct: data-center expansion underpins frontier model training, inference capacity, and enterprise AI adoption, but local opposition around power, water, land use, and noise can slow deployment. Executives should treat infrastructure permitting and community acceptance as material dependencies in AI capacity plans.
