# Google tests direct purchasing from Flipkart inside Gemini and AI Mode in India

Google has begun testing a “Buy” button on select Walmart-owned Flipkart listings surfaced in Gemini and AI Mode, sending users into a Flipkart-branded checkout without leaving the AI interface. The test is limited to some users and a narrow set of products — smartphones, electronics and mobile accessories — with a broader rollout planned for October, ahead of India's festive shopping season. The move extends Google's Universal Commerce Protocol work and puts it in direct competition with OpenAI's commerce push, shifting assistants from recommendation to transaction.
