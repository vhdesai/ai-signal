# Anthropic seeks Palantir-style voting control for its seven co-founders ahead of IPO

Anthropic is asking shareholders to approve a new corporate structure that would give CEO Dario Amodei and his six co-founders a special class of shares carrying a combined 50.1% of voting power — as long as three of the seven retain a minimum number of shares. The Long-Term Benefit Trust will still elect most board members, and employees get special stock for tie-breaker votes. It emulates Palantir's founder-control structure and strengthens the co-founders against public-shareholder pressure ahead of the IPO, cementing safety-focused governance despite the private-market compute obligations. It also lands the same week Reuters reports Anthropic annual revenue crossed $100B pace and while its IPO is now targeting late Oct/early Nov.
