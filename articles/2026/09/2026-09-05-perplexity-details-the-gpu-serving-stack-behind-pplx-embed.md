# Perplexity details the GPU serving stack behind pplx-embed

Perplexity published an engineering account of Ivy, Tulip, and ROSE — the three components that serve its pplx-embed retrieval model — covering CUDA graph capture, LazyTensor overlap, and comparative vLLM benchmarks. The disclosure is unusually specific about where embedding-serving cost accumulates at index scale. It is a useful reference for teams whose retrieval spend is growing faster than their inference spend.
