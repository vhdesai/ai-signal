# Alibaba's Qwen3.8-LiveTranslate Cuts Real-Time Interpretation Lag to 2.3 Seconds Across 60 Languages

Qwen released Qwen3.8-LiveTranslate, a simultaneous interpretation model built on a new "Interleave" architecture that reduces length-adaptive average lagging from 2.8 to 2.3 seconds — roughly an 18% cut. It understands 60 languages and speaks 29, and adds real-time speaker diarization, synchronized bilingual display, and long-context term disambiguation. Access is API-only via Alibaba Cloud Model Studio and QwenCloud; roughly one hour of speech in and out prices at about $1.54 in the Singapore region. Function calling, structured outputs, and fine-tuning are unsupported, which limits it to narrow interpretation workloads for now.
