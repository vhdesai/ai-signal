# Amazon, Meta, and Google risk losing multibillion-dollar data-center tax deals as states rip up terms

Several US states that granted long-dated tax exemptions to attract hyperscale data centers are now revisiting or clawing back the deals amid rising local backlash over power draw, water use, and job counts, per WSJ Wealth Adviser and Markets A.M. reporting. Amazon, Meta, and Google are the most exposed. The rethink lands as aggregate hyperscaler capex from the top five is tracking around $800 billion this year and is projected above $1 trillion annually for the next four years — making state-level fiscal concessions a fresh political flashpoint for AI infrastructure siting, and a new item on the risk register for capex-heavy vendors.
