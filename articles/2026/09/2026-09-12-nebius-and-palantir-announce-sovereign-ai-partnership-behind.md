# Nebius and Palantir Announce Sovereign-AI Partnership Behind a $250B Capacity Ambition

Nebius and Palantir announced a sovereign-AI partnership, with CEO Arkady Volozh putting the capital requirement for 5 GW of AI capacity through 2030 at roughly $250 billion. The pairing places Palantir’s government-facing software layer on top of a neocloud compute base aimed at national-scale deployments. Sovereign AI continues to be the channel through which non-hyperscaler compute providers reach regulated buyers. Single in-window source; the capacity figure is a company ambition, not a committed program.
