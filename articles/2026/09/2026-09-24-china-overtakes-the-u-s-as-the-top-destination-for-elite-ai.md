# China overtakes the U.S. as the top destination for elite AI talent

A Carnegie China study using NeurIPS authorship found the share of top AI researchers working in China rose from 27.1% in 2022 to 40.6% in 2025, while the U.S. share fell from 46.4% to 34.2%. The authors attribute the shift to China's booming AI job market and tighter U.S. visa restrictions on Chinese STEM graduates; 69% of Chinese-origin researchers stayed home in 2025. The U.S. nonetheless remains the largest net magnet for global AI talent, adding 2,145 researchers in 2025.
