# Authors dispute publisher and agent claims on Anthropic’s $1.5B settlement

Authors entitled to $3,000 per pirated title under Anthropic’s $1.5B copyright settlement report that publishers are claiming payments on works whose rights reverted years ago, or claiming 100% where they are entitled to 50%. Literary agencies — which are not rightsholders — are reportedly also filing claims. The Authors Guild attributes the pattern to poor recordkeeping rather than bad faith, but the volume suggests systemic issues in claims administration. For enterprises watching AI-training liability, this is the first large-scale test of how such settlements are actually distributed.
