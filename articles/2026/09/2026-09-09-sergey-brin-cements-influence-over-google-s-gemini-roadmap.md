# Sergey Brin Cements Influence Over Google's Gemini Roadmap

Business Insider reports that Alphabet co-founder Sergey Brin has gained significant influence over Gemini's direction, working out of a microkitchen at Google's Mountain View headquarters after a leadership shake-up in which Jeff Dean departed and Demis Hassabis switched roles. The pattern — founder returns to hands-on model work — echoes the way Anthropic and OpenAI have concentrated frontier-model decisions at their founder tier. For customers, expect continuity risk on Gemini's roadmap to hinge increasingly on a small number of principals.
