# Princeton Establishes Data and Intelligent Systems (DaIS) as a New Academic Unit

Princeton created DaIS, a new academic unit co-led by Tom Griffiths and Arthur Spirling that merges Princeton AI with Statistics and Data Science. Two initiatives launch this fall: AI Alignment and Safety, led by Elad Hazan, and Princeton Societal AI. The move signals that leading universities are now institutionalizing AI at the organizational-design level rather than through centers and affiliations — relevant to anyone building academic research partnerships or recruiting pipelines.
