# Palo Alto Networks paid $500 million for Thrive-backed Console, sources say

TechCrunch reports that Palo Alto Networks paid about $500 million for Console, an AI startup backed by Thrive. The transaction reinforces continued security-platform consolidation around AI-native workflows. It also suggests incumbents are willing to buy capabilities where generative AI changes analyst productivity, identity, telemetry, or incident-response interfaces.
