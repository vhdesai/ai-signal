# SpaceX signs another ~$1.11B/month compute rental deal, targets $100B annualized run rate

SpaceX CFO Bret Johnsen said at Goldman Sachs Communacopia that the company signed a new compute-rental deal at roughly $1.11 billion per month starting Dec. 1, and reiterated confidence in hitting its $100B annualized revenue target by year-end. Like almost all SpaceX compute deals, this contract is about six months long. It follows the previously disclosed $920M/month Google deal from June and the Anthropic rental deal from May, and further establishes xAI's data centers as a first-tier hyperscale compute vendor.
