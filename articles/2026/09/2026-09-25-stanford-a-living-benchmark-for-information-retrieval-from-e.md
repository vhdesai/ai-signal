# Stanford: a “living” benchmark for information retrieval from electronic health records

Stanford's Center for Biomedical Informatics Research (Nigam Shah, Emily Alsentzer, Jason Fries, Alison Callahan) released a clinician-authored benchmark for evaluating retrieval from real electronic health records, designed to be continuously updated rather than frozen at release. The large clinical author list indicates gold-standard retrieval targets drawn from actual chart-review workflows. It targets a known weak spot in LLM healthcare evaluation, where static benchmarks saturate quickly or leak into training data.
