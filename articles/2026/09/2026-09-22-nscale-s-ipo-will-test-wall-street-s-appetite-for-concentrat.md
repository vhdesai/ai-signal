# Nscale's IPO will test Wall Street's appetite for concentrated AI bets

British AI data-center developer Nscale filed to go public, with disclosures showing Microsoft and Anthropic collectively drive most of its revenue. The offering is the first neocloud IPO since a wave of infrastructure fundraising and puts customer-concentration risk squarely in front of public-market investors. Its reception will set the tone for planned follow-ons from CoreWeave-like peers.
