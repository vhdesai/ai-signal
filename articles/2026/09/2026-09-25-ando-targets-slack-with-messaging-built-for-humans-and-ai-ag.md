# Ando targets Slack with messaging built for humans and AI agents

TechCrunch reported that Ando is building a team-messaging app designed for humans and agents to work together in the same collaboration environment. The premise reflects a larger product shift: core workplace tools are being rebuilt around delegated agent work, shared context, and persistent task state. The adoption risk is whether teams can introduce agents into communication channels without creating audit, permissions, and notification noise problems.
