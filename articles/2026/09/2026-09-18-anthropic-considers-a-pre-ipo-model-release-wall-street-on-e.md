# Anthropic considers a pre-IPO model release; Wall Street on edge over waiting game

The Information reports Anthropic finance chief Krishna Rao and other executives met prospective public investors last month with numbers looking "spiffier than ever" — the company went from $2.30 of operating spend per $1 of revenue in spring 2025 to a slight operating profit in the June quarter — but told some investors those profits are unlikely to last as Anthropic aggressively signs new data-center deals with its cash reserves. IPO timing has slid to late October or November, and Reuters/Axios separately report Anthropic is considering releasing a new AI model ahead of the IPO and that revenue has reached a $100B run-rate pace. Anthropic is threading IPO prep, capex ramp, consumer expansion (Claude Money), scientific R&D (in-house biology lab), and a new flagship model simultaneously.
