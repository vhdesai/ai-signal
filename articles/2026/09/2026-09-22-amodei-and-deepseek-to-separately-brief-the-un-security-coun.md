# Amodei and DeepSeek to Separately Brief the UN Security Council on AI Risks This Week

Bloomberg reports Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei will brief the UN Security Council on AI risks this week, and Reuters exclusive reporting confirms DeepSeek will separately brief the same body. It is the first time two frontier labs from different countries have both addressed the Security Council on AI. The dual briefings follow Sunday's UN thematic brief on AI agents and misalignment; combined with the UK Parliament inviting OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and Google DeepMind to a hearing, formal multilateral AI governance has escalated meaningfully in a single week.
