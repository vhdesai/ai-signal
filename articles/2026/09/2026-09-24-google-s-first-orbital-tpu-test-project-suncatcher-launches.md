# Google’s First Orbital TPU Test (Project Suncatcher) Launches October 1 With Four Chips and 1 kW of Solar

Google confirmed that Project Suncatcher’s first in-orbit hardware test — a refrigerator-sized satellite named MVP built with Planet Labs — will launch October 1 on SpaceX’s Transporter-18 rideshare. It carries four Trillium TPUs drawing about 1 kW of solar power and will run Gemini workloads — but only in ~15-minute bursts, because radiative cooling cannot keep pace. Google reports the TPUs survived proton-beam testing at UC Davis above a five-year mission dose. Senior VP James Manyika was explicit that nothing usefully operational is expected for years — but it is the first serious signal that hyperscalers are hedging terrestrial power and permit constraints with orbital compute.
