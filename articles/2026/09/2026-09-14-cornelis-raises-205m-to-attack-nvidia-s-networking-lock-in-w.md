# Cornelis Raises $205M to Attack NVIDIA's Networking Lock-In With Active Compute Fabric

Cornelis, a 2020 Intel spinout, raised $205M led by IAG Capital Partners and launched Active Compute Fabric — networking that lets chips process and transmit data simultaneously to cut GPU idle time. Its open architecture supports mixed GPU and accelerator hardware, in contrast to NVIDIA's optimized full-stack lock-in.
