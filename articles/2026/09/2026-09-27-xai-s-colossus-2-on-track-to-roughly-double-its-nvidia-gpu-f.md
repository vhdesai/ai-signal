# xAI's Colossus 2 on Track to Roughly Double Its Nvidia GPU Fleet by Year-End

Elon Musk said Colossus 2 currently runs 110,000 GB200 and 440,000 GB300 chips, with 220,000 additional GB300s coming online imminently, another 220,000 in November and a possible further 220,000 in late December. That trajectory would surpass xAI's original one-million-GPU target for 2026; the initial 550,000 GPUs cost roughly $18 billion. Musk has said SpaceX will use Nvidia exclusively going forward, with the two companies also partnering on radiation-tolerant GPUs for planned orbital data centres.
