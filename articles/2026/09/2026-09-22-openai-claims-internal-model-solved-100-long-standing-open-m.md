# OpenAI Claims Internal Model Solved 100+ Long-Standing Open Math Problems in One Month

OpenAI announced that a new internal model — training only began August 28 — solved more than 100 long-standing open problems across most areas of mathematics, on top of a previously announced solution to the Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem. The company simultaneously launched an Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study, with Fields Medalist Timothy Gowers as a founding member; nine mathematicians named as initial members are unpaid and free to speak publicly, but OpenAI explicitly states the group "will not be responsible for advising us on how to pace our internal progress on mathematics." The advisory structure follows an open letter from 25 Fields Medalists on AI encroachment, and repeats the pattern of advisory boards scoped to exclude pacing decisions — a governance-shape point as important as the claim itself.
