# French finance minister warns Europe cannot rely on Mistral alone

Speaking in Silicon Valley, France's Finance Minister Roland Lescure warned that European AI sovereignty cannot rest on a single startup — "If European AI is just about Mistral, then we're doomed" — and called for a broader ecosystem amid US–China tensions. He cited the EU's Italian-German consortium building a public-supercomputer model and flagged the risk of the US restricting European access to top models, referencing a two-week Anthropic access cutoff for foreign nationals in June.
