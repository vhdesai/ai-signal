# Meta offers ~95% token discounts to users who share Muse Spark usage data

For its Muse Spark model, aimed at coding and agentic workflows, Meta has introduced a "contributor" pricing tier that cuts token costs by roughly 95% for customers who allow their usage to inform future model development. The move inverts the standard opt-out norm and prices interaction data explicitly. Enterprises should read this as a signal that real-world agent trajectories now carry more strategic value to model developers than subscription revenue — and treat contributor tiers as a data-governance decision, not a procurement discount. https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/meta-is-paying-to-peek-at-how-you-use-their-latest-ai-model/ PRODUCTPRIVACY
