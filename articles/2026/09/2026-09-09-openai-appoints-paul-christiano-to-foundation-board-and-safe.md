# OpenAI appoints Paul Christiano to Foundation Board and Safety and Security Committee

OpenAI named Paul Christiano — founder of the Alignment Research Center, an author of foundational RLHF work, and Senior Tech Advisor at NIST's Center for AI Standards and Innovation — to the OpenAI Foundation Board and its Safety and Security Committee, chaired by Zico Kolter. He will be a non-voting observer on the OpenAI Group PBC board and will recuse himself from OpenAI-related matters and model evaluations in his NIST role. The SSC holds authority over model release decisions, which makes the appointment operationally material rather than symbolic: a known critic of industry safeguards now sits on the body that gates frontier releases.
