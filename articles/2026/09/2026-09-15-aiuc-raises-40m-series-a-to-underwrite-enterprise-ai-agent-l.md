# AIUC Raises $40M Series A to Underwrite Enterprise AI-Agent Liability

Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company (AIUC), founded by an early Anthropic hire and a former COO of METR, raised a $40M Series A led by Ribbit Capital to insure and underwrite enterprise AI-agent deployments. The pitch is a market-based mechanism for AI safety: if actuaries can price rogue-agent liability, procurement teams can move it off the risk register.
