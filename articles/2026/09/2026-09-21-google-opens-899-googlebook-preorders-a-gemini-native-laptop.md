# Google Opens $899 Googlebook Preorders — A Gemini-Native Laptop Aimed at the Chromebook Base

Google opened preorders for the Googlebook at $899, an Android-OS AI laptop first unveiled in May that places Gemini at the center of the desktop experience. Headline features include "Magic Cursor," an AI pointer that pipes selections directly to Gemini, "Rambler" dictation cleanup, and vibe-coded widgets, bundled with twelve months of Google AI Pro. Devices ship October 4 in the US and October 5 in Canada, the UK, Ireland, France, Germany and Australia, built by Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP and Lenovo on Intel and Qualcomm silicon with dedicated NPUs. Google frames it as a long-term play to migrate roughly 50 million school Chromebooks onto Gemini — a direct competitive vector against Windows in education.
