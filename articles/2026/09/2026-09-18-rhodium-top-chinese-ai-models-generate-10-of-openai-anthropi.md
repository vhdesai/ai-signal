# Rhodium: top Chinese AI models generate ~10% of OpenAI + Anthropic revenue despite comparable valuations

Rhodium Group estimates the seven leading Chinese AI-model developers generated ~$10.7B in ARR between March and August 2026 versus more than $100B combined for OpenAI and Anthropic — roughly a 10:1 revenue gap. Valuations for Chinese labs have nonetheless kept climbing (Enflame IPO pop, Z.ai $5B raise, Moonshot dual listing). For investors and enterprise buyers, this puts hard numbers on the "Chinese AI labs are technically credible but commercially far behind" thesis and reframes IPO pricing risk. HARDWARE
