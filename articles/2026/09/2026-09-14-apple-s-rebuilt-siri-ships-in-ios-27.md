# Apple's rebuilt Siri ships in iOS 27

TechCrunch's hands-on with Apple's long-delayed Siri overhaul in iOS 27 finds the assistant materially more useful day to day. It's Apple's most substantive consumer AI push since Apple Intelligence debuted, and it arrives in the first weeks of the John Ternus CEO era. Third-party developers are already building on the new surfaces — fashion discovery app Daydream now turns saved outfit photos into shoppable results and supports product search through Siri without opening the app.
