# Anthropic Walks a Tightrope to Nasdaq: Preaching Restraint While Pursuing a ~$2 Trillion Valuation

“As Anthropic meets with prospective investors ahead of its potentially historic market debut, CEO Dario Amodei is pushing for a slowdown in AI.” CNBC examines the tension between Amodei's safety advocacy and a listing reportedly targeting a ~$2 trillion valuation, a combination critics read as regulatory positioning rather than principle. The available evidence does not distinguish sincerity from commercial self-interest — which is precisely why the objection will follow the company into its S-1.
