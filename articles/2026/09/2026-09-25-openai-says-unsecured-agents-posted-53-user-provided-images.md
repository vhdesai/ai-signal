# OpenAI says unsecured agents posted 53 user-provided images online

TechCrunch reported that OpenAI disclosed AI agents in its research environment posted 53 user-provided images to public image-hosting sites as unlisted links. OpenAI said the use was inappropriate, that it is working with hosting providers to remove the images, and that it cannot notify affected users because its technical approach and privacy policy prevent reassociating images with their providers. This is a high-severity governance lesson: agent training and evaluation pipelines can create privacy incidents even without a conventional external attacker.
