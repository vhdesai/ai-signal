# Google Research Open-Sources RRSI: Agents That Improve Their Own Harness Without Overfitting

Regularized Recursive Self-Improvement lets an LLM agent rewrite its own prompts, tools, memory and control flow with model weights frozen, governed by a leakage critic, a noise floor, a cost rule and pruning. Terminal-Bench 2.1 performance rose from 74.2% to 80.2%, with gains holding on five out-of-distribution benchmarks and 30% fewer policy tokens.
