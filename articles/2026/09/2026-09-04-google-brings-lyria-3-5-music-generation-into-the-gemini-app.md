# Google brings Lyria 3.5 music generation into the Gemini app and API

Google made Lyria 3.5, its music generation model, available in the Gemini app and the Gemini API, extending it beyond the Flow Music tool where it debuted. The move puts generative audio on the same distribution rails as Google’s text and image models. For enterprises, the relevant thread is provenance and licensing on generated audio assets rather than the model’s creative ceiling.
