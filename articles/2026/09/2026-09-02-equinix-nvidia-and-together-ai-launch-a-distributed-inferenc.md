# Equinix, Nvidia and Together AI Launch a Distributed Inference Exchange

Equinix announced Inference Exchange with Nvidia and Together AI, combining Nvidia enterprise reference architectures, Together AI's inference platform supporting 200+ open models, and Equinix's global data center footprint, with availability targeted for Q1 2027. The bet is that enterprises will distribute inference geographically for latency, cost, privacy and data-sovereignty reasons instead of centralizing it in hyperscale clouds.
