# Stanford/Caltech's HomeBody plugs GPT-6 Astra directly into a humanoid, has it tidy an unfamiliar kitchen

Stanford and Caltech researchers published HomeBody, a system that skips the specially trained low-level control policy that most humanoid stacks use and instead lets GPT-6 Astra call modular skills (grasping, navigation, door-opening) directly. In demos, the setup allowed a humanoid to clean up an unfamiliar kitchen it had never seen. It is the cleanest published version of the "frontier LLM as the whole planner-plus-controller" thesis and complicates the case for vertically-integrated robotics foundation models like Google's Gemini Robotics ER 2.
