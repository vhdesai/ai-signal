# Microsoft targets more than 38 gigawatts of data-center capacity by 2032

Microsoft plans to expand its global data-center footprint from roughly 12 gigawatts today to more than 38 gigawatts by 2032, per a Bloomberg report. Only about 2 gigawatts is AI-specific today; by 2032 AI is expected to be roughly one-third of the total. The buildout is a response to shortages that have already cost business — Temu moved a major cloud deal to Oracle, GitHub suffered an eight-hour outage in August tied to server constraints, and Xbox cloud gaming has been capacity-limited. Microsoft guides to about $50 billion of capex in fiscal Q1 2027 and is extending some leases from 15 to 25 years to smooth reported costs. ANALYSIS
