# Adobe is making its tools available in Slack

TechCrunch reports that Adobe is bringing its tools into Slack. While Adobe is outside the primary company watchlist, the item is relevant because it reflects a broader enterprise software pattern: AI and creative workflows are being embedded directly into collaboration surfaces. The competitive pressure is moving from standalone apps toward where work already happens.
