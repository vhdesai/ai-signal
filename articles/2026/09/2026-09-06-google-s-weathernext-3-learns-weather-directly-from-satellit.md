# Google's WeatherNext 3 learns weather directly from satellite data, skipping physics simulation

Google Research and DeepMind released WeatherNext 3, a forecasting model that learns weather patterns directly from real-time satellite data rather than relying on traditional physics-based simulation. The model produces hourly forecasts at up to five-kilometer resolution — five times more detailed than its predecessor — with Google saying the biggest accuracy gains will benefit regions in Africa, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific that have historically lacked precise forecasting. It's a concrete example of AI-first scientific modeling displacing legacy simulation approaches in a domain with direct economic and safety impact. Academic Research ACADEMIC
