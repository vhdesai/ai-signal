# Unsealed NYT Filings Show Microsoft and OpenAI Executives Privately Questioned AI Scraping

Newly unredacted material in The New York Times' three-year-old copyright suit quotes a January 2023 internal memo by Microsoft's director of applied science, Brent Hecht, describing AI training practices as “an astonishing theft of unprecedented proportions” and “the largest theft of labor in human history.” Microsoft's own data reportedly showed Copilot answers cutting click-through to the Times' domain by as much as 93% versus traditional Bing search. The filings allege OpenAI mid-training datasets held more than 91,692 copies of works from the Times, Daily News and Center for Investigative Reporting.
