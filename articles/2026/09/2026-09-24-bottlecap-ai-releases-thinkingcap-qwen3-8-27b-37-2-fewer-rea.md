# BottleCap AI Releases ThinkingCap-Qwen3.8-27B: 37.2% Fewer Reasoning Tokens for 0.86pp of Accuracy

A fine-tune of Qwen3.8-27B optimised purely for shorter reasoning traces: across 12 benchmarks it spends 37.2% fewer thinking tokens while macro-average accuracy moves from 86.65% to 85.79%. Long-context retrieval actually improved (AA-LCR up 2.25pp); competition math took the largest hit (AIME 2026 down 3.85pp). It ships as a drop-in vLLM/SGLang replacement with FP8, NVFP4, GGUF and MLX builds. Licensing is gated under PolyForm Small Business, which limits commercial reuse — but the result is a clean datapoint that reasoning-token spend is now an independently tunable cost lever.
