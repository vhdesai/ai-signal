# Google Appeals EU DMA Orders on Android AI Assistants and Search Data Sharing

Google filed two appeals at the EU General Court against July Digital Markets Act orders. The first would give rival AI assistants the same access as Gemini to 11 Android features, including voice activation and in-app actions, by August 2027; the second requires sharing anonymised search-and-click data with rival engines and AI chatbots from January 2027.
