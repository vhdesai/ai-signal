# Hikers rescued after following flawed Google Gemini trip-planning advice

A sheriff's office reported rescuing a hiking party that had been "advised by Gemini to bring far less food and water than their group required." The incident is minor in isolation but is a documented, attributable case of consumer-assistant advice producing physical-world harm, and it is already being cited in the wider debate over consumer AI reliability in safety-relevant contexts. Expect it to feature in arguments for stronger disclaimers and domain gating in general-purpose assistants.
