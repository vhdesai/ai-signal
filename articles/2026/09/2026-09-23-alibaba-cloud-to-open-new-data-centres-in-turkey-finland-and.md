# Alibaba Cloud to open new data centres in Turkey, Finland and the Netherlands

Alibaba Cloud will bring three new European regions online over the next 12 months, starting with the Netherlands in October, and expand capacity in Malaysia, Germany and the UAE. The buildout pairs with the Apsara Conference announcements of "China's most powerful" AI chip and a 10T-parameter model to pitch enterprise customers on full-stack AI. It also positions Alibaba to catch European regulatory tailwinds favoring non-US sovereign cloud options. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
