# US Senate Begins Drafting Duty-of-Care AI Bill With Model-Release Block and State Preemption

Senate negotiators are drafting legislation with two pillars: a "duty of care" letting the Commerce Secretary demand evidence of harm prevention and send auditors to test products directly, and a mechanism letting the government block release of models judged unsafe. That same court mechanism would preempt state AI laws covering certain risks such as nuclear or biological weapons uplift. Talks are led by Thune, Cruz, and Klobuchar, with roughly three voting weeks left before the November 3 midterms.
