# Obama urges Democrats to make AI a central agenda item

Former President Barack Obama told a Democratic fundraiser that the party needs "a very clear plan" on AI's economic and safety impacts, per The New York Times, and should build "a framework for a very public conversation" upon regaining the House. "This is something that is moving very fast in private hands, and if we don't get on top of it, I think can be dangerous," Obama said. He has reportedly offered himself as a sounding board to both Amodei and Altman. The remarks signal AI safety migrating from industry discourse into US electoral positioning.
