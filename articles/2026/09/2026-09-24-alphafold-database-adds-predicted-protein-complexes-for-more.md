# AlphaFold Database Adds Predicted Protein Complexes for More Than 2,800 Viruses

AI-predicted protein complex structures for over 2,800 viruses are now openly available in the AlphaFold Database, prioritising viral families known to infect humans. The dataset was produced with optimisation from NVIDIA’s BioNeMo Inference Runtime and announced at a CEPI/World Economic Forum roundtable, timed to the UN High-level Meeting on Pandemic Prevention. It is explicitly aimed at the “100 Days Mission” for rapid vaccine and therapeutic response. Strategically, it is one of the clearest examples yet of frontier-lab compute being channelled into open public-good biological infrastructure rather than a product.
