# Oracle Sends Force-Majeure Notice on Project Jupiter, Its Flagship Stargate Campus in New Mexico

Oracle sent a force-majeure notice to a Blue Owl Capital unit developing the 2.45-gigawatt Project Jupiter campus in Doña Ana County, preserving its ability to delay payments if the site misses its 2028 in-service date. Oracle says the project "remains on our planned schedule" and that such notices are routine at this scale; Blue Owl says financial commitments are unchanged. The site has faced repeated pipeline right-of-way denials from the New Mexico State Land Office, a pending air-quality permit with a November 23 decision deadline, and local opposition ahead of the midterms. The roughly $18B of associated project debt is trading below 90 cents on the dollar, and Oracle shares fell about 4%. It is the first visible crack in a marquee Stargate site — and it hits exactly the power-availability constraint Goldman flagged in the $1.2–1.4T 2027 hyperscaler capex trajectory.
