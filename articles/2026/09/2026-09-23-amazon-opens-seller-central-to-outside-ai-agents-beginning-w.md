# Amazon opens Seller Central to outside AI agents, beginning with Claude

At its Accelerate conference, Amazon launched a Selling Partner plugin that lets independent sellers check inventory, adjust prices and update listings from Anthropic's Claude (US beta) or Amazon's own Quick assistant without opening Seller Central. Seller Assistant — built on Amazon Bedrock with Claude models — gains persistent memory of each seller's pricing patterns and inventory cycles, plus background workflows that act only with seller approval and a full audit trail. Amazon says about 90% of sellers already use outside AI, and that seller fees generated $46.8B in Q2, more than AWS. Notably, Amazon opened this channel days after blocking Meta's Muse shopping agent — it is licensing agent access on its own terms, not conceding it.
