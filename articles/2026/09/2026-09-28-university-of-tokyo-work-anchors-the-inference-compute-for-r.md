# University of Tokyo work anchors the inference-compute-for-reliability thesis in robotics

The SAIL authors are Makoto Sato and Yusuke Iwasawa (University of Tokyo) with Yujin Tang and So Kuroki (Sakana AI), the work conducted during Sato's internship. The methodological claim matters beyond robotics: the LLM playbook of spending inference compute instead of retraining now shows measurable transfer to physical action, with search cost absorbed in simulation rather than on hardware. The paper publishes no wall-clock or compute-cost figures for the added search budget, which is the gating variable for any real control loop.
