# OpenAI engineer says internal Astra use pulled roadmap plans forward six months

OpenAI’s Thibault Sottiaux said GPT‑6 Astra was the company’s “biggest competitive advantage” while still internal-only, and that internal use accelerated parts of the roadmap by six months. The report ties this to an IAPS survey in which 20 of 25 frontier-lab researchers named automation of AI research itself as a top risk, and to Anthropic’s claim that Claude now writes more than 80% of its own production code. The compounding-returns argument for internal model deployment is becoming an explicit competitive thesis.
