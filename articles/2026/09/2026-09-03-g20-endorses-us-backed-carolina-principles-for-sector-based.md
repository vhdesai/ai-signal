# G20 Endorses US-Backed "Carolina Principles" for Sector-Based AI Rules

G20 nations endorsed a non-binding US-backed framework at the Chapel Hill technology gathering favoring sector-specific, use-based AI regulation over a universal regulatory regime or new AI-specific regulators. Attendees included Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Mark Zuckerberg and Elon Musk. US officials argued restrictive rules would cede ground to Chinese models; critics cited unresolved safety, labor, copyright and concentration concerns.
