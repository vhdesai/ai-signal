# Class Action Alleges Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI Illegally Coordinated an AI Slowdown

Four paying subscribers to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini filed suit Friday in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California, seeking to represent a nationwide class. The complaint argues that the coordinated pledge to decelerate capability development — anchored on Dario Amodei's September 12 "pacing the frontier" essay and same-day endorsements from Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis — is a horizontal restraint that reduces the value of paid subscriptions. "The antitrust laws do not permit competitors to decide among themselves that competition is too dangerous," the plaintiffs argue. Amodei had anticipated the exposure, writing that a narrow government waiver for safety discussions would help; none exists.
