# SoftBank launches $11B-equivalent junk bond sale to fund its next OpenAI tranche

SoftBank Group moved forward with $10B of dollar-denominated and €1B of euro-denominated senior unsecured notes, with proceeds earmarked for the ~$10B third tranche of its OpenAI follow-on investment, expected to close on or about October 1. The deal would rank among the largest speculative-grade corporate bond sales ever by a single issuer outside a distressed exchange, and would cancel an existing $10B bridge loan. SoftBank has now committed close to $65B to OpenAI, and its 2031 dollar bond yield has climbed to 8.2% from 6.7% in January. The financing structure is the story: an increasing share of frontier-AI capital formation is arriving as leveraged debt rather than equity.
