# Meta Connect: Muse gets video avatars, email addresses, Mac control, and dedicated hardware

Mark Zuckerberg used Connect 2026 to reposition Meta squarely around Muse. Updates include realistic video avatars, per-user email addresses that let people delegate work to Muse over email, a Mac control layer for direct system actions, camera-free AI glasses with up to 12-hour battery, and a Tamagotchi-style wearable that carries Muse away from the phone. Meta says Muse hit 500,000 users in its first week and topped the App Store — even as Meta concedes the product was "heavily inspired" by open-source OpenClaw.
