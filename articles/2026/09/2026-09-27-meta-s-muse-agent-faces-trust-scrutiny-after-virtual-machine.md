# Meta's Muse Agent Faces Trust Scrutiny After Virtual Machine Vulnerability

Meta's Muse agent — which handles shopping, travel booking, email and payments — topped US and Canadian free download charts with roughly 2.8 million downloads in its first two weeks, but is drawing scepticism tied to Meta's privacy record. A researcher-reported flaw, internally rated SEV-2, could have exposed a user's dedicated cloud virtual machine containing private files and email; Meta has added clearer safety warnings in response. Reviewers have also flagged the breadth of permissions Muse requests, including full disk access.
