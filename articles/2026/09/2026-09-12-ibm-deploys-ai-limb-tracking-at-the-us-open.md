# IBM Deploys AI Limb Tracking at the US Open

IBM has deployed AI limb-tracking at the US Open, scoring every tennis serve to give fans real-time biomechanical analytics. It is a high-visibility deployment for IBM Consulting's sports-AI stack, following watsonx-based commentary tie-ins. The launch is notable as a live-event, computer-vision showcase competing directly with sports-AI offerings from AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure — and a template IBM will likely re-run across other rights holders where legacy relationships give it a distribution edge.
