# OpenAI in early talks for a new funding round at $1.2T valuation

The Information reports OpenAI has held early conversations with investors about a new funding round that could lift its valuation to $1.2 trillion or higher, after CEO Sam Altman said the company will not IPO this year amid AI-safety concerns. The last announced round was $122B at an $852B valuation in March; Caplight secondary-market marks have since crossed $1T on Codex momentum and GPT-6 Astra benchmarks. Anthropic's planned IPO is reportedly targeting $1.5T or higher. The private-market alternative is now materially larger than any recent frontier AI raise.
