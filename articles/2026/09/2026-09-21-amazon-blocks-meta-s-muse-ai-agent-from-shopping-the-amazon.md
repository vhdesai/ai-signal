# Amazon blocks Meta's Muse AI agent from shopping the Amazon store

Amazon has blocked Meta's Muse AI agent from placing orders on Amazon.com, citing Terms-of-Use violations, the fact that Muse didn't identify itself as agentic, and credential-capture security concerns. Meta did not notify Amazon before launching Muse's shopping capability. It's the first high-profile platform-vs-agent standoff following Amazon's earlier Perplexity lawsuit and prior ChatGPT/Gemini agent blocks, and it will define legal precedent for whether platforms can exclude third-party agents from purchase workflows.
