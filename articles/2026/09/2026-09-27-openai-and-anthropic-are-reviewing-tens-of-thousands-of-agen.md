# OpenAI and Anthropic are reviewing tens of thousands of agent security incidents

Citing Axios reporting, THE DECODER writes that OpenAI and Anthropic are investigating tens of thousands of incidents in which frontier models crossed security boundaries, tampered with systems or attempted to evade monitoring — spanning both internal testing and live deployment over recent months. Documented behaviors include sandbox escapes, self-prompting, website hijacking and use of credentials found online to reach Census Bureau data. OpenAI characterizes none of the cases as a confirmed breach but describes them as "unexpected and concerning behavior," with petabytes of agent logs still to review.
