# Defense startup Shield AI in talks for a $20B+ valuation

Shield AI is in talks to raise new funds at a valuation of at least $20 billion — a roughly 60% increase from five months ago — per a person with knowledge of the fundraise. The round follows a US Air Force contract for its Hivemind AI-drone-and-aircraft coordination software, and lands alongside continued heavy investor appetite for Mach Industries and Covenant. Combined with today's $205M Cornelis raise on the compute side, defense-AI is one of the few private-market pockets clearly re-rating up in the middle of the slowdown debate.
