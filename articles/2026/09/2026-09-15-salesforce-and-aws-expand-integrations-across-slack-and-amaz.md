# Salesforce and AWS Expand Integrations Across Slack and Amazon Quick With Zero-Copy Data Access

Salesforce and AWS announced an expanded collaboration that embeds Salesforce CRM context natively into Amazon Quick, brings AWS frontier agents into Slack, widens zero-copy data access across both platforms, and adds real-time voice interoperability between Agentforce Voice and Amazon Connect. AWS's Rahul Pathak framed it as "data access without migration, model choice at the right economics, and AI agents where their people already work."
