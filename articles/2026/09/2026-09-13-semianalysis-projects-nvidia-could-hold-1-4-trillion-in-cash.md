# SemiAnalysis projects Nvidia could hold $1.4 trillion in cash and investments by fiscal 2031

A September 11 SemiAnalysis note argues Nvidia’s compounding cash generation could reach roughly $1.4 trillion in cash and investments by fiscal 2031 — an analyst projection, not a reported figure, extrapolated from consensus EBITDA of about $441 billion for fiscal 2028. The run-rate underneath it is real: operating cash flow rose from $28.09B (FY2024) to $64.09B (FY2025) to $102.72B (FY2026), on most-recent-quarter revenue of $96.22B, up 105.8% year over year. The strategic point is positioning rather than the headline: Nvidia has mobilized roughly $500 billion of third-party capital with Apollo, BlackRock and KKR, making it both supplier and financier of the buildout.
