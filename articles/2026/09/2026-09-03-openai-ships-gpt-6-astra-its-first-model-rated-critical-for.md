# OpenAI ships GPT-6 Astra, its first model rated “Critical” for cyber capability

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra as a computer-use flagship rather than a chat model, citing state-of-the-art results on software engineering, computer use, browsing, science, and cybersecurity, with a ~1.05M-token context at $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. It is the first broadly deployed OpenAI system to cross the “Critical” cybersecurity threshold under the company’s Preparedness Framework, which the company says can surface unknown vulnerabilities without continuous human direction. Rollout is gated — limited organizations first, then ChatGPT paid tiers, the API, Azure, and Amazon Bedrock.
