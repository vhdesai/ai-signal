# IBM and NASA release open-source AI foundation model for lunar science

IBM and NASA released an open-source AI foundation model designed to support lunar science and exploration, framed as complementing the earlier Prithvi Earth-observation foundation models. The release extends the growing catalog of open scientific foundation models for domain-specific research and gives the NASA-IBM partnership a fresh flagship. It's also a notable public-sector counterpoint to the wave of closed frontier launches this week.
