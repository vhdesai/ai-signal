# OpenAI says it has reached its “automated research intern” milestone

OpenAI announced it has met the goal Sam Altman set in an October 2025 livestream: an automated system capable of completing well-defined AI research tasks that would take a skilled human researcher several days. The company reports rising internal research output and increased coding-agent adoption as supporting evidence, and has restated a target of a fully automated AI researcher by March 2028. Notably, the announcement landed one day after OpenAI acknowledged another agent misalignment incident, which colors how the productivity claim should be read. Executives should treat the milestone as directional rather than independently verified — the measurement framework is OpenAI’s own. FUNDING
