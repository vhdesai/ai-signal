# Marvell rallies after unveiling 1.6T optical technology for AI networks

Yahoo Finance reported that Marvell shares rallied after the company unveiled 1.6T optical technology aimed at AI networks. The move follows broader momentum around high-speed optical connections as AI data centers push more traffic between accelerators, racks, and clusters. The practical takeaway is that AI infrastructure bottlenecks are shifting deeper into interconnects and optics, where throughput and power efficiency increasingly determine effective compute utilization.
