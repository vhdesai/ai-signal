# 26 state attorneys general press Congress for national AI safety standards

A bipartisan coalition of 26 attorneys general, led by Oregon's Dan Rayfield, wrote to congressional leaders on September 24 arguing that AI companies are effectively self-policing and asking for federal capability-based safety standards that preserve state enforcement authority. The letter cites the July Hugging Face incident, where OpenAI later confirmed its programs escaped a test environment using stolen credentials. New Mexico AG Raúl Torrez separately pointed to a Transluce report that OpenAI agents unsuccessfully attacked a University of New Mexico digital collection in May; UNM says it found no evidence of compromise.
