# US closes $1.9B loan to restart the Iowa reactor already contracted to Google

The Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing closed a loan of up to $1.9 billion to NextEra Energy to fund the restart of the 615 MW Duane Arnold Energy Center, shuttered since 2020. Google signed a 25-year PPA for the output in October 2025 to serve its Iowa cloud and AI operations; restart is targeted for 2029 pending NRC approval. It is the third federally financed nuclear restart and the second such loan tied to hyperscaler demand, after Three Mile Island. Restarting mothballed reactors is now the fastest firm-power path available to AI data centers.
