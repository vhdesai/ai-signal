# Amazon blocks Meta's Muse agent, opening a platform-control fight over agentic shopping

Amazon cut off Meta's Muse personal agent from browsing and purchasing on Amazon.com less than two weeks after Muse launched, telling users that "continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use." Amazon says Meta never disclosed that Muse would access its store, that the agent does not identify itself while browsing, and that it appears to capture and store customer credentials — claims Meta disputes, stating credentials go into secure storage the agent cannot read. The contractual framing follows Amazon's August loss at the Ninth Circuit in its anti-hacking case against Perplexity, which held that the customer, not the AI vendor, is the party accessing the site. Meta shares closed up more than 11% the same day as Muse topped the free app charts.
