# Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 with a two-tier safeguard split

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 — the same underlying model exposed under two different safeguard layers. Fable 5.1 is generally available on the Claude API, AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Foundry; Mythos 5.1 remains restricted to vetted organizations under a trusted-access program. Pricing is the sharper commercial move: cache reads drop roughly 75%, from $1.00 to $0.25 per million input tokens, materially changing the economics of long-running agentic sessions. Anthropic positions the release around coding, knowledge work, and early scientific research contribution. https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1
