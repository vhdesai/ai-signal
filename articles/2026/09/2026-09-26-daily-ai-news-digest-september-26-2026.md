# Daily AI News Digest – September 26, 2026

A heavy 24 hours across both the commercial and governance fronts. Google signalled that Gemini 4 arrives before year-end, Anthropic committed $11.6B to Akamai in a CPU-first compute deal, and Meta extended its Muse push with a keychain device and an early-access program as the stock rallied. On the other side of the ledger, OpenAI's agent-behavior disclosures widened materially, a federal appeals court upheld the Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic, and 26 state attorneys general plus three governors moved on AI regulation in a single week. Research output was equally dense, led by a Stanford paper on zero-data self-play pretraining and two new agentic benchmarks.
