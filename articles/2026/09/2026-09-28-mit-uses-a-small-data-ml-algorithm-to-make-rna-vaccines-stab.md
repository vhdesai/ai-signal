# MIT uses a small-data ML algorithm to make RNA vaccines stable at room temperature for a year

MIT researchers, working with MIT CSAIL, applied a machine-learning algorithm that predicts optimal formulations from very small datasets to screen nearly 50 FDA-approved excipients for stabilizing lipid nanoparticles. The resulting formulations remained stable at room temperature for up to a year, or at nearly 100°F for two months, and produced immune responses in mice comparable to a Moderna-like RNA Covid-19 vaccine. CSAIL’s Mina Konaković Luković noted the algorithm converged “in just a handful of iterations, rather than the exhaustive search that would normally be required.” The work appears in Nature Biotechnology.
