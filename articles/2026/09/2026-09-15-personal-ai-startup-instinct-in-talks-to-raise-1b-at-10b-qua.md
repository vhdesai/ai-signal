# Personal-AI startup Instinct in talks to raise $1B at ~$10B — quadrupling in under a month

The Information reports Instinct — the invite-only personal AI-assistant app — is in talks to raise $1 billion at approximately a $10 billion valuation, quadrupling in under a month from its $2.25B mark. Sequoia Capital and Benchmark are in talks to lead. Users have grown past 100,000 as the service opened up, and capacity has been a persistent constraint. It's one of the sharpest agent-startup valuation jumps of the year and lands the same week Meta launches Muse and Anthropic prepares Claude Money. - https://www.theinformation.com/articles/ai-agent-startup-instinct-in-talks-for-10-billion-valuation
