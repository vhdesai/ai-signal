# Google DeepMind releases Gemini 3.8 Live at $1.38/hr — undercuts OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 by ~two-thirds

Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, both topping the Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech leaderboard, at $1.38 per hour of voice conversation — roughly one-third the price of OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 API launched last week ($0.05/min ≈ $3/hr). GPT-Live-1 remains full-duplex and likely still sounds more natural; Gemini 3.8 Live wins on price and instruction-following. For any enterprise standing up voice-agent workloads at scale — customer support, telephony, sales — this shifts the default choice on unit economics. - https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-3-8-live-and-3-8-live-extended-thinking/ - https://the-decoder.com/google-launches-gemini-3-8-live-to-take-on-openais-gpt-live-1-at-a-fraction-of-the-cost/
