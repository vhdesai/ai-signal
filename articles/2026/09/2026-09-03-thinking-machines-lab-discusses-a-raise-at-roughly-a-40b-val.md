# Thinking Machines Lab discusses a raise at roughly a $40B valuation

The Information reports that Thinking Machines Lab, led by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, is in talks to raise at least $1B at a valuation of at least $40B. Existing investor Accel is reportedly in talks to lead the round, following the July release of the company’s first model, Inkling, with NVIDIA also discussed as a potential participant. The story shows how elite AI labs can still command major financing.
