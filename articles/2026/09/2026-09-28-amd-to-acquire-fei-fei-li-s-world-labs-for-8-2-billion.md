# AMD to Acquire Fei-Fei Li’s World Labs for $8.2 Billion

AMD signed a definitive agreement to acquire World Labs, the spatial-intelligence lab founded by Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock transaction valued at roughly $8.2 billion, with closing expected by year-end subject to regulatory approval. Li joins AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist reporting to CEO Lisa Su. It is AMD’s second-largest acquisition ever after Xilinx, and is explicitly framed as a way to shape silicon roadmaps around where frontier workloads — robotics, simulation, physical AI — are heading. At roughly 70 employees, the price implies more than $100 million per head.
