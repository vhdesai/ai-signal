# Clivet debuts magnetic-levitation cooling platform at AI Infra Summit as rack densities climb

Clivet used AI Infra Summit 2026 in Santa Clara to launch a dual-rotor variable-speed magnetic levitation compressor and a full-chain liquid-cooling portfolio, including CDUs rated to 3 MW at a 3K approach temperature difference and 5 MW at 5K, with predictive control holding fluctuation within ±0.5°C. The company also demonstrated a joint power-and-cooling coordination solution with CLOU. This is a vendor-issued press release rather than independent reporting, so treat the performance claims accordingly — the signal worth taking is the direction of travel: thermal design is now being specified against grid-side load variation, not just rack density.
