# Google’s First Orbital TPU Test Flies October 1 on SpaceX Transporter-18

Google confirmed that Project Suncatcher’s first prototype satellite — a refrigerator-sized craft built with Planet Labs and carrying four Trillium-generation TPUs — launches October 1 aboard SpaceX’s Transporter-18 rideshare from Vandenberg. Solar output is about 1 kW, and thermal limits in vacuum mean the TPUs will run Gemini workloads in bursts of ~15 minutes before shutting down to let heat pipes and radiators catch up. Ground testing at UC Davis showed Trillium TPUs surviving a total ionizing dose greater than a five-year mission would deliver. Google plans two further satellites in 2027 to test high-bandwidth laser interconnects, and is explicit that Suncatcher remains a research moonshot years from product.
