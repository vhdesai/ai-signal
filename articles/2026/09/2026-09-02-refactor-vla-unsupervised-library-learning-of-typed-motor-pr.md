# REFACTOR-VLA: Unsupervised Library Learning of Typed Motor Programs

Apple published research on REFACTOR-VLA, an approach for learning reusable typed motor-program abstractions for vision-language-action models. The work targets a known limitation in robotics models: monolithic action generation often struggles with long-horizon tasks and interpretability. The executive relevance is that practical robotics AI may depend as much on reusable action structure as on larger multimodal model backbones. Products & Tools WORKPLACE AISLACKCREATIVE TOOLS
