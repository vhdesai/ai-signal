# Beijing signals it may let ByteDance and Alibaba buy Nvidia's RTX Pro 5500

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology has indicated it may permit ByteDance and Alibaba to purchase Nvidia's new RTX Pro 5500 AI chip, according to The Information. The signal would mark a partial softening of Beijing's pressure on domestic firms to avoid U.S. silicon, and it lands as Alibaba pushes its own Zhenwu accelerator and a larger Qwen model roadmap. For supply planning, the read is that Chinese demand for export-compliant Nvidia parts remains live rather than foreclosed. Model Releases MODELS GOOGLE
