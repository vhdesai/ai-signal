# Nature Medicine publishes practical lessons from scaling clinical AI past one million patients screened

A Nature Medicine article documents the global scaling of a deep-learning screening tool from a single hospital to more than a million patients screened, drawing cross-cutting lessons from deployments in India, Thailand and Australia. The emphasis is on deployment mechanics — workflow integration, local validation and operating-model differences across health systems — rather than model accuracy. It is a useful counterweight to benchmark-driven claims: the constraint on clinical AI value is implementation, not capability. S RESEARCH
