# AfterQuery becomes Y Combinator's fastest unicorn at a $3.2B valuation

Model-training data startup AfterQuery reportedly closed a round valuing it at $3.2 billion, five months after a $30 million Series A at a $300 million valuation. The roughly tenfold step-up is the fastest path to unicorn status in Y Combinator's history. The valuation reflects scarcity pricing on high-quality training and evaluation data rather than model IP.
