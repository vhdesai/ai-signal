# TechCrunch: world-model companies are unusually secretive about what they're actually building

Russell Brandom's field survey concludes that world-model companies (Runway GWM-1, Wayve, Waabi, and multiple stealth peers) are collectively holding both major funding and heavy buzz — but neither founders nor their data suppliers will describe what they're training on or what their models actually do. The secrecy is unusual for a category attracting $1B+ round sizes, and it complicates external technical evaluation. For enterprise buyers considering world-model tooling for robotics or ADAS, TechCrunch's warning is the honest read: due diligence is currently limited to founder narrative.
