# Meta Launches Enterprise Platform, Hires MongoDB CEO CJ Desai

Mark Zuckerberg announced Meta Enterprise Platform as “the next major pillar of our business,” bringing the Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code to businesses and developers. Chirantan “CJ” Desai joins as Chief Enterprise Platform Officer reporting directly to Zuckerberg, leaving MongoDB after 11 months as CEO; Dev Ittycheria returns to MongoDB as interim president and CEO.
