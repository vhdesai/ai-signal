# Cornelis raises $205M to challenge Nvidia's networking dominance

Cornelis, a 2020 Intel spinout, raised $205M led by IAG Capital Partners and launched Active Compute Fabric — networking that lets chips process and transmit data simultaneously to cut GPU idle time. Its open architecture supports mixed GPU-and-accelerator hardware, in contrast to Nvidia's full-stack lock-in with NVLink and Spectrum-X. The product is already shipping, with a next generation due later this year, announced alongside a Qualcomm collaboration at the AI Infra Summit. It's a small but real crack in Nvidia's AI-networking moat.
