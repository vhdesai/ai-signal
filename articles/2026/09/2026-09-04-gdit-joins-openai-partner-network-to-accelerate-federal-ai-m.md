# GDIT joins OpenAI Partner Network to accelerate federal AI modernization

Homeland Security Today reports that GDIT joined OpenAI's partner network to support federal AI modernization. The move reflects a practical route to public-sector adoption: agencies need systems integrators that can translate model capability into compliant workflows, procurement constructs, and operating environments. It also shows frontier-model vendors deepening go-to-market through trusted government channels.
