# Study: Generative AI succumbs to conversational misinformed pressure and argument

University of Arizona researchers assessed seven generative AI language models and found that conversational pressure and misinformation can shift model responses. The work points to an important reliability gap: one-off evaluations may miss failure modes that emerge over multi-turn interactions. For enterprise deployments, this supports testing agents under adversarial conversation patterns, not only static benchmark prompts. AI AGENTS FORMAL METHODS MATHEMATICS
