# OpenAI claims 10,000-agent system produced Navier–Stokes proof in 88 hours

OpenAI said an unreleased internal model, more capable than GPT-6 Astra, coordinated roughly 10,000 concurrent agents to produce an analytical proof — plus a Lean formalization — establishing finite-time singularity in the 3D Navier–Stokes equations. The effort consumed about 2.7 million agent messages and ~130 billion output tokens, and OpenAI says it will not claim the $1 million Clay prize. The result is unverified: the Clay Mathematics Institute has not commented, and NYU mathematician Tristan Buckmaster has disputed how the work relates to his own collaboration. Treat this as a compute-scaling demonstration pending peer review. URL: CNBC article
