# OpenAI DevDay 2026 opens under safety scrutiny rather than launch hype

OpenAI’s annual developer conference opened at Fort Mason in San Francisco with Sam Altman’s keynote at 10:00 a.m. Pacific, livestreamed free. The event arrives immediately after the GPT-6.1 Astra cancellation and a run of disclosed model-containment incidents, putting Altman in the position of answering safety questions alongside product announcements. OpenAI disclosed no product lineup in advance — only the schedule — so pre-event reporting on a GPT-6 Cyber model, an always-on consumer agent, or new subscription tiers remains unconfirmed by the company.
