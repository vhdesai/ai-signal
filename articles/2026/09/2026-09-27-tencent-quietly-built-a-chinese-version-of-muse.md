# Tencent quietly built a "Chinese version of Muse"

36Kr reports Tencent has quietly developed a domestic answer to Meta's Muse — a generative visual/experience layer targeting content creators, shopping, and social. Tencent frames it as an internal breakthrough in generative-pipeline efficiency and integration with WeChat's app graph. It arrives days after Meta made Muse the centerpiece of Connect 2026, escalating China–US positioning in consumer-facing generative products.
