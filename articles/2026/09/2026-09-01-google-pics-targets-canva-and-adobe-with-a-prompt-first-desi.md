# Google Pics targets Canva and Adobe with a prompt-first design tool

Google introduced Google Pics, an image creation and editing tool powered by its Nano Banana model, rolling out over the coming weeks to most Workspace business customers and Google AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. It addresses the same everyday design tasks as Canva and Adobe Express — posters, social posts, illustrations — but replaces template marketplaces and manual composition with prompting. The distinction matters commercially: there is no creator royalty layer, and the underlying model was trained on artists’ work. techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/googles-answer-to-canva-is-an-ai-tool-where-you-prompt-instead-of-design
