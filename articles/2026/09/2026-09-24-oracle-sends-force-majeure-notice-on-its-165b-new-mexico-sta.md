# Oracle sends force majeure notice on its $165B New Mexico Stargate campus

Oracle sent a force majeure notice to a Blue Owl Capital unit developing Project Jupiter, the 2.45-gigawatt Stargate campus in Doña Ana County, New Mexico. Oracle is not exiting as principal tenant; the notice preserves its position to delay payments if the site misses its 2028 in-service date. Oracle shares fell more than 3% on the day and Blue Owl also declined; Bloom Energy, contracted to power the site with fuel cells, fell ~3%. An Energy Transfer pipeline intended to supply gas has slipped roughly six months to February 2027 after repeated permit denials, and an air-quality permit decision is due November 23. Oracle maintains the project “remains on our planned schedule.”
