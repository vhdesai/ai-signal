# Princeton and UCLA mathematicians press for norms on AI-assisted discovery

Follow-on coverage details the academic side of the declaration: Princeton's June Huh and UCLA's Terence Tao argue that AI's ability to produce fast correct answers can undermine conceptual understanding, insight and the training of the next generation. Tao confirmed the statement was drafted over a single week and released without the usual consultative process because the signatories judged the situation urgent. The open question the declaration does not resolve is what a jointly agreed validation and attribution process between labs and academic communities would actually look like.
