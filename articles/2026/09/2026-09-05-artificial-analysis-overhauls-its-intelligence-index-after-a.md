# Artificial Analysis overhauls its Intelligence Index after Astra scoring drew skepticism

Benchmarking firm Artificial Analysis shipped version 4.2 of its Intelligence Index, apparently in response to criticism that its earlier methodology understated GPT‑6 Astra’s gains. Under the revised index Astra scores four points above its predecessor but still trails Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.1. The episode is a reminder that third-party leaderboards are themselves moving targets and should not anchor procurement decisions on their own.
