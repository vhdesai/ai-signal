# Google Research TimesFM-3 Forecasts From Sales, Weather and Promotion Schedules in a Single Pass

TimesFM-3 analyzes time series alongside related covariates and known future events such as promotions or weather forecasts. Rather than predicting step by step, the 330-million-parameter model fills in all future time points in a single pass, cutting compute time and avoiding the error compounding inherent to autoregressive forecasting. The single-pass design is the technical claim of interest for demand-planning and capacity-forecasting workloads. Note the dating: only the coverage falls in this window — Google Research dates the release itself to August 31.
