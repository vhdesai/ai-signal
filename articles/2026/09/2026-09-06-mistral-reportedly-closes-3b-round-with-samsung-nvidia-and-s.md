# Mistral reportedly closes €3B round with Samsung, Nvidia, and Scaleup Fund

Reporting from Brief IA says Mistral has closed or is closing a €3 billion funding round with participation from Samsung, Nvidia, and the Scaleup Fund. If confirmed, the raise would be one of Europe's largest AI rounds and further concentrate strategic ties between Nvidia and frontier model labs. It also strengthens Mistral's positioning as Europe's sovereign-AI champion at a time when governments are actively partnering with the company.
