# Analysis: AI hyperscalers' "circular deals" now resemble 1980s S&L "daisy chains"

A senior opinion analysis in SCMP argues that interlocking multi-year commitments among AI vendors, chipmakers, cloud providers, and power/data-center partners — where each party simultaneously finances, supplies, and buys from the others — increasingly mirror the "daisy chain" financing structures that preceded the 1980s US savings-and-loan crisis. The piece flags that these arrangements can obscure counterparty risk and inflate reported asset values until a single link fails. It is a high-signal reframing of the current AI capex boom for any executive with balance-sheet or credit exposure to the compute stack.
