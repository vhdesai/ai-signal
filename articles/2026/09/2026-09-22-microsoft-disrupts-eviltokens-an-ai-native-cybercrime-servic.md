# Microsoft disrupts EvilTokens, an AI-native cybercrime service

Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit executed a court-authorized takedown of the infrastructure behind EvilTokens, a cybercrime platform that used AI at every step of the attack chain — from compromising email accounts to designing financial-fraud roadmaps. Ars Technica reported the platform was tied to roughly 12,000 compromised accounts. UK police arrested two suspects in connection with the operation. It is one of the first takedowns framed explicitly around AI as the operational core of the criminal service rather than an accessory.
