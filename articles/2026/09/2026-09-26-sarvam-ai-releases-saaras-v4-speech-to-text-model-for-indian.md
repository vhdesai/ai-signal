# Sarvam AI releases Saaras V4 speech-to-text model for Indian languages and global English

MarkTechPost reported that Sarvam AI released Saaras V4, a speech-to-text model covering all 22 scheduled Indian languages and global English. The release is strategically relevant because speech AI is becoming a key interface for public services, customer support, education, and mobile-first populations. For enterprises operating in India, model coverage across Indian languages can determine whether voice automation is inclusive or limited to English-heavy workflows.
