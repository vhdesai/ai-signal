# ABB launches Infinitus direct-current portfolio for AI data centers

ABB launched Infinitus, a source-to-rack direct-current portfolio for AI data centers that includes medium-voltage powertrains, DC distribution, protection, power quality, and cooling optimization. ABB says next-generation AI chips could draw 1 MW per rack, making conventional alternating-current conversion losses and power footprints more problematic. The announcement shows the AI power challenge moving from site selection into electrical architecture and rack-level power delivery. ENERGY NVIDIA ECOSYSTEM
