# ElevenLabs Releases Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo

ElevenLabs launched Eleven v4, built on a new architecture for expressive speech with inline direction tags, and Eleven v4 Turbo, a low-latency variant reporting ~100 ms median inference latency and ~150 ms median time to first speech. Both support more than 90 languages (up from 70 in v3), restore Professional Voice Clones, and can produce an Instant Voice Clone from about 10 seconds of audio. The models are available now across ElevenAgents, ElevenCreative and the ElevenAPI. ElevenLabs says over 55% of its business now comes from large enterprises, with annualised revenue up from roughly $330 million at the start of the year to over $600 million — voice agents are moving from novelty to contact-centre infrastructure.
