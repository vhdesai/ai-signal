# Indian AI-Chip Startup Agrani Labs Raising ~$50M at up to $200M Valuation

Bengaluru-based Agrani Labs, founded by former Intel and AMD executives, is in advanced talks to raise roughly $50 million at a $160–200 million valuation, with existing investor Peak XV expected to participate. It is building AI inference processors designed to work with Nvidia's CUDA ecosystem rather than against it — targeting the software-compatibility moat that has blocked most Nvidia challengers. The raise sits within India's Semicon 2.0 push.
