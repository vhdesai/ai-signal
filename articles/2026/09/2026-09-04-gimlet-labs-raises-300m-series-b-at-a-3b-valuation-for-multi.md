# Gimlet Labs Raises $300M Series B at a $3B Valuation for Multi-Silicon Inference

Gimlet Labs closed a $300 million Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz at a $3 billion valuation, roughly six months after a round that valued it near $400 million. Arm and Microsoft's M12 participated. The company's thesis is disaggregated inference — routing agentic workloads across competing accelerators rather than committing to a single vendor stack, which is a direct hedge against the concentration described above.
