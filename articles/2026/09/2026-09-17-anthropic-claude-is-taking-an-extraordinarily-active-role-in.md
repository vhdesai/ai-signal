# Anthropic: Claude is taking an "extraordinarily active" role in building its own successor

Anthropic disclosed that Claude is now contributing to development of its own successor, taking an "extraordinarily active" role while still working under human supervision — consistent with its same-week R&D Automation Index post finding Claude "leads" 26% of AI R&D work (up from under 1% in February) but is not operating fully autonomously in any measured subset. The disclosure lands as Anthropic eyes a possible IPO later this fall. Together with the OpenAI misalignment framework and the DeepMind Institute launch, this is the second frontier lab to publish primary-source data on AI-doing-AI-work within 24 hours. - https://www.usnews.com/news/business/articles/2026-09-17/anthropic-says-its-model-claude-is-helping-to-build-the-next-version-of-itself
