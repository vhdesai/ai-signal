# Blackstone's TPU spending surges into "multiples" of the original $5B Google deal

Blackstone's May joint venture with Google, initially $5B for 500MW of TPUs, is expanding into "several multiples" of that figure, according to a person with knowledge of the effort — putting Blackstone on track to commit tens of billions of dollars for multiple gigawatts of AI chips. Jas Khaira, who leads Blackstone's N1 AI-investing team and has direct ties to Sam Altman and Dario Amodei, called compute the firm's "biggest conviction bet" and said the infrastructure build-out is closer to an industrial revolution than a tech cycle. Blackstone is also newly evaluating investments in open-source AI.
