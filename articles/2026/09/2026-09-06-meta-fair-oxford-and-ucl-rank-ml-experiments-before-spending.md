# Meta FAIR, Oxford and UCL rank ML experiments before spending GPU hours

The teams introduced AI Research Preference Models (RPMs) — frozen LLM judges that score 15 unexecuted experiment candidates and execute only the most promising one, avoiding the cost of running them all. On AIRS-Bench, the average normalized score rises from 0.684 to 0.729 with no additional training. The practical implication is a cheaper research loop: selection quality substitutes for brute-force compute, which is directly relevant to any enterprise running large experiment portfolios.
