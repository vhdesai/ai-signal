# StepFun launches Step 5 Preview: 600B-total / 27B-active MoE with 1M context for long-horizon agentic work

StepFun released Step 5 Preview, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with roughly 600B total and 27B active parameters, a 1M-token context window, and text, image and video input, targeted at software engineering, knowledge work and finance. API pricing is $1.00 per 1M input tokens and $2.70 per 1M output tokens, with a $0.05 cache-hit rate; open weights are scheduled for October 15, 2026. Artificial Analysis scores it 44 on its Intelligence Index against a roughly 24 median for similarly priced reasoning models — though it consumed 160M output tokens on the index run versus a 92M median, which matters for real unit economics.
