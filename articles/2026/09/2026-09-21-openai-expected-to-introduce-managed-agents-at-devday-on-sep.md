# OpenAI Expected to Introduce Managed Agents at DevDay on September 29

OpenAI's annual developer conference lands September 29 at Fort Mason in San Francisco, and Sam Altman has signaled a significant agent-related launch. The company has been expanding tooling so agents can execute multi-step office work directly on its platform, following July's Presence product for deploying agents in customer support, sales and billing. The analytical case made is that single-feature SaaS vendors carry the most exposure, while platforms holding proprietary business data — Salesforce, ServiceNow, Snowflake — are structurally more defensible. For enterprise buyers, the practical question is which internal workflows currently sit on thin third-party tooling.
