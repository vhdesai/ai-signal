# Judge Rejects DOJ Push to Force Google to Divest AdX

A federal judge declined the Justice Department's request to compel Alphabet to sell its AdX ad exchange, removing a structural-remedy overhang on the same day Google shipped its third Flash-class model in six weeks. The ruling narrows the remedies phase of the ad-tech case to behavioral measures. For Alphabet, it preserves the cash-generative advertising base that funds an accelerating model release cadence. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/google-launches-gemini-3-8-114355899.html
