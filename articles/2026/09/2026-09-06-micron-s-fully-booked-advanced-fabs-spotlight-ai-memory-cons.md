# Micron's fully booked advanced fabs spotlight AI memory constraints

Micron's advanced production capacity is sold out through 2026, with tightness across high-bandwidth memory and NAND tied to AI data-center demand. The article notes upward revisions to HBM pricing expectations and flags Micron's September 30 earnings as the next proof point for whether pricing power is converting into financial performance. For AI infrastructure buyers, memory availability and pricing can be as constraining as accelerator supply.
