# MIT Senseable City Lab publishes "How AI Sees the City"

Fabio Duarte, Martina Mazzarello, Carlo Ratti and Fan Zhang examine how computer vision applied to street-level and satellite imagery is reshaping urban research. The book documents both the new measurement capability and the systematic bias and privacy risks of visual AI in public space, following the lab's recent New York City pollution study. The governance lesson generalizes: for any enterprise running vision models against real-world environments, the representativeness of the imagery drives downstream error.
