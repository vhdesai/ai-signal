# Appeals court rules the Pentagon can blacklist Anthropic over refused Claude features

The DC Circuit upheld 2-1 the Pentagon's designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, imposed after Anthropic refused to lift guardrails against autonomous lethal weapons and domestic mass surveillance. The majority cited “the deeply sobering prospect of overly constrained AI models shutting down unexpectedly.” Anthropic notes a parallel California ruling found the designation unlawful and is weighing en banc or Supreme Court review. The split sets up a direct conflict between lab safety policy and federal procurement leverage.
