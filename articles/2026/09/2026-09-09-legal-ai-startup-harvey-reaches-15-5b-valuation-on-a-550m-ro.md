# Legal AI startup Harvey reaches $15.5B valuation on a $550M round

Harvey announced a $550 million round at a $15.5 billion valuation, co-led by Diffusion and Lightspeed, with Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, a16z, Coatue, GIC, and Goldman Sachs Alternatives participating — up from an $11B mark in March. The raise follows Harvey Tenet, its first post-trained open-weight model, and LAB, its open-sourced legal-agent benchmark. The company reports 80% of Am Law 100 firms and five of the Fortune 10 among users, and has said the capital will fund development of its own models rather than continued reliance on third-party foundation models. Vertical AI leaders with proprietary domain data are moving down the stack — a shift that changes both their cost structure and their defensibility.
