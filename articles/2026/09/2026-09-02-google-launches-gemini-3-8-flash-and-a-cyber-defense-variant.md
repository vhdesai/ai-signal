# Google launches Gemini 3.8 Flash and a cyber-defense variant for trusted defenders

Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash, describing it as its strongest reasoning and coding model while preserving the speed and introductory pricing profile of 3.7 Flash. It also launched Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber through the Fairwind Program for vetted defenders, government agencies, and critical-infrastructure operators. The split release points to a market where general capability ships broadly while high-risk cyber capability moves behind trust gates.
