# HUMAIN and AMD Launch a $10 Billion AI Infrastructure Ecosystem

Saudi PIF-backed HUMAIN and AMD announced a partnership to build a $10 billion AI infrastructure ecosystem, with HUMAIN overseeing end-to-end delivery and AMD supplying its full AI compute portfolio. The structure gives AMD a large anchor deployment outside the U.S. hyperscalers.
