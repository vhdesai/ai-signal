# China's data regulator plans standards for embodied AI training data

The Next Web reported that China's National Data Administration plans to develop standards for embodied-AI data and guide local authorities on building the required data resources. The piece notes that robotics foundation models may need roughly 10 million hours of real-world training data, while only a fraction of that high-quality data exists today. This matters because embodied AI may be constrained less by model architecture than by access to standardized, high-volume, real-world interaction data. OPEN SOURCE BRICS
