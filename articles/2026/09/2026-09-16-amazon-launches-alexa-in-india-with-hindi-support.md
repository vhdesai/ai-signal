# Amazon launches Alexa+ in India with Hindi support

Amazon launched Alexa+ in India with Hindi support and code-switching between Hindi and English. It integrates with Swiggy, Zomato's District, MakeMyTrip, EazyDiner, and Amazon Now for grocery, and is free for Prime customers after early access, ₹2,000/mo otherwise. It's Amazon's first major generative-AI consumer launch localized for a non-English-native market, targeting 600M+ Hindi speakers, and it lands ahead of Google's expected Gemini-India push.
