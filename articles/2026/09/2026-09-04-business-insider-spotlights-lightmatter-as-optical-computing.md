# Business Insider spotlights Lightmatter as optical computing gains executive attention

Business Insider's AI Insider newsletter focused on Lightmatter and the case for photonic computing as the next major efficiency frontier after the current GPU buildout. CEO Nick Harris argues that optical chips can deliver more intelligence per watt by using light rather than electronic switching for core computation, with practical deployment still five to 10 years out.
