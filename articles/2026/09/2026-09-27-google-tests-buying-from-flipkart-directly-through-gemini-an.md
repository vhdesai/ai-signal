# Google tests buying from Flipkart directly through Gemini and AI Mode in India

Google is piloting agentic commerce in India, letting users purchase from Walmart-owned Flipkart entirely inside Gemini and AI Mode search — no redirect to a retailer app. It's one of the first live agentic-checkout deployments at scale in a major emerging market, and the first meaningful test of Google's answer to Amazon's Muse block and its ongoing Perplexity lawsuit.
