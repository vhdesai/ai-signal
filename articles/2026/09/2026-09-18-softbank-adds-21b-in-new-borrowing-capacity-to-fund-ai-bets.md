# SoftBank adds ~$21B in new borrowing capacity to fund AI bets

SoftBank raised its Arm-backed margin loan by $5B to $25B — the third upsizing of a facility that began at $8.5B in 2023 — and expanded a separate credit line to $6.5B. Combined with an $11.87B term loan and Apollo talks to lift another facility to $9B, the group has assembled roughly $20.9B in committed and potential new debt, with a $10–20B junk bond offering possibly pricing next week. The borrowing underwrites a near-$65B commitment to OpenAI at a moment when investors are openly questioning the revenue payoff. - https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/softbank-raises-arm-margin-loan-054546224.html
