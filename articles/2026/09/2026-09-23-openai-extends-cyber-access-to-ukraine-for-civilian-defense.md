# OpenAI extends cyber access to Ukraine for civilian defense

OpenAI announced that it is extending cyber access to Ukraine for civilian defense. The official update signals a continued move by frontier AI providers into national resilience and cyber-defense workflows, where access policy, misuse controls, and public-sector accountability are central. The strategic readout is that AI vendors are increasingly becoming part of allied cyber-defense capacity, not only commercial software providers. BIOLOGYANTHROPIC
