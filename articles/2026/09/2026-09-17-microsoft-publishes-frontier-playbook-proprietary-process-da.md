# Microsoft publishes Frontier Playbook: proprietary process data, not models, is the durable enterprise moat

Microsoft released a playbook based on its internal AI transformation, arguing that proprietary process and workflow data — not models — is the durable enterprise advantage. It reports 20% higher close rates in one sales group, up to 75% cycle-time reduction in selected supply-chain workflows, and a nine-person engineering team shipping an initial release in 35 days. The companion post frames the shift as moving from what AI can do to how companies create business value with it. Metrics are vendor self-reported and should be read accordingly, but the strategic frame — redesign workflows around outcomes rather than broadly deploying tools — is what boards will hear. - https://venturebeat.com/technology/microsoft-releases-new-ai-playbook-for-enterprises-based-on-its-own-learnings-and-it-reveals-a-surprising-moat-your-biz-may-already-have - https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/17/what-weve-learned-from-microsofts-own-ai-transformation/
