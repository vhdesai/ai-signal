# AI chatbots frequently flub voting questions ahead of midterms, study finds

NBC News reports that AI chatbots frequently answered voting-related questions incorrectly in a study ahead of the midterms. The risk is not only misinformation at scale, but authoritative-sounding errors in high-stakes civic workflows. For AI platform leaders, election information remains a domain where retrieval grounding, source controls, refusal behavior, and escalation paths need tighter design.
