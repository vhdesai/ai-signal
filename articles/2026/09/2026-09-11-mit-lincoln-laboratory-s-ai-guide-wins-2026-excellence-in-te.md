# MIT Lincoln Laboratory's AI-GUIDE Wins 2026 Excellence in Technology Transfer Award

The Federal Laboratory Consortium recognized AI-GUIDE, an AI-assisted handheld catheterization device developed by MIT Lincoln Laboratory and Massachusetts General Hospital, for its 2026 Excellence in Technology Transfer Award. The device pairs custom AI software with commercial handheld ultrasound to let minimally trained medics place a guidewire and catheter in pre-hospital settings. It moved from concept to working prototype and a spinout, AutonomUS Medical Technologies, within three years, securing FDA Breakthrough Device Designation. A useful counter-example to the assumption that federally funded AI research commercializes slowly.
