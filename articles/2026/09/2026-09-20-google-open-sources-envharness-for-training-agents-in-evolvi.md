# Google open-sources EnvHarness for training agents in evolving environments

VentureBeat reported that Google released EnvHarness, an open-source framework that lets AI agents train against environments that change with them. The direction is important because static benchmarks are increasingly poor proxies for deployed agents that face adaptive tools, users, and adversaries. For enterprises, this points toward evaluation environments that evolve over time rather than one-time test sets. Infrastructure DATA CENTER POWER DIRECT CURRENT
