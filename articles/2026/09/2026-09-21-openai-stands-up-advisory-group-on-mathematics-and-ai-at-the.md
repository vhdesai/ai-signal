# OpenAI stands up Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI at the Institute for Advanced Study

OpenAI announced the formation of an independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI, hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study, to review AI-generated mathematical results and guide their communication. It's the first formal institution to sit between frontier labs and the mathematics community after this month's Navier–Stokes and 25-Fields-Medalist dispute. For any executive relying on AI-in-science claims for strategic planning, this creates a concrete authoritative body whose verdicts will now filter enterprise-marketing claims about scientific breakthroughs.
