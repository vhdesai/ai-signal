# Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google quietly discussed forming an industry AI safety standards body

The Information reports Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google have been holding discussions about jointly creating a standards body for AI testing and auditing — talks that predate Amodei's Saturday slowdown essay. Sam Altman told an OpenAI town hall he supports a testing and auditing organization but believes major labs will have to build it themselves without US government support, since the Trump administration's draft executive order for an AI self-regulator has stalled. The move points toward industry-led self-regulation as the near-term governance path, with obvious antitrust and captured-regulator questions attached.
