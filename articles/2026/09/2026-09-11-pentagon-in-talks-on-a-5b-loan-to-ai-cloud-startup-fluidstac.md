# Pentagon in talks on a ~$5B loan to AI cloud startup Fluidstack

The Department of Defense is reportedly in discussions to lend roughly $5 billion to Fluidstack, which would be the largest loan ever made by the Office of Strategic Capital. Fluidstack, founded in London in 2017 and now headquartered in New York, has secured multi-year data-center leases, raised $1.5 billion from Jane Street, and holds relationships with Google and Anthropic. Erebor Bank is advising on the potential financing. The significance is categorical rather than financial: AI compute is being treated as strategic defense infrastructure alongside semiconductors and aerospace.
