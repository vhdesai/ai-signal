# Nvidia authorises an additional $150B in buybacks, raising the program to $235B

Nvidia's board approved a $150 billion increase to its existing share repurchase program, bringing the total remaining authorisation to $235 billion, which the company expects to execute through fiscal 2028. Huang framed it as cash generation sufficient to fund the AI build-out and return capital simultaneously. Landing the same morning as the safety platform, the announcement is a deliberate signal of balance-sheet confidence against persistent questions about AI capital-expenditure durability.
