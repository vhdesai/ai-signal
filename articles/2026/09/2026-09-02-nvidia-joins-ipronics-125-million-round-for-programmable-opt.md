# Nvidia joins iPronics' $125 million round for programmable optical networking

Valencia-based iPronics raised a $125 million Series B to scale programmable photonic switching for AI infrastructure, with Nvidia participating. Optical switching and interconnect efficiency are becoming core constraints alongside power and cooling. The investment fits a broader push to reduce latency and energy cost inside increasingly large AI clusters. Yahoo Finance DATA CENTERSQUANTUM
