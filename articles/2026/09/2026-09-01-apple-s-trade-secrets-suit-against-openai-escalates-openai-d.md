# Apple's Trade-Secrets Suit Against OpenAI Escalates; OpenAI Denies the Allegations

Apple filed what it calls “shocking evidence” that a former engineer now at OpenAI used a confidential Apple circuit schematic and an internally named tool, and sought a preliminary injunction blocking OpenAI hardware built on Apple technology. OpenAI denied the allegations, calling the dispute “a mess of Apple's own making.” With more than 400 former Apple staff at OpenAI and the Jony Ive hardware effort underway, the case is becoming the reference point for AI talent-mobility litigation.
