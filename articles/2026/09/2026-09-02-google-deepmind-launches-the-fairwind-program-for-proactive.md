# Google DeepMind launches the Fairwind Program for proactive cyber defense

Google and DeepMind launched Fairwind, a limited-access program giving governments and trusted partners access to its AI-powered cyber-defense tooling. It accompanies the Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber launch and is framed around proactive vulnerability discovery and patching for public-sector and enterprise defenders, paired with DeepMind's CodeMender patching harness. More than 650 early participants are reported, including Snowflake, CrowdStrike and Datadog.
