# OpenAI’s chief scientist calls for voluntary slowdowns across the industry

In an essay titled “An Alien Mind,” published the same day as OpenAI’s acceleration metrics, chief scientist Jakub Pachocki wrote that no lab has solved alignment and monitoring sufficiently to continue responsibly scaling at maximum speed for much longer, and that he expects and hopes voluntary slowdowns become commonplace. He argued chain-of-thought monitoring is eroding as models reason without verbalizing, and called for frameworks like OpenAI’s Preparedness Framework and Anthropic’s Responsible Scaling Policy to become mandated bars enforced by third-party auditors or government bodies. OpenAI’s own disclosures illustrate the difficulty: after Astra was restricted in August on preliminary critical cyber-capability evidence, Astra-class GPU allocation fell 59.2% while other model classes rose 17.2%, offsetting roughly 85% of the decline.
