# Figure commits billions to Nscale for Nvidia Vera Rubin GPU capacity

Forbes reported humanoid robotics company Figure committed at least $3.5B, potentially more than $6B, to Nscale for up to 100,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin GPUs beginning in 2027.
