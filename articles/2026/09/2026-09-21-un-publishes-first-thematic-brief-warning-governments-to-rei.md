# UN publishes first thematic brief warning governments to rein in AI agents

The United Nations published its first thematic brief on AI, titled "AI Agents, Misalignment and the Risk of Losing Human Control," warning governments to act before the risks are fully understood. The UN's involvement marks the first multilateral formal policy statement following King Charles's Scotland safety summit and lands the same weekend Trump-Xi are discussing an AI notification mechanism as a summit agenda item. Together with today's US-China dialogue and Trump's AI Force announcement, formal AI policy is moving fast on multiple continents simultaneously.
