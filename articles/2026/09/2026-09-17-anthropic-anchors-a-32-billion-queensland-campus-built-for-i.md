# Anthropic anchors A$32 billion Queensland campus built for inference only

Anthropic signed its first Australian data-center agreement, taking space in the proposed A$32 billion (US$21B+) Western Downs Digital Park near Dalby, Queensland, developed by Singapore-based Zerra DC across roughly 725 hectares. The campus is planned to reach 2.16 GW of capacity — a draw comparable to 1.5 million Australian households — and Anthropic will use it to run Claude for user queries rather than to train models. Anthropic targets first use in 2027; the lease requires Foreign Investment Review Board and local council approval, and the development application only reached council last month. Queensland secured a carve-out from the federal renewables mandate, so the campus will draw on coal and gas generation. - https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-anthropic-apac-australia-queensland-data-center/
