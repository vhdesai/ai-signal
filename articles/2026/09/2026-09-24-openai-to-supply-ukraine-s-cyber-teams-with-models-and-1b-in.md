# OpenAI to supply Ukraine's cyber teams with models and $1B in subsidized tokens

Under a partnership announced Wednesday, Ukrainian officials will get access to advanced OpenAI models and more than $1 billion in subsidized tokens to defend critical infrastructure against state-sponsored attackers. The same WSJ Pro edition reports cyber startup Mind Security raised $72 million led by Crosspoint Capital to guard against data leaks from increasingly autonomous AI agents. Both items point the same direction: agentic AI is now simultaneously the threat vector and the funded defense.
