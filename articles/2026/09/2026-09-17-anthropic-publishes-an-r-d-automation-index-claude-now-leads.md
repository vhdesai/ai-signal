# Anthropic Publishes an R&D Automation Index: Claude Now “Leads” 26% of Its Own AI Research

Anthropic released three measurement tools intended to make the internal pace of frontier development visible to the public. Using Epoch AI's Automation Level scale, the prototype R&D Automation Index finds Claude “leads” (AL4 — completes most of a task end-to-end from a high-level prompt under human supervision) 26% of Anthropic's AI R&D work as of August 2026, up from under 1% in February; work at “collaborates” or above exceeds 90%; and Claude is not operating fully autonomously for any measured subset. Anthropic also disclosed roughly 30,000 concurrent internal agents with online monitors blocking about 0.002% of actions (1 in 47,000), and a one-week snapshot showing 6% of AI R&D compute allocated to safety. The company flags the circularity of using a Claude judge to rate Claude's contribution, and a task basket frozen as of July. SECURITY
