# OpenAI answers 90 minutes later with GPT‑6 Sol and Luna at half the API price

OpenAI extended the GPT‑6 generation with Sol (complex coding and agentic work) and Luna (high-volume clerical tasks), cutting API prices 50% versus the GPT‑5.6 tier — Sol at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens. OpenAI attributes the reduction to caching and inference improvements, and claims GPT‑6 Sol makes about half as many factual errors as its predecessor on an internal evaluation built from user-flagged conversations. The models went live in ChatGPT Work and Codex for paid tiers, with Luna available to Free and Go users in the desktop app. The release landed roughly 90 minutes after Anthropic's.
