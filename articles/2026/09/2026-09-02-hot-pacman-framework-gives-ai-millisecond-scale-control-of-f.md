# Hot PACMAN framework gives AI millisecond-scale control of fusion plasmas

Princeton University and the DOE's Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory unveiled PACMAN, a modular AI framework that closes a multi-model control loop in roughly 20 milliseconds while enforcing hardware safety limits. Across five experiments on the DIII-D tokamak in San Diego, a reinforcement-learning model took full control of heating systems and predicted a tearing-mode instability about 200 ms in advance, avoiding it — conventional controllers can only detect such events after onset. The design and first results are published in Nuclear Fusion.
