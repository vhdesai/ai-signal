# Alibaba Open-Weights Qwen-Image-2.1 — But Moves It Off Apache 2.0

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen-Image-2.1, a unified text-to-image generation and editing model whose visual component is just 7B parameters, paired with a Qwen3-VL 8B text encoder and a 64-channel RGBA autoencoder. It natively generates and edits transparent RGBA images, accepts up to 10 reference images, supports mask- and annotation-driven local edits, and runs at native 2K, with day-zero support in Diffusers, ComfyUI, vLLM-Omni, and SGLang. The commercially material detail is the license: weights ship under a Qwen Research License barring commercial use absent a separate agreement — a shift from the Apache 2.0 terms of earlier Qwen image models, mirroring Z.ai's move off MIT for GLM-5.3.
