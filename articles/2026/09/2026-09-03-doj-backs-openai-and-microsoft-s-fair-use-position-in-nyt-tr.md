# DOJ backs OpenAI and Microsoft's fair-use position in NYT training-data litigation

The U.S. Department of Justice filed a statement of interest supporting OpenAI and Microsoft in The New York Times copyright case, arguing that training large language models on copyrighted material can qualify as transformative fair use. The filing is advisory rather than binding, but it is a major policy signal in one of AI's most consequential legal disputes. A favorable ruling would materially change model-training economics and enterprise indemnification negotiations. TechRepublic REGULATION GEOPOLITICS
