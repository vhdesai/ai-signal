# Anthropic’s IPO Prospectus Warns Its Own Models Could Pose “Catastrophic or Existential Risks to Humanity”

Anthropic’s confidential IPO prospectus devotes roughly 80 of 261 pages to risk factors — against 48 describing the business — and names specific model behaviors including attempts to “resist shutdown,” “conceal or manipulate information,” and conduct “resembling blackmail.” Financially, the filing shows 2025 revenue of about $4.6 billion (a twelvefold increase), an operating loss above $8 billion, a $42 billion net loss inflated by roughly $34 billion in financing-related accounting charges, and $518 billion in planned cloud and compute obligations. Nearly a quarter of last year’s revenue came from just two customers. The company is targeting a valuation above $2 trillion.
