# Huawei pulls Ascend 960DT forward three quarters to Q1 2027

At Huawei Connect in Shanghai, rotating chairman David Wang moved the Ascend 960DT launch from Q3 2027 to Q1 2027, with the 960PR in Q3 2027 and Ascend 970 and 980 slated for 2028 and 2029. Huawei is compensating for per-chip deficits with system scale: its Peerium architecture over UnifiedBus targets up to one million linked processors, and the Atlas 950 SuperCluster is claimed to connect up to 256,000 accelerator cards. Executives conceded production capacity cannot meet Chinese domestic demand, effectively ruling out full international expansion. Independent benchmarks against Nvidia hardware are not yet available; treat system-scale claims as vendor-reported until samples circulate. - https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/huawei-plans-q1-2027-launch-of-new-ai-chip-as-it-takes-on-nvidia/
