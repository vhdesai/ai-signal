# AMD crosses $1 trillion as agentic workloads reprice CPU demand

AMD shares rose about 10% to an intraday high of $615.52, pushing its market capitalization above $1 trillion for the first time and making it the fourth US chipmaker to do so after Nvidia, Broadcom and Micron. The move capped a five-day rally of roughly 25% and leaves the stock up more than 180% this year. The proximate catalyst was the market's read that agentic AI — sequential, tool-calling, orchestration-heavy work — consumes CPU cycles alongside GPU capacity, which favors AMD's EPYC line as much as its accelerators. AMD's Q2 data-center revenue was $6.7 billion, up 107% year over year.
