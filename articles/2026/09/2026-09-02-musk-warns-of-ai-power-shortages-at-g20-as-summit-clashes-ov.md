# Musk warns of AI power shortages at G20, as summit clashes over light-touch AI rules

Elon Musk told attendees at a G20 gathering in North Carolina that countries need to build out data-center power capacity or risk falling behind in AI, reiterating that electricity — not chips — is the binding constraint on frontier AI scaling. Separately, aggregated reporting citing Quartz described G20 squabbling over "light-touch" AI regulatory principles, with the policy debate playing out against a backdrop of straining global bond markets.
