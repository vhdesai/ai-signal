# G20 endorses U.S.-backed Carolina Principles for sector-based AI regulation

G20 nations endorsed a non-binding U.S.-backed framework favoring sector-specific, use-based AI regulation over a universal AI statute or new AI-specific regulators, according to the source feed. The move signals a light-touch international posture even as the EU continues implementing a more prescriptive AI Act. Multinationals should plan for regulatory divergence rather than convergence: sector rules in some jurisdictions, comprehensive AI law in others, and state-level obligations layered on top.
