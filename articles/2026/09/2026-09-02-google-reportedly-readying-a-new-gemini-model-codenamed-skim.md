# Google Reportedly Readying a New Gemini Model Codenamed “Skimaki”

Google is reportedly preparing a new Gemini model, codenamed Skimaki, that could be released as early as this week and is positioned to outperform rival models from OpenAI and Anthropic, particularly in coding. The report is single-source and pre-release, so treat it as directional rather than confirmed. If the coding claim holds, it lands directly against Anthropic's freshly shipped Fable 5.1 in the enterprise developer segment.
