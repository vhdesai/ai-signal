# SoftBank Launches $11B+ Junk Bond Sale to Fund Next OpenAI Tranche

SoftBank Group launched $10 billion of dollar-denominated senior unsecured notes across 3.5-, 5.5- and 7.5-year tenors plus €1 billion of euro notes, in what would rank among the largest speculative-grade corporate bond deals ever by a single issuer. Proceeds primarily fund SoftBank's $10 billion payment on the third tranche of its OpenAI follow-on investment, expected to close around October 1, and cancel an existing $10 billion bridge loan. Fitch assigned the notes BB+; pricing is slated for September 24 with settlement September 29. SoftBank has now committed close to $65 billion to OpenAI and is the largest junk-rated corporate borrower in global bond markets this year. M A N
