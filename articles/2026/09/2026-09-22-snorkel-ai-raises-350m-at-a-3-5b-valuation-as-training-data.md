# Snorkel AI raises $350M at a $3.5B valuation as training-data demand compounds

Snorkel AI closed a $350 million Series E co-led by Insight Partners and S32, nearly tripling the $1.3 billion valuation it held after its May 2025 Series D. The company says its annualized revenue run rate reached $375 million, an eighteenfold increase over twelve months, driven by its shift from data-labeling software to finished datasets and reinforcement-learning environments sold directly to frontier labs, hyperscalers and US government agencies. The signal for buyers is where margin is migrating: as model prices fall, differentiation moves from architecture to the quality of training and evaluation data. Note that a run rate is a projection, not booked revenue, and no customer concentration or margin detail was disclosed. ANALYSIS
