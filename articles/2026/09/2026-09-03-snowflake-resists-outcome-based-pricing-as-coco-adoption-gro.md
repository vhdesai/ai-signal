# Snowflake resists outcome-based pricing as CoCo adoption grows

Snowflake reported that accounts using CoCo, formerly Cortex Code, grew at least 28% from the prior quarter to 9,100. The company is adding open models alongside Anthropic and OpenAI and argues that customers value flexibility rather than commitment to one model supplier. Its 37% product-revenue growth provides a counterexample to the claim that every AI vendor must move to outcome-based billing. PAYMENTS AI OPERATIONS
