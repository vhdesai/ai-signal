# Nvidia Hires OpenAI’s Former Data Center Chief Chris Malone as VP, DSX Platform

Nvidia has reportedly appointed Chris Malone, previously OpenAI’s data center lead, as Vice President of its DSX Platform. The hire would deepen Nvidia’s push from chip supplier toward full AI-factory and data-center systems — the same direction its largest customers are moving in themselves. This item is currently reported by a single outlet and has not been confirmed by Nvidia’s newsroom; treat as unconfirmed pending corroboration.
