# Meta's Muse assistant tops 83,000 US iOS downloads and hits No. 2 on the App Store

Meta's consumer agent surpassed 83,000 US iOS downloads and reached No. 2 on the App Store Top Charts despite a slower launch. Distribution is converting into installs quickly, and the debate has shifted to subscription economics for the $20 and $100 tiers. Meta has not published an official install figure, so treat the number as trade-press estimate rather than company-reported.
