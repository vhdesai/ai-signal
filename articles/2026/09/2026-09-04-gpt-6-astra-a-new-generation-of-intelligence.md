# GPT-6 Astra: A new generation of intelligence

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra, describing it as a new generation of intelligence and its most capable broadly deployed model. OpenAI's own safety materials say Astra reaches the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework, including stronger performance on exploit-development and reverse-engineering evaluations. For enterprise leaders, the release raises both capability upside and governance requirements around access controls, monitoring, and acceptable-use boundaries. MODEL RELEASEMETACODING
