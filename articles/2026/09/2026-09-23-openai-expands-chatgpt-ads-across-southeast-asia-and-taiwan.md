# OpenAI expands ChatGPT Ads across Southeast Asia and Taiwan

Advertisers in Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan now have access to the ChatGPT Ads platform, including self-serve tools aimed at smaller businesses. It is OpenAI's largest single ad-market expansion in Asia to date. Coupled with the hiring of Patreon co-founder Sam Yam to lead a new Creator Product division, it signals renewed commercial focus on consumer monetization.
