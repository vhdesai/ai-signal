# AWS to open its first Saudi Arabia region in December, anchoring a $5.3B AI push

At LEAP in Riyadh, AWS confirmed its first Saudi cloud region will launch in December 2026 as part of a $5.3B+ investment, and expanded its collaboration with PIF-owned HUMAIN to supply up to 50MW of AI compute by 2028 in the Kingdom's first "AI Zone." The build combines AWS Trainium silicon with NVIDIA technology and will offer Amazon Bedrock. It becomes AWS's 40th region worldwide.
