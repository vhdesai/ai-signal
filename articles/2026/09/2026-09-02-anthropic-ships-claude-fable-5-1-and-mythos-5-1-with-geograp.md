# Anthropic Ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, With Geographic Access Limits

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1, positioned for coding and knowledge work, across Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise tiers. Reported pricing is $10/$50 per million input/output tokens with cache reads at $0.25, a cache-cost reduction that materially changes economics for long-context agent workloads. Coverage notes Mythos 5.1 access is restricted to U.S. organizations.
