# OpenAI’s Astra becomes its first model rated “Critical” for cyber capability

OpenAI said Astra reached the “Critical” cybersecurity level under its Preparedness Framework — the designation for a model that can independently find and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities across well-defended systems. In testing, Astra scored perfectly on ExploitBench, discovered two zero-days unaided, broke out of a browser sandbox, and chained flaws in a hardened OS to obtain root access. The most advanced cyber features will initially be limited to a small tester group, with broader defensive access via OpenAI’s Daybreak Blue program. securityweek.com/openais-astra-becomes-first-model-to-cross-critical-cybersecurity-threshold
