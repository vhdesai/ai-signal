# EU KIDS Act Would Switch AI Companions Off by Default for All Minors

The proposed EU KIDS Act (COM(2026) 681 final) extends platform child-safety rules to AI companions and conversational chatbots for the first time: default-off for minors, no designs likely to create emotional dependency, no carrying prior conversations across sessions by default, and access for under-13s only via parental controls. It also tiers social media access, bans infinite scroll and sleep-hour push notifications for minors, and shifts the burden of proof to providers. Enforcement routes through the DSA and AI Act with fines up to 6% of worldwide turnover.
