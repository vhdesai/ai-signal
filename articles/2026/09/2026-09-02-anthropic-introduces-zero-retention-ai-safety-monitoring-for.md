# Anthropic introduces zero-retention AI safety monitoring for enterprises

CSO Online reports that Anthropic introduced zero-retention AI safety monitoring for enterprise customers. The direction is strategically relevant because customers want stronger safety controls without creating new data-retention, privacy, or compliance exposure. If implemented well, privacy-preserving monitoring could become a differentiator for regulated-sector AI procurement.
