# Study: top AI experts badly underestimated how fast the field is moving

The Forecasting Research Institute's new tracking study finds median expert forecasts consistently trailed reality: AI reached IMO gold-medal level roughly five years earlier than predicted, and Anthropic's annualized revenue came in about 5× the expert median. Autonomous driving and other physical-world use cases showed the reverse — actual timelines slower than forecast. The two-sided miss makes a pointed methodological argument that expert-elicitation panels underweight capability progress on evaluable benchmarks while overweighting benchmarks that don't yet exist for the real world.
