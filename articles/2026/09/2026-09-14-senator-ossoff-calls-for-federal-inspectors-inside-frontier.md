# Senator Ossoff Calls for Federal Inspectors Inside Frontier AI Labs

Senator Jon Ossoff called for federal inspectors to be embedded inside frontier AI labs, following the OpenAI Senate probe and the coordinated Amodei/Altman/Hassabis safety warnings. The Next Web separately reports on a new Senate bill that would give Washington statutory authority to block AI models from release. Quartz reports King Charles is hosting AI executives in Scotland for safety talks. Taken together, the political attention on frontier AI escalated on three continents in a single 24-hour window.
