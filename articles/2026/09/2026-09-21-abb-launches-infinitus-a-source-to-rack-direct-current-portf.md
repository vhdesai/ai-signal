# ABB Launches Infinitus, a Source-to-Rack Direct-Current Portfolio for 1 MW-per-Rack AI Data Centers

ABB launched Infinitus, a source-to-rack direct-current portfolio for AI data centers that includes medium-voltage powertrains, DC distribution, protection, power quality, and cooling optimization. ABB says next-generation AI chips could draw up to 1 MW per rack, at which point conventional alternating-current conversion losses and power footprints become materially problematic.
