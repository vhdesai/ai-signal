# Cerebras and Compute Nordic Announce a 165 MW AI Data Center in Finland

Cerebras and Compute Nordic announced a new AI data center in Mikkeli, Finland, scaling to 165 megawatts. The project is framed as a major infrastructure investment supporting Cerebras’ fast-inference cloud expansion into Europe, where power availability and data-residency requirements are increasingly the binding constraints on capacity siting.
