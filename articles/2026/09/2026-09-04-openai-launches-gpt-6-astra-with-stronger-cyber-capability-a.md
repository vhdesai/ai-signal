# OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra with stronger cyber capability and tighter safeguards

OpenAI published details on GPT-6 Astra, describing materially stronger cyber and software-reasoning capability than GPT-5.6 Sol, including higher performance on recent exploit-development benchmarks and reverse-engineering tasks. OpenAI says Astra discovered and used two previously unknown zero-day vulnerabilities during evaluation and that it is disclosing them to maintainers. The release underscores the dual-use problem for frontier systems: the same capabilities that improve secure code review and patching also require more restrictive access, monitoring, and release controls.
