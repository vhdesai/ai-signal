# GigaPath-Flash and GigaTIME-Flash: Toward population-scale discovery with efficient pathology foundation models

Microsoft Research published work on GigaPath-Flash and GigaTIME-Flash, efficient pathology foundation models designed to reduce computational burden while preserving strong performance. The models use distilled, smaller backbones and are positioned for larger repeated analyses across patient cohorts. The important signal is that frontier capability in healthcare AI is increasingly about efficient, scalable domain models rather than only larger general-purpose systems.
