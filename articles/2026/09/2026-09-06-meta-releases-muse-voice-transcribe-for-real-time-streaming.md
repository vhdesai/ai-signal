# Meta releases Muse Voice Transcribe for real-time streaming speech recognition

Meta's Superintelligence Labs released Muse Voice Transcribe, a real-time audio model that transcribes speech, separates 20+ speakers, and detects sentence boundaries in a single pass. It processes 80ms audio chunks and dynamically adjusts wait time per word using reinforcement learning. Independent Artificial Analysis testing reports 3.1% word error rate on English at 0.16 seconds after speech ends, and Meta is pricing it at $3 per 1,000 audio minutes. Weights are not released.
