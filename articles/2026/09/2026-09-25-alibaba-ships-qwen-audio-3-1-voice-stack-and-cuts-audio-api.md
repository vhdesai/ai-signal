# Alibaba ships Qwen-Audio 3.1 voice stack and cuts audio API prices up to 95%

Alibaba's Qwen team released a five-model voice stack — upgraded ASR, TTS and Realtime models plus new TTS-Next and ASR-Next — and paired it with aggressive price cuts: ASR down up to 95%, TTS roughly 70%, Realtime roughly 85%. The upgraded ASR automatically cleans up filler words and repetitions and improves multilingual and dialect recognition. The release follows Alibaba's Apsara Conference hardware push (Qwen Book, Glasses N1, Clip, QwenNote A2) and puts further margin pressure on Western voice-API pricing.
