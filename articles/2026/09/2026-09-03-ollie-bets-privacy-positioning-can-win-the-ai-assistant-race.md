# Ollie bets privacy positioning can win the AI assistant race

Ollie, a family-focused AI assistant, is seeking deep access to users' everyday personal context while committing not to use that data for model training or to share it with third parties. The launch tests whether a contractual privacy posture is a durable differentiator in a category dominated by platform incumbents. It is a useful reference point for enterprises weighing data-use terms as a selection criterion for assistant deployments. https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/ollie-is-betting-privacy-can-win-the-ai-assistant-race/
