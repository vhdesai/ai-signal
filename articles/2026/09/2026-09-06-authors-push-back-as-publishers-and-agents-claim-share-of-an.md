# Authors push back as publishers and agents claim share of Anthropic settlement

Authors involved in the class action against Anthropic are objecting that publishers and literary agents are claiming a disproportionate share of settlement payments, complicating the distribution of what has become one of the largest AI-training copyright settlements to date. The dispute highlights that even where AI labs concede training-data liability, the downstream question of who gets paid remains unsettled. Executives tracking AI copyright exposure should treat this as the emerging template for post-settlement fragmentation, not a clean resolution. POLICY
