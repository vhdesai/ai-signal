# Obama urges Democrats to make AI safeguards a "central agenda" item

Former President Barack Obama said Democrats need to make artificial intelligence one of their "central agendas" and develop a "very clear plan" addressing both economic-impact and safety concerns. Combined with Trump's opposition to a slowdown, this positions AI safety as an explicit partisan divide going into the 2028 election cycle. For executives, this is worth noting because the regulatory environment for AI is now a live partisan variable, not a shared national-security consensus. FINANCE
