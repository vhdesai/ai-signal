# DeepSeek’s Annualized Revenue Crosses $1B After Price Hikes; Targets ~$7.5B Shanghai Round at $74B Valuation

DeepSeek’s annualized revenue run rate reached ~$1B, more than double a few months ago, after API price increases of roughly 2.3×–4.5× depending on model. Founder Liang Wenfeng disclosed the figure to investors as the company works to close a ~$7.5B financing round by late October, tied to a planned Shanghai STAR Market listing at ~$74B. The strategic signal is more consequential than the raise: the lab that built its reputation on undercutting Western frontier pricing has demonstrated pricing power without material demand loss. It also lands the same week Goldman raised hyperscaler capex to $1.2T — global training/inference demand is repricing upward in both hemispheres simultaneously. NEW EXCLUSIVE FUNDING
