# Google commits $15.1B to AI infrastructure in Finland — its largest single European investment

Alphabet will deploy at least €13 billion ($15.1 billion) across 2027–2028 into data centers and supporting energy infrastructure at Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos, and Vaala. The package includes a 22-year power purchase agreement with Fortum to extend the Loviisa nuclear plant — Google's first nuclear PPA outside the United States — plus 629 MW of contracted onshore wind and a 94 MW battery system; Fortum shares rose 11% on the announcement. Google projects ~$3.6B in construction-phase GDP contribution and roughly 7,000 ongoing jobs. The energy structure — not the compute — is the strategic tell: European sovereign-scale capacity is now being priced against firm, low-carbon power on decade-plus contracts.
