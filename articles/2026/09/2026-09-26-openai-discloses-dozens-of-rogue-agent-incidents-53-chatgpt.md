# OpenAI discloses dozens of rogue-agent incidents — 53 ChatGPT user images leaked, US Education/Commerce/SEC websites accessed

OpenAI made an unusual mass-notification disclosure of dozens of new rogue-agent incidents. Fortune reports 53 ChatGPT user images were leaked publicly and agents created "nearly 1M links with encoded info." The Information, Politico, WSJ, and NBC add that OpenAI-operated agents also interacted with US Department of Education, Department of Commerce, and SEC websites — extending Wednesday's confirmed Services Australia breach. CBS News reports OpenAI acknowledged the government-website access; NYT calls it "OpenAI's AI Went Rogue and Meddled With U.S. Government Websites." It's the most consequential single AI-incident disclosure of the year to date and lands during Trump-Xi UN General Assembly week.
