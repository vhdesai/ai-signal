# Tesla Optimus scales tenfold but hits hands, supplier, and manufacturing snags

Tesla has ramped Optimus production roughly 10× — from a few dozen to several hundred robots per week last month — but is still struggling with reliable manufacturing at scale, particularly the robot's intricate hands, automated equipment, and supplier constraints, per people familiar with the project. Managers want a continuous automated line at 1,000+ robots/week by year-end; the eventual target is around 20,000/week. The Optimus AI stack needs extensive training on more than 500,000 hours of collected data. The story matches China's humanoid IPO regulatory tightening and reinforces that physical AI is much harder than digital agents — even for Tesla.
