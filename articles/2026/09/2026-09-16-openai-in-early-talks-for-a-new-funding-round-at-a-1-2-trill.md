# OpenAI in early talks for a new funding round at a ~$1.2 trillion valuation

The Information, CNBC, and Fortune report OpenAI has held early conversations with existing investors about a new funding round that could lift its valuation to $1.2 trillion or higher, after Sam Altman said the company will not IPO in 2026. The last announced round was $122B at an $852B valuation in March; Caplight secondary-market marks have since crossed $1T on Codex momentum and GPT-6 Astra benchmarks. Anthropic's planned IPO is reportedly targeting $1.5T+. Barron's frames the $1.2T mark as a re-rate anchor for beaten-down Oracle and CoreWeave positions.
