# OpenAI in early talks for a new funding round at a $1.2 trillion valuation

The Information reports OpenAI is in early discussions for a new primary funding round that would value the company at approximately $1.2 trillion — a mark that would place it above every listed US software company outside the Magnificent Seven and re-anchor the private-market ceiling for AI. The reporting arrives in the same 24-hour window as Meta's Meta One AI subscription launch and SK Hynix–Intel memory talks, signaling continued escalation of both the capital stack and the vertical integration behind the frontier-model race. For CFOs and BD leaders, this is the reference point that AI-secondary valuations will reset against for the balance of Q4. - https://www.theinformation.com/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=OpenAI+1.2+trillion+funding+round
