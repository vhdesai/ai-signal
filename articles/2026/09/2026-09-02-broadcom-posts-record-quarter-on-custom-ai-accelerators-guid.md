# Broadcom Posts Record Quarter on Custom AI Accelerators, Guides Softer

Broadcom reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $29.6 billion, up 86% year over year, with AI semiconductor revenue of $16.7 billion — a roughly 221% increase driven by custom accelerators for hyperscale customers. The company began production shipments of Google's next-generation TPU v8i during the quarter. Shares fell after hours on a Q4 outlook that landed below the most aggressive expectations, a useful reminder that custom-silicon demand is lumpy and program-timing dependent even in a strong cycle. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/broadcom-avgo-q3-earnings-report-2026.html
