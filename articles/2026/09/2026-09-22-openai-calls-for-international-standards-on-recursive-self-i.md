# OpenAI calls for international standards on recursive self-improvement

OpenAI published a policy piece urging coordinated global standards on evaluation, reporting, and governance for AI systems that can improve their own successors. The company argues the US should lead on the measurement stack and oversight rules while warning that without guardrails "humans could lose control" over the RSI process. The framing appears aimed at pre-empting a fragmented rulemaking cycle across the EU AI Act, the Newsom California kill-switch order, and the emerging UN AI science-panel process.
