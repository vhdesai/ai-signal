# Fal and Fireworks AI raising at $15B–$30B on soaring inference demand

Inference-serving startups are the year's cleanest AI-infrastructure trade. The Information reports Fal — which specializes in video and image inference for models like Google's Nano Banana — is in early talks at a $15 billion valuation on $800M revenue pace, with prospective marks of $17–20B. Fireworks AI is considering a $30 billion valuation round. Both are riding open-source model demand and the same cost-collapse trends visible in Alibaba Qwen Audio 3.1's 95% price cut and OpenAI's Sol/Luna 50% API cut. Combined with yesterday's DensityAI $10B chip valuation, "reduce Nvidia dependency + own the inference layer" is now a first-order private-market thesis.
