# DensityAI, one-year-old ex-Tesla-Dojo startup, nears $10B valuation with AWS purchase-commitment kicker

The Information reports DensityAI — an AI chip startup founded a year ago by former leaders of Tesla's Dojo supercomputer program — is in advanced talks to raise hundreds of millions at a $10B valuation. The company's chips appear early in development and may be years from mass production, but the pitch includes a preliminary agreement in which AWS would purchase eventual chips if they meet performance requirements. Combined with today's Anthropic-Akamai deal, the "reduce Nvidia dependency" trade is now a first-order private-market thesis. Neuralink alum Precision Neuroscience separately raised $250M Series D led by Pershing Square in the same PitchBook cycle.
