# Trending Moonshot AI confidentially files for Hong Kong IPO at roughly $50B

China’s Moonshot AI, developer of the Kimi model family, has confidentially filed for a Hong Kong listing at a reported valuation near $50 billion. Backers include Alibaba, Tencent and HSG. A successful listing would establish a public-market benchmark for Chinese frontier labs. Because the filing is confidential, terms and timing should be treated as provisional.
