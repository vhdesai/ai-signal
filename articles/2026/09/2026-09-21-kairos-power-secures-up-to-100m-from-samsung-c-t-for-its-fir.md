# Kairos Power Secures up to $100M from Samsung C&T for Its First Reactor, with Google as Offtaker

Kairos Power, a future Google power supplier, signed a deal with Samsung C&T worth up to $100 million to help build its first 50-megawatt nuclear plant. The financing shows AI datacenter demand pulling advanced nuclear directly into the hyperscaler supply chain, with Google as the anchor offtaker. Power procurement continues to move upstream from site selection into generation-asset financing.
