# Google DeepMind's WeatherNext 3 learns weather directly from live satellite data

Google's WeatherNext 3 drops traditional numerical weather prediction inputs and trains directly on live geostationary satellite data plus weather station observations. It delivers hourly forecasts at up to 5km resolution — roughly five times sharper than WeatherNext 2 — with reported precipitation-accuracy gains of up to 60% versus IMERG on medium-range forecasts. The model adds turbine-height wind and irradiance outputs for renewable energy planning, and now powers weather features in Search, Maps, Gemini, and Earth Engine.
