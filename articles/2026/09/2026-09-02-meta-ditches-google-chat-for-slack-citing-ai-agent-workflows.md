# Meta ditches Google Chat for Slack, citing AI agent workflows

Meta is moving its internal communications off Google Chat and onto Slack, according to an internal memo reported by Business Insider. AI chief Alexandr Wang reportedly tied the switch to how the company wants employees and AI agents to collaborate going forward — a win for Salesforce-owned Slack, which has leaned heavily into agentic AI positioning amid a difficult stock year. *Source URL could not be independently verified within the search budget; story retained per editorial policy.*
