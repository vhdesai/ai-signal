# Crusoe Raises $3.9B Series F at $30.9B; AI Infrastructure Megarounds Continue

Denver-based AI-infrastructure provider Crusoe raised a $3.9B Series F at a $30.9B valuation, led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital, and Valor Equity Partners, with Nvidia, Founders Fund, GIC, QIA, and TPG participating. Alongside Nscale's ~$35B IPO filing and CoreWeave's $3.7B convertible, it is a busy week for non-hyperscaler AI-compute financings.
