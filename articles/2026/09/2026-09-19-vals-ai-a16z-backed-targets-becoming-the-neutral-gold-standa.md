# Vals AI (a16z-backed) targets becoming the neutral "gold standard" for AI benchmarking

Vals AI, backed by Andreessen Horowitz, is pitching itself as a neutral, trustworthy benchmarking resource — a lane opening up as model vendors increasingly train against public benchmarks and as ICLR is drowning in AI-generated papers. It joins Andon Labs (Vending-Bench), Artificial Analysis, and Irregular in a rapidly professionalizing third-party AI-evaluation category. For enterprise buyers, this segment is now worth building into procurement — vendor-reported benchmarks alone are increasingly unreliable inputs. Infrastructure FUNDING
