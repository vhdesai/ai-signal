# Anthropic Weighs a New Model to Blunt GPT-6 Astra — Days After Calling for a Slowdown

Reuters reports, citing three sources, that Anthropic is considering releasing a new model to counter OpenAI's momentum since the September 3 launch of GPT-6 Astra, ahead of an IPO now expected to be marketed no earlier than mid-October. Astra accounted for roughly 13% of enterprise AI spending tracked by expense platform Ramp versus about 8% for Claude Fable, and OpenAI models drew more developer spend than Anthropic's on OpenRouter last week — the first time in more than two and a half years. Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate topped $65B by end-July against roughly $40B for OpenAI, so the pressure is on positioning rather than scale.
