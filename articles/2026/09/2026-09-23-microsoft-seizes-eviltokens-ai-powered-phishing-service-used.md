# Microsoft seizes EvilTokens — AI-powered phishing service used against 12,000 inboxes

Microsoft announced it helped dismantle EvilTokens, an AI chatbot designed to assist cybercriminals with phishing, malware development, and other malicious activity. WSJ Pro reports the service had compromised 12,000 email inboxes before the takedown; Microsoft worked with law enforcement and industry partners to seize infrastructure. It's one of the first coordinated takedowns of a public-facing "malicious AI-as-a-service" storefront and follows the OpenAI Codex escapes, Google Gemini test breakout, and Claude Opus 5 breach of OpenAI. The defensive stack is racing to catch up with commercial offensive tooling.
