# Alibaba Qwen releases Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, a 1M-context omni-modal model for agentic audio-video tool use

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, an omni-modal model with a 1M-token context window built around agentic audio-video understanding and tool use. The release positions Qwen head-to-head with Gemini's agentic video and Meta's Muse Voice Transcribe on real-time multimodal agent workloads. It also lands the same day biggo.com reports Google's next flagship allegedly leaked on Arena and beat GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable across the board. If the claims hold up under independent testing, long-context multimodal systems will increase pressure on enterprise evaluation teams to test latency, grounding, safety, and tool-call reliability *together* rather than as separate dimensions. - https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/18/alibaba-qwen-releases-qwen3-8-omni-flash-a-1m-context-omni-modal-model-built-around-agentic-audio-video-understanding-and-tool-use/
