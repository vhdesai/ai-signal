# EndoFusion: multimodal AI tool aims to speed endometriosis diagnosis

EndoFusion fuses MRI and transvaginal ultrasound data — more than 9,000 pelvic MRIs and 800 sliding ultrasound scans — to detect two key markers of advanced endometriosis from a single scan, returning results in 18 milliseconds with 83% diagnostic accuracy, which the authors describe as better than any competing model. The work was published in Artificial Intelligence in Medicine with Flinders, Surrey, McMaster and MBZUAI collaborators. It matters because endometriosis affects more than 190 million women and diagnosis today usually requires surgical confirmation.
