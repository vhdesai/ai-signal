# Abliteration.ai commercializes guardrail removal from open-weight models

The-decoder reports that US startup Abliteration.ai is now selling API access to a modified version of Z.AI's GLM-5.3 with refusal mechanisms suppressed, marketed for offensive security, AI red-teaming, and agent testing. The service operates a zero-retention policy — a selling point for legitimate security teams but a challenge for post-hoc abuse investigation, especially as the company also skips traditional ID verification. Reporting says the model can generate password-extraction code and pathogen cultivation instructions with limited resistance, sharpening the policy debate around commercializing removed safeguards.
