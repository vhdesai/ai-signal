# Vals Raises $40M to Become the Independent Standard for AI Benchmarking

Vals AI, founded in 2024, raised a $40M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz following a seed round led by 8VC and Bloomberg Beta. It keeps test materials private to prevent training-to-the-test and evaluates models on domain tasks in law, finance and coding, plus benchmarks for recursive self-improvement, cybersecurity and biosecurity. Revenue is 8x last year’s and headcount tripled to 25; it recently launched model evaluations for federal agencies. This is the venture-funded counterpart to the embedded-evaluator and self-regulatory models above — three competing shapes for the same assurance market.
