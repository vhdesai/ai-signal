# PitchBook: Should PE partners know how to use AI? Carlyle at 90% adoption, 200% year-over-year usage

PitchBook's Weekend Pitch profiles Strattam Capital's Bob Morse writing his first code since college — using Claude Code to build a portfolio-performance dashboard that saved his team quarterly hours. Carlyle's chief information officer Lúcia Soares says 90% of Carlyle employees now use AI, with firm-wide usage up more than 200% over the past 12 months. The Weekend Pitch also flags Alexandre Armbruster (Caisse des Dépôts) saying the "specialist game" of LP due diligence has become "even more of an expert game" as AI resilience and AI opportunities become mandatory diligence KPIs.
