# Frontier-Lab Researchers Escalate Calls to Slow Development

Researchers at OpenAI and Anthropic went public with calls to slow frontier development after an Anthropic researcher resigned accusing both labs of "gambling with our lives." Anthropic alignment lead Evan Hubinger put the probability of catastrophic outcomes above 10%; OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki wrote that progress is likely to reach recursive self-improvement and that "this is a time that calls for extreme caution."
