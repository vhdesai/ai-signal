# OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra, Its Most Capable — and Most Contested — Model

OpenAI released Astra on Thursday, positioning it as "a new frontier on computer and browser use" and its best software-engineering model to date, with benchmark results placing it above GPT-5.6 Sol and Anthropic's Fable on bug finding, terminal tasks and codebase queries. Access opened first to customers in OpenAI's Daybreak cybersecurity program, with Pro, Plus, Enterprise, Business and API availability rolling out over the following week. The model is controversial for its use of opaque recurrence, a reasoning technique that reduces chain-of-thought auditability; chief scientist Jakub Pachocki acknowledged that "monitorability is getting more challenging" as capability rises. Greg Brockman declined to formally declare AGI — noting the Microsoft contract trigger no longer exists — while stating that personally, "I do think we're there." https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/openai-launches-astra-its-powerful-and-controversial-new-model/
