# OpenAI Counters 90 Minutes Later With GPT-6 Sol and Luna at Half Price

OpenAI extended the GPT-6 family below flagship Astra with Sol at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens — 50% cuts against GPT-5.6 equivalents, confirmed to VentureBeat as permanent rather than promotional. Both carry a 1.05M-token context window and a 90% cached-read discount. OpenAI's own AutomationBench figures put Sol at 33.2% at $0.27 per task versus Claude Opus 5 at 26.9% and roughly 11x the cost, though these are vendor benchmarks. The caveat worth reading twice: on DeepSWE and OSWorld 2.0, Sol scores below the GPT-5.6 Sol it replaces, so agentic-coding pipelines should benchmark before migrating.
