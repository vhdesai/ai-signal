# Apple unveils iPhone Duo and Siri AI at Ternus's first event

At the "Surprise and Shine" keynote — John Ternus's first as CEO — Apple introduced the $1,999 iPhone Duo (7.6-inch inner display, 5.4-inch outer, A20 Pro, in-house C2 modem), the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max at a $100 premium to last year, Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, and AirPods 5. Ternus argued the iPhone is the best device for AI, with Apple Intelligence 2, Siri AI, and Apple Reference Image for photo provenance. Apple shares traded down through most of the presentation. The strategic gap remains — Apple has no frontier model of its own, and its most likely partner has an October 1 hearing against it.
