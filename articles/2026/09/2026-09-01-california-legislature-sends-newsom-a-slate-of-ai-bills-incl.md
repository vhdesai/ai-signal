# California Legislature Sends Newsom a Slate of AI Bills, Including Limits on “Robo Bosses”

On the final night of its 2026 session, the California Legislature passed a package of AI-related bills, including a measure barring employers from relying solely on AI to discipline or terminate workers. Governor Newsom has 30 days to sign or veto. The package marks a strategic shift from a single flagship AI statute toward broader, sector-specific regulation — a pattern other states are likely to follow.
