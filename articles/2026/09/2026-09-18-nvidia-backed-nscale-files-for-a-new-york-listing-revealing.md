# Nvidia-backed Nscale files for a New York listing, revealing $103B in contracts against $141M of first-half revenue

Nscale filed a Form S-1 to list on the NYSE under "NSCL," disclosing a $1.02B net loss on $140.6M of revenue for the six months to June 30 — revenue up 1,252% year over year. The filing shows extreme customer concentration: one customer accounted for over half of first-half revenue, and Microsoft and Anthropic agreements together account for the large majority of its disclosed contract value. Nscale held roughly 25,000 active GPUs against 461,000 active or contracted, with line of sight to 10 gigawatts of power. For buyers of AI capacity, the filing is a useful read on how much neocloud capacity is contracted versus actually delivered.
