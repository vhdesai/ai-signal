# Salesforce and AWS expand AI integrations across Slack and Amazon Quick

Salesforce and AWS announced an expanded collaboration that embeds Salesforce CRM context natively into Amazon Quick, brings AWS frontier agents into Slack, widens zero-copy data access across both platforms, and adds real-time voice interoperability between Agentforce Voice and Amazon Connect. Pipeline, account, and service-case data reaches Quick with no migration or custom integration. AWS's Rahul Pathak framed it as "data access without migration, model choice at the right economics, and AI agents where their people already work."
