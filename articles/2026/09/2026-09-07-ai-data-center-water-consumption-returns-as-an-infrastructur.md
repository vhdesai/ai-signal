# AI data-center water consumption returns as an infrastructure constraint

IndexBox surfaced new analysis of AI data-center water consumption and mitigation options, reflecting growing scrutiny of the physical footprint behind AI capacity expansion. The issue is becoming a board-level infrastructure concern because water use, energy availability, permitting, and local opposition can directly affect build schedules and costs. For executives, sustainability constraints should be modeled as deployment risk, not treated as post-hoc ESG reporting.
