# Jeffrey Katzenberg teams up with former OpenAI Sora head on new AI video startup

Hollywood mogul Jeffrey Katzenberg is teaming with the former head of OpenAI's Sora app to launch a new AI video startup that would train its own video models and target filmmakers, per people familiar with the matter. The founders are talking to potential investors including Andreessen Horowitz about a significant funding round. It is the highest-profile Hollywood-plus-frontier-lab pairing yet in video generation, and it adds another well-capitalized rival to Runway, Pika, and OpenAI's own Sora — a category where studio distribution muscle plus former Sora leadership could reshape competitive dynamics quickly.
