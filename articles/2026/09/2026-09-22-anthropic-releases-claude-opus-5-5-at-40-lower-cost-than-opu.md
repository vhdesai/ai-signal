# Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5 at ~40% lower cost than Opus 5

Anthropic launched Opus 5.5 — first model in a new generation — calling it "the strongest-performing model we've tested to date." Anthropic's own benchmarks show Opus 5.5 matching Claude Fable 5.1 on most tasks at roughly 40% lower runtime cost than Opus 5, and edging out GPT-6 Astra despite being cheaper. Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are expected within weeks. The release, dropped hours before GPT-6 Sol/Luna, punctuated a coordinated pricing squeeze on the frontier tier.
