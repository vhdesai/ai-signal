# The Weekend: keeping Meta’s Muse on a short leash

Nick Wingfield offers a first-person assessment of Meta’s fast-growing personal AI agent Muse, arguing for tightly constrained permissions given the agent’s ability to browse, email and transact on a user’s behalf. It follows The Information’s September 25 report that Meta added an extra safety warning to Muse after a bug-bounty researcher found a vulnerability in its dedicated cloud VM. The piece is a consumer-side echo of the same agent-permission problem enterprises are now facing.
