# LITEON Invests $176M for 25% of Liquid-Cooling Specialist DCX

LITEON Technology announced a strategic investment of $176 million for a 25% stake in DCX Liquid Cooling Systems, a Warsaw-based supplier of liquid cooling for AI and high-performance computing. Rack densities driven by current-generation accelerators have made liquid cooling a requirement rather than an option, and supply of qualified vendors is thin.
