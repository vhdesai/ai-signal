# MIT Study: Student "Cognitive Surrender" to AI Is Degrading Learning Outcomes

A new MIT study warns of "cognitive surrender" — a measurable erosion in student confidence, learning retention, and assessment integrity as AI use becomes ubiquitous. The findings sit alongside earlier Stanford reporting on young workers losing AI-exposed jobs and Purdue/Elon research on rising AI-companion-driven social isolation. Taken together, the pattern suggests universities need to redesign both curriculum and evaluation before AI dependencies harden further — a policy question CTOs of talent-heavy firms should track directly, since it affects hiring pipelines.
