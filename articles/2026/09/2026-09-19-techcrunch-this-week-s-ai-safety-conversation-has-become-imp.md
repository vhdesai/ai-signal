# TechCrunch: this week's AI safety conversation has become impossible to parse

Julie Bort at TechCrunch writes that two viral AI-safety exchanges this week — Amodei's pacing essay and Coxon's extinction-risk resignation on one side, Huang's "not an alien mind" retort on the other — have made it functionally impossible for outside observers to tell serious risk claims from marketing. The piece is analysis, not new reporting, but it is the clearest single expression of the growing "signal fatigue" problem executives will now navigate. For CIOs, the practical takeaway is to weight independent technical incident evidence (this week's sandbox breakouts, US Navy hallucination near-miss, Claude→OpenAI breach) over CEO essays and X threads.
