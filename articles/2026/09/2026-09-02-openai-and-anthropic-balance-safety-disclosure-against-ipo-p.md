# OpenAI and Anthropic balance safety disclosure against IPO positioning

Axios analyzed the same-day Fable 5.1 and Astra announcements as evidence of a structural tension: both labs must convince public-market investors of durable growth while simultaneously disclosing frontier risk to governments. The two releases pulled in opposite directions — Anthropic emphasized cost and enterprise control, OpenAI emphasized capability that requires restraint. IPO preparation raises the cost of any safety disclosure that reads as a shipping delay. Boards evaluating vendor dependency should watch how that incentive resolves. https://www.axios.com/2026/09/02/openai-anthropic-fable-astra-ipo
