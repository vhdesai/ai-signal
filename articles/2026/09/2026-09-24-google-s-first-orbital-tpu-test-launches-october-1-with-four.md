# Google's first orbital TPU test launches October 1 with four chips and 1kW of solar

Google confirmed that Project Suncatcher's first in-orbit hardware test, a refrigerator-sized satellite named MVP built with Planet Labs, will launch October 1 aboard SpaceX's Transporter-18 rideshare. It carries four Trillium TPUs drawing about one kilowatt of solar power and will run Gemini workloads — but only in roughly 15-minute bursts, because radiative cooling cannot keep pace. Google reports the TPUs survived proton-beam testing at UC Davis above a five-year mission dose. Senior vice president James Manyika was explicit that nothing usefully operational is expected for years. POLICY
