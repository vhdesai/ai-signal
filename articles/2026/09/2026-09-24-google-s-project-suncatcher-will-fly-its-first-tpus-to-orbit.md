# Google’s Project Suncatcher Will Fly Its First TPUs to Orbit on SpaceX Transporter-18

Sundar Pichai said Google’s Project Suncatcher will place a TPU prototype in low Earth orbit aboard SpaceX’s Transporter-18 rideshare, in partnership with Planet Labs, to test AI accelerators in space. It is a feasibility prototype for solar-powered orbital machine learning — not an operational space data center — and results will turn on thermal behaviour and radiation tolerance. Planet Labs shares rose on the announcement. Treat it as a long-dated option on power-constrained compute, not a near-term capacity plan.
