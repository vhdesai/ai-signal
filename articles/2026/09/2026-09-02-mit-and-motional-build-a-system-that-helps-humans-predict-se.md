# MIT and Motional build a system that helps humans predict self-driving errors

MIT CSAIL researchers and autonomous-vehicle firm Motional developed the Concept-Wrapper Network (CW-Net), which translates an opaque driving planner's reasoning into human-readable concepts in real time without degrading performance. Trained on 130 million labeled scene examples and deployed on a Motional robotaxi on a private track, CW-Net helped safety drivers better anticipate vehicle behavior — in one case revealing the car had stopped via emergency braking rather than by detecting a cyclist. The work, co-led by MIT professor Julie Shah and former postdoc Eoin Kenny, appears in Nature.
