# John Ternus takes over as Apple CEO with the company trailing in frontier AI

John Ternus formally became Apple's chief executive, ending Tim Cook's 15-year tenure, with Cook remaining executive chair. Ternus, a hardware engineer who joined Apple in 2001, inherits memory-price inflation, China exposure and an AI strategy still centered on on-device features rather than frontier models. His first public test is the September 9 product event. The succession converts Apple's AI position from a product question into a CEO-level mandate.
