# New IBM Study Finds AI Adoption Is Outpacing K-12 Readiness

IBM released U.S. research showing that AI is already routine in many K-12 classrooms while training, guidance, and transparency lag. The study found 76% of middle school and 73% of high school educators report weekly AI use, but only 20% of educators say they have received extensive AI training. IBM also announced a K-12 AI leaders fellowship, positioning responsible AI adoption as an education-system capability gap. ORACLEWORKFORCEAI LEARNING
