# China reports removing 5.6M pieces of content in AI misuse crackdown

China's internet regulator reported removing more than 5.6M pieces of unlawful or rule-violating content in a campaign targeting AI misuse, including synthetic misinformation, impersonation, and content affecting minors. It offers one of the clearest views of state-scale enforcement of AI-generated-media rules.
