# Anthropic signs $11.6B Akamai cloud deal, taking compute spend past $500B in 11 months

Anthropic reportedly inked a seven-year, $11.6B cloud contract with Akamai and will receive a warrant for up to 5% of Akamai's equity. Combined with earlier commitments, Anthropic's disclosed compute-purchase footprint totals roughly $517B over 11 months — a figure Dario Amodei has publicly said would make the company vulnerable to bankruptcy if revenue forecasts slip even modestly. The deal also broadens Anthropic's supplier mix beyond AWS/Google/Nvidia into a CDN-and-edge player, a hedge against the Stargate-class concentration risk investors are now scrutinizing at Nscale.
