# Microsoft AI Ships MAI-Transcribe-2 at $0.10 an Hour, Undercutting OpenAI and Google

Microsoft AI released MAI-Transcribe-2 at an introductory $0.10 per audio hour through the end of 2026 — roughly 72% below the $0.36 it charged for the prior model five months ago. The model covers 60 languages (up from 43), adds diarization and word-level timestamps, and Microsoft claims a 5.2% average word-error rate on FLEURS. Coverage continued to expand through September 4–5. At 100,000 hours of audio the price move takes annual spend from about $36,000 to $10,000, which is large enough to force rebids on existing transcription contracts. Products & Tools PRODUCT
