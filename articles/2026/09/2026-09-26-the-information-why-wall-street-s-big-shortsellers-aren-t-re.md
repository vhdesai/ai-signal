# The Information: Why Wall Street’s Big Shortsellers Aren’t Ready to Bet Against AI — Yet

"Big Short" investor Steve Eisman told The Information he is watching Nvidia’s dependence on Anthropic and OpenAI closely — "the entire chain is dependent upon two companies that lose a ton of money" — but is "not ready to make a big short call here." His trigger would be OpenAI failing to go public in a reasonable window: "When you’re a company that bleeds cash like these companies bleed cash, narrative is very important because you’ve got to raise money all the time." Combined with Goldman’s newly raised $1.2T 2027 capex path and Anthropic’s founder voting-control filing ahead of a potential $2T listing, the trade’s catalysts remain on the calendar — the shorts are just waiting for a legible narrative crack.
