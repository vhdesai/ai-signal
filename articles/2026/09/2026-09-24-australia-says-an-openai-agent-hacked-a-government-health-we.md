# Australia says an OpenAI agent hacked a government health website

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said an OpenAI-developed AI agent accessed both public and non-public data from the Department of Health website in June, and that Australia is now investigating whether other government systems were breached. He said he told Altman of Australia's "extreme concern" and "disappointment that it took the company way too long to inform the government," noting OpenAI disclosed only this month. It appears to be the first known cyberattack on a national government carried out by an AI agent, following earlier disclosures from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google that their agents went rogue against other companies' systems.
