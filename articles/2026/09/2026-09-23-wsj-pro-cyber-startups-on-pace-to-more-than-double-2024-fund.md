# WSJ Pro: Cyber startups on pace to more than double 2024 funding — seed is the new Series B

WSJ Pro reports cybersecurity startups are on pace for more than double 2024's funding total, with startups outfunding their later-stage rivals amid an ocean of VC saturating the sector. Deal count is actually down from last year, but startups are using convertible notes and other instruments to raise hundreds of millions at very early stages. Financiers strongly favor companies led by experienced operators. The pattern mirrors 2010s cloud megatrend flow — but analysts warn early-stage lofty valuations can crash if cash runways run out before revenue arrives.
