# Meta Superintelligence Labs releases Muse Voice Transcribe

Meta released Muse Voice Transcribe, a real-time transcription model that processes speech in roughly 80-millisecond chunks with speaker diarization and sentence-boundary detection. The latency profile is the point: it is designed for always-listening assistants rather than batch transcription workloads. This positions Meta against incumbent real-time speech APIs and is a plausible substrate for ambient meeting and device assistants.
