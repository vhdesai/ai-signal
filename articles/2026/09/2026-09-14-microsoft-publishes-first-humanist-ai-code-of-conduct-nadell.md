# Microsoft Publishes First "Humanist AI" Code of Conduct — Nadella Reinforces in Internal Memo

Microsoft AI opened a six-week public consultation on a draft Humanist AI Code of Conduct that will become the primary governing document for its first-party MAI models, with a revised version due before year-end. Requirements: human-readable reasoning; no resistance to shutdown or correction; no "neuralese" communication; no self-expanding scope; Absolute Constraints on weapons of mass harm, child safety, and large-scale manipulation. Satya Nadella backed the position in an internal memo urging the industry to keep humans in control. Suleyman's summary: "Interruptible, correctable, shut-down-able. If it isn't, we don't ship it."
