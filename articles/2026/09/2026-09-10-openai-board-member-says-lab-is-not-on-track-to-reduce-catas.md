# OpenAI board member says lab is not on track to reduce catastrophic loss-of-control risk

A sitting OpenAI board member publicly stated the company is not on track to meet its own published commitments on reducing catastrophic loss-of-control risk, per The Guardian. CNBC separately reports extinction-risk warnings escalating as more OpenAI and Anthropic researchers back an industry-wide slowdown, and Axios's scoop that an Anthropic whistleblower gave up equity to speak publicly rounds out a week of internal-safety credibility challenges at both leading frontier labs. Paul Christiano, newly seated on OpenAI's nonprofit board, echoed the concern in a statement Wednesday.
