# OpenAI Expands OpenAI Academy with Role-Based Learning Paths

OpenAI expanded its OpenAI Academy program with new structured learning paths for distinct audiences — employees, developers, leaders, educators and students. The paths are designed so users build and demonstrate verifiable, practical AI skills rather than work through general documentation. It is a credentialing and enterprise-adoption motion as much as an education one, and lands the same day as OpenAI's mathematics advisory group announcement.
