# Lightspeed targets $250M for an India fund dedicated entirely to early-stage AI

Lightspeed is raising Lightspeed India Partners V at a $250 million target — half the size of its 2022 predecessor — with 80% of commitments already secured, per an investor letter. The fund is dedicated to AI across India and Southeast Asia, a narrower thesis than the firm’s historically sector-agnostic India portfolio, and is built around a compressed ~2.5-year investment period. It is also the first Lightspeed India fund aligned to the firm’s global fundraising cycle. Lightspeed manages more than $65 billion globally and backs Anthropic, xAI, Databricks and India’s Sarvam AI.
