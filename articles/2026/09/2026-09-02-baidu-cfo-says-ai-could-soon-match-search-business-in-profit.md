# Baidu CFO says AI could soon match search business in profit and cash returns

TechNode reports that Baidu’s CFO said AI could soon match the company’s search business in profit and cash returns. The statement is notable because it frames AI not only as a defensive response to search disruption, but as a potential second profit pool. For executives, the key question is whether agent, cloud, and model-service revenue can scale with search-like margins.
