# Crusoe reportedly raises $3B at a $30B valuation

AI data center developer Crusoe has reportedly raised a $3 billion round at a $30 billion valuation, per Bloomberg reporting relayed by TechCrunch. Crusoe counts Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI among its customers, and the round is reported to have come together after the company secured a roughly $13 billion contract with Jane Street.
