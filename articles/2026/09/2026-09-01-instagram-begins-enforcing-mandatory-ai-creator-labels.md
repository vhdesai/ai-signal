# Instagram Begins Enforcing Mandatory AI-Creator Labels

Instagram is now penalizing AI-generated creator accounts that do not self-identify with an AI creator label. The enforcement push is one of the first platform-level provenance regimes applied to synthetic personas rather than to individual pieces of content, and it sets a precedent that disclosure obligations may attach to the account, not just the post.
