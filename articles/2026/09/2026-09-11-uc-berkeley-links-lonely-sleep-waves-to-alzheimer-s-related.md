# UC Berkeley Links "Lonely" Sleep Waves to Alzheimer's-Related Tau Buildup

Postdoctoral researcher Omer Sharon and colleagues, working under Matthew Walker, published in Nature Neuroscience a mechanistic link between tau accumulation in the frontal cortex and the breakdown of traveling slow waves during non-REM sleep. Combining high-density EEG with PET imaging and spinal-fluid analysis with Washington University in St. Louis, the team found older adults' slow waves traveled shorter distances and in isolation, correlating with weaker overnight memory retention. The effect appeared in participants without an Alzheimer's diagnosis, suggesting a pre-clinical marker. RESEARCH
