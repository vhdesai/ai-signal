# GDIT joins OpenAI Partner Network for federal AI modernization

Homeland Security Today reported that General Dynamics Information Technology joined the OpenAI Partner Network to help deliver secure, mission-focused AI capabilities for federal agencies. The partnership reflects a maturing go-to-market pattern for frontier AI in regulated environments, where delivery partners must combine model access with compliance, procurement, security, and mission-system integration. The item is especially relevant for public-sector AI adoption because implementation capacity may become as important as model selection. Infrastructure DATA CENTERS LOCAL POLITICS
