# Weathernews launches global app with AI-powered high-resolution forecasts

Markets Insider reported that Weathernews launched a global weather app using AI-powered forecasting, 1 km grid forecasts, 10-minute rain radar, 5-minute short-term forecasts, and advisories from more than 140 countries. The app also includes AI weather explanations in local languages. This is a concrete example of domain AI moving into public-facing operational products where accuracy, localization, and trust are more important than generic chatbot capability.
