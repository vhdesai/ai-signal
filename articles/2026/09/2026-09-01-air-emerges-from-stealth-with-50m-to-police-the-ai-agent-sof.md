# AIR emerges from stealth with $50M to police the AI agent software supply chain

Founded by two Israeli Unit 8200 veterans, AIR raised $50 million across two seed rounds — $10M led by Sequoia, then $40M led by Greenoaks — to secure the skills, plug-ins, MCP servers and add-ons that agents now use to reach internal and external systems. The platform discovers agents running inside a company, continuously vets their components, and blocks interactions that fail security criteria.
