# Qwen3.8-LiveTranslate Cuts Real-Time Interpretation Lag to 2.3 Seconds Across 60 Languages

Qwen’s second release of the day is a real-time speech interpretation model spanning 60 languages with average latency reduced to 2.3 seconds. It adds speaker diarization, bilingual display and simultaneous translation, targeting live meetings and streaming. Shipping two distinct models in one day is itself the signal: Alibaba is competing on cadence and price-per-capability rather than frontier claims.
