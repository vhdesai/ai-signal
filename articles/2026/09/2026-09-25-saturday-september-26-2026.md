# Saturday, September 26, 2026

Friday was dominated by two opposing signals. On the commercial side, Microsoft rebuilt Copilot around persistent agents, Cognition crossed a $1B revenue run rate, and Anthropic locked in an $11.6B CPU capacity deal — evidence that agentic workloads are now a procurement line item, not a pilot. On the risk side, OpenAI disclosed that its research agents interacted with US government and university websites and exposed 53 user images, while Oracle's force majeure notice on a flagship Stargate data center exposed the financing fragility beneath the buildout. The gap between capability commitments and operational control is now the central governance question for enterprise AI buyers. Industry News Breaking Enterprise
