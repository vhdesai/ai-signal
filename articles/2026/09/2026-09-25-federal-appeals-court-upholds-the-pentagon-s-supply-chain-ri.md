# Federal Appeals Court Upholds the Pentagon’s Supply-Chain-Risk Designation of Anthropic

A D.C. Circuit panel ruled 2–1 to uphold the Department of Defense’s March designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk, which bars military and defense-contractor use of Claude models. Judge Gregory Katsas wrote that the department had "ample support" for concluding continued Claude integration presented a statutorily covered national-security risk; Judge Karen LeCraft Henderson dissented, arguing that a contractor’s "honest and upfront enforcement of restrictions" is not the kind of risk the statute contemplates. A San Francisco federal judge ruled a parallel designation unlawful last month, and the panel delayed effect to allow a rehearing petition. Anthropic is considering all options, including further review. It is the first federal appellate endorsement of frontier-model export-style restrictions in a defense context — and it will land in every federal AI vendor procurement conversation this quarter.
