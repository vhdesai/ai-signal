# U.S. and China agree to a “super intelligence” dialogue amid AI tensions

Axios reported that the U.S. and China agreed to a “super intelligence” dialogue amid rising AI tensions. The move follows recent discussion of incident-notification mechanisms between the two countries and highlights AI’s shift into strategic-stability diplomacy. Even if near-term outcomes are limited, a formal dialogue suggests both sides recognize that frontier AI incidents could have cross-border security implications.
