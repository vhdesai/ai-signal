The Information reported DeepSeek plans to install at least 160,000 Huawei AI chips in Inner Mongolia, while ByteDance is borrowing roughly $30B as AI infrastructure spending grows. The moves show China's top AI players shifting from model competition into domestic compute procurement and capital formation.
