# Google opens preorders for $899 Googlebook, an AI-native laptop built around Gemini

Google opened preorders for Googlebook, a $899 AI-powered laptop that runs Android OS with desktop Chrome elements and foregrounds Gemini features. The device includes an AI cursor, AI-enhanced dictation through Rambler, vibe-coded widgets, and a year of Google AI Pro. The strategic question is whether AI features can drive a hardware refresh cycle, particularly in education where Google says tens of millions of Chromebooks are already deployed.
