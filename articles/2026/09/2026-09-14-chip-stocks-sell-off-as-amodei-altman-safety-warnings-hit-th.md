# Chip Stocks Sell Off as Amodei/Altman Safety Warnings Hit the AI Trade

Intel fell ~7%, AMD ~6%, and NVIDIA pulled back sharply as investors priced in the possibility of a "pacing" moment following the coordinated safety warnings from Anthropic's Dario Amodei, OpenAI's Sam Altman, and DeepMind's Demis Hassabis. Memory names were hit hardest — Micron and SanDisk down 6%, SK Hynix down 7% — because they had ridden the biggest AI-driven upgrade cycle. Yahoo Finance separately reports cybersecurity stocks got a mirror-image lift on the same messaging.
