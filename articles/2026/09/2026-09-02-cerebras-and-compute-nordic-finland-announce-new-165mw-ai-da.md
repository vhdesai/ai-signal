# Cerebras and Compute Nordic Finland announce new 165MW AI data centre in Mikkeli, Finland

StorageNewsletter reports that Cerebras and Compute Nordic Finland announced a 165MW AI data center in Mikkeli. The item reinforces that AI infrastructure expansion is increasingly tied to power availability, regional siting, and sovereign compute considerations. For executives, the continuing bottleneck is not only accelerators, but the physical and energy footprint needed to operate them at scale.
