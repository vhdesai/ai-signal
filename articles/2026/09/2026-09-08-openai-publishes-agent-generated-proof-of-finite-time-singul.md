# OpenAI publishes agent-generated proof of finite-time singularity in 3D Navier–Stokes

OpenAI released an analytical proof and a Lean formalization showing that an initially smooth, at-rest fluid under a smooth applied force can develop a finite-time singularity in the 3D Navier–Stokes equations. The work used an unreleased internal model more capable than GPT-6 Astra, running on the order of 10,000 concurrent agents; the search reached its result in about 88 hours from a September 1 start, followed by roughly 17 hours of Lean formalization, consuming ~2.7 million agent messages and ~130 billion output tokens. OpenAI says it will not pursue the $1M Clay prize; the Clay Mathematics Institute has not commented. Regardless of the prize question, this is the first credible demonstration of frontier-scale agent orchestration producing a machine-checkable result at research-frontier difficulty.
