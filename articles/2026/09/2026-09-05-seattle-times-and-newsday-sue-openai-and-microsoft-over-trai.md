# Seattle Times and Newsday sue OpenAI and Microsoft over training data

Two more regional news organizations filed copyright suits against OpenAI and Microsoft, alleging their journalism was used to train commercial AI systems without license. The complaint describes generative AI as "a snake eating its own tail" that risks breaking the news industry it consumes. The filings extend a litigation pattern that now spans national and regional publishers, and they land while the U.S. government has signaled support for OpenAI's fair-use position in related proceedings.
