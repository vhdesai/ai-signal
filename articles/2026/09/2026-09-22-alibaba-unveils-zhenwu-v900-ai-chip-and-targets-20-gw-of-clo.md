# Alibaba unveils Zhenwu V900 AI chip and targets 20 GW of cloud data centers by 2032

Alibaba's T-Head semiconductor unit unveiled the Zhenwu V900 AI chip, with reported performance three times that of the prior Zhenwu M890. Alibaba CEO Wu Yongming said the company's AI strategy rests on models, chips, and cloud infrastructure, while Alibaba Cloud aims to operate more than 20 gigawatts of data-center capacity worldwide by 2032. The announcement reinforces that China's AI strategy is increasingly vertically integrated across model development, domestic accelerators, and hyperscale cloud buildout.
