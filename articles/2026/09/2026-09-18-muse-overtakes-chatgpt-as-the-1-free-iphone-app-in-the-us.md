# Muse Overtakes ChatGPT as the #1 Free iPhone App in the US

Meta's Muse took the top spot on the free iPhone charts in the US App Store, displacing OpenAI's ChatGPT from first place roughly ten days after its mobile launch. It is the first time a standalone Meta AI app has led the chart. Distribution advantage, not model quality, is the operative variable here — and it is the first consumer-assistant share move of the year that did not follow a frontier model release.
