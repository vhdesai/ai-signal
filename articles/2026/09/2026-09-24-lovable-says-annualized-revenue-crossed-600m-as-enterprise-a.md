# Lovable says annualized revenue crossed $600M as enterprise adoption grows

Lovable co-founder Fabian Hedin said the vibe-coding platform has crossed $600 million in annualized revenue, up from roughly $500 million in June. TechCrunch reported that people at two-thirds of Fortune 500 companies are using the product and that apps created on the platform collectively draw nearly a billion monthly views. The company’s traction suggests the AI software-creation market is shifting from code generation alone toward hosted, deployed business applications. TEAM SOFTWAREHUMAN-AGENT WORK
