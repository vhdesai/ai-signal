# Nvidia's growing dependence on a handful of AI customers

The Information's Sunday Insights analysis shows Nvidia's customer concentration is at historic highs: three customers were responsible for 44% of total sales in H1 FY26, up from two customers at 36% in FY25, and no customer at 10%+ in FY23. Top cloud firms drive 50%+ of Nvidia data-center revenue but are developing their own accelerators (Amazon Trainium, Google TPU, Microsoft Maia). Nvidia has extended payment terms for large customers and 70% of receivables now come from five customers — one reason Jensen Huang keeps investing in neoclouds and smaller AI firms as a diversification play.
