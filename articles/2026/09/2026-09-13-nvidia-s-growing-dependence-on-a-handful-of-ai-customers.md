# NVIDIA's Growing Dependence on a Handful of AI Customers

Three customers were responsible for 44% of total NVIDIA sales in H1 FY26, up from two customers at 36% in FY25, and no customer at 10%+ in FY23. Top cloud firms drive 50%+ of NVIDIA data-center revenue but are developing their own accelerators. NVIDIA has extended payment terms for large customers and 70% of receivables now come from five customers — one reason Jensen Huang keeps investing in neoclouds and smaller AI firms.
