# Company releases NeoMME, dropping the vision tower from multimodal encoders

NeoMME is a family of 260M and 800M-parameter bidirectional encoders that processes multilingual text tokens and raw 32×32 RGB image patches inside a single Transformer, with no pretrained vision tower and no causal decoder. That architectural simplification targets retrieval and document-search workloads where ColPali-style two-tower retrievers carry heavy serving costs. Small, open multimodal encoders of this shape are the practical building block for enterprise document retrieval at scale.
