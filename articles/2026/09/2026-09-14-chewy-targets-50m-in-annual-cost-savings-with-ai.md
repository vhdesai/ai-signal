# Chewy Targets $50M in Annual Cost Savings With AI

CIO Dive reports Chewy is deploying AI tools across customer care, pharmacy, and vet services to bolster productivity, targeting $50 million in annual cost savings as it moves toward agentic capabilities. The story is one of the clearer mid-cap enterprise ROI targets disclosed this week, alongside Sysco's $500M AI-driven cost-savings goal published in the same publication's weekend edition. Together, they suggest the enterprise AI ROI conversation is finally moving from proof-of-concept metrics to hard dollar commitments.
