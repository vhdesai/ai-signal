# TechCrunch: is the AI industry really ready to slow down?

TechCrunch's Equity panel argues the "pace the frontier" conversation — Amodei's essay, Altman/Musk/Hassabis endorsements, Huang's rejection, Trump's "AI Force" push — is inconsistent with the underlying capex, hiring, and launch cadence at every named lab. The piece's framing: watch behavior, not statements. For CIOs, that's the practical read: no near-term slowdown is priced in, and enterprise procurement planning should assume continued frontier-model release cadence through 2027 despite the safety-narrative volume.
