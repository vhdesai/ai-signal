# NVIDIA shows how frontier reasoning models can run on Jetson

NVIDIA published guidance for deploying compact reasoning and agentic models such as Nemotron 3.5 Lightning and Qwen3.8-27B on Jetson edge hardware. The post emphasizes local inference for robots, industrial systems, in-cab assistants, and remote environments where latency, connectivity, data exposure, and operating cost matter. The strategic signal is that some agentic workloads are moving from cloud-only inference toward edge-capable, locally governed deployments. GEOPOLITICS AI CHIPS
