# John Ternus Takes Over as Apple CEO; Tim Cook Becomes Executive Chair

Hardware chief John Ternus became Apple CEO, ending Tim Cook's roughly 15-year tenure; Cook remains as executive chair. Ternus inherits a ~$4.6T company widely seen as trailing Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI in generative AI, with his first public test at the September 9 event. Former hardware VP Laura Legros has returned, reinforcing silicon and device leadership. Expect Apple's AI strategy to be expressed through on-device silicon — Ternus's domain — rather than a frontier-model push.
