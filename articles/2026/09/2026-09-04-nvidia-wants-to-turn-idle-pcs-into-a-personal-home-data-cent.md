# Nvidia wants to turn idle PCs into a personal home data center with PAIR

PCMag reports on Nvidia's PAIR concept for using idle PCs as a personal AI compute resource. The story reflects a broader push to move AI capability closer to users and endpoints, reducing dependence on centralized cloud inference for some workloads. If workable, local compute orchestration could affect enterprise device strategy, data-residency posture, and cost management.
