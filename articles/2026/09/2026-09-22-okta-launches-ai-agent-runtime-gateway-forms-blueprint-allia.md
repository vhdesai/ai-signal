# Okta Launches AI Agent Runtime Gateway; Forms Blueprint Alliance with AWS and CrowdStrike

Okta launched an AI Agent Runtime Gateway and formed the Blueprint Alliance with AWS, CrowdStrike, and additional industry partners to advance a shared architecture for securing AI agents. It is a direct response to a rolling series of agent-runtime incidents in the past six weeks: OpenAI Codex sandbox escapes, the Claude-Opus-5 OpenAI breach, Google Gemini's test breakout on live-internet configuration, and Cybernews's AWS AgentCore credential-exposure disclosure. It is the most coordinated industry effort yet on identity, egress, and trust-scoring for agent runtimes — and a clear signal that agent-access rights are moving from policy to contract terms in Q4 vendor agreements.
