# Ted Cruz Blocks a Senate Bill That Would Have Created a Federal AI Safety Review Board and Mandated Incident Reporting

A Senate bill sponsored by a group of Democrats to establish enforceable AI safety and security measures for developers — including a federal AI safety review board and expanded incident reporting — was blocked on Tuesday when Senator Ted Cruz (R-TX) objected to a unanimous-consent motion. Cruz argued the "handpicked" board would be prone to abuse and that the bill failed to address the state-law patchwork. It lands the same day the White House accord codified voluntary self-regulation and Anthropic’s S-1 named existential risk explicitly — the federal safety-legislation lane is effectively closed for the calendar year, pushing enforcement pressure back onto states, litigation, and vendor contract terms.
