# OpenAI developer says Astra's internal use pulled roadmap plans forward by six months

An OpenAI developer, Thibault Sottiaux, said internally that GPT-6 Astra was the company's "biggest competitive advantage" before its public release, crediting internal productivity gains with accelerating some product plans by roughly six months. The claim is anecdotal and unverified by independent benchmarks, but it signals how OpenAI is messaging Astra's business impact to justify its rapid rollout pace.
