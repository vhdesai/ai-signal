# Perplexity Publishes the Internals of Its GPU Embedding Serving Stack

Perplexity's engineering team detailed the three-component stack — Ivy, Tulip and ROSE — that serves its pplx-embed model, covering CUDA graph capture, LazyTensor overlap, and benchmarks against vLLM. The disclosure is unusual in that it addresses the serving economics of retrieval rather than embedding model quality, which is where most published work concentrates. For teams running large internal indexes, this is a rare public reference architecture for driving down the per-query cost of retrieval at scale.
