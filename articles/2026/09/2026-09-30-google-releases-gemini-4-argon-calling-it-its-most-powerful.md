# Google releases Gemini 4 Argon, calling it its most powerful model

Google launched Gemini 4 Argon, pitched as a workhorse for coding and cybersecurity work and positioned as the first in the Gemini 4 generation. DeepMind's own framing calls it "our next era of frontier intelligence" and tightly bundles it with the Gemini 3.8 Live/Live Avatar and Flash TTS stack from the past two weeks. The naming shift (3.8 → 4 Argon) telegraphs that Google is now trading on cycle-time rather than linear number bumps and sets the stage for a competitive response to GPT-6.1 Sol and Opus 5.5 before Thanksgiving.
