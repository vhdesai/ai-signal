# AI agents in cyberattacks target Asian governments

Cybernews reports that AI agents are being used in cyberattacks targeting Asian government networks. Even if current use remains uneven, the direction is clear: attackers are adopting agentic tooling to scale reconnaissance, exploitation, and adaptation. Defenders should assume AI-assisted intrusion workflows will become faster and more persistent, raising the value of telemetry, automated containment, and hardened identity controls.
