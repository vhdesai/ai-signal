# Alibaba Unveils Zhenwu V900 Accelerator and a 20GW Data Center Target for 2032

At Alibaba Cloud's Apsara Conference in Hangzhou, CEO Eddie Wu introduced the Zhenwu V900, which Alibaba says delivers three times the performance of the Zhenwu M890 released in May and can be clustered up to 500,000 units for frontier training. Alibaba set a target of more than 20 gigawatts of global data center capacity by 2032 and said the chip enters mass production in Q1 2027; existing Zhenwu parts are already deployed with more than 650 customers. Wu also disclosed that Qwen 4 is in training and outlined plans for models in the 5–10 trillion parameter range. Shares rose roughly 3% in Hong Kong, with Citi estimating the buildout could support materially higher cloud revenue.
