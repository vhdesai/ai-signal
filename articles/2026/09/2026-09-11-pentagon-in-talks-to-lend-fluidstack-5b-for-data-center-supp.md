# Pentagon in Talks to Lend Fluidstack ~$5B for Data-Center Supply Chain

The Defense Department's Office of Strategic Capital is negotiating a roughly $5B loan to AI cloud provider Fluidstack — which would be the office's largest to date — first reported by The Wall Street Journal. The funds would back domestic manufacturing capacity for data-center components such as power and cooling equipment rather than new facilities. Fluidstack holds partnerships with Google and Anthropic. Terms are unsettled and the deal is not final. Infrastructure M CAPEX
