# Gemini Desktop App Arrives on Windows 10 and 11

Google's Gemini desktop app is now available on Windows 10 and 11, giving Windows users a native, always-available Gemini surface rather than the browser-only web experience. It is a notable competitive move on Microsoft's home turf, landing as Microsoft is pushing its own AI-native WinUI 3 app-generation flow. Expect the parallel Windows Copilot vs. Gemini desktop battle to shape the next round of consumer AI defaults on the world's most-installed OS.
