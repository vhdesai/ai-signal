# Oxford let OpenAI train models on Bodleian Library texts; staff flagged reputational risk

Internal documents obtained through a freedom-of-information request state that “the Bodleian material digitised by OpenAI has been used to populate the OpenAI training set” — a use not disclosed in the March 2025 partnership announcement, which was framed as a digitisation project for students and researchers. Meeting minutes show Bodleian governance committee members raised reputational-risk concerns. Oxford disputes any concealment, saying the material is “modest in scale, out of copyright,” non-exclusive, and will be published openly within months.
