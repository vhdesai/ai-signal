# Pension Giants Sound the Alarm: Texas Teacher's CIO Compares the AI Buildout to Five Prior Infrastructure Booms

At the $225.3B Teacher Retirement System of Texas investment-committee meeting, CIO Jase Auby compared today's AI infrastructure buildout to five prior US booms — canals, railroads, electrification, highways, and the early-2000s telecom/fiber buildout. "It's a cautionary tale for the period that we're in now," Auby said, noting that AI capex at ~3.5% of US GDP dwarfs all five prior booms. Earlier this week, Monte Tarbox, CIO of the $327B NYC Retirement Systems, said the plan has turned down managers because it needs to diversify away from AI, which is becoming difficult as it permeates every sector: "AI is a risk that can't possibly be fully appreciated asset class by asset class." The largest US pension pools are now formally documenting AI concentration as a systemic exposure, not a thematic tilt.
