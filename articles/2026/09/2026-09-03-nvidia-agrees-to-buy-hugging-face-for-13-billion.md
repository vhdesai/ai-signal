# Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for $13 billion

The Wall Street Journal reported that Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $13 billion, a move that would give Nvidia direct influence over one of the most important distribution hubs for open-weight AI models. Strategically, the deal would extend Nvidia beyond chips and systems into model discovery, deployment workflows, and developer community infrastructure. The key execution risk is neutrality: Hugging Face's value depends heavily on developers trusting it as a broad, multi-vendor platform rather than a captive Nvidia channel. CAPITAL MARKETSHOT
