# Black Forest Labs debuts FLUX 3 Action, an open-weights robotics model at half the size of rivals

FLUX 3 Action is a 7B open-weight world-action model that jointly predicts the next 32 robot actions (roughly 2.1 seconds at 15 Hz) along with future camera frames. Black Forest Labs reports 42.92% on RoboLab against Cosmos 3 Nano at 36.8% and π0.5 at 28.0% — with 56% fewer parameters and up to 3.95× faster runtime — plus 93.3% single-item success on a real Franka arm. Open weights at this performance-per-parameter meaningfully lower the barrier to in-house robotics policy development.
