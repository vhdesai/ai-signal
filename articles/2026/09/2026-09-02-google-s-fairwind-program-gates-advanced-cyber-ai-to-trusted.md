# Google's Fairwind Program gates advanced cyber AI to trusted defenders

Google launched the Fairwind Program to provide selected Google Cloud customers, government agencies, and cybersecurity partners access to advanced Gemini cyber-defense capabilities. The program is designed to give defenders early access to vulnerability discovery and remediation models while limiting misuse risk through institutional eligibility and operational controls. This is notable because it treats dual-use cyber capability as an access-governance problem, not only a model-safety problem. SAFETYCRITICAL CAPABILITY
