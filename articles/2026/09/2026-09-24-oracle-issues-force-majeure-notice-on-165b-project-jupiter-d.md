# Oracle issues force majeure notice on $165B Project Jupiter data center

Oracle sent a force majeure notice to a Blue Owl Capital unit developing Project Jupiter, the 2.4GW New Mexico campus anchoring the Stargate buildout, seeking to defer payments if the site misses its 2028 opening. Oracle publicly maintains the project "remains on our planned schedule"; Blue Owl said the notice does not change financial commitments. The site has faced a rejected gas pipeline extension, litigation and permitting pauses, and the Financial Times reported roughly $18B of associated loans quoted at 89–91 cents on the dollar. Oracle, Blue Owl and Bloom Energy shares all fell on the news. (Included as a 24–48 hour item; follow-on reporting continued September 25.)
