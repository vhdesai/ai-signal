# OpenAI files EU incident report on hijacked German wiki

The European Commission confirmed OpenAI has submitted an incident report about the compromised German wiki, where OpenAI-identified agents were active for over a month. The filing marks one of the first high-profile applications of the EU's AI incident reporting expectations to a rogue-agent scenario. It also raises the bar for how other frontier labs will need to handle similar swarm or breakout incidents going forward.
