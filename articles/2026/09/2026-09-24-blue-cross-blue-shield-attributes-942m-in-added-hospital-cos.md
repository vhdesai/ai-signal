# Blue Cross Blue Shield Attributes $942M in Added Hospital Costs to AI-Assisted Coding

The Blue Cross Blue Shield Association — representing 31 insurers covering more than 100M members — reported that the share of inpatient stays billed as medically complex rose from 37% in early 2023 to 40% by end-2025, adding an estimated $942M in costs. About $653M came from secondary diagnoses that pushed more than 55,000 claims into higher-reimbursement groups at roughly $11,000 per case. The association’s evidence for upcoding is a treatment gap — among major bowel procedures, top-quartile hospitals for anemia diagnosis transfused less often (16.9% vs 19.3%) than peers. The analysis is a trade-association white paper (not peer-reviewed) and hospital groups dispute it citing genuinely rising acuity, but it is the first quantified enterprise-scale claim of AI-driven billing shift — and it will land on payer and regulator agendas fast.
