# OpenAI Acquires Smartphone-Camera Maker Glass Imaging for Over $300M

OpenAI acquired Los Altos–based Glass Imaging in a deal worth more than $300M. Founded in 2019 by ex-Apple engineers Ziv Attar and Tom Bishop — who led Apple's Portrait Mode team — Glass Imaging uses neural networks tuned to individual camera systems to improve image quality at capture time. The deal deepens OpenAI's consumer-hardware push following its $6.5B io acquisition in 2025.
