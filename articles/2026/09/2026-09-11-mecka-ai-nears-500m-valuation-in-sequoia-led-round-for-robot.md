# Mecka AI Nears $500M Valuation in Sequoia-Led Round for Robot Training Data

Mecka AI is closing on a Sequoia-led round at a ~$500M valuation just three months after a $60M raise. The company pays operators to record everyday activities with body sensors, producing the physical-world equivalent of Scale AI's language corpora for humanoid robotics. Coupled with XDOF's reported $1.2B talks last week, robot-data platforms are quickly consolidating into a strategic infrastructure category — with Sequoia, 8VC, and Framework Ventures visibly leading the moves.
