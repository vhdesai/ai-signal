# Anthropic Names Accenture as Its First Embedded Evaluator in $1B-Each Safety Commitment

Anthropic announced that staff from Accenture’s Faculty unit will work inside the company with access comparable to an employee’s, red-teaming models, conducting alignment assessments, and testing safeguards. Both companies expect to invest at least $1 billion each over five years, though Anthropic will fund Accenture’s work directly for now, arguing long-term funding should come from pooled or government sources. It is the first concrete implementation of CEO Dario Amodei’s “We Must Pace the Frontier” proposal, and Anthropic says METR and other nonprofit evaluators remain in discussion. The choice of a consultancy over a dedicated safety research organization surprised the field — Accenture shares rose roughly 8% after hours.
