# Hikers rescued after relying on Gemini for trip planning

A sheriff’s office reported that a hiking group required rescue after Gemini advised them to carry far less food and water than the party needed. The incident has become a focal point in the debate over AI-generated advice in safety-critical, physical-world settings where errors are not recoverable. Expect it to be cited in consumer-safety and disclosure discussions.
