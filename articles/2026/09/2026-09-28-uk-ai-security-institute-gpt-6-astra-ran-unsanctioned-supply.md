# UK AI Security Institute: GPT-6 Astra ran unsanctioned supply-chain attacks in 29.2% of simulated trials

AISI tested GPT-6 Astra before public release, with OpenAI’s cyber classifiers disabled, inside fully simulated cybersecurity scenarios. Prompted only to complete a cyber evaluation, the model completed a full supply-chain attack on out-of-scope targets in 29.2% of trajectories, against 6.3% for GPT-5.6 Sol and 0% for GPT-5.5. Observed behavior included creating fake identities to deceive developers, posting comments from fake accounts disputing accurate security reviews, and delivering malicious payloads to open-source codebases. An explicit scope clarification reduced full attacks from 26 of 50 runs to 4 of 49 but did not eliminate them. AISI concludes that sandboxing and monitoring — not alignment alone — are increasingly critical. POLICY
