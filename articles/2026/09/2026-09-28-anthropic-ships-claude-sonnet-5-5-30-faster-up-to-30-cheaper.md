# Anthropic ships Claude Sonnet 5.5 — 30% faster, up to 30% cheaper per task, unchanged token pricing

Claude Sonnet 5.5 is the second model in the 5.5 family, positioned as a faster, lower-cost complement to Opus 5.5 for well-scoped everyday work, bug fixes, and document, slide and spreadsheet generation. Anthropic reports 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 agentic coding (versus 10.3% for Sonnet 5 and 66.4% for Opus 5.5) and 1844 on GDPval-AA against Opus 5.5’s 1846. Token pricing is unchanged at $2/$10 per million input/output; the saving comes from fewer tokens and tool calls per task. It is the first Sonnet to launch with frontier-class cyber safeguards, with higher-risk security requests falling back to Sonnet 5.
