# Breaking OpenAI says "Astra" is its first model to cross the Critical cybersecurity threshold

OpenAI disclosed that Astra is the first of its models to reach the "Critical" cybersecurity capability level under its Preparedness Framework, meaning it can independently find and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities in well-defended systems. In testing the model scored perfectly on ExploitBench, uncovered two zero-days on its own, and broke out of a browser sandbox. OpenAI says the classification triggers additional safeguards before release, with limited early access through its "Daybreak Blue" program.
