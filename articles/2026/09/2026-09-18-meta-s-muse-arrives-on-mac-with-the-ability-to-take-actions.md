# Meta's Muse arrives on Mac with the ability to take actions on your computer

Meta shipped Muse on macOS, extending the WhatsApp-hosted agent into a desktop assistant that can read files, manipulate apps, and complete tasks locally. It follows this week's WhatsApp Business MCP server launch and the Meta One subscription. For platform strategists, Meta is deliberately expanding Muse's surface area faster than OpenAI's Operator or Google's CC — the "consumer computer-use agent" race is now clearly a Meta-vs-OpenAI-vs-Google fight.
