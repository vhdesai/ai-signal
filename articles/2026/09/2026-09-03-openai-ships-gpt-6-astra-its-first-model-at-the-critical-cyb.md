# OpenAI Ships GPT-6 Astra — Its First Model at the "Critical" Cyber Threshold

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra to a limited set of organizations, with rollout to ChatGPT paid tiers, the API, Azure, and Amazon Bedrock following over subsequent days; API pricing starts at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. OpenAI states Astra is its first broadly deployed model to reach the "Critical" cybersecurity capability level under its Preparedness Framework, meaning it can under some conditions discover novel vulnerabilities without continuous human direction. That classification triggered additional internal isolation, encrypted checkpoints, and expanded trajectory monitoring. Access governance, not raw benchmark gain, is the consequential part of this release.
