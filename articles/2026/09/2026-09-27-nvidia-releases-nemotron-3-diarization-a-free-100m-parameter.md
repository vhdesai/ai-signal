# Nvidia releases Nemotron 3 Diarization, a free 100M-parameter real-time speaker-ID model

Nvidia open-released Nemotron 3 Diarization, a 100M-parameter model that identifies up to eight distinct speakers in a live conversation with sub-second attribution. The small footprint makes it deployable on consumer-tier GPUs and on Nvidia Jetson-class edge hardware. It fills a durable gap — none of the major frontier labs had shipped a competitive open diarization model in 2026 — and pressures ElevenLabs, Deepgram, and AssemblyAI on the enterprise-transcription tier.
