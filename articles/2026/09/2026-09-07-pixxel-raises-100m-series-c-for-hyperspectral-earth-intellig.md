# Pixxel raises $100M Series C for hyperspectral Earth-intelligence stack

Hyperspectral imaging startup Pixxel closed a $100M Series C led by Temasek and Seraphim, bringing total funding to roughly $195M in what is described as India’s largest spacetech round. The company sells orbital hyperspectral imagery paired with analytics — a sensing layer where AI models, not optics, increasingly determine commercial value. Relevant for enterprises evaluating geospatial AI inputs for supply chain, agriculture, and climate risk.
