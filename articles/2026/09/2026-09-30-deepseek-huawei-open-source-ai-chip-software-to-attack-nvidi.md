# DeepSeek + Huawei Open-Source AI Chip Software to Attack Nvidia’s CUDA Moat

DeepSeek open-sourced tooling that ports its models onto Huawei’s Ascend chips, and NYT, SCMP, and Techzine all frame the release as a direct assault on Nvidia’s most durable moat — CUDA. Yahoo Finance and Geopolitechs report DeepSeek is now "building for Ascend" natively, not as a fallback. Nvidia stock reacted, but Jensen Huang used the same day’s White House lunch to lock in domestic-buildout political air cover, pitching a "1 million jobs and a reshaped US economy" frame. The stack fight is now openly geographic: Chinese-lab price and CUDA-alternative tooling on one side; US industrial-policy air cover and hyperscaler capex on the other. EXCLUSIVE ECOSYSTEM
