# Trending Brain signals can sharpen LLM deductive reasoning, Nature Machine Intelligence study finds

Researchers from Peking University and Tsinghua University report that language models and the human brain represent deductive reasoning in similar — though not identical — ways. The team showed that human neural signals can be used to steer and improve an LLM’s deductive performance. The work is early-stage but adds empirical weight to interpretability approaches that treat model internals as comparable to cognitive representations.
