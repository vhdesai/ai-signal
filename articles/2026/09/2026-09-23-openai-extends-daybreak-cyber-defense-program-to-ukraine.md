# OpenAI extends Daybreak cyber-defense program to Ukraine

OpenAI is extending its Daybreak program to the Government of Ukraine to support the cyber-defense of civilian infrastructure. Daybreak provides tailored access and tooling for defenders under sustained state-level threat. It marks OpenAI's most explicit deployment of frontier tools into an active conflict-adjacent defensive posture and sets a precedent other US frontier labs will be asked to match. POLICY
