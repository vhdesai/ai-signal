# Altman, Musk and Hassabis Publicly Back the Pacing Proposal

Within a day of the essay, OpenAI's Sam Altman, Elon Musk and Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis all endorsed Amodei's position. Altman wrote that pacing the frontier "has been a primary topic of discussions we've had at OpenAI in recent weeks" and committed OpenAI to matching the independent-evaluator proposal, saying the company would "have more to share soon." Musk responded simply that "Dario is right." Hassabis called the direction correct while noting the details still need work, linking it to Google DeepMind's earlier call for an industry-wide standards body. A voluntary evaluator-access norm across the three largest Western labs would be the most consequential governance shift of the year — and it arrived without regulation. SECURITY
