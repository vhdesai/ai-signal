# Micron's Taiwan Unions Reject Record Bonus, Press for 15% Profit-Sharing

Micron's record fiscal 2026 rewards — 35 to 68 months of pay for Taiwan direct-labor staff, with minimum cash compensation of NT$1.7 million — have not settled the dispute. The Taoyuan union estimates the one-off award equals roughly 4.4% of operating profit and is demanding a permanent system allocating 15% of operating profit to bonuses, paid quarterly from fiscal 2027. No formal strike vote had been held as of September 14. A stoppage at Micron's largest manufacturing base would introduce direct DRAM and HBM supply risk. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
