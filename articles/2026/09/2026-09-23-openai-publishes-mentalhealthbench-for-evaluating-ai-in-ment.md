# OpenAI publishes MentalHealthBench for evaluating AI in mental-health conversations

OpenAI released MentalHealthBench, an expert-informed benchmark for evaluating whether AI models produce helpful and safe responses across realistic mental-health conversation scenarios. The publication follows sustained criticism of frontier models' behavior in emotional-support contexts and comes as ChatGPT Voice moves into an always-on assistant posture. The benchmark is licensed for third-party use and provides an early template for how safety evaluation will be handed over to independent groups. AI Safety & Policy POLICY
