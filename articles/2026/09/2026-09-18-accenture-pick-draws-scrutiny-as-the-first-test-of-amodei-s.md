# Accenture Pick Draws Scrutiny as the First Test of Amodei's Pacing Plan

TechCrunch questioned whether a large commercial consultancy is the right first independent evaluator, given Accenture's own business deploying AI for enterprises and governments. Faculty staff will work inside Anthropic evaluating and red-teaming models, which raises the standard independence objection that the evaluator is also a commercial counterparty. It is the first concrete implementation of Dario Amodei's “We Must Pace the Frontier” commitment, so the governance precedent it sets will likely be copied.
