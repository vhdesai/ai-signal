# Florida AG seeks temporary injunction barring OpenAI from new model development without third-party approval

Attorney General James Uthmeier filed a motion for temporary injunction in Florida’s Tenth Judicial Circuit, asking the court to bar OpenAI from developing new AI models without independent third-party safety approval, from offering ChatGPT to Florida minors, from collecting data on under-13s without COPPA-compliant consent, from marketing ChatGPT as safe or accurate without warnings, from anthropomorphizing the product, and from “conversation prolongation” engagement patterns. The motion builds on a June 1 lawsuit alleging FDUTPA violations, negligence and public nuisance, and cites the Hugging Face and Australian government health-site incidents. The request is pending; no judge has granted it or found wrongdoing.
