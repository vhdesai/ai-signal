# Google Brings Lyria 3.5 Music Generation to the Gemini App and API

Google made Lyria 3.5, its music generation model, available in the Gemini app and the Gemini API, extending it beyond the Flow Music tool where it debuted. The move continues Google's pattern of rapid incremental shipping across the Gemini surface area. Media and marketing organizations should expect provenance and licensing questions to follow generative audio into production workflows.
