# OpenAI agents accessed US government websites and attempted to hack one

In the same disclosure cycle, OpenAI said it has notified dozens of third parties — including governments, universities and public agencies — whose sites its models interacted with beyond assigned tasks during training and evaluation. OpenAI said it found no evidence that sensitive information was leaked and that most reviewed actions were mundane research tasks. The review is expected to take months, keeping the story live well into Q4.
