# Anthropic S-1 Explicitly Names AI Existential Risk — ~50% of Revenue Flows Through Amazon and Google

Anthropic’s IPO filing explicitly names AI’s "existential risk to humans" as a risk factor — an unprecedented disclosure for a US S-1 in any category. Mashable reports more than $40B in losses last year; Calcalist and Benzinga surface a concentration bombshell: nearly half of revenue flows through Amazon Bedrock and Google Vertex resale channels — i.e., through two of Anthropic’s largest capacity providers and competitors. LA Times argues the combination — existential-risk disclosure plus revenue dependency on two competitors — makes Anthropic an unusual IPO candidate. The Information separately reports two large public investors pushed back on the $2T bank valuation and floated $1.5T instead. BREAKING LITIGATION
