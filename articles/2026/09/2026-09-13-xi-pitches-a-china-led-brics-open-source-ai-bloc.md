# Xi pitches a China-led BRICS open-source AI bloc

At the BRICS summit in New Delhi, Xi Jinping said China will lead the creation of a BRICS AI open-source community supporting joint development and application of large language models, plus AI seminars and training. He also proposed a BRICS digital-ecosystem cloud platform, an engineer cultivation alliance and a youth science-and-technology exchange programme. The proposals position open-weight models as a lower-cost route to AI capacity for developing economies. Notably, the initiative did not appear in the bloc's joint declaration, suggesting members including India have not fully signed on.
