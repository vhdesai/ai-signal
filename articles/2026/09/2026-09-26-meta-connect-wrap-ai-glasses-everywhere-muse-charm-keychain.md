# Meta Connect wrap: AI glasses everywhere, Muse Charm keychain gadget, months-long Muse push

TechCrunch's Meta Connect wrap captures the theme: "the company's smart glasses were everywhere." Scripps News details Ray-Ban Meta Audio glasses (camera-free, $349), Muse upgrades, and the $1,299 Meta VR product; BGR explains Muse Charm — the keychain-sized Tamagotchi-style companion for the Muse agent. Business Insider's "Inside Meta's months-long grind to turn Muse into a hit" documents the internal push, while The Information's Friday Briefing continues to warn "Meta's moment won't last" — Meta stock at 52-week highs on Muse momentum vs. Rosenblatt's economics argument that Muse needs computing costs to fall or users to spend $1,000+/month to be profitable.
