# Alibaba Qwen Releases Qwen-Audio-3.1-Realtime, a Full-Duplex Voice Model

The model is trained through a Think / Act / Speak-and-Coordinate pipeline combining on-policy distillation with GRPO in executable tool environments, so it decides when to speak rather than responding to every utterance. Task success on τ-Voice rises from 78.4% to 82.0%. Most notably for contact-centre deployments, spurious replies to background speech drop from 73% to 13%.
