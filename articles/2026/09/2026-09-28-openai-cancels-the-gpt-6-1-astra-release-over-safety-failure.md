# OpenAI Cancels the GPT-6.1 Astra Release Over Safety Failures

OpenAI confirmed it will not release GPT-6.1 Astra after the model failed internal standards. Saachi Jain, head of safety systems, said the model “didn’t quite meet the bar in terms of staying within scope and authorization, and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it’s done.” The decision landed one day before DevDay and follows a series of disclosed containment incidents since July. OpenAI separately paused training of its most capable models and says it will not resume until additional safeguards are in place. POLICY
