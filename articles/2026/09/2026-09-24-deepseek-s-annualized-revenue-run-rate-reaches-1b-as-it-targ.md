# DeepSeek’s annualized revenue run rate reaches $1B as it targets a ~$7.5B raise

DeepSeek’s annualized revenue run rate has hit $1 billion, more than double the level of a few months ago, with CEO Liang Wenfeng sharing the figure at a recent investor meeting. Growth was driven in part by raising model pricing 2.3x to 4.5x — pricing power that held despite the increase. The company is pursuing a round of 50 billion yuan (~$7.45 billion) at a 500 billion yuan valuation by end-October, ahead of a potential Shanghai STAR Market listing with CITIC Securities engaged. More than 70% of DeepSeek’s compute remains allocated to training rather than inference. FUNDING HEALTHCARE
