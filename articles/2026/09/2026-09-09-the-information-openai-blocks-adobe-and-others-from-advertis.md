# The Information: OpenAI blocks Adobe and others from advertising competing AI in ChatGPT

The Information reports OpenAI has moved to bar Adobe and other advertisers from promoting some competing AI products inside ChatGPT, sharpening the platform-versus-partner tension inside the generative-AI ecosystem. The policy tightens OpenAI's control over its consumer surface at a moment when it is also expanding advertising monetization and pursuing agent commerce. For enterprises negotiating ChatGPT distribution or referral placements, the practical takeaway is that OpenAI's ad rules now materially constrain competitive presence on its own surface.
