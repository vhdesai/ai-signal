# Vance's "satanic" AI remark strikes a chord among Christian Republicans

The Wall Street Journal reports that Vice President Vance's description of AI as "satanic" resonated broadly among Christian Republican constituencies, reflecting a religious-right undercurrent in the broader AI policy debate that is often overlooked in Beltway coverage of AI regulation. The piece illustrates that AI's political fault lines increasingly cut through cultural and religious identity, not just traditional left-right regulatory framing. Executives tracking AI policy risk should note that faith-aligned constituencies are becoming an active variable in US AI politics.
