# Meta's Muse assistant lands on Mac with access to Messages, Calendar and Notes

Meta brought its Muse AI assistant to macOS, following iOS, Android and web releases earlier this month, with the ability to read and act on Messages, Calendar and Notes. The Verge's review found the assistant capable but raised privacy concerns, noting it cannot describe its own data handling when asked. For enterprises, the relevant issue is scope: a consumer assistant with default access to local mail, calendar and note stores on managed or BYOD Macs is an endpoint data-governance question, not a productivity one.
