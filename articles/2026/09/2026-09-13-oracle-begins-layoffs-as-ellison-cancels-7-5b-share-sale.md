# Oracle begins layoffs as Ellison cancels $7.5B share sale

Oracle has begun executing on the $700M expanded restructuring announced Friday, with layoffs starting despite a blowout Q1, $664B AI-cloud backlog, and 30% top-line growth. In an unusual insider signal, founder Larry Ellison canceled plans to sell up to 50 million shares (~$7.5B) that had been disclosed via a trading plan the day before. The mismatch — job cuts amid record demand plus insider-hold signaling — captures the "AI capex is squeezing headcount" pattern also visible at Meta and Google.
