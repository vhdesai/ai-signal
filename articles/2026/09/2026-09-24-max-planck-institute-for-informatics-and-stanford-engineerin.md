# Max Planck Institute for Informatics and Stanford Engineering to Establish a Joint AI Center

The Max Planck Center for Visual and Multimodal Artificial Intelligence (MPC-VMI) is financed with around €29 million, of which €11.6M comes from the State of Saarland’s Transformation Fund, with Google and Flawless as industry research partners. It will be co-led by Christian Theobalt (MPI Informatics) and Gordon Wetzstein (Stanford Engineering), with co-directors Bernt Schiele and Karen Liu, and is expected to begin operations at the end of 2026 with up to 50 researchers and two-year research stays at Stanford. The strategic read is a sizable transatlantic bet on multimodal and physical AI — care robotics, world models — deliberately outside the US hyperscaler orbit.
