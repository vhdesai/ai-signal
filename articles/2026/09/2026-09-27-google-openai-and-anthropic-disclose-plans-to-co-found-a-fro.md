# Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic disclose plans to co-found a frontier-AI standards body — Amodei calls for mandatory regulation

The three leading Western labs publicly disclosed plans to co-found a frontier-AI standards body focused on pre-deployment testing, incident reporting, and shared red-team disclosure. Critics noted AP News's parallel report that the same firms are simultaneously lobbying to shape how safety is regulated. In an ABC exclusive, Anthropic's Amodei broke ranks and explicitly called for mandatory regulation of frontier AI.
