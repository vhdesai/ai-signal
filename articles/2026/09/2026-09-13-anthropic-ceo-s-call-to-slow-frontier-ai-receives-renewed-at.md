# Anthropic CEO's call to slow frontier AI receives renewed attention

Anadolu summarized key points from Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's call to slow frontier AI development, including stronger external review and coordination among leading labs and governments. The coverage reflects how AI safety has moved from specialist discourse into mainstream policy debate after repeated reports of rogue-agent behavior. For executives, the relevant question is whether voluntary access for evaluators evolves into contractual, regulatory, or procurement requirements for frontier-model use.
