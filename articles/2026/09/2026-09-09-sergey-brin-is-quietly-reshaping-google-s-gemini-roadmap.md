# Sergey Brin is quietly reshaping Google's Gemini roadmap

Alphabet co-founder Sergey Brin has gained more direct influence over Gemini in recent months, working from a microkitchen inside Google's Mountain View campus and pulling resources through the company's bureaucracy for high-priority work, per eight current and former employees. The reshuffle follows Google's AI leadership shakeup earlier this year, in which Jeff Dean departed and Demis Hassabis moved into a new role. Brin's return to hands-on model direction is a notable governance signal ahead of Gemini's next major release cycle, and worth tracking for anyone modeling Google's frontier cadence.
