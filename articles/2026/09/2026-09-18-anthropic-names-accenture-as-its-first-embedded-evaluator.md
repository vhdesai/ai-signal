# Anthropic Names Accenture as Its First Embedded Evaluator

Anthropic is partnering with Accenture — led by its Faculty AI business — on independent, embedded evaluation of frontier models, including red-teaming, alignment assessments and safeguard testing. Each company expects to invest at least $1 billion over five years, and embedded evaluators receive access “comparable to an employee's.” The arrangement is non-exclusive, and Anthropic says it is in dialogue with METR and other nonprofits. This is the first time third-party frontier evaluation has been capitalized at this scale.
