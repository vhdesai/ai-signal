# Amazon Opens Seller Central APIs to Third-Party Agents, Starting With Anthropic's Claude

At its Accelerate conference, Amazon launched a Selling Partner plugin letting merchants manage inventory, pricing, listings, and analytics from Anthropic's Claude (US beta) or Amazon's own Quick assistant without opening Seller Central. Seller Assistant — built on Amazon Bedrock with Claude models — gains persistent memory of pricing patterns and inventory cycles, always-on workflows that act within seller-defined guardrails with full audit trails and approval gates, and a live-data canvas. Amazon says about 90% of sellers already use outside AI for parts of their operations, and is bundling a free 12-month Quick Plus subscription through December 31, 2026. Notable alongside Amazon blocking Meta's Muse shopping agent days earlier: Amazon admits agents whose scopes it controls, and refuses those it does not — a template for agent-access rights that will show up in every marketplace and enterprise API this quarter.
