# Amodei: "The Industry Lied About AI Risks" — Altman and Hassabis Align on Slowdown

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei told CBS News that "for too long the industry lied" about AI risks and publicly called for a frontier-model slowdown. Axios, The New York Times, DW, Forbes, and Reuters confirm both OpenAI's Sam Altman and Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis have aligned with the framing. Elon Musk tweeted "Dario is right." Yahoo Finance frames the coordinated messaging as likely to disrupt the AI stock trade — the same executives who built these companies are now publicly asking the market to price in slower progress and greater regulatory constraint.
