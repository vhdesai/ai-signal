# Ineffable Intelligence recruits six co-founders from Google DeepMind and InstaDeep

Stealth AI research startup Ineffable Intelligence added six co-founders in a coordinated hire from Google DeepMind and InstaDeep. The move mirrors the broader talent reshuffling underway across frontier labs — Meta's Superintelligence Labs, Thinking Machines, and Anthropic have all pulled senior researchers in recent months. Related reporting notes DeepMind veteran Thore Graepel is also leaving to pursue an AI reasoning venture, further tightening the talent market.
