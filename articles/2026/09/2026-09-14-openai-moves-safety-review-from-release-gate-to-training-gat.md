# OpenAI Moves Safety Review From Release Gate to Training Gate

Sam Altman said OpenAI now formulates explicit safety cases in advance of frontier reinforcement-learning runs it expects to significantly increase capability — in addition to, not instead of, pre-release safety work. It generalizes the ad hoc review behind OpenAI's August 2026 frontier RL pause over the Astra model's cyber rating into standing policy. Altman separately named the two failure modes he says the industry must avoid: loss of control, and excessive concentration of power in one lab or country. OpenAI says it welcomes federal safety requirements and independent audits but will not wait for legislation to act. POLICY
