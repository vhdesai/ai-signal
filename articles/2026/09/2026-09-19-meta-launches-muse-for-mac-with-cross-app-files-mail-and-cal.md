# Meta launches Muse for Mac with cross-app files, mail, and calendar access

MarkTechPost reports Meta launched Muse for Mac, a personal AI agent that operates across Files, Mail, Messages, Calendar, and Notes on macOS — Meta's most operating-system-integrated agent product to date. It follows this week's iPhone launch and adds calling capability alongside rival Instinct. Business Insider's own hands-on review remains skeptical: Muse struggles with the daily-scheduling coordination that consumer AI agents most need to solve.
