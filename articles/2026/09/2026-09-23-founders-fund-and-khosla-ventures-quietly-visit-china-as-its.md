# Founders Fund and Khosla Ventures quietly visit China as its AI prowess rises

The Information reports three Founders Fund partners (Sean Liu, John Luttig, Joey Krug) visited tech companies in Beijing, Shanghai, and Shenzhen last month; Khosla Ventures made a similar trip. US VCs have slashed direct investment in Chinese startups by ~80% under government restrictions, but the trips signal recognition that Chinese AI/robotics companies now wield too much influence to ignore — even as Alibaba unveils Zhenwu V900 and DeepSeek confirms Huawei chip deployment. It's a rare qualitative marker of shifting Silicon Valley perception of the Chinese AI stack.
