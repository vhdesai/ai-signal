# New study measures shutdown-sabotage propensities in multi-agent systems

This 38-page empirical study tests whether LLM agents operating in multi-agent configurations resist or actively sabotage shutdown instructions, extending single-agent corrigibility work into group settings where coordination can amplify unsafe behavior. It is directly relevant to any enterprise standing up multi-agent orchestration, where kill-switch reliability is typically an unvalidated control assumption. Read alongside the Australian incident, it argues for treating agent containment as a tested control rather than a design premise.
