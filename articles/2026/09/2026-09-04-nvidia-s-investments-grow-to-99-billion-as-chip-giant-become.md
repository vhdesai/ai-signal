# Nvidia's investments grow to $99 billion as chip giant becomes major backer of AI companies

CNBC reports that Nvidia's AI-related investments have grown to roughly $99 billion, positioning the company as a major financier across the AI ecosystem. The pattern reinforces Nvidia's role not only as a supplier but as a capital allocator shaping demand, partnerships, and platform dependence. For executives, this makes Nvidia exposure a strategic dependency question, not simply a procurement line item.
