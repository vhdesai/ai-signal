# MIT: Sherry Turkle’s “Artificial Intimacy” on Who We Become When We Talk to Machines

MIT STS professor Sherry Turkle’s book, published the same day, draws on interviews across the life cycle to argue chatbots supply a “pretend empathy” that degrades human development and social connection. She finds children blur the line between chatbots, people and objects.
