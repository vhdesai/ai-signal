# Meta Adds Explicit Safety Warning to Muse After SEV-2 Vulnerability in User Virtual Machines

Meta is adding a clearer in-app safety warning to Muse, its consumer AI agent, after an outside researcher reported through the bug bounty programme a flaw that could have let an attacker reach a user's dedicated virtual machine — the cloud account holding that user's emails and files. An internal Meta incident report reviewed by The Information classified the issue as a SEV-2, the third-highest level on Meta's five-point scale. Muse launched earlier in September and has topped free-app charts in the US and Canada, with Sensor Tower estimating about 2.8 million downloads in its first two weeks. The exposure follows a separately disclosed macOS dictation-endpoint zero-day that Meta hot-fixed on September 22.
