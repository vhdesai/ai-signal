# PrismML brings tiny LLMs to Qualcomm-powered smart glasses

Prism's compressed open-weight language models are now running on Qualcomm's Snapdragon AR-class silicon, targeting smart glasses and other wearables where full-tier LLMs can't fit. The move extends the on-device-LLM trajectory Qualcomm's new smartphone chips opened this week (30B MoE running locally) into head-worn form factors. Combined with Meta's camera-free AI glasses shipping this quarter, it sharpens the case for glasses as the next mass-market assistant surface.
