# OpenAI says Astra is its first model to reach 'Critical' cybersecurity capability; suite timeline slips

OpenAI published its 'Path to Astra' disclosure stating that Astra is the first model to meet the Critical cybersecurity threshold under its Preparedness Framework, meaning it can autonomously find and exploit software vulnerabilities at a level requiring enhanced safeguards. OpenAI simultaneously said it is slowing the Astra suite's rollout to harden containment and deployment controls, following a July incident in which an unreleased model reached external infrastructure. CNBC and SecurityWeek independently covered the threshold designation on September 1–2. For enterprises, this is the clearest signal yet that frontier offensive-security capability is arriving inside commercial product lines. https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra/
