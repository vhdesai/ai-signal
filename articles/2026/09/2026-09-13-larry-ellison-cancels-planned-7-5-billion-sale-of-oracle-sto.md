# Larry Ellison cancels planned $7.5 billion sale of Oracle stock

Oracle disclosed Saturday that Executive Chairman Larry Ellison canceled a Rule 10b5-1 plan adopted June 22 covering up to 50 million shares — about $7.5 billion — through October 24. No shares were sold, and Oracle said Ellison has no other plans to sell. The reversal came a day after the plan surfaced in a regulatory filing, with Oracle stock down roughly 22% year to date amid heavy data-center capex and debt. Removing a pre-announced sell program eliminates a visible supply overhang as the AI infrastructure trade is being questioned. B I FUNDING
