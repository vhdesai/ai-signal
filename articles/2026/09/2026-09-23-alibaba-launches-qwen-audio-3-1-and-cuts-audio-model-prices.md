# Alibaba launches Qwen-Audio 3.1 and cuts audio-model prices by up to 95%

Qwen released five audio models spanning ASR, TTS, and real-time interaction, alongside pricing reductions of up to 95%. ASR-Next adds multi-speaker identification with timestamps and detects emotion, ambient sounds, and machine noise; TTS covers multilingual synthesis. The price move undercuts Western speech-AI incumbents and reinforces Alibaba's aggressive audio-and-voice positioning heading into agentic-commerce workloads. Products & Tools PRODUCT
