# Microsoft targets 38 gigawatts of data-center capacity by 2032

Microsoft plans to grow global data-center capacity from roughly 12 gigawatts to more than 38 gigawatts by 2032, with AI-specific capacity reaching about 12.7 GW. The expansion follows Azure shortages that pushed some workloads to competitors and forced GitHub to redirect AI agent traffic to AWS. Microsoft added 88 data centers in fiscal 2026 and holds more than $329 billion in lease commitments. Grid interconnects, transformers and local political resistance — not financing — are the constraints most likely to slow delivery. SUPPLY CHAIN
