# NYC and Los Angeles school districts impose new AI moratoriums

York City and Los Angeles, the two largest US school districts, moved to restrict student-facing generative AI use ahead of the 2026-27 school year: NYC banned student-facing AI in K-8 classes while allowing limited approved tools for high schoolers, and LAUSD announced a one-year moratorium on generative AI use on district devices. The item shows organized institutional pushback against AI adoption where child safety, learning outcomes, and governance processes remain unresolved.
