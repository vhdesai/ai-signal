# DeepSeek plans a 160,000-chip Huawei cluster at a 1GW Inner Mongolia site

DeepSeek plans to deploy at least 160,000 Huawei AI accelerators at a new Inner Mongolia data center, citing Bloomberg. The chips are expected to support model operation rather than high-end training, where DeepSeek reportedly still relies on NVIDIA accelerators. If executed, the deployment would be one of the clearest tests of China’s domestic AI silicon stack at hyperscale.
