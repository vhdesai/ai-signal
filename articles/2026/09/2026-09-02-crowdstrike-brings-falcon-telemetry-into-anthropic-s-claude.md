# CrowdStrike brings Falcon telemetry into Anthropic's Claude Marketplace

CrowdStrike announced that its Falcon platform is coming to Anthropic's Claude Marketplace, allowing Claude-based agents to draw on Falcon security telemetry and controls. The move turns AI marketplaces into enterprise-security distribution channels rather than simple plug-in catalogs. It also shows security vendors racing to place controls directly where agent workflows are assembled.
