# Musk says xAI's Colossus now runs ~780,000 Nvidia GPUs, with 440,000 more due by October

Elon Musk disclosed that xAI's Memphis "Colossus" facility operates roughly 780,000 Nvidia Hopper and Blackwell processors, with 220,000 GB300 units going live next week and another 220,000 in October — potentially 1.44M processors by year end. Nvidia shares rose 0.7% premarket on the disclosure. The deployment velocity matters more than the headcount: Nvidia needs data centers built fast enough to absorb supply, and Colossus is the counterexample to Oracle's New Mexico delays. Figures are Musk's own and are not independently confirmed.
