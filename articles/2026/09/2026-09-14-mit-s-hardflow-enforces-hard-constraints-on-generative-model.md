# MIT's HardFlow enforces hard constraints on generative models without retraining

MIT researchers introduced HardFlow, a deployment-time algorithm that lets pretrained diffusion and flow-matching models satisfy nonnegotiable safety, physical or task constraints. Rather than forcing every intermediate sampling step to comply — the standard projection-based approach — HardFlow reformulates constrained sampling as a trajectory-optimisation problem and enforces constraints on the final output only. Across robotics, physical-process control and computer-vision experiments it met all required constraints while producing better solutions than baselines. The work, from senior author Navid Azizan with Zeyang Li and Kaveh Alim, appears in IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence.
