# Google Research maps the complete male fruit fly brain

Google Research announced a connectomics milestone: mapping the complete male fruit fly brain using AI-assisted reconstruction and verification workflows. The work builds on years of neural-mapping advances and shows how AI can reduce the cost of producing large, scientifically useful datasets. The broader point is that AI's research impact often comes from making previously impractical data products economically feasible. Google Research RESEARCH BIODESIGN
