# Apple’s new CEO builds up to unveiling the ideal AI device — then says it is the iPhone

In his first keynote as Apple CEO, John Ternus described the ideal AI device and then declared it already exists — the iPhone. Gurman reports insiders expected the home hub, codenamed J490, instead; it is in employee home testing and could ship as early as next month, described as a smaller Echo Show with facial recognition via the front camera. Siri AI shipped this month with iOS 27, rebuilt on a custom Google Gemini model reportedly costing around $1B per year, and launched with a waitlist. Apple also cut a handful of Fitness+ staff, mainly in audio.
