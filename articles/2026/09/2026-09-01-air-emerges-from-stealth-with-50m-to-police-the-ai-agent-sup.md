# AIR emerges from stealth with $50M to police the AI agent supply chain

AIR, founded by former Israeli Unit 8200 operators, launched publicly with $50 million raised across two seed rounds led by Sequoia and Greenoaks. Its platform discovers agents running inside an enterprise, inspects the plug-ins, skills, and MCP servers they call, and blocks components that fail security review.
