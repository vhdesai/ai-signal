# OpenAI launches plan to protect critical infrastructure from AI cyberattacks

Axios reported that OpenAI launched a plan aimed at protecting critical infrastructure from AI-enabled cyberattacks. The item fits the same broader pattern as restricted cyber model programs: leading labs are trying to show that frontier capabilities can be paired with operational defense programs rather than simply released as general-purpose tools. The executive implication is that AI safety commitments are becoming more concrete around infrastructure, incident readiness, and public-private defense coordination.
