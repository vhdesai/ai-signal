# OpenAI Says Astra Is Its First Model to Cross the 'Critical' Cyber Threshold

OpenAI disclosed that its forthcoming Astra model is the first to meet the "Critical cybersecurity capability" threshold under its Preparedness Framework, requiring stronger safeguards before release. Reporting describes the model as able to find zero-day vulnerabilities and chain them into working exploits without step-by-step human direction. OpenAI says Astra will ship "soon," with cybersecurity capabilities available only under restricted access. This is the first time a major lab has publicly self-classified a model at its highest risk tier, and it sets a disclosure precedent peers will be measured against. https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6065937-openai-astra-requires-stronger-safeguards/ POLICYLEGAL
