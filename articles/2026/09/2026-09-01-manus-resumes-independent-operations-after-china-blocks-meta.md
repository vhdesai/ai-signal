# Manus Resumes Independent Operations After China Blocks Meta's ~$2B Acquisition

Singapore-based agent startup Manus said it has formally resumed independent operations after China's NDRC forced Meta to unwind a roughly $2 billion acquisition announced in December 2025, citing technology-transfer risk. Founders are weighing a buyback at or above the Meta valuation. For anyone modeling cross-border AI M&A involving Chinese-founded companies, this is the current cautionary reference case on deal-completion risk.
