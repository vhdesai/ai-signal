# Anthropic explores bringing more billing and payments technology in-house

Anthropic job postings indicate that it is evaluating internal billing, fraud-detection, and other payments infrastructure as usage and token-based pricing grow more complex. The effort could reduce reliance on Stripe for selected services, but the reporting does not establish that Anthropic has replaced its payment providers.
