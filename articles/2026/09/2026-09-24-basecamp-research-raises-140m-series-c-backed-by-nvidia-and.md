# Basecamp Research raises $140M Series C backed by NVIDIA and Anthropic for AI-designed therapeutics

Basecamp Research closed a $140M Series C led by S32 with NVIDIA and Anthropic participating, for AI-designed therapeutics. Basecamp's EDEN model trains on the Trillion Gene Atlas — described as the largest biological dataset used in AI training — and is building candidates including in vivo cell therapies. The round joins Alphabet-backed Isomorphic Labs' $2.1B Series B and Enveda's $311M Series E this year, and pairs with today's Anthropic gene-editing news to cement AI-native biotech as the year's most consequential funding trend at the frontier lab / pharma boundary.
