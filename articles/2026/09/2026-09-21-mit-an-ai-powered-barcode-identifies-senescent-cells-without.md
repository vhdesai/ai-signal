# MIT: An AI-Powered "Barcode" Identifies Senescent Cells Without Destroying Them

MIT researchers combined Raman microscopy with single-cell spatial RNA sequencing to build an AI-derived optical signature that noninvasively identifies "zombie" senescent cells in aging tissue, published in Nature Aging. Unlike existing markers such as p16 and p21, which require destroying the cells being measured, the Raman-based method leaves them intact. Tests on skin and lung tissue from 2-month-old versus 26-month-old mice showed sharp increases in lipid synthesis, plus tissue-specific changes in collagen remodeling and immune gene activity. A single 1 mm² sample currently takes about 30 hours to analyze; the team is building faster imaging and adapting the method to human tissue.
