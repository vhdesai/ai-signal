# Google Researcher Dissents: “Stability Is the Speed Limit”

The substantive pushback to the pacing consensus came from Google researcher Peyman Milanfar, who argued recursive self-improvement is a naive premise because its feedback loops are inherently unstable: “Systems that reliably improve themselves will be governed, damped, slow, and bounded by margins that look wasteful. Because stability is the speed limit.” The disagreement matters because it separates two things the weekend’s coverage conflated — agreement on independent evaluation as a mechanism, versus agreement on the underlying risk model. Only the former is actually shared across the labs.
