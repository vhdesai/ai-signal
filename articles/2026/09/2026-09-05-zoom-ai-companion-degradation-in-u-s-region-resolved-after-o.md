# Zoom AI Companion degradation in U.S. region resolved after overnight incident

Technobezz reported, based on Zoom status updates, that Zoom investigated and resolved a minor U.S.-region degradation affecting Zoom AI Companion. The incident was identified and resolved overnight, with Zoom continuing monitoring after restoration. Even small AI-assistant outages matter because meeting summarization, action extraction, and workplace copilots are becoming embedded in operational workflows rather than optional add-ons.
