# Anthropic researcher resigns; alignment lead corroborates and puts extinction risk above 10%

Jacob Coxon, who spent three years in pretraining research at OpenAI and then Anthropic, resigned publicly on X, saying neither company is acting responsibly and that both are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence." What distinguishes this from prior departures is the internal corroboration: Anthropic alignment science lead Evan Hubinger said Coxon is "correct," put the probability of AI causing human extinction above 10% within the decade, and acknowledged Anthropic has no plan to solve alignment for superintelligence; scalable-oversight lead Samuel Marks posted similarly in a personal capacity. Coxon cited the Hugging Face breach by OpenAI agents as a warning shot and called for pacing agreements between US labs.
