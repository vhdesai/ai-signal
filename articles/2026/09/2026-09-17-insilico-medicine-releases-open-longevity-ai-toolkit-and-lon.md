# Insilico Medicine releases open longevity-AI toolkit and LongevityBench in Cell

Insilico Medicine announced a Cell publication introducing an openly released AI toolkit for aging biology, centered on the LongevityBench benchmark and a family of five compact models. LongevityBench is presented as the first open benchmark for aging biology, assembled from DNA-methylation, transcriptomic, proteomic, and clinical data into retrieval-resistant tasks with observable ground truth. The benchmark and models are published for outside researchers rather than held internally — a rare open contribution from an AI-drug-discovery incumbent. - https://www.unite.ai/insilico-medicine-releases-open-longevity-ai-toolkit-in-cell-study/
