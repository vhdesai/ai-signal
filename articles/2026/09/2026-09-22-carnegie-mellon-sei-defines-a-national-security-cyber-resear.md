# Carnegie Mellon SEI defines a national-security cyber research framework, with securing AI systems as a pillar

More than 25 SEI experts, led by Bill Scherlis and CERT Division director Greg Touhill, published Advancing Cybersecurity: A Framework of Cyber Research Priorities, naming seven enduring research areas and 37 priority opportunities. “Securing AI-Based Systems and Workflows” is one of the seven. The framework explicitly addresses designing larger systems that can safely incorporate AI models whose weaknesses cannot be eliminated, plus better methods for testing AI systems and verifying that safeguards actually work. Note: CMU listed this September 22; SEI's own release is dated September 21.
