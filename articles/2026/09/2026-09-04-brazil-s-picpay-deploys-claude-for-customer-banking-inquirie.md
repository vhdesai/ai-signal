# Brazil’s PicPay deploys Claude for customer banking inquiries

Brazilian digital bank PicPay began rolling out an Anthropic Claude integration that lets customers query financial information conversationally. It is a narrow, high-volume deployment in a regulated sector — the kind of scoped use case that has held up better in production than open-ended assistants. Watch the disclosure and audit posture around account data access as the pattern spreads across financial services.
