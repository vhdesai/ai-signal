# Oracle sends force majeure notice on New Mexico Stargate data-center site

Oracle sent a force majeure notice to the developer of Project Jupiter, a Stargate data-center campus in New Mexico, while saying it still expects the project to stay on schedule. TechCrunch reported that the 2.45 GW campus depends on gas-powered fuel cells from Bloom Energy and faces delays tied to a gas pipeline and pending air-quality permit. The episode is a concrete reminder that AI infrastructure schedules depend on energy permitting, gas supply, and local politics, not just capital commitments. OPTICAL NETWORKINGAI DATA CENTERS
