# KAIST and Naver AI Lab: Written Reasoning Steps Map to Separable Internal Patterns

Researchers examined Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen3-8B and Gemma4-31B solving mathematics problems, segmenting solutions into eight recurring operations such as decomposition, formula recall, deduction and computation. Across all three models, classifiers separated the reasoning categories from internal representations, most sharply in the middle layers. The controls are what make it interesting: a token-only classifier underperformed, and blocking attention to the preceding 30 tokens weakened the signal. Critically, operations stayed identifiable even when the answer was wrong — “a faulty computation still looked internally like a computation” — which bears directly on whether chain-of-thought text is a faithful window into computation. Mathematics-only, small model set, no independent reproduction yet.
