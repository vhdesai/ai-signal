# Google: attackers built and ran a credential-harvesting campaign with AI agents in under six hours

Google Threat Intelligence Group's report "From Prompting to Autonomy" documents a financially motivated actor that, after compromising a cloud environment, assembled an autonomous multi-agent framework from an AI coding chatbot, a prompt, and Markdown playbooks — then ran scanning, IP rotation, and credential harvesting largely unsupervised, compromising thousands of third-party credentials in under six hours. A separate exposed C2 server managed more than 23,800 harvested secrets. GTIG also tracks TeamPCP/UNC6780 poisoning PyPI, npm, and Docker Hub with the DUSTMAKER stealer, which plants files in hidden AI-assistant directories. Google says it has not yet observed fully autonomous end-to-end intrusion pipelines in the wild — a threshold that now looks near, not distant.
