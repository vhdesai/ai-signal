# WSJ: AI Usage Reaches 18.8% of Working-Age Population as Meta Pivots to Business Buyers

The WSJ reported AI adoption at 18.8% among working-age people, alongside a separate piece on Meta's push to convert its consumer AI spending into business-customer revenue. The two stories together frame the central question for 2027 budgets: consumer reach is broad but monetization still concentrates in enterprise contracts. Both are relevant to any 2027 planning assumption that treats consumer AI adoption as a proxy for commercial demand. URL behind paywall. ENTERPRISE
