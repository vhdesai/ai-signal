# Trump "Super Intelligence" Lunch: Six Labs Sign a Voluntary Safety Accord — Palantir’s Karp Refuses, Apple Is Absent

At Trump’s AI safety lunch, executives from Google, Anthropic, Meta, Nvidia, OpenAI, and xAI committed to "implement robust internal controls" on training and deployment (especially cybersecurity and biosecurity) and to call on independent evaluators to verify adherence — commitments DealBook notes largely mirror the voluntary ones made under the Biden administration. Palantir CEO Alex Karp declined to sign and reportedly snapped at reporters when pressed. Apple was conspicuously absent despite Tim Cook’s recent proximity to the White House. Trump separately signed an executive order rebranding AI as "super intelligence." Left unaddressed: state-law patchwork, cross-border coordination, and civil liability — the exact issues Anthropic’s S-1 and today’s OpenAI lawsuit now surface publicly.
