# PitchBook: AI's safety slowdown spooks the market and slows IPOs, but hands incumbents more room

PitchBook's Susan Hu argues venture is at a paradox — 2026 exit value hit a record $2.1T (2.4× 2021), yet the median VC-backed IPO trails the Morningstar Growth Index by 38.2% in the first 120 days as public investors resist elevated venture pricing. Of 110 IPO-ready and backlogged companies since 2023, 86 have continued to raise venture capital. Amodei's slowdown call and Altman ruling out an OpenAI 2026 listing extend that liquidity gap; secondaries (record $107.1B in the last year) are filling the void. The winners of a real slowdown: Anthropic and OpenAI (compliance costs absorbable at their scale), incumbents (regulatory moat), and smaller open-source labs lose R&D bandwidth to compliance.
