# Nscale seeks $3.5B in pre-IPO financing on the back of a $45B Anthropic deal

AI compute provider Nscale is in talks to raise roughly $3.5 billion ahead of a planned IPO, following a reported $45 billion capacity agreement with Anthropic. The raise would fund buildout against contracted demand rather than speculative capacity.
