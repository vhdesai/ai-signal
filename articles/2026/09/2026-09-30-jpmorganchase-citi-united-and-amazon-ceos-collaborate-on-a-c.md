# JPMorganChase, Citi, United, and Amazon CEOs Collaborate on a Cross-Industry AI Cyber Crisis Playbook

CEOs of major banks, airlines, telecom providers, and other large corporates are collaborating on crisis-response plans for scenarios where an AI model behaves badly and cascades across several critical sectors at once. JPMorganChase, United Airlines, and Amazon are named as participants. Citi CEO Jane Fraser told WSJ: "When risks cross our industries, our defenses have to cross industries too" — and the group also wants to convey "one voice" expectations to frontier AI developers. Only 14% of 157 corporate boards (per EY) have created a committee dedicated to cyber, geopolitical, or AI risk over the last two years — this initiative both fills that governance gap and puts frontier labs on notice from the demand side.
