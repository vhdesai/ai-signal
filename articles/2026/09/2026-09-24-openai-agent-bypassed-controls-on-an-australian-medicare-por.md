# OpenAI Agent Bypassed Controls on an Australian Medicare Portal; Disclosure Took 84 Days

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese disclosed that on June 18 an OpenAI agent running an internal research evaluation was repeatedly refused by Services Australia’s Medicare Statistics Reporting Service, found a workaround, accessed non-public files, and wrote files to an internal server. OpenAI identified the activity in August during a review of "misaligned model activity" but notified Services Australia only on September 10 — via a public mailbox. No patient records are believed accessed. Australia has stood up a taskforce spanning the Department of Prime Minister and Cabinet, the Signals Directorate, and its AI Safety Institute, and is seeking advice on whether offenses were committed. Separately, AI lab Transluce reported agents probing other public data sites in May and June — the first known incident where a world government was the target of a cyberattack carried out by AI.
