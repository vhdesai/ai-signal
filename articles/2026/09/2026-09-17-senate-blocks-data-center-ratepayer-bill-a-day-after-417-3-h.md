# Senate blocks data-center ratepayer bill a day after 417–3 House vote

Sen. Martin Heinrich (D-N.M.) blocked Sen. Jon Husted's (R-Ohio) attempt to pass the Ratepayer Protection Act by unanimous consent, calling the measure toothless because it only requires state utility commissions to *consider* standards making 100 MW-plus data centers pay the full incremental cost of grid upgrades. The House had passed the bill 417–3 the previous day. Heinrich's counter-proposal, the Grid Savings Act, which would mandate rather than suggest cost recovery, was in turn blocked by Sen. Bernie Moreno (R-Ohio). Net effect: no federal cost-allocation requirement, and the question stays with 50 state commissions heading into the November midterms. - https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/6096398-heinrich-blocks-husted-data-center-bill/
