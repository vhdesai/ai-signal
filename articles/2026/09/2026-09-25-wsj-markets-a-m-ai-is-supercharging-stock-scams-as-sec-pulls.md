# WSJ Markets A.M.: AI is supercharging stock scams as SEC pulls back

WSJ's Markets A.M. profiles how AI-generated hype is turbocharging microcap pump-and-dumps, citing Astrotech's 2,600% surge on 9,000× normal volume in May. Even as securities regulators shed staff and drop investigations, private-sector defenses are emerging — ScamDunk uses proprietary algorithms to scan Reddit, StockTwits, and YouTube daily and flagged Astrotech two months before the surge. FINRA predicts AI-enabled fraud could grow to $40B by next year. It's a striking narrative counterweight to today's Copilot / Muse launches: the same generative capabilities that anchor enterprise product roadmaps are directly measurable in retail-investor harm.
