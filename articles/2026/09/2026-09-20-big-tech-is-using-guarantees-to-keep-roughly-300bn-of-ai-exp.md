# Big Tech is using guarantees to keep roughly $300bn of AI exposure off its balance sheets

The Financial Times reports that technology companies issued up to $300bn of residual-value guarantees over the past year to back debt for AI data centres and chips, routed through special-purpose vehicles so the liabilities sit in footnotes rather than on the balance sheet. Cited structures include Broadcom's $29bn exposure on a chip sale to an SPV leasing to Anthropic, and Nvidia's guarantees to SB Energy for an Ohio campus serving OpenAI. Projects carrying these guarantees price roughly 100–150 basis points above the guarantor's own debt. Morgan Stanley estimates more than $3.1tn in off-balance-sheet commitments and credit support across major hyperscalers and chipmakers — the core reason credit analysts, not equity analysts, are now driving the AI-bubble debate.
