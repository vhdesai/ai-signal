# What's New in Oracle AI? September 2026 Edition

Oracle Blogs published its September update on Oracle AI capabilities and learning resources. The main signal is that enterprise AI competition is increasingly packaged as platform-level enablement: skills, data services, application integration, and governance are being bundled alongside model access. For senior leaders, vendor roadmaps should be evaluated by operational fit, not just headline model partnerships.
