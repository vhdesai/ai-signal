# Microsoft consolidates Copilot into a single enterprise AI platform with flexible pricing

Microsoft unified its fragmented Copilot SKUs into one enterprise AI platform this weekend, framed by execs as "the AI OS" for workflow automation. Chosun and Foreign Policy Journal report new flexible pricing tiers that decouple seat licensing from usage-based agent runs. It's Microsoft's clearest attempt yet to counter Salesforce Agentforce and Google Workspace + Gemini in the enterprise agent race — with FinOps for AI billed as the pricing-control side of the story.
