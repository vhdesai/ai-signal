# Meta Acquires Stilla.ai to Expand Business Agent Commerce

Meta has acquired Stilla.ai, a startup focused on business agent commerce, to accelerate its enterprise commerce agent stack. The acquisition complements Meta's rumored Hatch agent tests and its "Argentina AI messaging" push flagged by SimplyWall.st. For CIOs, it signals Meta plans to compete for agentic commerce in WhatsApp Business and Instagram Shops alongside Alibaba's Accio and Amazon's Rufus — a category that is quickly becoming a distribution proxy for the frontier-model race.
