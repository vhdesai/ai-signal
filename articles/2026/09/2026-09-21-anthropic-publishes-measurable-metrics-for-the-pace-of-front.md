# Anthropic Publishes Measurable Metrics for the Pace of Frontier AI Development

Anthropic released measurement tools intended to show how quickly frontier AI is advancing inside labs, including how much AI contributes to AI R&D, how well internal agents are overseen, and what share of compute is allocated to safety. The company disclosed that Claude currently "leads" 26% of measured Anthropic AI R&D work and collaborates on more than 90%. This turns the "pace the frontier" debate into something more auditable: lab-level operational metrics that third-party evaluators and governments could monitor, rather than principles-based rhetoric.
