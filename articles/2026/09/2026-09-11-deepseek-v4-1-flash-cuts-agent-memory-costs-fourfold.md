# DeepSeek V4.1-Flash Cuts Agent Memory Costs Fourfold

DeepSeek closed the first half of September with V4.1-Flash, which reduces KV cache footprint to roughly 25% of V4-Flash for long agent sessions. It caps the densest ten-day stretch of frontier releases this year — Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 (Sep 1), Gemini 3.8 Flash and its gated Cyber variant (Sep 2), Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 (Sep 2) and GPT-6 Astra (Sep 3). Gains are coming from post-training scaling and architectural efficiency rather than new base architectures, and four of five frontier launches now ship tiered, gated cyber-capability access.
