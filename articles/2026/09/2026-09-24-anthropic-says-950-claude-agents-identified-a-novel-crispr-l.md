# Anthropic says ~950 Claude agents identified a novel CRISPR-like enzyme system

Anthropic disclosed the first result from its molecular biology lab: roughly 950 Claude agents searched DNA sequence databases for 21 hours, screened more than 200,000 genes for one enzyme class and narrowed to 20 candidates. One agent flagged short repeating DNA segments in bacteriophages adjacent to a known enzyme and an uncharacterized protein — an architecture resembling CRISPR. The system, named ART, has been confirmed in wet-lab work but its function remains unknown, and the preprint is not peer reviewed. CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang called the finding “genuinely intriguing” and worth further investigation.
