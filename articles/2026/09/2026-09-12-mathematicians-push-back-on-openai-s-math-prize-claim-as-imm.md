# Mathematicians Push Back on OpenAI's Math-Prize Claim as "Immature Playground Boasting"

Leading mathematicians are pushing back on OpenAI's claim that GPT-6 Astra solved a major open math prize, with one describing the public messaging as "immature playground boasting." Two prominent mathematicians have also demanded OpenAI disclose the training data used, arguing that without provenance, benchmark scores cannot be interpreted. The exchange is a preview of what evaluation-credibility disputes will look like in the AGI-messaging era, and a signal that CIOs should demand contamination-audit evidence before pricing benchmark deltas into procurement.
