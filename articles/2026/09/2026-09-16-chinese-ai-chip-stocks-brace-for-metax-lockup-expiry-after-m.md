# Chinese AI-chip stocks brace for MetaX lockup expiry after Moore Threads shed ~49B yuan

14 million restricted MetaX Integrated Circuits shares unlock Thursday, and Chinese analysts expect "significant selling pressure" — weeks after Moore Threads's equivalent lockup expiry wiped nearly 49B yuan. For executives tracking the Chinese AI-infrastructure trade, this is a concrete near-term event risk in a segment where Enflame's 179% pop and Z.ai's ~$5B raise had signaled durable strength. The Moore Threads precedent argues that the Chinese AI-chip rally is partly technical, not entirely fundamental. - https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3367745/chinas-ai-chip-stocks-face-crucial-test-metax-lock-period-expires
