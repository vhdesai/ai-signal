# OpenAI releases GPT-6 Astra and keeps initial access restricted

OpenAI began releasing GPT-6 Astra, positioning it as a major advance in commercial tasks including financial modeling, engineering design, computer use, programming, presentations, and spreadsheets. The model is initially limited to selected organizations, including customers in OpenAI's Daybreak cybersecurity program, before broader paid-tier and API rollout. The launch ties frontier capability directly to access controls because Astra is also being framed as a high-capability cyber model. The Information search · OpenAI · TechCrunch
