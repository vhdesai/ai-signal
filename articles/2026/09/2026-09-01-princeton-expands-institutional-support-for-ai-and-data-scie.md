# Princeton expands institutional support for AI and data-science scholarship

Princeton announced expanded university-level support for AI and data-science research and scholarship, formalizing investment across its Center for Statistics and Machine Learning and related programs. The move adds to a wave of universities restructuring around AI as a core research pillar rather than a satellite specialty.
