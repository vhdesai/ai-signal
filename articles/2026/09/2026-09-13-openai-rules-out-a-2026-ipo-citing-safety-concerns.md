# OpenAI Rules Out a 2026 IPO, Citing Safety Concerns

Sam Altman told Fortune that listing now would be "ill-advised," pushing what was expected to be a near-trillion-dollar offering to 2027 at the earliest. CFO Sarah Friar had told employees only last month that a 2027-or-sooner listing was likely. The statement came the same weekend Amodei published his three-step pacing framework, which Altman and Musk both endorsed publicly. This is the clearest evidence yet that safety positioning is now shaping capital-markets timing, not just product messaging. FUNDING
