# ChatGPT, Claude and Grok hit by overlapping outages

Anthropic confirmed a Claude outage beginning around 9:41 AM ET on September 3 with elevated error rates across multiple models, coinciding with degraded service reported by other major providers including OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex. OpenAI opened an incident on its status page the same day citing elevated errors across transactions.
