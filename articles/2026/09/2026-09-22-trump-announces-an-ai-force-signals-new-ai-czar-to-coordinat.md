# Trump Announces an "AI Force," Signals New AI Czar to Coordinate Federal AI Policy

President Trump announced an "AI Force" modeled on Space Force and signaled plans to appoint a new AI czar, calling recent industry safety warnings a "hoax" and reiterating that federal policy will not "hinder or stifle the Growth of this incredible Industry." Former AI/crypto czar David Sacks (still an external adviser) opposes creating a self-regulatory body, and it remains unclear how AI Force would differ from the Commerce Department's existing Center for AI Standards and Innovation. The announcement lands as CISOs, insurers, and academic critics push for the opposite direction. Industry News BREAKING HOT FINANCE
