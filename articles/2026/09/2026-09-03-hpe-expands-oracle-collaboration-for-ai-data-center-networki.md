# HPE expands Oracle collaboration for AI data center networking

Engineering.com reports that HPE expanded collaboration with Oracle around AI data-center networking. The story reflects an increasingly important bottleneck: AI infrastructure performance depends on networking, storage, and cluster operations as much as accelerator supply. For executives, vendor ecosystem depth and reference architectures may become decisive in time-to-capacity.
