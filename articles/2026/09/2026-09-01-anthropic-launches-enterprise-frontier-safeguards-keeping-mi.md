# Anthropic Launches Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, Keeping Misuse Logs in the Customer's Cloud

Enterprise Frontier Safeguards combines the privacy of zero data retention with automated misuse detection by storing monitoring data in the customer's own cloud account under the customer's keys, rather than Anthropic's. Detection remains Anthropic-run while custody and flag review sit with the customer. It targets regulated enterprises that need misuse monitoring without surrendering log custody — a direct answer to the data-sovereignty objection that stalls AI procurement in financial services, healthcare, and government. Rollout is scheduled for this fall.
