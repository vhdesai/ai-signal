# Katzenberg Teams With Former OpenAI Sora Head on New AI Video Startup

Hollywood mogul Jeffrey Katzenberg's investment firm is partnering with the former head of OpenAI's Sora app to launch a new AI video startup that will train its own foundation models and target filmmakers directly. The founders are in talks with Andreessen Horowitz and other investors about significant funding. The move signals continued capital appetite for verticalized video-generation labs even as Sora, Runway, and Meta ship broad-market tools — and reinforces Hollywood as an increasingly explicit customer segment for frontier video models.
