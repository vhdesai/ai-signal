# Antitrust suit alleges OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and SpaceXAI illegally agreed to slow AI development

Four named plaintiffs who pay for ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Gemini subscriptions filed a proposed nationwide class action Friday in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California, arguing that coordinated "pacing" of AI development violates antitrust law and reduces the value subscribers receive. The complaint centers on September 12, when Amodei published his slowdown essay and Altman, Musk and Hassabis publicly concurred the same day. "The antitrust laws do not permit competitors to decide among themselves that competition is too dangerous," the plaintiffs argue. Amodei had anticipated the exposure, writing that a narrow government waiver for safety discussions would help.
