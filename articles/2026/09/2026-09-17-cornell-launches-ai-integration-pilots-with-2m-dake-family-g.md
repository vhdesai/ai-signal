# Cornell launches AI Integration Pilots with $2M Dake family gift

Cornell launched AI Integration Pilots and expanded its AI Undergraduate Fellows program, funded by a $2 million gift from the Dake family. Undergraduates serve as embedded classroom researchers partnering with faculty to evaluate AI's effect on learning. Vice provost Steven Jackson emphasized a deliberately discipline-specific design, noting AI may make academic areas more distinct rather than more uniform. Pilot teams run from spring 2027 through spring 2028, with results shared in 2028. - https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/09/ai-education-initiatives-emphasize-experimentation-balanced-use
