# AI leaders warn the UN about security risks as systems grow more powerful

Reuters reported that AI leaders warned the United Nations about security risks as AI systems become more powerful; OpenAI also published Sam Altman's remarks to the UN Security Council. The discussion underscores that frontier AI risk is now being treated as a matter of international security, not only domestic technology policy. The emerging policy focus is on incident reporting, shared safety standards, and mechanisms for cross-border coordination before AI failures escalate. URL behind paywall.
