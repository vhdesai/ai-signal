# Moonshot AI targets $2B annual revenue as Kimi K3 sees 300B daily tokens on OpenRouter

Moonshot AI is now targeting $2B in annualized revenue, per TechCrunch's read of company financial signals, even as Kimi K3 usage has slipped modestly in recent months. OpenRouter data still shows K3 generating up to 300 billion tokens per day on that platform alone — a genuine top-3 open-weights consumption rate. Combined with this week's Hong Kong/Shanghai dual-IPO exploration, it positions Moonshot as the highest-revenue Chinese frontier lab moving into public markets. PEOPLE
