# Meta's Muse agent draws privacy and access backlash on multiple fronts

Meta's Muse personal agent faced compounding trust problems: reporting that it requested broad disk and credential access, claims it reached private messages, a patched zero-day that could have exposed the per-user virtual machines holding emails and files, and Amazon blocking Muse from shopping on its platform for lack of permission. Elon Musk amplified the privacy criticism and Meta shares gave back their Muse-driven gains. For enterprises, this is a preview of the permissioning fights that follow consumer agents into corporate data.
