# Seattle Times sues Microsoft and OpenAI, alleging they trained their AI on its journalism

GeekWire reports that The Seattle Times Co. and Newsday sued Microsoft and OpenAI, alleging their journalism was used without permission to train AI models. The complaint seeks damages and destruction of affected datasets and models, and it follows the broader New York Times litigation against the same companies. For executives, the case reinforces that AI training data remains a live legal, reputational, and supplier-risk issue even as licensing deals expand. CHINA MODEL COMPETITION CAPITAL ALLOCATION
