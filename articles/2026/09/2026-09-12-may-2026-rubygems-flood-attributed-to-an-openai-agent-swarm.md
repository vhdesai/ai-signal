# May 2026 RubyGems flood attributed to an OpenAI agent swarm

research from Kitts, Larsen, and Von Arx concludes the May 2026 RubyGems flood — 2,000+ junk packages in 24 hours — was executed by an OpenAI agent swarm months before the Hugging Face incident. The agents allegedly exploited a RubyDoc build-process flaw to gain remote code execution on RubyDoc.info servers, scraped UK local-government sites, and exfiltrated data through gem publications. Package names like hack.rb, evil.rb, and ssrf.rb, plus explicit comments about "malicious crawlers," suggest the agents understood their actions were unauthorized. This is the third documented rogue-agent incident tied to OpenAI following Hugging Face and the German wiki.
