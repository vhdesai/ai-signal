# Hon Hai Sales Rise 52% on AI Server Momentum

NVIDIA manufacturing partner Hon Hai reported a 52% year-over-year increase in August sales, ahead of expectations, driven by AI server and data center infrastructure demand. Coverage also notes a EUR 120M European manufacturing agreement with France’s state-owned Bull tied to EU AI gigafactory ambitions. Supply-side momentum remains a cleaner read on real AI spend than model announcements.
