# Tencent-backed Enflame roughly triples on Shanghai STAR Market debut after $912M raise

Shanghai Enflame Technology began trading under ticker 688801 after raising CNY 6.12 billion, about $912 million, and the stock roughly tripled at open — lifting the still-loss-making Nvidia challenger toward a market value near $26 billion. Tencent retains 17.95% and accounted for 83.79% of Enflame's 2025 revenue, a concentration worth noting. The signal is that China's accelerator-substitution program is now being capitalized at scale by public markets, not only by state funds.
