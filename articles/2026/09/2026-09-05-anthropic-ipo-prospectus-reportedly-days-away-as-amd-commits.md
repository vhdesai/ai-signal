# Anthropic IPO prospectus reportedly days away as AMD commits up to $5B

Anthropic's public listing is reportedly weeks out, with the prospectus expected imminently in what would be one of the largest AI-sector debuts to date. Separately, AMD has committed up to $5 billion to Anthropic with conditions attached — the latest instance of a chip supplier taking an equity position in a major model customer. A public Anthropic price would give the sector its first clean, continuously marked comparable to OpenAI's private valuation.
