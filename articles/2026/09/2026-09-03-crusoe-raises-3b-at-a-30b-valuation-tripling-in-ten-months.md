# Crusoe raises $3B at a $30B valuation, tripling in ten months

Data center developer Crusoe raised more than $3 billion at a roughly $30 billion valuation, co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners with Mubadala Capital participating — about ten months after a $1.38B round at roughly $10B. Customers reportedly include Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI, and the round follows a reported five-year, ~$13 billion GPU capacity agreement with Jane Street. The arc from flared-gas bitcoin mining to neocloud provider illustrates where AI margin is currently accruing: power, land, and capacity contracts. N A FUNDING
