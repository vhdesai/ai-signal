# Oracle and Adobe face opposite AI moat tests

Saxo framed Oracle and Adobe's upcoming earnings as a clean comparison of two AI-era business models: Oracle must prove AI demand can convert into cash after heavy data-center spending, while Adobe must prove AI strengthens rather than commoditizes creative workflows. Oracle's challenge is capital intensity; Adobe's is product differentiation as generative creation becomes easier.
