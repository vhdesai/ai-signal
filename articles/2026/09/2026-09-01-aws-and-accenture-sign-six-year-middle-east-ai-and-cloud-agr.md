# AWS and Accenture Sign Six-Year Middle East AI and Cloud Agreement

AWS and Accenture entered a six-year agreement to accelerate cloud, data modernization, and AI deployment across the Middle East, with Accenture standing up a dedicated regional delivery team. The deal rides the Gulf's compute buildout, including PIF-backed HUMAIN and AWS's planned Saudi region. The signal is a regional shift from GPU acquisition toward enterprise deployment and services capacity.
