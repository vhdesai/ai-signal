# Sequoia-incubated Empirik launches with $21M to predict infrastructure outages

Empirik came out of Sequoia incubation with $21 million to apply predictive modeling to infrastructure failure, targeting outage prevention rather than post-incident response. It fits the broader pattern in this window of AI capital moving toward operational reliability and control planes rather than foundation models. Details on customers and deployment scale were not disclosed in available coverage.
