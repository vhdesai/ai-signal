# Snorkel AI Raises $350M at $3.5B as Training-Data Demand Compounds

Snorkel AI closed a $350M Series E co-led by Insight Partners and S32, nearly tripling the $1.3B valuation it held 17 months ago. The Stanford spinout reports an annualized revenue run rate of $375M, roughly an eighteenfold increase over twelve months, driven by its shift from labeling software to delivering finished datasets and reinforcement-learning environments. The round is a clean read on where value is migrating: as model prices fall, differentiation moves upstream to data and evaluation. The open risk investors are underwriting is customer concentration among a handful of frontier labs that could build the capability in-house.
