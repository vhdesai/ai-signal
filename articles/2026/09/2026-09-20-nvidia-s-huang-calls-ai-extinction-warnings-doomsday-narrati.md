# Nvidia’s Huang Calls AI Extinction Warnings “Doomsday Narratives”

Jensen Huang told CBS News there is “0% chance” AI ends the world by 2030, calling recent researcher warnings “not grounded in science” and “irresponsible.” He dismissed the need for new AI-specific regulation, arguing existing product-liability and unauthorized-access law suffices. Asked why the public should trust a CEO whose company is worth roughly $5.3 trillion on AI demand, Huang said Nvidia’s success is “directly connected to the safe deployment of products and services.” The remarks put the largest supplier in the stack publicly at odds with Amodei, Altman and the state and federal proposals now circulating — a divergence that will shape whether assurance obligations land on model developers, deployers or hardware vendors.
