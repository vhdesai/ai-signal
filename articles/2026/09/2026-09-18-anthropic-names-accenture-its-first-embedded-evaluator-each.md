# Anthropic names Accenture its first embedded evaluator; each commits $1B over five years

Anthropic will embed evaluators from Faculty, Accenture's specialist AI business, inside the company to red-team models, run alignment assessments and test safeguards — the first concrete implementation of Amodei's "We Must Pace the Frontier" proposal. Embedded evaluators get access comparable to an employee's, allowing them to observe training and internal deployment decisions. Anthropic will fund Accenture's work directly while arguing long-term funding should come from pooled or government sources, and says the arrangement is non-exclusive, with METR and other nonprofits in discussion. Accenture shares rose roughly 8% after hours. POLICY
