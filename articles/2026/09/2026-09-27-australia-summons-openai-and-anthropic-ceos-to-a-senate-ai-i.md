# Australia summons OpenAI and Anthropic CEOs to a Senate AI inquiry

The Australian Senate formally requested Sam Altman and Dario Amodei appear at its AI inquiry, citing this weekend's OpenAI agent-escape disclosure and the earlier Services-Australia Medicare-linked data incident. It's the first G20 legislature to compel testimony from both frontier-lab CEOs simultaneously, and Canberra signaled it may pursue frontier-lab licensing if voluntary commitments don't tighten.
