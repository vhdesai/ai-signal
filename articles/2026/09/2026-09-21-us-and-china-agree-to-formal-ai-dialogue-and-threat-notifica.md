# US and China agree to formal AI dialogue and threat-notification hotline ahead of Trump–Xi summit

After Sunday's eight-hour bilateral at JPMorgan's New York headquarters, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer, and Chinese Vice-Premier He Lifeng announced an official US–China AI dialogue, including a proposed AI threat-notification system — the first bilateral AI channel with concrete crisis-prevention structure. SCMP's analyst framing is that it is meaningful but limited: deep divisions on chip access, distillation allegations, and market dominance are unresolved.
