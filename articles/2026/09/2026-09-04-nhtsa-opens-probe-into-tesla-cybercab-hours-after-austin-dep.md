# NHTSA Opens Probe Into Tesla Cybercab Hours After Austin Deployment

NHTSA opened an investigation into nearly 1,000 Tesla Cybercabs, examining whether vehicles without steering wheels, pedals, or conventional mirrors comply with federal motor vehicle safety standards. Tesla self-certified compliance, the standard process, but existing standards were written around human-operated vehicles. The outcome will set precedent for every purpose-built driverless platform, not just Tesla. It is another example of AI-enabled physical products colliding with regulatory regimes built for human control.
