# US–China AI rivalry moves to standards-setting, with Nvidia in the middle

AI featured prominently at the Trump–Xi summit and the UN General Assembly, with Chinese officials promoting lower-cost open-weight models as an alternative to US systems and UNGA speakers calling for global standards on AI development and governance of high-compute infrastructure. The framing cuts both ways for Nvidia: rules favoring locally controlled or open ecosystems would pressure the assumption that its GPU-plus-CUDA stack remains the default for frontier systems, while its role in cross-industry alliances supports the standards-shaping case. Aggregated reporting also points to a proposed US–China notification channel for national-security AI incidents; no binding agreement has been confirmed. 11 items.
