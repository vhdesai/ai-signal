# WSJ / CBS: Jensen Huang publicly splits from the slowdown camp, emerges as Trump's key AI-policy ally

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang rejected AI extinction warnings as "doomsday narratives" and told CBS News AI should be developed "as fast as we can," arguing for engineering solutions rather than new laws or industry-wide speed bumps. WSJ frames it as "Dario says AI should slow down, Jensen wants to go full steam ahead," and TradingKey/CNBC characterize Huang as emerging as the Trump administration's key AI-policy ally as Nvidia opposes any coordinated R&D slowdown. The public split with Amodei/Altman/Hassabis fractures the "coordinated pacing" consensus and has real implications for the pending pacing-collusion lawsuit filed against Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, and Google.
