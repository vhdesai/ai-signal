# Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic advance "Standards Authority for Frontier AI" — no government oversight

The Information reports Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are pushing to launch the "Standards Authority for Frontier AI" (SAFA) by late 2026 or early 2027, deliberately without government oversight. The self-regulatory body will define safety commitments and support third-party model testing, filling what the labs frame as a government regulatory vacuum. The working group has approached Sriram Krishnan — a former VC and top Trump-admin AI policy adviser — to be CEO. The move extends yesterday's DeepMind Institute launch and Hassabis's US-led standards-body proposal, and lands as Sam Altman and Dario Amodei brief the UN Security Council together in person on Wednesday.
