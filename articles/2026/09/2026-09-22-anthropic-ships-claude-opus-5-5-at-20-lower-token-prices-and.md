# Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5.5 at 20% lower token prices and 40% lower run cost

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in a new 5.5 family, priced at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output — down from $5/$25 — with cache reads cut 60% to $0.20 per million. The company says it performs at Claude Fable 5.1 level on most work while costing about 40% less to run on typical workloads, and generates output more than 30% faster. Anthropic reports 66.4% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 1,846 Elo on GDPval-AA v2.1, and says the model is materially less likely to take hard-to-reverse actions or cross containment boundaries. It was externally evaluated by METR and Frontier Design before release; Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 follow in the coming weeks.
