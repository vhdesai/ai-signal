# Nscale reportedly seeks $3.5B ahead of a potential IPO

Nscale is reportedly discussing $1.5B in convertible notes and another $2B in financing from NVIDIA, according to TechCrunch citing Bloomberg. The AI infrastructure provider has discussed a possible listing as early as this month, but the financing remains under discussion.
