# Chinese State-TV-Affiliated Account Flags Anthropic Data and Privacy Risks

A social-media account affiliated with China Central Television said Anthropic's privacy-policy revisions increase risks to user data by permitting sharing with US intelligence agencies when the company deems it necessary, without legal process. The post also noted that Anthropic changed its model-training terms in September 2025 to use user data by default. The timing — days into the IPO run-up — makes this a notable state-adjacent pressure signal on a US lab.
