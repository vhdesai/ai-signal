# OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra, Positioned as a Computer-Use Flagship

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra, framing it as a model that operates software the way a person does — working across browsers, spreadsheets, websites and desktop applications to produce finished documents and presentations. Reported results include 72.6% on OSWorld V2-Offline, and enterprise access is being staged through a gated program rather than a broad launch. The controversy is as notable as the capability: a model that reliably drives arbitrary software shifts the enterprise question from prompt quality to permissioning, auditability and blast-radius control. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/open-ai-astra-gpt-6-cyber.html
