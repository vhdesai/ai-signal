# Accel reportedly in talks to lead $1B round for Thinking Machines at $40B valuation

Accel is reported to be in discussions to lead a roughly $1 billion round for Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab at a pre-money valuation near $40 billion. That would mark a sharp increase from the $12 billion valuation set by the company's $2 billion seed round, but sits below the $50 billion the company reportedly sought late last year. With a reported revenue run rate above $100 million, the implied multiple remains extreme even by current frontier-lab standards. https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/accel-reportedly-in-talks-to-lead-1b-round-for-thinking-machines-at-40b-valuation/
