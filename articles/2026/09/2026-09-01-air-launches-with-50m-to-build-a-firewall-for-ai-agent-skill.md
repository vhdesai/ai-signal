# AIR launches with $50M to build a firewall for AI agent skills and add-ons

AIR emerged from stealth with $50 million in seed financing for a platform that discovers agents already running inside a company, continuously vets the skills, plug-ins, and MCP servers those agents invoke, and blocks unwanted behavior inline. The founding team comes out of Israeli intelligence. The category thesis is that an agent software supply chain is forming faster than enterprise controls for it — a governance gap most large organizations currently cannot inventory, let alone enforce against. https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/air-raises-50m-to-help-companies-vet-the-skills-and-add-ons-ai-agents-use/ FUNDING
