# OpenAI pauses new $200/month Pro subscriptions, citing "unprecedented" Astra demand

OpenAI paused new subscriptions to its $200/month Pro plan for its Astra flagship model. Thibault Sottiaux, who leads OpenAI's Codex coding agent, cited system strain and a desire to preserve service for existing users, saying Astra has met "unprecedented demand" and OpenAI is "pulling all the levers possible to sustain the demand." The pause follows April's Anthropic API tightening, June's 20% Amazon AI-workload price hike, and Musk's warnings of an impending compute shortfall — a sign the AI-serving supply-demand mismatch is now visible at the top of the subscription funnel.
