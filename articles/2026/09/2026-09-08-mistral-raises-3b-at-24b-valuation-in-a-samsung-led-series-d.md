# Mistral raises €3B at ~$24B valuation in a Samsung-led Series D

Mistral closed €3 billion (~$3.5 billion) led by Samsung Electronics, with the EU-backed Scaleup Europe Fund (managed by EQT) and PSG Equity as co-leads, roughly doubling its ~€11.7 billion valuation from a year ago. CEO Arthur Mensch told CNBC the capital goes primarily into owned compute — he expects Mistral-owned capacity to roughly double over five years — and into training "bigger and faster models." Mistral reports 125+ enterprise customers across 20 countries and expects to exceed $1B in ARR this year. The round is the clearest signal yet that European "sovereign AI" is being funded as an infrastructure play, not just a model play.
