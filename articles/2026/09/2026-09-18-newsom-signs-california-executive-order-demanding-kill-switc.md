# Newsom signs California executive order demanding "kill switch" and independent auditors for AI models

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed an executive order directing state agencies to require an independent-auditor arrangement inside frontier AI labs and a technical "kill switch" for AI models operating in California, with an expert panel due to deliver detailed recommendations in two months. Newsom explicitly cited the absence of federal incident-reporting requirements. For any company with California AI operations, this is the first US state-level mandate concretely aimed at implementing what Amodei called "embedded auditors" — one week after the Anthropic essay proposed the idea.
