# Purdue Libraries and School of Information Studies Advance AI Across Research and Knowledge Stewardship

Purdue detailed a portfolio of IMLS-funded projects totaling more than $818,000, with partners including Ohio State, the Illinois Data Bank, and AWS/NASA Open Data. The flagship, AIMI, is a multi-agent system that identifies metadata gaps, retrieves ontologies, and documents provenance across major data repositories; other projects restore structure to digitized archives and train librarians in campus AI literacy. The signal for enterprises: making data “agent-ready” is now a funded research discipline, not just an internal IT chore.
