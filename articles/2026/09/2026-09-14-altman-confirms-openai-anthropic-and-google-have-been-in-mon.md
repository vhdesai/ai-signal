# Altman confirms OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google have been in months-long informal joint self-regulation talks

Sam Altman confirmed that OpenAI now runs formal safety checks before major training runs and — per The Information — has been in months-long informal talks with Anthropic and Google about joint self-regulation on frontier training. This is the most concrete admission yet that the three US frontier labs have been coordinating on pacing off the record while publicly positioning it as newly proposed. For regulators, it is also concrete grounds for the antitrust question OpenAI raised in Congress last week; for enterprise buyers, it signals that release cadence for the largest models may already be being informally aligned.
