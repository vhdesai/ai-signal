# Google Brings Lyria 3.5 Music Generation to Gemini App and API

Google made Lyria 3.5 available in the Gemini app and Gemini API, extending it beyond the Flow Music tool where it debuted. The move puts generative audio on the same distribution rails as Google’s text and image models. Media and marketing organizations should expect provenance and licensing questions to follow generated audio into production workflows. The timing is notable as music publishers are escalating copyright litigation against frontier labs.
