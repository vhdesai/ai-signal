# Anthropic names Accenture as its first embedded AI safety evaluator

Anthropic announced that Accenture's Faculty business will work inside the company on model evaluation, red-teaming, alignment assessments, and safeguard testing. Anthropic and Accenture each expect to invest at least $1 billion over five years in the work, while Anthropic said it will also pursue other evaluators and that embedded evaluation does not reduce its accountability. The announcement operationalizes a major AI-governance concept: independent evaluators with employee-like access inside frontier labs. STATE REGULATION KILL SWITCH
