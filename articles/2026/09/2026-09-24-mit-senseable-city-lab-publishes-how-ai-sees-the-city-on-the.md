# MIT Senseable City Lab Publishes “How AI Sees the City” on the Promise and Peril of Visual AI

Authors Fábio Duarte, Martina Mazzarello and Carlo Ratti (MIT) with Fan Zhang (Peking University) argue that with computer vision “each image is a dataset,” citing lab work that applied machine learning to 331 New York City traffic cameras to estimate per-vehicle emissions. The book weighs that against surveillance and bias risk, noting that eight of the world’s ten most camera-heavy cities are in China and that Shanghai has over 5,000 cameras per square mile. Published this month by Routledge, it is a usable governance framing for any organisation deploying vision models across public or semi-public space.
