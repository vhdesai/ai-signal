# Nvidia invests $3.5B in MediaTek and opens NVLink Fusion to custom accelerators

Nvidia purchased $3.5B of MediaTek-issued convertible bonds and expanded a partnership spanning cloud AI factories, local AI PCs, and automotive platforms. MediaTek will adopt NVLink Fusion, allowing customers to attach custom XPUs to Nvidia rack-scale systems rather than replacing them. MediaTek shares rose roughly 10% on the announcement. The strategic read is defensive-offensive: Nvidia monetizes the shift toward custom silicon instead of ceding it, keeping its interconnect and networking layer as the point of control. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/01/nvidia-deal-mediatek-shares.html
