# China's Moonshot AI confidentially filed for a Hong Kong IPO

WSJ reported that Moonshot AI has confidentially filed for a Hong Kong listing, adding a major Chinese foundation-model company to the AI IPO pipeline. Moonshot's Kimi models have become part of the broader open-weight and coding-model discussion, including downstream use by AI developer tools. The listing process will test investor appetite for Chinese AI platforms amid export controls, intensifying model competition, and high infrastructure costs. IPOCOMPUTE
