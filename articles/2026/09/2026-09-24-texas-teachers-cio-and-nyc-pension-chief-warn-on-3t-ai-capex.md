# Texas Teachers CIO and NYC pension chief warn on $3T AI capex boom

Jase Auby, CIO of the $225B Teacher Retirement System of Texas, warned at an investment committee meeting that the AI capex cycle — running at roughly 3.5% of US GDP — dwarfs the five prior US infrastructure booms (canals, railroads, electrification, highways, telecom/fiber), all of which had years of overbuild and bankruptcies before demand caught up. NYC Retirement Systems CIO Monte Tarbox separately told Bloomberg the $327B plan has turned away managers because it needs to diversify away from AI. Yahoo Finance covered Michael Burry warning that ~$3 trillion in off-balance-sheet AI commitments could "blow a hole" in Big Tech revenue if returns disappoint, matching Oracle's -6% move dragging Snowflake/CoreWeave lower.
