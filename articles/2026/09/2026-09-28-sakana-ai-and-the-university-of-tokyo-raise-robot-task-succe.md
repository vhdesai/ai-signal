# Sakana AI and the University of Tokyo raise robot task success from 25% to 73% with test-time search

SAIL (Scaling In-Context Imitation Learning) treats robot control as Monte Carlo tree search over whole trajectories: a frozen vision-language model generates candidate motions from retrieved demonstrations, a simulator executes them, and an evaluator VLM returns step-level feedback for refinement. Across six simulated manipulation tasks, raising the search budget from one node to 45 lifted average success from 25% to 73%, peaking at 95% on one task, using Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 with no weight updates. Physical validation was narrow — five of six trials on a single block-placement task — and execution is open-loop, so the deployment question remains open. Accepted to IROS 2026.
