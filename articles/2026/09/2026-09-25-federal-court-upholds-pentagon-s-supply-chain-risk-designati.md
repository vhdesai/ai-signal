# Federal court upholds Pentagon's supply-chain-risk designation on Anthropic

A federal appeals court upheld the DoD's designation of Anthropic as a security supply-chain risk, cutting the company out of military contracts. Defense Secretary Hegseth argued Anthropic's safety restrictions could jeopardize military operations; Anthropic says the label has already cost it billions. The ruling institutionalizes what had been an executive-branch preference and sharpens the contrast with OpenAI's expanding Daybreak-program relationships with Ukraine and other allied governments.
