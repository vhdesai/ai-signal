# How AI-native companies turn workflows into operating capability

OpenAI published examples from Basis, Clay, and Exa Labs on using AI agents in onboarding, account management, and developer integrations. The piece is less a product launch than an operating-model signal: AI-native firms are converting repeated workflows into institutional capability. For large enterprises, the implication is that durable advantage may come from workflow redesign and governance, not simply model procurement. IBMEDUCATIONAI READINESS
