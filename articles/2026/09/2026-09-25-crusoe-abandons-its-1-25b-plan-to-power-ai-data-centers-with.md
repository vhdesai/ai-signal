# Crusoe abandons its $1.25B plan to power AI data centers with Boom stationary turbines

Crusoe has walked away from a $1.25B commitment to source stationary power turbines from Boom Supersonic for AI data-center backup and primary power. Boom's CEO Blake Scholl confirmed the stationary plants are no longer in Crusoe's near-term plans. The pull-back is another datapoint — alongside Oracle's Stargate force-majeure notice this week — that novel non-grid power schemes for AI compute are running into commercial reality faster than the marketing suggested.
