# Anthropic Confirms It Operates a Physical Wet Biology Lab

Anthropic confirmed it runs a wet lab where its models can execute real biological experiments, with life-sciences head Eric Kauderer-Abrams telling Reuters that “the final test is still… in real lab work.” The company says the lab is not focused on drug discovery, to avoid competing with pharma customers; it recently announced a Novo Nordisk drug-discovery deal and acquired Coefficient Bio in April. It also launched a Life Sciences Verification Program giving vetted researchers access to its most capable models.
