# Meta Superintelligence Labs Launches Muse Voice Transcribe, a Single Real-Time Audio Model

Meta's first “real-time audio perception model” collapses streaming ASR, speaker diarization across 20+ speakers, and endpointing into one autoregressive system, replacing three stitched components. Meta reports 3.1% final-transcript word error rate at 0.16 seconds and claims first place on Artificial Analysis streaming STT and public diarization benchmarks as of September 1. It is API-only at $3.00 per 1,000 audio minutes with no open weights, and already powers dictation in Meta AI for Mac. Benchmark figures are vendor-reported and not independently reproduced.
