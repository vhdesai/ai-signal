# Google to build a new AI chip in Israel, recruiting from Hailo

Google is assembling an Israeli silicon team for a new AI chip effort, drawing talent from edge-AI chipmaker Hailo. The move extends Google's in-house accelerator strategy beyond its US TPU program into a second design center, and tightens the talent squeeze on independent Israeli chip startups. For buyers, it is another signal that hyperscalers intend to keep reducing dependence on merchant GPU supply. INDUSTRY
