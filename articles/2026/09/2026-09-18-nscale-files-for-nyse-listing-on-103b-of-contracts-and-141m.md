# Nscale Files for NYSE Listing on $103B of Contracts and $141M of First-Half Revenue

The London-based neocloud filed its Form S-1 to list on the NYSE under ticker NSCL, disclosing $140.6M in first-half 2026 revenue — up 1,252% year over year — against a $1.02B net loss. Nscale reported $103.4B in active and contracted value supporting roughly 461,000 active or contracted GPUs, of which only 25,000 were live as of August 31, and $56.4B in remaining performance obligations. Anthropic's agreements alone run to approximately $44.6B for 460 MW at the Monarch campus in West Virginia, with Microsoft agreements up to $43.8B; a single customer produced 52% of first-half revenue.
