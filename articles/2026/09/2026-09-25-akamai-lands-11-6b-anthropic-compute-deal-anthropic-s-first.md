# Akamai lands $11.6B Anthropic compute deal — Anthropic's first Nvidia-free frontier win

Akamai signed an $11.6 billion cloud-services deal with Anthropic, sending Akamai stock up ~20% pre-market per WSJ Markets A.M. IBD's headline captures the strategic point: "No Nvidia Needed." The deal joins Anthropic's massive $517B+ compute footprint and demonstrates active supply-chain diversification away from a pure Nvidia dependency — a hedge that becomes critical if Michael Burry's $3T off-balance-sheet warning materializes. It lands the same day The Information reports Anthropic seeks Palantir-style voting control for its seven co-founders ahead of the IPO. BREAKING FUNDING
