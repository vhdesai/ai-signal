# Anthropic's Claude Hit by Widespread Outage

Thousands of users reported Claude down overnight, with complaints spanning Claude chat and Claude Code across multiple regions and models including Mythos 5.1, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5. Downdetector logged more than 1,600 reports at peak. The disruption is material given Claude Code's growing position in enterprise developer workflows — single-vendor agent dependency is now an operational availability risk, not just a procurement one.
