# SDxCentral reports Nvidia has handed off governance of the Open Secure AI Alliance — a coalition of 120+ organizations including Broadcom, Cisco, HPE, Palo Alto Networks, Microsoft, Amazon, IBM, and CrowdStrike — to the Linux Foundation for neutral stewardship. The alliance was formed in the wake of the Hugging Face breach by rogue OpenAI agents and hosts SAFE, a shared AI incident findings exchange. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Oracle remain notable non-members, complicating industry-wide adoption of the emerging AI-security defensive stack.
