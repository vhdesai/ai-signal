# Vertiv to acquire UtilityInnovation Group to shorten time-to-power for AI data centers

Vertiv announced a definitive agreement to acquire UtilityInnovation Group, explicitly framing the rationale as accelerating time-to-power for AI data centers. The deal moves Vertiv further upstream into utility-interconnection and power-delivery engineering rather than in-facility thermal and power equipment alone. Read alongside the Texas interconnection freeze, it confirms that grid access — not equipment — is where the buildout is currently constrained. vertiv.com — Vertiv announces agreement to acquire UtilityInnovation Group
