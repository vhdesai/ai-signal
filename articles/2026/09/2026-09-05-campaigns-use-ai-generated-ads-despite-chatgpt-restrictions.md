# Campaigns use AI-generated ads despite ChatGPT restrictions

The Washington Post reported that political campaigns are using AI-generated advertising despite ChatGPT restrictions on campaign ad creation. The story illustrates a recurring governance gap: acceptable-use policies can be hard to enforce once content moves across tools, vendors, and distribution channels. For leaders, this points to the need for provenance, audit trails, and monitoring controls in high-risk communications workflows.
