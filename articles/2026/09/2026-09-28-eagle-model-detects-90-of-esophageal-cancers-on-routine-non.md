# EAGLE Model Detects 90% of Esophageal Cancers on Routine Non-Contrast Chest CT

EAGLE (Esophageal AI-Guided malignant Lesion Evaluation) was trained on 6,813 patients at two Chinese centres and validated across 12 centres in China, the Czech Republic and Australia covering 80,612 patients. In external testing across eight centres (n=11,466) it reached 90.0% sensitivity for cancer at 98.5% specificity, though sensitivity fell to 60.1% for stage 1 disease and 52.5% for high-grade precancerous lesions. Recalibration on 35,402 real-world patients cut false positives 72.7%; prospective testing in 17,446 patients showed a 42.2% positive predictive value.
