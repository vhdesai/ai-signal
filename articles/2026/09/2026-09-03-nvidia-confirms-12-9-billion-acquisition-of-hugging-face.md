# Nvidia confirms $12.9 billion acquisition of Hugging Face

Nvidia signed a definitive agreement to acquire Hugging Face for $12.93 billion in cash, ending weeks of reported negotiations. The platform hosts roughly three million models and is the default distribution point for open-weight AI, giving Nvidia a position in the model supply chain above its GPU franchise. Jensen Huang framed the deal as scaling Hugging Face’s platform and infrastructure rather than restricting it.
