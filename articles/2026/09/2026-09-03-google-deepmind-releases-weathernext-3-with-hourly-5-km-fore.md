# Google DeepMind releases WeatherNext 3 with hourly, 5-km forecasts

Google DeepMind and Google Research introduced WeatherNext 3, a global AI weather model producing hourly forecasts at resolutions down to five kilometers, and are integrating it into Google consumer surfaces. The release continues the pattern of frontier-lab science models moving directly into production products rather than remaining research artifacts.
