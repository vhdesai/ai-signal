# Nvidia's Jensen Huang Rejects the Slowdown: “As Fast As We Can”

In an interview released ahead of its September 20 broadcast, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told CBS News that AI development should proceed “as fast as we can irrespective of anybody else,” while insisting the company would never ship unsafe or unfinished products. He separately put the odds of an AI-driven civilizational catastrophe by 2030 at “zero percent,” questioning the motives behind doomsday framing. The position extends remarks he made at Dreamforce on September 15, where he argued safety is an engineering problem rather than a legal one and that no new AI-specific regulation is needed. As the supplier of the accelerators underpinning every frontier training run, Nvidia's commercial interest in the answer is not incidental. POLICY
