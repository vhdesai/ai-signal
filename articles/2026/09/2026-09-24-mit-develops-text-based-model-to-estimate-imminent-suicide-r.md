# MIT develops text-based model to estimate imminent suicide risk

MIT researchers reported a language-processing tool that estimates suicide risk from crisis-text conversations using a curated lexicon tied to 49 risk factors. The team analyzed roughly 16,000 de-identified Crisis Text Line conversations and found that mentions of lethal means, substance use, active suicidal ideation, and self-injury were stronger predictors of imminent risk than general depressed mood. With more validation, the work could inform clinical and crisis-support triage, but it will require careful governance because errors carry high human stakes. ON-DEVICE AIAPPLE
