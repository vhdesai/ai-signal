# NTNU Review of 173 Studies: Generative AI Cuts Both Ways on Critical Thinking

NTNU PhD fellow Hilde Storrøsæter and Czech collaborators systematically reviewed 173 studies published between 2022 and spring 2025 on generative AI in education, in the Journal of Information Technology Education: Research (DOI 10.28945/5834). They found a dual effect: AI strengthens critical thinking when used to question and compare, and weakens it when used as a shortcut to ready answers. The review also flags three evidence gaps — 80% of studies use university students rather than children, most rely on self-report, and geographic coverage skews toward Asia. Context: Google search use among Norwegian 9–18-year-olds fell from 72% in 2024 to 47% in 2026.
