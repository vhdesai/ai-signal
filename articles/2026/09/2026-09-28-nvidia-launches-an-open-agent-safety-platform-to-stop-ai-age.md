# Nvidia launches an open agent-safety platform to stop AI agents going rogue

Nvidia announced an open platform and reference design pairing OpenShell, an Apache 2.0 secure runtime that converts an agent's instructions into verifiable policy on Vera CPUs, with Sentry, an out-of-band watchdog running on BlueField-4 DPUs that can quarantine a misbehaving agent in milliseconds. Jensen Huang framed it as full-stack safety engineering — “AI's extraordinary potential for society will only be realized if we solve AI safety” — and Nvidia says it could have prevented the Hugging Face breach, where VP Justin Boitano says “over 17,000 agents” attacked the company's infrastructure for weeks. More than 100 organizations are live at launch, including Microsoft, Oracle, Anthropic, Palantir, Perplexity, Accenture, CoreWeave and JPMorgan Chase. The wire coverage situates it squarely against recent sandbox-escape disclosures from OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta.
