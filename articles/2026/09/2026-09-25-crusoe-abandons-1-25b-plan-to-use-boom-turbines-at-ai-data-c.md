# Crusoe abandons $1.25B plan to use Boom turbines at AI data centers

Crusoe ended plans to use Boom Supersonic’s stationary gas turbines at AI data centers, including a previously announced $1.25 billion order for 29 units. TechCrunch reported that Crusoe said turbines remain one option in a broader power portfolio, but the partnership is not the right fit today. The change illustrates how quickly AI data-center power strategies are evolving as developers balance grid power, turbines, batteries, renewables, cost, speed, and permitting risk. INFERENCE FUNDING
