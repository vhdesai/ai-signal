# iPronics Raises $125M, With Nvidia Participating, for Programmable Optical Networking

Valencia-based iPronics closed a $125 million Series B co-led by Maverick Silicon and Light Street Capital, with participation from Nvidia, to scale programmable photonic chips that switch optical circuits in sub-millisecond timeframes. As clusters scale toward hundreds of thousands of accelerators, interconnect — not raw GPU throughput — is the binding constraint.
