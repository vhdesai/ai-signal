# Amazon gives sellers 12 months of Quick Plus for free as part of AI freebies race

At Amazon Accelerate, Amazon gave the merchants that sell on its platform 12 free months of Quick Plus — the AWS AI workplace assistant that normally costs $20/month. Amazon also announced integrations that let sellers use Quick and Claude with Seller Assistant, plus new autonomous-action capabilities. Both products use Anthropic Claude models under the hood, which will be more expensive for Amazon to run when a new pricing arrangement takes effect next year. The offer is the latest data point in the AI-freebie race — following yesterday's Software Firms Discount AI reporting and matching Bank of America's plan to double its AI budget next year (per CIO Dive).
