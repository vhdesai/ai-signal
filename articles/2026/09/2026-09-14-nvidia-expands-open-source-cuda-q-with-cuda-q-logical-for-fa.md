# NVIDIA expands open-source CUDA-Q with CUDA-Q Logical for fault-tolerant quantum development

NVIDIA announced CUDA-Q Logical, an orchestration layer that lets researchers design, swap and verify the components of fault-tolerant quantum applications — algorithm, error-correction code and hardware architecture — without rebuilding the toolchain each time. Fermilab reported compressing fault-tolerant architecture development from five months to three weeks, a 7x speedup, and Sandia’s QUOPS readiness benchmark is now available in CUDA-Q. Early adopters include Fermilab, Infleqtion, IQM, Quantum Motion and Sandia.
