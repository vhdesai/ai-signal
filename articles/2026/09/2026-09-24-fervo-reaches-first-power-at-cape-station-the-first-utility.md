# Fervo reaches First Power at Cape Station, the first utility-scale enhanced geothermal plant on the grid

Fervo Energy (Nasdaq: FRVO) announced that its Cape Station development in Beaver County, Utah has synchronized to the grid and begun exporting electricity — the first time a utility-scale enhanced geothermal system has reached this milestone anywhere. Phase I is roughly 100 MW across three 33 MW GeoBlocks, with the first expected to reach contractual commercial operation by October 1, 2026 and the remaining two by January 1, 2027; a further 400 MW is under construction for 2028, against roughly 900 MW of contracted offtake. Enhanced geothermal is one of the few sources that can deliver 24/7 carbon-free baseload to data center load, which is why hyperscaler offtake interest has followed.
