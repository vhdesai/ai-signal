# xAI ships Grok 4.7 at unchanged $2/$6 pricing

Grok 4.7 launched on a larger base model with a longer reinforcement-learning run weighted toward multi-hour tasks, at the same $2 per million input and $6 per million output tokens as Grok 4.6. Vendor-reported scores improve on every published row versus 4.6 — CursorBench 4.0 at 46.3%, DeepSWE v1.1 at 71.0%, Terminal-Bench 4.0 nearly doubling to 38.0%, and EEBench at 64.0% — though Fable 5.1 Max still leads four of seven benchmarks. The model is live in the xAI API, Cursor, Grok Build, OpenRouter, Vercel and Cloudflare. Launch-day analysis at VentureBeat flagged high reasoning-token consumption, so cost per completed task, not per token, is the number procurement teams should measure. MODEL OPEN WEIGHTS
