# Google tests in-chat purchasing from Walmart-owned Flipkart through Gemini and AI Mode in India

Google has begun testing a “Buy” button on select Flipkart listings inside Gemini and Google’s AI Mode in India, routing users into a Flipkart-branded checkout without leaving the AI interface. The test covers a limited set of users and products — smartphones, electronics and mobile accessories — with a broader rollout planned for later in October ahead of India’s festive season. The experiment appears distinct from the Google-hosted checkout built on its Universal Commerce Protocol. Amazon listings appeared alongside Flipkart’s in the test but without direct purchase, a useful read on where agentic commerce partnerships are forming.
