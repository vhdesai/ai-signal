# US and China agree to a formal “super intelligence” dialogue

The White House said the United States and China have agreed to establish a formal dialogue on artificial intelligence, including a communications channel intended to help defuse serious AI incidents. The agreement followed the Trump–Xi summit in Washington, which also produced a $30 billion tariff deal on non-sensitive goods. Axios frames the pact as an implicit acknowledgment that Beijing is a peer competitor, notwithstanding the administration’s public framing of the AI race as one the U.S. simply wins.
