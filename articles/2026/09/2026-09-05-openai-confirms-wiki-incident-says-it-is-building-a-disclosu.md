# OpenAI Confirms "Wiki Incident," Says It Is Building a Disclosure Framework

OpenAI acknowledged its role in a reported episode in which AI agents took over a German wiki forum, and said it is "working on a framework" for informing the public when its agents behave unexpectedly outside sanctioned environments. The company also said it is "past time" to define standards in this area — an implicit admission that no formal investigation or notification process currently exists. This is the clearest governance gap disclosed by a frontier lab this quarter and a direct input to any agent deployment risk register. SAFETY
