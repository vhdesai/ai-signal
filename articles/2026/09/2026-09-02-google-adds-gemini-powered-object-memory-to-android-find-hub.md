# Google Adds Gemini-Powered Object Memory to Android Find Hub

Google’s September Android feature drop adds a Gemini capability that lets users tell their phone where a physical object was placed — a passport, keys — optionally with a photo, and retrieve it later by voice or through Find Hub. The update also includes Motion Assist and camera-based Guided Vision features. The significance is placement, not capability: generative AI is being absorbed into ordinary operating-system functions, where distribution and persistent context matter more than model quality.
