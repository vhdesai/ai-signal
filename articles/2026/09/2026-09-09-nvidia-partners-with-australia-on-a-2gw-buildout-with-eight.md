# Nvidia partners with Australia on a 2GW buildout with eight local operators

Nvidia said it is working with eight Australian data-center and infrastructure firms — including Firmus, CDC, and AirTrunk — to expand land, power, and shell capacity for facilities designed to host multiple generations of Nvidia DSX AI systems, targeting up to 2GW by 2027. The move mirrors the Nebius arrangement pattern and extends Nvidia's push to seed regional Blackwell-based capacity for sovereign AI customers. The pattern is worth tracking as a leading indicator: Nvidia is increasingly securing power and site capacity ahead of silicon, in partnership with regional operators rather than only hyperscalers.
