# OpenAI launches MentalHealthBench for AI mental-health safety testing

EdTech Innovation Hub reports OpenAI launched MentalHealthBench, a benchmark specifically designed to evaluate model behavior on mental-health-adjacent prompts. It follows a string of lawsuits over ChatGPT's alleged role in suicide and self-harm cases earlier this month plus the Tumbler Ridge shooting-related complaints. Combined with Microsoft's Copilot super-app launch today and Wednesday's GPT-6 Sol/Luna alignment improvements, the industry is racing to close visible harm-reduction gaps ahead of expected regulatory action.
