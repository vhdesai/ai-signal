# Can Muse overcome Meta's trust issues?

TechCrunch's Equity team assessed Meta's consumer AI push around Muse, the personal agent unveiled at Connect and now heading to smart glasses and a Tamagotchi-style wearable. The contrast drawn is strategic: Meta is betting consumer while OpenAI and Anthropic drive enterprise revenue ahead of their respective IPOs. Sean O'Kane, who tested the product, called it more “a party-trick type thing” than a daily driver, and the hosts returned repeatedly to the distribution problem Meta cannot engineer around — “Meta's business is to sell you ads.”
