# Malaysia weighs adopting Huawei AI chips despite explicit US warnings

The Malaysian government is seriously considering Huawei-made AI accelerators to advance its national AI agenda, notwithstanding direct warnings from the United States. The deliberation reflects Huawei’s growing role as an alternative supplier for countries navigating US export controls. For multinationals operating in Southeast Asia, divergent national chip-sourcing decisions increasingly imply divergent compliance and architecture paths.
