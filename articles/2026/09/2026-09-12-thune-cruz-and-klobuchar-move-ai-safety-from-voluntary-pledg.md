# Thune, Cruz, and Klobuchar move AI safety from voluntary pledge to legal duty

Tech Times reported that Senate leaders are drafting bipartisan legislation that would impose a legal duty of care on developers of the most powerful AI models and potentially allow the U.S. government to block unsafe releases. The proposal would move beyond audits and voluntary commitments toward liability for foreseeable catastrophic harms. If advanced, it would materially change frontier model development by pushing safety obligations earlier into design, evaluation, and release planning. ALIGNMENT FRONTIER RISK
