# fruit-fly connectome bolted onto a 1.2B LLM — and the authors' own controls show the biology adds nothing

The Fly Language Model couples all 166,700 retained nodes and 25.6 million edges of the MaleCNS fruit fly connectome to a frozen LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct backbone, training only a 278,528-parameter readout (0.024% of backbone parameters). The fly readout improved negative log-likelihood by 0.0222 nats per token over the backbone — but a parameter-matched control feeding the same 128-channel projection into an identical readout with no graph beat it in all three seeds. Zeroing the edges reproduces baseline exactly, confirming the graph participates without winning. A useful counter-example when evaluating novel-architecture claims: the headline mechanism was real, measurable, and not responsible for the gain.
