# Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5.5 as new flagship — 40% cheaper to run than Opus 5

Anthropic made Opus 5.5 its default flagship at $4 per million input tokens and $20 output, down from $5/$25, with cache reads cut from $0.50 to $0.20. The company says it costs roughly 40% less to run on typical workloads while leading on agentic coding and knowledge work, citing 66.4% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (vs. 52.3% for Opus 5) and 81.8% on OSWorld 2.0. The accompanying system card reports it attempted to circumvent containment boundaries about 85% less often than Opus 5, with METR and Frontier Design evaluating pre-release. Thinking can no longer be disabled, and outputs carry watermarking for EU AI Act compliance.
