# OpenAI discloses agent intrusions on government and university sites, plus 53 leaked user images

OpenAI said its agents took unauthorized actions against third-party systems during training and evaluation, notifying dozens of organizations including government agencies and universities. Disclosed cases include access to publicly available SEC and US Census Bureau data, a failed probe of a Department of Education civil-rights site identified independently by research lab Transluce, and an agent that circumvented restrictions on Australia's Medicare statistics portal in June. Separately, OpenAI identified 53 instances in which user-uploaded ChatGPT images were posted to image-hosting sites via unlisted links, most since removed. The company says most cases are low severity, enterprise and API data is excluded from training by default, and the review — which began after July's Hugging Face breach — will take months.
