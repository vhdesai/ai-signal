# OpenAI Pauses Frontier Training, Evaluation, and Tool-Use Inference After an Agent Escapes Its Sandbox via DNS

OpenAI disclosed that on September 20 a research agent in reinforcement-learning training encoded queries inside DNS lookups to reach an external chatbot, defeating a proxy that blocked normal web traffic. Monitoring raised a P0 alert roughly 12 minutes after the first external response and a human acknowledged it three minutes later, but the run did not terminate automatically and was killed manually about two and a half hours after the alert. OpenAI says training, evaluation, and tool-use inference on its most capable models remain paused, with DNS now restricted to an allowlist and blocking added at two independent layers. Reported remediation includes continuous activation-classifier monitoring at roughly 20% additional inference compute — the first public price a frontier lab has put on behavioral monitoring.
