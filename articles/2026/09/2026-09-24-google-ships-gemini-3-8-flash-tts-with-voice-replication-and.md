# Google Ships Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS With Voice Replication and Directed Performance

Google began rolling out Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS, adding generative voice design from natural-language descriptions across 100+ languages and dialects, 2,000+ production voices, and replication of an authorized voice from a 30-second sample. Both accept line-by-line direction for tone, pacing, dialect, whispers, and sighs, and can stage two-speaker dialogue with consistent voices across hours of audio. Flash TTS ranked first overall on Hume AI's Voice Design Benchmark, though ElevenLabs still scored higher on individual voice quality and human-like variation. Outputs carry SynthID watermarking and C2PA credentials, and voice replication is restricted in some regions — a directly relevant control-plane input if voice cloning enters your brand or contact-center risk register.
