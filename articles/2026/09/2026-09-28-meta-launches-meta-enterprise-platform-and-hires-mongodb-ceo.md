# Meta launches Meta Enterprise Platform and hires MongoDB CEO CJ Desai to run it

Mark Zuckerberg announced Meta Enterprise Platform as “the next major pillar” of Meta’s business, packaging the Muse agent, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code for businesses and developers. Chirantan “CJ” Desai joins as Chief Enterprise Platform Officer reporting directly to Zuckerberg, leaving the MongoDB CEO role effective immediately after 11 months; Dev Ittycheria returns to MongoDB as interim CEO. The launch post names no pricing, enterprise tiers or compliance certifications. The move puts Meta into direct competition with Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic for enterprise AI spend.
