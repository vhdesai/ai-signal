# PitchBook: Should PE partners master AI? Carlyle at 90% adoption, usage +200% YoY

PitchBook's Weekend Pitch profiles Strattam Capital's Bob Morse writing his first code since college — using Claude Code to build a portfolio-performance dashboard that saved his team quarterly hours. Carlyle's CIO Lúcia Soares says 90% of Carlyle employees now use AI, with firm-wide usage up more than 200% over the past 12 months. Alexandre Armbruster (Caisse des Dépôts) says the "specialist game" of LP due diligence has become "even more of an expert game" as AI resilience becomes mandatory KPI.
