# Google unveils $899 "Googlebook" — an AI-native laptop built around Gemini

Google introduced the Googlebook, a $899 AI-native laptop that ties Gemini directly to the cursor, dictation, widgets, and desktop workflow. It's a Chromebook successor positioned explicitly as "buy a laptop for the model" — a first meaningful attempt at the AI-native personal-computing device category from a major OEM/OS owner. For CIOs standing up next-generation productivity fleets, the Googlebook is now the reference AI-first Windows-alternative to evaluate against Copilot+ PCs. Infrastructure ANALYSIS HARDWARE
