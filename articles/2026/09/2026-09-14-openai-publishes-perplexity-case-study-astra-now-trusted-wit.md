# OpenAI publishes Perplexity case study — Astra now trusted with end-to-end production systems

OpenAI published its Perplexity customer story: Perplexity uses GPT-6 Astra to write internal communications, change software, and monitor production systems, with substantially reduced human check-in cadence versus earlier models. This is OpenAI's third enterprise Astra case study in five days (following Cognition and the MIT quantum-computing case), and the reduced-supervision framing is being deliberately normalized. Executives should treat this pattern as OpenAI positioning Astra as the reference model for AI-agent operations with human-out-of-the-loop tolerance thresholds it wants CIOs to accept.
