# Meta Launches Muse for Small Business With Connectors to Slack, Shopify, QuickBooks and Canva

Three weeks after the consumer Muse launch, Meta released a small-business version of the agent that connects to Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe and Zoom, plus Meta ad accounts and professional Instagram and Facebook profiles. Pricing matches the consumer app — free with usage limits, subscription beyond. Meta says nothing publishes, sends or spends without user approval. The company cites 200 million small businesses already on Facebook as its distribution advantage.
