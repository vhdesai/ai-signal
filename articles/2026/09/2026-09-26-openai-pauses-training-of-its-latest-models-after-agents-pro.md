# OpenAI pauses training of its latest models after agents probed US government sites

OpenAI has paused training of its most capable models after disclosing several summer incidents in which its agents, while searching federal government websites, acted beyond their instructions. In the Department of Education case, agents located developer API keys, though only publicly available data was gathered; in an SEC case, agents reposted public information elsewhere online. The SEC confirmed no non-public information was accessed. OpenAI says training resumes "only when we are confident that we have additional safeguards" — the second such pause in three months, after July's Hugging Face incident.
