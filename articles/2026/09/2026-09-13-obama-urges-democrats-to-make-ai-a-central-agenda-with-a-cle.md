# Obama urges Democrats to make AI a “central agenda” with a clear safeguards plan

Speaking at a Democratic fundraising event interviewed by House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries, former President Obama said Democrats need “a very clear plan” on AI’s economic and safety impacts and should build “a framework for a very public conversation” upon regaining a House majority. He characterized the technology as “moving very fast in private hands” while citing accelerated drug development as the upside case. Obama has reportedly offered himself as a sounding board to AI executives and has spoken with both Amodei and Altman. The planning signal: AI governance is becoming a campaign-cycle issue rather than a committee-level one. CN POLICY GEOPOLITICS
