# Perplexity trains its computer agent on real mistakes with hint-guided self-distillation

MarkTechPost reported that Perplexity is training its computer-use agent on real mistakes using hint-guided self-distillation. The approach is aimed at improving agents on the types of errors they actually make in real workflows, rather than only synthetic benchmark failures. That is directionally important because computer-use agents will need continuous error-driven improvement before they can operate reliably across enterprise desktops and browsers.
