# AI Data-Center IPOs Freeze: SoftBank's SB Energy Delayed at $50B Target, Holtec Pauses Indefinitely

SB Energy — the SoftBank subsidiary proposing the largest data-center project in the world in Ohio — delayed its planned September IPO after bankers struggled to find buyers at the sought $50B+ valuation, per four people familiar with the marketing effort. Nuclear-energy supplier Holtec paused its own IPO indefinitely citing "impaired investor confidence in the market for new public offerings." PIMCO and others still forecast $5T+ of AI-infrastructure spending by 2030, and Nvidia disclosed an additional $1.5B stake in SB Energy at a discount. But the political overlay is tightening: Governor Newsom signed California data-center regulation legislation this weekend, and Governor Abbott extended a Texas moratorium on new data-center permits pending an electric-grid audit.
