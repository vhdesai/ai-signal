# Equinix partners with Nvidia to carve a niche in the AI data-center buildout

Equinix is positioning its colocation and interconnection footprint as the neutral middle layer of the multitrillion-dollar AI data-center boom, partnering with Nvidia rather than competing with hyperscaler-owned capacity. The strategy targets enterprises that want GPU capacity adjacent to their existing network and cloud on-ramps.
