# CAIS and Scale AI release HLE-Diamond, resetting the frontier knowledge benchmark

The Center for AI Safety and Scale AI published HLE-Diamond, a frozen 1,000-question refinement of Humanity's Last Exam split evenly between reasoning and expert-knowledge partitions, with stable question IDs so scores remain comparable quarter to quarter. Reported results put GPT-6 Astra first at 60.6% overall, Claude Opus 5.5 at 55.0% and Claude Fable 5.1 at 51.3%, with substantial headroom below the noise ceiling. The leaderboard also reports calibration error alongside accuracy — Opus 5.5 at 7.1% versus Astra at 28.1% — which is arguably the more decision-relevant number for enterprise deployment.
