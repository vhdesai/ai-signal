# Nscale’s contract book reaches $103B — roughly 85% of it Microsoft and Anthropic

Nscale, founded in May 2024, has gone from about $100M in contracted revenue to more than $103B ahead of a NYSE listing under the ticker NSCL at a target valuation near $30B. Microsoft accounts for roughly $43.8B in total agreements and Anthropic for $44.6–45B covering 460MW at the Monarch campus in West Virginia, expected online by late 2027. First-half 2026 revenue was $140.6M, and the company’s largest single client represented 52% of it. Concentration and unfinanced build-out — not demand — are the risks a buyer is underwriting here.
