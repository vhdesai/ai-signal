# Nvidia-backed Nscale files for U.S. IPO amid AI infrastructure buildout

The Information reported that Nscale filed to go public, showing a large revenue jump alongside steep losses. Reuters separately reported that the Nvidia-backed AI cloud firm disclosed a revenue surge in its U.S. IPO filing. The filing highlights both sides of the AI infrastructure cycle: strong demand for GPU capacity and cloud partnerships, but large capital requirements and profitability questions as neoclouds scale.
