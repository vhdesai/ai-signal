# Buildots raises $130M at a near-$1B valuation on data-center construction demand

Tel Aviv-based Buildots closed $130 million led by O.G. Venture Partners with Lightspeed and Intel Capital participating, bringing total raised to $297 million. Its computer-vision platform converts 360-degree site capture into a continuously updated digital model, comparing installed work against schedules and BIM to forecast delays. Demand is driven directly by AI data-center construction, where schedule slippage defers the revenue window on rapidly depreciating GPUs; customers include Intel, Digital Realty, JE Dunn and STO Building Group. The company reports roughly threefold annual revenue growth. Infrastructure N ANALYSIS
