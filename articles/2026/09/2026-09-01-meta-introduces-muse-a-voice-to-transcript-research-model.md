# Meta introduces Muse, a voice-to-transcript research model

Meta's research division unveiled Muse, a model designed to transcribe and structure spoken audio with an emphasis on speaker attribution and downstream usability. The release is aimed more at research and internal tooling than at a consumer product, but it signals continued investment in audio-first foundation models alongside Meta's text and image efforts.
