# SoftBank plans $11B+ in risky bonds to fund another OpenAI stake payment

SoftBank plans to borrow more than $11B via a risky bond issuance to fund its next tranche of OpenAI investment, according to The Decoder. It is now one of the largest single leverage events tied specifically to a private AI position and highlights how strained the financing side of the biggest AI bets has become — even ahead of OpenAI's already-delayed 2027 IPO. For LP-and-credit-sensitive investors, this is a concrete data point on the debt profile that will underwrite the OpenAI equity value. PRODUCT
