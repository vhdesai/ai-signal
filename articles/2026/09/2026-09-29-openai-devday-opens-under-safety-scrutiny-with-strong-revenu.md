# OpenAI DevDay Opens Under Safety Scrutiny — With Strong Revenue Signals

OpenAI's annual developer conference began at Fort Mason in San Francisco, with Sam Altman's keynote at 10:00 a.m. Pacific and no product announcements disclosed in advance. Reporting on the day indicates OpenAI's enterprise business more than doubled during its third quarter, that it generated more consumer run-rate revenue in the last 90 days than in all of last year, and that quarter-to-date revenue growth of about 70% would imply a quarterly run rate near $68 billion, up from roughly $40 billion in August. The company confidentially filed its IPO prospectus with the SEC in June at a valuation near $1 trillion. Altman is facing safety questions on stage one day after the Astra cancellation.
