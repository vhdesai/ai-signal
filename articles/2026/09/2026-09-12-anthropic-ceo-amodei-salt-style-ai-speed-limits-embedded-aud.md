# Anthropic CEO Amodei: SALT-style AI speed limits, embedded auditors, and 6–12 month RSI warning

Amodei's essay warns that recursive self-improvement could destabilize significant portions of the internet within 6–12 months and proposes: (1) embedded independent auditors at frontier labs with structural access; (2) shared safety-testing and eval standards; and (3) international treaties modeled on SALT. Coming just before the Anthropic IPO, the timing is politically loaded, but the specifics — particularly the embedded-auditor idea — are the most concrete institutional proposal from a frontier lab CEO to date. Expect this to become the reference proposal in the coming Congressional slowdown conversation. POLICY
