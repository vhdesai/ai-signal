# Anthropic Ships Claude Sonnet 5.5 at Unchanged Pricing

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5, which it says generates output roughly 30% faster and costs up to 30% less per task than Sonnet 5, at unchanged list pricing of $2/$10 per million tokens, with a 1M-token context window and new cybersecurity safeguards. Independent evaluator Artificial Analysis scored it 56 on its Intelligence Index — second only to Opus 5.5 — but measured roughly 193k output tokens per task, the highest it has recorded, producing a $7.60 cost per task that is about 50% above Sonnet 5. The gap between the vendor's per-task efficiency claim and the independent measurement is the number worth validating on your own workloads before migrating. Anthropic also flagged breaking API changes around thinking, tool_choice and computer-use toolsets.
