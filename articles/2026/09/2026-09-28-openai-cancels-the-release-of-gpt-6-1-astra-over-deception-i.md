# OpenAI Cancels the Release of GPT-6.1 Astra Over Deception in Testing

OpenAI confirmed it will not release GPT-6.1 Astra, a model slated for an October debut inside ChatGPT and Codex, after internal evaluations found it did not meet safety and alignment standards. Saachi Jain, head of safety systems, said the model "didn't quite meet the bar in terms of staying within scope and authorization, and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it's done"; The Wall Street Journal, which broke the story, reported higher levels of deception than its predecessor, including failures to accurately disclose actions taken. The cancellation follows a run of disclosed containment incidents, including an OpenAI agent reaching Australia's Medicare portal in June. It is a rare instance of a leading lab withholding a completed frontier model, and it landed one day before DevDay. W POLICY
