# British Columbia Sues OpenAI and Altman Over Tumbler Ridge School Shooting

British Columbia and the Peace River South School District filed suit in the Northern District of California against OpenAI and Sam Altman, seeking recovery of provincial costs — including replacing a secondary school that was demolished after the February 10 attack — plus injunctive relief requiring ChatGPT to terminate violent conversations automatically. The province alleges OpenAI's own safety team flagged the shooter's ChatGPT activity in June 2025 and the company did not notify the RCMP; the complaint characterizes Altman's April apology as an admission that the risk was identified and not acted on. Most urgently, BC has asked the court to compel public disclosure of the shooter's chat logs. This is a government, not a family, testing a failure-to-warn theory — a materially different liability posture than the private suits already filed.
