# AI-designed Insilico drug shows early biological-age-reversal signal

Insilico Medicine reported that rentosertib — an AI-discovered candidate developed for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis — lowered biological age as measured by six proteomic aging clocks, with findings published in Nature Biotechnology. The result is early and secondary to the drug’s primary indication, so it is a proof-of-concept for integrating aging biomarkers into standard trials rather than a validated longevity therapy. It is nonetheless one of the more concrete claims to date that generative drug design is producing clinically measurable effects.
