# KPMG forward-deployed engineer says AI is moving the role beyond writing code

Business Insider profiled a KPMG forward-deployed engineer whose work has shifted beyond writing code as AI takes on more implementation tasks. The item is directionally important because it shows enterprise AI adoption reshaping technical roles toward workflow design, customer translation, integration, and governance. For senior leaders, the implication is that reskilling should target problem framing and deployment accountability, not just prompt literacy or coding acceleration.
