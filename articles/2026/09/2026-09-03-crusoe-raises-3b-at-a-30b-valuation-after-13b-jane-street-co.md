# Crusoe Raises $3B at a $30B Valuation After $13B Jane Street Contract

Data-center developer Crusoe raised $3B at a $30B valuation, co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners with participation from Mubadala Capital. The round follows a reported five-year, $13B cloud contract supplying GPUs and AI infrastructure to quantitative trading firm Jane Street, and comes ten months after a $1.38B round at a $10B valuation — a 3x markup in under a year. Crusoe counts Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI and Oracle among customers and has met with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley regarding a potential near-term IPO. https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/crusoe-reportedly-raises-3b-at-a-30b-valuation/ PARTNERSHIP
