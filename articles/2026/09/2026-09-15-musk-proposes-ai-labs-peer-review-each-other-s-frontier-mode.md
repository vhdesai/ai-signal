# Musk proposes AI labs peer-review each other's frontier models

At the All-In Summit, Elon Musk proposed that competing AI companies, including his own xAI, "peer-review" each other's models before release — "instead of grading your own homework, you would at least have competitors grading your homework and raising the alarm if they see concerns." He said the proposal shouldn't necessarily replace regulation but could be implemented faster, and suggested Chinese labs could be brought in. It's the most operational alternative floated by the pro-slowdown camp and complements Anthropic/OpenAI/Google's quiet standards-body talks.
