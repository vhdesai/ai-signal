# Island raises $400M at a $6.4B valuation as enterprises budget against rogue agents

Dallas-based enterprise browser and security company Island announced a $400 million Series F led by Evolution Equity Partners, more than doubling its valuation since 2024 to $6.4 billion. CEO Mike Fey framed the demand explicitly around autonomous agents: “Every old control is breaking, so everything’s up for grabs.” Island has positioned its platform as an “agentic control plane” spanning browser, endpoint, network, data, and identity, and plans to grow from 1,000 to 1,500 employees by mid-2027 while expanding into Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. The round is a clean read on how quickly agent-security spending is repricing.
