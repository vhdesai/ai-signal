# NVIDIA, Palantir, and Booz Allen Restrict Anthropic and OpenAI Model Use Over Data Fears

NVIDIA, Palantir, and Booz Allen — all major AI infrastructure vendors and government contractors — have restricted or eliminated use of Anthropic's and OpenAI's most advanced models, demanding zero data retention or moving to private, air-gapped servers. Anthropic answered with "Enterprise Frontier Safeguards" letting customers keep activity logs in their own cloud under their own keys.
