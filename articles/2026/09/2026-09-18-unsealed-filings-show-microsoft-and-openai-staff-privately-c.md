# Unsealed Filings Show Microsoft and OpenAI Staff Privately Called AI Scraping “Theft”

Newly unredacted material in the New York Times’ three-year-old copyright suit quotes Microsoft applied-science director Brent Hecht calling large-model training on journalism “the largest theft of labor in human history,” and OpenAI’s ChatGPT head Nick Turley describing an “existential threat to publishers.” Microsoft’s own internal data, per the filing, showed Copilot answers cutting click-throughs to the Times’ domain by as much as 93% versus traditional Bing search. Microsoft disavowed the comments as one employee’s personal view. Much of the material comes from the plaintiffs’ brief rather than the underlying exhibits, which remain sealed — but the quotes bear directly on the market-harm prong of the fair-use defense.
