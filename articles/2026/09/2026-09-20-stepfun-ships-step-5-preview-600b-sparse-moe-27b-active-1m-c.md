# StepFun ships Step 5 Preview: 600B sparse MoE, 27B active, 1M context

StepFun opened API access to Step 5 Preview the same day it announced the model — a sparse mixture-of-experts system with 600B total parameters, ~27B active per token, a 1M-token context window and vision input, aimed explicitly at agentic software engineering and finance-heavy analysis. Pricing opened at $1.00 per million input tokens (cache miss), $0.05 on cache hits, and $2.70 per million output tokens; open weights are scheduled for October 15. The company’s claim is about the cost/intelligence Pareto frontier, roughly 65% lower cost at matched intelligence, not about topping the leaderboard. Benchmarks published to date are vendor-run; independent evaluation had not landed as of September 21. Launch Open Weights
