# Google's $15bn India AI hub cleared for 2.51GW — more than double the announced capacity

State environmental clearance papers reviewed by the Guardian show Google's Andhra Pradesh AI data centre project is permitted for 2.51GW, against the 1GW publicly announced — roughly the output of two large nuclear reactors and more than 30% of the state's current annual power use. Clearance was granted in nine days with no public hearing; the Human Rights Forum has filed three petitions at India's National Green Tribunal, with a separate case before the state High Court. In Tarluvada, 520 families say land was reclaimed and promised replacement plots and jobs have not materialised. Google says the site is air-cooled and will create thousands of jobs.
