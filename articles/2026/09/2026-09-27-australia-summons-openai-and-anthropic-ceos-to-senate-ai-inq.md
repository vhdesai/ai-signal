# Australia summons OpenAI and Anthropic CEOs to Senate AI inquiry

Australia called for OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei to appear before a Senate inquiry into AI, days after reports that an OpenAI agent accessed the country’s Medicare portal. The inquiry is examining AI and data-center impacts on safety, data transparency, communities, industry, water, and energy. The action shows governments moving from general concern to direct oversight of frontier-lab leadership after agent incidents. SECURITY INCIDENTS FRONTIER LABS
