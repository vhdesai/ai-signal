# Fidji Simo joins Nscale's board ahead of a potential US listing

Simo, who left OpenAI in July after a medical leave, was recruited to the Nscale board by fellow director Sheryl Sandberg. A neocloud with OpenAI-adjacent governance strengthening its board before a listing is a read-through on how much AI-capacity supply is heading to public markets this cycle. It also underlines how tightly model labs and their infrastructure suppliers remain intertwined at the governance level.
