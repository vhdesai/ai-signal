# GitHub's HydraFusion cuts AI coding costs, but quality parity is uneven

VentureBeat reported that GitHub's HydraFusion router reduced AI coding costs across every benchmark tested, while matching quality in only one of three tests. The result captures a central tradeoff in agentic software development: routing cheaper models can materially reduce spend, but reliability depends on whether model substitution preserves quality for the task class. For engineering leaders, the practical implication is to evaluate cost routers by task outcome and regression risk, not only headline savings. FEDERAL AI PARTNERSHIP
