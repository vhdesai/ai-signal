# Scienaptic AI launches ELEVATE conference for credit-union lending strategy

Yahoo Finance reported that Scienaptic AI will host ELEVATE 2026, a conference focused on practical AI implementation for credit-union lending. The agenda emphasizes fraud prevention, loan pricing, expanding access to credit, and board-level decisions about build-versus-partner choices. While narrower than frontier-model news, it is a useful adoption signal: financial-services AI is moving into operational lending workflows where fairness, compliance, and measurable member outcomes matter.
