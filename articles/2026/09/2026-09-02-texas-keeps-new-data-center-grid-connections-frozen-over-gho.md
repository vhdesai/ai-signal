# Texas Keeps New Data-Center Grid Connections Frozen Over 'Ghost Demand'

Texas continues to hold new data-center interconnections while PUCT and ERCOT audit an interconnection queue inflated by speculative requests — so-called ghost demand, where the same project is filed with multiple utilities. Reporting puts the queue in the hundreds of gigawatts against roughly 1,800 affected projects. For anyone siting AI capacity, the practical effect is schedule risk: Texas has moved from fastest-path jurisdiction to a gated one, and power availability, not GPU supply, is increasingly the binding constraint. https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/2026/09/02/ghost-demand-prompts-texas-to-halt-new-data-center-grid-connections/ INFRASTRUCTUREPARTNERSHIP
