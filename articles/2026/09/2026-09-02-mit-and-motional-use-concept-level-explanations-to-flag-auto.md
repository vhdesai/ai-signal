# MIT and Motional use concept-level explanations to flag autonomous-vehicle mistakes

MIT and Motional researchers developed the Concept-Wrapper Network, which translates an autonomous-vehicle planner's internal reasoning into human-understandable concepts without changing driving performance. The system is designed to help flag when a vehicle's decision process diverges from expected concepts before behavior becomes unsafe. The work points to interpretability as an operational safety layer for autonomous systems.
