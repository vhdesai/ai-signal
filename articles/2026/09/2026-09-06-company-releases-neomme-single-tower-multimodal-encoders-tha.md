# Company releases NeoMME, single-tower multimodal encoders that drop the vision tower entirely

NeoMME is a family of 260M and 800M-parameter bidirectional Transformer encoders that process multilingual text tokens and raw 32×32 RGB image patches in one shared tower — no separate pretrained vision tower and no causal decoder — trained from scratch as a masked-diffusion denoiser. The retrieval fine-tune NeoMME-Retriever-260M reaches 0.523 nDCG@10 on ViDoRe v3, matching the 3.75B-parameter ColQwen2.5 at roughly one-fourteenth the size, with all checkpoints under Apache 2.0. Text-only retrieval remains a clear weak spot. Underlying paper: arXiv:2609.01657.
