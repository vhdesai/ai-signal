# Sanders and Bannon share a stage to demand tighter AI restrictions

Senator Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon delivered back-to-back speeches at the Future of Life Institute's Pro-Human Assembly in Washington on Tuesday, both urging tighter federal restrictions on frontier AI. Sanders — who has announced legislation to permanently ban superintelligence development — said "when you are racing towards a cliff, you hit the brakes"; Bannon called for a presidential executive order to slow development and limit Big Tech's grip on the sector. The alignment of the populist left and the MAGA right on AI restriction is the most consequential political development of the week: it removes the assumption that AI regulation stalls on partisan lines. - https://www.hawaiitribune-herald.com/2026/09/16/nation-world-news/news-in-brief-for-september-16-2/
