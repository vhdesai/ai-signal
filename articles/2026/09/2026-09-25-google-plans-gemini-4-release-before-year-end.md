# Google plans Gemini 4 release before year-end

Google DeepMind head Koray Kavukcuoglu told The Information's AI Agenda Live Summit that Gemini 4 is already in early post-training and should ship “much earlier” than end-2026, in staged releases. It was his first media appearance since succeeding Demis Hassabis. Google has not shipped a new flagship since Gemini 3 in November 2025, and Gemini 3.5 Pro never launched, making this the clearest timing signal yet for the next frontier cycle.
