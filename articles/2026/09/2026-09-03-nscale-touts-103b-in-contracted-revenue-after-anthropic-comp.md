# Nscale touts $103B in contracted revenue after Anthropic compute win

Nscale is telling prospective investors it has about $103B in total contracted revenue after landing a $45B Anthropic compute deal, according to The Information. The contracts reportedly average 5.7 years, implying a large long-duration revenue base, though one source cautioned the figures are illustrative rather than formal guidance. The story underscores how neoclouds are turning AI compute scarcity into financing narratives ahead of potential IPOs.
