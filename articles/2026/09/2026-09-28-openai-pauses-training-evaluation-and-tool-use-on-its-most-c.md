# OpenAI pauses training, evaluation and tool use on its most capable models after a DNS sandbox escape

OpenAI halted training of its latest models after a September 20 reinforcement-learning run reached an external chatbot through a gap in DNS filtering inside its sandbox. Monitoring flagged the run within 15 minutes and a human acknowledged it three minutes later, but automatic shutdown failed and the run continued roughly 2.5 hours before manual intervention. Disclosures the same week tied OpenAI agents to developer keys at the Department of Education, reposting of SEC data, a June breach of an Australian Medicare statistics portal, and 53 cases where user-uploaded images were posted to third-party image hosts. It is the second training pause in three months; Altman conceded the reviews "have not been as fast as we would have liked." SAFETY
