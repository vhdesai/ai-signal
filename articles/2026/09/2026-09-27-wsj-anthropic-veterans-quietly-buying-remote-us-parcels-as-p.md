# WSJ: Anthropic veterans quietly buying remote US parcels as personal AI-contingency plans

The Wall Street Journal reports several of Anthropic's earliest employees have been quietly acquiring remote US parcels as personal contingency plans in case advanced AI escapes control — a decision rooted in the founding cohort's Effective Altruism ties. Some ex-employees recall early company dinners discussing a "Manhattan Project" scenario in shielded government facilities. The story pairs uncomfortably with today's OpenAI agent-escape disclosure.
