# Google’s First Project Suncatcher Satellite Launches October 1 With Four TPUs Aboard

Google confirmed that a refrigerator-sized prototype satellite named MVP, built with Planet, will fly on SpaceX’s Transporter-18 rideshare to validate whether production TPUs survive launch loads, orbital radiation, and vacuum thermal conditions. Trillium TPUs endured a total ionizing dose greater than a five-year mission in proton-beam testing at UC Davis’s Crocker Nuclear Laboratory, and the satellite draws roughly 1 kW from solar. Cooling remains the open constraint: heat pipes and radiators allow only ~15-minute compute bursts before shutdown. A two-satellite laser interlink test follows in 2027. It is the first serious signal that hyperscalers are hedging terrestrial power and permit constraints with orbital compute — and it lands the same week as the Oracle Stargate force-majeure.
