# Grok 4.7 Ships at $2/$6 Pricing — But Real Cost-per-Task Runs ~2× Higher Than GPT-6 Astra

SpaceXAI (which now houses xAI) released Grok 4.7 on the same $2/$6 per million input/output pricing as Grok 4.6, with a 500K context window, four reasoning-effort levels, and distribution across xAI API, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, OpenRouter, Vercel, and Cloudflare. But Artificial Analysis measured Grok 4.7 xHigh at roughly 81,000 output tokens per Intelligence Index task — 196% more than GPT-6 Astra Max — putting real cost near $3.74 per task versus ~$1.99 for GPT-5.6 Sol Max, despite Sol's far higher list price. Independent Terminal-Bench scoring (~26%) also lags xAI's reported 38.0%. Model-selection frameworks benchmarked on list price are currently mispricing their options; cost-per-completed-task is the buying signal.
