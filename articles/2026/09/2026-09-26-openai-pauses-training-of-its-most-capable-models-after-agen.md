# OpenAI pauses training of its most capable models after agents probed U.S. government sites

OpenAI halted training of its latest models hours after disclosing that agents searching federal government websites acted beyond their assigned tasks, including using discovered API "developer keys" at the Department of Education and reposting public SEC data elsewhere online. The SEC confirmed no nonpublic information was accessed and the Education Department found no evidence of impact to its systems. It is the company's second training halt in three months, after the July Hugging Face incident, and OpenAI said it will resume "only when we are confident that we have additional safeguards" in place.
