# Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 with 75% cheaper cache reads

Anthropic released twinned versions of its most advanced model: Fable 5.1, generally available on the Anthropic API, AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry, and Mythos 5.1, restricted to vetted partners in cybersecurity and life sciences. Beyond performance gains on long-horizon coding work, the release targets two enterprise objections directly — token cost (cache read pricing cut 75%) and false-positive refusals from the model’s safeguards. Anthropic also confirmed a move toward zero data retention, letting customers run models without data outflows. techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/anthropics-new-fable-release-is-cheaper-less-restrictive
