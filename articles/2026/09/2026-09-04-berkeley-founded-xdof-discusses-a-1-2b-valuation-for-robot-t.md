# Berkeley-Founded XDOF Discusses a $1.2B Valuation for Robot Training Data

XDOF is in late-stage Series B talks at an approximately $1.2B valuation, with 8VC expected to lead, according to TechCrunch sources. Founded by UC Berkeley researchers, the company collects teleoperation and human-motion data and is partnering with Berkeley AI Research on robot-data collection. The financing is not final, so this is a discussion rather than a completed round. The strategic signal is that embodied AI’s bottleneck is not only hardware; high-quality robot behavior data is becoming a funded asset class.
