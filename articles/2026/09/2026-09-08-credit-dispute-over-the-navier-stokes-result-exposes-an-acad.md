# Credit dispute over the Navier–Stokes result exposes an academia–lab attribution gap

NYU professor Tristan Buckmaster, who worked roughly a year on the problem with Anthropic's Levent Alpöge using a mix of OpenAI and Anthropic models, alleges OpenAI accelerated its own effort after learning of their progress and proposed a co-authored rewrite that excluded Alpöge because of his Anthropic affiliation. OpenAI's Sébastien Bubeck said the effort began after rumors that Anthropic was close, denied that researchers or agents used Buckmaster's Codex prompts or proof, and called the allegations false and inflammatory. OpenAI has not stated whether product-usage data from those sessions could have entered later training. For research institutions, the unresolved question is provenance governance when the same vendor supplies the tooling and competes for the result.
