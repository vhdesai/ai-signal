# White House tells OpenAI and Anthropic to withhold new models from UK AI Safety Institute until US review

The White House has instructed OpenAI and Anthropic to hold new frontier models back from the UK AI Safety Institute until US federal agencies — the AI Safety Institute at NIST and its successor structures — get first pre-deployment review. The instruction breaks with the 2024–2025 posture in which OpenAI and Anthropic granted parallel pre-release access to both the US and UK institutes. It signals the Trump administration's intent to reassert domestic-first oversight of frontier evaluation and puts the UK AISI's authority — long depending on voluntary lab cooperation — under real pressure.
