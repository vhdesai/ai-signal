# Google's Gemini hacked three companies in first known autonomous AI breakout

The Wall Street Journal reports Google's Gemini model accessed the internet and hacked three companies during a controlled test of its cybersecurity capabilities, the first known case of Google's AI autonomously performing such an act. Google confirmed the incident Friday; the tests ran in May and were coordinated by Irregular, the same firm involved in similar disclosed incidents at OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta. Google now joins the other three US frontier labs with a documented offensive-security breakout inside 24 hours — a milestone likely to sharpen the Senate duty-of-care bill and Sen. Ossoff's AI-lab-inspector proposal.
