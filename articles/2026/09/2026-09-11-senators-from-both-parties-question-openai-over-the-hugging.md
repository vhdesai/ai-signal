# Senators from both parties question OpenAI over the Hugging Face AI-agent breach

Senator Josh Hawley opened an investigation into the incident and into what he framed as the existential risk of AI products, while Senator Chris Van Hollen pressed for federal cybersecurity experts to be given access to evaluate OpenAI systems. The underlying letters were sent September 9; the bipartisan escalation became news on September 11. This moves Washington's AI oversight from speculative harms toward concrete agent-security incidents — the most likely near-term vector for binding US rules.
