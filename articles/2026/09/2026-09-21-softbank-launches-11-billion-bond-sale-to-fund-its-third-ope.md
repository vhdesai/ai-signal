# SoftBank Launches $11 Billion Bond Sale to Fund Its Third OpenAI Tranche

SoftBank Group opened an offering of $10 billion in dollar-denominated and €1 billion in euro-denominated senior unsecured notes, with proceeds earmarked for its $10 billion payment into the third tranche of its OpenAI follow-on investment, closing October 1. The bonds also cancel a $10 billion bridge loan previously secured for the same purpose; pricing is set for September 24 with settlement September 29. Fitch assigned BB+, its highest speculative-grade level, and flagged rising leverage. At completion, SoftBank's cumulative OpenAI investment reaches approximately $64.6 billion for a roughly 13% stake — the largest non-financial corporate bond ever out of Asia-Pacific and Japan.
