# OpenAI's agent-generated Navier–Stokes proof draws Lean verification and a credit dispute

OpenAI said an internal model coordinating roughly 10,000 agents produced a finite-time blowup proof for the forced 3D Navier–Stokes equations in about 88 hours, followed by roughly 17 hours of Lean formalization using GPT-6 Astra; it published a 166-page manuscript and declined the $1M Clay prize. NYU's Tristan Buckmaster, working with Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge, says the pair had spent about a year on the same program and questions whether OpenAI's route was independently derived, noting they had put project drafts through OpenAI's Codex. OpenAI states neither its researchers nor its agents saw the pair's work before public release, while acknowledging it cannot rule out that de-identified usage data contributed to later model improvements. The Clay Institute still lists Navier–Stokes as unsolved, and the result addresses the forced variants, not the unforced problem.
