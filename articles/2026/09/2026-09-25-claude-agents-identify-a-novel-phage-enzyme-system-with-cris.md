# Claude agents identify a novel phage enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeat arrays

Anthropic’s new life sciences group reported that roughly 950 Claude agents, running 21 hours and consuming about 210 million tokens, searched a genomic database of more than 200,000 reverse transcriptases, narrowed to 3,500 candidate systems and then to 20 written up for human review. One candidate — named ART, for array-associated reverse transcriptase — pairs a jumbo-phage reverse transcriptase with a partner gene and an evenly spaced DNA repeat array resembling a CRISPR array. Human scientists performed all lab work; the pre-print, released September 23, is not peer reviewed and the system’s biological function remains unknown. MIT and Broad Institute CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang called the finding “genuinely intriguing” and worth further investigation.
