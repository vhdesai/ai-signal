# Ando targets Slack with messaging built for humans and AI agents

TechCrunch reported that Ando is building a team-messaging app intended to let humans and AI agents collaborate in the same workspace. The premise reflects a broader product shift: collaboration tools are being redesigned around delegated work, agent identity, and shared context rather than only human chat. The key adoption question is whether teams will trust agents inside core communication channels without creating permission, audit, and noise problems.
