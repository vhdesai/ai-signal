# Berkeley Talks: W. Kamau Bell on AI, Creativity and Resisting the Pull of Automation

Berkeley News published the recording and write-up of the keynote from the AI, Journalism and Public Trust Symposium, hosted by UC Berkeley Journalism and the Pulitzer Center. Bell argued that AI should not be treated as a neutral or inevitable force, flagging the siting of data centers near Black, rural and low-income communities and the downstream harm of models trained on historically biased data. This is AI-and-society commentary from a monitored institution rather than new research, and is included as sentiment signal.
