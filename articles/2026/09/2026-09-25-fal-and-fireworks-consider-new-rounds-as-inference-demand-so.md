# Fal and Fireworks Consider New Rounds as Inference Demand Soars — $15–30B Valuations on the Table

The Information reports Fal — which specializes in inference for video and image-generation models including Google's Nano Banana — is talking to investors about a new round at a $15B valuation, with a potential push to $17–20B; revenue is running near $800M. Fireworks AI is reportedly targeting a $30B valuation. Both companies are riding a burst of open-source demand and the same agent-runtime economics that drove the Akamai commitment. The category is being validated in real time: inference is no longer a commodity margin play but a strategic capacity layer that late-stage capital wants exposure to.
