# Justice Department Backs OpenAI's Fair-Use Defense in Publisher Copyright Case

The DOJ filed a statement of interest in Manhattan federal court supporting OpenAI — with Microsoft also a defendant — in the consolidated copyright suits brought by The New York Times, the Daily News, and other publishers. The government argued that training large language models on copyrighted text constitutes fair use and that mandatory licensing costs would carry national-security consequences. A statement of interest is non-binding, but executive-branch alignment with the defense meaningfully shifts the risk profile of U.S. training-data litigation. Enterprises with model-provenance obligations should note this does not change EU exposure, where the AI Act's text-and-data-mining opt-out regime still applies. https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/trump-justice-department-weighs-openai-233400177.html POLICY
