# Alibaba Ships Qwen-Audio 3.1 With Voice API Price Cuts of Up to 95%

Alibaba released five Qwen-Audio 3.1 models — upgraded ASR, TTS, and Realtime, plus new ASR-Next for audio understanding and TTS-Next for audio creation. Reported reductions run up to 95% on ASR, ~70% on TTS, and ~85% on Realtime, with TTS covering 16 languages and 20 Chinese dialect regions and Realtime Plus carrying a 262K context window. Alibaba published percentage reductions rather than per-unit prices and gave no effective date, so the headline discount should be treated as a ceiling rather than a quoted rate. The release fits the same pattern driving Chinese models toward majority token share on OpenRouter and Vercel — price-led displacement in a workload category (voice) where enterprise budgets have been rising fast.
