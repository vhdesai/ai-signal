# Amodei calls for pacing the frontier; OpenAI says it will match the monitoring step

Dario Amodei published "We Must Pace the Frontier," proposing a three-step plan: embedded third-party evaluators with employee-level access (Anthropic has committed unilaterally, naming METR as the model), then common safety standards among labs in democratic countries, then coordination including authoritarian governments. "Pacing does not mean halting model training or technical progress," Amodei wrote, "but ensuring companies take adequate time to align and safeguard their models." Altman publicly agreed and said OpenAI "will do the same" on the monitoring commitment, without signing the broader plan. Amodei cited recursive self-improvement now running industry-wide and warned a misaligned agent swarm could do "hundreds of billions of dollars in damage" within 6–12 months. TALENT
