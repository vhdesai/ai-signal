# Bill Gates says unchecked AI could cause “a billion deaths” and calls for regulation

Bill Gates called for U.S. lawmakers and law enforcement to regulate AI development, saying unchecked AI in the wrong hands could cause mass-casualty events. He argued that safeguards and monitoring should be mandatory and characterized the resulting burden on industry as overhead, not a dramatic slowdown. The comment adds another prominent voice to the regulation camp, though the practical challenge remains translating catastrophic-risk concerns into enforceable, testable controls.
