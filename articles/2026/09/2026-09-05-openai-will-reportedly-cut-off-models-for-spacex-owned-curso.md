# OpenAI will reportedly cut off models for SpaceX-owned Cursor

Reuters reported that OpenAI will cut off model access for Cursor following Cursor's acquisition by SpaceX, escalating the broader conflict between OpenAI and Elon Musk's companies. The decision highlights a strategic risk for AI application companies that depend on third-party frontier models: ownership changes, competitive positioning, and commercial disputes can affect model supply. Enterprises using AI coding tools should treat model portability and provider substitution as part of vendor risk management.
