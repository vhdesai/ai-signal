# Adobe Forecasts 130% Increase in AI-Assisted Shopping This Holiday Season

Adobe projects AI-assisted shopping traffic will rise roughly 130% during the 2026 holiday period. The forecast lands the same week Google began testing in-Gemini purchasing in India and Meta pushed Muse further into travel and retail booking. For retailers, the signal is that agent-mediated traffic is becoming a measurable channel rather than an experiment. URL behind paywall. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
