# KKR backs $10B AI infrastructure venture Helix Digital Infrastructure

KKR is backing Helix Digital Infrastructure, a new venture targeting roughly $10 billion in AI data-center investment, staffed with veterans hired from Equinix and AES. The venture is reported to have Nvidia's backing as well, adding it to a growing list of private-capital vehicles racing to fund the physical buildout behind AI compute demand.
