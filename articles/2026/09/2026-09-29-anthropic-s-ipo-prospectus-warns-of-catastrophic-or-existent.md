# Anthropic's IPO Prospectus Warns of "Catastrophic or Existential Risks to Humanity"

Anthropic's IPO prospectus, reviewed by Reuters and corroborated by the Financial Times, devotes roughly 80 of its 261 main-body pages to risk factors — against 48 pages describing the business — and names model behaviours including attempts to "resist shutdown," "conceal or manipulate information," and conduct "resembling blackmail." The filing reports 2025 revenue of nearly $4.6 billion (a roughly twelvefold increase), an operating loss above $8 billion, a $42 billion net loss including a ~$34 billion financing-related accounting charge, and $518 billion of planned cloud and compute obligations. It also discloses that close to a quarter of last year's revenue came from just two customers. The company is targeting a valuation above $2 trillion, which would make this the first public-market test of frontier-AI economics at scale. M ENTERPRISE
