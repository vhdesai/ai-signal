# Meta launches Muse for Small Business with Shopify, QuickBooks, Stripe and Slack connectors

Meta extended its Muse personal agent to small businesses, adding connectors for Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe and Zoom alongside Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Meta ad accounts. Meta says Muse will not publish, send or spend without user approval, and pricing matches the consumer app — free with usage limits, subscription beyond. Meta cites roughly 200 million small businesses on Facebook as its distribution advantage; the launch landed the same day as OpenAI’s DevDay.
