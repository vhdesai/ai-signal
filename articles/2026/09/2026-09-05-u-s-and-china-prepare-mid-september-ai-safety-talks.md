# U.S. and China prepare mid-September AI safety talks

CNBC, citing Reuters reporting, said U.S. and Chinese officials are preparing a mid-September dialogue focused specifically on AI safety risks. The proposed agenda includes monitoring AI-directed cyberattacks, encouraging AI labs to share information, and managing cross-border incidents as frontier agents become more capable. The talks may produce limited immediate commitments, but the creation of a dedicated bilateral channel would be strategically significant given the concentration of frontier AI capability in the U.S. and China.
