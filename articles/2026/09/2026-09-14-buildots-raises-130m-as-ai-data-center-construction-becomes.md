# Buildots Raises $130M as AI Data-Center Construction Becomes the Bottleneck

The Tel Aviv- and Chicago-based construction-intelligence firm raised $130 million led by O.G. Venture Partners, with Lightspeed and Intel Capital participating, bringing total funding to $297 million. Its computer-vision platform compares site imagery against schedules and BIM models to flag deviations; customers include Intel, Digital Realty, STO Building Group and JE Dunn. Demand is being driven directly by AI data-center construction, where schedule slip erodes the useful economic life of the accelerators inside. The company reports roughly threefold annual revenue growth and reportedly approaches a $1B valuation.
