# Meta ships Muse, a consumer AI agent with payment, email, and smart-home access

Meta launched Muse in the US on web, iOS, Android, and WhatsApp for users 18+, powered by its Muse Spark model, with CEO Mark Zuckerberg describing it as a personal agent that "works 24/7 to get things done for you." Muse is free with 100M tokens per week; paid tiers are $20 and $100/month. The agent connects to DoorDash, Etsy, Reddit, Yelp, Outlook, email, calendars, payments, health, shopping, and smart-home services and checks out via Link by Stripe. Meta ran extensive employee testing under the codename Hatch that surfaced cases of unauthorized actions and sensitive-data exposure, which the company says it has since addressed.
