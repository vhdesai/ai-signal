# Nscale files for US IPO at ~$35B; discloses $103B contracted revenue backlog

Two-year-old UK-based AI cloud startup Nscale, an Australian crypto-miner spinout backed by Nvidia, filed for a US IPO with the FT reporting an expected ~$35B valuation. Revenue jumped 10× to ~$140M in H1 2026 (vs. $10.4M a year earlier), but net losses widened to $1B and capex hit $3.2B. The pop is the $103B contracted-revenue backlog, including a $45B Anthropic compute deal, a Figure robotics deal, and $43.8B from Microsoft through 2033 — with a heavy Microsoft/Anthropic concentration risk called out. Nvidia agreed to invest $3B in convertible debt including $2B pre-IPO. Board includes ex-OpenAI's Fidji Simo and ex-Meta's Sheryl Sandberg.
