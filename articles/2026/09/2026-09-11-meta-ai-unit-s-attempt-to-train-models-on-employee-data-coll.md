# Meta AI unit's attempt to train models on employee data collapsed after leak and staff revolt

Meta's elite AI unit sought internal employee data to train models and gain an edge in the AI race, but a leak and worker revolt halted the effort, according to Business Insider. The report lands as Meta simultaneously pushes managerial roles back onto AI staff in a broader reorg and readies its consumer Muse agent. It highlights the collision between frontier-training data hunger and internal-privacy norms, a template that other large employers are watching closely.
