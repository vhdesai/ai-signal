# Tencent-backed Enflame’s IPO draws 4,073 times retail demand

Bloomberg reports that Tencent-backed AI chipmaker Enflame saw heavy retail demand for its IPO. The demand illustrates how domestic AI semiconductor capacity remains a strategic and financial priority in China, especially under continuing export-control pressure. It also shows that local capital markets are still willing to fund alternatives to U.S. GPU supply despite execution risk. URL behind paywall. CHINACLOUDMONETIZATION
