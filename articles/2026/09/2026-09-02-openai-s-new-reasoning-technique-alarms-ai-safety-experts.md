# OpenAI’s new reasoning technique alarms AI safety experts

TechCrunch reports that a new OpenAI reasoning technique has raised concerns among AI safety experts. The issue is that stronger reasoning methods can improve usefulness while also making model behavior harder to inspect, constrain, or predict. This reinforces the need for safety evaluation that focuses on capability elicitation, not just observed behavior in standard tests.
