# Lovable's annualized revenue crosses $600M as vibe coding takes off

Lovable's co-founder Fabian Hedin says the vibe-coding platform is now at $600M+ ARR, with apps built on it drawing close to a billion monthly views. The growth curve reinforces a pattern also visible at Cursor and Replit: natural-language app authoring is compounding faster than most enterprise SaaS reference curves, and the ceiling on "code as a service" is being reset upward roughly every quarter.
