# OpenAI publishes GPT‑6 Astra prompting guide, including a “slop word” blocklist

OpenAI released a detailed prompting guide for GPT‑6 Astra aimed at developers deploying it in agentic workflows. It covers how to make the model take more initiative, how to suppress characteristic AI “slop” phrasing via an explicit blocklist, and how to stop it over-testing generated code. Practical guidance of this kind is a useful signal of where the model’s default behaviors need correcting in production.
