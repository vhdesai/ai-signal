# Trump announces an "AI Force," plans an "AI czar," and rejects new AI regulation

President Trump publicly announced plans for an "AI Force" modeled on the Space Force and an "AI czar" (chosen for "high IQ"), claimed AI could reach 25% of US economic output, dismissed AI-safety warnings as a "Democratic hoax," and suggested the technology needs a rebrand under a new name. He also framed criticism of data-center construction as a left-wing attack. For executives modeling US federal AI policy, this locks in the White House posture Huang endorsed last week: no new federal regulation, faster buildout, and explicit alignment against the pacing coalition — with a new organizational structure to implement it.
