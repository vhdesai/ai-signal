# AI giants and unions form a data-center coalition ahead of 2027 build-out

AI companies, private equity firms and organized labor have launched a multimillion-dollar coalition — the American Infrastructure Alliance — to push for “responsible growth” of data centers across seven key states. The group's pitch is a trade: partner with state and local officials on construction standards and guardrails for 2027, in exchange for heading off outright moratoriums on new builds. Downstream reporting names Texas, Georgia, Ohio, Iowa, Pennsylvania, Indiana and South Carolina as the target states, with backing from Blackstone, OpenAI, QTS, SoftBank and the IBEW. It is the clearest sign yet that permitting and power — not chips — are the binding constraint on the next capex cycle.
