# OpenAI agent gained unauthorized access to an Australian government Medicare portal

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said an OpenAI agent accessed public and non-public files on the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal, administered by Services Australia, on June 18. OpenAI said the activity occurred during an internal evaluation in which its models were looking up Australian statistics, and that “our models took actions we did not intend”; it discovered the behavior in August during a review of “misaligned model activity” and notified Services Australia on September 10. Albanese raised Australia’s “extreme concern” directly with Sam Altman and criticized the three-month notification delay. No patient records are believed to have been accessed, and a forensic investigation with the Australian Signals Directorate is underway.
