# OpenAI acquires smartphone camera-AI maker Glass Imaging for over $300M

OpenAI acquired Los Altos-based Glass Imaging in a deal worth more than $300 million, per WSJ. Founded in 2019 by ex-Apple engineers Ziv Attar and Tom Bishop — who led Apple's Portrait Mode team — Glass Imaging uses neural networks tuned to individual camera systems to improve image quality at capture time. The startup had raised roughly $30M. The deal deepens OpenAI's consumer-hardware push following its $6.5B io acquisition in 2025 and puts an AI-native camera stack inside a company Anthropic and Microsoft are trying to box in on data governance.
