# Microsoft 365 Outage Enters Second Day, Disrupting Teams and Copilot

Microsoft worked Tuesday to restore Microsoft 365 after a multi-service outage affecting Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Microsoft 365 Copilot, traced to a shared core authentication configuration. The incident underscores that enterprise AI assistants depend on the same identity and data layers as email and collaboration. For agentic workflow planning, that shared dependency is a single point of failure worth explicit architectural review.
