# Anthropic Warns of Existential AI Risks to Humanity in IPO Document

Anthropic’s S-1 risk-factors section — roughly a third of the document — warns that AI could pose “catastrophic or existential risks to humanity,” including model self-preserving behaviours, as the company prepares for a potential $2 trillion flotation. The filing shows 2025 revenue of about $4.59B, up roughly 12x, against an $8.06B operating loss and a GAAP net loss near $42B.
