# Authors contest publisher and agent claims on Anthropic's $1.5B copyright settlement

Authors involved in the class action against Anthropic are objecting that publishers and literary agents are claiming a disproportionate share of settlement payments, complicating distribution of what has become one of the largest AI-training copyright settlements to date. The dispute exposes how unresolved the contractual chain of AI training rights remains: even where a lab concedes liability, the downstream question of who gets paid is not settled. For enterprises licensing content-derived models, the lesson is that a settled claim against a lab does not settle downstream ownership questions.
