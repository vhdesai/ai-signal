# SK Hynix in talks with Intel to make memory chips in the US for the first time

Reuters reports SK Hynix is in talks with Intel about manufacturing memory chips on US soil for the first time — either leasing part of Intel's long-planned Ohio fab or forming a JV with Intel and major cloud firms eager to secure supply. It would be a significant shift for SK Hynix, which keeps its most advanced production at home, and would expand its US footprint beyond the $4B advanced-packaging plant it is already building in Indiana. Talks remain preliminary; Seoul may resist because advanced memory is treated as sensitive technology.
