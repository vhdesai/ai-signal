# New Trending Google DeepMind introduces WeatherNext 3 with hourly, 5‑kilometer forecasts

Google DeepMind and Google Research introduced WeatherNext 3, described as their most accurate global weather model to date and roughly five times sharper than the prior generation. It produces hourly forecasts at up to 5‑kilometer resolution and is being integrated into Google consumer and cloud products. Scientific-domain foundation models continue to be one of the clearest areas where AI delivers measurable, verifiable accuracy gains over incumbent numerical methods.
