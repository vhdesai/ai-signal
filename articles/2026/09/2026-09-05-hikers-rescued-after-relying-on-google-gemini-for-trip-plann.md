# Hikers Rescued After Relying on Google Gemini for Trip Planning

A sheriff's office reported rescuing a hiking party that had been "advised by Gemini to bring far less food and water than their group required." The incident is small in scale but notable as a documented, attributable case of consumer assistant advice producing physical-world harm. It strengthens the argument for domain-specific guardrails and explicit competence boundaries in any consumer- or field-facing assistant deployment.
