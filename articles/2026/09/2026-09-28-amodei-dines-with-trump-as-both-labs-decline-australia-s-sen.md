# Amodei dines with Trump as both labs decline Australia's Senate inquiry

Dario Amodei held his first one-on-one White House dinner with President Trump on Sunday, weeks after calling publicly for a slowdown in frontier development. Separately, Bloomberg reported Sam Altman will not attend Thursday's Australian Senate inquiry into AI and data centres, and The Guardian reported Anthropic will not appear either, amid fallout from the OpenAI Medicare access. Trump reiterated he is not worried about rogue agents and will not put "brakes" on US development — leaving a widening gap between lab-level caution and US federal posture.
