# New Tencent's Hy4 open-weight preview lands 8th on Code Arena WebDev

Tencent's Hy4 preview, part of its Hunyuan series, ranked 8th on Code Arena's WebDev leaderboard, just behind Claude Fable 5 and ahead of Alibaba's Qwen 3.8-Flash-Next, and scored 64.3 on the DeepSWE software-engineering benchmark. The model grew to 770 billion parameters from 295B in Hy3 and carries a 1-million-token context window. Analysts noted Tencent deploys preview models across WeChat and its gaming properties to gather usage data ahead of full release.
