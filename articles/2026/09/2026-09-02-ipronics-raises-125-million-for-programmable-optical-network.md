# iPronics raises $125 million for programmable optical networking, with Nvidia participating

Valencia-based iPronics closed a $125 million round co-led by Maverick Silicon and Light Street Capital, with participation from Nvidia, to commercialize silicon-photonics optical circuit switching for AI data centers. The company’s thesis is that interconnect, not accelerator throughput, is becoming the limiting factor as clusters scale past tens of thousands of GPUs.
