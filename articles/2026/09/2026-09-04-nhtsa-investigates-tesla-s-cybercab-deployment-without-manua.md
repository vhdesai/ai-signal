# NHTSA investigates Tesla's Cybercab deployment without manual controls

TechCrunch reported that NHTSA opened an investigation into Tesla's Cybercab launch on public roads without a steering wheel or pedals. While the case is autonomous vehicles rather than generative AI, it is relevant to the broader AI governance pattern: regulators are testing whether self-certification is sufficient when AI systems move from demos into public deployment. The outcome could shape expectations for how physical AI products document safety cases before commercialization.
