SYN Ventures' Jay Leek, former Blackstone CISO, told PitchBook: "Anthropic and Mythos did what a decade of CISO warnings couldn't — best security-awareness education for CEOs anywhere." The kicker: buyers are scared into spending but "paralyzed by the prospect of choosing the wrong solution in a sea of choice," and pricing is becoming a race to the bottom.
