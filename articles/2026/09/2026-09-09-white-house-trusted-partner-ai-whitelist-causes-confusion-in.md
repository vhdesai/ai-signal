# White House "trusted partner" AI whitelist causes confusion in corporate America

The Trump administration's "trusted partner" program — banks, cybersecurity firms, and power utilities that get pre-release access to frontier AI models to sniff out vulnerabilities — has become one of the most sought-after tickets in corporate America, but the criteria for getting on the list remain opaque. A June executive order established the voluntary testing framework, but the public testing framework has been delayed and no clear application process exists.
