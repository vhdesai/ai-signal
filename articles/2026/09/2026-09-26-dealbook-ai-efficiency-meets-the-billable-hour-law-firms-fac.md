# DealBook: AI Efficiency Meets the Billable Hour — Law Firms Face 20–30% Client Discount Requests

After OpenAI released a lawyer-specific version of its model last week, Sullivan & Cromwell ("Agreement Analyzer"), Cooley ("Go Public"), and Ropes & Gray (issue-level diligence in hours, not weeks) all unveiled tools this week — and clients are already using their own AI agents to line-item-audit legal invoices and demand 20–30% markdowns. Legora and Norm AI (which raised $260M at a $1.2B valuation from Khosla, Blackstone, and Bain Capital in July) are marketing upfront pricing as an alternative. 35% of 55 large firms surveyed by Citi said they expect billable-hour changes within a year; 65% by 2035. It is the sharpest current example of AI compressing a revenue model from the client side inward. Infrastructure BREAKING CAPEX RISK
