# University of Cincinnati: Children Trust Generative-AI Toys Far More Readily Than Their Parents Do

Researchers Kelly Merrill Jr. and Nancy Jennings studied 41 children aged 5–10 playing with the commercial generative-AI robot Miko, finding that children tended to believe what the AI robot told them was true, and that half already used AI agents at home. The team observed guardrails working in practice — when a child’s story turned too frightening, Miko redirected — but call for early AI-literacy education, set against Common Sense Media assessments finding AI toys regularly generated age-inappropriate content. Findings were presented at the Comm Horizons conference at UC Davis.
