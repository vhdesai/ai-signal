# Quanta: the proof is Lean-verified, but scope and precedence are contested

Quanta reports the OpenAI Navier–Stokes result has been formally checked in Lean, giving mathematicians confidence in its correctness, and frames it as by a significant margin the most important AI-derived proof to date. It also notes OpenAI's announcement came 12 hours after NYU's Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic's Levent Alpöge announced resolution of several closely related problems, and that both efforts leaned heavily on a strategy developed by Diego Córdoba and Luis Martínez-Zoroa. Princeton's Charles Fefferman, who authored the Clay problem description, credited Córdoba and Martínez-Zoroa as the central figures. Analysts elsewhere note OpenAI's result addresses the forced equations, not the unforced formulation the Clay criteria specify.
