# OpenAI says Astra reaches a critical cybersecurity capability threshold

The Hacker News reported that OpenAI says its forthcoming Astra model meets the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework, while the company plans to make advanced cyber features available through the Daybreak Blue program. The reported capability includes finding and chaining vulnerabilities in evaluations, making safeguards, monitoring, and release pacing central to deployment decisions. The broader implication is that frontier cyber-capable models are entering a phase where controlled access programs may become the operating norm.
