# HPE carries a $7.6B AI order backlog as memory supply lags

HPE reported record third-quarter results but disclosed a $7.6 billion backlog of AI server orders it cannot fulfil immediately, attributing the bottleneck to memory supply chains. The gap converts demand into deferred revenue rather than lost revenue, but it extends enterprise lead times for AI infrastructure through the fiscal year. Buyers planning 2027 capacity should model procurement lead-time volatility explicitly.
