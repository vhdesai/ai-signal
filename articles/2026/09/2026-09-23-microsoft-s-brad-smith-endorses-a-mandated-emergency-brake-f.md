# Microsoft’s Brad Smith Endorses a Mandated “Emergency Brake” for Advanced AI Systems

Microsoft Vice Chair and President Brad Smith told the WSJ Leadership Institute that some AI regulation is “inevitable” and argued that any AI automating critical infrastructure or autonomous systems should carry an emergency brake — with shutdown authority held redundantly by the model developer, the cloud infrastructure provider, and the enterprise operating the agents. Smith said he would not object to regulation requiring such a mechanism, a notable position from the largest commercial deployer of frontier models. Arriving the same week as the Australian disclosure, it signals the hyperscaler position shifting from voluntary frameworks toward enforceable controls at the deployment layer.
