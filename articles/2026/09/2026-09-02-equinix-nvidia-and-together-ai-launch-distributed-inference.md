# Equinix, Nvidia and Together AI launch distributed inference exchange

Equinix announced Inference Exchange with Nvidia and Together AI, pairing Nvidia enterprise reference architectures, Together AI's inference platform, and Equinix's global data-center footprint. The program is designed to run enterprise inference closer to data and users, with availability targeted for Q1 2027. It signals that inference placement — latency, sovereignty, privacy, and cost — is becoming a strategic architecture decision rather than a deployment detail.
