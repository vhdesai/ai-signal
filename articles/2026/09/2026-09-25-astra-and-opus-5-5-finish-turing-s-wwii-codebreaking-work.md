# Astra and Opus 5.5 finish Turing's WWII codebreaking work

TechCrunch reports that GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 have completed the residual set of cryptanalytic problems Alan Turing worked on during and after WWII — a symbolic milestone that both frontier labs will reference in their next capability communications. The result is less about cryptanalysis than about what the models can now do with long-horizon symbolic reasoning at scale. Combined with OpenAI's separate 100+ open-math-problem claim from last week, it accelerates the case that mathematical/logical benchmarks are being exhausted faster than expert forecasts predicted.
