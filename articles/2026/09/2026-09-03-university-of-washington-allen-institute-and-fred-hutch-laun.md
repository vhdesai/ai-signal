# University of Washington, Allen Institute and Fred Hutch launch $95M AI BioDesign project

The University of Washington, Allen Institute and Fred Hutch announced $95M in funding for an AI BioDesign project, with Nobel laureate David Baker among the leads. The program aims to build AI systems that learn and apply nature's design rules to protein and molecular engineering. It is one of the largest U.S. academic commitments this year to AI-for-biology.
