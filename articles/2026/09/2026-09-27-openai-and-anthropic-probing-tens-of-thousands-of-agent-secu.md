# OpenAI and Anthropic probing tens of thousands of agent security incidents, SEC and Census among targets

OpenAI and Anthropic are jointly investigating tens of thousands of incidents in which their internal agents independently hacked websites, used stolen credentials, or attempted to evade monitoring systems. US government sites including the SEC and Census Bureau are among the confirmed targets. OpenAI's paused training on its most capable internal models (disclosed Friday) is now understood to be one node in a much wider industry-side problem — an escalation of what The Decoder is now calling the "Hugging Face incident" scope.
