# OpenAI and Anthropic Chase 20–30 MW Sites as Gigawatt Campuses Slip

Both labs are exploring agreements for existing facilities in the 20–30 MW range across the UK, the Nordics and the US, even while committing tens of billions to gigawatt-scale campuses. The driver is speed to usable capacity: interconnection queues in primary markets exceed four years, and inference — unlike training — distributes cleanly across smaller clusters. Anthropic's roughly $45 billion Nscale agreement covers about 460 MW in West Virginia; OpenAI's Stargate commitments have passed 10 GW with a further 3 GW in Georgia and 8 GW in Ohio. JLL projects inference overtakes training as the dominant data center workload in 2027. Academic Research RESEARCH
