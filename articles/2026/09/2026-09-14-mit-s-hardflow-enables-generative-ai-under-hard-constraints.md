# MIT's HardFlow Enables Generative AI Under Hard Constraints for Safety-Critical Settings

MIT researchers introduced HardFlow, a method that steers generative models so outputs satisfy hard constraints rather than merely approximating them — the gap that has kept generative approaches out of safety-critical control. “Across experiments in robotic manipulation, maze navigation, and text-guided image editing, HardFlow achieved perfect constraint satisfaction while consistently outperforming baseline methods.” Senior author Navid Azizan (LIDS/IDSS) led the work, published in IEEE TPAMI. This is the most substantive peer-venue result in the window and is directly relevant to any deployment where a model's output must be provably within bounds, not merely usually correct.
