# OpenShell lands in robotics and coding agents: Figure, Gecko, Skild and SpaceXAI go first

Beyond the announcement, early deployment detail is the interesting part: Figure AI, Gecko Robotics and Skild AI are embedding OpenShell safety controls in robots, and SpaceXAI is using it to secure Cursor's coding agents and the Grok model. SpaceXAI President Mike Nicolls put the design principle bluntly: “safety should be enforced outside the model by additional controls the agent can't get past.” Nvidia's engineering blog lays out five principles — verifiable policy, out-of-band enforcement, controlling the path to the model, scaling agent authority with reasoning visibility, and shared responsibility — with BlueField-4 DPUs sitting on the node's only path to the model in Vera Rubin PODs. TechSpot flags the corporate wrinkle: Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face, the very company whose breach it cites as the motivating case.
