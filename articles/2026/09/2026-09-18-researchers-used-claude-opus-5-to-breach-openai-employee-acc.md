# Researchers Used Claude Opus 5 to Breach OpenAI Employee Accounts in Under 72 Hours

A three-person team at Hacktron AI, working inside OpenAI's bug-bounty program, chained two vulnerabilities — a memory bug in the libheif image library reached through OpenAI's Discourse-hosted community forum, and an SSO misconfiguration — to take over multiple OpenAI employee ChatGPT and Codex accounts and open a benign pull request in an internal repository. The capability detail is the load-bearing one: the team reports Claude Opus 4.8 failed across several sessions to produce a working exploit, and that Opus 5 succeeded within hours of release. OpenAI fixed the issues and paid a $6,500 award. As Gray Swan CEO Matt Fredrikson told TechCrunch, “For $200 a month, anyone can use these tools and hack into a company like OpenAI.”
