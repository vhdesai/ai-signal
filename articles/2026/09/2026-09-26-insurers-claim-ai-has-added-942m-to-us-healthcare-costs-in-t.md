# Insurers claim AI has added $942M to US healthcare costs in two years

Blue Cross Blue Shield published data showing hospitals' use of AI documentation and billing tools drove an additional $942M in health-care spending over a two-year measurement window. Vendors like Abridge are singled out; providers push back that the tools raise accuracy rather than upcode. It is the first major payer-side disclosure quantifying the cost-multiplier effect, echoing the Decoder's earlier reporting on NSA and insurer spend.
