# DeepSeek revenue hits $1B run rate; targeting a $7.5B raise

DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate crossed $1 billion, more than double the roughly $500M reported months earlier. CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors the jump followed API price increases of 2.3× to 4.5×, with demand holding — the API business reached an 82.9% gross margin through July. The company is targeting a $7.5B raise in Shanghai, a notable datapoint on Chinese frontier-lab pricing power.
