# Google Ships Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gates Its Cyber Variant Behind the Fairwind Program

Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.8 Flash alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, described as its most capable security model, with claimed frontier-level autonomous vulnerability discovery exceeding Anthropic's Mythos 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol. The standard model holds Gemini 3.7 Flash pricing at $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens while improving long-running agent performance. The cyber variant is restricted to roughly 650 vetted partners — governments, healthcare, telecom, plus CrowdStrike, Datadog, Palo Alto Networks and Snowflake — through the new Fairwind Program. Google explicitly prioritized vulnerability fixing over exploitation capability. https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html
