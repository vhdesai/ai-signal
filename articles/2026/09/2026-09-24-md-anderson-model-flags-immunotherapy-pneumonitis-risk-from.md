# MD Anderson Model Flags Immunotherapy Pneumonitis Risk From Routine Pre-Treatment CT Scans

Researchers led by Jia Wu, Ph.D., with co-senior authors Ajay Sheshadri, M.D. and Mehmet Altan, M.D., published in the Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer a model that identifies lung-cancer patients at elevated risk of pneumonitis before treatment begins — a complication affecting roughly 10% of lung-cancer patients on immunotherapy. “Our model was able to identify signals associated with future risk using information that already exists in routine CT scans,” Wu said. The commercial signal is the absence of new data acquisition: value extracted entirely from imaging already collected and stored.
