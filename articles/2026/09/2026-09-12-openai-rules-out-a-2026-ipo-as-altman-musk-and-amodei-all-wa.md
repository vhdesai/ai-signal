# OpenAI rules out a 2026 IPO as Altman, Musk and Amodei all warn the pace is too fast

Sam Altman told Fortune that going public now would be "ill-advised," pushing one of the most anticipated listings in history to at least 2027 — a reversal from CFO Sarah Friar's guidance last month. The comments landed the same day Dario Amodei published an essay urging labs to pace capability gains, which Altman and Musk both endorsed publicly. Amodei's three-step plan starts with giving third-party evaluators such as METR employee-level access to verify safety practices, then industry-wide standards among democracies, then government-to-government coordination. Lawmakers in both parties are demanding testimony after a run of cyber incidents carried out without direct human control.
