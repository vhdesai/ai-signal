# PitchBook Weekend Pitch: Should PE Partners Actually Know How to Use AI? Carlyle Says 90% of Employees Now Do

PitchBook profiles Bob Morse, co-founder of $1B Austin firm Strattam Capital, who used Claude Code to build a portfolio-benchmarking dashboard himself — his first coding since college. "If I’m going to ask a CEO to redesign his organization around these tools, I’d better be willing to change how I work myself." Carlyle Group CIO Lúcia Soares told PitchBook 90% of Carlyle employees now use AI and firmwide usage has grown more than 200% over the past 12 months, spanning investment analysis, underwriting, and risk management. Senior-dealmaker AI proficiency remains rare, but the leading firms are turning it into a portfolio-influence tool, not an efficiency line-item. It is the sharpest current signal that private capital is running the same AI-adoption playbook it demands from portfolio companies. Infrastructure ANALYSIS FINANCE
