# Alibaba appoints Dayiheng Liu head of Qwen LLM team ahead of Apsara conference

Alibaba appointed senior AI researcher Dayiheng Liu as Head of Qwen LLM Project, bringing clarity after multiple 2026 reorganizations. Liu — a Sichuan University CS PhD (2020), Alibaba since 2021 — has been a core Qwen contributor and is prominently featured on the Apsara conference site alongside Chair Joe Tsai and CEO Eddie Wu. Alibaba's most recent Qwen3.8 Max is currently ranked seventh on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — behind only flagship Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 models. The team has lost Qwen tech lead Junyang Lin (started own startup, March) and executive Jingren Zhou (moved to chief scientist role focused on long-term research, June).
