# ElevenLabs doubles its valuation to $22B in a $300M employee tender

AI voice startup ElevenLabs doubled its valuation to $22B via a $300M employee tender co-led by Wellington and T. Rowe Price. The round lands as Nvidia's open Nemotron 3 diarization model and Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS both pressure the proprietary-voice tier. ElevenLabs' CEO told TechCrunch last week that IPO timing is live; the tender signals investors expect it on a 2027 window. INDUSTRY
