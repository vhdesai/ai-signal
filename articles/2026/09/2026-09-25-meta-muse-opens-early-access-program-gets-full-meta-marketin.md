# Meta Muse opens early-access program, gets full Meta marketing push behind it

Muse opened a new early-access program (users request the queue slot inside the Muse conversation itself) and Meta is now driving cross-app promotion of the agent from Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp into Muse's install funnel. Muse is topping the App Store and the pace continues to outrun ChatGPT's own early mobile numbers. TechCrunch's Equity podcast bluntly notes Muse stole this month's spotlight from OpenAI and Anthropic despite arriving in the same 90 minutes as GPT-6 Sol/Luna and Claude Opus 5.5. PRODUCT
