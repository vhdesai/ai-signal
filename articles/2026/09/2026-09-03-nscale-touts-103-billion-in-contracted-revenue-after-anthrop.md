# Nscale touts $103 billion in contracted revenue after Anthropic compute win

Nscale is telling prospective investors it has roughly $103 billion in total contracted revenue after landing a $45 billion Anthropic compute deal. The figures show AI-native cloud providers converting compute scarcity into long-duration revenue commitments. The watchpoint is whether those bookings translate into reliable capacity, power, and margin once facilities come online.
