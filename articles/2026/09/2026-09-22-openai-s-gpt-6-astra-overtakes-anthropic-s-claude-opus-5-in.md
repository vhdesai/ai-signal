# OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra Overtakes Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 in Weekly Business AI Spend (Ramp)

data from expense-management platform Ramp show OpenAI overtook Anthropic in business AI spend last week: GPT-6 Astra captured about 19% of dollars spent on AI models — the single-model leader — ahead of Claude Opus 5 at 17%. "Astra is cheaper to run than it first appears, because it’s significantly more efficient," Ramp lead economist Ara Kharazian told DealBook; enterprise buyers describe a rebalance rather than a defection ("majority Anthropic for quite a long time" now shifting, per Spellbook CEO Scott Stevenson). The shift is material for Anthropic's November IPO window and reinforces the trend of enterprises staying platform-agnostic across Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google.
