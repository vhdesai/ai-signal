# Wall Street raises the bar for AI stocks

CNBC reported that market positioning turned more defensive as investors reassessed AI exposure against higher oil prices, Treasury yields, and elevated expectations for AI-linked equities. The shift is less about any one company and more about hurdle rates: AI growth narratives now need clearer proof of durable earnings, not just capacity expansion or model momentum. Executives should expect capital markets to scrutinize AI spending discipline and near-term return visibility more closely.
