# Cornell: Trust Is the Missing Ingredient for AI-Driven Food Safety

A peer-reviewed study by Linda Kalunga and Renata Ivanek with UC Davis and UC Berkeley collaborators interviewed 27 U.S. food-industry leaders. Companies see clear value in pooling confidential safety data to train predictive models, but incompatible record systems and fear of legal, regulatory and competitive exposure block participation. The structural finding generalises well beyond food: benefits accrue industry-wide while risk lands on the individual contributing firm.
