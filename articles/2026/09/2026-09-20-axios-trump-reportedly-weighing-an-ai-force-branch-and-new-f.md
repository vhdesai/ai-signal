# Axios: Trump reportedly weighing an "AI Force" branch and new federal AI czar

Axios reports the Trump administration is considering standing up an "AI Force" modeled on Space Force, plus a new federal AI czar to coordinate frontier-AI policy across agencies. The proposal joins Sen. Ossoff's federal-inspector proposal and the pending Senate duty-of-care bill giving Washington authority to block AI models judged unsafe. Together with today's Palantir/Iran-strike Gizmodo report and the pacing-collusion suit, the weekend cycle marks AI governance shifting decisively from lab-internal coordination into formal federal-policy territory.
