# StepFun Ships Step 5 Preview: 600B Sparse MoE at $1 per Million Input Tokens

China's StepFun released Step 5 Preview, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 600B total parameters and 27B active per token (about 4.5% activation), a 1M-token context window, and native image and video input, positioned for agentic software engineering and financial analysis. API access opened the same day at $1.00 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output, with open weights scheduled for October 15. StepFun reports 49.0 on its own StepCodeBench and 67.7 on DeepSWE v1.1 — ahead of Kimi K3 and GLM-5.3, below Claude Opus 5 and GPT-6 Astra; Artificial Analysis scores it 44.
