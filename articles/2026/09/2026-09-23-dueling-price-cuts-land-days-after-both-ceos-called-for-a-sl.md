# Dueling Price Cuts Land Days After Both CEOs Called for a Slowdown

Anthropic and OpenAI released cheaper models within hours of each other just days after the heads of both companies publicly argued for slowing AI development — a tension the coverage frames as unresolved rather than hypocritical. Both firms are under pressure to show returns on very large capital commitments while facing low-cost competition, particularly from Chinese labs. The timing is financially loaded: Anthropic's release comes weeks before an expected public listing, while OpenAI has pushed its own IPO to next year. For buyers, the signal is that competitive pressure, not safety posture, is currently setting the price curve.
