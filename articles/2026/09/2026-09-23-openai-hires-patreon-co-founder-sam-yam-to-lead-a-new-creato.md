# OpenAI hires Patreon co-founder Sam Yam to lead a new Creator Product division

Sam Yam is leaving Patreon after 13 years to lead a new "Creator Product" group at OpenAI, bringing two Patreon executives with him. First deliverables are expected as early as next week's DevDay. The hire signals OpenAI's intent to build monetization and workflow tools directly for creators rather than depending on third-party platforms. POLICYINDUSTRY
