# Google commits €13B ($15.1B) to Finnish AI infrastructure — its largest European investment

Google will deploy at least €13 billion ($15.1 billion) into Finnish AI infrastructure through 2028, spanning data centers, grid upgrades, battery storage, and clean energy. The package includes a 22-year life-extension power purchase agreement with Finnish utility Fortum, whose stock rose 11% on the news, plus work on commercial models for potential new nuclear reactors at the Loviisa site. Google projects ~$3.6B in construction-phase GDP contribution and roughly 7,000 ongoing jobs. The energy structure — not the compute — is the strategic tell. URL: CNBC article
