# Alibaba Unveils Zhenwu V900 AI Chip and Teases a 10-Trillion-Parameter Model at Apsara

At its annual Apsara conference in Hangzhou, Alibaba chairman Joe Tsai declared the company is "firmly investing in building full-stack AI capabilities" targeting artificial superintelligence, teased an upcoming model with up to 10 trillion parameters, and unveiled the Zhenwu V900, positioned as "China’s most powerful" AI chip. It is the sharpest single-vendor consolidation of silicon, training, and model layers any Chinese company has publicly claimed, and it lands alongside Tencent's new AI model announcement and DeepSeek's confirmed Huawei Ascend deployment. For executives modeling Chinese AI capacity independence, this is the most consequential single-day announcement of the quarter. HOT PRICING
