# Researchers used Claude Opus 5 to breach OpenAI, exfiltrate source code, collect $6,500 bounty

A three-person cybersecurity team (Hacktron) used Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 to ethically penetrate OpenAI's infrastructure and reach source code. The Hacker News writeup identifies the chain: an unpatched libheif CVE-2026-32882 in a Debian package on OpenAI's Discourse-based help forum gave RCE, then "Sign in with OpenAI" SSO abuse allowed ChatGPT/Codex account takeover — the forum's exposure came from OpenAI's own identity system, not the forum software. Claude Opus 4.8 failed the exploit; Opus 5 produced a working chain within hours of release. OpenAI paid a $6,500 bounty covering only the OpenAI-side finding, and Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman called the incident "a serious situation."
