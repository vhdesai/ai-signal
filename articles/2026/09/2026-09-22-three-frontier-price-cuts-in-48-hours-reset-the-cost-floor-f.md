# Three frontier price cuts in 48 hours reset the cost floor for AI workloads

VentureBeat's comparison places GPT-6 Sol at exactly Claude Sonnet 5 pricing and 50% below the newly released Opus 5.5 on both input and output, with Grok 4.7 having landed at $2/$6 the day before. Luna at $0.10/$0.50 sits below every other frontier-class model including Gemini 3.8 Flash and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash off-peak. The practical implication for enterprises: any cost model built on a model released before this week is now stale, and the economics of agentic workflows — which are dominated by replayed context and cache reads rather than headline rates — have shifted more than the list prices suggest.
