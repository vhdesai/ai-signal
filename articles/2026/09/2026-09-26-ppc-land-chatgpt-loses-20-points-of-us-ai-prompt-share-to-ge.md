# PPC Land: ChatGPT loses 20 points of US AI prompt share to Gemini and Claude

PPC Land reports ChatGPT has lost 20 points of US AI prompt-share to Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude over the past several months. The pattern matches CNBC's coverage of Chinese AI models surging in global popularity and Business Insider's report that "the new agent craze gives OpenAI a Mark Zuckerberg-shaped problem." Combined with OpenAI's IPO delay to 2027+ and the reported $280B cumulative cash-burn forecast through 2030, competitive pressure on OpenAI's consumer moat has become one of the year's most consequential shifts — and it plays directly into Anthropic's IPO-pricing narrative.
