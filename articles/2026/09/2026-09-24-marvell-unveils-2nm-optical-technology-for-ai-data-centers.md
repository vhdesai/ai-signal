# Marvell unveils 2nm optical technology for AI data centers

Yahoo Finance reported that Marvell will demonstrate 2nm optical technology for AI data centers, including 400G-per-lane PAM4, an 800G ZR/ZR+ pluggable with MACsec, and 1.6T ZR and O-band coherent-lite technology. The announcement targets hyperscalers and cloud operators that need higher bandwidth without proportionally higher power use. It underscores how AI scaling depends on optical interconnect progress as much as accelerator supply. SPACE COMPUTEGOOGLE
