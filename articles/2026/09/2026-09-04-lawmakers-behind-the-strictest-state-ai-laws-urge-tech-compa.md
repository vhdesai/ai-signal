# Lawmakers behind the strictest state AI laws urge tech companies to slow down

NBC News reports that lawmakers behind stricter state AI laws are urging technology companies to slow down amid safety concerns. The signal is that state-level AI governance remains a live constraint even as federal policy remains contested. Companies shipping advanced AI systems should prepare for uneven compliance obligations and more pressure to document safety processes before deployment.
