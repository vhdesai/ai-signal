# Oracle Begins Layoffs as Ellison Cancels $7.5B Stock Sale

Oracle has begun executing the $700M expanded restructuring plan announced Friday, with layoffs underway despite the blowout $664B AI-cloud backlog. The pattern — AI capex squeezing headcount at the largest platforms — is now visible at Meta and Google as well. Separately, Larry Ellison canceled a planned $7.5B stock sale, an unusually strong insider-confidence signal in the middle of a capex-heavy news cycle.
