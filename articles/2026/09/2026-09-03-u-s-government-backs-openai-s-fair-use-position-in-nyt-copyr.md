# U.S. government backs OpenAI's fair-use position in NYT copyright case

The U.S. Department of Justice filed a brief supporting OpenAI's position that training large language models on copyrighted material constitutes transformative fair use, in the litigation brought by The New York Times. The filing frames a competitive domestic AI industry as a national interest, and extends support to Microsoft as a co-defendant. If the position holds, it materially reduces the expected cost of training-data licensing across the industry — a swing factor in model economics and in indemnification terms enterprises negotiate with vendors. https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-openai-us-government-ai-copyright-fight/ Coverage Notes • No qualifying academic-lab announcements from the tracked universities fell inside the 24-hour window; the most recent MIT AI items (CW-Net autonomous-vehicle interpretability, MIT–IBM computing research) published September 2, 2026 and were held out. • No qualifying frontier-model releases from DeepSeek, Alibaba, Baidu, Tencent, or Huawei were identified in the window.
