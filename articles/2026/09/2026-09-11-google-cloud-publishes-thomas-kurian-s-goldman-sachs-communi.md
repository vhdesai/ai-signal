# Google Cloud publishes Thomas Kurian's Goldman Sachs Communicopia disclosures

Primary-source metrics worth recording: 17 product lines with more than $1 billion in revenue, customers on average exceeding their commitments by more than 50%, more than 300 customers each with $100 million-plus contractual commitments, and a stated two-year AI server payback period with TPUs paying back materially faster than GPUs. The TPU payback claim is the competitively significant one for anyone benchmarking accelerator economics. Note the post is dated September 11 but reports a September 8 appearance.
