# Google confirms Gemini 4 replaces Gemini 3.5 Pro entirely, targets pre-year-end release

Google confirmed Gemini 4 will fully retire Gemini 3.5 Pro rather than run in parallel, with launch targeted well before year-end 2026. Early Chinese-market benchmarking claims real-world Gemini 4 Pro traces already outperform Claude Opus 5.5 on multi-step reasoning tasks. It's a more aggressive posture than the staggered Gemini 3.x rollout earlier this year and extends Kavukcuoglu's Wednesday AI Agenda Live comments that Gemini 4 could ship "much earlier" than year-end.
