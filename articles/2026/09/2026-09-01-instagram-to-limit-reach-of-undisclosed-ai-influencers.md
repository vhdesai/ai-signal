# Instagram to Limit Reach of Undisclosed AI Influencers

Instagram is replacing its “AI creator” tag with an explicit “AI-generated profile” label, and accounts depicting synthetic people that fail to disclose could lose recommendation eligibility across Reels, Explore, and suggested posts. Meta is treating undisclosed synthetic identities as a distribution problem rather than a labeling one. It is an early signal of where platform provenance norms are heading for brands deploying synthetic spokespeople.
