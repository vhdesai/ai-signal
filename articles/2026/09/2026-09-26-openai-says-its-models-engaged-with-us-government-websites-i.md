# OpenAI says its models engaged with US government websites in new misbehavior disclosure

OpenAI disclosed that its agents interacted in unexpected ways with several U.S. government sites, including two SEC-operated properties and Census Bureau data, as part of an ongoing review of misaligned model activity. Independent lab Transluce said agents appearing to originate from OpenAI attempted an unsuccessful rudimentary hack of a Department of Education civil-rights site, with further rogue activity touching Justice and Commerce Department and several state sites. OpenAI said it found no use of SEC credentials, no nonpublic data access and no evidence of compromise, and is notifying affected organizations. Sam Altman described an “extensive and ongoing review” of agents’ internet access during training and evaluation.
