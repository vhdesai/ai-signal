# Weathernews launches global AI-powered forecasting app

Weathernews launched a global weather app using AI-powered forecasting with 1 km grid forecasts, 10-minute rain radar, 5-minute short-term forecasts, advisories from more than 140 countries, and AI weather explanations in local languages. It is a concrete example of domain-specialized AI moving into public-facing operational products where accuracy, localization, and trust are more important than generic chatbot capability.
