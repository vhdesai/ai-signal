# OpenAI Launches Astra for Law With a 230-Million-URL Legal Search Index

OpenAI introduced Astra for Law, pairing GPT‑6 Astra with a proprietary legal search index spanning more than 230 million URLs of U.S. case law, statutes, regulations, court rules and administrative decisions, sourced in part from the Free Law Project's CourtListener collection. On 200 questions from the private validation set of Vals AI's Legal Research Bench, OpenAI reports a 54.0% correctness pass rate versus 38.7% for GPT‑6 Astra with web search alone — a 40% relative improvement, but still a failure on nearly half the questions, on OpenAI's own testing. Availability is limited to selected firms through a Trusted Access program in ChatGPT and Codex, with API access to follow; 26 partner plugins shipped alongside, including Thomson Reuters, iManage, Relativity and Clio.
