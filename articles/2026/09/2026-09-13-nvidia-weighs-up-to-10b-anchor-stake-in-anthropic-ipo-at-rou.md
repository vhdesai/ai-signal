# Nvidia Weighs Up to $10B Anchor Stake in Anthropic IPO at Roughly $2 Trillion

Anthropic is reportedly seeking to raise as much as $100 billion in an offering that could value the company near $2 trillion, with Nvidia in talks to take an anchor position of up to $10 billion. Reuters first reported the talks on September 11; terms remain under negotiation and neither the valuation nor Nvidia's participation is settled. The valuation math leans on 2028 revenue projections of roughly $190–200 billion against a current annualized run rate that passed $65 billion in July, versus about $9 billion at the end of 2025. The structural question for buyers is circularity: the largest compute supplier would be financing the exit of a customer that buys its systems, a pattern that blurs organic demand from vendor-financed demand.
