# Oracle Posts $664B AI Cloud Backlog — and Negative Free Cash Flow

Oracle's fiscal Q1 FY27 showed revenue of $19.3B (+30%), cloud infrastructure up 121% to $7.4B, and remaining performance obligations of $664B, roughly $33B above consensus. The same release showed $28.5B of quarterly capex against that revenue, pushing free cash flow to negative $5.4B. Management said the $30B+ of new AI contracts signed in the quarter came via customer prepay or bring-your-own-hardware structures requiring no incremental Oracle cash.
