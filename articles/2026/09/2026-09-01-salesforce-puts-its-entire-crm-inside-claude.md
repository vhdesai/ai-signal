# Salesforce Puts Its Entire CRM Inside Claude

Salesforce has made its full CRM surface available inside Anthropic’s Claude, with the company positioning the integration as removing the need to open the Salesforce application at all. It is one of the most aggressive examples yet of a major SaaS vendor conceding the interface layer to a third-party assistant. For enterprise buyers, it raises real questions about where system-of-record governance and audit trails live when the primary interaction surface is someone else’s model. CIO Dive Daily Dive — Sept. 1
