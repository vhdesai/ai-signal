# CoreWeave prices $3.7B convertible bond; coupon jumps to 2.875% on AI-debt jitters

CoreWeave priced $3.7B in convertible bonds on Friday, above the $3B initially marketed. The notes are due 2033 with a 2.875% coupon at the top of the marketed range — up from 1.75% on the company's April 2032 issue. Borrowing costs have risen broadly, and investors are demanding more compensation for AI-related debt amid heavy issuance and concerns about the AI buildout. It lands the same weekend The Information's Dakin Campbell reported that debt for a Jane Street–leased data center has soured — yields on those bonds rose to 11.3%, more than 2 points above their August pricing — a data-point that both AI-infrastructure credit spreads are widening and that the debt-funded capex model has real limits.
