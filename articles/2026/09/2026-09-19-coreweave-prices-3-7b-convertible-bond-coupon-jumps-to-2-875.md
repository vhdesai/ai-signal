# CoreWeave Prices $3.7B Convertible Bond; Coupon Jumps to 2.875% on AI-Debt Jitters

CoreWeave priced $3.7B in convertible bonds on Friday, above the $3B initially marketed. The notes are due 2033 with a 2.875% coupon at the top of the marketed range — up from 1.75% on the company's April 2032 issue. Borrowing costs have risen broadly, and investors are demanding more compensation for AI-related debt amid heavy issuance.
