# NVIDIA, Palantir, and Booz Allen Restrict Anthropic Model Use Over Data Fears

The Information reports NVIDIA, Palantir, and Booz Allen Hamilton have quietly restricted or eliminated use of Anthropic's most advanced models — and in some cases OpenAI's — after concluding that current data-retention and training-use terms do not adequately protect their sensitive IP. Customers are now demanding zero data retention or moving to private, air-gapped deployments. Microsoft is reportedly pouncing to lure clients to its own offerings. This is the first clean example of frontier-lab data-use policy becoming a first-order enterprise-procurement gate rather than a legal-review footnote.
