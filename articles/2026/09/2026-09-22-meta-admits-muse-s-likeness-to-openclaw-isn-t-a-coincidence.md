# Meta admits Muse's likeness to OpenClaw isn't a coincidence

Meta acknowledged that its new Muse AI assistant was "heavily inspired" by OpenClaw, down to some workspace filenames and content structure — walking back earlier claims that Muse was built from scratch. The admission lands as Muse's mobile launch outpaces ChatGPT's own early trajectory and as OpenClaw's rights-holders weigh their options. It sharpens the emerging question of what counts as legitimate reference vs. derivation between competing AI products. INDUSTRY
