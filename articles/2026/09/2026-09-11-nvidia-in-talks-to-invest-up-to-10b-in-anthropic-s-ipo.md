# NVIDIA in Talks to Invest Up to $10B in Anthropic's IPO

Reuters and The Information report NVIDIA is discussing an anchor commitment of up to $10 billion in Anthropic's forthcoming IPO — a scale that would rank among the largest strategic anchor checks in tech-IPO history. The move deepens NVIDIA's ownership of the AI supply chain following the Hugging Face acquisition and its financing of Nscale, Mistral, and Nebius, and further entwines it with Anthropic's disclosed ~$517B in multi-year compute obligations. For enterprise buyers, the read is that model-lab and infrastructure economics are increasingly a closed loop that a small number of principals control.
