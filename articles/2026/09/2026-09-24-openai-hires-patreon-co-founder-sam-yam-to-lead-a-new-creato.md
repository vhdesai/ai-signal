# OpenAI hires Patreon co-founder Sam Yam to lead a new Creator Product division

OpenAI hired Patreon co-founder Sam Yam plus two other former Patreon executives (Drew Rowny, Shannon Ma) to lead a new Creator Product division reporting to Codex head Thibault Sottiaux. The hire signals renewed consumer focus following the March shutdown of the Sora video app and API, and it lands a week before OpenAI's DevDay. It also adds to a year of aggressive OpenAI recruiting including Ruoming Pang (Meta), Noam Shazeer (Google), and Barret Zoph (Thinking Machines Lab).
