# Sakana AI and the University of Tokyo triple robot task success with test-time search

SAIL (Scaling In-Context Imitation Learning) applies Monte Carlo tree search to whole robot trajectories: a vision-language model generates candidate motions from a few demonstrations, a simulator executes them, a second model scores progress from video, and only the selected trajectory reaches the physical robot. Across six simulated manipulation tasks, raising the search budget from one node to 45 lifted the average success rate from 25% to 73%. A physical LeRobot SO-101 trial succeeded in five of six attempts. Model weights were never updated, and the paper is accepted to IROS 2026. Academic Research RESEARCH EMBODIED AI
