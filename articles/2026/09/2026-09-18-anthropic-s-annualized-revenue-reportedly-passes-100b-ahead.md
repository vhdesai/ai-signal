# Anthropic's Annualized Revenue Reportedly Passes $100B Ahead of November IPO

Anthropic is now pacing above $100B in annualized revenue — roughly 50% higher than its reported pace two months ago and more than 10x its end-of-2025 run rate — per New York Times reporting relayed by Axios and Bloomberg. The Wall Street Journal separately reported the listing target has moved from October to November, reportedly to put third-quarter results in front of investors before pricing. The Information reports CFO Krishna Rao is navigating the timing while Wall Street waits. Growth is attributed largely to enterprise adoption of Claude Code and Cowork.
