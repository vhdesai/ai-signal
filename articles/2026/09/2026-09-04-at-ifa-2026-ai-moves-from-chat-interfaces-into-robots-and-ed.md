# At IFA 2026, AI moves from chat interfaces into robots and edge devices

Forbes reported that IFA 2026 showcased AI moving into embodied and sensor-rich products, including robots, smart binoculars, and dive technology. The significance is not that every product category becomes strategically important, but that AI capability is increasingly being packaged with perception, mobility, and local-device interaction. This supports a broader shift from chatbot-centric AI to physical and edge AI experiences that will require different safety, reliability, and support models.
