# YouTube adds AI custom feeds and Gemini-powered Creator Studio tooling

YouTube shipped user-side custom feeds — describe the videos you want and Gemini assembles a personalized feed — alongside a Creator Studio update with a storytelling script coach, smart-thumbnail evaluation, chat-based Gemini editing for Shorts, and an English-to-Spanish live-stream translation feature. Together the changes push Gemini deeper into both the consumption and creator sides of YouTube and reset the pressure on TikTok's algorithm-transparency positioning. PRODUCTPOLICY
