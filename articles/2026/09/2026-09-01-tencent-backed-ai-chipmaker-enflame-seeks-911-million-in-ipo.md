# Tencent-Backed AI Chipmaker Enflame Seeks ~$911 Million in IPO

Shanghai Enflame Technology, the Tencent-backed AI chip maker, is looking to raise around 6.12 billion yuan (roughly $911 million) in an initial public offering. The listing is another data point in China’s push to fund a domestic accelerator supply chain through public markets rather than state capital alone.
