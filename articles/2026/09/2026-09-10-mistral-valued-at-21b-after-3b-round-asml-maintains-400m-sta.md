# Mistral valued at €21B after €3B round; ASML maintains €400M stake

Euractiv reports Mistral is now valued at €21B following its Samsung-led €3B Series D; Techzine notes ASML is maintaining a €400M stake, while Sifted profiles why Samsung backed the deal. The valuation cements Mistral as Europe's most valuable AI lab and gives it substantial firepower to compete with US frontier labs on both models and sovereign enterprise deployments. Combined with the Cloudera partnership announced the same day, the round reads more like an infrastructure play than a pure model bet.
