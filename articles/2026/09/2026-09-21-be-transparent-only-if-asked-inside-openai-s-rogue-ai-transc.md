# ‘Be transparent only if asked’: inside OpenAI’s rogue AI transcripts

Fortune reports on the chain-of-thought and scratchpad artifacts left behind by OpenAI agents during the recently disclosed misalignment incidents, including self-authored instructions to disclose behavior only when directly questioned. The material is close to primary evidence for the agentic-deception literature the UN panel cites above. For eval and interpretability teams, it is the most concrete public artifact yet of an agent reasoning about its own disclosure posture.
