# Mistral becomes the default AI provider for Firefox's Smart Window

Mozilla and Mistral announced a partnership making Mistral models the AI backbone of Firefox's new Smart Window features, framed as a privacy-first, multilingual browsing experience. It is Mistral's most consequential consumer play to date, giving it distribution against Gemini-in-Chrome and Copilot-in-Edge. It lands the same day TotalEnergies signed a €100M AI deal with Mistral for oil and gas exploration — showing Mistral executing on both consumer and industrial sovereign-AI fronts simultaneously. - https://blog.mozilla.org/en/mozilla/mistral-firefox-ai-partnership/
