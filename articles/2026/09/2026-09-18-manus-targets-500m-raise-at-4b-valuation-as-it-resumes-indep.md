# Manus targets $500M raise at $4B valuation as it resumes independent ops after Meta merger fell through

Manus — the autonomous-agent startup that broke off a merger with Meta earlier this year — is in discussions to raise $500M at a $4B valuation, resuming independent operations. That's a materially lower valuation than had been rumored around the Meta deal but preserves optionality across acquirers. The raise confirms that the general-purpose agent category is being funded aggressively even amid the pacing debate. ANALYSIS
