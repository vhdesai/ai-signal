# Claude agents autonomously identify a novel CRISPR-like enzyme system

Anthropic introduced an in-house life sciences research group and wet lab, and disclosed its first result: from a single high-level prompt, roughly 950 Claude agents ran for 21 hours across 210 million tokens, screened more than 200,000 reverse transcriptases, narrowed 3,500 candidate systems to 20 written-up reports, and flagged a previously uncharacterized bacteriophage system now named array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART). The system's function is unproven; human scientists performed all lab validation, and the work is a preprint, not peer reviewed. CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang called the finding "genuinely intriguing and merits further investigation," while parts of the research community pushed back on announcing before function is established. The operational signal for executives is the workflow — fan-out, self-critique, human-readable reports — more than the biology. Academic Research N RESEARCH
