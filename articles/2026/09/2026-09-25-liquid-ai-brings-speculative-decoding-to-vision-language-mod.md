# Liquid AI brings speculative decoding to vision-language models with LFM2.5-VL-3B-DSpark

Liquid AI released a 279.5M-parameter draft model that brings speculative decoding to its LFM2.5-VL-3B vision-language model, reporting up to 3.13× faster decoding on Apple M5 Max and 2.66× on H100. Output is identical under greedy decoding, making the speedup lossless rather than a quality trade. Support ships in llama.cpp, MLX-VLM and SGLang, which matters for on-device multimodal deployments.
