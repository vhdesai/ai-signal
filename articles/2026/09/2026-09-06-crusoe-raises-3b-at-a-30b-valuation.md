# Crusoe raises $3B at a $30B valuation

Crusoe closed more than $3 billion in new funding at a $30 billion valuation, citing continued demand for AI data center and cloud capacity. The round roughly re-rates a company that began as a flared-gas compute operator into a top-tier AI infrastructure provider. Valuation velocity in neocloud names remains the clearest indicator that compute scarcity has not yet eased.
