# Meta previews Muse Charm, a pocket wearable for its AI agent

Meta previewed Muse Charm, a small Tamagotchi-like wearable that keeps users connected to the Muse agent through a tiny screen, fingerprint sensor, voice interface, and digital avatar. Zuckerberg said the device is intended to ship around the holidays after final component layout work. The product is lower-stakes than smart glasses but strategically useful: Meta is trying to give its AI agent persistent physical presence beyond phones and browsers.
