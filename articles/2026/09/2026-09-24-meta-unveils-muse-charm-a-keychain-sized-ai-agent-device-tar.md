# Meta Unveils Muse Charm, a Keychain-Sized AI Agent Device Targeting the December Holiday Window

Meta previewed Muse Charm, a small wearable puck that carries its Muse AI agent off the phone screen. Reporting points to an AirPods-case-sized design with its own connectivity and cameras, a December ship target, and pricing not yet finalised. CNBC framed it as Meta reaching the consumer AI-device market ahead of rivals, with the strategy still unproven. It lands days after Amazon refused Muse access to its consumer store — Meta’s answer to being locked out of other companies’ surfaces is to own the device.
