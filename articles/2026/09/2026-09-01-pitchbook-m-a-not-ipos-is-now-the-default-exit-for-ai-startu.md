# PitchBook: M&A, Not IPOs, Is Now the Default Exit for AI Startups Outside the Frontier Tier

A new PitchBook analyst note argues that Stripe’s acquisition of OpenRouter — at roughly 6x the model-routing startup’s $1.3 billion Series B valuation set just three months earlier — confirms a broader pattern: with IPO activity weak outside a handful of megacap names and the Anthropic and OpenAI listings still pending, well-capitalized strategic acquirers are the likeliest path to liquidity. The deal’s cash-plus-stock structure, which lets value compound while the acquirer stays private, is one PitchBook expects more AI buyers to adopt.
