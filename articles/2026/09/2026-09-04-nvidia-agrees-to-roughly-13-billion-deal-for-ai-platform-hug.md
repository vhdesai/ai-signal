# Nvidia agrees to roughly $13 billion deal for AI platform Hugging Face

Bloomberg reports that Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face in a deal valued around $13 billion, with overlapping coverage from CNBC, Financial Times, Axios, The New York Times, and Yahoo Finance. Strategically, this would extend Nvidia from accelerator supply into model distribution, developer workflow, and open-model infrastructure. The executive risk is whether Hugging Face can remain a trusted neutral hub while owned by the industry's dominant AI infrastructure supplier. URL behind paywall. NVIDIAAI INVESTINGECOSYSTEM CONTROL
