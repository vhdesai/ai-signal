# NYC parents push back after discovering classroom AI use

Business Insider reported that some New York City parents were surprised to learn how extensively AI tools were being used in elementary and middle-school classrooms. Parent coalition AIM has pushed Mayor Zohran Mamdani for a one-year AI moratorium, while supporters argue early exposure builds AI literacy. The debate is shifting from whether students will encounter AI to who decides when, how, and under what safeguards.
