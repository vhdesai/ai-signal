# Côte d'Ivoire enlists Mistral AI for national AI projects

Côte d'Ivoire announced a strategic partnership with France's Mistral AI to identify AI applications across public administration, healthcare, education, agriculture and public services, beginning with an audit of national data and infrastructure. The agreement, signed August 17 in Abidjan, sits within a 2026–2028 digital roadmap backed by roughly $2.3B. Mistral will deliver the work alongside Artefact Côte d'Ivoire.
