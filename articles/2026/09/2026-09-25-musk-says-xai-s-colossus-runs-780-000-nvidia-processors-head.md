# Musk says xAI's Colossus runs ~780,000 Nvidia processors, heading toward 1.44M GPUs

Elon Musk said xAI's Memphis “Colossus” site now runs roughly 780,000 Nvidia processors, with 220,000 GB300 Blackwell chips going live next week and another 220,000 in October — potentially 1.44M GPUs by year-end. Nvidia rose 0.7% premarket to $226.24 on the disclosure. The pace stands in sharp contrast to Oracle's troubled New Mexico buildout for OpenAI.
