# Vocci ships an AI meeting-notes ring at $249 — new form factor, live privacy questions

Vocci released a $249 smart ring that continuously listens and generates AI meeting notes, adding a new form factor to the meeting-notes category that Fathom, Otter, and (as of last week) Superhuman are consolidating. TechCrunch's framing is deliberately mixed: the always-listening form factor raises the same consent/legal questions as Apple's new AirPods-based summarization and this week's ambient-AI debate. For enterprise executives, ring-format continuous capture is now a real BYOD security concern to add to phone-based consent frameworks. Infrastructure ANALYSIS
