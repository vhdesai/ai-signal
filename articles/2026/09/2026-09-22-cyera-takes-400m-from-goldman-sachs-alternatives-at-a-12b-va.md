# Cyera takes $400M from Goldman Sachs Alternatives at a $12B+ valuation to govern agent access

Cyera received a $400 million Series G extension from Growth Equity at Goldman Sachs Alternatives, bringing total funding past $2.7 billion and its valuation above $12 billion. The thesis is explicit: enterprise security architecture was built for people and applications, not for millions of autonomous agents that inherit identities, call tools and query databases at machine speed. Cyera's July acquisition of non-human-identity specialist Oasis Security for roughly $1 billion underpins the combined data-plus-identity platform. The round follows a $600 million raise in June at the same headline valuation. Products & Tools Hot Agentic Commerce
