# xAI ships Grok 4.7 cheap — but benchmarks show a wide gap to Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6

xAI released Grok 4.7 at aggressive price points, but the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index scores it at 46 vs. 53 for both Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra — a mid-pack finish that widens further in agentic coding evaluations. For enterprise buyers with real capability requirements the delta is decisive; for hobby or high-volume/low-stakes workloads the price advantage may still matter. It's another data point that top-tier capability and low pricing rarely coincide in this cycle. Products & Tools PRODUCT
