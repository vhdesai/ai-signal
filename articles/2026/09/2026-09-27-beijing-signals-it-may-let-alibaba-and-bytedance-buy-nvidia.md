# Beijing signals it may let Alibaba and ByteDance buy Nvidia's RTX Pro 5500

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology asked domestic firms including Alibaba and ByteDance to report planned order volumes and intended uses for the RTX Pro 5500 — questioning that reporting frames as intent to approve. The card is a Blackwell-generation workstation part (84GB, up to 600W) sitting outside the data-center accelerators blocked by US export controls, and Nvidia's $108B quarterly outlook assumes zero China data-center revenue. Chinese chipmaking stocks fell Monday on the report, with Moore Threads down 6.3%, Cambricon 5.7% and SMIC 3.7%. Neither Beijing nor the companies has confirmed a decision, and clearance still depends on US export rules. ENTERPRISE
