# The Information: CFTC extends review of CME's Nvidia-GPU rental futures — October launch off the table

The Information reports the CFTC has extended its review of CME Group's planned futures contracts on Nvidia GPU rental prices, blowing past the exchange's target early-October launch. The regulator flagged broad market-manipulation concerns and requested industry comment on how such contracts would work. The delay pushes back the timeline for AI compute to trade as a formal asset class — a category Wall Street sees as key infrastructure for financing the AI capex cycle now that AI-related debt spreads are widening (see Jane Street data-center bonds at 11.3%).
