# Google DeepMind Says Gemini 4 Is Coming “As Soon As Possible”

DeepMind signalled that Gemini 4 has entered early post-training and will be pushed out well ahead of year-end, after Google deliberately “took a step back” following Gemini 3/3.1 to prioritise cheaper Flash-tier models. DeepMind chief Koray Kavukcuoglu said he remains confident Google is at the frontier, and the model is reportedly already in internal use on Google’s Antigravity coding tool. No dated launch window was given — the remarks are directional rather than a commitment. Plan model-selection reviews on the assumption the frontier tier re-prices again inside this quarter.
