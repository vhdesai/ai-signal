# Travis Kalanick’s Atoms reportedly preparing a robotaxi push

Citing Financial Times reporting, TechCrunch says Kalanick’s startup Atoms is preparing a hiring spree and acquisitions that could make it a significant autonomous-vehicle player. The move would put a former Uber founder back into a mobility market now defined by capital intensity and regulatory sequencing rather than app-layer differentiation. Funding scale and launch geography were not disclosed. FUNDING
