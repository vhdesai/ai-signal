# Jeff Dean's stealth AI startup reportedly raising at ~$50B valuation

Business Insider reports former Google Chief Scientist Jeff Dean is raising a new round for his stealth AI startup at approximately a $50 billion valuation. If confirmed, the raise would sit among the largest early-stage private valuations in AI history, alongside Ilya Sutskever's SSI and Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab. It's a striking data point on how much LP capital is chasing frontier-lab senior scientists on reputation alone.
