# New Jersey Fines AI Data-Center Operator $1.07M Over 62 Unpermitted Gas Generators

Jersey’s Department of Environmental Protection assessed a record $1.07M penalty against DataOne for installing and operating 62 Caterpillar generators — roughly 123 MW of nameplate capacity — at its 2.6M-square-foot Vineland campus without preconstruction permits or operating certificates. The violations surfaced through thermal drone imagery published by Floodlight and The Guardian. DataOne has 45 days to obtain permits or cease operation, and may continue running the units in the interim. The site is being built for Nebius under a contract serving Microsoft AI capacity — a direct data point that permit-compression on data-center power is starting to yield material enforcement actions.
