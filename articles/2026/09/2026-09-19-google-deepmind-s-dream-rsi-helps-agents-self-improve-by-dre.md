# Google DeepMind's Dream-RSI helps agents self-improve by "dreaming" through past search runs

Google DeepMind's Dream-RSI lets AI agents evaluate new search-strategy hypotheses against replays of prior runs — a form of counterfactual training that avoids costly re-execution. In tests, it matched or beat existing results while cutting search iterations by up to 2.43x. Notably, only the agent's search strategy is updated; the underlying model is unchanged — deliberately positioning Dream-RSI as tractable self-improvement without full recursive self-training, and thus less politically loaded than the ByteDance/Tsinghua RSI roadmap from last week.
