# Anthropic Launches Claude Sonnet 5.5

The second model in the Claude 5.5 family holds Sonnet 5 pricing at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens while running over 30% faster and up to 30% cheaper per task. It scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against Sonnet 5’s 10.3%, and 1844 on GDPval-AA v2.1 — two points below Opus 5.5. It is the first Sonnet-tier model to ship with the cyber safeguards previously reserved for Anthropic’s most capable models, and is available on AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure.
