# Nvidia's Hugging Face deal draws scrutiny over control of the open-model layer

Barron's analyzed the strategic rationale behind Nvidia's reported purchase of Hugging Face, emphasizing that the deal would give Nvidia influence over a core distribution layer for open-source and open-weight AI models. The move would extend Nvidia's position beyond accelerators and developer tooling into the platform where many enterprises discover, compare, and deploy models. The central watchpoint is whether Hugging Face can preserve perceived neutrality while owned by the dominant AI hardware vendor.
