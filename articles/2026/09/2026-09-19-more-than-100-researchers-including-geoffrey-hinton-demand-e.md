# More than 100 researchers, including Geoffrey Hinton, demand evaluator independence

Hours after the Accenture announcement, over 100 AI researchers signed a public letter arguing that evaluators embedded in AI companies must be "meaningfully independent" — at minimum, not owned or governed by frontier AI companies, without other significant commercial business with them, and not accepting payment contingent on findings. The letter lands directly on Anthropic's decision to fund Accenture's work itself. Critics including former White House AI adviser David Sacks have separately questioned METR's independence; METR says it takes no funding from AI companies or their executives. REGULATION
