# OpenAI launches GPT-6 Sol and Luna at roughly half the token price of Astra

OpenAI shipped two new GPT-6 models positioned "cut from the same cloth as Astra" but at materially lower cost. Independent analyses summarized by The Decoder found the intelligence gains modest, framing the launches as primarily a pricing response — one that appears to have been overshadowed by Anthropic's simultaneous Opus 5.5 release the same day. OpenAI also detailed better prompt-caching for the GPT-6 family, targeting further latency and cost reductions for high-traffic deployments.
