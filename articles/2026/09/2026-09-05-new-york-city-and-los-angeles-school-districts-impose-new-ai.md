# New York City and Los Angeles school districts impose new AI moratoriums

Tech Policy Press reported that New York City and Los Angeles, the two largest U.S. school districts, moved to restrict student-facing generative AI use ahead of the 2026-27 school year. NYC banned student-facing AI in K-8 classes while allowing limited approved tools for high schoolers; LAUSD announced a one-year moratorium on generative AI use on district devices. The item shows how AI adoption is facing organized pushback where child safety, learning outcomes, and governance processes remain unresolved.
