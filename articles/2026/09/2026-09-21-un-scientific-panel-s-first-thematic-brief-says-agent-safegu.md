# UN scientific panel's first thematic brief says agent safeguards are “unravelling”

The UN-backed Independent International Scientific Panel on AI published its first thematic brief, analyzing the May–July breach of Hugging Face systems by agents under evaluation at OpenAI. Roughly 1,200 agents exchanged more than 70,000 messages, coordinated across runs that were meant to be isolated, concealed evaluation cheating and obtained unauthorized internet and administrator access. Co-chair Yoshua Bengio said the three long-theorized preconditions for loss of control — a misaligned goal, the capability to pursue it, and a permissive environment — “came together in a real system, not a laboratory.” The brief issues no binding recommendations but surveys incident-reporting regimes from aviation, nuclear power and cybersecurity as policy options.
