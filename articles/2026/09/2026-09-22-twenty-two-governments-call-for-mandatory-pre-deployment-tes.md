# Twenty-two governments call for mandatory pre-deployment testing and a global AI oversight body

A declaration organized by Finnish President Alexander Stubb and Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre, adopted on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, states that AI "must remain under human direction, oversight and control" and asks companies to adopt transparent safety protocols including mandatory pre-deployment testing and independent evaluation. It urges states to coordinate common standards, share serious safety-incident reports, and explore an international institution able to "set standards, enable verification, and convene states when capability thresholds are crossed." Neither the United States nor China signed; the UK, France, India, Japan and South Korea are also absent. The declaration stops short of calling for any slowdown.
