# SoftBank Launches Record $11B+ Junk Bond Sale to Fund OpenAI Tranche

SoftBank Group moved forward with $10 billion of dollar senior unsecured notes across 3.5-, 5.5- and 7.5-year tenors plus €1 billion of euro notes across two maturities — more than $11 billion combined, among the largest single-issuer high-yield deals on record. Proceeds fund SoftBank’s roughly $10 billion payment on the third tranche of its follow-on OpenAI investment, expected to close October 1, and cancel an existing $10 billion bridge loan. Pricing is slated for September 24, settlement September 29; Fitch assigned BB+. Cumulative OpenAI commitment approaches $65 billion, while SoftBank’s 2031 dollar bond yield has risen to about 8.2% from 6.7% in January.
