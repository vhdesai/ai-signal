# Amazon and Meta AI bots account for most website visits

Press Gazette reports that Amazon and Meta AI bots account for a large share of AI-related website visits. For publishers and enterprise content owners, the issue is no longer only search-indexing; AI crawlers and agents are becoming material consumers of web content. That raises new questions about traffic attribution, licensing, robots policy, and content monetization.
