# S&P: Asia-Pacific chip foundries are the most insulated segment against an AI-spend slowdown

S&P Global Ratings stress-tested four APAC tech-hardware sectors — foundries, memory, cooling components, and ODMs — against declining AI capex and concluded that foundries (TSMC, SMIC) are the most insulated because leading-edge capacity remains supply-constrained across multiple end markets. Memory and cooling suppliers are the most exposed. For anyone with APAC AI-hardware exposure, this is a useful decomposition of the AI-capex-cycle risk. HARDWARE CHINA
