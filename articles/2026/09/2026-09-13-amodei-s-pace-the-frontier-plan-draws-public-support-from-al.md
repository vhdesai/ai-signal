# Amodei’s “pace the frontier” plan draws public support from Altman and Musk

Amodei published a plan Saturday proposing that frontier labs give independent safety evaluators access to leading models and develop common cross-company safety standards. Altman responded that OpenAI would also commit to “having independent evaluators with employee-like access,” and Musk responded positively; a three-way alignment among direct competitors on development pace has no recent precedent. It follows an Anthropic researcher’s resignation warning that leading labs are “racing straight to self-improving superintelligence,” amplified by the company’s own alignment lead. The commercial subtext is material: both OpenAI and Anthropic are reported to be preparing listings, and formalized risk language would have to appear in registration filings. US POLICY
