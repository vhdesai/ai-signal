# Google Makes Gemini 3.8 Live Generally Available With “Live Avatar” for Enterprise Agents

Google moved Gemini 3.8 Live to general availability and added Live Avatar, giving enterprise voice agents a lip-synced animated video face. Google cites low-latency speech-to-speech across 97 languages, simultaneous vision and audio input, and asynchronous tool calls so an agent can act mid-conversation. All generated video carries SynthID watermarking. For contact-center and field-support deployments this converts a voice channel into a video channel without new infrastructure — and adds a brand-identity control question that did not exist last week.
