# Amodei meets Trump one-on-one at the White House

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei joined President Trump for a private White House dinner on Sunday — their first one-on-one meeting — after missing the state dinner for Chinese President Xi Jinping earlier in the week. The relationship has been strained since Amodei publicly urged the industry to slow advanced model development, a position Trump attacked on Truth Social. The timing matters commercially: Anthropic is preparing for a widely anticipated IPO, and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick recently said the company is "back on the right side." AGENT RISK
