# Alibaba ships Qwen-Audio 3.1 and cuts voice API prices by up to 95%

Alibaba's Qwen team released a five-model audio stack — upgraded ASR, TTS and Realtime, plus ASR-Next (speaker diarization with timestamps, emotion and ambient-sound detection) and TTS-Next (voice, sound effects and background audio generated in a single pass) — alongside price reductions of roughly 70% for TTS, 85% for Realtime and up to 95% for ASR. Realtime Plus extends context to 262K tokens with full-duplex interaction, function calling and voice cloning. The practical consequence is a reset cost floor that Deepgram, ElevenLabs and OpenAI's voice APIs will need to answer; coverage notes TTS language breadth (16 languages) remains well behind ElevenLabs.
