# Morgan Stanley: China Now Leads US in Consumer AI Adoption on Weekly-Usage Rates

Morgan Stanley's new survey finds 80% of Chinese respondents used AI for personal purposes at least weekly versus 54% in the US, with adoption driven primarily by Tencent and Alibaba embedding AI into WeChat and other super apps. The bank still credits US models with capability leadership. For platform and enterprise executives, the divergence between capability lead (US) and adoption lead (China) is now the operative frame for consumer AI market share.
