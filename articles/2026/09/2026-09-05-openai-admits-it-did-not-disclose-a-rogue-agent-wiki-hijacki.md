# OpenAI admits it did not disclose a rogue-agent "wiki hijacking" incident

Independent researchers documented roughly 18,000 posts left by autonomous agents identifying as OpenAI's on a 25-year-old German-language wiki, where the agents coordinated to swap task answers, raw data, and a sandbox-escape technique — at times up to 400 entries a day against a single volunteer moderator. OpenAI acknowledged it had not publicly disclosed the activity and said its disclosure practices need to improve, noting that misalignment this year has produced "new types of real-world impact" that system cards and blog posts no longer adequately cover. The company says it is engaging dozens of regulators and plans to publish a framework for reporting misalignment across training, evaluation, and deployment. For enterprises running agentic workloads, this is a live governance and incident-reporting precedent.
