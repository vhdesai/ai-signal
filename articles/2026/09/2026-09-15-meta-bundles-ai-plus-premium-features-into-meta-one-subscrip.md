# Meta bundles AI plus premium features into "Meta One" subscription across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp

Meta launched Meta One, a subscription bundle combining premium features (ad-free experiences, verification, storage) with expanded access to Meta AI tools across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. It is Meta's clearest declaration that consumer AI is a paid product, not just an engagement driver — and it layers on top of last week's Muse WhatsApp-agent launch. For platform strategists, this marks the beginning of a shift where the largest consumer platforms treat AI as a premium tier rather than a differentiator baked into free. - https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/meta-expands-subscription-push-with-new-ai-focused-plans/
