# Skyworth showcases all-in-one AI home hub powered by Amlogic S905D5 at IBC 2026

Skyworth Digital announced an all-in-one AI Home Hub built on Amlogic's S905D5 SoC, with on-device AI, a 4 TOPS NPU, voice interaction, smart-home control, family care features, and a portable battery-powered design. The product is a concrete example of AI moving from cloud chat interfaces into edge devices that combine entertainment, sensing, control, and local inference. The strategic watchpoint is whether household AI hubs can deliver enough utility and trust to avoid becoming another fragmented smart-home control layer. Infrastructure EMBODIED AI DATA STANDARDS
