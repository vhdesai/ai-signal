# DeepSeek's annualized revenue hits $1B as it finalizes a $7.5B round ahead of a Shanghai listing

CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors that DeepSeek's run-rate revenue more than doubled from under $500M in a matter of months, despite raising model prices 2.3–4.5× last month. The company is targeting 50 billion yuan ($7.5B) at a 500 billion yuan valuation, aiming to close by end of October ahead of a Shanghai Stock Exchange listing. Revenue comes almost entirely from API access, and DeepSeek still allocates more than 70% of its compute to training rather than inference — a notable posture given its reported compute shortage.
