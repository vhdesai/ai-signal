# Fei-Fei Li's World Labs unveils Atlas, a photorealistic interactive world model

World Labs unveiled Atlas, a world model that can generate and modify photorealistic, interactive virtual environments from text, image, and video prompts. The research direction matters because world models can move beyond media generation into simulation, robotics, training, and synthetic-data workflows. If robust, Atlas-style systems could become a foundation for testing agents and embodied AI before deployment in the physical world. The Information search Academic Research RESEARCHSAFETY
