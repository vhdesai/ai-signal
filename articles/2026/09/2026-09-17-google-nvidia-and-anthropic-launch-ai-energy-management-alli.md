# Google, Nvidia, and Anthropic launch AI Energy Management Alliance with Emerald AI

Nvidia, Google, and startup Emerald AI launched the AI Energy Management Alliance (AEMA), a coalition promoting data centers that dynamically adjust grid draw by shifting workloads, discharging storage, or using paired generation. Google has disclosed demand-response capacity exceeding 1 GW; Nvidia is contributing its DSX Flex software for Rubin-based facilities. Eighteen launch partners include Anthropic, Analog Devices, National Grid, AES, RWE, Constellation, and NRG. The alliance is technology-neutral and performance-based, potentially unlocking 100 GW of additional grid capacity. It's the most consequential utility-tech coalition yet aimed at solving the AI infrastructure power problem — though it is a policy framework rather than evidence of a deployed flexible fleet. - https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/17/nvidia-google-and-emerald-ai-launch-flexible-data-center-consortium/
