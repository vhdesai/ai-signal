# Sam Altman addresses the UN Security Council on AI safety and human control

OpenAI's CEO delivered formal remarks to the UN Security Council covering AI safety, human control, and international cooperation — the highest-level UN engagement to date by a frontier-lab principal. The remarks were paired with OpenAI's earlier calls for shared measurement, reporting, and governance standards on recursive self-improvement. The venue signals that AI oversight is being treated as a peace-and-security matter, not just a tech-policy one. INDUSTRY
