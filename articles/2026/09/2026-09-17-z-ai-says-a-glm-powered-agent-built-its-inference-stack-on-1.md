# Z.ai says a GLM-powered agent built its inference stack on 100,000+ Chinese chips

Z.ai published a technical account describing how an "Infra Agent" powered by GLM-5.3 did most of the work to bring GLM-5.3-Flash (320B total / 18B active, 1M context) into production on a cluster of more than 100,000 Chinese-made accelerators — a scale the company says had not previously been operated on domestic silicon. End-to-end throughput reached 3.22× the initial baseline over 13 days, with per-token cost and hardware efficiency the company describes as comparable to mainstream Nvidia GPUs. The transferable contribution is methodological: a "dense feedback" discipline that gives an agent local, cheap, objectively verifiable signals rather than end-to-end metrics. Fixes from the run were merged upstream into Flash Linear Attention. - https://www.unite.ai/z-ai-details-glm-5-3-flash-inference-build-on-100-000-chinese-chips/
