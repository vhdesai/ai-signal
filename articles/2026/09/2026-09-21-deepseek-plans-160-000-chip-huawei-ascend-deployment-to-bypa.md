# DeepSeek Plans 160,000-Chip Huawei Ascend Deployment to Bypass U.S. Export Controls

The Information reports that DeepSeek CEO Liang Wenfeng is preparing a large-scale Huawei Ascend deployment, with a reported 160,000-chip build at a new Inner Mongolia data center and delivery expected in late 2026 or early 2027. DeepSeek has already tested Ascend hardware for post-training.
