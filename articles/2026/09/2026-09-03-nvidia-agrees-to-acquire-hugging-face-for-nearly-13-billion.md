# Nvidia Agrees to Acquire Hugging Face for Nearly $13 Billion

Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for approximately $12.9 billion, its largest acquisition to date and a move up the stack from silicon into open-model hosting and developer distribution. Hugging Face's CEO told CNBC the company approached Jensen Huang about a transaction weeks ago, and Huang framed the rationale as expanding access to AI for developers and institutions worldwide. For enterprises, the deal concentrates the open-weights supply chain — model registry, datasets, and inference tooling — under the same vendor that supplies training compute.
