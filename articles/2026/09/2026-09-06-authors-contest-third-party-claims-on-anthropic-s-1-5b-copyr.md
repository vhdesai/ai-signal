# Authors contest third-party claims on Anthropic's $1.5B copyright settlement

Authors expecting payouts from Anthropic's $1.5 billion copyright settlement received notices this week that publishers and literary agents were asserting claims against their shares. The dispute is over allocation between rights holders rather than the settlement's validity, but it exposes how unresolved the contractual chain of AI training rights remains. For enterprises licensing content-derived models, the lesson is that a settled claim against a lab does not settle downstream ownership questions.
