# Anthropic Reportedly Postpones Its IPO From October to November

Anthropic is reportedly delaying its listing to November 2026 so it can put third-quarter results in front of investors before pricing. Investors are said to expect roughly a $2 trillion valuation, while rising infrastructure costs weigh on the story. The delay follows Sam Altman’s statement that OpenAI will not list in 2026, and arrives in the middle of the industry’s safety-slowdown debate — a timing tension worth watching, since the growth narrative an IPO requires runs against the pacing commitments its CEO has publicly urged.
