# UN calls for "urgent action" on AI, framing "unprecedented risks"

The United Nations human rights chief called on countries and frontier AI companies to act urgently on a technology he described as posing "unprecedented risks." The appeal lands in the same news cycle as the industry slowdown pledges and adds multilateral pressure to what has so far been an industry-led governance conversation. Combined with Senate drafting and Beijing's rebuke of Amodei, AI governance has become a first-order multilateral policy front in under a week.
