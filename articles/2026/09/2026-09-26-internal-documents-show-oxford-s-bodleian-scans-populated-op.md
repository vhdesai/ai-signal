# Internal documents show Oxford's Bodleian scans populated OpenAI's training set

Internal Oxford documents reported by The Guardian show digitized Bodleian Library material was used to "populate the OpenAI training set" — a use not stated in the March 2025 partnership announcement, which framed the work as digitization for students and researchers. Roughly 125,000 images of historical dissertations had been shared by June 2025, alongside scans of 16th-century broadside ballads. Oxford says the material is modest in scale, out of copyright, non-exclusive, and will be published openly within months; meeting minutes obtained by freedom-of-information request show staff and Bodleian governance committee members raising reputational concerns. The story is a live indicator of how institutional data partnerships are being renegotiated as web-scale training data degrades.
