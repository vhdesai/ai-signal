# Google ships agentic video understanding for Gemini Flash — up to 88% fewer video tokens

Rather than ingesting video at a fixed one-frame-per-second rate, Gemini now navigates the timeline — choosing what to watch, at what frame rate, and through which modality (frames, audio, or transcript), loading only what the prompt requires. Google reports up to 88% fewer tokens, up to 66% lower cost, and up to 7% higher accuracy on standard video benchmarks. It ships as a hosted Gemini API feature with no open weights, and gains concentrate on long-form content.
