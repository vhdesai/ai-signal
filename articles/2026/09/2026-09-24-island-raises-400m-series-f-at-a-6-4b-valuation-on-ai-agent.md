# Island Raises $400M Series F at a $6.4B Valuation on AI-Agent Security Demand

Enterprise-browser company Island raised $400M led by Evolution Equity Partners, lifting its valuation from $4.8B to $6.4B, with Sequoia, Coatue, Insight, Cyberstarts and J.P. Morgan Growth participating. Island’s pitch is the browser as a control plane for both humans and autonomous agents that inherit user credentials. The company says it employs around 1,000 people and has doubled ARR every fiscal year since 2022. Agent identity and credential inheritance are now a funded security category, not a conference topic.
