# OpenAI Says Astra Is Its First Model to Meet the “Critical” Cybersecurity Threshold

In “Path to Astra,” OpenAI published the safeguard framing for its next frontier model, stating that Astra is the first OpenAI model to meet the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold under the Preparedness Framework and will ship with correspondingly stronger safeguards. The disclosure is a deliberate escalation signal ahead of a broader rollout. For enterprise security teams, it raises the practical question of how cyber-capable models are gated, logged, and restricted at the API boundary.
