# Cornell and BTI use neuro-symbolic AI to map small-molecule chemistry

Cornell and the Boyce Thompson Institute described AIMe, an AI Molecule Explorer combining chemical fragmentation rules with neural predictions. Its MS2KOSMOS resource contains more than 800 million predicted mass spectra for over 100 million small organic molecules. The study is still a preprint, but synthesis-based validation for selected compounds shows a practical discovery workflow that keeps lab confirmation in the loop.
