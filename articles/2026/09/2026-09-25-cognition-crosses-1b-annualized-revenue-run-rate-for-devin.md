# Cognition crosses $1B annualized revenue run rate for Devin

Cognition said its annualized revenue run rate passed $1B, roughly doubling from $492M in May and up from about $900M earlier in September. The company named GE Aerospace, Rivian, Rohlik and Exa among engineering teams running Devin, less than two years after general availability. The milestone follows a September 8 round of more than $2B at a $48B valuation. Bloomberg reported the figure independently, noting it is a company-reported run rate rather than audited revenue.
