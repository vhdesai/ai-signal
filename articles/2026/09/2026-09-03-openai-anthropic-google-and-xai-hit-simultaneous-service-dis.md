# OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI Hit Simultaneous Service Disruptions

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok all reported degraded service on September 3, with elevated error rates affecting logins, file uploads and API traffic for millions of users. The concurrence of outages across four independent providers reopens questions about shared upstream dependencies and concentration risk.
