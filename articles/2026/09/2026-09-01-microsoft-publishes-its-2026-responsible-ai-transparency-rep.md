# Microsoft Publishes Its 2026 Responsible AI Transparency Report

Microsoft's third annual transparency report describes a re-engineered Responsible AI Standard, new governance controls specific to agentic AI, and evaluation tooling including an AI Red Teaming Agent, RAMPART, and ASSERT. It also announced an External Red Team Alliance spanning 18 universities and expanded participation in MLCommons' AILuminate reliability benchmarks. For governance leads, the agentic-AI control set is the section worth reading closely.
