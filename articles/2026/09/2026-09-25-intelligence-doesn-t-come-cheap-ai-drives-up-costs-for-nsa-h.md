# Intelligence doesn't come cheap: AI drives up costs for NSA, hospitals, and insurers

Reporting compiled by The Decoder finds the NSA is already spending billions on advanced-model testing (mostly on compute); congressional analysts now expect full-scale AI oversight to cost "tens of billions" per year against a prior CBO estimate of $20M; and AI-assisted billing-code review has driven US health-care costs up by nearly $1B over two years. The through-line is a hard rebuttal to the "AI-will-cut-costs" case in specific regulated verticals — the technology is currently a cost multiplier in national security, health administration, and insurance underwriting.
