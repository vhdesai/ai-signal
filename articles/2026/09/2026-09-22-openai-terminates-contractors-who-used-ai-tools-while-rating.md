# OpenAI terminates contractors who used AI tools while rating ChatGPT responses

Following 404 Media reporting, OpenAI terminated multiple contractors who used AI tools while rating ChatGPT responses, with firing letters citing authenticity issues and over-reliance on AI; banned tools included GPTZero and Grammarly. Internal documents referenced a rating programme spanning more than ten thousand contractors. The underlying concern is that model-generated feedback quietly corrupts RLHF, which depends on genuine human judgment. It follows earlier reporting on “Project Lily,” in which contractors read real ChatGPT conversations.
