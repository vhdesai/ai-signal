# Google Research introduces an AI “video co-director”: four agentic frameworks for minutes-long generation

Google Research published a model-agnostic orchestration layer over Gemini and Veo built from four frameworks — Co-Director (COLM 2026), CANVAS (EMNLP 2026), A²RD and VQQA — aimed at identity drift and cascading error in multi-shot video pipelines. Co-Director uses a multi-armed bandit with an MLLM judge for creative planning; CANVAS holds persistent visual memory of characters, locations and object state; A²RD is a training-free autoregressive-diffusion loop that produced a 10-minute film. Reported gains include 81.4 average on the new GenAD-Bench, 21.6% better background continuity for CANVAS and up to 30% better consistency for A²RD. Note: the underlying Google Research post is dated September 24 — the in-window item is MarkTechPost's September 27 analysis.
