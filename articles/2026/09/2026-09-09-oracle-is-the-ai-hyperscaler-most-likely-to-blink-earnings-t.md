# Oracle is the AI hyperscaler most likely to blink; earnings today are the tell

WSJ's Markets A.M. flags Oracle as the financially weakest of the hyperscalers ahead of Thursday's earnings, with S&P recently downgrading its debt to one notch above junk and CDS spreads roughly four times Microsoft's. Oracle's last update disclosed $56B of FY25 capex and $70B planned this year — spend growth that spooked investors and left the stock trailing the S&P 500 by 51 points over 12 months. A materially cautious tone from CFO Hilary Maxson would ripple through peers, chipmakers, and the OpenAI/Anthropic IPO pipeline; a "pedal to the metal" tone would extend the debt-financed AI capex cycle.
