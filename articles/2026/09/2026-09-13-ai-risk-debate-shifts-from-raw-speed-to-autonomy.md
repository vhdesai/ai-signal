# AI risk debate shifts from raw speed to autonomy

DW framed the central AI risk question as whether the bigger danger is rapid capability gain or increasing autonomy. That distinction is useful for executives because mitigation strategies differ: speed concerns point toward pacing and external audits, while autonomy concerns point toward containment, permissioning, monitoring, and rollback controls. The near-term operating implication is that agent deployments need explicit boundaries, not just model-level safety testing.
