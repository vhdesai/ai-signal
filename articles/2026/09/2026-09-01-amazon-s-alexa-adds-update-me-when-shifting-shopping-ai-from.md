# Amazon’s Alexa adds “Update Me When,” shifting shopping AI from reactive to anticipatory

Amazon launched “Update Me When” inside its Alexa for Shopping assistant, sending personalized notifications when a tracked trigger occurs — a brand’s new product line, a tour announcement, a new book or show. It was one of several AI shopping features announced Tuesday, and marks a strategic shift from answering product questions on demand to anticipating purchase intent.
