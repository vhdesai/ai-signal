# DeepSeek Confirms Huawei Ascend Chip Deployment for Q4 as First Frontier Customer, Bypassing U.S. Export Controls

DeepSeek CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors at a Sunday closed-door meeting that Huawei will start delivering training chips to DeepSeek as early as Q4 2026 — a major priority as it moves to domestic silicon. This is training hardware (harder than inference) and is the strongest signal yet that Huawei's Ascend roadmap has a captive Chinese frontier customer, validating last week's accelerated 960DT timeline. Reports separately peg the Inner Mongolia buildout at ~160,000 chips.
