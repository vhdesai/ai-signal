# Beijing pushes back on Anthropic CEO's call to curb China's AI development

AP reported that China's Foreign Ministry rejected Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's argument for keeping advanced AI chips and chipmaking equipment from China as part of a global AI pacing strategy. Beijing framed the proposal as fearmongering and competition that could disrupt global AI governance. The exchange previews the likely challenge for any U.S.-China AI safety process: cooperation on risk is entangled with compute controls and industrial rivalry. GLOBAL GOVERNANCE ROYAL SUMMIT
