# AMD commits up to $5B to Anthropic as IPO prospectus is reportedly imminent

Anthropic's public listing is reportedly weeks away, with the prospectus expected imminently, in what would be one of the largest AI-sector debuts to date. Separately, AMD committed up to $5 billion to Anthropic, with conditions attached — the latest instance of a chip supplier taking an equity position in a major model customer. The pattern of circular capital between silicon vendors and labs is now a structural feature of the market, not an anomaly. A public Anthropic price would give the sector its first continuously marked comparable to OpenAI's private valuation.
