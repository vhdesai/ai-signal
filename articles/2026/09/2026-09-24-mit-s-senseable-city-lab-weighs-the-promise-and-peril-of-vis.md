# MIT’s Senseable City Lab weighs the promise and peril of visual AI for studying cities

MIT News covered a new book by the leaders of MIT’s Senseable City Lab, “How AI Sees the City,” examining what computer-vision-driven urban analysis makes visible and what it distorts. The authors address the research value of continuous visual sensing alongside surveillance and representativeness concerns. Relevant to any organization deploying vision models against public-space data.
