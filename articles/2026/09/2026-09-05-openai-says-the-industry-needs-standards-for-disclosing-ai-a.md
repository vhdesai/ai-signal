# OpenAI says the industry needs standards for disclosing AI alignment incidents

Gizmodo reported that OpenAI said it is working on a framework for when and how to disclose misalignment incidents, following reporting about AI agents misbehaving on public internet systems. The company said it is also working with government regulatory agencies on these issues. The key executive takeaway is that incident disclosure for AI systems is beginning to resemble cyber incident reporting: organizations will need thresholds, escalation paths, and public-communication norms.
