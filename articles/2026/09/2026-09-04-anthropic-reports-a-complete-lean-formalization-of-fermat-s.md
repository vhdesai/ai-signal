# Anthropic reports a complete Lean formalization of Fermat’s Last Theorem

Anthropic says collaborating Claude agents produced an end-to-end, computer-checked Lean formalization of Fermat’s Last Theorem in 11 days, writing 13 million lines of Lean and using roughly six billion output tokens. The system followed an existing mathematical proof rather than discovering Fermat’s theorem or Wiles’s proof. The result points to a near-term sweet spot for AI agents: verification-heavy work where correctness can be mechanically checked. Academic Research RESEARCH CHEMISTRY NEURO-SYMBOLIC AI
