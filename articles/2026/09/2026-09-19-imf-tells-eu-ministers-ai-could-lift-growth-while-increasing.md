# IMF tells EU ministers AI could lift growth while increasing economic strains

Reuters reported that the IMF told European Union finance ministers AI could improve growth but also intensify labor-market, competitiveness, and fiscal strains. The framing is notable because AI policy is moving beyond innovation ministries into finance ministries and macroeconomic planning. For executives, the signal is that governments will increasingly evaluate AI through productivity, inequality, energy demand, and strategic-dependency lenses. URL behind paywall.
