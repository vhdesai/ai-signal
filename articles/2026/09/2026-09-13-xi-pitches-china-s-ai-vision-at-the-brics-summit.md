# Xi pitches China's AI vision at the BRICS summit

Xi Jinping used the BRICS summit to position China as the convener of AI and technology cooperation across member states, framing it against US leadership of the field. Separately, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and intelligence officials warned that losing the AI race to China would outweigh other American strategic advantages. The two statements bracket the same week in which both leading US labs endorsed slowing capability gains — a tension neither side has reconciled.
