# Nvidia commits to Morocco for two African AI compute projects including a 500MW Casablanca factory

iAfrica reports Nvidia has committed to Morocco for two African AI compute projects, including a 500MW Casablanca AI factory. The commitment adds to Nvidia's Australia 2GW buildout and Sweden Einride autonomous-truck partnership from earlier this month, and further tightens the global sovereign-AI infrastructure network Nvidia is anchoring. It also pairs with SpaceX's Colossus 2 doubling to 990K chips (per Barron's) — Nvidia's ecosystem execution continues at a pace competitors cannot match, even as Broadcom, AMD, and Chinese alternatives gain relative ground.
