# Nscale’s $35B NYSE Listing Tests Appetite for Customer-Concentrated AI Infrastructure

British neocloud Nscale is preparing an NYSE listing at a targeted $35B valuation, seeking to raise $3B on a contract book of more than $103B — roughly 85% of which comes from two customers: a $43.8B Microsoft compute agreement running to 2033 and a $44.6B Anthropic supply agreement. The Anthropic deal is contingent on Nscale securing financing, and the filing describes the milestones as "stringent," with the lab able to walk away if they are missed. Revenue for the six months to June 30 was $140.6M against a net loss of $1.02B. It is the cleanest public-market read yet on whether investors will underwrite leveraged take-or-pay AI capacity backlogs, and it lands squarely inside the concentration warning Burry, Slok, and the pension CIOs just made public.
