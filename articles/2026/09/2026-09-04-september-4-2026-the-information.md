# [September 4, 2026] · The Information

Reporting citing Bloomberg says DeepSeek plans a large Huawei accelerator deployment in Inner Mongolia, primarily to run models rather than train them. Huawei's memory and production constraints could stretch delivery beyond a year, while DeepSeek continues to rely on NVIDIA for training. The plan is a test of domestic inference capacity, not evidence that the training stack has already been replaced.
