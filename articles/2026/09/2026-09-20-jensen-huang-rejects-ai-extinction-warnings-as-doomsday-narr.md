# Jensen Huang rejects AI extinction warnings as “doomsday narratives”

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told CBS News there is "0%" chance AI ends the world by 2030 and argued that existing liability and cybersecurity laws should be applied before adding new AI-specific rules. Huang's comments came as Anthropic, OpenAI, and others urged more caution after recent agent-security incidents. The remarks underscore the incentive divide: AI labs are emphasizing control risks, while the infrastructure leader powering the boom is arguing for continued deployment speed.
