# Big Tech uses guarantees to keep $300B of AI exposure off balance sheets

The Financial Times reported that Big Tech companies are using guarantees to keep roughly $300 billion of AI exposure off balance sheets. The accounting structure matters because AI infrastructure risk may be underrepresented in conventional leverage and capital-expenditure views. For boards and investors, the story is a reminder to look through vendor guarantees, capacity commitments, and project-finance structures when assessing true AI-infrastructure exposure.
