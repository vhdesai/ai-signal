# CoreWeave introduces limited Vera Rubin availability

Cognition is the first production customer for CoreWeave’s limited NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 offering. Cognition reports up to 4.8× higher throughput on its SWE-2 workload versus GB200; this is not independently benchmarked.
