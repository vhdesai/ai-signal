# CIO Dive Weekender: AI is completely absent from most organizations' ethics codes

CIO Dive's weekend edition leads with an LRN survey finding AI is completely absent from most organizations' ethics codes — even as workplace-misconduct reports increase. It pairs with New Relic's finding that 1 in 4 enterprise agents run unmonitored, McKinsey's report that most businesses are overrunning AI budgets while coding agents deliver lackluster results, and ABC News reporting that 23 US states urged Congress to regulate AI. Reuters separately reports AI tools generated nearly $1B in extra costs for Blue Cross insurers. Enterprise AI governance is now visibly lagging the pace of adoption at exactly the moment agent-safety incidents multiply.
