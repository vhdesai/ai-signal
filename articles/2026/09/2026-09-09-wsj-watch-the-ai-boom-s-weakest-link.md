# WSJ: Watch the AI boom's "weakest link"

The Wall Street Journal's Markets A.M. flagged the AI trade's "weakest link" as investors reassessed cyclicality across the AI capex chain — chip supply, power availability, and the sustainability of hyperscaler outlays into a period of higher hurdle rates. The framing continues a shift from AI-narrative buying toward focus on capital efficiency and durable earnings from AI infrastructure spending. Executives should expect capital markets to keep pricing AI-linked capex less on top-line growth and more on ROIC visibility.
