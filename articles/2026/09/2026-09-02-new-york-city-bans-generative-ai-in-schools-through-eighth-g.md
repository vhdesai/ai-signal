# New York City bans generative AI in schools through eighth grade

York City Public Schools imposed a one-year moratorium on student-facing generative AI and companion chatbots for pre-K through eighth grade, affecting roughly 600,000 students, with limited permitted use at the high school level. It is the largest US district-level restriction to date and cuts against the prevailing direction of education-sector AI adoption. Vendors selling into K-12 should expect procurement freezes and renewed scrutiny of companion-chatbot features specifically. ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/news/2026/09/02/new-york-city-bans-ai-use-in-schools-through-eighth-grade
