# Authors push back as publishers and agents stake claims on Anthropic's $1.5B settlement

Authors expecting payouts from Anthropic's $1.5 billion copyright settlement began receiving notices that publishers or literary agents had filed competing claims against the same works. The dispute turns on pre-AI publishing contracts that never contemplated licensing text for model training, leaving the split of roughly $3,000 per book contested through the settlement's disagreement portal. First payments are expected in the November 1–15 window. The precedent matters well beyond publishing: whoever wins these claims sets the template for who is paid in future training-data settlements.
