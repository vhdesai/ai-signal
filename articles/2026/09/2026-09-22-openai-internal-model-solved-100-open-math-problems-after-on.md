# OpenAI: internal model solved 100+ open math problems after one month of training

OpenAI says a new internal model has solved more than 100 long-standing open math problems after roughly one month of dedicated training, alongside forming an independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study to review and communicate emerging AI-math results. Critically, OpenAI explicitly excluded its research pace from the advisory group's remit — the review is downstream, not upstream. The move is a direct response to the 25-Fields-Medalist backlash from earlier this month; for scientific-computing executives, treat "100+ open problems" as a company-published claim pending independent verification, which is exactly what this new IAS group is now set up to provide.
