# Google launches Pics, an AI-first design tool aimed at Canva and Adobe Express

Google Pics is a prompt-driven design and image-editing product built into Google Workspace, now broadly available to Workspace and AI Pro subscribers. Rather than replicating a template-and-canvas editor, it asks users to describe the artifact — poster, flyer, social asset — and generates it, with object-based editing, translation, upscaling, and direct Docs integration. It puts Google in direct competition with Canva, Adobe Express, and Microsoft Designer in a market where distribution, not model quality, decides the outcome. https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/googles-answer-to-canva-is-an-ai-tool-where-you-prompt-instead-of-design/ PRODUCT
