# Anthropic publishes three internal safety metrics, including AI-led R&D and compute allocation

Anthropic published three metrics — AI-led R&D, oversight of AI agents, and internal compute allocation — along with methodologies, days after CEO Dario Amodei's essay calling for a coordinated industry slowdown. It reported approximately 30,000 concurrent agents doing research and engineering work across its most-used internal platform, and that in a July 13–20 snapshot roughly 6% of compute going to AI R&D was allocated to safety. Anthropic framed the release as minimizing the gap between what frontier labs know and what the public knows, and paired with U.S. News reporting that Claude is now taking an "extraordinarily active" role in developing its own successor. - https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/anthropic-shares-3-metrics-to-help-ai-companies-monitor-development.html
