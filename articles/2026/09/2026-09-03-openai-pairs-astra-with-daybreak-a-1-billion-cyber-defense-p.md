# OpenAI pairs Astra with Daybreak, a $1 billion cyber-defense program for essential services

OpenAI announced Daybreak for Frontline Defenders, a $1 billion commitment to expand frontier cyber AI, training, and support for organizations protecting essential services. The program positions high-capability cyber models as defensive infrastructure while raising execution questions around misuse controls. Daybreak also helps explain why Astra access is being staged through trusted organizations first. OpenAI Daybreak · OpenAI Astra LEGALCOPYRIGHT
