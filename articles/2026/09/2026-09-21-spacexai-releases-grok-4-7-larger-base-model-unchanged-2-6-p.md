# SpaceXAI Releases Grok 4.7 — Larger Base Model, Unchanged $2/$6 Pricing

SpaceXAI, which now houses xAI, released Grok 4.7 as its flagship for coding, agentic tasks and knowledge work, built on a new larger base model with a longer RL run on multi-hour tasks. It retains Grok 4.6 pricing at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens, a 500K-token context window, a May 2026 knowledge cutoff, and four reasoning-effort levels up to "xhigh." It leads the launch table on EEBench (64.0%) and Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark (19.6%), with a safeguard stack that let only 3.3% of risky dual-use prompts through HackerBench v0.3. Distribution is broad at launch: xAI API, Cursor, Grok Build, OpenRouter, Vercel and Cloudflare.
