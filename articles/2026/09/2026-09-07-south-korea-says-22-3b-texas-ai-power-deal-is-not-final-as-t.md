# South Korea says $22.3B Texas AI power deal is not final as talks continue

Dallas Express reported that South Korea said a $22.3 billion Texas AI power deal is not final, with talks continuing. The item matters because AI infrastructure projects increasingly depend on cross-border financing, power procurement, and political coordination, any of which can delay or reshape capacity plans. The takeaway is that the AI buildout is no longer just a data-center leasing story; it is becoming an energy and diplomacy story as well.
