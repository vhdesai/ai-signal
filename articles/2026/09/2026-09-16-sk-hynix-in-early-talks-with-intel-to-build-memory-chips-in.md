# SK Hynix in early talks with Intel to build memory chips in the US

SK Hynix confirmed to TechCrunch it is in early-stage discussions with Intel about US memory-chip manufacturing, though no plans have been finalized. The framing is directly aligned with the Trump administration's semiconductor-onshoring push and Intel's ongoing search for foundry-customer volume. If the deal lands, it would be a material shift in HBM and DDR sourcing for US AI hyperscalers, currently concentrated in Korea and Taiwan. - https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/sk-hynix-reportedly-in-talks-with-intel-to-build-memory-chips-in-us/
