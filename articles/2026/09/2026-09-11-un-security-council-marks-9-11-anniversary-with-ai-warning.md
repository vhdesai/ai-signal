# UN Security Council marks 9/11 anniversary with AI warning

teleSUR reported that the UN Security Council used the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks to warn that terrorist groups are adapting AI, drones, and other emerging technologies. The story is a reminder that AI safety and security concerns are not limited to model labs; they intersect with terrorism, critical infrastructure, and international security policy. For executives, the relevant question is how enterprise AI governance aligns with broader cyber, physical security, and geopolitical risk programs.
