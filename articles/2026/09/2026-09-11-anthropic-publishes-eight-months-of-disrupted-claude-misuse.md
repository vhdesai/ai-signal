# Anthropic publishes eight months of disrupted Claude misuse across seven harm categories

Anthropic's "Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026" covers operations disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026 across cyber operations, influence operations, surveillance, scams and fraud, biological misuse, conventional weapons development, and illicit distillation. The most extensive cyber case (GTG-20006), which Anthropic says is consistent with public reporting on Midnight Blizzard, targeted more than 20 organizations and included theft of over 300,000 national identity records; Anthropic reports the actor's agents autonomously rebuilt malware when detected. Five biological cases involved gain-of-function-adjacent work, toxin redesign and venom-peptide optimization by users circumventing geographic restrictions.
