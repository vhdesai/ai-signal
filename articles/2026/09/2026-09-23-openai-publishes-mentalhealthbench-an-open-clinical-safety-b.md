# OpenAI publishes MentalHealthBench, an open clinical-safety benchmark

Co-authored with more than 80 licensed psychologists and psychiatrists across 22 countries, 19 languages and roughly 20 subspecialties, the benchmark comprises 1,215 synthetic conversations spanning adults, teens, caregivers and clinicians across non-acute, high-acuity and emergency scenarios. Expert-written criteria are graded from −10 to +10; initial results placed GPT-6 Astra at 57.3, Opus 5.5 at 52.4 and Fable 5.1 at 46.4. The release gives regulators and health buyers a shared, published yardstick for a use case now drawing active litigation.
