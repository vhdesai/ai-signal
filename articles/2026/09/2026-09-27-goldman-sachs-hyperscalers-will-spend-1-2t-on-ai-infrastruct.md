# Goldman Sachs: hyperscalers will spend $1.2T on AI infrastructure in 2027

Goldman's newly published projection has Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft, Oracle, and Meta pouring a combined $1.2T into AI infrastructure in 2027 — more than 50% above 2026 levels. Measured against US GDP, the note calls it the largest domestic capex cycle since 19th-century railroad construction. Goldman flags power, skilled labor, and memory-chip supply as the three bottlenecks most likely to compress the trajectory before the number lands. INDUSTRY
