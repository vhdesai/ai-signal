# ElevenLabs releases Music v2.5 via app and API, licensed-training only

ElevenLabs released Music v2.5 across its app and API with free and pro tiers. In a blind test of ~48,000 comparison pairs, listeners preferred the new version over v2.0; ElevenLabs says the model was trained exclusively on licensed music. The release lands the same week Suno v6 shipped with major-label partnerships, confirming that the enterprise-safe AI-music market is now consolidating around vendors who can credibly demonstrate licensed training data — a defensive posture driven directly by the current wave of copyright litigation.
