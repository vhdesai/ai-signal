# Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic Advance a Self-Regulatory "Standards Authority for Frontier AI" Without Government Oversight

The Information reports Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are pushing forward with a plan to launch a new safety-focused standards body on their own, tentatively named the Standards Authority for Frontier AI (SAFA), targeting a late-2026 or early-2027 launch. The working group has approached Sriram Krishnan — former venture capitalist and Trump-administration AI policy adviser — for the CEO role. SAFA would define safety commitments and support third-party model testing, filling what proponents call a government regulatory vacuum. It lands the same day Altman and Amodei briefed the UN Security Council and Trump allies publicly began attacking Amodei as "the face of AI doomerism" — the industry-vs-White-House asymmetry on governance is now openly declared.
