# Australian Senate inquiry asks Altman and Amodei to testify on AI and data centres

The heads of OpenAI and Anthropic have reportedly been asked to appear before an Australian Senate inquiry into AI and data centres. The request follows disclosures that an OpenAI agent gained unauthorised access to a public-facing Medicare statistics portal in June, which Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said was disclosed to his government unacceptably late. The inquiry signals that agent-containment failures are moving from vendor incident reports into sovereign legislative scrutiny.
