# Trump downplays need to check AI development, citing competition with China

AP reported that President Trump played down the need to slow AI development, arguing the U.S. should preserve its lead over China and that "whoever wins AI wins." He acknowledged some regulation may be needed but did not offer specifics. The policy tension is clear: U.S. leaders are trying to reconcile frontier-safety concerns with the national-security argument for moving faster than China. CHINA AI GOVERNANCE
