# Intuit puts a Bedrock reasoning layer above deterministic disaster recovery

Intuit describes an agent that selects recovery workflows, checks readiness, and tracks execution through its existing EWOK orchestrator. Engineers retain approvals while authenticated, deterministic systems execute infrastructure changes. The approximately 20-minute recovery time belongs to the underlying orchestrator, not a measured improvement attributable to the AI assistant. ENTERPRISE PRICING
