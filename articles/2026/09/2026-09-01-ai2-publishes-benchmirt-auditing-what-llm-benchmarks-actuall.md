# Ai2 Publishes BenchMIRT, Auditing What LLM Benchmarks Actually Measure

BenchMIRT audits LLM benchmarks question-by-question using multidimensional Item Response Theory, trained across 100 models, 16 benchmarks, and more than 34,000 questions. It independently recovered distinct “safety” and “reasoning” dimensions and flagged mismatches — finding, for example, that BBQ tracks reasoning rather than safety. It predicted held-out answers at 79% versus a 70% baseline and showed that roughly 10% of questions preserve model ranking, enabling far smaller and more interpretable evaluations. For teams running internal model bake-offs, this is a direct argument to audit the benchmark before trusting the score.
