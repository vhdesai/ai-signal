# DeepSeek reportedly plans 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT accelerators for an Inner Mongolia data center

DeepSeek is reported to be planning deployment of at least 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT accelerators at a gigawatt-scale facility in Inner Mongolia, which would rank among the largest known Huawei clusters. The chips would primarily serve inference rather than training. Huawei’s constrained output — low hundreds of thousands of units in 2026, limited by HBM supply — means fulfillment could take more than a year. Separate US allegations that DeepSeek also obtained Nvidia Blackwell parts remain unverified.
