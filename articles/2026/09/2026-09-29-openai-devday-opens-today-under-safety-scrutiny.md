# OpenAI DevDay Opens Today Under Safety Scrutiny

OpenAI’s annual developer conference begins at Fort Mason in San Francisco, with Sam Altman’s keynote at 10:00 a.m. PT and a free livestream. No product announcements were disclosed in advance; the event follows a September in which OpenAI already shipped the Agents API beta, GPT-Live-1 and the GPT-6 Sol and Luna tiers. CNBC reports OpenAI’s enterprise business more than doubled in Q3 and that quarter-to-date revenue growth is up roughly 70%, implying a run rate near $68 billion. Treat any pre-keynote “expected announcement” list as speculation.
