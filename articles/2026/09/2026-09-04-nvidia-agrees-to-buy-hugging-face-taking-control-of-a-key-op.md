# Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face, taking control of a key open-model layer

Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for roughly $12.9 billion, giving the dominant AI accelerator supplier ownership of a platform used by millions of developers and thousands of companies. Nvidia says Hugging Face will remain open, but the deal moves it into model discovery, evaluation, customization, and deployment workflows. The strategic question is whether the acquisition makes Nvidia an open-model champion or a gatekeeper over a critical developer layer.
