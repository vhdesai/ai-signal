# Patreon co-founder Sam Yam joins OpenAI to lead a new creator division

Yam is bringing two other former Patreon executives — head of product Drew Rowny and head of engineering Shannon Ma — onto a team led by Codex head Thibault Sottiaux. The hire marks a renewed consumer push after OpenAI shut down its Sora video app and API in March to concentrate on coding and business customers. It caps a year of aggressive recruiting that has pulled researchers from Meta, Google and Thinking Machines Lab, and lands a week before DevDay.
