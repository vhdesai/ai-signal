# How to know when the AI boom is about to go bust

Jonathan Weil argues the AI boom’s core vulnerability is its dependence on a continuous supply of fresh capital, and names the dot-com-era term “funding gap” as the signal to watch for a turn. He cites early signs of nervousness: OpenAI ruling out an IPO this year, Holtec Nuclear pausing its listing, and Anthropic pushing its IPO from October to November. Weil notes that OpenAI’s continued ability to raise from backers such as Nvidia and SoftBank is precisely what allows Oracle to justify debt-funded data-center capex against hundreds of billions in projected OpenAI revenue.
