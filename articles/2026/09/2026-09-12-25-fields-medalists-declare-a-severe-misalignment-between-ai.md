# 25 Fields Medalists declare a "severe misalignment" between AI labs and mathematics

Twenty-five Fields Medalists — including Terence Tao, Peter Scholze, Maryna Viazovska, June Huh and 2026 laureate Yu Deng — signed "A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics," published on Tao's blog on September 11. The declaration does not dispute that LLMs can now solve major open problems; it argues that using famous problems as marketing benchmarks produces "mass production" of true/false statements while skipping the writeups, method isolation and prior-work attribution that turn a proof into durable knowledge. The trigger was OpenAI's September 8 Navier-Stokes announcement and the credit dispute that followed. Academic Research ACADEMIA
