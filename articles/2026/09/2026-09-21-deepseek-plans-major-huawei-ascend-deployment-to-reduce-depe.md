# DeepSeek plans major Huawei Ascend deployment to reduce dependence on Nvidia

Crypto Briefing reported that DeepSeek is preparing to use Huawei AI accelerators at large scale, including a reported 160,000-chip deployment at a new Inner Mongolia data center. The report says Huawei will begin delivering chips around late 2026 or early 2027 and that DeepSeek has already tested Ascend hardware for post-training.
