# South Korea and African Development Bank agree to launch AI hub starting in Tanzania

The Rio Times reported that South Korea and the African Development Bank agreed to create a Korea-Africa AI Hub, beginning with a school in Tanzania. The initiative reflects AI's growing role in development policy, skills formation, and regional industrial strategy. For technology leaders, this is another sign that AI capability-building is becoming part of national competitiveness agendas, not just private-sector digital transformation. SMART CITIES GOVERNMENT
