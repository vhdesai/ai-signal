# Oracle Invokes Force Majeure on the 2.45GW “Project Jupiter” AI Data Center

Oracle sent a force majeure notice to a Blue Owl Capital unit tied to Project Jupiter, a roughly 2.45GW New Mexico campus, seeking protection from payment obligations if the site misses its 2028 start. Oracle is reportedly not abandoning the project and remains anchor tenant. Oracle stock fell about 4% on the news. This is the first major hyperscale AI commitment to be publicly tested against its own contractual escape hatch — read contracted backlog and funded backlog as separate diligence line items.
