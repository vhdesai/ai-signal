# AutoScheduler Launches a Warehouse App Builder for Logistics Teams

AutoScheduler released a low-code app builder that lets warehouse and logistics operations teams assemble their own AI-driven scheduling and orchestration applications. The launch targets supply-chain operators who want to configure workflows without waiting on vendor engineering cycles. It fits the broader September pattern of multi-agent execution moving into physical operations rather than knowledge work alone.
