# OpenAI says Astra crosses its critical cybersecurity threshold

OpenAI disclosed that its forthcoming Astra model is the first to meet the critical cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework. The finding triggered stronger access controls, monitoring, behavior constraints, and a restricted initial release. The disclosure raises the stakes for frontier-model launch governance because the model is useful enough for defenders and potentially dangerous enough for offensive misuse.
