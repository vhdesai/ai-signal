# Banks warn AI shopping bots raise scam, fraud, and privacy risks

Reuters reported that banks including NatWest, Bank of America, ING, ASB Bank, and Capital One warned that AI shopping agents could increase scams, fraud, and data-privacy exposure. Their report highlights risks such as agents entering card details into websites, steering users toward weaker payment protections, and leaving customers uncertain about recourse when purchases go wrong. The practical implication is that agentic commerce will need disclosure, interoperability, payment-protection standards, and clearer liability before mainstream financial institutions are comfortable scaling it.
