# Alibaba launches Qwen Audio 3.1 with up to 95% audio-AI price cuts

Alibaba launched Qwen Audio 3.1 with new models and cut AI audio prices by up to 95% — a savage price move that pressures Meta's Muse Voice Transcribe, xAI's Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0, ElevenLabs Scribe v2, and OpenAI's GPT-Realtime-Whisper. Combined with the Zhenwu V900 chip and the 10T-parameter Qwen roadmap unveiled at Apsara, Alibaba is executing a full-stack commercial and technical push to define the "affordable frontier" tier for global enterprise buyers.
