# Jensen Huang: Nvidia chip sales to double in 2027

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told analysts the company expects chip sales to double next year, forecasting roughly 70% revenue growth in 2027 and calling Nvidia "the world's first and only growth value stock." Barron's flagged the stock as undervalued on that outlook. Combined with reported Nvidia participation in Anthropic's IPO at up to $10B and the Palantir/Nebius partnership, the guidance runs directly against WSJ Markets A.M.'s data-center-buildout-limits argument and the software-vs-chips market split.
