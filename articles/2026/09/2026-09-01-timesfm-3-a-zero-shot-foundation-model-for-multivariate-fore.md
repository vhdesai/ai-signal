# TimesFM-3: A zero-shot foundation model for multivariate forecasting

Google Research introduced TimesFM-3, a 330-million-parameter time-series foundation model pretrained on more than 1 trillion time points. The main advance is native multivariate forecasting: the model can incorporate multiple targets, historical covariates, and known future events without task-specific fine-tuning. This matters for enterprise forecasting workloads in retail, finance, observability, manufacturing, healthcare, and operations planning. MODEL RELEASEANTHROPICSAFETY
