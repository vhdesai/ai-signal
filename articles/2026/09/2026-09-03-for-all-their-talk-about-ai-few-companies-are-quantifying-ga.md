# For all their talk about AI, few companies are quantifying gains

Yahoo Finance reports that despite broad corporate AI messaging, relatively few companies are quantifying realized productivity or financial gains. The signal for technology leaders is that AI programs are entering a more disciplined measurement phase. Executive scrutiny is likely to shift from pilot volume and model access toward business cases, baseline metrics, and repeatable operating leverage.
