# OpenAI's "recurrent depth" reasoning technique draws safety objections

OpenAI's Astra model uses a technique described as "recurrent depth," which allows the model to reason outside the sequential, token-by-token chain that characterizes most current reasoning systems. Safety researchers have objected that this reduces the legibility of the model's intermediate reasoning, weakening chain-of-thought monitoring as an oversight mechanism.
