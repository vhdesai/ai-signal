# Nvidia's disclosed equity portfolio reaches $99B

CNBC reported that Nvidia's equity investments have risen to $99B, up from roughly $7B a year earlier, as the company finances companies across the AI stack. The strategy makes Nvidia both supplier and capital partner to labs, neoclouds, photonics firms, and infrastructure developers. For enterprise buyers, Nvidia exposure is becoming a concentration-risk question as much as a procurement question.
