# Buildots Raises $130M, With Intel Capital Participating, on AI Data-Centre Construction Demand

“Artificial intelligence-powered construction management startup Buildots Ltd. said today it has raised $130 million in new late-stage funding,” bringing total capital raised to $297 million. O.G. Venture Partners led, with Intel Capital, Lightspeed, Qumra and Viola Growth participating. Demand is “driven in part by the rapid construction of artificial intelligence data centers, where delays can postpone the availability of costly computing infrastructure.” A useful second-order indicator: capital is now flowing to the schedule-risk layer of the buildout, not just to chips and power.
