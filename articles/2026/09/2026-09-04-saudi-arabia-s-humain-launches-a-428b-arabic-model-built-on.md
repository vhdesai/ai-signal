# Saudi Arabia’s HUMAIN launches a 428B Arabic model built on China’s MiniMax

PIF-backed HUMAIN unveiled humain-m3, a 428B-parameter MoE model commissioned from Chinese lab MiniMax and further pre-trained on more than one trillion tokens of Arabic-native text. HUMAIN reports the highest average score among frontier systems across seven public Arabic benchmarks, with a research preview live and open weights planned. A Gulf sovereign AI program building on a Chinese open base is a meaningful signal about how U.S. allies may assemble independent AI stacks.
