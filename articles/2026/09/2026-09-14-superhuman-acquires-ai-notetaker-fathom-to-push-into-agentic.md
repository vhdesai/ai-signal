# Superhuman acquires AI notetaker Fathom to push into agentic work

Superhuman is acquiring Fathom, a Y Combinator-backed AI meeting notetaker with 400,000+ monthly active users, rather than building its own. Fathom had raised over $30M and was valued at $94M in 2024 per PitchBook, backed by the Zoom Apps Fund and Steve Huffman. CEO Shishir Mehrotra said internal testing showed how hard notetaking is to build well, and teased real-time meeting context feeding into agentic workflows — a template for horizontal-productivity vendors buying meeting AI as the connective tissue for agents.
