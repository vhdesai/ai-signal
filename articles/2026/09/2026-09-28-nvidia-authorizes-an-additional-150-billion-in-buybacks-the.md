# Nvidia Authorizes an Additional $150 Billion in Buybacks — the Largest Increase on Record

Nvidia’s board authorized another $150 billion under its existing repurchase program, lifting remaining authorization to $235 billion, to be executed through fiscal 2028. The increase exceeds Apple’s $110 billion authorization from May 2024. Nvidia generated $74.4 billion in operating cash flow in the first half of fiscal 2027 and repurchased roughly $39.8 billion of stock over that period, alongside $366 billion in disclosed future commitments for supply, capacity, cloud and equity investments. Read it as a capital-allocation signal from the company with the clearest view of AI demand.
