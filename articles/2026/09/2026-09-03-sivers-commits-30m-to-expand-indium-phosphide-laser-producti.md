# Sivers Commits $30M to Expand Indium Phosphide Laser Production in Glasgow

Sivers Semiconductors said it will invest $30 million to expand its Indium Phosphide manufacturing facility in Glasgow, Scotland, in anticipation of AI data-center optical demand. InP lasers underpin the high-bandwidth optical links that current interconnect roadmaps assume.
