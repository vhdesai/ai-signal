# Anthropic proposes metrics to track the pace of frontier AI development

Anthropic published measurement tools intended to show how quickly frontier AI is advancing inside labs, including how much AI is contributing to AI R&D, how well agents are overseen, and what compute resources are being allocated. The company said Claude currently leads 26% of measured Anthropic AI R&D work and collaborates on more than 90%.
