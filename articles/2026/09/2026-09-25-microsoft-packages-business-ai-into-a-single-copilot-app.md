# Microsoft packages business AI into a single Copilot app

Microsoft introduced an updated Copilot app that combines coding, complex productivity tasks, and custom-agent creation as it tries to improve enterprise AI adoption. CNBC reported that fewer than 7% of Microsoft’s more than 450 million commercial Office 365 seats currently have licenses for the AI add-on, and that the new Cowork, Code, and Autopilot features will use consumption-style pricing. The move is a strategic response to simpler agent bundles from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and others. VIBE CODINGREVENUE
