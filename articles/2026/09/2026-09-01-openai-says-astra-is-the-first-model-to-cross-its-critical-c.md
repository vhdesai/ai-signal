# OpenAI says Astra is the first model to cross its Critical cybersecurity threshold

OpenAI published its safeguards plan for Astra, disclosing that the model is the first to meet the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold under the company's Preparedness Framework — meaning it can find previously unknown security flaws and exploit them without human guidance. OpenAI detailed the stronger release-time safeguards and access controls it is applying as a precondition of shipping. This follows OpenAI's August disclosure that it had slowed parts of Astra's development over these same concerns, and it sets a precedent other labs will be measured against. https://openai.com/index/path-to-astra/
