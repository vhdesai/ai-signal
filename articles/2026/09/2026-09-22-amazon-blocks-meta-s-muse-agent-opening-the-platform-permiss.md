# Amazon Blocks Meta's Muse Agent, Opening the Platform-Permission Fight

Amazon cut off Meta's Muse personal AI agent from shopping on Amazon.com, twelve days after Muse launched, citing its Conditions of Use after Meta declined a request to exclude the site voluntarily. Amazon's stated objections are disclosure, non-identification of the agent while browsing, and alleged capture and storage of customer credentials; Meta says Muse has no visibility into passwords or payment methods. The legal framing matters more than the dispute: after the Ninth Circuit vacated Amazon's injunction against Perplexity in August on the reasoning that the user, not the AI firm, accesses the site, Amazon has shifted from anti-hacking claims to contract enforcement. Enterprises deploying customer-facing agents should assume terms-of-service, not technical capability, will be the binding constraint.
