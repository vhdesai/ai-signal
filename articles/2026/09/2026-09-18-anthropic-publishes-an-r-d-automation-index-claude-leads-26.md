# Anthropic publishes an R&D Automation Index: Claude "leads" 26% of its own model development

Anthropic's Institute released three prototype measurements of internal AI development pace. As of August 2026, Claude "leads" 26% of the company's AI R&D work on Epoch AI's AL0–AL5 automation scale, up from under 1% in February, with more than 90% at "collaborates" or above and nothing measured as fully autonomous. A second index reports roughly 30,000 concurrent internal agents, with online monitors blocking about one action in 47,000 across more than a billion decisions; a third puts about 6% of R&D compute on safety work. The ratings were largely produced by Claude itself and no part has been externally audited.
