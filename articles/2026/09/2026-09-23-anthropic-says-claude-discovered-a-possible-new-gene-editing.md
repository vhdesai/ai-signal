# Anthropic says Claude discovered a possible new gene-editing tool

Anthropic reported that Claude surfaced a previously uncharacterized bacteriophage system it calls array-associated reverse transcriptases — a reverse transcriptase paired with a CRISPR-like non-coding repeat array. Roughly 950 agents consumed about 210M tokens over 21 hours to screen more than 200,000 reverse transcriptases and write up 20 candidates for human review at Anthropic's Bay Area wet lab. Dario Amodei publicly cautioned that the system's "precise function, biotechnological utility (if any), or level of significance" is not yet established. It is the first notable output of the biotech research group Anthropic stood up in the spring, and the most concrete claim to date that frontier models can run industrial-scale hypothesis generation in biology.
