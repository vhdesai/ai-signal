# Mapping global methane emissions from space with deep learning

Google Research published work on mapping global methane emissions from space using deep learning, in collaboration with NASA JPL contributors. Methane detection is a high-value climate and industrial monitoring problem because emissions are uneven, time-sensitive, and hard to attribute. The work shows how AI applied to satellite data can turn environmental sensing into operational intelligence for regulators, energy firms, and climate-risk teams.
