# OpenAI Ships Astra for Law with a 230-Million-URL Legal Search Index

Astra for Law is not a new model but GPT-6 Astra wrapped in a purpose-built US legal search index covering case law, statutes, regulations, court rules, and administrative decisions, plus instructions tuned for legal analysis. On OpenAI's own testing against 200 questions from Vals AI's Legal Research Bench, it passed the overall correctness check on 54.0% versus 38.7% for GPT-6 Astra with web search — a 40% relative gain that still leaves nearly half the questions failed. Twenty-six partner plugins launched alongside it (Relativity, Clio, iManage, Intapp, DeepJudge), with Thomson Reuters previewing a CoCounsel connector.
