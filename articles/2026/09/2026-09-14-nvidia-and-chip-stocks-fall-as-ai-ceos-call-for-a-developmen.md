# Nvidia and chip stocks fall as AI CEOs call for a development slowdown

Nvidia fell roughly 2% pre-market Monday, with Broadcom down 3%, Intel 5%, and Marvell 7%, after Dario Amodei’s weekend essay urging AI companies to pull back on their most powerful models. Nasdaq-100 futures were off more than 1.5%, the Kospi fell 3.26%, and the Nikkei closed down 0.81%. Sam Altman’s separate statement that an OpenAI IPO this year would be “ill-advised” compounded the uncertainty, given Nvidia’s $30B OpenAI position and up to $10B committed to Anthropic. Oil above $103 WTI on a Saudi pipeline closure added a second macro pressure point. O SIGNAL
