# Alibaba's Qwen-Audio 3.1 cuts voice API pricing by up to 95%

Alibaba's Qwen team released a five-model audio stack — upgraded ASR, TTS and Realtime plus new ASR-Next and TTS-Next — and cut voice API pricing by up to 95% for speech recognition, roughly 70% for text-to-speech and about 85% for real-time voice. ASR-Next adds multi-speaker identification with timestamps, emotion detection and ambient-noise captioning; TTS-Next generates voice, effects and background audio in a single pass. The models shipped on September 23; Alibaba published percentages rather than per-unit prices and gave no effective date, so the durable signal is the direction, not the arithmetic.
