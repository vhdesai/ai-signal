# OpenAI urges binding global safety standards for frontier AI

OpenAI published a proposal calling for the U.S. government to develop binding global safety and security standards for frontier AI systems. The proposal calls for shared benchmarks for self-improving AI systems and protected pathways for exchanging information about emerging threats, with U.S. standards bodies coordinating with peer AI safety institutes in the U.K., France, and Singapore. The shift is notable because OpenAI is moving from voluntary frameworks toward support for government-backed standards as frontier-agent incidents raise pressure for oversight.
