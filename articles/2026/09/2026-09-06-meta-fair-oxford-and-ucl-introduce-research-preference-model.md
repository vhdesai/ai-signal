# Meta FAIR, Oxford and UCL introduce Research Preference Models that rank experiments before spending GPU hours

Researchers from Meta FAIR, the University of Oxford and University College London introduce Research Preference Models — frozen pretrained LLMs that rank an AI research agent’s unexecuted candidate experiments so only the most promising one is actually run. On AIRS-Bench, average normalized score rises from 0.684 for a random pick to 0.711 for an inference-only judge and 0.729 for an agentic judge that runs short pilots, reaching the baseline’s 24-hour score in roughly 15 hours — a 1.5–1.6× speedup. The team reports new state of the art on WinoGrande (94.1%) and SVAMP (95.7%), with the AIRA-dojo scaffold and benchmark open-sourced. Underlying paper: arXiv:2608.13940.
