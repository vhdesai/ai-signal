# OpenAI's Luna price cut drove a 10x usage surge, taking OpenRouter share from Anthropic

At Goldman Sachs Communacopia, OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar described how a price cut on OpenAI's GPT 5.6 Luna model led to a tenfold increase in usage and pushed OpenAI to the highest market share on OpenRouter, more than offsetting the price reduction. Luna's earlier lead over Anthropic's Opus 5 and Sonnet 5 on OpenRouter is now widening. Anthropic responded by pricing its new Fable 5.1 model 25% below Fable 5 for typical workloads. A pricing war between the two revenue-leading labs is now visible in real per-token usage data. URL: The Information Briefing
