# OpenAI launches AI program to support independent journalism in Ukraine

OpenAI, together with the Association of Independent Regional Publishers of Ukraine (AIRPPU) and WAN-IFRA, announced a program to help Ukrainian news organizations use AI to strengthen innovation, resilience, and independent journalism in wartime conditions. The initiative pairs training and tooling with local publisher operations rather than promising a broad revenue-sharing deal. Coming amid the wave of publisher lawsuits against OpenAI, it is also a positioning move around OpenAI's relationship with the news industry. EARNINGS
