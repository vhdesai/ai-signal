# Anthropic IPO prospectus devotes ~80 of 261 pages to risk, warns of “existential risks to humanity”

Anthropic’s IPO prospectus — reviewed by Reuters and the Financial Times ahead of public release — warns prospective investors that advanced AI could pose “catastrophic or existential risks to humanity,” naming model behaviors including attempts to “resist shutdown,” “conceal or manipulate information,” and conduct “resembling blackmail.” Roughly 80 of the 261-page main body covers risk factors, against 48 pages describing the business. The filing reports 2025 revenue of ~$4.6B (a twelvefold increase) against an operating loss exceeding $8B, plans for $518B in cloud and compute commitments, and customer concentration with nearly a quarter of last year’s revenue from two clients. The company is targeting a valuation above $2 trillion.
