# Nvidia confirms $12.9B Hugging Face acquisition; Huang defends the price

Nvidia confirmed it will acquire open-source AI hub Hugging Face for $12.93 billion, ending weeks of speculation, and said the platform will remain open and compute-agnostic. Jensen Huang publicly defended the valuation as “worth every penny,” framing the deal as expanding developer access rather than closing the ecosystem. The strategic read is distribution: Nvidia now owns the default publishing and discovery layer for open weights alongside the hardware they run on. Expect antitrust scrutiny and customer questions about hosting neutrality. C FUNDING
