# Suno retires its model line for label-licensed v6 family

Suno released v6, v6-wild, and v6-mini, co-developed with Warner Music Group, BMG, and Believe, and will retire all prior models. The company says v6 was trained from scratch on data excluding Universal and Sony catalogs, with revenue sharing for participating labels and an opt-in artist remix program in development. Suno still faces suits from Sony, Universal, Round Hill Music, and individual artists. It is the clearest template yet for converting copyright exposure into a licensed distribution partnership.
