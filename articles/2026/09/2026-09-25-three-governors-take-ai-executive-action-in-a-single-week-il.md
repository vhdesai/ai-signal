# Three governors take AI executive action in a single week; Illinois creates an AI Cabinet

Illinois Governor Pritzker signed an executive order creating an unpaid expert AI Cabinet to formulate policy proposals through 2027, with a remit spanning data-center incentives tied to safety and transparency standards, grid strain, state procurement, and accountability mechanisms. In the same week Oregon issued standards for third-party AI safety review plus a feasibility study of a frontier-model “kill switch,” and California moved to accelerate independent oversight of AI companies. Trackers now count 85 new AI-related laws enacted across 27 states in 2026, with six legislatures still in session — a compliance surface that keeps expanding.
