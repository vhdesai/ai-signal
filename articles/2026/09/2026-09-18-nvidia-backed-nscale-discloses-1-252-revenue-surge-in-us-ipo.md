# Nvidia-Backed Nscale Discloses 1,252% Revenue Surge in US IPO Filing

The London-based neocloud posted revenue of $140.6M for the six months ended June 30, up from $10.4M a year earlier, against a net loss of $1.02B. Nscale is targeting a valuation near $30B and reports more than $103B in total contracted value, anchored by Anthropic's $45B deal to rent capacity from its West Virginia campus. The filing also discloses $3.1B in convertible bonds sold this week, including $1B to Nvidia.
