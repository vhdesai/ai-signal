# Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash Undercuts Gemini 3.8 Flash on Multimodal Pricing

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, described as its first multimodal model purpose-built for agents, processing audio and video jointly with tool use and a 1-million-token context window. API pricing is $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.47 per million output — roughly a fifth of Gemini 3.8 Flash's introductory $0.75/$3.75, which Google is scheduled to double on January 1, 2027. Alibaba estimates audio input at under $0.01 per hour and 720p video with audio at about $0.20 per hour at one frame per second. Performance parity with Gemini on audio-video tasks rests on Qwen's own benchmarks and has not been independently verified; the release ships alongside open-source Qwen-MM-Plugins compatible with Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Qwen Code.
