# Obama Urges Democrats to Have a ‘Clear Plan’ for AI Safeguards

Former President Barack Obama said Democrats must make AI a “central agenda” and “have a very clear plan,” per NYT reporting of a Thursday fundraising-event interview with House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries. He warned: “This is something that is moving very fast in private hands, and if we don't get on top of it, I think can be dangerous.” Obama has offered himself as a “sounding board” and has spoken with both Amodei and Altman. The piece also quotes Trump from Sunday — “we're leading China in AI... whoever wins AI wins” — making AI policy an explicit partisan dividing line heading into the cycle.
