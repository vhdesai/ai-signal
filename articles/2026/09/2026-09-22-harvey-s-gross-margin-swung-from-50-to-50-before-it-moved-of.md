# Harvey's gross margin swung from +50% to −50% before it moved off frontier APIs

Bloomberg reported that the legal AI company, valued at about $15.6 billion, watched gross margins collapse after a March agent update drove roughly a twentyfold increase in token usage against flat per-seat pricing. Harvey's response was not repricing but model substitution: an in-house model post-trained on Moonshot AI's open-weight Kimi K3, launched in August, after which margins returned to positive territory. Harvey's own engineering write-up claims roughly 90% cost reduction per query on some workloads. Abridge, Ramp, Decagon and Rogo are reported to be weighing similar open-weight pivots — the first coherent wave of enterprise AI vendors leaving closed-model dependence for cost rather than capability reasons.
