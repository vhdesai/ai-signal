# Gemini 4 is in post-training, and Google wants it out "much earlier" than year-end

Google DeepMind chief Koray Kavukcuoglu — in his first media appearance since taking over from Demis Hassabis in the August reshuffle — said Gemini 4 has entered post-training and is already being tested inside Google's Antigravity coding tool, with intent to "release an early post-training output" as soon as possible. No date was given. The pressure is visible: Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 5.5 on 22 September and OpenAI halved GPT-6 pricing with Sol and Luna the same day, while Google's most capable shipping model remains a Flash-tier release.
