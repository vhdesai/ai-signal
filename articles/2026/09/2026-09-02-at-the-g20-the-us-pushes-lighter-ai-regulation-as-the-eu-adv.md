# At the G20, the US pushes lighter AI regulation as the EU advances new law

At the G20 innovation ministerial, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick pitched international adoption of the US AI stack and pressed for accelerated data-center buildout, while the EU continued advancing its own binding rules. Al Jazeera reported the same day on the widening transatlantic split between a lighter-touch US posture and the EU’s legislative track.
