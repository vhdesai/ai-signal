# Anthropic Commits $11.6B to Akamai for CPU Capacity, Takes a Warrant for Up to 5% of the Company

Akamai announced a seven-year, $11.6B contractual commitment from Anthropic, expandable by a further $9B to a ceiling near $20B. Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for 7.7M shares (~5% of common stock on an as-converted basis) at $111.33, with roughly 2% vesting on the initial commitment and ~1% more per additional $3B purchased. Crucially, the capacity is CPU, not GPU — aimed at Anthropic’s agentic orchestration workloads. Akamai expects ~$5.5B in related capex, including $1.7B in 2026 partly to pre-purchase memory. The deal signals that the emerging agent-runtime economy needs a distinct compute layer from training, and it establishes a template for capacity commitments backed by equity upside instead of pure take-or-pay contracts.
