# Nvidia partner Hon Hai (Foxconn) posts 52% August sales jump on AI-server demand

Foxconn/Hon Hai reported August sales up 52% year-on-year, driven by AI-server demand and continued cloud infrastructure buildout, beating expectations. The result is a clean read-through on sustained momentum in the Nvidia-centric AI server supply chain. European operators are also reported to be lining up their own orders.
