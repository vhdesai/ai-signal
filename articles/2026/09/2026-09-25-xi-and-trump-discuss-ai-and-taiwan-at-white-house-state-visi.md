# Xi and Trump discuss AI and Taiwan at White House state visit

Xi Jinping used his White House meeting with Donald Trump to press for AI cooperation rather than containment, telling Trump "the right thing to do is to draw on each other's strengths, not guard against each other" while backing continued dialogue on risks, joint misuse prevention, and keeping AI under human control. The state dinner seated US tech titans alongside both presidents — a signal, analysts told SCMP, that Washington now views the bilateral relationship "largely through the prism of technology." Concrete deliverables were absent, but the framing sets the terrain for the broader trade-war truce extension.
