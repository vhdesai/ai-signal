# The Information: Confusion reigns over the White House's AI whitelist

The Information reports growing confusion inside the tech industry over the scope and criteria of the White House's still-unfinalized AI "whitelist" — a policy vehicle intended to designate approved models and providers for sensitive government workloads. Vendors and legal teams are unclear on eligibility, review cadence, and whether inclusion carries export-control or procurement consequences. The uncertainty matters because a whitelist that lacks clear rules functions less as a safety framework than as a distributive market advantage.
