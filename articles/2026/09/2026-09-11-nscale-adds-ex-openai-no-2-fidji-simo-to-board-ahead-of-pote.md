# Nscale adds ex-OpenAI No. 2 Fidji Simo to board ahead of potential IPO

Nscale, the AI compute provider that recently struck a $45B deal with Anthropic and is exploring $3.5B in pre-IPO financing, added Fidji Simo — until recently OpenAI's No. 2 and previously the CEO who led Instacart through its 2023 IPO — to its board. The appointment is a strong operational signal that Nscale intends to move to public markets and that alignment with the OpenAI ecosystem is being formalized at the governance layer. For enterprise buyers, this makes Nscale a more credible Nvidia-adjacent long-term supplier. FUNDING
