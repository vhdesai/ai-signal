# Startup ARR is less secure than ever as enterprises constantly reassess AI vendors

TechCrunch reported on new Madrona research showing that 74% of surveyed enterprise IT professionals plan to expand AI budgets, but fewer than half of AI pilots reach production and 77% of enterprises reevaluate AI vendors every six months or continuously. This creates a different revenue profile from traditional SaaS, where multi-year contracts and workflow lock-in historically protected ARR. The implication for AI vendors is that enterprise adoption can grow while revenue durability weakens unless pricing and outcomes become clearer. Infrastructure AI CLOUDFUNDING
