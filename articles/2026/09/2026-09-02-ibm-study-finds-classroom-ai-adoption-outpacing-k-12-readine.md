# IBM study finds classroom AI adoption outpacing K-12 readiness

IBM published Morning Consult research covering more than 2,000 educators and parents, finding that 76% of middle-school and 73% of high-school educators report at least weekly classroom AI use, while only 20% of educators have received extensive AI training. IBM also launched a K-12 AI leaders fellowship for superintendents and district leaders to support responsible adoption. Note: sourced from a paid press release.
