# Princeton Researcher Proposes the Recurrent Looped Transformer (RLT)

Princeton's Yifan Zhang proposes a transformer variant that carries decoder state across every token, so that “each token therefore executes 96 logical blocks” — an architectural bet on depth-through-recurrence rather than parameter scale. The proposal is worth tracking as a direction, but read it with the author's own caveat in view: the write-up “explicitly reports no measured efficiency, reasoning quality, or scaling results.” Treat as a design paper pending empirical validation, not a demonstrated advance.
