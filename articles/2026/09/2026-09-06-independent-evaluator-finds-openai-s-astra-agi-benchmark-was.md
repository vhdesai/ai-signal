# Independent evaluator finds OpenAI's Astra AGI benchmark was harness-dependent

ARC Prize scored GPT-6 Astra at 62.7% on ARC-AGI-3 using its own independent evaluation harness, versus the 99.9% OpenAI reported using its own harness, and OpenAI subsequently revised five metrics after launch. The gap illustrates how much of a headline frontier-model benchmark result is attributable to scaffolding rather than the underlying model itself. Technology leaders should treat vendor-reported benchmark deltas as harness-dependent until independently reproduced.
