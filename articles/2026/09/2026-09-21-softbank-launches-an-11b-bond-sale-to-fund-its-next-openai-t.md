# SoftBank launches an ~$11B bond sale to fund its next OpenAI tranche

SoftBank Group launched $10 billion of dollar-denominated and €1 billion of euro-denominated senior unsecured notes, with dollar tranches at 3.5, 5.5 and 7.5 years and euro tranches at 4 and 6 years. Proceeds fund SoftBank's $10 billion payment for the third tranche of its follow-on OpenAI investment, expected to close October 1, and cancel a prior $10 billion bridge facility. At the planned size it would be the largest non-financial corporate bond deal on record from Asia-Pacific and Japan. Pricing is set for September 24 with settlement September 29; Fitch assigned a BB+ rating and expects leverage to rise as commitments are funded. ANALYSIS UNIT ECONOMICS
