# PitchBook: VC evergreen funds jump from $3B to $20B+ on AI and IPO tailwinds; AI = 50% of Greater China VC deal value

PitchBook's Q3 US Evergreen Fund Landscape shows VC evergreen fund AUM has jumped from ~$3B in 2024 to more than $20B now, powered by strong AI markets and this year's record-setting IPOs. Separately PitchBook's 2026 Greater China Private Capital Breakdown shows AI now accounts for half of Greater China's VC deal value. Its 2026 Sustainable Investment Survey finds 45% of ESG investors think AI will be a net positive by solving data problems, 27% think it will be net-negative on energy/water demands, and 28% haven't formed a view. Emily Zheng of PitchBook calls Anthropic and OpenAI stakes "some of the most contested real estate in venture."
