# Anthropic Shifts Planned IPO to November as Revenue Run-Rate Heads Past $100B

Anthropic has moved its initial public offering from an expected October debut to November, per people familiar with the matter. The later date allows the company to present third-quarter financials following OpenAI's September release of GPT-6 Astra; sources indicated the shift was decided before the current industry debate over development pace. Separately, Bloomberg reported (citing The New York Times) that Anthropic's annualized revenue is expected to exceed $100B this year, up from a $65B run-rate at the end of July and $9B at the end of 2025. Bankers have discussed a valuation near $2 trillion and a raise of up to $100 billion.
