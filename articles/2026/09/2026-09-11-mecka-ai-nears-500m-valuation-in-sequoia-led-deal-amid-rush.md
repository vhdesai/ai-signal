# Mecka AI nears $500M valuation in Sequoia-led deal amid rush for robot training data

Mecka AI, which collects and analyzes human motion data to train humanoid and other robotics systems, is nearing a round led by Sequoia Capital at a valuation of about $500 million. Human-motion datasets are becoming the scarce input for physical AI — the robotics analogue of the text-data land grab that preceded the current LLM generation. Expect data-rights questions in robotics to follow the same trajectory they took in language.
