# Anthropic Ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, Cutting Agentic Workload Costs Up to 45%

Anthropic released two models: Fable 5.1, broadly available, and Mythos 5.1, restricted to organizations vetted through its Cyber Verification and Life Sciences Verification programs. Anthropic reports comparable or better performance than the prior generation at lower reasoning settings, with up to 45% lower cost on some highly agentic workloads alongside reduced cache-read pricing. The tiering is the strategic signal: broadly available capability separated from controlled frontier capability, gated by customer verification rather than price. https://sdtimes.com/claude-fable-5-1/61089/
