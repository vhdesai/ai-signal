# OpenAI ships Astra for Law, a GPT-6 configuration for legal work

Astra for Law is not a new model: OpenAI wrapped GPT-6 Astra in a legal search index spanning more than 230 million URLs of US case law, statutes, regulations, and administrative decisions — sourced in part from the Free Law Project's CourtListener — plus instructions tuned for legal analysis and drafting. On 200 questions from the private validation set of Vals AI's Legal Research Bench, it passed the overall correctness check on 54.0% versus 38.7% for GPT-6 Astra with web search alone, a 40% relative improvement on OpenAI's own testing. Access is limited to selected firms via a Trusted Access program in ChatGPT and Codex, with zero data retention on the API and Harvey and Legora named as API customers. Twenty-six partner plugins, nine community plugins, and general availability of ChatGPT for Word shipped alongside. - https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/17/openai-launches-astra-for-law-a-gpt-6-configuration-for-legal-research/ - https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law
