# Anthropic rolls out "Enterprise Frontier Safeguards"

Anthropic introduced a new enterprise safeguards package aimed at giving corporate customers more control over model behavior, data handling, and misuse monitoring in deployed Claude instances. The announcement was corroborated by Help Net Security's coverage of the rollout, framing it as part of a broader industry push to make frontier models more auditable for regulated customers.
