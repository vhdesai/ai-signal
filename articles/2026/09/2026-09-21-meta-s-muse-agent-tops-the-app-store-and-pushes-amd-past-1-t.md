# Meta's Muse Agent Tops the App Store and Pushes AMD Past $1 Trillion

Meta rallied 6.5% on Monday, lifting the Nasdaq 100 by 2.15%, after its Muse consumer agent reached the top of Apple's US free app chart 13 days post-launch. CPU-linked semiconductor names moved harder than the GPU complex: Arm gained roughly 15%, Intel 13%, and AMD 9%, taking AMD across a $1 trillion market capitalization for the first time. The thesis pushed to clients is that autonomous agents run continuous background routines and orchestration loops that are materially more CPU-intensive than one-shot generative workloads. Wells Fargo raised its Meta price target from $640 to $796 on SensorTower download data. FUNDING
