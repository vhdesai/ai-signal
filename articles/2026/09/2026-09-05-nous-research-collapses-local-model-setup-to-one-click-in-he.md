# Nous Research Collapses Local Model Setup to One Click in Hermes Desktop

Hermes Desktop now reads local hardware, fit-checks its model catalog against the available GPU, selects the highest-quality build that fits, downloads it, and configures llama.cpp automatically. The defaults are opinionated — a hard 4-bit quantization floor and a 64K minimum context window — which removes the most common source of poor local-model experiences. This continues a clear near-term trend toward pushing routine inference onto endpoint hardware and off metered cloud tokens. Academic Research RESEARCH
