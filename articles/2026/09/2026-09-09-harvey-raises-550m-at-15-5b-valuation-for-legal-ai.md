# Harvey raises $550M at $15.5B valuation for legal AI

Harvey announced a $550 million round at a $15.5 billion valuation, co-led by Diffusion and Lightspeed, with Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, a16z, Coatue, GIC, and Goldman Sachs Alternatives participating. The raise follows Harvey Tenet, its first post-trained open-weight model, and LAB, its open-sourced legal agent benchmark. The company reports 80% of Am Law 100 firms and five of the Fortune 10 among users, up from an $11 billion valuation in March.
