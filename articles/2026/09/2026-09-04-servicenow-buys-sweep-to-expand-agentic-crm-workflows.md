# ServiceNow buys Sweep to expand agentic CRM workflows

ServiceNow acquired Israeli startup Sweep in a transaction reported in the hundreds of millions of dollars. Sweep's agentic workspace monitors CRM metadata across ServiceNow, Salesforce, and HubSpot, surfaces configuration issues, and helps automate documentation and operational cleanup. The acquisition extends ServiceNow's AI-native workflow strategy into revenue operations, where system-of-record quality is often the practical blocker to reliable agents.
