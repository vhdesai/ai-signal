# Alibaba unveils the Zhenwu V900 and a 20 GW compute roadmap

At the Apsara Conference in Hangzhou, CEO Eddie Wu introduced the Zhenwu V900 — which Alibaba calls the most powerful AI chip in China — claiming three times the performance of the M890 released four months earlier, with clusters scaling to 500,000 cards. Mass production is scheduled for Q1 2027, and Alibaba said it expects to operate more than 20 gigawatts of global data center capacity by 2032. On the model side, Qwen 4 is in training and the Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 series are projected at 5–10 trillion parameters, versus 2.4 trillion for today's Qwen 3.8-Max. Shares rose about 3% in Hong Kong; the announcements land days before the Trump–Xi summit. FUNDING INFRA
