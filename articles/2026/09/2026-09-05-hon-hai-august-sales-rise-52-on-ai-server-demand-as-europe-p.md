# Hon Hai August sales rise 52% on AI server demand as Europe places its own orders

Nvidia manufacturing partner Hon Hai (Foxconn) reported a 52% year-over-year increase in August sales, ahead of expectations, driven by AI server and data center infrastructure demand. Coverage also notes a EUR 120M European manufacturing agreement with France’s state-owned Bull, tied to EU AI gigafactory ambitions. Supply-side momentum remains a cleaner read on real AI spend than model announcements — and Europe is beginning to buy sovereign capacity rather than rent it. Products & Tools M A
