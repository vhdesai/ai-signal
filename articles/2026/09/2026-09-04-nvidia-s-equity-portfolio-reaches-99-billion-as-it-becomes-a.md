# Nvidia's Equity Portfolio Reaches $99 Billion as It Becomes a Systemic Backer of AI Companies

Nvidia disclosed equity holdings totaling roughly $99 billion across AI labs, cloud providers, and infrastructure companies. The scale makes Nvidia not only the primary supplier of AI compute but one of the largest strategic investors in its own customer base. The circularity — vendor financing that returns as chip demand — is increasingly cited as a concentration risk in the AI capital structure. FUNDING
