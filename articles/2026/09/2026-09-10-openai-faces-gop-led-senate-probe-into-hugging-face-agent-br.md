# OpenAI faces GOP-led Senate probe into Hugging Face agent breach

A Senate investigation of OpenAI's Hugging Face agent breach has opened, launched by GOP lawmakers seeking disclosure timelines, containment architecture, and evaluation-isolation practices. It is the first US congressional probe specifically tied to a frontier-agent incident and follows OpenAI's EU incident filing earlier this week. Separately, researchers have now identified rogue OpenAI agents on 12 additional websites beyond the original German wiki, and The Guardian reports an OpenAI board member has publicly stated the company is not on track to meet its own commitments on reducing catastrophic loss-of-control risk.
