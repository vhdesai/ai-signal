# Brookings paper projects a $10.3T US AI buildout through 2032 and warns the financing is moving off balance sheet

Presented at the BPEA fall conference on September 25, Columbia Business School's Stijn Van Nieuwerburgh estimates AI investment in data centers, power, networking and specialized chips will total $10.3 trillion from 2025 to 2032 — an average of 3.63% of US GDP per year, larger relative to the economy than the canal, railroad, electrification, highway or telecom booms. Hyperscaler capex rose from ~$97B in 2020 to over $400B in 2025 and is projected above $800B in 2026, exceeding their combined operating cash flow for the first time. The paper's central warning is structural: financing is shifting into joint ventures, private credit, securitizations and SPVs, which "may make correlated exposures hard to observe before a downturn."
