# Travis Kalanick's Atoms is reportedly moving into robotaxis

Citing Financial Times reporting, TechCrunch says Atoms — which raised $1.7 billion led by Andreessen Horowitz earlier this summer — is preparing a hiring push and acquisitions aimed at making it a significant autonomous ride-hailing player. Nothing has been publicly launched; this is attributed reporting rather than a company announcement. If it holds, it adds a well-capitalized third entrant to a market currently framed as Waymo versus Tesla. INDUSTRY
