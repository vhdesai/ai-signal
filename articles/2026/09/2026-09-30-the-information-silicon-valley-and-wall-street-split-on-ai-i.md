# The Information: Silicon Valley and Wall Street Split on AI IPOs — Public Investors Push Anthropic to $1.5T From $2T

The Information reports shifting economic forces are driving a widening divide between Silicon Valley and Wall Street views on tech IPOs, clouding the outlook for trillions invested in AI. Two large public investors said Tuesday that bankers should value Anthropic closer to $1.5T rather than the $2T some bankers have floated. Anthropic is still likely weeks away from an official roadshow. Combined with delays across mid-sized tech IPOs and DealBook’s note that OpenAI is now nearing ~$70B in annualized revenue but shelved its newer Astra model on safety grounds, the pricing conversation is being reframed from "how high?" to "what discount does dilution + disclosure risk deserve?"
