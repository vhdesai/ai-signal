# Reuters: financing the historic AI buildout is raising systemic risks

Reuters reported that financing for the historic AI buildout is raising systemic risks in the U.S., citing researcher concerns. The warning aligns with recent scrutiny of guarantees, off-balance-sheet exposure, and increasingly complex funding structures behind AI data centers and compute capacity. For senior leaders, the infrastructure question is no longer just whether capacity can be built, but how much hidden financial risk is accumulating around that capacity. URL behind paywall.
