# US Pushes Deregulation at G20 Innovation Ministerial as EU Advances New AI Law

At the G20 Innovation Ministerial in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, US officials argued for a looser regulatory approach to AI, emphasizing industry growth and data-center buildout, while the EU moved forward with new legislation. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick pitched international adoption of US AI stacks; Jensen Huang and Sam Altman also participated.
