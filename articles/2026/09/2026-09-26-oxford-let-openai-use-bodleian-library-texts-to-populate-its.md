# Oxford let OpenAI use Bodleian Library texts to populate its training set

Internal Oxford documents obtained by the Guardian through a freedom-of-information request show that Bodleian Library material digitised by OpenAI was used to “populate the OpenAI training set” — a use not stated when the partnership was announced in March 2025. By June 2025 the library had shared 125,000 scans of 19th- and 20th-century doctoral theses. Meeting minutes record staff concerns about reputational risk and the energy intensity of the technology. Oxford says the material is modest in scale, out of copyright, non-exclusive, and will be published openly within months. Oxford is the only UK member of OpenAI's NextGenAI consortium.
