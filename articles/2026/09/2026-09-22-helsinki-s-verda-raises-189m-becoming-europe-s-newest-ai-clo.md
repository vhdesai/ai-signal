# Helsinki's Verda Raises $189M, Becoming Europe's Newest AI Cloud Unicorn

Verda, the Helsinki-founded full-stack AI cloud formerly known as DataCrunch, raised $189 million in an oversubscribed Series B led by Emergence Capital, with MUFG Innovation Partners, Supermicro, Varma, Lifeline Ventures, 6 Degrees Capital, byFounders and Tesi participating. The round takes total funding above $450 million and values the company over $1 billion. Verda reported a $165 million annualized revenue run rate in July and plans to multiply compute capacity over the next year, with inference a named investment priority. The raise is a datapoint on European sovereign-compute demand: Verda operates its own Nordic data centers rather than reselling hyperscaler capacity.
