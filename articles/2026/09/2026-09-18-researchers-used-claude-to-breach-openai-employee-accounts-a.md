# Researchers used Claude to breach OpenAI employee accounts and reach an internal repository in under 72 hours

Three researchers at Hacktron chained a heap-overflow flaw in the libheif image library — used by the Discourse software behind OpenAI's community forum — with an SSO misconfiguration to take over multiple OpenAI employee ChatGPT and Codex accounts, reaching an internal code repository. The work was done under OpenAI's bug bounty in late July and disclosed publicly this week; OpenAI patched the issues and paid $6,500. The researchers noted Claude Opus 4.8 failed to produce a working exploit across several sessions, while Opus 5 succeeded within hours of release. The underlying libheif bug had been fixed upstream months earlier but never received a CVE, which is why the vulnerable version was still in production — a supply-chain hygiene lesson well beyond AI. ________________________________ POLICY
