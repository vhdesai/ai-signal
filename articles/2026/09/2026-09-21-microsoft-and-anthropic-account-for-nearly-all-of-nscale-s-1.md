# Microsoft and Anthropic Account for Nearly All of Nscale’s $103B Backlog

Nscale’s contracted revenue has risen roughly 1,000-fold in about two and a half years, from approximately $100 million to more than $103 billion, driven overwhelmingly by two customers: Microsoft, with some $43.8 billion in total agreements, and Anthropic, with a deal valued near $45 billion for roughly 460 MW at the Monarch campus in West Virginia. The neocloud filed for an NYSE listing under ticker NSCL on September 18 at a target valuation near $30 billion, with Nvidia taking $1 billion of a $3.1 billion convertible note. Concentration is the central risk: the largest single client was 52% of first-half 2026 revenue, against $140.6 million of revenue and a $1.02 billion net loss.
