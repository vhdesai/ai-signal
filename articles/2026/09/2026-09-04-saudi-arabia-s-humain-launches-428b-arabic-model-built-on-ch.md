# Saudi Arabia’s HUMAIN Launches 428B Arabic Model Built on China’s MiniMax

PIF-backed HUMAIN unveiled humain-m3, a 428B-parameter MoE model commissioned from Chinese lab MiniMax and further pre-trained on more than one trillion tokens of Arabic-native text. HUMAIN reports the highest average score among frontier systems across seven public Arabic benchmarks. A research preview is live on HUMAIN Node, with open weights planned under the MiniMax Community License. A Gulf sovereign AI program building its flagship model on a Chinese open base is a meaningful signal about how U.S. allies may assemble independent stacks.
