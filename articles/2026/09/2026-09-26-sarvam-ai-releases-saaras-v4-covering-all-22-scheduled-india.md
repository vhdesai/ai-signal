# Sarvam AI releases Saaras V4, covering all 22 scheduled Indian languages

Sarvam AI launched Saaras V4, an encoder-decoder speech-recognition system covering all 22 scheduled Indian languages plus globally accented English, decoded by the in-house 3B hybrid state-space model Sarvam-3B. It adds keyterm prompting of up to 50 terms, five output modes including verbatim, codemix and translate, and sub-150 ms streaming time-to-first-token. Sarvam reports language-ID error of 2.9% across the top 10 Indian languages and 5.22% across all 22, and claims error rates under half those of Deepgram Nova-3 and GPT-4o Transcribe on noisy Kathbath audio — vendor-reported figures without independent replication. Weights are not public; access is API-only at ₹30 per hour.
