# Sam Altman: An OpenAI IPO in 2026 Would Be “Ill-Advised”

In a Fortune interview, Altman said “We’re not rushing into an IPO. I actually think that given everything happening with safety, right now would be an ill-advised moment to go public,” and, pressed on the year, “I would say not 2026, yeah. We’ve got a lot of stuff to do.” OpenAI has filed confidentially. The Decoder offers a competing reading — that OpenAI’s financials do not currently support a listing, particularly alongside Anthropic’s — and concludes both explanations could be true. Treat the stated safety rationale as one of two plausible readings, not an established one.
