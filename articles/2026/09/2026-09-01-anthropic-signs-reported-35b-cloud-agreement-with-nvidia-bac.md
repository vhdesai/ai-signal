# Anthropic signs reported $35B cloud agreement with Nvidia-backed Lambda

Anthropic has signed an agreement valued at roughly $35 billion with neocloud provider Lambda to secure Nvidia capacity for Claude, according to a Wall Street Journal report. The arrangement centers on Hut 8’s Beacon Point campus in Nueces County, Texas, with Nvidia holding the facility lease and Lambda supplying compute. Term, GPU count, payment schedule and capacity were not disclosed, and the split of contractual obligations among the four parties remains unclear — Lambda separately closed a $926M senior secured term loan on August 27 to fund GPU infrastructure for an unnamed investment-grade offtaker. finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-signs-35-billion-lambda-144113886.html Industry News FUNDING
