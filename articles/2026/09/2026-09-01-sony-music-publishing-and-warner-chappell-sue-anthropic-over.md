# Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic Over Claude Training Data

The two music publishers filed a federal copyright suit alleging Anthropic used tens of thousands of copyrighted songs — including “I Am the Walrus” and “Hallelujah” — to train Claude, seeking up to $150,000 per pirated song. The complaint characterizes the conduct as among the largest ongoing thefts of intellectual property in history. Landing the same week as the $35B Lambda commitment, it reinforces that training-data provenance is a balance-sheet exposure, not merely a compliance matter.
