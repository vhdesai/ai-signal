# AI Investment Figures Reach a New Order of Magnitude

Yahoo Finance argues that the past two weeks of capital commitments have effectively retired the "peak AI" thesis, citing the scale of recent infrastructure and acquisition activity. The piece frames the current cycle as a step-change in absolute dollars rather than a continuation of prior trend growth. Executives should read this alongside the week ahead — fresh inflation data and Oracle earnings will test whether public markets are still willing to underwrite AI capex at these levels.
