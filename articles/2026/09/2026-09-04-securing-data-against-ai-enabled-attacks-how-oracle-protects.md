# Securing data against AI-enabled attacks: How Oracle protects the database estate at the source

Omdia published analysis on Oracle's approach to protecting database estates against AI-enabled attacks. The executive takeaway is that AI security is moving closer to data platforms rather than remaining a perimeter or application-layer concern. As attackers use AI to accelerate reconnaissance and exploitation, database-native controls, privilege governance, and monitoring become more central to enterprise resilience.
