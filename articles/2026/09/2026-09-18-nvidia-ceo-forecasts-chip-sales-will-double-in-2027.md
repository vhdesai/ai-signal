# Nvidia CEO forecasts chip sales will double in 2027

Jensen Huang told analysts NVIDIA expects chip sales to double next year, forecasting roughly 70% revenue growth in 2027 and calling NVIDIA "the world's first and only growth value stock." Barron's separately flagged the stock as undervalued on this outlook. Combined with the $10B Anthropic anchor discussion, the Palantir/Nebius partnership, and CoreWeave's Vera Rubin cluster expansion, Nvidia is running the fullest ecosystem playbook in enterprise tech — even as the Crux AI TPU-collateralized loan validates a merchant alternative. - https://qz.com/jensen-huang-nvidia-chip-sales-double-2027
