# Trump Calls AI Safety a “Hoax,” Announces an “AI Force” and an AI Czar

President Trump posted on Truth Social that AI-safety concerns are a hoax “generated by the Radical Left Dumocrats,” and ran a poll on renaming “Artificial Intelligence” altogether. He said he will stand up an “AI Force” modeled on the Space Force and appoint an AI czar — “Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!” — without specifying duties for either; the prior AI/crypto czar, David Sacks, stepped down earlier this year. The posts land days after Dario Amodei’s “pace the frontier” proposal, which Sam Altman and Elon Musk had both endorsed.
