# Sakana AI hires Jürgen Schmidhuber to lead new Recursive Self-Improvement Lab

Tokyo-based Sakana AI has appointed Jürgen Schmidhuber — LSTM co-inventor and long-time RSI theorist — as Chief Scientific Advisor and head of its new RSI Lab. His 1990s meta-learning and self-referential-machine work already underpins Sakana's Darwin Gödel Machine project. The hire lands the same week OpenAI called for international standards on recursive-self-improvement and gives Sakana one of the field's most durable voices at exactly the moment RSI is becoming a policy focal point.
