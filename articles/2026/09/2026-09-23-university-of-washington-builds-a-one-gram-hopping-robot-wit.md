# University of Washington builds a one-gram hopping robot with precise jump-height control

UW's Autonomous Insect Robotics Lab demonstrated "DirectHop," a grasshopper-inspired robot that modulates jump height on demand, clears a standard stair step, self-rights and reloads for successive hops. Controllable height has been the missing piece for hopping locomotion, which is far more energy-efficient than flight at insect scale. The practical target is low-power inspection and search-and-rescue swarms where drone endurance is the binding constraint.
