# Google Turns CC Into a Shared Household Agent With Its Own Google Account

Google refocused its CC agent on family coordination, giving it a dedicated verified Google account, its own per-member permissions model, and support for up to six members. Each member chooses what to share — specific senders, forwarded mail, Drive folders, calendars — and CC produces a shared "Your Day Ahead" brief, maintains a family calendar and task list, and completes chores such as pre-filling permission slips and registration PDFs. Each instance runs in an isolated cloud environment on Google's Antigravity harness with Gemini 3.8 Flash. US-only Labs waitlist, personal Gmail, 18+.
