# Blackstone: "We have not mapped out who's going to buy all the debt"

Jas Khaira, who heads Blackstone's AI-focused investment team, said the firm has mapped the market's compute needs but not which investors will finance them. He sketched three buckets — investment-grade public debt, private credit, and debt issued by OpenAI and Anthropic once public — adding that the last depends on "their ability to be investment grade over time." Blackstone is both a buildout participant, via a neocloud venture with Google renting TPUs, and a potential buyer of lab debt at the right price.
