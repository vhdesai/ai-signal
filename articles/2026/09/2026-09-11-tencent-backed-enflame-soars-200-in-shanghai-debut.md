# Tencent-Backed Enflame Soars ~200% in Shanghai Debut

Shanghai Enflame Technology jumped about 200% on its STAR Market debut after raising 6.12 billion yuan ($913 million), completing the public listing of all four of China's domestic GPU challengers. The retail tranche was oversubscribed more than 6,000 times. Enflame's 2025 revenue rose 37% to 990 million yuan but it remains loss-making, and Tencent accounted for roughly 84% of sales — concentration risk the debut pricing does not reflect. For Western vendors, the read-through is that China's import-substitution buildout now has public-market funding behind it.
