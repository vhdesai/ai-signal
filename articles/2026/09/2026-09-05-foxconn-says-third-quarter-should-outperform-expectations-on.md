# Foxconn says third quarter should outperform expectations on AI strength

Reuters reported that Foxconn expects third-quarter results to outperform market expectations on AI-related strength. The item matters because AI demand is still flowing through the broader hardware manufacturing chain, not just GPU designers and cloud providers. For executives, supplier commentary remains a useful leading indicator of whether AI infrastructure demand is translating into production orders.
