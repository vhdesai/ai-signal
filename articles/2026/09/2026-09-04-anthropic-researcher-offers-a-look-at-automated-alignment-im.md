# Anthropic researcher offers a look at automated alignment improvement

TechCrunch covered Anthropic research showing automated systems improving model performance on alignment benchmarks by searching literature, proposing methods, training, and iterating. The reported system improved performance across targeted misalignment categories without degrading general capabilities in the tested setting. The promise is faster safety research; the risk is that benchmark quality and monitorability become the limiting factors for automated self-improvement.
