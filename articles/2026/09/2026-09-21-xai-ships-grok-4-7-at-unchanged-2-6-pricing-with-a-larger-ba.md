# xAI Ships Grok 4.7 at Unchanged $2/$6 Pricing With a Larger Base Model

xAI released Grok 4.7 on a new, larger base model with a longer reinforcement-learning run weighted toward multi-hour tasks, holding pricing at $2 per million input and $6 per million output tokens. Vendor-reported benchmarks show gains over Grok 4.6 across every published row — Terminal-Bench 4.0 rose from 20.3% to 38.0%, DeepSWE v1.1 to 71.0%, CursorBench 4.0 to 46.3% — while Fable 5.1 Max still leads four of seven benchmarks. Grok 4.7 tops the table on EEBench (64.0%) and the Harvey Legal Agent Benchmark (19.6%). It is live via the xAI API, Cursor, Grok Build, OpenRouter, Vercel and Cloudflare, with a 500K context window.
