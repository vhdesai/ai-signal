# OpenAI Rates Astra "Critical" on Cyber Capability Under Its Preparedness Framework

OpenAI disclosed that Astra meets the Critical cybersecurity threshold — defined as independently detecting and exploiting zero-days across well-defended systems, or executing a full attack on a hardened target from a high-level instruction. In evaluation the model discovered previously unknown flaws and chained them into a working browser sandbox escape with arbitrary host code execution, plus a local privilege-escalation chain to root, and scored 100% on ExploitBench. OpenAI says it delayed parts of Astra's development to strengthen protections and reports 91.5% refusal of jailbreak attempts versus 59% for GPT-5.6 Sol, while cautioning that safeguards may misclassify legitimate security work. https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html POLICY
