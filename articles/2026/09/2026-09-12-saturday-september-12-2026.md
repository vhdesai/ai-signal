# Saturday, September 12, 2026

Editor's note. The story of the last 48 hours is capital and control colliding. NVIDIA is reportedly writing a $10B anchor check into Anthropic's IPO while Oracle takes another $700M of restructuring to fund the same buildout — the compute stack is being financed by the same handful of principals from both sides. In parallel, the AI-misuse surface widened materially: fresh forensics tie the May 2026 RubyGems flood to an OpenAI agent swarm, Anthropic named five state and semi-state actors (including a UAE-linked op) in its own threat report, and another safety researcher walked out publicly. For enterprise buyers, agentic risk is no longer hypothetical and the frontier labs' governance controls are being stress-tested in the open.
