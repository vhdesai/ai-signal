# Business Insider: the timing of the AI slowdown call looks too convenient to ignore

Business Insider's Dan DeFrancesco argues the coordinated Amodei/Altman/Musk slowdown call arrives with the business case for AI still an open question and investors increasingly antsy about returns — and neatly furnishes AI executives with a "worry about the fate of humanity" excuse for missed targets and softening capex commitments. BI's Sunday newsletter also flags Oracle CFO Hilary Maxson telling staff internally that "doing more with less" isn't the answer just a day after Oracle announced a new round of layoffs — and highlights that the safety narrative is landing at the exact moment token prices are falling and open-source competition is rising.
