# Breaking Hot Simultaneous outage takes down ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini

A Thursday-evening incident knocked out ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini at essentially the same time, with more than 35,000 US reports logged for ChatGPT alone. Reporting attributes the likely common cause to a Microsoft Azure failure, though Microsoft has not officially confirmed a root cause. OpenAI marked the incident resolved and Anthropic reported elevated error rates. The event is a concrete illustration of correlated concentration risk across nominally competing AI providers — a material consideration for enterprise resiliency planning.
