# Rocket Doctor AI reports U.S. operating momentum ahead of town hall

Yahoo Finance carried Rocket Doctor AI's operational update showing 3,625 completed U.S. patient visits and 1,555 clinical hours across July and August. The company positioned the numbers as progress for its AI-powered virtual and hybrid care platform, while also noting reimbursement timing lags and uncertainty for out-of-network visits. The item underscores that healthcare AI businesses still depend on payer integration and revenue-recognition mechanics, not only clinical or model capability. Infrastructure COMPUTE ANTHROPIC
