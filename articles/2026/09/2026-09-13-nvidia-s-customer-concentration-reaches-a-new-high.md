# NVIDIA's Customer Concentration Reaches a New High

NVIDIA's own disclosures now show three customers accounted for 44% of first-half FY2025 revenue; two customers accounted for 36% of full-year FY2024. As recently as FY2023, NVIDIA had zero 10%+ customers. The trajectory explains Jensen Huang's aggressive investment strategy across neoclouds and frontier labs — the company is materially exposed if any one of the top three hyperscaler or lab buyers trims its buildout. It also frames the equity re-rating this week around the Amodei/Altman slowdown call.
