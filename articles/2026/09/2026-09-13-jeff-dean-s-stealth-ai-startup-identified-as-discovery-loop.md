# Jeff Dean's stealth AI startup identified as "Discovery Loop"; ~$50B target

Livemint reports the stealth AI startup being raised by former Google Chief Scientist Jeff Dean has been identified as "Discovery Loop," targeting a ~$50 billion valuation. The naming signals Dean is positioning the company as a scientific-discovery-first competitor to the frontier labs, echoing SSI and Anthropic's disciplined-lab narrative. Business Insider first reported the fundraise on Friday; Discovery Loop would join Sutskever's SSI and Murati's Thinking Machines Lab in a small cohort of star-founder AI companies commanding tens of billions on reputation alone.
