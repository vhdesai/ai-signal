# Seattle Times and Newsday sue Microsoft and OpenAI over training data

The publishers filed a lawsuit alleging their journalism was used without permission to train AI models, seeking damages and destruction of affected datasets and models. These are allegations, not findings of infringement. Microsoft told GeekWire it was surprised by the lawsuit and was open to discussing solutions. PRE-LAUNCH AGENT SAFETY
