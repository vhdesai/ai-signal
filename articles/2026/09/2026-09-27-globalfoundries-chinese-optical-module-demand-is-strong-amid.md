# GlobalFoundries: Chinese optical-module demand is "strong" amid the AI data-center boom

GlobalFoundries' SVP of photonics and RF Shankaran Janardhanan told SCMP demand for Chinese-made optical networking modules — used to move data between server racks and across data-center campuses — is "strong" enough to justify expanding GF's China presence. The signal cuts against the Beltway framing that US chip-adjacent supply is decoupling from China; on the photonics tier, it's the opposite. That matters for anyone modeling AI-infra capex: optical is one of the three bottlenecks Goldman named this weekend.
