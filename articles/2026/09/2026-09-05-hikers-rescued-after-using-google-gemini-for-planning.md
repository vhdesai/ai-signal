# Hikers rescued after using Google Gemini for planning

A sheriff’s office reported that a rescued hiking party “were advised by Gemini to bring far less food and water than their group required.” The episode is minor in isolation but is the kind of consumer-facing reliability failure that shapes regulatory and liability narratives around general-purpose assistants giving consequential real-world advice. Expect this class of incident to feature in duty-of-care arguments as assistants move further into planning and logistics. Industry News LEGAL
