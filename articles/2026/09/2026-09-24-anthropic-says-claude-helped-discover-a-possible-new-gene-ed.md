# Anthropic says Claude helped discover a possible new gene-editing tool

Anthropic said Claude helped discover a previously unknown molecular system that could represent a new gene-editing tool akin to CRISPR. In an X post, Amodei cautioned the "precise function, biotechnological utility (if any), or level of significance" is not yet clear, but framed it as a promising sign of AI-driven medical breakthroughs. It's the first notable achievement of a new Anthropic biotech research group formed this spring, produced by collaboration between Claude and the company's life-sciences team — Claude scoured medical literature and genome data and proposed the experiments that Anthropic scientists then ran. It arrives the same week Anthropic announces the OpenEvidence partnership and continues to define the "AI-native scientific R&D" pattern that also drives Novo Nordisk, Isomorphic Labs, and Enveda.
