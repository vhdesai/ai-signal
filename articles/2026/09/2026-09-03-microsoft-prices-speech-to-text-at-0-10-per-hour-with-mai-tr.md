# Microsoft prices speech-to-text at $0.10 per hour with MAI-Transcribe-2

Microsoft AI released MAI-Transcribe-2 at an introductory $0.10 per audio hour through end-2026 — roughly 72% below the prior model’s $0.36 — covering 60 languages with diarization, configurable styles, and word-level timestamps. Microsoft claims first place on the FLEURS multilingual benchmark at a 5.2% average word error rate, with substantial latency advantages over GPT-Transcribe, ElevenLabs Scribe v2, and Gemini 3.5 Transcribe. Distribution runs through Foundry, MAI Playground, and OpenRouter — first-party MAI models, not OpenAI models inside Azure. At 100,000 hours of audio the delta is roughly $36,000 versus $10,000, enough to trigger contract rebids across contact center and media workloads. Industry News N H
