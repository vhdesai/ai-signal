# Microsoft AI's MAI-Transcribe-2 Undercuts Rivals at $0.10 per Audio Hour

Microsoft AI launched MAI-Transcribe-2, a speech-recognition model priced at 10 cents per hour of audio — a steep discount to OpenAI, Google and ElevenLabs — with claimed gains in both speed and accuracy across languages and difficult audio. Microsoft reports strong results on multilingual benchmarks and is exposing the model through Azure Speech's fast transcription path. For enterprises with large transcription estates in clinical, legal and accessibility workflows, the pricing shift is material enough to warrant re-bidding existing contracts. https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/microsoft-ais-mai-transcribe-2-undercuts-openai-google-and-elevenlabs-on-price-and-speed
