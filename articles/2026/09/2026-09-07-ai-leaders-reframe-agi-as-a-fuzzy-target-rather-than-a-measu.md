# AI leaders reframe AGI as a “fuzzy target” rather than a measurable milestone

Bloomberg reports that senior AI figures are backing away from AGI as a discrete, datable achievement, describing it instead as a diffuse threshold that will be recognized only in hindsight. The shift matters commercially because AGI timelines have been used to justify capex, valuations, and regulatory urgency. Executives should expect vendor roadmaps to lean more on task-level benchmarks and less on milestone rhetoric.
