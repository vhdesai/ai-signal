# World-model companies remain secretive about paths to market

TechCrunch examined the opacity around world-model companies such as AMI Labs and World Labs, noting that the technology could apply to robotics, interactive video, self-driving systems, manufacturing, biomedicine, and spatial intelligence. The strategic point is that world models are still a broad capability thesis rather than a clearly productized market. With heavy funding and unclear go-to-market focus, the category resembles a pre-standardization race where labs may avoid revealing use cases until a defensible commercial wedge emerges.
