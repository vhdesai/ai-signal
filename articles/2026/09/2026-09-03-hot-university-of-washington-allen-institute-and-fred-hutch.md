# Hot University of Washington, Allen Institute and Fred Hutch launch $95M AI BioDesign project

The three institutions announced $95 million in funding to jointly pursue AI models that design novel biological tools, with Nobel laureate David Baker among the leads. The program targets AI systems that learn and apply nature’s design rules to protein and molecular engineering. It is one of the largest single commitments to AI-for-biology at a US academic consortium this year.
