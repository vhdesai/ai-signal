# Huawei raises Ascend AI chip prices ~60% as China's Nvidia alternatives face supply crunch

Bloomberg reports Huawei has raised prices on its most advanced Ascend AI chips by 60% this summer as demand for Chinese-made alternatives outpaces supply, with the 950DT indicated above 250,000 yuan (~$37,300) — broadly in line with Nvidia's B200. TrendForce separately notes broader Chinese AI-chip price hikes tied to HBM costs, with Cambricon and MetaX also repricing 20–30% higher. Coupled with Huawei's new 7.2T near-packaged optical module challenge to Nvidia and Broadcom, the signal is that China's AI-silicon supply chain is now bound by memory and packaging, not compute logic — and Chinese hyperscalers are willing to pay the premium.
