# Google brings Lyria 3.5 music generation directly into the Gemini app

Google released Lyria 3.5, an updated AI music-generation model, directly inside the Gemini app and via API, alongside availability in Flow Music, AI Studio, and Google Vids. The company says the model produces more expressive vocals and richer instrumental arrangements than its predecessor and was trained exclusively on licensed content. This is a consumer- and creator-facing expansion rather than an enterprise capability, but it reflects Google's continued push to embed generative media natively across its Gemini surface.
