# Stanford and Caltech wire a frontier model directly to a humanoid, skipping the learned control layer

The HomeBody system from Stanford's TML lab and Caltech gives GPT-6 Astra direct control of a Unitree G1's composable skill library, removing the vision-language-action policy normally trained between reasoning and motor control. The robot first explores an unfamiliar kitchen, builds a Real2Sim digital twin in Nvidia Isaac Sim as persistent spatial memory, then executes underspecified instructions with no environment-specific training data. The researchers are candid about limits: model latency between skills, Real2Sim setup cost and API spend, and finger-servo overheating during extended operation.
