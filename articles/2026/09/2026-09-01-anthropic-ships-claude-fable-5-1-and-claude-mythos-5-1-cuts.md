# Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1, cuts cache-read costs 75%

Anthropic released two new model variants — Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 — reporting gains on Terminal-Bench and science benchmarks alongside a 75% reduction in cached-token read costs. The pricing change is notable for enterprise deployments that lean on long, repeatedly-reused context windows. Early positioning suggests Anthropic is optimizing for agentic and long-context workloads rather than chasing a single headline benchmark.
