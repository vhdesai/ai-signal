# Nvidia in talks to invest up to $10B as anchor investor in Anthropic's IPO at ~$2T valuation

Reuters reports Anthropic is in talks to bring Nvidia in as an anchor investor in what could be the largest IPO in history, seeking to raise as much as $100 billion at a valuation near $2 trillion, with Nvidia considering up to $10 billion. Anthropic's annualized run rate is reported to have climbed above $65 billion by end of July, from about $9 billion at the end of 2025, and the listing is expected to complete before the November US midterms. This fuses Nvidia's supplier position with equity in its largest model customer and sets the first true public-market price test for a frontier lab. The story is sourced to two people familiar and explicitly states plans remain under discussion and could change.
