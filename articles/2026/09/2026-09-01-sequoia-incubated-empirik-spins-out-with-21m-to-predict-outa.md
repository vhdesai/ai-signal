# Sequoia-incubated Empirik spins out with $21M to predict outages before they occur

Empirik, incubated inside Sequoia in 2023 by the firm’s CIO Avon Puri and IT leader Sudheer Dhurjati, announced a $21 million seed round and its spin-out as an independent company. The product tracks system changes and infers their ripple effects across infrastructure, positioning itself as predictive observability rather than post-incident response.
