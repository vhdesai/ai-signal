# The Counterpoint: Grok 4.7's Token Consumption Undercuts Its Headline Price

VentureBeat's analysis argues the list price is misleading. Artificial Analysis measured Grok 4.7 xHigh at roughly 81,000 output tokens per Intelligence Index task, versus 36,000 for Grok 4.6 and 27,000 for GPT-6 Astra Max — 196% more than Astra. Real cost lands near $3.74 per task for Grok 4.7 xHigh against roughly $1.99 for GPT-5.6 Sol Max, despite Sol's far higher list price, and independent Terminal-Bench scoring (~26%) lags SpaceXAI's reported 38.0%. GitHub is nonetheless rolling Grok 4.7 across Copilot Pro, Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise.
