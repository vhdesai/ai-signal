# Pentagon Deploys ChatGPT Mil and Grok for Government to GenAI.mil; Anthropic Excluded

The Department of Defense added ChatGPT Mil and Grok for Government to its centralized GenAI.mil portal, extending frontier AI to three million civilian and military personnel; the portal already reported more than 1.7 million unique users and launched with Google Gemini. Anthropic's Claude is absent following a “supply-chain risk” designation after Anthropic insisted on usage guardrails, and Anthropic is challenging the designation in court. xAI's Grok is delivered over SpaceX's Starshield network.
