# Google Tests Flipkart Purchasing Directly Through Gemini and AI Mode in India

Google began a limited test allowing users in India to buy from Walmart-owned Flipkart inside Gemini and AI Mode, covering select products and users. A broader rollout is planned for later in October. The test extends agentic commerce into India's largest e-commerce base and puts Google in direct competition with Meta and Amazon over who owns the transaction layer.
