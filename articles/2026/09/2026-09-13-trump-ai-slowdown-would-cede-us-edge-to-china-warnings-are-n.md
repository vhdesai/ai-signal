# Trump: AI slowdown would cede US edge to China; warnings are "negative rhetoric"

President Trump publicly played down the need to check AI development, telling reporters he is unwilling to cede America's edge over China and dismissing warnings from Amodei, Bengio, and others as "negative rhetoric." He acknowledged the need for some regulation but offered no specifics. This is the clearest White House signal to date that federal AI policy over the next 12 months will prioritize competition with China over safety pacing — a direct headwind to the industry-led slowdown discussion.
