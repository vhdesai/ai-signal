# OpenAI pulls $1M sponsorship of Caltech AI maths contest after "slop mathematics" letter

OpenAI withdrew its $1 million sponsorship of a student-run AI mathematics event at Caltech after an open letter signed by 771 mathematicians called for the event to be suspended, labelling the format "slop mathematics" — results produced fast and left for others to verify. Anthropic's matching $1 million remains in place and the event is proceeding. The withdrawal preceded a separate declaration from 25 Fields Medallists on AI's role in mathematics. The episode marks the sharpest institutional pushback yet from an expert community against benchmark-driven AI marketing. AI Safety & Policy Breaking Policy
