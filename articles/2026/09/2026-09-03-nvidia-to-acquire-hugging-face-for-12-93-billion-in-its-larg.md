# Nvidia to Acquire Hugging Face for $12.93 Billion in Its Largest Deal Ever

Nvidia confirmed an agreement to acquire Hugging Face, the platform hosting roughly 3 million models, 500,000 datasets and more than 1 million applications for a community of some 18 million developers. Jensen Huang said the platform will continue to operate openly and that developers will not be required to use Nvidia hardware. The deal extends Nvidia's position from accelerators into the layer where models are discovered, fine-tuned, evaluated and deployed — a hedge as hyperscalers build custom silicon. Expect meaningful antitrust scrutiny given common ownership of critical compute and the industry's principal model marketplace. https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-12-9b-acquisition-of-ai-hosting-platform-hugging-face/
