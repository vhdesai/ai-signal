# Crusoe signs multi-year deal to run Perplexity's full model lifecycle

Crusoe announced a multi-year partnership under which Perplexity will train frontier models on dedicated Nvidia GB300 NVL72 clusters on Crusoe Cloud and serve them in production through Crusoe's Managed Inference service — training through serving on one provider. The arrangement is reciprocal: Crusoe is adopting Perplexity Enterprise Pro and Max across its 1,800 employees. Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas framed the choice around latency economics, noting "every millisecond of latency is felt by users." The deal is another data point that inference serving, not training capacity, is becoming the competitive axis among neoclouds. - https://www.unite.ai/crusoe-signs-multi-year-deal-to-power-perplexity-training-and-inference/
