# Nvidia and CrowdStrike develop new cybersecurity AI models

The Wall Street Journal reports that Nvidia and CrowdStrike are developing cybersecurity-focused AI models. The partnership reflects the next phase of security AI: domain-specialized models trained or tuned for threat detection, incident response, and enterprise telemetry rather than generic assistant use. The commercial question is whether specialized security models can deliver defensible outcomes without increasing operational complexity or attack surface. URL behind paywall.
