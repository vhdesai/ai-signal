# Nvidia-Backed Firmus Pushes Ahead With a ~$5B Australian IPO Despite a Forecast Loss

Australian AI data-center operator Firmus is preparing one of Australia’s largest-ever listings, seeking about $5B while forecasting a roughly $77M after-tax loss for the first half of FY2027. Firmus counts Nvidia, Meta and OpenAI among its customers and is slated to begin ASX trading in October. Coming the same day as Oracle’s force majeure notice, it is a direct public-market test of investor appetite for capital-intensive AI compute buildouts that are not yet profitable.
