# Meta’s real-time audio model targets always-listening assistants

Meta Superintelligence Labs’ Muse Voice Transcribe processes speech in 80-millisecond chunks, diarizes more than 20 speakers, covers roughly 25 languages, and detects sentence boundaries in-stream. Artificial Analysis rated it the most accurate streaming transcription model at the lowest price point. Meta positions it as infrastructure for persistent, always-on personal agents — notably on camera glasses. Original release Sept 1; analysis in-window.
