# Anthropic and OpenAI now capture 89% of AI startup revenues

The Information reports Anthropic and OpenAI's combined share of AI startup revenues has risen to 89%, per new data circulated to Information subscribers. Reader commentary on the story highlights the parallel question of profit concentration — Apple, Microsoft, and Google saw scale drive margin expansion, but token-price competition has so far prevented the same at the model layer, though 2026 post-IPO price increases are expected. Nvidia is called out as the standout margin engine in the stack.
