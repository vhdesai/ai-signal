# Insurers say hospital AI tools added $942M to healthcare spending

A Blue Cross Blue Shield Association analysis attributes an additional $942 million in healthcare spending over a two-year period to hospitals' use of AI tools when submitting insurance claims. The association reports "a sharp increase in patients being documented as having complex conditions" alongside "no evidence of corresponding change in care delivered." Abridge founder Dr. Shiv Rao acknowledged the risk of "bots fighting bots, agents fighting agents," while arguing the same tools could also reduce friction and cost.
