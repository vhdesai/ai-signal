# Alibaba's Amap AI ranking as a natural experiment in AI-driven consumer traffic redistribution

The Amap case is also being read academically as one of the first at-scale natural experiments in AI-mediated local-merchant discovery: a single algorithmic weighting change created stable multi-month order-volume shocks of 50–60% for individual merchants. It is a live-data version of the algorithmic-recommender welfare studies published in 2024–25 by CMU and UT Austin, at a scale those studies could not access. Expect follow-on academic work using Amap's disclosures.
