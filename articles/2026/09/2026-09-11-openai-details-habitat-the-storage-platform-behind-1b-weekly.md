# OpenAI Details Habitat, the Storage Platform Behind 1B Weekly ChatGPT Users

OpenAI published the first of a two-part engineering series on Habitat, the online storage platform serving its products. Habitat now handles more than 70 million requests per second across nearly 40 geographic regions and over 500 petabytes of data, supporting products used by more than a billion people each week. It began at DevDay 2023 as a Python client-side library against a single database. The post is a rare first-party look at the operational substrate under frontier AI products — and at what sustained 10x-per-year growth demands of infrastructure teams.
