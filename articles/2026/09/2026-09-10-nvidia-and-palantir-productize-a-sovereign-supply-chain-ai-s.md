# Nvidia and Palantir productize a sovereign supply-chain AI stack, starting with Nvidia's own chain

NVIDIA and Palantir announced a productized sovereign AI stack for supply chains, integrating Nemotron models with Palantir Foundry and NVIDIA cuOpt. The first deployment target is NVIDIA's own supply chain — a single Vera Rubin rack alone has 1.3M components. Dell, Cisco, Rackspace, and Nebius are named infrastructure partners, and enterprises can run the stack on-prem or via Palantir's Sovereign AI OS reference architecture.
