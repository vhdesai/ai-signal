# Trending Anthropic's triple release read as a $2 trillion IPO playbook

Analysis of Anthropic's September 1 launch of Claude Fable 5.1, Mythos 5.1 and Enterprise Frontier Safeguards frames the bundle as positioning for a reported ~$2 trillion IPO in October 2026. Key commercial levers include a 75% cut to cache-read pricing, from $1.00 to $0.25 per million tokens, aimed at capturing agentic workloads, and gating Mythos 5.1 behind US government verification programs. Zero-data-retention safeguards were built with more than 100 institutions including Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Citi and Visa. Note: the underlying model release was September 1; this analysis published September 2.
