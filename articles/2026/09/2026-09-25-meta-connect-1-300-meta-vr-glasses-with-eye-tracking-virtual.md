# Meta Connect: $1,300 Meta VR Glasses with eye tracking, virtual workspaces, and Muse integration

TechRepublic details the $1,300 Meta VR Glasses that Meta previewed at Connect — 100 g, eye tracking, virtual workspaces, and deep Muse integration. The device joins the Muse Charm keychain companion, the audio-only Ray-Ban Meta Audio ($349), and the third-generation Ray-Ban Meta ($449). Meta stock hit a 52-week high; JPMorgan sees Meta's AI strategy running "from hearing aids to a $1,299 cinema" as a giant market opportunity, with three analyst price targets now approaching $920. The Information's Martin Peers cautions the rally may not last — a Rosenblatt report finds Muse would need computing costs to "drop substantially" or users spending $1,000+/month for it to be profitable.
