# PitchBook: Software Dealmaking Goes Underground — AI-Native Multiples 10–15× ARR vs 3–6× for Non-AI SaaS

PitchBook reports software M&A is going underground as buyers and sellers negotiate away from formal auctions to avoid failed processes. The valuation gap is stark: software companies without a credible AI narrative now trade at 3–6× ARR, while AI-native or AI-enabled companies clear 10–15×. Sponsors are drawn to businesses AI struggles to replicate — proprietary data or regulatory accreditation. The subtext: buyers cannot underwrite AI’s impact fast enough for a public process, so private deals with strategic acquirers and PE with visible AI theses are taking share. It matters for anyone modeling exit scenarios in the next 18 months.
