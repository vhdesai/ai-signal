# YouTube ships Gemini-powered creator tools: draft feedback, A/B testing, dynamic thumbnails, live dubbing

At Made on YouTube 2026, Google introduced a feature that analyzes unpublished drafts and suggests improvements to pacing, structure and storytelling, alongside an AI research feed surfacing content ideas in Studio. The same event added video A/B testing, AI-generated dynamic thumbnails and real-time dubbing for live streams. It is Google's largest creator-tooling AI release of the year and a visible consumer proof point for Gemini.
