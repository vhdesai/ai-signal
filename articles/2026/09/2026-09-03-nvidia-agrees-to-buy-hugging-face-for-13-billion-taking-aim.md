# Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for $13 billion, taking aim at the open-model layer

The Wall Street Journal reported that Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for $13 billion as it ramps up efforts around open-weight models and related services. Strategically, the deal would give Nvidia a major distribution and workflow layer across open models, datasets, evaluation, inference, and developer adoption. The acquisition would extend Nvidia's influence from hardware and systems software into the collaboration layer where open AI work happens. FINANCEAI CHIPS
