# Tencent restructures Bilibili stake into debt as it funds AI initiatives

Tencent is converting part of its Bilibili exposure from equity into a $700M convertible bond package, giving it more capital flexibility to fund AI capex while preserving strategic ties with the video platform. Analysts read the move as a template for how Chinese Big Tech is rebalancing legacy internet portfolios to free up cash for the expensive AI buildout. For executives, this is early evidence that portfolio restructuring — not just fundraising — will be a lever hyperscaler-scale AI programs use to finance themselves.
