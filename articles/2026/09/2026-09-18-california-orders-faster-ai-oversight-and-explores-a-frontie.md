# California Orders Faster AI Oversight and Explores a Frontier-Model Kill Switch

Governor Gavin Newsom issued an executive order directing California agencies to accelerate implementation of AI oversight laws and convene experts on stronger frontier-model safeguards. The order calls for recommendations on embedded independent verification, verified safety frameworks, updated incident definitions, and a potential frontier-model kill switch.
