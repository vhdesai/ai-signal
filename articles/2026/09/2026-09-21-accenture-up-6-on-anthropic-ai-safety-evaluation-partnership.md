# Accenture Up 6% on Anthropic AI Safety Evaluation Partnership

Seeking Alpha reports Accenture (ACN) jumped 6% on news of an AI safety evaluation partnership with Anthropic. The deal follows Anthropic's expanded Claudeforce partnership with Salesforce and the Novo Nordisk drug-discovery arrangement — Anthropic's third enterprise-services or research partnership in two weeks.
