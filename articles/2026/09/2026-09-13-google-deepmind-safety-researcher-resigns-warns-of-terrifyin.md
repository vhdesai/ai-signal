# Google DeepMind Safety Researcher Resigns, Warns of "Terrifying Chance" of Major AI Harm

Another Google DeepMind AI safety researcher has resigned, publicly citing a "terrifying chance" AI could cause immense harm. Times of India separately reports Google is moving its AI responsibility team out of DeepMind entirely starting this month — a structural shift critics say weakens safety integration inside model development. Combined with the Amodei / Altman / Hassabis slowdown call, the day's news suggests unusually coordinated concern from within the industry itself, and a governance question boards should be asking their AI vendors this quarter. What to Watch - Whether Amodei's proposed three-step slowdown framework gets concrete adoption by any of the frontier labs — or remains rhetoric with no procurement or release-cadence implications. - Whether Anthropic's board narrows Chinese-tenant API access following the Tom's Hardware disclosure, and how that interacts with the pending IPO. - Google's structural move of AI responsibility out of DeepMind: whether it centralizes governance across Alphabet or dilutes safety inside the model-building org. - Grok-in-Copilot governance signals from Microsoft: SLA, data-flow disclosures, and per-app model routing controls for regulated customers. - Whether Meta's Stilla.ai acquisition produces a shipped commerce-agent surface in WhatsApp Business or Instagram Shops before year-end.
