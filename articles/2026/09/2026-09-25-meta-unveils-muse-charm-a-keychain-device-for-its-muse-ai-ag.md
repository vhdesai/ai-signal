# Meta unveils Muse Charm, a keychain device for its Muse AI agent

Meta detailed Muse Charm, a Tamagotchi-like keychain carrying the full real-time voice and avatar stack for its Muse agent, revealed as the closing item of Zuckerberg's Connect keynote. It activates via a fingerprint tap and ships in December; no price has been announced. Meta positioned it for users who don't wear smart glasses — an explicit attempt to widen the hardware funnel beyond eyewear.
