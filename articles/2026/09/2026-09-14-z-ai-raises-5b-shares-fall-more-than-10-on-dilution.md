# Z.ai Raises $5B; Shares Fall More Than 10% on Dilution

The Beijing-based GLM developer, formerly Zhipu AI, priced 21.97 million new H shares at HK$714 — a 10% discount to Friday's close — alongside RMB 20.14B ($3B) of zero-coupon convertible bonds due 2027. About 60% of net proceeds are earmarked for next-generation model R&D and training/inference infrastructure. It is the company's second major raise in two months following a ~$4B July placement, and shares fell more than 10% on Monday. Rival MiniMax fell about 5% in sympathy. FUNDING
