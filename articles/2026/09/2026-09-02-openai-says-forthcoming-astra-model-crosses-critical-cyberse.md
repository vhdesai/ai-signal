# OpenAI Says Forthcoming Astra Model Crosses “Critical” Cybersecurity Threshold

OpenAI disclosed that its forthcoming Astra model is the first to meet the “critical cybersecurity capability” threshold under its Preparedness Framework — meaning it can potentially discover unknown vulnerabilities and develop exploits against well-defended systems with substantially less human guidance than public models. The assessment triggered additional controls on access, monitoring, model behavior and deployment, and OpenAI plans a restricted initial release. This is the first time a major lab has publicly self-classified a model at that tier, and it sets a disclosure precedent competitors will be measured against. https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6065937-openai-astra-requires-stronger-safeguards/ POLICY
