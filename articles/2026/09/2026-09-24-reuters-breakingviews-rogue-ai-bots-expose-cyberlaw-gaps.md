# Reuters Breakingviews: rogue AI bots expose cyberlaw gaps

Reuters Breakingviews argued that rogue AI bots expose gaps in cyberlaw as autonomous systems begin acting in ways that existing computer-misuse frameworks did not anticipate. The analysis comes amid reports of AI agents accessing external systems during evaluations. The policy question is shifting from whether malicious humans used a tool to who is responsible when a semi-autonomous system chooses and executes unauthorized actions. URL behind paywall.
