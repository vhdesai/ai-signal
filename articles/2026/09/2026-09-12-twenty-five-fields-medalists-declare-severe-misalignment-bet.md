# Twenty-Five Fields Medalists Declare "Severe Misalignment" Between AI Labs and Mathematics

Terence Tao and 24 other Fields Medal recipients signed a joint declaration, "A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics," arguing that AI companies' use of famous open problems as benchmarks is damaging the discipline. The signatories explicitly concede that LLM mathematical capability has improved dramatically and can now solve major outstanding problems — their objection is to process, not correctness. Rushed announcements, they write, leave no time for proper write-ups, isolation of genuinely new methods, or citation of prior human work. The declaration carries no enforcement mechanism, but it creates a reference point that journal editors, funding agencies and policymakers can cite when handling AI-generated claims.
