# Anthropic Signs $35B Nvidia-Backed Lambda Cloud Deal in Texas

Anthropic signed a roughly $35 billion agreement with Nvidia-backed neocloud Lambda for capacity at a ~350 MW Hut 8-developed data center in Nueces County, Texas, where Nvidia itself holds the lease. It stacks on recent commitments to Nscale ($45B), Fluidstack ($50B), and SpaceX ($45B). Nvidia now sits simultaneously as investor, landlord, and chip supplier — the circular-financing pattern drawing antitrust and credit scrutiny. Compute-contract concentration is the single largest counterparty exposure in the sector right now.
