# OpenAI launches GPT-6 Sol and Luna, halving API prices below Astra

OpenAI extended the GPT-6 family below flagship Astra with Sol ($2/$10 per million tokens) and Luna ($0.10/$0.50), roughly halving API pricing versus the GPT-5.6 series. Both carry ~1.05M-token context windows; Sol targets complex coding and agentic automation, Luna high-volume clerical work, and OpenAI says Sol makes about half as many factual mistakes as its predecessor. Reported results include Sol at 33.2% on AutomationBench 1.0.6 at $0.27 per task and 68.8% on DeepSWE v1.1, with Luna at 66.6% on DeepSWE at ~93% lower cost per task than Opus 5. The launch landed within hours of Anthropic's Opus 5.5 price cut.
