# China's Z.ai raises ~$5B through combined HK share placement and convertible bond

Z.ai (Zhipu AI), one of China's leading foundation-model developers, is raising approximately $2B via a Hong Kong share placement of ~22M new H shares at HK$714 and another $3B via a 20.14B yuan convertible bond. The raise comes as Z.ai's stock is down 73% from its July peak but still trades meaningfully above its IPO price. Combined with Enflame's Shanghai debut and DeepSeek's IPO underwriter selection, it confirms that Chinese AI vendors are being funded aggressively across multiple public-market venues even in a soft equity tape.
