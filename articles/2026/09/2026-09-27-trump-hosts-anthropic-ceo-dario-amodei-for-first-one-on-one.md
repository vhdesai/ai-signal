# Trump Hosts Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for First One-on-One White House Dinner

Dario Amodei dined privately with President Trump on Sunday evening, the first direct meeting between the two after publicly opposing positions on pacing frontier AI development. Amodei was not on the invite list for last week's state dinner for Xi Jinping, and Anthropic remains in litigation over the Pentagon's “supply chain risk” designation.
