# Google tests buying from Flipkart through Gemini and AI Mode in India

Google began testing a way for shoppers in India to buy select Flipkart products directly through Gemini and AI Mode, with a Buy button that moves users into a Flipkart checkout flow. The trial currently covers a limited set of users and products, including smartphones, electronics, and accessories, ahead of India’s festive shopping season. This is a high-signal agentic commerce test: Google is moving from product discovery toward transaction mediation, raising questions about platform preference, payment protection, and merchant access.
