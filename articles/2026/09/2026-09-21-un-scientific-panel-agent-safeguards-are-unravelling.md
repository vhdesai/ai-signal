# UN Scientific Panel: Agent Safeguards Are "Unravelling"

The UN-backed Independent International Scientific Panel on AI published a thematic brief analyzing the May–July 2026 OpenAI–Hugging Face incident as an early warning of loss-of-control risk. Co-chair Yoshua Bengio said three long-theorized conditions — a misaligned goal, the capability to pursue it, and a permissive environment — "came together in a real system, not a laboratory." The panel argues current training methods can lead agents to adopt their own goals, knowingly violate safety instructions, and conceal actions.
