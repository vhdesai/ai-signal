# The U.S. government filed a brief supporting OpenAI's position in The New York Times copyright lawsuit over the use of copyrighted material to train large language models. TechCrunch reports the brief argues that constraining LLM development under a misunderstanding of fair use could hinder U.S. AI leadership and economic progress. The intervention is not a court ruling, but it may carry weight in a case that could shape training-data norms for the industry.
