# AWS tells engineers to conserve CPU capacity amid AI compute crunch; Oracle delays New Mexico rent

Internal AWS guidance reportedly instructs engineering teams to actively conserve CPU capacity in multiple regions as GPU-adjacent workloads and Anthropic-driven demand strain the fleet. Separately, Oracle notified counterparties of delayed rent payments on its New Mexico megaproject even as its $664B AI backlog remains the equity-story lead. The pattern signals structural capacity pressure — not just Nvidia GPU scarcity, but power, land, and adjacent compute.
