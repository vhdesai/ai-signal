# Google’s WeatherNext 3 drops physics simulation and learns weather from live satellite data

Google Research and DeepMind released WeatherNext 3, which skips traditional numerical physics simulation and learns directly from live geostationary satellite feeds. It produces hourly forecasts at up to 5 km resolution — five times sharper than WeatherNext 2 — with precipitation accuracy up to 50% better. The model also emits renewable-energy-specific outputs such as turbine-height wind and solar irradiance, and is already live across Search, Maps, Gemini and the Maps Platform Weather API. Underlying rollout first surfaced Sept 3; this write-up is in-window.
