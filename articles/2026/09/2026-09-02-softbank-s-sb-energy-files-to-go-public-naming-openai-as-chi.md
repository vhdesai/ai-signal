# SoftBank's SB Energy files to go public, naming OpenAI as chief tenant and 5% owner

SB Energy, a SoftBank-backed data-center power developer, filed to go public. Its prospectus reportedly reveals that OpenAI is both its largest data-center tenant and a 5% equity owner — a structure Business Insider's coverage flagged as illustrating the increasingly circular dealing between AI compute customers and infrastructure investors. The filing is one of the clearer public windows yet into how power and compute financing are becoming intertwined.
