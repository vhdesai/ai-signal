# PitchBook: software borrowers under stress lead private-credit watchlist; PSG raises $5.1B Europe fund as tech PE rebounds

PitchBook's Daily Pitch reports the private-credit watchlist rose to 583 companies by June 30 — up 8% QoQ and 25% since year-end 2025 — and software borrowers now account for 36% of debt under pressure, the largest sector share, per LCD's analysis of 180+ BDCs. Dollar exposure of stressed loans grew 92% since year-end 2025 to $47B. Separately PSG Equity closed a $5.1B European software fund, up 70% from its predecessor, joining Francisco Partners' $21B combined July raise as evidence of a tech-PE rebound favoring genuine sector specialists over "tourists." Island (enterprise agent control plane) raised a $400M Series F at $6.4B.
