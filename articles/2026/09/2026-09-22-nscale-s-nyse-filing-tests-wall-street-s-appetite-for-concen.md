# Nscale's NYSE Filing Tests Wall Street's Appetite for Concentrated AI Bets

British neocloud Nscale filed to list on the NYSE at an expected $35B valuation, seeking to raise $3B against more than $103B in contracted revenue. Roughly 85% of that book comes from two customers: $43.8B of compute for Microsoft through 2033 and $44.6B with Anthropic — and the Anthropic agreement is contingent on Nscale securing financing and hitting milestones the filing itself describes as "stringent." Revenue reached $140.6M for the six months to June 30, up from $10.4M, while net losses widened to $1.02B. Nvidia recently agreed to provide $1B in convertible debt as part of a $3.1B financing package.
