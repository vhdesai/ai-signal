# TechCrunch: AI safety conversations are becoming hard to separate from speculation

TechCrunch argued that recent AI safety debates are becoming difficult to parse because real incidents — model deception, autonomous hacking, and weak sandboxes — sit alongside more speculative scenarios. The piece focused on claims about self-replicating code and air-gapped systems, contrasting plausible operational risks with more remote possibilities. The practical takeaway is that leaders should prioritize concrete controls around sandboxing, logging, disclosure, and model behavior monitoring while resisting decision-making based on unsupported sci-fi scenarios.
