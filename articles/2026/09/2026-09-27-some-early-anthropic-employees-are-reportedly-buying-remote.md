# Some early Anthropic employees are reportedly buying remote land

Citing a Wall Street Journal report, THE DECODER writes that several of Anthropic's longest-serving employees have discussed buying land in remote parts of the US to relocate to "if AI goes awry." The piece traces how closely early hires at Anthropic and OpenAI are tied to the Effective Altruism community and a Bay Area network long focused on catastrophic risk.
