# Texas halts new data-center grid connections over “ghost demand”

Texas placed a hold on new data-center grid interconnections while it determines how many queued projects requesting very large power allocations will actually be built. The concern is speculative or duplicated interconnection requests inflating apparent demand and distorting grid planning. For anyone modeling AI capacity in ERCOT, this introduces schedule risk independent of chip supply — power availability is now a gating item on its own timeline. hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/2026/09/02/ghost-demand-prompts-texas-to-halt-new-data-center-grid-connections
