# Oracle Health Expands Clinical AI Agent to Nurses

Oracle expanded its Clinical AI Agent — which the company says has saved physicians more than 400,000 hours — to help nurses reduce documentation burden. The launch follows Oracle's $664B AI-cloud backlog and $700M restructuring and shows Oracle is pushing hard into vertical AI adjacent to its core cloud franchise. Nurse-facing agents also target one of the industry's most measurable ROI axes: minutes-per-shift saved on charting and handoff.
