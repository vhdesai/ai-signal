# Moonshot's Kimi K3 Lands on AWS Bedrock — First Major Chinese Frontier Model in a U.S. Hyperscaler Catalog

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 is now available on Amazon Bedrock, AWS's mainstream generative-AI platform, positioned by Amazon as "a powerful new option for coding and knowledge work." It is a landmark distribution moment: the first time a Chinese frontier-model developer has secured a first-party listing inside a U.S. hyperscaler's mainstream catalog, effectively bypassing the API-access friction that had confined Chinese models to niche integrations.
