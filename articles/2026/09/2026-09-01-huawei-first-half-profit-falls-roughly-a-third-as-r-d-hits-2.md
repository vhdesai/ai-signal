# Huawei first-half profit falls roughly a third as R&D hits 26% of revenue

Huawei reported first-half net profit of about RMB 23.4 billion, down by more than a third year-over-year, while revenue rose nearly 10% to RMB 467.8 billion. R&D spending climbed about 25% to RMB 121.4 billion, roughly a quarter of revenue, directed at AI, semiconductors, cloud and autonomous driving. The margin sacrifice is deliberate: Huawei is funding domestic substitutes for restricted US and allied technology.
