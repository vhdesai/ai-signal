# Blue Cross analysis attributes $942M in added healthcare spend to hospital AI coding tools

A Blue Cross Blue Shield Association analysis found that hospitals' use of AI tools in submitting insurance claims drove an additional $942 million in healthcare spending over two years. The analysis reported “a sharp increase in patients being documented as having complex conditions” alongside “no evidence of corresponding change in care delivered.” BCBSA senior vice president Luke Chalker characterised the dynamic bluntly: “It's not a war. It's a completely one-sided blood bath.” This is one of the first hard dollar figures attaching AI adoption to cost inflation rather than savings. PRICING
