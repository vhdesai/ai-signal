# Anthropic's 200M-exchange distillation report becomes evidence behind U.S. intelligence accusations

Yahoo Finance reported that Anthropic's September threat intelligence report describes roughly 200 million exchanges across five alleged distillation campaigns, including 151 million attributed to Alibaba's Qwen team. The report ties corporate evidence to a CISA/FBI/NSA advisory accusing Chinese AI firms of industrial-scale model distillation. The strategic issue is not only IP protection; it is whether the open-weight price war is partly subsidized by unauthorized extraction from closed frontier systems. CHINA AI DISTILLATION
