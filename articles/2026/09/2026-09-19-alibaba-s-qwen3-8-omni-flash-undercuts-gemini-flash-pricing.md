# Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash undercuts Gemini Flash pricing at comparable multimodal benchmarks

Alibaba shipped Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, its first multimodal model designed for agentic use — it processes audio and video simultaneously and can autonomously use tools to edit vlogs, translate clips, or summarize movies. On audio-video benchmarks it nearly matches Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash at a fraction of the API cost. Coming a week after Google's Gemini 3.8 Live also undercut GPT-Live-1 by ~2/3, this is the second consecutive multimodal-pricing floor drop against OpenAI and confirms Alibaba is willing to be the price-setter in the multimodal-agent segment.
