# Google tests Flipkart purchasing via Gemini and AI Mode in India

Google is running a limited India pilot letting Gemini and the AI Mode surface complete purchases directly on Walmart-owned Flipkart, with a broader rollout planned for October. The move is Google's most explicit push into agentic commerce in a market where Amazon and Flipkart split the e-commerce baseline. It sits alongside Ant Group's Alipay agentic-commerce reorg and OpenAI's Sponsored Agents integration with Shopify — Q4 2026 is shaping up as the quarter agentic commerce moves from demo to real GMV.
