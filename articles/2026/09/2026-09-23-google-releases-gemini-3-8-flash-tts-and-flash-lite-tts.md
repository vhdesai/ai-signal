# Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS, two text-to-speech models with prompt-based voice design, long-form generation, two-speaker staging, nonverbal cues, and voice replication controls. MarkTechPost reported that Flash TTS is aimed at creative direction while Flash-Lite targets high-volume production, with support across more than 100 languages and dialects and a library of more than 2,000 production voices. Safety controls include SynthID watermarking and consent checks for voice replication. SPEECH AIOPEN WEIGHTS
