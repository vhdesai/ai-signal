# Appeals court upholds Pentagon's “supply chain risk” designation of Anthropic

A DC Circuit panel ruled 2–1 that the Department of Defense had “ample support” for concluding that continued integration of Claude into its systems presented a covered national-security risk, rejecting Anthropic's arbitrary-action, authority and constitutional claims. The designation bars the military and its contractors from using Claude, and stems from Anthropic's refusal to permit use in fully autonomous lethal weapons or mass domestic surveillance. Anthropic said it disagrees, noted a San Francisco court struck down a parallel designation in August, and is weighing further review — a live issue ahead of its anticipated IPO. POLICY
