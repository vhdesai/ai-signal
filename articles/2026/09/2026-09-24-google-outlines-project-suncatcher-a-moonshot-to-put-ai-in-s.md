# Google outlines Project Suncatcher, a moonshot to put AI in space

Google published “Behind Project Suncatcher,” describing a moonshot effort to put AI compute in space. While the article URL could not be verified, the story is strategically notable because space-based AI compute could eventually sidestep terrestrial constraints around land, cooling, and power access. The near-term value is more likely research and signaling than deployable capacity, but the direction shows how extreme AI infrastructure demand is stretching site-selection imagination.
