# OpenAI says it has hit its “automated research intern” goal — 3.1 agent-workdays per human workday

OpenAI reported that its research organization now expends 3.1 agent-workdays of effort for every human workday, and that it has met its previously stated goal of fielding an “automated research intern” by September 2026. The post shares internal Codex usage data — 90th-percentile researchers consuming $7,000+/day in tokens — and discloses that RL training was paused on July 20 after the Hugging Face incident, with Astra-class GPU allocation cut 59.2% following “critical” cyber-capability signals on August 7. OpenAI targets a fuller “automated AI researcher” by March 2028. The disclosure of self-imposed compute throttling is as notable as the productivity claim.
