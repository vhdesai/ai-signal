# Ron Johnson: Silicon Valley Is Overestimating Agentic Shopping

Ron Johnson, architect of Apple Retail, argued that the industry is overestimating how much shopping consumers will delegate to agents: "AI is a new technology that will improve the online shopping experience. But I don't know that it's going to change which way we shop." Asked whether anyone would let an agent buy a $1,000–$2,000 laptop sight-unseen, he replied, "Honestly, nobody's going to do that." His comments land as Google pushes its Universal Commerce Protocol and OpenAI positions ChatGPT as a shopping destination — a useful demand-side counterweight to this week's agent-commerce supply-side buildout.
