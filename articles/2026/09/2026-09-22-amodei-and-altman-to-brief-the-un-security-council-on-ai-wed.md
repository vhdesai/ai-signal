# Amodei and Altman to Brief the UN Security Council on AI Wednesday

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei will brief the UN Security Council remotely on Wednesday alongside OpenAI's Sam Altman, with Yoshua Bengio — co-chair of the UN's AI advisory panel — and Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue also listed as briefers. France, holding the rotating council presidency, convened the session around malicious use of AI and the risk of losing control of advanced models. The session follows Amodei's September 12 essay calling on labs to pace frontier development and grant independent evaluators continuous access, a position Altman and Elon Musk publicly supported and which Beijing dismissed as "fearmongering."
