# New Equinix, Nvidia and Together AI launch a distributed “Inference Exchange”

The three companies announced the Equinix Inference Exchange, designed to run enterprise inference physically closer to data and end users. It pairs Nvidia architectures with Together AI’s catalog of 200-plus open models across Equinix facilities. General availability is targeted for Q1 2027, positioning colocation providers as an emerging control point in the inference market.
