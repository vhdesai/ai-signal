# CMU takes No. 1 for undergraduate AI; Berkeley No. 1 in data science, No. 2 in engineering

The new U.S. News rankings placed Carnegie Mellon at a historic high of No. 14 overall, taking the No. 1 spot for undergraduate Computer Science and, separately, for Artificial Intelligence. UC Berkeley's College of Computing, Data Science, and Society announced a No. 1 national ranking in undergraduate data science and No. 4 in computer science, while Berkeley Engineering rose to No. 2 and is now tied as the top public engineering program. For technical recruiting pipelines, these two institutions now anchor the undergraduate AI talent market.
