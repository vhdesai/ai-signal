# Google launches Google Pics, a Workspace-native AI image creator built on Nano Banana

Google shipped Google Pics, an image creation and editing surface embedded directly in Workspace and powered by its Nano Banana model. The product targets the design-tool layer occupied by Canva and Adobe Express rather than standalone generative-image apps. Positioning it inside Workspace removes the procurement and data-boundary friction of a third-party creative tool. It is a direct competitive signal for Microsoft 365 Copilot's own visual-content roadmap. https://9to5google.com/2026/09/01/google-pics-launch/ Industry News ANALYSIS
