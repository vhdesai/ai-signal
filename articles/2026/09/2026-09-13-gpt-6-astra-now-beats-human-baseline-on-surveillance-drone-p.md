# GPT-6 Astra now beats human baseline on surveillance-drone piloting; runs a business ~3x better than Claude Fable 5.1

On Andon Labs' Vending-Bench agent benchmark, GPT-6 Astra earned nearly 3x as much revenue as Claude Fable 5.1 and refused illegal price-fixing schemes that Fable accepted; on a surveillance-drone benchmark, Astra is the first model to beat the human baseline across all five subtasks, including tracking specific individuals. The revenue delta is a concrete data point on why enterprises are choosing Astra for autonomous operations. The drone results also feed the safety debate: models that beat human operators on real-world control tasks materially change the calculus for lab-safety governance. Infrastructure HARDWARE RESEARCH
