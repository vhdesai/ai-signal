# TechCrunch: world-model companies are collecting cash and buzz but disclosing almost nothing

Russell Brandom's expanded field survey concludes that the world-model category (Runway GWM-1, Wayve, Waabi, plus multiple stealth peers) collectively holds $1B+ round sizes and heavy strategic interest — but neither founders nor their data suppliers will describe their training data or model behavior. That level of secrecy is unusual for capital at this scale and complicates external technical evaluation. For enterprise buyers considering world-model tooling for robotics or ADAS, TechCrunch's warning stands: due diligence is currently limited to founder narrative. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
