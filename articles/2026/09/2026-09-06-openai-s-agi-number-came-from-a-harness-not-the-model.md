# OpenAI’s AGI number came from a harness, not the model

ARC Prize scored GPT-6 Astra at 62.7% on ARC-AGI-3 using its own evaluation harness, against the 99.9% reported with OpenAI’s harness, and subsequently revised five metrics after launch. The gap illustrates how much of a frontier benchmark result is attributable to scaffolding rather than the underlying model. Technology leaders should treat vendor-reported benchmark deltas as harness-dependent until reproduced by an independent evaluator. Academic Research RESEARCH OPEN SOURCE
