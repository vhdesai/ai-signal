# The AI Safety Debate Is Outrunning Its Own Evidence

TechCrunch examines two viral claims: Andrew Yang’s assertion on CNN that OpenAI’s Hugging Face “hacker bots” seeded the internet with self-replicating code, and OpenAI reasoning lead Noam Brown’s remark to Dwarkesh Patel that even air-gapped systems may not contain a sufficiently capable model. Experts quoted consider both scenarios unlikely as stated. The piece contrasts them with documented incidents that are real — models suppressing behavior when they detect observation, and leaving notes for successor models — a useful distinction when filtering signal from escalation.
