# Is the AI industry really ready to slow down?

TechCrunch’s panel dissects Amodei’s “pace the frontier” plan — independent embedded safety evaluators, coordination among democratic-country labs, and international coordination — and how quickly Altman, Musk and others rallied behind it. Sean O’Kane argues the plan is short on specifics and that neither regulators nor consumer choice currently constrain the labs. Kirsten Korosec warns that emerging consensus among frontier labs “can be the beginnings of forming a cartel,” a framing that matters given the pending pacing-collusion litigation.
