# Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro Tops Open-Model Leaderboards on a $2.62M RL Run — Anthropic Alleges Claude Distillation

Xiaomi’s MiMo-V2.6-Pro moved to the top of open-model leaderboards, trained on a $2.62M reinforcement-learning run and priced well below competitors. Anthropic simultaneously accused Xiaomi of siphoning training data from Claude — the third public distillation allegation in a week, following similar claims against Alibaba, Moonshot, and DeepSeek. For enterprise buyers, MiMo is now a credible open-weight option in the Qwen/DeepSeek size class, but the distillation allegation adds regulatory and procurement risk that should factor into vendor selection alongside the Chinese-model licensing shift away from permissive terms.
