# Alibaba unveils Zhenwu V900 AI chip and plans for a 10-trillion-parameter model at Apsara

At its Apsara conference, Alibaba unveiled the Zhenwu V900 AI chip and disclosed plans for a 10-trillion-parameter frontier model — well beyond Qwen's current lineup and a direct challenge to Nvidia-dependent training infrastructure. It arrives the same day DeepSeek confirmed Huawei chip deployment for Q4 and Tencent announced a new AI model (Tencent stock rose on its own news). This is the most consequential day for China's frontier-AI stack in 2026 and is reshaping how Wall Street models sovereign-AI supply chains.
