# Apple Ships Siri AI and the Next Generation of Apple Intelligence

Apple released its rebuilt Siri alongside iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27 — adding personal-context understanding across messages, mail, and photos, onscreen awareness, and systemwide app actions. Siri AI ships in beta, English-only, with five more languages due in October; unavailable on iOS/iPadOS in the EU and for mainland-China accounts. Inference runs on-device or through Private Cloud Compute, though Apple's foundation models were developed under a multi-year Google/Gemini agreement.
