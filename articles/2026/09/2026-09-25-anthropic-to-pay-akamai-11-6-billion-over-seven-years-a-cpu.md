# Anthropic to pay Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years — a CPU-first compute bet

Anthropic committed $11.6B over seven years to Akamai's cloud infrastructure, the largest deal in Akamai's history and more than six times the $1.8B arrangement reported in May. Unusually, it is a bet on CPUs rather than GPUs, reflecting the compute profile of sustained agentic workloads. Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for up to roughly 5% of its common stock at $111.33/share, with about 2% vesting on first payment and the remainder unlocking as spend grows toward a potential $20B. Akamai shares rose as much as 17% after hours.
