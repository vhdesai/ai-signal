# Nvidia to acquire Hugging Face for roughly $13 billion

Nvidia confirmed a deal to buy the open-model platform Hugging Face for approximately $13 billion (some coverage cites $12.9B), its most significant move up the AI stack beyond hardware. Hugging Face hosts more than 3 million models used by over 18 million developers. Nvidia said the platform will remain open to the whole ecosystem, with closing expected in the first half of 2027 subject to regulatory approval.
