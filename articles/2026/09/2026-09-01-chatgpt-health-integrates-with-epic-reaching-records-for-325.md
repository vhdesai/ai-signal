# ChatGPT Health integrates with Epic, reaching records for 325M+ patients

OpenAI is connecting ChatGPT Health to Epic’s electronic health record system, letting clinicians import patient data — appointment notes, labs, medications, specialist documentation — and query it conversationally. In some deployments ChatGPT is embedded directly in EHR workflows for pre-visit review and clinical timeline construction without leaving the patient chart. Access is read-only; the model writes nothing back.
