# Unsealed Filings Put Microsoft and OpenAI Internal Warnings on the Record

A newly unsealed brief in the New York Times' three-year-old copyright case quotes a Microsoft applied-science director calling AI training scraping "an astonishing theft of unprecedented proportions" and possibly the "largest theft of labor in human history"; OpenAI's ChatGPT head Nick Turley is quoted describing an "existential threat to publishers." The filing cites internal Microsoft data showing answer-engine results cut click-through to Times domains by as much as 93% versus traditional Bing search, and alleges mid-training datasets held more than 91,000 copies of plaintiff works. Microsoft says the quoted remarks reflect one employee's individual perspective.
