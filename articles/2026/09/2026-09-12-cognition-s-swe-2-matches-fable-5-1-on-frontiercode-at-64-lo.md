# Cognition's SWE-2 matches Fable 5.1 on FrontierCode at 64% lower cost

Cognition released SWE-2, post-trained with reinforcement learning from Moonshot AI's 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3. It scores 50.0% on Cognition's FrontierCode 1.1 Main benchmark — within one point of Anthropic's Fable 5.1 (50.9%) — at 64% lower cost, and leads the compared field on Terminal-Bench 2.1 at 92.8%. The notable weakness is Terminal-Bench 4 at 27.3%, roughly 30 points behind Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra, so long-horizon autonomous work is not where parity holds. There are no open weights and no standalone API: SWE-2 runs only inside Devin, and all comparison numbers are vendor-reported.
