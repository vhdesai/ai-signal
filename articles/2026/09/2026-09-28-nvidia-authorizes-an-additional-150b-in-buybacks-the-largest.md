# Nvidia authorizes an additional $150B in buybacks, the largest increase on record

Nvidia's board approved a further $150 billion under its existing repurchase program, taking the remaining authorization to $235 billion, to be executed through fiscal 2028. The company called it the largest share repurchase authorization increase in history; shares rose roughly 1.3% premarket on a $5.42T market cap. Jensen Huang framed it as cash generation from "a once-in-a-generation platform shift" — a signal Nvidia sees no near-term ceiling on AI demand, with hyperscaler capex projected to exceed $1.3 trillion by 2027.
