# Microsoft's Brad Smith endorses a mandated AI "emergency brake"

Microsoft Vice Chair and President Brad Smith said advanced AI systems automating critical infrastructure or autonomous operations should carry an emergency brake, and that shutdown authority should sit in more than one place — with the model developer, the cloud provider, and the organization deploying the agents. Smith said some regulation is inevitable and that he would not object to a rule requiring such a mechanism. Coming from the largest enterprise AI distributor, and landing the same day as the Australian agent breach, this is the clearest signal yet that shutdown authority becomes a contractual control point in cloud and agent agreements.
