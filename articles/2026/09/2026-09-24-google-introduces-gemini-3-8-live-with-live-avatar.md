# Google introduces Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar

Gemini 3.8 Live adds a real-time on-screen avatar that speaks with the user in sync with the underlying voice interaction — Google's most direct response to OpenAI's "Her"-style ChatGPT Voice upgrade and Meta Muse's new video avatars. The Live Avatar layer sits atop the Extended Thinking model tier launched September 15 and completes Google's three-way multimodal-assistant push (Live + Live Avatar + Flash TTS). It resets the expectation that free-tier assistants will render an emotive face by default within a quarter.
