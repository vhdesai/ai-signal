# Google DeepMind releases WeatherNext 3 with higher-resolution AI forecasting

Google DeepMind and Google Research released WeatherNext 3, an AI weather model that generates hourly global forecasts at resolutions down to five kilometers and is being integrated into Google products. TechCrunch reported that the model outperformed leading contenders on Operational WeatherBench across variables including temperature, wind speed, and humidity.
