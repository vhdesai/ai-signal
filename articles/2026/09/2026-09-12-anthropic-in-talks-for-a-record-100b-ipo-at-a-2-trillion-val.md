# Anthropic in talks for a record ~$100B IPO at a ~$2 trillion valuation, with Nvidia weighing a $10B anchor

Two people familiar with the matter told Reuters that Anthropic is seeking to raise as much as $100B in an offering that could value it near $2T, with Nvidia considering up to $10B as an anchor investor. That would be roughly 3.4x the current record IPO (Saudi Aramco, $29.4B) and more than double Anthropic's $965B post-money valuation from its $65B May round. Annualized revenue run rate passed $65B by end of July, up from about $9B at the end of 2025, and the valuation leans on internal projections of $190–200B of 2028 revenue. Terms remain under discussion; the listing is expected to complete before the November US midterms.
