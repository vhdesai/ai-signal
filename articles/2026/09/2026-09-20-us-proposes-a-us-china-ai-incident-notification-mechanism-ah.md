# US proposes a US–China AI incident notification mechanism ahead of Trump–Xi summit

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, emerging from talks with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng in New York, said the two sides discussed standing up a “U.S.-China AI Dialogue,” with the US proposing a notification mechanism for AI incidents rising “up to a national security level.” Bessent framed the rationale as moving “from opaque to more transparency between the number one and the number two AI powers in the world.” US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer confirmed that export controls on advanced AI chips and semiconductor equipment were not on the agenda. The proposal goes to Trump and Xi at their September 24 summit; notification timing and obligations remain undefined.
