# Pangram’s Max Spero on why AI detection is harder than “Real or Fake”

TechCrunch covered Pangram’s view that AI-generated-content detection is more complex than binary “real or fake” classification. The topic matters because provenance, policy enforcement, education integrity, and platform moderation all depend on error-prone detection signals. Leaders should expect detection to remain a probabilistic control, not a definitive governance layer.
