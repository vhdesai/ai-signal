# Google DeepMind launches the DeepMind Institute, proposes US-led frontier AI standards body

Google DeepMind launched the DeepMind Institute with Shane Legg, James Manyika, and Demis Hassabis as directors. The inaugural four essays cover AGI economic disruption, preserving human-readable model reasoning, human flourishing, and frontier-model evaluation. Hassabis proposes a US-led frontier AI standards body where developers would initially submit models voluntarily for review up to 30 days before release, eventually using independent held-out tests. A separate essay by Rohin Shah and Anca Dragan argues for limiting opaque serial depth to preserve monitorability. Together with OpenAI's disclosure framework and Anthropic's safety metrics, three frontier labs published safety mechanisms within roughly 36 hours of one another — a coordinated response to Amodei's slowdown call. - https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/google-deepmind-launches-institute-to-widen-the-agi-debate/
