# connectomics milestone: Mapping the complete male fruit fly brain

Google Research announced a connectomics milestone: mapping the complete male fruit fly brain using AI-assisted reconstruction and verification workflows. The work extends years of neural-mapping progress and highlights how AI can reduce manual correction costs in large scientific datasets.
