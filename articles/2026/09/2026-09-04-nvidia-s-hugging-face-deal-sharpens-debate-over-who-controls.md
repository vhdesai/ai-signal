# Nvidia's Hugging Face deal sharpens debate over who controls open AI infrastructure

VentureBeat analyzed Nvidia's acquisition of Hugging Face in the context of Stripe's purchase of OpenRouter, arguing that the infrastructure around open AI may become more commercially valuable than many individual models. The pattern suggests a shift from model scarcity toward control of routing, hosting, distribution, and developer workflows. For enterprises, the key watchpoint is whether nominally open-model ecosystems remain neutral as strategic buyers consolidate the surrounding platforms. SUPPLY CHAIN HARDWARE
