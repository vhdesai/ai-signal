# Oracle Begins Layoffs as Ellison Cancels $7.5B Share Sale; Severance Capped at 26 Weeks

Oracle has begun executing the $700M expanded restructuring, with severance capped at 26 weeks as mounting debt from an AI-infrastructure buildout reportedly reaching ~$95B pressures the P&L. Larry Ellison canceled plans to sell up to 50 million shares (~$7.5B) that had been disclosed via a trading plan the day before.
