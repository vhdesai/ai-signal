# MIT: ML-Designed Formulation Lets RNA Vaccines Survive Without Refrigeration

MIT’s Koch Institute, working with CSAIL, used a small-data machine learning algorithm to screen roughly 50 FDA-approved excipients, converging within a handful of iterations on lipid-nanoparticle formulations stable at room temperature for a year — or about 100°F for two months. Mouse immune responses matched a Moderna-style mRNA Covid vaccine.
