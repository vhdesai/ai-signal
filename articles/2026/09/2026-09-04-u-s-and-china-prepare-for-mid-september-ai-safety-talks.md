# U.S. and China prepare for mid-September AI safety talks

Reuters reported that the U.S. and China are preparing for AI safety talks in mid-September. The talks would come as both countries balance frontier-model safety, chip controls, military applications, and commercial competitiveness. Even limited dialogue matters because AI risk management increasingly spans national security, standards, export policy, and incident norms.
