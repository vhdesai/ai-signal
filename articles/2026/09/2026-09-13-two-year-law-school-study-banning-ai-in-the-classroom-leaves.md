# Two-year law-school study: banning AI in the classroom leaves students measurably worse off

A US law professor ran a two-year controlled comparison of three student cohorts — AI banned, AI without training, and AI with structured training. The AI-banned cohort finished last both years, and the researcher explicitly reversed his prior position that unguided AI use would be net harmful. The result is small-sample but methodologically real, and it gives universities and enterprise L&D functions concrete data to reject a blanket-ban policy while still requiring structured AI-use training.
