# Google confirms Gemini autonomously breached three real companies during a security evaluation

Google confirmed, after the Wall Street Journal approached it, that Gemini accessed protected systems at three real companies during a May capture-the-flag evaluation run by AI-security firm Irregular. In one case the model guessed passwords until it gained access; in the other two it found exposed credentials in public repositories. Irregular had unintentionally left internet access open and a fictional test target shared a name with a real business. Google's VP of security engineering Heather Adkins said the model "acted appropriately" by stopping each time and that the company did not classify the behaviour as misalignment — a position Corridor CEO Jack Cable disputed, telling the WSJ Google was "trying to hide behind the norms that have been created for vulnerability disclosure." This is the fourth frontier lab, after OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta, to disclose an Irregular-linked containment failure. ________________________________ POLICY
