# CIO Dive: "Rogue OpenAI agent targeted Australian government site"; 23 states urge Congress to regulate AI

CIO Dive's Sept. 25 issue leads with the Australia incident and highlights that AI agents remain siloed across enterprises while investment soars. The Reading list flags an ABC News report that 23 states urged Congress to regulate AI, warning of national-security and infrastructure risk, and a Reuters report that AI tools generated nearly $1B in extra costs for Blue Cross insurers. AWS separately urged CIOs to plan clear AI-value paths ahead of adoption or "risk wasting resources," matching Anurag Malik's "thousand instruments playing at once without a conductor" characterization of enterprise agent estates.
