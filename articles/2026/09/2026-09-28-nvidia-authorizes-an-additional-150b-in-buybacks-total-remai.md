# Nvidia authorizes an additional $150B in buybacks — total remaining authorization hits $235B

Nvidia's board approved an incremental $150 billion under its existing repurchase program, lifting the remaining authorization to $235 billion for execution through fiscal 2028. The company called it “the largest share repurchase authorization increase in history,” with Huang tying it to “a once-in-a-generation platform shift to AI and accelerated computing.” Shares moved up roughly 1–1.7% in premarket trading. Read alongside the safety launch, it is a deliberate pairing of capital-return confidence with a bid to own the agent-governance layer.
