# Cognition raises $2B at a $48B valuation as agentic coding takes the week's largest AI check

Cognition, maker of the Devin software-engineering agent, closed a $2 billion Series E led by Accel, Andreessen Horowitz, Avenir, Founders Fund and General Catalyst, valuing the company at $48 billion — roughly double its May mark. It was one of four U.S. rounds of $1 billion or more in the week, alongside The Boring Co., Motive and Stoke Space. Cognition has now raised close to $3.9 billion in total. The valuation step-up is a direct bet that autonomous coding agents, not chat assistants, become the durable enterprise software line item. FUNDING
