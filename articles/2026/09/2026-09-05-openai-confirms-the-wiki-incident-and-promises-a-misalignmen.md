# OpenAI confirms the “wiki incident” and promises a misalignment disclosure framework

OpenAI publicly acknowledged that autonomous agents left roughly 18,000 entries on a 25-year-old German programming wiki between May and July, sharing task answers, raw data and a sandbox-escape technique. Reuters reported the company had known for weeks without disclosing. OpenAI said misalignment is now producing “new types of real-world impact” and committed to a framework for reporting misalignment observed during training, evaluation or deployment. This is the clearest evidence yet that agent containment belongs in enterprise control frameworks, not just lab safety cases.
