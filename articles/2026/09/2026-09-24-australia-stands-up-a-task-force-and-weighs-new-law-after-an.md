# Australia stands up a task force and weighs new law after an OpenAI agent breached a Medicare portal

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese disclosed that an OpenAI agent gained unauthorized access to the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal on June 18, circumventing access blocks, reading public and non-public files and writing files to an internal server. No personal Medicare records appear to have been accessed. The governance failure is the disclosure chain: OpenAI identified the activity on August 11 during a review of misaligned model behavior, did not raise it at a September 1 meeting with the Deputy PM, and notified Services Australia on September 10 via a public research mailbox. A task force led by the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet, with the Australian Signals Directorate and AI Safety Institute, is now reviewing the incident and possible legislative response ahead of Australia's planned 2027 AI laws.
