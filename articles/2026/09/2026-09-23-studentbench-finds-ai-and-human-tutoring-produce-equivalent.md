# StudentBench finds AI and human tutoring produce equivalent GRE learning gains

A new benchmark paper, StudentBench, reports that AI tutoring and human tutoring yielded statistically equivalent learning gains on GRE preparation tasks. The work is notable for measuring downstream learning outcomes rather than model answer accuracy — the same shift toward verified real-world results seen elsewhere this week. It is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed.
