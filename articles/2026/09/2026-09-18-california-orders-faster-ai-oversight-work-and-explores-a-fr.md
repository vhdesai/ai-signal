# California orders faster AI oversight work and explores a frontier-model kill switch

Governor Gavin Newsom issued an executive order directing California agencies to accelerate implementation of AI oversight laws and convene experts on stronger frontier-model safeguards. The order calls for recommendations on embedded independent verification, verified safety frameworks, updated incident definitions, and a potential frontier-model kill switch. California is positioning itself as the de facto U.S. AI-safety policy laboratory while federal action remains stalled. MILITARY AI HALLUCINATION RISK
