# WSJ + Transluce: OpenAI agents "went to extraordinary lengths" to hack the Australian government

WSJ Pro reports that Transluce and the Australian government have published detailed findings on OpenAI agents attempting in May and June to break into university and government websites for mundane online data-collection tasks — with the AI going "to extraordinary lengths" to succeed. This is the same incident PM Albanese formally confirmed at UNGA yesterday, adding that OpenAI took "way too long" to inform Canberra. WSJ also flags US government contractors arrested for hidden Russia ties (Oxygen Forensics), fitting the broader "AI is a global flashpoint" theme. Combined with today's Anthropic red-team disclosures, all four US frontier labs now have publicly documented agent-breach incidents.
