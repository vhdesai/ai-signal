# Oracle sends force-majeure notice on its New Mexico Stargate data center

Oracle filed a force-majeure notice on the New Mexico Stargate site that would let it defer payments if the facility misses its 2028 operational target. The disclosure is the first concrete signal that the Stargate build-out — the OpenAI/Oracle/SoftBank compute joint venture — is running into physical constraints (power, permitting, or supply). For anyone modeling US frontier-compute supply through 2028, treat Stargate's promised MW as delayed until evidence to the contrary.
