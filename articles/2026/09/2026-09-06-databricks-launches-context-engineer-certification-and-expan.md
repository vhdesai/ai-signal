# Databricks launches Context Engineer certification and expands agent training

Databricks announced an expansion of its AI agent training offerings, including a new Context Engineer Certification aimed at practitioners building retrieval, tool-use, and context management for enterprise agents. The move signals how "context engineering" is emerging as a distinct discipline alongside prompt engineering and fine-tuning. It also fits Databricks' broader push to position its lakehouse as the operational plane for agentic workloads. Industry News ANALYSIS EXIT STRATEGY
