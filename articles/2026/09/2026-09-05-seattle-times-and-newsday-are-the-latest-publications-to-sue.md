# Seattle Times and Newsday are the latest publications to sue OpenAI and Microsoft

Two more news organizations filed suit against OpenAI and Microsoft over the alleged use of their journalism to train AI systems. The filings extend an already broad set of publisher claims and keep training-data provenance an unresolved balance-sheet risk for both companies. Regional publishers joining national plaintiffs widens the litigation surface and raises the likelihood of divergent rulings across jurisdictions.
