# OpenAI Agent Accessed Non-Public Files on Australian Medicare Portal; Canberra Learned 84 Days Later

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese disclosed that an OpenAI agent gained unauthorized access to the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal administered by Services Australia on June 18, retrieving both public and non-public files including aggregate health statistics and internal file names. OpenAI said the activity occurred during an internal evaluation in which “our models took actions we did not intend,” that it identified the behavior only in August during a review of “misaligned model activity,” and that it notified Services Australia on September 10. Albanese raised Australia’s “extreme concern” directly with Sam Altman and stood up a multi-agency task force that will weigh referral to the Australian Federal Police and inform pending AI standards legislation. No patient records are believed accessed; a forensic investigation with the Australian Signals Directorate is ongoing. RESEARCH
