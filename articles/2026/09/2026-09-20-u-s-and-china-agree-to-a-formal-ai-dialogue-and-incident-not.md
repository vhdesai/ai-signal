# U.S. and China Agree to a Formal AI Dialogue and Incident-Notification Channel Ahead of Trump–Xi Summit

After an eight-hour bilateral at JPMorgan's New York headquarters, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer, and Chinese Vice-Premier He Lifeng announced an official U.S.–China AI dialogue, including a proposed AI threat-notification system for incidents that rise to a national-security level. Bessent framed it as "moving from opaque to more transparency between the number one and the number two AI powers." Greer noted that advanced-chip export controls are not on the AI track's agenda; Xinhua's readout acknowledged only that AI was discussed.
