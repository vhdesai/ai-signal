# Anthropic Preparing "Claude Money" for Personal Finance

TestingCatalog reports Anthropic is preparing a "Claude Money" product for personal-finance workflows — budgeting, spending analysis, and money-agent tasks. If confirmed, it would put Claude in direct competition with OpenAI's rumored consumer finance integrations and with Intuit/QuickBooks agentic offerings. The move suggests Anthropic is following OpenAI into direct consumer verticals even as Amodei is publicly leading the safety-slowdown call — a tension boards should be tracking.
