# Exclusive: Anthropic Weighs a New Model Release Before the IPO

Three sources told Reuters that Anthropic is considering rolling out a new model to counter OpenAI's momentum since the September 3 launch of GPT-6 Astra. A release would land immediately ahead of the expected IPO and could support the valuation narrative. Anthropic has not confirmed any release, and the reporting rests entirely on unnamed sources.
