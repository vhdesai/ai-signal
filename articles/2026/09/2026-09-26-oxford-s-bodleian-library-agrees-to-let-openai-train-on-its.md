# Oxford's Bodleian Library agrees to let OpenAI train on its collections

The Guardian reports Oxford University's Bodleian Library — one of the oldest research libraries in the world — has agreed to let OpenAI train its AI models on the library's collections. It's a landmark bibliographic-data deal that gives OpenAI historical and academic corpus access at a moment when the company is fighting news-publisher copyright suits from the Seattle Times, Newsday, and The New York Times. It's also a notable diplomatic move given the reported White House ask to withhold OpenAI models from the UK — the training-data deal proceeds independently of model-access diplomatic frictions.
