# DOJ urges judge to rule for OpenAI, Microsoft in New York Times lawsuit

The Washington Post reports that the U.S. Department of Justice urged a judge to rule for OpenAI and Microsoft in litigation brought by The New York Times over AI training and copyright. The case remains strategically important because it could shape the legal boundary between model training, fair use, licensing, and publisher compensation. A ruling favorable to AI developers would lower legal uncertainty, while a narrower outcome could accelerate licensing and data-provenance controls. URL behind paywall.
