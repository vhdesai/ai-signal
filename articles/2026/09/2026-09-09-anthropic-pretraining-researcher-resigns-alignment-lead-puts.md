# Anthropic pretraining researcher resigns; alignment lead puts extinction risk above 10%

Jacob Coxon, 27, who spent three years on pretraining research at OpenAI and then Anthropic, resigned on September 8 and left the industry, writing that neither company is "acting responsibly" and that both are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence." Evan Hubinger, who leads alignment science at Anthropic, responded publicly that he personally puts the odds of AI killing all humans above 10% within the decade, and that Anthropic does "not yet have a plan to solve alignment for superintelligence" and is "not clearly on track to." The resignation lands as Anthropic prepares a public listing; internal dissent at this seniority is now a disclosure and diligence factor.
