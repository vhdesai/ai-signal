# The Information: SpaceX overhauls data-center build-out, potentially slowing expansion

The Information reports SpaceX has overhauled its data-center build-out plans, a shift that could slow near-term capacity expansion tied to its Starlink and internal AI workloads. Details on scope, delayed sites, and revised delivery timelines are limited but the piece underscores how quickly hyperscaler-adjacent players are rebalancing capex against power, siting, and supply-chain constraints already visible at Google and Meta. For executives modeling AI-infrastructure supply through 2027, treat SpaceX's plans as a moving variable rather than a locked commitment.
