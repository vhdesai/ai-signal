# Apple opens claims for $250M Siri AI delay settlement

The claims website went live for Apple's $250 million settlement of a US class action over the delayed launch of Siri AI, which Apple agreed to in May. Eligible iPhone owners are estimated to receive around $25 each. Apple shipped the rebuilt Siri AI as part of the next generation of Apple Intelligence earlier in September 2026. The settlement is a useful marker of legal exposure attaching to publicly announced AI capabilities that slip.
