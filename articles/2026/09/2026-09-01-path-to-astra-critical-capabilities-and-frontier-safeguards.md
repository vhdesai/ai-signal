# Path to Astra: critical capabilities and frontier safeguards

OpenAI said Astra is its first model to meet the “Critical” cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework, with stronger safeguards planned for release. This is an important governance milestone: frontier capability is being explicitly gated by risk classification and release controls. For enterprise buyers, it underscores that model access, monitoring, and contractual safeguards will become more material as capability thresholds rise. ANTHROPICSECURITYAI TRAINING
