# Nvidia's Equity Portfolio Reaches $99B as It Becomes a Systemic AI Financier

Nvidia disclosed $99B in equity investments as of July 26, up from roughly $7B a year earlier, with more than $40B committed during 2026 alone. CFO Colette Kress said nearly $50B has gone into frontier AI labs, including a $30B commitment to OpenAI's $110B round, alongside stakes in CoreWeave, Nebius, Intel (now valued near $30B) and $6.5B across photonics vendors. Analysts framed the strategy as vendor financing that expands demand while reinforcing switching costs around CUDA and NVLink. For buyers, it concentrates counterparty exposure: Nvidia is increasingly both supplier and shareholder to its own customers. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/nvidia-ai-investments-99-billion.html
