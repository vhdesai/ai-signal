# Meta Connect: camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio, $1,300 VR glasses, and a Gen 3 refresh

At Connect in Menlo Park, Meta unveiled its first camera-free smart glasses — Ray-Ban Meta Audio at $349 — dropping the camera entirely in favor of an audio-only assistant to blunt the privacy objections that dogged earlier models. Meta also announced VR glasses shipping in the spring at $1,300 and a third-generation Ray-Ban Meta at $449 with a slimmer design and longer battery. Mark Zuckerberg framed the lineup around his thesis that glasses become the primary interface to AI.
