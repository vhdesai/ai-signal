# NVIDIA launches DSX Ready to qualify power and cooling hardware for AI factories

NVIDIA introduced DSX Ready, a qualification program certifying that partner products meet applicable NVIDIA DSX AI-factory reference-design requirements, launching with two categories: battery energy storage systems and coolant distribution units. Initial qualified vendors include Hitachi Energy, LG Energy Solution and Tesla for BESS, and LG Electronics, LiquidStack and Vertiv for CDUs. NVIDIA is explicit that qualification does not replace site-level engineering or imply site-level stability. The program is a procurement-side response to power, cooling, water and grid constraints now shaping what operators can physically deploy.
