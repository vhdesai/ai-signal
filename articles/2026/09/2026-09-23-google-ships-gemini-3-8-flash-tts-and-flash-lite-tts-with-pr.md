# Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS with prompt-designed voices

Google released two production text-to-speech models into the Gemini API and AI Studio: Flash TTS for creative direction and character work, and Flash-Lite TTS for high-volume dubbing and voice agents. The release replaces preset selection with voice design — describing a voice in natural language across 100+ languages and dialects — plus a library of 2,000+ production voices and 30-second voice replication gated by verbal consent verification, SynthID watermarking and C2PA credentials. Voice replication is withheld in Illinois, Texas, the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and India. This is Google's third audio release in under a month. AO CONTEXT
