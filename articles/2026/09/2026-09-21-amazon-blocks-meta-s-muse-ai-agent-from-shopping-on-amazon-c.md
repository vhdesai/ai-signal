# Amazon blocks Meta's Muse AI agent from shopping on Amazon.com

TechCrunch reported that users of Meta's Muse assistant received an error saying the agent's access violated Amazon's Conditions of Use when trying to buy goods on Amazon. The standoff highlights two issues that will shape agentic commerce: large platforms may prefer their own agents, and merchants may not want external agents creating customer-service, fraud, or vendor-liability exposure. This is an early example of AI agents encountering platform-level gatekeeping rather than purely technical limits. Model Releases IMAGE MODEL OPEN WEIGHTS
