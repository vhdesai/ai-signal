# Nvidia agrees to roughly $13 billion deal for AI platform Hugging Face

Bloomberg reports that Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face in a deal valued around $13 billion, with overlapping coverage from CNBC, The New York Times, Financial Times, Axios, and Yahoo Finance. Strategically, this would give Nvidia a deeper position in model distribution and developer workflow, not just accelerators.
