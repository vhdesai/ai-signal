# Amodei says "the industry lied" about AI risks; Altman and Musk publicly back a slowdown

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei told CBS News that "for too long the industry lied" about AI risks and, in a Saturday essay, proposed a three-step slowdown for frontier development. Sam Altman publicly agreed the pace is now a "primary topic" at OpenAI and told Fortune "right now would be an ill-advised moment to go public" — effectively confirming OpenAI will not IPO in 2026. Elon Musk tweeted "Dario is right." Axios, The New York Times, DW, Forbes, and Reuters confirm Demis Hassabis has also joined the call, marking an unusually coordinated public position from the leaders of the three top US frontier labs, and one likely to reprice the AI trade.
