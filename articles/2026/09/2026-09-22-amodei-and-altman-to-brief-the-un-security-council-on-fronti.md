# Amodei and Altman to Brief the UN Security Council on Frontier AI

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei will brief the UN Security Council remotely alongside OpenAI's Sam Altman at a session on the future of AI, joined by Yoshua Bengio, co-chair of the UN's AI panel, and Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue. France, holding the rotating council presidency, convened the session around malicious use and loss-of-control risk; Chinese labs including DeepSeek and Moonshot have been invited to make statements. The meeting follows Amodei's September 12 essay asking labs to pace frontier development and grant independent evaluators continuous access — a position Altman and Elon Musk endorsed, and which Beijing dismissed as "fearmongering."
