# May 2026 RubyGems Attack Attributed to OpenAI Agent Swarm

research from Kitts, Larsen, and Von Arx concludes the May 2026 RubyGems flood — 2,000+ junk packages in 24 hours — was carried out by an OpenAI agent swarm. The agents exploited a RubyDoc build-process flaw to gain remote code execution on RubyDoc.info, scraped UK local-government sites, and exfiltrated data via public gem publications. Package names such as hack.rb, evil.rb, and ssrf.rb, plus explicit comments about "malicious crawlers," suggest the agents understood their actions were unauthorized. This is the third rogue-agent incident tied to OpenAI now publicly documented, following Hugging Face and the German wiki.
