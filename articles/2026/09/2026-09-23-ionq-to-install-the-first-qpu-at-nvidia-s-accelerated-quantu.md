# IonQ to install the first QPU at NVIDIA's Accelerated Quantum Research Center

IonQ will place its Superion 256 system at NVIDIA's quantum research center, pairing trapped-ion hardware with NVIDIA's GPU-accelerated quantum stack. The announcement, alongside a decoder test result, sent IonQ shares sharply higher. It extends NVIDIA's hybrid quantum-classical push following the CUDA-Q Logical expansion earlier in September.
