# Cerebras announces 200MW European AI compute expansion

Cerebras Systems said it will accelerate its European expansion with 200MW of AI compute capacity to come online by end of 2027. The announcement extends Cerebras's recent capacity buildout, which already includes a 165MW Finland deployment revealed earlier this month. Related reporting notes Cerebras is sitting on a $25.4 billion backlog, much of it driven by a single OpenAI agreement — a reminder that AI-inference capacity commitments are concentrating around a few very large customers.
