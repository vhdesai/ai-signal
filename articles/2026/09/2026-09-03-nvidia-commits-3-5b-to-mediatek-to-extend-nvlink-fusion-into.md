# Nvidia Commits $3.5B to MediaTek to Extend NVLink Fusion Into Custom Silicon

Nvidia is investing $3.5B in MediaTek convertible bonds while MediaTek adopts NVLink Fusion for custom AI accelerators and other computing platforms, spanning cloud, local AI systems and automotive. The strategic logic is that Nvidia remains embedded through interconnect, memory and rack-scale architecture even where it does not supply the primary processor.
