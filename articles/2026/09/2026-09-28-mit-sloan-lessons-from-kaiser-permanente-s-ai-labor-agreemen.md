# MIT Sloan: Lessons From Kaiser Permanente’s AI Labor Agreement

An MIT report details how Kaiser Permanente and the Alliance of Health Care Unions reached a landmark agreement embedding worker input directly into AI deployment decisions. It is one of the first empirical templates for AI governance negotiated inside a large employer rather than imposed by regulation.
