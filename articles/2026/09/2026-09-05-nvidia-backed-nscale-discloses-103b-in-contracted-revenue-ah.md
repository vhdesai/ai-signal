# Nvidia-backed Nscale discloses $103B in contracted revenue ahead of IPO

Compute provider Nscale disclosed $103 billion in contracted revenue — roughly a doubling of its backlog — following a reported $45 billion agreement with Anthropic, ahead of a planned New York listing. The figure is a clean read on how frontier-lab compute commitments are underwriting an entire new tier of AI infrastructure providers. For anyone modeling AI infrastructure exposure, backlog concentration in a handful of lab counterparties is the risk to watch.
