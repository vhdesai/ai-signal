# Sanders and Casar Introduce a Bill to Permanently Ban Artificial Superintelligence

Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar announced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act, which would permanently prohibit development and deployment of artificial superintelligence, temporarily pause certain advanced AI development until federal safety rules exist, and direct the U.S. to pursue international agreements to the same end. Reported penalties include prison terms of up to 20 years. Passage is unlikely, but the framing matters: the U.S. debate has moved from regulating capabilities to prohibiting a category. REGULATORY
