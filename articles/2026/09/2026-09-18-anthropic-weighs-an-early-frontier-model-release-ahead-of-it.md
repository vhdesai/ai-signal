# Anthropic weighs an early frontier model release ahead of its IPO

Reuters reported, citing three sources, that Anthropic is considering rolling out a new AI model to counter OpenAI's momentum since the September 3 launch of GPT-6 Astra, ahead of an expected IPO. Ramp data cited in the report puts Astra at roughly 13% of tracked enterprise AI spending versus about 8% for Claude Fable, and OpenRouter spend on OpenAI models exceeded Anthropic's for the first time in more than two and a half years. The deliberation sits awkwardly beside CEO Dario Amodei's September 12 essay calling for an industry-wide slowdown; Anthropic declined to comment. INFRASTRUCTURE DATA
