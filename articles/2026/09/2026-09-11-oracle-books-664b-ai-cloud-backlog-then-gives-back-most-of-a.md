# Oracle Books $664B AI Cloud Backlog, Then Gives Back Most of a 7% Rally

Oracle's fiscal Q1 put remaining performance obligations at $664 billion, up $209 billion year over year, on more than $30 billion of new AI cloud contracts and 121% growth in cloud infrastructure revenue to $7.4 billion. Shares opened up roughly 7% to $165.80 and closed near $153.74, a gain of about 0.6%. The reversal is the story: a backlog roughly 34x quarterly revenue is only worth what Oracle can physically deliver, and negative free cash flow of $5.4 billion against $90–95 billion of planned annual capex keeps the conversion risk front and center.
