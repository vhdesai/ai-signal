# Anthropic Targets a November IPO as Revenue Surges

Anthropic is now aiming to launch its IPO in November, later than the October debut many investors had expected, according to WSJ sources. Revenue is reported to be surging into the offering, with IPO marketing expected to begin in mid-October. Timing remains contingent on market conditions and SEC review, so treat the month as a target rather than a date.
