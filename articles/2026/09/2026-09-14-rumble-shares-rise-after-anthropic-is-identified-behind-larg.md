# Rumble shares rise after Anthropic is identified behind large compute deal

Blockonomi reported that Rumble shares climbed after The Information identified Anthropic as the AI client behind a six-year, $13.7 billion computing services contract. The deal reportedly involves Rumble's Quake AI infrastructure, roughly 22,000 Nvidia Hopper GPUs obtained through its Northern Data acquisition, and capacity at a Georgia data-center facility. The watchpoint is financing quality: long-duration AI compute contracts can rapidly re-rate infrastructure companies, but dilution, buildout risk, and utilization assumptions remain material.
