# Google DeepMind safety researcher resigns as Google moves AI Responsibility team out of DeepMind

Firstpost, Moneycontrol, and Storyboard18 report another Google DeepMind AI safety researcher has resigned, publicly citing a "terrifying chance" AI could cause immense harm. Times of India separately reports Google is moving its AI Responsibility team out of DeepMind entirely starting this month — a structural change critics say weakens safety integration inside model development. Combined with the Amodei/Altman/Hassabis slowdown call and last week's Anthropic exits, coordinated senior-safety concern is now a durable pattern rather than a single-lab story.
