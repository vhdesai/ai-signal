# Meta Oversight Board calls Meta's AI-deepfake rules "fundamentally inadequate"

Meta's Oversight Board issued a sharply critical assessment of the company's policies on AI-generated deepfakes, faulting both the substance of Meta's manipulated-media rules and their enforcement across platforms. The finding arrives as generative video tools make convincing synthetic media cheap to produce at scale, ahead of election cycles. Board decisions on individual cases are binding on Meta; its broader policy recommendations are not. - https://www.engadget.com/2260894/oversight-board-says-metas-rules-ai-deepfakes-fundamentally-inadequate/
