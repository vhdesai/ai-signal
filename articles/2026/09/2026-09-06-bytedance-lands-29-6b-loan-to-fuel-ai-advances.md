# ByteDance lands $29.6B loan to fuel AI advances

PYMNTS reported that ByteDance secured a $29.6 billion unsecured loan from nearly 30 banks, citing Reuters reporting that the financing will largely support AI initiatives and overseas projects. The scale and unsecured structure indicate strong lender confidence in ByteDance's credit profile despite the capital intensity of AI infrastructure. Strategically, this underscores that major AI buildouts are increasingly financed through balance-sheet engineering as much as operating cash flow. EARNINGS AI MOATS
