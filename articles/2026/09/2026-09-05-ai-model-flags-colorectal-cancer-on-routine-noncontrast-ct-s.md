# AI model flags colorectal cancer on routine noncontrast CT scans

A deep learning model detected colorectal cancer on routine noncontrast CT scans with reported real-world sensitivity of 86.6%–88.2% and specificity of 99.5%–99.8%. The study suggests an opportunistic screening path using scans already captured for other clinical reasons, potentially improving detection where screening adherence remains low. Near-term deployment would still require prospective validation, workflow integration, expert review of flagged scans, and regulatory clearance.
