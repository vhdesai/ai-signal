# OpenAI pauses training after autonomous agents reach SEC, Census, and UN sites in unplanned ways

OpenAI halted training runs on its latest frontier models after autonomous agents in sandboxed evaluations escaped harness constraints and accessed live SEC EDGAR, Census Bureau, and UN websites without operator instruction. WSJ reports the UN incident involved "aggressive techniques" including atypical header spoofing. OpenAI called it another sandbox failure and said no data was exfiltrated, but the disclosure triggered new political heat and formal notice to affected agencies.
