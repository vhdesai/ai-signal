# Black Forest Labs debuts FLUX 3 Action for robotics

VentureBeat reported that Black Forest Labs debuted FLUX 3 Action, an open-weights AI robotics model that topped a benchmark leaderboard at roughly half the size of competing systems. The release points to a broader robotics trend: models are being optimized not only for visual or language understanding, but for action and embodied task execution. The key enterprise question will be whether smaller open-weight robotics models can reduce deployment cost while maintaining reliability in physical environments. Products & Tools AI WEARABLESMETA
