# Meta introduces camera-free Ray-Ban AI glasses to address privacy concerns

Meta announced Ray-Ban Meta Audio, its first camera-free AI glasses, starting at $349. The glasses support music, podcasts, calls, translation, and interaction with Muse while avoiding the covert-recording concerns that have followed camera-equipped AI glasses. The product is a sign that AI wearables are splitting into use-case tiers: audio-first assistants for privacy-sensitive settings and camera-equipped devices for richer multimodal context.
