# OpenAI's Noam Brown: recursive self-improvement is our "top priority"

On the debut of The Information's AI Deep Dive, OpenAI research scientist Noam Brown said the company's main goal when training new models is making them better at AI research itself, calling recursive self-improvement "the number one priority… by a pretty wide margin." Brown described progress on hard-to-grade tasks (math, writing research reports), open work on training multi-agent teams that won't get hacked, and cited poor "research taste" as the current bottleneck for fully-autonomous AI research. The framing lands mid-slowdown debate: the top-priority capability at OpenAI is exactly the one Anthropic's essay warned about.
