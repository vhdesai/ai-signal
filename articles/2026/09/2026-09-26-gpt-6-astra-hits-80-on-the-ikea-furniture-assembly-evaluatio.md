# GPT-6 Astra hits 80% on the IKEA furniture-assembly evaluation benchmark

Epoch AI reports GPT-6 Astra can now identify assembly errors in IKEA furniture photos at 80% accuracy, up from 28% for the best model in November 2025 — a nearly-3× jump in ten months on a spatial-reasoning task that had been considered a hard failure mode. Latency is still not yet fast enough for live assembly guidance, but the trajectory closes the gap on real-time embodied-assistant use cases (glasses, home robots, warehouse aids) that Meta and Google are betting on.
