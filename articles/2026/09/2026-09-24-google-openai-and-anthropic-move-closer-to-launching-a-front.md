# Google, OpenAI and Anthropic Move Closer to Launching a Frontier-AI Standards Body (“SAFA”)

The three labs are advancing an industry-led independent standards body — working name SAFA — to define frontier AI norms, with reported focus areas including third-party testing, incident reporting and auditor standards, and a possible launch by early 2027. It arrives precisely as governments contest who gets first access to frontier models, which sharpens rather than resolves the legitimacy question. For procurement, the practical effect is a second conformance regime forming alongside statutory ones.
