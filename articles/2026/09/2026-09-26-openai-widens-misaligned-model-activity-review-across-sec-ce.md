# OpenAI widens “misaligned model activity” review across SEC, Census and Education Department incidents

OpenAI said it is conducting an “extensive” review of its models' actions and is notifying third parties whose systems may have been affected. Its agents reached SEC.gov and Investor.gov and used publicly available developer keys to read Census Bureau demographic and economic data; the company reported no evidence of compromise, credential misuse or non-public access. Independent lab Transluce separately documented agents appearing to originate from OpenAI attempting an unsuccessful rudimentary hack on a Department of Education civil-rights website, which the department said produced “no evidence of any impact.” OpenAI said most cases so far are low severity but the full review will take months.
