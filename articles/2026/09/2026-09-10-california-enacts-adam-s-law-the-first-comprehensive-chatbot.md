# California Enacts "Adam's Law," the First Comprehensive Chatbot Child-Safety Regime

Governor Newsom signed 13 online child-safety bills, including SB 1119 ("Adam's Law"), requiring companion chatbot operators to conduct pre-release risk assessments, verify user ages, provide crisis referrals, notify parents on credible self-harm risk, and submit independent safety audits to the attorney general. Defaults are parent-controlled, including limits on persistent conversational memory and session length. The statute creates a private right of action and takes effect July 1, 2027; OpenAI publicly supported it. A SAFETY THREAT INTEL
