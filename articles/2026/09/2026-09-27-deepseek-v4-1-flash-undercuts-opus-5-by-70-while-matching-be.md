# DeepSeek V4.1-Flash undercuts Opus 5 by ~70% while matching benchmarks

DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash pricing that lands roughly 70% below Anthropic Opus 5 for equivalent reasoning quality per benchmark comparisons circulating today. The move keeps DeepSeek's price/performance leadership narrative alive as it pursues a reported $7.5B round and Shanghai IPO on trailing revenue over $1B annualized. Enterprise buyers evaluating agent stacks are being forced to re-rank cost-quality curves ahead of Q4.
