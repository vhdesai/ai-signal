# OpenAI Says 80–90% of Its Research Already Targets GPT-7 and Beyond

Boris Power, OpenAI’s Head of Applied Research, told the Fellows Forum that 80–90% of the company’s research is directed at GPT-7, GPT-8, and later generations, on the view that generational jumps rather than intra-generation tuning create most of the value. Power characterized within-generation upgrades as deliberately short-horizon bets driven by specialized training data, and said they are viewed internally as "extremely shortsighted" despite their iteration value. He argued the binding constraint on assistants today is onboarding rather than model quality — most ChatGPT users do not know what the tool can already do. For enterprise planners, this implies thinner incremental gains between point releases and larger step changes at generation boundaries — and it suggests that internal AI-adoption budgets need to shift toward enablement, not toward chasing point-release upgrades.
