# Nvidia-Backed Firmus Seeks Up to $5B in an ASX Float at a $10.5B Valuation

Nvidia- and Blackstone-backed AI data-centre developer Firmus Technologies is seeking up to $5 billion on the Australian Securities Exchange — one of the largest floats the exchange has handled — at a $10.5 billion valuation, roughly double its $5.5 billion mark in April. Reuters puts the expected timing at end of October. “OpenAI signed a multi-year compute contract across two Malaysian sites last week, becoming the anchor customer and pushing Firmus past 900MW of contracted capacity,” with flagship Project Southgate targeting 1.6GW across five Australian sites by 2028. Neocloud capacity is now financeable in public markets outside the US, which widens the pool of compute suppliers competing with hyperscalers for anchor tenants.
