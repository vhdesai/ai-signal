# Anthropic's founders move to lock in 50.1% voting control ahead of IPO

Anthropic is asking shareholders to approve a governance structure that would give its seven co-founders a combined 50.1% of the vote on most corporate matters. The move mirrors the dual-class control structures Meta, Google, and Snap put in place pre-IPO, and lands as Anthropic prepares for a November listing on the back of $517B in disclosed compute commitments in 11 months. It is the clearest signal yet that the founders view the "pace-the-frontier" ideological line as a governance question, not just a strategy one. INDUSTRY
