# How the FDA is building a secure, AI-ready data foundation on Databricks for Government

Databricks detailed how the FDA built HALO, a governed enterprise data platform for regulated AI workloads. The FDA migrated more than 5,000 users and 8,000 jobs and pipelines with zero downtime, consolidated more than 40 data sources, and reported improvements in query performance, cost, provisioning time, and operational overhead. The story is a practical blueprint for AI deployment in high-control environments: governance and platform readiness precede model value. AI DATA CENTERSCEREBRASEUROPE
