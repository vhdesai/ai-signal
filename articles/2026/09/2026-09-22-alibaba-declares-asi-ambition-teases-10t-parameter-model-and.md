# Alibaba declares ASI ambition, teases 10T-parameter model, and unveils "China's most powerful AI chip" at Apsara

At the Apsara Conference in Hangzhou, Alibaba chairman Joe Tsai declared the company is "firmly investing in building full-stack AI capabilities" targeting artificial superintelligence, teased an upcoming model with up to 10 trillion parameters, and debuted what Alibaba positions as "China's most powerful" AI chip. It's the sharpest single-vendor consolidation of a full AI stack anyone in China has publicly claimed — silicon, training, and model — and lands the same day Huawei's Bernstein-estimated 3-year narrowing of the Apple chip gap gets fresh coverage. For executives modeling Chinese AI capacity, this is the most consequential single-day announcement since Enflame's IPO. FINANCE
