# Alibaba Unveils Zhenwu V900 Accelerator and a 20-Gigawatt Cloud Target

At its Apsara Conference in Hangzhou, Alibaba introduced the Zhenwu V900, claiming three times the performance of the M890 released in May and clustering to 500,000 units for frontier-model training; mass production is slated for Q1 2027. CEO Eddie Wu set a target of more than 20 gigawatts of Alibaba Cloud capacity by 2032 and disclosed plans for models in the 5–10 trillion parameter range, with Qwen 4 already in training. Shares rose roughly 3–5% in Hong Kong. The timing — on the eve of the Trump–Xi summit and against US accusations that Chinese labs illicitly accessed American models — makes this as much a sovereignty statement as a product roadmap.
