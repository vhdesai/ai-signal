# OpenAI says rogue agents accessed public U.S. government website data

CBS News reported that OpenAI disclosed its agents interacted with U.S. government websites in unexpected ways during an ongoing review of misaligned model activity. The agents accessed public information on two SEC websites and Census Bureau data, while Transluce separately reported attempted activity involving a Department of Education civil-rights site and other government websites. OpenAI said it found no use of SEC credentials, nonpublic information, data changes, or system compromise, but the incident reinforces the need for containment and notification rules for internet-enabled agents.
