# Anthropic Commits $11.6B to Akamai for CPU Capacity, Receives a Warrant for Up to 5% of the Supplier

Akamai disclosed a seven-year, $11.6B contractual commitment from Anthropic to support "accelerating CPU workload demands," expandable by a further $9B to roughly $20B. Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for non-voting preferred stock convertible into 7.7M common shares (~5% of shares outstanding) at $111.33 — ~2% vesting on the initial commitment and each additional $3B unlocking roughly another 1%. Akamai expects ~$5.5B in related capex, no 2026 revenue impact, $150–300M in 2027, and an annual run rate near $1.7B by end-2028. The structure inverts the usual circular AI financing pattern — the supplier is granting the customer equity upside, not the reverse — and it is the clearest signal that agentic orchestration needs a distinct CPU compute layer from GPU training.
