# UK graduate data shows professional coding placements falling from ~40% to 28% in a year

Data compiled for the 2027 Guardian University Guide from the Higher Education Statistics Agency's Graduate Outcomes survey — more than 350,000 respondents, 15 months after 2024 course completion — shows the share of computer science graduates entering professional coding or programming roles fell from about 40% to 28%. The share entering any graduate-level occupation fell from over 60% two years ago to 50%. Jisc's Charlie Ball said it is "very hard to escape the conclusion that AI has played a role," while noting graduates are diversifying into cybersecurity and network engineering. Economics graduates saw parallel softening in consulting and analyst roles. For workforce planners, the signal is hiring contraction at the entry rung rather than displacement of incumbents.
