# NVIDIA and CrowdStrike launch SafeMind, an agentic cybersecurity system, at Fal.Con 2026

Jensen Huang and George Kurtz used Fal.Con 2026 in Las Vegas to introduce CrowdStrike SafeMind, built on frontier models running on NVIDIA infrastructure. SafeMind pairs AI agents in a closed loop so that detection, triage and response operate autonomously against machine-speed attacks. Huang framed automated attack volume as forcing an equally automated defense — and named CrowdStrike NVIDIA's leading cybersecurity partner. This is the most concrete example to date of agentic AI entering the SOC as a product rather than a pilot. https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-crowdstrike-fal-con-2026/
