# Meta shares extend rally as Muse outpaces ChatGPT's launch curve

Meta stock gained 4.5% Thursday after an 11%+ Monday jump, recovering to roughly $778. Muse reached 1.8M iPhone downloads in North America in under two weeks, versus 1.3M for ChatGPT's 2023 launch, per Apptopia. JPMorgan raised its target to $920, calling Muse potentially “the most widely used consumer AI application since ChatGPT.” Amazon blocked Muse from its site over identification and credential-storage concerns — an early signal of platform friction for agentic shopping.
