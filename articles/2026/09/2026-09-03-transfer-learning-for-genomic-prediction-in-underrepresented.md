# Transfer learning for genomic prediction in underrepresented populations

Google Research published work on transfer learning for genomic prediction in underrepresented populations. The research addresses a persistent bias problem in genomics: models trained primarily on European-ancestry datasets can underperform for other populations. For healthcare and life-sciences leaders, the work signals how AI methods may improve equity and utility in precision medicine, but also how dependent outcomes remain on representative data and validation.
