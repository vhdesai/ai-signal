# McDonald's and Meituan launch China's first restaurant-brand drone-delivery route in Shanghai

McDonald's and Meituan launched what SCMP calls China's first catering-brand-exclusive drone-delivery route, operating along Shanghai's West Bund with orders air-dropped to designated kiosks along the riverfront. It is a small-footprint deployment, but the strategic point is that Meituan is now integrating brand-exclusive drone lanes into its logistics platform for named enterprise customers — a template that would translate directly to pharmacy and consumer-goods verticals. For executives with regional supply chains, this is a concrete data point on how quickly Chinese urban drone logistics is commercializing.
