# Meta’s AI agent Muse hits No. 1 on the App Store — with fewer downloads than ChatGPT needed

Muse reached No. 1 on Apple’s US App Store on Sept 18, ten days after launch, with roughly 730,000 US downloads per Sensor Tower — ahead of ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Instagram. By comparison ChatGPT took 697,000 downloads in its first eight days in 2023 and only reached No. 3. JPMorgan upgraded Meta to Overweight and raised its target from $640 to $820, while an Oppenheimer survey found only 8% of consumers would trust Meta with their passwords, versus 30% for Google and 23% for Apple. Android performance lags badly at No. 338 in Google Play Productivity.
