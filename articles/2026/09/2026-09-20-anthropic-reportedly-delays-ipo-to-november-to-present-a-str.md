# Anthropic reportedly delays IPO to November to present a stronger Q3

Anthropic is reportedly pushing its mega-IPO from October to November 2026 to present stronger Q3 results to investors ahead of what is expected to be a roughly $2T valuation. The Decoder cites monthly compute-obligation figures including ~$1.25B/month for its SpaceX Starcloud deal alone as complicating the offering narrative, alongside unresolved security-incident exposure. Combined with OpenAI's already-delayed 2027 offering, the two most-anticipated AI IPOs of the cycle have both slipped inside a week — a meaningful signal about how bookrunners are reading current AI-valuation sentiment.
