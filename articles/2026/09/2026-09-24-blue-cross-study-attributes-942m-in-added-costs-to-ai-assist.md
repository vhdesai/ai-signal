# Blue Cross study attributes $942M in added costs to AI-assisted clinical coding

The Blue Cross Blue Shield Association analyzed inpatient claims from Q1 2023 through Q4 2025 and found the share billed as medically complex rose from 37% to 40%, translating to an estimated $942M in additional costs to member plans, $653M of it from secondary diagnoses that pushed claims into higher-reimbursement DRGs (about $11,000 per excess case). Critically, BCBSA found no corresponding increase in treatment intensity — ICU use, transfusion rates and length of stay were flat or lower at the hospitals coding most aggressively. More than 60% of hospital systems now use AI-enabled coding or ambient scribe tools. This is the first credible quantification of AI's second-order cost effects inside a regulated payment system.
