# Sanders and Casar introduce bill to ban artificial superintelligence

Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar introduced legislation to permanently ban artificial superintelligence and pause certain advanced AI development until federal safety rules exist. Passage is unlikely, but the U.S. debate now includes prohibition of a capability class, not only disclosure or liability. REGULATION AUTONOMOUS VEHICLES
