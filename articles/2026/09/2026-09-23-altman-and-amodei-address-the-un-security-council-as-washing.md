# Altman and Amodei address the UN Security Council as Washington rejects global AI rules

OpenAI's Sam Altman and Anthropic's Dario Amodei told a UN Security Council session on AI that the moment "calls for extreme care," warning about loss of human control and misuse by hostile actors, and both said their companies would slow releases if they identified a genuine threat. The appearance sits awkwardly against the record: both labs shipped new models in the week following their initial calls to "pace the frontier." President Trump has separately said the US "rejects" global attempts to regulate AI, leaving a widening gap between multilateral rhetoric and national policy that multinational deployments will have to navigate.
