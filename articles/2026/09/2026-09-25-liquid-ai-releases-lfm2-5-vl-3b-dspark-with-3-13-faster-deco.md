# Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-VL-3B-DSpark with 3.13× faster decoding via speculative decoding

MarkTechPost reports Liquid AI released LFM2.5-VL-3B-DSpark, a 3B-parameter vision-language model that uses speculative decoding to achieve up to 3.13× faster decoding at the same quality. It's a notable practical release for edge and low-latency deployments, and it fits Liquid AI's positioning against the cost-optimized-inference releases coming out of Alibaba's Qwen team. The launch complements this week's Anthropic Opus 5.5, OpenAI GPT-6 Sol/Luna, and Alibaba Qwen Audio 3.1 releases plus Exa's Agent Ultra subagent-swarm deep-research API.
