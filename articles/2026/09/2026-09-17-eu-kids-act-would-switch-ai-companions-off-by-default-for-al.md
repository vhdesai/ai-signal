# EU KIDS Act Would Switch AI Companions Off by Default for All Minors

The European Commission adopted its proposed EU KIDS Act, covering social media, video-sharing platforms, online games, app stores and — for the first time in EU platform regulation — AI companions and chatbots. AI companions must be off by default for minors, may not be designed to create emotional dependency, and by default may not carry earlier conversations into later sessions; under-13s reach them only through parental controls. Social media access is tiered: no accounts under 13, guardian-managed “mini accounts” capped at one hour daily for 13–15, autonomous accounts from 15. The burden of proof shifts to providers, with penalties up to 6% of worldwide annual turnover.
