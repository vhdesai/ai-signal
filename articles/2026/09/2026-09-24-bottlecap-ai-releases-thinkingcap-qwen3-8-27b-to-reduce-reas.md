# BottleCap AI releases ThinkingCap-Qwen3.8-27B to reduce reasoning-token usage

MarkTechPost reported that BottleCap AI released ThinkingCap-Qwen3.8-27B, claiming 37.2% fewer thinking tokens at a 0.86 percentage-point accuracy cost. The release targets a practical production issue: reasoning models can be expensive when hidden or intermediate token use grows quickly. The signal is that model optimization is increasingly about cost-shaping and latency management, not only headline accuracy. Products & Tools CONSUMER AGENTSGEMINI
