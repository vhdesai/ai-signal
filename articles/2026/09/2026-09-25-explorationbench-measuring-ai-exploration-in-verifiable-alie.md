# ExplorationBench: measuring AI exploration in verifiable “alien worlds”

A new benchmark drops AI agents into procedurally defined worlds whose rules are unknown but verifiable, measuring genuine exploration and hypothesis formation rather than recall of pretrained knowledge. The alien-world construction is specifically designed to resist training-data contamination. It isolates open-ended discovery — a capability standard agentic benchmarks such as WebArena and OSWorld do not measure.
