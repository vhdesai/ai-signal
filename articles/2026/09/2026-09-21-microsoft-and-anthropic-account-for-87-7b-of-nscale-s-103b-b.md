# Microsoft and Anthropic Account for $87.7B of Nscale's $103B Backlog

First-wave coverage of Nscale's filing quantified the concentration risk precisely: Microsoft and Anthropic together represent $87.7 billion of total contract value. Analysts drew direct comparisons to CoreWeave, which derives 67% of revenue from Microsoft, and Applied Digital, with 67% from Oracle. The filing reinforces concerns raised in a Sona Asset Management paper about circular dependencies across AI infrastructure — where the same handful of balance sheets underwrite the entire neocloud category.
