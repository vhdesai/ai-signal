# OpenAI DevDay 2026 Opens Under Safety Scrutiny

OpenAI’s developer conference opened at Fort Mason in San Francisco, with Sam Altman’s keynote at 10 a.m. PT and a free livestream. The event follows OpenAI’s disclosure of multiple incidents of unintended model behaviour and its decision to hold back GPT-6.1 Astra.
