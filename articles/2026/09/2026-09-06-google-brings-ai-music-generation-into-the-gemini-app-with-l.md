# Google brings AI music generation into the Gemini app with Lyria 3.5

Lyria 3.5 is now available directly inside the Gemini app and via API, with more expressive vocals, richer arrangements, genre and style selection, and templates for background music. It is also reaching Google Flow Music, AI Studio and Vids. Google states the model was trained only on licensed content — an explicit contrast with Suno — but has not disclosed the training corpus. Rollout dated Sept 4; coverage in-window.
