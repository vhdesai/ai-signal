# Anthropic Reports Claude Now Leads 26% of Its Own AI R&D, Up from Under 1% in February

Anthropic released a prototype R&D Automation Index measuring how much of its internal AI R&D its own models lead. Claude reached 26% of that work as of August (AL4 "leads" on Epoch AI's scale), with more than 90% of tasks at AL3 "collaborates" or above and zero at AL5 (full autonomy). The methodology sampled ~15,000 granular R&D tasks across a 542-node hierarchy, weighted by person-time, with an independent Claude judge assigning levels — a circularity Anthropic acknowledges (59% exact agreement with human raters, versus 35% between humans). The disclosure also covers ~30,000 concurrent internal agents, online monitors blocking ~0.002% of agent actions, and 6% of AI R&D compute allocated to safety in the sampled week. It is the first lab-published quantification of the recursive-improvement curve. Academic Research PREPRINT
