# Texas halts new data-center grid connections after speculative demand surge

Texas paused new large-load grid interconnections after requests from data centers and other major power users jumped from about 48 GW in 2023 to more than 474 GW. Data centers reportedly account for roughly 90% of the increase. The pause shows that AI data-center growth is now colliding with utility-planning and grid-reliability constraints.
