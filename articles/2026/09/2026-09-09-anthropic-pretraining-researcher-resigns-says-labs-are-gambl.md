# Anthropic pretraining researcher resigns, says labs are "gambling with our lives"

Jacob Coxon, 27, who spent three years on pretraining research at OpenAI and then Anthropic, resigned on September 8 and left the industry, writing that neither company is "acting responsibly" and that both are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence." Anthropic alignment lead Evan Hubinger publicly endorsed the substance, putting his own extinction-risk estimate above 10% within the decade and stating the company does "not yet have a plan to solve alignment for superintelligence." The resignation lands as Anthropic prepares a public listing. Internal dissent at this seniority is now a disclosure and diligence factor. URL: POLITICO article
