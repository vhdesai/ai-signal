# Toward Physical AI: Nature Reviews Physics Survey on Hardware That Is Itself the Neural Network

Nature Reviews Physics published an international review (Caravelli et al., DOI 10.1038/s42254-026-00978-x) co-authored by UCLA's Adam Stieg and James Gimzewski with collaborators at Sydney and Canterbury, covering self-organizing memristive networks as physical learning systems. In these nanowire and nanoparticle networks the hardware itself performs the computation, rather than software running on a conventional chip. The review reports such systems have already run speech and image recognition benchmarks in real time by exploiting network physics. The trajectory points toward low-power "physical AI" at the edge — satellites, robotics, and distributed sensing.
