# Anthropic's Annualized Revenue Passes $100B Ahead of a November IPO

Anthropic is now pacing above $100 billion in annualized revenue — roughly 50% higher than its reported pace two months ago and more than 10x its end-of-2025 run rate — according to New York Times reporting relayed by Axios and Bloomberg. The Wall Street Journal separately reported the listing target has moved from October to November, reportedly to put third-quarter results in front of investors before pricing. Growth is attributed largely to enterprise adoption of Claude Code and Cowork. Note the metric: this is an annualized run rate, not booked revenue, and Anthropic declined to comment.
