# WSJ: some Anthropic veterans are buying remote land in case "AI goes awry"

The Wall Street Journal reports that some of Anthropic's longest-tenured employees are purchasing remote US land as a personal hedge against "AI going awry," with the piece tracing the company's roots in Effective Altruism and a Bay Area doomer network that has been gaming out worst-case scenarios for over a decade. The story lands as the founders move to lock in 50.1% voting control ahead of the IPO. Together, they raise the question of how underwriters and public-market investors should read the founders' governance ask. POLICY
