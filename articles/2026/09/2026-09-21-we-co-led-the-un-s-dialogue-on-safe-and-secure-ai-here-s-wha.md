# ‘We co-led the UN’s dialogue on safe and secure AI. Here’s what needs to happen now’

Costa Rica’s science and technology minister Paula Bogantes Zamora and Partnership on AI CEO Rebecca Finlay set out what the UN governance track must deliver next — incident reporting, independent scrutiny, and layered safeguards borrowed from aviation and medicine. Published the same day as the scientific panel’s brief, it maps the institutional path the panel’s technical findings are meant to feed. Useful as a preview of the obligations likely to be formalized ahead of the 2027 Global Dialogue.
