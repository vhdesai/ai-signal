# OECD PISA 2025: students using AI for specific schoolwork tasks score lower in science

PISA 2025, covering roughly 760,000 students across 91 countries and economies, shows reading and mathematics at their lowest recorded levels — reading down 28 points since 2015, about one and a half years of learning. On AI, the OECD found the relationship is conditional rather than uniformly negative: students who used AI for discrete tasks such as summarizing, drafting or research scored lower in science on average, while students who used AI generally and had school-based AI-literacy instruction scored slightly higher. The share of “hasty readers” rose from about 7% to 11% between 2018 and 2025.
