# Alibaba's open-weight Qwen-Image-2.1 (7B) claims to beat closed image models on quality benchmarks

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen-Image-2.1, a 7B open-weight image-generation and editing model that supports transparency and up to ten reference images at once, runs on consumer GPUs, and — per Qwen's own benchmarks — outperforms closed image models in its size class. The research license bars commercial use without a separate Qwen agreement, which limits the enterprise pull but preserves the technical demonstration. It joins Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash and Qwen-Drive-1.0 in Alibaba's rapid open-weight release cadence, deliberately capping the pricing power of closed generative-media APIs. MODEL RESEARCH
