# OpenAI connects ChatGPT to Epic electronic health records

OpenAI opened ChatGPT to trusted healthcare data sources, including an Epic EHR integration covering a record system that holds data for more than 325 million patients. Clinicians can pull authorized patient data into ChatGPT to review histories, identify changes, and prepare for appointments; a public health data connector shipped alongside it. UCSF Health is piloting the EHR connection. This moves ChatGPT from a general assistant into a regulated clinical workflow, with the attendant compliance surface. https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-connects-health-records-and-healthcare-sources/ PRODUCT
