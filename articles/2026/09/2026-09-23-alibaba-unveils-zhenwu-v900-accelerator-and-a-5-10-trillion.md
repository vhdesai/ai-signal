# Alibaba Unveils Zhenwu V900 Accelerator and a 5–10-Trillion-Parameter Qwen Roadmap

Alibaba used its Apsara Conference to introduce the Zhenwu V900, a T-Head training-and-inference processor with 216 GB of memory, 1,200 GB/s inter-chip bandwidth, and native FP8/FP4 support, claiming 3× the performance of the M890 released in May and scaling to clusters of up to 500,000 cards, with mass production targeted for Q1 2027. Alibaba confirmed Qwen 4 is in training and projected Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 at 5–10 trillion parameters against 2.4 trillion for the current Qwen3.8-Max flagship, plus a target of 20 GW of data-center capacity by 2032. The performance claim is measured against Alibaba's own prior silicon, not Nvidia; pricing, software compatibility, and independent benchmarks remain undisclosed — but the announcement locks in the single largest publicly declared full-stack AI capacity commitment from any Chinese firm this cycle.
