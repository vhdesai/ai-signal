# Crusoe raises $3.9B Series F at $30.9B; AI infrastructure funding megarounds continue

Denver-based AI-infrastructure provider Crusoe raised a $3.9B Series F at a $30.9B valuation, led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital, and Valor Equity Partners. Alongside Nscale's $35B IPO filing and CoreWeave's $3.7B convertible, it's a busy week for non-hyperscaler AI-compute financings. PitchBook separately notes SoftBank is raising over $11B in junk bonds to fund its OpenAI stake — debt markets are becoming increasingly central to how the largest AI positions get financed even as the Jane Street data-center paper trades at 11.3%.
