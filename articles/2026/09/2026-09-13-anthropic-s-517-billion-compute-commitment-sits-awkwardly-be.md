# Anthropic's $517 Billion Compute Commitment Sits Awkwardly Beside Its Slowdown Call

“Anthropic agreed to $517 billion in compute commitments covering 14.8 gigawatts of capacity in the 11 months through August 2026, according to The Information, even as its CEO publicly called for a slowdown.” Crypto Briefing reported the same figures a day earlier, framing the cloud, chip and data-center package as pre-IPO capacity lock-in. The numbers put a hard ceiling on how much restraint is operationally plausible in the near term, whatever the rhetoric. Note the derivative dating: both pieces were published in-window but rest on a September 6 report by The Information.
