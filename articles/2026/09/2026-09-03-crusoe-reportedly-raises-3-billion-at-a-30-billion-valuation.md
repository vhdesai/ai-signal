# Crusoe reportedly raises $3 billion at a $30 billion valuation

TechCrunch reported that AI infrastructure provider Crusoe raised a $3 billion round at a $30 billion valuation, after recently signing a reported $13 billion cloud contract with Jane Street. Crusoe has become a major AI cloud and data-center developer serving customers including Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Oracle, and others. The transaction highlights how AI data-center capacity has become a capital-markets asset class, with private financing scaling ahead of public-market exits. EMBODIED AIIFA
