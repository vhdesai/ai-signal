# Foxconn says Q3 should beat expectations on AI strength

Reuters reported that Foxconn expects third-quarter results to outperform market expectations, citing AI-related demand. As a major electronics manufacturer and server supply-chain participant, Foxconn's outlook is another data point that AI infrastructure demand is flowing beyond chip vendors into contract manufacturing and systems assembly.
