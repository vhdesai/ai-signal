# China Drafts Rules Banning "Virtual Intimacy" AI Companions for Minors

The Cyberspace Administration of China published draft rules — "Ensuring minors' safe and healthy use of the internet" — that would explicitly bar platforms from offering "virtual relatives or companions" or intimate AI-companion services to users under 18. It is the first concrete national-level rule anywhere aimed at intimate AI-companion regulation for minors, landing the same week as California's kill-switch executive order and the EU KIDS Act.
