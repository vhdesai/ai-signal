# Hot DeepSeek to deploy ~160,000 Huawei Ascend chips in Inner Mongolia

DeepSeek plans to deploy at least 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT chips in a gigawatt-scale data center in Inner Mongolia, explicitly to reduce reliance on Nvidia. Order and delivery specifics remain undisclosed. If executed at that scale, it would be the most substantial demonstration to date that Chinese frontier labs can train on domestic silicon, with direct implications for export-control efficacy. CNBC-TV18
