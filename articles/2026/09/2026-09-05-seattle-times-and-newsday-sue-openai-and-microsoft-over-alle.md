# Seattle Times and Newsday sue OpenAI and Microsoft over alleged copyright infringement

Reuters reported that the Seattle Times and Newsday sued OpenAI and Microsoft, alleging copyright infringement. The case adds to the expanding legal pressure around use of publisher content in training data and AI-generated outputs. For enterprises, the continued litigation means indemnity, data provenance, content licensing, and retrieval grounding remain material procurement issues for generative AI deployments.
