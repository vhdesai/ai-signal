# Databricks commits $350M+ to Singapore as its APAC AI hub

Databricks will invest $350M+ (up to $445M per some sources) in Singapore, expanding its regional HQ and doubling its workforce to 500+ over three years. The commitment tightens Databricks' Asia-Pacific footprint at a moment when sovereign AI, model-agnostic data platforms, and enterprise Agent Bricks are the primary axes of competition. The scale of the pledge also indicates Databricks continues to plan for a public listing amid its rising $190B valuation. - https://dealroom.co/blog/databricks-350m-singapore-apac-ai-hub
