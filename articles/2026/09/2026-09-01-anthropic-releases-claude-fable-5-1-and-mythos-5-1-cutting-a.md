# Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, cutting agentic costs up to 45%

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 for general availability alongside Mythos 5.1, a same-architecture model gated behind trusted-access programs for cybersecurity and life-sciences work. Pricing is the headline for buyers: roughly 25% cheaper for standard use and up to 45% cheaper for agentic workloads via optimized cache-read pricing. Anthropic also says refined safeguards cut cybersecurity false positives by about 60%, addressing a persistent enterprise complaint that over-blocking made the models unusable for security teams. The release is paired with Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, allowing customers to retain data in their own cloud with zero retention by Anthropic. https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1
