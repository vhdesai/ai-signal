# Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, cutting agent costs up to 45%

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.1 broadly and restricted the more capable Mythos 5.1 to vetted organizations because of its cybersecurity and biology capabilities. Anthropic reports comparable or better performance at lower reasoning settings, with up to 45% lower cost on some highly agentic workloads and a 75% cut to cache-read pricing.
