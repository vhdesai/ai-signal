# Meta's Elite AI Unit Sought Employee Data for Training — Halted After Revolt

Business Insider details how Meta's superintelligence unit tried to acquire employees' internal data — chat, screen, and productivity signals — to train frontier models, and how a leak and staff revolt forced the effort to stand down. The episode is a rare public look at how far a top lab was willing to push data collection to catch up on the training-data axis, and it will fuel scrutiny of internal AI data governance across every large enterprise that runs its own model pipeline. Expect works-council and privacy-officer pushback to intensify.
