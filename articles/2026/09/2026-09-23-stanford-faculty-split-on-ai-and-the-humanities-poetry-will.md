# Stanford faculty split on AI and the humanities: “Poetry will not optimize”

An op-ed built on interviews with HAI senior fellow Prof. Michele Elam and journalism Prof. Cheryl Phillips. Elam argues literature cannot be made more efficient and that “the coercion of tech in shaping your decisions is often invisible.” Phillips counters that in data journalism AI enables analysis that would otherwise be out of reach. Clearly labeled opinion rather than a research finding, but a useful read on how AI adoption debates are landing inside research universities.
