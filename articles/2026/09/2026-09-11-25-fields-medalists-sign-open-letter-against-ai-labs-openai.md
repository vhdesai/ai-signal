# 25 Fields Medalists sign open letter against AI labs; OpenAI withdraws Caltech sponsorship

Twenty-five mathematicians, each a Fields Medal recipient, signed an open letter arguing that AI labs are threatening their intellectual work, warning that rushed announcements raise severe attribution and plagiarism questions; TechCrunch notes OpenAI's proof remains unverified. Separately, OpenAI withdrew its sponsorship of a Caltech math event after criticism from university researchers, and NYU's Tristan Buckmaster has alleged OpenAI pressured him not to credit an Anthropic-employed collaborator. The executive read is an IP and attribution governance problem with direct analogues for any enterprise whose proprietary work flows through a vendor's coding agent.
