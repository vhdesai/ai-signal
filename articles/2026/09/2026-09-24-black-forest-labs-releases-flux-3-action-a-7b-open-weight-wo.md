# Black Forest Labs releases FLUX 3 Action, a 7B open-weight world-action model

MarkTechPost reported that Black Forest Labs released FLUX 3 Action, a 7B open-weight world-action model that tops RoboLab-120. The release highlights a growing segment of models optimized for action in physical or simulated environments rather than only text, image, or video generation. If the benchmark result holds up, smaller open-weight action models could lower experimentation costs for robotics and embodied-agent teams.
