# Seattle Times and Newsday sue OpenAI and Microsoft over AI training

Two more U.S. newspapers, the Seattle Times and Newsday, have filed suit against OpenAI and Microsoft, alleging the companies used their copyrighted journalism without authorization to train AI models. The filing adds to a growing roster of publisher litigation against the two companies, following similar suits from other major outlets. It underscores that media-industry legal pressure on OpenAI and Microsoft is intensifying rather than settling. COMMENTARY
