# Nvidia Confirms $12.93B Acquisition of Hugging Face

Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.93B, its largest transaction to date, taking ownership of a platform hosting roughly 3 million models, 500,000 datasets and over 1 million applications across 18 million users and 200,000 companies. Jensen Huang stated Hugging Face will continue to operate as an open platform with no requirement to use Nvidia hardware. The deal moves Nvidia up the stack into the developer ecosystem just as hyperscalers accelerate custom-silicon programs to reduce GPU dependence.
