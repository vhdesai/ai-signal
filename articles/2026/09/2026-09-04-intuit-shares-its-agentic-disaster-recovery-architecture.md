# Intuit Shares Its Agentic Disaster-Recovery Architecture

Intuit described adding a Bedrock-powered reasoning layer to its EWOK disaster-recovery orchestrator, with teams using the agent for eight months. The agent selects workflows, checks readiness and policy gates, and tracks execution while authenticated deterministic systems perform infrastructure changes. Engineers retain judgment and approvals, and production failovers require change records. The mature pattern is clear: let the agent reason and coordinate, but keep execution inside auditable deterministic control planes.
