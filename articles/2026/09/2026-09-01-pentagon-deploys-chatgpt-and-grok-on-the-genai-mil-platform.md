# Pentagon deploys ChatGPT and Grok on the GenAI.mil platform

The Department of Defense added secured versions of ChatGPT and Grok to GenAI.mil, a portal already serving roughly 1.7 million personnel. The tools are tailored to what the department describes as warfighter needs and run inside government-controlled boundaries. Placing two competing commercial frontier models side by side on one platform is a deliberate multi-vendor posture. It also sets a de facto federal reference architecture other agencies are likely to copy. https://qz.com/pentagon-grok-chatgpt-genai-mil-military-090126 SAFETY
