# Google DeepMind launches "DeepMind Institute" for safe AGI; Hassabis proposes US-led frontier standards body

Google launched the DeepMind Institute for the safe development of artificial general intelligence, with Demis Hassabis proposing a US-led frontier AI standards body that would evaluate the most advanced models on cybersecurity, biological threats, and other high-risk domains with quarterly-updated benchmarks. OpenAI simultaneously announced plans to let third-party groups evaluate its models — following Tuesday's Anthropic–Accenture safety-evaluation partnership. Standardized third-party evaluation is now the industry's operational answer to the last two weeks of safety incidents. CIO Dive's Sept. 23 issue leads with OpenAI's global-standards proposal and highlights that 1 in 4 enterprise agents run unmonitored per New Relic — the observability gap is the flip side of this policy push.
