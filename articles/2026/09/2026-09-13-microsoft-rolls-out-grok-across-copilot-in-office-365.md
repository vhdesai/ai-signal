# Microsoft rolls out Grok across Copilot in Office 365

NDTV Profit reports Microsoft is rolling out xAI's Grok across Copilot inside Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, joining OpenAI and Anthropic models already available in the Microsoft 365 lineup. The move formalizes Microsoft's multi-model Copilot strategy — the same platform now spans three frontier vendors. It also raises governance questions for enterprise customers around per-app model selection, data flow, and per-vendor safety posture at exactly the moment when the three vendors themselves are calling for a slowdown.
