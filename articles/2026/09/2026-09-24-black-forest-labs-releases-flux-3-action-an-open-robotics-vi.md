# Black Forest Labs releases FLUX 3 Action, an open robotics vision-action model

BFL's first robotics model, FLUX 3 Action, is a 7B-parameter open vision-language-action model that predicts next-step robot actions from camera feeds. On the RoboLab-120 benchmark it takes the top score at up to 3.95× the speed of the previous leader — a notable open-weights entry into a category dominated so far by proprietary stacks from Figure, 1X, and Google's Gemini Robotics ER. BFL's move gives industrial-robotics integrators a permissive-license baseline for embodied-agent research.
