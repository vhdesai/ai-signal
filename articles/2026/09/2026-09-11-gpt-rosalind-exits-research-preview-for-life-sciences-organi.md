# GPT-Rosalind Exits Research Preview for Life Sciences Organizations

OpenAI moved GPT-Rosalind, its life-sciences research model, out of research preview to global availability for eligible organizations under its trusted-access program, with published pricing effective October 5, 2026. The model pairs GPT-5.5's agentic coding and tool use with domain strength in medicinal chemistry and genomics, evaluated against an internally built, externally expert-judged benchmark called LifeSciBench. The gating structure — trusted access rather than open API — is the notable part for regulated-industry buyers.
