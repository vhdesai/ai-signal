# Chinese researchers demonstrate ~100x endurance improvement in wurtzite ferroelectric memory

A Chinese research team demonstrated more than 10 billion write cycles in wurtzite ferroelectric memory devices — roughly 100x prior endurance — potentially removing one of the main reliability barriers to using the technology in high-performance and AI systems. Ferroelectric memory sits between DRAM and NAND on the density/latency curve and has been an active research target for post-2028 AI accelerators. For executives tracking the memory-bandwidth-wall problem in AI infrastructure, this is a concrete step forward in a path that could complement HBM rather than replace it.
