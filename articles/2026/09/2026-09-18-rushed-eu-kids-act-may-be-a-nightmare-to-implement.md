# Rushed EU Kids Act May Be a Nightmare to Implement

Same-day critique of the Commission proposal argues the Act's breadth — covering online games and general chatbot features embedded in platforms, not just companion apps — imposes restrictions poorly matched to some services, such as blanket screen-time caps on gaming. The sharper objection is structural: mandatory age verification across every covered service would require building a continent-scale identity-data collection layer, which critics warn undermines privacy, enables surveillance at scale and creates a high-value target for attackers. The proposal now faces examination by Parliament and Council, where the age threshold and the treatment of AI companions are the provisions most likely to move. Coverage notes.
