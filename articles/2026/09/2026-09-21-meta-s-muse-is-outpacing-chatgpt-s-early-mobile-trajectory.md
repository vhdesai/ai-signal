# Meta's Muse Is Outpacing ChatGPT's Early Mobile Trajectory

Apptopia estimates show Meta's Muse assistant ahead of ChatGPT's mobile debut curve: 1.8M iOS downloads across the US and Canada in its first twelve days versus ChatGPT's 1.3M, and 2.8M total installs globally. US daily active users reached 642,000 against ChatGPT's 231,000 at the same stage. The app has climbed from No. 2 to No. 1 on the US App Store, and more than 95% of Muse users are also Facebook users — confirming that distribution, not model quality, is driving the curve.
