# Google Is Retiring Gemini’s Gems in Favour of “Skills”

An in-app notice confirms Gems — the custom assistants Google launched in 2024 — will be automatically migrated to Skills starting November 17, 2026. Existing Gems remain usable until migration and carry over custom instructions, knowledge files and share links.
