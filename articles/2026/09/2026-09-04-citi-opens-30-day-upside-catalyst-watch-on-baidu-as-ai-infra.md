# Citi opens 30-day upside catalyst watch on Baidu as AI infrastructure business grows

Citi opened a 30-day upside catalyst watch on Baidu, reiterating a Buy rating and pointing to growth in its AI cloud and infrastructure segment as the near-term driver. The call reflects renewed analyst optimism on Baidu's AI monetization trajectory. Note that the article published September 4, while the underlying analyst action was dated September 2.
