# ChatGPT Voice adds email, calendar, and Slack access, moves onto GPT-6 Sol/Luna/Astra

ChatGPT Voice is now running on the just-launched GPT-6 Astra, Sol, and Luna models and can act inside Gmail/Calendar/Slack plugins to book time, send messages, and build sites conversationally. The mobile Work tab surfaces the agentic tasks for Pro and Plus users. OpenAI is explicitly framing this as the "Her"-style always-on assistant Altman has long referenced, arriving the same day Meta pushed Muse into email and Mac control. PRODUCT
