# Google tests letting Gemini call businesses for users

TechCrunch reported that Google is testing a Gemini capability that can call businesses on a user’s behalf. The feature points toward voice agents moving from passive assistant roles into active real-world interactions such as gathering information, booking, or resolving tasks. As with other agentic commerce and assistant workflows, the risk surface shifts to consent, call disclosure, misrepresentation, and error recovery.
