# Goldman Sachs Lifts 2027 Hyperscaler AI Capex Forecast to $1.2 Trillion — "Largest Since the Railroads"

Goldman Sachs now expects Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft, Oracle, and Meta to spend a combined $1.2T on AI infrastructure in 2027 — more than 50% above the ~$800B projected for 2026 and above the $1.1T Wall Street consensus, per Bloomberg citing strategist Ryan Hammond. Measured against GDP, the bank frames it as the largest investment cycle since 19th-century railroad construction, though growth decelerates from near 100% in 2026 to 54% in 2027 and 12% in 2028. Goldman estimates the group needs roughly $300B in annual AI revenue simply to break even, against cloud revenue growth that rose from 25% in 2024 to 48% in Q2 2026. With spending now exceeding operating cash generation, a rising share of the buildout is debt-financed, and power, labor, and memory supply remain the stated bottlenecks — the exact constraints Oracle just invoked force majeure on.
