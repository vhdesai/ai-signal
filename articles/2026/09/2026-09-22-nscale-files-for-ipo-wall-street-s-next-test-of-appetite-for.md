# Nscale files for IPO — Wall Street's next test of appetite for concentrated AI infrastructure bets

British AI data-center developer Nscale — the counterparty behind Anthropic's ~$45B compute deal and last week's Fidji Simo board appointment — filed for an IPO. TechCrunch's framing highlights the concentration risk: most of Nscale's revenue comes from Microsoft and Anthropic, and it lands into a public-market backdrop where both OpenAI (2027) and Anthropic (November) IPOs have already slipped. For investors and enterprise buyers, this is the first pure-play neocloud IPO test since CoreWeave and a real datapoint on how public markets are pricing single-customer AI concentration. FINANCE
