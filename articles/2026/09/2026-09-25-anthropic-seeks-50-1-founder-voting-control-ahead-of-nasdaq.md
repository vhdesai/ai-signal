# Anthropic Seeks 50.1% Founder Voting Control Ahead of Nasdaq Listing

Anthropic is asking shareholders to approve a special share class giving CEO Dario Amodei and his six co-founders a combined 50.1% of the vote on most corporate matters, with a vote expected within days. Each founder holds roughly 2% of the company economically; the new shares carry no added economic value and lapse if fewer than three of the seven retain a minimum stake. The Long-Term Benefit Trust would still appoint most of the seven-seat board, while founder-appointed seats rise from two to three and employees receive a tie-breaking class. The structure mirrors Palantir's Class F arrangement and comes ahead of a listing reportedly targeted at a valuation near $2 trillion. ANALYSIS
