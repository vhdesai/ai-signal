# Personal-AI startup Instinct in talks for a $10 billion valuation on a $1B raise

The startup behind personal AI assistant Instinct is in talks to raise roughly $1 billion at about a $10 billion valuation, per The Information. The revaluation places Instinct near the top of the consumer-AI-agent pack alongside the incumbents' own agent efforts (OpenAI Data Agent, Meta Muse) and validates that private capital is willing to write growth-stage checks for personal AI on limited revenue history. For enterprise leaders watching the assistant category, expect Instinct-caliber capital to compress product-differentiation windows to a quarter or less. - https://www.theinformation.com/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=Instinct+AI+agent+10+billion+valuation
