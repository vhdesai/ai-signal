# Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN Launches 428B-Parameter Arabic Model Built on China's MiniMax

PIF-backed HUMAIN unveiled humain-m3, a 428-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model commissioned from Chinese lab MiniMax and further pre-trained on more than a trillion tokens of Arabic-native text. HUMAIN reports the highest average score among frontier systems across seven public Arabic benchmarks, with a research preview live on HUMAIN Node and open weights planned under the MiniMax Community License. A Gulf sovereign AI program building its flagship model on a Chinese open base is a meaningful data point on how far U.S. allies will go to assemble independent stacks.
