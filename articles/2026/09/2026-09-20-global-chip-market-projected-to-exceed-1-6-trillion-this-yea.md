# Global Chip Market Projected to Exceed $1.6 Trillion This Year Despite AI Slowdown Talk

A Korea Eximbank research report drawing on TrendForce and other sources projects the global semiconductor market will top $1.6 trillion in 2026 — more than 1.5x the earlier forecast — driven by hyperscaler AI infrastructure spending. Memory from Samsung and SK hynix is expected to exceed half the market; DRAM and NAND spot prices stood at $25 and $30.5 at end-August, up 4.4x and 9x year-on-year. The report forecasts roughly $2 trillion by 2030. For anyone modeling input costs, the memory line is the one to track.
