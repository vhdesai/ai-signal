# House votes 417–3 to make data centers pay their own grid-upgrade costs — Senate then blocks

The US House voted 417–3 to require large AI data centers to pay their own grid-upgrade costs rather than shifting them onto household ratepayers, working through PURPA — which only requires states to *consider* the federal standard. The next day, Sen. Martin Heinrich (D-N.M.) blocked Sen. Jon Husted's (R-Ohio) attempt to pass the Ratepayer Protection Act by unanimous consent, calling the measure toothless; Heinrich's counter-proposal (the Grid Savings Act, which would mandate rather than suggest cost recovery) was in turn blocked by Sen. Bernie Moreno (R-Ohio). Net effect: no federal cost-allocation requirement, and enforcement stays with 50 state public utility commissions heading into the November midterms — even as the near-unanimous House margin makes this one of the few genuinely bipartisan AI issues in Congress. - https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327645/20260917/house-voted-417-3-make-data-centers-pay-electricity-costs-states-can-still-say-no.htm - https://thehill.com/policy/energy-environment/6096398-heinrich-blocks-husted-data-center-bill/
