# Bernie Sanders floats a ban on superintelligent AI

Axios reported that Senator Bernie Sanders floated a ban on superintelligent AI. The proposal may be politically unlikely in near-term legislative form, but it reflects widening public concern that frontier AI development is outpacing democratic oversight and institutional readiness. For executives, the signal is that AI policy risk is broadening from privacy and copyright into capability ceilings, deployment licensing, and public trust.
