# Crusoe raises $3 billion at a $30 billion valuation after Jane Street AI cloud contract

Crusoe reportedly raised $3 billion at a $30 billion valuation after signing a five-year, $13 billion cloud contract to supply Jane Street with GPUs and AI infrastructure. The step-up shows AI capacity becoming a capital-markets asset class. Long-duration compute contracts are increasingly being treated like infrastructure finance rather than ordinary cloud procurement. URL: TechCrunch
