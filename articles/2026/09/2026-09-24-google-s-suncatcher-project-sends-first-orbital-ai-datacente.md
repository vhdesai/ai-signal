# Google's Suncatcher project sends first orbital-AI-datacenter satellite up October 1

Google's Suncatcher program will launch a fridge-sized experimental TPU satellite on a SpaceX Falcon 9 on October 1, testing whether orbit-native solar-powered compute is technically viable. The economics remain distant: matching a single 1-GW terrestrial data center would require roughly 10,000 satellites, and Jeff Bezos has said it could take 20 years to be cost-competitive. But it's the first hyperscaler to actually put an inference-class experiment on a real launch manifest. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
