# Meta pushes to monetize AI spending through business customers

Meta is mounting a new push to sell AI tools and services to companies, seeking a return on infrastructure commitments analysts peg at up to ~$145B for 2026. The move reframes Meta's AI story from consumer engagement toward an enterprise revenue line where it has historically been absent. It lands in the same week investors have been discounting the consumer Muse narrative on trust and distribution problems. Infrastructure POLICY DATA CENTERS
