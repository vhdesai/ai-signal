# LG Electronics qualifies 2.6 MW cooling distribution unit for NVIDIA DSX AI infrastructure

The Korea Herald reported that LG Electronics was named an NVIDIA partner for AI data-center cooling, with related coverage noting qualification of a 2.6 MW cooling distribution unit for NVIDIA DSX AI infrastructure. The development is part of a wider move to standardize power and cooling components for AI factories. For infrastructure leaders, liquid cooling and validated reference designs are becoming core procurement criteria as rack densities rise.
