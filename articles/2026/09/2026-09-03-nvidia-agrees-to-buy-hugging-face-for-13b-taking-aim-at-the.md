# Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for $13B, taking aim at the open-model layer

The Wall Street Journal reported that Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for $13 billion as it ramps up efforts around open-weight models and related services. Strategically, the deal would give Nvidia a major distribution and workflow layer across open models, datasets, evaluation, inference, and developer adoption.
