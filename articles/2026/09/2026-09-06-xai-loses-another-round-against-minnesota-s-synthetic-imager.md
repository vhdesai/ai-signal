# xAI loses another round against Minnesota's synthetic-imagery law

A judge allowed Minnesota to continue enforcing its law penalizing platforms that enable non-consensual synthetic imagery of real people, over xAI's objection, with the underlying litigation still proceeding. It is xAI's second setback in the case. The ruling strengthens the position that state-level content laws can bind model providers directly, not merely the platforms distributing outputs — an important data point for the fragmented US regulatory patchwork.
