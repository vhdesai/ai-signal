# Jeff Dean Reportedly Raising for New AI Startup at ~$50B Valuation

Business Insider reports former Google Chief Scientist Jeff Dean is raising a new round for his stealth AI startup, targeting approximately a $50B valuation. If confirmed, the raise sits among the largest early-stage private valuations in AI history, mirroring Ilya Sutskever's SSI and Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab. The underlying signal is that senior ex-frontier-lab principals continue to command growth-round valuations at seed on reputation alone — reshaping how downstream managers must underwrite talent.
