# Anthropic commits $11.6B to Akamai for CPUs, with a 5% equity warrant

TechCrunch confirms Anthropic's seven-year Akamai deal is $11.6B and could scale to roughly $20B, with an unusual warrant giving Anthropic up to 5% of Akamai's stock that vests as spend grows. The unusual CPU-heavy composition suggests Anthropic is investing in inference-side optimization (data movement, retrieval, tool-use serving) at the edge rather than pure GPU training — the same pattern that made CoreWeave and Nscale attractive to lenders. The reciprocal warrant is a structural signal that this is a supplier-of-last-resort relationship, not a commodity buy. INDUSTRY
