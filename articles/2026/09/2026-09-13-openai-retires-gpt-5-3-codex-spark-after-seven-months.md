# OpenAI retires GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark after seven months

KuCoin, 36Kr, and finance.biggo.com report OpenAI is shutting down GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark, its fastest coding model, after just seven months in production. The retirement fits the broader "model fatigue" narrative — even OpenAI is retiring versions faster than enterprises can standardize on them. Users are expected to migrate to GPT-6 Astra or the more recent Codex line, with likely transition friction for teams that built agent tooling around Spark.
