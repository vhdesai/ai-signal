# Enterprise buyers say open weights and price cuts have finally flattened AI spend

Speakers at The Information's AI Agenda Live in San Francisco said open-source models plus cheaper releases from Anthropic and OpenAI are letting customers hold costs flat while getting more out of AI. "The existence of open source [models] adds pricing pressure on the labs, which is great," said Replit CEO Amjad Masad — though he noted OpenAI's price cuts are actually slowing open-source adoption at Replit. Uber reported flattening token spend through efficiency work and open models. The unresolved theme: many companies still cannot demonstrate clear ROI on AI spending.
