# Marvell and GlobalFoundries Rise After Expanded SiGe Capacity Deal for AI Data Centers

Yahoo Finance analyzed Marvell and GlobalFoundries after Reuters reported an expanded agreement to increase capacity for silicon germanium technology used in high-speed optical connections for data centers. The deal supports Marvell's optical networking products, including pluggable optics, near-packaged optics, and co-packaged optics, while GlobalFoundries said related demand remains strong.
