# Enterprise AI startup Wonderful raises $550M at a $5 billion valuation

Israeli-Dutch enterprise AI company Wonderful closed a $550 million Series C on September 2 at a $5 billion valuation, more than doubling its mark in roughly six months. The round is another data point that late-stage capital is concentrating in enterprise-deployment companies rather than foundation-model labs.
