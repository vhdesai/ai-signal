# GPT-6 Astra draws scrutiny over cybersecurity capability score

The Tech Edvocate reported continued scrutiny of OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra after the model was described as reaching a critical cybersecurity capability tier, scoring strongly on exploit-oriented hacking benchmarks. The business implication is that frontier models are moving into territory where release gating, customer eligibility, and monitoring become central product decisions rather than afterthoughts. Leaders evaluating such systems should treat cyber capability as both a defensive accelerant and a material governance risk.
