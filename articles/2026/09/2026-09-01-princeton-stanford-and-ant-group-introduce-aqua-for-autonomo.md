# Princeton, Stanford and Ant Group introduce AQuA for autonomous quant research

Researchers proposed AQuA, a pair of language-model-driven research systems that improve their own research process across iterations while the evaluator judging them stays frozen — a design aimed at the core failure mode of self-directed research agents, where a leaky feature that scores well is stored as a successful precedent and propagated forward. One system discovers symbolic alpha factors on crypto; the other develops time-series models on US equities, sharing no agents, memories or research state. The authors argue prompt-level instructions and reviewer agents cannot close this gap because author and reviewer share blind spots. marktechpost.com/2026/09/01/aqua-a-two-part-agentic-framework-for-autonomous-factor-discovery
