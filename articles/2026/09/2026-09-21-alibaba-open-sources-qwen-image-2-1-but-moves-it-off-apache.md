# Alibaba Open-Sources Qwen-Image-2.1 — but Moves It Off Apache 2.0

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen-Image-2.1, a unified text-to-image generation and editing model whose visual generation component is 7B parameters, paired with a Qwen3-VL 8B text encoder and a 64-channel RGBA autoencoder. It generates and edits transparent (RGBA) images natively, accepts up to 10 reference images, supports mask- and annotation-driven local edits, and outputs native 2K, with day-zero support in Diffusers, ComfyUI, vLLM-Omni and SGLang. The commercially material detail: it ships under the Qwen Research License Agreement rather than Apache 2.0, barring commercial use without a separate agreement.
