# Federal appeals court upholds Pentagon's supply-chain-risk designation of Anthropic

A D.C. Circuit panel ruled 2-1 to uphold the Department of Defense's March designation of Anthropic as a supply chain risk, which bars military and defense-contractor use of Claude models. Judge Gregory Katsas wrote that the department had "ample support" for concluding that continued integration of Claude presented a statutorily covered national-security risk; Judge Karen LeCraft Henderson dissented, arguing that a contractor's "honest and upfront enforcement of restrictions" is not the kind of risk the statute contemplates. A San Francisco federal judge ruled a parallel designation unlawful last month, and the panel delayed effect to allow a rehearing petition. Anthropic says it is considering all options, including further review. GEOPOLITICS
