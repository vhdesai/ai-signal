# Nvidia discusses $2.5B investment in Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab

The Information reported that Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab is in talks to raise $5B to $6B at a pre-money valuation of at least $40B, with Nvidia expected to contribute roughly half the capital. The financing would deepen Nvidia's role as a strategic backer of open-source and frontier-model developers, not merely their chip supplier. It also shows that new labs with meaningful revenue can still command extreme valuations despite investor scrutiny of model economics.
