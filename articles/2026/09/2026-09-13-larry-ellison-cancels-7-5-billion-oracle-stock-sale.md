# Larry Ellison cancels $7.5 billion Oracle stock sale

Oracle confirmed that executive chairman Larry Ellison cancelled a Rule 10b5-1 plan that would have allowed the sale of 50 million shares, worth roughly $7.5 billion. "No Oracle stock was sold under that plan, and he has no other plans to sell any of his Oracle stock," the company said, offering no reason for the reversal. Oracle shares are down 22% year to date amid heavy data-center capital spending. The cancellation removes a multibillion-dollar supply overhang at a moment when investors are scrutinising AI infrastructure capex.
