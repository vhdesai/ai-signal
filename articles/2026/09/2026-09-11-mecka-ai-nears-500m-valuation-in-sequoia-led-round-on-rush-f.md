# Mecka AI nears $500M valuation in Sequoia-led round on rush for robot training data

Mecka AI, a two-year-old startup focused on producing training data for embodied/physical AI, is raising a new round led by Sequoia at a valuation approaching $500M — just months after its Series A. The pricing reflects how urgently robotics teams need real-world manipulation and locomotion data now that Gemini Robotics ER 2, Meta, and Nvidia+Palantir are pushing embodied AI into commercial deployment. Robot data is the current bottleneck; the vendors who supply it are being priced as strategic infrastructure. COMMENTARY
