# Nebius Lifts Year-End Contracted Power Target from 3 GW to 5 GW

Nebius Group raised its year-end contracted power target from more than 3 gigawatts to 5 gigawatts following strong second-quarter results and several large AI data-center agreements. Contracted power is emerging as the clearest forward indicator of neocloud capacity — more informative than announced capex, which can lag or never convert. The revision reinforces that secured electricity, not chip allocation, is the scarce input in 2026. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nebius-group-ai-data-center-230625246.html INFRASTRUCTURE
