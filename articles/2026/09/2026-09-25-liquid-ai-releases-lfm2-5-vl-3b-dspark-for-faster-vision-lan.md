# Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-VL-3B-DSpark for faster vision-language decoding

MarkTechPost reported that Liquid AI released LFM2.5-VL-3B-DSpark, using speculative decoding for vision-language models with up to 3.13x faster decoding. The release is part of a broader push to reduce serving cost and latency for multimodal systems without requiring proportionally larger infrastructure. For enterprises, decoding efficiency is becoming a major determinant of whether vision-language workflows can scale economically. SECURITY MODEL OPEN WEIGHTS
