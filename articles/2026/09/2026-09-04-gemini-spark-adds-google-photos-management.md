# Gemini Spark Adds Google Photos Management

TechCrunch reports that Gemini Spark can now handle Google Photos tasks such as editing images, curating albums, and turning concert-flyer photos into calendar appointments. The capabilities are rolling out to eligible AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the U.S., in English, over the next few weeks. Users must connect Google Photos to Gemini, which makes consent and revocation flows central to the product experience. Strategically, this is another step from assistant-as-interface toward assistant-as-operator across personal data stores.
