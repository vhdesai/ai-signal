# New Nvidia debuts DLSS 5 with 3D-guided neural rendering on GeForce NOW

Nvidia launched DLSS 5, introducing “3D-Guided Neural Rendering,” with NBA 2K27 as the lead title among more than 26 new GeForce NOW games. The technology streams via RTX 5080-powered GeForce NOW Ultimate tiers. Beyond gaming, DLSS generations remain a useful proxy for how quickly Nvidia moves neural rendering research into shipping consumer silicon.
