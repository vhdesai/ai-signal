# Anthropic and OpenAI sound the alarm on AI safety while seeking to shape control frameworks

Associated Press reported that Anthropic and OpenAI are publicly warning about AI safety risks while also trying to influence the rules and institutions that will govern frontier models. The story captures the central tension of the current moment: leading labs are calling for safety coordination, but any coordination could also shape competitive advantage and barriers to entry. Boards should watch for standards that are genuinely risk-reducing versus standards that primarily entrench incumbent model providers.
