# Nvidia and CrowdStrike develop new cybersecurity AI models

The Wall Street Journal reports on Nvidia and CrowdStrike developing cybersecurity-focused AI models. The partnership points to a shift from generic assistants toward domain-specific models trained around security telemetry, threat detection, and response workflows.
