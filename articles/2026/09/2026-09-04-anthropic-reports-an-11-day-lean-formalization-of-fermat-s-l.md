# Anthropic reports an 11-day Lean formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem

Anthropic says collaborating Claude agents produced an end-to-end, computer-checked formalization using 13 million lines of Lean and the Prove2Me coordination platform. The work follows an existing proof; it is an automated verification result, not discovery of the theorem or its original proof. The company reports that Lean checked the result using its three standard axioms and that a comparator matched the theorem statement to Mathlib. Academic Research RESEARCH CHEMISTRY
