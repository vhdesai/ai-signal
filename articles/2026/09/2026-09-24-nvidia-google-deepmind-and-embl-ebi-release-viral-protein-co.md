# NVIDIA, Google DeepMind, and EMBL-EBI release viral protein-complex structures for pandemic preparedness

NVIDIA joined Google DeepMind, EMBL-EBI, and other research organizations to release predicted 3D structures for protein complexes from more than 2,800 viruses through the AlphaFold Database. NVIDIA said the structures were generated with AlphaFold2 optimized by BioNeMo Inference Runtime, and that roughly 30% of the protein interactions added are new to science. This is high-signal because AI infrastructure is directly expanding the shared scientific substrate for vaccines, diagnostics, and outbreak response. Academic Research HEALTH AIMENTAL HEALTH
