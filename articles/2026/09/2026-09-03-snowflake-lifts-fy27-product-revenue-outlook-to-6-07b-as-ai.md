# Snowflake Lifts FY27 Product Revenue Outlook to $6.07B as AI Workloads Convert

Snowflake shares jumped more than 24% after the company raised its fiscal 2027 product revenue guidance to $6.07 billion from $5.84 billion, with second-quarter product revenue up 37% year over year. CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy attributed roughly half the acceleration to AI products; the Cortex Code assistant is now in more than 9,100 accounts. The read-through for executives is that enterprise AI spend is beginning to lift the governed-data layer beneath the models, not just model APIs.
