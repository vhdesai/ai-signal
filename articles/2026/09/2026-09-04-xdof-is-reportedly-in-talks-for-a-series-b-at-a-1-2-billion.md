# XDOF is reportedly in talks for a Series B at a $1.2 billion valuation

XDOF, a robotics data startup founded by UC Berkeley researchers, is reportedly in late-stage talks to raise a Series B at about a $1.2 billion valuation. The company collects real-world teleoperation and human-motion data for general-purpose robotics and says annualized revenue is approaching $50 million. The signal is that robotics foundation-model progress is creating demand for specialized physical-world data supply chains, analogous to the data-labeling infrastructure that supported LLM growth.
