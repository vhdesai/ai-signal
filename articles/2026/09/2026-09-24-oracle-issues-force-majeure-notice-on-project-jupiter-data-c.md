# Oracle issues force majeure notice on Project Jupiter data-center campus

Oracle served a force majeure notice tied to Project Jupiter, the New Mexico campus Blue Owl's STACK Infrastructure is building to support OpenAI, after delays securing power — reportedly a natural gas pipeline — pushed the project back roughly one year. Blue Owl said the notice does not change its financial commitments and put its equity investment at about $3B. Oracle and Blue Owl shares closed down 3.5% and 3.6% respectively. Read alongside the Brookings financing paper, this is a concrete instance of the execution risk in long-dated AI infrastructure: power access and completion schedules, not compute demand, are the binding constraint.
