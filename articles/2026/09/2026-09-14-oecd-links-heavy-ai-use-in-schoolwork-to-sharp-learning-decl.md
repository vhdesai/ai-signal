# OECD Links Heavy AI Use in Schoolwork to Sharp Learning Declines

The OECD reported that AI use for schoolwork is associated with students falling roughly 1.5 years behind, with chatbot use described as pervasive across Asian economies. The finding aligns with PISA 2025 results showing the worst average math and reading scores ever recorded across participating jurisdictions. The pattern of concern is substitution rather than assistance — students who request direct answers show measurable capability erosion, while those asking for hints do not. The same substitution dynamic is the live question for enterprise AI deployment and workforce skill retention.
