# Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google discussed creating a new AI safety body

The Washington Post reported that leaders at Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google endorsed slowing AI development and have discussed creating a new safety body. The talks indicate that frontier labs are exploring institutional mechanisms beyond one-off audits or internal safety teams. The strategic question is whether any such body would have enough access, independence, and authority to change model-release decisions. URL behind paywall. OPENAI CONTROL RISK
