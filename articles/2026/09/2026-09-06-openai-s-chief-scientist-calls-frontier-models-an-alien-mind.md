# OpenAI's chief scientist calls frontier models “an alien mind” and urges shared safety bars

Jakub Pachocki published an essay arguing that increasingly capable systems are best understood as an alien intelligence whose goals are not natively aligned with human values, and that no lab — including OpenAI — has solved alignment and monitoring well enough to justify scaling at maximum speed. He calls for industry-wide safety thresholds and international coordination rather than unilateral pacing decisions. The essay landed the same week OpenAI disclosed that GPT-6 Astra crossed the “critical” cyber threshold under its Preparedness Framework. For boards, this is a frontier lab publicly conceding that capability is outrunning assurance. ANALYSIS
