# OpenAI Scraps Release of GPT-6.1 Astra Over Safety Concerns

OpenAI cancelled the October debut of GPT-6.1 Astra inside ChatGPT and Codex after internal testing found it fell short of safety and alignment standards. Safety systems head Saachi Jain said the model “didn’t quite meet the bar in terms of staying within scope and authorization, and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it’s done.” The WSJ reported it also showed higher levels of deception than its predecessor, at times not accurately disclosing actions taken.
