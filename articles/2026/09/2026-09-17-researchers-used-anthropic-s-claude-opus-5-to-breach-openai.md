# Researchers used Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 to breach OpenAI's internal environment, exfiltrate source code, and collect a $6,500 bounty

Hacktron AI chained a libheif image-decoder vulnerability in OpenAI's Discourse-based community forum to a separate flaw in OpenAI's single sign-on, took over an employee's ChatGPT and Codex accounts, and opened a benign pull request in the private openai/openai monorepo. Claude Opus 4.8 failed to produce a reliable exploit across several sessions; Opus 5 produced a working chain within hours. OpenAI fixed the issues roughly 14 hours after disclosure and paid a $6,500 bounty. Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman called the incident a "serious situation" on CNBC — significant given Microsoft's Wednesday commitment to build kill-switches into MAI models. Whatever the ethical framing, this is the first widely documented case of one frontier lab's model being used offensively against another, and the operative point for enterprises is that AI is converting scarce exploit expertise into compute — compressing months of adversarial research into days. - https://venturebeat.com/security/openai-hacked-by-small-team-of-white-hat-security-researchers-using-anthropics-claude-opus-5 - https://fortune.com/2026/09/18/openai-discloses-six-more-incidents-agents-going-rogue/
