# Reports warn hackers are turning Claude, Qwen and DeepSeek into attack agents

CybersecurityNews and related security feeds reported that attackers are using models such as Claude, Qwen, and DeepSeek as AI agents for real-world cyberattacks, including activity against government systems. Even where individual claims require technical validation, the trend is directionally consistent with the broader shift from prompt-based misuse to autonomous attack workflows.
