# Sanders and Casar Propose a U.S. Ban on 'Artificial Superintelligence'

Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar proposed federal legislation to prohibit development of so-called artificial superintelligence, arguing the government should set limits before systems exceed human control. The proposal follows Sanders' earlier letters to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta calling for a voluntary pause after a series of agent-related security incidents. Passage prospects are low in the current Congress, but the framing — a capability ceiling rather than a use-case rule — is a departure from prior U.S. legislative approaches and worth tracking as a template.
