# China's Moonshot and Z.ai bring AI-model subscriptions to Alibaba's Tmall retail platform

Moonshot AI (developer of the Kimi K3 model) and Z.ai/MiniMax are in advanced talks to open flagship stores on Alibaba's Tmall e-commerce platform, selling AI-model subscription plans alongside physical consumer goods. It is the first major push by Chinese frontier-model developers into mainstream e-commerce distribution rather than app stores or direct sign-up. The move signals that the Chinese consumer AI market is being commoditized into a subscription retail category, and reflects vendors' need for cheaper user-acquisition channels than paid app-install campaigns.
