# Google tests Flipkart checkout inside Gemini and AI Mode in India

Google has begun testing a "Buy" button on selected Walmart-owned Flipkart listings inside Gemini and AI Mode in India, opening a Flipkart-branded checkout without leaving the AI interface. The test covers a limited user set and a narrow catalog — smartphones, electronics and mobile accessories — with a broader rollout planned for October, ahead of India's festive shopping season. The flow differs from the Google-hosted checkout previously demonstrated under Google's Universal Commerce Protocol.
