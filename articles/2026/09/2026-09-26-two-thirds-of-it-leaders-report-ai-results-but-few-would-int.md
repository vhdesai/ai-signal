# Two-thirds of IT leaders report AI results, but few would interrupt the CEO's vacation over them

At an event with 160 IT VPs, roughly two-thirds raised hands to say they had measurable AI results — but only 8 said the results were consequential enough to interrupt the CEO's summer vacation. The gap is a compact framing of the "AI adoption vs. AI impact" divergence and lines up with a WSJ Anthropic-veterans story running the same day. It sharpens the bubble-vs-bottleneck question executives are being asked to price at every earnings call. INDUSTRY
