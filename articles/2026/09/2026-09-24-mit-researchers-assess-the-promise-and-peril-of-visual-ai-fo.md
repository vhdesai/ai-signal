# MIT researchers assess the promise and peril of visual AI for studying cities

MIT News covered a new book from Senseable City Lab researchers on how visual AI can help study urban dynamics at scale. The authors describe applications in emissions, traffic flow, safety, street-level imagery, sidewalks, greenery, and urban interiors, while warning about surveillance and bias risks. The practical implication is that visual AI may become a planning tool for cities, but only if governance keeps pace with data collection and model interpretation.
