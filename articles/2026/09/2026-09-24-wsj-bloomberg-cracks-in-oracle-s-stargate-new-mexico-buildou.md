# WSJ / Bloomberg: Cracks in Oracle's Stargate New Mexico buildout for OpenAI

Oracle, which is set to lease the New Mexico data center on behalf of OpenAI, sent the developer a notice asserting its contractual rights to withhold payments if project delays persist, according to Bloomberg. The reporting compounds this week's WSJ coverage of Oracle's massive New Mexico AI DC project showing execution cracks, with the company invoking force majeure to protect against commitments. 24/7 Wall St. framed it "Oracle's Collapse—$200 Billion In Data Center Shut Downs." The story lands as Oracle stock continues to underperform peers and Blackstone's Jas Khaira publicly says the firm still hasn't mapped out "who's going to buy all the debt" of the AI buildout.
