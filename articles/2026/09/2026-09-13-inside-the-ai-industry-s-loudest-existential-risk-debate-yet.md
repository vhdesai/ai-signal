# Inside the AI industry's loudest existential-risk debate yet

TechCrunch's Equity podcast examined the doomer wave triggered by an Anthropic researcher's resignation warning that leading labs are "gambling with our lives," amplified when Anthropic's alignment lead publicly put extinction risk above 10% within the decade. Hosts questioned whether the timing — with Anthropic preparing an IPO — makes the warnings a capability signal as much as a safety one, and what such statements mean for S-1 risk disclosure. The discussion is a useful proxy for how institutional investors are likely to interrogate these claims.
