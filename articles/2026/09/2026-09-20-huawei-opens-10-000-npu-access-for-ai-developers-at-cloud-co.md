# Huawei opens 10,000-NPU access for AI developers at Cloud Connect 2026

Huawei opened access to 10,000 Ascend NPUs for AI developers as part of Cloud Connect 2026 announcements, alongside the AgentArts platform and the Agentic Cloud Stack. Combined with Wednesday's Ascend 960DT roadmap pull-forward to Q1 2027 (nine months earlier than planned) and Huawei's Fintelligent AI Solution launch, this is a coordinated full-stack Chinese AI-cloud push spanning hardware, cloud, agent platforms, and developer access — squarely aimed at Nvidia/AWS.
