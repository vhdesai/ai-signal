# PitchBook: factory robotics still needs more than smarter foundation models

PitchBook argued that robotics developers are progressing on AI controllers, but industrial deployment remains constrained by hardware variation, contact dynamics, safety requirements, and expensive real-world data. Foundation models provide high-level reasoning; embodiment-specific adaptation and real-time controls still matter. RESEARCH INFRASTRUCTURE NATIONAL AI
