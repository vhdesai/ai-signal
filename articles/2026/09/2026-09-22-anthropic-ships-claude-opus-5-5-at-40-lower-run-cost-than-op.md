# Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5.5 at 40% lower run cost than Opus 5

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in its Claude 5.5 family, stating it performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most work while costing roughly 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads. Input and output pricing is $4 and $20 per million tokens (20% below Opus 5), with cache reads at $0.20 per million — 60% cheaper — which matters disproportionately for agentic and coding traffic. Anthropic cites an early tester completing a 680,000-line code migration in under a day and a 200,000-line codebase audit in under three hours versus more than 20 hours for Opus 5. It is the company's first release since its public call to "pace the frontier," and was tested pre-release by external evaluators including METR and Frontier Design.
