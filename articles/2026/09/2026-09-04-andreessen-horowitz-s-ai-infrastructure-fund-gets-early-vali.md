# Andreessen Horowitz’s AI infrastructure fund gets early validation from Cursor and OpenRouter

The Information profiles Martin Casado’s AI infrastructure investing strategy at Andreessen Horowitz, noting that recent Cursor and OpenRouter exits are together worth more than $8B to the firm. The fund’s focus has broadened from AI tools and software into hardware and agent tooling. The piece shows investors treating agent tools, developer platforms, and model marketplaces as strategic control points.
