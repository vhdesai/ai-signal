# Anthropic in talks to lease up to 1GW at Apollo-backed Stream Data Centers, filled with Google/Broadcom TPUs

The Information reports Anthropic is in early talks to lease up to 1 gigawatt of compute capacity from Stream Data Centers — a 27-year-old developer majority-owned by Apollo Global Management — as a direct tenant, filling the sites with tensor processing units designed by Broadcom and Google. It's a significant step to reduce reliance on cloud providers and cement cost control ahead of Anthropic's IPO. The move fits the pattern of frontier labs owning more of the compute stack and joins the $45B Nscale deal, the $517B compute stack disclosed earlier, and the $13.7B Rum Group deal.
