# China invites DeepSeek and Moonshot to the UN Security Council briefing on AI risks

Global Times reports China has invited DeepSeek and Moonshot AI to participate in the UN Security Council briefing on AI risks, aligning with Reuters' earlier scoop. That participation lands despite Beijing's active CAC data-routing investigation into both firms following Anthropic's public allegations (Alibaba stock fell 4% on Bloomberg's coverage yesterday). The "brief the UN while under domestic investigation" dynamic captures how quickly AI governance is now moving on both sides of the US–China frontier-lab divide, and it arrives the same day Trump meets Xi in Washington.
