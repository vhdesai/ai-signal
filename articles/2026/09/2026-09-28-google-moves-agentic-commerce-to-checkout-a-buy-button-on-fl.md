# Google moves agentic commerce to checkout: a “Buy” button on Flipkart inside Gemini and AI Mode

Google is testing direct purchase on selected Flipkart listings inside Gemini and Search AI Mode in India — limited users, a narrow catalogue of smartphones, electronics and mobile accessories, with broader rollout planned for October ahead of the festive season. The plumbing is the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), introduced at NRF in January, which supports catalogue access, checkout sessions, account linking and order updates while keeping the retailer as merchant of record. Google has also added AI performance reporting in Merchant Center spanning AI Mode, AI Overviews and Gemini, giving retailers their first attribution view into agent-mediated demand.
