# SoftBank Launches Record $11B+ Junk-Bond Sale to Fund Its Next OpenAI Tranche

SoftBank Group launched $10B of dollar senior unsecured notes across 3.5-, 5.5-, and 7.5-year tenors plus €1B of euro notes across two maturities — more than $11B combined, among the largest single-issuer high-yield deals on record. Proceeds fund SoftBank's roughly $10B payment on the third tranche of its OpenAI follow-on investment, expected to close October 1, and cancel an existing $10B bridge loan. Fitch assigned BB+; pricing is slated for September 24, settlement September 29.
