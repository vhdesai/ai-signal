# Anthropic considers releasing a new model ahead of IPO, Reuters reports

Reuters reported that Anthropic is considering releasing a new AI model ahead of its expected IPO, citing people familiar with the matter. The timing matters because capability releases, safety posture, and investor narrative are increasingly linked for frontier labs approaching public-market scrutiny.
