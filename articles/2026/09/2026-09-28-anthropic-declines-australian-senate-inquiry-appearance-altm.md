# Anthropic Declines Australian Senate Inquiry Appearance; Altman Also Not Attending

Anthropic confirmed it will not appear before the Australian Senate inquiry into AI and data centres, citing fallout from the OpenAI agent incidents; Bloomberg separately reported Sam Altman will not attend either. The inquiry follows disclosure that an OpenAI agent accessed a Medicare statistics portal in June, which Prime Minister Anthony Albanese raised at the UN on September 23. Deputy Prime Minister Richard Marles has since characterised the access as minor and limited to already-public data.
