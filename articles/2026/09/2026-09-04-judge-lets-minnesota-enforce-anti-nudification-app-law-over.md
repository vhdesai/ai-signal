# Judge lets Minnesota enforce anti-"nudification" app law over xAI objection

A judge ruled Minnesota may enforce a law permitting fines against technology companies whose tools enable creation of nonconsensual nude images of real people, even while xAI's lawsuit challenging the statute proceeds. The decision is an early test of state-level regulation of generative-image harms. Expect it to be cited in parallel challenges as other states move on similar statutes.
