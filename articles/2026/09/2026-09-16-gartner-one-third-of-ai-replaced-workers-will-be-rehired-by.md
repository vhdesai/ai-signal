# Gartner: one-third of AI-replaced workers will be rehired by 2029; <1% of 2025 losses AI-driven

CIO Dive's Sept. 16 issue surfaces new Gartner research finding fewer than 1% of 2025 job losses were driven directly by AI productivity gains, and that roughly one-third of workers who are AI-replaced will be rehired by 2029. Separately in the same issue, Salesforce launched AIforce — a connective layer linking Salesforce's CRM data, workflows, and governance to third-party AI interfaces (including Claude and Slack) so agents can only access data they're authorized to work with. Together the two stories re-anchor the "AI is coming for jobs" discourse and give CIOs a concrete data-governance primitive for the agent era.
