# SpaceX Shakes Up Data Center Leadership After Aggressive Build-Out

Elon Musk has replaced several leaders on the SpaceX data center team with executives from rocket and Starlink operations following civil engineering concerns and reliability issues at SpaceXAI's Tennessee and Mississippi facilities. Facilities reportedly ran for months without backup cooling and power. The reshuffle signals that even well-capitalized entrants struggle with the physical-infrastructure realities of AI at scale.
