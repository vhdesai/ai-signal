# Meta employees flagged Muse security failures during pre-launch testing

Reuters-reviewed internal posts show Meta staff testing Muse reported guardrail bypasses and unreliable behavior in the days before launch, including a case where the agent surfaced a user's iCloud photos after being asked to identify toys in birthday party images, and another where the agent stalled mid-task without surfacing any failure signal. The launch arrives two weeks after Meta agreed to a settlement of up to $17.1 billion with state attorneys general over child-safety claims. The architectural assurances announced with Muse are launch claims, not independently audited controls — a distinction procurement teams should be explicit about when evaluating consumer or SMB agent products.
