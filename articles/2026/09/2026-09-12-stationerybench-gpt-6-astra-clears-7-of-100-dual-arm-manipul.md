# StationeryBench: GPT-6 Astra Clears 7 of 100 Dual-Arm Manipulation Tasks; Ai2’s MolmoAct2 Clears Zero

A new robotics benchmark ran both models on identical dual-arm YAM robots across 200 trials and five desk-object tasks — uncapping a marker, pouring out paper clips, passing a ruler between arms. Astra fully completed 7 of 100 tasks against MolmoAct2’s zero, with median progress scores of 46 versus 12. Cornell/DeepMind researcher Yoav Artzi called Astra a “step change in spatial reasoning” while cautioning that “even ASTRA doesn’t get to what humans do in other scenarios.” Read the absolute numbers, not the headline: 7% full completion is a low bar, and the claim is relative competence.
