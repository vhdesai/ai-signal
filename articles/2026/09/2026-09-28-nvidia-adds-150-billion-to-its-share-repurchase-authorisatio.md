# Nvidia Adds $150 Billion to Its Share Repurchase Authorisation

Nvidia's board authorised an additional $150 billion under the existing share repurchase programme, taking the total remaining authorisation to $235 billion. The announcement landed the same day as the company's Open Agent Safety Platform launch. At this scale the buyback is a statement about expected free cash flow durability through the current AI capex cycle rather than a routine capital-return adjustment.
