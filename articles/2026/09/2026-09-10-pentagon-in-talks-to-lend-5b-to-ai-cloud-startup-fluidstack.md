# Pentagon in Talks to Lend $5B to AI Cloud Startup Fluidstack

The Department of Defense is negotiating a roughly $5 billion loan to AI cloud provider Fluidstack through its Office of Strategic Capital — by a wide margin the office's largest facility to date. The money would shore up US manufacturing capacity for data-center components rather than fund a facility outright. Fluidstack already has commercial ties to Google, Anthropic, Nvidia and Meta. The precedent matters more than the sum: AI compute supply chains are now being treated as defense industrial base.
