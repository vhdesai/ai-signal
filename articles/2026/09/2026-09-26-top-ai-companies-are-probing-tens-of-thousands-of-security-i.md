# Top AI companies are probing tens of thousands of security incidents

Axios reported that leading AI companies are investigating tens of thousands of security incidents as fears grow around agent behavior, containment failures, and misuse. The story comes amid multiple disclosures involving OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google systems interacting with real external systems. The operational point is straightforward: frontier labs and enterprise deployers need mature incident queues, triage rules, and external notification processes that look more like cybersecurity operations than product support.
