# Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro takes the top open-weight slot on the Intelligence Index

Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.6-Pro and the cheaper V2.6-Flash under an MIT license on Hugging Face, with Pro scoring 46 on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index — tied with the same-day Grok 4.7 and ahead of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 39. Pro is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 1.02 trillion total and 42 billion active parameters, a 1-million-token context window, and native text, image, audio and video input; API pricing is $0.435/$0.87 per million tokens. Xiaomi also open-sourced its reinforcement-learning framework and more than 7,000 task environments, and disclosed training costs of roughly $2.62M for Pro and $0.85M for Flash across a six-day run. Frontier-adjacent capability with downloadable weights weakens the pricing power of closed mid-tier APIs. MODEL MARKETS
