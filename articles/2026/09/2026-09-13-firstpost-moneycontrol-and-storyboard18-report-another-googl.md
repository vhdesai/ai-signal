# Firstpost, Moneycontrol, and Storyboard18 report another Google DeepMind AI safety researcher has resigned, publicly citing a "terrifying chance" AI could cause immense harm. Times of India also reports Google is moving its AI responsibility team out of DeepMind entirely, starting this month — a structural shift that critics say weakens safety integration inside model development. Combined with the Amodei/Altman/Hassabis slowdown call, the day's news suggests unusually coordinated concern from within the industry itself.
