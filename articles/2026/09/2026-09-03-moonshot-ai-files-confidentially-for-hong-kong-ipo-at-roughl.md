# Moonshot AI Files Confidentially for Hong Kong IPO at Roughly $50B Valuation

Beijing-based Moonshot AI, developer of the Kimi model family and backed by Alibaba, Tencent and HSG, has confidentially filed for a Hong Kong listing reported to target about $3 billion at a valuation near $50 billion. It would be among the largest AI listings to emerge from China and follows earlier listings that built investor appetite for Chinese model developers. A completed offering would establish a public-market valuation benchmark for Chinese frontier labs and fund chips, training capacity and talent — a sharp contrast to the still-private financing model of leading US labs. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/moonshot-ai-confidentially-files-hong-122635290.html EARNINGS
