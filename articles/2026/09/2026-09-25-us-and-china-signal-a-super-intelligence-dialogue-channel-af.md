# US and China Signal a "Super-Intelligence" Dialogue Channel After the Trump–Xi Summit

Following the Trump–Xi summit at the White House, the two governments signaled agreement to establish a dialogue channel on advanced AI, with a mutual notification mechanism for national-security-relevant AI incidents floated as a candidate for cooperation. Xi stated that AI development "should always be kept under human control." No binding agreement on export controls or model access was reported, and working-level follow-through remains undefined. The practical signal for technology planners is that incident notification, not capability restriction, is the first area where the two governments appear willing to coordinate — consistent with the direction Bessent and He Lifeng started earlier in the month. *(
