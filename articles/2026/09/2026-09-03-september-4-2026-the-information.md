# [September 4, 2026] · The Information

NVIDIA agreed to acquire Hugging Face for approximately $12.93 billion; the agreement should not be confused with a completed transaction. Jensen Huang says the platform will remain open to competing models, clouds, and accelerators, without requiring NVIDIA hardware. The acquisition would combine a major model-distribution platform with the leading AI accelerator supplier, making neutrality a central customer concern.
