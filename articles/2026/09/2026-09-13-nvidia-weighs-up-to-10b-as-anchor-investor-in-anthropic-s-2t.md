# Nvidia weighs up to $10B as anchor investor in Anthropic's ~$2T IPO

Reuters reported that Anthropic is seeking to raise as much as $100 billion at a valuation near $2 trillion, with Nvidia considering an anchor commitment of up to $10 billion. For scale, US IPOs excluding SPACs raised a record $137 billion through August; a single listing would absorb roughly three-quarters of that. Anthropic's May round valued it at $965 billion on a $65 billion run rate, so the IPO mark is more than double in under four months. The structural question is circularity: the supplier being paid is also the investor validating the price. WORKFORCE
