# Boehringer Ingelheim Licenses Owkin's K Pro 'AI Scientist' and Multimodal Patient Data

Owkin signed a license agreement giving Boehringer Ingelheim access to K Pro, its agentic AI research system, together with multimodal oncology and immunology datasets. The deal builds on a 2025 pilot using Owkin's MOSAIC dataset for tumor-microenvironment analysis and follows K Pro licenses with AstraZeneca in May and Sanofi in June. Three top-20 pharma licensees in four months is a credible signal that agentic AI is moving from pilot to procured capability in drug discovery — one of the few verticals where that transition is now documented by contract rather than by survey. https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/owkin-license-k-pro-ai-120000387.html
