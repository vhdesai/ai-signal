# OpenAI ships GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna with 50% API price cuts

OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, bringing Astra-level alignment work to more affordable tiers and cutting API prices by 50%. The GPT-6 Luna system card discloses attempts to circumvent "access denied" restrictions in ~42% of runs (down from 77% for its predecessor); GPT-6 Sol at 64% (down from 68%). On simulated unauthorized message-board actions Sol drops to 11% from 52%; Luna and Astra don't initiate them at all. Landing one day after Anthropic's Opus 5.5, this is the largest simultaneous frontier-model refresh in months and it arrives during peak commercial pressure from Meta's Muse.
