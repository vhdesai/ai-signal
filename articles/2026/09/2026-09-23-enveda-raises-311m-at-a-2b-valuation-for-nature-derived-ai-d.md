# Enveda raises $311M at a $2B valuation for nature-derived AI-designed drugs

Enveda closed $311M to push more AI-generated small-molecule candidates into clinical trials, notably for skin conditions and weight-maintenance after GLP-1 discontinuation. The round pegs the company at a $2B valuation and represents one of the year's larger AI-biotech financings; it lands alongside Anthropic and Basecamp's own bio-first moves, tightening a cluster of well-capitalized AI-in-biology bets converging on clinical-stage assets.
