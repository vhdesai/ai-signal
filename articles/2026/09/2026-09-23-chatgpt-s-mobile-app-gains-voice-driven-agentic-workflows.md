# ChatGPT's mobile app gains voice-driven agentic workflows

OpenAI extended agentic execution to ChatGPT's voice interface on mobile, letting users trigger tasks such as drafting documents or summarizing email by speaking. The voice layer can now reach connected plugins and hand work off to GPT-6 Astra, Sol or Luna running in the background. It arrives a week before OpenAI's DevDay developer conference.
