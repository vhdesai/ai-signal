# Princeton, Stanford, and Ant Group introduce AQuA, self-improving quant research agents

Researchers from Princeton University, Stanford University, and Ant Group published AQuA, a two-part agentic framework pairing a symbolic factor-discovery system with a trainable model-development system; the two share no agents, memories, or candidate spaces. The core contribution is structural rather than prompt-level: a sealed sandbox makes data leakage physically unwritable, addressing the failure mode where a leaky feature scores well, gets stored as a successful precedent, and propagates through later iterations. The result generalizes beyond finance to any self-improving research agent that learns from its own recorded outcomes. https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/01/aqua-a-two-part-agentic-framework-for-autonomous-factor-discovery/
