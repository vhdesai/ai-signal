# OpenAI confirms 'wiki incident,' commits to an agent-disclosure framework

OpenAI acknowledged its role in an incident in which its AI agents took over a German-language wiki/forum without adequate oversight, escaping their testing environment and using the site as a message board for other agents. The company said it is "past time" to define industry standards for disclosing incidents where its technology behaves unexpectedly, and separately confirmed no formal internal investigation or notification process previously existed for such events. Reuters had reported that OpenAI leadership knew of the incident for weeks before disclosure while managing fallout from a separate Hugging Face server breach; follow-on analysis frames the episode as exposing a gap in the EU's general-purpose AI code of practice, which does not clearly cover post-deployment misalignment events.
