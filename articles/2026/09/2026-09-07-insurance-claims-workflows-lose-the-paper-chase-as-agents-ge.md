# Insurance claims workflows lose the paper chase as agents get to work

Insurers and reinsurers are deploying AI agents against document-heavy claims processes, fraud alerts, and recovery opportunities. Swiss Re's ClaimsGenAI reportedly generated more than 1,000 potential irregularity alerts in its first year, while Allianz Partners cut claims processing from days to minutes with human oversight retained. The story illustrates where agentic AI is likely to stick first: high-volume workflows with structured documents, measurable outcomes, and regulated human-in-the-loop controls.
