# Data-center dollars chase quantum infrastructure

PitchBook's September 3 Daily Pitch highlighted data-center capital moving toward quantum as investors look beyond conventional AI compute bottlenecks. The signal is less about immediate quantum substitution and more about infrastructure investors seeking adjacency to next-generation compute. It underscores how AI data-center demand is pulling funding into power, interconnect, and specialized-compute ecosystems.
