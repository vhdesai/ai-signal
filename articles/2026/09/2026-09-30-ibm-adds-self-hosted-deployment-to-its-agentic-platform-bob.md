# IBM Adds Self-Hosted Deployment to Its Agentic Platform Bob

IBM added a self-hosted deployment option to Bob, its agentic AI-software-development platform, as vendors move to address enterprise governance concerns. The self-hosted mode targets regulated verticals and customers whose data classification precludes cloud-only agent runtimes. It lands the same day OpenAI shipped Dots for Enterprise and Business Premium subscribers, and Anthropic published its Zero Trust for agents paper — the enterprise-agent runtime is quickly bifurcating into consumer "always-on" and enterprise "in-tenant with audit trails," and vendors are declaring their lane openly.
