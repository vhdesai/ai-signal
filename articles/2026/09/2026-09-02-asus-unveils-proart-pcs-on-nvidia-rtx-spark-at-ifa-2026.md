# ASUS unveils ProArt PCs on NVIDIA RTX Spark at IFA 2026

ASUS announced ProArt P16/P14 laptops and a GR1X mini PC built on NVIDIA's RTX Spark superchip, pairing a Blackwell RTX GPU with a Grace CPU and up to 128GB of unified memory for roughly one petaflop of AI performance. The machines are positioned as the first Windows PCs built for personal agents and can run models up to 120B parameters locally. They were shown at ASUS's IFA showroom in Berlin, September 2–5.
