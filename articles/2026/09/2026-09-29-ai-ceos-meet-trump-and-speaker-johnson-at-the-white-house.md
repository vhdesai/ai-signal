# AI CEOs Meet Trump and Speaker Johnson at the White House

Leaders of the major AI companies are attending a lunch meeting with President Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson, with Mark Zuckerberg, Dario Amodei, Sundar Pichai, Jensen Huang, Alex Karp and OpenAI's Greg Brockman expected. The meeting follows industry calls for a coordinated slowdown, which Trump has publicly dismissed as a "hoax" while stating the US "rejects" international attempts to regulate AI; Johnson has argued the companies "can self-police." The House has left Washington until after the midterms, so no near-term federal legislation is in prospect. The practical outcome for enterprises is that binding US federal AI rules remain unlikely this year, leaving state law, sector regulators and vendor contracts as the operative controls.
