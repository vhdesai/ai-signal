# Two more Google DeepMind researchers resign over AI safety

Two Google DeepMind AI-safety researchers, Bilal Chughtai and Josh Engels, resigned citing concerns that powerful systems could endanger humans. Engels wrote "there's a terrifying chance that AI systems cause immense harm in the next five years" and is joining METR; Chughtai wrote "AI has the potential to kill us all" and is joining BlueDot Impact. They follow recent Anthropic exits Jacob Coxon and Joe Benton, and the earlier DeepMind safety departure. The senior-safety-exit pattern is now a durable, cross-lab story ahead of Anthropic and OpenAI IPOs.
