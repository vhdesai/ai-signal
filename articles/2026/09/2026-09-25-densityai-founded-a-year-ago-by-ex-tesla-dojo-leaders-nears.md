# DensityAI — Founded a Year Ago by ex-Tesla Dojo Leaders — Nears $10B Valuation on an AWS Chip Purchase Commitment

DensityAI, founded just a year ago by former leaders of Tesla’s Dojo supercomputer program, is in advanced talks to raise hundreds of millions of dollars at a $10B valuation, per two people with direct knowledge. The valuation is aggressive for a company whose chips remain years from mass production, but the founders told investors they have secured an agreement in which AWS will purchase eventual DensityAI chips subject to performance targets. It is a rare structured off-take from a hyperscaler in the AI-silicon startup market, and it explains why late-stage capital continues to flow into custom-silicon plays even as spot GPU credit spreads widen.
