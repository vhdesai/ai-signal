# Runway wants to stream AI video generation as a live, real-time controllable feed

Runway is repositioning around live-streaming video generation built on its GWM-1 world model — you prompt, the model generates frames continuously, and you steer output in real time. Beyond creative tools, Runway is explicitly targeting robotics and autonomous-driving simulation as adjacent use cases. For enterprises building simulation-heavy R&D pipelines (robotics, ADAS, gaming), the "video stream you can steer" primitive is a materially different tooling category than Sora- or Veo-style clip generation.
