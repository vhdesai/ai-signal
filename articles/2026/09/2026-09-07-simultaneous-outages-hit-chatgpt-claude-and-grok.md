# Simultaneous outages hit ChatGPT, Claude and Grok

ChatGPT, Claude and Grok all degraded within hours of each other, prompting speculation about a shared cloud dependency. Reporting attributes the correlation to shared underlying AI infrastructure rather than a single named cloud provider failure; root cause has not been confirmed by the vendors as of publication. The episode is a concrete argument for multi-provider fallback in any workflow where an LLM sits on a critical path.
