# HKT pilots identity verification for AI-agent payment flows

Markets Insider reported that HKT Payment was selected for Hong Kong's GenA.I. Sandbox++ to pilot registration and verification for AI agent-initiated payment flows. HKT and Red Date Technology plan an Agentic ID framework using decentralized identifiers and verifiable credentials to bind AI agents to verified individuals or enterprises. This is a practical governance issue: payment-capable agents need accountability, audit trails, and controls before they can safely act on behalf of users or firms.
