# Researchers unveil AI that can design viruses from scratch; another DeepMind safety resignation

The Cool Down reports researchers demonstrated an AI capable of designing viruses from scratch, framing the concern as "fear over who uses it next." Times of India and Inshorts report yet another Google DeepMind employee has resigned over AI safety concerns, saying "I want future work to be pro-human." NPR published a guide to the AI-safety debate's competing factions the same day, and SMBtech complemented with UNSW research on why AI safety frameworks keep failing — a rare weekend cycle of substantive AI-safety coverage. It stacks with today's OpenAI rogue-agent disclosures and the Grok-API malware to make this weekend the year's largest safety-incident news volume.
