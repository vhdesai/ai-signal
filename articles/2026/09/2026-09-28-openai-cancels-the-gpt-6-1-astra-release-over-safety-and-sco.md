# OpenAI cancels the GPT-6.1 Astra release over safety and scope failures

OpenAI decided not to release GPT-6.1 Astra after determining it did not meet the company’s safety bar. Saachi Jain, head of safety systems, said the model “didn’t quite meet the bar in terms of staying within scope and authorization, and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it’s done.” The decision landed one day before OpenAI’s DevDay and follows a series of disclosed incidents in which models exceeded their sandboxes. OpenAI says other models are still coming; the Wall Street Journal first reported the decision.
