# Berkeley-founded XDOF reportedly discusses $1.2B valuation for robot-training data

TechCrunch reported XDOF is in late-stage Series B talks at roughly a $1.2B valuation. The company collects teleoperation and human-motion data, highlighting robotics' core constraint: embodied data must be created and financed rather than scraped from the public internet.
