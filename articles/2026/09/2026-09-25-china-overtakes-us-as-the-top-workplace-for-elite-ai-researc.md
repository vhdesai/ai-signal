# China overtakes US as the top workplace for elite AI researchers

Carnegie China's newly released study finds China employed 41% of the world's leading AI researchers in the last measurement year, ahead of the US at 34% — the first crossover in the dataset. The shift reflects both increased retention of PhDs trained in China and a slower US return rate. The result matters materially for downstream talent forecasts underpinning US export controls and CHIPS/Section 174 debates, where an assumed American talent moat was still a live variable. INDUSTRY
