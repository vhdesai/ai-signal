# Xiaomi Releases MiMo-V2.6 Pro and Flash as Open-Weight Omnimodal Models

Xiaomi published the MiMo-V2.6 series on Hugging Face under an MIT license: an omnimodal Pro model and a 309-billion-parameter Flash mixture-of-experts with roughly 15 billion active parameters. Reported scores put Pro at 46 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and 72.57% on DeepSWE v1.1, with Flash at 65.68% — a very large single-generation jump, with all figures currently self-reported pending independent evaluation. Xiaomi also open-sourced the RL framework and task environments and shipped day-0 serving support via vLLM. Combined with Grok 4.7 and StepFun's Step 5 Preview, three models landed in the same capability band inside 48 hours, none from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. Products & Tools PREVIEW
