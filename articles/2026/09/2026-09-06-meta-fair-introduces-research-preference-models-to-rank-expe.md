# Meta FAIR introduces Research Preference Models to rank experiments before spending GPU hours

Meta FAIR, with Oxford and UCL, released AI Research Preference Models — frozen LLM judges that rank unexecuted experiment candidates so only the most promising one is run. On AIRS-Bench, average normalized score rose from 0.684 to 0.729 with no additional training. The practical implication is cost governance for AI research pipelines: selection quality, rather than raw compute, becomes the lever on experiment throughput. RESEARCH
