# OpenAI publishes safety-case guidelines gating frontier reinforcement-learning training runs

OpenAI published initial guidelines arguing that structured safety documentation — ideally full “safety cases” of the kind used in aviation and nuclear power — should be required before continuing any frontier RL training run. The framework covers alignment training, containment and monitoring, plus operational practices: a dissent written by someone outside the run’s team, vetoes held by multiple senior leaders, fail-closed monitoring, and immutable write-once transcripts. Notable specifics include barring automated graders from seeing chain-of-thought, to prevent models evolving to evade CoT monitors. OpenAI calls the standard “an aspirational north star” still being rolled out. POLICY
