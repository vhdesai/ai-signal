# Anthropic's Amodei and DeepSeek Will Separately Brief the UN Security Council on AI Risks This Week

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei will brief the UN Security Council on AI risks, and Reuters separately reports DeepSeek will address the same body this week — the first time two frontier labs from different countries have both taken the Security Council podium on AI. The dual briefings follow the UN Independent International Scientific Panel's thematic brief on AI agents and misalignment (co-chaired by Yoshua Bengio), and coincide with the UK Parliament inviting OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and Google DeepMind to a hearing.
