# TechCrunch: AI industry remains split over whether it is really ready to slow down

TechCrunch analyzed the widening divide between frontier-lab leaders calling to "pace the frontier" and infrastructure leaders such as Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang arguing against a slowdown. The piece notes that proposals around embedded evaluators and international coordination still lack operational detail and face antitrust and incentive questions. For executives, the safety debate is now a business-model debate: slowing development affects capital plans, compute demand, competitive positioning, and public trust. REGULATION NVIDIA
