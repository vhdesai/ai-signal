# US Proposes an AI Incident "Hotline" With China Ahead of the Trump–Xi Summit

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent disclosed that Washington has proposed a "notification mechanism" with Beijing for AI incidents rising to national-security level, following an eight-hour meeting with Vice Premier He Lifeng in New York. The framework would be branded the US-China AI Dialogue and covers uncontrollable agents, non-state cyber actors and bioweapons risk, with a follow-up planned in Shenzhen in roughly two months. US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer confirmed that chip and semiconductor-equipment export controls were excluded from the AI track. Xinhua called the talks "candid, in-depth and constructive" but did not confirm the mechanism. POLICY
