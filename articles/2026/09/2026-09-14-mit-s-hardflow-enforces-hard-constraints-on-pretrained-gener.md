# MIT's HardFlow enforces hard constraints on pretrained generative models at deployment time

MIT researchers developed HardFlow, a deployment-time algorithm that lets pretrained diffusion and flow-matching models satisfy hard constraints without retraining. Rather than forcing every intermediate sample to obey constraints, it reformulates constrained sampling as a trajectory-optimization problem drawn from optimal control and enforces constraints on the final output. Across robotic manipulation, maze navigation, and text-guided image editing it achieved perfect constraint satisfaction while beating baselines on solution quality. The work appears in IEEE TPAMI from Navid Azizan (senior author), Zeyang Li, and Kaveh Alim.
