# xAI Ships Grok 4.7 at Unchanged $2/$6 Pricing, Leading on Engineering and Legal Benchmarks

xAI released Grok 4.7 built on a larger base model and a longer reinforcement-learning run weighted toward multi-hour tasks, holding Grok 4.6's $2 per million input / $6 per million output pricing with a 500,000-token context. Vendor-reported scores improve on every published row versus Grok 4.6, including Terminal-Bench 4.0 rising from 20.3% to 38.0%, EEBench to 64.0% and the Harvey Legal Agent Benchmark to 19.6%. Fable 5.1 Max still leads four of seven benchmarks and GPT-5.6 Sol Max holds the top DeepSWE result, but at five to eight times the output price. The commercial read is price-performance pressure on frontier output pricing, not a capability leapfrog.
