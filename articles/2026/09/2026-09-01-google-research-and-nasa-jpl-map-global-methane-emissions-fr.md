# Google Research and NASA JPL map global methane emissions from space using deep learning

Google Research, in collaboration with NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, published work using deep learning models to detect and map methane emissions globally from satellite imagery. The approach improves detection speed and coverage compared with prior manual and heuristic methods, offering climate researchers and regulators a faster way to identify large emission sources.
