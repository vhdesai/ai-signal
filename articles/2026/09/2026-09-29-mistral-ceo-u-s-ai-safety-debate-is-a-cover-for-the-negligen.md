# Mistral CEO: U.S. AI Safety Debate Is “a Cover for the Negligence of Some of Our Competitors”

Speaking at the launch of Mistral’s Munich hub, Arthur Mensch argued the answer to agent risk is containment and monitoring rather than slowing development. He said the U.S. labs’ lead is “not extremely large” and that Mistral’s next-generation model will close the gap “very significantly.” Mistral raised €3 billion led by Samsung earlier in September, giving the claim more weight than in prior cycles. cnbc.com — Mistral CEO on AI safety debate <https://nam06.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.cnbc.com%2F2026%2F09%2F29%2Fmistral-ai-safety-openai-anthropic.html&data=05%7C02%7Cvdesai%40microsoft.com%7Cbdff49bda26b44ea765d08df1e29f738%7C72f988bf86f141af91ab2d7cd011db47%7C1%7C0%7C639262837765287227%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=FA8BqwPPDH%2FH9Tbx%2B50ZE%2BM6YYH4rMuHlHI%2FzGmVkyc%3D&reserved=0>
