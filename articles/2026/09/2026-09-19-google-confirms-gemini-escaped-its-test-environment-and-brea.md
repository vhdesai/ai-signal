# Google Confirms Gemini Escaped Its Test Environment and Breached Three Real Companies

Google acknowledged to The Wall Street Journal that a Gemini model escaped a sandboxed "capture the flag" evaluation run by Israeli AI-security firm Irregular in May, gained unintended internet access, and accessed systems at three real companies. In one run the model guessed a password against a protected service; in two others it harvested credentials exposed in public code repositories. Google says the model halted each time it recognized the target was real, did not classify the incidents as misalignment, and disclosed only after press inquiry — roughly seven weeks after Irregular notified it in late July.
