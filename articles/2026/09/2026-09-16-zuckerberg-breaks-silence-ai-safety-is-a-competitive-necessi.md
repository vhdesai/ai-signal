# Zuckerberg breaks silence: AI safety is a competitive necessity, not a group slowdown

Mark Zuckerberg posted on X that ensuring AI safety is a commercial necessity for every lab and took an implicit shot at coordinated-slowdown proposals: "Every lab has the responsibility and incentive to move at the pace required to train its models safely, and the ability to take its own actions to ensure that happens." He pointed to Meta's own delay of Muse for safety and security fixes, adding "we didn't call for everyone else to do this before we would." His framing — "any lab that doesn't focus on alignment will fall behind" — reframes alignment as a competitive moat rather than a public-goods problem, and sits between the Amodei-Altman slowdown camp and the Trump-Huang no-brakes camp.
