# Anthropic Builds a Bay Area Wet Lab Where Claude Directs Robotic Experiments

Anthropic is standing up its own biology laboratory in the San Francisco area to move AI-assisted drug development beyond simulation into physical experimentation, per Reuters reporting. The design intent is for Claude to guide robotic lab equipment through experiments with minimal human involvement, enabled by two tools Anthropic has already shipped: Claude Science, a research workspace, and the Model Hardware Standard for controlling lab instruments. Human oversight remains a stated safety requirement. Life sciences head Eric Kauderer-Abrams points to "undruggable" disease targets and multi-target antibody design as the largest opportunity; the lab builds on Anthropic's roughly $400 million acquisition of Coefficient Bio in April and the addition of Novartis CEO Vas Narasimhan to its board. Anthropic says it will not run its own clinical trials for now.
