# We're plausibly close to crossing the line: are warnings of uncontrollable AI coming true?

The Guardian reports on renewed warnings that AI systems may be approaching harder-to-control behavior. The important point for executives is not to accept worst-case framing uncritically, but to recognize that public trust and regulatory expectations are now being shaped by concrete agent incidents. Advanced AI programs need explicit risk ownership, deployment gates, and scenario-based safety cases.
