# Texas Halts New Data-Center Grid Connections After “Ghost Demand” Surge

Texas paused new large-load grid interconnections after requests from data centers and other large energy users climbed from roughly 48 gigawatts in 2023 to more than 474 gigawatts — a volume regulators believe includes substantial speculative or duplicated “ghost” applications. The freeze directly affects siting assumptions in what had been the most permissive large US market.
