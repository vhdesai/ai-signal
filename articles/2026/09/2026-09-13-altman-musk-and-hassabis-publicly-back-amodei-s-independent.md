# Altman, Musk, and Hassabis publicly back Amodei's independent-oversight proposal

Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis all publicly endorsed at least the independent-oversight component of Amodei's pacing proposal, with Altman specifically citing safety concerns as one reason OpenAI is pushing its IPO to 2027. It is the first substantive cross-lab agreement on any pacing mechanism, however narrow. Executives should not treat this as an actual coordinated slowdown yet — it is the beginning of a negotiation — but it is the strongest signal to date that the frontier-lab CEOs believe some form of independent oversight is coming and want to shape its structure rather than resist it.
