# New PwC and Palantir expand strategic alliance for enterprise AI

PwC US and Palantir announced an expanded alliance targeting enterprise AI scaling, M&A transformation and ERP modernization. The tie-up pairs Palantir’s platform with PwC’s deal and integration practice. Palantir shares moved roughly 7% on the day. Notably for corporate development teams, the explicit M&A-transformation framing signals AI tooling moving into post-merger integration workflows.
