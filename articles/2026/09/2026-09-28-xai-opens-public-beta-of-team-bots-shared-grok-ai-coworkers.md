# xAI Opens Public Beta of Team Bots — Shared Grok AI Coworkers

Team and Enterprise customers can now build shared Grok Bots around a team role, combining context (files, instructions, skills), plugins for Salesforce, Notion and GitHub, credentials for API access, and persistent memories. Each bot receives its own Slack handle; individual conversations stay private while skills are shared across the team. Pre-built templates cover sales, product management, marketing and data analytics. No separate pricing or GA date was disclosed.
