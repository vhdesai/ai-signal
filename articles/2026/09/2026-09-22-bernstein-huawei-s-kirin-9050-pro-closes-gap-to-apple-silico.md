# Bernstein: Huawei's Kirin 9050 Pro closes gap to Apple silicon to ~3 years and beats A17 Pro on Geekbench multi-core

Equity-research firm Bernstein estimates Huawei's Kirin 9050 Pro (the Tau Scaling Law–based chip that shipped in the Mate XT 2) is roughly a 7nm-equivalent process yet beats Apple's 3nm A17 Pro on Geekbench 6 multi-core — narrowing Huawei's lag to Apple to about three years, from roughly four before. For anyone watching the China semiconductor autonomy thesis, this is a concrete third-party validation that Tau Scaling Law is delivering measurable performance gains, not just marketing. PRODUCT
