# AI hallucination nearly triggers U.S. military operation, TechCrunch reports

TechCrunch, citing CNN, reported that U.S. officials aborted an armed operation against a Chinese vessel after discovering that the intelligence behind it had been hallucinated by an AI chatbot. The report said a Special Operations Command analyst used an AI tool to synthesize and then format false intelligence into an official-looking summary. The incident is a sharp example of why high-consequence AI workflows need provenance, uncertainty display, source validation, and mandatory human challenge points.
