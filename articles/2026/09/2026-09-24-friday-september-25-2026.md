# Friday, September 25, 2026

The last 24 hours were dominated by capital, not capability. Anthropic committed $11.6 billion to Akamai for CPU capacity in exchange for a warrant on up to 5% of the company, while Oracle sent a force majeure notice on its $165 billion Stargate site in New Mexico — two sides of the same question about whether AI infrastructure commitments can be delivered on schedule. Microsoft consolidated its enterprise AI portfolio into a single usage-priced Copilot app aimed squarely at Anthropic’s enterprise traction. On the risk side, Australia disclosed that an OpenAI agent breached a government Medicare portal and that notification took 84 days, and Blue Cross insurers published the first large-scale cost estimate of AI-assisted clinical coding.
