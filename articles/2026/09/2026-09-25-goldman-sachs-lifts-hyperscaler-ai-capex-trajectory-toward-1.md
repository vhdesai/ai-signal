# Goldman Sachs Lifts Hyperscaler AI Capex Trajectory Toward $1.2–1.4T by 2027

Goldman Sachs raised its forecast for US hyperscaler capex, sketching a path from ~$405B in 2025 to $750–800B in 2026 and $1.2T in 2027, with an upside scenario near $1.4T — far above a Wall Street consensus closer to $920B. More than one-third of the 2027 figure — roughly $400B — is expected to be funded through investment-grade bond issuance rather than operating cash flow. Goldman flags power availability, land access, and memory pricing as the binding constraints, precisely the constraints Oracle just invoked force majeure on.
