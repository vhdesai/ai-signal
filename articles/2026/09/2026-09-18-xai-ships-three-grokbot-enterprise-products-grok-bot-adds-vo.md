# xAI ships three GrokBot enterprise products; Grok Bot adds voice mode

xAI unveiled three GrokBot enterprise products at Galaxy Day 3, including Grok Build (with memory across coding sessions) and Grok Bot voice mode for real-time conversations on desktop and mobile. The launches follow Microsoft's Grok-in-Copilot rollout and Google Workspace integration announcements earlier this month. Together, they mark xAI's most aggressive enterprise push to date, tightly coupled with the SpaceX/Cursor integration. - https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/xai-ships-three-grokbot-enterprise-products-galaxy-day-3
