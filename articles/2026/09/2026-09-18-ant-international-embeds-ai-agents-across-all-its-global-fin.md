# Ant International embeds AI agents across all its global financial-services platforms in its biggest-ever product upgrade

Ant International launched what it calls its largest product upgrade ever, embedding AI agents across Alipay+, Antom, WorldFirst, and its treasury platform for cross-border payments, FX, and treasury operations. Combined with last week's open-sourcing of the Agentic Mobile Protocol (AMP), this is a rapid consolidation of Ant's position as the most vertically-integrated agentic-payments stack.
