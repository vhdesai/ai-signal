# Zoom AI Companion outage in US region resolved after overnight incident

Technobezz reported, based on Zoom's own status updates, that Zoom investigated and resolved a minor US-region degradation affecting Zoom AI Companion, identified and fixed overnight with continued monitoring after restoration. Even small AI-assistant outages matter as meeting summarization, action-item extraction, and workplace copilots move from optional add-ons to embedded operational infrastructure.
