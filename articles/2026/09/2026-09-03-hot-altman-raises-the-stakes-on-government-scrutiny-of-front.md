# Hot Altman raises the stakes on government scrutiny of frontier models

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman called the administration's voluntary safety review of the forthcoming Astra model a "productive process" and argued for closer government engagement as capabilities advance. The piece situates the remarks amid growing federal scrutiny of frontier AI and emerging national-security review processes. It signals an industry shift toward accepting pre-release government evaluation.
