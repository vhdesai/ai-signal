# Alibaba Cuts Qwen Voice API Prices by Up to 95% With Qwen-Audio 3.1

Alibaba shipped Qwen-Audio 3.1 on September 25 with five models: upgraded ASR, TTS, and Realtime, plus new ASR-Next for audio understanding and TTS-Next for audio creation. Reported reductions run up to 95% on ASR, ~70% on TTS, and ~85% on Realtime, with TTS covering 16 languages and 20 Chinese dialect regions and Realtime Plus carrying a 262K context window. Alibaba published percentage reductions rather than per-unit prices and gave no effective date, so "up to 95%" should be treated as a headline figure pending a published rate card. The move runs directly counter to DeepSeek’s 2.3×–4.5× API price increase, underlining divergent monetization strategies among Chinese labs even as Chinese models keep taking token share on OpenRouter and Vercel.
