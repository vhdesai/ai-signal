# The case against letting AI auto-repair enterprise data pipelines

Enterprise data pipelines fail quietly — schema drift, semantic corruption, ETL jobs that succeed while silently dropping payload — and the argument here is that AI-driven anomaly detection belongs in alerting, not auto-remediation. In mission-critical environments, remediation should stay deterministic rather than letting a model guess at a fix. The piece draws on large-scale data transformations in banking, healthcare and distribution to argue that data velocity has outpaced traditional governance. The recommended direction is autonomous data governance with resilient infrastructure rather than simple self-healing.
