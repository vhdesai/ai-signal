# California Enacts Adam's Law and Bans Addictive Feeds for Under-16s

Governor Newsom signed a package of 13 child-safety bills led by SB 1119 ("Adam's Law"), the first comprehensive US framework governing AI companion chatbots and minors. Operators must run risk assessments before releasing new or substantially modified companion chatbots, submit independent safety audits to the attorney general, use OS-level age signals, and default to usage limits, disabled notifications, and restricted persistent memory — changeable only by a parent. Companion legislation AB 1709 bars autoplay and history-based algorithmic feeds for users under 16, with penalties up to $1M per child. Adam's Law takes effect July 2027; OpenAI publicly backed it.
