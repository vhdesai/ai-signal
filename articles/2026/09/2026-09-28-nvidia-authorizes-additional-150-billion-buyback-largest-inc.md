# Nvidia Authorizes Additional $150 Billion Buyback, Largest Increase on Record

Nvidia's board approved another $150 billion under its existing repurchase program, taking the remaining authorization to $235 billion, to be executed through fiscal 2028. The company called it the largest share repurchase authorization increase in history; shares rose in Monday trading on a market cap near $5.4 trillion.
