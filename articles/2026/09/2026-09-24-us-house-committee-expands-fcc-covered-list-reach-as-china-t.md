# US House committee expands FCC "Covered List" reach as China tech scrutiny widens

The House Energy and Commerce Committee unveiled the Communications and Technology Transparency Act, which would expand the FCC's Covered List beyond "communications equipment or service" to a materially broader product scope. A separate patent case has drawn Lenovo into memory-device litigation. Together, the moves signal a widening surface for US restrictions on Chinese AI-adjacent hardware, ahead of the Trump–Xi Washington meeting.
