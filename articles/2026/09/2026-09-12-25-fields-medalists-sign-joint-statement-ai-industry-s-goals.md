# 25 Fields Medalists sign joint statement: AI industry's goals are "severely misaligned" with mathematics

Twenty-five Fields Medal winners signed a joint statement arguing that the AI industry's goal of mass-producing solutions to solved problems is "severely misaligned" with mathematics' actual purpose — building understanding, not accumulating answers. They frame this week's Navier–Stokes dispute as a symptom rather than an outlier, and warn that the same misalignment threatens other intellectual disciplines next. This is the highest-signal collective academic pushback against AI-in-science claims to date and gives regulators, universities, and enterprise research leaders unambiguous cover to demand independent verification.
