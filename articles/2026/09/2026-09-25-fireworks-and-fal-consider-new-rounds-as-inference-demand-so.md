# Fireworks and Fal consider new rounds as inference demand soars

The Information reported that inference providers Fireworks and Fal are considering new funding rounds as demand for model serving rises. The story is important because the AI infrastructure market is moving beyond training clusters toward high-volume inference, routing, and model-serving economics. For executives, this is another sign that production AI cost structures are shifting toward runtime optimization and provider competition rather than only model access.
