# OpenAI answers with GPT-6 Sol and Luna, halving API prices

OpenAI extended the GPT-6 line with Sol ($2/$10 per million tokens) and Luna ($0.10/$0.50), both 50% below their GPT-5.6 predecessors, released roughly 90 minutes after Anthropic's launch. OpenAI attributes the reduction to caching and inference efficiency rather than promotional pricing, and an OpenAI spokesperson confirmed to VentureBeat the rates are permanent. On AutomationBench, Sol scores 33.2% at $0.27 per task against Claude Opus 5's 26.9% at roughly 11x the cost, and OpenAI says Sol makes about half as many factual errors as its predecessor. The caveat for buyers: Sol regressed against GPT-5.6 Sol on the OSWorld 2.0 computer-use benchmark, so cheaper is not uniformly better. Products & Tools DEVELOPER
