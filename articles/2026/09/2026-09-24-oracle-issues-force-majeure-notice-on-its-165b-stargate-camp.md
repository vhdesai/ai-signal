# Oracle Issues Force Majeure Notice on Its $165B Stargate Campus in New Mexico

Oracle sent a force majeure notice to a Blue Owl Capital unit developing Project Jupiter, the 2.45-gigawatt Stargate campus in Doña Ana County. Oracle is not seeking to exit as anchor tenant; the notice preserves its right to defer payments if the site misses its 2028 delivery date, potentially winning a three-year rent delay. The trigger is power: an Energy Transfer pipeline has slipped nearly six months to February 2027, and a fuel-cell air permit remains pending until a November 23 state deadline. Oracle shares fell 4%+, its credit-risk gauge hit a record, and the $18B construction loan is trading below 90 cents on the dollar. It is the first visible crack in a marquee AI buildout — and it is the exact constraint (power availability) Goldman just called out as binding on the 2027 capex trajectory. INFRASTRUCTURE
