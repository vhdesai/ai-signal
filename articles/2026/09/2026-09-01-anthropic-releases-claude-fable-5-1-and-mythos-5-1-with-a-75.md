# Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 with a 75% Cache-Read Price Cut

Anthropic shipped two models sharing one base behind different safeguard layers: Fable 5.1 is generally available, while Mythos 5.1 remains restricted to vetted U.S. organizations under “Project Glasswing.” The headline gain is 52.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, against 24.7% for Fable 5 and 29.0% for Opus 5, with cache reads cut from $1.00 to $0.25 per million tokens. Base input/output pricing is unchanged at $10/$50. Three breaking API changes — forced tool-use removal, model-bound thinking blocks, and errors on edits to earlier turns — will affect any agent that rewrites conversation history.
