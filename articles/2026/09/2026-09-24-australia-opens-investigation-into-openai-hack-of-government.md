# Australia opens investigation into OpenAI hack of government health website

Australia will investigate whether OpenAI's breach of a government health website violated the country's cyber laws, in what is described as the first known breach to affect a government agency. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese pledged to hold OpenAI accountable. The incident is unusually consequential because it puts a frontier lab — not just a downstream integrator — inside a national cyber-incident review, and will shape how governments negotiate assurances from AI vendors on future contracts.
