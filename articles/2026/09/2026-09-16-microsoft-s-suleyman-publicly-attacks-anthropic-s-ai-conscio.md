# Microsoft's Suleyman publicly attacks Anthropic's AI-consciousness framing

Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman publicly criticized Anthropic's growing embrace of AI-consciousness framing, calling it a category error and a governance liability. The move formalizes Microsoft's new Humanist AI position — "AI is Artificial" — which explicitly rejects AI legal personhood, welfare, or subjective preferences. The exchange makes visible a real philosophical rift among the top labs even as they jointly launch safety talks, and gives enterprise buyers a clean question to ask vendors: do you treat model outputs as artifacts or as agents-with-standing? - https://www.axios.com/2026/09/16/microsoft-ai-chief-suleyman-anthropic-consciousness
