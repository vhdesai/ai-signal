# Gartner: fewer than 1% of 2025 job losses were AI-driven; one-third of AI-replaced workers will be rehired by 2029

CIO Dive's Sept. 16 issue surfaces new Gartner research finding fewer than 1% of 2025 job losses were driven directly by AI productivity gains, and that roughly one-third of workers who are AI-replaced will be rehired by 2029. The data re-anchors the "AI is coming for jobs" discourse and gives CHROs and CIOs a concrete counter-narrative for workforce-planning conversations. Combined with FedEx Freight's CTO absorbing the commercial role and Salesforce's AIforce launch in the same issue, it reads as a shift from "AI replaces the worker" to "AI reshapes the role." - https://www.ciodive.com/news/salesforce-aiforce-agentic-governance-dreamforce-2026/
