# Oracle Books $664B AI Backlog — and Negative Free Cash Flow

Oracle reported Q1 FY27 revenue up 30% to $19.3B, cloud infrastructure up 121% to $7.4B, and remaining performance obligations of $664B after booking more than $30B of new AI cloud contracts. Capital expenditure of $28.5B — more than the quarter's total revenue — drove free cash flow to negative $5.4B. Management said roughly half the backlog should convert within 36 months and that newer contracts rely on customer prepayments and customer-supplied hardware. Shares fell 5.4% intraday before recovering 5–6% premarket the following morning.
