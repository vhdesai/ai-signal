# Altman Tells Staff OpenAI Is Open to Slowing Frontier Development

Sam Altman told employees at a company-wide meeting that OpenAI is considering pacing its frontier development, potentially in coordination with other labs, while acknowledging some may not participate — first reported by Bloomberg. The shift follows OpenAI's disclosure that it delayed the Astra model to test safeguards against cyber misuse and unauthorized model actions, and its determination that Astra was the first model to meet its "Critical" cybersecurity threshold. An Anthropic spokesperson said the company is interested in industry coordination on release pacing.
