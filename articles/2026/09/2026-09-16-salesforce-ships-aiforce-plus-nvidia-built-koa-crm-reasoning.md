# Salesforce ships AIforce plus Nvidia-built "Koa" CRM reasoning model at Dreamforce

At Dreamforce, Salesforce unveiled AIforce — a live interface layer that governs how third-party agents (including Anthropic's Claude and Slack) access Salesforce data, enforcing per-field authorization and precise data-definition semantics. Alongside AIforce, Salesforce debuted Koa, a reasoning model built on Nvidia's Nemotron open-weights platform and post-trained on a synthetic dataset derived from nearly 30 years of Salesforce deployment experience (deliberately avoiding direct customer data). Jensen Huang, Dario Amodei, and Sam Altman appeared onstage with Marc Benioff. The strategic contrast: Salesforce is betting on one deeply integrated model plus governance, while Microsoft four days earlier added xAI models to Copilot — betting on a model marketplace. - https://www.ciodive.com/news/salesforce-aiforce-agentic-governance-dreamforce-2026/ - https://www.crn.com/news/ai/2026/salesforce-dreamforce-2026-ceo-benioff-touts-new-aiforce-interface-layer
