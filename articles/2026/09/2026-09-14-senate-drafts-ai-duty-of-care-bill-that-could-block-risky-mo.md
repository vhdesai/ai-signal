# Senate drafts AI "duty of care" bill that could block risky models and preempt state law

Senate negotiators led by Thune, Cruz, and Klobuchar are drafting legislation with two pillars: a "duty of care" letting the Commerce Secretary demand evidence of harm prevention and send auditors to test products directly, and a mechanism letting the government block release of models judged unsafe, challengeable in federal court. That court mechanism would preempt state AI laws covering certain risks such as nuclear or biological weapons uplift. Roughly three voting weeks remain before the November 3 midterms — the bill's live window is narrow but real.
