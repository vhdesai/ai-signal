# Google tests "Call for Me," letting Gemini phone businesses on your behalf

Google is piloting an outbound-phone agent that has Gemini dial local businesses to check hours, prices, or appointment availability, then report back in the assistant. The feature is rolling out first to US Pixel 11 owners on a paid Gemini subscription. It extends Google's Duplex lineage into the modern LLM-agent stack and puts pressure on both Apple's Siri roadmap and independent voice-agent startups on the same turf. PRODUCT
