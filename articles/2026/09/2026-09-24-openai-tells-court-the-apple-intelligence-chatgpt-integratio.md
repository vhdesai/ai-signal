# OpenAI Tells Court the Apple Intelligence ChatGPT Integration “Dramatically Underperformed”

In court filings, OpenAI said the 2024 deal placing ChatGPT inside Apple Intelligence produced far less usage than expected, with internal forecasts cut shortly after launch. OpenAI had anticipated a halo effect converting Apple’s installed base into paid subscribers; Apple’s optional, account-free, privacy-first design limited conversion. Apple has since deepened its work with Google’s Gemini for Siri. The lesson is distribution economics: default placement inside a privacy-preserving OS surface does not translate into subscription conversion.
