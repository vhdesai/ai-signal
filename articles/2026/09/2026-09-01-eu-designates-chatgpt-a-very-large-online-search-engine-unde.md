# EU designates ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act

The European Commission placed ChatGPT under direct supervision as a Very Large Online Search Engine, the DSA's strictest tier, alongside VLOP designations for Reddit and Roblox. The designation triggers systemic-risk assessment, independent audit, data-access and transparency obligations. It is the first time a general-purpose assistant has been regulated as search infrastructure rather than as a chatbot.
