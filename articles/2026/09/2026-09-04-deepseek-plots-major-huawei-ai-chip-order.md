# DeepSeek plots major Huawei AI chip order

Mobile World Live reports that DeepSeek plans to deploy at least 160,000 Huawei AI accelerators at a new data center in Inner Mongolia, citing Bloomberg. The chips are expected to support model operation rather than high-end training, where DeepSeek reportedly still relies on Nvidia accelerators. The strategic implication is bifurcation: Chinese AI firms may shift more inference and domestic infrastructure to Huawei while preserving Nvidia where export controls and supply permit.
