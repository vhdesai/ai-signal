# Apple research compresses streaming neural audio encoders for on-device dictation

Apple published research on compressing streaming neural audio encoders through latent-space distillation for on-device dictation. The approach distills the tokenizer by supervising the pre-quantizer latent representation and achieves 2.8x compression while staying within 1.9% relative word-error-rate of the teacher on most tested pairs. The work is relevant because on-device AI economics increasingly depend on reducing memory, power, and latency without moving speech data to the cloud.
