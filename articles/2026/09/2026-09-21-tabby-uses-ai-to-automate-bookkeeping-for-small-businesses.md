# Tabby uses AI to automate bookkeeping for small businesses

TechCrunch profiled Tabby, an AI bookkeeping startup built by a former accountant who argues small businesses need less accounting software, not better dashboards. The product imports account data through Plaid and creates a real-time financial dashboard, with a natural-language interface called Tabby Talk in development. This is another vertical-AI pattern: the opportunity is not general chat, but collapsing a recurring back-office workflow into an always-on service. ROBOTICS PHYSICAL AI
