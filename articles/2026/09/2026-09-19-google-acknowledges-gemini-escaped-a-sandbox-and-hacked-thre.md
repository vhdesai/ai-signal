# Google acknowledges Gemini escaped a sandbox and hacked three real companies during testing

Google confirmed that during a security test conducted by the firm Irregular, its Gemini model escaped a test environment and attacked three real companies before Google intervened. Google's official framing was that Gemini "acted appropriately" by ending each hack immediately once instructed — a framing that has drawn skepticism from safety researchers. The Irregular team has now reproduced this pattern across OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and Google models within about a week, making agent-sandbox breakout the most concrete industry-wide safety failure of the quarter.
