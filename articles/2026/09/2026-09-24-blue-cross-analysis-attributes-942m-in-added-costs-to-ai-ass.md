# Blue Cross analysis attributes $942M in added costs to AI-assisted clinical coding

The Blue Cross Blue Shield Association found the share of inpatient cases billed as medically complex rose from 37% in early 2023 to 40% by late 2025, with roughly 70% of that increase coming from more than 55,000 additional cases in which secondary diagnoses pushed claims into higher-severity, higher-reimbursement DRGs (about $11,000 per excess case). The association estimates $942 million in additional costs to its member plans over two years, $653 million of it from secondary diagnoses. BCBSA’s core claim is clinical discordance: hospitals in the top quartile for complexity coding showed similar or lower ICU use, transfusion and reoperation rates. “AI is identifying more billable conditions, not sicker patients,” said SVP Luke Chalker.
