# New Allen Institute, University of Washington and Fred Hutch launch $95M "AI BioDesign" initiative

The Allen Institute, the University of Washington and Fred Hutch Cancer Center launched AI BioDesign, a $94.6M five-year effort to generate data and train models that design proteins and genes not found in nature. It is led by UW Nobel laureate David Baker and genome scientist Jay Shendure, funded by the Paul Allen estate's Fund for Science and Technology. The bet is on narrow, purpose-built models trained on massively parallel in-tube experiments — one run tested six million sequences — with results shared as open science.
