# Bank of England Governor Warns Frontier AI Models Raise Systemic Cyber Risk

Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey warned that increasingly powerful frontier AI models are raising the threat level for a disruptive cybersecurity attack on the financial system, citing the Hugging Face incident as an illustrative case. The intervention moves AI risk from a technology-policy topic to a prudential supervision one, which is where it starts to affect capital planning and third-party risk requirements at regulated institutions. CIO Dive Daily Dive — Sept. 1
