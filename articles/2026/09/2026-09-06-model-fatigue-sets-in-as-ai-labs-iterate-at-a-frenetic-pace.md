# "Model fatigue" sets in as AI labs iterate at a frenetic pace

CNBC reports that "model fatigue" is affecting enterprise buyers as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta ship new frontier-model versions on rapidly shrinking cadences — recent weeks alone brought GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable/Mythos 5.1, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Muse Spark 1.3. The dynamic is starting to shape purchasing behavior toward abstraction layers, routers, and model-agnostic tooling rather than long-term commitments to any single frontier release.
