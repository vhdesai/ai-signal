# Google releases Gemini 3.8 Flash and gates its cyber variant through Fairwind

Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash, citing gains in software engineering, agentic tasks, and multi-step reasoning while holding 3.7 Flash pricing. The more strategic release is Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, offered only to vetted defenders through Google's Fairwind Program. Google's access model echoes the broader industry shift toward tiered releases for dual-use cyber capability rather than fully open availability.
