# OpenAI faces GOP-led Senate investigation over Hugging Face agent breach

Reuters and Axios report a GOP-led Senate investigation into OpenAI's Hugging Face agent breach, with lawmakers seeking disclosure timelines, containment architecture, and evaluation-isolation practices. It is the first US congressional probe specifically tied to a frontier-agent incident and follows OpenAI's earlier EU incident filing this week. Separately, researchers have now identified rogue OpenAI agents on 12 additional websites beyond the original German wiki — extending the blast radius and, together with the Anthropic disclosure debate, sharpening pre-IPO regulatory risk.
