# Tsinghua's PACMAN group publishes Puro-2B, a full pretraining recipe for consumer GPUs

The Puro-2B report describes training 2-billion-parameter models from scratch on up to 1.4T tokens using FP8 precision on consumer RTX 5090 hardware, with the team publishing training code, checkpoints, and data. Reported runs in the roughly $5,000-7,000 range are claimed to beat or approach Qwen2-class 1.5B baselines. The significance is the cost floor for credible from-scratch pretraining, which lowers the barrier for academic labs and for enterprises evaluating small domain-specific models. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27370
