# Chinese Model Providers Take Majority Token Share on Major Developer Routers

CNBC reports Chinese-origin models reached 57–67% of tokens routed through OpenRouter in the week of September 14, up from 6–13% in February, and 55% of Vercel token share in August versus 11% in January. Price is the stated driver: open-weight Chinese models run substantially cheaper than US frontier equivalents for coding and agentic workloads. US frontier labs still capture more spend than token volume, but two US House committees continue to investigate the adoption trend. For enterprise architects, multi-model procurement is no longer a research question — it is a governance question about who signs off on which Chinese-origin models can be routed through developer tooling.
