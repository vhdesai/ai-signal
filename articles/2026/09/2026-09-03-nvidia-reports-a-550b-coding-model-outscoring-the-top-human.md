# Nvidia Reports a 550B Coding Model Outscoring the Top Human at IOI 2026

Nvidia researchers report that Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC scored 535.4 of 600 on the IOI 2026 problem set, ahead of the top human contestant's 498.27. The result comes from Nvidia's own preprint and has not been independently replicated. Treat it as a vendor-reported capability claim pending third-party evaluation, but the direction of travel on competitive-programming benchmarks is consistent with other frontier results this quarter.
