# CoreWeave Ships Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 at Production Scale — Cognition Is the Launch Customer

CoreWeave announced production-scale deployment of Nvidia’s Vera Rubin NVL72 systems, with agent-lab Cognition as launch customer. It is the first commercial general-availability of Rubin outside NVIDIA’s own reference designs, and gives Cognition training capacity commensurate with frontier labs — a meaningful shift in agent-lab compute access, days after Cognition’s Devin crossed $1B in ARR. Watch whether Anthropic and OpenAI follow onto CoreWeave-Rubin capacity this quarter; if they do, it validates CoreWeave’s independent-cloud thesis against the Nscale-style concentration story.
