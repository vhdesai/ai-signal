# Cerebras breaks ground on a 165MW data center in Mikkeli, Finland

Cerebras began construction on a 165MW campus in Mikkeli, Finland with partner Compute Nordic, starting at 50MW and scaling through 80MW to 165MW. Investment is estimated at €1bn–€1.7bn, using closed-loop cooling with waste-heat recovery for the surrounding community. The site is part of Cerebras's plan to reach 200MW of European capacity by the end of 2027.
