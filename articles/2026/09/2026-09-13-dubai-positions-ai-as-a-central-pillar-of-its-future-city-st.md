# Dubai positions AI as a central pillar of its future-city strategy

Daily Times reported that Dubai is positioning artificial intelligence as a central element of its future-city agenda, spanning smart services and a broader AI economic ecosystem. The story is directionally important because it shows governments treating AI as municipal infrastructure and an economic-development platform. The execution question is whether public-sector AI programs can move beyond branding into measurable improvements in service delivery, productivity, and trust.
