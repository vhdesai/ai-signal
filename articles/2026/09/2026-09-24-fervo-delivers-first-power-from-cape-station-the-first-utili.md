# Fervo delivers first power from Cape Station, the first utility-scale enhanced geothermal plant on the grid

Fervo Energy's Cape Station project in Beaver County, Utah delivered its first electricity to the regional grid — a first for a utility-scale enhanced geothermal system, which uses oilfield fracturing techniques to reach deep high-temperature rock. Fervo has already signed a 396 MW power purchase agreement with Google for a prospective Utah data center, with an option for an additional 600 MW through 2030. The milestone matters because AI capacity planning is now constrained by firm, 24/7 carbon-free power and interconnection queues rather than by chips alone.
