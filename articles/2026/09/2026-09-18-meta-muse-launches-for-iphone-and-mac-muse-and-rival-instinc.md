# Meta Muse launches for iPhone and Mac; Muse and rival Instinct both add calling

Meta launched its Muse personal AI agent on iPhone and Mac, and both Muse and rival Instinct simultaneously added the ability to place phone calls to businesses — restaurant booking, waitlist management, customer service. WSJ separately flags concerns that Muse has a "trust problem" given ongoing lawsuits over Meta AI's kid-facing behavior. Regardless, the consumer AI-agent category is now clearly Muse vs. Instinct vs. Wajo vs. Town vs. Ollie — happening the same week Anthropic prepares Claude Money. The strategic issue is user trust and liability: once assistants initiate external communications, platforms need stronger consent, identity, and escalation models. - https://www.macobserver.com/news/meta-launches-muse-personal-ai-agent-iphone-mac
