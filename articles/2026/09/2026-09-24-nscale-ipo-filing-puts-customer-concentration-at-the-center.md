# Nscale IPO filing puts customer concentration at the center of the neocloud model

Follow-on reporting on Nscale's September 18 S-1 identified ByteDance as the unnamed customer behind 73% of the company's $33M 2025 revenue — still 52% of first-half 2026 revenue — via B200 capacity at its Glomfjord, Norway site. Nscale reported $140.6M of revenue for H1 2026 against a $1.02B net loss, with more than $100B in contracted value anchored by Microsoft and Anthropic agreements. The filing is the clearest public test yet of whether long-duration compute contracts support a roughly $35B listing. For buyers of AI capacity, the read-through is counterparty risk: contracted value and delivered capacity are not the same number.
