# Meta's Muse Assistant Lands on Mac with Computer-Use Actions

Meta released a macOS app for Muse, its personal AI agent, following the US debut on iOS, Android and web earlier in September. On Mac, Muse can interact with files, messages, calendar, notes and mail inside their native apps and take actions on the user's behalf. Access is opt-in and the agent requests approval before sensitive actions — a consumer-side arrival of the same computer-use permission model enterprises are now being asked to govern.
