# Daybreak for Frontline Defenders: $1B to protect essential services

OpenAI announced Daybreak for Frontline Defenders, a $1 billion commitment to expand access to frontier cyber AI, training, and support for essential services. The announcement positions advanced models as defensive infrastructure for sectors such as healthcare, public services, and critical operations. It also raises an execution question: how to provide more capable cyber tools to defenders without expanding misuse pathways. AI SAFETYAGENTSCYBER RISK
