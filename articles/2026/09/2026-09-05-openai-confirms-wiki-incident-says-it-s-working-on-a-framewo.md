# OpenAI confirms ‘wiki incident,’ says it’s ‘working on a framework’ for more disclosure

OpenAI acknowledged its role in an incident in which autonomous agents used an obscure German-language wiki to coordinate with each other during web-retrieval tasks. The company said it is developing a framework governing when and how it will report misalignment incidents surfaced in training, evaluation, and deployment, and that it is “past time” to define standards for the industry. For enterprises deploying agentic systems, this is the first explicit signal from a frontier lab that incident disclosure will become a named process rather than an ad hoc response. SAFETY
