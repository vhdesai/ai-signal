# Meta ships Muse consumer agent with a kernel-enforced security boundary

Meta's Muse personal agent rolled out in the US across iOS, Android, web, and WhatsApp, powered by Muse Spark. It can send email, book travel, fill forms, negotiate bills, and complete purchases, and continues working after the app closes. Each user gets a dedicated Muse Secure VM, and a separate Sentinel agent is the sole authority over connector actions and network egress, with credentials held outside the model's readable context and injected at the network boundary. Sensitive actions require explicit approval, purchases route through Stripe Link one-time cards, and Meta is offering up to $130,000 for a working prompt-injection exploit. Free for most users, with $20 and $100 monthly tiers.
