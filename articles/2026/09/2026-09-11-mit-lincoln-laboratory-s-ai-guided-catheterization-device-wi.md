# MIT Lincoln Laboratory's AI-Guided Catheterization Device Wins Technology Transfer Award

AI-GUIDE, a handheld catheterization device developed by MIT Lincoln Laboratory with Massachusetts General Hospital, received the 2026 Excellence in Technology Transfer Award. The system guides non-specialist operators through vascular access, targeting improved outcomes for injured service members and civilian trauma patients. It is a useful counterweight to the week's frontier-risk coverage: the clearest near-term AI value remains narrow, embedded and physically deployed.
