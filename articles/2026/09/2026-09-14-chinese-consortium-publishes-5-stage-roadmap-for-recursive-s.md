# Chinese Consortium Publishes 5-Stage Roadmap for Recursive Self-Improvement ("Last AI Built by Humans")

Researchers from ByteDance, Tsinghua University, and the Shanghai AI Laboratory published a joint paper titled "The Last AI Built by Humans," laying out a five-stage roadmap for recursive self-improvement — from human-assisted training pipeline optimization through fully autonomous AI-designs-AI systems. It is the first major Chinese public research statement explicitly targeting RSI as an engineering goal.
