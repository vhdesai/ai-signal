# OpenAI pairs Astra with Daybreak, a $1B cyber-defense program for essential services

OpenAI announced Daybreak for Frontline Defenders, a $1B commitment to expand frontier cyber AI, training, and support for organizations protecting essential services. The program sits alongside Astra's staged release to cybersecurity customers and reflects a broader lab strategy: position high-capability cyber models as defensive infrastructure, not just general-purpose tools. The execution challenge is ensuring that more capable defensive tooling does not widen misuse pathways.
