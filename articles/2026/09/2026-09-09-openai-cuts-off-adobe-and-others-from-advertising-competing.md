# OpenAI cuts off Adobe and others from advertising competing AI products in ChatGPT

OpenAI has told business partners it will no longer accept advertising in ChatGPT for image- and audio-generating products that compete with its own features — a policy change that has not been previously reported or reflected in its published ads terms, and one that blindsided advertisers such as Adobe. OpenAI's ad revenue is at roughly $1B annualized, below aggressive 2024 investor projections, so blocking competing categories could narrow the very growth pool the company promised investors as a key monetization lever off its huge free audience. For enterprises negotiating ChatGPT distribution or referral placements, OpenAI's ad rules now materially constrain competitive presence on its own surface.
