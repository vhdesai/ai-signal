# OpenAI Cuts Off Adobe and Others From Advertising Competing AI Products in ChatGPT

OpenAI has told partners it will no longer accept advertising in ChatGPT for image- and audio-generating products that compete with its own features — a policy change not reflected in its published ads rules that blindsided incumbent buyers like Adobe. If the exclusion extends to other competing categories, it materially narrows OpenAI's ad-revenue path just as annualized advertising revenue reached ~$1B, below prior investor projections. Expect competing app makers to reprice their assumed distribution through ChatGPT.
