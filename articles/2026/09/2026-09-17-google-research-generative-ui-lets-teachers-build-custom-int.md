# Google Research: generative UI lets teachers build custom interactive simulations

Google Research published a research experiment that lets educators generate custom, interactive, guided educational simulations matched to their stated objectives and curriculum rather than selecting from pre-authored content. It is the follow-on application of Google's earlier generative-UI line of work — the only first-party frontier-lab research blog post confirmed inside the 24-hour window. - https://research.google/blog/the-future-of-practice-enabling-teachers-to-create-learning-interactives-with-generative-ui/
