# Ricursive Intelligence: ex-AlphaChip leads raise $335M to have AI design its own hardware

Ricursive Intelligence — founded by former Google AlphaChip co-leads Anna Goldie and Azalia Mirhoseini, both early Anthropic employees — is building AI that designs chips and transfers learning across designs, aiming to compress 2–3 year silicon cycles to weeks. The company raised $335M at a $4B valuation within four months of its late-2025 launch, including a $300M Series A with Nvidia among the investors. The founders will discuss the approach at TechCrunch Disrupt.
