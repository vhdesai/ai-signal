# U.S. government sides with OpenAI in New York Times training-data lawsuit

The Trump administration filed a brief supporting OpenAI in The New York Times' copyright case, arguing that barring AI companies from training on publisher content would harm U.S. AI competitiveness. The filing is a meaningful policy signal in one of the industry's most consequential training-data disputes.
