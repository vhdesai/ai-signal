# Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash to general availability, plus a Flash Cyber variant

Google made gemini-3.8-flash generally available on September 2, its third Flash-tier release in roughly six weeks, alongside a Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber variant that runs the same underlying model with different safety mitigations. Introductory API pricing was set at $0.75 / $3.75 per million tokens through December 31. The cadence signals that Google is competing primarily on cost-per-agentic-task in the workhorse tier rather than on frontier capability. ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/changelog
