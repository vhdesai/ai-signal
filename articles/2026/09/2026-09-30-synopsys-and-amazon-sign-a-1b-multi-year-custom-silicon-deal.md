# Synopsys and Amazon Sign a $1B+ Multi-Year Custom Silicon Deal

Synopsys and Amazon inked a multi-year, $1B+ agreement centered on custom AI silicon design tooling — reinforcing AWS’s Trainium and Inferentia roadmap and its separately reported stake in Gold Circuit. Wedbush said Amazon’s hedging looks aggressive against an emerging AI-parts squeeze, and AWS’s recent engineering CPU-conservation memo takes on new meaning in that context. It is a concrete data point that hyperscalers are locking multi-year EDA capacity for custom silicon, not just GPU capacity — a supply-chain move enterprise infrastructure teams should track for downstream availability.
