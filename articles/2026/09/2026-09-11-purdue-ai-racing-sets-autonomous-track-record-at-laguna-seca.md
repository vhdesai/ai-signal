# Purdue AI Racing sets autonomous track record at Laguna Seca, finishes runner-up

Purdue's student-faculty team ran a 1:27.731 lap, becoming the first autonomous car to beat 1:28 at Laguna Seca, and was the only American team qualified for the Indy Autonomous Challenge's first road-course passing competition. Faculty director Dan Williams cites new perception and strategic-planning requirements for competitive passing. Multi-agent autonomy at high speed with tight spacing and no incidents is a credible stress test of perception-plus-planning stacks, and these programs remain a direct recruiting pipeline for autonomy talent.
