# Meta quietly rebuilds AI management layers after its flattening push

Fortune reports Meta is asking individual contributors inside its Applied AI division whether they want to return to manager roles, partially reversing the flatter-org structure Zuckerberg made central to his efficiency agenda. Roughly 7,000 employees were reassigned into Applied AI during 2026. The move suggests coordination overhead in large AI organizations is reasserting itself after a year of delayering.
