# Hot Google DeepMind launches Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber

Three weeks after its last release, Google launched two models built on a single core foundation: a general-purpose Gemini 3.8 Flash aimed at agentic workloads, and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, tuned for finding and patching vulnerabilities. Google reports 3.8 Flash outperformed Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on 9 of 16 benchmarks, while Flash Cyber scored 86.2% on CyberGym. The variants are differentiated by safety mitigations rather than model size, with 3.8 Flash generally available at introductory pricing of $0.75/$3.75 per million tokens.
