# Oracle issues force majeure notice on Project Jupiter, OpenAI's New Mexico campus

Oracle issued a force majeure notice tied to Project Jupiter, the New Mexico campus being built to support OpenAI, after delays securing power; a source told Reuters the project faces a one-year delay. Blue Owl, which has roughly $3B of equity in the project alongside STACK Infrastructure, said its financial commitments are unchanged. Oracle and Blue Owl shares closed September 24 down 3.5% and 3.6% respectively. This is the second consecutive day of escalating disclosures on the site.
