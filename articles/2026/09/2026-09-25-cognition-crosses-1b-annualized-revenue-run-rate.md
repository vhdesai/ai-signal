# Cognition crosses $1B annualized revenue run rate

Cognition said its Devin coding agent business passed a $1B annualized revenue run rate, roughly double the $492M reported in May and up from about $900M earlier in September. The company named GE Aerospace, Rivian, Rohlik and Exa among production customers; prior disclosures added Nvidia, Citi, Mercedes-Benz, Dell and US military branches. The milestone follows a $2B round announced September 8 at a $48B valuation. The figure is company-reported and unaudited, and run rate is a projection rather than booked annual revenue.
