# Apple's $250M Siri settlement enters the claims phase

Eligible iPhone owners can now file for payouts of up to $95 under Apple's $250 million settlement over delayed Siri Intelligence features. The suit stemmed from Apple marketing assistant capabilities that shipped well past their promised timeline. Separate Sept 24 reporting cited OpenAI characterizing ChatGPT's integration inside Siri as persistently underperforming.
