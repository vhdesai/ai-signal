# Google says flagship Gemini 4 is nearing release

Koray Kavukcuoglu, in his first media appearance as the new head of Google DeepMind, said Gemini 4 has entered early post-training and that he hopes to ship it "much earlier" than year-end. The comments, made at The Information's AI Agenda Live Summit, are Google's clearest timeline yet after falling behind Anthropic and OpenAI at the frontier. A Q4 Gemini 4 launch would land directly into the pricing war opened this week by Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Sol/Luna.
