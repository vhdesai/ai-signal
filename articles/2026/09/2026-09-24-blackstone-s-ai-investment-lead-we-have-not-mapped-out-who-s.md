# Blackstone's AI Investment Lead: "We Have Not Mapped Out Who's Going to Buy All the Debt"

Jas Khaira, head of Blackstone's AI-focused investment team, said at The Information's AI Agenda event that Blackstone has mapped the AI compute demand curve but is still working out which categories of debt investors will underwrite the buildout: "We have some hypotheses, but the honest answer is we don't know where all of it will come from." Khaira sketched three financing buckets — investment-grade public debt, private credit, and debt issued by OpenAI and Anthropic after they go public — with the third contingent on their ability to hit and maintain investment-grade status. Blackstone is already inside the trade via a Google TPU neocloud venture, and Khaira said it may buy AI-lab debt at the right price. This is the clearest admission from a major private-markets participant that the demand side of AI capex is real but the funding architecture is still being invented.
