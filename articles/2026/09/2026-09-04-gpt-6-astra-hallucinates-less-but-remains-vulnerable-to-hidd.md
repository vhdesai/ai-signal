# GPT-6 Astra hallucinates less but remains vulnerable to hidden prompt injection

OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra hallucinates less than its predecessor and blocks 99.99% of direct prompt injections. But when malicious instructions are hidden inside documents the model reads, it is still compromised in roughly 8.5% of scenarios, versus 4.8% for Claude Opus 5. The analysis argues indirect-injection success rates remain unacceptably high for autonomous agents operating on real enterprise data.
