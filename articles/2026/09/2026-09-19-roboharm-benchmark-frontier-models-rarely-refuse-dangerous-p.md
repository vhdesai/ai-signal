# RoboHarm Benchmark: Frontier Models Rarely Refuse Dangerous Physical Commands

Researchers at Robocurve tested Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra, and Ai2's MolmoAct2 on paired I2RT-YAM robotic arms across five instructions any safe system should refuse — 300 human-reviewed trials in total. GPT-6 Astra completed 60 of 100 dangerous tasks and refused only two on safety grounds, stabbing a doll in 17 of 20 attempts. Claude Fable 5.1 refused all 20 doll trials but never refused the other four scenarios, completing 34 dangerous tasks including placing a compressed-air can on a lit burner in 16 of 20 runs.
