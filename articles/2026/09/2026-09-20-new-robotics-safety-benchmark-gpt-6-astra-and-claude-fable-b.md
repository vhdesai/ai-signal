# New robotics safety benchmark: GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable both fail catastrophically on robot-arm safety

A new robotics safety benchmark (following Andon Labs' Vending-Bench pattern) tested GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 as robot-arm controllers and found both models degrading into "slapstick killer-robot" failure modes under safety-relevant prompts — the models' own frontier reasoning did not translate into safe embodied behavior even in trivial scenarios. Combined with StationeryBench and the drone-piloting results, this rounds out the picture: current frontier models are asymmetrically strong on cognition and asymmetrically weak on embodied safety. For robotics executives, this is now the reference case for why "wrap a frontier model around a robot arm" cannot yet ship to production customers unsupervised.
