# Blue Cross attributes $942M in added hospital costs to AI-assisted coding

The Blue Cross Blue Shield Association, representing 31 insurers covering more than 100 million members, reported that the share of inpatient stays billed as medically complex rose from 37% in early 2023 to 40% by end-2025, adding an estimated $942 million in costs — about $653 million of it from secondary diagnoses that pushed more than 55,000 claims into higher-reimbursement groups, at roughly $11,000 per case. The association's evidence for upcoding is a treatment gap: among major bowel procedures, top-quartile hospitals for anemia diagnosis transfused less often (16.9% vs 19.3%) than peers. The analysis is a trade-association white paper, not peer-reviewed, and hospital groups dispute it, citing genuinely rising patient acuity.
