# Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics ER 1.6 with upgraded spatial reasoning and live instrument-reading for…

Nvidia released Lyra 2.0 (single image → explorable 3D worlds) and open-sourced GR00T-WholeBodyControl for humanoid robotics. Nomagic hired a former DeepMind chief scientist to lead robotics foundation model development.
