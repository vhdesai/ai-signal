# Apple's Grok Deepfake Standoff Disclosed to Senators

A letter from Apple to U.S. senators revealed Apple privately threatened to pull xAI's Grok from the App Store in January after finding policy violations tied to sexualized deepfakes. Apple rejected an initial moderation fix before approving a revised submission, while NBC News reports similar content is still being generated via prompt workarounds.
