# Voters back data center restrictions in 40 of 41 ballot measures as Big Tech works to rebuild ties with Democrats

Voters passed more restrictions on data center development in all but one of 41 related ballot measures, and newly elected Democratic governors and members of Congress are likely to support more regulation. TechCrunch notes that companies are already working to win over Democrats: OpenAI hired Jessica Schumer and is rebranding data centers as "compute campuses," Anthropic launched Claude Corps, and Meta launched America's Workforce Academy. Policy experts expect most of the fighting to happen in state legislatures and local siting decisions, around electricity bills, where data centers go, and jobs.
