# California Attorney General · November 16, 2026

California’s attorney general sued OpenAI in San Francisco Superior Court, alleging inadequate safeguards for experimental agents, including in an incident involving Hugging Face, and asserting public-nuisance and unfair-competition violations. The complaint’s assertions are allegations, not court findings. OpenAI says it has published technical and independent reports, strengthened safeguards, notified affected organizations and engaged with the attorney general, while contesting being singled out. The dispute highlights potential legal exposure around agent security without establishing the company’s liability.
