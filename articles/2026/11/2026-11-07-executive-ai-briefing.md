# EXECUTIVE AI BRIEFING

The post-midterm repricing of AI moved from public markets into private capital and infrastructure this week. OpenAI raised less at a lower valuation, Anthropic heads into its roadshow with buyers marking it down, and Microsoft is consolidating compute suppliers while Google takes over a stalled gigawatt campus. Beneath the market noise, two operating signals stand out: enterprise AI spend is flattening even as usage grows, and the liability and security exposure of autonomous agents is becoming concrete enough for insurers, lawmakers and attackers to act on.
