# House panel questions reported Microsoft–ByteDance AI discussions

Reuters reports that the House Select Committee on China requested a Microsoft briefing after reporting that ByteDance could obtain access to OpenAI models through Azure in a potential $500 million arrangement. Microsoft said it enforces its service terms and applicable laws, while declining to discuss confidential negotiations; the reporting does not establish that a deal has closed. Executive takeaway: model distribution through cloud partners is attracting scrutiny that extends beyond technical access controls into customer eligibility and geopolitical risk. HOT M · Microsoft
