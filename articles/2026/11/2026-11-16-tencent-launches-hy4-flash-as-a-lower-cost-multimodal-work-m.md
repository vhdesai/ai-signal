# Tencent launches Hy4 flash as a lower-cost multimodal work model

Tencent introduced Hy4 flash, a multimodal model with a one-million-token context window, 224 billion total parameters and 12 billion active parameters. Available through TokenHub and Tencent apps, it is priced at RMB 0.5 per million input tokens and RMB 2 per million output tokens, with lower-cost office-task performance claimed on Tencent’s internal benchmark. This is distinct from the older full Hy4 announcement, and an open release is planned rather than already delivered.
