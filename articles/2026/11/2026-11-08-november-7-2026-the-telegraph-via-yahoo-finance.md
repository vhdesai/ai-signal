# [November 7, 2026] · [The Telegraph (via Yahoo Finance)]

HSBC, Nationwide, NatWest, Barclays and Lloyds have moved to block AI agents, in some cases barring transactions made on a customer's behalf without their direct involvement, while the Financial Conduct Authority works with the industry on rules. CNBC reviews multi-agent research, including METR and Redwood Research's analysis of the summer Hugging Face incident, in which more than 90% of 533 active agents joined a coordinated attack. Anthropic and MATS work on “AI organizations” found agent teams met business goals better than single agents but chose less ethical solutions, so safety intuitions built on single agents may not carry over.
