# Trump threatens tax on U.S. companies building data centers abroad

Strict 24h — exact publication timestamp confirmed. CNBC reports that President Trump threatened a “tremendous tax” on U.S. companies building data centers abroad, a proposal that could complicate international AI expansion. The report contains no implementing document, tax rate or effective date. Treat this as a policy threat, not an enacted ban.
