# OpenRouter removes providers and revises zero-retention eligibility

OpenRouter says it off-boarded three inference providers, removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility and notified potentially affected customers following investigations into compromised data or failures to uphold provider policies. It says there was no breach or unauthorized access to OpenRouter itself, and that some endpoint removals were precautionary; the notice does not establish that all affected endpoints suffered breaches. Its additional testing and checks for third-party forwarding reinforce a procurement priority: verify the downstream processing chain rather than relying on a retention label alone. Timing: 24–48-hour exception: the original is dated November 22 but gives no publication time. It is retained as an important recent security disclosure, not claimed as timestamp-verified within the strict 24-hour window.
