# OpenAI signs Magnite and PubMatic to fill unused ChatGPT ad space

According to three people familiar with the deals, OpenAI has signed deals with Magnite and PubMatic, whose software lets advertisers buy ad placements automatically across websites and other platforms, to fill unused ad space in ChatGPT. Using these automated ad marketplaces suggests OpenAI's own ad sales are not yet filling its inventory, and that it wants advertising revenue at scale rather than only from premium deals. It also raises the question of what ChatGPT conversation context advertisers can target, which privacy and enterprise teams will want answered. APPLE MEMORY EARNINGS
