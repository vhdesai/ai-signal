# AMD and Google chip away at Nvidia as Huang's exit shakes customers

Even before Huang resigned, AMD chips were winning converts for inference, and cloud provider Cirrascale, which runs 25 data centers, says customer interest in AMD's AI chips has grown since last year. Google is expanding sales of its TPUs and has signed major customers including OpenAI and Oracle. The Information says Nvidia faces market-share erosion despite strong sales and its priority access to manufacturing capacity, and Huang's departure is likely to reinforce the shift.
