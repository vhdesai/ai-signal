# MIT presents a smaller, locally run model for analog-chip design

MIT researchers working with IBM Research and Analog Devices introduced SLIC, a training method that teaches a small language model to design analog integrated circuits through multi-turn reinforcement learning. MIT reports that it came close to a frontier model's performance while using substantially less computing, with local training and execution helping keep confidential circuit and manufacturing data off cloud services. Today's item is MIT's research announcement: the underlying preprint was posted November 10, and the work is scheduled for presentation at NeurIPS 2026.
