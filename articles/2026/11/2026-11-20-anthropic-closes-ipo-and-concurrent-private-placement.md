# Anthropic closes IPO and concurrent private placement

24–48h exception — exact publication time unavailable. Anthropic announced the close of its IPO at $83 per share, including the underwriters’ full 147 million-share option, with approximately $93.5 billion in public-offering gross proceeds. A concurrent $26 billion private placement brought combined gross proceeds to approximately $119.5 billion. These source-reported totals include selling shareholders and are not net cash received by Anthropic.
