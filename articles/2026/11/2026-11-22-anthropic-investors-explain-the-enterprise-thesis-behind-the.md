# Anthropic investors explain the enterprise thesis behind their pre-IPO bets

Notable Capital, GIC and Baillie Gifford describe why they backed Anthropic at valuations that appeared aggressive before its IPO. The investors point to developer preference for Claude and growing enterprise adoption as central evidence for their investment thesis. TechCrunch reports that the flotation has produced substantial paper gains and that GIC holds roughly 1% of the company; this is newly published analysis of earlier investments, not a new financing announcement.
