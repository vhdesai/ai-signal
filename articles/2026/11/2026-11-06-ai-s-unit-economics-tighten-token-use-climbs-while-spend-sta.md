# AI's unit economics tighten: token use climbs while spend stalls and venture returns lean on markups

Ramp's AI Index shows token spending grew only 1.5% across August and September despite rising usage, before an 8.9% rebound into late October; Ramp attributes the stall to OpenAI and Anthropic price cuts and a plateau among the heaviest users, and Anthropic's filing says its own cuts trimmed revenue growth by about $600 million. PitchBook says the VC J-curve has deepened because recent AI returns rest on markups rather than exits, while vertical AI applications took 42% of Q3 AI deal value and 87.1% of disclosed exit value among the top 50 exits. Business Insider adds that Anthropic spent $1.7 billion on 5,300 employees in Q3, yet generates about $6.6 million in revenue per employee.
