# AI's unit economics tighten: token use climbs while spend stalls and venture returns lean on markups

Ramp's AI Index shows business spending on AI tokens grew only 1.5% across August and September despite rising usage, before an 8.9% rebound into late October; Ramp economist Ara Kharazian points to OpenAI and Anthropic price cuts and a plateau among the heaviest users. PitchBook says the VC J-curve has deepened because recent AI returns rest on portfolio markups rather than exits, warning post-COVID vintages will struggle if the AI trade cracks. Its Q3 AI report finds vertical applications took 42% of AI deal value and 87.1% of disclosed exit value among the top 50 AI exits.
