# ByteDance nears a $500 million Azure deal for OpenAI models, irking OpenAI

ByteDance is in advanced talks to spend roughly $500 million more on Microsoft cloud services, mainly to use OpenAI models through Azure. OpenAI blocks most Chinese firms from its own products and has complained to Microsoft that Chinese companies are distilling its models to build competitors, but Microsoft does not impose the same restrictions. The deal could add friction to the Microsoft-OpenAI relationship just as Washington tightens Chinese access to American AI. ENTERPRISE ANALYSIS
