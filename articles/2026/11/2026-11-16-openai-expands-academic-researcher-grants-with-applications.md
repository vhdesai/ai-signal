# OpenAI expands academic researcher grants, with applications opening November 23

OpenAI plans to provide free access to up to 200,000 academic researchers worldwide. The next application round opens November 23 with 50,000 first-come grants, each providing ChatGPT Plus to a recipient and up to two collaborators, rather than the pilot's Pro access. Reported survey time savings are self-reported responses, not independently measured productivity gains. Published November 16; exact time not disclosed, so strict 24-hour placement is unconfirmed.
