# CME delays launch of Nvidia GPU rental-price futures for a second time

At the Commodity Futures Trading Commission's request, the CME agreed to delay its futures contract tied to Nvidia GPU rental prices by another 60 days, so any approval will come in January at the earliest. Futures would let neoclouds, lenders, and large buyers hedge compute costs and would create a public market price for GPU capacity. The delay means GPU-backed lending and long-term capacity deals will remain hard to hedge until next year.
