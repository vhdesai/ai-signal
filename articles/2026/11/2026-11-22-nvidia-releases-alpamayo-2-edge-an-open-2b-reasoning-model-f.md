# Nvidia releases Alpamayo 2 Edge, an open 2B reasoning model for in-car autonomous driving

Alpamayo 2 Edge is an open vision-language-action model of about 2 billion parameters that turns six camera feeds into a 6.4-second driving plan and explains its reasoning, running on Nvidia's DRIVE AGX Thor at 160 to 190 milliseconds per plan. In Nvidia's simulator it drove 15.15 kilometers per at-fault incident, about 60 times better than a 4B Qwen-based baseline, though the results are Nvidia's own. The release extends Nvidia's open-model strategy into the vehicle and pulls automakers toward its Thor chips.
