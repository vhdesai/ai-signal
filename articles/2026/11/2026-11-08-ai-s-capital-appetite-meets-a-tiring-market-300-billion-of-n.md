# AI's capital appetite meets a tiring market: $300 billion of new stock, souring GPU loans and a $20 billion OpenAI target

Tech giants, chipmakers and AI clouds have sold or priced more than $300 billion of new stock and mandatory convertibles this year, with about $100 billion more possible, including a proposed $60 billion Meta at-the-market program, The Information reports. Debt is tightening as well: Semafor says GPU-backed loans are being shopped at steep discounts on fears they will outlast the chips, Ares is passing on GPU financing, and a $40 billion SpaceX chip financing is the next test. Neocloud Iren has cut its fiscal 2027 capex projections and CoreWeave reports Tuesday with expected quarterly cash burn of $10.7 billion, even as PitchBook notes OpenAI is targeting a $20 billion round and Thinking Machines a $5 billion one.
