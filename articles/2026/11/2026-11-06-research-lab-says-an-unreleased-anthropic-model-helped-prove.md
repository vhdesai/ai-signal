# Research lab says an unreleased Anthropic model helped prove a quantum computing conjecture that had been open since 2008

Physical Superintelligence (PSI), a physics research lab, says a research system built around an unreleased Anthropic model proved the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture in three days. The conjecture concerns when quantum computers can be dramatically faster than classical ones, and the proof shows those advantages depend on the problem having special structure. Stanford researchers who have worked on the problem called it a "huge result," according to PSI's announcement. It is not clear which model PSI used, and Anthropic did not comment. This follows OpenAI's September Navier-Stokes claim and adds to evidence that unreleased models are already doing research-level mathematics. Academic Research MIT CHEMISTRY FOUNDATION MODELS
