# Copyright pressure moves down the AI supply chain: EU adviser on Gemini, NYT sues Brave

In the first EU Court of Justice case on generative AI and copyright, an Advocate General said training on press articles falls under the text-and-data-mining exception, but that Gemini retrieving and reusing an article to answer users may infringe publishers' rights. In New York, The Times and three other papers sued Brave, alleging it scraped more than 30 billion pages and resold content to AI companies. Buyers of third-party search and retrieval data should review provenance and indemnity terms.
