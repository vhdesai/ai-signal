# Anthropic's IPO deck says Claude ported its models to AMD MI355 in three days, chipping at CUDA lock-in

A slide in Anthropic's investor presentation says Claude Mythos Preview ported and optimized Anthropic's models for AMD MI355 GPUs in three days, writing or porting all of the kernels. Anthropic says onboarding a new accelerator previously took about a dozen engineers six to 12 months, and it describes a goal of a fungible compute pool spanning Nvidia, AMD, Trainium and TPUs. If the claim holds up, AI-written kernels weaken the software moat that has kept large buyers on Nvidia. The Motley Fool DATA CENTERS
