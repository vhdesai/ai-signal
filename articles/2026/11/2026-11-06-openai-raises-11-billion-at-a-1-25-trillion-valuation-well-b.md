# OpenAI raises $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation, well below its earlier target

Citing Bloomberg, TechCrunch reports that OpenAI has closed the first $11 billion of a round valuing it at $1.25 trillion. The full round could reach about $20 billion when it closes in the coming weeks, compared with a reported September target of $30 billion at $1.4 trillion. The Information separately reports that OpenAI will brief investors on its third-quarter results next week. A lower amount at a lower valuation suggests investors are becoming more sensitive to price after a sell-off in AI stocks following the midterm elections. ANTHROPIC IPO
