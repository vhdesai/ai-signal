# Sen. Tom Cotton says Lutnick repeatedly ignored his warnings about Nvidia chips reaching China

Senate Republican Tom Cotton said Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick repeatedly ignored his warnings that Nvidia chips were being smuggled to China. The criticism from a senior member of Lutnick's own party adds to the pressure on Commerce's export-control enforcement, which is already under congressional scrutiny over Megaspeed and the rushed H200 licenses. Expect tighter licensing and end-use checks as Washington reacts.
