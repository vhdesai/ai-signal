# Forensic analysis of model weights suggests DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3 were trained on export-banned Blackwell chips

Former OpenAI researcher Will DePue, who leads the nonprofit Unemployment Labs, analyzed the open weights of models from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group. He found patterns in the weights that point to training on Nvidia Blackwell GPUs, which cannot legally be exported to China. Training could have happened in offshore data centers such as those in Malaysia, which may not violate current rules but defeats their purpose. Bloomberg separately reported that a company linked to Alice Huang helped smuggle banned chips to a Shanghai data center. The findings strengthen the case for controls on who uses chips, not just where chips are shipped, which would affect cloud providers in Southeast Asia. SECURITY SUPPLY CHAIN CURSOR
