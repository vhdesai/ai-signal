# Anthropic positions Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 for specialist workloads

Anthropic says Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 produce outputs up to 30% faster than their 5.1 predecessors, with pricing of $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Fable is available through AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, while Mythos access is restricted to verified cybersecurity and life-sciences organizations. These are company-reported claims rather than independently verified results, and Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 for standard coding work.
