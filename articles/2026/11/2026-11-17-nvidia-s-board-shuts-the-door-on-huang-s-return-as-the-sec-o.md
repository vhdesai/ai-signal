# Nvidia's board shuts the door on Huang's return as the SEC opens a disclosure probe

Hours after Jensen Huang said he would be "honored" to return as CEO, board chair Harvey Jones told the earnings call that the board unanimously accepted his resignation over personal behavior inconsistent with Nvidia's code of conduct and does not intend to reinstate him. Nvidia has hired Heidrick & Struggles and says the next CEO could come from inside or outside the company, but it will not rush the search. Separately, the SEC has opened a civil investigation into whether Nvidia violated investor-disclosure laws. A reinstatement petition now has nearly 200,000 signatures, including about 10,000 employees, so pressure on the board is unlikely to fade.
