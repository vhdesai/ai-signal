# Musk says Grok Bot costs are "unsustainably high relative to revenue" as usage limits tighten

Users complained that their weekly allowances were running out faster than usual. Musk responded that serving Grok Bot, SpaceX's agent that operates a virtual computer, costs too much relative to its revenue. Grok Bot reported 800,000 paid weekly users last quarter and is being bundled into an $8-a-month X subscription that also includes Cursor. It also routes some work to outside models such as Claude Opus 5.5. This is an unusually direct admission that agent unit economics are still unresolved, so buyers should expect tighter usage limits and higher prices across agent products. FUNDING AI ROI
