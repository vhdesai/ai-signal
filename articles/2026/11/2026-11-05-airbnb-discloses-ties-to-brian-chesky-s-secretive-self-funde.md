# Airbnb discloses ties to Brian Chesky's secretive, self-funded AI lab

Airbnb disclosed an agreement that could let it invest in, and license technology from, a stealth AI startup controlled by CEO Brian Chesky, according to The Information. Chesky has self-funded the lab and plans to keep financing it, and the startup has acquired Airbnb intellectual property. Airbnb CTO Ahmad Al-Dahle, who previously ran parts of Meta's AI organization, has been advising the startup and holds equity in it. Infrastructure EXCLUSIVE
