# Ricursive Intelligence, a startup founded by former Googlers, released Ripple-65B Preview, a dense 65B Apache-2.0 model…

Ricursive Intelligence, a startup founded by former Googlers, released Ripple-65B Preview, a dense 65B Apache-2.0 model pretrained on 12T tokens with 1M-token context. It scores 80.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 79.1 on SWE-Bench Pro v2.0 Hard, edging out Reflection AI's 501B Beam and Meta's Muse Spark Open. Ricursive says total R&D cost was about $32M, roughly a tenth of what the previous open-weight coding leader cost.
