# Anthropic pushes Claude into factories and Google Meet days before its IPO

Claude for Manufacturing ships 17 skills for quoting jobs, preparing failed-inspection reports and diagnosing breakdowns, with connectors to Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365 and NetSuite and human approval kept in the loop. Claude Meetings entered beta in Google Meet for Team and Enterprise customers, taking notes, answering questions and drafting editable documents and whiteboards during calls. Organizations with zero data retention, HIPAA-ready setups or customer-managed keys cannot use the beta yet.
