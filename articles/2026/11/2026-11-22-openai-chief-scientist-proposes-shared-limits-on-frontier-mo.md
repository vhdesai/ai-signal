# OpenAI chief scientist proposes shared limits on frontier-model development

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki proposes using perplexity—a measure of predictive performance—as part of a shared framework for tracking frontier-model progress. Labs would disclose results and agree to pause beyond defined thresholds until their ability to understand and monitor model behavior improves. TechCrunch reports that the proposal has no specified threshold or commitments from other labs, making it an early governance proposal rather than an adopted standard. xAI / GROK
