# Baidu introduces ERNIE Evolve for automated research

Baidu says ERNIE Evolve can propose research directions, run experiments, and evaluate results with less manual intervention. The company reports improvements on public benchmarks and faster inference when applying the system to its forthcoming ERNIE 5.5 Flash model on Kunlunxin hardware. These are vendor-reported results rather than independent validation; Baidu says future expansion will retain defined boundaries, safety evaluation, and human oversight.
