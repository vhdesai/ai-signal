# Anthropic tells IPO investors that Claude is speeding up the design of its own AI chips

According to four people familiar with the discussions, Anthropic told investors that its new 15-person chip design team is using Claude for engineering and design work on its custom chips. Its engineers estimate that this saves time compared with traditional workflows at large chip designers such as Nvidia and AMD. The IPO, expected later this month, could value Anthropic at around $1.5 trillion. A 15-person team is very small for chip design. If the claim holds up, it would make it easier for AI labs to build their own chips and would put pressure on GPU makers' margins; for now it is an unverified claim made to investors.
