# NEW Claude for Manufacturing targets quoting, quality, and maintenance workflows

Anthropic's manufacturing plugin includes 17 skills, covering work such as quoting jobs, preparing failed-inspection reports, and diagnosing breakdowns. Connectors link it to Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365, and NetSuite. Human approval remains part of the workflow: the assistant produces outputs for review and does not operate equipment.
