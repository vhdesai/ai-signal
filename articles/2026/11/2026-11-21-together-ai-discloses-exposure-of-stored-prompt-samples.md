# Together AI discloses exposure of stored prompt samples

Publication-time exception: Together AI displays November 21, 2026, without a publication time; precise inclusion in the UTC/PST source window could not be verified. Together AI discloses that an AI coding agent committed a credential exposing a private repository containing stored prompt samples, which an unauthorized party downloaded. The investigation is ongoing, and zero data retention is becoming the default for new accounts. This is a new disclosure concerning requests from June and July, not an assertion that the incident occurred today. Review provider retention, credential controls, and coding-agent workflows.
