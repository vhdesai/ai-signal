# Censys finds more than 800 instances of the AI hacking tool Artex open to the internet without a password

Artex is an open-source AI agent system from China that carries out cyberattacks from initial scouting through data theft. It has been linked to the ConnectWise breach and attacks on South Korean banks. Censys found more than 800 copies that anyone on the internet could access without a password, some exposing attack tools and stolen data. Capable autonomous attack tools are now widely available outside the frontier labs, so defenders should assume attackers have AI agents. AGENTS LIABILITY
