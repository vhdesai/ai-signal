# IBM Newsroom · November 16, 2026 · 08:00 PST

IBM, Fermilab and NVIDIA outlined a proposed co-design environment that would colocate NVIDIA DGX B200 infrastructure with IBM quantum systems. The planned integration uses IBM’s Quantum Resource Management Interface to bring quantum-resource scheduling into Slurm, positioning the effort as AI-adjacent scientific infrastructure rather than a completed quantum-computing breakthrough. A related IBM, RIKEN and NVIDIA simulation reportedly improved accuracy over an earlier version of the same experiment; that result does not establish general quantum advantage.
