# San Francisco declaration outlines international AI security and infrastructure cooperation

The United States and nine partners—Australia, India, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Qatar, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom—issued the San Francisco Declaration on the Secure Foundations of Enduring Prosperity. It proposes five working groups covering innovation policy, adoption, supply-chain cooperation, security hubs and infrastructure resilience. The declaration is a cooperation framework, not a binding announcement of new export restrictions, funding or enforcement.
