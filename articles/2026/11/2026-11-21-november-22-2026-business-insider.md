# [November 22, 2026] · [Business Insider]

President Trump said Friday that Nvidia should give Jensen Huang his job back ("We're a country of second chances"), and Bill Ackman and Garry Tan have floated assembling a 15% ownership block to call a special meeting and reshape the board, a plan a Columbia law professor called "somewhere between extremely unlikely and remote," The Information reports. A letter backing Huang claims 10,263 employee signatures, roughly a quarter of the workforce, while the board still says it won't rehire him. Business Insider reports that Nvidia now risks losing the engineers behind its chip roadmap after shedding about $500 billion in market value since the ouster, and the WSJ examines the dangers of Huang's handshake deals.
