# Nvidia open-sources Nemotron-Labs-Sigma, a family of continuous diffusion language models

Nvidia released 14 open checkpoints at 3B and 8B parameters. Unlike LLaDA or Dream, which work on discrete tokens, Sigma generates text by denoising points in a learned 16-dimensional embedding space. Versions distilled to as few as eight steps keep most of their quality (70.34 average versus 71.16 for the base 8B), and the models run at about 120–140 tokens per second on one H100. Reasoning is still weak (17.0 on AIME25). Diffusion language models are not ready for production, but this is the most complete open release so far for low-latency text generation.
