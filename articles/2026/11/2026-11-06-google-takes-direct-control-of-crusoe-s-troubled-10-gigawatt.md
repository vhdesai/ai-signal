# Google takes direct control of Crusoe's troubled 10-gigawatt Wyoming campus

Crusoe confirmed that Google acquired its unfinished southeast Wyoming site, ending Crusoe's role in site selection, approvals and power strategy; Google reportedly had cost and schedule concerns and plans to invest $17 billion there. Crusoe continues to build for Google near Amarillo, Texas, and for other hyperscalers. The pattern is clear: when independent developers stumble on gigawatt-scale projects, hyperscalers take the strategic sites in-house. Sources: Crusoe | TechCrunch
