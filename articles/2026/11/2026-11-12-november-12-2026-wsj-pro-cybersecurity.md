# [November 12, 2026] · [WSJ Pro CyberSecurity]

United will block personal AI assistants such as Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots from booking or managing trips on its own app and website and through travel agencies and aggregators. The airline cites exposure of account credentials, unintended purchases and possible conflicts with FAA and Transportation Department rules, such as the requirement that a person attest at check-in that bags hold no banned items. JPMorgan Chase has taken similar steps while Delta and American have adopted more permissive policies, and Meta shares fell 2% on the report. WSJ | CBS News | The Information
