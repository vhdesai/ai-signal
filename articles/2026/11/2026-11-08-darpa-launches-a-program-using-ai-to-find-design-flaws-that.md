# DARPA launches a program using AI to find design flaws that adversaries could exploit in electronic markets

DARPA's new Intrinsic Market Resilience (IMR) program wants teams to use large language models to turn regulatory filings and technical specifications into formal models of markets for stocks, bonds, energy, and cloud computing. AI-driven automated reasoning would then search those models for rules that could be abused to cause disruption. The concern is adversaries who want to cause instability, not traders seeking profit; DARPA cites California's energy crisis in the early 2000s and the 2010 flash crash as examples.
