# [November 3, 2026] · [DealBook]

Oracle filed to raise $20 billion through $10 billion of investment-grade bonds and $10 billion of common stock to fund its AI buildout, as the Nasdaq 100 slipped into correction territory. Apple CEO John Ternus called memory pricing unprecedented, a sign AI demand is now squeezing even the strongest hardware buyer. The Wall Street Journal reports AI investors are seeking downside protection, and DealBook quotes a warning of a shift from FOMO to FOGO, or fear of getting obliterated.
