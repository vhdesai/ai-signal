# Fireworks discloses attackers accessed customer model weights as open AI platforms face rising attacks

Inference provider Fireworks said attackers accessed some customers' model weights, datasets and billing information, and it temporarily disabled self-service dataset functions; The Information reports the hack threatens a roughly $1 billion business. Hugging Face policy chief Irene Solaiman warned that attacks such as the October OpenCode API-key theft could force open platforms to restrict access. For enterprises, third-party inference and fine-tuning providers are now a concentrated IP-exfiltration risk. POLICY
