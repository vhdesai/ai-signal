# Hiding benchmark tests widens the gap between open-weight and frontier models from 3 points to 12

Artificial Analysis, an independent firm that benchmarks AI models, overhauled its Intelligence Index and now keeps many of its tests private, which makes it harder for model makers to train specifically to pass them. Under the new system, the best open-weight models, including Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro, score 32, compared with 58 for the leader, Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5. Even the smaller Claude Haiku 5.5 beat every open-weight model. Nathan Lambert, an AI researcher who studies open-weight models, said the old index "was flattering the open weight models too strongly." Before switching to cheaper open models to save money, buyers should test them on their own private evaluations. ATTENTION LONG CONTEXT INFERENCE
