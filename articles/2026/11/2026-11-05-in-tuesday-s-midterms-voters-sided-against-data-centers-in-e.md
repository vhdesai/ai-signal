# In Tuesday's midterms, voters sided against data centers in every local measure to restrict them except one, in…

In Tuesday's midterms, voters sided against data centers in every local measure to restrict them except one, in Harwood, North Dakota. The vote count differs by outlet: CNBC and Ballotpedia counted 41 measures in 10 states, and Axios put the tally at 43–1. Ohio accounted for 21 of the measures, and Democrat Amy Acton, who backs a conditional data-center moratorium, won the governorship. That puts pressure on OpenAI and SoftBank's planned 8 GW PORTS-Pike campus there. Chip, neocloud and data-center stocks sold off as investors priced in a tougher siting and regulatory climate.
