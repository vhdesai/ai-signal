# Data-center supply chains reshape: Fervo's geothermal backlog nears $12 billion as Microsoft fights a Chinese optics ban

Fervo's contracted revenue rose to $11.9 billion from $7.2 billion a quarter earlier, its Utah site is now estimated to support 8.4 GW, and cost per kilowatt has fallen to $5,500 on a 396 MW plant for Google due by 2028. Separately, Reuters reports Microsoft is lobbying the administration to drop an expected FCC ban on Chinese optical transceivers, arguing that China supplies about two-thirds of the market and a ban would leave purchased GPUs idle. Together they show that firm power and networking components, not just chips, now gate how fast capacity comes online.
