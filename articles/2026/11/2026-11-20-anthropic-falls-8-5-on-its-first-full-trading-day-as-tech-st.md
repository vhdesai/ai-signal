# Anthropic falls 8.5% on its first full trading day as tech stocks sell off

Anthropic opened at $111.50 on its Thursday debut and closed at $97.17, 17% above the $83 IPO price. It then fell 8.5% on Friday as Nvidia and AMD each dropped more than 13% and the hyperscalers closed lower. Including the underwriters' overallotment, the offering raised a record $93.5 billion. Analysts split: Seaport rated the stock neutral, calling it "an incredible leap of faith," while William Blair rated it outperform. Weakness in the most-watched AI IPO points to a tighter funding market for frontier labs and the neoclouds that rely on them.
