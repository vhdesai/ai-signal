# OpenAI raises $15 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation, half the amount it sought

CNBC's David Faber reported that OpenAI first sought up to $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation. The final valuation is still about 47% above its March round, which raised $122 billion at $852 billion. According to Faber, Nvidia has provided about $30 billion in total and SoftBank about $65 billion, and OpenAI keeps raising because it burns heavy cash despite revenue growing about 50% from the second to the third quarter. The smaller raise lands days before Anthropic's IPO, which bankers are reportedly trying to price above the published range. Investors now have a direct public comparison for the two frontier labs.
