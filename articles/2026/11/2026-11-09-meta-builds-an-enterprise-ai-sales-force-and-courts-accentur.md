# Meta builds an enterprise AI sales force and courts Accenture, Deloitte and IBM

Meta has begun forming a sales organization to sell its AI products to small and midsize businesses and is building relationships with consulting firms and systems integrators, including Accenture, Deloitte and IBM, to reach large companies, according to job postings and a person familiar with the plan. It is Meta's clearest attempt yet to turn roughly $145 billion of planned capital spending this year into enterprise revenue, putting it in direct competition with Microsoft, Google, OpenAI and Anthropic. Its enterprise agents have met resistance so far but have found traction on messaging apps, so expect Meta offerings to arrive through existing integrator relationships rather than direct procurement.
