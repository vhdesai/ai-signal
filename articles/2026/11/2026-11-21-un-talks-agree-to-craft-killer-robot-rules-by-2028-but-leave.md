# UN talks agree to craft "killer robot" rules by 2028 but leave binding status open

Talks under the UN Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons ended in Geneva on Saturday with a pledge to develop new rules within two years for weapons that can identify and engage targets with little or no human oversight. About 100 countries backed legally binding rules on a short timeline, but opposition from major arms producers including the US, Russia and Israel produced a watered-down agreement that leaves open whether future measures will be binding. The meeting's Swiss chair hailed the outcome, while many rights groups criticized it.
