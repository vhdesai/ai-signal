# [November 11, 2026] · [The Information]

UK inference-chip startup Fractile raised a $720 million Series C led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint, and DealBook notes SoftBank and Nvidia backed Agile Robots at an $8.5 billion valuation. The Information's 50 most promising startups list adds a security category for the first time, and three of its four most valuable companies are security startups worth more than $1 billion each. PitchBook says 10 of 12 aging unicorns that exited in the third quarter sold below their last marks at a median 72.8% discount, putting about 22% of total unicorn value at risk, while $145 billion manager ICG says investors outside venture-style bets are not paid enough for AI risk.
