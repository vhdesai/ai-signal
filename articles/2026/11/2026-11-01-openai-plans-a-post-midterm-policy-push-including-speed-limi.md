# OpenAI plans a post-midterm policy push, including speed limits for frontier systems

Axios reports OpenAI will launch a policy and public-relations campaign right after Tuesday's midterms, timed to the lame-duck session opening November 9. Proposals reportedly include mandatory safeguards, or speed limits, for the most powerful AI systems and community compacts to ease data-center opposition. The move positions OpenAI to shape rules before a potentially more adversarial Congress takes over.
