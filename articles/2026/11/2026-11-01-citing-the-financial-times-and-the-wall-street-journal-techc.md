# Citing the Financial Times and The Wall Street Journal, TechCrunch reports OpenAI has lowered the target for its…

Citing the Financial Times and The Wall Street Journal, TechCrunch reports OpenAI has lowered the target for its pre-IPO round to $20 billion, down from $30 billion at a $1.25–1.4 trillion valuation. The round is led by UAE investors, and the Journal reports $11 billion has been secured so far. Together with Anthropic's reported speculative-grade rating outlook, it shows capital markets tightening on frontier labs just as their compute commitments peak.
