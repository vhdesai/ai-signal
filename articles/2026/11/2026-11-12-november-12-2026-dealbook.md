# [November 12, 2026] · [DealBook]

Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday, three days after the board opened an inquiry into whether he was the target of a foreign intelligence effort and a day after The New York Times reported a second affair, with direct report Kimberly Powell. Finance chief Colette Kress becomes interim CEO and longtime director Harvey Jones becomes board chair, according to TechCrunch and Business Insider. DealBook notes Huang had roughly 60 direct reports and no succession plan, with Lisa Su, Rene Haas, Lip-Bu Tan and Johny Srouji floated as outside candidates, and asks what happens to the financial backstops Nvidia extended to neoclouds and startups. The Information put Nvidia near $3.85 trillion after hours on Wednesday, down from a $5.8 trillion peak in October.
