# NEW Google introduces Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its fast, lower-cost model tier

Hydrogen sits below Carbon and Argon and becomes the default model in Search AI Mode, with flat pricing across its entire one-million-token context: $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. Google's Orchestrator can switch models within a session; Google reports near-Carbon task success while consuming an average of 24% less quota.
