# Insurers hold $85 billion of AI debt as data centers drive a record quarter for climate tech VC

US life insurers held $85.2 billion of AI debt as of June 30, up 24% in six months, and Moody's estimates that could reach $185 billion by the end of next year, The Times reports. The exposure is concentrated in a handful of borrowers including Alphabet, Anthropic, Meta, Nvidia and OpenAI, and Ares warns that off-balance-sheet structures such as Meta's Hyperion financing look safer than they are. PitchBook says AI-driven power and data center demand pushed climate tech VC to a record $22.5 billion in the third quarter, up about 62% from the second. The New York Times RESILIENCE UPDATE
