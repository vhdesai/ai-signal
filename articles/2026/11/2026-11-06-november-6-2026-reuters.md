# [November 6, 2026] · [Reuters]

Microsoft opted not to renew a deal to rent AI cloud capacity from Oracle and is evaluating which neocloud partners, a roster that includes CoreWeave, Nebius, IREN, Lambda and Nscale, it will stop using as it prioritizes its own data centers. Shares of CoreWeave, Nebius and IREN are down roughly half over the past month, and the Oracle decision lands days after Oracle began a $20 billion stock and bond raise for AI capacity. Separately, The Information reports CME agreed at the CFTC's request to delay Nvidia GPU rental-price futures another 60 days, leaving compute prices without a listed hedge until at least January.
