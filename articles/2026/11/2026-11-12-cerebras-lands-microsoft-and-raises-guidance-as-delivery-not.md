# Cerebras lands Microsoft and raises guidance as delivery, not demand, becomes the bottleneck for neoclouds

Cerebras reported core revenue up 70% to $230.9 million, an unexpected adjusted profit, and a Microsoft deal signed in October that begins contributing revenue in 2027, sending shares up about 17%. It also said land, power and construction constraints left it behind on its 750-megawatt OpenAI commitment, which it expects to be back on schedule in the second half of 2027. Nebius plans about $40 billion of 2027 capital spending funded mostly by prepayments and contract-backed loans, but only about half of its Microsoft capacity in New Jersey will be delivered by year-end.
