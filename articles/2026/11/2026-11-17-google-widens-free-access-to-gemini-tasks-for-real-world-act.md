# Google widens free access to Gemini Tasks for real-world actions

Google is expanding free access to Gemini Tasks for business calls, appointments, and purchases; official November 17 release notes list the United States, India, Canada, Australia, Japan, and Korea, with some advanced features restricted to eligible U.S. users. Google Pay purchases require explicit approval. Broader consumer-agent distribution makes authorization boundaries and consequential-action approvals important operating controls, not optional interface details.
