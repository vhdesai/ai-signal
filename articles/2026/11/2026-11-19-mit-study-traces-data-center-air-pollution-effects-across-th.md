# MIT study traces data-center air-pollution effects across the grid

MIT researchers modeled how data-center electricity demand changes generation across the U.S. grid, then estimated the resulting pollution and health effects. Their analysis associates that additional air pollution with approximately 650 premature deaths in 2023, including impacts far from major data-center clusters; this is a model-based estimate for data-center demand, not an observed death count attributable specifically to AI. Infrastructure planning should therefore consider marginal generation and downwind health effects alongside local power prices, water use and carbon accounting.
