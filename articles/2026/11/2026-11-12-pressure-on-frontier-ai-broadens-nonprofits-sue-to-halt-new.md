# Pressure on frontier AI broadens: nonprofits sue to halt new models as Congress and the EU set agendas

Four nonprofits including Public Citizen and the Alliance for Secure AI sued OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta and SpaceX's AI unit in San Francisco Superior Court, seeking an injunction against developing models more capable than today's frontier systems under California's unfair competition law and public-nuisance doctrine. An injunction is unlikely, but discovery could force disclosure of internal safety assessments. Democrats taking control of Congress in January say AI security, threat-intelligence sharing and rebuilding CISA rank high on their agenda, and EU leaders held their first AI-focused summit in Dublin.
