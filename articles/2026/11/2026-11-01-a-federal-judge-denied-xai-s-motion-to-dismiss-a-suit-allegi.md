# A federal judge denied xAI's motion to dismiss a suit alleging it ran at least 35 combustion turbines at its Colossus…

A federal judge denied xAI's motion to dismiss a suit alleging it ran at least 35 combustion turbines at its Colossus data center near Memphis without required air permits. The court rejected xAI's arguments that the turbines are exempt mobile sources and that citizen suits are unconstitutional. The ruling raises legal risk for behind-the-meter gas power, a common stopgap for fast AI data center builds.
