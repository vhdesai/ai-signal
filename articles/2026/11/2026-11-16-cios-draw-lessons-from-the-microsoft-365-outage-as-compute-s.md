# CIOs draw lessons from the Microsoft 365 outage as compute strain threatens more disruptions

CIO Dive says last week's 19-hour Microsoft 365 outage shows CIOs should spread workloads across providers and strengthen incident response, because strain on compute could bring future disruptions. An Akamai survey found fewer than one in six IT decision-makers say critical workloads consistently meet response-time goals despite higher infrastructure spending.
