# [November 1, 2026] · [The Tactical Allocation Letter]

The Tactical Allocation Letter notes the Nasdaq fell 5.3% in October, its worst month since March 2025, while the Dow rose 1.7%, with technology down about 7.5% and communications about 9%. It concludes money did not leave stocks but left the AI trade after Intel's cash-burn warning and Amazon's spending plans. Wall Street Journal analysis frames the same shift: Wall Street is starting to call the shots in the AI build-out, and new forms of vendor financing magnify the AI trade on the way up and down.
