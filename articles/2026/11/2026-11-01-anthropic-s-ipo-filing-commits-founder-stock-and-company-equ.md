# Anthropic's IPO filing commits founder stock and company equity to charitable giving

According to the S-1, Anthropic's seven cofounders have pledged 80% of their stock to charity, a stake that could be worth about $80 billion. The company has also earmarked 5% of its equity for a public-good fund, with another 2% for each sustained 50% rise in the share price. A new Class E share carries limited voting rights and no economic value, and is awarded for tenure and for employees' giving pledges. Philanthropy experts called the design credible but said it should be judged by what is actually given. Investors should model the dilution and the effect on governance. APPLE STRATEGY
