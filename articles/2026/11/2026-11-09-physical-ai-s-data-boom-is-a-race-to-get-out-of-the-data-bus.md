# Physical AI’s data boom is a race to get out of the data business

PitchBook reports that robotics-data suppliers are expanding beyond collection into testing, deployment and fine-tuning infrastructure. Maxinsights says it has 4,000 people collecting footage across eight countries and projects a $250 million annualized revenue run rate; these are company statements, not independently established operating results. The strategic issue is defensibility: deployed robots could eventually generate their own experience, while a concentrated customer base limits suppliers’ negotiating power.
