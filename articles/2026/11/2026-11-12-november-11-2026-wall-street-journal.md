# [November 11, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

Inference startups that resell rented cloud capacity, including Fal, Together and Baseten, are buying their own Nvidia GPUs and data-center space because hyperscalers prioritize direct customers, a shift The Information says will drastically change their economics. Others are spreading workloads across dozens of smaller neoclouds, an approach the Fireworks hack shows carries risk. CoreWeave shares jumped 25% after it beat revenue estimates and raised 2026 guidance, and DealBook reports it plans at least $4 billion of investment-grade bonds backed by chips and contracts at roughly 7% to 7.5%, about half the yield on its junk bonds.
