# LangChain raises $300 million at a $3 billion valuation on its pitch that enterprises should own their agents

CapitalG led the Series C, with GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP and Sapphire participating. The valuation is more than double the $1.25 billion LangChain was valued at a year ago. The company has moved from its open-source framework to LangSmith, a platform for building and managing agents that works with any model or cloud. It claims 7,000 customers, including Harvey, Rippling, Nvidia, Toyota and Salesforce.
