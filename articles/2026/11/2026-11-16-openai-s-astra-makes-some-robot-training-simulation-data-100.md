# OpenAI's Astra makes some robot-training simulation data 100 times faster, says Nvidia's robotics chief

Nvidia robotics chief Deepu Talla told The Information's AI Deep Dive that OpenAI's Astra model, released in September, is a breakthrough for general-purpose robots because of how well it understands the physical world. He said Astra has made generating some kinds of simulation data for training robot models 100 times faster. Talla added that AI agents can now write the code to bring robots into factories, putting them within reach of small and medium-sized manufacturers. Academic Research HEALTH RESEARCH
