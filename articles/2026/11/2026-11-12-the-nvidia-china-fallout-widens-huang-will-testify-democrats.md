# The Nvidia-China fallout widens: Huang will testify, Democrats probe export approvals and Lutnick is under pressure

Huang agreed to a closed-door briefing for three Republican-led House committees before Thanksgiving, while Senator Ron Wyden and Representative Richard Neal asked Commerce to preserve records on H200 export approvals and why Megaspeed kept receiving licenses after intelligence warnings. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is fighting for his job as Trump aides blame him for the fallout, and The Information reports that companies tied to Alice Huang invested in DeepSeek to secure banned Blackwell chips. Reuters identified about $3.2 billion of Chinese computing projects designed around Nvidia accelerators now at risk, Malaysia denied clearing Megaspeed and is checking chip re-exports, and Singapore has barred Alice Huang. Organizations with Asia-Pacific GPU capacity should expect tighter export enforcement and customer due diligence.
