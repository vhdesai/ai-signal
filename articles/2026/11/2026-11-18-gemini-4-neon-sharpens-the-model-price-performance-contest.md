# Gemini 4 Neon sharpens the model price-performance contest

VentureBeat reports that Google has introduced Gemini 4 Neon across its API, Gemini app and Antigravity development platform, with standard pricing of $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Its coverage describes competitive coding results and a million-token multimodal context window, but also notes that agent configurations and reasoning settings materially affect comparisons. Executive takeaway: evaluate cost per completed workflow and human intervention, rather than choosing a default model from token prices or a single benchmark.
