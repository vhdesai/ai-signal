# Alibaba introduces Qwen4-Max with a one-million-token context window

Alibaba unveiled Qwen4-Max, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion total parameters, 71 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window. On its own Work360 evaluation, Alibaba claims 35% fewer tokens at High effort and approximately 60% lower task cost than Qwen3.8-Max; these are vendor-reported results rather than independent findings. The model is available through Qwen Cloud and Qwen apps, while smaller open-weight releases are promised for later rather than available now. Publication date verified; original publication time unavailable.
