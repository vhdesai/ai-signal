# Oracle says its OpenAI infrastructure commitment is fully funded

Oracle said its final capital raise this month fully funds its OpenAI infrastructure contract, according to Reuters. The company said its Abilene campus is nearly complete and the Shackelford campus is fully permitted, with Nvidia Vera Rubin rack handovers expected in the coming weeks. These are company statements about funding and delivery readiness, rather than independent verification of completed capacity. Academic Research AI · MATHTRENDING
