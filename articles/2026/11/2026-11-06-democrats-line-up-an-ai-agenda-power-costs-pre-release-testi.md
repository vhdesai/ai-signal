# Democrats line up an AI agenda: power costs, pre-release testing and liability

Reuters reports Democrats will introduce a wave of AI bills when they take control of Congress in January, built on protecting consumers from data-center power costs, independent testing of advanced models before release and clearer liability for AI harms; a presidential veto and the filibuster remain hurdles. TechCrunch notes Big Tech is already courting the new majority, with OpenAI hiring Jessica Schumer and rebranding data centers as compute campuses. Policy experts expect most near-term fights in state legislatures and local siting decisions. Sources: Crypto Briefing (Reuters) | TechCrunch
