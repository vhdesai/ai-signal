# Democrats line up an AI agenda: power costs, pre-release testing and liability

Reuters reports Democrats will introduce a wave of AI bills when they take control of Congress in January, built on protecting consumers from data-center power costs, independent testing of advanced models before release and clearer liability for AI harms; a presidential veto and the filibuster remain hurdles. TechCrunch notes voters backed data-center restrictions in 40 of 41 ballot measures and that Big Tech is already courting the new majority, with OpenAI hiring Jessica Schumer. Policy experts expect most near-term fights in state legislatures and local siting decisions.
