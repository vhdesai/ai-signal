# [November 5, 2026] · [Google Cloud Blog]

Google made its Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash video model generally available, with clips of up to 15 seconds, conversational editing and 720p pricing of $0.07 per second, 30% below the prior version. Thinking Machines Lab introduced Inkling 2 Small, a mixture-of-experts model with 594 billion total and 17 billion active parameters that accepts image inputs and is available first through Tinker. The lab says it intends to release the weights under an Apache 2.0 license next week, after external safety testing and red-teaming.
