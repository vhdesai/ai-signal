# Inference plumbing matures: Buoyant open-sources Kinference and NVIDIA speeds RL weight refits

Buoyant, the maker of Linkerd, released Kinference, an Apache 2.0 control plane that sits in front of vLLM fleets on Kubernetes and applies routing policies, token rate limits and KV-cache-aware load balancing; its self-reported benchmark cut median time to first token sharply only for prompts with shared prefixes. NVIDIA detailed Reshard, a collective that it says speeds weight refits during reinforcement-learning post-training by 2.7 to 5.7 times. For teams running open-weight models in-house, cost-aware routing and RL efficiency are becoming platform layers in their own right. Sources: MarkTechPost | NVIDIA
