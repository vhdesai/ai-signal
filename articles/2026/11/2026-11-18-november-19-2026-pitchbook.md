# [November 19, 2026] · [PitchBook]

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, above the $70 to $78 range, and sold 956.4 million shares instead of the planned 820 million, raising about $81 billion at a fully diluted valuation near $1.5 trillion. Adding a $26 billion private placement from partners including Nvidia, Google and Microsoft brings the total above $107 billion, topping SpaceX's $86 billion record and putting Anthropic ahead of OpenAI in total capital raised, PitchBook says. Shares begin trading on Nasdaq today under ANT, and The Information bets they reach $150 at the open, while cautioning that heavy spending on its own data centers could weigh on its first year as a public company.
