# Meta expands AI age estimation worldwide as Washington presses Google on AI in schools

Meta is rolling out AI age estimation globally on Instagram, moving suspected teens into restricted Teen Accounts even when they gave an adult birthday, and is using Apple's age-range signal in Australia, with similar tools coming to Facebook and Threads. Senator Ed Markey urged Alphabet to let K-12 schools use Google Search without also accepting its AI features, after Google said student accounts would have AI tools off by default. Both moves show automated age assurance and AI defaults in education becoming regulatory pressure points.
