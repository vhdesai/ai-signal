# Apple reportedly targets more agent-like Siri capabilities

A report citing Bloomberg says Apple aims to add agent-style capabilities to Siri as early as next year, with Craig Federighi leading the work. It also reports pressure from CEO John Ternus to accelerate Apple's AI roadmap. Apple has not confirmed the plans; this is a report on intended direction, not a product launch or availability commitment. Publication time is verified for this secondary report; the original Bloomberg report's exact publication time was not confirmed.
