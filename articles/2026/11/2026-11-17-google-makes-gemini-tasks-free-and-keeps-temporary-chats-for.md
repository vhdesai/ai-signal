# Google makes Gemini Tasks free and keeps temporary chats for two months

Google is rolling out Tasks for free to people using its Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, letting Gemini call businesses, book appointments, buy through Google Pay and read and send email. The move answers Meta's Muse, which passed 3 million weekly users within weeks, and OpenAI's Dots agent. Separately, Google will keep temporary and history-off Gemini chats for about two months instead of 72 hours, citing safety and security, while splitting chat history from consent to AI training. Longer retention for assistants that act on users' behalf is likely to draw privacy scrutiny.
