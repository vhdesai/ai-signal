# The Journal reports that large data centers in Southeast Asia gave Chinese companies crucial computing power and served…

The Journal reports that large data centers in Southeast Asia gave Chinese companies crucial computing power and served as cover for alleged chip smuggling, and examines Huang's role in building that route. The article is paywalled; this summary is based on its published description. It comes as U.S. investigators examine Singapore's Megaspeed, the company at the center of Huang's resignation, which has denied wrongdoing. Expect closer scrutiny of third-country cloud capacity as a gap in U.S. export controls.
