# Anthropic's IPO filing warns it may soon meet its own threshold for AI-accelerated progress

risk factors in Anthropic's amended S-1 say internal estimates about future models "indicate that we may imminently meet" the threshold its Responsible Scaling Policy defines as "dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI." The filing also says Anthropic has in the past failed to recognize capability jumps until after they occurred. It says no current model is above the threshold, and that Anthropic is considering updating the policy to keep humans in control of frontier model development. A securities filing is partly a sales document, but putting this risk on record raises pressure on governance and is likely to draw regulators' attention to AI that improves itself.
