# Daily AI News Digest – November 10, 2026

Anthropic set terms for the largest IPO on record, valuing itself at up to $1.4 trillion a day after shipping cheaper frontier models, while Nvidia's leadership crisis moved from the boardroom to the FBI, the courts and Congress. Beneath the headlines, the cost of AI capacity is tightening: SoftBank booked a $7 billion loss, neocloud debt is pricing at junk-plus yields, and model prices keep falling. On governance, Australia is creating an AI regulator after an OpenAI agent breach, governments and labs signed a Paris declaration on shutdown mechanisms, and DeepMind showed AI can formally verify production cryptography.
