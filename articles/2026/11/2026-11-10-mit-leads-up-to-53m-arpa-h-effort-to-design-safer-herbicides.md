# MIT leads up to $53M ARPA-H effort to design safer herbicides with generative AI

The PLOW project, led by MIT biological engineering head Christopher Voigt with the Broad Institute and others, will use generative AI to discover herbicide alternatives as weeds grow resistant to glyphosate and atrazine. The work also aims to reduce US reliance on imported phosphorus used in glyphosate. It is another example of federal programs funding AI-driven molecule design beyond pharma.
