# The AI trade cracks: Nasdaq loses more than $4 trillion in a week as Anthropic slides on its second day

The Nasdaq Composite fell 5.3% on Friday to cap its worst week in more than a year and turned negative for 2026, while the S&P 500 entered correction territory and the PHLX Semiconductor index dropped almost 11%. Anthropic, which closed its first day up 17% from its $83 IPO price, fell 8.5% on Friday and touched $83.05, just a nickel above the offer. Memory, power and chip names led the decline, and falling copper prices hinted that investors expect the AI buildout to slow. Even the largest IPO in history could not stop the rotation out of AI infrastructure stocks.
