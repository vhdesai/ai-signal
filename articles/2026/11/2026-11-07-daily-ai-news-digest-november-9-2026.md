# Daily AI News Digest – November 9, 2026

Nvidia's governance crisis dominated the cycle: its board opened an independent probe into reports that Jensen Huang was a foreign intelligence target, putting key-person risk at the center of the AI trade just as Anthropic starts its IPO roadshow and OpenAI closes the first $11 billion of a smaller round. Underneath, the build-out is meeting harder limits: equity and GPU-debt markets are tiring, neoclouds face a pivotal earnings week, and power, permitting and post-midterm politics are constraining data centers. Agents are moving from demos into liability questions, robotics led the research agenda at CoRL, and Washington is signaling openness to guardrails while AI-driven security risks multiply.
