# Marvell agrees to buy Lumentum in a $115 billion all-stock deal

The Information reports that Marvell agreed on Monday to buy laser maker Lumentum in an all-stock deal valuing Lumentum at $115 billion. That would make it one of the largest semiconductor mergers ever. The deal would put Lumentum's optical components alongside Marvell's custom data center and networking chips. Interconnect bandwidth is becoming a binding limit on the size of AI clusters, and owning both sides would give Marvell more influence over hyperscaler networking designs. The terms come from The Information's briefing summary; the full article is paywalled. XAI AGENT ECONOMICS
