# Baidu launches ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview and a Token Exchange marketplace for model services

At Baidu World 2026, Baidu introduced ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, a mixture-of-experts model with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token and a one-million-token context window, with weights promised by November 25. It also launched Token Exchange, a marketplace connecting model-service providers with buyers, alongside an optical-interconnect Tianchi supernode and its Kunlunxin chip roadmap. CEO Robin Li said daily active agents inside Baidu grew from 5,000 to 37,000 over the past year.
