# The financing squeeze shows up in results: SoftBank's $7 billion loss and CoreWeave debt near 15%

SoftBank swung to an unexpected quarterly net loss of about $7 billion, including a $3.7 billion charge tied to realigned power-equipment procurement for US AI data centers, while its OpenAI stake was held at $99.6 billion. The WSJ's Markets A.M. notes that CoreWeave's most-traded junk bond now yields around 15% ahead of Tuesday's earnings, and that such borrowing costs could make new chip and data-center plans uneconomic for anyone but hyperscalers. Secured debt backed by facilities or hyperscaler contracts is still clearing, so the neoclouds' task is to show they can grow without leaning on expensive unsecured borrowing.
