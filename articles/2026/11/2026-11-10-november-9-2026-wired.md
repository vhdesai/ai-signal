# [November 9, 2026] · [WIRED]

In a four-week proof of concept, Gemini-powered agents generated millions of lines of Lean proofs to show that Signal's library meets specified cryptographic properties, with humans reviewing every specification. When previously fixed bugs were reintroduced into older versions, the system correctly failed to verify the flawed code. The scope excludes parts of the app such as group messaging, but it suggests machine-checked assurance for high-value code is becoming practical in weeks rather than years.
