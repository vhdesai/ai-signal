# [November 22, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Reuters reports that Anthropic, borrowing SpaceX's playbook, held its 22-bank syndicate to tight deadlines and strict information controls, paid fees of about 0.68% and required banks to arrange a $15 billion revolving credit facility in advance to win roles, which chafed some bankers. The WSJ describes a blame game over the post-debut slide, citing allocations that left many firms without shares, a possibly rich opening price and immediate employee share sales. Business Insider says Anthropic's next task is finding a hit bigger than Claude Code in areas such as biotech, cybersecurity and robotics, while PitchBook calculates that Series A investors in the round led by Jaan Tallinn are sitting on a roughly 3,254x paper multiple.
