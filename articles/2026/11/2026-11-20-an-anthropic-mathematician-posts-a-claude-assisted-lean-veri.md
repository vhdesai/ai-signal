# An Anthropic mathematician posts a Claude-assisted, Lean-verified proof of the Riemann hypothesis

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge released a proof of the 1859 Riemann hypothesis, one of the Millennium Prize problems, written in the Lean proof language and developed with Claude, and independent checkers including MIT's Andrew Sutherland confirmed the code compiles. A compiling Lean file is not the same as community acceptance, a human-readable explanation is still to come, and the Clay Institute requires publication and two years of scrutiny before awarding its prize. The same day, Stanford's Ravi Vakil posted a separate, human-written proof of the generalized hypothesis that has not been formally checked. If the AI-assisted proof holds, machine verification will shift the debate from whether such results are credible to how humans can understand them.
