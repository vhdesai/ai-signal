# Amazon signs a $7.5 billion deal for Decart's optimization stack as startups bet on compute 'beyond Nvidia'

Amazon agreed to buy the technology behind Decart's Optimization Stack, software that makes training and inference more efficient across chip architectures including Trainium, and to hire its three co-founders and engineering team for about $7.5 billion in cash and stock paid in installments. Nvidia and Anthropic both pursued Decart earlier, and Anthropic walked away from a roughly $6 billion deal in September. Separately, Marlowe, from Convoy co-founder Dan Lewis and former Microsoft AI leader Kutta Srinivasan, raised a $277 million seed at a $1.15 billion valuation to run AI on infrastructure tuned to specific models rather than general-purpose GPU clusters. Software that narrows the gap with CUDA is now worth billions to Nvidia's rivals.
