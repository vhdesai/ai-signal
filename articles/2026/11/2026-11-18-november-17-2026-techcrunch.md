# [November 17, 2026] · [TechCrunch]

Google is rolling out Tasks for free, letting Gemini call businesses, book appointments, buy through Google Pay and read and send email, in answer to Meta's Muse, which passed 3 million weekly users within weeks, and OpenAI's Dots. Separately, Google will keep temporary and history-off Gemini chats for about two months instead of 72 hours, citing safety and security, while separating chat history from consent to AI training. Longer retention for assistants that act on users' behalf is likely to draw privacy scrutiny. Infrastructure CHIPS ANALYSIS
