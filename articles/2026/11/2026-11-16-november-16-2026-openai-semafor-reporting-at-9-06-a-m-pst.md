# November 16, 2026 • OpenAI; Semafor reporting at 9:06 a.m. PST

OpenAI announced 50,000 new grants, offered first come to eligible applicants, with applications opening November 23. Each grant provides free ChatGPT Plus for the recipient and up to two collaborators, while the wider program aims to reach 200,000 researchers rather than reporting that many current participants. The initiative could broaden academic distribution and adoption of AI tools, but access alone does not establish a measured productivity gain. Industry News ISO
