# [November 18, 2026] · [CNBC]

JPMorgan Chase and South Korea's Hyundai Card completed a proof of concept in which AI agents handled a simulated foreign-exchange transaction, and are targeting the first half of 2027 for a phase in which multiple agents negotiate and pay one another. Meanwhile, banks are warning customers that agents with access to their accounts may be treated as extensions of the user. Ally told customers they are responsible for any actions an AI takes, and Chase warned that authorized transactions may not be reversible if information is misused. Infrastructure POWER CLIMATE
