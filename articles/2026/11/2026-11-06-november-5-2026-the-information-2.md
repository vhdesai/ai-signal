# [November 5, 2026] · [The Information]

With Democrats winning Congress and governorships in states such as Texas, Ohio and Georgia, the Journal reports investors expect more roadblocks to data-center construction, a shift that could play into the hands of Big Tech. The Information's Applied AI notes executives fear delays will make existing servers pricier to run and could slow falling AI costs; Artificial Analysis says GPT-6.1 Sol matches GPT-6 Astra on high reasoning at about a fifth of the per-task cost. Texas Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa told CNN the state will adopt "laws to protect Texans" and stop working with developers until those protections exist. Many facilities due online next year already hold approvals, so the larger impact may fall on projects targeting 2028 and beyond.
