# AI power rush sends data centres hunting for used turbines and jet engines

Reuters reports that utilities and AI data-center developers are buying used turbines, reviving mothballed equipment and adapting jet engines as new-equipment deliveries stretch toward 2030–2031. Worldwide turbine orders exceed 100 GW against estimated annual manufacturing capacity of 60–70 GW, according to the report’s industry interviews. Second-hand equipment may accelerate deployment, but reliability, maintenance, permitting and grid access remain separate constraints. Verified source: Read the original Reuters report
