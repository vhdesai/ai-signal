# OpenAI and Le Figaro combine content discovery with enterprise adoption

Le Groupe Figaro has agreed to make current and archival journalism discoverable through ChatGPT, with responses linking readers back to the original publications. The agreement also includes plans to deploy ChatGPT Enterprise across teams and explore additional workflow applications, while the publisher retains editorial control. Financial terms were not disclosed, leaving the commercial value of the arrangement unclear.
