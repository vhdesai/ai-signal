# ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's agents complete purchases with tokenized cards as Gemini connects to Google Wallet

OpenAI's wallet lets its "dot" agents buy items using payment tokens from Visa, Mastercard and American Express, so neither the agent nor the merchant sees card numbers. Each purchase requires biometric approval, and card rewards and dispute rights are preserved. It is launching to a limited group of US ChatGPT Pro subscribers. OpenAI plans to add spending limits for pre-approved autonomous purchases and has published a Commerce Partner Playbook for retailers. The same day, Google let all US Gemini app users connect Google Wallet so the assistant can answer questions informed by their spending. Retailers such as Amazon that block third-party agents remain the main obstacle. OPENAI DATABRICKS ENTERPRISE
