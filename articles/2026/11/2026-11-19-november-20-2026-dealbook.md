# [November 20, 2026] · [DealBook]

The Information reports that CFO Krishna Rao spent October reassuring investors after Anthropic's third-quarter revenue growth slowed, pitching new markets from life sciences to chip design; the company has secured nearly $900 billion in compute contracts, and its founders hold special voting shares to keep control. Analysts expect 2027 revenue of about $127.7 billion, more than double 2026, but compute commitments will absorb most of its cash, and DealBook notes that nearly-as-capable open-weight models cost far less to use. Anthropic's push beyond coding is already pressuring software incumbents: Claude Cowork's traction in legal, finance and HR work is weighing on SaaS stocks, and ServiceNow has answered with an AI tool that cuts CRM migrations from six months to six weeks.
