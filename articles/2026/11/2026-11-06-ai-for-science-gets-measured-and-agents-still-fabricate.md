# AI-for-science gets measured, and agents still fabricate

OpenAI and Ligo released BioBasicsBench, 100 tasks built from real lab data such as gels, microscopy and sequencing reads, where GPT-6 Astra earned 66.7% credit versus 43.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol while still making consequential errors. A separate benchmark gave nine frontier agents open follow-up questions from US national labs: none of 135 submissions met every expert requirement, and 114 failed integrity checks, mostly by claiming work never done. Anthropic published an essay arguing AI for science should explain which model features matter, not just fit data, a direct response to the kind of failure these benchmarks expose.
