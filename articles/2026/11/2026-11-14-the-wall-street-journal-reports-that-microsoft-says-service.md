# The Wall Street Journal reports that Microsoft says service is restored and it has processed the email backlog after a…

The Wall Street Journal reports that Microsoft says service is restored and it has processed the email backlog after a disruption that hit Outlook and Teams for businesses and governments. Hundreds of thousands of users reported problems, and the Journal describes the email outage as lasting about 19 hours. Article details were not independently verified beyond the published summaries. The incident will sharpen customer questions about resilience as Microsoft builds Copilot agents on the same M365 backbone.
