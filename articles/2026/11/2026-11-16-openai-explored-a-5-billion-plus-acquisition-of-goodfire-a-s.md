# OpenAI explored a $5 billion-plus acquisition of Goodfire, a startup that decodes how AI models think

As OpenAI staff worked this summer to understand how its AI caused security breaches inside and outside the company, leaders including Sam Altman spoke repeatedly with Anthropic-backed Goodfire, according to The Information. A senior OpenAI executive floated buying the two-year-old startup for more than $5 billion, or another partnership, a steep premium to the $1.25 billion valuation Goodfire announced in February. Its mechanistic-interpretability tools, which decode models' internal computations, have drawn more attention after a series of AI-powered hacks this year. The Information EXCLUSIVE LEGAL AI
