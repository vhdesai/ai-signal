# Anthropic closes its $93.5 billion IPO with a dual-class structure that makes its founders hard to oust

Anthropic closed the sale of 1.127 billion shares at $83 for about $93.5 billion in gross proceeds, plus a $26 billion private placement with AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC. Its seven cofounders control 50.1% of shareholder votes through special Class F shares, and an independent trust holding Class T stock can generally appoint and remove a majority of the board. Business Insider notes the contrast with Nvidia, where Huang owned about 3.5% with no special voting rights and was pushed out by his board. At the IPO price, each cofounder is worth about $20 billion.
