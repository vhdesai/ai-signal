# Nvidia lets Korean chips plug into its rack systems and plans investments in four Korean startups

Nvidia, Korean chipmaker Rebellions, and SK Telecom announced that Rebellions will connect its next-generation R200 inference chips to Nvidia's platform using NVLink Fusion, Nvidia's high-speed chip interconnect. The systems will run in SK Telecom's AI data center and support Korea's "AI for All" sovereign AI project. Separately, Nvidia invested in interconnect startup Point2 and plans investments in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD, and Upstage. It will also help Korean model builders run inference on its Dynamo software. Opening NVLink to rival chips lets Nvidia stay part of national programs that want to use their own processors.
