# Frontier valuations reset: OpenAI banks $11 billion as Anthropic heads into a cooler IPO roadshow

Citing Bloomberg, TechCrunch reports OpenAI has closed the first $11 billion of a round valuing it at $1.25 trillion that could reach about $20 billion, well short of the $30 billion at $1.4 trillion it reportedly sought in September. The Information reports Anthropic will still begin formally marketing its IPO next week, even as some prospective buyers now put its value as low as $1.1 trillion against roughly $2 trillion expected a month ago. WSJ's Markets P.M. says Anthropic could raise less money or lower its target, and the price it sets will become the public benchmark for frontier labs.
