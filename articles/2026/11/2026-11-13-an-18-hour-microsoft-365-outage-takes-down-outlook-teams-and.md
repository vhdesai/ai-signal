# An 18-hour Microsoft 365 outage takes down Outlook, Teams and Copilot worldwide

A global outage tied to Exchange Online spread from Outlook and Teams to SharePoint, Copilot, Defender, Intune and some cloud-based Windows PCs, with Microsoft citing a large number of servers becoming unexpectedly unavailable. Microsoft said restored servers became unresponsive again under load, that it saw no evidence of malicious activity, and that it extended email timeouts to 48 hours to preserve delayed mail. Kentik reported traffic recovering after about 18 hours, while Richemont delayed its earnings release and a Colorado sheriff's office could not process bond payments. As AI assistants embed in productivity suites, their availability inherits the reliability of the underlying platform.
