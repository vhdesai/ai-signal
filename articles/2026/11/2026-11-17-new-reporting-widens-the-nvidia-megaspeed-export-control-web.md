# New reporting widens the Nvidia–Megaspeed export-control web, from Alibaba introductions to bank loans

Nvidia's sales team introduced Alibaba to Alice Huang twice in 2022, months before US export controls took effect, helping her company land Alibaba as a customer and secure bank loans to buy Nvidia chips, The Information reports. Former sales chief Jay Puri oversaw customers including Alice Huang and Supermicro, whose former executives were indicted in March over alleged chip diversion. The Journal reports that Alice Huang's husband built a data-center empire backed by the Chinese state, while JPMorgan and Citi lent $2.8 billion to Bridge Data Centres, an operator that did business with Megaspeed. Lenders to Southeast Asian data centers now face indirect export-control exposure through their borrowers' customers.
