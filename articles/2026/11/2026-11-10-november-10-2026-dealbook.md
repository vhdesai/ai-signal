# [November 10, 2026] · [DealBook]

Anthropic's amended filing offers 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78, raising up to about $64 billion and implying a fully diluted value of up to $1.404 trillion, with Nasdaq trading under ANT expected around November 19; the Wall Street Journal says the target is near $1.3 trillion, below Wall Street's early hopes and the $2 trillion floated in October. AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC will buy $26 billion of non-voting stock at the IPO price, with Google and Nvidia taking $10 billion each, while founders keep 50.1% of the vote. The filing shows an $81 billion revenue run rate, a $104 billion nine-month net loss and $900.9 billion of compute commitments. The Information reports outside counsel had to retract an erroneous prospectus sent to banks on Monday. TechCrunch | WSJ | CNBC | The Information
