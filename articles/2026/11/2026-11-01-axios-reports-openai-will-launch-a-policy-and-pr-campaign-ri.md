# Axios reports OpenAI will launch a policy and PR campaign right after Tuesday's midterms, timed to the lame-duck…

Axios reports OpenAI will launch a policy and PR campaign right after Tuesday's midterms, timed to the lame-duck session that opens Nov. 9. Proposals include mandatory safeguards, or speed limits, for the most powerful AI systems, community compacts to ease data center opposition, and rebranding data centers as AI compute campuses. The move positions OpenAI for a likely more adversarial Congress and an early 2028 presidential field.
