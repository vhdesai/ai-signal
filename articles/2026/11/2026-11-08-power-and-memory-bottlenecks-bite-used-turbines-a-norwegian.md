# Power and memory bottlenecks bite: used turbines, a Norwegian grid freeze and TSMC eyeing memory

Utilities and data-center developers are buying used turbines and adapting jet engines as new turbine deliveries stretch toward 2030–31, Reuters reports, with worldwide orders above 100 GW against 60–70 GW of annual manufacturing capacity. Norway's grid operator Statnett has paused new data-center connection reservations after receiving 26,000 MW of requests this year, more than the country's current capacity, until a licensing scheme that favors defense, health and other critical users is in place. On the silicon side, The Information reports that TSMC is weighing making advanced memory chips to capture more AI spending.
