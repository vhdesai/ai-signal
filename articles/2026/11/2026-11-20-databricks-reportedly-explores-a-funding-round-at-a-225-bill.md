# Databricks reportedly explores a funding round at a $225 billion valuation

Databricks is in early discussions to raise several billion dollars at a valuation of about $225 billion, Reuters reported, citing The Wall Street Journal. Its preceding financing round in August valued the company at $190 billion. The discussions are preliminary, so neither the financing nor its terms should be treated as finalized.
