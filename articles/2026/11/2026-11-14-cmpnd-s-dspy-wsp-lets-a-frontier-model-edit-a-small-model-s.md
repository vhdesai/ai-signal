# Cmpnd's dspy-wsp lets a frontier model edit a small model's weights directly, with no gradients

Cmpnd released dspy-wsp, an MIT-licensed DSPy optimizer in which a frontier LLM reads a small model's weights and edits them directly, with no gradients or training data. It builds on research in which GPT-6-Astra fixed a looping Qwen3.5-0.8B by changing 0.016% of its weights, lifting GSM8K from 13.8% to 67.1%. On decision models from 0.6B to 9B parameters, Cmpnd reports gains of 1.9 to 20.0 points for about $5 to $10 in API calls, though runs take 78 to 167 minutes and gains shrink on larger models.
