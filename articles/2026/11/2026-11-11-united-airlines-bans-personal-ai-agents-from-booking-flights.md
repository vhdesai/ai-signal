# United Airlines bans personal AI agents from booking flights as gatekeepers push back on consumer agents

United will block personal AI assistants such as Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots from booking or managing trips, both on its own channels and through travel agencies and aggregators. The airline cites exposure of account credentials, unintended purchases and possible conflicts with FAA and Transportation Department rules, such as the in-person hazardous-materials attestation at check-in. JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo and Amazon have taken similar steps, while Delta and American have adopted more permissive policies, and Meta shares fell 2% on the report.
