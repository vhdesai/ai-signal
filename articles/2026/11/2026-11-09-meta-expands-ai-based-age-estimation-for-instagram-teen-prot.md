# Meta expands AI-based age estimation for Instagram teen protections

Meta is expanding AI-based age estimation worldwide to move suspected underage Instagram users into Teen Account protections even when their registered birthday indicates adulthood. The rollout includes notices to US parents and use of Apple's age-range signal in Australia. The change extends automated enforcement of age-based safeguards, an area of continued legal and regulatory scrutiny.
