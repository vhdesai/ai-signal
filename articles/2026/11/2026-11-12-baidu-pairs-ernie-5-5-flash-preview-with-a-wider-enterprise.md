# Baidu pairs ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview with a wider enterprise AI stack

Baidu introduced ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview through Qianfan, describing a one-million-token context window and a mixture-of-experts architecture with 280 billion total parameters and 14 billion activated per token. The launch also includes Token Exchange for purchasing model services, an optical-interconnect Tianchi supernode, and confirmation that its M100 inference chip has shipped; model weights are promised by November 25, not available as part of this announcement. The strategic proposition is an integrated model, distribution, and infrastructure offering, with performance and efficiency assertions still requiring customer validation.
