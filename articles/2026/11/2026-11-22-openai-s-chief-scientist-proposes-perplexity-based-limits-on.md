# OpenAI's chief scientist proposes perplexity-based limits on AI development as Meta's Alexandr Wang warns superintelligence is arriving

In a post Sam Altman called "the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while," chief scientist Jakub Pachocki proposed perplexity, how well a model predicts the next token, as a shared yardstick under which labs would agree not to cross set thresholds until their ability to understand and monitor AI reaches concrete milestones. He notes that transformative cybersecurity and math capabilities appeared once losses fell below 0.8, but the post sets no thresholds and no other lab has signed on. Separately, Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang wrote that "superintelligence is arriving," said humans are unlikely to control AI capable of unconstrained self-improvement, and argued society should not accept a "handover" to AI.
