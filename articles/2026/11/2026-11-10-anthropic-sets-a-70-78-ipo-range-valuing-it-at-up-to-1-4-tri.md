# Anthropic sets a $70–$78 IPO range, valuing it at up to $1.4 trillion with a $26 billion strategic side deal

Anthropic's amended filing offers 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78, raising up to about $64 billion and implying a fully diluted value of up to $1.404 trillion, with Nasdaq trading under ANT expected around November 19; the WSJ reports a target near $1.3 trillion, below Wall Street's early expectations and the roughly $2 trillion floated in October. AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC will buy $26 billion of non-voting stock at the IPO price, with Google and Nvidia taking $10 billion each, while founders keep 50.1% of the vote. The filing shows a $104 billion nine-month net loss and $900.9 billion of compute commitments, and The Information reports outside counsel had to retract an erroneous prospectus sent to banks on Monday.
