# Daily AI News Digest – November 9, 2026

Governance at the center of the AI supply chain dominates the day: Nvidia's board opened an independent probe into reports that Jensen Huang was a foreign intelligence target, and the stock fell about 5% before Monday's open. Around it, the pressures are financial and physical: equity and GPU-debt markets are tiring, power and turbines are scarce, and Google and Nvidia are both repositioning their silicon. Robotics leads the research agenda as CoRL opens, while Washington signals openness to guardrails.
