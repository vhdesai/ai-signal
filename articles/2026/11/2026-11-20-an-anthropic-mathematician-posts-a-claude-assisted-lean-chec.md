# An Anthropic mathematician posts a Claude-assisted, Lean-checked proof of the Riemann hypothesis

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge released a proof of the 167-year-old Riemann hypothesis developed with Claude and written in the Lean proof language; independent checkers including MIT's Andrew Sutherland confirmed it compiles, and mathematicians told Scientific American it is "almost certainly true." A compiling Lean file is not the same as community acceptance: a human-readable explanation is still to come, and the Clay Mathematics Institute requires publication and two years of scrutiny before awarding its prize. The same day, AMS president Ravi Vakil posted an all-human proof of the generalized hypothesis by Stanford postdoc Alessandro Masullo, which has not yet been formally checked.
