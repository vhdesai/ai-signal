# VCs and founders expect Nvidia's startup funding to shrink under Kress, with OpenAI and Oracle most exposed

According to PitchBook, Nvidia has taken part in 105 funding rounds this year, often as the largest investor, in neoclouds, inference providers and open-weight labs such as CoreWeave, Crusoe, Mistral, Lambda and Reflection AI. Investors told TechCrunch that Huang made most of those decisions himself. They describe Kress as "much more conservative" and expect smaller checks and lower valuations, especially for European and open-source companies. Separately, Yahoo Finance, citing The Information, reports that as finance chief Kress pushed back on a proposed $250 billion credit backstop for OpenAI. That leaves Oracle, whose $664 billion backlog is roughly half OpenAI, as the company with the most at stake. Investors will look to Nvidia's November 17 earnings for its financing stance.
