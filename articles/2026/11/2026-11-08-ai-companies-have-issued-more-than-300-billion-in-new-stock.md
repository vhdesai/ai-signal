# AI companies have issued more than $300 billion in new stock this year, and about $100 billion more could follow

Tech giants, chipmakers, and AI cloud providers have sold or priced more than $300 billion of new stock and mandatory convertible securities so far in 2026. About $100 billion more could follow, mostly through programs that let companies sell shares gradually on the open market. That includes a proposed $60 billion program from Meta that could start in early 2027. Last week's volatility in AI stocks raises the question of whether investors will absorb that supply at today's prices, which directly affects how quickly data center plans can be funded.
