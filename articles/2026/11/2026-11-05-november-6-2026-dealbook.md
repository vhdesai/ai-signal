# [November 6, 2026] · [DealBook]

DealBook reports that OpenAI cut its fundraising target by at least a third and is now raising up to $20 billion in two parts at a $1.25 trillion valuation, down from hopes of up to $30 billion at as much as $1.5 trillion in September. An Emirati investor group that includes MGX is expected to be the largest investor, while SoftBank, which has put roughly $65 billion into OpenAI, and Coatue are each discussing only hundreds of millions this time. Separately, Greg Brockman told the Journal that OpenAI had a "rough" first half of the year before its coding business took off. FUNDING
