# [November 12, 2026] · [WSJ Pro CyberSecurity]

The Commerce Department is finalizing a rule, possibly to be announced this month, that would bar Chinese AI firms from remotely using advanced chips in overseas data centers and extend controls to seven more countries including Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand. Senators Elizabeth Warren and Chris Coons asked the administration to preserve its Nvidia-China records, the Journal reports Nvidia stopped breaking out Singapore revenue as scrutiny of Megaspeed rose, and WSJ Pro cites Reuters reporting that a PLA-linked university bought H200 chips in October despite sanctions. CIO Dive reports President Trump has signaled openness to AI guardrails after the midterm losses.
