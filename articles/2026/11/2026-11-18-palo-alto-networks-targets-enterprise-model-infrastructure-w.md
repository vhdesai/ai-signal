# Palo Alto Networks targets enterprise model infrastructure with acquisition

Palo Alto Networks agreed to acquire Applied Compute in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion, subject to adjustments. The company plans to combine Applied Compute’s open-weight-model post-training and inference platform with security capabilities for public-cloud and sovereign deployments; closing is expected in its fiscal third quarter of 2027, subject to approvals and other conditions. For enterprise buyers, the strategic signal is consolidation across model customization, operation and security—not immediate availability of an integrated product.
