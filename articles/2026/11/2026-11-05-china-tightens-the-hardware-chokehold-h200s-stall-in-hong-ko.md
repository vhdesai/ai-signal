# China tightens the hardware chokehold: H200s stall in Hong Kong as SoftBank's ABB deal is blocked

About 600,000 Nvidia H200 chips are sitting in Hong Kong because Beijing has not approved large-scale imports, though several Chinese internet companies have each received about 10,000. CNBC reports Kimi maker Moonshot AI discussed leasing Blackwell clusters of 8,000 or more GPUs from European neoclouds. Separately, China blocked SoftBank's $5.4 billion acquisition of ABB's robotics unit, a setback for its physical-AI strategy. Sources: Yahoo Finance | Unite.AI | Wall Street Journal
