# Cmpnd's MIT-licensed WeightSpace optimizer lets a frontier LLM read a small model's weights and edit them directly,…

Cmpnd's MIT-licensed WeightSpace optimizer lets a frontier LLM read a small model's weights and edit them directly, with no gradients or training data. It builds on MIT research in which GPT-6-Astra fixed a looping Qwen3.5-0.8B by changing 0.016% of its weights, lifting GSM8K from 13.8% to 67.1%. In Cmpnd's tests on 0.6B to 9B decision models, gains ranged from 1.9 to 20.0 points for about $5 to $10 of API calls, but runs took 78 to 167 minutes and gains were weakest on larger models. The technique is experimental, but it points to edits that ship as small, auditable weight patches.
