# Microsoft's Brad Smith warns of months of outage risk as AI speeds up the race to patch security flaws

Microsoft President Brad Smith said on CBS's Face the Nation that the rush to fix security flaws exposed by AI tools could cause more outages for months, and that Microsoft's recent outage was not caused by AI or a cyberattack.
