# [November 12, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

SpaceX outlined about $24 billion of supercomputing investment in Texas with on-site power and a proposed $50 billion campus in Arizona that still needs approvals, according to the Journal. SpaceX's AI group has laid off about 60 people from Grok Imagine, its image and video generator, leaving a handful to keep it running as the group prioritizes coding tools and its Grok Bot agent, The Information reports. The cuts echo OpenAI's shutdown of Sora and suggest compute-hungry consumer media products are losing out to agents, while Bloomberg reports a SpaceX-Tesla merger could be announced as soon as December.
