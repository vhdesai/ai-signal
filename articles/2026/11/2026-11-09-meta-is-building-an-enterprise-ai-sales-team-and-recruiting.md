# Meta is building an enterprise AI sales team and recruiting consulting partners

Meta has begun forming a sales organization to sell its AI products to small and midsize businesses, according to a person familiar with the matter and job postings reviewed by The Information. It is also building relationships with systems integrators, including Accenture, Deloitte, and IBM, to help sell and deploy its products to large companies. This is Meta's clearest move yet to turn its model investment into enterprise revenue, putting it in direct competition with Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic for corporate customers. Enterprise buyers should expect Meta offerings to show up through their existing consulting relationships. CAPITAL MARKETS AI INFRASTRUCTURE
