# MIT method guarantees AI models of chaotic systems stay physically bounded

Researchers at MIT's Laboratory for Information and Decision Systems published a method in PNAS that adds physics-based limits to machine-learning models of chaotic systems such as weather, climate and turbulence. The limits keep long simulations from running off into physically impossible values. The team says it is the first formal guarantee of bounded behavior for machine-learning models of chaos, and it is releasing the necessary information freely. The method addresses a key obstacle to using AI emulators in place of traditional simulations in weather forecasting, aerospace and energy. AI Safety & Policy POLICY OPEN WEIGHTS
