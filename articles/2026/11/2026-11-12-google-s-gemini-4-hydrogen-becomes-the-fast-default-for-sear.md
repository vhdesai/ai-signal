# Google's Gemini 4 Hydrogen becomes the fast default for Search and agent work

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen immediately rather than in stages, replacing Gemini 3.8 Flash across its products including Search, with a one-million-token context window and pricing of $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. Google says it runs 2.5 times faster than 3.8 Flash, makes more than five times fewer fabricated claims and more than triples Flash's Terminal-Bench score, although these figures are self-reported. A new Orchestrator in Antigravity lets Carbon plan while Hydrogen executes, reaching near-Carbon success on Terminal-Bench while using 24% less quota.
