# Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; shares surge about 30%

Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Huang as CEO effective immediately. It said it "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and is confident his return "poses no national security risk." Shares jumped 29–30%, recovering nearly all of the roughly 33% drop since Huang disclosed the affair on Nov. 8. President Trump called it "the correct and proper decision" and said the Attorney General has no evidence of unlawful conduct, while urging Nvidia to continue its internal export-control review. The Wachtell Lipton investigation will proceed to conclusion, so governance questions remain open even though Nvidia's leadership risk has largely been removed. M&A SECURITY
