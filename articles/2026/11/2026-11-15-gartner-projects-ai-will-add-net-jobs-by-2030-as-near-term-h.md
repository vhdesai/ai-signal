# Gartner projects AI will add net jobs by 2030 as near-term hiring freezes bite

Gartner projects AI will create 10.6 million jobs and eliminate 8.9 million globally between 2025 and 2030, for a net gain of about 1.7 million. It expects more than 20% of workers (435.5 million) to need to adapt how they work. Gartner's John Lovelock describes a sequence in which early AI adoption pauses hiring, deeper use cuts roles, and mature use adds jobs. Challenger, Gray & Christmas counts more than 100,000 AI-attributed US job-cut announcements this year through October.
