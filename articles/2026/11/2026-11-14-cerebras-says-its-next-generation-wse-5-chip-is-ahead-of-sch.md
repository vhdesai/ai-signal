# Cerebras says its next-generation WSE-5 chip is ahead of schedule, with CS-5 systems due in early summer 2027

CEO Andrew Feldman said the WSE-5 chip, taped out June 15 on TSMC's 5nm process, is already running models in chassis testing. He said the CS-5 will be more than twice as fast as the CS-4, which is about to enter full production. Cerebras ended the third quarter with $7.36 billion in cash and a $26.1 billion revenue backlog, mostly from OpenAI. The new AWS, Oracle and Microsoft agreements are not yet in that backlog. Hardware sales fell 28.8% and hardware gross margin was only 11% as the factory ramps and older, discounted contracts are delivered, so execution in 2027 matters more than demand.
