# OpenAI's revenue rebounds 50% to about $10 billion as losses deepen ahead of a $1.25 trillion raise

OpenAI told investors that third-quarter revenue jumped about 50% to roughly $10 billion, a sharp reacceleration from 17% growth in the prior quarter, driven by newer models and enterprise products. The disclosure came as it seeks to close a funding round at a $1.25 trillion valuation, and the Journal notes that losses continue to mount.
