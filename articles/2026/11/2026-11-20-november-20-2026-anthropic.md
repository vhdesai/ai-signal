# [November 20, 2026] · [Anthropic]

Anthropic closed the sale of 1.127 billion shares at $83, including the underwriters' full 147-million-share option, for about $93.5 billion in gross proceeds ($91.6 billion to the company), plus a $26 billion private placement with AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC. Business Insider notes that its seven cofounders control 50.1% of shareholder votes through special Class F shares, while an independent trust holding Class T stock can generally appoint and remove a majority of the board. That is a sharp contrast with Nvidia, where Huang held about 3.5% with no special voting rights and was pushed out by his board; at the IPO price, each Anthropic cofounder is worth about $20 billion.
