# NetApp closed its acquisition of PEAK:AIO, adding metadata services and parallel file system technology to its NetApp…

NetApp closed its acquisition of PEAK:AIO, adding metadata services and parallel file system technology to its NetApp Novus high-performance storage line. NetApp is targeting zettabyte-scale file systems for AI clouds and GPU-as-a-service providers, where keeping GPUs continuously fed with data drives infrastructure economics. Storage vendors are consolidating to compete for neocloud and AI factory build-outs.
