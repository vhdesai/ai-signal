# November 9, 2026 · Wall Street Journal (WSJ Politics)

Nvidia said independent directors and outside counsel will investigate reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, after he told employees he had been unfaithful with a former Megaspeed executive whom US officials suspect of working for China's Ministry of State Security. The board says it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, Megaspeed no longer appears as a preferred cloud partner, and shares fell about 4.5% before Monday's open, roughly $190 billion of value. DealBook frames the strategic question: Huang has no clear successor, owns only about 3.5% of the company with no supervoting stock, and runs a company that acts as the de facto central bank of AI through its stakes in and loans to OpenAI, Anthropic and the neoclouds. Republican leaders of three House committees want a closed-door briefing, and WSJ notes the White House faces questions after awarding Huang the National Medal of Science a month ago.
