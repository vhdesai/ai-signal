# Data-center backlash reprices AI infrastructure: neoclouds sell off while incumbents gain

Voters sided against data centers in 40 of 41 local ballot measures, the lone exception being Harwood, North Dakota. Neoclouds took the hit, with CoreWeave down nearly 10% and its $104 billion contract backlog facing timing risk, while Microsoft, Amazon, Apple and Meta added a combined $750 billion as investors bet existing capacity becomes scarcer and capex can be trimmed without penalty. One developer told DealBook to expect moratoriums from January and six to nine months of delays, and South Korea's Kospi fell more than 5% on chip-demand worries. Business Insider's satellite analysis ties part of Microsoft's $5 billion capex shortfall to delays at IREN and Nebius-linked sites.
