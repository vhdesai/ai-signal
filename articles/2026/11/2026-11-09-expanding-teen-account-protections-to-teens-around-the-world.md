# Expanding Teen Account Protections to Teens Around the World

Meta announced global expansion of AI age estimation on Instagram to place suspected teenagers into Teen Account protections, including users who supplied adult birthdays. It says Facebook rollout continues and similar Threads technology is planned later this year; in Australia, Apple’s optional age-range signal is another input.
