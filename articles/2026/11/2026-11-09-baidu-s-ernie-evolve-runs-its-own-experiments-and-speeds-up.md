# Baidu's ERNIE Evolve runs its own experiments and speeds up production inference

ERNIE Evolve gives agents a code repository, a goal and success criteria, then keeps only changes that pass held-out checks it cannot see and a separate reviewer designed to catch benchmark gaming. Baidu reports lifting throughput of the in-development ERNIE-5.5-Flash by 33.7% on GPUs in about three days and beating the public NanoGPT Speedrun record. The results are self-reported and the system is unreleased, but it adds to evidence that automated research loops are already compounding inside labs. The Next Web Academic Research RESEARCH
