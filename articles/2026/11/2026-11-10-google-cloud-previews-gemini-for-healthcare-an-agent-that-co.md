# Google Cloud previews Gemini for healthcare, an agent that coordinates work across health records, claims, and payer systems

The new product extends the Gemini agent Google introduced last month. It is aimed at hospitals and health insurers, whose patient records, coverage rules, and clinical evidence are spread across separate systems. For providers, it compiles patient histories, prepares charts, and assembles prior-authorization packets checked against insurer policy. For insurers, it checks requests against medical policy and explains benefits to members. Each agent has its own identity and inherits the user's permissions, and the product is built to support HIPAA compliance. It is available now in preview. ADOBE MCP CREATIVE TOOLS
