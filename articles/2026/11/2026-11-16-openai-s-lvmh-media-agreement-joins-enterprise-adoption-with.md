# OpenAI’s LVMH media agreement joins enterprise adoption with content access

Source: OpenAI; Les Echos LVMH’s media division will deploy ChatGPT Enterprise and other OpenAI tools across its publications, including Les Echos, Le Parisien and Paris Match. The agreement also enables ChatGPT to provide attributed summaries and excerpts with links to the publishers’ reporting. Les Echos describes the arrangement as non-exclusive, an important distinction for assessing how publisher partnerships affect distribution and competitive access.
