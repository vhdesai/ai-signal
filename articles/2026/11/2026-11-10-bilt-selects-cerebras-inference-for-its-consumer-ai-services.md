# Bilt selects Cerebras inference for its consumer AI services

Cerebras says Bilt selected its inference infrastructure for NeighborhoodOS and Bilt Concierge, services within an ecosystem the announcement describes as serving more than eight million members and 50,000 merchants. The release discloses neither contract value nor measured performance figures, so the selection should not be read as independently demonstrated speed or scale.
