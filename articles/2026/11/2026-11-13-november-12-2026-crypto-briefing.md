# [November 12, 2026] · [Crypto Briefing]

In its semiannual Financial Stability Report, the Federal Reserve said 87% of surveyed market contacts named AI as a salient risk, up from 50% in May, and that debt tied to advanced computing reached $680 billion by October, with pending AI infrastructure debt, guarantees and leases that could exceed $3 trillion. Four nonprofits including Public Citizen sued OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta and SpaceX's AI unit in San Francisco seeking to halt development of models more capable than today's frontier systems; an injunction is unlikely, but discovery could expose internal safety assessments. WSJ Pro CyberSecurity reports Democrats taking control of Congress in January rank AI security, threat-intelligence sharing and rebuilding CISA high on their agenda.
