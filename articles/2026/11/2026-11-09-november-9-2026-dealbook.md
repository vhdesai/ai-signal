# [November 9, 2026] · [DealBook]

Nvidia's board said Sunday that independent directors and outside counsel will investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, after Huang apologized to staff for an affair with a former Megaspeed executive whom US officials suspect of working for China's Ministry of State Security. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised, and Megaspeed no longer appears as a preferred Nvidia cloud partner. Shares fell about 5% before Monday's open, and Republican leaders of three House committees have asked Huang to a closed-door briefing. DealBook frames the real question as how indispensable Huang is to a company that acts as the de facto central bank of AI, with no clear successor and Anthropic's IPO in the balance.
