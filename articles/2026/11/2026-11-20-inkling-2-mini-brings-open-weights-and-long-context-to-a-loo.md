# Inkling 2 Mini brings open weights and long context to a looped architecture

24–48h exception — exact publication time unavailable. Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an Apache 2.0 open-weight multimodal looped transformer with 26.4 billion total parameters, 3.4 billion active parameters and support for up to one million tokens of context. Its approximately 20 GB checkpoint supports Apple silicon systems with at least 32 GB of memory, and the model is also available through Tinker. Performance claims are developer-reported; the model card warns of factual errors, repetitive reasoning and destructive agent actions.
