# Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, a 26B looped MoE that runs on a 32GB Mac

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini under Apache 2.0. It is a 26.4B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 3.4B active parameters that reruns its middle layers three times per token, which keeps the checkpoint near 20GB. The company reports, without independent verification, 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified, 86.6% on GPQA Diamond and a 1M-token context. It scores weaker on knowledge recall (15.2% on HLE-Diamond). Reusing layers instead of adding parameters gives near-frontier coding performance that runs locally, a credible US open-weight alternative to Qwen. OPEN WEIGHTS RESEARCH MODEL
