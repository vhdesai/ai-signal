# OpenAI plans post-midterm policy push, including rebranding data centers as "AI compute campuses"

Citing Axios, TechCrunch reports that OpenAI global affairs chief Chris Lehane is planning a policy campaign for after the election. He called the midterms "a bit of a movie trailer" for 2028, when he expects AI to be the central issue. Ideas include AI "speed limits," US leadership on global standards, and new branding for data centers. Lehane called the debate over data centers "good and healthy."
