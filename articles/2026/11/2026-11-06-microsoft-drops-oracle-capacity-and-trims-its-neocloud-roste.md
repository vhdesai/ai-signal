# Microsoft drops Oracle capacity and trims its neocloud roster as GPU futures stall again

Microsoft declined to renew its 2023 arrangement to rent Oracle cloud servers, which used older Nvidia hardware, and is narrowing a neocloud roster that includes CoreWeave, Nebius, IREN, Lambda and Nscale to focus on a few suppliers and its own data centers. The move lands days after Oracle launched a $20 billion stock-and-bond raise for AI capacity, leaving it more dependent on a handful of large AI customers. Separately, CME agreed at the CFTC's request to delay Nvidia GPU rental-price futures another 60 days, so neoclouds and their lenders will have no listed hedge for compute prices until at least January.
