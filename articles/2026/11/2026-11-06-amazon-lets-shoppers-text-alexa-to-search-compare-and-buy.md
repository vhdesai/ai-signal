# Amazon lets shoppers text Alexa to search, compare and buy

Selected US customers can now text Alexa to search for products, compare items, track prices, manage lists, and complete purchases, with wider rollout in the coming weeks. Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent from querying its site in September, and DoorDash has said third-party agents have placed only a few hundred orders on its platform. Large marketplaces are choosing to run their own text-message agents rather than let outside agents shop on their sites, which limits what general-purpose agents can do in commerce.
