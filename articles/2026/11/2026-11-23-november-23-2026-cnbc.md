# [November 23, 2026] · [CNBC]

Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, saying it was satisfied with his candor and ability to lead and that it had heard from senior levels of the US government that his return poses no national-security risk. The reversal came six days after chair Harvey Jones ruled out a return, following pressure from President Trump, employees, investors and Elon Musk, and after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent softened his opposition on Sunday; Jones stays chair and Colette Kress remains CFO. Nvidia shares jumped about 29% after having fallen 48% from their early-October high to as low as $125 on Friday, a loss of about $2.4 trillion in market value. The board's investigation into Huang's conduct and the company's China sales continues.
