# Model-weight analysis raises questions about Nvidia export controls

TechCrunch A single researcher’s analysis is not proof of an export-control violation. TechCrunch reports that Will DePue examined model weights from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group and identified what he describes as Blackwell training fingerprints in models including Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4. The claimed signatures do not establish that the models were trained entirely on Nvidia hardware or that any export law was violated.
