# Replit makes Fabric and Teams integrations generally available

Replit announced general availability for Microsoft Fabric integration, Local Work on Windows and Microsoft Teams integration, alongside early access to publishing apps into customers’ Azure tenants. The Fabric integration is designed to preserve existing per-user access controls, while Teams connects app-building and investigations to workplace conversations. Azure publishing does not yet mean Azure-hosted development: development remains on Replit, with full bring-your-own-cloud described as a future step. Date verification: Original announcement dated November 18; publication time is not displayed. Retained as an important possible 24–48-hour exception because of its enterprise deployment and governance implications.
