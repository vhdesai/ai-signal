# Drogue raises $20 million to put data centers on ships powered by ocean heat

The six-month-old startup in Washington, D.C., plans to use ocean thermal energy conversion, which generates power from the temperature difference between warm surface water and cold deep water. Its first multi-megawatt prototype will go on an oceangoing ship in early 2028. Investors include XYZ Venture Capital, Toyota Ventures and Slow Ventures. Its founder argues that grid connections, which take 8-10 years, cannot keep pace with AI demand. The technology is unproven at this scale, but the round shows capital moving toward off-grid power for AI.
