# MIT's CheMeleon predicts molecular properties when lab data is scarce

MIT chemical engineers, working with BASF researchers, describe CheMeleon, an open-source model pretrained on about 1,600 computed descriptors for a million molecules and then fine-tuned for specific properties where little experimental data exists. Nine of the top 20 entries in this year's OpenADMET prediction contest used it, and at least one pharmaceutical company has made it its default starting point. The underlying paper appeared earlier in the Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling; MIT's report is new.
