# Trump signals openness to AI guardrails without naming a legislative plan

In a CBS interview, President Trump said AI risks can be managed and indicated openness to working with Democrats on safeguards. He also argued that regulation should not put the United States behind China. No specific bill or new regulatory requirement was announced, so the comments signal political positioning rather than an enacted policy change.
