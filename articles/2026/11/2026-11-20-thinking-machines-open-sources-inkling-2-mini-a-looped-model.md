# Thinking Machines open-sources Inkling 2 Mini, a looped model that runs on a laptop

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini under Apache 2.0, a 26.4-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 3.4 billion active parameters that runs its middle layers three times per token, keeping the checkpoint near 20GB with up to 1 million tokens of context. The company says it leads open-weight models under 100 billion parameters on agentic-coding benchmarks, and MarkTechPost reports a self-reported 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified, though knowledge recall is weaker. Reusing layers rather than adding parameters offers near-frontier coding that runs locally, and a credible US open-weight alternative to Qwen.
