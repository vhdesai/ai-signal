# Google energy executive says data centers in space could cost up to 100 times more than on Earth

Amanda Peterson Corio, Google's head of data center energy, said at The Information's WTF Summit: "Let's explore all the terrestrial options before we go extraterrestrial." Her comments come days after Google's Suncatcher TPU satellite came online. They are a useful counterweight to the orbital data center plans behind part of SpaceX's roughly $1.8 trillion valuation.
