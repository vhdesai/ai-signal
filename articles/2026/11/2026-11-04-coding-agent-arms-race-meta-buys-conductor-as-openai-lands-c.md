# Coding-agent arms race: Meta buys Conductor as OpenAI lands Claude Code's product chief

Meta acquired Conductor, whose platform runs teams of coding agents in parallel workspaces on Anthropic and OpenAI models; founder Charlie Holtz joins Meta Superintelligence Labs and Conductor's cloud service shuts down November 20. Separately, OpenAI confirmed that Cat Wu, who led product for Claude Code until last week, will run product management for its core products. The contested layer is now orchestration of many agents rather than single-agent assistance, and both capital and talent are moving toward it. Sources: TechCrunch | Business Insider
