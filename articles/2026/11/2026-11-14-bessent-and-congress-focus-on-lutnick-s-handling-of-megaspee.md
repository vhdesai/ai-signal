# Bessent and Congress focus on Lutnick's handling of Megaspeed

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Treasury, War, USTR and State had pushed "for months" to add Megaspeed to the entity list, but that "Commerce BIS has the pen." That puts responsibility on Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. Lutnick has agreed to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on Dec. 10, and House Intelligence Democrats want him before their committee too. The Financial Times separately reported that lawmakers are moving to tighten AI-chip export rules. Expect stricter enforcement of where chips end up, which would affect Nvidia's and its rivals' sales in Southeast Asia. INVESTIGATION CHINA
