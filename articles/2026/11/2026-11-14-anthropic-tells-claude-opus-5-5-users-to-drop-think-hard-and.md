# Anthropic tells Claude Opus 5.5 users to drop 'think hard' and other outdated prompting habits

Anthropic published guidance for Claude Opus 5.5, now the default on its Pro, Max, Ultra and Team plans, saying habits carried over from older models hold it back. Phrases like think hard are unnecessary because the model scales its own reasoning, and removing them made responses start faster with no measurable quality loss in Anthropic's tests. It recommends starting at the default Medium effort and describing the full goal, output format and check-in points in one message instead of step-by-step instructions. The Decoder AUTONOMY
