# MIT MIT puts human judgment inside AI-assisted structural design

MIT researchers developed a human-in-the-loop topology-optimization system whose machine-learning model flags regions a designer is likely to want to change while a structure is being generated. In a Communications Engineering paper, the team reports a 39% improvement in linear buckling load and better manufacturability for about 15 seconds of additional design time. The results are promising evidence for human-guided engineering workflows, not proof of production readiness.
