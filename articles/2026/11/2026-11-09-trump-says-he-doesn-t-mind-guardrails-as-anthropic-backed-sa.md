# Trump says he doesn't mind guardrails as Anthropic-backed safety groups map a post-midterm agenda

On 60 Minutes, President Trump said AI risks can be managed and signaled openness to working with Democrats on safeguards, while warning that the US must not fall behind China. He also said his administration once told the unnamed leader of one of the big AI companies to get ready to go to jail over alleged misuse of AI. A memo from Public First Action, which receives significant Anthropic funding, sets 2027 goals including federal AI laws that do not preempt state rules and says $30 million on hand will go toward making AI a top-tier issue in 2028 races.
