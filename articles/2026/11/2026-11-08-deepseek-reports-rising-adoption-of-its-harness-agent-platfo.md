# DeepSeek reports rising adoption of its Harness agent platform

Bloomberg reports that DeepSeek said its open-source Harness agent application reached 1.54 million daily active users by November 4, following its August launch. The report also cites roughly 92 trillion tokens consumed daily and plans for a plug-in marketplace. The adoption and usage figures are reported claims, not independently audited measures, but point to the infrastructure demands of agent applications.
