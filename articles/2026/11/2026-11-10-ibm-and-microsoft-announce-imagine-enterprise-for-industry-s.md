# IBM and Microsoft announce Imagine Enterprise for industry-specific agents

IBM and Microsoft announced Imagine Enterprise, a platform for building, running and governing industry-specific AI agents, with an emphasis on regulated industries. The companies describe more than 6,000 IBM Consulting prebuilt agents and assets combined with Microsoft cloud, data, identity and security capabilities, joint engineering for qualifying customers, and SEI as an early example. These are company-stated capabilities; buyers should validate integration effort, governance controls and production results in their own environment.
