# Agents start spending money and making calls as Amazon plans a product graph to blunt them

Meta's Muse, with more than 10 million users, can now phone US businesses for paid subscribers starting at $20 a month, and Replit Agent can buy supplies through Stripe's Link wallet and Visa and run Google Ads campaigns, pausing at checkout for owner approval. An internal Amazon memo reviewed by Reuters describes a World Product Graph that would collect data on virtually every product for sale so that AI shopping agents search Amazon first, with an aspirational launch in October 2027. The Journal separately examines who will shoulder the compute costs as Big Tech pushes everyone toward agents.
