# November 9, 2026 · Semafor

On 60 Minutes, President Trump said he did not mind AI guardrails but that the US cannot accept "a deep second position" to China, and said his administration once told the leader of a major AI company to get ready to go to jail over alleged misuse of AI; DealBook notes he also played down voters' AI concerns. A memo from Public First Action, which receives significant Anthropic funding, targets federal AI laws that do not preempt state rules and says $30 million on hand will make AI a top-tier 2028 issue. AP reports staff at the federal AI safety center, funded at about $10 million a year, now report to the White House, raising questions about the independence of model testing. Sources - Reuters: Trump says AI risks can be managed, signals openness to guardrails - Business Insider: Trump tells 60 Minutes his team threatened an AI leader with jail - Semafor: AI safety groups outline their plans for a new Washington - The Boston Globe (AP): Staff at US center for AI safety reporting to White House
