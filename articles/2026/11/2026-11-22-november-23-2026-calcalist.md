# [November 23, 2026] · [Calcalist]

Amazon signed a roughly $7.5 billion deal, paid in cash and stock over installments, for Israeli startup Decart's Optimization Stack technology, with co-founders Dean Leitersdorf, Moshe Shalev and Orian Leitersdorf joining along with their team. Decart's software helps AI models run more efficiently on chips, and it was an early customer of AWS's Trainium3. Calcalist reports that Nvidia had also sought to buy Decart and that Anthropic nearly struck a deal worth about $6 billion before withdrawing in early September.
