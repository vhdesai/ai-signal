# Nvidia's sharp selloff adds pressure to the AI trade

Nvidia fell 14.01% to close at $125.53 on Friday, according to The Motley Fool's market report. The coverage linked the decline to the broader AI selloff and reports of an SEC accounting investigation, despite strong earnings earlier in the week. The investigation was reported before this digest's window; Friday's market reaction is the new development. ANTRENDING
