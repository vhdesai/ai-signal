# Bland's Tofu-1 handles audio in and audio out in one model, without a transcript step, and can be interrupted…

Bland's Tofu-1 handles audio in and audio out in one model, without a transcript step, and can be interrupted mid-sentence while it works on a task. Bland targets noisy calls in banking, healthcare, insurance and government, and lists speaker tracking, noise separation and detection of hesitation or frustration. It has not disclosed model size, latency, pricing or benchmarks, and its only figure, that over 92% of screened listeners could not identify it as AI, is self-reported. Access is a closed enterprise research preview, with broader availability cited for January.
