# Microsoft AI's Suleyman says models now help improve their successors, and he's "really, really nervous"

In an interview with Deirdre Bosa recorded at Ignite, Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman said models inside Microsoft are increasingly "fully involved" in improving their successors by checking for bugs, producing training data and building evaluations. He called recursive self-improvement "definitely a dangerous capability," described the past three months as "a total watershed moment" and said industry warnings are not alarmist. His prescription is to prove models safe step by step before they reach production, with engineers still in the loop.
