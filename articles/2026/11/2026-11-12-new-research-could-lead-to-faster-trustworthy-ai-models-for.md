# New research could lead to faster, trustworthy AI models for climate prediction

MIT researchers optimized synthetic emissions scenarios to train climate emulators with less expensive simulation data. Their deliberately irregular training scenarios improved generalization to unfamiliar emissions combinations in the reported tests. Validation using an emissions scenario generated by a full Earth-system model remains future work, so the results should not be read as established performance across operational climate-model pipelines.
