# Databricks launches a weekly index of enterprise AI usage and token economics

Databricks introduced a public weekly AI Index combining anonymized Unity Gateway usage from more than 10,000 companies with coding-agent evaluations using private codebases and hidden tests. Within this platform cohort, single-provider reliance moved from approximately 64% in July to 28% on November 2, while open-weight token share moved from 7.3% to 30.9%. These are observational cohort figures, not market-share estimates; costs use estimated list prices, and coding results depend on the harness and tasks.
