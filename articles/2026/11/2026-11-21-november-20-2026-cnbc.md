# [November 20, 2026] · [CNBC]

The Nasdaq Composite fell 5.3% on Friday, capping its worst week in more than a year with more than $4 trillion in losses and turning negative for 2026, while the S&P 500 slid into correction territory. Anthropic fell 8.5% on its second trading day and dipped as low as $83.05, a nickel above its IPO price, after Thursday's 17% gain left it valued near $1.8 trillion on a fully diluted basis. Chip, memory and power names led the rout, with the PHLX Semiconductor index down almost 11%, and sliding copper prices hinted that investors expect the AI buildout to slow.
