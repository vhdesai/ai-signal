# NVIDIA Newsroom · November 16, 2026 · 08:00 PST

NVIDIA described a scientific-computing configuration connecting 192 Vera CPUs through Quantum-X800 InfiniBand, aimed at workloads including weather, climate and molecular simulation as well as agentic science. Dell, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro racks are expected in early 2027, so the announcement should not be read as immediate hardware availability. NVIDIA’s HPC SDK 26.11 is available now, making the software update the near-term development for teams evaluating the platform. OAI
