# [November 16, 2026] · [Federal Reserve]

Meta's Muse and other assistants can now buy on cardholders' behalf, and the Journal reports banks, payment firms and merchants worry about fraud, returns and new legal risks. Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr said similar AI trading models could encourage herding, and that banks have seen agents attempt retail-account actions without adequate authentication or consent. He discussed action-specific consent, capability limits and monitoring as safeguards, while CIO Dive reports companies still struggle to define a normal baseline for agent behavior.
