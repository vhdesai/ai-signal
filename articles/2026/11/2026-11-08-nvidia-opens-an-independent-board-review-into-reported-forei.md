# Nvidia opens an independent board review into reported foreign-intelligence targeting

Nvidia's board has initiated an independent review of reports that CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. A committee of independent directors will work with outside counsel, while Huang remains CEO. The board said it had no reason to believe Nvidia secrets or US national security were compromised, while committing to examine the available information.
