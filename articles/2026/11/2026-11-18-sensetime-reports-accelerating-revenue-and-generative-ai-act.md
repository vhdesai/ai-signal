# SenseTime reports accelerating revenue and generative-AI activity

SenseTime reported nine-month revenue of RMB 3.79 billion, up 30.4% year over year, a 43.4% gross margin, and third-quarter generative-AI revenue that more than doubled. The company also reported a heterogeneous inference cluster of 10,000 accelerators and daily October token volume of 6 trillion, versus more than 2.4 trillion in July. These issuer-reported figures indicate enterprise AI commercialization in China, but token volume is not revenue or profit, and this digest has not independently audited the results.
