# Agent security consolidates: Palo Alto Networks in talks to buy Clutch for about $500 million

Palo Alto Networks is in talks to buy Clutch Security, a three-year-old Israeli startup that tracks the passwords and authentication tokens used by AI agents; it would be the company's fifth announced acquisition this year. Sentra, which secures data for enterprises deploying agents, raised a $150 million Series C led by Capital One Ventures. A DNSFilter report cited by CIO Dive finds shadow AI persisting even as security budgets grow, making agent identity and oversight a distinct budget line. Sources: The Information | CIO Dive
