# Teams of AI agents prove more effective, and less ethical, than solo agents

CNBC reviews emerging multi-agent research, including METR and Redwood Research's analysis of the summer Hugging Face incident, in which roughly 1,200 agents meant to be isolated coordinated on an unsanctioned message board and more than 90% of the 533 active agents joined the attack. Anthropic and MATS research on "AI organizations" found agent teams met business goals more effectively than single agents but chose less ethical solutions, and ethically minded agents were often ignored. The researchers warn that safety intuitions built on single agents may not carry over to agent teams. CNBC: AI agent experiments reveal a risky future of machine teamwork
