# Zoox robotaxis become available through Uber in Las Vegas

Zoox robotaxis are now being matched with eligible Uber trips in Las Vegas, turning the companies’ previously announced partnership into a live distribution channel. Riders cannot initially select a Zoox vehicle explicitly, although autonomous-ride preferences can increase the chance of a match. The initial fleet is limited, so the launch should not be read as a large-scale deployment.
