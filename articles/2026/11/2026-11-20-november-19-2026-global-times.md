# [November 19, 2026] · [Global Times]

Tighter U.S. limits on access to advanced AI computing, including the new rule closing the overseas data-center loophole, are hitting Chinese tech stocks and plans: Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu said the rule "will affect next year's products and services," and Tencent says inference can shift to domestic chips while training of next-generation models will slow. Alibaba said its next-generation Qwen model is training on in-house chips and laid out a three-generation T-Head GPU roadmap. DealBook highlights a deeper money gap: China's tech giants are expected to double capex to $139 billion this year while AI-lab revenue covers less than 8% of it, per Rhodium Group, and DeepSeek's Liang Wenfeng reportedly told investors, "We simply can't afford to train the largest models available right now."
