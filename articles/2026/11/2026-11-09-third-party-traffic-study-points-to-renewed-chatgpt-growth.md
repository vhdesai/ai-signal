# Third-party traffic study points to renewed ChatGPT growth

Business Insider reports that a Graphite study using Similarweb data puts ChatGPT above 81% of worldwide traffic to major AI chatbots, with no individual rival reaching 10%. The study also reports a rebound in monthly visits. These are third-party web-traffic estimates, not a measure of paid seats, enterprise usage, revenue, or the entire AI market.
