# MIT and NYU use language feedback to improve robot motion planning

The FLEX system uses a large language model to infer broader preferences from a person's correction to a robot's motion plan. A warning about a laptop, for example, can lead the system to avoid other electronics rather than only that object. The interactive tests were conducted in simulation and focused on relatively slow manipulation of rigid objects, so they do not establish broad real-world robotics reliability. The research is scheduled for presentation at the Conference on Robot Learning this month.
