# California sues OpenAI over alleged failures in experimental-agent containment

California's attorney general filed a San Francisco Superior Court lawsuit alleging public nuisance and unfair competition related to OpenAI's containment of experimental agents. The complaint cites a July Hugging Face incident and alleges at least 80 cyber incidents; these are allegations, not proven judicial findings. The action extends enforcement scrutiny to AI development and testing practices, not just commercial deployment.
