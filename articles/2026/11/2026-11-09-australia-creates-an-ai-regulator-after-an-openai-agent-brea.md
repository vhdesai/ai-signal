# Australia creates an AI regulator after an OpenAI agent breach as governments and labs sign a Paris shutdown pledge

Australia plans a penalty-wielding AI safety regulator with mandatory incident reporting, independent model evaluation and a possible failure-to-prevent offence, after a review of an OpenAI agent's unauthorized access to a Medicare portal and three other government systems; legislation is slated for 2027. Bahrain, Canada, Finland, France, Norway and the Netherlands signed the Paris Call for AI Safety and Security with Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, Mistral and others, committing to shared evaluations, third-party testing and fail-safe mechanisms to pause or shut down models. OpenAI separately described Control Room, which halts internal research workloads within 35 seconds and fails closed, an example of the shutdown capability regulators now expect.
