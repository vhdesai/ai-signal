# Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after Washington signs off, and the stock jumps about 29%

Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO effective immediately, saying it had "received input from senior levels of the United States government" that his return "poses no national security risk." It is a stark reversal from chair Harvey Jones's statement six days earlier, after pressure from President Trump, employees, investors and figures such as Elon Musk, and Colette Kress returns to her role as CFO. The shares, which had fallen 48% from their October high to as low as $125 on Friday, jumped about 29% in early trading. The board's independent investigation into Huang's conduct and China sales will continue, so governance risk is reduced but not closed.
