# Google Cloud launches Gemini for healthcare with HCA, Highmark and Northwell

Gemini for healthcare connects EHR, billing, clinical-guideline and Medicare data to run multi-step workflows such as records synthesis and prior-authorization review under human oversight. It launches in preview with HCA Healthcare, Highmark Health and Northwell Health. It is a vertical agent play aimed at administrative burden, where Google cites clinicians losing nearly 28 hours a week. Infrastructure INFRA
