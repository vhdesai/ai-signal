# Pressure shifts to Commerce as Bessent says BIS 'has the pen' on Megaspeed and the NYT details Lutnick's chip-rule push

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said several agencies had pushed for months to add Megaspeed to the entity list but that Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security has the pen, and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has agreed to testify to the Senate Banking Committee on Dec. 10. The New York Times reports Lutnick advocated a less stringent version of planned controls on AI-chip sales to Southeast Asian data centers while Cantor Fitzgerald, now run by his sons, had dealings with regional data-center firms serving Chinese clients; Commerce says he has complied with all ethics agreements. The Financial Times reports lawmakers are moving to tighten AI-chip export rules, and The Information reports Commerce is finalizing its long-awaited AI remote access rule.
