# Bland releases Tofu-1, a full-duplex speech-to-speech model for phone agents

Bland's Tofu-1 hears, reasons and replies in audio without a transcription step, and callers can interrupt or correct it mid-sentence while it keeps working on a task. Bland says it tracks multiple speakers, separates voices from background noise and detects hesitation or frustration, and it is targeting banking, healthcare, insurance and government calls. It is a closed research preview, and Bland has not disclosed model size, latency or benchmarks beyond a self-reported listener test.
