# Anthropic's amended S-1: $17 billion quarter, $23 billion loss and an $895 billion compute bill

Anthropic's updated prospectus shows nearly $17 billion in third-quarter revenue, up 47% from the second quarter and about twelvefold year over year, but quarter-over-quarter growth slowed from 144% and the net loss reached $23.3 billion. The company attributed part of the loss to $5 billion of costs for securing short-term computing capacity. Committed spend on chips, data centers and leases now totals about $895.4 billion through 2045, with nearly $70 billion kicking in over the next 12 months, making compute obligations the central diligence question for its IPO later this month.
