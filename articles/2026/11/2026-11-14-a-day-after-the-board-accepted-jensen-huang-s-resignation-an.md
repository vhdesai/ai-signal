# A day after the board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation and named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO, reporting shifted to…

A day after the board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation and named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO, reporting shifted to succession and capital allocation. The Wall Street Journal reported Huang had privately questioned Kress's strategic range, and investors told TechCrunch they expect her to be far more conservative with Nvidia's venture book, which joined 105 rounds this year per PitchBook. Neoclouds, inference providers and open-weight labs that relied on Nvidia as anchor investor face smaller checks and slower deals, which could loosen Nvidia's ecosystem lock-in.
