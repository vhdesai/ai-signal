# U.S. export-control scrutiny puts Nvidia governance in focus

Source: CNBC Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for Nvidia to recruit an outside chief executive and raised questions about potential illicit sales of advanced AI chips. His comments extend scrutiny beyond leadership succession to the company’s export-compliance oversight. These are allegations and policy pressure reported by CNBC, not an adjudicated finding of wrongdoing; the enterprise implication is heightened uncertainty around a critical infrastructure supplier.
