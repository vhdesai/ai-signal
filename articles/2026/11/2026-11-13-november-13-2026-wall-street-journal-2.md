# [November 13, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

CNBC reports OpenAI raised $15 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation, half the $30 billion it first sought and below the $1.4 trillion it pitched, as it keeps burning cash despite roughly 50% quarterly revenue growth. Anthropic's IPO is reportedly about six times oversubscribed, with shares expected to start trading Thursday at a valuation the Journal puts at roughly $1.33 trillion or more. In a livestream with Jamie Dimon, Dario Amodei called forecasting compute Anthropic's hardest problem given about $900 billion of disclosed infrastructure commitments, saying it relies on strong gross margins and more than $95 billion of cash to buy spot capacity. Reuters reports a Virginia software company has sued Anthropic for trademark infringement over the Claude Mythos name.
