# [November 9, 2026] · [Education Week]

The third report of Stanford HAI's One Hundred Year Study on AI credits scaling for recent progress while flagging the rising cost and scarcity of training data, concentration of frontier development and limited public input. A separate Stanford review of 52 randomized and quasi-experimental studies finds that using AI for explanations and practice generally beats using it to complete work, but neither yields consistent learning gains. Separately, an MIT-led team won up to $53 million from ARPA-H to design herbicide candidates with generative AI, extending AI molecule design from drugs to agriculture.
