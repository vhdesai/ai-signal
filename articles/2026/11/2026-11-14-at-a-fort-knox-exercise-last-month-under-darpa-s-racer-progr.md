# At a Fort Knox exercise last month under DARPA's RACER program, four uncrewed ground vehicles mapped terrain and…

At a Fort Knox exercise last month under DARPA's RACER program, four uncrewed ground vehicles mapped terrain and searched for targets on their own without GPS, using only onboard sensors. A remote human operator assigned tasks, reviewed findings and made every engagement decision. The software runs on both wheeled and tracked platforms and draws on lessons from Ukraine. Field-ready autonomy with a human in the loop will feed into rules for military AI and into the market for commercial dual-use robots.
