# MIT and Cornell test adaptive defenses against disguised AI writing

MIT and Cornell researchers are applying test-time adaptation so that an AI-text detector can adjust to changing inputs without a complete retraining cycle. In the reported disguised-text experiments, the adaptive approach detected more than 93% of machine-written examples, compared with about 27% for the existing approach. The work, due to be presented at NeurIPS, suggests a way to address the continuing contest between detection tools and text-disguising services. The researchers nevertheless caution that increasingly natural model output could undermine detection over time, so these results are not a guarantee of reliable authorship judgments in production.
