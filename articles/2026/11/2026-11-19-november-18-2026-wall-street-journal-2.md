# [November 18, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

At Nvidia's first all-hands since Huang's exit, interim CEO Colette Kress promised pay raises and extra equity in December to head off a talent exodus, with former sales chief Jay Puri on stage and no open employee Q&A. Shares slid almost 3% after record results, Muddy Waters disclosed a short citing alleged illicit China sales, and Bill Ackman attacked the board for refusing to bring Huang back. The Journal reports Nvidia has struck at least 293 deals since 2023 and built a portfolio worth more than $100 billion, while the $500 billion Wall Street pledged to mobilize for its customers has not materialized.
