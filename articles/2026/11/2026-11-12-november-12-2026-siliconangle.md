# [November 12, 2026] · [SiliconANGLE]

LangChain raised $300 million led by Alphabet's CapitalG with IVP, Sapphire, GIC and DFJ Growth, more than doubling its valuation to $3 billion on the strength of its LangSmith suite for building and monitoring agents. IBM is acquiring AuthMind to add runtime visibility into how agents use credentials and secrets in HashiCorp Vault, including detection of unsanctioned shadow agents. LangChain plans to nearly double headcount to 850 by the end of 2027, and investors are increasingly backing the tooling that makes agents governable in production.
