# Oracle says OpenAI contract funding is secured

24–48h exception — exact publication time unavailable. In a November 20 statement datelined Austin, Texas, Oracle says it has secured all funding needed to fulfill its OpenAI contract, including vendor financing. Oracle describes further vendor discussions as optional rather than required and reports progress at Abilene and Shackelford. These financing and delivery assertions are Oracle’s statements, not independently established findings.
