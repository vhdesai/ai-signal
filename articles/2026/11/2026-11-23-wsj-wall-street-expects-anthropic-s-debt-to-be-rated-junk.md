# WSJ: Wall Street expects Anthropic's debt to be rated junk

After its turbulent IPO, Anthropic, which has about $900 billion in spending commitments, is expected to receive a sub-investment-grade credit rating. The WSJ notes the rating will affect its capital plans and the wider AI industry. The article is paywalled, so this summary is based on the publisher's description. A junk rating would raise borrowing costs for the lab funding much of the AI compute buildout, putting more of the burden on equity and partner financing.
