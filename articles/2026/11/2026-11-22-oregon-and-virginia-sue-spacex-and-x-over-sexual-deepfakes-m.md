# Oregon and Virginia sue SpaceX and X over sexual deepfakes made with Grok

The attorneys general of Oregon and Virginia filed consumer-protection suits against SpaceX, which acquired xAI and Grok earlier this year, and X Corp, alleging the companies misled users about Grok's safety and deliberately shipped weak safeguards against child sexual abuse material and nonconsensual intimate images. The states seek civil penalties and injunctions; the claims are allegations, not findings. State attorneys general are becoming the main AI enforcement route, using existing consumer-protection law rather than new AI statutes.
