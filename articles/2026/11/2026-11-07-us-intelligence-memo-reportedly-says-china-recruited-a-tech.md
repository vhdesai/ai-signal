# US intelligence memo reportedly says China recruited a tech executive to spy on Nvidia's Jensen Huang

Citing The New York Times and the Financial Times, TechCrunch reports that a memo based on US intelligence said China's Ministry of State Security recruited Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, to spy on Nvidia's CEO. Nvidia said Jensen Huang's interactions with her were "not unusual" and that it does not share classified information with customers. A separate Times report found only an empty office at the listed address of New Matrix, a Chinese company the Commerce Department has authorized to buy advanced Nvidia chips. Together, the reports will add pressure for stricter checks on who ultimately uses exported AI chips, which could slow approvals for Nvidia's sales to China. DARPA AI FOR SECURITY MARKETS
