# Nvidia, Rebellions, and SK Telecom partner on sovereign AI infrastructure

Rebellions plans to connect its next-generation R200 inference processors to Nvidia's ecosystem using NVLink Fusion and MGX rack designs. SK Telecom plans to deploy the resulting systems in its AI data center, with support from South Korea's science ministry. Systems are expected in 2028, making this a future infrastructure partnership rather than immediately available capacity.
