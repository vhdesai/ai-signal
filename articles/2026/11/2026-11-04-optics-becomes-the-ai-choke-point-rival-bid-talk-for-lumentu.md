# Optics becomes the AI choke point: rival-bid talk for Lumentum and a fiber squeeze

After Lumentum fell in the election-driven selloff, The Information argues Marvell's all-stock offer, worth about $80 billion as of Tuesday, could draw a rival bid from AMD, Intel or Nvidia or a sweetened cash component; Lumentum's optical business is growing 122% at roughly 50% gross margins. Business Insider reports AI demand is straining fiber supply as Amazon and Nvidia lock up capacity and Corning and startups expand production. The constraint in AI clusters is moving from compute toward interconnect. Sources: The Information | Business Insider
