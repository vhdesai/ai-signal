# Speaking at a WSJ tech conference in Napa, OpenAI president Greg Brockman said the company should be ready to go public…

Speaking at a WSJ tech conference in Napa, OpenAI president Greg Brockman said the company should be ready to go public next year. He added that business customers now account for most of its sales, driven heavily by Codex. OpenAI confidentially filed a draft prospectus in June, and Anthropic is expected to list first, before year-end.
