# Data centers on the ballot as OpenAI pledges grid relief

The Information finds at least one major-party nominee backs a data-center moratorium in 21 of 36 governor's races, and pause supporters could win in up to 15 states. OpenAI launched its Grid Protectors program, piloted at its planned Effingham County, Georgia campus, committing to shed up to 1,000 MW during grid emergencies. Today's results will help set siting costs and permitting risk for the next build cycle.
