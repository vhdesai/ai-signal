# Nvidia earnings put AI infrastructure demand back in focus

Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, a 103% increase from a year earlier. Its next-quarter revenue outlook is $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, with no assumed China Data Center compute revenue. The release also confirms that Vera Rubin NVL72 shipments have begun, providing an operational milestone alongside the financial demand signal.
