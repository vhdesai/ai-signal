# Nvidia's selloff highlights governance and concentration risks

Yahoo Finance carried a Decrypt report that Nvidia shares fell sharply as concerns surrounding its chief executive escalated into board, legal, and congressional scrutiny. The article reports a board-led investigation, a shareholder lawsuit, and a threat by House Republicans to subpoena Jensen Huang. For technology executives, the episode highlights governance uncertainty at a supplier central to many AI infrastructure plans.
