# Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen as its fast "daily driver" for agents and puts it in Search worldwide

Hydrogen is a closed-weight model adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon. It has a 1M-token context window and a new xhigh reasoning setting, and costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. Google reports, without independent verification, that it completes tasks 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, scores 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, and makes more than 5x fewer fabricated claims. A new Orchestrator in Google Antigravity lets Carbon plan while Hydrogen edits code and runs tests; Google says this pairing came close to Carbon's success rate on Terminal-Bench while using 24% less quota. Hydrogen is also rolling out globally in Google Search, and Gemini 3.8 Flash will rise to the same price on January 1, 2027. Products & Tools META REPLIT AGENTS COMMERCE
