# AI raises the stakes on both sides of security as free Chinese models, deepfakes and vendor breaches spread

Tenzai's CTO says freely downloadable Chinese open-weight models can now break into corporate networks, citing an AI hacking framework used against more than 200 organizations in 11 days, while Artificial Analysis says frontier cyber capabilities are getting costlier to improve. North Korean hackers stole more than $400 million by using AI-manipulated video to pose as trusted contacts on calls, and the Fireworks breach reached Notion AI chat transcripts for about 1% of Notion customers. On defense, OpenAI introduced Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory to test web apps and prepare patches for human review, and CIO Dive reports AI found widespread IoT flaws faster and more cheaply than humans.
