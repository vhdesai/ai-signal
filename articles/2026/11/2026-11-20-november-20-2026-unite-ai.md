# [November 20, 2026] · [Unite.AI]

A joint report from the House Homeland Security and China select committees found Airbnb running customized Qwen models for about 80,000 guest and host interactions a day and DoorDash using 25 Chinese model families, and concluded the US lacks a competitive open-weight alternative at the price and performance the market demands. Cursor maker Anysphere told investigators it will explore phasing out Chinese-developed open-weight models, though no timetable was set. Separately, Sen. Tom Cotton said Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick repeatedly ignored his warnings that Nvidia chips were being smuggled to China, and a WSJ analysis says China's AI firms, long dependent on Nvidia chips, now face withdrawal.
