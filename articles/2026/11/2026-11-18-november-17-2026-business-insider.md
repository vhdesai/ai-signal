# [November 17, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Hours after Jensen Huang said he would be honored to return as CEO, board chair Harvey Jones told the earnings call the board unanimously accepted his resignation over personal behavior inconsistent with Nvidia's code of conduct and does not intend to reinstate him. Nvidia hired Heidrick & Struggles for a search that could pick an insider or outsider, and Business Insider noted that CEO candidate Ian Buck joined the call but said nothing. Separately, the Journal reports the SEC has opened a civil investigation into whether Nvidia violated investor-disclosure laws, while a reinstatement petition nears 200,000 signatures, including about 10,000 employees.
