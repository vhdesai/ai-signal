# CoreWeave doubles revenue but burns $7.5 billion, leaning on customer prepayments as debt costs climb

CoreWeave's third-quarter revenue more than doubled to $3.66 billion, but interest expense rose to $846 million and its net loss widened to $709 million. It raised 2026 capital-spending guidance to as much as $39 billion and expects fourth-quarter interest costs above $1 billion. CEO Michael Intrator said 70% of this year's deals carried prepayments, some covering the full contract value, and that CoreWeave will not tap unsecured debt at current yields.
