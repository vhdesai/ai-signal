# Nvidia's board opens an independent probe, and key-person risk moves to the center of the AI trade

Nvidia's board said Sunday that independent directors and outside counsel will investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, after Huang told staff he had an affair with a former Megaspeed executive whom US officials suspect of working for China's Ministry of State Security. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised, Huang remains CEO, and Republican leaders of three House committees have asked him to a closed-door briefing. Shares fell roughly 5% before Monday's open, and DealBook framed the real question as how indispensable Huang is to a company that acts as the de facto central bank of AI, investing in and lending to much of the ecosystem, including Anthropic as it starts its IPO roadshow.
