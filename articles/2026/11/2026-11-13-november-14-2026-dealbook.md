# [November 14, 2026] · [DealBook]

Huang agreed to a closed-door briefing for three House committees before Thanksgiving, Democrats asked Commerce to preserve records on H200 export approvals to Megaspeed, and the Journal reports Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is fighting for his job as Trump aides blame him for the fallout. The Information reports companies tied to Alice Huang invested in DeepSeek to secure banned Blackwell chips, and former OpenAI researcher Will DePue found hardware fingerprints consistent with Blackwell training in open models including DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3. DealBook says China hawks are pushing three export bills ahead of next week's APEC summit in Shenzhen and that Oracle canceled a roughly $7 billion contract giving Tencent access to AI chips in Southeast Asia after officials raised concerns. Reuters estimates about $3.2 billion of Chinese computing projects built around Nvidia accelerators are now at risk.
