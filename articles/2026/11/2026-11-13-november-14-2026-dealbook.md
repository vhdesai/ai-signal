Huang agreed to a closed-door briefing for three House committees before Thanksgiving, Democrats asked Commerce to preserve records on H200 export approvals to Megaspeed, and the Journal reports Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is fighting for his job as Trump aides blame him for the fallout.
