# NetApp completes its acquisition of PEAK:AIO for AI-scale storage

NetApp closed the acquisition of PEAK:AIO, which makes metadata services and parallel file system technology. NetApp will add it to its Novus high-performance storage line for zettabyte-scale AI clouds and GPU-as-a-service providers. Keeping GPUs continuously supplied with data is now a direct factor in infrastructure costs, which is pulling established storage vendors into the AI cluster market.
