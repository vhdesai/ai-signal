# Democrats open a second congressional probe into Nvidia's China sales as Huang agrees to testify behind closed doors

Sen. Ron Wyden and Rep. Richard Neal wrote to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, asking him to preserve records. Their letter questions the H200 export arrangement, which routed chips through the US so a tariff could be applied. It also asks why Megaspeed, the company that employed Alice Huang, kept receiving licenses after intelligence warnings, and whether Nvidia's $26 million in donations to Trump projects played a role. Separately, Huang agreed to a recorded, closed-door briefing for three Republican-led House committees before Thanksgiving. The Wall Street Journal reports that he once called interim CEO Colette Kress "a deer in the headlights," partly because she questioned his credit-backed chip deals. The Information reports that companies tied to Alice Huang invested in DeepSeek, and that Nvidia will continue its internal investigation of Huang.
