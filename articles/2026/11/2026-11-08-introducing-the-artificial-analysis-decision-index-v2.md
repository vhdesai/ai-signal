# Introducing the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2

Artificial Analysis revised its decision-making benchmark to reward confidence calibration alongside correctness and increased private-dataset weighting from 25% to 45%. New tests examine document-level extraction and decisions, plus consistency across equivalent question formulations. For enterprise evaluations, the useful change is attention to reliability and calibrated uncertainty rather than answer accuracy alone; benchmark rankings still require validation against the buyer’s own workloads.
