# In a Wired interview summarized by TechCrunch, new Apple CEO John Ternus defended Apple's AI position after the delayed…

In a Wired interview summarized by TechCrunch, new Apple CEO John Ternus defended Apple's AI position after the delayed Siri overhaul shipped this summer. He said Apple will use AI broadly but wants users not to have to think about whether a feature is AI, and pushed back on the idea that agents will make apps unnecessary. The comments signal continuity in Apple's product-led, integrate-quietly approach rather than a pivot to frontier-model competition.
