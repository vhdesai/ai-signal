# Hot Nvidia faces reported CEO succession uncertainty

TechCrunch reports that Nvidia’s board is preparing for a possible replacement of CEO Jensen Huang; the report does not establish that a leadership change has occurred. Shares fell in after-hours trading as succession questions intensified. For AI infrastructure buyers, the development is a leadership-continuity risk to monitor rather than evidence of a change in product availability or delivery commitments.
