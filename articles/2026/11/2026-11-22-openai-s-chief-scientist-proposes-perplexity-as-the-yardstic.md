# OpenAI's chief scientist proposes perplexity as the yardstick for industry AI limits

In a post Sam Altman called "the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while," chief scientist Jakub Pachocki argues that perplexity could serve as a shared benchmark for general intelligence. Perplexity measures how well a model predicts the next token. He argues that downstream benchmarks go stale quickly. He notes that losses below 0.8 coincided with "transformative capabilities" in cybersecurity and mathematics. Labs would agree not to cross a threshold until the ability to monitor models reaches a set milestone. It is the most concrete proposal yet for putting into practice Amodei's "pace the frontier" plan. LITIGATION CONTENT SAFETY
