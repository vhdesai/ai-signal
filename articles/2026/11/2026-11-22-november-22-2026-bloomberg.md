# [November 22, 2026] · [Bloomberg]

A bipartisan group of US governors is urging Washington to set rules for the most powerful AI systems while preserving states' ability to protect consumers, workers and children, Bloomberg reports, wading into the fight over federal preemption as a new Congress prepares to take office. Ahead of the November 23 Bridgetown Summit on AI Safety, convened by UN Secretary-General António Guterres and Barbados Prime Minister Mia Mottley, Renata Dwan and Amandeep Singh Gill argue in the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists that no government has binding measures for loss-of-control risks and call for pre-deployment testing, independent evaluation and transparent results.
