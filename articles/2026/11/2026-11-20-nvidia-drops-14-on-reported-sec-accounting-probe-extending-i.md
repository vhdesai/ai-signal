# Nvidia drops 14% on reported SEC accounting probe, extending its longest losing streak since 2008

Nvidia fell 14% on Friday after Reuters reported that the SEC is looking into possible accounting issues. That made seven straight losing weeks, with shares down 46% and about $2.6 trillion of market value gone. The stock trades at roughly 8.4 times forward earnings, against 29 times for AMD, even after interim CEO Colette Kress projected 75% revenue growth next fiscal year. Nvidia is now the fourth most valuable company, behind Apple, Microsoft and Alphabet. Uncertainty about governance and accounting is now outweighing strong fundamentals, which could weaken Nvidia's hand in negotiations with large customers. LEADERSHIP GOVERNANCE
