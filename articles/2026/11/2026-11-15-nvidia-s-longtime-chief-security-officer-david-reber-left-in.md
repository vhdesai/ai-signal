# Nvidia's longtime chief security officer, David Reber, left in August for health reasons, about two and a half months…

Nvidia's longtime chief security officer, David Reber, left in August for health reasons, about two and a half months before Huang resigned over relationships including one with a Megaspeed executive. Citing The New York Times, TechCrunch notes that Nvidia's top lawyer had been engaged with the government's Megaspeed inquiry for more than a year, and it is unclear whether the security team was told. Nothing public links the security leadership change to the case, and Nvidia did not answer questions. Specialists argue corporate security teams should cover foreign-intelligence targeting of executives, not just cyber and physical threats.
