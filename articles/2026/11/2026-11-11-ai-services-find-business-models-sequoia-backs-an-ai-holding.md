# AI services find business models: Sequoia backs an AI holding company as Anthropic's Ode adds results-based fees

Sequoia Capital is investing hundreds of millions of dollars in Sequence Holding, a one-year-old company that buys businesses in established industries and uses AI to replace or complement human work, and which just agreed to a $7.7 billion insurance acquisition co-led by Michael Dell's family office. Separately, Ode, the Anthropic venture backed by $1.5 billion from Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and others, charges most clients a fixed fee to build custom AI products and lets them keep the intellectual property. Ode is now layering on performance-based fees tied to client financial targets, although fewer than 10% of clients pay them so far. Both models suggest that capturing AI value increasingly means owning the workflow or the outcome rather than selling software seats.
