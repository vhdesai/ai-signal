# Bloomberg reports that Apple cut its engineering teams' daily AI spending cap from $300 to $200 in October

Bloomberg reports that Apple cut its engineering teams' daily AI spending cap from $300 to $200 in October. Some allowances are headed to $100 a day, and non-engineering staff could see caps as low as $25. The move under new CEO John Ternus reflects a wider shift from 'tokenmaxxing' to token rationing as enterprise AI bills grow faster than measurable productivity gains.
