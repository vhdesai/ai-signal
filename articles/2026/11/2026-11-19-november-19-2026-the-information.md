# [November 19, 2026] · [The Information]

Meta plans to charge $300 for its pocket-sized Muse Charm device and bundle up to 12 months of free Verizon 5G service to drive use of its Muse personal agent, The Information reports; Meta has also pushed the wider release of its smartwatch to 2027 and aims to sell 10 million wearables in the second half of 2026. The same day, TechCrunch, citing Reuters, reported internal employee posts describing Muse surfacing childcare requests, product orders and bank balances that were not theirs. Meta says employee builds differ from the public app, and it is unconfirmed whether real user data was exposed.
