# [November 3, 2026] · [The Information]

Salesforce agreed to acquire Cresta, the contact-center automation company that began in Stanford's AI Lab, for $2.7 billion according to The Information; Salesforce did not disclose a price. CrowdStrike agreed to pay a combined $495 million for Fiddler AI, which monitors and explains agent behavior, and Dreadnode, which probes AI models for flaws before deployment. Incumbents are buying their way into agent operations, from customer service to agent oversight.
