# Adaptive AI-text detection improves experimental robustness

MIT and Cornell researchers applied test-time adaptation so an AI-text detector could learn from new, unlabeled inputs after deployment. In their experiments, detection of disguised AI writing improved from roughly 27% with existing methods to more than 93%, while accuracy under simulated language shifts improved from 73% to 85%. These controlled results are not universal proof of authorship; organizations should test distribution shift and adversarial behavior and avoid using detector scores alone for consequential decisions.
