# Alibaba launches Qwen4-Max and Tencent ships Hy4 flash, both with million-token context

Alibaba made Qwen4-Max generally available, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion total and 71 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window, and claims about 60% lower task cost than Qwen3.8-Max on its own Work360 evaluation. Tencent released Hy4 flash, with 224 billion total and 12 billion active parameters and a million-token context, priced at 0.5 yuan per million input tokens and 2 yuan per million output tokens. Both performance claims are vendor-reported, and smaller open-weight Qwen models and an open Hy4 flash release are promised rather than available now. Alibaba Cloud | ThePrint | Tencent News
