# [November 21, 2026] · [DealBook]

Nvidia fell 14% on Friday after Reuters reported that the SEC is exploring potential accounting issues, extending a seven-week losing streak, its longest since 2008, that has cut about $2.6 trillion from its market value; the stock now trades at roughly 8.4 times forward earnings versus 29 times for AMD. Wired reports that Jensen Huang's alleged lying alarmed the board ahead of his resignation, not only his affairs. At the DealBook Summit, Elon Musk defended Huang, likening his ouster to "taking Michael Jordan off your team when you're on a winning streak," while Business Insider lays out Huang's long-shot routes back, such as a proxy fight, given he owns only about 3.5% of the company.
