# Nonprofits sue Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI, and SpaceX's AI unit to halt new frontier models

The Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen, Heat Initiative, and the Alliance for Secure AI filed suit in San Francisco Superior Court under California's unfair competition law. They ask a judge to stop the companies from developing or deploying new models. The complaint cites documented AI-driven cyberattacks and the companies' own statements about catastrophic risk. The suit is unlikely to win an injunction, but it opens a state-court front just as a Democratic Congress signals tougher AI regulation and Anthropic prepares to go public.
