# Oregon and Virginia sue SpaceX and X over Grok sexual deepfakes

The attorneys general of Oregon and Virginia filed separate consumer-protection suits against SpaceX, which took ownership of xAI and Grok earlier this year, and X Corp. They allege the companies misled users about Grok's safety and deliberately shipped weak safeguards against child sexual abuse material and nonconsensual intimate imagery. The complaints cite insufficient third-party testing, permissive safety prompts and incomplete reports to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children.
