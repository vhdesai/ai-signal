# Anthropic's IPO deck says Claude ported its stack to AMD MI355 in three days

According to a slide in Anthropic's investor presentation, Claude Mythos Preview ported and optimized Anthropic's models for AMD MI355 GPUs in three days, writing or porting 100% of the kernels. Anthropic said that onboarding a new accelerator previously took a dozen engineers six to 12 months. Anthropic describes the goal as a "fungible compute pool" spanning Nvidia, AMD, Trainium and TPUs, so it can buy on price-performance. If the claim holds up, it directly erodes CUDA lock-in, which is Nvidia's biggest moat.
