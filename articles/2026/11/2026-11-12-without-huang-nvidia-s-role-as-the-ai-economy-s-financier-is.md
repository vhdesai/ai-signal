# Without Huang, Nvidia's role as the AI economy's financier is in doubt

Nvidia committed about $140 billion in investments and credit guarantees in just two months this year, has joined 296 venture deals since 2023 and backed nearly one in three AI unicorns founded since then, and disclosed up to $164.5 billion in customer guarantees in August. Investors expect interim CEO Colette Kress, who as CFO pushed back on Huang's riskier bets, to slow that pace, and the most exposed companies are those mid-round with Nvidia expected to invest, while its planned $10 billion stake in Anthropic's IPO now faces scrutiny. The stock trades near 13.7 times forward earnings, and The Information reports that customers expect chips to keep selling out but may feel freer to test alternatives. CIO Dive quotes Forrester calling the exit a stress test for the AI boom, and analysts are urging IT leaders to diversify AI infrastructure.
