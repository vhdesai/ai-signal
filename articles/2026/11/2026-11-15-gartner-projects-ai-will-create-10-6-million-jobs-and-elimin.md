# Gartner projects AI will create 10.6 million jobs and eliminate 8.9 million worldwide between 2025 and 2030, a net gain…

Gartner projects AI will create 10.6 million jobs and eliminate 8.9 million worldwide between 2025 and 2030, a net gain of about 1.7 million. It expects more than 20% of workers, about 435.5 million people, to change what they do and how they do it. Gartner's John Lovelock describes a sequence of hiring freezes, then cuts, then net additions as adoption matures. Challenger, Gray & Christmas counts more than 100,000 AI-related U.S. job-cut announcements this year through October.
