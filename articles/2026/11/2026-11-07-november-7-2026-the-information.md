# [November 7, 2026] · [The Information]

CoreWeave CEO Michael Intrator wrote to Texas Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa, who campaigned on a statewide data-center moratorium, offering to work on the “responsible completion of permitted projects” and “clear, durable standards” for new ones. The Information reports that public records, job listings and turbine-flanked steel structures indicate SpaceX has begun a multigigawatt “Starbase supercomputer” at its Starship site, which the new administration could delay but probably not stop. TechCrunch's Equity podcast notes that nearly every data-center restriction measure on the ballot passed, making Texas, home to Stargate, the first test of whether campaign promises become permitting limits.
