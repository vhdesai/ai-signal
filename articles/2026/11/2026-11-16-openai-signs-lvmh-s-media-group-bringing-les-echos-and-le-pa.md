# OpenAI signs LVMH's media group, bringing Les Echos and Le Parisien journalism into ChatGPT

LVMH's media division will deploy ChatGPT Enterprise and other OpenAI tools across titles including Les Echos, Le Parisien and Paris Match. The deal also lets ChatGPT show attributed summaries and excerpts that link back to the publishers' reporting.
