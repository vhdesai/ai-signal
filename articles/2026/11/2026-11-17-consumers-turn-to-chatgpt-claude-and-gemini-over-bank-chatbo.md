# Consumers turn to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini over bank chatbots for banking tasks

A McKinsey report covered by CIO Dive finds nearly three in four consumers who use AI for banking tasks choose ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, almost double the share using their bank's own chatbot. McKinsey warns that data silos threaten banks' competitive edge in AI and that they are running out of time to benefit. The finding lands as general-purpose assistants gain the ability to make purchases on cardholders' behalf. Infrastructure EXCLUSIVE CHIPS
