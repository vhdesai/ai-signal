# Capital narrows at the top: AI venture funding slides as OpenAI signals a 2027 IPO

PitchBook data show global AI venture deal value fell 43.4% quarter over quarter to $94.3 billion in Q3, with funding for horizontal platforms such as frontier labs dropping from $201.8 billion in Q1 to $27.6 billion. Large checks still clear for differentiated bets: Fal raised $1.65 billion at a $16 billion valuation, and Isomorphic Labs is reportedly in talks to raise about $5 billion at roughly $50 billion. OpenAI president Greg Brockman said the company should be ready to list next year and that enterprise customers now account for most of its sales. Sources: The Next Web | Yahoo Finance EXCLUSIVENEW
