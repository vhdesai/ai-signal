# Capital keeps flowing to AI chips and robotics even as some allocators step back from the AI trade

UK chip startup Fractile raised a $720 million Series C led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint to commercialize inference chips that stack DRAM directly on the processor, and SoftBank led a $500 million round in Munich-based Agile Robots at an $8.5 billion valuation with Nvidia participating. Applied Intuition is in talks to raise $1.5 billion at about $30 billion, Torus raised $500 million for modular AI power infrastructure, and sales-agent developer Rox reached a $1.3 billion valuation. Against that backdrop, $145 billion manager ICG says investors are not paid enough for AI risk outside venture-style bets, and PitchBook estimates that about 22% of total unicorn value is at risk as stale valuations reprice.
