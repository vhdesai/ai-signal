# Spy scandal revives push for tighter Nvidia export controls

The Information reports a new push for tighter chip export controls is emerging in the wake of the Huang scandal; a separate report details how Huang's ties to Megaspeed's Alice Huang enabled more than $7 billion in Nvidia chip sales to China. Tighter rules would hit Nvidia's China revenue and could reshape allocation for other buyers. Details are behind a paywall.
