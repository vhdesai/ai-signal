# OpenAI says its models improved AMD's flagship GPU design by up to 14% and shrank its own Jalapeno chip

OpenAI published results showing its models achieved up to 14% improvements in power, performance and area on AMD's flagship GPU design, which it called roughly a year of progress in less than five months, though it did not publish the baseline. AMD has already included some of the changes in a recent design sent for manufacturing and added others to its production design files. OpenAI is also using the models to redesign parts of its in-house Jalapeno chip to take up less space without losing capacity. Academic Research RESEARCH SECURITY
