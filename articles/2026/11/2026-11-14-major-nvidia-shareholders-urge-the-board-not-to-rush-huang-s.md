# Major Nvidia shareholders urge the board not to rush Huang's replacement

Altimeter Capital's Brad Gerstner and Atreides Management's Gavin Baker published a letter asking Nvidia's board for a "slow, measured, and deliberate" succession process that keeps "the potential return of Jensen Huang" on the table. The letter backs interim CEO Colette Kress but stops short of calling for immediate reinstatement. A "Stand with Jensen" petition says it has more than 100,000 signatures, including over 5,000 current employees, and Elon Musk has signed it.
