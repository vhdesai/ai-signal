# Post-election AI rulebook takes shape as OpenAI pitches a community-first data-center framework

Pentagon CTO Emil Michael, vice chair of the White House's Super Intelligence Task Force, said the government will lay out the laws and regulations that apply to domestic AI labs in the next week or two. The Information argues Democrats' new majority raises the odds of frontier-AI regulation and data-center restrictions, with AI safety a campaign theme for winners such as Michigan Senate victor Abdul El-Sayed. OpenAI, in a framework shared with POLITICO, backed making large power users cover the grid costs they add, letting utilities throttle data centers at peak demand and requiring public reporting of water and energy use.
