# Enterprise AI consolidation: Salesforce buys Cresta as CrowdStrike buys two AI-security startups

Salesforce agreed to acquire Cresta, the contact-center automation company that began in Stanford's AI Lab, for $2.7 billion according to The Information; Salesforce did not disclose a price. CrowdStrike agreed to pay a combined $495 million for Fiddler AI, which monitors and explains agent behavior, and Dreadnode, which probes AI models for flaws before deployment. Incumbents are buying their way into agent operations, from customer service to agent oversight. Salesforce | The Information | WSJ Pro CyberSecurity
