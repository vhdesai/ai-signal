# NVIDIA, Rebellions and SK Telecom Announce Collaboration to Advance Sovereign AI Infrastructure With NVIDIA NVLink Fusion

Rebellions plans to integrate its R200 inference processors with NVIDIA NVLink Fusion and MGX rack architecture, with SK Telecom deploying the resulting systems for Korean sovereign AI. The proposed design splits inference between NVIDIA GPUs for prefill and Rebellions NPUs for decoding. Systems are expected to be deployment-ready in 2028, making this a heterogeneous-inference roadmap rather than immediately available capacity. Timing: Date-only publication; exact cutoff unverified.
