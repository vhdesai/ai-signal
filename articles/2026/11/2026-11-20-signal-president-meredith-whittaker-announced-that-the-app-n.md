# Signal President Meredith Whittaker announced that the app now blocks screenshots by default on iOS 27 to keep built-in assistants such as Siri AI and Gemini from capturing conversations, and on Android automatically turns on screen security when it detects an AI assistant accessing the screen. Signal says it cannot reliably block capture on macOS, recommends that users exclude Signal in Siri settings, calls its fix incomplete, and is urging platform vendors to give developers a simple way to opt out of AI screen access.
