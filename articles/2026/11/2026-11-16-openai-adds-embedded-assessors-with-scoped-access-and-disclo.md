# OpenAI adds embedded assessors with scoped access and disclosure limits

OpenAI announced agreements with Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire for scoped internal access to checkpoints, code, evaluations and training resources as relevant to their assessments. The work covers alignment, monitoring and safeguards rather than granting unlimited access. Publication can involve confidentiality redactions and responsible-disclosure delays, while an oversight committee receives unredacted reports; the expansion of independent scrutiny therefore needs to be considered alongside its scope and disclosure constraints. Published November 16; exact time not disclosed, so strict 24-hour placement is unconfirmed. POLICY N / J
