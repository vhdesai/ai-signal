# Google introduces a low-cost decision layer for agent workflows

Gemini Decision 1 is designed for bounded choices such as tool selection, policy checks, model routing, and rubric scoring, returning option probabilities instead of generated prose. Google says the API can evaluate up to 1,000 questions against shared multimodal input at $0.10 per million input tokens, without output-token charges, and reports 150–250 ms latency under its evaluation conditions. For enterprise agents, a specialized control layer could reduce cost and delay while escalating uncertain decisions to a stronger model or a human reviewer.
