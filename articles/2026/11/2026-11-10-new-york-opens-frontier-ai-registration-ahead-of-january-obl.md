# New York opens frontier-AI registration ahead of January obligations

TechCrunch reports that New York has opened registration for large frontier-AI developers as it prepares to implement the RAISE Act. The framework combines safety documentation with incident-reporting requirements, adding a concrete state-level compliance process for major laboratories. The immediate news is registration and enforcement preparation: the principal obligations begin January 1, 2027, not on the article's publication date.
