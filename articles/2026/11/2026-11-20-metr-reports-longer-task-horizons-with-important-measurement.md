# METR reports longer task horizons, with important measurement limits

24–48h exception — exact publication time unavailable. METR’s institutional capability update reports approximately 3.6-month doubling in measured task time horizons across combined TH1.1 and TH2 tasks, with strong coding and optimization performance where feedback is clear and weaker performance on judgment-heavy, hard-to-verify research. Its aggregate public-frontier METR Capability Index does not show strong acceleration. Human baselines directly underpin only 9% of completion-time estimates, with the remainder relying on other estimation methods. This is not an established peer-reviewed result or evidence of autonomous research acceleration.
