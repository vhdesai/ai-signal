# Nvidia's crisis turns legal and political: FBI interest, the first shareholder suit and a governance reckoning

President Trump said the FBI is looking into Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang while insisting Huang did not know she was a suspected spy, and Rosen Law Firm filed the first proposed class action alleging misleading statements about the ties. Nvidia closed down 4.3% on Monday, erasing about $192 billion, as AMD rose 13% and Broadcom 8.8%, and the WSJ describes a chairman-less board with no named successor that has largely done things Huang's way. The Information reports the relationship helped route at least $7.5 billion of Nvidia chips through Chinese companies since 2023, and DealBook notes Democrats are probing how a Commerce Department inquiry into Megaspeed was halted after Nvidia's intervention, with earnings due November 17.
