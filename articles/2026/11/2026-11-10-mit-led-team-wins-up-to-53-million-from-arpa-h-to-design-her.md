# MIT-led team wins up to $53 million from ARPA-H to design herbicides with generative AI

The PLOW project, led by MIT's Christopher Voigt with the Broad Institute and others, will use generative AI to design chemical and RNA-based herbicide candidates as weeds grow resistant to glyphosate and atrazine. Candidates will be screened for cost, resistance and human and environmental risk before lab and greenhouse testing. It is a funded program rather than a proven product, but it extends AI-driven molecule design beyond pharmaceuticals into agriculture.
