# Meta launches Spaces, letting Muse build trackers and planners that update automatically

Spaces are project workspaces inside Muse: users describe an idea, and the agent builds a tracker, planner, or project board. When users connect their apps, the space updates in the background. It is Meta's second Muse feature in a week, after Muse Voice, and Meta said in October that Muse had 10 million users in the US and Canada. Consumer agents are now competing on staying power, keeping users through ongoing tasks rather than single chat sessions. AMAZON AGENTIC COMMERCE
