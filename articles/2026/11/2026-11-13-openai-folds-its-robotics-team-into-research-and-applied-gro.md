# OpenAI folds its robotics team into research and applied groups, betting general models can run robots

OpenAI dissolved its standalone robotics team, moving research under Mark Chen and applied robotics and hardware into Greg Brockman's Applied organization, according to Infobae's account of a Fortune report. OpenAI frames the move as integration rather than retreat, saying its general GPT-6 Astra models can be used directly for robotics, although that claim has not been independently validated.
