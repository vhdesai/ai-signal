# Anthropic's S-1 locks in founder control and unusual equity-based giving

PitchBook notes that Anthropic's seven cofounders will hold 50.1% of voting power and 10% of the stock after the IPO, with analyst Harrison Rolfes observing that public shareholders won't control the company. Business Insider reports the cofounders have pledged 80% of their stock to charity, potentially about $80 billion, while 5% of company equity is earmarked for a public-good fund. Experts called the design credible but reserved judgment until money is actually given.
