# [November 19, 2026] · [The Information]

Nvidia is in advanced talks to acquire Inception, a two-year-old startup building diffusion large language models, for at least $500 million plus performance-based earnouts, The Information reports; the team and technology are meant to bolster Nvidia's Nemotron open models even as it works through a leadership crisis. Separately, Nvidia released Nemotron-Labs-Sigma, an open family of 14 continuous-diffusion checkpoints at 3B and 8B scale that denoise text in a learned 16-dimensional embedding space rather than over discrete tokens. Its 8-step distilled 8B variant keeps a 70.34 average versus 71.16 for the base model, and the family runs at roughly 120 to 140 tokens per second on a single H100.
