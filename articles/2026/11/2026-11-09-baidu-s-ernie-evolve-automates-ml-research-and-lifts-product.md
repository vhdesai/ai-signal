# Baidu's ERNIE Evolve automates ML research and lifts production inference throughput

ERNIE Evolve runs nested agent loops that choose research directions, edit code and keep only changes that pass a hidden evaluator and a separate reviewer meant to catch benchmark gaming. Baidu reports beating public bests on 3 of 4 metrics (NanoChat, NanoGPT speedrun) and, on ERNIE-5.5-Flash, gains of 33.7% in deterministic throughput and 14.9% in decode speed on Kunlunxin P800. Results are self-reported and the system is not released; Baidu frames it as a practical step toward recursive self-improvement. Academic Research RESEARCH
