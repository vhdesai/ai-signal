# Google's /teamwork multi-agent system helps prove the Courtade-Kumar conjecture, with proofs checked in Lean 4

Google says Antigravity's /teamwork harness runs many solution attempts in parallel, checks each with independent verifiers such as Lean 4, test suites and simulators, and gives each agent a narrow context to avoid drift on multi-day tasks. Researchers from Google, CUHK, USC and CMU used it to prove the 2013 Courtade-Kumar conjecture in full generality, and Google reports further results in formal verification and kernel optimization that have not all been independently reviewed. Google is opening these patterns to external Antigravity users, moving verifier-backed agent workflows from demonstrations toward products. Google Research RESEARCH ANALYSIS
