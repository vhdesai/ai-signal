# MIT guarantees that AI models of chaotic systems stay within physically possible bounds

Researchers at MIT's Laboratory for Information and Decision Systems built physics-based constraints into machine-learning models so their long-horizon predictions of chaotic systems, such as weather, climate and turbulence, cannot drift into physically impossible values. The work, by Sunbochen Tang, Navid Azizan and Themistoklis Sapsis and published in PNAS, is described as the first formal guarantee of bounded behavior for such models. Runaway drift has been a key barrier to replacing traditional simulations with AI emulators in forecasting, aerospace and energy.
