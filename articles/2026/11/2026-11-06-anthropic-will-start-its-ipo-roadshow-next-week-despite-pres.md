# Anthropic will start its IPO roadshow next week despite pressure on its valuation

According to people familiar with the plans, Anthropic will start formally marketing its IPO to potential investors next week, after two weeks of swings in tech stocks driven by the AI spending plans of Amazon and others. TechCrunch, citing Bloomberg, reports that some prospective buyers put Anthropic's value as low as $1.1 trillion, compared with roughly $2 trillion expected a month ago. The price the IPO sets will become the public market's benchmark for valuing frontier labs, including OpenAI's expected 2027 listing.
