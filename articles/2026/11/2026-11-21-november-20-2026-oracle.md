# [November 20, 2026] · [Oracle]

Oracle said it has secured all the funding it needs to fulfill its OpenAI contract, including vendor financing, calling further vendor talks optional; its Abilene campus is nearly complete, Shackelford is fully permitted and Nvidia Vera Rubin rack handovers are due in the coming weeks. PitchBook data show chip and data-center deal value jumped 64.3% quarter over quarter to $26.3 billion in Q3, lifting the year-to-date total to a record $53.7 billion. Private equity is rotating from software into grids, power equipment and data centers, with global deal value in these hard-asset businesses reaching $313.1 billion through Q3 as US software PE deal value fell from a $110 billion quarterly peak to $10.7 billion.
