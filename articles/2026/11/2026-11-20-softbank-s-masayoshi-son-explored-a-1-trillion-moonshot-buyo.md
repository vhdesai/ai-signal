# SoftBank's Masayoshi Son explored a $1 trillion moonshot buyout of SK Hynix

Masayoshi Son recently approached Wall Street institutions about financing a possible trillion-dollar buyout of SK Hynix, the memory giant that supplies high-bandwidth memory for advanced AI chips, The Information reports. A SoftBank spokesperson said the deal "is no longer under active consideration." The approach shows how strategic HBM supply has become, though a deal of that size would likely face steep financing and Korean regulatory hurdles.
