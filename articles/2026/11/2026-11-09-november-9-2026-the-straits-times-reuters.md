# [November 9, 2026] · [The Straits Times (Reuters)]

Utilities and data-center developers are buying used turbines and adapting jet engines as new turbine deliveries stretch toward 2030–31, Reuters reports, with worldwide orders above 100 GW against 60–70 GW of annual manufacturing capacity. Norway's grid operator Statnett paused new data-center connection reservations after receiving 26,000 MW of requests this year, more than the country's current capacity, until a licensing scheme favoring defense, health and other critical users is in place. The Wall Street Journal reports builders are weighing their options as winning candidates promise moratoriums in key states.
