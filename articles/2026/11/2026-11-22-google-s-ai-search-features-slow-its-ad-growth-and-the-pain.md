# Google's AI search features slow its ad growth, and the pain spreads to Apple and Reddit

The Information reports that Google's AI-driven search features are slowing growth in search advertising, and the effects are spreading to companies that depend on Google search. Google shares are down 18% since its latest earnings report, Apple's services growth slowed to 10% from 12%, and Reddit shares are down 34%.
