# Trump threatens a "tremendous tax" on US companies that build data centers abroad

President Trump said US companies will be banned from building data centers in other countries and will face a "tremendous tax" if they do, acknowledging he had not yet told his own staff. Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta and Oracle already run data centers abroad, often to serve nearby customers and meet regulatory requirements, and no tax rate, mechanism or effective date was given. The threat lands as CNBC's survey finds 64% of registered voters oppose an AI data center within 10 miles of their home, squeezing hyperscalers between federal pressure to build at home and local resistance.
