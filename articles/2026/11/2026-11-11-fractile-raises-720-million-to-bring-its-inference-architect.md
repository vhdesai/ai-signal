# Fractile raises $720 million to bring its inference architecture to market

Fractile raised a $720 million Series C, with backing from Britain’s Sovereign AI Fund and investors including Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and Redpoint. The company is developing a hardware-and-compiler stack using stacked DRAM to address inference memory and speed constraints, with its CEO targeting first chips and systems next year. The financing expands the field of alternatives to incumbent AI hardware, but it is a development milestone rather than evidence of shipped production systems or independently demonstrated superiority.
