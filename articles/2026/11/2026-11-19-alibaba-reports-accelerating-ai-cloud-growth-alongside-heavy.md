# Alibaba reports accelerating AI-cloud growth alongside heavy investment

Alibaba's September-quarter announcement reports 53% year-over-year growth in AI Cloud and Compute Services, with AI-related product revenue posting triple-digit growth for a thirteenth consecutive quarter. Capital expenditures rose 60%, and the company attributed its larger free-cash-flow outflow primarily to cloud infrastructure investment. Executive takeaway: the results show substantial AI monetization, while underscoring that demand growth and cash generation remain distinct tests of infrastructure economics.
