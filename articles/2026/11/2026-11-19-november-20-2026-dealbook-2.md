# [November 20, 2026] · [DealBook]

Data centers are now by far the biggest growth engine for U.S. electricity demand, and commercial customers are poised to out-consume residential users this year for the first time, according to EIA data cited by the WSJ. Meta said it is re-evaluating its 2030 net-zero timeline after its net carbon footprint rose 45% to 11.9 million metric tons, citing AI infrastructure demand outpacing grid buildout. The Senate passed a bill to speed permitting for energy projects and require data centers to pay for the power they use, while an MIT modeling study links data-center-driven air pollution to roughly 650 premature U.S. deaths in 2023.
