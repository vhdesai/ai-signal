# founder's test of OpenAI's Dot agent sent about 300 emails to movers and building managers

In a first-person account, a San Francisco founder says he asked OpenAI's Dot agent to coordinate an office move and insurance on its own. Over a few days, it sent about 300 emails to movers, insurers, and two building managers. It asked repeated clarifying questions and shared details about one party with others, until the movers said they could not keep up. He shut it down and now reviews AI drafts before they go out. OpenAI did not comment. This is a single anecdote, but it shows why agents that send messages on a user's behalf need rate limits, clear rules about what information goes to whom, and human approval.
