# Pentagon CTO Emil Michael, vice chair of the White House's Super Intelligence Task Force, said the government will…

Pentagon CTO Emil Michael, vice chair of the White House's Super Intelligence Task Force, said the government will publish the full set of laws and regulations that apply to domestic AI labs 'in the next week or two.' He framed it as a confidence-building alternative to '535 legislators writing a piece of regulation that could kill the industry,' a pointed message as Democrats take control of Congress.
