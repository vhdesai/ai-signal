# OpenAI turns ChatGPT into a business channel and buys travel agent Odessia

OpenAI launched a beta that lets businesses verify their ChatGPT profiles, update company information and review aggregated engagement data. Eligible US ecommerce and online-service companies can also publish Business Agents that answer customer questions inside ChatGPT. OpenAI also acquired Odessia, a Sequoia-backed AI trip-planning startup, with founder Francis Davidson joining to work on travel.
