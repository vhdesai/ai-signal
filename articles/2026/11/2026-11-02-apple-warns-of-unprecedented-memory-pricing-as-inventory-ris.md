# Apple warns of "unprecedented" memory pricing as inventory rises 154% and margin guidance falls

On CEO John Ternus's first earnings call, Apple reported $14.54 billion in inventory, up 154% from a year earlier. It guided December-quarter gross margin to 45-46%, down from 47.9% in the September quarter, mainly because of memory costs. Ternus said memory costs will keep rising after December. Apple's manufacturing purchase obligations rose $33 billion between the June and September quarters, and COO Sabih Khan did not rule out further price increases on Apple devices.
