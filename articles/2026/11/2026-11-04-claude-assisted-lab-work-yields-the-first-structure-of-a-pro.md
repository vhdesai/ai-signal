# Claude-assisted lab work yields the first structure of a prokaryotic vault particle

Anthropic's biology lab reports the first 3D structure of a vault particle found in prokaryotic cells, with Claude assisting in sequence-literature search, optimizing particle isolation and mapping the structure. Vault function remains unknown, and the work is a preprint submitted to bioRxiv rather than peer-reviewed research. It adds to evidence of AI acting as a collaborator inside human-directed experiments.
