# Baidu's ERNIE Evolve agents beat public benchmark records and speed up its production models

ERNIE Evolve gives AI agents a code repository, a goal, and success criteria. Agents choose research directions, edit code, and run experiments, and a hidden evaluator and a separate reviewer reject changes that game the scoring. Baidu reports new bests on three of four public measures, including NanoGPT Speedrun at 38.9 seconds versus the previous 39.9-second record. On ERNIE-5.5-Flash it increased production inference throughput by 33.7%, and generation speed on its in-house Kunlunxin P800 chips reached 6.2 times baseline. The results are self-reported and the system has not been released. Baidu describes it as a step toward AI that improves itself, joining similar efforts from Tencent and others.
