# Anthropic hires a $400,000-a-year natural-gas lead as off-grid data centers drive up emissions

Anthropic is recruiting a Gas Supply and Transport Lead paying up to $405,000, and research firm Cleanview now ranks it among the leading off-grid data-center developers, a category it was not in last year. Off-grid gas engines and turbines let developers skip grid queues and federal permitting, but climate scientist Zeke Hausfather estimates they cause 63% to 80% more greenhouse-gas emissions than grid power. Bipartisan permitting legislation in Congress could ease grid connections, though it would also make data centers pay for grid upgrades. Research Breakthroughs CHIPS RESEARCH
