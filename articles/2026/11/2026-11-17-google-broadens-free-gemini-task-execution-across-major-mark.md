# Google broadens free Gemini task execution across major markets

Crypto Briefing reports that Gemini task features are now free in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The reported capabilities extend beyond answers into business calls, Google Pay transactions, messaging, and Workspace actions. The competitive implication is wider distribution of action-taking assistants through an existing consumer-services stack; authorization and recovery from mistaken actions remain important deployment considerations.
