# The White House AI task force's second meeting focuses on cyber threats, critical infrastructure and the labs' safety accord

According to an administration official, the task force met Thursday to work on carrying out an accord between major AI companies, including Anthropic and OpenAI, and to review the administration's voluntary framework for testing models. Cyber threats and critical infrastructure were central topics.
