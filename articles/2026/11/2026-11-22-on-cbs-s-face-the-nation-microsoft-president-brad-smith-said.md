# On CBS's Face the Nation, Microsoft President Brad Smith said AI has made flaws far easier to find, setting off a global race to patch before attackers can exploit them — often with less time to test fixes, especially on older systems. "We have to be prepared, frankly, for the coming months," he said, while stressing that Microsoft's recent outage was not AI-related or a cyberattack. He urged better update testing, replacement of aging infrastructure and closer government–industry threat sharing.
