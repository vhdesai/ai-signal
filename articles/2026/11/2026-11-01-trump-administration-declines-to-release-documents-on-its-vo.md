# Trump administration declines to release documents on its voluntary AI testing framework

In a September settlement of a Freedom of Information Act lawsuit, the administration had agreed with the Protect Democracy Project to release key documents. In a letter sent Friday, it declined to produce them. The decision makes it harder to scrutinize how the federal government evaluates frontier models under its voluntary regime.
