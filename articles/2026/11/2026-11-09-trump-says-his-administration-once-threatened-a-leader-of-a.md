# Trump says his administration once threatened a leader of a major AI company with jail

On "60 Minutes" Sunday, President Trump said his team called an unnamed leader of "one of the big" AI companies over a report that the person was "doing something bad with AI" and told them to prepare for jail. He said the person changed course, and that the conduct was probably not malicious, though it "coulda been." Trump said he did not object to safety guardrails but would not let the US fall behind China. The White House and the Justice Department did not comment.
