# AI likeness licensing puts consent and reuse terms in focus

By Lucas Ropek. Published November 21, 2026, 16:00 UTC / 08:00 PST. Original interview reporting describes Aki Avni licensing his likeness to Shortical for an AI microdrama that premiered the previous week. The broader public face-licensing offering had not yielded signed contracts, and compensation was undisclosed. This is not a fresh launch; the executive implication is to specify consent, reuse permissions, and royalty terms before licensing a digital likeness. Products & Tools ANALYSIS VB
