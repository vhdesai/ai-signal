# Texas clears SpaceX's 1.456GW supercomputer power plant as Abbott lifts a data-center permit freeze

Texas regulators approved an air permit for SpaceX's 1.456-gigawatt gas plant to power a planned supercomputer in the Rio Grande Valley, as outgoing Governor Greg Abbott's administration resumed data-center permits it had frozen pending a grid review. The timing lets projects advance before Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa, who vowed to pause unpermitted data centers, takes office on January 19. Texas is the country's most active data-center market, with hundreds of billions of dollars of projects awaiting approval. Academic Research RESEARCH
