# Google's open-source TorchTPU takes aim at Nvidia's CUDA moat

Google has open-sourced TorchTPU, a software layer that makes it easier to move PyTorch workloads built for Nvidia GPUs onto its tensor processing units. Developed with Meta, itself a TPU customer, it targets the gap that kept developers on CUDA, since Google's own JAX framework was widely seen as clunky. With Nvidia under leadership and export scrutiny, lower switching costs could help Google, AMD and Amazon win more AI chip business.
