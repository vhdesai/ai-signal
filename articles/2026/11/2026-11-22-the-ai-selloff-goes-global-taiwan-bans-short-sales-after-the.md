# The AI selloff goes global: Taiwan bans short sales after the Taiex's worst week since 1991 as Chinese AI stocks shed $1.7 trillion

Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission banned short selling of local stocks below the previous close from November 23, raised the minimum short-sale margin to 150% and stopped accepting new put-warrant listings, its third round of curbs this month, after the chip-heavy Taiex fell 6.2% on Friday and almost 18% for the week, its worst since 1991. Chinese AI-linked stocks have lost a combined $1.7 trillion in market value since late June, with Hong Kong's Hang Seng Tech index at a two-year low and ChiNext down 31% this year amid tighter US limits on access to Nvidia processors. In the US, the S&P 500 lost 8.6% for the week and the Nasdaq 10%, its biggest weekly drop in more than six years, and The Tactical Allocation Letter notes the selling has spread beyond AI to banks and small caps as the Fed leans toward a December hike.
