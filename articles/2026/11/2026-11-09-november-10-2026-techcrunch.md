# [November 10, 2026] · [TechCrunch]

At MAX, Adobe released a local Model Context Protocol server that exposes Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and Premiere features to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, alongside an always-on agent and a Figma-like Firefly Builder prototyping tool. Apple introduced one-time $49.99 Intelligence Usage Packs that extend cloud AI features in Logic Pro and other Creator Studio apps after monthly limits are reached. Third-party assistants are becoming a front door to software, and heavy AI use is being priced as consumption rather than bundled into subscriptions.
