# [November 13, 2026] · [DealBook]

A global outage tied to Exchange Online spread from Outlook and Teams to SharePoint, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 services from Thursday into Friday, with Microsoft saying restored servers became unresponsive again under load and that it saw no sign of malicious activity. DealBook reports Richemont delayed its earnings release and a Colorado sheriff's office could not process bond payments. Microsoft said early Saturday that service was restored and the email backlog processed, after an outage the Journal put at 19 hours. As AI assistants embed in productivity suites, their availability inherits the reliability of the underlying platform.
