# Q&A: Why building math muscles remains important in the AI era

In a published MIT News interview, John Urschel discusses people working with AI on longstanding mathematical problems while preserving independent reasoning and verification skills. The discussion is relevant to AI-assisted science and to training researchers who can check an automated result rather than simply accept it. This is interview coverage, not a new paper or a newly announced mathematical result.
