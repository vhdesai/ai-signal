# [November 7, 2026] · [The Information]

The Information's weekend column reports a Dutch bank is trying to quickly offload about $500 million of data-center loans, including debt tied to OpenAI's Stargate, after Thursday's 5.5% Nasdaq drop, while Google's $15 billion Malaysia expansion shows builders looking abroad. The Tactical Allocation Letter's weekly recap ties the rout to investors pricing in a Democratic Congress slowing data-center construction, after Oracle fell 8% earlier in the week on plans to raise $10 billion in stock and $10 billion in bonds for its AI buildout. Chip stocks rebounded 4.2% on Friday, with AMD up 11%, but the letter warns that AI stocks now fall whenever anything threatens the spending plans that lifted them.
