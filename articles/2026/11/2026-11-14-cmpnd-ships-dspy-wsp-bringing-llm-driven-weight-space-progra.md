# Cmpnd ships dspy-wsp, bringing LLM-driven "weight-space programming" to DSPy

Cmpnd released dspy-wsp, an MIT-licensed DSPy optimizer in which a frontier model (GPT-6-Astra in tests) reads a small model's weights and edits them directly, with no gradients or training data. It builds on MIT's "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming" research. For about $5–$10 per run, Cmpnd reports gains of 1.9 to 20.0 points on decision models from 0.6B to 9B parameters. Gains were smallest on the largest model, and runs take 78–167 minutes. The tool is experimental, but it points to cheap, auditable patches for small models as an alternative to fine-tuning.
