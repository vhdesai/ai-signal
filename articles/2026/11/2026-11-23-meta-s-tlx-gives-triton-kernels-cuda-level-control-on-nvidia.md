# Meta's TLX gives Triton kernels CUDA-level control on Nvidia and AMD GPUs

Meta detailed TLX (Triton Low-Level Extensions), an MIT-licensed extension that lets Python Triton kernels reach GPU features previously available only through CUDA, on Nvidia Hopper and Blackwell and AMD MI300 and MI350 chips. Meta reports H100 matrix-multiply performance within -0.9% to +3.7% of cuBLAS, a 4x speedup on an ads-inference kernel and about 87% of FlashAttention-4 on B200, all self-reported. Portable kernels that approach hand-tuned performance further weaken CUDA lock-in.
