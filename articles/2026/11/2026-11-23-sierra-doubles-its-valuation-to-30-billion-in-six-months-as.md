# Sierra doubles its valuation to $30 billion in six months as AI customer-service revenue takes off

Bret Taylor's Sierra raised $1.75 billion in a Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed, doubling the $15 billion valuation it reached in May. It serves nearly half of the Fortune 50, including BNY, HSBC, Wells Fargo and JPMorganChase, and says outcome-based pricing helped double run-rate revenue to $400 million in five months. Customer-service agents remain one of the clearest enterprise AI categories with real revenue, even as public AI stocks swing.
