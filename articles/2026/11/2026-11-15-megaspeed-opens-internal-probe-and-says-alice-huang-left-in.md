# Megaspeed opens internal probe and says Alice Huang left in 2025

Singapore-based Megaspeed told CNBC that Alice Huang left the company effective Aug. 30, 2025. It said it has hired outside counsel to investigate media reports about its business and supply chain, which it says are "not supported by verifiable evidence." Megaspeed calls itself a "core business partner" of Nvidia's cloud business in the region and says it complies with US export controls.
