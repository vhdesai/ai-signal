# NEW Meta expands Muse's business-calling capability to paid subscribers

Meta's Muse can now place calls to U.S. businesses for paid subscribers, following a September test, with plans starting at $20 per month. The rollout brings another agent capability into everyday transactions, but acceptance is not universal: TechCrunch reports that some businesses hang up on AI callers.
