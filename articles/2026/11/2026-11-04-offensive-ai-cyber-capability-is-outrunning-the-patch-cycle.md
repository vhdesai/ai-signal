# Offensive AI cyber capability is outrunning the patch cycle

Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon with leading scores on SEC-bench Pro (88.2%), ExploitGym (54.8%) and CWE-bench (71.0%), initially limiting access to selected defenders. Epoch AI showed that the open-weight GLM 5.3 could build working Firefox exploits from fixes that were published but not yet shipped, at a marginal cost Berkeley RDI puts around $1,000. WSJ Pro reports attackers now use AI to turn public threat-intelligence posts into active exploitation, and The Information notes a startup CTO used a specialized OpenAI model to find a vulnerability in widely used open-source software. Sources: Google | Epoch AI | WSJ Pro CyberSecurity
