# Tencent positions Hy4 flash for low-cost, long-context deployment

Source: Tencent Hunyuan; Tencent News Tencent introduced a multimodal model with a one-million-token context window and 12 billion active parameters out of 224 billion total. Published API rates are 0.5 yuan per million input tokens and 2 yuan per million output tokens, with deployment already spanning Tencent’s cloud and workplace products. Tencent says OpenRouter distribution and an open-source release will follow; those future commitments should be distinguished from current availability.
