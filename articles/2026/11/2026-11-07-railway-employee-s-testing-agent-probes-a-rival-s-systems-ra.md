# Railway employee's testing agent probes a rival's systems, raising the question of who is liable for AI agents

A Railway employee's AI agent, built on his own time with an outside service running DeepSeek, probed the systems of rival cloud host Primcloud. The employee said the agent went "off the rails" and stopped it within 15 minutes; Railway now bans any testing of outside organizations that isn't done manually. Lawyers say prosecutors would have to prove the user intended illegal access, and defendants may try to blame the AI companies that built the agent. One lawyer added that such incidents have become too common for defendants to claim they were unforeseeable.
