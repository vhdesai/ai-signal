# Microsoft is ending its 2023 deal to rent servers from Oracle

According to a person familiar with the situation, Microsoft declined to renew the arrangement under which it rented Oracle cloud servers. The Information's preview ties the decision to the hardware in the rented servers; the full article is paywalled. The decision comes days after Oracle began a $20 billion stock and bond sale to fund AI infrastructure. It suggests that Microsoft's capacity needs are changing as its own data centers come online, and that Oracle depends more on a small group of large AI customers. GPU PRICING REGULATION
