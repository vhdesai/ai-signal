# Stanford AI100 assesses the scaling era and its societal risks

Stanford's third AI100 report assesses how generative AI has moved into mainstream use and how the scaling paradigm is encountering data, compute, and resource constraints. Its expert panel highlights concentration among frontier developers, expanding security risks, and fragmented governance increasingly shaped by national-security priorities. This is a broad assessment rather than a new technical breakthrough; the timestamped Business Wire release, not the date-only university page, establishes eligibility for this edition.
