# Chatbot election answers draw scrutiny days before the midterms

AFP reports that the November 3 vote is the first federal election in which millions of voters rely on AI tools for political information. A study by evaluation startup Forum AI found almost 40% of chatbot answers to election queries across eight models contained at least one factual error. Organizations deploying assistants should route voting questions to authoritative sources.
