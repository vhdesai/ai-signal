# Former OpenAI researcher Will DePue analyzed open weights from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group and reports…

Former OpenAI researcher Will DePue analyzed open weights from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group and reports hardware fingerprints consistent with Blackwell training in models including Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4. Training offshore, for example in Malaysia, would not necessarily violate current rules, which is why critics want controls on who uses chips, not just where they ship. Arriving amid the Megaspeed inquiry, the findings raise Nvidia's potential corporate exposure and strengthen the case for tighter controls.
