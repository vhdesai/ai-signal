# Private benchmarks widen the open-weight gap from 3 points to 12

Artificial Analysis overhauled its Intelligence Index to keep many test sets private, making it harder to train to the benchmark. The best open-weight models, including Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro, now trail leader Claude Opus 5.5 by a wide margin, and even Claude Haiku 5.5 beats every open-weight model. Teams planning to swap to cheaper open models should validate on their own private evaluations first.
