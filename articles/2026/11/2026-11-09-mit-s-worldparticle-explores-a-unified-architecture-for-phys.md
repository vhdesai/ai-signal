# MIT's WorldParticle explores a unified architecture for physical simulation

MIT describes WorldParticle, a transformer-based framework that represents materials as particles and predicts their motion instead of relying exclusively on hand-coded physical solvers. The architecture was applied to six categories, including cloth, fluids, granular materials, and molecular dynamics. Crucially, it was trained separately for the categories: this is a unified architecture, not one trained model that simulates every material. Today's article covers work whose preprint dates to May, with presentation planned at SIGGRAPH Asia.
