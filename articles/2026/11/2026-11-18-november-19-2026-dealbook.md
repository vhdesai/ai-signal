# [November 19, 2026] · [DealBook]

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde said AI carries a very ambitious price valuation that stands to be corrected eventually, while Sam Altman dismissed the ups and downs of valuations and called the trend strong. Greg Brockman said models are now capable enough that safety, security and alignment have become a huge cultural shift at OpenAI, though Satya Nadella sounded skeptical that alignment is fully possible. Altman also criticized Elon Musk's suggestion that AI could keep humanity as its pet, and Polymarket's Shayne Coplan said AI agents were trading on his laptop while he spoke.
