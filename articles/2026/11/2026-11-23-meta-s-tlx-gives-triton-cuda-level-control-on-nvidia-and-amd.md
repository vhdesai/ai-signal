# Meta's TLX gives Triton CUDA-level control on Nvidia and AMD GPUs

Meta detailed TLX (Triton Low-Level Extensions). This MIT-licensed extension gives Python Triton kernels fine-grained control of GPU features previously reachable only through CUDA, on Nvidia Hopper and Blackwell and AMD MI300 and MI350 chips. Meta reports H100 matrix-multiply performance within -0.9% to +3.7% of Nvidia's cuBLAS library, a 4x speedup on one ads-inference kernel, and about 87% of FlashAttention-4 on B200. Meta's own engineering post is dated Nov. 10, and these performance numbers are self-reported. Portable kernels that perform close to hand-written code reduce dependence on CUDA, adding to the multi-vendor trend Anthropic signaled last week. Infrastructure M&A CUSTOM SILICON
