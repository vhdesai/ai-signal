# [November 11, 2026] · [Google]

The new Gemini Decisions API and its first model answer yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric questions with a confidence score instead of generating text, and can evaluate up to 1,000 questions against shared multimodal input in one call. Pricing is $0.10 per million input tokens with no output tokens billed, which Google says makes it nearly five times cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on its benchmark suite, with tool selection, policy checks, routing and evaluation as target uses. Business Insider separately reports Google is letting employees test Hydrogen, a fast, cost-efficient Gemini model similar to its Flash line.
