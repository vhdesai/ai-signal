# [November 6, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Stocks reversed sharply on Thursday as fears centered on AI companies: the Nasdaq fell 5.5%, the S&P 500 lost 4.4% and the PHLX Semiconductor Index dropped more than 12%, erasing $2.5 trillion of Nasdaq value in the worst one-day destruction of value in its history. Voters had backed candidates and ballot measures to ban or slow data-center development, and Meta President Dina Powell McCormick said at WSJ Tech that getting data centers built is "going to be harder" after the midterms. Strategists also pointed to weeks of worry about falling hyperscaler free cash flow, while benchmarks in Seoul, Tokyo and Taipei slid into or near bear markets. Futures pointed to a partial rebound on Friday, led by chipmakers and neoclouds.
