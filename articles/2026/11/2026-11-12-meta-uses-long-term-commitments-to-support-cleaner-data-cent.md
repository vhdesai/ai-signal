# Meta uses long-term commitments to support cleaner data-center supply chains

Meta announced commitments supporting lower-carbon cement, near-zero-emission steel, electric freight, and recycled plastics, including at least 180,000 tons of Sublime Systems cement production. The agreements use Environmental Attribute Certificates, so supported materials and services may enter local markets rather than physically supply Meta facilities. The distinction matters for procurement and sustainability reporting: financing industrial decarbonization is not equivalent to directly sourcing the supported materials for a particular data center.
