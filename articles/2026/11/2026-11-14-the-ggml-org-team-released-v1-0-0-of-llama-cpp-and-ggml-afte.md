# The ggml-org team released v1.0.0 of llama.cpp and ggml after 3.5 years, adopting semantic versioning in which breaking…

The ggml-org team released v1.0.0 of llama.cpp and ggml after 3.5 years, adopting semantic versioning in which breaking changes to the public C API require a major-version bump. Distribution now covers Docker, Debian, Homebrew, conda-forge, MacPorts, Winget and an iOS/macOS XCFramework. A stable ABI lowers the maintenance risk for the many products that embed llama.cpp for on-device and private inference.
