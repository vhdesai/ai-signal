# MIT's CheMeleon model predicts molecular properties from very little data

CheMeleon is an open-source model from William Green's group at MIT, built with three BASF researchers. It is pretrained on about 1,600 computed chemical properties for a million molecules and can then be fine-tuned to predict a specific property when little experimental data exists. Nine of the top 20 entries in this year's OpenADMET drug-property prediction competition used it. At least one pharmaceutical company has made it the default starting point for property modeling. The paper appeared in the Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling and shows that domain-specific pretraining pays off where lab data is scarce.
