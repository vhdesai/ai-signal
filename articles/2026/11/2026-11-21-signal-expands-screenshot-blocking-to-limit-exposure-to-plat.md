# Signal expands screenshot blocking to limit exposure to platform AI agents

TechCrunch reports that Signal is enabling screenshot blocking by default on Android and iOS, extending an approach already used on Windows. The change addresses the risk that operating-system AI assistants could capture private conversations; users can disable the restriction, and Signal acknowledges that the protection is incomplete. The article falls within this digest’s publication window, but the underlying announcement was made on November 20.
