# NVIDIA reports few-demonstration robotics results with GR00T 2

NVIDIA introduced GR00T 2 at CoRL and reports average success of 71% across 50 tasks with one demonstration per task, rising to 95% with five demonstrations. These are vendor evaluation results rather than independent validation, and task-level generalization still needs testing in deployment settings.
