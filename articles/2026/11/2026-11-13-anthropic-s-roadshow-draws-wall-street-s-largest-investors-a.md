# Anthropic's roadshow draws Wall Street's largest investors as orders from China are reportedly rebuffed

JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon hosted Dario Amodei at a fireside chat at the bank's headquarters on Friday. Attendees included Tiger Global's Chase Coleman and other large institutional investors. Bloomberg separately reported that bankers are turning away share orders from mainland China and Hong Kong institutions, mirroring the approach SpaceX took. With listing expected around November 19, the IPO's pricing and allocation will be the market's first direct read on a frontier lab's value. That comes one day after OpenAI's private round came in below its own target. AGENTS FUNDING LANGCHAIN
