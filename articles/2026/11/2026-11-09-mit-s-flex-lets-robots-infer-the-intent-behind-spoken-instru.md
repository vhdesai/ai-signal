# MIT's FLEX lets robots infer the intent behind spoken instructions

Researchers from MIT and NYU built FLEX, which uses a large language model to turn casual instructions into general preferences that guide how a robot plans its movements. In simulation, a robot told "Don't spill on the laptop" also learned to avoid a nearby phone, without being told. The work will be presented at the Conference on Robot Learning this month. It addresses a practical problem for home and assistive robots: users will not spell out every constraint. AI Safety & Policy POLICY WHITE HOUSE AI SAFETY
