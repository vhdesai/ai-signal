# Tesla opens Cybercab pre-orders for personal use, limited to robotaxi geofences with a $299 monthly fee

Select customers can now pre-order the $29,990 Cybercab for personal use, with deliveries estimated for December 2026 in a few Texas cities. It drives unsupervised only inside Tesla's robotaxi geofences and carries a $299 monthly operating fee, which Elon Musk called too high. Musk said wider availability depends on regulatory approval.
