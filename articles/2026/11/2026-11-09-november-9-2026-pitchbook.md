# November 9, 2026 · PitchBook

Anthropic plans to begin formal marketing to IPO investors this week and still aims to list the week of November 16, though The Information says two weeks of AI-stock volatility could push it below the $1.5 trillion valuation and $80 billion-plus raise it had been expected to seek. OpenAI closed the first $11 billion of a $20 billion round at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, down from an original goal of $30 billion at $1.4 trillion, and is expected to brief investors on third-quarter results. PitchBook adds that Thinking Machines Lab is raising $5 billion led by Accel at a $40 billion pre-money valuation, while Meta strategy chief Dave Wehner is leaving to become CFO of inference startup Fireworks, which is targeting a valuation of at least $30 billion.
