# Australia plans an AI safety regulator and mandatory protections

The Australian Government has announced plans for a targeted AI safety regulator and mandatory protections, with the proposed regulator able to impose significant penalties. Legislation is planned for Parliament in 2027, so the announcement should not be treated as an enacted regulatory regime. The next substantive milestone is the proposed legislation, which will determine the framework’s legal scope and obligations. Read the original — Australian Government SAFETY 24–48H EXCEPTION
