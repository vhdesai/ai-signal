# Nadella highlights the security exposure of hard-to-patch infrastructure

At the DealBook Summit, Satya Nadella warned that internet-connected infrastructure includes firmware and operational systems that cannot readily be patched, creating exposure as AI accelerates vulnerability discovery. He also described the tension between immediate remediation and the instability introduced when patch testing must be compressed. Executive takeaway: cyber readiness should include modernization and containment for legacy systems, alongside faster detection and carefully managed emergency patching.
