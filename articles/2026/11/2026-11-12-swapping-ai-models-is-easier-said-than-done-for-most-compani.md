# Swapping AI models is easier said than done: For most companies, it takes weeks, VB Intelligence finds

VentureBeat’s October survey found that 70% of respondents whose organizations replaced a production model reported weeks or more of additional engineering work. Its reporting locates the switching burden in prompts, harnesses, evaluations and governance, not simply API compatibility. The finding supports budgeting for model migration and regression testing, while the publisher cautions that changes in the respondent mix limit month-to-month comparisons.
