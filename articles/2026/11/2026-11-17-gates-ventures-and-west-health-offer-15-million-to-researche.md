# Gates Ventures and West Health offer $15 million to researchers building AI tools for Alzheimer's

Gates Ventures and West Health will provide up to $15 million in philanthropic funding to scientists developing AI tools for Alzheimer's research, alongside a new research network called the AlzInsights AI Collective. The push aims to use AI to mine existing data, develop new drugs and match patients with clinical trials. Bill Gates, whose father died of Alzheimer's in 2020, has pointed to earlier blood-test diagnosis and new drugs as signs more breakthroughs are coming.
