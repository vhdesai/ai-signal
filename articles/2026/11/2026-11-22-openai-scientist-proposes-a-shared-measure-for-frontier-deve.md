# OpenAI scientist proposes a shared measure for frontier-development limits

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki proposes using pretraining perplexity, alongside other measurements, to help laboratories agree on development thresholds tied to progress in understanding and monitoring AI. OpenAI's underlying proposal shares internal measurements but acknowledges problems with cross-lab comparability and improvements that occur outside pretraining. This is a governance proposal, not an adopted industry agreement or an enforceable development limit.
