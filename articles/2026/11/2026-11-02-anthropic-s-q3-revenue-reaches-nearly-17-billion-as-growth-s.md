# Anthropic's Q3 revenue reaches nearly $17 billion as growth slows and losses widen

The Information reports that Anthropic's amended IPO filing shows nearly $17 billion in revenue for the quarter that ended in September, up 47% from the second quarter. The filing also shows a $23.3 billion net loss and a return to an operating loss. Revenue growth is still very high but slowing ahead of an IPO expected later this month. For enterprise buyers, the figures show how far compute spending at frontier labs still runs ahead of revenue, which bears on how long current prices last and how stable vendors are. The figures come from The Information's briefing summary; the full article is paywalled. M&A CONTACT CENTER AGENTS
