# AI infrastructure becomes a target: Fireworks breach exposes model weights as export-control and insider risks rise

Fireworks disclosed that attackers accessed some customers' model weights, datasets and billing data in its second incident in weeks, and The Information reports large customers including Harvey are weighing a switch, threatening a planned $30 billion fundraise. Hugging Face policy chief Irene Solaiman warned repeated attacks could force open platforms to restrict access, while security executive Lisa Thiergart told The Information that more spies inside US AI companies are pretty likely. The Information also reports White House engagement on export-control provisions in the defense bill and a renewed Commerce push on remote access to advanced chips, raising the policy stakes of the Nvidia scandal.
