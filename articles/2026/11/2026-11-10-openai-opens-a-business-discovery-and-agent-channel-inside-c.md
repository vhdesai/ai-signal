# OpenAI opens a business-discovery and agent channel inside ChatGPT

OpenAI introduced a beta service that lets businesses verify their ChatGPT profiles, update company information, and review aggregated engagement data. Eligible U.S. ecommerce and online-service companies can also publish Business Agents that answer customer questions inside ChatGPT. The strategic implication is a new customer-acquisition channel in which an AI assistant mediates discovery and commercial interaction.
