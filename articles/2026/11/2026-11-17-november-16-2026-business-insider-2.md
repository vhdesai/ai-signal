# [November 16, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh said the central bank's jobs task force is modeling scenarios for how AI could reshape the economy, including whether workers will be displaced. Apollo's Scott Kleinman said the firm plans to grow head count 3% to 5% a year instead of its usual 12% to 15%, mainly because of AI, and a Caltech math professor told Business Insider he no longer grades homework because students use AI. A Resume Now report covered by CIO Dive finds judgment and decision-making are now among the most wanted skills as technologists become managers of AI.
