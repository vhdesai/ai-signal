# LVMH’s media group partners with OpenAI on newsroom tools and linked journalism

LVMH’s media group announced a strategic partnership to deploy OpenAI tools, including ChatGPT Enterprise, across publications such as Les Echos, Le Parisien, Paris Match and Challenges. The agreement includes attributed ChatGPT summaries and excerpts that link readers to original journalism, alongside newsroom support for document research, comparison, preliminary translation and training. The announcement combines internal productivity tools with a new route for audiences to discover the group’s reporting.
