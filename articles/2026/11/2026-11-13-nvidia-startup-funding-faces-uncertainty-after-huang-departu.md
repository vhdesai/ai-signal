# Nvidia startup funding faces uncertainty after Huang departure

TechCrunch New analysis examines the funding implications of Jensen Huang’s earlier departure, rather than reporting a fresh resignation. TechCrunch cites PitchBook data showing Nvidia participated in 105 funding rounds this year. Investors expect interim CEO Colette Kress to slow the pace of startup investments, potentially affecting neoclouds and open-weight model developers. Those views are investor expectations, not a confirmed change in Nvidia policy.
