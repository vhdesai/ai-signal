# Policy authors call for interoperable AI safety practices

By Renata Dwan and Amandeep Singh Gill. Publication-time exception: the publisher displays November 22, 2026, without a publication time; precise inclusion in the UTC/PST source window could not be verified. Ahead of the November 23 Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety, the authors advocate international cooperation to support national action. Their proposals include independent pre-deployment evaluation, transparent safety information, interoperable incident reporting, and response capacity for countries without substantial infrastructure. This is policy opinion, not a new law or summit outcome, and descriptions of existing frameworks were not independently audited.
