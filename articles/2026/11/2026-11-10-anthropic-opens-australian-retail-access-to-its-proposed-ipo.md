# Anthropic opens Australian retail access to its proposed IPO

CNBC reports that Australian retail investors can apply through participating brokers for shares in Anthropic's proposed offering, with an indicative price range of $70–$78. The report puts the combined IPO and private-placement fundraising at up to $90.4 billion. For the sector, the transaction would provide a substantial public-market test of the capital requirements and valuations attached to frontier AI businesses.
