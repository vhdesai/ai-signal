# Anthropic opens an Australian retail channel for its IPO

Australian retail investors can apply for Anthropic shares through participating brokers led by CommSec, with the offer scheduled to close November 17. The shares will trade on Nasdaq rather than the Australian exchange. The offering extends the distribution of a major AI capital raise beyond the U.S. institutional market.
