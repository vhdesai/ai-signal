# New Google introduces Gemini Decision 1 for high-volume decision workloads

Gemini Decision 1 brings a specialized model to Google’s Gemini Decisions API, supporting up to 1,000 questions over the same input in a single request. VentureBeat reports pricing of $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charge, alongside support for mixed media such as recordings and PDFs. Google’s launch tests and an independent ranking present different pictures of relative performance, making workload-specific evaluation more useful than treating launch benchmarks as a general capability claim.
