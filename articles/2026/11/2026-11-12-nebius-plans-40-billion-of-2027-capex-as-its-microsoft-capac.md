# Nebius plans $40 billion of 2027 capex as its Microsoft capacity slips and Nvidia-backed deals face new doubts

Nebius will fund the buildout mostly through customer prepayments and loans backed by customer contracts. Only about half of its Microsoft capacity at Vineland, New Jersey, from a contract worth up to $19.4 billion, will now be delivered by year-end. The rest is pushed to the second quarter of 2027, and the contract allows Microsoft to cancel and impose penalties for late delivery. The Information reports that deals by Nscale and Firmus depend on Nvidia continuing its investment pace, which is now uncertain after Huang's exit.
