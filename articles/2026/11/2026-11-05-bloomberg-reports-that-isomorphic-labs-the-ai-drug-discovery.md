# Bloomberg reports that Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug-discovery company spun out of Google DeepMind, is in talks to raise…

Bloomberg reports that Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug-discovery company spun out of Google DeepMind, is in talks to raise about $5B at roughly a $50B valuation to expand its programs. Terms are not final. If completed, it would be one of the largest private rounds ever for AI-driven biotech.
