# Sierra raises $1.75 billion at a $30 billion valuation as AI customer-service demand takes off

Bret Taylor's Sierra raised a $1.75 billion Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed at a $30 billion valuation, doubling its value in about six months. The company says it now serves almost half of the Fortune 50, including BNY, HSBC, Wells Fargo and JPMorganChase. Its run-rate revenue doubled to $400 million in five months, helped by outcome-based pricing.
