# [November 13, 2026] · [Databricks]

Anthropic shipped Claude for Manufacturing with 17 skills for quoting jobs, diagnosing breakdowns and preparing failed-inspection reports, connecting to Autodesk Fusion and Infor, and put Claude Meetings into beta in Google Meet for Team and Enterprise customers. A Databricks Genie plugin for ChatGPT lets employees query company data using governed metric definitions while enforcing each user's existing permissions. Perplexity brought Teammates, persistent background agents that plan work and spawn sub-agents in the cloud, to its Mac app for $200-a-month Max subscribers.
