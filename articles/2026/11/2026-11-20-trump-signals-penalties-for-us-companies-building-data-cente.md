# Trump signals penalties for US companies building data centers overseas

President Donald Trump said US companies building data centers abroad could face a ban or substantial taxes, CNBC reported. He said he had not yet discussed the proposal with his staff, and the report did not establish an implemented rule or enforcement mechanism. The comments introduce policy uncertainty for hyperscalers with international AI-infrastructure plans.
