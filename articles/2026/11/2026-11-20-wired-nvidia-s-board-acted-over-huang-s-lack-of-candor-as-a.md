# Wired: Nvidia's board acted over Huang's "lack of candor" as a faction weighs his return

Sources told Wired the board's main concern was Jensen Huang's "lack of candor," not only the affairs, and chair Harvey Jones told staff it was a "necessary decision." Semafor reported that a small faction of directors wants Huang back. Nvidia also faces shareholder suits, and its general counsel has been called to testify in Washington. CNBC separately reported that Huang will testify publicly before the Senate Banking Committee, after a closed-door House interview on Dec. 1. This is no longer only a succession question: the governance and export-control exposure could take quarters to resolve. FUNDING DATA PLATFORMS
