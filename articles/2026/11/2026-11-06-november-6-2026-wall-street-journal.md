# [November 6, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

The Wall Street Journal reports AI companies are scrambling to navigate a world suddenly hostile to data centers after the midterm results. In Amsterdam, about 100 activists broke into the construction site of a planned $1 billion-plus, three-tower data center where Microsoft is the sole intended tenant, climbing cranes and hanging anti-AI banners before arrests were made. The protest opened two days of European rallies over power, water and housing, so capacity plans now need political-risk assumptions on both sides of the Atlantic.
