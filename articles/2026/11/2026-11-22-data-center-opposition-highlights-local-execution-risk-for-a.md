# Data-center opposition highlights local execution risk for AI infrastructure

In a newly published interview, Hyperscale author Paris Marx argues that electricity costs, water use, noise and construction make data centers a visible focus for broader dissatisfaction with technology companies. He questions whether transparency commitments and changes to cooling systems adequately address community concerns. His stronger environmental and economic judgments are interviewee views rather than independently established findings; the executive implication is to include local resource competition and community acceptance in infrastructure planning.
