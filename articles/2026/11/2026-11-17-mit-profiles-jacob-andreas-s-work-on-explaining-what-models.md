# MIT profiles Jacob Andreas’s work on explaining what models learn

A newly published MIT News profile examines Jacob Andreas’s research into expressing models’ learned knowledge and internal representations in natural language, alongside work on predicting the effects of training data. These directions could help researchers investigate model behavior and inform decisions about high-stakes deployment. This is a research profile, not a newly released paper or product, and it does not establish that reliable model self-explanation has been solved.
