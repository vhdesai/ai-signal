# OpenAI launches Grid Protectors, committing future campuses to cut power use when the grid is strained

Under the national program, OpenAI will work out agreements with utilities and regulators for its campuses to cut electricity use at peak times. Where possible, the campuses will also supply power from on-site generation or storage. The pilot is OpenAI's planned campus in Effingham County, Georgia. OpenAI says it will pay the full infrastructure and electric-service costs there, and its Georgia Power agreement allows up to 1,000 MW of demand to be shed on request, which OpenAI equates to the average demand of roughly 700,000 households. Coming as opposition to data centers becomes a midterm election issue, the program makes flexible power use the industry's main answer to that opposition. It may also become a condition for getting large new builds approved. ORACLE CAPITAL MARKETS
