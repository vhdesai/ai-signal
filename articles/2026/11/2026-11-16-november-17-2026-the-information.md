# [November 17, 2026] · [The Information]

Blackstone's data-center trust is buying a 75% stake in two joint ventures holding 13 new data-center shells on two AWS campuses, valued at about $5 billion, with AWS leasing them back for 12 years and options out to 32 years. It is Amazon's first sale-leaseback of its data centers, another sign hyperscalers are turning to creative financing for the buildout. The Journal's Heard on the Street notes that all of OpenAI's roughly 14GW of planned, not-yet-started capacity sits in states whose governors back a pause on data-center development, so builders with more spread-out bets look better placed.
