# Nace AI released open weights for Drex Max v1, a dense 27B multimodal model that returns calibrated probabilities over…

Nace AI released open weights for Drex Max v1, a dense 27B multimodal model that returns calibrated probabilities over typed options instead of generating text. Quantized builds and llama.cpp and Ollama forks let it run on Apple Silicon Macs with 24–48 GB of memory, and Nace reports 67.52 on the public Decision Index 0.4 (self-reported). It joins a fast-growing class of decision models for routing, triage, and guardrails that can stay on local hardware.
