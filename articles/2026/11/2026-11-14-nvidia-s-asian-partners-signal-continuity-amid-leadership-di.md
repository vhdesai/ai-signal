# Nvidia’s Asian partners signal continuity amid leadership disruption

AI infrastructure buyers have fresh government assurances to weigh against Nvidia’s leadership uncertainty. TechCrunch reports that South Korea’s Science Ministry received confirmation that Nvidia would maintain its commitment to 260,000 advanced GPUs and joint AI projects, while Taipei officials said the company’s overseas headquarters project remains on course. Executive implication: these statements support near-term planning continuity, but they are not a substitute for confirming delivery schedules and contractual commitments with suppliers.
