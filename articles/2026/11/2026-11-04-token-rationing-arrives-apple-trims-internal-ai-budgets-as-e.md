# Token rationing arrives: Apple trims internal AI budgets as enterprise AI bills climb

Bloomberg reports Apple cut some engineering teams' daily AI allowances from $300 to $200 in October, with further reductions under consideration. VentureBeat's survey links rising prices for GPUs, memory and AI-enabled software to enterprise budget overruns, though its self-selected sample is not representative. Business Insider adds that executives are souring on the low-quality output their own AI mandates produced. The enterprise conversation is shifting from adoption volume to unit economics per task. Sources: TechCrunch | VentureBeat
