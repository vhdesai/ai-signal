# OpenAI proposed rules for large electricity users

OpenAI proposed rules for large electricity users. Under them, utilities would make large users cover the grid costs they add, could throttle data centers during peak demand, and would require public reporting of water, energy, noise and jobs. The proposal would also narrow, but not ban, NDAs imposed on local officials. For its own campuses, OpenAI pledged to pay full energy and infrastructure costs, prioritize closed-loop cooling, hire through local unions and fund projects chosen by residents.
