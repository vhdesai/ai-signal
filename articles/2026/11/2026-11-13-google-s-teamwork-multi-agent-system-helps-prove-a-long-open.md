# Google's /teamwork multi-agent system helps prove a long-open conjecture, with proofs checked in Lean 4

Google says Antigravity's /teamwork harness runs many solution attempts in parallel, checks each with independent verifiers such as Lean 4, test suites and simulators, and gives each agent a narrow context to avoid drift over multi-day tasks. Researchers from Google, CUHK, USC and CMU used it to prove the 2013 Courtade-Kumar conjecture in full generality, and Google reports further results in formal verification, molecular-structure identification and kernel optimization that have not all been independently reviewed. Google is opening these patterns to external Antigravity users, moving verifier-backed agent workflows from lab demonstrations toward products. https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/ RESEARCH ANALYSIS
