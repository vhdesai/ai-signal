# Larridin raises $30 million to measure what companies get from AI spending

Pelion Ventures led the Series A, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz and Gradient Ventures. Larridin's platform analyzes employees' AI usage to score adoption, productivity gains, and training needs, and says it does not store prompt contents. Lead investor Jeff Kearl says AI has become one of companies' five to 10 largest expenses. That shift is creating demand for independent tracking of AI spend and returns, as Datadog did for cloud.
