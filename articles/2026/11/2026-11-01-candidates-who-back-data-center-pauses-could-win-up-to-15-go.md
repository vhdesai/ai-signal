# Candidates who back data center pauses could win up to 15 governor's races

The Information's analysis finds that in 21 of the 36 states electing a governor, at least one major-party nominee backs a data center moratorium. Both nominees support pauses in Michigan, New Hampshire, Ohio, Oregon, and Texas, and a pro-pause candidate is likely to win in Maine, New Mexico, and New York. Those 15 states hold more than a third of the US population. Whatever the results, the industry should plan for fewer places to build and higher costs where it still can. GOOGLE SPACE COMPUTE
