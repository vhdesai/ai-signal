# [November 18, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

Palo Alto Networks will buy Applied Compute, a startup founded by three former OpenAI researchers that helps businesses train and run customized open-source models, for $4.5 billion in stock, weeks after a round valued it at $3.25 billion. Bret Taylor's customer-support startup Sierra is in early talks to raise about $1.5 billion at roughly $30 billion, nearly double its May valuation, with Lightspeed, Greenoaks and Benchmark in discussions. Cybersecurity stocks rallied on Wednesday, with Okta up 12%, as investors bet outages and AI-driven threats will lift security spending. ANALYSIS EVENTS
