# SpaceX appears to be building a multigigawatt supercomputer in Texas as a new governor promises data center restrictions

Public records, job listings, and steel structures with gas turbines next to them indicate that SpaceX has started building a multigigawatt "Starbase supercomputer" at its Starship launch site on the Texas-Mexico border. Democrat Gina Hinojosa, newly elected governor, campaigned on restricting data centers and could delay parts of the project, though The Information does not expect her to stop it. TechCrunch's Equity podcast also notes that Elon Musk's roughly $500 million in Republican donations did not stop Texas from swinging Democratic. Texas, home to OpenAI's Stargate and other major campuses, is now the key test of whether election promises turn into real permitting limits.
