# [November 8, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Agility Robotics became the first US-listed humanoid robot company after a SPAC merger that raised about $465 million, but its shares (AGLT) closed Friday at $9.90, down nearly 14%. CEO Peggy Johnson must turn last year's $1.8 million in revenue and $138.1 million net loss into a business; Digit robots work at nine customer sites, and more than $300 million of orders come from a single customer. Separately, CNBC's review of job postings shows Amazon has formed a Personal Robotics Group building a “new-to-world” home robot, drawing on former Astro staff and its March acquisition of Fauna Robotics.
