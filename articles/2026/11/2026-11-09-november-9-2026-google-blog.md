# [November 9, 2026] · [Google Blog]

Google introduced TPU 8tx, which pairs the TPU 8t chip with AMD hosts and a hardware secure enclave for regulated industries and governments, and TPU 8i Max, which doubles TPU 8i's compute and memory for very long contexts; Google says TPUs now have nearly 100 customers. Korean chipmaker Rebellions will connect its inference processors to Nvidia's NVLink Fusion and MGX racks for SK Telecom's sovereign AI build-out, and Nvidia also backed interconnect startup Point2 and plans stakes in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD and Upstage. Hyperscaler silicon is splitting by workload, while Nvidia opens its platform to keep national chip programs inside its ecosystem. Google Blog | SiliconANGLE | NVIDIA Newsroom | NVIDIA Blog
