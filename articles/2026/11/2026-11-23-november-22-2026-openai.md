# [November 22, 2026] · [OpenAI]

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published a post proposing perplexity as a way to measure, and potentially limit, how capable AI systems become, which Sam Altman called "the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while." The post sets no thresholds and makes no commitments. Separately, Meta's Alexandr Wang warned that "superintelligence is arriving" and said humans are unlikely to control unconstrained recursive self-improvement.
