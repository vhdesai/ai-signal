# The Information reports that Google's energy chief put the cost of orbital data centers at up to 100 times that of…

The Information reports that Google's energy chief put the cost of orbital data centers at up to 100 times that of facilities on Earth. The full article is paywalled and details were not independently verified. The remark is a counterweight to Google's own Suncatcher TPU satellite demonstration last week, which suggests space compute remains a long-horizon research bet rather than near-term capacity.
