# [November 13, 2026] · [Crypto Briefing, citing the Financial Times]

Nvidia committed about $140 billion in investments and credit guarantees in just two months this year, has joined 296 venture deals since 2023 and backed nearly one in three AI unicorns founded since then, according to The Information and PitchBook. Investors expect interim CEO Colette Kress, who as CFO pushed back on Huang's riskier bets, to write smaller checks, and PitchBook flags a Justice Department inquiry into the $12.9 billion Hugging Face deal while calling Anthropic's IPO the bigger test for late-stage AI funding. The Financial Times reports the board has approached Arm CEO Rene Haas, Business Insider says customers may use the leadership gap to renegotiate terms, and Reuters reports Huang misled general counsel Tim Teter about his ties to Alice Huang. Kress faces her first earnings call on Tuesday.
