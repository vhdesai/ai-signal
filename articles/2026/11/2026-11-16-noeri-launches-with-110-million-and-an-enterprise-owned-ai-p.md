# Noeri launches with $110 million and an enterprise-owned AI proposition

Noeri, founded by Stanford professors Yejin Choi and Carlos Guestrin, launched with $110 million in funding led by Menlo Ventures, with Madrona, NEA, Cisco Investments, NVIDIA and Snowflake participating. Its systems propose changes to instructions, tools, model routing and sometimes parameters, with every change requiring human approval. The release positions company-owned customization as an alternative to standardized AI services but provides no independently measured customer results.
