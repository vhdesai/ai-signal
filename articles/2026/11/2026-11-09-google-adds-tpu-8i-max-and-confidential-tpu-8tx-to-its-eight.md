# Google adds TPU 8i Max and confidential TPU 8tx to its eighth-generation lineup

TPU 8i Max packages two TPU 8i chips together for double the compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth in the same rack footprint, targeting long-context, multi-agent workloads. TPU 8tx pairs TPU 8t silicon with AMD EPYC hosts for hardware-level confidential computing, aimed at regulated enterprises and governments, with up to 2x lower latency than TPU 8t per Google. The lineup now spans training, low-latency reasoning, confidential inference and density-optimized long context. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
