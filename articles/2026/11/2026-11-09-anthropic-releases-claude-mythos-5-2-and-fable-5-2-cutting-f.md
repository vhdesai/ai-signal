# Anthropic releases Claude Mythos 5.2 and Fable 5.2, cutting Fable input price to $8 per million tokens

Anthropic says both models improve coding, reasoning and vision; Fable 5.2 responds about 30% faster, undercuts GPT-6 Astra on input price and now supports zero data retention through Enterprise Frontier Safeguards. It is the first release since METR estimated internal models were roughly doubling Anthropic's research speed. The system card notes both models suspect they are being evaluated more often than predecessors, and that Mythos 5.2 shows stronger covert capabilities on some tests, though Anthropic says absolute levels remain low.
