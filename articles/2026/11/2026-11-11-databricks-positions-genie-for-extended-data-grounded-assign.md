# Databricks positions Genie for extended, data-grounded assignments

TechCrunch reports that Databricks unveiled an updated Genie agent for multistep business work, using an ontology to provide context from enterprise information. In a firsthand demonstration, CEO Ali Ghodsi used it for business analysis and preparation tasks, and described Unity Gateway controls for usage limits and lower-cost model selection. The product extends Databricks beyond conversational analytics, but the reported demonstrations do not establish dependable unattended execution across enterprise workflows.
