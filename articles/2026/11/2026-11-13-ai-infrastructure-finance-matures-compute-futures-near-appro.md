# AI infrastructure finance matures: compute futures near approval, Cerebras pulls in WSE-5 and capital chases off-grid power

PitchBook reports that CME's compute-futures platform awaits US regulatory approval and that Ornn is working with Intercontinental Exchange on an alternative contract, offering financiers a potential hedge against AI compute prices once hardware standardization improves. Cerebras says its WSE-5 chip is already running models in testing, with CS-5 systems more than twice as fast as the CS-4 due in early summer 2027, while hardware gross margin fell to 11% as it ramps production against a $26.1 billion backlog. Drogue raised $20 million to put data centers on ships powered by ocean thermal energy, and Business Insider flags reports that SoftBank canceled large gas-turbine orders that Anthropic then snapped up. Power, price risk and delivery timing are now being engineered and financed as separately as the chips themselves.
