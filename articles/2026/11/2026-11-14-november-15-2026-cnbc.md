# [November 15, 2026] · [CNBC]

The Wall Street Journal reports that large Southeast Asian data centers gave Chinese companies crucial computing power and cover for alleged chip smuggling, and examines Huang's role in building that route. Megaspeed told CNBC that Alice Huang left the company in 2025, that it has hired outside counsel to review media reports it says are not supported by verifiable evidence, and that it complies with U.S. export controls. South Korea said Nvidia reaffirmed plans to supply 260,000 advanced GPUs and Taipei said Nvidia's overseas headquarters remains on track, while TechCrunch asks whether Nvidia's executive-security oversight fell short.
