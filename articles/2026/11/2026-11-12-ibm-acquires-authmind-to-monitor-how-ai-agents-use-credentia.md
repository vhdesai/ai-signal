# IBM acquires AuthMind to monitor how AI agents use credentials after they are granted

IBM will integrate AuthMind's identity-activity graph into HashiCorp Vault, adding runtime visibility into how agents use credentials and secrets across applications, tools, and data. IBM frames agent security as five stages: register, govern authority, enforce least privilege, audit, and revoke in real time. AuthMind fills the audit and response stages, including detecting unsanctioned "shadow" agents through network-traffic analysis. Agent governance is drawing investment elsewhere too: Kindo raised $30 million to expand its platform for controlling agent actions, bringing its total to $57.6 million.
