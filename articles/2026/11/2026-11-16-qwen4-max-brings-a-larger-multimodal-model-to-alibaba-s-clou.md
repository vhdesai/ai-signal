# Qwen4-Max brings a larger multimodal model to Alibaba’s cloud

Source: Alibaba Cloud; Bloomberg via ThePrint Alibaba announced general availability of Qwen4-Max, combining native vision and a one-million-token context window with a mixture-of-experts architecture that activates 71 billion of its 2.4 trillion parameters per token. The company reports lower task costs and stronger coding results, but these remain vendor claims rather than independently reproduced launch findings. The immediate enterprise development is cloud availability; smaller open-weight models are planned rather than available as part of this announcement. Read source · Timestamped coverage NEW Tencent
