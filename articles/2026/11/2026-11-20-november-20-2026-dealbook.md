# [November 20, 2026] · [DealBook]

Anthropic opened on Nasdaq Thursday at $111.50, 34% above its $83 IPO price, and closed up 17% at $97.17, leaving it worth about $1.76 trillion on a fully diluted basis after an $81.3 billion IPO ($107.3 billion including a $26 billion private placement with compute suppliers). The debut did not rescue the AI trade: the Nasdaq fell 2.3% and the Dow shed 1,101 points on the day. By Friday morning the selloff had spread overseas, with TSMC down about 5% and Samsung about 7%, and Anthropic shares were off 6% to 9% in premarket trading.
