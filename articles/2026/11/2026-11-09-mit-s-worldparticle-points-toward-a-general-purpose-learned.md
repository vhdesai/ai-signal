# MIT's WorldParticle points toward a general-purpose learned simulator

Researchers at MIT, the University of British Columbia and collaborators built WorldParticle, which represents materials as particles and uses a neural network to predict their motion instead of a hand-coded solver for each type of physics. They demonstrated it on six categories including cloth, fluids, granular materials and molecular dynamics, reporting protein-motion simulation about 100 times faster than numerical simulators. It is a unified architecture trained separately for each category rather than one model that simulates everything, and the work will be presented at SIGGRAPH Asia.
