# Google expands Gemini 4 Carbon beyond restricted access

Google is opening Carbon to paying API customers and beginning access for AI Ultra subscribers across Antigravity, Gemini, and AI Mode, following an earlier restricted cybersecurity deployment. Introductory API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. A coordinated-agent preview in Antigravity, called /swarm, extends the announcement from model access to project execution; enterprise buyers should evaluate complete workflow cost rather than token pricing alone.
