# MIT's UQ-DAAAM teaches robots what they don't know

MIT SPARK Lab researchers added uncertainty quantification to DAAAM, a vision-language memory system, so a mobile robot can flag low-confidence object descriptions and correct them in real time from new viewpoints. Against baselines, it improved accuracy on questions about objects, their locations and when they were seen. The work will be presented at NeurIPS, and senior author Luca Carlone argues that this kind of self-knowledge is a prerequisite for deploying physical AI safely.
