# [November 16, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for a dramatic shake-up and an outside CEO at Nvidia, asking whether the company pursued seemingly illicit chip sales to China-linked intermediaries to boost revenue. DealBook reports Huang is on board with the campaign to reinstate him, citing the Financial Times, while Elon Musk argues many of Nvidia's supply deals were a handshake with Huang, and Business Insider says new reports cast doubt on the intelligence case behind his ouster. Interim CEO Colette Kress makes her first public appearance in charge on Tuesday's earnings call, with The Information noting projected revenue growth of about 89%, an HSBC downgrade and a stock down 32% since early October.
