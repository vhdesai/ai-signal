# [November 7, 2026] · [The Decoder]

On Vals AI's CyberOffense benchmark, where agents attack simulated corporate networks, OpenAI's restricted GPT-6 Sol Daybreak led with 80.8% of objectives, but the open-weight DeepSeek V4.1 Flash reached 72.8% at about $3 per run against roughly $22. Vals warns that once weights are public, no provider can control how they are used. Tencent chief AI scientist Yao Shunyu said in a rare public appearance that models' cyber and, soon, biology abilities “will reach a threshold where it's actually possible to do harm,” suggesting release-gating by frontier labs loses force as open models close the gap.
