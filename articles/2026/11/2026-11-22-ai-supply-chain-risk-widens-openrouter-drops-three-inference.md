# AI supply-chain risk widens: OpenRouter drops three inference providers and Microsoft warns of months of patch-driven outages

OpenRouter off-boarded three inference providers, removed 125 endpoints from zero-data-retention eligibility and notified potentially affected customers after investigating compromised data and policy failures, while saying OpenRouter itself was not breached. On CBS's Face the Nation, Microsoft President Brad Smith warned that AI has made security flaws far easier to find, setting off a race to patch that could bring more outages "in the coming months," though he said Microsoft's recent outage was not AI-related. Enterprises should verify the full downstream processing chain behind AI providers and test updates more rigorously before rollout.
