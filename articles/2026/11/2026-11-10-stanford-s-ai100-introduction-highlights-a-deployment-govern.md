# Stanford's AI100 introduction highlights a deployment–governance gap

Stanford HAI's introduction to the third AI100 report presents the study panel's assessment that institutions and public governance are struggling to keep pace with AI adoption. It connects scientific progress with cybersecurity risks, infrastructure demands, and limited public participation in decisions about deployment. This is an attributed research-policy assessment, not a new capability benchmark; the verification here covers Stanford's introduction rather than a full review of the underlying report.
