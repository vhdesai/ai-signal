# ChatGPT holds more than 81% of chatbot traffic as its growth picks up again

A study by research firm Graphite, using Similarweb data, finds that ChatGPT accounts for more than 81% of worldwide traffic to major AI chatbots, and no rival has 10%. ChatGPT's monthly visits rose almost 20% to a record 61.35 billion in September, after about seven months of flat usage. Claude and Gemini grew quickly but from much smaller bases. Google's AI Overviews now appear in 62% of US searches, and the US accounts for only 8.8% of worldwide AI traffic. Despite enterprise momentum at Anthropic and Google, consumer distribution still strongly favors OpenAI.
