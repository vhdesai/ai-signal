# Google's Multimodal Knowledge Agent benchmark grades professional deliverables, not just answers

The MKA benchmark tests agents on 200 private tasks across ten industries, including turning visual source material into reports, presentations and spreadsheets. Expert rubrics score both factual quality and presentation, which makes it closer to enterprise acceptance testing than question-answering leaderboards. Google co-developed it with Artificial Analysis, so its model comparisons should be read with the limits of a private task set and automated grading in mind. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/multimodal-knowledge-agent-benchmark/ RESEARCH SECURITY
