# Cursor reportedly weighs phasing out Chinese-developed AI models

CNBC reported that Cursor told House investigators it would explore moving away from Chinese-developed open-weight models because of geopolitical risk. The report identifies Moonshot AI’s Kimi K2.5 as the foundation of Cursor’s Composer 2 model. For enterprise buyers, the development underscores why model provenance and supplier-dependency reviews should extend beyond the application vendor.
