# TSMC weighs making 3D DRAM at Nvidia's request as the memory shortage deepens

TSMC is considering expanding into advanced memory manufacturing and has started drawing up plans to make 3D DRAM at the request of Nvidia, its biggest customer, according to two people with direct knowledge of the matter. An intense AI-driven memory shortage has sent prices soaring and pushed chip designers such as Nvidia and Apple to look for additional supply. The move would give the world's largest chipmaker an even bigger share of the AI hardware supply chain. EXCLUSIVE
