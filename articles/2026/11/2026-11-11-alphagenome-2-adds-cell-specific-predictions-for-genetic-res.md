# AlphaGenome 2 adds cell-specific predictions for genetic research

DeepMind’s AlphaGenome 2 predicts genetic-variant effects in specific cell types and states, rather than relying only on tissue-level averages. The company reports training on more than 107 million cell profiles and identifying causal variants in 30% of cases versus 16% for its comparison model on the cited TenK10K evaluation; noncommercial research access is available, with commercial research in private preview. The potential application is prioritizing disease-mechanism and drug-discovery work, not diagnosis: DeepMind says the model is not clinically validated or approved for diagnostic use.
