# Databricks seeks funding at a $225 billion valuation as private money keeps backing enterprise AI platforms

Databricks is in early talks to raise several billion dollars at about a $225 billion valuation, up from $190 billion in August. The round would extend the private-market premium for enterprise data and AI platforms even as public AI stocks correct. Investors appear to be separating software and data platforms from the compute-heavy names bearing the brunt of the selloff.
