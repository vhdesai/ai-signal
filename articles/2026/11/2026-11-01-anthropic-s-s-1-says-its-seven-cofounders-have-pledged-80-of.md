# Anthropic's S-1 says its seven cofounders have pledged 80% of their personal stock to charity, potentially about $80…

Anthropic's S-1 says its seven cofounders have pledged 80% of their personal stock to charity, potentially about $80 billion. It also earmarks 5% of company equity for a public-good fund, adds 2% for each sustained 50% rise in the stock price, and matches employee donation pledges with new shares. Experts interviewed by Business Insider called the design credible but said they will judge it on governance, grantee selection, and reporting. The structure is unusual for an IPO and will shape how investors view dilution and mission commitments.
