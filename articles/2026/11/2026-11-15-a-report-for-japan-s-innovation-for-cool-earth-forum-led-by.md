# A report for Japan's Innovation for Cool Earth Forum, led by Columbia University's David Sandalow, warns that tens of…

A report for Japan's Innovation for Cool Earth Forum, led by Columbia University's David Sandalow, warns that tens of thousands of orbital AI data centers could outshine stars, add collision and debris risk, and become military targets. It also notes that the launches needed to deploy them emit greenhouse gases and black carbon, even though the satellites are solar-powered. The authors recommend a slow, careful rollout. As land-based data centers face local opposition, the report signals that space-based alternatives will face their own regulatory scrutiny.
