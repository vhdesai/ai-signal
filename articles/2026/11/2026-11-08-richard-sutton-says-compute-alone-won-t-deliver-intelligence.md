# Richard Sutton says compute alone won't deliver intelligence, reigniting the scaling debate

Turing Award winner Richard Sutton, author of “The Bitter Lesson,” argued that raw compute is not enough and that AI science still needs clever new methods and is “far from finished.” Former OpenAI researcher Jerry Tworek countered that the lesson holds but algorithms matter more as compute grows, saying its misreading has “reward-hacked the brains of a whole generation of scientists.” With labs committing hundreds of billions of dollars to compute, the argument over returns to scale is no longer academic.
