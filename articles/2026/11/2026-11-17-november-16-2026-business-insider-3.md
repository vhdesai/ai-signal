# [November 16, 2026] · [Business Insider]

The Bureau of Industry and Security issued its long-awaited rule blocking Chinese AI companies from reaching restricted chips through data centers outside China, mainly in Southeast Asia, in a release saying it was written at the direction of Treasury and the National Security Council. Nvidia's board created a committee chaired by Suzanne Nora Johnson, with outside legal, national-security and trade experts, to oversee export-control compliance. The Justice and Commerce departments said they have investigated Megaspeed since 2024 and interviewed then-CEO Alice Huang in Singapore in 2025, while President Trump defended Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick ahead of this week's summit with Xi Jinping.
