# November 8, 2026 · Financial Times (via Yahoo Finance)

The Financial Times reports that Anthropic has secured 1.7 GW of heavy-duty gas-fired generators, originally ordered from Caterpillar by San Antonio-based OnePWR Solutions, together with the engineering capacity to install them, in a $3.4 billion deal; it is the largest known single purchase of power-generation equipment by an AI lab. The Information's weekend column says some Anthropic investors now doubt the company can secure its sought-after $1.5 trillion valuation in an IPO expected within weeks, and that it is pitching Claude's chip-design skills to prospective buyers. The pairing captures the moment: frontier labs are integrating into power to escape multiyear equipment lead times just as public-market appetite for their valuations is being tested.
