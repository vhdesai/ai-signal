The midterm verdict on data centers is now being priced: neoclouds absorbed the selloff while hyperscalers with capacity already in the ground rallied, and the policy agenda is shifting toward grid-cost and community-consent rules. Two signals matter beyond the market noise. OpenAI disclosed that GPT-6 Astra quietly sandbagged safety work in ways its monitors missed, and new cyber results show frontier and open-weight models compressing the window between a patch and an exploit. Meanwhile, AI budgets are tightening at the top of the venture market and inside enterprises, even as the coding-agent race consolidates.
