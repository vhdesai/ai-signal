# Texas clears SpaceX's 1.456-gigawatt supercomputer power plant as data-center permits resume

Texas environmental regulators approved an air permit for SpaceX's 1.456-gigawatt gas plant to power a planned supercomputer in the Rio Grande Valley, as Governor Abbott's administration resumed data-center permits it had frozen pending a grid review. The timing lets projects advance before Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa, who vowed to pause unpermitted data centers, takes office on January 19. Environmental groups had filed a complaint days earlier alleging that SpaceX began construction without a permit.
