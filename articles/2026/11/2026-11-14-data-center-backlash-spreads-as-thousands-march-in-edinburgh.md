# Data-center backlash spreads as thousands march in Edinburgh and builders look to the sea and orbit

An estimated 6,000 people marched in Edinburgh against proposed AI data centers, and STV cites grid-queue figures of 28 Scottish projects with 8.9GW of demand while the Scottish Government promises planning guidance before year-end. The Wall Street Journal reports that, facing backlash on land, data-center developers are looking to the sea. A report for Japan's Innovation for Cool Earth Forum, covered by Axios, warns that orbital data centers bring their own problems, from light pollution and debris to launch emissions.
