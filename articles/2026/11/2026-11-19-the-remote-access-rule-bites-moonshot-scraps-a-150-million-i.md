# The remote-access rule bites: Moonshot scraps a $150 million Indonesia deal and Tencent says it sets China back

Moonshot AI, maker of the Kimi models, cancelled a $150 million contract to rent Nvidia Blackwell Ultra B300 chips from a Southeast Asian neocloud in Indonesia after Washington barred Chinese firms from remote access to advanced chips. Tencent President Martin Lau said the rule sets everyone back in China and could delay model releases, even as Tencent's quarterly capex rose 54% from the prior quarter to 81.4 billion yuan, or $12.2 billion. US banks led by JPMorgan and Citi are also adding export-control default clauses to an upsized $6.6 billion loan for Bridge Data Centres, which once hosted Megaspeed, Bloomberg reports.
