# MIT's design copilot lets engineers steer AI-generated structures as they form

MIT researchers made topology optimization interactive, with a machine-learning model flagging regions an engineer is likely to want to change while the structure is still being generated. In a Communications Engineering paper, the approach improved linear buckling load by 39% and manufacturability for about 15 seconds of extra design time. Interventions are currently limited to thickening or thinning selected regions, so the result supports targeted human-AI collaboration rather than autonomous structural design.
