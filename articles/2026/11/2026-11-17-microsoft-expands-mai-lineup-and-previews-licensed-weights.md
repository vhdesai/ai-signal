# Microsoft expands MAI lineup and previews licensed weights

Microsoft’s seven-model lineup spans coding, cybersecurity, image generation, real-time speech, clinical transcription, and decision models, emphasizing deployment economics. Microsoft says MAI Code2 achieves 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol’s performance at 40% of its price across four selected coding benchmarks, while noting it is not tuned for the hardest scientific or long-horizon work. The coming-soon MAI Licensed Weights offering would let customers customize and deploy models within their own Azure environments; it is neither generally available nor an open-source release. The wider procurement choice warrants workload-specific evaluation rather than extrapolation from selected benchmarks. Freshness: The originals’ November 17 dates are verified; precise publication times are unavailable. Independent same-day reporting by Thurrott corroborates the release.
