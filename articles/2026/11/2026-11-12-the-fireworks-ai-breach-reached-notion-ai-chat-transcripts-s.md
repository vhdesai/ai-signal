# The Fireworks AI breach reached Notion AI chat transcripts, showing how inference-vendor incidents spread

Notion confirmed that attackers who breached Fireworks, after getting in through Fireworks' cloud provider, took chat transcripts from Notion AI's "Insights" feature. Those transcripts include model inputs and outputs, and the incident affected about 1% of Notion customers. Notion cut its connection to Fireworks on November 3. Fireworks says the attackers appear financially motivated and aim to resell LLM access.
