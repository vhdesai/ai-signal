# Stanford policy report emphasizes planning for multiple AI economic outcomes

Stanford's newly published account of its November 12 Fall Policy Forum argues for preparing economic policy for several possible AI outcomes rather than a single forecast of growth or displacement. Participants discussed wage insurance, retraining incentives, job sharing, exposure to lower labor-income tax receipts and municipal conditions on data-center development. These are expert perspectives and policy proposals reported today about an earlier event, not newly enacted measures or established estimates of AI's effects. Timing: Date verified; publication time not specified. The original displays November 23, while the underlying forum took place on November 12.
