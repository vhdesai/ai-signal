# Anthropic IPO preview sets proposed range ahead of expected pricing

CNBC reported that Anthropic expects to price its IPO on the evening of November 18, with Nasdaq trading under ANT expected on November 19. The proposed offering comprises 825 million Class A shares at $70–$78 each, with up to 20% allocated to retail investors. These remain proposed terms rather than a final price or completed listing; investor demand should be distinguished from evidence of durable operating economics.
