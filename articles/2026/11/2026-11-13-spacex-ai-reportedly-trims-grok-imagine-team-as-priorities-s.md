# SpaceX AI reportedly trims Grok Imagine team as priorities shift

Yahoo Finance / Investing.com, citing The Information Yahoo Finance and Investing.com, citing The Information, report that roughly 60 jobs were cut from the Grok Imagine video team, leaving a small maintenance crew. The reporting describes resources shifting toward coding tools and Grok Bot. This is attributed reporting rather than first-party confirmation, and the original article is paywalled. The publication falls within this digest’s window; the reporting does not establish that the cuts themselves occurred during that period.
