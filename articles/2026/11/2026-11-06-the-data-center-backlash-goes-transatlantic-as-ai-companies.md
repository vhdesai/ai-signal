# The data-center backlash goes transatlantic as AI companies rethink siting

The Wall Street Journal reports AI companies are scrambling to adapt to a world newly hostile to data centers after US voters backed restrictions in 40 of 41 local ballot measures. In Amsterdam, about 100 activists stormed the construction site of a planned $1 billion-plus, three-tower campus where Microsoft is the sole intended tenant, opening two days of European protests over power, water and housing. Capacity plans now need explicit political-risk assumptions on both sides of the Atlantic. Sources: Wall Street Journal | Yahoo News
