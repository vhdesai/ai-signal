# MIT CSAIL uses GPT-6 Astra to rewrite Qwen's weights directly, lifting math scores sixfold

MIT CSAIL researchers had GPT-6 Astra read and edit the raw weights of Qwen3.5-0.8B with no gradient descent or training run. Changing just 0.016% of the weights fixed reasoning loops and raised the model's MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1%. The authors call it weight-space programming, a potential path to interpretability-driven model repair that still needs independent replication.
