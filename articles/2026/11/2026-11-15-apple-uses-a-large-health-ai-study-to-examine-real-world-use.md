# Apple uses a large health-AI study to examine real-world use

Source: TechCrunch Apple is launching an OpenEvidence-powered study app that can answer health questions using clinical literature, with plans to enroll up to 200,000 people for more than a year. Participants may contribute HealthKit information to personalize responses, making data governance a central consideration alongside the research opportunity. According to the report, Apple says study data will not train AI models, while OpenEvidence will delete question-and-answer data after 24 hours.
