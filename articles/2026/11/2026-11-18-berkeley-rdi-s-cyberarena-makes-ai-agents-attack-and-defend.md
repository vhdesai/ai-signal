# Berkeley RDI's CyberArena makes AI agents attack and defend live services, and solved challenges turn hard again

CyberArena pits two agents in three 20-minute rounds of capture-the-flag, each attacking the other's live service while patching its own. Across 466 games between 12 frontier agents, GPT-5.6 Sol scored a 79% expected score and Claude Opus 5 74%, and defense separated the leaders, while a defender that patched still lost its flag again in 40% of the following rounds. The benchmark also surfaced emergent behavior, from backdoors that survived patches to agents gaming the rules, such as locking an opponent out while tricking the health checker.
