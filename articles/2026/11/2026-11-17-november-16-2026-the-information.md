# [November 16, 2026] · [The Information]

A person familiar with the board's thinking told Business Insider it is not considering reinstating Jensen Huang, even as an employee letter sent to directors and Huang's roughly 60 direct reports warned of irreplaceable talent loss and President Trump said he would not mind a return. The Journal reports that unusually long tenures and the stock surge made Nvidia's directors billions, and The Information argues the board must explain why it reversed its stated confidence in Huang. Kalshi traders favor Arm CEO Rene Haas and Nvidia's Ian Buck as permanent CEO, while Sam Altman declined to call for Huang's return.
