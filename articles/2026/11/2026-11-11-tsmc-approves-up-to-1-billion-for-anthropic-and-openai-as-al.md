# TSMC approves up to $1 billion for Anthropic and OpenAI as Alibaba wins approval to list its chip unit

TSMC's board approved investing $500 million apiece in Anthropic and OpenAI, extending the pattern of suppliers taking equity in their largest AI customers. Hong Kong regulators also cleared a listing of Alibaba's T-Head semiconductor business, which Nomura estimates could be worth $85 billion. Together with CoreWeave's results, the news lifted chip stocks premarket and helped Nvidia begin recovering from a 9.5% weekly decline.
