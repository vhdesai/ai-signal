# Mistral opens a Singapore science hub and plans to double its Asia-Pacific staff

Mistral opened its Asia-Pacific headquarters and a science hub in Singapore to build sovereign AI models for government, high-tech manufacturing and robotics customers. It plans to grow its regional headcount from about 100 to 200 by 2027, including forward-deployed engineers and scientists. The launch came a day after new US licensing rules for advanced AI chip exports to Singapore and four other countries took effect. Mistral is selling against US vendors on control and data residency, which matters more as US export rules tighten.
