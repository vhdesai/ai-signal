# Google's Gemini Decision 1 adds a low-cost scoring layer for agent workflows

The new Gemini Decisions API and its first model answer yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric questions with confidence scores instead of generating text, and can evaluate up to 1,000 questions against shared multimodal input in a single call. Pricing is $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charge, which Google says makes it nearly five times cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on its benchmark suite. Target uses include tool selection, policy checks, model routing and evaluation, with uncertain cases escalated to stronger models or people. Business Insider separately reports that Google is letting employees test Hydrogen, a fast, low-cost Gemini model in the Flash lineage. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/ https://venturebeat.com/technology/googles-gemini-decision-1-model-scores-up-to-1-000-questions-in-one-api-call
