# Anthropic heads into Thursday's Nasdaq debut with $900 billion in commitments and a $104 billion nine-month loss

Anthropic is expected to list on Thursday at $70 to $78 a share, with AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC together investing $26 billion at the IPO price. Its prospectus shows more than $900 billion in spending commitments, including at least $110 billion with Amazon over a decade, against a $104 billion net loss for the first nine months of 2026 and $132 billion in cash. CNBC frames the offering, which could raise more than $90 billion, as a referendum on the frontier-lab spending that underwrites chip and cloud suppliers' revenue. CNBC WORKFORCE ANALYSIS
