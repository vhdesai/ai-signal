# CNBC reports that Kimi maker Moonshot AI discussed leasing Nvidia Blackwell clusters of 8,000+ GPUs from European…

CNBC reports that Kimi maker Moonshot AI discussed leasing Nvidia Blackwell clusters of 8,000+ GPUs from European neocloud operators for inference and possibly training. One proposal reportedly asked Moonshot to pay 30–50% more upfront than non-Chinese customers, to hedge against a future ban on remote access. The report follows Nvidia's push this week to cap the share of partners' cloud business that goes to Chinese customers.
