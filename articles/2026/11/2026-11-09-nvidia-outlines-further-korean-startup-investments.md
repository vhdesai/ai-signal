# Nvidia outlines further Korean startup investments

At a forum with South Korea's science ministry, Nvidia outlined planned equity investments in Upstage, Motif Technologies, and RLWRLD. MoneyToday separately reports that Nvidia had already invested in Point2 Technology in April. Investment amounts were not disclosed, and the prospective commitments should not be treated as completed transactions.
