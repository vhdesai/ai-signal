# Wall Street wants to trade AI compute like oil as private credit funds the buildout

PitchBook reports that a futures contract for GPU computing power, modeled on the crude-oil futures of the early 1980s, may arrive soon after Anthropic's IPO. Private credit is overtaking venture capital as the funder of AI infrastructure and other hard tech, but lenders have little price transparency if compute prices fall. New GPU price indexes and futures built on them would let lenders and buyers hedge that risk.
