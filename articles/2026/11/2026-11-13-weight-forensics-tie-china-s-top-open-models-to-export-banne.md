# Weight forensics tie China's top open models to export-banned Blackwell chips as Washington feuds over Megaspeed

Former OpenAI researcher Will DePue found hardware fingerprints consistent with Blackwell training in open models including DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3; offshore training would not necessarily break current rules, which strengthens the case for controlling who uses chips rather than where they ship. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Treasury, the Pentagon, State and the US trade representative had favored blacklisting Megaspeed for months and that Commerce holds the pen, adding to pressure on Howard Lutnick. DealBook reports that China hawks are pushing three bills to tighten tech exports ahead of next week's APEC summit in Shenzhen, and that Oracle canceled a roughly $7 billion contract giving Tencent access to AI chips in Southeast Asia after US officials raised concerns. Cloud providers hosting Chinese workloads or models abroad should expect end-user controls to tighten.
