# Meta deploys specialized Muse and Meta AI models with Active Memory and Active Focus

Meta is rolling out specialized versions of the models behind Muse and Meta AI, improved using its Muse Wonder series rather than deploying Wonder itself. It reports Active Memory cut forgetting of goal-critical information by 2.4 times and that the current Muse model scores 47% on its Personal Superintelligence Index, up from 38%. All figures are vendor-reported on Meta's internal benchmarks.
