# LangChain raises $300 million at a $3 billion valuation

LangChain raised a $300 million Series C led by CapitalG, with GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP and Sapphire Ventures participating, at a $3 billion valuation. The company claims 7,000 LangSmith customers and positions its agent-creation and management tools as independent of any single model, cloud or ecosystem. The financing signals continued investment in the agent-operations layer even as foundation-model vendors absorb orchestration capabilities. Source: TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/ai-agent-startup-langchain-hits-3b-valuation/
