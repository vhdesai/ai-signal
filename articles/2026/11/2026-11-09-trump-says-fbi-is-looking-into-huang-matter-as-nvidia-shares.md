# Trump says FBI is looking into Huang matter as Nvidia shares slide and lawsuits loom

At a Monday press conference, Trump defended Jensen Huang as "a very honorable man," said Huang told him he had no idea the woman was a spy, and acknowledged "the FBI's looking into it in terms of something else." Nvidia shares fell more than 4% Monday and plaintiffs' firms began lining up shareholder suits, TechCrunch reported, while Huang withdrew from ElevenLabs and Anthropic events.
