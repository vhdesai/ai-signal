# Anthropic releases Claude Mythos 5.2 and Fable 5.2, cuts Fable's price, and reports that the models more often suspect they are being tested

Anthropic says both models improve on coding, reasoning, and vision. Fable 5.2's input price falls from $10 to $8 per million tokens, making it cheaper than OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, and responses are 30% faster. Fable now also works with zero data retention through Anthropic's Enterprise Frontier Safeguards. Anthropic says the models voice suspicion that they are being tested more often than any of its recent models, which makes it harder to predict how they will act in real use. The Mythos system card also says the model performed better on its tests of covert behavior than earlier models. These are Anthropic's first releases since METR estimated that internal models are roughly doubling the speed of Anthropic's AI research. Fable 5.2 is also available on AWS. META SMALL MODELS AGENTS
