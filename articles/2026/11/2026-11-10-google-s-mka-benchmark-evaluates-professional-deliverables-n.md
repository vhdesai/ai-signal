# Google's MKA benchmark evaluates professional deliverables, not just answers

The Multimodal Knowledge Agent benchmark tests agents on 200 private tasks across ten industries, including work that turns visual source material into reports, presentations, or spreadsheets. Expert rubrics assess both factual quality and presentation, making the benchmark relevant to enterprise acceptance testing beyond question answering. Google co-developed the benchmark with evaluation partner Artificial Analysis, so its promoted model comparisons should be read alongside the limits of a private task set and automated grading.
