# LiquidCodec: High-Fidelity Audio Tokenization Optimized for Speed

Liquid AI presented a roughly 74-million-parameter streaming audio codec designed to improve downstream speech generation as well as audio reconstruction. In controlled text-to-speech comparisons, the company reports leading naturalness scores across eight test sets but leading intelligibility on only four, an important distinction from a blanket quality claim. The work targets more efficient audio generation, although the evaluations remain developer-reported rather than independently reproduced.
