# Recent advances in AI are creating a ‘paradigm shift’ for robotics

UC Berkeley roboticist Ken Goldberg, NVIDIA’s Spencer Huang and collaborators describe agents that generate robot-control programs, test them in simulation and revise them after failures. Their Graph-as-Policy framework decomposes work into skills, and a demonstration transferred sponge-wiping control code from a video-derived simulation to a physical robot; the article also announces open-source code and an industrial-task benchmark. A failed trial involving a metal bar illustrates unresolved simulation-to-reality errors, and the researchers caution against extrapolating industrial advances to general-purpose home assistants.
