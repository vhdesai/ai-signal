# [November 23, 2026] · [The Wall Street Journal]

DealBook reports a split among private-credit giants: Ares calls AI chips "one of the fastest-depreciating assets we can name," while Apollo arranged about $44 billion in GPU financing for SpaceX that must be repaid even if the chips fail, and Blackstone is helping finance a $63 billion Anthropic loan for Broadcom chips. The WSJ says the debt market expects Anthropic's bonds to be rated junk despite its $1.3 trillion market value, given roughly $900 billion in spending commitments, and a WSJ column argues that buying the dip won't deliver Magnificent Seven-style returns. The Information notes that AI-exposed stocks fell 12% to 16% on Friday before futures rebounded on Monday.
