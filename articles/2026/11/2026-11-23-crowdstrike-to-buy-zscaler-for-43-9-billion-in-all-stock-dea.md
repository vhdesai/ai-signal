# CrowdStrike to buy Zscaler for $43.9 billion in all-stock deal

CrowdStrike will acquire Zscaler at 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per Zscaler share, expected to close in the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028. Zscaler shares rose more than 20% and CrowdStrike's fell about 5%. CEO George Kurtz framed the combined company as "a single control plane to deploy AI, control AI, and enable any agentic application to run safely." Their combined value of about $265 billion approaches Palo Alto Networks' $282 billion. Security buyers should expect faster platform consolidation around controls for AI agents.
