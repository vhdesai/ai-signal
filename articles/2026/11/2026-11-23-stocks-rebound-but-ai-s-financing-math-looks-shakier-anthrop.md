# Stocks rebound, but AI's financing math looks shakier: Anthropic's debt is expected to be rated junk and AMD's valuation runs ahead

Futures pointed to a strong rebound on Monday after the S&P 500 fell more than 8% last week and entered correction territory, but Wall Street expects bonds Anthropic sells to fund its roughly $900 billion in spending commitments to be rated junk, which would raise its borrowing costs. The Information argues AMD's 98% rally has left it at about 25 times forward EBITDA, against roughly 7 times for Nvidia, with $76.8 billion in commitments set against $6.5 billion in cash. Meanwhile, Google's AI search features are slowing its ad growth, and the drag is spreading to Apple's services revenue and Reddit's traffic. The selloff is forcing investors to price financing and execution risk across the AI stack rather than treat every AI exposure as upside.
