# November 9, 2026 · Yahoo Finance

DeepSeek said its open-source Harness agent application reached 1.54 million daily active users by November 4, less than three months after launch, with daily usage of about 92 trillion tokens. Bloomberg, cited by Yahoo Finance, reports that Apple aims to add agent-style capabilities to Siri as early as next year, and Amazon now lets shoppers text its Alexa for Shopping assistant, following Meta and DoorDash in making messaging a standard agent channel. The usage figures are company claims, and Apple has not confirmed its plans.
