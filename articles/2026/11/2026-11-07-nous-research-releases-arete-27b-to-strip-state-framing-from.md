# Nous Research releases Arete-27B to strip state framing from Qwen

Nous further trained Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B on about 35,000 prompts, without reinforcement learning, and released the weights, data and evaluation under open licenses. On Nous's own 803-prompt test, the share of answers adopting state propaganda fell from 22% to 0.9%, while factual accuracy rose and general benchmarks held flat. The results are self-reported and model-graded, but they offer enterprises a reproducible way to use capable Chinese open-weight models without their political alignment.
