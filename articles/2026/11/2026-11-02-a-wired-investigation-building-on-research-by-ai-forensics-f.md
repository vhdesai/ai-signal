# A Wired investigation, building on research by AI Forensics, found a network of more than 400 connected apps and…

A Wired investigation, building on research by AI Forensics, found a network of more than 400 connected apps and domains posing as everyday tools that covertly load explicit deepfake generators. The apps were promoted through thousands of Meta ads, distributed through Apple and Google app stores, and monetized through Apple Pay and Google Pay. They appear tied to a Hong Kong-registered entity. The findings increase regulatory exposure for platforms whose app review and ad enforcement failed to catch cloaked apps.
