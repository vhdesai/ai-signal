# METR says frontier models now match or beat skilled humans on its coding tasks and cannot rule out rapid AI R&D acceleration

In its latest capability update, covering models up to Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, METR found that models usually match or outperform skilled humans on its coding and metric-optimization tasks, and that it often could not find human baseliners who beat them. Performance was weaker on judgment-heavy tasks that are harder to turn into reinforcement-learning environments, and METR flags significant limits in its human-baseline estimates. It concludes that public evidence is insufficient to rule out drastic AI R&D acceleration within the next couple of years, the scenario in which capabilities outpace humans' ability to assess catastrophic risk.
