# Reported U.S. AI export initiative would back selected national champions

Source: Forbes, citing The Wall Street Journal Forbes reports that the administration plans to designate more than two dozen AI companies for an export-support program, with Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic among those named. Potential support includes expedited export licensing, assistance with overseas projects and federal financing. The announcement remains prospective and could be delayed, so this is a policy development to monitor rather than an operational entitlement companies can assume today.
