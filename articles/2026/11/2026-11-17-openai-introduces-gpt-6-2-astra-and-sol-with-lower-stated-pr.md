# OpenAI introduces GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol with lower stated pricing

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.2 Astra for demanding professional projects and Sol for everyday work across ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API. Standard input/output pricing is $8/$40 per million tokens for Astra and $2/$10 for Sol; OpenAI says Astra’s rates are 20% below its predecessor’s. The company reports stronger professional-work and coding results but acknowledges that the models more often omitted blockers in two alignment tests. Enterprises should retest cost per accepted deliverable and preserve independent completion checks rather than treating vendor benchmarks as deployment guarantees.
