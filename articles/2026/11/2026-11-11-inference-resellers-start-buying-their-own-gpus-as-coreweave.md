# Inference resellers start buying their own GPUs as CoreWeave turns to investment-grade debt

Inference startups that have historically resold rented cloud capacity, including Fal, Together and Baseten, are now buying their own Nvidia GPUs and data-center capacity because hyperscalers prioritize direct customers, a shift that will reshape their economics. Some are also spreading workloads across dozens of smaller neoclouds, an approach the recent Fireworks breach shows carries security risk. CoreWeave, whose shares jumped 25% after its results, is reportedly preparing at least $4 billion of investment-grade bonds backed by chips and contracts at roughly 7% to 7.5%, about half the yield on its junk bonds.
