# [November 7, 2026] · [MarkTechPost]

Buoyant, the maker of Linkerd, released Kinference, an early-alpha Apache 2.0 control plane that sits in front of vLLM fleets on Kubernetes and routes requests by cost, data sensitivity and prompt-cache locality. In Buoyant's own benchmark, median time to first token fell 95% only for prompts sharing the same prefix. Nvidia detailed Reshard, a collective it says speeds weight refits during reinforcement-learning post-training by 2.7 to 5.7 times, targeting communication overhead rather than end-to-end training time.
