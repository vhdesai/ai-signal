# Lawmakers seek records on Nvidia's China chip-sales approvals

Senator Ron Wyden and Representative Richard Neal asked the Commerce Department to preserve records concerning approvals for H200 chip sales to China. Separately, Jensen Huang agreed to closed-door testimony before Republican-led committees. These are distinct congressional oversight developments; the inquiry itself does not establish wrongdoing.
