# Weil Gotshal quietly incubated Kepler, an AI-native challenger to Big Law, before the firm collapsed

In the months before Weil, Gotshal & Manges dissolved, a small team at the firm was in talks to raise hundreds of millions of dollars for Kepler, an AI-native legal services firm set up as an independent entity with the quiet backing of Weil's leadership, PitchBook reports. Kepler is led by Arnie Fridhandler, the private-equity partner who oversaw Weil's AI deal engine DealVision360, and had taken shape inside the firm for most of a year. Partners voted last week to dissolve the 95-year-old firm, and Kepler could now emerge from the wreckage as a challenger to the partnership model it came from. Infrastructure FINANCE ANALYSIS
