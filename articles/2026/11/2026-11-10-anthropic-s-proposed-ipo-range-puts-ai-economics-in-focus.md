# Anthropic's proposed IPO range puts AI economics in focus

Anthropic proposed a $70–$78 per-share IPO range that could imply a valuation of roughly $1.4 trillion. The filing also describes a $26 billion private share sale involving major chip and cloud suppliers, underscoring how closely AI financing is tied to the infrastructure ecosystem. This article was published within the reporting window, although the underlying filing may predate it.
