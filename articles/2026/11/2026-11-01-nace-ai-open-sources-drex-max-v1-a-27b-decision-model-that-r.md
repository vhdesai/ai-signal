# Nace AI open-sources Drex Max v1, a 27B decision model that runs locally

Nace AI released open weights for Drex Max v1, a dense 27-billion-parameter multimodal model that returns probabilities over defined options rather than free text, aimed at agent routing, ticket triage and guardrail checks. Quantized builds and llama.cpp and Ollama forks let it run on Apple Silicon Macs with 24 GB or more of memory; Nace reports 67.52 on its Decision Index, a self-reported figure. Small decision models increasingly keep agent control logic on local hardware instead of frontier APIs.
