# Microsoft spent $5B less than expected on AI infrastructure last quarter

Microsoft spent $5B less than expected on AI infrastructure last quarter. Satellite analysis by Aterio links part of the gap to IREN's Texas campus, where one building still lacked generators and cooling, and to a Nebius-linked New Jersey project with about 140 MW at risk of slipping into 2027. The Information separately reported that Microsoft lowered its data-center spending after IREN's delays. One research firm estimates 30–50% of capacity planned for 2026 could slip.
