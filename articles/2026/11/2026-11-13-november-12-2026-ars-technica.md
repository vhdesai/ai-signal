# [November 12, 2026] · [Ars Technica]

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen immediately rather than in stages, replacing Gemini 3.8 Flash across its products including Search, and says it runs 2.5 times faster, makes more than five times fewer fabricated claims and more than triples Flash's Terminal-Bench score. A new Orchestrator in Antigravity lets Carbon plan while Hydrogen executes, reaching near-Carbon Terminal-Bench success while using 24% less quota. Perplexity separately open-sourced Decider v1.3, 27-billion and 4-billion-parameter models under Apache 2.0 that return probabilities for yes/no, multiple-choice and scoring questions instead of text. Fast executors and cheap decision models are becoming the default economics of agent workloads. Google | Ars Technica | MarkTechPost: Decider
