# Corma raises $125 million for defensive AI agents as the Fireworks breach reaches Uber drivers

Cybersecurity startup Corma raised $125 million at a $1 billion valuation to build defensive agents on cyber-trained models, aimed at attackers using agents built on general-purpose models. Uber is notifying drivers that driver's-license images and personal data were exposed after attackers used compromised Fireworks.ai credentials to reach its account. The FBI also warned that several hacking groups are abusing Artex, an open-source LLM penetration-testing platform, in attacks on financial, government, tech and defense targets.
