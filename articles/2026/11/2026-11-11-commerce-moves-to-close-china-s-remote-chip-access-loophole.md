# Commerce moves to close China's remote chip-access loophole as Congress digs into Nvidia's China records

The Commerce Department is finalizing a rule, possibly to be announced this month, that would bar Chinese AI firms from remotely using advanced chips in overseas data centers and extend controls to seven more countries including Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand. Senators Elizabeth Warren and Chris Coons asked the administration to preserve its Nvidia-China records, the Journal reports that Nvidia stopped breaking out Singapore revenue last year as scrutiny of Megaspeed rose, and Reuters reports that a PLA-linked university bought H200 chips in October despite sanctions. CIO Dive reports that President Trump has signaled openness to AI guardrails after the midterm election losses.
