# Meta changes leadership of its AI infrastructure buildout

Meta infrastructure chief and engineering co-head Santosh Janardhan is stepping down, with Dave Brown taking over. Brown joined Meta earlier this year after a long tenure at Amazon. The transition places new leadership over the infrastructure organization as Meta expands the data-center capacity supporting its AI strategy.
