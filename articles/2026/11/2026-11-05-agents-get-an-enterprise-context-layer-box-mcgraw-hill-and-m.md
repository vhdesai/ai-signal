# Agents get an enterprise context layer: Box, McGraw Hill and multiagent finance

At BoxWorks, Box launched a retrieval engine built on Nvidia's Nemotron models that turns unstructured files into a permission-aware knowledge base for agents, alongside a multistep workflow orchestrator and rogue-agent threat detection; its accuracy and token-savings figures are vendor-reported. McGraw Hill is connecting district-adopted K-12 curriculum to Claude for Teachers through an MCP connector. Capgemini research cited by CIO Dive finds finance firms moving to multiagent systems and model routing despite operational complexity and unproven value.
