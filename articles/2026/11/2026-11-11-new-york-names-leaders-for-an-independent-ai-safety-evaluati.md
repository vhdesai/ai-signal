# New York names leaders for an independent AI-safety evaluation center

Cornell Tech dean Greg Morrisett and Columbia's Jeannette Wing will serve as interim co-directors of the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research. The Binghamton-based center will convene more than 80 scientists to develop independent evaluations and testing standards for AI systems, supported by Empire AI computing and linked to oversight under the state's RAISE Act. Together with RAISE Act registration, New York is building institutional capacity for external scrutiny of frontier models.
