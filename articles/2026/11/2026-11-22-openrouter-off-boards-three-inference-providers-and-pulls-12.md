# OpenRouter off-boards three inference providers and pulls 125 endpoints from zero-data-retention eligibility

OpenRouter removed three inference providers and made 125 endpoints ineligible for zero data retention after canary-token tests by Dragos researchers raised concerns about how some providers handled request data. OpenRouter says its own systems were not breached. It is now checking providers' network paths for forwarding to undisclosed third parties. SECURITY
