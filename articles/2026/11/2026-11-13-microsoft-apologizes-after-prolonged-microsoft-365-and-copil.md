# Microsoft apologizes after prolonged Microsoft 365 and Copilot disruption

CNA / AFP Microsoft apologized following a disruption lasting roughly 24 hours that affected Microsoft 365 and Copilot, according to CNA’s AFP report. The company attributed the incident to a technical issue during an earlier network-capacity upgrade, not a cyberattack or AI agents. The outage also interrupted Microsoft 365 connectors used by OpenAI and Anthropic. This summary reflects the cited article and does not assume a later restoration update.
