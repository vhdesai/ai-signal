# MIT and Cornell lift detection of disguised AI text from 27% to 93% with test-time adaptation

Researchers from MIT and Cornell Tech showed that AI-text detectors fail when writing styles shift, new models appear or AI output is disguised to look human. Using test-time adaptation, which lets a deployed detector adjust to new unlabeled text without retraining, they caught 93% of disguised AI text, against about 27% for a commercial detector. The work matters for publishers, educators and hiring teams, and for security teams fighting phishing and search fraud. AI Safety & Policy EXCLUSIVE EXPORT CONTROLS
