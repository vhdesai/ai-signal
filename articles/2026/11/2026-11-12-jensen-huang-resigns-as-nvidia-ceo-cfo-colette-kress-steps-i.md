# Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress steps in while the board searches for a successor

Nvidia said Jensen Huang has resigned as president, CEO and director after reports of two extramarital affairs, one with a Megaspeed executive whom US officials believe was a Chinese intelligence asset and another with a direct report. CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO while keeping her finance role, longtime director Harvey Jones becomes the company's first board chair, and Nvidia says customer commitments and its product roadmap will continue without interruption. DealBook notes that Huang had roughly 60 direct reports and no succession plan, with outside names such as Lisa Su, Rene Haas, Lip-Bu Tan and Johny Srouji floated, while analysts ask what happens to the financial backstops Nvidia has extended to neoclouds and startups. For infrastructure buyers, the near-term risk is continuity of relationships and deal-making at the industry's central supplier rather than product availability.
