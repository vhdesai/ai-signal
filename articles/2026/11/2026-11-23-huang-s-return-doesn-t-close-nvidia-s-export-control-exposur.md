# Huang's return doesn't close Nvidia's export-control exposure as the Megaspeed probe stays open

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton said the investigation into Alice Huang and Megaspeed is "ongoing," and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the real question is how Nvidia chips reached China, pointing to "malicious actors down at the bottom of the company." The New York Times reported that Alice Huang was in contact with an officer of China's Ministry of State Security for more than a year, partly overlapping her relationship with Huang. Expect scrutiny to shift from the CEO to Nvidia's sales compliance in Southeast Asia.
