# [November 19, 2026] · [9to5Mac]

Google added multi-step computer use to Gemini's Mac app, letting it work across native apps, local files and websites in the background while users keep their screen and can pause, stop or approve actions; it launches in English in the U.S. for Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers. An Apple job listing reported by Bloomberg describes an agent that browses the web, works across apps, runs commands and writes code, and Apple shares rose premarket Friday on the report. OpenAI began testing its always-on Dots agents with a small group of ChatGPT Plus subscribers and expanded Business access, after launching them for Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise plans.
