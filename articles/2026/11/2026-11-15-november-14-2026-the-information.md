# [November 14, 2026] · [The Information]

Altimeter's Brad Gerstner and Atreides' Gavin Baker urged Nvidia's board to run a slow, deliberate CEO search that keeps the potential return of Jensen Huang on the table, while a reinstatement petition passed 115,000 signatures, including 5,965 current employees and Elon Musk. Business Insider says Nvidia lost $573 billion in market value last week and that the next CEO must rebuild trust with the workforce, and The Information reports the board acted only at general counsel Tim Teter's insistence. Axios notes Nvidia is about 8% of the S&P 500, so its near-30% monthly drop reaches ordinary index-fund retirement savings.
