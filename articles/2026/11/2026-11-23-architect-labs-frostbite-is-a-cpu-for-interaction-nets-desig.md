# Architect Labs' Frostbite is a CPU for interaction nets designed end to end by AI agents

Architect Labs says AI agents took Frostbite, a processor built around interaction nets, from theory to final GDS layout on a 7nm process. Across 25 workloads the company reports gains of 4.59x in performance per watt and 8.77x in performance per area, rising to 23.12x performance per watt on conv2d. The results are pre-silicon and self-reported, and the company plans an FPGA playground so others can test the design. Academic Research RESEARCH
