# MIT's AI "copilot" for structural design asks engineers for feedback while it optimizes

MIT researchers made topology optimization, an algorithmic method for generating structural designs, interactive. While the design is being generated, the system shows regions to the engineer for feedback instead of revealing only the finished design. A paper in Communications Engineering reports that the resulting structures were easier to manufacture and had higher buckling loads, at a cost of only about 15 seconds of extra design time. The authors argue that letting engineers steer the process addresses the trust gap that has limited the method's use in buildings and bridges.
