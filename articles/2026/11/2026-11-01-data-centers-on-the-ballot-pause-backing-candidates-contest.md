# Data centers on the ballot: pause-backing candidates contest 15 governor's races as OpenAI pledges grid relief

The Information finds at least one major-party nominee backs a data-center moratorium in 21 of 36 governor's races, and pause supporters could win in up to 15 states holding more than a third of the U.S. population. OpenAI launched its Grid Protectors program, piloted at its planned Effingham County, Georgia campus, committing to shed up to 1,000 MW during grid emergencies; The Verge notes that capacity figure dates to a July agreement. Today's results will help set siting costs and permitting risk for the next build cycle.
