# A petition asking Nvidia's board to bring back Jensen Huang while an independent investigation runs had more than…

A petition asking Nvidia's board to bring back Jensen Huang while an independent investigation runs had more than 115,000 signatures by Saturday afternoon, including 5,965 current employees, 1,108 former employees and 63,063 shareholders, according to the petition site. Elon Musk, Replit CEO Amjad Masad, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney and Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan are among the signers, and The Information separately reported the staff support. The signer counts are self-reported, and the petition has no formal standing. Still, it adds pressure on a board that has named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and is running a CEO search.
