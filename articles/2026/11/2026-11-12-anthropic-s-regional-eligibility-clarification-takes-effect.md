# Anthropic’s regional eligibility clarification takes effect

TechTimes UK reports that Anthropic’s revised regional eligibility policy takes effect today, extending its ownership-and-control restrictions to some firms operating in otherwise supported countries. The coverage includes access through cloud platforms and authorized resellers, making corporate structure relevant alongside employee location. This is implementation of a previously announced policy clarification, not a newly announced release or evidence of a fresh wave of account closures.
