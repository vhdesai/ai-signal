# [November 18, 2026] · [The Information]

Nvidia reported October-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, more than double a year earlier, with data-center revenue of $107.6 billion, gross margin of 74.8% and fourth-quarter guidance of $130 billion, all above estimates. Kress raised next fiscal year's growth forecast to 75% from 70%, said supply commitments rose to $390 billion from $279 billion, and noted customers brought up Vera Rubin systems in half the time Grace Blackwell took. Nvidia also said it will stop shipping licensed Hopper chips to China, a $2.4 billion business last quarter, and will delay its quarterly SEC filing for weeks, while shares rose about 5% after hours.
