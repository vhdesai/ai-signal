# [November 3, 2026] · [The Information]

Marvell will acquire laser and optics maker Lumentum in an all-stock deal valued at about $80 billion by the Wall Street Journal and The Information, or about $115 billion in other accounts cited by DealBook. Marvell shareholders would own 67% of the combined company, and Nvidia invested $2 billion in each company this year. It is a large bet that data-center interconnects shift from copper to optics, though shares in both companies fell on the news.
