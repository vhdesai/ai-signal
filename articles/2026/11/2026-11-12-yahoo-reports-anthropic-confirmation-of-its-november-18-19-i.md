# Yahoo reports Anthropic confirmation of its November 18–19 IPO timetable

Yahoo Finance reports that Anthropic announced plans to price its IPO on Wednesday, November 18, and begin Nasdaq trading under ANT on Thursday, November 19. The dates had already been reported from unnamed sources on November 10; this item concerns Yahoo's report of company confirmation, not the initial disclosure. Pricing and the trading debut remain scheduled events, not completed milestones.
