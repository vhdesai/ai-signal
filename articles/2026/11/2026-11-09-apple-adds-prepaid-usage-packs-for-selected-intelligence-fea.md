# Apple adds prepaid usage packs for selected intelligence features

Apple has added $49.99 intelligence usage packs as a one-time purchase for selected AI features, according to 9to5Mac. Unused usage rolls over each month but expires after 12 months. The packs put a consumption-based price on access to those cloud-inference features rather than requiring a subscription for the purchased usage.
