# WSJ: Southeast Asian data centers gave Chinese AI firms a "backdoor" to Nvidia compute

A Wall Street Journal investigation examines how massive data centers in Southeast Asia gave Chinese companies crucial computing power and provided cover for alleged chip smuggling. The piece ties that route to strategy under Huang. The article is paywalled, so this summary is based on the publisher's description. It adds to evidence that the compute reaching China through offshore hosting, rather than direct chip sales, is the real export-control gap. INVESTIGATION GOVERNANCE
