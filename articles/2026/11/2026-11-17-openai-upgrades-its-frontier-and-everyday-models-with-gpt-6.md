# OpenAI upgrades its frontier and everyday models with GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

OpenAI is refreshing both its high-end and everyday-work offerings, with GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol available through ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API. The launch emphasizes professional deliverables and coding workflows, with OpenAI claiming improved task completion and lower output-token use. For enterprise evaluation, the central question is whether these gains reduce the cost of an accepted deliverable, rather than simply improve benchmark scores. OpenAI also assigns Astra a High AI self-improvement rating, making the accompanying safeguards relevant to deployment reviews.
