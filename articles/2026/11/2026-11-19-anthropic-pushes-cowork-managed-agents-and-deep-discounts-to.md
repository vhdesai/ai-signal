# Anthropic pushes Cowork, Managed Agents and deep discounts to grow beyond coding

To justify its new valuation, Anthropic is gaining traction with Claude Cowork for nontechnical business users and persuading some customers to spend on new areas such as cybersecurity. It is also pitching Claude Managed Agents, which lets developers build and run agents without managing the underlying servers. Competition is pushing Anthropic toward deep discounts and share-of-wallet clauses, and some customers are wary of sudden spending surges. AGENTS PAYMENTS
