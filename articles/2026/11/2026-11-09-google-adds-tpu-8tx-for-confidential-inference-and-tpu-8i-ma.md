# Google adds TPU 8tx for confidential inference and TPU 8i Max for very long contexts

Google added two systems to its eighth-generation TPU line, so it now has four TPU systems this year. TPU 8tx uses the same chip as TPU 8t with custom AMD host processors. It keeps data and model weights protected even while they are being processed, for regulated companies and governments, and Google says latency is up to 2x lower than TPU 8t. TPU 8i Max puts two TPU 8i chips in one package, doubling compute, on-chip memory, and memory bandwidth, and it fits twice the silicon in the same rack space. Google also made two TPU management tools generally available, open-sourced TPU Sync for moving model memory between chips, and says TPUs now have nearly 100 customers. Google is competing more directly with Nvidia for regulated and agent workloads. NVIDIA SOVEREIGN AI KOREA
