# [November 18, 2026] · [PitchBook]

Anthropic plans to raise its IPO price after demand topped six times the 825 million shares on offer, with advisers guiding toward a valuation near $1.5 trillion, or about $83 to $85 a share against the $70 to $78 range, ahead of Nasdaq trading under ANT on Thursday. Spark Capital, Factorial Funds, Sound Ventures and Menlo Ventures will hold stakes worth more than 10 times their investment, and Iconiq and Lightspeed each hold stakes valued above $25 billion, The Information reports. PitchBook holds its estimate at $1.42 trillion, about $79 a share, and calculates that a $1.5 trillion price implies only about 9.9% annual returns on its forecast. EXCLUSIVE CHINA
