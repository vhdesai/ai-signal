# Tech leaders press Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang

Source: TechCrunch · By Anthony Ha A public petition is calling on Nvidia to reinstate recently ousted CEO Jensen Huang, with backers including Elon Musk, Replit CEO Amjad Masad, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, and Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan. Signature totals are not a dependable measure of support because organizers have been removing fake entries, a problem also described in Business Insider reporting republished by Yahoo (November 14, 2026, 3:35 p.m. EST). The campaign adds public pressure on Nvidia’s board, but it is not a board decision and does not establish that Huang will return.
