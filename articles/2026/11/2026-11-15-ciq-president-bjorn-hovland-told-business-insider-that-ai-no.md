# CIQ president Bjorn Hovland told Business Insider that AI now writes more than 90% of the company's code, but CIQ…

CIQ president Bjorn Hovland told Business Insider that AI now writes more than 90% of the company's code, but CIQ "cannot hire enough engineers". It hires almost no junior developers and wants five to 10 years of experience, because someone has to verify AI-generated code for enterprise and government customers. Founder Gregory Kurtzer said he built a Linux-compatible kernel with AI in 11 days. The account adds to evidence that AI coding shifts demand toward review and system judgment, which strains the pipeline that produces senior engineers.
