# Anthropic’s proposed IPO could raise about $64.4 billion

Reuters reports that Anthropic’s latest draft prospectus outlines an IPO of up to 825 million shares at $70–$78 each, raising as much as approximately $64.4 billion. A separate $26 billion cornerstone private placement includes Alphabet’s Google, Nvidia and AMD, and should not be confused with the public offering proceeds. CNBC, citing sources, reports targeted pricing on November 18 and trading on November 19; those dates are reported plans, not completed milestones. The proposed financing underscores the scale of capital behind frontier AI, but an offering range is not evidence of a settled valuation or successful listing.
