# [November 10, 2026] · [OpenAI]

IBM and Microsoft launched Imagine Enterprise, which pairs more than 6,000 prebuilt IBM Consulting agents and assets with Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, Fabric, Entra and Purview, plus forward-deployed engineers for regulated industries. OpenAI made ChatGPT Enterprise purchasable through AWS Marketplace, and Google Cloud previewed Gemini for healthcare with HCA Healthcare, Highmark Health and Northwell Health to automate records synthesis and prior authorization. Agent vendors are increasingly selling through existing procurement, cloud and consulting relationships rather than standalone. CIO Dive | IBM Newsroom | OpenAI | Google Cloud Blog
