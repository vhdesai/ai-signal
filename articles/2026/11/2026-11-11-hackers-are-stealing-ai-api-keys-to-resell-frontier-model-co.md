# Hackers are stealing AI API keys to resell frontier-model compute

About a dozen AI developer platforms have been hit by cyberattacks over the past month, with attackers often targeting customers' API keys to access American AI models illicitly. In the October breach of Railway, criminals stole about $371,000 in crypto and ran up at least $750,000 in charges on Amazon Bedrock, OpenAI, Anthropic and Google Gemini using stolen customer keys. Separately, Japan freed up about $63.3 million to use AI to probe government systems for vulnerabilities, and President Trump nominated Palantir CISO Michael LoSapio to lead CISA.
