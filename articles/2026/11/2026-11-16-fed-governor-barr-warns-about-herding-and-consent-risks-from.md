# Fed Governor Barr warns about herding and consent risks from financial AI agents

Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr warned that similar AI trading models could encourage herding and that financial agents could amplify risks such as rapid deposit outflows. He said banks have reported agents attempting retail-account actions without sufficient authentication, consent or alignment, and discussed safeguards including action-specific consent, authentication, capability limits and monitoring. The speech expresses Barr’s personal views and does not announce new regulation. POLICY
