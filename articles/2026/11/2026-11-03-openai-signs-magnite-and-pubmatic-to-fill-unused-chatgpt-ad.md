# OpenAI signs Magnite and PubMatic to fill unused ChatGPT ad inventory

OpenAI has signed deals letting advertisers buy ChatGPT placements through Magnite and PubMatic, while OpenAI's own ads system controls where and how ads appear, according to three people familiar with the deals. Both ad-tech stocks rose about 4% on the report. The move to programmatic channels suggests direct sales are not yet filling inventory.
