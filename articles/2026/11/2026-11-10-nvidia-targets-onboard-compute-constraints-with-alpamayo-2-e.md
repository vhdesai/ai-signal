# NVIDIA targets onboard compute constraints with Alpamayo 2 Edge

NVIDIA introduced Alpamayo 2 Edge to fit reasoning-based autonomous-driving models within vehicle compute and response-time limits. The company describes a 2-billion-parameter backbone and reports optimized end-to-end latency of 160–190 milliseconds on DRIVE AGX Thor, with shorter explanations during routine driving. The release also covers explanation evaluation and training feedback; latency and other performance claims remain vendor-reported, not an independent vehicle-safety validation.
