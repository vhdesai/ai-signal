# Report: Alice Huang's companies routed at least $7.5 billion in Nvidia chips to China as Congress revisits export controls

According to two people who know her, Alice Huang, her sister, and her husband built a web of companies in China and Singapore that has moved at least $7.5 billion of Nvidia chips through Chinese companies since 2023. In a separate report, The Information says White House officials gave feedback in late October on chip export controls being considered in the annual defense authorization bill. People familiar with the talks read that as a sign the administration may not block the controls, as it has before.
