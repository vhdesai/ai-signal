# MIT-led PLOW project receives up to $53 million for AI-designed herbicide alternatives

An MIT-led team headed by Christopher Voigt received an ARPA-H award of up to $53 million for PLOW, a project using generative AI to design chemical or small-interfering-RNA herbicide candidates. The planned process screens candidates for cost, resistance and risks to people and the environment before assays and greenhouse testing. This is a funded research program, not a demonstrated commercial replacement for existing weed killers, making experimental validation the key milestone to watch.
