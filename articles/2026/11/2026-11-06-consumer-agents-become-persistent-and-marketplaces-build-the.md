# Consumer agents become persistent, and marketplaces build their own

Meta launched Spaces, project workspaces that Muse builds from a chat description, such as trackers, planners and dashboards, and that refresh in the background from connected apps; it is Meta's second Muse feature in a week. Amazon, which blocked Muse from querying its site in September, now lets invited US customers text Alexa to search, compare, track prices and buy, with a wider rollout in the coming weeks. Large marketplaces are choosing to run their own agents rather than let general-purpose agents shop on their storefronts. Sources: TechCrunch | TechCrunch
