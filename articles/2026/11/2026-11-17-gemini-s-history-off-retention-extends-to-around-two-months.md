# Gemini’s history-off retention extends to around two months

Google’s updated Gemini privacy guidance describes retention of temporary or history-off chats for around two months, up from 72 hours according to TechCrunch. Google says temporary chats are not used to train models, but retention and safety processing still apply; history and model-training controls are separate. Organizations should distinguish “not used for training” from “not retained” and verify applicable enterprise terms, because work and school arrangements may differ.
