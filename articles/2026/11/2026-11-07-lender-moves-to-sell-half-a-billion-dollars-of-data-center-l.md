# lender moves to sell half a billion dollars of data center loans, including Stargate debt, as builders look abroad

The Information's weekend column reports that a Dutch bank is trying to quickly sell about $500 million of data center loans, including some tied to OpenAI's Project Stargate. This came after the Nasdaq fell 5.5% on Thursday. The column also points to Google's $15 billion data center and cloud expansion in Malaysia as an example of AI companies looking for sites outside the US. Its author argues that Democrats' anti-AI rhetoric will soften once they take office. When lenders start reducing data center exposure, borrowing costs for neoclouds and developers could rise before any new law is passed.
