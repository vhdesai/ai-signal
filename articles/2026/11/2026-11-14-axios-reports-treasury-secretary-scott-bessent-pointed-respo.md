# Axios reports Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent pointed responsibility for the Megaspeed chip matter at the Commerce…

Axios reports Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent pointed responsibility for the Megaspeed chip matter at the Commerce Department, saying "Commerce has the pen," and a follow-up piece says MAGA-aligned voices are turning on Howard Lutnick. Separately, Sen. Tim Scott has asked Huang and Lutnick to testify before the Senate Banking Committee, and Democrats Richard Neal and Ron Wyden opened an inquiry into the administration's China chip approvals. Axios article text was not independently verified. Expect export-licensing decisions for Nvidia and peers to slow while the probes continue.
