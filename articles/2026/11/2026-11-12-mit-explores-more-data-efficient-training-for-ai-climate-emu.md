# MIT explores more data-efficient training for AI climate emulators

MIT researchers developed a method that optimizes synthetic emissions scenarios to train climate emulators that adapt better to unfamiliar conditions using less training data. MIT reports potential to reduce the expensive earth-system-model runs needed for training-data generation from six or eight to one, including better predictions for scenarios such as local pollution controls. This could lower the cost of updating climate-risk analysis, but validation with full-scale earth-system models remains future work rather than an established operational result.
