# Cerebras surges 17% after beating on quarterly revenue and announcing Microsoft deal

Cerebras disclosed a Microsoft agreement alongside quarterly results that exceeded expectations. CEO Andrew Feldman told CNBC the agreement was signed in October, with revenue expected to begin in the first quarter of 2027. Reported quarterly core revenue of $230.9 million, up 70% year over year, provides a concrete demand signal, but the Microsoft relationship should not be described as already contributing revenue.
