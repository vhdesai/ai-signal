# [November 20, 2026] · [Hacked.com]

Amazon Threat Intelligence detailed a September campaign by a China-nexus actor whose agents, running the open-source Cairn framework and given only targets and a goal of shell access, found a previously unknown flaw in the MONAI Label medical-imaging tool and achieved an unauthorized file write in about 11 minutes, later escalating to root. Amazon's account does not establish that patient records were stolen. Separately, Signal now blocks screenshots by default on iOS 27 and Android to keep Siri, Gemini and other built-in assistants from reading chats, with president Meredith Whittaker saying platforms give apps no robust way to restrict AI access and calling the fix incomplete.
