# Bharat Semi raises $24 million for programmable AI sensor chips

Bharat Semi raised a $24 million all-equity seed round led by Info Edge Group, South Park Commons and Indus Bridge Ventures to develop chips combining programmable image capture, processing and AI. The pre-revenue company is targeting initial production in 2027 and reports design wins with General Atomics and Kaynes Semicon, which should not be confused with production revenue. A separate fabrication-plant proposal is not being funded by this round. AI Safety & Policy POLICY
