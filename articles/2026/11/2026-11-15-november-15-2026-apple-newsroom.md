# [November 15, 2026] · [Apple Newsroom]

Apple launched a research app that answers health questions with citations to clinical literature through OpenEvidence, aiming to enroll up to 200,000 U.S. adults aged 21 and older for more than a year. Participants can optionally share HealthKit data to personalize answers, and Apple says study data will not be used to train AI models. The study is a health-information experiment rather than a diagnostic service.
