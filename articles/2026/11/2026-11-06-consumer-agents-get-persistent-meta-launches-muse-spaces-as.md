# Consumer agents get persistent: Meta launches Muse Spaces as Amazon lets shoppers text Alexa

Meta launched Spaces, project workspaces that its Muse agent builds from a chat description, such as trackers, planners and dashboards, that refresh in the background from connected apps; it is Meta's second Muse feature in a week. Amazon, which blocked Muse from querying its site in September, now lets invited US customers text Alexa to search, compare, track prices and buy, with a wider rollout in the coming weeks.
