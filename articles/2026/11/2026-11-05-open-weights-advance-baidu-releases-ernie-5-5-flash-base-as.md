# Open weights advance: Baidu releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base as Ricursive claims a US open coding lead

Baidu released downloadable weights for ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, a 280-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 14 billion active per token, as a pre-training checkpoint for domain adaptation and reinforcement-learning research; a post-trained version will follow. Ricursive Intelligence's dense, Apache-2.0 Ripple-65B Preview reports 80.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 79.1 on SWE-Bench Pro v2.0 Hard at a claimed total R&D cost of about $32 million. Benchmark scores and costs are self-reported. Sources: Unite.AI | VentureBeat Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
