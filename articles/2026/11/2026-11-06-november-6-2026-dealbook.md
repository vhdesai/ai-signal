# [November 6, 2026] · [DealBook]

Major investors are increasingly skeptical that Anthropic's blockbuster IPO can proceed at the expected $1.5 trillion valuation after the AI selloff, according to the Journal. DealBook reported that one investor suggested the company might raise less and another said it may need to delay a listing widely expected the week after next, while Kalshi odds of a listing before December 1 have fallen sharply. Michael Burry told Business Insider that Anthropic is "betting its entire existence" on optimism and that he has no plans to own the stock. EXCLUSIVE FUNDING
