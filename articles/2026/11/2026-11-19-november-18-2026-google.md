# [November 18, 2026] · [Google]

Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon with a 1-million-token context window at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, rolling out in the Gemini API, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity and paid Gemini app plans. Google says Neon ranks #2 on the Vals Index (70.9%) and #1 on Zapier's AutomationBench and Andon Labs' Vending-Bench 2, though these are vendor-cited results. Alphabet shares rose close to 4% in premarket trading after the release, the WSJ reported.
