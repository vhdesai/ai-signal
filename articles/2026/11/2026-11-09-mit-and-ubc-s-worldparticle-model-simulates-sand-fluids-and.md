# MIT and UBC's WorldParticle model simulates sand, fluids, and proteins with one network

WorldParticle breaks any material into particles and uses a neural network to predict each step of their motion. It does this by learning the underlying physics instead of hand-coding an equation and solver for each material. The researchers say it adapts to materials, forces, and environments it was not trained on. The aim is a general simulator that works across physics domains, which today require separate custom simulators. The work, with collaborators from Georgia Tech, Meta, and others, will be presented at SIGGRAPH Asia. MIT ROBOTICS LLM
