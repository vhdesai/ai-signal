# Nvidia sinks 14% on a reported SEC accounting probe, extending its longest losing streak since 2008

Nvidia fell 14% on Friday after Reuters reported that the SEC is examining possible accounting issues, its seventh straight losing week, leaving the stock down 46% and about $2.6 trillion lighter, at roughly 8.4 times forward earnings against 29 times for AMD. Wired reports the board's central concern was Jensen Huang's "lack of candor," not only the affairs, and Huang will face a closed-door House interview on December 1 before testifying publicly in the Senate. At the DealBook Summit, Elon Musk, a major Nvidia customer, defended Huang, likening his ouster to "taking Michael Jordan off your team when you're on a winning streak." Governance and accounting uncertainty now outweighs Nvidia's strong earnings, which could weaken its hand with large customers.
