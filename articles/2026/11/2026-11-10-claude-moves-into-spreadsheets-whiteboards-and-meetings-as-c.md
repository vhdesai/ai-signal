# Claude moves into spreadsheets, whiteboards and meetings as Cursor makes coding agents multiplayer

Anthropic launched betas of Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards, made Claude Data generally available, and previewed Claude Meetings for Team and Enterprise customers with Google Meet support. The Information reports that Microsoft shares fell more than 3.5% on the news before paring losses, because the apps let users do Office- and Workspace-style work directly inside Claude. Cursor separately rolled out a multiplayer beta of Projects that shares requirements, decisions, task state and agent communications across a team.
