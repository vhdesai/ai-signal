# Salesforce agrees to buy contact-center AI startup Cresta, reportedly for $2.7 billion

Salesforce announced Monday that it will acquire Cresta, a nine-year-old maker of AI software that automates contact-center work. Salesforce did not disclose the price; The Information reports it is $2.7 billion. The deal continues Salesforce's recent acquisition spree and adds to its customer-service offering, one of the first areas where AI agents are replacing per-seat software. Standalone contact-center AI vendors should expect more consolidation. OPENAI ADVERTISING
