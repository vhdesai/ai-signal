# Claude adds meetings, whiteboards, and spreadsheets to its workplace push

Anthropic introduced Claude Meetings alongside Whiteboards and Sheets, extending its assistant into collaborative work previously centered on office suites. Meetings is entering beta for Team and Enterprise customers with Google Meet support, allowing the assistant to begin tasks during a discussion. For enterprise buyers, the competitive question is shifting from standalone model quality to how effectively agents participate in existing workflows.
