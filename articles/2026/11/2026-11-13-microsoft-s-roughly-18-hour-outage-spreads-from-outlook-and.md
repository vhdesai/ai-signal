# Microsoft's roughly 18-hour outage spreads from Outlook and Teams to Copilot, Defender, and Intune

The outage began Thursday with Outlook and Teams, then spread worldwide to consumer and business email, files, Copilot, Defender, Intune, and some cloud-based Windows PCs. Microsoft attributed it to "a large number of servers being unexpectedly unavailable." It said there is no evidence of malicious activity but has not named a root cause. Kentik reported that traffic began returning after about 18 hours, and service was still being restored Friday morning. This is Microsoft's second major cloud outage since September, which will add to customer questions about the reliability of AI services that depend on its productivity stack.
