# [November 20, 2026] · [METR]

METR's Q4 capability update, covering public models up to Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, finds models usually match or outperform skilled humans on its coding and metric-optimization tasks, and its recruited human baseliners typically could not beat them; Unite.AI reports measured time horizons are doubling roughly every 110 days. Models do worse on judgment-heavy tasks that are costlier to turn into reinforcement-learning environments, and METR says capabilities are getting harder to measure. It concludes that public evidence is insufficient to rule out drastic AI R&D acceleration within the next couple of years, a scenario in which capabilities improve faster than humans can assess catastrophic risks.
