# Nvidia shares fall about 5% before Monday's open as investors weigh key-person risk

Just after 8 a.m. ET, Nvidia was down about 5% in premarket trading, pointing to an open near $177. That would erase more than $220 billion from Friday's $4.5 trillion market value. The stock had already fallen about 20% over the past month, amid worries about neocloud debt and post-midterm limits on data center construction. Investor Mohamed El-Erian said markets probably have not fully priced in the key-person risk.
