# Frontier-lab valuations reset: OpenAI banks $11 billion as Anthropic heads into a cooler roadshow

OpenAI has wired roughly $11 billion, the first tranche of a round valuing it at $1.25 trillion that could reach about $20 billion, well short of the $30 billion at $1.4 trillion it reportedly sought in September. Anthropic will still begin formal IPO marketing the week of November 9, but some prospective buyers now put fair value at $1 trillion to $1.25 trillion, against a roughly $2 trillion ambition a month ago; WSJ's Markets P.M. notes it could raise less or lower its target. Whatever price Anthropic sets becomes the public benchmark for frontier labs, including OpenAI's expected 2027 listing.
