# Anthropic heads into Thursday's Nasdaq debut with $900B+ in spending commitments

Anthropic is expected to list on the Nasdaq on Thursday at a price range of $70–$78 a share. AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC are together investing $26 billion at the IPO price. The prospectus shows over $900 billion in outstanding commitments, including at least $110 billion with Amazon over a decade and $125–150 billion for Google chips starting in 2028, against a $104 billion net loss for the first nine months of 2026. Proceeds could exceed $90 billion on top of $132 billion in existing cash. The offering is effectively a referendum on the economics of frontier-lab spending that underwrites suppliers' revenue.
