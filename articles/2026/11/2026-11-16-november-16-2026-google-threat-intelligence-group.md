# November 16, 2026 · Google Threat Intelligence Group

Google Threat Intelligence Group reported finding exploit_agent on an exposed threat server, automating patch comparison, flaw discovery, exploit creation and execution. Google described this as its first observed end-to-end LLM exploitation without a human in the loop, not an industry-wide first. Separately, TechCrunch reported an FBI warning that attackers using Artex sometimes breached networks in under three hours, often through exposed credentials or weak authentication rather than novel AI-discovered vulnerabilities. The reports concern different tools; together, they reinforce patching and identity controls as defensive priorities.
