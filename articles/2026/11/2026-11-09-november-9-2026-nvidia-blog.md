# November 9, 2026 · NVIDIA Blog

At the Conference on Robot Learning, NVIDIA introduced GR00T 2, an open world-action model built on Cosmos and trained on robot and human data, including 100,000 hours of egocentric video; NVIDIA says it reached 71% average success across 50 tasks with one demonstration per task and 95% with five. PitchBook reports that robotics-data suppliers such as Maxinsights, which projects a $250 million annualized run rate from 4,000 people recording household tasks, are among the few robotics startups with real revenue, as physical-AI startups have raised $2.1 billion this year. As models learn from human video and deployed robots generate their own data, those vendors must move into testing and fine-tuning before their window closes.
