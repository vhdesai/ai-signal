# The Information reports the administration declined to release information about its AI testing framework

The Information reports the administration declined to release information about its AI testing framework. The briefing is paywalled and specifics, including which framework and what was requested, were not independently verified. Limited transparency on federal pre-release testing matters for labs participating in voluntary evaluations, such as Google's Gemini 4 Carbon, and is likely to draw scrutiny from a new Congress.
