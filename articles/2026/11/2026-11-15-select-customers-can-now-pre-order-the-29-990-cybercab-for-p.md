# Select customers can now pre-order the $29,990 Cybercab for personal use, with delivery estimated for December 2026 in…

Select customers can now pre-order the $29,990 Cybercab for personal use, with delivery estimated for December 2026 in a few Texas cities. The vehicle runs unsupervised only inside Tesla's robotaxi geofences and carries a $299 monthly operating fee, which Elon Musk called "too high" and said should be closer to about $115. Musk said nationwide availability depends on regulatory approval. Tesla is ending Cybertruck production next year to free capacity for Cybercab.
