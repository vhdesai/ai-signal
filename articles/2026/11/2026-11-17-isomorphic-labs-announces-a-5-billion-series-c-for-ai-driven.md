# Isomorphic Labs announces a $5 billion Series C for AI-driven drug development

Isomorphic Labs announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia, with a16z, other new investors and continued investment from Alphabet. The company says the funding will expand its IsoDDE drug-design models, therapeutic programs, and bioengineering and clinical infrastructure. Its candidates remain preclinical: a goal of entering clinical testing is not evidence of approved medicines or demonstrated clinical success.
