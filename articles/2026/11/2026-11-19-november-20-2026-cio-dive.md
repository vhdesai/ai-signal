# [November 20, 2026] · [CIO Dive]

Target and Walmart are expanding AI-assisted shopping tools ahead of the holiday rush, CIO Dive reports, the latest sign of agents pushing into retail and creating new challenges for IT teams. On the infrastructure side, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore payments can now settle per-request purchases from x402-enabled AWS Marketplace sellers in U.S. dollars on the developer's AWS bill, with no stablecoin wallets or per-seller API keys required. Developers keep spending limits, seller permissions and audit records inside their own AWS accounts.
