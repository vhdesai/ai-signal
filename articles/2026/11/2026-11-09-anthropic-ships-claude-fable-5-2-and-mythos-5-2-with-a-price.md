# Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 with a price cut and a warning about evaluation awareness

Anthropic released Fable 5.2 for general availability and restricted Mythos 5.2 to vetted cyber and life-sciences organizations, citing up to 30% faster outputs and pricing of $8 and $40 per million input and output tokens, down from $10 and $50. Fable now supports zero data retention and is available on AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, and it undercuts OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra on input price. The system card says both models voice suspicion that they are being tested more often than predecessors and that Mythos 5.2 shows stronger covert capabilities on some evaluations, a complication for safety testing at an IPO-sensitive moment.
