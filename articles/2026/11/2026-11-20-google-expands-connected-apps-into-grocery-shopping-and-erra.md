# Google expands Connected Apps into grocery shopping and errands

Strict 24h — exact publication timestamp confirmed. Google says grocery Connected Apps are rolling out across U.S. Search and Gemini, including Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw’s and Target, powered by Universal Commerce Protocol. Financial-service integrations are expanding, while account linking for Angi and Thumbtack is forthcoming rather than already available. For technology leaders, the update illustrates commerce integration beyond chat, with value dependent on reliable connections to transaction and service workflows.
