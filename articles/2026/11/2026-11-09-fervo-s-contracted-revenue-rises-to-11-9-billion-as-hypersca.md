# Fervo's contracted revenue rises to $11.9 billion as hyperscalers seek steady power

Fervo reported contracted revenue of $11.9 billion, up from $7.2 billion last quarter, and is building a 396-megawatt grid-connected plant for Google due by the end of 2028. Its Utah site is now estimated to support 8.4 gigawatts, up from about 4. Cost per kilowatt fell from $7,000 in the first phase to $5,500 for the Google project, and the company's long-term target is $3,000. Fervo builds in blocks of 33 to 50 megawatts, about the size of a data center hall, so capacity can come online in phases. Falling costs and phased construction make geothermal a more realistic option for always-on data center power.
