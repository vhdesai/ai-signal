# SpaceX's AI arm plans $74 billion of data centers while cutting most of the Grok video team

SpaceX outlined about $24 billion of supercomputing investment in Texas with on-site power and a proposed $50 billion campus in Arizona that still depends on approvals and utility coordination. At the same time, SpaceX's AI group laid off about 60 people from Grok Imagine, its image and video generator, leaving a handful to keep it running as the group prioritizes coding tools and its Grok Bot agent. The moves echo OpenAI's earlier shutdown of Sora and suggest compute-hungry consumer media products are losing out to agents and coding. Bloomberg reports that a SpaceX and Tesla merger could be announced as soon as December.
