# [November 16, 2026] · [BleepingComputer]

Google joined the FBI in warning that agentic AI frameworks such as the Artex penetration-testing platform have pushed the hacking threat to an inflection point. Because AI speeds up both finding and exploiting flaws, Google Project Zero is dropping its 90-plus-30-day disclosure policy, moving to a 60-day deadline in February 2027 and 30 days in January 2028, after which details are published even without a fix. Separately, Microsoft traced last week's 19-hour Microsoft 365 outage to a Windows bug that had been hidden since July.
