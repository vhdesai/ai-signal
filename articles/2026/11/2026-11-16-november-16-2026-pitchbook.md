# [November 16, 2026] · [PitchBook]

The Wall Street Journal reports Kress is expected to rein in the deals through which Nvidia aggressively financed computing contracts and startup investments, and PitchBook's new analyst note asks how much Nvidia's broad venture footprint depended on Huang. Nvidia has lost more than $1.8 trillion in market value in just over a month, and the Journal notes the equal-weight S&P 500 is now up 17.0% this year against 11.5% for the cap-weighted index. Samsung, TSMC and SK Hynix fell about 4%, 6% and 8% in Monday trading, and Business Insider says the slump is testing Asia's AI boom.
