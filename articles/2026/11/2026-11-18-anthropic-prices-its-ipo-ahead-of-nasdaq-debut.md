# Anthropic prices its IPO ahead of Nasdaq debut

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 per share, with 980 million shares offered by the company and selling stockholders. Trading under ANT is expected to begin on Nasdaq on November 19, with closing expected November 20, subject to customary conditions. The pricing is a significant financing milestone, but the offering includes secondary shares whose proceeds do not go to Anthropic. Date verification: Official announcement dated November 18; contemporaneous TechCrunch reporting is timestamped November 18 at 2:11 p.m. PST, within the window.
