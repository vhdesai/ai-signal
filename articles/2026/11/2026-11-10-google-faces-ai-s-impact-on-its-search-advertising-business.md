# Google faces AI's impact on its search advertising business

Some advertisers say part of their Google search budgets now goes unspent because too few people see or click on the ads. They attribute this to more users researching in AI chatbots instead of search. One Los Angeles advertiser who spends about $2,000 a day on Google search ads noticed the pattern about eight months ago. Combined with Monday's finding that ChatGPT has about four-fifths of chatbot traffic, this is an early sign that ad spending may be moving away from Google's most profitable business.
