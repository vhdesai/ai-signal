# Google adds confidential-computing and long-context TPUs as Nvidia opens NVLink to Korean chips

Google introduced TPU 8tx, which pairs the TPU 8t chip with custom AMD hosts and a hardware secure enclave for regulated industries and governments, and TPU 8i Max, which packages two TPU 8i chips together to double compute and memory for very long contexts; Google says TPUs now have nearly 100 customers. Korean chipmaker Rebellions will connect its R200 inference processors to Nvidia's NVLink Fusion and MGX racks, with SK Telecom deploying the systems for Korea's sovereign AI program from 2028, and Nvidia also backed interconnect startup Point2 and plans stakes in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD and Upstage. The common thread is specialization: hyperscaler silicon is splitting by workload, and Nvidia is opening its platform to keep national programs that want their own chips inside its ecosystem.
