# Tencent opens the weights of its flagship Hy4 multimodal model

Tencent released downloadable weights for Hy4, the flagship model it launched on November 4, under the Apache 2.0 license, which permits commercial use and modification. Hy4 handles text, image and video understanding with a context window of about one million tokens, on a mixture-of-experts backbone with 770 billion total parameters and 49 billion active per token. Tencent is shipping vLLM and SGLang deployment guides, fine-tuning resources and a safety evaluation card alongside the release.
