# AI cheating upends education: South Korea's education minister resigns as Caltech stops grading homework for accuracy

South Korean Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigned Sunday over an AI-assisted cheating scandal that erupted ahead of the Suneung college entrance exam, after the top scorer on a national tax-accountant exam confessed to using a miniature camera and smartphone, prompting last-minute steps such as blocking public Wi-Fi near test sites. In the US, Business Insider sat in on an intro math class at Caltech, where professor Yi Ni says he no longer grades homework for accuracy because students use AI, which is far harder to spot in math than in writing.
