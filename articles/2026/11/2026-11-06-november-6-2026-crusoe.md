# [November 6, 2026] · [Crusoe]

Crusoe confirmed it sold its unfinished southeastern Wyoming campus, originally planned to scale to 10 gigawatts, to Google, which reportedly had cost and schedule concerns and plans to invest $17 billion in the site. The price was not disclosed, and Crusoe continues to build a separate campus for Google near Amarillo, Texas. The deal shows hyperscalers pulling strategic gigawatt-scale sites in-house when independent developers stumble.
