# Ramp data shows AI token use rising while spending stalls

Ramp's AI Index shows spending on AI tokens grew only 1.5% across August and September, after jumping 42% in July. It grew 8.9% from late September to late October. Ramp economist Ara Kharazian links the slowdown to price cuts by OpenAI and Anthropic and to slightly lower spending by the heaviest users, which spend about $10,000 per employee each month; the median company spends $12-15. Anthropic's filing says its own price cuts reduced its revenue growth by about $600 million. AI vendors are in a buyer's market, which strengthens enterprise customers' hand in contract renewals.
