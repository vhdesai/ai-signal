# Nvidia releases Alpamayo 2 Edge, a 2B reasoning driving model for in-car hardware

Nvidia released Alpamayo 2 Edge, an open vision-language-action model of about 2B parameters that turns six camera feeds into a 6.4-second driving plan and explains its reasoning. It runs on DRIVE AGX Thor at 160–190 ms latency using an FP8 recipe. It writes out full reasoning only when a scene needs it. Nvidia reports 15.15 km per at-fault incident in simulation, about 60 times better than a 4B Qwen-based baseline. The release extends Nvidia's open-model strategy into the vehicle and strengthens the pull toward its in-car Thor chips. Products & Tools OPEN SOURCE GPU COMPILERS
