# Anthropic prepares to price its IPO above range at a valuation near $1.5 trillion

Anthropic plans to raise its IPO price after demand topped six times the 825 million shares on offer, with advisers guiding toward a fully diluted valuation near $1.5 trillion, or about $83 to $85 a share against the $70 to $78 range. Pricing is expected late Wednesday and Nasdaq trading under the ticker ANT on Thursday; even at $78 a share the deal would raise about $90 billion, surpassing SpaceX as the largest IPO. PitchBook holds its own estimate at $1.42 trillion, about $79 a share, and argues that a $1.5 trillion price implies only about 9.9% annual returns on its forecast.
