# Consumer agent platforms scale: DeepSeek Harness passes 1.5 million daily users as Apple readies a more capable Siri

DeepSeek said its open-source Harness agent application reached 1.54 million daily active users by November 4, less than three months after launch, with about 92 trillion tokens a day flowing through its API and a plugin marketplace planned. Bloomberg reports that Apple aims to add agent-style capabilities to Siri as early as next year under Craig Federighi, while Shanghai's regulator has registered Apple Intelligence for iPads and Macs, extending July's iPhone approval for the Alibaba Qwen-backed service in China. The usage figures are company claims, and Apple has not confirmed its Siri plans.
