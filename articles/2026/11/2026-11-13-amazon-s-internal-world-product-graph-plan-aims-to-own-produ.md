# Amazon's internal "World Product Graph" plan aims to own product data for every item sold and blunt AI shopping agents

An internal Amazon planning memo (a "PR/FAQ") reviewed by Reuters describes collecting data on virtually every product for sale anywhere, whether or not Amazon stocks it. Brands would contribute data and Amazon would crawl other retailers' sites, and Coca-Cola and Bayer have reportedly signed on. The memo concedes that Amazon's own AI shopping agent often gives inaccurate information, and it sets an aspirational launch date of October 1, 2027. Amazon called it a draft, brainstorming document.
