# [November 5, 2026] · [Anthropic]

Anthropic began rolling out Personal Finance in beta on Claude's Pro, Max and Ultra plans in the US, letting users connect bank and card accounts through Plaid and ask questions about spending, bills and fees. Access is read-only, so Claude cannot move, manage or invest money, and conversations in the Personal Finance hub are not used to train models. Anthropic says it is exploring how Claude could work alongside licensed professionals and regulated firms on bigger money decisions. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
