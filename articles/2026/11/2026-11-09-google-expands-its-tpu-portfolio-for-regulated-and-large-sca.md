# Google expands its TPU portfolio for regulated and large-scale workloads

Reuters reports two new Google chip variants: TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max. The 8tx pairs Google's accelerators with AMD processors and hardware-level encryption for government and regulated-industry use, while the 8i Max combines two chips in one package to increase memory and computing capacity. The designs target different enterprise requirements: protected deployments and very large AI workloads.
