# Lab says an unreleased Anthropic model helped prove a quantum-computing conjecture open since 2008

Physical Superintelligence (PSI), a physics research lab, says a research system built around an unreleased Anthropic model proved the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture in three days, showing dramatic quantum speedups require problems with special structure. PSI cites Stanford researchers calling it a huge result. Which model was used remains undisclosed, independent verification is pending, and Anthropic did not comment.
