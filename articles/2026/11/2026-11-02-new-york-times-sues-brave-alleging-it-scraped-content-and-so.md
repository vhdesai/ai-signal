# New York Times sues Brave, alleging it scraped content and sold it to AI companies

The lawsuit, filed in federal court in Manhattan, alleges that Brave built an index of more than 30 billion web pages using a crawler that publishers could not block. It says Brave distributed copyrighted content to customers the suit names as Anthropic, Perplexity, Mistral, xAI, and Cohere. The Daily News, the Chicago Tribune, and the Orlando Sentinel filed a similar suit. Publishers are now suing the search and data providers that supply AI companies, not just the model makers. That raises questions about content origin and legal liability for anyone buying third-party search data or search services that supply source material to AI models. AI SAFETY MONITORING
