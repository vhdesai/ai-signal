# Model-weight analysis raises questions about restricted Nvidia compute

TechCrunch reports that Unemployment Labs researcher Will DePue found patterns consistent with Blackwell training in released model weights from Chinese developers including DeepSeek and Moonshot. Those fingerprints do not prove that the entire training process used Nvidia hardware or establish export-control violations; offshore training is one possible explanation. The analysis may intensify policy attention to end-user access to advanced compute rather than shipment destinations alone.
