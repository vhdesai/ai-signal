# [November 5, 2026] · [The Information]

Nvidia has approached Apollo Global Management about creating a network of small AI data centers, potentially spanning 20 states, inside existing buildings of struggling telecom firm Brightspeed, according to The Information. The approach could sidestep new data-center moratoriums, with the buildings offering about 1 gigawatt of power for Nvidia servers. Business Insider separately reports that Nvidia must now decide how much financial support to give the neoclouds it helped create as their financing grows more expensive.
