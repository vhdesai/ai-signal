# ChatGPT's traffic rebounds as it holds more than 81% of chatbot visits

A Graphite study of Similarweb data finds ChatGPT accounts for more than 81% of worldwide traffic to major AI chatbots. After seven flat months, monthly visits climbed nearly 20% to a record 61.35 billion in September, while Claude and Gemini grew from much smaller bases. These are web-traffic estimates rather than paid seats or enterprise usage, but they show consumer distribution still heavily favors OpenAI even as rivals gain at work.
