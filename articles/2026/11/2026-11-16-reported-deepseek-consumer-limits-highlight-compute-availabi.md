# Reported DeepSeek consumer limits highlight compute availability risk

Source: BigGo Finance, reporting user accounts BigGo reports that DeepSeek’s free app and website have introduced tighter conversation limits, with users encountering restrictions on resuming long chats, message length and daily activity. The article attributes the changes to insufficient computing capacity and says the developer API is unaffected. DeepSeek has not published a unified policy or restoration timetable, so the specific restrictions should be treated as reported user experience rather than a fully documented service change.
