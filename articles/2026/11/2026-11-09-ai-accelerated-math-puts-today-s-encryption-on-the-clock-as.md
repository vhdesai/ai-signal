# AI-accelerated math puts today's encryption on the clock as AI misuse drives new breaches

WSJ reports that AI is advancing mathematics fast enough that cryptographers worry it could find new attacks on the problems underpinning today's encryption before quantum computers arrive; Johns Hopkins cryptographer Matthew Green says "we don't have any idea what's coming." Chainalysis is still restoring systems after a multifront cyberattack involving agentic AI that exposed names, usernames, password hashes and live tokens for all users.
