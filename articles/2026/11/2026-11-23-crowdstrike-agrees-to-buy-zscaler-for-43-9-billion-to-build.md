# CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler for $43.9 billion to build a control plane for AI-era security

CrowdStrike will pay 1.135 of its shares for each Zscaler share, valuing Zscaler at about $260.41, a 34% premium, in one of the largest cybersecurity deals on record. CEO George Kurtz pitched the combination as "a single control plane to deploy AI, control AI, and enable any agentic application to run safely," and the companies expect more than $10 billion in annual recurring revenue at closing in the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028. Zscaler jumped and CrowdStrike fell premarket, and analysts expect more consolidation as AI makes it easier to break into corporate systems.
