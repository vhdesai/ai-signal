# Opposition to data centers becomes a midterm election issue

TechCrunch reports that AI and data centers dominated discussion at a New York midterm event five days before the November 3 election. It cites Data Center Watch's estimate that nearly $200 billion in data center projects were delayed, stalled, or blocked in the first half of 2026 by political and community opposition. Pennsylvania, Georgia, Texas, New York, and Virginia are considering transparency rules and other guardrails. Permitting and community acceptance are now a binding constraint on compute capacity, alongside chips and power.
