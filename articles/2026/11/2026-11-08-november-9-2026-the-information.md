# [November 9, 2026] · [The Information]

Anthropic plans to begin formal marketing to IPO investors this week despite two weeks of tech-stock volatility and a Democratic midterm landslide, The Information reports. Yahoo Finance, citing Bloomberg, says prospective buyers have discussed valuations of $1.1 trillion to $1.3 trillion, down from estimates as high as $2 trillion in early October, even as revenue reached $33.2 billion in the first nine months. OpenAI is expected to brief investors on third-quarter results after raising $11 billion, and Meta strategy chief Dave Wehner is leaving to become CFO of Fireworks.
