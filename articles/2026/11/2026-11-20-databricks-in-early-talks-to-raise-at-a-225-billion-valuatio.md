# Databricks in early talks to raise at a $225 billion valuation

The WSJ reports that Databricks is in early talks to raise new funding at a $225 billion valuation, nearly 20% above its August valuation. The article is paywalled, so this summary is based on the publisher's description and Reuters' follow-up. Investors are still paying up for enterprise AI platforms even as public AI stocks sell off. That suggests private capital is separating data and application platforms from compute-heavy bets. SOVEREIGN AI EXPANSION
