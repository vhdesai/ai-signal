# Nvidia's succession search turns to Arm's Rene Haas as earnings put Kress's financing stance to the test

Nvidia's board has approached Arm CEO Rene Haas as a possible successor, the Financial Times reports, while an internal post urging the board to weigh employee sentiment drew more than 1,600 comments, and Reuters reports that Huang misled general counsel Tim Teter about his ties to Alice Huang. Investors told TechCrunch they expect interim CEO Colette Kress to write smaller, more selective checks after Nvidia joined 105 funding rounds this year, and Yahoo Finance, citing The Information, reports that she pushed back on a proposed $250 billion credit backstop for OpenAI. PitchBook expects the venture program to outlast Huang but its largest bets and acquisitions to slow, noting a Justice Department inquiry into the $12.9 billion Hugging Face deal.
