# Anthropic looks set to price above its range ahead of Thursday's 'Grandthropic' debut as crypto loses out

The Information says demand suggests Anthropic will price above its $70 to $78 range, likely near $84 to $85 for a valuation of about $1.5 trillion, and bets the stock opens well above $100. Business Insider calls Thursday, when Anthropic lists and Grand Theft Auto VI launches, Grandthropic, and frames the IPO as the cleanest referendum yet on the AI trade. DealBook reports Bitcoin fell below $65,000 as investors rotate from crypto into AI, and says Sound Ventures has reportedly built a $7 billion Anthropic stake.
