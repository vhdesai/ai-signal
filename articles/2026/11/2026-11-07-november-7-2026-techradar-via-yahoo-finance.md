# [November 7, 2026] · [TechRadar (via Yahoo Finance)]

Amanda Peterson Corio, Google's global head of data-center energy, said at The Information's WTF Summit that orbital data centers could cost up to 100 times more than terrestrial ones and that the real constraint on Earth is permitting, not power. Her remarks come weeks after Google's first Project Suncatcher prototype ran Gemma 4 on a TPU in orbit. In Canada, CBC reports protests across Nova Scotia against a data centre planned to support Anthropic, showing local opposition is not confined to the US.
