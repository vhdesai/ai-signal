# [November 11, 2026] · [CNBC]

Santosh Janardhan, who led Meta's infrastructure as capital spending grew from $32 billion in 2022 to as much as $145 billion this year, is stepping down in favor of Dave Brown, a nearly 19-year AWS veteran hired in July, and will stay through the first quarter of 2027 as CTO of Meta Compute. Microsoft cloud operations president Noelle Walsh said the company will power its data centers without government subsidies or higher electricity costs for other customers. With voters turning against data centers in the midterms, community economics are becoming part of hyperscaler capacity strategy.
