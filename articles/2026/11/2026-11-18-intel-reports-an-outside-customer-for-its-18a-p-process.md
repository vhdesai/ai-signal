# Intel reports an outside customer for its 18A-P process

Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan said the company signed its first outside customer for its advanced 18A manufacturing technology, with technology chief Pushkar Ranade clarifying that the agreement covers 18A-P. The customer was not named, and the report provides neither production volumes nor a shipment timetable. This is evidence of external commercial demand for Intel Foundry, not confirmation of a particular hyperscaler win or successful high-volume production.
