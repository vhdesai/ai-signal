# Cornell names leadership for independent New York AI-safety evaluations

Cornell Tech dean Greg Morrisett has been named interim co-director of the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research alongside Columbia’s Jeannette Wing. The Binghamton-based center will convene more than 80 scientists for independent AI-system evaluation and testing standards, supported by Empire AI computing resources and linked to oversight under New York’s RAISE Act. The fresh development is leadership and the evaluation agenda—not a new funding commitment—and it signals growing institutional capacity for external scrutiny of advanced AI systems.
