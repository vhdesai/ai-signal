# The Financial Times reports that China's National Development and Reform Commission told SoftBank it will not approve…

The Financial Times reports that China's National Development and Reform Commission told SoftBank it will not approve the acquisition of ABB's robotics division, whose biggest market is China. The decision is a setback to Masayoshi Son's physical-AI ambitions and shows Beijing using control over AI-adjacent technology deals. SoftBank shares fell about 5% as the company considers whether to seek approval again.
