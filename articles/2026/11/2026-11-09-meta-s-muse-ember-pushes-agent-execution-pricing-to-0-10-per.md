# Meta's Muse Ember pushes agent-execution pricing to $0.10 per million input tokens

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Ember, its smallest multimodal model, built for tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction and long-document parsing. At 10 cents per million input tokens, Meta positions it as cheaper and smarter than Anthropic's Haiku and OpenAI's Luna and price-competitive with open models such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, though the benchmarks are self-reported. The pitch is explicit: Muse Wonder plans and Ember handles the volume, making planner-worker routing a mainstream cost lever for agent fleets.
