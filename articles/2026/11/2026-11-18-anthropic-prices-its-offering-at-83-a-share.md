# Anthropic prices its offering at $83 a share

CNBC reports that Anthropic priced its enlarged offering at $83 per share, above its marketed range, with approximately 980 million shares sold and $81.3 billion raised. The report puts its fully diluted valuation at about $1.5 trillion and says Nasdaq trading under ANT is expected on November 19; this briefing does not treat the trading debut as completed. Executive takeaway: public-market reporting will give technology buyers and investors a more transparent view of frontier-model growth, spending and financial risk. TRENDING A · Alibaba
