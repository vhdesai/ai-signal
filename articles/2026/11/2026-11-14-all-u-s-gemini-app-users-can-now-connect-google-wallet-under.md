# All U.S. Gemini app users can now connect Google Wallet under Personal Intelligence, with optional bank and card…

All U.S. Gemini app users can now connect Google Wallet under Personal Intelligence, with optional bank and card linking through Plaid, to get answers based on their spending. Google stresses that access is read-only: Gemini cannot move, manage or invest money. Google also added Memory Summary, a page where users can view and edit what Gemini remembers. Read-only finance data contrasts with OpenAI's same-day move to let agents spend.
