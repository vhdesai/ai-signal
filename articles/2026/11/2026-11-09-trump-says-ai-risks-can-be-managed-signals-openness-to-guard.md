# Trump says AI risks can be managed, signals openness to guardrails

Reuters reports that President Trump told CBS’s 60 Minutes he was open to AI safeguards and potentially working with Democrats, while opposing restrictions that would leave the United States behind China. This is a political signal, not an announced regulatory framework or legislative agreement. The scope of any safeguards remains unclear, so enterprise compliance assumptions should not change on this interview alone. Verified source: Read the original Reuters report POLICY SECURITY
