# OpenAI redistributes robotics work across research and applied teams

Infobae, reporting a Fortune exclusive Infobae, relaying a Fortune exclusive, reports that OpenAI dissolved its standalone robotics team, placing research under Mark Chen and applied robotics and hardware in Greg Brockman’s Applied organization. OpenAI says its general GPT-6 Astra models can be used directly for robotics and presents the reorganization as integration rather than a retreat; that capability claim has not been independently validated here. The reported plan still calls for a first public humanoid demonstration in 2027. The original Fortune article was unavailable, so this account relies on Infobae’s reporting.
