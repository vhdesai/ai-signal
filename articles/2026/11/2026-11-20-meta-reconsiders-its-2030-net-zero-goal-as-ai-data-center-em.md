# Meta reconsiders its 2030 net-zero goal as AI data center emissions hit a record

Meta's 2026 sustainability report, published Nov. 19, says the company is "re-evaluating" the 2030 timeline for its net-zero goal and its near-term targets. Total emissions rose from 15.6 million tonnes of CO2-equivalent in 2024 to more than 21.7 million in 2025, roughly Idaho's annual total, driven mostly by data center hardware. Meta is the first of the big hyperscalers to publicly reopen its 2030 target. Amazon, Google and Microsoft have not changed theirs. Expect more scrutiny from investors and regulators of AI buildouts powered by natural gas.
