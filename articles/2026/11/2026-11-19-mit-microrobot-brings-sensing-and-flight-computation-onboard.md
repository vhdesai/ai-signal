# MIT microrobot brings sensing and flight computation onboard

An MIT-led team, including a Cornell researcher, demonstrated a flapping-wing microrobot that uses onboard sensors and computation instead of relying on laboratory motion-capture cameras for flight navigation. It can follow trajectories and land on a flower, but remains connected to an external power supply; untethered outdoor operation and onboard visual recognition are future goals. Executive takeaway: this is an enabling step for miniature inspection and agricultural robotics, not a field-ready autonomous product.
