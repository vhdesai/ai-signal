# Virginia and Oregon announce lawsuits over Grok image-generation safeguards

Virginia and Oregon announced coordinated consumer-protection lawsuits against SpaceX over Grok's generation of nonconsensual sexualized images, including images involving children. The states allege inadequate safeguards and misleading safety representations, and seek financial remedies and court-ordered protections. These are allegations, not court findings; the announcement highlights legal exposure around generative-image product design and safety claims.
