Relevant teams will report to the committee and interim CEO Colette Kress. In a separate joint statement, Commerce and Justice said Commerce began investigating Megaspeed in 2024, NVIDIA placed the firm on its no-sell list in June 2025, and Justice now leads the criminal investigation.
