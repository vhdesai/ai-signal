# NVIDIA reports $115.8 billion quarter and higher revenue guidance

NVIDIA reported fiscal 2027 third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% year over year, including $107.6 billion in Data Center revenue. Its fourth-quarter revenue outlook is $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, assuming no China Data Center compute revenue—not a statement of permanent exit from China. The company says Vera Rubin NVL72 production shipments have started at OCI, CoreWeave, and Nebius. The results indicate continued infrastructure demand, while geographic exposure remains a consideration for procurement and capacity planning.
