# SpaceX Plans More Than $74 Billion in Texas, Arizona Data-Center Investments

SpaceX outlined approximately $24 billion of supercomputing investment in Texas and a proposed $50 billion campus in Arizona. The Texas plan includes on-site power generation; the Arizona proposal depends on approvals and coordination with local utilities. These are announced investment plans rather than completed spending or available compute, with power and permitting remaining central execution dependencies. VENTUREBEAT NEW INFRASTRUCTURE
