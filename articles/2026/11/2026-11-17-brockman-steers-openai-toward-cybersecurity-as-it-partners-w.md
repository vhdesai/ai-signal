# Brockman steers OpenAI toward cybersecurity as it partners with NATO on cyber defense

OpenAI president Greg Brockman is pushing the company deeper into cybersecurity, including recruiting Wiz president Dali Rajic in August to replace Denise Dresser as chief revenue officer. The push sets up a clash with AI-safety advocates who worry about the offensive potential of cyber-capable models. The Journal reports that OpenAI has also partnered with NATO on cyber defenses.
