# Meta deploys specialized Muse models with Active Memory and Active Focus

Meta is rolling out specialized versions of the models behind Muse and Meta AI, trained with its Muse Wonder series. It reports that Active Memory cut forgetting of goal-critical information by 2.4 times and Active Focus improved completion of interrupted tasks by 67%, lifting Muse's score on Meta's own Personal Superintelligence Index to 47% from 38%. All figures are vendor-reported on Meta's internal benchmark. Sources: Unite.AI
