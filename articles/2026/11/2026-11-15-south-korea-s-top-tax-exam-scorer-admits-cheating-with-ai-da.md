# South Korea's top tax-exam scorer admits cheating with AI days before the national college entrance exam

The top scorer on South Korea's national tax accountant exam, who missed only one of 160 questions, confessed to photographing questions with a microcamera, sending them to a generative AI tool and reading the answers on a smartphone. He submitted a written confession to Seoul police. The presidential office called an emergency meeting days before Thursday's national college entrance exam.
