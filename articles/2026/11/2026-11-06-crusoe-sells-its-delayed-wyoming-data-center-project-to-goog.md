# Crusoe sells its delayed Wyoming data center project to Google

Crusoe confirmed it has sold the unfinished southeastern Wyoming campus, originally planned to grow to 10 GW, to Google. Google is taking over development after reportedly raising concerns about cost and schedule, and it plans to invest $17 billion in the site; the price was not disclosed. Crusoe is still building a separate campus for Google near Amarillo, Texas. Independent developers are struggling to deliver gigawatt-scale campuses, so hyperscalers are taking direct control of the projects most important to them. MICROSOFT ORACLE CLOUD CAPACITY
