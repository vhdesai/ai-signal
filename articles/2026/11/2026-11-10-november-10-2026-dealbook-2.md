# [November 10, 2026] · [DealBook]

President Trump said the FBI is looking into Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang while insisting Huang did not know she was a suspected spy, and Rosen Law Firm filed the first proposed class action over the ties. Nvidia closed down 4.3% on Monday, erasing about $192 billion, and the Wall Street Journal describes a chairman-less board with no named successor that has largely done things Huang's way. The Information reports the relationship helped route billions of dollars of Nvidia chips to Chinese buyers and that a new push for export controls is building, while DealBook notes Democrats are probing how a Commerce Department inquiry into Megaspeed was halted after Nvidia's intervention.
