# China ships: Hydrogen-1.0 for science, Baidu's Apollo World and SenseNova 6.8 Flash

Hangzhou-based Deep Principle released Hydrogen-1.0, a 753B-parameter MIT-licensed model post-trained from Z.ai's GLM-5.3 on chemistry, materials and life-sciences data; its self-reported FrontierScience-Research score (38.3%) tops GPT-6 Astra (33.3%). Baidu launched Apollo World, a world model that simulates up to 100,000 km of driving a day to train Apollo Go robotaxis, and SenseTime shipped SenseNova 6.8 Flash with office and design agent skills. Benchmark claims are vendor-reported and unverified. MarkTechPost — Deep Principle Hydrogen-1.0 The Star (Reuters) — Baidu launches Apollo World TechNode — SenseTime launches SenseNova 6.8 Flash
