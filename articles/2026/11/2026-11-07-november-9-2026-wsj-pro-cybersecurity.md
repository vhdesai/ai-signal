# November 9, 2026 · WSJ Pro CyberSecurity

WSJ reports that AI is advancing mathematics fast enough that cryptographers worry it could find new attacks on the problems underpinning today's encryption before quantum computers arrive; Johns Hopkins cryptographer Matthew Green says "we don't have any idea what's coming." Chainalysis is still restoring systems after a multifront cyberattack involving agentic AI that exposed names, usernames, password hashes and live tokens for all users. Weill Cornell Medicine is notifying radiology-study patients that their health information was entered into an unauthorized AI tool and has suspended the study.
