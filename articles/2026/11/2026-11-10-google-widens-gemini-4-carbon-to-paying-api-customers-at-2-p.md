# Google widens Gemini 4 Carbon to paying API customers at $2 per million input tokens

Google is extending Gemini 4 Carbon beyond its restricted cybersecurity preview to paid API customers, business users and AI Ultra subscribers across Antigravity, the Gemini app and AI Mode. Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, which VentureBeat says undercuts Claude Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5. A coordinated-agent preview in Antigravity, called /swarm, shifts the pitch from model access to multi-agent project execution.
