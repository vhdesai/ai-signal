# Cerebras signs Microsoft as its third hyperscaler customer and raises guidance, but its OpenAI capacity is late

Third-quarter core revenue rose 70% to $231 million and cloud revenue rose 272%. Cerebras posted its first positive non-GAAP net income as a public company and raised its 2026 core revenue guidance to $940-960 million. The Microsoft agreement, signed on the day of the earnings call, joins AWS and Oracle; revenue from it begins in 2027. Cerebras said land, power, and construction constraints, not chip supply, left it behind on its 750 MW OpenAI commitment. It expects to be back on schedule in the second half of 2027 and said less than 5% of that commitment could face cancellation this year. Shares rose nearly 18% in premarket trading on Friday.
