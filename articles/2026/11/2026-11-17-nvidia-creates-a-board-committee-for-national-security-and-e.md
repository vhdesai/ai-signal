# NVIDIA creates a board committee for national security and export-control oversight

NVIDIA established a standing board committee chaired by Suzanne Nora Johnson to oversee national-security and export-control matters. Existing security and global-trade teams will report directly to the committee and Interim CEO Colette Kress, with support from outside legal, security and trade specialists. The governance change is relevant to GPU procurement and compliance due diligence, but it does not itself change export rules.
