# Oracle files to raise $20 billion in stock and bonds to fund AI infrastructure

Oracle is selling $10 billion in investment-grade bonds and $10 billion in common stock, its latest financing as it spends heavily on AI infrastructure. Issuing stock as well as debt suggests that even cloud providers with large signed contracts are approaching the limits of borrowing to fund their build-outs. Customers counting on Oracle Cloud capacity should treat Oracle's financing as a delivery risk. The details come from The Information's briefing summary. NVIDIA CHINA EXPORT CONTROLS
