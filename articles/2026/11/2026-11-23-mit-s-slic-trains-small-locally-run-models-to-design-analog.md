# MIT's SLIC trains small, locally run models to design analog chips nearly as well as frontier LLMs

MIT researchers, working with IBM Research and Analog Devices, developed SLIC (Small LM Iterating on Circuits), which combines demonstrations, simulator feedback and multi-turn reinforcement learning to train a small model for analog-circuit design. MIT reports performance approaching frontier models with far less compute, including on unseen circuit structures and more advanced process nodes. Because training and inference stay on-premises, chipmakers can use proprietary design and foundry data without sending it to cloud models; the work will be presented at NeurIPS 2026.
