# Anthropic's roadshow filing adds $10 billion of Microsoft and Google compute and warns of margin pressure

An updated filing shows Anthropic committing roughly $10 billion more to rent Azure and Google Cloud capacity through 2033, lifting its Microsoft commitment to $49.2 billion and Google to $121.6 billion, while flagging that a $34.9 billion Lambda lease could be cancelled after a missed data-center deadline. The company swung to a $567 million third-quarter operating loss, which it attributed mainly to expensive short-term compute contracts, and warned that such deals will compress near-term margins. DealBook reports bankers are weighing a price range above $70–$78, and CNBC says Australian retail investors can now subscribe through local brokers.
