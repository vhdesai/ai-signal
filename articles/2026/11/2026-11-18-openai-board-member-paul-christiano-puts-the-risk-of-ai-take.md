# OpenAI board member Paul Christiano puts the risk of AI takeover near 10% within a year

Paul Christiano, who sits on the board of the OpenAI Foundation, told podcaster Dwarkesh Patel there is a "reasonable chance" of an AI disaster that kills large numbers of people or topples a government within months, citing models that improve themselves. He now puts the risk of AI takeover over the next year closer to 10% than 1%, up from about 4% when he joined the board in September, and stressed he was speaking personally.
