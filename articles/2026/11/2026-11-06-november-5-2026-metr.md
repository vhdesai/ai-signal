# [November 5, 2026] · [METR]

After a high-access evaluation inside Anthropic from August 12 to October 29, METR estimated that Anthropic's capabilities on well-specified tasks are improving roughly twice as fast with AI assistance. METR stressed substantial uncertainty and said Anthropic redacted a majority of the evidence it found most informative, so the public must rely on METR's judgment more than is ideal. The report has quickly become a reference point in the debate over AI-accelerated AI research. Academic Research RESEARCH
