# [November 17, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Kress told analysts Nvidia is rigorously evaluating future investments, commitments and guarantees as other sources of capital emerge, and warned that if a customer's buildout slips, the guarantee no longer exists. PitchBook data show Nvidia has joined 27.2% of global VC deals this year, worth a combined $219.5 billion, with its deal count up from 49 in 2023 to 129. A pullback would push the AI labs and neoclouds that leaned on its balance sheet toward other lenders, even as Nvidia's venture arm has reassured at least three robotics startups of continued support.
