# Dreamteam raises $53 million across seed and Series A rounds for AI-native CRM

Dreamteam, founded by Freshworks alumni, raised a combined $53 million across a seed round led by Together Fund and a Series A led by Accel, with Premji Invest participating. Its CRM uses email, meeting and conversation context to support agents for prospect research, follow-ups, deal-risk detection and workflow configuration. Management reports more than 100 customers in 13 countries; revenue and valuation were not disclosed.
