# Stanford's AI100 report calls the last five years a scaling watershed as a 52-study review finds mixed learning gains

The third report of Stanford HAI's One Hundred Year Study on AI credits scaling for recent progress while flagging the rising cost and scarcity of training data, concentration of frontier development and limited public input. A separate Stanford review of 52 randomized and quasi-experimental studies finds that using AI for explanations and practice generally beats using it to complete work, but neither yields consistent learning gains. Only 10 of those studies were in US K-12 settings, so the authors caution against sweeping conclusions.
