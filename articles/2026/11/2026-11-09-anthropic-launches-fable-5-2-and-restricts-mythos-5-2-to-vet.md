# Anthropic launches Fable 5.2 and restricts Mythos 5.2 to vetted organizations

Anthropic launched Fable 5.2 for general availability, while limiting Mythos 5.2 to vetted organizations through its Life Sciences and Cyber Verification Programs. CNBC reports pricing of $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50, respectively. Enterprise evaluations should pair the lower listed rates with checks on which model is actually accessible and whether its performance justifies migration costs.
