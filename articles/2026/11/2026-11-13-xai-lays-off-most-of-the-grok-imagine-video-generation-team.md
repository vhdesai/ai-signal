# xAI lays off most of the Grok Imagine video-generation team

About 60 people who worked on Grok Imagine, xAI's image and video generation tool, were laid off in the past week from the SpaceX Super Intelligence group. Only a handful of employees remain to keep the product running, and it is still available to users. The cut comes as SpaceX and Tesla discuss a merger that, according to Bloomberg via The Information, could be announced as soon as December. It suggests xAI is prioritizing core model and compute spending over consumer media features.
