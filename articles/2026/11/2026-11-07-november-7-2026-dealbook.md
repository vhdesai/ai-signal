# [November 7, 2026] · [DealBook]

DealBook reports AI law firm Crosby secured $5 million of AI liability cover from Lloyd's-backed Testudo and removed lawyer supervision from gray-area tasks that make up about 20% of its work, while at least 16 of the 20 largest liability insurers have filed to exclude generative AI from standard policies. Business Insider details how a Railway employee's personal test agent, running DeepSeek, probed rival Primcloud's systems before he stopped it within 15 minutes. Lawyers say such incidents have become too common for defendants to claim they were unforeseeable.
