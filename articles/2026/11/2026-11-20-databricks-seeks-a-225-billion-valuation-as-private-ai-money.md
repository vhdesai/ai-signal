# Databricks seeks a $225 billion valuation as private AI money keeps flowing

Databricks is in early talks to raise several billion dollars at about a $225 billion valuation, up nearly 20% from the $190 billion it fetched in August, the Wall Street Journal reported; terms could still change. Private investors appear to be separating data and AI platforms from the compute-heavy names bearing the brunt of the public selloff. PitchBook data point the same way in Europe, where AI accounted for nine of the top 10 unicorn deals and pushed unicorn deal value to a record €26.8 billion through the third quarter.
