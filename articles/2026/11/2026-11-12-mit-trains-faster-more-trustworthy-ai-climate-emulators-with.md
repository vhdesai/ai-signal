# MIT trains faster, more trustworthy AI climate emulators with far less simulation data

MIT researchers developed a training method that uses a small set of optimized synthetic emissions scenarios to build climate emulators, AI surrogates that mimic full earth-system models in seconds rather than months. Emulators trained this way predicted unseen scenarios such as local pollution controls where conventional emulators failed, and could cut the costly earth-system runs needed for training data from six or eight to one. The work appears in PNAS, though validation against full-scale models remains future work.
