# Alibaba introduces Qwen4-Max; smaller open-weight models remain planned

Alibaba introduced Qwen4-Max as a mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion total parameters, 71 billion active parameters per token, native vision and a one-million-token context window. The model is available through Alibaba's cloud and apps, while smaller open-weight Qwen4 models are planned rather than released. Performance and task-cost improvement claims come from vendor benchmarks and should be assessed against the relevant workloads. Published November 16; exact time not disclosed, so strict 24-hour placement is unconfirmed.
