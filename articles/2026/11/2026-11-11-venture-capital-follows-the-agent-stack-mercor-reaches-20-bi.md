# Venture capital follows the agent stack: Mercor reaches $20 billion as security and agent oversight draw new rounds

Mercor, which supplies human expertise for training and evaluating AI models, raised a $750 million Series D at a $20 billion valuation, while Path Robotics raised $300 million for physical AI in manufacturing. Smaller rounds point to the next layer of enterprise needs: WhiteMatter raised $20 million for guardian agents that supervise other agents, and Graphon Labs raised $35 million for AI memory systems. The Information's 2026 list of 50 promising startups adds a security category for the first time, and three of its four most valuable companies are security startups valued above $1 billion. PitchBook also notes that frontier labs are crowding into drug design while clinical-trial infrastructure has drawn only $250 million in venture funding. https://www.theinformation.com/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=50+most+promising+startups
