# New MIT explores more data-efficient training for climate emulators

MIT researchers describe a training approach that uses deliberately constructed synthetic scenarios to improve AI models that approximate full climate simulations. The method reduces the amount of expensive simulation data needed and performed well on previously unseen scenarios in the reported tests. Published in PNAS, the work offers a potentially more practical route to fast climate-policy analysis, although the reported results should not be read as proof of reliability across every climate application.
