# Reuters reports that about 600,000 Nvidia H200 chips are sitting in Hong Kong waiting for shipment to mainland China,…

Reuters reports that about 600,000 Nvidia H200 chips are sitting in Hong Kong waiting for shipment to mainland China, because Beijing has not approved large-scale imports. A few major Chinese internet companies have received about 10,000 each, and some have arrived through intermediaries. Gray-market prices have soared: an eight-card H200 server sold for more than 6M yuan (about $900K) by late October, about three times its price a year earlier.
