# [November 20, 2026] · [CNBC]

House Foreign Affairs Chairman Brian Mast released Commerce license records showing approvals for more than 879,000 of 3.4 million requested Nvidia H200 and AMD Instinct chips for China between December 2025 and May 2026, including about 268,000 for Tencent, Alibaba and Baidu; it is unclear how many shipped. SpaceX-owned Cursor told lawmakers it is considering phasing out Chinese-developed open-weight models, according to a House report reviewed by CNBC; its Composer 2 coding model was built on Moonshot AI's Kimi K2.5. AMD shares fell as much as 11% after short seller Hunterbrook alleged ties to a blacklisted Chinese IT firm, which AMD disputes.
