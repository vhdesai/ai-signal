# Britain's biggest banks block AI agents from customer accounts

HSBC, Nationwide, NatWest, Barclays and Lloyds have moved to block AI agents, in some cases explicitly barring transactions made on a customer's behalf without their direct involvement. The Telegraph describes the bans as pre-emptive, driven by fears of rogue agents and unclear liability as consumer assistants spread, while McKinsey warns AI advice could move up to £700 billion out of low-yield accounts. The Financial Conduct Authority is working with the industry on rules, making UK banking an early test of how agentic commerce gets regulated.
