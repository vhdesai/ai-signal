# [November 17, 2026] · [OpenAI]

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol in ChatGPT, Codex and the API, saying both finish demanding tasks with fewer output tokens. Astra remains the pick for the hardest work and its standard API prices are 20% lower than GPT-6 Astra's, while Sol came within half a point of Astra on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 at about one-fifth of the cost. A new Ultrafast mode generates tokens up to 4.5 times faster for Pro subscribers and eligible Enterprise and Edu workspaces.
