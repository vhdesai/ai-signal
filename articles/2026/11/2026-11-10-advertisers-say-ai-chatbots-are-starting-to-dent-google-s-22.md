# Advertisers say AI chatbots are starting to dent Google's $224 billion search-ads engine

Some advertisers tell The Information that part of their Google search budgets now goes unspent because too few people see or click the ads, which they attribute to users researching in chatbots instead. Alphabet shares have fallen 13% as AI slowed search-ad revenue growth, some advertisers are shifting budgets to Meta, and Google is exploring acquisitions of AI creative tools and new data partnerships. It is an early, anecdotal signal, but it targets the profit pool that funds Google's AI build-out. The Information Infrastructure ANALYSIS
