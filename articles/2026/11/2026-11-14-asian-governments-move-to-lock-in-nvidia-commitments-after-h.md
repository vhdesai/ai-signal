# Asian governments move to lock in Nvidia commitments after Huang's exit

South Korea's Science Ministry said Nvidia reaffirmed its plan to supply 260,000 advanced GPUs, and the country's AI strategy council held an emergency meeting on the leadership change. Taipei said Nvidia's planned overseas headquarters remains on track. Singapore says its investigation into Megaspeed and six other individuals is ongoing. Reuters reporting cited by TechCrunch puts more than $3 billion of Chinese AI computing projects that rely on restricted Nvidia chips at risk. Nvidia's sovereign-AI deals look stable for now, but its China-adjacent business faces much more scrutiny. WORKFORCE ANALYSIS
