# CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler for $43.9 billion in stock to build an AI-era security platform

CrowdStrike will pay 1.135 of its shares for each Zscaler share, about $260.41 apiece or a 34% premium, in an all-stock deal valued at $43.9 billion that is expected to close in the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028. CEO George Kurtz pitched the combination as "cybersecurity's operating system: a single control plane to deploy AI, control AI, and enable any agentic application to run safely." Zscaler shares surged and CrowdStrike's fell in early trading, and DealBook says analysts are watching for more consolidation across the security industry.
