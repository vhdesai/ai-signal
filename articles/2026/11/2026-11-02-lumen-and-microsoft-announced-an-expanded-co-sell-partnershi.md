# Lumen and Microsoft announced an expanded co-sell partnership to align enterprise network transformation with Azure…

Lumen and Microsoft announced an expanded co-sell partnership to align enterprise network transformation with Azure modernization for AI workloads. Lumen plans to bring its full cloud networking and application connectivity portfolio to Azure. The pitch targets a common AI adoption bottleneck: fragmented networks linking data across clouds, private data centers, offices, and partners.
