# Anthropic sets an IPO price range of $70 to $78 per share, valuing the company at up to $1.404 trillion

Anthropic's updated filing offers 825 million Class A shares, which would raise $57.75 billion to $64.35 billion, and it plans to list on Nasdaq as "ANT" around November 19. Separately, it will sell $26 billion of non-voting stock at the IPO price to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC, with Google and Nvidia buying $10 billion each, for total net proceeds of about $85 billion to $94 billion. The filing shows third-quarter revenue of $17 billion, a $104 billion net loss over nine months, and $900.9 billion in compute and infrastructure commitments. The founders' Class F shares hold 50.1% of the vote, so public shareholders will have little say. The valuation is below the roughly $2 trillion Anthropic reportedly targeted earlier. Its outside counsel also had to retract a prospectus sent to banks on Monday because it contained errors, according to The Information.
