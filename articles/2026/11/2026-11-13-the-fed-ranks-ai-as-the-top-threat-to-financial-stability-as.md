# The Fed ranks AI as the top threat to financial stability as the White House task force turns to cyber risk

In its semiannual Financial Stability Report, the Federal Reserve said 87% of surveyed market contacts named AI as a salient risk, up from 50% in May and now ahead of every traditional threat. It said debt tied to advanced computing reached $680 billion by October, nearly a third of nonfinancial investment-grade issuance, and that pending AI infrastructure debt, guarantees and leases could exceed $3 trillion. Separately, the White House AI task force held its second meeting, focused on cyber threats, critical infrastructure and implementing the labs' safety accord. AI is now a macro-prudential concern, not just a technology-sector story.
