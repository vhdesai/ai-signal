# Nvidia tells some cloud customers to cut their exposure to Chinese companies

According to three people familiar with the matter, Nvidia recently told some cloud computing customers to reduce their business with Chinese companies. That could narrow a key way Chinese AI developers get advanced computing power: renting it from cloud providers outside China. In a separate report, The Information describes how smugglers move high-bandwidth memory from South Korea to China by packaging it with low-end server processors that fall below the export-control threshold. Together, the reports show that export controls are being tightened at the cloud layer while still leaking at the component layer. ANTHROPIC CUSTOM SILICON
