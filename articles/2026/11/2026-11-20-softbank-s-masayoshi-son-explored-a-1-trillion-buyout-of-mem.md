# SoftBank's Masayoshi Son explored a $1 trillion buyout of memory maker SK Hynix

Masayoshi Son recently approached Wall Street institutions about financing a possible trillion-dollar buyout of SK Hynix, the leading supplier of high-bandwidth memory for AI accelerators. SoftBank said the deal "is no longer under active consideration." The approach shows how strategic HBM supply has become, even though a deal of that size would face steep financing and Korean regulatory hurdles.
