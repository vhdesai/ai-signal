# [November 3, 2026] · [The Information]

Nvidia has verbally told data-center and cloud partners that future contracts should direct at least 51% of services to non-Chinese customers, the Financial Times reported, with existing leases exempt. The Information reports the guidance is not yet a hard rule but asks providers to identify chip end users. The move targets the remote-access loophole that lets Chinese developers rent advanced compute abroad.
