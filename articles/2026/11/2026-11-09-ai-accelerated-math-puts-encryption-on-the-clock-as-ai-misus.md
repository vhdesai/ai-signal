# AI-accelerated math puts encryption on the clock as AI misuse drives new breaches

WSJ Pro reports that AI is advancing mathematics fast enough that cryptographers worry it could find new attacks on the problems underpinning today's encryption before quantum computers arrive; Johns Hopkins cryptographer Matthew Green says nobody has any idea what's coming. Chainalysis is still restoring systems after a multifront cyberattack involving agentic AI that exposed names, password hashes and live tokens for all users. Weill Cornell Medicine is notifying radiology-study patients that their health information was entered into an unauthorized AI tool and has suspended the study.
