# [November 3, 2026] · [Reuters]

Hangzhou-based Deep Principle released Hydrogen-1.0, a 753-billion-parameter MIT-licensed model post-trained from GLM-5.3 on chemistry, materials and life-sciences data, with a self-reported research score above GPT-6 Astra. Baidu launched Apollo World, a world model that simulates up to 100,000 km of driving a day to train its robotaxis, and SenseTime shipped SenseNova 6.8 Flash with office and design agent skills. Benchmark claims are vendor-reported and unverified.
