# Amazon nears a $7.5 billion deal for Israeli AI-efficiency startup Decart

Amazon is close to acquiring Decart, an Israeli startup whose software helps AI models run more efficiently on chips, for about $7.5 billion, according to reports from the Financial Times and The Information. The deal would rank among Amazon's largest acquisitions and could strengthen AWS's position against Nvidia in AI infrastructure. Amazon has not confirmed the talks. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
