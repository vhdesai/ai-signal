# Meta hands its AI buildout to an ex-AWS leader as Microsoft pledges to pay its own way on power

Santosh Janardhan, who led Meta's infrastructure as capital spending grew from $32 billion in 2022 to as much as $145 billion this year, is stepping down and handing the role to Dave Brown, a nearly 19-year AWS veteran who joined Meta in July. Microsoft cloud operations president Noelle Walsh said the company will power its data centers without government subsidies or higher electricity costs for other customers, as it builds about $117 billion of AI and cloud capacity this year. Meta also signed long-term agreements supporting lower-carbon cement, steel and freight in data-center supply chains. With voters turning against data centers in the midterms, community economics are becoming a core part of hyperscaler capacity strategy.
