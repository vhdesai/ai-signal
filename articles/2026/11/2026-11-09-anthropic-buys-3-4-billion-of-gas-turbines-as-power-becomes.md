# Anthropic buys $3.4 billion of gas turbines as power becomes the AI bottleneck

Citing the Financial Times, TechCrunch reports Anthropic is buying Caterpillar generators capable of up to 1.7 GW that OnePWR had ordered but could not finance. It marks a shift for a company that has mostly rented compute, including $1.25 billion a month from xAI through May 2029. Gas turbine backlogs now stretch into the early 2030s, making on-site generation a competitive asset rather than a utility input.
