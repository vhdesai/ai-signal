# AI's capital appetite meets a tiring market as neoclouds head into a pivotal earnings week

Tech giants, chipmakers and AI clouds have sold or priced more than $300 billion of new stock and mandatory convertibles this year, with roughly $100 billion more on tap, including a proposed $60 billion Meta at-the-market program that could launch in early 2027. Debt is tightening too: Semafor says portfolios of GPU-backed loans are being shopped at steep discounts and Ares is passing on GPU financing, Iren has cut its fiscal 2027 capex plans, and CoreWeave, down roughly 50% in a month with expected quarterly cash burn of $10.7 billion, reports Tuesday alongside SoftBank, with Nebius on Thursday. Capacity discipline is spreading: Microsoft declined to renew its 2023 deal to rent Oracle servers with older chips, and the CME delayed GPU rental-price futures for a second time, into January, at the CFTC's request.
