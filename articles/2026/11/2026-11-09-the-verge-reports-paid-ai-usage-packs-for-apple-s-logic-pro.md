# The Verge reports paid AI usage packs for Apple’s Logic Pro

The Verge reports that Logic Pro offers one-time $49.99 “Buy More Usage” packs for cloud AI features after users reach monthly limits. The Verge says more Creator Studio applications are expected to follow this month. For organizations buying creative software, the development makes AI allowance limits and incremental usage charges explicit procurement questions rather than assuming every feature is covered by the base subscription.
