# Trending OpenAI’s reported quarterly revenue exceeds $10 billion

TechCrunch reports, citing Bloomberg’s sources, that OpenAI’s quarterly revenue passed $10 billion and that its adjusted operating margin remained negative despite improving from the prior quarter. The figures point to strong demand without resolving questions about the economics of serving that demand. Comparisons with Anthropic require care because the companies reportedly use different approaches to counting annualized revenue, including cloud-partner sales.
