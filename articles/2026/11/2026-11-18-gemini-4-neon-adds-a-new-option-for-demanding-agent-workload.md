# Gemini 4 Neon adds a new option for demanding agent workloads

Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon with a one-million-token context window, four thinking levels, and API pricing of $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Rollout spans the Gemini API, Gemini for business, Antigravity and paid Gemini app plans, with Google positioning it for demanding, frequently repeated coding and enterprise workflows. Buyers should benchmark completed-task cost, latency and reliability against their own workloads rather than treating vendor benchmark rankings as universal results. Date verification: Original page displays a date but no clock; 9to5Google’s contemporaneous report is timestamped November 18 at 1:34 p.m. PST. The corroborating report’s clock is not assigned to Google’s announcement.
