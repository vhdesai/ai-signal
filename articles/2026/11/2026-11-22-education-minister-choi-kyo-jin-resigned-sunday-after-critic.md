# Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigned Sunday after criticism of last-minute measures to stop AI-assisted cheating on the Suneung college entrance exam. The scramble began when the top scorer on a national tax-accountant exam confessed to cheating with a miniature camera and a smartphone. Officials blocked public Wi-Fi near test sites and covered clothing buttons with stickers, and identified 111 cases of device-related misconduct during the exam; President Lee Jae Myung accepted the resignation.
