# Staff at the US AI safety center now report to the White House

The Associated Press reports that staff at the federal AI safety center now report to the White House, prompting concerns about the independence of safety testing, though some argue the move could bring more prominence and funding. The center receives about $10 million a year, roughly one-eighth of its UK counterpart's budget, has had no permanent director after a series of departures, and drew congressional questions in August after it stopped publishing model-safety reports. For labs, the shift raises the question of whether federal evaluations will be seen as technical or political.
