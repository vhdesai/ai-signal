# Meta brings its Muse assistant to Apple Watch and Wear OS

Meta's Muse assistant is expanding to Apple Watch and Wear OS, supporting voice, text, and dictation from the wrist. The release extends the company's strategy of making its assistant available across device categories rather than relying on a single app surface. This report was published within the reporting window; the underlying announcement occurred earlier.
