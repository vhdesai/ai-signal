# Gartner sees AI adding jobs on net by 2030, but junior and young workers take the first hit

Gartner projects AI will create 10.6 million jobs and eliminate 8.9 million worldwide between 2025 and 2030, a net gain of about 1.7 million, while changing the work of more than 20% of workers. At CIQ, AI now writes more than 90% of the code, yet the company says it cannot hire enough senior engineers and hires almost no junior developers. An analysis for the Reserve Bank of Australia finds job postings fell most in AI-exposed fields such as software and customer service, with workers aged 15 to 24 and recent graduates faring worst, though it does not establish causation.
