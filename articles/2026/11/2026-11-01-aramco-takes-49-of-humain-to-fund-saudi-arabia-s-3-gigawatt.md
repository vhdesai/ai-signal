# Aramco takes 49% of HUMAIN to fund Saudi Arabia's 3-gigawatt AI compute plan

PIF and Aramco closed a transaction in which Aramco acquires a 49% stake in HUMAIN by transferring AI assets and contributing about $2.6 billion in cash in 2026, with PIF retaining 51%. The deal supports HUMAIN's plan to deploy 3 gigawatts of AI data-center capacity by 2030. Sovereign energy balance sheets are becoming an alternative source of AI infrastructure capital as US markets tighten.
