# OpenAI introduces ChatGPT Wallet for agent purchases

TechCrunch OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet to let agents complete purchases, with American Express, Mastercard and Visa as partners. Tokenized payments keep raw card details away from agents and merchants, and each purchase requires biometric approval. Initial access is limited to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 or older with eligible US-issued cards; OpenAI says it will not initially take a transaction cut. Executive implication: approval gates and tokenized credentials deserve attention as agent-driven commerce expands. Secondary source: OpenAI’s official ChatGPT Wallet announcement . Eligibility is anchored to the timestamped TechCrunch report above.
