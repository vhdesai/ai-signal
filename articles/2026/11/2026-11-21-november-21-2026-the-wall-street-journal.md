# [November 21, 2026] · [The Wall Street Journal]

A Wall Street Journal investigation identifies Li Ming, a Chinese national in Singapore with close ties to Megaspeed's Alice Huang, as a central figure in an alleged network smuggling restricted Nvidia AI chips into China; one company in the network, Novagate, received at least $300 million of hardware in early 2025. Li was charged in Singapore with fraud in February 2025 and is out on bail, with no conviction. Separately, the New York Times reports that China's espionage agency has for years run a covert campaign to help Chinese companies obtain advanced AI chips and gather intelligence on the sector.
