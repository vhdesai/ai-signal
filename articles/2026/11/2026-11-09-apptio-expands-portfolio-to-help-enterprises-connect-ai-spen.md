# Apptio Expands Portfolio to Help Enterprises Connect AI Spending to Business Value

IBM announced AI consumption-and-usage connections and expanded forecasting, budgeting and cost-versus-value analysis across Apptio. Cloudability capabilities address token usage, AI application costing and unit economics, while MCP connections are intended to expose financial and operational data to external AI tools. Availability is mixed: some capabilities are generally available, others are scheduled for Q4 or 2027, and AI Value & ROI was already available on November 6—not newly launched today.
