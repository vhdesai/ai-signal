# The first shareholder lawsuit against Nvidia over Jensen Huang's ties to Megaspeed is filed as rivals' shares rise

Rosen Law Firm filed a proposed class action in the Northern District of California on Monday against Nvidia and Huang. It covers people who bought Nvidia securities between October 9, 2025, and November 8, 2026. The suit says three public statements were misleading, including Nvidia's statement that Huang's interactions with Alice Huang were "not unusual or unexpected." At least four other law firms have announced investigations. According to The Information, Nvidia closed down 4.3% on Monday while Broadcom rose 8.8% and AMD rose 13%, and President Trump said the FBI is investigating the relationship. A short-selling firm had questioned the relationship and Megaspeed's funding back in May. GOOGLE ADVERTISING SEARCH
