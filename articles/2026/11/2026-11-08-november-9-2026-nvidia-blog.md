# November 9, 2026 · NVIDIA Blog

Google introduced TPU 8tx, built on TPU 8t silicon with 5th Gen AMD EPYC hosts and hardware-level protection for sensitive data and model weights in regulated and government deployments, and TPU 8i Max for larger, longer-context workloads. Korean chipmaker Rebellions will connect its R200 inference processors to NVLink Fusion and MGX racks for SK Telecom's sovereign AI build-out, with systems expected in 2028, and Nvidia announced an investment in Point2 plus planned stakes in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD and Upstage. The Information reports TSMC is weighing advanced memory production to capture more AI spending. Silicon is specializing by workload, and Nvidia is opening its platform to keep national chip programs inside its ecosystem.
