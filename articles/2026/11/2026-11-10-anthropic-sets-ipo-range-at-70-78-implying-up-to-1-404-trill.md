# Anthropic sets IPO range at $70–$78, implying up to $1.404 trillion valuation

Anthropic's amended filing offers 825 million Class A shares to raise $57.75–$64.35 billion, plus a $26 billion private sale of non-voting stock at the IPO price to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC (Google and Nvidia $10 billion each). The top of the range is well above May's $965 billion but below the roughly $2 trillion reportedly targeted earlier. Filings show Q3 revenue of $17 billion, a nine-month net loss of $104 billion and $900.9 billion in compute and infrastructure commitments; founders keep 50.1% of voting power. Nasdaq trading under "ANT" is expected around November 19.
