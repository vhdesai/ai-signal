# Apple launches a research study of AI-assisted health information

Apple launched an OpenEvidence-backed research app offering clinically grounded health answers with citations to study how people use AI for health information. The study aims to enroll up to 200,000 U.S. adults aged 21 and older for more than 12 months, with optional HealthKit participation; Apple says participant data will not be used to train models. This is a health-information research study, not a diagnosis service or the launch of a Siri health chatbot. Publication time unavailable; 24–48-hour recency exception
