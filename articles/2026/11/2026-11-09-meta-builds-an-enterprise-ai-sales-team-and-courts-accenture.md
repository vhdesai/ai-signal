# Meta builds an enterprise AI sales team and courts Accenture, Deloitte and IBM

Meta has begun forming a sales organization to sell its AI products to small and midsize businesses and is building relationships with systems integrators including Accenture, Deloitte and IBM to reach large companies, according to The Information. It is Meta's clearest attempt yet to turn roughly $145 billion of planned capital spending this year into enterprise revenue, putting it in direct competition with Microsoft, Google, OpenAI and Anthropic. Meta's enterprise agents have met resistance so far but have found traction on messaging apps.
