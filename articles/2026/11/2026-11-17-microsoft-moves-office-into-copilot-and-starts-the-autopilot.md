# Microsoft moves Office into Copilot and starts the Autopilot rollout

Microsoft is making Office creation and coauthoring available directly within Copilot, alongside the generally available Home experience that brings Chat and Cowork together. Autopilot is beginning its Frontier rollout as a persistent agent for responsibilities such as follow-ups and recurring work; it is not yet a broad general-availability release. The announcement pairs greater autonomy with tenant-based identity, scoped access, auditability, and spending controls, putting governance and operating economics alongside capability.
