# House report: Cursor maker exploring a phase-out of Chinese open-weight models

A joint report from the House Homeland Security and China Select committees says Anysphere, which makes Cursor, told investigators it will explore phasing out Chinese-developed open-weight models. The report notes that Cursor's Composer 2 was built on Moonshot's Kimi K2.5. It also found that Airbnb runs customized Qwen models for about 80,000 interactions a day and that DoorDash has 25 Chinese model families in use. The committees found no competitive US open-weight alternative at comparable price, with Chinese models listed at $1–5 per million tokens versus $30–35 for leading US models.
