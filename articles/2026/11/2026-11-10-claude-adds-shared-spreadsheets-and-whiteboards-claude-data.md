# Claude adds shared spreadsheets and whiteboards; Claude Data reaches general availability

Anthropic announced paid-plan betas for Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards, alongside general availability of Claude Data, previously called Dashboards. Embedded partner applications and controls for company metrics support its effort to keep collaborative work inside Claude rather than export every result into another tool. This is a workspace expansion, not a newly verified model release; Claude Meetings remains forthcoming and is not counted as launched. Timing: Original launch article dated November 10; publication time unavailable. 24–48-hour exception. Verified URL: https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data
