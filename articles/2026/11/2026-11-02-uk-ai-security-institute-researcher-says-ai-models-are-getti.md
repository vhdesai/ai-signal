# UK AI Security Institute researcher says AI models are getting harder to monitor

"In general, we're seeing monitorability—in various respects or from various angles—decrease," said Joseph Bloom, head of model transparency at the UK AI Security Institute. It was one of the first on-the-record interviews given by a member of the institute's technical staff. Being able to monitor what a model is trying to do, and why, is a core safeguard in most AI labs' safety frameworks. Together with Anthropic's filing, the message is that capabilities are advancing faster than the tools to oversee them.
