# Larridin, founded by former Comscore executives, raised $30 million led by Pelion Ventures, with a16z and Gradient…

Larridin, founded by former Comscore executives, raised $30 million led by Pelion Ventures, with a16z and Gradient Ventures participating. Its platform analyzes employee AI usage across tools, without storing prompt contents, to score adoption, productivity, and training needs. Investors describe AI as moving from a negligible line item to a top-10 enterprise expense, creating demand for independent measurement similar to Nielsen or Datadog.
