# [November 12, 2026] · [TechCrunch]

Cerebras reported core revenue up 70% to $230.9 million, an unexpected adjusted profit and a Microsoft deal signed in October that starts contributing revenue in 2027, and guided fourth-quarter revenue to $310 million to $330 million, sending shares up about 17% after hours. It says CS-5 systems built on its WSE-5 chip, more than twice as fast as CS-4, are due in early summer 2027, while hardware gross margin fell to 11% as it ramps production against a $26.1 billion backlog. Nebius plans about $40 billion of 2027 capital spending funded mostly by prepayments and contract-backed loans, but expects only about half of its Microsoft capacity in New Jersey to be delivered by year-end. Delivery dates, not chip supply, are becoming the key term in neocloud contracts.
