# The Huang scandal becomes an export-control fight as Nvidia's board hires Wachtell

Lawmakers from both parties are demanding tighter chip-export restrictions after reports that Jensen Huang had an affair with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive whom US officials believe was a Chinese intelligence asset, and Senator Kevin Cramer said Huang should step down. DealBook, citing The Times, Axios and the Financial Times, reports that Secretary of State Marco Rubio briefed President Trump in May, that Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick received a classified memo, and that Commerce did not blacklist Megaspeed despite other agencies' urging. Nvidia's board hired Wachtell partner Joon Kim to lead its independent investigation after the company lost about $450 billion in market value this week, and analyst Dan Ives estimates Nvidia would be worth about 30% less without Huang.
