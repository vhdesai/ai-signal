# Enterprises chase agentic AI value as "workslop" sparks a backlash from bosses

CIO Dive reports Allstate is preparing to serve customers through personal AI agents such as Meta's Muse, while Manulife is targeting $1 billion in enterprise AI value by next year; a CompTIA report also shows tech employment rebounded in October on demand for AI specialists. Business Insider finds leaders who pushed AI mandates are now drowning in AI-generated "slop" and cracking down on it, and GoDaddy's CEO says he will not read work written with AI. The Wall Street Journal separately examined what the latest data say about whether AI is taking white-collar jobs. The lesson for executives is that AI value increasingly depends on usage norms and governance, not access to tools. Sources:
