# South Korea's Science Ministry said Nvidia reaffirmed its plan to supply 260,000 advanced GPUs, and the country's AI…

Taipei said Nvidia's planned overseas headquarters remains on track, and Japan said it will monitor Nvidia closely. China's foreign ministry rejected the spy allegations as a smear, while Reuters reports more than $3 billion in Chinese AI computing projects that rely on restricted Nvidia chips now face uncertainty.
