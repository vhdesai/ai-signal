# Democratic midterm sweep rattles the data-center bets that lifted US infrastructure funds

PitchBook data show one-year IRRs for North American infrastructure funds rising to 12.6% in the first quarter while European funds slipped to 3.1%, with digital-infrastructure specialists returning 15.7%. Democrats' capture of both chambers of Congress adds to doubts about how long the data-center boom can last as power, permits and community support become scarcer. The Information warns a blue wave could engulf the data-center industry and reports that Nvidia is exploring retrofits of Apollo-owned former phone buildings as one answer to local backlash. Site selection and community terms are becoming as decisive as AI demand. https://www.theinformation.com/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=blue+wave+data+center
