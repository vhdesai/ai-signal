# New AI “copilot” helps engineers design better structures

Researchers developed a structural-design assistant that predicts where engineers may want to intervene during topology optimization and asks for their feedback. MIT reports gains in manufacturability and linear buckling performance with approximately 15 seconds of additional design time. Current interventions are limited to making selected regions thicker or thinner, so the result supports targeted human–AI collaboration rather than autonomous structural-design readiness.
