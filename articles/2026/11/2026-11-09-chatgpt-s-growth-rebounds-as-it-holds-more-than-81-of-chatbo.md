# ChatGPT's growth rebounds as it holds more than 81% of chatbot traffic

A Graphite study of Similarweb data finds ChatGPT accounts for more than 81% of worldwide traffic to major AI chatbots, with no rival above 10%. Monthly visits rose almost 20% to a record 61.35 billion in September after seven flat months, while Claude and Gemini grew quickly from much smaller bases. These are web-traffic estimates rather than paid seats or enterprise usage, but they show consumer distribution still heavily favors OpenAI even as Anthropic and Google gain in the enterprise.
