# [November 6, 2026] · [Business Insider]

Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab is closing in on a $5 billion round at a $40 billion pre-money valuation led by Accel, which could close this week and more than triple its last public valuation, according to Business Insider. PitchBook, citing Bloomberg, reported that London-based AI drug-discovery startup Isomorphic Labs is in talks to raise about $5 billion at roughly $50 billion. Business Insider also reported that legal AI startup Harvey has held early IPO talks, even as PitchBook warns the midterms could add to slowing momentum in AI venture investment. EXCLUSIVE
