# Microsoft Research demonstrates backdoors implanted through dormant neurons

Microsoft Research describes a method for changing fewer than ten seldom-used neurons to implant a hidden behavioral switch in an AI agent's underlying model, without retraining. The attack assumes an adversary can modify model weights; it does not show that a normal prompt can make the same changes. The deployment implication is that behavioral safety tests should be complemented by checkpoint provenance, integrity comparisons, and constrained tool permissions, while no scan alone can certify complete safety.
