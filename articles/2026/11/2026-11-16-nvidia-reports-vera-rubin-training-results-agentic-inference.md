# NVIDIA reports Vera Rubin training results; agentic inference remains provisional

NVIDIA says Vera Rubin leads every MLPerf Training v6.1 benchmark and delivers up to twice the per-GPU throughput of GB300 NVL72. These vendor-submitted training results require attention to benchmark configurations when informing infrastructure decisions. A separate agentic inference result is provisional pending MLCommons verification and should not be treated as an officially verified training result.
