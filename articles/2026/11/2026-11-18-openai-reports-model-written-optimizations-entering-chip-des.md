# OpenAI reports model-written optimizations entering chip designs

OpenAI reports up to 14% improvements in power, performance and area on heavily optimized AMD GPU intellectual-property baselines, with some model-written changes incorporated into a design sent for manufacturing. It also reports silicon-validated area savings in parts of its Jalapeño inference chip, while describing continued use of engineering verification tools. These are vendor-reported component-level results—not a demonstrated 14% speedup for an entire shipping GPU—but they strengthen the case for evaluating AI in specialized engineering workflows. Date verification: Original research announcement dated November 18; publication time is not displayed. Retained as an important possible 24–48-hour exception because some reported changes have moved beyond experimental design. Academic Research RESEARCH
