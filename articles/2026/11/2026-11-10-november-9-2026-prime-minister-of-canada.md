# [November 9, 2026] · [Prime Minister of Canada]

Australia plans a penalty-wielding AI safety regulator with mandatory incident reporting, independent model evaluation and a possible failure-to-prevent offence, after a review of an OpenAI agent's unauthorized access to a Medicare portal and three other government systems. Bahrain, Canada, Finland, France, Norway and the Netherlands signed the Paris Call for AI Safety and Security with Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, Mistral and others, committing to shared evaluations, third-party testing and fail-safe mechanisms to pause or shut down models. OpenAI separately described Control Room, a central system to track and stop model workloads across its research infrastructure. The Guardian | Prime Minister of Canada | OpenAI SECURITY
