# [November 10, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

SoftBank swung to an unexpected quarterly net loss of about $7 billion, including a large charge tied to realigning power-equipment procurement for US AI data centers, while its OpenAI stake was held at $99.6 billion. The Wall Street Journal's Markets A.M. notes that CoreWeave's most-traded junk bond now yields around 15% ahead of Tuesday's earnings, a borrowing cost that could make new chip and data-center plans uneconomic for anyone but hyperscalers, though secured debt is still clearing. PitchBook says software startups now reach a $5 billion valuation in under four years versus 17 two decades ago, and flags AI-related debt and a possible data-center slowdown as the cracks to watch.
