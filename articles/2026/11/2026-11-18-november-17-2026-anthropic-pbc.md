# November 17, 2026 · Anthropic, PBC

Anthropic’s preliminary prospectus offers 825 million Class A shares at an estimated $70–$78 range and describes a separate $26 billion nonvoting Class N strategic placement by AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and TSMC Partners, contingent on the IPO and other conditions. Yahoo Finance reported that pricing was expected November 18 and Nasdaq trading under ANT November 19, subject to clearance; neither a final price nor a completed listing is verified here. The proposed financing highlights the interdependence of model developers and compute suppliers, rather than establishing a completed capital raise. Timing: The prospectus is dated November 17 and marked “Subject to Completion”; the pricing expectation comes from Yahoo Finance’s November 18, 7:38 a.m. EST (12:38 UTC) update, within the source window.
