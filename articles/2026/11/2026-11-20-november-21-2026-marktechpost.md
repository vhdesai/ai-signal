# [November 21, 2026] · [MarkTechPost]

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini under Apache 2.0, a 26.4-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 3.4 billion active parameters that reruns its middle layers three times per token, keeping the checkpoint near 20GB so it runs on a 32GB Mac; it self-reports 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified but weaker knowledge recall. Baseten released bison-1 and groundhog-1, open "decision models" built on Google's Gemma 4 that return calibrated probabilities instead of text, with bison-1 ranking first of 19 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index. RadixArk's DaisyRose-27B, post-trained from Qwen3.8-27B with reinforcement learning only, more than doubled Terminal Bench 4.1 pass@1 from 5.6 to 12.0.
