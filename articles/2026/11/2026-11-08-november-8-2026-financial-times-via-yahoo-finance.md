# November 8, 2026 · Financial Times (via Yahoo Finance)

The Wall Street Journal reports AI companies are scrambling to navigate a world turning hostile to data centers, and Business Insider reads the Democratic midterm win partly as a backlash against data centers and Silicon Valley elites. Reuters says utilities and developers are buying used turbines and adapting jet engines as new deliveries stretch toward 2030 to 2031, with worldwide orders above 100 GW against 60 to 70 GW of annual manufacturing capacity, while Norway paused new data-center grid reservations after 26,000 MW of connection requests, three-quarters from data centers. The FT reports Anthropic secured 1.7 GW of gas-fired generators originally ordered from Caterpillar, a $3.4 billion bet on owning power rather than waiting in the queue.
