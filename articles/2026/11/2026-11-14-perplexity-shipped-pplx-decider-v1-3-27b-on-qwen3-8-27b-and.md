# Perplexity shipped pplx-decider-v1.3 (27B, on Qwen3.8-27B) and a distilled 4B Flash variant under Apache 2.0

Perplexity shipped pplx-decider-v1.3 (27B, on Qwen3.8-27B) and a distilled 4B Flash variant under Apache 2.0. The models return probabilities over labeled options rather than generated text, and the new Locate feature extracts exact text spans for entity extraction, PII detection and extractive QA. Perplexity reports a 0.27% answer-flip rate and about $26 per million production requests; all figures are self-reported. Classifier-style models like this are a cheaper, more deterministic option for routing, moderation and extraction in agent pipelines.
