# [November 16, 2026] · [Wall Street Journal]

Analysts expect Nvidia to report that quarterly revenue nearly doubled to about $109 billion after the bell today, but questions will center on its China exposure, its financing of the AI ecosystem and its CEO search. The S&P 500 fell 1.9% and the Nasdaq 2.5% on Monday as Nvidia slid another 4.1% to $156.14, down 35% since early October, and Michael Burry said he won't buy the stock at any price. The Journal says the volatility since the Nasdaq's October 6 peak has ended the era when nearly every AI-linked stock rose together.
