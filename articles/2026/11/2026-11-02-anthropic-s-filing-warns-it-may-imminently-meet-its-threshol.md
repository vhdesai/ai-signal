# Anthropic's filing warns it may imminently meet its threshold for AI-accelerated progress

risk factors in Anthropic's amended S-1 say internal estimates indicate the company may imminently meet its Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI, a recursive self-improvement marker. The filing concedes Anthropic has previously failed to spot capability jumps until after they occurred and says no current model crosses the line. Putting this in a securities filing raises governance stakes and invites regulatory scrutiny.
