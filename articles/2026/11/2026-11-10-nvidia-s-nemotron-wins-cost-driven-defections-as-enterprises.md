# Nvidia's Nemotron wins cost-driven defections as enterprises begin rationing tokens

Booz Allen Hamilton moved its cybersecurity and defense software from Anthropic models to Nvidia's open Nemotron models, citing significant cost savings, although its developers still mostly use Claude Code. Nemotron's share of tokens on Kilo Code rose to nearly 17% in October, but it remains about 4% on OpenRouter, where Anthropic captured 31% of November spending. Meanwhile, Goldman Sachs, Cisco and JPMorgan are deploying AI tools more selectively, and some job candidates are negotiating larger token budgets as a perk.
