# AI shopping agents emerge as a potential rival to Amazon's e-commerce dominance

The Wall Street Journal reports that AI shopping bots are emerging as a potential rival to Amazon in the e-commerce market it has long dominated. The story lands days after DoorDash said agents had placed only several hundred orders, underscoring the gap between strategic threat and current volume. Agent discoverability and checkout integration are becoming defensive priorities for retailers well ahead of meaningful revenue.
