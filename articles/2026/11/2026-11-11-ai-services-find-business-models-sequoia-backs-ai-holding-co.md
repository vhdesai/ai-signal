# AI services find business models: Sequoia backs AI holding company Sequence as Anthropic's Ode adds performance fees

Sequoia Capital is investing hundreds of millions of dollars in Sequence Holding, a one-year-old company that buys businesses in industries such as insurance and accounting and uses AI to replace or complement human work, and which just agreed to a $7.7 billion insurance acquisition co-led by Michael Dell's family office. Ode, the Anthropic venture backed by $1.5 billion from Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman and others, charges most clients a fixed fee to build custom AI tools and lets them keep the intellectual property, unlike Anthropic's embedded engineering team. Ode is now adding performance-based fees tied to client financial targets, though fewer than 10% of clients pay them so far.
