# [November 9, 2026] · [Semafor]

Tech giants, chipmakers and AI clouds have sold or priced more than $300 billion of new stock and mandatory convertibles this year, with roughly $100 billion more on tap, including a proposed $60 billion Meta at-the-market program, The Information reports. Semafor says GPU-backed loans are being shopped at steep discounts on fears they will outlast the chips, Ares is passing on GPU financing, and a $40 billion SpaceX chip financing is the next test. DealBook notes SoftBank and CoreWeave report Tuesday and Nebius on Thursday, with CoreWeave's stock down by more than half in a year and the cost of insuring its bonds soaring.
