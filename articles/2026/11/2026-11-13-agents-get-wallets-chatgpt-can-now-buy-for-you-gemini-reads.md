# Agents get wallets: ChatGPT can now buy for you, Gemini reads your spending and banks scramble to keep up

OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet lets its dots agents complete purchases for a limited group of US ChatGPT Pro subscribers using tokenized Visa, Mastercard and American Express credentials, with biometric approval required for every purchase and spending limits for pre-approved categories planned. Google let all US Gemini app users connect Google Wallet and, through Plaid, bank accounts and cards, but kept access read-only so Gemini cannot move money. The Journal reports that banks are racing to adapt fraud, authentication and liability rules as AI bots start using credit cards, while large retailers such as Amazon still block third-party agents.
