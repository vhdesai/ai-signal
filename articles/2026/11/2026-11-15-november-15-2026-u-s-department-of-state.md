# [November 15, 2026] · [U.S. Department of State]

The Trump administration will designate Nvidia among more than two dozen AI national champions, a Commerce-run program meant to help US AI firms win business overseas through faster export approvals and federal financing, the Journal reports. The US and nine partners, including Japan, India, South Korea and the UK, signed the San Francisco Declaration, setting up working groups on AI adoption, supply chains, security hubs and infrastructure resilience. The White House AI task force also held its second meeting, focused on a voluntary framework for testing frontier models before release and on hardening critical infrastructure against cyberthreats.
