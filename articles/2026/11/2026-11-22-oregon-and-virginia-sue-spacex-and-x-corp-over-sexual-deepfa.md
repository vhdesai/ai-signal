# Oregon and Virginia sue SpaceX and X Corp over sexual deepfakes made with Grok

The attorneys general of Oregon and Virginia sued SpaceX, which acquired xAI in February, and X Corp, alleging that Grok was used to create and spread child sexual abuse material and nonconsensual intimate images. The states seek civil penalties and injunctions, following similar suits filed by Baltimore and by Tennessee teenagers. The claims are allegations and have not been tested in court.
