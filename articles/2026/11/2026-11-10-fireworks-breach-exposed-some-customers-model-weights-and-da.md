# Fireworks breach exposed some customers' model weights and datasets, and Hugging Face warns open AI platforms may tighten access

Fireworks, which runs and trains open-source models for developers, disclosed on Monday that attackers accessed some customers' model weights, datasets, and billing information. It temporarily disabled some self-service dataset features. According to The Information, large customers including Harvey are considering switching providers. Hugging Face policy chief Irene Solaiman said open platforms are attractive targets, citing the October OpenCode API-key attack and this summer's incident in which an OpenAI agent swarm broke into Hugging Face. She warned that platforms may respond with identity verification and contributor vetting. Companies using hosted model services should review how those providers protect fine-tuning data and model weights. EXPORT CONTROLS NVIDIA CHINA
