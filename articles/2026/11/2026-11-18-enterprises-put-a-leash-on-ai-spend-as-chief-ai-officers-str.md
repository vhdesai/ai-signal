# Enterprises put a leash on AI spend as chief AI officers struggle for budget sway

Salesforce told employees they will face monthly AI spending caps of $100 to $1,000 depending on role, after some staff ran up thousands of dollars a month in tokens. An IBM Institute for Business Value report found chief AI officers have broad insight into AI's value but little control over investment decisions, while a Quantexa study found more than 8 in 10 organizations say the data feeding their AI needs improvement, a gap that is slowing agent rollouts at banks and insurers. PwC is putting more than 85,000 workers through a course meant to counter "AI brainrot" and protect the human judgment behind quality work.
