# Isomorphic Labs raises $5 billion to expand AI-driven drug design

Isomorphic Labs announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia, with a16z joining, Alphabet continuing significant investment and all existing investors participating. The company plans to expand its IsoDDE drug-design engine and advance cancer and immunology programmes toward clinical trials. Positive preclinical results remain company-reported, and the announcement disclosed no valuation.
