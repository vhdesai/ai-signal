# 'Black October' shows AI's edge going to cybercriminals as DOJ forms a superintelligence task force

WSJ Pro reports that recent AI-powered hacks in Japan, South Korea, the US and Europe have pushed responders to their limits, because attackers face none of the regulations, audits and approvals that slow defenders' adoption of AI. India's top cyber agency said hackers linked to the Chinese government used publicly available AI tools, including PentAGI and CyberStrikeAI, to break into major banks. The Justice Department set up a "super intelligence" task force to coordinate enforcement against AI misuse by foreign states, transnational criminal groups and fraudsters. POLICY
