# Nvidia turns 'disciplined and selective' on the investments that fueled the AI startup boom

Kress told analysts that Nvidia is "rigorously evaluating future investments, commitments, and guarantees" as other sources of capital emerge, and warned that if a buildout slips, "our guarantee doesn't exist anymore." PitchBook data show Nvidia has taken part in 27.2% of global VC deals this year, worth a combined $219.5 billion, with its deal count up from 49 in 2023 to 129. A slowdown would push AI labs and neoclouds that leaned on Nvidia's balance sheet toward private credit and other lenders, even as its venture arm has reassured at least three robotics startups of continued support.
