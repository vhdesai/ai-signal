# Nous Research releases Arete-27B to strip Chinese state framing from Qwen

Nous Research further trained Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B on about 35,000 prompts without reinforcement learning and released the weights, training data and evaluation under Apache 2.0 and MIT licenses. On Nous's own 803-prompt test, answers adopting state propaganda fell from 22% to 0.9%, factual accuracy rose from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5, and general benchmarks held flat. The results are self-reported and model-graded, but they give enterprises a reproducible way to use capable Chinese open-weight models without their political alignment.
