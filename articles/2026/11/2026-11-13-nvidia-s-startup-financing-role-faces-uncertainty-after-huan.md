# Nvidia’s startup-financing role faces uncertainty after Huang’s departure

Investors and founders told TechCrunch that Jensen Huang’s departure could reduce Nvidia’s appetite for large investments in young AI infrastructure and open-model companies; the article cites PitchBook data showing Nvidia participated in 105 funding rounds this year. Expectations that interim CEO Colette Kress may favor smaller checks or greater selectivity are investor views, not an announced Nvidia policy. A shift would underscore Nvidia’s influence beyond chips, potentially tightening financing for compute-intensive startups and opening opportunities for competing platforms. Source: TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/jensen-huangs-departure-blasts-big-hole-in-ai-startup-funding-vcs-and-founders-say/
