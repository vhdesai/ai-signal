# [November 17, 2026] · [The Information]

Nvidia's sales team introduced Alibaba to Alice Huang twice in 2022, months before export controls took effect, helping her company land Alibaba as a customer and secure bank loans to buy Nvidia chips, The Information reports. Former sales chief Jay Puri oversaw customers including Alice Huang and Supermicro, whose former executives were indicted in March over alleged chip diversion, and his wife was a Supermicro executive. The Journal reports Alice Huang's husband built a data-center empire backed by the Chinese state, while JPMorgan and Citi lent $2.8 billion to Bridge Data Centres, an operator that did business with Megaspeed.
