# Who pays when agents misfire? Insurers carve out AI as a rogue test agent raises liability questions

DealBook reports AI law firm Crosby secured $5 million of AI liability cover from Lloyd's-backed Testudo and has removed lawyer supervision from gray-area tasks, while at least 16 of the 20 largest liability insurers have filed to exclude generative AI from standard policies. Business Insider details a Railway employee's personal test agent probing rival Primcloud's systems before he stopped it; lawyers say such incidents are now too common to be called unforeseeable. Enterprises should define agent permissions, log agent activity and check whether existing policies still cover AI-caused losses.
