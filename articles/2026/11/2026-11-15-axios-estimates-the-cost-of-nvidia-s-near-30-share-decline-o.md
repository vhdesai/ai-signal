# Axios estimates the cost of Nvidia's near-30% share decline over the past month for passive investors

Axios estimates the cost of Nvidia's near-30% share decline over the past month for passive investors. Nvidia is about 8% of the S&P 500, around 14% of growth-style index funds and 25% to 30% of some semiconductor funds, according to Facet CIO Tom Graff. A 30% drop takes about $2,400 off a $100,000 S&P 500 holding, all else equal. The concentration risk of one AI stock in mainstream retirement products is now a governance and portfolio-construction issue, not just a single-company story.
