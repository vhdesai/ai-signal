# Google DeepMind’s WeatherNext team released historical archives and live feeds of SatWeave, an hourly 17-channel global satellite mosaic, and PARDIG precipitation estimates at 15-minute intervals and approximately 10-kilometer resolution. This is an observational dataset release, not a new model-capability breakthrough; the sampling interval should not be read as delivery latency. The datasets expand inputs for weather, flood, drought and climate modeling, including in regions with limited radar coverage.
