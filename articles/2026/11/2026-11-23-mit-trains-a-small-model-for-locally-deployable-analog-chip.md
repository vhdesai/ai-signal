# MIT trains a small model for locally deployable analog-chip design assistance

MIT researchers developed Small LM Iterating on Circuits, or SLIC, which combines demonstrations, simulator feedback and multi-turn reinforcement learning to train a small language model for analog-circuit optimization. MIT reports performance approaching GPT-6-Astra with fewer computational resources, including generalization to previously unseen circuit structures and more advanced technology nodes. Local deployment could allow customization without sending proprietary circuit and foundry data to an external inference service, but the article does not establish commercial savings or production-scale results.
