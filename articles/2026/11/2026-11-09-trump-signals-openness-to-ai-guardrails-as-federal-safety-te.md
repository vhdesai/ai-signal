# Trump signals openness to AI guardrails as federal safety testing moves under the White House

On 60 Minutes, President Trump said he did not mind AI guardrails but that the US cannot accept "a deep second position" to China, and said his administration once told the leader of a major AI company to get ready to go to jail over alleged misuse of AI; DealBook notes he also played down voters' AI concerns. A memo from Public First Action, which receives significant Anthropic funding, targets federal AI laws that do not preempt state rules and says $30 million on hand will make AI a top-tier 2028 issue.
