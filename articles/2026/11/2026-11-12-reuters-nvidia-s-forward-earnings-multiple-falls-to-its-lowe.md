# Reuters: Nvidia's forward earnings multiple falls to its lowest since 2012

Reuters reports that Nvidia trades at about 12 times forward earnings, its lowest multiple since 2012, with the stock more than 30% below its record. Investors are watching November 17 earnings and interim CEO Colette Kress's outlook. This is a new market assessment following the previously announced leadership transition, not a new resignation announcement; the multiple reflects forecast earnings rather than a dollar measure of lost market value.
