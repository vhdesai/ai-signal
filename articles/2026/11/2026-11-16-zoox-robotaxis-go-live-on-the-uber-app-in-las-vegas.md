# Zoox robotaxis go live on the Uber app in Las Vegas

Amazon's Zoox robotaxis are now being matched with eligible Uber trips in Las Vegas, turning the companies' announced partnership into a live distribution channel. Riders cannot pick a Zoox vehicle directly at first, though setting a preference for autonomous rides raises the chance of a match. The initial fleet is small, so this is a distribution milestone rather than a large-scale rollout. TechCrunch PARTNERSHIP MEDIA
