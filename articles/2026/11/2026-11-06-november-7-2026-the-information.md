# [November 7, 2026] · [The Information]

The Financial Times reports Anthropic secured 1.7 GW of heavy-duty gas-fired generators, originally ordered from Caterpillar by San Antonio-based OnePWR Solutions, plus the engineering capacity to install them, in a $3.4 billion deal that is the largest known power-equipment purchase by an AI lab. The Information says Anthropic is touting Claude's chip-design skills to prospective IPO investors, while the Wall Street Journal reports major investors doubt the offering can reach its targeted $1.5 trillion valuation. Frontier labs are integrating into power to escape multiyear equipment lead times just as public markets test their valuations. Yahoo Finance (FT) | The Information | WSJ
