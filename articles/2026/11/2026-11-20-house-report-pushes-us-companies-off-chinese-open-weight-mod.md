# House report pushes US companies off Chinese open-weight models as Cursor explores a phase-out

A joint report from the House Homeland Security and China Select committees says Anysphere, maker of Cursor, will explore phasing out Chinese-developed open-weight models, noting that its Composer 2 was built on Moonshot's Kimi K2.5. The committees found Airbnb running customized Qwen models for about 80,000 interactions a day and DoorDash using 25 Chinese model families, and saw no US open-weight alternative at comparable price, with Chinese models at $1 to $5 per million tokens versus $30 to $35 for leading US models. Enterprises should inventory where they depend on Chinese model weights ahead of possible restrictions.
