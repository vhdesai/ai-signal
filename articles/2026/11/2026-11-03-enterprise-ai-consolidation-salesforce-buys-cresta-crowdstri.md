# Enterprise AI consolidation: Salesforce buys Cresta; CrowdStrike buys two AI-security startups

Salesforce agreed to acquire Cresta, the contact-center automation company spun out of Stanford's AI Lab, for $2.7 billion according to The Information; Salesforce did not disclose a price. CrowdStrike separately agreed to pay a combined $495 million for Fiddler AI, which monitors and explains agent behavior, and Dreadnode, which red-teams models before deployment. Both deals show incumbents buying their way into agent operations: customer service on one side, agent oversight and explainability on the other.
