# Scotland’s AI build-out faces organized public opposition

Public acceptance is emerging as a practical constraint on AI capacity expansion in Scotland. STV reports that an estimated 6,000 demonstrators marched in Edinburgh against AI data centres; its reporting cites September grid-queue figures covering 28 proposed projects with 8.9 GW of demand. The Scottish Government said new data-centre planning guidance would be published before year-end. Executive implication: location assessments should include community engagement and planning-policy exposure alongside power availability and construction costs.
