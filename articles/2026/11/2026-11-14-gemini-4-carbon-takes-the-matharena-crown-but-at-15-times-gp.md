# Gemini 4 Carbon takes the MathArena crown, but at 15 times GPT-6.1 Sol's cost

Google's Gemini 4 Carbon (high) topped the MathArena benchmark with an expected 85.4%, ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at 82.8% and GPT-6.1 Sol at 80.6%, ending a long run of OpenAI models at the top. At the tested settings, Carbon and Fable 5.2 cost about 15 and 40 times as much per run as GPT-6.1 Sol. Carbon's most common failure is exceeding the time limit, and in one case it ran a script for more than 11 hours without checking its progress. The Decoder RESEARCH OPEN SOURCE
