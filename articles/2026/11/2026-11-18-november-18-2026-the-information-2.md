# [November 18, 2026] · [The Information]

Salesforce told employees they will face monthly AI spending caps of $100 to $1,000 depending on role, after some staff ran up thousands of dollars a month in tokens, and internal messages show it has started cutting more jobs. An IBM Institute for Business Value report finds chief AI officers have broad insight into AI's value but little control over investment decisions, and a Quantexa study finds more than 8 in 10 organizations say the data feeding their AI needs improvement, slowing agent rollouts at banks and insurers. PwC plans to put more than 85,000 workers through a course meant to counter AI brainrot.
