# Anthropic tells IPO investors Claude is speeding up design of its own AI chips

Anthropic has told investors that a new 15-person chip-design team is already using Claude for engineering and design work on its custom silicon, according to four people familiar with the discussions. OpenAI is pursuing a similar approach for its Jalapeño chip. If the claim holds, AI-assisted design could cut chip development from years to months, but for now it is an unverified pitch to investors.
