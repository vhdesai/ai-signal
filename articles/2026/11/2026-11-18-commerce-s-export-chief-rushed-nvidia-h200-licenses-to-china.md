# Commerce's export chief rushed Nvidia H200 licenses to China over other agencies' objections

Jeffrey Kessler, who runs the Bureau of Industry and Security, pressured staff in recent months to speed approvals of Nvidia H200 sales to China, in some cases over objections from other departments, according to three people. BIS approved H200 licenses for firms tied to the Chinese military or to chip-smuggling networks, The Information reports. President Trump cleared H200 sales to China in December 2025 after lobbying by Huang, with the US government taking a 25% cut. SECURITY
