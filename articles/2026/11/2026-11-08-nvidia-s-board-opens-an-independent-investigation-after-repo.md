# Nvidia's board opens an independent investigation after reports that Jensen Huang was targeted by Chinese intelligence

On Sunday, Nvidia's board said independent directors and outside counsel will review reports that Huang "has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort." The board said it has "no reason to believe" that national security or company secrets were compromised, and that it has confidence in Huang. Nvidia also published Huang's note to staff, in which he admitted an affair. Reports have linked him to Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, whom a US intelligence memo says China's Ministry of State Security recruited. The Information reports that a congressional inquiry into the same matter will run alongside the board's review, so Nvidia now faces an open-ended review of its most important executive.
