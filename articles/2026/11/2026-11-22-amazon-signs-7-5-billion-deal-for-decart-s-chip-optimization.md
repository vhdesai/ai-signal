# Amazon signs $7.5 billion deal for Decart's chip-optimization technology and team

Amazon agreed to buy the technology behind Decart's Optimization Stack and hire its three co-founders and engineering team, in a deal valued at about $7.5 billion. It will pay in cash and stock over several installments. Decart had already been helping Amazon get more performance out of its Trainium chips since 2025. Nvidia and Anthropic each held acquisition talks earlier, and Anthropic walked away from a roughly $6 billion deal in September.
