# Nous Research releases Arete-27B, a Qwen3.8-27B version trained to drop Chinese state framing

Nous further trained Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B on about 35,000 prompts, using answers from Thinking Machines' open Inkling model as examples; it used no reinforcement learning. The weights, training data, and evaluation are all public under Apache 2.0 and MIT licenses. On Nous's own 803-prompt test, the share of answers that adopted state propaganda fell from 22% to 0.9%. Its factual-accuracy score rose from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5, and Hermes Bench stayed flat. These results are self-reported and use another AI model as the grader, and 216 answers still score zero. Companies that want Chinese open-weight models for their capability but not their political alignment now have a reproducible way to retrain them. Products & Tools GOOGLE CODING AGENTS OPEN SOURCE
