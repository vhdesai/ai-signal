# ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's dots agents buy on a user's behalf using tokenized card credentials, so neither the agent…

ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's dots agents buy on a user's behalf using tokenized card credentials, so neither the agent nor the merchant sees the card number. Each purchase needs biometric approval, and the launch is limited to some U.S. Pro subscribers with Visa, Mastercard or Amex cards. OpenAI plans spending limits and pre-approved categories and has published a Commerce Partner Playbook, but large retailers such as Amazon are blocking third-party agents.
