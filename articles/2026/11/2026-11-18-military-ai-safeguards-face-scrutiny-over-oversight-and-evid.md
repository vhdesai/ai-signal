# Military AI safeguards face scrutiny over oversight and evidence

TIME reports that a Palantir white paper promotes civilian-harm-mitigation workflows in Gaia, the geospatial interface associated with its Maven Smart System. The proposed controls include civilian-infrastructure information, no-strike data, data-quality assessments, action logs and legal review, while experts interviewed by TIME question whether they provide adequate oversight. The procurement lesson is to demand auditable human review and evidence of operational effectiveness rather than treating a safeguards description as independent validation.
