# Sam Altman's home struck by gunfire two days after a Molotov cocktail attack

San Francisco police arrested two people who allegedly fired shots from a vehicle at OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's home early Sunday and seized three firearms, though it is unclear whether the home was deliberately targeted. On Friday, a 20-year-old was arrested after allegedly throwing an incendiary device that set fire to an exterior gate at the house and then threatening to burn down OpenAI's headquarters; no one was injured. Investigators are still working to establish a motive, and prosecutors have not yet decided whether the Friday case will be handled locally or federally.
