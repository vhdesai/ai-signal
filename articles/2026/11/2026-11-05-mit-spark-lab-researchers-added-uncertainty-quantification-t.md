# MIT SPARK Lab researchers added uncertainty quantification to DAAAM, their vision-language robot memory system

MIT SPARK Lab researchers added uncertainty quantification to DAAAM, their vision-language robot memory system. Robots can now measure confidence in their object descriptions and correct wrong memories in real time as they see objects from new viewpoints. The system improved accuracy on questions about objects, locations and timing. Senior author Luca Carlone argues that this kind of self-awareness is essential for deploying physical AI safely. The work will be presented at NeurIPS.
