# Sierra doubles valuation to $30 billion in six months

Bret Taylor's Sierra raised $1.75 billion in a Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed, doubling its valuation from $15 billion in May. It serves nearly half the Fortune 50, including BNY, HSBC, Wells Fargo and JPMorganChase. Sierra says outcome-based pricing helped double its run-rate revenue to $400 million in five months. Customer-service agents remain one of the clearest enterprise AI categories with real revenue, even as public AI stocks swing. CAPITAL MARKETS CREDIT
