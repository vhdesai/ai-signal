# SoftBank losses expose AI infrastructure procurement risk

CNBC reports that SoftBank recorded a quarterly net loss of about $7 billion, including a ¥597.2 billion ($3.7 billion) charge tied to changed procurement of power equipment for U.S. AI data centers. The report also puts the fair value of SoftBank’s OpenAI stake at $99.6 billion. The procurement charge illustrates how commitments made to build AI capacity can create financial exposure before that infrastructure is operational.
