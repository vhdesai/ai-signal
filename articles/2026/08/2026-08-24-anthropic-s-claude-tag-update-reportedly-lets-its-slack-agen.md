# Anthropic's Claude Tag update reportedly lets its Slack agent read full conversations and jump in unprompted

VentureBeat reported that Anthropic's new Claude Tag update lets its Slack agent read the full conversation and participate without being explicitly prompted. The product direction is clear: workplace agents are moving from request-response assistants toward context-aware collaborators embedded in communication flows. The risk is equally clear: enterprises will need strong controls over channel access, memory, notification behavior, and when an agent is allowed to speak.
