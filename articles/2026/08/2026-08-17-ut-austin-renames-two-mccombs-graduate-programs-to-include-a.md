# UT Austin renames two McCombs graduate programs to include "artificial intelligence"

The University of Texas at Austin is renaming two graduate programs at the McCombs School of Business to foreground artificial intelligence. The change is administrative rather than research output, but it reflects how quickly business schools are repositioning curricula around AI to match hiring demand. Talent-pipeline signals like this are worth tracking for recruiting planning.
