# XPENG Robotics Raises Over $900M at a $6.3B+ Post-Money Valuation

XPENG's robotics business closed a first external round of more than US$900 million at a post-money valuation above US$6.3 billion, earmarked for accelerating physical-AI deployment. The raise reflects continued investor appetite for embodied AI as an adjacent expansion path for automotive platforms. Capital intensity in this category remains high, and commercial deployment timelines are still largely unproven.
