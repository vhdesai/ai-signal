# Nvidia-led Open Secure AI Alliance issues first agent-defense proposals within a week

The Open Secure AI Alliance, spearheaded by Nvidia and formed roughly a week earlier, has grown past 120 member companies and already circulated proposals for defending against malicious AI agents. The speed is notable relative to typical industry standards bodies and suggests vendors are trying to set agent-security norms ahead of regulation. Security leaders should track the proposals now, since early drafts tend to become procurement checklist items.
