# Suno Strikes Copyright Deal With BMG, Agreeing to Revenue Share

AI music startup Suno signed its second major label deal with BMG (world’s 4th-largest). Suno pays musicians and BMG drops training-data copyright claims. Mirrors the 2025 Warner Music settlement. Suno is restricting downloads and adding digital fingerprints. UMG and Sony lawsuits remain outstanding.
