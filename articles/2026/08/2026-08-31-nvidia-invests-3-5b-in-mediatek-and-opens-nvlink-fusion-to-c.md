# Nvidia Invests $3.5B in MediaTek and Opens NVLink Fusion to Custom Accelerators

Nvidia is investing $3.5 billion in MediaTek convertible bonds and extending its partnership across data centers, PCs, and automotive. The commercially significant term is NVLink Fusion access: MediaTek customers can attach custom accelerators to Nvidia rack systems rather than replacing them. MediaTek shares rose ~10%. Strategically, this converts the custom-ASIC threat into an interconnect toll — buyers who build their own silicon still pay Nvidia for the fabric. CNBC →
