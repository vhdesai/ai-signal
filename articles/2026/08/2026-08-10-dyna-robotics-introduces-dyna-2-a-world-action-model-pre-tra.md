# Dyna Robotics introduces Dyna-2, a world-action model pre-trained on one million hours of human video

Dyna Robotics announced Dyna-2, a world-action model trained to predict both visual outcomes and appropriate actions from one million hours of human video. The company reports that scaling across four orders of magnitude of human data predicted performance on unseen robot data, enabled cross-embodiment transfer, and reached 87% zero-shot quality at new sites without site-specific retraining. If the scaling relationship holds, human video becomes a viable substitute for scarce teleoperation data. Robotics deployment economics change materially if per-site retraining can be dropped.
