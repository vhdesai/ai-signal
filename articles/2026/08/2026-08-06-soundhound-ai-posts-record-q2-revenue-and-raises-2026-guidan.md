# SoundHound AI posts record Q2 revenue and raises 2026 guidance

Voice-AI company SoundHound AI reported record second-quarter revenue of $61.9 million and raised its full-year 2026 guidance, sending shares up about 10%, per The Motley Fool. Management cited expanded partnerships across automotive, restaurants, and other sectors. It is a modest but concrete data point on enterprise voice-AI monetization.
