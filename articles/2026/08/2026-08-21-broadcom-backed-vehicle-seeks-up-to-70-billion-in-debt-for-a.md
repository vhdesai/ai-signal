# Broadcom-Backed Vehicle Seeks Up to $70 Billion in Debt for AI Chip Buildout

A Broadcom-backed SPV is in talks to raise $60–70B in senior secured debt plus junior financing for custom AI accelerator infrastructure benefiting Anthropic and other firms. The financing shifts capex off corporate balance sheets into structured credit. AI compute is now being underwritten like energy and telecom infrastructure, with the attendant refinancing risk.
