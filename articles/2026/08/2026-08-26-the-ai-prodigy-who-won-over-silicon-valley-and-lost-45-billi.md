# The AI Prodigy Who Won Over Silicon Valley—and Lost $45 Billion

WSJ reports on the unraveling of Situational Awareness, detailing how founder Leopold Aschenbrenner forged close ties with tech's top players—including raising a massive AI fund—even as he rankled his own bosses. The fund's $45B loss marks one of the most dramatic reversals in AI investing to date.
