# Nvidia Warns Largest Customers of 15%+ Price Increases on AI Servers

Several of Nvidia's largest customers have been told that prices for servers containing its AI chips will rise by more than 15%, driven by soaring memory costs. The increases apply to Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell configurations shipping early 2027. Contract server manufacturers supplying Microsoft, Google, and Oracle have already passed notice. This is the clearest signal yet that the AI compute cost curve has stopped falling. Any 2027 infrastructure plan built on flat or declining per-rack pricing should be re-baselined.
