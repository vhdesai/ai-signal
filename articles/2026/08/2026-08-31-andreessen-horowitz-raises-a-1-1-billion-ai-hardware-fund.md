# Andreessen Horowitz raises a $1.1 billion AI hardware fund

Andreessen Horowitz's new Machine Age Fund will invest in the physical infrastructure behind AI, including chips, memory, networking, storage, data centers, robotics, and home AI appliances. PitchBook notes that hardware startups now represent more than 20% of the firm's deal flow, with recent bets including Nexthop AI and Mind Robotics. The fund is another sign that embodied AI and infrastructure are pulling venture capital back toward capital-intensive hardware.
