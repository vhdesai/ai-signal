# Local data-center restrictions pass 500 U.S. jurisdictions

More than 500 U.S. cities, counties and states have now enacted bans or temporary moratoriums on new data center construction, with over 150 jurisdictions acting in July alone. New York imposed a one-year statewide moratorium on large facilities and Texas paused approvals. Permitting, not capital, is becoming the binding constraint on near-term capacity.
