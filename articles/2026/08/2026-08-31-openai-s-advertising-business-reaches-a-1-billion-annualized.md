# OpenAI’s Advertising Business Reaches a $1 Billion Annualized Run Rate — Well Short of Its 2026 Target

OpenAI said its ChatGPT advertising business hit a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in fewer than 200 days, spanning tens of thousands of advertisers in more than 40 countries. The figure nonetheless implies OpenAI will fall well short of the $2.4 billion in 2026 ad revenue it had projected. OpenAI now describes advertising as “one pillar” alongside consumer subscriptions, enterprise offerings and usage-based APIs — notably softer framing than its earlier bet that ads would become its largest revenue driver by decade’s end.
