# Federal judge rules Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic unlawful

A federal judge ruled in Anthropic's favor in its dispute with the Pentagon, characterizing the department's measures against the company as illegal and baseless. The decision is a meaningful check on federal procurement actions directed at a frontier AI lab. It may shape how agencies structure usage-policy conditions in future AI contracts.
