# Tencent reports Q2 2026 results, touting an AI-empowered pivot

Tencent's second-quarter 2026 results were headlined "Substantial Progress towards Building a New, AI-empowered Tencent." Coverage flagged a revenue beat driven by games and AI-driven advertising, alongside a significant AI capital expenditure step-up that is compressing profit growth. AI highlights included the Hunyuan 3 model and the WorkBuddy agent.
