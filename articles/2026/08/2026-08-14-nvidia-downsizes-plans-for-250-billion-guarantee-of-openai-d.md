# Nvidia Downsizes Plans for $250 Billion Guarantee of OpenAI Data Center

Nvidia is scaling back its plans to guarantee up to $250 billion in financing for an OpenAI data center project. The move suggests limits to the chipmaker's willingness to backstop AI infrastructure buildouts even as it launches a broader $500 billion financing alliance with Wall Street firms. The downsizing signals growing caution about concentration risk and the sheer scale of capital commitments in the AI infrastructure race.
