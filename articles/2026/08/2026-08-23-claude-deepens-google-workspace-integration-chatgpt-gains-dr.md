# Claude Deepens Google Workspace Integration; ChatGPT Gains Drive File Editing

Anthropic expanded Claude's Google Workspace integrations to allow direct interaction with Gmail and Google Drive. In parallel, ChatGPT gained the ability to edit files stored in Google Drive. Both moves push assistant products from retrieval toward direct action on systems of record, raising the governance bar for DLP and audit logging in Workspace-standardized enterprises.
