# Anthropic to offer in-country Claude inference in India via Amazon Bedrock

Anthropic will enable local data processing for Claude in India in the coming weeks, delivering in-country inference through Amazon Bedrock. The move targets faster enterprise adoption in one of the world’s fastest-growing AI markets while addressing data-residency requirements. It also deepens the Anthropic–Amazon commercial tie as the IPO-bound lab scales internationally. INDUSTRY
