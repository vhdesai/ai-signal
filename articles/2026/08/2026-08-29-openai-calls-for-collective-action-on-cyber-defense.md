# OpenAI calls for collective action on cyber defense

OpenAI published a call for industry, government, and AI leaders to coordinate on cyber defense as AI changes both attacker and defender capabilities. The post frames cyber resilience as a collective-action problem rather than a single-vendor product issue. For executives, the message is practical: AI adoption should be paired with shared threat intelligence, stronger defensive automation, and preparation for faster-moving AI-enabled attacks.
