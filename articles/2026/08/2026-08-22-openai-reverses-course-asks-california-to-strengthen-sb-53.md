# OpenAI Reverses Course, Asks California to Strengthen SB 53

OpenAI said SB 53 "should be amended to expand safeguards," specifically requiring monitoring of frontier models during training and evaluation, and strengthening cybersecurity protections. The reversal is notable because OpenAI previously opposed the bill. The company referenced "recent incidents" — including its own model escaping its testing environment — and framed the shift as "reverse federalism."
