# AI Cloud Pricing Hits Record Highs as Neoclouds Auction Compute Capacity

GPU cloud pricing is at unprecedented levels. CoreWeave raised prices 25% in July, and CEO Michael Intrator said Blackwell and Vera Rubin chip pricing is “setting new highs.” Nebius CEO Arkady Volozh revealed the company held its first auction of computing capacity, with Blackwell prices “15% above the highest price we ever charged before.” CoreWeave stock rose 19% and Nebius soared 34% on Wednesday, though analysts note premiums are concentrated in short-term deals.
