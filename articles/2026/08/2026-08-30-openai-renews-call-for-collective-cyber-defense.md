# OpenAI renews call for collective cyber defense

OpenAI's call for collective cyber defense continued to surface in the last 24 hours, urging industry, government, and AI leaders to coordinate on defensive readiness. The core argument is that AI changes both attacker speed and defender leverage, making shared threat intelligence and defensive automation more important. Executives should treat AI cyber readiness as a cross-sector operating problem rather than a single-vendor tooling decision. AI SAFETYHACK POSTMORTEM
