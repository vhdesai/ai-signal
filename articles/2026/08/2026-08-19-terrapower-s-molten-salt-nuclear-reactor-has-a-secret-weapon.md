# TerraPower's Molten-Salt Nuclear Reactor Has a Secret Weapon for AI Data Centers

Bill Gates–founded TerraPower plans to announce its first data center project this year (Meta previously agreed to buy 8 Natrium plants). Its strategic advantage: the reactor stores excess heat in molten sodium, enabling rapid power ramping without changing reactor output — critical for data centers where GPU loads spike unpredictably. Natural gas turbines have been "breaking under the stress." First reactor already under construction in Wyoming; data center plant breaks ground 2027.
