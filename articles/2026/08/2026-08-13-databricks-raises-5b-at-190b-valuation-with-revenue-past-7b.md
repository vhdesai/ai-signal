# Databricks Raises $5B at $190B Valuation With Revenue Past $7B Annualized

Databricks closed a $5 billion round at a $190 billion valuation led by Coatue, Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price, and Sixth Street Growth, after annualized revenue passed $7 billion. Reporting indicates the company targeted roughly $1 billion while investors pushed for up to $15 billion — a signal of how much late-stage capital is chasing AI data infrastructure.
