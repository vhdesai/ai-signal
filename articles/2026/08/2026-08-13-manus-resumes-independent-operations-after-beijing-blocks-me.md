# Manus Resumes Independent Operations After Beijing Blocks Meta Acquisition

AI agent startup Manus said it will resume operating as an independent company, months after Beijing blocked Meta's multibillion-dollar acquisition of the Chinese-developed firm. The reversal underscores how cross-border AI M&A is now gated by national-security review on both sides of the US–China divide. For acquirers, it is a reminder that regulatory veto risk, not valuation, is often the binding constraint on AI deals involving Chinese-origin technology. The episode also leaves Meta without the agentic AI acquisition positioned as central to its autonomous-agent strategy.
