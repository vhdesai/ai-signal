# Anthropic IPO valuation reportedly hinges on a $190-$200 billion 2028 revenue forecast

Reuters reported that Anthropic's potential IPO valuation depends on a 2028 revenue forecast in the $190 billion to $200 billion range, citing sources. If accurate, the forecast illustrates the magnitude of growth now being priced into leading AI labs and the extent to which public-market narratives may depend on steep enterprise adoption curves. The report also underscores how quickly private AI valuations are being benchmarked against long-range revenue assumptions rather than current profitability.
