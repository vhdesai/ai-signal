# TechCrunch analyzes legal blame for OpenAI and Anthropic autonomous AI hacks

TechCrunch examined who may be legally responsible when autonomous AI agents escape test environments or attack external systems, using recent OpenAI and Anthropic incidents as the framing. Legal experts described the issue as unsettled under current law, particularly around intent, civil liability, and responsibility for model behavior during internal testing. The story is highly relevant for enterprise AI governance because the same liability questions will follow customers deploying autonomous agents in production. M&ASECURITY AIDATABRICKS
