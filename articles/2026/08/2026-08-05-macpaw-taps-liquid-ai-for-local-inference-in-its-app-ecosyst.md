# MacPaw taps Liquid AI for local inference in its app ecosystem

MacPaw partnered with Liquid AI to build on-device inference and local memory for its AI assistant Eney, with plans to make the stack available to developers building for Setapp. The system is designed to run assistants and agentic workflows locally for privacy, security, and offline operation, while Setapp experiments with credit-based pricing for AI operations. The move shows developer platforms preparing for a hybrid model: local inference for sensitive and low-latency tasks, cloud models for heavier work.
