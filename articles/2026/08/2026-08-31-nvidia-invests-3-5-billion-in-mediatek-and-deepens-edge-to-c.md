# Nvidia Invests $3.5 Billion in MediaTek and Deepens Edge-to-Cloud Partnership

Nvidia invested $3.5 billion in convertible bonds issued by Taiwanese chipmaker MediaTek — its largest direct deal outside the United States. MediaTek will formally produce Nvidia’s NVLink Fusion chiplets, switches and custom memory, which connect non-Nvidia GPUs, CPUs and specialized accelerators. “Nvidia is an AI infrastructure provider, not a chip builder,” said Nvidia senior director Dion Harris. The two firms will also collaborate on future Spark chips for consumer and business PCs.
