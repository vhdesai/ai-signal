# Stanford HAI: governing world models is AI's next big policy challenge

A Stanford HAI policy brief co-authored by Fei-Fei Li, Amy Zegart and Russell Wald argues world models introduce physical rather than informational risk, warning that their errors "can injure, destroy property, or cost lives." The authors contend existing frameworks built for text-generating systems do not transfer to models that act on the physical environment. They call for governance anchored in simulation fidelity, testing regimes, and deployment licensing. This is likely to shape the next round of embodied-AI regulatory drafting. NewMarkTechPost
