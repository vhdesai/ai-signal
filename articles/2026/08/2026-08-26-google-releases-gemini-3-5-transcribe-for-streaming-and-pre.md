# Google Releases Gemini 3.5 Transcribe for Streaming and Pre-Recorded Speech-to-Text

Google introduced Gemini 3.5 Transcribe — auto-detecting 85+ languages, learning custom vocabulary, capturing alphanumeric strings, and attributing speech to up to three speakers with word-level timestamps. It removes filler words and resolves self-corrections, which differs from verbatim ASR. Artificial Analysis word error rates: 4.0% streaming, 2.6% non-streaming. Governance note: because the model edits disfluencies, compliance and legal workflows should retain original audio as the system of record.
