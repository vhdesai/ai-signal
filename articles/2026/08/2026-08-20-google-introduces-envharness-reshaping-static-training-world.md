# Google Introduces EnvHarness: Reshaping Static Training Worlds for Agent Learning

Google researchers propose Environment Harness (EnvHarness), a programmable layer of plug-in components that wraps a static training environment to reshape its behavior for LLM agents without modifying the underlying logic or losing the original verifier. A companion system, EnvRigger, treats the target policy as a black box, observes its trajectories to synthesize harness components targeting diagnosed weaknesses, then validates them with fresh rollouts. Across five benchmarks in four domains it improved held-out performance by up to 9.0 points while using about 9.8% fewer execution steps. It also produced a stronger reinforcement-learning signal for co-evolving policy and environment.
