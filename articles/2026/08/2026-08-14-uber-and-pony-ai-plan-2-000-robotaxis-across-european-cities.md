# Uber and Pony.ai plan 2,000+ robotaxis across European cities

Uber and Pony.ai announced plans to deploy more than 2,000 robotaxis across European cities, extending beyond their Zagreb launch into four additional markets plus Middle East expansion. It is one of the largest announced autonomous fleet commitments in Europe to date.
