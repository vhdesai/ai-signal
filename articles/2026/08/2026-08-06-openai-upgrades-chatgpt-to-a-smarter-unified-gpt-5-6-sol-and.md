# OpenAI upgrades ChatGPT to a smarter unified GPT-5.6 “Sol” and removes free text limits

OpenAI is rolling out an upgraded GPT-5.6 “Sol” for Plus/Pro users — unifying instant and deeper-reasoning modes behind a single model with an effort slider — and making GPT-5.6 “Luna” the default for Free/Go tiers, with unlimited text chat arriving this week. OpenAI says factual errors fell substantially versus GPT-5.5 Instant in internal evaluations. The move tightens the consumer gap with Google and Anthropic while shifting free users onto a more capable default. Products & Tools PRODUCTDEV TOOLS
