# Twin1 AI Raises $20M for Per-Employee “AI Twins”

Twin1 AI raised $20 million to build persistent AI counterparts for individual knowledge workers, positioned as always-on delegates rather than session-based assistants. The category tests whether per-seat agent licensing can survive alongside platform-bundled agents from Microsoft, Google, and Salesforce. Identity, data-access scoping, and audit trails will be the practical gating factors for enterprise adoption.
