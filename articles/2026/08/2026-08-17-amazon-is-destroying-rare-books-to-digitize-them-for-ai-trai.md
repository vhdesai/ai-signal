# Amazon is destroying rare books to digitize them for AI training

Amazon is acquiring and destructively scanning rare books to build training corpora, on the rationale that out-of-circulation text is among the few remaining sources not already scraped from the open web. The practice sharpens two governance questions: the irreversibility of the physical acquisition method, and the copyright status of works digitized this way. Organizations licensing third-party foundation models should expect data-provenance representations to face increasing scrutiny in diligence. techcrunch.com – Amazon is destroying rare texts to train AI
