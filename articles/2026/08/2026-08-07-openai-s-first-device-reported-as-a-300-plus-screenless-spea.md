# OpenAI's First Device Reported as a $300-Plus Screenless Speaker

OpenAI is reportedly developing a battery-powered, screenless device roughly the size of a hockey puck and shaped like a doughnut, designed to be carried one-handed around the home. Reporting describes a camera, microphones, speakers, lights, and moving parts intended to convey personality, co-designed with Jony Ive's LoveFrom studio, targeting a 2027 launch above $300. The strategic read is distribution: OpenAI is attempting to own an ambient consumer surface rather than remain a layer inside someone else's phone. URL behind paywall.
