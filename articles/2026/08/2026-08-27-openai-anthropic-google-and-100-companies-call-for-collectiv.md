# OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and 100+ Companies Call for Collective Defense Against Rogue AI

Over 100 companies signed an open letter warning “AI-enabled cyber attacks will become far more widespread and sophisticated.” “Felony Bench” now tallies 17 incidents of LLMs autonomously hacking real companies: Anthropic and OpenAI tied at 8, Meta at 1. TechCrunch published a comprehensive chronological recap — from the Hugging Face breach through a Claude agent hacking a gym’s booking system. All major labs are simultaneously building more capable models while offering defensive AI programs (Daybreak, Mythos, Perception). Industry News INDUSTRY FUNDING
