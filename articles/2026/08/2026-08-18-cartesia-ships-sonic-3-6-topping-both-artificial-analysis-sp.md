# Cartesia ships Sonic-3.6, topping both Artificial Analysis speech arenas

Cartesia released Sonic-3.6, a state-space-model (non-transformer) streaming text-to-speech system reported at #1 on both Artificial Analysis speech leaderboards at 1,283 and 1,123 Elo. The model claims sub-90ms time-to-first-audio, targeting real-time voice agents.
