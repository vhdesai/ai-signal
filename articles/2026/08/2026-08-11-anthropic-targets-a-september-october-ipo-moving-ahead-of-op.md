# Anthropic Targets a September–October IPO, Moving Ahead of OpenAI

Anthropic is preparing an initial public offering for the September to early-October window, according to WSJ reporting, positioning it to reach public markets well ahead of OpenAI. The company is in late-stage IPO preparation following substantial private financing and a recent push into healthcare. An Anthropic listing would establish the first public-market comparable for a pure-play frontier lab, giving enterprise buyers and competitors visible unit economics for the first time.
