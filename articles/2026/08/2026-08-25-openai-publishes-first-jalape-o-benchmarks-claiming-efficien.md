# OpenAI publishes first Jalapeño benchmarks, claiming efficiency lead over Nvidia Blackwell

OpenAI released the first performance results for Jalapeño, the custom LLM inference processor it co-developed with Broadcom, claiming up to 1.9x more AI work per watt and materially lower latency than Nvidia's top-end Blackwell-generation parts. The results were presented at Hot Chips and measured on SemiAnalysis's public InferenceX benchmark, with the vendor-selected comparison set an obvious caveat. The timing — hours before Nvidia's quarterly report — underscores that OpenAI intends to shift a meaningful share of inference off merchant silicon. For buyers, the signal is that inference economics, not training capacity, is becoming the contested layer.
