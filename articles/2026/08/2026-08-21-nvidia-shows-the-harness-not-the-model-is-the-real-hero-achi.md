# Nvidia Shows the Harness, Not the Model, Is the Real Hero — Achieves 100% on ARC-AGI-3

Nvidia achieved a perfect 100% on ARC-AGI-3 by wrapping Claude Opus 5 in a custom harness (AVO) with a “supervisor” component. Without the harness, Opus 5 scored only 30%. VP Adel El Hallak: “The world interprets an agent almost as an API of the model. But an agent is the model, the scaffolding, the tools, the runtime, and the skills.” The research validates the growing consensus that harness optimization is more impactful than model choice for long-horizon agentic tasks. Nvidia positions open harnesses as key to user control and safe agentic deployment. AI Safety & Policy SAFETY ANTHROPIC
