# Vantage Data Centers explores IPO or sale at a $100B valuation

Vantage Data Centers, a major hyperscale developer and operator, is weighing options including an IPO or sale at roughly a $100B valuation, according to Reuters sources. It would rank among the largest listings ever in the data-center sector and follows a pattern of infrastructure operators seeking public capital to fund AI-driven expansion.
