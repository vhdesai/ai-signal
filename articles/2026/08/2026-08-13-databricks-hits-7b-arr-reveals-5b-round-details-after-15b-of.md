# Databricks Hits $7B ARR, Reveals $5B Round Details After $15B of Demand

(Continued coverage) Databricks disclosed it has reached $7B in annualized revenue growing 80% YoY. CEO Ali Ghodsi shared the inside story of the $5B raise at $190B valuation: he originally sought $1B, but after The Information reported the round mid-conference, $15B of investor demand materialized. The company's AI agent database Lakebase hit $100M ARR, and its core data warehouse is still growing at 100% YoY. Databricks has raised ~$25B over 20 months while remaining cash-flow positive. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/databricks-wanted-to-raise-1b-investors-wanted-15b-it-settled-on-5b-at-a-190b-valuation/
