# AI data-center imports are largely exempt from US tariffs

Bloomberg argues the AI buildout has been quietly insulated from Trump-era protectionism: GPU boards were excluded from Section 232 metal tariffs in April, and roughly 80% of the compute stack enters the US duty-free. Taiwan alone shipped about $86 billion in servers in 2025. The residual tariff exposure sits in power and cooling equipment rather than silicon — a nuance that matters for anyone modeling data center build costs.
