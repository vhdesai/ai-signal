# Neocloud Lambda Secures $1B in Debt to Buy Nvidia Chips for Microsoft

Lambda raised $1B in private short-dated debt (arranged by JP Morgan) to buy Nvidia chips and lease them to Microsoft. This follows a $1B secured credit facility in May and a $926M loan for Nvidia GB300 GPUs this week. Lambda is reportedly in talks for a $3B pre-IPO round. Broader context: banks and tech companies have raised over $400B in AI-related debt globally in 2026 so far, per Bloomberg — underscoring how much of the AI buildout is now leveraged. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/neocloud-lambda-secures-1b-in-debt-to-buy-more-chips/
