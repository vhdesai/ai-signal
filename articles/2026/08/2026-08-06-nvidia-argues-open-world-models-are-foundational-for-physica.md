# NVIDIA argues open world models are foundational for physical AI

NVIDIA published an Omniverse-focused post on how open world models can help train, test, and validate physical AI systems. The company argues that robotics, autonomous vehicles, and vision systems require models that understand physical consequences, generate training data, and simulate rare or long-tail scenarios before real-world deployment. The strategic point is that physical AI needs open, adaptable world models and simulation tooling, not just closed general-purpose models.
