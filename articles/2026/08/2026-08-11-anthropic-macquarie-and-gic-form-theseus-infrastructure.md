# Anthropic, Macquarie, and GIC Form Theseus Infrastructure

Anthropic, Macquarie, and GIC announced Theseus Infrastructure — a platform to develop and lease purpose-built data center capacity to Anthropic. GIC and Macquarie-managed funds own Theseus and fund project equity; Anthropic is anchor tenant. The structure keeps balance-sheet risk with infrastructure investors while securing dedicated capacity. DatacenterDynamics EARNINGS
