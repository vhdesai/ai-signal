# OpenAI to terminate Cursor's model access after SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Anysphere

OpenAI has notified SpaceX that it will wind down the contract supplying OpenAI models to the AI coding tool Cursor, with a proposed shutoff date of November 12, 2026. OpenAI invoked a change-of-control clause after SpaceX acquired Cursor's parent Anysphere for roughly $60B, citing distrust of Musk-controlled entities. Multiple outlets report Anthropic is already moving to fill the resulting model-supply gap for Cursor users. OpenAI's own announcement is dated August 28; the coverage wave falls inside this window.
