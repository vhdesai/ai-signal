# OpenAI Keeps Its Largest Frontier Training Run on Hold Over Cyber Risk

OpenAI is holding a major frontier training run for its Astra model while it hardens safeguards, following a security incident and internal evaluations suggesting the system could reach "critical" cybersecurity capability. New controls include stronger sandbox isolation, restricted tool and internet access, and automated behavioral monitoring during training. This is the first instance of a frontier lab formally pausing its largest run on safety grounds — a precedent competitors and regulators will both reference.
