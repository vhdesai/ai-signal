# Anthropic Begins Embedding Invisible Watermarks in Claude's AI-Generated Text

Anthropic announced it will embed invisible, machine-readable watermarks in all text and images generated by Claude, bringing it into compliance with the EU's Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content. The watermark "travels with the text when copied and pasted" and applies across all Claude products — users cannot opt out. The policy drew sharp backlash from proofreading users and coders worried about output degradation.
