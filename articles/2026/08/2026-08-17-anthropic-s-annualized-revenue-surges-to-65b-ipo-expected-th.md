# Anthropic's Annualized Revenue Surges to $65B — IPO Expected This Fall at $2T+

Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate surpassed $65B at the end of July, up from $47B in May and just $9B at year-end 2025 — adding $18B in two months. Investors expect the company to finish 2026 between $100–$120B. By comparison, OpenAI has doubled to $40B ARR. Anthropic has filed confidential IPO paperwork and is expected to go public ahead of OpenAI, possibly this fall, seeking a valuation of $2T+ — which would be the largest market debut ever. The company was last valued at $965B in its May round. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/anthropics-annualized-revenue-surges-to-65b/
