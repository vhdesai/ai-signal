# Rippling's AI Spend Console shows enterprise token-cost discipline emerging

Rippling launched AI Spend Console after discovering that AI token usage was on track to equal 40% of its R&D headcount budget, with one engineer reportedly spending $50,000 per month. The tool maps AI spend by employee, team, and role and tries to connect usage to productivity outcomes rather than raw consumption. Although just outside the strict 24-hour window, it remains relevant because it captures a fast-moving enterprise pattern: AI usage is shifting from unmanaged enthusiasm to cost governance, routing, and ROI measurement.
