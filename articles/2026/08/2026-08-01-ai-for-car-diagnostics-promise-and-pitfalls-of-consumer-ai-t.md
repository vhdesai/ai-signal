# AI for Car Diagnostics: Promise and Pitfalls of Consumer AI Tools

The WSJ examines the growing trend of consumers using AI chatbots and specialized tools to diagnose vehicle mechanical problems, finding a mix of genuinely useful triage capability and dangerous overconfidence. The piece serves as a microcosm of a broader enterprise pattern: AI tools perform well on common, well-documented problems but struggle with edge cases, ambiguous symptoms, and situations requiring physical inspection or domain-specific judgment. For technology executives, the auto-diagnostics case study illustrates the liability and trust challenges that arise when AI systems are deployed in domains where incorrect advice carries safety or financial consequences — the same dynamic playing out in healthcare, legal, and financial advisory AI applications.
