# Pokee AI Releases Isaac 28B With a 10M-Token Context Window

Pokee AI released a 28B-parameter text-only foundation model with a 10M-token context window, scoring 93.3% on RULER at full length where baselines return 0.0 beyond 2M tokens. It leads BFCL v4 at 70.94 and places second on Terminal-Bench 2.1. Prefill reaches 137,200 tok/s on a single B200. Weights are not open — deployment is licensed into VPC, on-premises, or on-device at $0.15/$1.00 per million tokens. The play: regulated industries that cannot send data to external APIs.
