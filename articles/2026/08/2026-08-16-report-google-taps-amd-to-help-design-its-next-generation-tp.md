# Report: Google taps AMD to help design its next-generation TPU

Google is reported to be working with AMD on a future TPU that would integrate on-package CPU cores, aimed specifically at agentic and reinforcement-learning workloads. The design would push TPUs further toward a self-contained accelerator complex rather than a GPU-style co-processor. Treat as unconfirmed: the report is sourced to industry rumor, and neither company has commented.
