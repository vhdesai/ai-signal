# Nvidia Reportedly Agrees to Acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion

The Information reported Nvidia has agreed to buy the dominant open-model repository. Business Insider says talks haven’t produced a signed agreement yet. Strategically, Nvidia would own the primary open-source distribution layer at a moment when hyperscalers build in-house silicon — a thriving open ecosystem keeps more of the market on Nvidia hardware. Treat this as reported, not closed.
