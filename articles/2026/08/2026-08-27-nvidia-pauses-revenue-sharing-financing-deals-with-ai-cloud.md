# Nvidia Pauses Revenue-Sharing Financing Deals With AI Cloud Providers

Nvidia paused some deals under the AI Compute Partnership program less than two months after announcing it. Reporting attributes the pause to antitrust concerns. The program was a key financing mechanism for smaller “neocloud” operators. If it doesn’t restart, capacity plans at second-tier GPU providers become materially less certain. WSJ →
