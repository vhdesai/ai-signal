# Google rolls out new personalization controls across Search, Discover and News

Google introduced a “Preferred Sources” control that lets users prioritize specific publishers, whose content can then surface in Top Stories, AI Overviews and AI Mode. It also added natural-language controls for tuning the Discover feed and personalized audio briefings in Google News on Android. Google says more than 600,000 unique sources have already been selected by users.
