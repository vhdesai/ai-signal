# OpenAI tightens controls on Astra after it could not rule out “critical” cyber capability

OpenAI said internal evaluations of its upcoming Astra model showed agentic coding and offensive-security performance strong enough that it cannot rule out a “Critical” classification under its own preparedness framework. The company paused portions of internal development, imposed stricter access controls on higher-risk modes, and expanded external testing. This is the first time a frontier lab has publicly throttled a launch on cyber-offense grounds, and it sets a precedent regulators are likely to cite. POLICY
