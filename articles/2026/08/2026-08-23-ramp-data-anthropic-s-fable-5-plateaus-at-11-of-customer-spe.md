# Ramp data: Anthropic’s Fable 5 plateaus at ~11% of customer spend as cheaper Opus 5 overtakes it

Drawing on Ramp token-management data covering 70,000 companies, the FT reports that Anthropic’s flagship Fable 5 has plateaued at roughly 11% of customer spending on Anthropic models two months after launch. It generates about 75% of the model-attributed revenue GPT-5.6 Sol produces for OpenAI, despite costing twice as much per token. Opus 5, launched in late July at $5/$25 per million tokens, has already overtaken it in enterprise spend — evidence that willingness to pay a premium for frontier performance has hit a ceiling.
