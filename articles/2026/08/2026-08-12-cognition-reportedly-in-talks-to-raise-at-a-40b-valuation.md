# Cognition reportedly in talks to raise at a $40B valuation

Cognition, maker of the Devin coding agent, is reportedly seeking a new round at a valuation of at least $40 billion, months after raising $1 billion at $26 billion in May. The step-up is predicated on reaching roughly $1 billion in annualized revenue, up from $492 million three months earlier, with customers including Mercedes-Benz, NASA, and Goldman Sachs. Coding agents are clearly moving from pilot budgets into enterprise-scale spend, though the valuation assumes very rapid conversion of usage into durable revenue.
