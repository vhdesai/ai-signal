# Z.ai Ships GLM-5.3 With Long-Horizon Coding and Frontier Cyber Capability

Z.ai released GLM-5.3, reporting significant gains in long-horizon coding and cybersecurity achieved largely through post-training on the same base model rather than a new pretraining run. The model scored 84.5% on CyberGym, narrowly ahead of Anthropic's Mythos 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, with independent real-CVE testing used to validate the claim. Open weights are expected to follow. The dual-use implication is direct: a near-frontier offensive-security capability is on track to become freely downloadable. siliconangle.com
