# Nvidia Guarantees ~$105B in Leases for OpenAI's Ohio Data Center

Nvidia is backing roughly $105 billion of lease financing and taking a $1.5 billion equity position in SB Energy's PORTS-Pike campus in Ohio, which will exclusively host Nvidia AI compute under OpenAI's 20-year lease (~8 GW-IT, first 800 MW by 2028). This is a further step in Nvidia converting balance-sheet strength into guaranteed long-duration demand — effectively underwriting its own customers' infrastructure. A chip supplier guaranteeing its largest customer's lease obligations concentrates vendor, credit, and demand risk in a single counterparty chain. OpenAI adds $40M in community grants and $84M in Codex credits for Ohio students, paying only as capacity delivers.
