# Academic AI sources were quiet in the date window

No date-verified qualifying AI research items were found from the specified university and academic research sources in the strict 24-hour window or the 24-48 hour fallback window. Checked sources included BAIR, MIT News AI, Apple Machine Learning Research, Berkeley, Stanford, CMU, Georgia Tech, Princeton, UW, Cornell, UT Austin, UC San Diego, and Purdue. This is consistent with the Saturday-Sunday publication cycle; the most recent verified academic items clustered on July 29-30 and were outside the allowed window.
