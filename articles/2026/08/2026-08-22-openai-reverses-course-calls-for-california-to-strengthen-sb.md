# OpenAI Reverses Course, Calls for California to Strengthen SB 53 Safety Bill

OpenAI is now calling for California to strengthen SB 53 — the AI safety bill it previously opposed — by adding monitoring requirements for frontier models during training and evaluation, and strengthening cybersecurity protections. OpenAI referenced “recent incidents” (its own Hugging Face breach) and now advocates “reverse federalism” where state protections form the foundation for national standards.
