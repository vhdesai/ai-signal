# Georgia Tech to lead NSF cloud laboratory for autonomous manufacturing and materials

Georgia Tech announced an $18.1 million NSF award to build a programmable cloud laboratory for advanced manufacturing and materials. The system will use AI agents to translate experimental recipes into robotic workflows, coordinate autonomous experiments, and learn through digital twins.
