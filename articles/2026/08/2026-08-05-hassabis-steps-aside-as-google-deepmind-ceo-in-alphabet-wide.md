# Hassabis steps aside as Google DeepMind CEO in Alphabet-wide AI reorganization

Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai moved Demis Hassabis into newly created roles as Google DeepMind chair and Alphabet chief scientist, with Koray Kavukcuoglu taking operational leadership of models, research, and the Gemini product teams. The split separates long-horizon AGI and Isomorphic Labs work from the day-to-day competitive fight in assistants and coding. Several senior scientists are departing alongside the change. The reorganization centralizes product accountability at a moment when Gemini release timing is under scrutiny. Hot Talent
