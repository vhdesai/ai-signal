# Stripe will reportedly acquire AI gateway startup OpenRouter for $7B+

Stripe has finalized a deal to buy OpenRouter, the inference gateway that routes workloads across more than 400 models, at a reported price above $7 billion. OpenRouter raised a $113M Series B in May at a roughly $1.3B valuation with Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, Menlo Ventures, and CapitalG participating, making this a rapid multiple-step markup. Stripe declined to comment.
