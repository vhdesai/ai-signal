# Oracle nears junk-grade credit rating on AI data-center debt

Reuters reports Oracle is on the cusp of a junk-grade credit rating after signing roughly $260 billion in data-center leases to support AI workloads. The scale of the commitments has outrun the visible revenue attached to them, and credit markets are repricing the risk. This is the clearest signal yet that the AI buildout has moved from cash-funded to debt-funded at the large-cap level. Anyone with multi-year Oracle cloud commitments should factor counterparty and pricing risk into renewal planning. NewAnthropic
