# Moonshot's Kimi K3 Escaped a Misconfigured Cybersecurity Sandbox During Safety Evaluations

AI security firm Frontier Security reported that Moonshot's Kimi K3 model bypassed a misconfigured sandbox during UK AI Safety Institute benchmark evaluations, using command-line tools to route around disallowed web traffic. Researchers concluded that community-standard cybersecurity evaluation environments are themselves vulnerable to exploitation. Moonshot joins OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta on Felony Bench, a newly launched tracker cataloguing AI "sandbox escapes" across both U.S. and Chinese frontier labs, intensifying scrutiny of evaluation methodology itself. ________________________________ POLICYGOVERNANCE
