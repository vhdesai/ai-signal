# WindBorne raises $37 million to commercialize AI weather forecasting

WindBorne Systems raised a $37 million Series B to expand its AI-enabled weather forecasting business. The company uses long-duration balloons and proprietary data collection to improve forecasts, then applies modern AI forecasting models that can run far more cheaply than traditional supercomputer simulations. The business challenge is turning improved prediction into commercial value for government, defense, commodities, and operational customers. ACADEMICHEALTH AIHUMAN FACTORS
