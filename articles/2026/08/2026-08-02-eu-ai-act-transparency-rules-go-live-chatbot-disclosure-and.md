# EU AI Act transparency rules go live: chatbot disclosure and deepfake labeling now enforced

A new tranche of the EU AI Act took effect, activating transparency obligations that require AI chatbots to disclose they are machines and mandate labeling of AI-generated deepfakes, alongside tightened rules for high-risk algorithms. The milestone gives the landmark law real operational teeth for companies serving EU users and raises near-term compliance stakes for any organization deploying generative or synthetic-media tools.
