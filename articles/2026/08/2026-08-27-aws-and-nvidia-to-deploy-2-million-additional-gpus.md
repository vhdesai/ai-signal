# AWS and Nvidia to deploy 2 million additional GPUs

AWS said it will bring online roughly 2 million additional Nvidia GPUs across 2027–2028, with cloud capacity reservations now extending into 2028. The expanded partnership spans Vera CPUs, advanced networking, Nemotron open models, and physical-AI tooling. It follows AWS's earlier plan to deploy more than 1M GPUs in 2026, which demand has already outpaced.
