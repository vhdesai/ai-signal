# Sam Altman continues pitching ChatGPT as a family and parenting assistant

TechCrunch reported that Sam Altman promoted a use case in which ChatGPT Work could use family calendars and children's interests to generate personalized morning-drive content. The reaction was mixed, with critics framing it as another example of AI intermediating ordinary family interactions. The broader product signal is that OpenAI is pushing deeper into household workflows, where trust, safety, and emotional context will be harder to manage than workplace productivity.
