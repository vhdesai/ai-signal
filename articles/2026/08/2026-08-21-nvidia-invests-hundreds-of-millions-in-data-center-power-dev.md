# Nvidia Invests Hundreds of Millions in Data Center Power Developer Cloverleaf

Nvidia partnered with Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a data center site preparation company acting as middleman between utilities and data centers. WSJ reports the investment at several hundred million dollars; Nvidia now holds a minority stake. Extends Nvidia’s strategy of financing the AI buildout that buys its chips — following this week’s $1.5B investment in SB Energy for OpenAI’s Ohio facility. Cloverleaf raised $300M in 2024. ________________________________ INFRASTRUCTURE SPACE
