# AI Glasses Land Meta, Oakley, and Ray-Ban in German Court

Meta’s AI-equipped Ray-Ban smart glasses face legal action in Germany over privacy concerns around always-on AI capture in public spaces. The case targets Meta alongside partners Oakley and Ray-Ban, raising questions about liability when AI hardware is sold through brand partnerships. Germany’s strong privacy-enforcement tradition makes this an early test case. For companies shipping products with ambient AI capabilities, the regulatory surface extends beyond the AI model to the hardware distribution chain. PROCUREMENT
