# Google DeepMind puts sign-language AI into Gboard and Live Transcribe

Google DeepMind detailed SL2T 1.0, a sign-language-to-text capability integrated into Gboard and Live Transcribe, first on Pixel 11. The project emphasizes participatory development with Deaf users and an AI Sign Language Advisory Committee, plus a joint impact report documenting capabilities and limitations. Strategically, this is a concrete example of multimodal AI moving from lab demonstration into an accessibility feature with explicit community governance. DATA RIGHTSCREATOR ECONOMY
