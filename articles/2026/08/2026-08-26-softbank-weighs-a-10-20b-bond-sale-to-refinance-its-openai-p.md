# SoftBank weighs a $10–20B bond sale to refinance its OpenAI position

SoftBank is discussing a $10–20B dollar and euro bond, possibly in September, to refinance part of the $40B bridge loan behind its OpenAI stake. A final $10B installment is due October 1, lifting total commitment to roughly $65B for about a 13% stake. The financing structure is becoming a live systemic variable in AI capital markets.
