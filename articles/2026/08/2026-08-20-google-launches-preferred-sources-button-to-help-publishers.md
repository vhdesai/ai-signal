# Google Launches “Preferred Sources” Button to Help Publishers Fight AI Traffic Losses

Google released a “Preferred Sources” button publishers can embed on their sites, letting readers mark favorites that get highlighted across Search, Discover, and News. Users who mark preferred sources are 2x more likely to click through. The move is a concession as AI search features continue reducing referral traffic—a structural shift reshaping the economics of online publishing. ________________________________ Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
