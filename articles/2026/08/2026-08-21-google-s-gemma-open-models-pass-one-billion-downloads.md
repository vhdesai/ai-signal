# Google's Gemma open models pass one billion downloads

Google said its open Gemma family has surpassed one billion cumulative downloads, with developers publishing more than 100,000 derivative variants since launch, alongside a curated community repository. The milestone reinforces open-weight distribution as a strategic complement to Gemini rather than a hedge. For enterprises, it strengthens the case for local and edge deployment where data residency rules out API calls.
