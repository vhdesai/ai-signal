# CME Group to Launch GPU Futures; Compute Exchange Adds Token Forwards

CME Group announced an October launch for two exchange-traded GPU futures contracts, pending regulatory review. Separately, Compute Exchange is launching token forwards — privately negotiated contracts letting enterprises lock in AI token prices for up to six months. Deloitte estimates inference will account for two-thirds of AI workloads by year-end 2026, making token pricing critical to enterprise budgets.
