# Alibaba releases Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter MoE model

Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with roughly 95 billion active parameters, aimed at coding and long-horizon professional work. Reported API pricing of about $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens undercuts several Western frontier models, with downloadable weights and day-zero tooling support expected. It is another marker of China's accelerating push into competitive, lower-cost frontier models.
