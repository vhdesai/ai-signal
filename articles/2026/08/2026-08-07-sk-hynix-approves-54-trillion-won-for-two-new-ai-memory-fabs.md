# SK hynix Approves 54 Trillion Won for Two New AI Memory Fabs

SK hynix approved roughly 54 trillion won (about $38 billion) for new fabs at Yongin (Y2) and Cheongju (M17), targeting cleanroom completion in 2029 and 2028 respectively. The investment expands HBM, DRAM, and NAND capacity on the assumption that AI memory demand is structural rather than cyclical. Memory, not logic, is increasingly the binding constraint on inference economics — and this commits capacity on a timeline that will not relieve near-term tightness. F FUNDING
