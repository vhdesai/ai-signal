# Zuckerberg lays out Meta's personal superintelligence vision

Mark Zuckerberg published a long-form vision for personal AI and “personal superintelligence,” arguing that AI should be broadly distributed to individuals rather than concentrated among a few companies. The strategy is consistent with Meta's push into personal agents, local models, and AI embedded across consumer surfaces. The challenge is trust: Meta's history with social platforms means its AI vision will be judged not only on capability, but also on privacy, safety, and public legitimacy.
