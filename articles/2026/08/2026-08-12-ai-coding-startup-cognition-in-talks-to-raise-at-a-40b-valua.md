# AI Coding Startup Cognition in Talks to Raise at a $40B Valuation

Cognition is reportedly already in discussions for a new round at roughly $40B, shortly after its prior raise. Coding agents remain the highest-conviction application category for growth investors because usage is measurable, adoption is bottom-up, and the workload is inherently token-intensive. The compressed round cadence is itself the signal — valuation is being set by capital competition, not by a new funding need. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/ai-coding-startup-cognition-reportedly-already-in-talks-to-raise-at-40b-valuation/
