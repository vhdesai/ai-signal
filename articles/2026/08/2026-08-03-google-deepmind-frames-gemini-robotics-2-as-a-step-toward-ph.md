# Google DeepMind frames Gemini Robotics 2 as a step toward “physical AGI”

Google DeepMind is describing Gemini Robotics 2 — which adds whole-body humanoid control, multi-robot coordination, and a new ASIMOV-Agentic safety benchmark — as progress toward “physical AGI.” Analysts argue the framing warrants scrutiny given the industry's recent AI containment failures. The launch signals that frontier labs increasingly view embodied, multi-agent control as the next competitive front. Products & Tools PRODUCT
