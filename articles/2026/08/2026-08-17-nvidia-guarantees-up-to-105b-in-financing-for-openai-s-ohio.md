# Nvidia guarantees up to $105B in financing for OpenAI's Ohio data center

Nvidia agreed to backstop up to $105 billion in lease financing for an SB Energy-developed campus in Ohio, with OpenAI as anchor tenant on a 20-year lease, alongside a separate $1.5 billion Nvidia equity investment in SB Energy. The site is planned to start at roughly 4.25 GW of compute with headroom to 8 GW, phased from 2028.
