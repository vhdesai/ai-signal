# Anthropic Begins Watermarking Claude-Generated Text and Images Under the EU AI Act

Anthropic confirmed that all models released after August 2 automatically watermark generated text and files, using the C2PA open standard for files, to comply with the EU AI Act's Transparency Code. The watermark is applied at the model level and persists across Claude products including the API, Claude Code and Claude Cowork; Anthropic says it survives copy-paste and some editing, though it has not specified how much editing defeats it. Support is being extended to older models. Text watermarking has been generative AI's hardest provenance problem, and a model-level implementation at this scale is a meaningful test case for enterprise content-governance workflows. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/ POLICY
