# Draft Pentagon Memo Directs $244M to Palantir Without Competition

A draft DoD memo directs up to $243.9M in Palantir services by March 2027 without competitive process. ~Half of Palantir’s $3.2B in federal obligations since 2024 came through no-bid awards. The memo remains a draft with no signed sole-source justification. For defense-AI vendors, procurement speed is being prioritized over competitive process in AI-adjacent contracts.
