# Google will allow users to remove visible watermarks from AI-generated media

Google will let users remove visible watermarks from AI-generated images, videos, and songs produced by models including Nano Banana, Omni, and Lyria. The company said invisible SynthID watermarks and C2PA metadata will remain in place, and it is open-sourcing a C2PA validation library called Credentio. This is a pragmatic shift: visible labels can reduce creative usefulness, while invisible provenance mechanisms remain central to compliance and trust.
