# US corporate capex set to grow three times faster than Europe’s on AI, Oxford Economics finds

Oxford Economics data cited by the FT shows US corporate spending on equipment and facilities rising 40% between 2021 and 2027 — more than three times the European pace — with the AI buildout as the primary driver. The gap underscores how far AI capex has become the dominant force in US business investment. For multinationals, it also widens an already meaningful transatlantic productivity divergence.
