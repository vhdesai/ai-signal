# TSMC July revenue surges roughly 45% year over year on AI chip demand

TSMC reported a ~45% year-over-year jump in July revenue, reinforcing that AI accelerator demand has not cooled despite investor jitters over capex sustainability. Because TSMC fabricates for Nvidia, Google and other large buyers, its monthly figures function as the cleanest near-real-time read on AI hardware demand. The print supports the view that supply, not demand, remains the binding constraint. ________________________________ M&A
