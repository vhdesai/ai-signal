# Meta Tests Robots Inside Its Data Centers as Tech-Sector Job Cuts Mount

Meta is piloting robots within its data center operations. The report situates the test against roughly 140,000 US technology job eliminations in 2026, with Amazon, Oracle, Meta, and Microsoft accounting for nearly 50,000. AI-driven headcount effects are extending from knowledge work into facilities and logistics functions — a signal that automation's reach now includes the physical infrastructure that supports it.
