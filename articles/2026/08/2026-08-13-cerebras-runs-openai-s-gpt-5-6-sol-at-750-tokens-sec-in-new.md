# Cerebras runs OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol at 750 tokens/sec in new "Ultrafast" tier

OpenAI opened a limited-preview "Ultrafast" API tier serving its flagship GPT-5.6 Sol model on Cerebras wafer-scale hardware at up to 750 output tokens/sec — roughly 14x the Standard tier. The tier extends a previously announced $10B OpenAI–Cerebras compute agreement, with Jane Street and Rogo named as early users. Throughput figures are vendor benchmarks and have not been independently replicated.
