# Anthropic posts $11.5B Q2 revenue and its first profitable quarter

Anthropic told investors that preliminary Q2 2026 revenue exceeded $11.5 billion — roughly 14x the $787M reported in Q2 2025 and more than double Q1's $4.73B — with positive adjusted operating income for the quarter. The disclosure lands as Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan prepare a potential fall IPO. For enterprise buyers, the signal is durability: frontier model vendors are no longer purely capital-consuming, which changes the vendor-risk calculus on multi-year commitments. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-revenue-jumps-to-over-11point5-billion-in-q2-report.html
