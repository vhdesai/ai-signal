# Nvidia Plots China Comeback With New U.S.-Compliant AI Chip

Nvidia plans to ship a new AI chip tailored for Chinese customers by year-end — a variant of its language processing unit (LPU) using Groq-licensed technology that works alongside GPUs to speed AI inference. Several Chinese customers have already ordered. The chip complies with U.S. export rules, targeting China’s inference chip shortage.
