# Broadcom Seeks Up to $80 Billion in Debt for an AI Chip Financing Package

Broadcom is in talks with lenders on a structure that could reach roughly $100B in total, combining senior secured and junior tranches, to fund custom AI silicon benefiting Anthropic and other AI companies. Earlier reporting put the senior portion above $60B; the figure has since been characterized as up to $80B. Broadcom has previously worked with Blackstone and Apollo on Anthropic-linked compute financing, and the pattern establishes AI capex as a distinct asset class rather than ordinary corporate spending.
