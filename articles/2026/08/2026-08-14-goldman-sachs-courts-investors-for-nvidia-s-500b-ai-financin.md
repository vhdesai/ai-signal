# Goldman Sachs courts investors for Nvidia's $500B AI financing vehicle

Goldman Sachs is marketing Nvidia's roughly $500B chip-backed financing program to institutional investors, with Jensen Huang stating Nvidia has the option to backstop up to $125B — about 25% of potential deals — and likening GPUs to durable collateral. The structure effectively creates chip-backed securities to fund compute buildouts that no single balance sheet can absorb. It is the clearest sign yet that GPU supply is being financialized, and it imports credit-cycle risk into AI capacity planning. Rising bond yields and multi-trillion-dollar debt estimates for chip and data-center projects are already drawing bubble comparisons. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/goldman-sachs-courts-investors-nvidia-175521279.html EARNINGS
