# New Zealand moves to ban under-16s from social media with facial age checks

Zealand’s coalition government introduced legislation requiring “high-risk” social platforms to verify users are 16 or older using existing account information, facial-recognition technology, or digital ID, with fines up to 10% of global revenue for non-compliance. Prime Minister Christopher Luxon said the bill targets addictive design and harmful content.
