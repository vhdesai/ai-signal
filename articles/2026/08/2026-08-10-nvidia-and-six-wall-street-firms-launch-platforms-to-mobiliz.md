# Nvidia and six Wall Street firms launch platforms to mobilize $500B for AI compute

Nvidia signed memoranda of understanding with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to establish AI compute infrastructure financing platforms targeting more than $500B of third-party capital. The structure is designed to give data center developers long-duration, lower-cost funding rather than to put Nvidia's own balance sheet at risk. Strategically, it turns GPU capacity into a financeable, securitizable asset class — extending Nvidia's influence from silicon supply into the cost of capital for the entire buildout. It also concentrates a large share of future AI capacity financing among a handful of counterparties. ________________________________ FUNDING
