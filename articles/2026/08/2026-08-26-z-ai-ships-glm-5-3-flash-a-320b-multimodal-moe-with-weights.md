# Z.ai Ships GLM-5.3-Flash — a 320B Multimodal MoE with Weights Promised in Two Weeks

Z.ai released GLM-5.3-Flash, the first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 series — a MoE architecture with 320B total parameters and 18B active per token. Z.ai positions it beyond coding, into office-document and financial-research workflows with autonomous goal decomposition. Cloudflare made it available on Workers AI same-day, describing it as approaching Claude Opus 4.8 on coding and agentic benchmarks. Weights promised in two weeks after safety hardening.
