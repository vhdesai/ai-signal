# Ex-a16z Bio Lead on Why AI-Native VC Requires a Different Playbook

Vijay Pande — who left a16z's ~$4B biotech practice to start AI-native firm VZVC — argues biology is shifting from "discovery" to "engineering" science, but success requires open shared datasets over walled-off data moats.
