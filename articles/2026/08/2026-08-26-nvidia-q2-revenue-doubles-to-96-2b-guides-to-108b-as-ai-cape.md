# Nvidia Q2 revenue doubles to $96.2B, guides to $108B as AI capex holds

Nvidia reported net income of $59.69B ($2.46/share) for the May–July period, against $26.42B a year earlier, with revenue more than doubling to $96.22B versus a $92.27B consensus. Adjusted EPS of $2.22 beat the $2.09 FactSet consensus, while operating expenses rose 55% to $8.41B. Guidance of roughly $108B for the current quarter implies about 89% year-over-year growth, and the company said its outlook assumes no data center compute revenue from China.
