# AI Complex Slips as Markets Brace for Nvidia Earnings and Jackson Hole

US equity futures and Asian AI-linked names traded lower Monday ahead of a week featuring Nvidia's results and the Federal Reserve's Jackson Hole symposium. Japan's Nikkei swung between gains and losses as AI-related stocks came under pressure on concerns over rising costs. The setup makes Nvidia's print the single largest near-term datapoint on whether AI demand is still outrunning the cost of supplying it. ANALYSIS
