# Flock Safety Adds AI Surveillance Safeguards After Backlash

Surveillance-tech company Flock Safety will add safeguards to its AI-powered license plate cameras after public backlash. All law-enforcement customers must adopt abnormal-activity flagging by end of 2026, with case codes required for searches and default data retention cut from 30 to seven days. Flock has 12,000+ customers and scans 20 billion license plates monthly.
