# Flock Safety Adds AI Surveillance Safeguards After Backlash

Flock Safety will add safeguards to its AI-powered license plate cameras: all law-enforcement customers must adopt abnormal-activity flagging by end of 2026, with case codes required for searches and default data retention cut from 30 to 7 days. Flock has 12,000+ customers scanning 20 billion plates monthly. One of the first cases of a major AI surveillance vendor unilaterally tightening access controls under public pressure rather than regulatory mandate — a template other vendors may follow.
