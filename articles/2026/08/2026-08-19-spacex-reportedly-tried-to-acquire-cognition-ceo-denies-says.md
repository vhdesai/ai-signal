# SpaceX Reportedly Tried to Acquire Cognition — CEO Denies, Says "Not for Sale"

Bloomberg reported SpaceX attempted to acquire Cognition (maker of the Devin coding agent), which would have added Mercedes-Benz, Citi, and Goldman Sachs to its enterprise roster. CEO Scott Wu denied the report. Cognition remains in talks for a $40B funding round. Musk recently told SpaceX employees AI will be "99% of the value" within 4–5 years — a signal of vertical-integration ambitions across the SpaceX/xAI stack.
