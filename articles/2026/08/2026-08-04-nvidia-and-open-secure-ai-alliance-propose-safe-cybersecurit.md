# NVIDIA and Open Secure AI Alliance propose SAFE cybersecurity transparency guidelines

NVIDIA reports that the Open Secure AI Alliance proposed Shared AI Findings Exchange, or SAFE, a framework for confidentially collecting and sharing agentic-AI cybersecurity incidents across the ecosystem. The initiative is timed with Black Hat 2026 and includes participation from companies across cloud, security, financial services, and AI infrastructure. This is a significant step toward an industry-level incident-sharing model for AI systems, analogous to vulnerability disclosure practices in traditional security. LEGAL RISKAGENTSCYBERSECURITY
