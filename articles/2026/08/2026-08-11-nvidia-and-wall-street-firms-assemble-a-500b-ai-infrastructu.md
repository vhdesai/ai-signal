# Nvidia and Wall Street firms assemble a $500B+ AI infrastructure financing vehicle

Nvidia is working with Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock's Global Infrastructure Partners, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to mobilize more than $500 billion of third-party capital for chips, power and data centers. The structure moves a large share of buildout risk off hyperscaler balance sheets and into private credit and infrastructure funds. Nvidia shares softened as investors weighed the vendor-financing optics against demand visibility. For executives, this is the clearest sign yet that AI capacity growth is now gated by capital structure, not chip supply alone.
