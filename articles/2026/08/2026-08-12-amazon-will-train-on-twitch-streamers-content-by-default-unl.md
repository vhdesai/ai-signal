# Amazon will train on Twitch streamers' content by default unless they opt out

Twitch will make creator content available for Amazon AI training on an opt-out basis; Twitch CPO Mike Minton said plainly that an opt-in model would see almost no participation. The decision sharpens the ongoing conflict between platform-scale training data and creator consent. It is a template other content platforms are likely to follow — and a likely target for regulators. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/amazon-will-train-on-twitch-streamers-content-by-default-unless-they-opt-out/ POLICY
