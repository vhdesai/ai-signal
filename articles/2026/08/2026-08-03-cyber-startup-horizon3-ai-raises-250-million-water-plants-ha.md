# Cyber Startup Horizon3.ai Raises $250 Million; Water Plants Hacked in Seven States

Horizon3.ai, an AI-powered autonomous penetration testing startup, has raised $250 million in fresh funding as demand for AI-driven security tools accelerates. The funding comes alongside news that water treatment plants in seven US states were compromised by cyberattacks, underscoring the vulnerability of critical infrastructure. For enterprise security leaders, the juxtaposition is stark: AI is simultaneously the most promising defensive tool and the most dangerous offensive weapon, creating an arms-race dynamic where the startups best positioned to win are those that can simulate AI-powered attacks before adversaries deploy them.
