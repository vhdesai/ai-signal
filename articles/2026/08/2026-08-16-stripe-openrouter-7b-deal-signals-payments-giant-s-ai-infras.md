# Stripe–OpenRouter $7B+ Deal Signals Payments Giant's AI Infrastructure Ambition

(Continued coverage) Stripe's finalized $7B+ acquisition of OpenRouter — a model routing platform providing access to 400+ AI models — represents one of the largest fintech-to-AI infrastructure deals. OpenRouter raised $113M at $1.3B valuation just three months prior. The deal creates a unified billing, routing, and orchestration layer for AI model consumption, positioning Stripe as critical plumbing for the AI economy beyond payments. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/ * Stories are ordered by editorial significance within each theme.*
