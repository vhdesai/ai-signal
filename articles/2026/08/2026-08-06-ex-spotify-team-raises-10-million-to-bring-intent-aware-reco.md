# Ex-Spotify team raises $10 million to bring intent-aware recommendations to commerce

Malachyte, founded by former Spotify employees who worked on recommendation infrastructure, raised $10 million to build real-time intent-aware personalization for e-commerce. Its system uses session behavior such as clicks, searches, hovers, and context to infer what a shopper wants now rather than relying only on purchase history or demographic segments. The company reflects a broader shift toward AI systems that personalize live workflows from sparse signals.
