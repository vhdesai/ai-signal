# OpenAI's Enterprise Revenue Tops Consumer for the First Time at ~$40B ARR

CFO Sarah Friar reportedly told a closed-door investor meeting that OpenAI's enterprise business now out-earns consumer subscriptions, a crossover reached two quarters ahead of plan, with ARR near $40B. The shift is being read as IPO positioning. Two senior enterprise-team departures were reported the same week. Treat with caution: the revenue figure rests on a single primary source.
