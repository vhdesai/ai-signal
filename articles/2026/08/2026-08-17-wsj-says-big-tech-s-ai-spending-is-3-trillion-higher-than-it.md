# WSJ says Big Tech's AI spending is $3 trillion higher than it seems

The Wall Street Journal reported that Big Tech's AI spending exposure is substantially larger than headline capital expenditure suggests, estimating an additional $3 trillion when broader commitments and financing structures are considered. The core issue is that AI infrastructure buildout increasingly includes leases, guarantees, power agreements, and partner-financed capacity that may not show up as straightforward capex. For executives, the implication is that AI economics should be evaluated across total infrastructure obligations, not just reported cloud or data-center spending. URL behind paywall.
