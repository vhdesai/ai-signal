# Cognition in Early Talks at $40B+ Valuation; River AI Raises $1.1B

Cognition AI is in early discussions at $40B+ (>50% step-up), signaling the premium on autonomous coding agents. Separately, River AI (founded by xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin) announced $1.1B with backing from Nvidia, AMD Ventures, and General Catalyst for an open-weights post-training cloud metered per million tokens rather than per GPU hour.
