# Amazon Confirms Training AI on Twitch Livestreams — Users Opted In by Default

Twitch confirmed Amazon is using streamer content to train generative AI models, with creators opted in by default. Twitch CPO Mike Minton was blunt: "If this was opt-in, nobody would opt in. That's honestly the answer." The announcement sparked fierce backlash — streamers are alarmed that hours of live audio/video per week may have already been used without disclosure.
