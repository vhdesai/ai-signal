# Spotify adds Merlin to its AI remix and covers effort, following Universal

Merlin, which represents more than 30,000 independent labels and distributors, has joined Universal Music Group in backing Spotify's forthcoming AI-powered remix and covers product. Securing both major-label and independent-catalog consent materially de-risks the launch against the licensing litigation that has stalled rival generative music efforts. It is an early template for how rights-holder coalitions, rather than courts, may end up defining generative media economics.
