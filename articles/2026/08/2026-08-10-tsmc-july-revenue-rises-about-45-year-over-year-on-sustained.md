# TSMC July revenue rises about 45% year over year on sustained AI chip demand

TSMC reported roughly 45% year-over-year growth in July revenue, extending a streak driven by AI accelerator and advanced-packaging demand from customers including Nvidia and Google. As the sole leading-edge foundry for most frontier AI silicon, TSMC’s monthly figures are the cleanest available read on whether AI capex is converting into shipped hardware. The number undercuts the “AI demand is cooling” thesis that pressured semiconductor equities earlier this quarter.
