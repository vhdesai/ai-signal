# AMD Acquires Taalas to Etch AI Models Directly Into Silicon

AMD agreed to acquire Toronto-based Taalas, which builds chips that hardwire trained model weights into silicon to cut the memory and compute overhead of inference. Taalas had raised roughly $219 million since its 2023 founding; terms were not disclosed. AMD plans to fold the technology into its accelerator roadmap alongside Instinct GPUs, EPYC CPUs, and ROCm. The deal confirms that the competitive frontier in AI hardware is moving from training throughput toward inference cost and energy per token. INFRASTRUCTURE
