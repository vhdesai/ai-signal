# SB Energy Granted OpenAI $5.5B in Warrants to Secure a 20-Year Data-Center Lease

SoftBank's SB Energy issued OpenAI stock warrants now valued at roughly $5.5 billion in exchange for signing a 20-year lease on its Ohio AI campus. The structure deepens the circular financial ties between compute buyers and compute developers ahead of a possible SB Energy IPO. For executives evaluating counterparty risk, the pattern matters: anchor tenancy is now being purchased with equity dilution rather than priced into rent. https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/the-5-5-billion-perk-softbanks-data-center-venture-offered-to-land-openai-a5c7fb5e
