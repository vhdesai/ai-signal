# Micron tells Hot Chips 2026 the memory wall is widening, not closing

At Hot Chips 2026, Micron laid out that accelerator compute is tripling every two years while HBM bandwidth scales at under 2x, a gap it says is widening each generation. A GPU package with eight 12-high HBM4 stacks now exceeds 12,000 mm², with memory silicon area more than 8x the GPU die, and HBM3E consumes roughly 3x the silicon of DDR5 for equivalent capacity. Micron pointed to liquid cooling, hybrid bonding and custom HBM base dies with integrated logic as the required responses. The procurement implication is direct: a growing share of accelerator cost is memory packaging overhead, not compute.
