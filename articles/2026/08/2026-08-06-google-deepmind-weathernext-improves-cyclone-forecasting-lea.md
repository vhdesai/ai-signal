# Google DeepMind WeatherNext improves cyclone forecasting lead time

Google DeepMind released WeatherNext updates that use functional generative networks to generate large forecast ensembles quickly, including 1,000-member cyclone predictions. The system can produce a 15-day forecast in under a minute on a TPU and is being open-sourced with code and model weights. WeatherNext is strategically relevant because weather is becoming a high-value AI-for-science domain where better forecasts can directly affect energy, logistics, insurance, agriculture, and disaster response.
