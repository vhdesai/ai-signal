# DeepSeek nears ~$74B pre-IPO round, eyes 2027 STAR Market debut

DeepSeek is closing a round valuing it near 500B yuan (~$74B) pre-money, raising roughly 50B yuan (~$7B) by end of August. Backers reportedly include Monolith, Shixiang Capital and CATL. The round positions the company for a Shanghai STAR Market listing targeted at 2027 and further validates the low-cost model strategy.
