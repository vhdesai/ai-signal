# Stanford and Arc Institute use AI to design viable bacteriophages from scratch

Published in Science, researchers at Stanford and the Arc Institute used the Evo 2 biological foundation model to design 285 AI-generated bacteriophages—viruses that target bacteria—and obtained 16 viable, replicating viruses, some of which overcame bacterial resistance that natural phages could not. It is the first demonstration of generative AI designing functional viral genomes from scratch, a result with profound implications for both medicine and biosecurity. The work could accelerate development of phage therapies for antibiotic-resistant infections, but it also raises urgent questions about dual-use risk. Notably, this research arrives the same week Anthropic loosened biology guardrails on its frontier model while warning of bioweapon uplift potential.
