# AI disruption in electronic health records accelerates as venture capital inflows surpass $1 billion in 2025

Yahoo Finance reports that disclosed venture funding into AI-enabled electronic health record platforms exceeded $1 billion across 2024–2025. The data point reinforces that enterprise AI adoption is moving into workflow systems of record, where ambient documentation, predictive analytics, and interoperability may generate more defensible value than standalone chat interfaces.
