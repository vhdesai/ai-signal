# Bain Joins Anthropic's Claude Partner Network at Global Premier Tier

Bain & Company and Anthropic announced a global partnership placing Bain at Global Premier, the top tier of the Claude Partner Network. The arrangement extends the pattern of frontier labs using tier-one consultancies as their enterprise deployment channel. For buyers, it means model selection is increasingly bundled with a systems-integration relationship rather than negotiated independently. ________________________________ FUNDING
