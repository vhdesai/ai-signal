# Forward-deployed engineers become the enterprise AI adoption bottleneck

CIO Dive reported that enterprises are increasingly relying on forward-deployed engineers to connect AI rollouts with real business processes. The story shows that deployment friction has shifted from model access to workflow redesign, systems integration, and change management. This also explains why startups such as June are attracting funding to automate AI deployment playbooks and reduce dependence on bespoke services teams.
