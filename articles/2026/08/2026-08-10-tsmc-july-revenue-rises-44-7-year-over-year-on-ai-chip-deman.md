# TSMC July revenue rises 44.7% year over year on AI chip demand

TSMC reported July revenue of NT$467.58 billion (about $14.5 billion), up 44.7% year on year and running ahead of its own raised full-year guidance of slightly above 40% growth in dollar terms. The company has lifted 2026 capital expenditure guidance to $60–64 billion, and high-performance computing — where AI chip revenue is booked — accounted for 66% of second-quarter revenue. First-half 2026 revenue reached NT$2.4 trillion, 35.6% ahead of the same point last year. Because TSMC's customer list includes Nvidia and Google, the monthly print remains the cleanest single barometer of AI hardware demand; European chip equities rose on the release. FUNDING
