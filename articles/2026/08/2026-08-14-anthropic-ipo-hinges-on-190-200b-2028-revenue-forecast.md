# Anthropic IPO Hinges on $190–200B 2028 Revenue Forecast

Anthropic projects ~$190–200B annual revenue by 2028 — the figure bankers are using to underwrite one of the largest IPOs on record. Wall Street is pricing the company on a 3-year-out forecast, concentrating valuation risk in assumptions rather than results. Commentary questioned whether ~$2T implied value is defensible given rising compute costs and open-weight competition. Treat Anthropic-dependent commercial terms as negotiated against a pre-IPO backdrop where growth metrics are essential.
