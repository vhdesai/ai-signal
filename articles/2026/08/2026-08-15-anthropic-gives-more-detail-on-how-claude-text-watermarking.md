# Anthropic gives more detail on how Claude text watermarking will work

Anthropic explained that future Claude models will use a SynthID-Text-style watermark to comply with the EU AI Act's Transparency Code. The company said the watermark changes the source of randomness in low-stakes word choices, does not add hidden characters, carries no identifying information, and should be less detectable in short, factual, lightly edited, or code-heavy outputs. The executive issue is practical governance: watermarking may help provenance, but detection confidence, editing robustness, and developer adoption will determine its operational value.
