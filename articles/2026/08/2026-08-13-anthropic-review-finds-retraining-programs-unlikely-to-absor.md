# Anthropic review finds retraining programs unlikely to absorb AI displacement

Anthropic's Economic Research team, with independent researcher David Roodman, reviewed 56 randomized U.S. studies plus European evidence on worker retraining. Typical programs lift employment by only about 2–3 percentage points and earnings by roughly $1,000 per year against roughly $13,000 in cost, leaving most programs close to fiscally break-even after tax and benefit offsets. A handful of sector-specific programs produced much larger gains but have repeatedly failed to replicate — a direct challenge to "retraining will handle it" as a workforce strategy. https://www.anthropic.com/research/reviewing-the-evidence-on-worker-retraining-programs Academic Research RESEARCH
