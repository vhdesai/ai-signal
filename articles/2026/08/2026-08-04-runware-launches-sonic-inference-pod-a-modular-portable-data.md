# Runware launches Sonic Inference Pod, a modular portable data center

AI infrastructure company Runware unveiled the Sonic Inference Pod, a self-contained modular data center designed to be deployed where power and cooling are available rather than where traditional facilities exist. The pitch is speed-to-capacity and siting flexibility for inference workloads, which are less latency-tolerant of centralized megacampuses than training. Portable pods are a direct commercial response to exactly the interconnection bottlenecks now surfacing in markets like Texas.
