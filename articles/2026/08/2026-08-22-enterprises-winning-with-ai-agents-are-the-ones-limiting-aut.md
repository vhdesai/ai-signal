# Enterprises Winning With AI Agents Are the Ones Limiting Autonomy

VentureBeat argues that the enterprises succeeding with agentic AI in mid-2026 are deliberately constraining agent autonomy. Gartner forecasts 40%+ of agentic AI projects running today will be cancelled before 2028. Scoped, bounded agent design with human checkpoints is the primary differentiator between production deployments and abandoned pilots.
