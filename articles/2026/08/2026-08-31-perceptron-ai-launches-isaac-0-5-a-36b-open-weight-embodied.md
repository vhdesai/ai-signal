# Perceptron AI Launches Isaac 0.5, a 36B Open-Weight Embodied Foundation Model

Perceptron AI launched Isaac 0.5, a 36-billion-parameter open-weight embodied foundation model combining video understanding, embodied reasoning, and robot control. The model is positioned as a frontier open-weight entry for robotics rather than a text-only LLM, reinforcing the shift toward physical-world foundation models.
