# Anthropic loosens Fable 5 biology safeguards, cutting fallbacks by about 85%

Anthropic retuned Claude Fable 5’s biology safety classifiers to reduce false positives, reporting that biology-related "fallbacks" — where the system reroutes to a less capable model — dropped by roughly 85% across its product surfaces. Everyday health, educational, and clinical questions should now be answered directly far more often. Anthropic stated that Fable 5 still falls back to Opus 5 for dual-use requests spanning virology, toxicology, and molecular design, because the model "could provide significant uplift" to a malicious actor developing a biological weapon. The change is a deliberate recalibration of usefulness against catastrophic-misuse risk.
