# Z.AI first-half revenue rises roughly fivefold to 954M yuan on API demand

Hong Kong-listed Z.AI (Zhipu), developer of the GLM model family, reported first-half 2026 revenue of 954M yuan (about $142M), up roughly 400% year over year, driven by cloud API and model-as-a-service consumption. Losses remained substantial and revenue still came in below some analyst estimates despite a valuation reported near $71B.
