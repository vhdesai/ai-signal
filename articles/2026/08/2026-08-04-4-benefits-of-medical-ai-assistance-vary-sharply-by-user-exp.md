# 4. Benefits of medical AI assistance vary sharply by user expertise

A new MIT-led study in Nature Medicine tested non-experts and clinicians diagnosing skin disease with and without explainable-AI aids. Non-experts deferred to LLM-based assistance even when it was wrong — and found vague, generic explanations more convincing — while clinicians performed best with a prediction alone and no explanation. The takeaway: explainable AI can amplify error among novices via automation bias, so interfaces must be tailored to the user’s expertise level. Products & Tools NewApple
