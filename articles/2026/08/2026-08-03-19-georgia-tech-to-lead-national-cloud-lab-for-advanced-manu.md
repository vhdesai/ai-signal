# 19. Georgia Tech to lead national cloud lab for advanced manufacturing and materials

Backed by $18.1M from the NSF, Georgia Tech will lead a Programmable Cloud Laboratory pairing AI, simulation, and autonomous robotics with its Advanced Manufacturing Pilot Facility, letting researchers nationwide run AI-driven materials-discovery experiments remotely. Part of a national network of 20 AI-enabled cloud labs, it aims to serve 400+ users across 150 institutions, with AI agents translating high-level “recipes” into robotic experimental workflows.
