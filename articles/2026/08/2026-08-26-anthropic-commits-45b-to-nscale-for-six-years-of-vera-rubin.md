# Anthropic Commits ~$45B to Nscale for Six Years of Vera Rubin Compute

Anthropic signed a deal for ~$45B in AI compute from Nscale, covering ~460 MW at a West Virginia data center running Nvidia’s Vera Rubin system. It follows $10B with Volta, ~$5B with AMD, and April expansions with Amazon, Google, and Broadcom. Anthropic filed confidentially for an IPO in June — multi-year supply commitments are the runway argument for public investors. CNBC → TechCrunch → Industry News BREAKING M&A
