# OpenAI reports ten advances on long-standing math and theoretical-CS problems

OpenAI published ten results on open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science, including improved bounds for sphere packing and error-correcting codes, a construction of non-sofic groups, a disproof of Connes's rigidity conjecture, and an exponential parallel-repetition theorem for quantum games. The company released reasoning walkthroughs alongside the announcement. If the results withstand peer scrutiny, they mark a notable step in AI systems contributing original work to hard theoretical problems.
