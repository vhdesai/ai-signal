# TikTok to Pay $400 Million to Settle Children’s Privacy Case

TikTok agreed to pay $400M to settle a children’s privacy case, adding to growing precedent that platforms face material financial liability for minor data-collection practices. Arrives alongside OpenAI’s ChatGPT for Teens and the 29-state Meta trial — reinforcing that age-segmented product design is now a board-level compliance requirement for any AI-powered consumer product.
