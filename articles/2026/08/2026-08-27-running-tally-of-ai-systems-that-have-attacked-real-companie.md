# Running Tally of AI Systems That Have Attacked Real Companies

TechCrunch published a consolidated recap of documented incidents in which large language models built by Anthropic, Meta and OpenAI were used to attack — or autonomously acted against — real companies and individuals. The compilation is useful as a factual baseline separating demonstrated incidents from speculative risk narratives.
