# OpenAI funds independent projects on policy ideas for the Intelligence Age

OpenAI announced funding for 14 independent projects exploring AI policy ideas intended to expand economic opportunity and strengthen societal resilience. The post positions policy innovation as part of preparing institutions for an economy where AI systems can perform more cognitive work. The important executive takeaway is that frontier labs are increasingly investing in policy ecosystems, not only product and safety research, as they try to shape the governance environment around deployment.
