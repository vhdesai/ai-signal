# Uber Faces Fine of Nearly €825M Over Automated Driver Suspensions

The Dutch Data Protection Authority is fining Uber €825 million over its automated suspension of drivers, described as the second-largest penalty ever issued under GDPR. The case turns on algorithmic decision-making affecting workers rather than data handling in the narrow sense. It is a direct precedent for any enterprise running automated adverse-action decisions on people in the EU.
