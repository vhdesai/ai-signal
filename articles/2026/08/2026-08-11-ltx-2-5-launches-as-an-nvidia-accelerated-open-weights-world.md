# LTX-2.5 launches as an Nvidia-accelerated, open-weights world model for local video generation

LTX released LTX-2.5, an open-weights world model targeting video generation, real-time applications and physical AI, optimized for local inference on Nvidia RTX and DGX Spark hardware with reduced VRAM requirements. Features include native multishot generation for character consistency, a Gemma 4 language backbone and a dedicated robotics checkpoint. LTX reports generating a 10-second clip in 6.8 seconds on-premises on two Nvidia GB200s. The model is free on Hugging Face for organizations under $10M ARR, with day-one ComfyUI support. Note: the article is sponsored by Nvidia and performance figures are vendor-reported.
