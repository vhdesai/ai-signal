# SpaceXAI Ships Grok 4.6 for Long-Running Agents; Musk Says 4.7 Is Weeks Away

SpaceXAI (formerly xAI) released Grok 4.6, a flagship model tuned for long-running agents, coding, and sustained knowledge work, with same-day availability in Cursor, Grok Build, and the API at $2/$6 per million tokens. Vendor-reported benchmarks place it at 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Musk previewed Grok 4.7 as “significantly better,” said it has finished initial training on SpaceX operational data, with a 3–4 week timeline. The company credited engineering work from Cursor, the coding company it is acquiring — signaling the competitive axis is shifting from single-answer quality benchmarks to sustained, multi-hour tool-use reliability. For enterprises evaluating coding agents, Grok 4.6 represents another credible option alongside Claude Code, Codex, and Devin. Unite.AI TRENDING SILICON
