# Naive raises $28.5 million to automate company setup and back-office infrastructure

Naive raised a $28.5 million Series A for infrastructure that lets AI agents provision company formation, email, payments, cloud resources, storage, and other operational systems through a single API. The startup says it has more than 30,000 developer customers and has scaled annual run-rate revenue by 10x in six months. The broader signal is that agentic automation is expanding from software creation into company formation and back-office orchestration.
