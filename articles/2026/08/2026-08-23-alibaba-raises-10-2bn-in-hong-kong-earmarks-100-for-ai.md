# Alibaba Raises $10.2bn in Hong Kong, Earmarks 100% for AI

Alibaba is placing HK$80bn (roughly $10.2bn) of new shares in Hong Kong and states that all net proceeds will fund full-stack AI capabilities including infrastructure. The company calls it the largest primary follow-on ever by a Hong Kong-listed company and the largest Regulation S equity offering on record; globally it ranks third this year behind Alphabet and Intel. The timing is pointed — it comes three days after Alibaba reported a 75% drop in quarterly net profit driven by a 75% increase in capex to ¥67.68bn, against cloud and AI revenue up 45% to ¥48.44bn. For context, a single afternoon's placement is roughly half of the EU's entire ~€20bn AI gigafactory commitment.
