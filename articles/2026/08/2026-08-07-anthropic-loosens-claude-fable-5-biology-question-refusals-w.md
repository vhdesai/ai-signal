# Anthropic loosens Claude “Fable 5” biology-question refusals while flagging bioweapon risk

Anthropic updated Claude Fable 5's biology safety classifiers, cutting automatic “fallbacks” — where sensitive biology queries are silently routed to weaker models — by roughly 85% to reduce false positives for legitimate users (e.g., interpreting lab results or explaining vaccines). Anthropic says it is retaining safeguards for genuinely dual-use domains such as virology, toxicology, and drug/molecular design, and continues to warn about bioweapon-uplift risk. The change illustrates the tightening usefulness-versus-biosecurity trade-off as models grow more capable — landing the same week as the AI-designed-virus research.
