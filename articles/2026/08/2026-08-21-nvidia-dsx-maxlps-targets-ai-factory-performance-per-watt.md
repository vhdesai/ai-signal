# NVIDIA DSX MaxLPS targets AI factory performance per watt

NVIDIA detailed DSX MaxLPS, a suite of chip, thermal, system, and software techniques aimed at maximizing AI factory throughput within fixed land, power, and shell constraints. The post argues that only a portion of site power turns into revenue-generating compute after facility overhead, rack losses, cooling, and operational inefficiency. For executives, the key metric is shifting from raw GPU count to application-level output per megawatt. NEOCLOUDBUYER INTELLIGENCE
