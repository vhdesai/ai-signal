# Xiaomi's MiLM Plus releases PROVE, perception-aligned object-removal metrics with a real-world video benchmark

A Xiaomi MiLM Plus team released PROVE to address mis-ranking by legacy image and video erasure metrics, pairing two no-reference metrics, RC-S for spatial and RC-T for temporal quality, computed via sliding-window Maximum Mean Discrepancy over DINOv2 features. It ships alongside PROVE-Bench, a real-world 1080p video benchmark. Against human rankings, RC-S reaches 0.59 average Kendall's tau versus 0.26 for ReMOVE and 0.16 for CFD, released as Apache-2.0 code. The work is vendor-origin research, so results are self-reported.
