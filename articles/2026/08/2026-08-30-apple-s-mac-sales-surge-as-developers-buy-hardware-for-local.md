# Apple's Mac sales surge as developers buy hardware for local AI work

The Information reported that Apple's hottest products are increasingly Mac models used by developers and power users running AI workloads locally. The trend suggests that AI demand is not limited to cloud GPUs; edge and workstation hardware can also benefit when inference, coding, and prototyping move closer to users. It is a reminder that the AI hardware cycle includes client devices as well as data centers.
