# Marvell’s $120B Google Custom-Silicon Deal Meets a Timing Reality Check

Marvell shares fell ~8% after management indicated revenue from its Google AI-chip agreement becomes meaningful only in FY2029. The reaction marks a shift: announcement value is no longer treated as near-term revenue. Custom accelerator programs remain strategic but on multi-year cycles.
