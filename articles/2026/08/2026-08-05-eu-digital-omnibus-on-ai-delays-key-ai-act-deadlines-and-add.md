# EU Digital Omnibus on AI delays key AI Act deadlines and adds new obligations

Analysis published in the last day details the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744), which entered into force after publication in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and postpones several AI Act compliance deadlines while introducing new rules. The deferral gives providers and deployers additional runway on high-risk obligations but does not remove them. Compliance programs built to the original timetable should be re-baselined rather than paused.
