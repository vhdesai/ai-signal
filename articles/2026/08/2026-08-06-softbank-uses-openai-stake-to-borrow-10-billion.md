# SoftBank uses OpenAI stake to borrow $10 billion

SoftBank is using its OpenAI stake to borrow $10 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal. The financing shows how private AI equity is becoming collateral for broader capital needs as major investors seek liquidity without selling scarce AI exposure. It also reinforces OpenAI's role as a financial asset in the AI ecosystem, not only a model provider.
