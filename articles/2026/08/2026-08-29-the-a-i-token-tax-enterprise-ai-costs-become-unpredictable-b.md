# The A.I. Token Tax: Enterprise AI Costs Become Unpredictable Budget Line Items

DealBook's Sarah Kessler examines how AI usage costs are becoming a significant and often unpredictable "token tax" on enterprise budgets. As companies move from pilots to production, cumulative token costs are forcing CIOs to grapple with cost governance, model selection, and the tension between AI capability and operational efficiency. Record labels are also fighting over AI-generated music copyright.
