# Enterprises seek help deploying AI as forward-deployed engineers become a bottleneck

CIO Dive reported that forward-deployed engineers are increasingly helping companies connect technical AI rollouts to business processes and ease adoption. The rise of FDEs shows that enterprise AI friction has shifted from model access to workflow integration, process redesign, and operational change management.
