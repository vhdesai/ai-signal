# Applied Materials posts record Q3 revenue of $9.12B on AI demand

Applied Materials reported record third-quarter revenue of $9.12B, up roughly 25% year over year, and guided to as much as $10.75B in the current quarter as AI-driven semiconductor equipment spending accelerates. Separately, Lam Research committed more than $3B over five years to expand its global R&D lab network for AI-era chipmaking, and Reuters reported SMIC raising prices as demand surged. Equipment orders are the leading indicator here: this is capacity being committed 18–24 months ahead of the compute it will produce. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/applied-materials-q3-2026-earnings-114815272.html FINANCE
