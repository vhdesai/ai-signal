# Private Equity Deploys Army of AI Wonks to Embed in Portfolio Companies

PE firms are deploying dedicated AI specialists directly into portfolio companies to drive operational improvements. The trend represents a shift from consulting to permanent embedded AI teams — with buyout shops viewing AI as a core value-creation lever rather than a bolt-on initiative.
