# Wall Street Is Counting on Nvidia to Keep the AI Party Going

Nvidia reports Q2 FY2027 results on Wednesday, August 26 at 2:00pm PT, carrying an approximately $5T market capitalization. It has been the largest single contributor to S&P 500 earnings growth in seven of the past eleven quarters, and Yardeni Research expects its share of index earnings to reach 7.2% this year versus 4.9% in 2025, excluding one-off investment gains at Alphabet and Amazon. Analyst attention centers on commentary about the Rubin ramp rather than the headline beat.
