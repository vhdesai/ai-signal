# WSJ: AI Threatens Existential Reset for SaaS Business Models

A WSJ front-page story by Kate Clark argues generative AI is steamrolling the once-booming software-as-a-service industry, with incumbents scrambling to remake both products and business models. The threat is described as structural to seat-based SaaS economics rather than a competitive feature gap. This is the kind of narrative shift that accelerates procurement-cycle changes when it reaches the WSJ front page. Products & Tools OPEN SOURCE
