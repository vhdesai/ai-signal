# Mistral releases Shieldstral for policy-adaptive safety screening

Mistral released Shieldstral, a 3B open-weights classifier that screens text and images against moderation policies supplied in plain language at inference time. For enterprises, this points toward lower-cost, in-house content governance rather than relying only on a frontier vendor's built-in refusal behavior.
