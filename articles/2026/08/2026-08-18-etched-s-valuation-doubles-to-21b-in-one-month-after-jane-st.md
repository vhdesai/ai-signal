# Etched's Valuation Doubles to $21B in One Month After Jane Street Validates Hardware

AI chip startup Etched raised $700M at a $21B valuation led by Jane Street — doubling from $10.3B just one month prior. Jane Street installed Etched's first "frontier inference cluster" and was so impressed it led the round. Etched created a low-voltage prefill chip and "cluster-scale memory" interconnect that promises higher speeds and lower costs for inference workloads. Other investors include Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia, a16z, Peter Thiel, Tiger Global, and Blackstone. INDUSTRY CURSOR SPACEX
