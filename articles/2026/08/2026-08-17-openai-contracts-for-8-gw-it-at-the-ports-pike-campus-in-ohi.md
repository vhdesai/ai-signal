# OpenAI contracts for ~8 GW-IT at the PORTS-Pike campus in Ohio

OpenAI announced an agreement to secure approximately 8 gigawatts-IT at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus, working with SB Energy, Nvidia, and the U.S. Department of Energy on remediated land at the former Portsmouth Gaseous Diffusion Plant. SB Energy will build, own, and operate the site under a 20-year lease; the first 800 megawatts are expected in 2028, with further capacity requiring new generation and transmission. OpenAI committed $40M to a community fund and up to $84M in Codex credits for roughly 844,000 Ohio students, and states the project will pay its own grid-upgrade costs. Nvidia and OpenAI plan to jointly publish a technical white paper on resilient cluster-scale data center design. https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project/
