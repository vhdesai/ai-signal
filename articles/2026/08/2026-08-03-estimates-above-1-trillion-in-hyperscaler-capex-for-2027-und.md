# Estimates above $1 trillion in hyperscaler capex for 2027 underscore that AI scaling is becoming one of the largest…

Estimates above $1 trillion in hyperscaler capex for 2027 underscore that AI scaling is becoming one of the largest capital deployment waves in tech history. - The implication is that future winners will be judged not just on technical momentum but on cash conversion, utilization rates, and return on massive fixed investment. - Markets are already differentiating among the major platforms based on perceived resilience of free cash flow and the credibility of AI monetization paths. - This raises the stakes for management teams to articulate when today’s infrastructure build will translate into durable high-margin revenue. - For boards, AI is now a balance-sheet strategy as much as a product strategy.
