# Monday Funding Cycle Skews Toward Security AI and Compute Substrate

Monday's verified funding rounds were low in volume but thematically coherent, concentrated in cybersecurity AI (Apate.AI), quantum-computing infrastructure (S-Transistors), and clean-air technology. The pattern is consistent with a market rotating away from generic application-layer AI toward defensive tooling and the physical substrate beneath model training.
