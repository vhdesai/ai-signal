# Meta Ordered to Pay Additional $567M in New Mexico Child Safety Case, Total Now $942M

A New Mexico court ordered Meta to pay an additional $567M in damages related to its ongoing child safety litigation, bringing the total penalty in the case to $942M. The case concerns Meta's alleged failure to adequately protect minors on Instagram and Facebook.
