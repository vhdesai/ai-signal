# Google DeepMind Pilots the First Double-Blind Evaluation of a Frontier Model

DeepMind ran Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite inside a cryptographic enclave (Google Cloud Confidential Space, Intel TDX, NVIDIA H100 Confidential GPU) so evaluators never saw model weights and Google never saw the test prompts. Partners included the Singapore AI Safety Institute, OpenMined, AVERI, and MLCommons, using reserved AILuminate safety prompts. The stated aim is to defeat benchmark contamination and make third-party audits tamper-resistant.
