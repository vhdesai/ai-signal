# MarketWatch: AI Productivity Payoff Shifting Investor Focus from Infrastructure to Beneficiaries

MarketWatch identifies 20 stocks positioned to capture gains as AI adoption moves from infrastructure buildout to productivity realization. The analysis argues the market is at an inflection point: investors who've focused on AI infrastructure (Nvidia, hyperscalers) should now look at companies whose operations will be most transformed by AI-driven productivity improvements — a shift from supply-side to demand-side beneficiaries. 🔗
