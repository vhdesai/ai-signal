# Red Hat Launches Asago Community to Automate AI Governance From Policy to Runtime

Red Hat announced Asago Community, an open source project that maps external governance requirements — including EU AI Act obligations — directly onto live runtime controls for AI agents. Founding collaborators include Alquimia AI, Brave Software, the EvalEval coalition, IBM Research and academic partners. The project targets the gap most enterprises currently bridge manually: translating written policy into enforceable controls that operate at inference time rather than at review time. ________________________________ PRODUCTS
