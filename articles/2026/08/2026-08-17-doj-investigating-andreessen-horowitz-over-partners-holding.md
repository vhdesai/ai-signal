# DOJ investigating Andreessen Horowitz over partners holding seats at competing AI/data companies

The Justice Department has spent nearly a year examining whether a16z partners violated Section 8 of the Clayton Act by sitting simultaneously on the boards of competing companies, with Ben Horowitz's Databricks seat and Martin Casado's Fivetran seat reported as central. This is the first known interlocking-directorate action aimed at a venture firm, a practice historically scrutinized in private equity.
