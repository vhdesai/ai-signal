# Meta reports its Muse Spark models won gold across five STEM olympiads using no tools

Meta said its internally trained Muse Spark models achieved gold-medal-level results across five STEM olympiads, including a perfect theoretical score at the International Physics Olympiad and strong marks at the IMO and International Chemistry Olympiad — reportedly via multi-agent parallel reasoning with no external tools, search, or code execution. If independently verified, the results suggest pure LLM reasoning can reach elite technical performance without symbolic scaffolding. The figures are company-reported and await third-party confirmation.
