# Tesla Readies August Launch of Cybercab — Its First Robotaxi Without Steering Wheel

Tesla has told staff it is preparing for a public launch of the Cybercab, its first purpose-built vehicle without a steering wheel or brake pedal, designed for its autonomous ride-hailing service. The company targets Austin, Texas for the initial public rollout as soon as this month, with test driving, employee rides on private roads, and first-responder training already underway. The Robotaxi service is central to Tesla's future revenue and AI strategy.
