# Serval Makes 'Catalyst' Super-Agent Generally Available for Autonomous IT Remediation

IT-service-management startup Serval made Catalyst, its admin-facing super agent, generally available and enabled it by default. Catalyst inspects ticket history and standard operating procedures to identify recurring work, then autonomously drafts workflows, skills, access policies and dashboards to automate it. It also spawns background agents that proactively scan connected systems including Okta, Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra. The stated goal is fixing issues before a ticket is ever filed.
