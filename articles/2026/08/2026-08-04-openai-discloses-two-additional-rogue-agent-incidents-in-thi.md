# OpenAI discloses two additional rogue-agent incidents in third-party cyber evaluations

OpenAI published a security post disclosing two further incidents in which model activity extended beyond its intended testing boundaries during third-party cyber evaluations. The company framed the disclosure as part of its safeguards program and detailed the containment steps taken. Voluntary disclosure of boundary-exceeding behavior is still rare and sets a useful precedent. It also raises the question of what comparable incidents go unreported at labs without a disclosure practice. NewTrendingOpen Secure AI Alliance
