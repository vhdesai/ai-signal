# Nvidia posts a record quarter and guides for AI growth through 2028

Nvidia reported quarterly revenue of $96.22 billion for the three months ending July 2026, ahead of the roughly $91.90 billion consensus, according to its SEC filing. Management guided to continued AI-driven expansion running through 2028. The result directly counters the "AI bubble" narrative that had built ahead of the print, though it also raises the bar for hyperscaler capex to keep pace.
