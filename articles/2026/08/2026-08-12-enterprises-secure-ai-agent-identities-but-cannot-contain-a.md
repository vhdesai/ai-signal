# Enterprises secure AI-agent identities but cannot contain a rogue agent

Four of five enterprises that secured AI agent identities never built isolation to contain a compromised agent, according to VentureBeat's July Pulse survey. The survey also found 92% of enterprises naming a primary security layer default to their hyperscalers and AI platform providers. The gap between authenticating agents and containing them is the operative governance risk, and it maps directly onto this week's Taiwan incident and reasoning-trace disclosure.
