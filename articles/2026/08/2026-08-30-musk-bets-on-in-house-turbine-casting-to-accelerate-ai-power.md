# Musk Bets on In-House Turbine Casting to Accelerate AI Power — With an Emissions Cost

Elon Musk says a new SpaceX foundry will cast gas turbine blades in-house and bring generation online roughly 18 months faster than competitors, addressing the power bottleneck constraining large AI training clusters. The strategy doubles down on a fuel source already drawing regulatory and community opposition — the NAACP alleges xAI has run Colossus turbines in Memphis without permits. Vertical integration into power hardware is a pattern worth watching across hyperscalers.
