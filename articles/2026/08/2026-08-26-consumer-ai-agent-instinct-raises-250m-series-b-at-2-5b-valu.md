# Consumer AI Agent Instinct Raises $250M Series B at $2.5B Valuation

Instinct, led by 23-year-old founder Noah Shinn, closed a $250M Series B co-led by Index Ventures and Benchmark at a $2.5B valuation. The product is a personal agent users connect to apps and devices, interacting by text and phone call. The company is barely a year old and still in private beta. Notably drawing scrutiny over the breadth of permissions it requests and terms-of-use language that privacy observers have flagged — a live example of the consent surface enterprise buyers should expect to have to govern.
