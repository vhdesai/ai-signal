# NVIDIA releases Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34B open vision-language-action model for robotaxis

NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34-billion-parameter vision-language-action model for autonomous driving under the permissive OpenMDW-1.1 commercial license. The architecture pairs a 32B reasoning backbone with a diffusion action decoder and scores 79.2 on LingoQA. Opening a commercially licensed VLA model materially lowers the barrier for robotaxi and ADAS developers. Note: NVIDIA's underlying announcement was August 4; the in-window item is the August 5 technical analysis.
