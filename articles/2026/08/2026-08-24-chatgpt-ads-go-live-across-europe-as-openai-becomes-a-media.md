# ChatGPT Ads go live across Europe as OpenAI becomes a media businessNew

OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT Ads to 31 European countries including Germany, France, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands, with advertiser access initially routed through its Ads Solutions team and agency partners. A self-service Ads Manager is expected later. OpenAI states that ads are labeled and do not influence model responses. The move formalizes a third revenue line beyond subscriptions and API.
