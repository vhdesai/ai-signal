# MIT Study: AI-Generated Images Often Cannot Be Traced to Any Training Example

MIT CSAIL researchers developed a method for surgically removing training examples from a model and found that at large dataset scales, removing any single image, every image by a given artist, or every photograph of a given person frequently leaves generated outputs essentially unchanged. The finding has direct bearing on copyright litigation and on data-provenance claims in enterprise contracts — attribution-based remedies may be technically unenforceable at scale.
