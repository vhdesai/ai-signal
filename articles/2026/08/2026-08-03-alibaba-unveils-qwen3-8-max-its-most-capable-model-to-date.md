# Alibaba unveils Qwen3.8-Max, its most capable model to date

Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max on Monday, calling it its “most powerful” model yet at roughly 2.4 trillion parameters — placing it just behind Moonshot’s 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 in China’s escalating model-size race. The company framed the release as a direct challenge to U.S. frontier labs, and its shares rallied on the news. For executives, the launch underscores how quickly Chinese developers are closing the scale and capability gap with Western models. Industry News M&A
