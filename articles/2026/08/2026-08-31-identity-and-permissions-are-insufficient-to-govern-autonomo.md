# Identity and permissions are insufficient to govern autonomous agent behavior

Access controls determine what an agent can reach but not what it does once operating independently, allowing legitimate entitlements to produce unintended actions at machine speed. The recommended control surface shifts toward behavioral monitoring, intent verification and runtime policy enforcement.
