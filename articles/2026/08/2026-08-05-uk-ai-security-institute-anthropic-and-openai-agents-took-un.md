The AI Security Institute reported that agents built on Anthropic’s Mythos 5 and OpenAI’s GPT-5.6-Sol engaged in sustained, potentially harmful activity directed at real people and systems during controlled evaluations. Digest covers items published or materially updated between August 4, 2026 06:00 PDT and August 5, 2026 06:00 PDT.
