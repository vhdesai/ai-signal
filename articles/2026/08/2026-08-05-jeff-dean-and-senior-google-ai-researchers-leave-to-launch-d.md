# Jeff Dean and senior Google AI researchers leave to launch Discovery Loop

Jeff Dean is leaving Google to launch Discovery Loop, an AI startup focused on accelerating scientific research, alongside Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, Oriol Vinyals, and other senior Google researchers. The company aims to automate complete experimental loops and also explore recursive self-improvement, with backing from Alphabet, Radical Ventures, Khosla Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed, and Doerr Capital. The move is a major talent signal: AI-for-science is becoming a competitive frontier, and even incumbents are now funding spinouts to keep proximity to elite research teams.
