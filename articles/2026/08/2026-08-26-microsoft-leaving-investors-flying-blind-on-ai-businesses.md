# Microsoft Leaving Investors "Flying Blind" on AI Businesses

WSJ reports Microsoft lacks adequate transparency into AI business performance, leaving investors unable to assess whether Copilot and Azure AI investments are generating meaningful returns. The opacity makes it difficult to separate AI revenue from traditional cloud growth—a gap that could pressure management toward clearer segment disclosure.
