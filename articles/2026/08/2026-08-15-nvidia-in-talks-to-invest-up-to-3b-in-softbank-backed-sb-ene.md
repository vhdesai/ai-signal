# Nvidia in talks to invest up to $3B in SoftBank-backed SB Energy

Nvidia is discussing an investment of as much as $3B in SB Energy, the data-center power developer building the Ohio megasite OpenAI has committed to lease, according to a report by The Information. The talks are tied to credit support for the campus, and SB Energy is preparing an IPO. The move continues Nvidia's expansion from silicon into the electricity supply that now gates GPU deployment. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/nvidia-mulls-3b-investment-in-sb-energy-in-openai-data-center-deal-report.html
