# MIT Framework Pushes Protein Design Beyond Naturally Occurring Sequences

MIT researchers introduced a machine-learning framework intended to raise the success rate of computational protein design while steering models away from simply reproducing sequences found in nature. Models trained on natural proteins tend to regress toward them, limiting novelty. The work targets a practical failure mode in AI-driven biological design — plausible-looking outputs that are functionally derivative.
