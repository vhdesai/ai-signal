# Investors Battle for Anthropic Shares on Secondary Markets Ahead of IPO

Buying Anthropic shares on secondary markets has become extraordinarily competitive, with demand far outstripping supply. The frenzy comes as Anthropic’s backers have signaled expectations of a roughly $2 trillion IPO as soon as this fall. The dynamics underscore investor conviction in Anthropic’s position as one of two dominant frontier AI labs.
