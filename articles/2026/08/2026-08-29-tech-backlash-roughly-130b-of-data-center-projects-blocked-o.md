# Tech backlash: roughly $130B of data-center projects blocked or delayed by local opposition

CNBC reports that about $130 billion of US data-center projects were delayed or cancelled in the first quarter of 2026 alone due to community opposition — approaching the $156 billion disrupted across all of 2025. Public sentiment data cited in the piece shows majority concern about AI, and the issue is becoming a bipartisan midterm campaign theme. Siting, permitting, and community consent are now first-order schedule risks for any AI capacity plan. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/tech-backlash-ai-data-centers-elections.html EXCLUSIVE
