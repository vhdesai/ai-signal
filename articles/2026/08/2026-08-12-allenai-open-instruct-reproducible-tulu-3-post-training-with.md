# AllenAI Open Instruct: reproducible Tulu 3 post-training with SFT, DPO, and RLVR/GRPO

A detailed technical walkthrough builds an end-to-end post-training pipeline for a compact instruction-tuned model using AllenAI's Open Instruct framework, moving through supervised fine-tuning, Direct Preference Optimization, and Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards, plus verifier-based evaluation. It operationalizes AllenAI's open Tulu 3 recipe into something practitioners can reproduce. For teams building domain-specific models, it lowers the barrier to serious post-training without frontier-lab budgets.
