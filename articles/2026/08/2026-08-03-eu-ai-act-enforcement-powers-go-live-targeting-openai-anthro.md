# EU AI Act enforcement powers go live, targeting OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google

The European Commission gained binding enforcement powers over general-purpose AI providers on Sunday, and can now demand model evaluations, restrict EU market access, and levy fines. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are among the first firms in scope. The change moves the EU AI Act from framework to active oversight, raising compliance stakes for any provider serving the European market. POLICY x
