# Anthropic Turns On Invisible Watermarking for Claude Text Output

Anthropic has begun watermarking text generated by Claude, using a statistically embedded signal invisible to readers but detectable by tooling. It is the first large-scale text watermarking deployment by a frontier lab, and its durability under paraphrasing and translation is still unproven. Enterprises should treat this as a near-term compliance input: watermark detection will migrate quickly into procurement, academic integrity, and content-provenance policy. https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/claudes-new-scarlet-letter-watermark-is-invisible-for-now/
