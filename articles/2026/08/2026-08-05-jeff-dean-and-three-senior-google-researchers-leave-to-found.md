# Jeff Dean and Three Senior Google Researchers Leave to Found Discovery Loop

Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le are leaving Google to launch Discovery Loop, a public-benefit company aiming to automate complete experimental cycles — agents that propose hypotheses, run thousands of experiments, analyze results, and iterate. The founders intend to start with machine-learning research and engineering, using themselves as the first customer, before targeting broader science problems. Google is reported to retain a stake and provide first-year compute capacity, with Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures leading the seed. Dean's departure ends a 27-year tenure spanning MapReduce, BigTable, TensorFlow, and TPUs. ________________________________ FUNDING
