# Lovable Confirms $400M Series C at $13.3B Valuation

European vibe-coding startup Lovable confirmed a $400M Series C led by Menlo Ventures, valuing the company at $13.3B — doubling its December 2025 valuation. The raise follows Lovable hitting $500M in annualized revenue in June, with 60 million hosted projects attracting 900 million monthly visitors.
