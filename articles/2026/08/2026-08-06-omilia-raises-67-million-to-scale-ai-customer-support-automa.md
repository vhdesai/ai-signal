# Omilia raises $67 million to scale AI customer-support automation

Omilia raised a $67 million Series B to expand its customer-support automation platform, which combines generative AI with narrower task-specific tools for voice, chat, and messaging. The company argues that many contact-center interactions do not require large language models and that strong unit economics will separate durable vendors from hype-driven ones. The funding underscores that customer service remains one of the clearest enterprise AI markets, but ROI discipline is becoming central.
