# Apple's rebuilt Siri is now useful, but TechCrunch argues the market has moved on

TechCrunch reviews Apple's iOS 27 beta Siri experience and argues that Apple has largely delivered the AI assistant it promised, including better personal context, more natural conversation, app integration, and camera-based scene understanding. The analysis is more strategic than technical: by the time Siri works well, capable AI assistants have become table stakes. Apple's differentiation now rests on privacy, device integration, and distribution across its installed base. DATA PLATFORMDATABRICKSOPEN STANDARD
