# British AI Chip Founder Becomes Europe's Youngest Self-Made Billionaire at 25

James Dacombe, 25, became Europe's youngest self-made billionaire after his photonic AI chip startup Olix tripled its valuation to $3.3B in six months, raising $312M from Arm, Hudson River Trading, Reed Hastings, and the UK Sovereign AI fund. Olix is developing specialized inference chips that use light instead of electrical signals and deliberately avoid expensive HBM hardware. The company plans customer shipments by H2 2027. Dacombe holds ~30% of Olix and a 12% stake in his earlier startup CoMind. 🔗 https://www.forbes.com/sites/aliciapark/2026/08/15/british-ai-chip-founder-becomes-europes-youngest-self-made-billionaire/
