# Business Insider maps how major Wall Street banks are using AI

Business Insider published a roundup of how the largest Wall Street banks are using AI across their businesses. The item is directionally important because banks are moving beyond experimentation into concrete use cases spanning productivity, research, coding, operations, client service, and risk workflows. The takeaway for senior leaders is that regulated industries are not waiting for perfect certainty; they are building controlled AI adoption paths around high-value internal workflows.
