# NVIDIA releases Alpamayo 2 Super for commercial autonomous-driving use

NVIDIA made Alpamayo 2 Super commercially available as an open reasoning model for robotaxis and autonomous vehicles. The model is built on NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Super Reasoner, post-trained with reinforcement learning, and released under the OpenMDW-1.1 license to support fine-tuning, derivative models, and commercial redistribution. The release matters because AV developers need inspectable models for rare long-tail driving scenarios where reasoning, validation, and policy control are as important as perception.
