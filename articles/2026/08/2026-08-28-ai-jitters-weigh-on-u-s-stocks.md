# AI jitters weigh on U.S. stocks

The Wall Street Journal reported that U.S. stocks were mixed amid artificial-intelligence jitters. The market signal is that AI enthusiasm is increasingly being balanced against questions about capex intensity, debt financing, monetization timelines, and supplier concentration. Senior leaders should expect investor scrutiny of AI spending plans to keep rising as AI infrastructure commitments become more visible. URL behind paywall.
