# Google Tightens Control Over DeepMind as Gemini Faces Competitive Pressure

A leadership reorganization at Google DeepMind, reportedly accompanied by pressure from co-founder Sergey Brin, reflects Alphabet's push to close the gap between Gemini and rival frontier models. The restructuring pulls research and product decision-making closer to Alphabet's core organization.
