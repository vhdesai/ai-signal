# 1. Alibaba moves Qwen3.8-Max to general availability

Alibaba’s flagship 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model moved from preview to GA on Alibaba Cloud Model Studio and QwenWork, described as its most capable system to date. It offers a 1M-token context window, native vision and video, and aggressive pricing of roughly $2 / $6 per million input/output tokens, with open weights expected next week. The GA release, corroborated by MarkTechPost, sharpens the Chinese frontier push into enterprise workloads. HotDeepSeek
