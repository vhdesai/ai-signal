# OpenAI publicly disputes Apple's lawsuit claims

OpenAI published a direct response titled "Apple is getting this wrong," disputing what it calls a baseless Apple lawsuit and releasing internal messages to support its account. The post is unusually direct for OpenAI, naming Apple and contesting specific factual claims in public. For executives, this is a material signal that legal disputes around AI talent, IP, and confidential information are moving from private negotiation into public positioning.
