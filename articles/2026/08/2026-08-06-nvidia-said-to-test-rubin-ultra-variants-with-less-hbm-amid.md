# Nvidia said to test Rubin Ultra variants with less HBM amid tight memory supply

The Information reports Nvidia is testing Rubin Ultra configurations with less high-bandwidth memory than originally planned, as HBM supply remains constrained. Separately, Nvidia open-sourced storage/direct-I/O tooling to reduce CPU mediation and ease inference memory bottlenecks. Together the signals point to memory — not raw compute — as the binding constraint on next-generation accelerator economics. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCHBENCHMARK
