# California agencies expand access to Anthropic Claude, according to implementation partner

AiThority republished a Capitol Tech Solutions announcement stating that California has expanded access to Anthropic's Claude AI platform across state government and that CTS is positioning itself as an implementation partner. The article is vendor-originated, but the underlying signal is material if accurate: a large U.S. state is moving Claude into broader public-sector workflows. Public-sector adoption remains a strategically important battleground for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft.
