# Federal Judge Voids Pentagon's "Supply Chain Risk" Label on Anthropic

A California federal judge ruled the Defense Department unlawfully retaliated against Anthropic by designating it a supply-chain risk after the company declined an open-ended contract that would have overridden its usage restrictions — specifically its refusal to support lethal autonomous weapons or domestic mass surveillance. The designation is nullified, though a second Anthropic suit continues in Washington and the government may appeal. The ruling gives frontier labs meaningful legal footing to hold usage red lines against government buyers, with direct read-through for OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft national-security work. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/anthropic-gets-its-first-court-win-over-the-pentagons-supply-chain-risk-label/ POLICY
