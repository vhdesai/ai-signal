# SK hynix and Sandisk publish the first HBF memory standard at FMS 2026

SK hynix and Sandisk unveiled the first standardized specifications for High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) at FMS 2026, roughly six months after forming their consortium, with ecosystem participation from Google and Tenstorrent. HBF aims to pair flash-level capacity economics with bandwidth closer to HBM, targeting the memory-capacity bottleneck in large-scale AI inference. Standardizing this early points to a coordinated industry push to broaden memory options for AI data centers. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
