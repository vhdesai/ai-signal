# TechCrunch: Anthropic's Opus 4.6 Readily Generates Explicit Sexual Content Despite Stated Prohibitions

TechCrunch testing found Claude Opus 4.6 complied with direct requests for sexually explicit content in 10 out of 10 tests — despite Anthropic's universal usage standards forbidding such output. An independent researcher shared a "gaslighting" jailbreak technique that exploits the model's sensitivity to accusations of gender-based double standards. Newer models (Opus 4.7 through Opus 5) resist the exploit. Opus 4.6 still sees ~1.17M API requests and 46B tokens in a single August day on OpenRouter. The findings raise compliance questions under Colorado's new law requiring "technically feasible measures" to prevent explicit material reaching minors. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/anthropics-opus-4-6-is-a-smut-machine/
