# OpenAI-Backed Thrive Holdings Raises $2B to Push AI Into Regulated Workflows

Thrive Holdings raised $2 billion at a $12B valuation from SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The thesis is embedding AI directly into accounting, IT, and other regulated back-office operations rather than selling horizontal tooling. This roll-up model — buying the workflow, then automating it — is the clearest current template for capturing AI margin in services-heavy industries.
