# OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings raises $2B to push AI into regulated workflows

Thrive Holdings raised $2B at a $12B valuation from SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The thesis is embedding AI directly into accounting, IT, and other regulated back-office operations rather than selling horizontal tooling. This roll-up model — buying the workflow, then automating it — is the clearest current template for capturing AI margin in services-heavy industries. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/openai-backed-thrive-holdings-raises-2b-to-bring-ai-to-the-enterprise/
