# PitchBook Analysts Examine What Nvidia's $500B Financing Play Means for AI Capital Formation

PitchBook has published an analyst deep-dive on Nvidia's $500 billion financing alliance with BlackRock, Apollo, Blackstone, and others, assessing its implications for AI capital formation. The analysis examines how the initiative shifts systemic risk from chipmakers to financial institutions and whether the new lending structures — including GPU-backed securitization — represent a sustainable funding model or an emerging credit bubble for AI infrastructure.
