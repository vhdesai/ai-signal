# Inherent, founded by DeepMind alumni, says its AI 'teammate' outperformed Anthropic and OpenAI at replicating research

British AI lab Inherent, founded by Google DeepMind alumni, released Faraday, an agent positioned as a research "teammate" that the company says beat Anthropic's and OpenAI's systems at replicating scientific papers. Inherent frames paper replication as a stepping stone toward autonomous scientific discovery. Benchmark results are company-reported and independent verification is not established in the coverage; treat the claim as directional rather than settled. Included as a 24-48 hour item.
