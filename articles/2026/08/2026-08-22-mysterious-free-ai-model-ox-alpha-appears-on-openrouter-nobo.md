# Mysterious Free AI Model 'Ox Alpha' Appears on OpenRouter — Nobody Knows Who Made It

A stealth reasoning model called Ox Alpha appeared on OpenRouter — free, with 100T tokens/day capacity (~100x Visa’s monthly consumption). Stripe CEO Patrick Collison called it “very impressive.” Early speculation points to Chinese lab Z.ai (which previously tested GLM-5 anonymously as “Pony Alpha”), though competing analysis suggests Microsoft’s MAI family.
