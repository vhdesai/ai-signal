# AI systems begin producing solutions to long-standing open mathematics problems

Fields Medalist James Maynard described a discipline adjusting rapidly after OpenAI disclosed solutions to ten long-standing mathematics problems, some open for decades. The significance is less the individual proofs than the shift in where novel formal reasoning originates and how it gets verified. Mathematics is the cleanest available proving ground for machine-generated novelty because results are checkable — making it the leading indicator for AI contribution in other formal domains.
