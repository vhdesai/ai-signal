# Pokee AI Launches Isaac 28B — 10 Million Token Context, Single-GPU Serving

Pokee AI released Isaac 28B, a 28B-parameter model with a verified 10M-token context window scoring 93.3% on RULER at full length — where every evaluated baseline returns 0.0 beyond 2M tokens. Targets regulated industries that cannot send data to external APIs. Prefill throughput reaches 137,200 tokens/s at 10M context on one B200 GPU. Priced at $0.15/$1.00 per million input/output tokens. Weights are licensed (not open), deployed into VPC/on-prem. ________________________________ 🛠️ Products & Tools OPEN-SOURCE NVIDIA
