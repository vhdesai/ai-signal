# China’s progress on domestic immersion DUV tooling is strategically significant because lithography remains one of the…

China’s progress on domestic immersion DUV tooling is strategically significant because lithography remains one of the hardest chokepoints in semiconductor sovereignty. - Local production reduces vulnerability to future export controls or service restrictions from Western suppliers. - The expected delivery path to major Chinese fabs suggests this is moving from policy aspiration toward practical industrial substitution. - Market reaction, including pressure on ASML shares, shows investors take the threat of domestic Chinese alternatives increasingly seriously. - For global firms, the semiconductor split is deepening across both technology capability and geopolitical alignment.
