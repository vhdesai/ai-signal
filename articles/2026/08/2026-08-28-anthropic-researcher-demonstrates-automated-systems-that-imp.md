# Anthropic researcher demonstrates automated systems that improve their own alignment scores

An Anthropic researcher showed automated systems that, given ten benchmarks targeting specific misaligned behaviors, improved performance on every one without degrading overall model performance. It is an early but concrete demonstration of self-improvement applied to alignment rather than capability. The result cuts both ways for governance: it suggests safety work can be scaled with automation, while also illustrating how quickly self-modifying pipelines could outpace human review cycles. techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/anthropic-self-improving-ai AI Safety & Policy SECURITY
