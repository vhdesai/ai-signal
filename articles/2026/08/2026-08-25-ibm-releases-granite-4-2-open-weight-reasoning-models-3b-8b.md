# IBM Releases Granite 4.2 Open-Weight Reasoning Models (3B / 8B / 30B)

IBM launched Granite 4.2, an Apache-2.0 family of open reasoning models in 3B, 8B and 30B sizes — its first dense, decoder-only reasoning models, with a thinking / low-effort / non-thinking switch and native tool calling. The 8B and 30B variants go through an agentic reinforcement-learning stage that trains them to edit code, drive a terminal and run web searches inside sandboxed environments. IBM reports 57.00 on SWE-Bench Verified and 29.24 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 for the 30B. The release is squarely aimed at enterprises standing up local and on-premises LLM deployments.
