# Weekly Capital Flows to Physical Substrate, Not Applications

Crunchbase’s weekly tally: Databricks ($5B), River AI ($1.1B), data center/energy storage companies, defense startups, and AI coding. Capital is flowing to the physical and data substrate rather than application-layer differentiation — consistent with a thesis that compute, power, and proprietary data are the durable moats, not model access.
