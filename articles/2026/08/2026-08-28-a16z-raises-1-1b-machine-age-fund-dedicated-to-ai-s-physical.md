# a16z raises $1.1B "Machine Age Fund" dedicated to AI's physical infrastructure

Andreessen Horowitz announced a $1.1 billion vehicle that will invest exclusively in the hardware layer beneath AI — chips, memory, networking, storage, power and data centers — rather than applications or models. The fund is new capital rather than a carve-out of the firm's flagship. Its thesis is a straightforward read on where the binding constraint now sits: power and silicon supply, not software. finance.yahoo.com — a16z's $1.1B fund EUROPE
