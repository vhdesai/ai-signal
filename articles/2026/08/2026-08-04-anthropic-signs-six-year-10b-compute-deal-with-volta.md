# Anthropic signs six-year, $10B compute deal with Volta

Anthropic signed a roughly $10 billion, six-year compute agreement with Volta Infra, a young Nvidia-backed AI infrastructure provider. The deal shows compute supply remains a strategic constraint and that new AI cloud entrants can win huge contracts if they can deliver capacity quickly.
