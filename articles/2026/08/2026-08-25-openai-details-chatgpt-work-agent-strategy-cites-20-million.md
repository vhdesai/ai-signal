# OpenAI Details ChatGPT Work Agent Strategy, Cites 20 Million Users

OpenAI head of product Thibault Sottiaux discussed the rollout of ChatGPT Work, the company's agent platform for white-collar workflows, citing 20 million users. OpenAI is positioning agents as the default interaction model rather than an add-on to chat. The open questions for buyers remain permissioning, audit trails, and failure containment at scale.
