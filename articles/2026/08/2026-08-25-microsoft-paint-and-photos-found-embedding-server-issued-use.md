# Microsoft Paint and Photos Found Embedding Server-Issued, User-Linked Watermarks in AI Images

Researcher Xusheng Li reverse-engineered a Watermarker.dll component in Windows Paint and Photos showing that both apps embed a server-issued GUID as an invisible pixel watermark in AI-generated images — including images generated locally on Copilot+ PCs — with prompts sent to Microsoft for moderation. Because the identifier is server-issued, it is potentially traceable to the user rather than merely marking content as synthetic. This goes beyond C2PA-style provenance labeling and raises disclosure questions for regulated and confidential workflows. ________________________________ Academic Research RESEARCH
