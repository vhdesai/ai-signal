# Google Research Releases TimesFM-3, a 330M-Parameter Zero-Shot Multivariate Forecasting Foundation Model

Google Research introduced TimesFM-3, the newest generation of its TimesFM time-series foundation model family. The 330M-parameter model produces state-of-the-art multivariate and univariate forecasts in a single forward pass with no task-specific training or fine-tuning. It extends the family from univariate toward native multivariate forecasting, which matters for demand, capacity and financial planning workloads where per-series model training has been the bottleneck.
