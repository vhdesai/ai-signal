# SaferAI report: open-weight models near frontier capability, still trail on safety mitigations

A new SaferAI assessment finds Z.ai's open-weight GLM-5.2 approaching frontier capability levels while lacking several safety mitigations standard among closed frontier labs. The finding sharpens a governance problem regulators have not solved: capability diffusion through open weights is irreversible once released. Enterprises adopting open models for cost or sovereignty reasons should assume they inherit the mitigation gap and budget for their own evaluation layer.
