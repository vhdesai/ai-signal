# OpenAI acquires presentation startup NextSlide

OpenAI disclosed the acquisition of NextSlide, which converts prompts, notes, and documents into editable presentations. The entire team joined OpenAI to build presentation capability directly into ChatGPT. A direct move into productivity-suite territory occupied by incumbent office software.
