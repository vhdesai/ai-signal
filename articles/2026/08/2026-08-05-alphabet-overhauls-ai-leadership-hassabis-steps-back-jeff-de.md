# Alphabet overhauls AI leadership: Hassabis steps back, Jeff Dean departs to found Discovery Loop

Alphabet announced a leadership overhaul of its AI division, with Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis relinquishing his day-to-day managerial role at the lab. Separately, Google chief scientist Jeff Dean is leaving after 27 years to launch Discovery Loop alongside Google veterans Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le, with a stated mission of accelerating scientific discovery with AI. The simultaneous moves concentrate an unusual amount of institutional research capital into a single new venture and raise succession questions at the lab responsible for Gemini. Expect near-term competitive recruiting pressure across frontier labs. M&A
