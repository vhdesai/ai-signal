# Firebird Opens Armenian AI Factory, Targets 2 GW With Intended Nvidia Investment

Firebird opened its first AI factory in Hrazdan, Armenia, and outlined a pipeline reaching 2 gigawatts of capacity by end-2028. The Armenian site is planned to scale beyond 70,000 Nvidia Rubin and Blackwell GPUs and 300 MW by end-2027, built on the Nvidia DSX AI Factory reference design with Spectrum-X Ethernet. A second market in Kazakhstan has 125 MW secured at Data Center Valley, backed by host-government approval and a U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security export authorization. Nvidia stated an intent to invest in the company, and Perplexity is among the first customers. The pattern worth noting is the packaging of U.S. export licensing plus host-state approval as a repeatable market-entry template for frontier economies. SUPPLY CHAIN
