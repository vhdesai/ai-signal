# Business Insider reported that Sony and Warner Chappell accused Anthropic of a "brazen campaign" to train Claude on copyrighted music and sought damages that could reach $150,000 per song. The lawsuit adds another major copyright front to generative AI training-data litigation, with attention on both dataset provenance and how copyrighted content was acquired. The risk signal for enterprises is that model supply chains and training-data governance remain material legal and reputational issues.
