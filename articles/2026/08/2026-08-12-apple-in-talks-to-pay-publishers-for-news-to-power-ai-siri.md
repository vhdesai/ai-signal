# Apple in Talks to Pay Publishers for News to Power AI Siri

Apple has approached major publishers about multiyear licensing deals, reportedly with a nine-figure aggregate budget, to let a revamped AI Siri draw on current news content. The strategy grounds answers in licensed editorial material rather than scraped data, addressing both quality and legal concerns that have plagued competitors. The move sets an important market-price signal for editorial content at a moment when data-rights disputes are intensifying, with active litigation involving the NYT, book publishers, and Getty Images. For publishers, Apple’s willingness to pay establishes a pricing benchmark that strengthens their negotiating position against other AI companies. For Apple, it is a competitive catch-up play — Siri remains behind ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude on generative AI capability.
