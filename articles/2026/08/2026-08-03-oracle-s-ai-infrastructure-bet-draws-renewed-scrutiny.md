# Oracle's AI infrastructure bet draws renewed scrutiny

Yahoo Finance reported that Oracle's large AI infrastructure bet has weighed heavily on Larry Ellison's wealth, citing analyst warnings that the AI bubble may be far larger than the dot-com cycle. The article reflects growing investor concern over whether infrastructure-heavy AI strategies can generate enough durable revenue to justify their capital intensity. Oracle remains central to the AI buildout conversation because its cloud and data-center ambitions increasingly compete with hyperscalers and neoclouds.
