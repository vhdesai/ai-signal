# IREN Delivers Horizon 1 to Microsoft and Achieves NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud Status on GB300 NVL72

IREN delivered its Horizon 1 facility to Microsoft and secured NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud status on GB300 NVL72 systems. The delivery advances Microsoft's strategy of contracting third-party neocloud capacity to add GPU supply without carrying the full build on its own balance sheet. Exemplar certification matters commercially — it is the validation gate that lets a neocloud sell reference-grade capacity at hyperscaler standards. https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/markets-news/Tipranks/3850479/iren-advances-microsoft-ai-cloud-deal-with-horizon-1/
