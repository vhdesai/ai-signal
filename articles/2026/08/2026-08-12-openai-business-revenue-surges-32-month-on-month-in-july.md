# OpenAI Business Revenue Surges 32% Month-on-Month in July

OpenAI’s run-rate revenue from business customers increased 32% month-on-month in July, driven largely by big enterprises, DealBook has learned. Overall run-rate revenue grew 20% month-on-month. OpenAI has been focused on growing its enterprise base to close the gap with Anthropic; both companies are preparing to go public.
