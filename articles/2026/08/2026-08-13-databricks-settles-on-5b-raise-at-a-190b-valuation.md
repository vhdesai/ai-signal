# Databricks settles on $5B raise at a $190B valuation

CEO Ali Ghodsi said he set out to raise $1B, saw roughly $15B of investor interest, and closed $5B at a $190B valuation led by Coatue — up from $188B in July. Databricks cites approximately $7B in annualized run-rate revenue growing 80% year over year, and says it is cash-flow positive. Proceeds are earmarked for AI research and acquisitions.
