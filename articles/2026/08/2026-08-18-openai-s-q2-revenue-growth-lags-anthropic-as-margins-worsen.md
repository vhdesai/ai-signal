# OpenAI's Q2 revenue growth lags Anthropic as margins worsen

OpenAI's Q2 2026 revenue grew about 18% quarter-over-quarter to $6.7B while its operating margin sank further into the red. Anthropic's revenue nearly doubled to $11.6B over the same period with a small operating profit.
