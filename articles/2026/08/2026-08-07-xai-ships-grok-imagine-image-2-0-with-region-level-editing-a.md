# xAI ships Grok Imagine Image 2.0 with region-level editing and a #2 arena ranking

xAI made Grok Imagine Image 2.0 generally available on August 7 as the "Quality Mode" on grok.com/imagine and in its iOS and Android apps. The release centers on editing rather than raw generation: a region-specific "magic wand," segmentation, background removal, multi-image references of up to five inputs, and smart resize across nine aspect ratios. xAI says the model ranks second globally in both the text-to-image and image-editing arenas as of August 7. The models are now listed under the "SpaceXAI" name following corporate changes.
