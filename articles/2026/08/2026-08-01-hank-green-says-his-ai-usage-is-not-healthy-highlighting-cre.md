# Hank Green says his AI usage is “not healthy,” highlighting creator trust risk

YouTuber and science communicator Hank Green apologized after viewers questioned whether a video script showed signs of chatbot-assisted writing. Green said he had used ChatGPT for research and would reduce his output while clarifying his process. The episode is a useful case study in AI trust: audiences may accept assisted workflows, but they increasingly expect disclosure, process clarity, and a clear line between research support and voice substitution.
