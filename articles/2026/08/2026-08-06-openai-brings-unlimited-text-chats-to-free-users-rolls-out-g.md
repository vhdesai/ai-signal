# OpenAI Brings Unlimited Text Chats to Free Users, Rolls Out GPT-5.6 Luna and Sol

OpenAI announced unlimited text-based chats for Free and Go-tier users, powered by a new default model, GPT-5.6 Luna, which replaces GPT-5.5 and includes a "Think" button for deeper reasoning. Plus and Pro users receive GPT-5.6 Sol with a tunable reasoning-depth slider. OpenAI reports 62% fewer factual errors for Luna and 68% fewer for Sol versus GPT-5.5 Instant. The free-tier expansion supports OpenAI's goal of a billion weekly active users and strengthens ChatGPT against Gemini and Copilot at the low end of the market. ________________________________ SAFETYMODEL
