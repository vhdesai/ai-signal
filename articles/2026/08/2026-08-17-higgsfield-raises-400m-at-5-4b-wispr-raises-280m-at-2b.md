# Higgsfield Raises $400M at $5.4B; Wispr Raises $280M at $2B

Two product-layer raises signal continued investor appetite beyond infrastructure. Higgsfield (generative video for professional creators) raised $400M at $5.4B — roughly 4× its January mark — with ARR reported at ~$700M. Wispr (AI dictation expanding to voice-driven interaction) raised $280M at $2B. Voice remains an under-deployed enterprise interface, and raises of this size suggest investors expect it to become a primary agent input modality.
