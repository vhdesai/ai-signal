# Big Tech profits get $160B boost from gains on stakes in other AI companies

Financial Times coverage surfaced a material accounting issue for AI investors: large technology companies' reported profits are being lifted by mark-to-market gains on AI-related equity stakes. That complicates assessment of underlying cloud, advertising, and software operating performance because financial gains from the AI ecosystem can mask core-business trends. For executives, the item is a reminder to separate strategic AI exposure from durable operating leverage when reading sector earnings.
