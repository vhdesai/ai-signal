# AI Assistant Instinct Raises $350M at $2.5B — One Year Old, Still in Private Beta

One-year-old Instinct raised $250M Series B (Index Ventures, Benchmark) at $2.5B valuation — total funding $350M. Founded by 23-year-old Noah Shinn, the AI assistant handles life management (groceries, travel, subscriptions) via text/call. Still in private beta with notable privacy concerns over invasive permissions and sweeping terms of use.
