# Broadcom-backed vehicle seeks up to $70B in debt for AI chip buildout

A Broadcom-backed special-purpose vehicle is in talks with lenders to raise more than $60 billion — potentially approaching $70 billion in senior secured debt plus junior financing — to fund custom AI accelerator infrastructure benefiting Anthropic and other AI firms. The financing shifts capex off corporate balance sheets and into structured credit. AI compute is now being underwritten like energy and telecom infrastructure, with the attendant refinancing risk.
