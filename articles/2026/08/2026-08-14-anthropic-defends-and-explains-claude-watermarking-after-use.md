# Anthropic defends and explains Claude watermarking after user backlash

Business Insider reported that Anthropic defended and explained its AI watermarking feature after users raised concerns and some said they were canceling subscriptions. The controversy follows broader debate over whether AI-generated text should carry machine-detectable provenance, particularly in work and education settings. The executive implication is that transparency obligations are no longer only legal or trust-and-safety matters; they can affect user retention, enterprise adoption, and product positioning.
