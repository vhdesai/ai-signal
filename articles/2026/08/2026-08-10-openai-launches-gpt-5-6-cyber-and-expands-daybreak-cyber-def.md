# OpenAI Launches GPT-5.6-Cyber and Expands Daybreak Cyber Defense Service

OpenAI expanded Daybreak with two tiers: Blue (incident response, malware analysis, patch validation) and Red (purpose-trained security models, vulnerability research). Red includes GPT-5.6-Cyber, built on GPT-5.6 Sol with enhanced cybersecurity capabilities. Currently limited to trusted partners including Accenture, IBM, CrowdStrike, and Cloudflare. The launch comes amid multiplying AI agent hacking incidents.
