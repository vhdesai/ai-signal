# Tsinghua's PACMAN Group Publishes Puro-2B, a Full Pretraining Recipe for Consumer GPUs

Tsinghua University's PACMAN group published Puro-2B, an open end-to-end recipe for pretraining a 2-billion-parameter model on consumer RTX 5090 GPUs. A ~$5,090 run reportedly outperforms Qwen2-1.5B; a ~$6,890 run approaches Qwen2.5-1.5B. Training code, checkpoints, and data are released. The significance is the cost floor: credible small-model pretraining is now within reach of individual research groups and corporate teams without frontier-scale clusters. arXiv →
