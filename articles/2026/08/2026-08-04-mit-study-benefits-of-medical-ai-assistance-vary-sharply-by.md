# MIT Study: Benefits of Medical AI Assistance Vary Sharply by User Expertise

An MIT-led study (Marzyeh Ghassemi, EECS/IMES/Jameel Clinic, with Columbia and Stanford co-authors) tested non-experts and clinicians on AI-assisted skin-disease diagnosis. Non-experts improved accuracy largely through blind deference to LLM explanations — including incorrect ones — while clinicians proved resilient to flawed AI rationales and performed best with prediction-only assistance. Published in Nature Medicine, this quantifies automation-bias risk in clinical and consumer AI-explainability tools. Included just outside the strict 24-hour window given its direct relevance to healthcare AI deployment strategy.
