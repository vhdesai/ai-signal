# Inherent's "Faraday" Agent Outperforms Frontier Models at Reproducing Published Research

London-based Inherent, founded by Google DeepMind alumni, released Faraday, an agent that independently reproduces findings of published scientific papers. Running on Qwen 3.6 (27B open-weight), it reportedly outperformed both Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5. The key: reinforcement learning rewarding "research taste" over rule-following. Reinforces this week's theme that harness/training approach matters more than raw model size. Results are vendor-reported and not yet independently verified.
