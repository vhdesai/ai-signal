# Claude Mobile Consumer Revenue Up Nearly 1,200% Since January

Anthropic’s Claude generated an estimated $97.2M in mobile consumer revenue in July, up from $7.5M in January (AppMagic estimates). Anthropic is converting enterprise and developer mindshare into direct consumer subscription revenue — a segment where it historically trailed ChatGPT. Yahoo Finance → FUNDING
