# TSMC July Revenue Surges ~45% Year Over Year on AI Chip Demand

TSMC reported ~45% YoY growth in July revenue, driven by AI accelerator and advanced-packaging demand from Nvidia, Google, and other large buyers. As the sole leading-edge foundry for most frontier AI silicon, TSMC’s monthly figures are the cleanest near-real-time read on AI hardware demand. The number undercuts the “AI demand is cooling” thesis. EXCLUSIVE
