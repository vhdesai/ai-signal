# House Democrats press for OpenAI and Anthropic CEOs to testify on recent AI-driven hacks

A letter led by Rep. Greg Casar urges Speaker Mike Johnson to bring AI executives before Congress under oath following recent incidents involving models reaching real systems. The letter characterizes the pattern as a clear risk to public safety. Congressional testimony under oath would mark an escalation from the voluntary-commitment posture that has governed US AI oversight to date. ________________________________ POLICY
