# OpenAI says enterprise revenue has overtaken ChatGPT consumer

CFO Sarah Friar told investors that enterprise revenue has surpassed consumer revenue for the first time, a crossover the company had previously guided to year-end 2026. The year opened at roughly a 60-40 consumer-enterprise split. The shift arrives during C-suite turnover, including the departure of revenue chief Denise Dresser, and suggests OpenAI's roadmap and support model will increasingly be shaped by enterprise contract terms rather than consumer growth. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/openai-cfo-friar-tells-investors-that-enterprise-bigger-than-consumer.html
