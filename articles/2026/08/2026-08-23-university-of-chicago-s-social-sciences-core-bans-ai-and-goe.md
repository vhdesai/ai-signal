# University of Chicago’s Social Sciences Core bans AI and goes device-free this fall

UChicago’s Social Sciences Core will require students to read primarily on paper and participate in device-free discussions this fall, prohibit AI-assisted grading unless carefully validated against human grading, and mandate human-authored syllabi. Limited exceptions cover examining datasets, troubleshooting code, and accessing library resources. The policy parallels the Law School’s device ban this academic year and follows a 2025 University AI and Education Working Group report.
