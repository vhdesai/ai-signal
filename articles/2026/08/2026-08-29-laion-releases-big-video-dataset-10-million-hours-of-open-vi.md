# LAION Releases Big Video Dataset: 10 Million Hours of Open Video for AI Research

LAION published one of the largest open video datasets available for research, distilled from 1.3 billion CommonCrawl video URLs down to 80 million downloaded videos (~10 million hours), yielding 55 million captioned clips and 300 million stills. Models trained on BVD outperformed InternVid-trained models by up to 2.1 points on video-to-text benchmarks. The corpus is released for non-commercial research use. The Decoder → | Project page →
