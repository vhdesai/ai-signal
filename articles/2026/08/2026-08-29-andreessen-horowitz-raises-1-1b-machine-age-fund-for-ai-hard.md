# Andreessen Horowitz Raises $1.1B "Machine Age Fund" for AI Hardware and Infrastructure

a16z closed a $1.1 billion vehicle dedicated to AI chips, memory, networking, storage, data centers, and robotics — its first fund focused on physical infrastructure rather than software. General partner Martin Casado attributed the shift to capacity constraints running through the entire hardware supply chain. The allocation signals that a leading software investor now views the hardware bottleneck, not model quality, as the scarce asset worth underwriting. thehindubusinessline.com
