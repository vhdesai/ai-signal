# OpenAI's Only Dedicated Ethicist Departs Less Than a Year After Joining

Chloé Bakalar, OpenAI's head of ethics and previously Meta's chief ethicist, has left the company; a person familiar with the role told the Financial Times she was OpenAI's only dedicated ethicist and that there are no plans to replace her directly. OpenAI said ethical responsibility is distributed across research teams rather than owned by one function. The exit follows departures by safety systems lead Johannes Heidecke and chief futurist Joshua Achiam, and lands the same week OpenAI shipped a deliberately less-restricted offensive-security model — a governance juxtaposition boards should note. https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/08/11/openai-head-of-ethics-chloe-bakalar-departs-after-brief-tenure-486215/ POLICY
