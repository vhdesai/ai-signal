# Google Releases TimesFM-3, a 330M-Parameter Zero-Shot Forecasting Model

TimesFM-3 handles multivariate time-series forecasting in a single forward pass without task-specific training, at a parameter count small enough for routine deployment. Zero-shot forecasting at this size is directly relevant to demand planning, capacity forecasting, and financial modeling workloads. The practical value is operational: fewer models to train, tune, and maintain.
