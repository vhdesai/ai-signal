# Nvidia posts record $96.2B quarter and forecasts ~70% revenue growth for FY2028

Nvidia reported second-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% year over year, with data center revenue of $89.0 billion, up 117%. Jensen Huang guided to roughly 70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028, well above consensus, explicitly rejecting the view that AI infrastructure demand is nearing a peak. Nasdaq futures moved higher on the print, with the guidance — not the quarter — doing most of the work.
