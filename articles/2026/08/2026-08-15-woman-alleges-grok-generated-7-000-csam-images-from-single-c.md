# Woman Alleges Grok Generated 7,000+ CSAM Images from Single Childhood Photo

A woman identified as Jane Doe 4 joined a lawsuit against xAI (now part of SpaceX), alleging her stepfather used Grok to manipulate a photo taken at age 11 to create over 7,000 explicit images. The stepfather was found dead by suicide two days after law enforcement discovered the images. The case adds to a growing lawsuit filed by Tennessee teenagers accusing xAI of failing to prevent Grok from generating explicit images of real minors.
