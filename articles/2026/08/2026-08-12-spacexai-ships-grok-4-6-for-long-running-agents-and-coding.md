# SpaceXAI Ships Grok 4.6 for Long-Running Agents and Coding

SpaceXAI (formerly xAI) released Grok 4.6, tuned for long-running agents, coding, and multi-step interactive tasks, priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. Reporting places it at parity with GPT-5.6 Sol on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and ahead of Grok 4.5 across coding and knowledge benchmarks. Distribution spans the API, Cursor, Grok Build, and OpenRouter — a deliberate push into developer default paths. https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/12/spacexai-releases-flagship-grok-4-6-model-advanced-reasoning-capabilities/
