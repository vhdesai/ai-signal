# Open Secure AI Alliance proposes SAFE incident-sharing guidelines

NVIDIA reported that the Open Secure AI Alliance proposed Shared AI Findings Exchange, or SAFE, a framework for confidentially collecting and sharing agentic-AI cybersecurity incidents. If adopted, SAFE could become the AI equivalent of vulnerability-disclosure practices in traditional security.
