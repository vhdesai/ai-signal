# Google DeepMind Ships Sign-Language Translation to Android Phones

SL2T, a sign-language-to-text model, now ships inside two consumer Android products — the first time sign-language AI has reached a native phone feature rather than a research demo. A proof point for on-device multimodal inference at consumer scale.
