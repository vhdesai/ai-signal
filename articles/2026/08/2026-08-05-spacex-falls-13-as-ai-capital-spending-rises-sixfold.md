# SpaceX Falls 13% as AI Capital Spending Rises Sixfold

SpaceX shares dropped 13% after earnings disclosed AI-related capital spending rising roughly sixfold to $18.4B, compounded by a large upcoming share unlock. Elon Musk pulled forward the company's $1T annual revenue target to 2030 and said SpaceX will standardize exclusively on Nvidia GPUs. The reaction is a useful datapoint on investor tolerance: capex is no longer automatically rewarded absent a visible path to outside customer revenue.
