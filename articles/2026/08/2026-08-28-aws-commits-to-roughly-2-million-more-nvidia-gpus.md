# AWS Commits to Roughly 2 Million More Nvidia GPUs

Amazon and Nvidia expanded their partnership, with AWS committing to approximately two million additional Nvidia GPUs across 2027–2028 for agentic and physical AI workloads. Amazon shares rose about 4% on the capacity signal while Nvidia fell about 4%, reflecting investor concern over custom silicon and circular financing. The deal underscores that capacity commitments, not model launches, are setting the industry’s pace. 24/7 Wall St. →
