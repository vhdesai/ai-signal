# Ukraine Says Nvidia Chip Found Inside a Russian Cruise Missile

Ukrainian officials report recovering an Nvidia chip from a Russian cruise missile, which they say may indicate integration of AI-capable compute into weapons systems. The claim has not been independently verified and Nvidia has not confirmed the chain of custody. If substantiated, it strengthens the case for tighter downstream diversion controls on commercial accelerators — not just export licensing at point of sale. Compliance teams with hardware supply-chain exposure should expect renewed end-use scrutiny from both regulators and customers.
