# CMU and University of Washington appear in early Model Hardware Standard deployments

Academic labs are part of the early MHS ecosystem, with Carnegie Mellon using the standard for dose-response experiments and University of Washington researchers connecting multiple instruments in under a week. The academic relevance is that AI-assisted lab automation is moving from single-instrument demos toward multi-device orchestration. The open question is whether these systems can maintain safety and reproducibility as they move from supervised research previews into routine lab workflows. AI Safety & Policy AI SAFETYCOPYRIGHT
