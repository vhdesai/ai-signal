# DiG-bench: Isolating Scientific-Discovery Ability in LLMs

Researchers released DiG-bench (Discovery in Games), 70 handcrafted interactive text games with undisclosed rules played by humans and frontier models through an identical interface across seven difficulty tiers. The benchmark isolates hypothesis generation: the ability to form and test hypotheses about unknown rules through experimentation, which underlies scientific discovery. Frontier models improved on easier tiers, but the hardest remain largely unsolved, and agentic harnesses with tool use did not outperform a basic non-coding harness. The most revealing finding: supplying hidden rules directly produced near-perfect results, precisely localizing the failure in hypothesis generation, not execution. For AI research leaders, DiG-bench measures the gap between what frontier models can execute and what they can discover. DiG-bench NEW POST-TRAINING
