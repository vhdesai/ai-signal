# Mistral Commits to In-Region Inference and Up to 1 GW of European Compute

Mistral announced in-region inference endpoints, hosting for third-party open models starting with GLM-5.2, and a coalition targeting up to 1 GW of European compute capacity by 2030. The offer includes “European Compute Units” for long-term capacity commitments and regional priority-tier endpoints, giving European enterprises a credible alternative to routing AI workloads through U.S. hyperscaler infrastructure. Notably, the strategy lets Mistral win sovereignty-constrained customers without requiring them to standardize on Mistral’s own models — a platform play that decouples compute sovereignty from model choice. For enterprise buyers operating under GDPR, the EU AI Act, or national security requirements, Mistral’s in-region offering removes a primary objection to frontier AI deployment.
