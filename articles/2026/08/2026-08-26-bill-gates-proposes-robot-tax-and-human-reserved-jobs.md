# Bill Gates Proposes 'Robot Tax' and 'Human Reserved' Jobs

Gates published a Gates Notes essay proposing: (1) a “robot tax” to slow automation replacement, noting the tax system “nudges you toward replacing people with machines”; (2) “Human Reserved” job categories barring AI — some economic (workers who can’t retrain), some ethical (“imagine a robot giving you awful news about an incurable disease”). He endorsed the Pacing the Frontier slowdown letter but doubted its sustainability. Both ideas would significantly dent frontier lab profits.
