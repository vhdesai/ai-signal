# Anthropic reportedly eyeing ~$6B acquisition of Decart

Anthropic is reported to be in discussions to acquire Decart for roughly $6 billion, with efficiency gains cited as the strategic rationale. The report rests on a single source and should be treated as unconfirmed. If it proceeds, it would be Anthropic's most significant acquisition ahead of a listing.
