# SpaceX Completes $60B All-Stock Acquisition of Cursor Maker Anysphere

SpaceX closed its acquisition of Anysphere, developer of the Cursor coding platform, in what is described as the largest startup acquisition on record at roughly $60 billion in stock. Cursor's team moves into SpaceXAI and will work across both Grok and Cursor product lines, following the recent Grok 4.6 release. The deal consolidates a leading AI coding surface, a frontier model team, and a compute-heavy parent under one owner. Enterprises standardizing on Cursor should expect roadmap and data-governance questions as the product folds into the Grok stack — particularly around how SpaceX operational data trains future models.
