# Looking beyond natural sequences: MIT framework for computational protein design

MIT biologists introduced a machine-learning framework designed to raise the success rate of computational protein design. Unlike prevailing methods, the approach deliberately avoids generating sequences that simply mirror those already found in nature, widening the accessible design space for genuinely novel proteins. The work is aimed squarely at the gap between in-silico design and validated laboratory results.
