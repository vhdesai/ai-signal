# Anthropic's Annualized Revenue Tops $65 Billion Ahead of IPO

Anthropic's annualized revenue run-rate reached roughly $65 billion in July, an acceleration rather than a plateau. The growth is concentrated in enterprise API and coding workloads rather than consumer subscriptions. For buyers, the number matters mainly as a durability signal: it reduces near-term vendor-viability risk in multi-year commitments. FUNDINGHOT
