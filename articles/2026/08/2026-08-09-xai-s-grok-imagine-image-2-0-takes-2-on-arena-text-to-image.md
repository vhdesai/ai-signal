# xAI’s Grok Imagine Image 2.0 takes #2 on Arena text-to-image and image-edit boards

xAI’s updated image model claimed the number two spot on both the Arena text-to-image and image-edit leaderboards. The result places xAI credibly in a tier previously dominated by Google and OpenAI image stacks. Note the product shipped August 7; this coverage and the leaderboard placement landed August 9.
