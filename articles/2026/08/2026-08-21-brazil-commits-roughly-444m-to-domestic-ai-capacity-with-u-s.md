# Brazil commits roughly $444M to domestic AI capacity with U.S. and Chinese partners

Brazil announced approximately $444 million in AI investments, including about $250 million for a supercomputing project involving Huawei and iFlytek alongside U.S. participants. The deliberate multi-bloc sourcing illustrates how emerging markets are extracting leverage from U.S.–China competition. Expect more sovereign compute programs that decline to pick a single technology stack. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
