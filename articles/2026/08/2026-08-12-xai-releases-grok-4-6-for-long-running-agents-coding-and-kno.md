# xAI releases Grok 4.6 for long-running agents, coding, and knowledge work

xAI released Grok 4.6, positioning it as a post-training upgrade focused on long-running agents, coding, interactive applications, and knowledge work. The model supports a 500,000-token context window, text and image input with text output, a new xhigh reasoning-effort level, and availability through the xAI API, Grok Build, Cursor, OpenRouter, Vercel, and Cloudflare. The release underscores the market shift from generic chat models toward agentic models tuned for sustained work across codebases and multi-step tasks. ROBOTICSWORLD MODELS
