# Liquid AI open-sources Pipette, a reproducible benchmarking suite for on-device models

Liquid AI released Pipette under Apache 2.0, benchmarking the full deployment configuration — model plus quantization plus runtime plus device — rather than the model alone, with methodology validated by Artificial Analysis. The launch dataset spans 1,000+ configurations across 30+ models and three verified devices. The headline finding for edge deployments: two 350M models at identical quantization on the same phone retained 78.4% versus 33.8% of decode throughput at 4,096 tokens, meaning model-card numbers are a poor proxy for on-device behavior. Note that quality scores are measured on H100 reference systems and matched to device runs, not measured on-device.
