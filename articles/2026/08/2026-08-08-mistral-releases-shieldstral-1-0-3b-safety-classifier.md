# Mistral releases Shieldstral 1.0 3B safety classifier

MarkTechPost reported that Mistral released Shieldstral 1.0 3B, an open-weights, policy-adaptive multimodal safety classifier. The model is positioned as matching much larger systems on safety classification while remaining small enough for practical deployment. Lightweight safety models are strategically important because enterprises need controls that can run close to applications, agents, and content workflows rather than depending only on centralized moderation APIs.
