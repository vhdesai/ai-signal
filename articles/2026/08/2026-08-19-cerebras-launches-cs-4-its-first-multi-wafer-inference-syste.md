# Cerebras Launches CS-4, Its First Multi-Wafer Inference System

Cerebras introduced the CS-4 at SUPERNOVA 2026, packing three wafer-scale engines into a single rack and claiming up to 750 petaflops and roughly 30x GPU throughput on inference workloads. The underlying processor is a higher-clocked WSE-3 rather than new silicon, so independent benchmarking will matter. The strategic point stands: as spend shifts from training to inference, non-GPU architectures have a wider commercial opening than they did two years ago.
