# Anthropic reports AI-derived improvement to a Riemann zeta-function bound

Unreleased Claude model raised bound on proportion of Riemann zeta zeros on critical line from 41.6% to 67.2%. Consumed ~31M output tokens, human-validated and formalized in Lean. Most concrete claim yet of frontier models contributing original results in pure mathematics.
