# India's AM Intelligence places a binding order for 9,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin systems

AM Intelligence, part of the group behind renewable producer Greenko, ordered 9,000 Vera Rubin rack-scale systems for a Hyderabad facility coming online next year, under a broader $8B plan for 1 gigawatt of compute capacity. Founder Mahesh Kolli said initial capacity is already contracted to an unnamed US customer and that the group will sell capacity into India, the US, Finland and Malaysia. His stated advantage is low-cost renewable power — "in global token economics, energy prices play a huge part." The order underlines that multiyear Nvidia backlogs are now pulling capacity buildouts toward cheap-power geographies. SUPPLY CHAIN
