# DOJ Investigating a16z Under 112-Year-Old Antitrust Law Over Board Conflicts

The Department of Justice has reportedly been investigating Andreessen Horowitz for nearly a year under a rarely-used 112-year-old antitrust law. The issue: a16z has partners on the boards of Databricks (Ben Horowitz) and Fivetran (Martin Casado), which have become direct competitors.
