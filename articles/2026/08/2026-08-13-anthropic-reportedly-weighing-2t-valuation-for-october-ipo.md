# Anthropic Reportedly Weighing ~$2T Valuation for October IPO

Anthropic may seek a valuation above $2T in an autumn listing, with backers modeling $100B–$120B in revenue by end-2026 against IDC estimates of $40–50B annualized today. The figure is investor-modeled and unconfirmed by Anthropic — framed against OpenAI’s recent $7B buyback at $852B. WSJ separately reports active investor meetings, fielding questions on Chinese competition, infrastructure spend, and regulatory friction. A successful offering would force public markets to price frontier-lab economics for the first time. Treat as directional signaling on private-market appetite, but the IPO timeline itself appears increasingly firm. Yahoo Finance M&A
