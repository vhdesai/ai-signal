# DeepSeek Revenue Reaches $70 Million Through July — Tenfold Jump From 2025

DeepSeek generated approximately 475 million yuan ($70.7 million) in revenue in the first seven months of 2026, roughly tenfold its full-year 2025 revenue. The Chinese AI lab is going "full steam ahead" on commercialization, with its aggressively priced models (V4-Pro at $0.435/$0.87 per million tokens) continuing to exert pricing pressure on U.S. frontier labs.
