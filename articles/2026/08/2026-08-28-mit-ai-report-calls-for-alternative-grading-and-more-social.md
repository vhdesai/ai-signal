# MIT AI report calls for alternative grading and more social learning

An MIT student, faculty, and staff committee released a report concluding that AI is upending foundational elements of the MIT educational experience. It recommends against grade-rationing caps, urges exploration of competency- and mastery-based grading, and warns against reliance on unreliable AI-detection tools. The committee favors department-level policy “menus” and more in-person social learning over a single institute-wide AI policy.
