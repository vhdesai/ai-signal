# Ex-a16z Bio Lead Vijay Pande on Why AI-Native VC Requires a Different Playbook

Vijay Pande — who left a16z's ~$4B biotech practice to start AI-native firm VZVC — argues biology is finally shifting from "discovery" to "engineering" science, but success requires open shared datasets rather than walled-off data moats. He discussed why clinical trials remain "brutally expensive" despite AI, and why his smaller, more focused fund model ("not doing 30 bets a year") reflects the maturing AI investment landscape where depth matters more than breadth. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/were-not-doing-30-bets-a-year-vijay-pande-on-betting-small-after-running-4-billion-at-a16z/
