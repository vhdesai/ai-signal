# Stripe Confirms $7.5 Billion OpenRouter Acquisition — Gains Leverage Over Frontier Labs

Stripe officially confirmed the $7.5B deal (founders received $1.5B, investors $6B). PitchBook's analyst called it "Stripe's deliberate attempt to embed itself into the middle of capital flows in the AI era," gaining leverage over frontier lab suppliers. OpenRouter will continue operating independently. The deal fits a growing market for token expense management alongside Databricks, Rippling, and Ramp.
