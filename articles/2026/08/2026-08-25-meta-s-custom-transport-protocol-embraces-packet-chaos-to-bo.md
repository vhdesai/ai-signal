# Meta's custom transport protocol embraces packet chaos to boost AI throughput

Meta detailed MetaRoCE, a clean-sheet RDMA transport for AI-scale Ethernet that tolerates lossy fabrics instead of requiring Priority Flow Control, moving multipathing, loss recovery, and congestion control into NIC endpoints. Reported behavior holds roughly 86% of throughput at 1% packet loss, the failure regime that stalls conventional RoCE. As clusters approach million-GPU scale, network transport design is becoming as decisive as accelerator choice for effective utilization.
