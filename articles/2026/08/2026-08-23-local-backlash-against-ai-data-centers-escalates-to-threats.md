# Local Backlash Against AI Data Centers Escalates to Threats and Building Restrictions

Local officials who supported AI data center projects report death threats and, in at least one case, gunfire, with more than 500 towns now imposing restrictions on new builds and some councils closing public comment. Axios separately reports that data centers — alongside Flock camera deployments — are emerging as a live issue in the 2026 midterms. Siting risk, not just power and water availability, is becoming a material schedule risk for capacity plans.
