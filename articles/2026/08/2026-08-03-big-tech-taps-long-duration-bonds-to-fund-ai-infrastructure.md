# Big Tech taps long-duration bonds to fund AI infrastructure

The Wall Street Journal reported that large technology companies are borrowing for decades to finance AI infrastructure buildouts. The move reflects the increasingly industrial character of AI capex, where data centers, power contracts, and server fleets have long asset lives.
