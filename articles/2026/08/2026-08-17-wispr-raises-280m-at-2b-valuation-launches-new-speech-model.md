# Wispr Raises $280M at $2B Valuation, Launches New Speech Model to Fix Quality Issues

AI dictation startup Wispr raised $280M Series B led by Menlo Ventures at a $2B valuation, bringing total funding to $361M. The raise accompanies a new model called Canto to address user complaints about quality degradation, targeting error rates below 10% (down from 30%). Wispr is expanding into meeting notetaking and exploring new human-computer interfaces through its Wispr Interface Labs, led by former Amazon Alexa architect Ariya Rastrow. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/wispr-raises-280m-at-2b-valuation-as-it-looks-beyond-dictation/
