# Nvidia in talks to put up to $3B into SoftBank-backed SB Energy

Nvidia is discussing an investment of as much as $3 billion in SB Energy, the SoftBank-backed power developer building the Ohio megasite OpenAI has committed to lease. The proposed investment is part of broader talks with OpenAI and SB Energy on credit support for the campus; SB Energy has already raised roughly $1.8B and is preparing an IPO. Power procurement, not GPU supply, is now the binding constraint on capacity timelines. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/nvidia-mulls-3b-investment-in-sb-energy-in-openai-data-center-deal-report.html
