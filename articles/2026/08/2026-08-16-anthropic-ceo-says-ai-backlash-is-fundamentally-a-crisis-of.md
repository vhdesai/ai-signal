# Anthropic CEO says AI backlash is 'fundamentally a crisis of trust'

Dario Amodei rejected investor Gavin Baker's argument that his risk warnings fueled US anti-AI sentiment, saying public negativity predates AI and reflects distrust of companies, governments, and the tech industry. He conceded the sharpest fair criticism is that AI companies “haven't yet delivered on our big promises,” and that curing cancer, not promising to, is what changes opinion.
