# Inherent Exits Stealth With $50M — Claims Its Agent Beats Frontier Labs at Reproducing Papers

London-based Inherent, founded by Google DeepMind alumni including chief scientist Edward Hughes, emerged from stealth with a $50M seed round. Its Faraday agent — built on a 27B open-weight base model — claims to have outperformed Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at independently reproducing findings from published scientific papers. Results are vendor-reported and not yet independently replicated, but reproduction benchmarks are an increasingly credible proxy for real research capability.
