# Half of AI's Record $407B in H1 2026 Funding Went to OpenAI and Anthropic

AI startups raised a record $407 billion in the first half of 2026, with roughly half concentrated in two companies. The distribution confirms a barbell market: a handful of frontier labs absorbing mega-rounds, and a long tail of application startups raising seed and pre-seed capital. For corporate development teams, the implication is that mid-stage AI assets are relatively starved of capital and may be more attractively priced.
