# Google releases Gemini 3.5 Transcribe with 2.6% average word error rate

Google shipped Gemini 3.5 Transcribe as two distinct endpoints: a batch interface via the Interactions API and a streaming interface via the Live API. Google reports average word error rates of 4.0% streaming and 2.6% non-streaming as measured by Artificial Analysis, with time-to-final-transcription improved 70% over its prior Chirp 3 model and automatic detection across 85+ languages. The endpoints do not share features — streaming forgoes diarization and word-level timestamps for sub-second latency. It is API-only with no open weights, making this a managed-service decision rather than an infrastructure one.
