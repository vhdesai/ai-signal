# Anthropic’s IPO could break SpaceX’s recordTrending

Reports indicate Anthropic’s expected public listing could match or exceed the roughly $86.2B record set by SpaceX. The company could reportedly file publicly as soon as late this year. A filing would deliver the first audited view of frontier-lab unit economics, making it the single most informative disclosure event on the sector calendar.
