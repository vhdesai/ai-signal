# OpenAI employees sell $7 billion in tender offer

OpenAI employees sold approximately $7 billion in shares through the latest tender offer, reflecting the company's surging valuation and the growing liquidity pressure from long-tenured employees. The tender size — one of the largest in private tech history — signals investor confidence in OpenAI's commercial trajectory even as safety scrutiny intensifies. URL: The Information
