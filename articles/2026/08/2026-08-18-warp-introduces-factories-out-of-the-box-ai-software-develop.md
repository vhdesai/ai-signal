# Warp Introduces Factories — Out-of-the-Box AI Software Development Infrastructure

Warp launched Warp Factories, a pre-built environment for deploying and managing AI coding agents across the full development lifecycle. The model-agnostic system works with Codex, Claude Code, and others, integrates with Linear/Jira/Slack/Teams, and includes performance analytics. CEO Zach Lloyd says Warp currently automates 30–35% of its own engineering tasks weekly. The product targets teams that have proven agentic coding in pilots but stalled on the orchestration plumbing required to scale.
