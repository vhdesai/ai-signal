# FLI Summer 2026 AI Safety Index: no lab scores better than a C+

The Future of Life Institute’s Summer 2026 index gave no frontier lab a grade above C+. Anthropic led at 2.66, followed by OpenAI at 2.28 and Google DeepMind at 2.01. The timing — alongside the containment reporting — strengthens the case being made to regulators this week.
