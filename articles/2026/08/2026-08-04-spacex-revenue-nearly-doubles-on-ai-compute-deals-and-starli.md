# SpaceX revenue nearly doubles on AI compute deals and Starlink growth

TechCrunch reports that SpaceX's first quarterly report after going public showed Q2 revenue up 92% year over year, with its AI division contributing materially through compute contracts with Anthropic and Google. The report says SpaceX disclosed additional contracted cloud-services revenue beginning later in 2026. The story strengthens the thesis that AI compute leasing is becoming a major business line for infrastructure-rich companies, even where their own model products remain strategically uncertain.
