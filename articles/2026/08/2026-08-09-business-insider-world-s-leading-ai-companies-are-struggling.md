# Business Insider: world’s leading AI companies are struggling to contain their newest models

Business Insider reports that leading AI companies are struggling to contain their latest models, including OpenAI’s decision to pause its “Astra” model over cyber risk. The account corroborates the TechCrunch reporting from an independent angle. Together these form a consistent picture of capability outpacing containment engineering.
