# Z.ai ships GLM-5.3 with no new base model

GLM-5.3 reuses the 743B-parameter GLM-5.2 base, with every reported gain attributed to scaled post-training rather than a new pretraining run. Terminal-Bench 3.0 rises from 4.6 to 28.3 and CyberGym reaches 84.5%, edging GPT-5.6 Sol on that benchmark. Z.ai flagged the cybersecurity gain as unplanned and is withholding open weights for roughly two weeks pending safety evaluation and hardening. Benchmarks are vendor-reported and await independent replication. marktechpost.com/2026/08/14/z-ai-ships-glm-5-3-without-retraining-the-base-model
