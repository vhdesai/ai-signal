# OpenAI's ChatGPT Ads Business Reaches $1B Annualized Run Rate in Under 200 Days

OpenAI disclosed that ChatGPT Ads has reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate less than 200 days after launch, with tens of thousands of advertisers across more than 40 countries. The self-serve Ads Manager is expanding into India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa.
