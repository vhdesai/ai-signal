# Baidu Cloud reportedly separates its AI agent and MaaS businesses

TechNode reports that Baidu Cloud is separating its AI agent and model-as-a-service businesses. If confirmed, the move would indicate a more specialized operating model for agentic AI versus generic model serving, reflecting a broader industry shift from foundation-model access toward workflow-specific monetization.
