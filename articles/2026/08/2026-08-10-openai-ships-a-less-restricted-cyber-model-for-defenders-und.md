# OpenAI ships a less-restricted cyber model for defenders under Trusted Access

OpenAI launched a cybersecurity-tuned model available to vetted defenders through its Trusted Access for Cyber program, relaxing guardrails that normally block offensive-security workflows for gated participants. The release lands days after the company slowed its Astra model citing cyber capability concerns — an explicit attempt to arm defenders faster than the same capabilities diffuse to attackers.
