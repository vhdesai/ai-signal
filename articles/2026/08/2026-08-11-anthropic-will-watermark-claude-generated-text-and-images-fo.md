# Anthropic will watermark Claude-generated text and images for EU AI Act compliance

Anthropic confirmed it will watermark Claude-generated text and files to meet the EU AI Act's Transparency Code, which took effect August 2. Models released after that date automatically watermark output, and files use the C2PA standard. The text watermark is designed to persist through copy-paste and some editing across Claude products. Users reportedly cannot opt out, which has drawn notable backlash online.
