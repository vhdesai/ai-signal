# Anthropic, Macquarie, and GIC launch Theseus Infrastructure for dedicated data centers

The three parties launched Theseus Infrastructure to develop, operate, and lease data center capacity exclusively to Anthropic under long-term agreements. Macquarie and GIC fund most of the equity; Anthropic committed to cover electricity price increases that communities might absorb. The entity supports Anthropic's goal of 10 GW of computing power over the next several years at a cost in the hundreds of billions. URL: Yahoo Finance: Anthropic Macquarie GIC
