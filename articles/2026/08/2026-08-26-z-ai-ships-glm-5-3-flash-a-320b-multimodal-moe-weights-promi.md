# Z.ai Ships GLM-5.3-Flash, a 320B Multimodal MoE — Weights Promised in Two Weeks

Z.ai released GLM-5.3-Flash, the first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 series — a MoE architecture with 320B total parameters and 18B active per token. Cloudflare made it available on Workers AI the same day, describing it as approaching Claude Opus 4.8 on coding and agentic benchmarks.
