# Defunct startups' Slack archives are becoming agent training assets

Mercor and rival data vendors are pursuing internal Slack archives, ticketing histories, and workflow recordings from startups that are shutting down or being acquired, using them to build reinforcement-learning environments where labs train agents on realistic enterprise tasks. The report cites Mercor approaching Warmly's CEO following its HubSpot transaction. This creates a live diligence question for acquirers: post-close data rights over internal communications now carry standalone commercial value. https://www.theinformation.com/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=Mercor+Slack+training+data
