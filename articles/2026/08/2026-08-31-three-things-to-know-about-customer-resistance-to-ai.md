# Three Things to Know About Customer Resistance to AI

MIT Sloan Management Review published analysis on customer resistance to AI adoption. For senior leaders, the practical point is that deployment strategy must account for trust, perceived control, and value clarity; technical capability alone is insufficient when customers believe automation changes accountability or service quality.
