# The Information: Musk Told Cursor Staff 'Grok Is Falling Behind' in First All-Hands

In his first address to Cursor staff after the $60B acquisition closed, Elon Musk called into an all-hands video meeting and told employees that SpaceXAI had fallen behind competitors and that "he wasn't used to losing." Unlike Tesla or SpaceX, which dominate their markets, Grok isn't the leader in AI and "desperately needs to catch up."
