# Lovable confirms $400M Series C at a $13.3B valuation

The European "vibe coding" startup confirmed a $400 million Series C led by Menlo Ventures, doubling its December valuation to $13.3 billion. The raise follows a reported $500 million annualized run rate in June, with 60 million hosted projects drawing 900 million monthly visitors. Investors are betting AI software creation supports multiple platform winners alongside Cursor, Replit, and OpenAI.
