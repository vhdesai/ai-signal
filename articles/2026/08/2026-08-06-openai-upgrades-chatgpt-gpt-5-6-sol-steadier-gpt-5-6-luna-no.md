# OpenAI upgrades ChatGPT: GPT‑5.6 Sol steadier, GPT‑5.6 Luna now default for Free tier

OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT updates that make GPT‑5.6 Luna the default model for Free users — with unlimited text chats and a new “Think” control — while giving Plus/Pro users a steadier GPT‑5.6 Sol via a reasoning-effort slider. OpenAI says factual errors were about 62% less common with GPT‑5.6 Luna than with GPT‑5.5 Instant on an internal financial/medical/legal evaluation. The change reaches ChatGPT’s roughly one billion weekly users.
