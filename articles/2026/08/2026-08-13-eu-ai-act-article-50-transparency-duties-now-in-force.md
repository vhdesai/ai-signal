# EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Duties Now in Force

Article 50 obligations began applying Aug 2, requiring disclosure and machine-readable marking of AI-generated content. GPAI providers already on the EEA market have until Dec 2 to comply — that deferred deadline drives vendor watermarking announcements from Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and Microsoft. Organizations deploying GPAI in EU-facing products should map obligations against December. Provenance metadata is on track to become a default enterprise content-pipeline expectation.
