# EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Duties Now in Force

Article 50 obligations began applying Aug 2, requiring disclosure and machine-readable marking of AI-generated content. Providers of GPAI already on the EEA market before Aug 2 have until Dec 2, 2026 to comply — that deferred deadline is the specific driver behind vendor watermarking announcements now landing from Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and Microsoft. Organizations deploying GPAI in EU-facing products should map obligations against the December date. Provenance metadata is on track to become a default expectation in enterprise content pipelines.
