# Bill Gates Proposes 'Robot Tax' and 'Human Reserved' Jobs to Mitigate AI Harms

Bill Gates published a Gates Notes essay proposing two novel policy ideas: (1) a "robot tax" to slow the replacement of human labor with automation and fund retraining, noting the current tax system "nudges you toward replacing people with machines"; and (2) "Human Reserved" job categories where AI would be barred — some for economic reasons (construction workers who can't easily retrain) and some for ethical ones ("imagine a robot giving you the awful news that you have an incurable disease").
