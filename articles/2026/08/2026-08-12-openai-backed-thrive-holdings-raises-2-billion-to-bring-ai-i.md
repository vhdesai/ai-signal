# OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings raises $2 billion to bring AI into traditional businesses

Thrive Holdings raised $2 billion at a $12 billion valuation from investors including SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The firm buys traditional businesses, embeds AI into workflows, and has focused so far on accounting and IT services, with reported gains such as 30% lower tax preparation time and faster help-desk resolution. The raise reinforces that enterprise AI implementation, not just model development, is becoming a large capital category.
