# Anthropic Forcibly Signs Out Claude Users After Infostealer Malware Hijacks Sessions

Anthropic has begun locking users out of Claude accounts after commodity infostealer malware on customer machines captured active login sessions and was used to drain paid usage. The company removed saved payment methods and issued refunds to affected users, with reporting naming multiple stealer families involved. Notably, this is not a breach of Anthropic's infrastructure — it is endpoint compromise propagating into AI service accounts, which makes it a direct concern for any organization where employees hold personal or shadow-IT AI subscriptions. https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/31/claude-accounts-compromised-through-infostealer/ POLICY
