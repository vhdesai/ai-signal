# NVIDIA Opens Alpamayo 2 Super for Commercial Robotaxi and AV Development

NVIDIA made Alpamayo 2 Super — a reasoning model roughly 3x the scale of the 10-billion-parameter Alpamayo 1.5, built on Cosmos 3 Super Reasoner and post-trained with reinforcement learning — available for commercial use on Hugging Face under the Linux Foundation's permissive OpenMDW-1.1 license, covering fine-tuning, derivatives and redistribution. NVIDIA reports the model ranks first on the LingoQA driving-reasoning benchmark against roughly 40 models, outperforming Qwen2.5-VL 72B by 17.0 points and Gemini 2.5 Pro by 15.1 points on the Lingo-Judge metric. It emits inspectable chain-of-causation traces that integrate with NVIDIA Halos safety validation and ISO/PAS 8800 workflows. Licensing the whole Alpamayo family commercially removes the main legal blocker to open-weights adoption in regulated autonomy programs.
