# VentureBeat reports that observability startup groundcover raised a $100 million Series C led by One Peak, bringing…

VentureBeat reports that observability startup groundcover raised a $100 million Series C led by One Peak, bringing total funding to $160 million. The company's pitch is that agent telemetry should remain inside the customer's cloud environment and be priced per host rather than by ingestion volume. The funding round reflects investor belief that autonomous agents will materially change observability scale, cost, and data-governance requirements.
