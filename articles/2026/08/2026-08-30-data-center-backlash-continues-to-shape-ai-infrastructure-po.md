# Data-center backlash continues to shape AI infrastructure politics

The Wall Street Journal published new commentary and coverage on data-center disenchantment and the political middle ground around AI infrastructure siting. The issue is increasingly bipartisan and local: communities are weighing jobs and tax revenue against power demand, land use, utility bills, and environmental impact. For AI infrastructure planners, community acceptance is becoming a recurring gating factor alongside chips, capital, and grid interconnection. URL behind paywall.
