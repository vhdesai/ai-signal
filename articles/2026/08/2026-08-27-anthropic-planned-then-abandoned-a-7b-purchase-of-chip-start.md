# Anthropic Planned, Then Abandoned, a $7B Purchase of Chip Startup MatX

Anthropic discussed acquiring MatX for ~$7B before abandoning the deal, with talks shifting to partnership. Frontier labs increasingly view silicon design as a strategic dependency. Vertical integration at this scale remains difficult to finance and govern even for well-capitalized labs.
