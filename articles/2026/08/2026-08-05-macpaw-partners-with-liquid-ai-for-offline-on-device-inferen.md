# MacPaw partners with Liquid AI for offline on-device inference

MacPaw is adopting Liquid AI's locally hosted models to power an offline AI assistant and plans to expose the stack to developers in its Setapp ecosystem. The deal validates small-model local execution as a distribution and privacy strategy for consumer app developers.
