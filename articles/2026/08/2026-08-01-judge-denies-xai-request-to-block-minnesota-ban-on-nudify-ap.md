# Judge denies xAI request to block Minnesota ban on nudify apps

A federal judge denied xAI's emergency request to block Minnesota's new ban on AI "nudify" apps that create non-consensual sexual imagery. The ruling lets the law take effect while litigation continues, with the judge reportedly citing xAI's delayed filing as a factor against emergency relief. For executives, the case is an early state-level precedent for regulating abusive synthetic-media applications and a reminder that AI product governance risk is now moving through courts as well as legislatures.
