# Nvidia 13F Reveals $21B SpaceX and $30B Intel Positions

Nvidia’s 13F disclosed a $20.98B SpaceX stake and $29.99B Intel position as of June 30. Both are in exclusive Nvidia chip customers. Nvidia is now simultaneously hardware supplier, financing guarantor, and equity owner of its largest customers — a concentration of roles that sharpens circular-financing questions and would draw scrutiny in any other industry.
