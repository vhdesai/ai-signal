# Anthropic’s ARR Reaches $65B; Pre-IPO Contracts Imply ~$1.8T

ARR hit $65B at end of July (up from $47B in May, ~$9B at end-2025). Tokenized pre-IPO contracts imply ~$1.8T — 88% above the last private round. These are thin derivative-like venues reflecting scarcity. The revenue acceleration signals sustained pricing power and investment in the Claude line. For enterprise buyers modeling multi-year vendor leverage, this trajectory changes the negotiating posture. CNBC | TechCrunch REGULATORY
