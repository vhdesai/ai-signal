# S1-mini: A 462 MB Open-Weights Text Normalizer for Cleaning ASR Transcripts

Superwhisper released S1-mini, a 0.6B-parameter open-weights text-normalization model that cleans raw automatic-speech-recognition output by removing fillers and resolving self-corrections. It does not transcribe or chat; it sits downstream of an ASR system to turn spoken output into clean written text. Its Q4_K_M GGUF build is just 462 MB and runs on a laptop CPU, making on-device deployment realistic. The narrow scope is the point: a small specialist model handling a step that general assistants do inconsistently.
