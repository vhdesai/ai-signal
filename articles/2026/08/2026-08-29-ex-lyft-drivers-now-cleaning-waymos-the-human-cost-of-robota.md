# Ex-Lyft Drivers Now Cleaning Waymos — The Human Cost of Robotaxi Transition

BI profiles former Lyft drivers who now work cleaning Waymo robotaxis, illustrating how the self-driving transition transforms labor — from skilled driving into lower-wage vehicle maintenance. Workers describe "ceding the road to the robots" while maintaining the very machines that replaced them.
