# Harvey introduces Tenet, a Kimi K3 base post-trained for long-horizon legal agent work

Legal AI vendor Harvey released its first post-trained model, built on a Kimi K3 base with Fireworks and aimed at sustained, multi-step legal agent tasks. Harvey reports the model nearly doubles task completion on its LAB benchmark. MarkTechPost notes that only one of the claimed benchmark numbers currently survives independent verification — a caveat worth carrying into any vendor conversation.
