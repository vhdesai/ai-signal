# Meta Reaches $18 Billion Settlement With 48 States — But Wider Legal War Looms

Meta reached an $18B settlement with 48 states over child-safety claims — the largest tech-platform regulatory settlement in history. The Information's analysis noted the settlement won't likely help the social media company avoid further legal battles, as the ongoing Oakland trial and additional private lawsuits remain live. The scale sets a new precedent for what platforms face when algorithmic engagement design is challenged as harmful to minors.
