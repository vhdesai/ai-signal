# Nvidia Posts Record $96.2B Quarter, Guides to ~70% Revenue Growth for FY2028

Nvidia reported Q2 FY2027 revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% YoY, with data center revenue of $89.0B (+117%). Net income hit $59.69B ($2.46/share), crushing the $2.09 consensus. Jensen Huang guided to ~70% revenue growth for FY2028, explicitly rejecting the view that AI infrastructure demand is nearing a peak. The guidance — not the quarter — did the heavy lifting for sentiment.
