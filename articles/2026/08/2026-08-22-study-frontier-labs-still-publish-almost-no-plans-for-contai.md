# Study: Frontier Labs Still Publish Almost No Plans for Containing a Rogue Model

Guidelight AI Standards graded five labs on containment preparedness. OpenAI scored highest; Anthropic and Meta scored lowest. Chief scientist Steven Adler was “surprised how little companies have said publicly about handling a serious loss-of-control incident.” With California and New York moving toward disclosure requirements, this gap becomes a compliance question.
