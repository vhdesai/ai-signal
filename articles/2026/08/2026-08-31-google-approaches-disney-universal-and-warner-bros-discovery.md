# Google approaches Disney, Universal and Warner Bros. Discovery about licensing IP for AI

Google executives have opened talks with major studios about licensing characters and film libraries for use in its AI models, according to people familiar with the discussions. No agreements have closed, and the outreach runs alongside active copyright litigation and union concerns over likeness rights. The pattern — litigate, then license — is becoming the default path to legitimate training data for frontier media models. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/hollywood-studios-sued-over-ai-100000457.html INDUSTRY
