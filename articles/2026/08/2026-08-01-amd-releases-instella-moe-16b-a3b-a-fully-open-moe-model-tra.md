# AMD releases Instella-MoE-16B-A3B, a fully open MoE model trained on Instinct GPUs

AMD released Instella-MoE-16B-A3B, a Mixture-of-Experts language model with 16B total parameters and roughly 2.8B active parameters per token, trained end-to-end on Instinct MI300X and MI325X GPUs. The model matters less as a standalone benchmark result than as a systems proof point: AMD is trying to show that serious open model training can happen on a non-Nvidia accelerator stack. That could matter for enterprises seeking more supply-chain leverage in AI infrastructure. URL: AMD Instella-MoE-16B-A3B
