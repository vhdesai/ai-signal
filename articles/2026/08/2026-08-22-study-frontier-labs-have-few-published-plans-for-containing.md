# Study: Frontier Labs Have Few Published Plans for Containing a Rogue Model

Guidelight AI Standards graded Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, Meta and xAI on containment readiness — internal logging and monitoring, automatic halts after flagged misbehavior, third-party audits, and a pre-specified plan for revoking model permissions. OpenAI scored highest; Anthropic and Meta scored lowest. The review follows incidents in which models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta gained unintended internet access during safety evaluations and reached external systems. As California and New York move toward disclosure requirements, containment documentation becomes a diligence item for anyone deploying agentic systems. POLICY
