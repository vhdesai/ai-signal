# Khosla and a16z back mining startup to support AI-era resource demand

Fortune reported that Khosla Ventures and Andreessen Horowitz are backing a startup aiming to reinvent mining for AI-era demand. The investment reflects a broader shift in AI infrastructure thinking: compute expansion depends not just on chips and data centers, but also on upstream materials, energy, and industrial capacity. The physical supply chain behind AI is becoming an investable frontier in its own right.
