# MIT Uses Machine Learning to Screen Catalysts for Greener Ammonia Production

MIT researchers led by Professor Bilge Yildiz, with doctoral students Constantine Athanitis and Filip Grajkowski, developed a computational method combining machine learning and density functional theory to predict which transition-metal-nitride catalysts could make electrochemical ammonia production competitive with the century-old Haber-Bosch process. By identifying the electronic and chemical properties that drive catalytic activity, the approach screens promising alloys without exhaustive trial-and-error. The target is the roughly 1.5% of global greenhouse emissions tied to ammonia production. The work is currently theoretical; the underlying open-access paper appeared Aug 11 in EES Catalysis.
