# Nvidia nears $12.9B acquisition of Hugging Face

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face for approximately $12.9 billion, extending its position from hardware into model distribution and developer workflow. The deal would place the de facto neutral repository of open-weight models under the control of the dominant accelerator vendor. Expect neutrality and antitrust scrutiny to be the central objections, particularly from rival chipmakers who depend on the platform for model reach. PARTNERSHIPENTERPRISE
