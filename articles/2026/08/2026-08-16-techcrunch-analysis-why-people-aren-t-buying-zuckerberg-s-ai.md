# TechCrunch Analysis: Why People Aren't Buying Zuckerberg's AI Vision

TechCrunch's Equity podcast dissected why Zuckerberg's 6,500-word "The Future Is for Everyone" manifesto has drawn widespread negative reaction. The team argued the problem is the messenger — Meta's history of promising social connection but delivering "ragebaiting and advertisements" — combined with a vision that feels "Pollyannish" without acknowledging costs. The analysis noted Zuckerberg is positioning as "an anti-Dario" — rejecting safety slowdowns — while Meta's actual consumer AI products remain inaccessible to most users (Muse Glimmer requires specific hardware, not a MacBook). 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/why-people-arent-buying-mark-zuckerbergs-ai-future/
