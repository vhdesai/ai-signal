# Apple proposes locking pretrained weights to limit unauthorized model adaptation

Apple researchers published DLR-Lock, a method that replaces pretrained MLP layers with deep low-rank residual networks to preserve model capabilities while making fine-tuning more costly and difficult. The work targets a core tension in open-weight AI: broader adoption and research value versus the risk that released weights can be adapted for unauthorized uses. It is a technical contribution to model-control debates, showing that governance may increasingly be encoded into architectures as well as licenses.
