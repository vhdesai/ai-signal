# Grok Lite Sends Gibberish Responses to Users Amid xAI Staff Turnover

xAI's Grok chatbot began sending extended nonsense responses (e.g., "match it without and your they and two for planets can practical and often cheese…") to users of Grok Lite. Reddit was "overwhelmed with complaints." The Grok X account confirmed the bug but called it "a rare temporary generation glitch." xAI has lost most of its founding team and at least 50 researchers/engineers since the SpaceX merger, per The Information. The incident adds to xAI's reliability challenges as it tries to win enterprise customers. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/grok-keeps-sending-gibberish-responses-to-users/
