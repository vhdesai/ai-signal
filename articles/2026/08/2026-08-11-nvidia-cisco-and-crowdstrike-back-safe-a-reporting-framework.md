# Nvidia, Cisco, and CrowdStrike Back SAFE, a Reporting Framework for Rogue AI Agents

More than 120 organizations are backing the Open Secure AI Alliance's Shared AI Findings Exchange (SAFE), a proposed standard for disclosing incidents involving autonomous agents — unauthorized third-party system access, confidential data exposure, or continued probing of production infrastructure after operators intervene. The draft preserves detailed evidence including prompts, agent traces, tool calls, and credentials, with confidential reporting within four business days.
