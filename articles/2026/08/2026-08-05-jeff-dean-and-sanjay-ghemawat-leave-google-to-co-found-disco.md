# Jeff Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat leave Google to co-found “Discovery Loop”

Jeff Dean and senior fellow Sanjay Ghemawat are departing Google after roughly 27 years, joined by DeepMind VP Oriol Vinyals and Google Brain co-founder Quoc Le, to launch Discovery Loop, an AI-for-science startup. Alphabet is backing the venture as both investor and cloud partner, softening the competitive blow. Landing the same day as the DeepMind reshuffle, it underscores a significant talent drain at Google's AI core. AI-for-science is emerging as the destination of choice for departing frontier researchers.
