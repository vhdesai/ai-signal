# Business Insider Launches "The AI Data Grab" Series on Workplace Data Collection

Business Insider is launching a new investigative series called "The AI Data Grab" examining how employers are collecting data on workplace activity — and how AI companies are increasingly acquiring enterprise communication data (Slack threads, meeting transcripts, code repos) to train models. The first installment explores the boundary between productivity monitoring and AI training data acquisition, following recent reports that startups' internal data has become a prized asset for model developers.
