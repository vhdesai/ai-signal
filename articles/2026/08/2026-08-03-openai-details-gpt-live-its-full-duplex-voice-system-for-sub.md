# OpenAI details GPT-Live, its full-duplex voice system for sub-second, turnless conversation

OpenAI published an engineering deep-dive on GPT-Live, a full-duplex voice architecture that can listen and speak simultaneously using a dedicated real-time media path, fast startup, and asynchronous reasoning and tool use. The design targets near-instant, “turnless” back-and-forth at ChatGPT scale, including conversational acknowledgments while the user is still speaking. It signals voice moving from feature to platform-grade interface, with implications for contact-center, accessibility, and assistant workloads. PRODUCTTRUST & SAFETYMICROSOFT
