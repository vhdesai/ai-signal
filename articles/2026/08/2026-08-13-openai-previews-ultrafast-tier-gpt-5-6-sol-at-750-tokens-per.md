# OpenAI Previews Ultrafast Tier: GPT-5.6 Sol at 750 Tokens Per Second on Cerebras

OpenAI opened a limited preview of Ultrafast, an API service tier running GPT-5.6 Sol up to 14× faster than standard processing at up to 750 output tokens per second, powered by Cerebras wafer-scale hardware. OpenAI frames the use cases as latency-bound work: incident response, real-time financial research and fraud screening, voice support, and in-session commerce. Early testers named include Jane Street, Podium, Basis, and Rogo. Access is capacity-gated and will expand over time. https://openai.com/index/previewing-ultrafast/ ________________________________ Infrastructure CAPITALHOT
