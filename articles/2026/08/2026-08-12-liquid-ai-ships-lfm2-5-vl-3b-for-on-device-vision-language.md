# Liquid AI Ships LFM2.5-VL-3B for On-Device Vision-Language

Liquid AI released LFM2.5-VL-3B, a 3.1B-parameter open-weight vision-language model designed to run on phones, laptops, and single GPUs. The low-latency, non-reasoning model adds visual grounding, screen understanding, and function calling — making it practical for real-time image-to-text tasks at the device edge. For enterprises with field, retail, or industrial inspection use cases, small open-weight VLMs are becoming a credible alternative to per-image frontier API calls, eliminating both latency and data-residency concerns. The release continues a clear 2026 pattern: capable multimodal models are being pushed to consumer and edge hardware, changing cost and privacy calculus faster than most deployment roadmaps anticipate.
