# xAI Launches Grok 4.6 for Long-Running Agents; Grok 4.7 Previewed

Grok 4.6 is tuned for long-running agents, coding, and sustained knowledge work, with same-day availability in Cursor, Grok Build, and the API at $2/$6 per million tokens. Musk previewed Grok 4.7 as ~3–4 weeks out. The release reinforces the market shift toward agentic models built for multi-step, multi-hour work.
