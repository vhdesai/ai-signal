# Japan makes non-consensual AI voice cloning a civil violation

Japan's Ministry of Justice issued final guidelines establishing that AI-generated audio mimicking a person's voice without consent constitutes a civil violation of publicity rights—the first binding governmental guidance on AI voice cloning from a major Asian economy. Enterprises operating synthetic-voice products in Japan should expect consent documentation to become a compliance requirement. URL: Tech Times: Japan AI voice cloning consent
