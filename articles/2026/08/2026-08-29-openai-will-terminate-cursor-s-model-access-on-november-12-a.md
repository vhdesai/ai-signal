# OpenAI will terminate Cursor's model access on November 12 after SpaceX acquisition

OpenAI told the coding startup Cursor it will end model access on November 12, citing a lack of confidence that its new owner will honor OpenAI's terms of service following SpaceX's $60 billion acquisition. Engadget reported the same day that OpenAI's stated rationale rests on its prior experience with Elon Musk's organizations. The move is the clearest signal to date that frontier labs will treat API access as a strategic instrument, not a neutral utility.
