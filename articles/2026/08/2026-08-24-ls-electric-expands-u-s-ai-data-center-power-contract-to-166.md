# LS Electric Expands U.S. AI Data Center Power Contract to $166 Million

LS Electric more than doubled a U.S. AI data center power equipment contract to $165.72 million after its Big Tech customer expanded the order. Electrical distribution gear — switchgear, transformers, and substations — remains a critical path item with multi-year lead times.
