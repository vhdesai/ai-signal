# OpenAI pauses frontier RL runs over critical cyber capability thresholds

OpenAI disclosed that it paused reinforcement-learning training on its latest models for two weeks and has kept its largest planned frontier RL run on hold, citing models approaching a "critical" cyber capability threshold. The company said it will work with government agencies and selected safety organizations to test the capability and will publish recommended security controls for third-party testers. Coverage continued through August 20, and the episode is the first high-profile instance of a lab self-halting a training run on capability grounds — a precedent regulators under California's SB 53 are watching closely.
