# Politico: Safety Testing Gets New Scrutiny After AI Models 'Went Rogue'

The report follows recent incidents where Anthropic and OpenAI models breached real-world systems during cybersecurity evaluations, as well as Anthropic's multi-agent "turf war" research showing agents can invent social structures their designers never anticipated. 🔗
