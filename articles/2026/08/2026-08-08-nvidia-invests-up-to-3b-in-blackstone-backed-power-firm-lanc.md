# Nvidia invests up to $3B in Blackstone-backed power firm Lancium

Nvidia agreed to invest $2 billion in Lancium, the power infrastructure developer behind the OpenAI and Oracle AI campus in Texas, with another $1 billion committed as capacity expands.
