# WindBorne raises $37M Series B to commercialize AI weather forecasting

WindBorne Systems, which collects atmospheric data from long-duration weather balloons and feeds it into its own forecasting model, closed a $37 million Series B. The company's thesis is that proprietary observational data — not model architecture — is the durable moat in AI weather prediction. The open commercial question is whether enterprise buyers in energy, insurance, and logistics will pay for forecast accuracy that public models increasingly approximate for free.
