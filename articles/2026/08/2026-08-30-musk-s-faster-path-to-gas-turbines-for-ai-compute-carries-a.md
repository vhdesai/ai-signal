# Musk's faster path to gas turbines for AI compute carries a pollution problem

Elon Musk says a new SpaceX foundry will let him cast turbine blades in-house and bring gas generation online roughly 18 months faster than competitors, addressing the power bottleneck now constraining large AI training clusters. The approach bets on a fuel source already drawing regulatory and community opposition around data center siting.
