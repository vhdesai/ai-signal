# Unreleased Anthropic model advances a related Riemann hypothesis bound

TechCrunch reported on Anthropic's research showing that an unreleased Claude model improved a longstanding lower bound related to zeros of the Riemann zeta function, increasing it from 41.6% to 67.2%. Anthropic said the result was validated by in-house mathematicians and formalized in Lean. The result does not prove the Riemann hypothesis, but it is a meaningful signal that AI systems are beginning to contribute to frontier mathematical workflows through search, proof drafting, validation, and formalization.
