# Horizon3 raises $250 million at $2 billion valuation as autonomous pentesting demand rises

Horizon3.ai raised a $250 million Series E at a $2 billion valuation for its AI-powered autonomous penetration-testing platform, NodeZero. The company positions continuous autonomous security validation as an alternative to periodic manual pentesting, and the timing follows public scrutiny of agent-related security failures at major AI labs. The funding round indicates that AI security validation is moving from technical specialty to board-level budget line.
