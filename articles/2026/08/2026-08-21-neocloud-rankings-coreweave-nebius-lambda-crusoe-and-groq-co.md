# Neocloud rankings: CoreWeave, Nebius, Lambda, Crusoe and Groq compared

MarkTechPost published a ranked comparison of GPU “neocloud” providers based on published pricing and contracted power capacity. The analysis is a useful proxy for the compute economics now underpinning both frontier labs and academic research budgets. It is market analysis rather than a reported result. Academic Research Note: the tracked university sources — MIT News AI, BAIR, Stanford HAI, CMU, Georgia Tech, Cornell, Princeton, Purdue and Apple Machine Learning Research — published nothing dated August 21–22.
