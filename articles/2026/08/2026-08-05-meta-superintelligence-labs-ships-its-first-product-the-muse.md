# Meta Superintelligence Labs ships its first product, the “Muse Code” agent

Alexandr Wang's Meta Superintelligence Labs, assembled after Meta's roughly $14.3B Scale AI deal, shipped Muse Code, a terminal-based coding agent with persistent async background agents and a replay-exact event log. It runs on Muse Spark 1.2, a model co-trained with the harness on repository-scale work, and vendor benchmarks claim 82.9% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, trailing only Claude Opus 5 at 86.7%. The strategic hook is a cut-rate contributor tier at roughly $0.10/$0.20 per million tokens where developers opt into having prompts and completions used to train Meta's models. InfoWorld notes analysts do not yet see the approach as differentiated from rivals, and the benchmarks remain vendor-reported.
