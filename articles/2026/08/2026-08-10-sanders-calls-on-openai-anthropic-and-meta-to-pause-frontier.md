# Sanders calls on OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta to pause frontier AI development

Senator Bernie Sanders sent a letter to leading AI CEOs urging them to pause development of systems "humans cannot control," raising the prospect of Senate action if the industry does not respond. The letter has no binding force, but it moves the pause argument from advocacy groups into the legislative arena and signals the framing a future Senate hearing would use. It also pairs labor-displacement arguments with existential-risk language, broadening the political coalition behind restrictive proposals.
