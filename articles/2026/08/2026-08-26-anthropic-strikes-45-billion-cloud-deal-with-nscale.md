# Anthropic strikes $45 billion cloud deal with Nscale

Anthropic has agreed to a roughly $45 billion cloud arrangement with Nscale, renting approximately 460 megawatts of capacity at an Nscale data center development in West Virginia. The deal continues Anthropic's pattern of large, multi-vendor compute commitments rather than dependence on a single hyperscaler. At this scale, power availability and site development timelines — not chip supply alone — become the governing risk. EARNINGS
