# New Plaintiff Joins CSAM Lawsuit Against xAI Over Grok

A woman identified as “Jane Doe 4” joined a lawsuit — originally filed by three Tennessee teenagers — against xAI, now part of SpaceX, alleging Grok was used to transform a childhood photo into more than 7,000 explicit images. The suit accuses xAI of failing to take basic precautions against generating child sexual abuse material. It sharpens the liability question around image-generation safeguards for any provider shipping consumer image models.
