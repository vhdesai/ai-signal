# As open-source AI adoption grows, the attack surface expands from model weights to packages, plugins, datasets,…

As open-source AI adoption grows, the attack surface expands from model weights to packages, plugins, datasets, evaluation harnesses, and deployment pipelines. - Openness improves transparency and flexibility, but it also gives adversaries more artifacts to inspect, tamper with, or socially engineer into production environments. - This becomes especially important as governments and large enterprises embrace open models for sovereignty and customization reasons. - Security posture therefore has to cover the entire open AI supply chain, not just the base model itself. - For executives, open-weight adoption should be paired with software-supply-chain controls, provenance checks, and disciplined dependency governance.
