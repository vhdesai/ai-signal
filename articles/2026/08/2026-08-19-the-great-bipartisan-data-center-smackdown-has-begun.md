# The Great Bipartisan Data Center Smackdown Has Begun

Pennsylvania Gov. Shapiro signed an executive order enabling the state to block new data centers, while Texas Gov. Abbott forced companies to commit to water/electricity/quality-of-life standards. The bipartisan pushback is a backlash against Big Tech.
