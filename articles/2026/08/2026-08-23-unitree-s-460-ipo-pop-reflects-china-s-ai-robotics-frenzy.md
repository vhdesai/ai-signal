# Unitree’s 460% IPO Pop Reflects China’s AI Robotics Frenzy

Unitree surged 460% in its Shanghai debut — not unusual for China’s current AI/robotics market. The pop reflects extraordinary investor conviction about physical AI’s potential, even as most humanoid companies have yet to deploy commercially at scale.
