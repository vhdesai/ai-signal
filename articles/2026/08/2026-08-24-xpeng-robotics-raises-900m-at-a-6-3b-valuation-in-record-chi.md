# XPeng Robotics Raises $900M+ at a $6.3B Valuation in Record China Embodied-AI Round

XPeng said its robotics unit raised more than $900 million in its first outside round, led by IDG Capital with strategic participation from Tencent and Alibaba, valuing the business above $6.3 billion. It is the largest single private financing in China's embodied-AI sector. Proceeds are earmarked for robotics hardware and software, physical-AI model training, data collection and mass-production capacity. XPeng targets 1,000 units per month of its IRON humanoid by year-end, with commercial deliveries scheduled for 2027.
