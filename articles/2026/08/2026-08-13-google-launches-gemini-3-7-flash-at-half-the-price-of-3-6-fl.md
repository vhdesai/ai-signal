# Google launches Gemini 3.7 Flash at half the price of 3.6 Flash

Google shipped Gemini 3.7 Flash three weeks after 3.6 Flash, describing it as its most capable workhorse model for coding and agents, with a 1M-token context. Introductory pricing is $0.75 per 1M input tokens and $3.75 per 1M output tokens — half the prior list rate — running through December 31, 2026. Google reports DeepSWE v1.1 at 65.3% (vs 49.0%) and WebDev Arena Elo of 1588 (vs 1538).
