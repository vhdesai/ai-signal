# Google DeepMind leadership shake-up: Kavukcuoglu takes over, Hassabis becomes chair

Koray Kavukcuoglu now leads Google DeepMind while Demis Hassabis moves to a chair role, amid pressure to close the coding-capability gap with OpenAI and Anthropic. Reuters separately reported that Sergey Brin has pushed staff to go "all-in" on Gemini. For a lab that has defined itself by research independence, the reorganization signals a decisive tilt toward product velocity and competitive delivery.
