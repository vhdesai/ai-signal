# States reconsidering data-center tax incentives adds a new and increasingly political cost variable to AI…

States reconsidering data-center tax incentives adds a new and increasingly political cost variable to AI infrastructure planning. - The economics of site selection are no longer defined only by power, land, cooling, and network access, but also by shifting public sentiment and fiscal policy. - That matters because hyperscalers and AI labs have assumed relatively stable incentives while scaling aggressively into local communities. - If repeal momentum spreads, jurisdictional strategy could become a material source of cost variance and project delay. - Executives should treat policy risk as a first-class infrastructure input rather than a secondary government-relations issue.
