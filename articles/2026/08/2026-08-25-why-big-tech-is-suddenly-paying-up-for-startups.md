# Why Big Tech Is Suddenly Paying Up for Startups

Big Tech is suddenly willing to pay premium valuations for AI startups because competitive dynamics have shifted: acquiring market position is now cheaper than building organically against entrenched players. The OpenRouter deal ignited a broader M&A frenzy across the AI infrastructure layer.
