# Sam Altman promotes ChatGPT for parenting and draws public backlash

TechCrunch reports that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman encouraged parents to use ChatGPT Work to generate personalized morning podcasts for children, prompting a highly visible backlash from creator Alex Hirsch and others. The reaction illustrates widening public discomfort with AI substitution in intimate or developmental contexts. The business implication is reputational: consumer AI expansion into family use cases will face heightened scrutiny, especially while OpenAI is also navigating safety-related lawsuits.
