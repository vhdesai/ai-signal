# Attackers target open-source AI as Big Tech embraces it

Defense One reported that attackers are targeting open-source AI at the same time large technology companies and government-adjacent buyers are embracing open models and tools. The issue is strategically important because open AI stacks expand transparency and flexibility, but also create dependency and software-supply-chain risks that adversaries can probe. Enterprises adopting open models should treat model weights, packages, plugins, and evaluation harnesses as part of the attack surface.
