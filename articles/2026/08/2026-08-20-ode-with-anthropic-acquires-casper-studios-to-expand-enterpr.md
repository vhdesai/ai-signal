# Ode with Anthropic acquires Casper Studios to expand enterprise deployment services

Ode with Anthropic, an enterprise AI transformation firm, announced the acquisition of Casper Studios, an AI services company focused on embedding Claude into customer workflows. The transaction consolidates deployment and change-management capacity around a single frontier model vendor. It reflects a broader pattern of services roll-ups forming around specific model ecosystems rather than remaining model-agnostic. MARKETS
