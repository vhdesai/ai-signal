# ECB Blog Predicts AI Tech Stock Market Correction Is 'Likely'

The European Central Bank published a blog post warning that a market correction to tech stock exuberance in the U.S. is "likely" and could have far-reaching consequences. The warning follows a week of heightened skepticism from investors including Michael Burry (who called AI capex a bubble with a "2028 clock") and concerns about circular financing structures in the AI ecosystem. 🔗
