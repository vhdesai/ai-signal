# EU AI Act transparency obligations take effect across Europe

Key transparency requirements under the EU AI Act are now enforceable, including clear labeling and machine-readable marks for deepfakes and certain AI-generated content, plus disclosure when users interact with chatbots, agents, or avatars. Regulators can inspect general-purpose models, restrict market access, and levy fines up to €15M or 3% of global turnover. Combined with California’s parallel rules, provenance and disclosure are moving from best practice to compliance baseline for any company shipping generative features. REGULATIONDEEPFAKESPOLICY
