# Harvard Launches AI Avatar Professors for $699 Entrepreneurship Bootcamp

Harvard Business School’s “Foundry” uses AI avatars of professors to simulate pitch meetings, sales calls, and board sessions. Tested at 100+ universities, the $699 program aims to scale HBS far beyond 900 annual MBA admits. PwC, law firms, and a church are testing similar approaches.
