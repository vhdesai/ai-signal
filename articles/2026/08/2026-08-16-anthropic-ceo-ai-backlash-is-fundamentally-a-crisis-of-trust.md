# Anthropic CEO: AI Backlash Is 'Fundamentally a Crisis of Trust'

Dario Amodei responded to investor Gavin Baker's criticism (that Amodei's safety warnings have fueled anti-AI backlash) by arguing the public negativity is "fundamentally a crisis of trust" in companies, governments, and the tech industry — "just the latest iteration of it." Amodei said the most accurate criticism of AI companies is that "we haven't yet delivered on our big promises to benefit the world" and that actually curing cancer would change opinion more than messaging. He defended Anthropic's regulatory proposals as deliberately designed to "disadvantage frontier AI companies while *advantaging* smaller competitors" and argued AI "structurally tends to concentrate power." 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/anthropic-ceo-says-ai-backlash-is-fundamentally-a-crisis-of-trust/
