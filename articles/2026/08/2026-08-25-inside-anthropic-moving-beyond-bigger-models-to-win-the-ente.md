# Inside Anthropic: Moving Beyond Bigger Models to Win the Enterprise

Anthropic executives Eric Kauderer-Abrams and Jonathan Pelosi describe Claude shifting from assistant use cases toward end-to-end enterprise workflows shaped by data, agents and governance. The emphasis falls on deployment reliability and controls rather than raw model scale. It is a notable convergence: the three leading labs are now competing on workflow ownership and trust posture, not parameter counts.
