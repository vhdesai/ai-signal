# Nvidia pays $6 billion to license Poolside's AI model software

Nvidia agreed to pay $6 billion to license Poolside's "Model Factory" model-building infrastructure, invest a further $1 billion in the company, and extend offers to more than 100 of its engineers. The staff will work on Nvidia's open-weight Nemotron family, extending the company beyond silicon into the software that produces models. Structured as a license rather than an acquisition, the deal is a bet that cheap, capable open-weight coding models expand GPU demand rather than cannibalize it. Follow-on analysis published August 26 frames it as a hedge against low-cost models absorbing a growing share of inference workloads.
