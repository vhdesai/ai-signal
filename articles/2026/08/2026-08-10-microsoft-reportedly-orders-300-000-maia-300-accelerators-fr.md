# Microsoft Reportedly Orders 300,000+ Maia 300 Accelerators from TSMC

Microsoft is reported to be in talks with TSMC to produce more than 300,000 Maia 300 AI accelerators for 2027 delivery, with an unveiling expected this fall. Maia 200 is already deployed in Azure data centers while Maia 300 remains in design, and Microsoft hopes large Azure customers — including Anthropic — will adopt the parts. The move is a direct attempt to reduce Nvidia dependence, though reporting notes Nvidia still controls roughly 60% of TSMC's advanced packaging queue, which is the real constraint on any second source.
