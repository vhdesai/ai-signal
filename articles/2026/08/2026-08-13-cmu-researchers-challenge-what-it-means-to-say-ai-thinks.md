# CMU Researchers Challenge What It Means to Say AI “Thinks”

Published in IEEE Annals of the History of Computing, arguing anthropomorphic AI vocabulary rests on decades of “strategic ambiguity.” Benchmarks like MMLU and Humanity’s Last Exam measure classification accuracy more than human-style understanding. A caution against treating benchmark scores as capability proof in procurement — especially timely as three frontier models shipped in 36 hours, each leading with benchmarks. CMU What to Watch * Anthropic’s IPO filing and whether ~$2T firms up or retreats under public-market scrutiny. * DeepSeek price increases (effective Aug 16) and whether cost-driven users migrate workloads. * OpenAI organizational stability — the Wiz-pedigreed CRO may stabilize or accelerate further turnover. * Nvidia’s GPU residual-value guarantee — watch for regulatory or rating-agency attention. * Flock Safety’s guardrails as a template for other AI surveillance vendors under public pressure. * Ultrafast tier expansion and whether Cerebras sustains the throughput advantage over Nvidia inference.
