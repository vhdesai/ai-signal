# OpenAI CRO Dresser Departs After 8 Months; Replaced by Former Wiz COO

OpenAI’s CRO Denise Dresser is leaving after just eight months, continuing rapid senior-leadership turnover as the company approaches a potential IPO. Her replacement is Dali Rajic, formerly president and COO of Wiz (acquired by Google for $32B). At Wiz, Rajic oversaw revenue growth from ~$200M to nearly $2B and is credited with closing eight-figure enterprise deals in regulated industries. The hire signals cybersecurity expertise is becoming central to AI go-to-market strategy. For enterprise accounts, the CRO transition introduces near-term relationship risk, but the Wiz pedigree suggests OpenAI is orienting sales around the security-first posture large regulated buyers now require.
