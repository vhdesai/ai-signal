# OpenAI Buys Tens of Thousands of Mac Minis and Mac Studios to Train Computer-Use Agents

OpenAI has purchased tens of thousands of Apple Mac mini and Mac Studio machines to run reinforcement-learning workloads for computer-use agents — memory-bound, trial-and-error tasks distinct from GPU-heavy frontier pretraining. Reporting indicates the buying wave is contributing to supply shortages and helps explain Apple’s accelerated M6/M5 Ultra desktop refresh. Anthropic is said to be renting comparable Mac capacity through AWS.
