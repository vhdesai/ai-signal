# Waymo opens Dallas robotaxi service to all riders

Waymo removed the waitlist for its Dallas robotaxi service, opening access to any rider using the app after an initial restricted period. The move follows its scaling pattern in Phoenix, Los Angeles, and San Francisco and marks an important commercial milestone outside California. The relevance is practical: robotaxi operators are moving from technical pilots toward market-by-market demand generation and operational learning.
