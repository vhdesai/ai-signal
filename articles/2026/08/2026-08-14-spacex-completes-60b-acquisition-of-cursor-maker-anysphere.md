# SpaceX Completes $60B Acquisition of Cursor Maker Anysphere

SpaceX closed its all-stock acquisition of Anysphere (Cursor) — the largest startup acquisition on record at ~$60B. Cursor’s team moves into SpaceXAI, working across Grok and Cursor product lines. The deal consolidates a leading AI coding surface, a frontier model team, and a compute-heavy parent under one owner. Enterprises on Cursor should expect roadmap and data-governance questions as the product folds into the Grok stack — particularly around how SpaceX operational data trains future models. 9to5Mac
