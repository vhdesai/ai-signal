# OpenAI Moves to Cut Cursor Off From Its Models After SpaceX Acquisition

OpenAI notified SpaceX it intends to wind down the contract supplying its models to Cursor, proposing a shutoff date of November 12, 2026. The company cited an inability to be confident SpaceX will honor its terms of service, referencing prior contract breaches by Musk-controlled entities and Musk's sworn admission that xAI violated OpenAI's terms. OpenAI also tied the decision to accountability for its upcoming model, Astra, which it will not supply to Cursor. Enterprises with coding-assistant standardization on Cursor should treat model-provider continuity as a live procurement risk. https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex/
