# AWS Broadens Bedrock Model Roster With Anthropic, Meta and OpenAI Models

Amazon shares rose as AWS expanded the set of third-party models available to customers, adding Anthropic, Meta and OpenAI models. The move reinforces Bedrock’s multi-model strategy at a moment when enterprise buyers are explicitly optimizing for model portability. Management again pointed to AI workloads and Amazon’s custom-silicon program as the principal drivers of AWS growth.
