# AWS and Nvidia to deploy two million additional GPUs across 2027–2028

AWS and Nvidia announced a partnership expansion covering two million additional Nvidia GPUs in 2027 and 2028, alongside next-generation infrastructure for agentic and physical AI. The scope includes new CPU, networking and robotics work. AWS framed the commitment as meeting demand from frontier labs, large enterprises and government customers — a notable counterweight to the custom-silicon narrative landing the same day.
