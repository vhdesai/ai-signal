# Bill Gates calls for "human reserved" jobs and an AI/robot tax

In a roughly 6,000-word essay, Gates argued governments are unprepared for the AI labor transition and proposed designating certain occupations "human reserved" — caregiving, delivering terminal diagnoses, childcare, jury service and parts of teaching and mental health. He revived AI-token and robot taxes and called for new institutions to manage the shift. He suggested an extreme version could reserve up to 40% of jobs.
