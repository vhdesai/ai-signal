# Memory Stocks Surge on AI Demand; 'Perps' Derivatives Amplify AI Stock Moves

The WSJ Markets A.M. newsletter highlights two converging forces in AI-related financial markets: memory chip stocks are surging on continued AI infrastructure demand (validating hyperscaler capex narratives), while a new class of derivatives called "perps" (perpetual futures) is amplifying price movements in AI equities. The combination creates a feedback loop where strong fundamental demand in AI infrastructure attracts speculative capital through leveraged instruments, potentially magnifying both rallies and corrections. For CFOs and investors, the emergence of AI-specific derivatives instruments adds a new layer of volatility risk that didn't exist in previous technology cycles.
