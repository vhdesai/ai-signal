# Cummins Lands Largest-Ever Battery Storage Contract for a U.S. AI Data Center

Cummins announced its biggest battery energy storage systems contract to date, supplying a utility-scale BESS deployment for a major U.S. data center project. The deal is explicitly framed around rising AI-driven power demand and grid-interconnection constraints. Storage is increasingly the gating factor for AI capacity siting, not chip supply — a shift executives planning multi-year compute commitments should factor into location and cost models.
