# Musk Says Grok 'Can Earn You Money' as xAI Pushes Beyond Chat

Elon Musk posted on X that Grok can earn you money, without providing specifics, drawing attention as xAI pushes Grok beyond chat into automation. Reporting framed the remark as a commercial repositioning of Grok's existing bot functionality on X, letting creators and businesses automate customer questions, content and workflows. It was not a new product announcement, nor a promise that xAI pays users directly. The source is a smaller outlet and the claim remains unelaborated by xAI.
