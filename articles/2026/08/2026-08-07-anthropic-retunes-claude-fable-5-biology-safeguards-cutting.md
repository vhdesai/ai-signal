# Anthropic retunes Claude Fable 5 biology safeguards, cutting blocked queries ~85%

Anthropic reduced biology-related "fallbacks"—where queries are silently handed off to weaker models—by roughly 85%. Users now get direct answers on lab reports, symptom explanation, and biology education. Restrictions remain on virology, toxicology, and molecular design. The change arrives the same week as Stanford's AI-designed virus research, illustrating the tightening usefulness-versus-biosecurity tradeoff.
