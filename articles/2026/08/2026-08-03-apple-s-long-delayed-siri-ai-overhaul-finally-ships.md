# Apple's long-delayed Siri AI overhaul finally ships

Apple's revamped, AI-driven Siri is now rolling out, delivering the more capable assistant the company promised more than a year ago. TechCrunch's read is that the upgrade is competent but arrives when a capable assistant is table stakes rather than a differentiator. The muted reception underscores how far Apple trails frontier-lab assistants even after closing core functionality gaps. ENTERPRISE
