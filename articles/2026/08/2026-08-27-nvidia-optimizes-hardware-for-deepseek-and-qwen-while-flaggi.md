# Nvidia optimizes hardware for DeepSeek and Qwen while flagging China-model restriction risk

Nvidia disclosed a local AI initiative with optimizations for leading open models including DeepSeek's V4 Flash and Alibaba's Qwen 3.8, positioning its stack as the default runtime for open-weight models regardless of origin. Simultaneously, an SEC filing tied to its second-quarter results warned that potential U.S. restrictions limiting its ability to support applications built on Chinese-origin models such as DeepSeek, Qwen, or Kimi could be material to the business. The company argues optimization keeps developers on the American technology stack; lawmakers increasingly read it as amplifying Chinese model adoption. This is now a live board-level tension for anyone standardizing on open-weight models. Academic Research RESEARCHPROTEIN DESIGN
