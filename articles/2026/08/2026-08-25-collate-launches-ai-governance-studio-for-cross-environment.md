# Collate launches AI Governance Studio for cross-environment AI oversight

Collate Inc. announced AI Governance Studio, a capability intended to let organizations govern AI systems regardless of where they run — across clouds, on-premises, and third-party model providers. The product targets the gap between rapid agent deployment and the controls needed to satisfy audit, risk, and regulatory review. It joins a crowded field of AI governance tooling launched in 2026.
