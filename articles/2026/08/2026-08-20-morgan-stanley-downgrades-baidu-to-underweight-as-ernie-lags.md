# Morgan Stanley Downgrades Baidu to Underweight as ERNIE Lags

Following Baidu's Aug 18 Q2 earnings miss, with revenue down for a fifth straight quarter and net income off 68%, Morgan Stanley analyst Gary Yu issued the stock's first major Underweight rating and cut his price target 38.5% to $80. Advertising revenue fell 19% year over year, an eighth consecutive quarterly decline, while AI-cloud revenue grew 25%. CEO Robin Li acknowledged on the earnings call that Baidu needs to bring its ERNIE model back to the top tier. The downgrade is a notable instance of model competitiveness being priced directly into equity research.
