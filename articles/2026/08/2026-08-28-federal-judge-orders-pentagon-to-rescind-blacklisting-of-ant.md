# Federal Judge Orders Pentagon to Rescind Blacklisting of Anthropic

A federal judge ruled the Trump administration was wrong to label Anthropic a supply-chain risk, calling the designation "unlawful retaliation" and ordering the Pentagon to rescind it. The ruling restores Anthropic's eligibility for government contracts and creates precedent for how frontier labs are treated in the national-security procurement process.
