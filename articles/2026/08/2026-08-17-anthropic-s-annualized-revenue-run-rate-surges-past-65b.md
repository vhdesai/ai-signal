# Anthropic’s annualized revenue run rate surges past $65B

Anthropic told investors its annualized revenue run rate exceeded $65 billion at the end of July, roughly a sevenfold increase year over year and up from about $47 billion in May. The figure was first reported by Bloomberg and independently confirmed by CNBC. For enterprise buyers, the acceleration signals continued pricing power and sustained investment in the Claude model line; for competitors, it raises the bar on commercial traction ahead of an anticipated public listing. techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/anthropics-annualized-revenue-surges-to-65b
