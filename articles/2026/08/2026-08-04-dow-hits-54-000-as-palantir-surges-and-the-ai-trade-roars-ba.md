# Dow Hits 54,000 as Palantir Surges and the AI Trade Roars Back

The Dow Jones Industrial Average crossed 54,000 for the first time as stocks rallied broadly, with Palantir shares surging on blockbuster enterprise AI sales and the broader AI trade regaining momentum after weeks of rotation anxiety. Oil dropped on signals of an Iran deal, further boosting risk appetite. The rally caps a remarkable turnaround from the post-earnings volatility that saw Apple fall 7% and the Situational Awareness fund collapse. For technology executives, the market message is clear: investors are rewarding AI companies that demonstrate tangible revenue growth and operational leverage, while punishing those that cannot articulate a clear path from AI spending to AI revenue.
