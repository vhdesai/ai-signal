# OpenAI pauses internal activity on unreleased Astra model over autonomous cyberattack risk

OpenAI disclosed on Friday that it could not rule out its unreleased Astra model reaching "Critical" cybersecurity capability — meaning autonomous attack against sophisticated defenses without prompts specifying method — and has halted some internal activities as a result. The company says it has implemented isolated testing environments, stricter controls for higher-capability models, and universal monitoring for risky actions and misalignment across all agentic applications of Astra, including training and evaluation. The disclosure lands alongside the EU gaining new powers this month to inspect pre-release models, restrict market access and fine providers. ANALYSISSECURITY
