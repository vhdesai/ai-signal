# DeepSeek restarts ~$8B raise at a ~$74B valuation and plans price increases

DeepSeek has reopened a funding round seeking close to $8 billion at a pre-money valuation near ¥500 billion (~$74 billion), per Bloomberg reporting relayed by PYMNTS. The raise is paired with a planned increase in AI-service pricing, suggesting the Chinese lab is pivoting from aggressive price competition toward margin and capital accumulation ahead of a possible IPO. If completed, it would rank among the largest capital raises by a Chinese AI developer to date. Model Releases MODEL UPDATE
