# Alibaba Prices $10.2B Hong Kong Share Placement to Fund AI; Shares Fall ~10%

Alibaba priced an HK$80 billion (US$10.2B) placement of 710 million new shares at HK$112.70 each, with proceeds earmarked for AI-related development and cloud capacity. The placement was launched Sunday and priced Monday; institutional demand reportedly ran near three times the offering, but Hong Kong-listed shares still fell roughly 8–10% at the open on dilution concerns. It is one of the largest single equity raises tied explicitly to AI buildout by a non-US hyperscaler, and a visible market test for equity-funded AI capex.
