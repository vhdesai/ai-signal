# OpenAI reveals its next major model “Astra” inside a post claiming ten decade-old math advances

OpenAI used a blog post about solving ten long-standing mathematics problems to quietly disclose that the results “were achieved by an internal version of Astra, our next major model.” The low-key reveal — a research claim doubling as a model teaser — drew immediate attention for both the mathematical claims and the strategic timing. The model itself has not been released, and the math results await independent verification.
