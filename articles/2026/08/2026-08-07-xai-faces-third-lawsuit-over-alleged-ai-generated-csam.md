# xAI faces third lawsuit over alleged AI-generated CSAM

Potts Law Firm announced a third civil lawsuit against xAI, the company behind Grok, on behalf of a family alleging that the platform was used to generate illegal AI-generated child sexual abuse material from a child’s real photographs. The filing states that additional families continue to come forward with similar allegations. These are unproven claims in a civil complaint and are reported here strictly as a legal and policy development. The cluster of filings adds to mounting liability pressure on image-generation platforms.
