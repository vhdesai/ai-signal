# Anthropic prepares a record IPO that could top SpaceX’s debut

Anthropic is preparing to file publicly as soon as the end of this month, in a listing that could match or exceed SpaceX’s record ~$75–86B debut. Analysts have floated valuations above $2T against a private mark near $965B. Anthropic declined to comment, so this remains a planning report rather than a confirmed filing.
