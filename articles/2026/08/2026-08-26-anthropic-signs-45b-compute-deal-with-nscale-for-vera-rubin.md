# Anthropic Signs $45B Compute Deal with Nscale for Vera Rubin Chips

Anthropic signed a ~$45B, six-year deal with British infrastructure company Nscale for Nvidia Vera Rubin compute, expected online late 2027. This adds to: $10B Volta deal, $5B AMD deal, $1.25B/month from SpaceX, 5GW Amazon expansion, and expanded Google/Broadcom deals — making Anthropic the most aggressive compute acquirer among frontier labs.
