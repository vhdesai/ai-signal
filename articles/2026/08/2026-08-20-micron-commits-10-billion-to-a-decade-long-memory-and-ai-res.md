# Micron Commits $10 Billion to a Decade-Long Memory and AI Research Institute

Micron announced Micron Research Labs, a U.S.-based long-horizon research institution in Boise, backed by a $10B commitment over ten years. The scope centers on memory architecture and AI systems research.
