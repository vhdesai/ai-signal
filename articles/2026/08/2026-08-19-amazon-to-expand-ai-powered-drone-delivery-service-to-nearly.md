# Amazon to Expand AI-Powered Drone Delivery Service to Nearly 500 Locales

Amazon is expanding its drone delivery service to nearly 500 locations, using AI-powered autonomous navigation and routing systems. The expansion represents a major scaling milestone for commercial AI-driven autonomous delivery, moving from pilot programs to a system operating at significant geographic breadth. The company's drone AI handles real-time obstacle avoidance, weather assessment, and delivery optimization.
