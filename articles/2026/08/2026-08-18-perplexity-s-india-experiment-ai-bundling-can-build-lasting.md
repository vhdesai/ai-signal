# Perplexity's India Experiment: AI Bundling Can Build Lasting Revenue

Perplexity's 12-month free offer via Indian telecom Airtel (360M eligible) generated 56M downloads and peaked at 22M MAU. After the offer closed: India revenue rose ~60%, daily IAP revenue climbed 9–27% above baselines, and monthly net mobile revenue grew from $34K (Jan 2025) to $156K (Jul 2026). MARKETS APPLE
