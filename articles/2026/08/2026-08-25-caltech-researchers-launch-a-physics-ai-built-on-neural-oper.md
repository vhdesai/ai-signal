# Caltech researchers launch a physics AI built on neural operators rather than Transformers

Caltech's Anima Anandkumar and Benedikt Jenik unveiled Accelerated Understanding Inc, an enterprise physics AI using neural operators instead of Transformer architectures, which the founders say ingested 5 trillion data points in a single prompt during testing. Target applications include chip design optimization, robotics, weather prediction and geological analysis. The claimed context handling is vendor-reported and has not been independently benchmarked. If the approach holds up under third-party evaluation, it would support the broader argument that the next capability step comes from architecture rather than scale.
