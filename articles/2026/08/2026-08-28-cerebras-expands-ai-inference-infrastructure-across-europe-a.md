# Cerebras Expands AI-Inference Infrastructure Across Europe and Canada

Cerebras is expanding inference capacity across Europe and Canada to meet global demand, positioning itself as a high-speed alternative to Nvidia- and Microsoft-hosted inference. The expansion targets latency-sensitive and sovereignty-sensitive workloads. Coverage is currently single-source and analyst-oriented, so treat specifics as provisional.
