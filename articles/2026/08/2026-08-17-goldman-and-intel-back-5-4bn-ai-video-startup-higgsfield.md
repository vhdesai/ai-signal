# Goldman and Intel back $5.4bn AI video startup Higgsfield

Higgsfield closed a $400M Series B at a $5.4B valuation, with Goldman Sachs and Intel joining alongside existing backers — up from a $1.3B valuation in January. The company positions itself as an AI video and image platform for professional creators, brands, agencies, and studios. The round is a signal that generative video, which cooled through 2025, is being re-rated as production workflows mature. cityam.com/goldman-and-intel-back-5-4bn-ai-video-startup
