# OpenAI Passes $40B Run Rate as Revenue Chief Exits Ahead of Expected IPO

OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate has reached $40 billion, roughly double its rate at end-2025, driven by coding tools, subscriptions, and advertising. CFO Sarah Friar told investors that enterprise is now a bigger business than consumer — a material shift in revenue mix that signals where roadmap and support investment will concentrate. The figure lands as the company's Chief Revenue Officer departed after roughly eight months, the latest in a sustained run of senior exits; Dali Rajic, formerly president and COO of Wiz, was named incoming CRO. Analysts caution that a run rate is not booked annual revenue, a distinction that will matter considerably under public-market disclosure. The combination of accelerating revenue and an unstable executive bench is the central diligence question for anyone underwriting an OpenAI listing.
