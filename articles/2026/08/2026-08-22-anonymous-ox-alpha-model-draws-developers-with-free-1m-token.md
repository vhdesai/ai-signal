# Anonymous "Ox Alpha" Model Draws Developers with Free 1M-Token Access

A stealth model called Ox Alpha appeared on OpenRouter from an unidentified third-party provider — free to use, with a context window just over one million tokens and support for text, image, and video input. Adoption has been rapid on the strength of coding and long-horizon agent performance, with the provider reportedly supplying capacity on the order of 100 trillion tokens per day. Attribution theories have converged on nothing: tokenizer analysis and prior-release patterns point in conflicting directions. The material issue is not identity but terms — OpenRouter's listing states prompts and completions are retained by the anonymous provider, which makes the model unusable under EU data-protection requirements and a clear policy exception for any regulated enterprise workload.
