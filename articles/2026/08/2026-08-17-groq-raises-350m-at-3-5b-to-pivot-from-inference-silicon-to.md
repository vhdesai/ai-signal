# Groq Raises $350M at $3.5B to Pivot From Inference Silicon to Neocloud

Groq closed $350 million as it repositions from a custom AI-chip vendor to a capacity provider, expanding an Nvidia-powered data center footprint. The pivot is notable because it concedes that differentiated inference silicon alone has been insufficient to hold share against the incumbent accelerator stack. For buyers, it adds another mid-tier capacity supplier to the inference market while validating that custom silicon's value may sit in cloud delivery rather than standalone chip sales.
