# AI May Extend Fossil Fuel Dominance More Than Data Center Power Demand

A new Nature paper argues AI’s most profound energy impact won’t come from data center power consumption but from what it’s used for — particularly boosting oil-and-gas productivity. AI-driven efficiency gains in fossil fuel production could push global CO₂ emissions up by 0.47 to 1.8 billion tons per year, “about the emissions of Mexico annually” at the lower end.
