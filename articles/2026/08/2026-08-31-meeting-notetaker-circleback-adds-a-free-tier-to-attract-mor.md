# Meeting notetaker Circleback adds a free tier to attract more customers

TechCrunch reports that Circleback is adding a free tier for its AI meeting notetaker. The move reflects continued pressure in the AI productivity market: meeting transcription and summarization are becoming table stakes, pushing vendors toward freemium acquisition, workflow integration, and differentiated enterprise controls.
