# Uber and Pony.ai to Deploy More Than 2,000 Robotaxis Across Europe

Pony.ai and Uber expanded their partnership to deploy over 2,000 autonomous vehicles across European markets, extending beyond their initial Zagreb launch into four additional cities. The scale-up puts a Chinese autonomy stack onto European public roads at meaningful volume, ahead of most Western robotaxi operators outside the US. City-by-city regulatory approval remains the gating factor rather than vehicle supply. Expect this to sharpen European scrutiny of foreign-operated autonomy data collection. cnbc.com
