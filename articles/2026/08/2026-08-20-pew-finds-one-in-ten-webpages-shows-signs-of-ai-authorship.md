# Pew finds one in ten webpages shows signs of AI authorship

In a random sample of 10,000 webpages collected in July 2026, Pew found roughly one in ten showed signs of being written or substantially edited by AI, rising above one-third among pages published after ChatGPT's debut. The study is a measured empirical anchor in a debate usually conducted with estimates. For enterprises, it raises concrete questions about the provenance of retrieval corpora, the reliability of web-grounded agents, and the growing risk of training on synthetic text.
