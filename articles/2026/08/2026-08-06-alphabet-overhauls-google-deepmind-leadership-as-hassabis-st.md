# Alphabet overhauls Google DeepMind leadership as Hassabis steps aside and Jeff Dean departs

Alphabet is restructuring Google DeepMind: Demis Hassabis is moving out of the CEO seat into a chairman/AGI-focused role, with Koray Kavukcuoglu taking over day-to-day operations, while veteran Jeff Dean is leaving after nearly three decades. The shake-up centralizes AI under Mountain View amid repeated Gemini 3.5 Pro delays and signals concern about execution speed against OpenAI and Anthropic. Analysts read it as the end of DeepMind's semi-autonomous era; watch for further senior attrition and the knock-on effect on the Gemini 4 timeline. FUNDINGCHINA
