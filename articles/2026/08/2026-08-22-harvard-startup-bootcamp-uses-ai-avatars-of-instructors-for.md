# Harvard startup bootcamp uses AI avatars of instructors for feedback

Harvard Business School's Foundry bootcamp is using AI avatars created by HeyGen to provide individual feedback during practice pitches and board meetings. The avatars supplement weekly live sessions and appear to be popular with participants despite concerns about realism and faculty identity.
