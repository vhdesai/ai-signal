# Anthropic to watermark all Claude output for EU AI Act compliance

Anthropic will apply embedded text watermarks and signed provenance metadata to Claude outputs, including files and images, to satisfy EU AI Act transparency requirements effective August 2. The scheme applies to EU models launched on or after that date, with older models backfilled, and Anthropic says it will help third parties detect the marks. OpenAI, Meta and Microsoft have made parallel commitments.
