# MIT Committee Report Calls for Alternative Grading and More Social Learning

An MIT student–faculty–staff committee concluded that AI is upending foundational elements of an MIT education, noting that models can produce credible solutions to nearly any written assignment, including essays, proofs, mathematics, and coding. The report urges rethinking assessment through alternative grading and expanding collaborative, social learning. It is an early signal of how technical institutions will restructure credentialing around capable models.
