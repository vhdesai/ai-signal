# OpenAI Pauses Astra Model Development After Reaching Critical Cybersecurity Threshold

OpenAI disclosed that its unreleased Astra model triggered the "critical cybersecurity threshold" under its Preparedness Framework — meaning it could independently identify and execute cyberattacks against well-protected real-world systems. OpenAI paused internal activities involving Astra that don't meet stricter guardrails and is coordinating with government agencies. The disclosure is notable for its voluntariness: companies rarely announce holds on pre-release products publicly. SAFETY INCIDENT
