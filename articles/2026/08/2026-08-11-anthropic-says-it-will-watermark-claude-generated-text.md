# Anthropic says it will watermark Claude-generated text

Anthropic said it will watermark text generated by Claude and other models to comply with EU AI Act transparency requirements. The watermark will apply at the model level, including Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, Claude Tag, and platform API outputs, and Anthropic says it will also extend support to older models. The announcement shows provenance moving from optional platform labeling to a regulatory compliance layer for generated text and files.
