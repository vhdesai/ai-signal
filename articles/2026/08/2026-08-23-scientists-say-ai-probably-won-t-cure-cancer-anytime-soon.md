# Scientists Say AI Probably Won’t Cure Cancer Anytime Soon

Prominent researchers caution that AI’s contribution to cancer treatment remains modest despite marketing claims. Drug discovery pipelines remain long and regulatory approval slow regardless of computational acceleration. The story surfaces the risk of over-promising — particularly for boards and investors pricing in near-term health-sector AI revenue.
