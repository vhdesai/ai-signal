# "I Let Google's Personal Intelligence Access My Life — And It's a Huge Help"

The WSJ daily digest led with a first-person review of Google's Personal Intelligence product, which integrates deeply into a user's digital life across email, calendar, files, and communications. The reviewer finds it a "huge help," suggesting that personal AI assistants are maturing from novelty to genuine productivity tools — though the level of data access required raises significant privacy questions.
