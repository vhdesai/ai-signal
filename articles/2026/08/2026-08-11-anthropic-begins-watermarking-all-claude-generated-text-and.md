# Anthropic Begins Watermarking All Claude-Generated Text and Images

All models released after August 2 automatically embed an imperceptible watermark in text and C2PA metadata in images, under the EU AI Act’s Transparency Code. Applied at the model level across API, Claude Code, and Cowork — users cannot opt out. The text signal reportedly survives copy-paste and light editing. Drew sharp backlash from developers. Notably, OpenAI has had this capability but chose not to deploy it. First frontier-scale model-level provenance implementation. TechCrunch | Forbes GOVERNANCE
