# White House authorizes vetted private firms to run offensive cyber operations

A presidential memorandum directs the National Coordination Center to establish a program letting vetted U.S. companies conduct cyber-surveillance and cyber-effects operations against foreign cyber-enabled transnational criminal groups, under joint DOJ and DHS oversight. Participants face contractual vetting, detailed operating procedures, full legal-compliance obligations, $1M bonds, and exclusions on high-consequence outcomes. It is a material expansion of private-sector authority in national cyber policy and will draw both security-vendor interest and civil-liberties scrutiny. https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/expanding-capabilities-to-combat-transnational-cyber-enabled-crime/ POLICY
