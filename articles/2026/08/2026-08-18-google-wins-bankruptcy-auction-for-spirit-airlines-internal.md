# Google wins bankruptcy auction for Spirit Airlines' internal data at $10M

Google outbid rivals to acquire a Spirit Airlines corpus reported to include roughly 100 million emails, 500 million Teams messages, 30 million lines of code, and 175,000 employee records dating to 1986, with PII to be scrubbed by a third party. Google says the data will support product and AI model development; passenger and loyalty data are excluded. The transaction establishes a live market price for distressed enterprise data estates — a consideration that now belongs in M&A and wind-down planning, not just IT asset disposal. https://www.techspot.com/news/113526-google-pays-10-million-100-million-spirit-airlines.html
