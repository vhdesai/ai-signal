# Groq raises $350M Series A at a $3.5B valuation, pivoting from chips to neocloud

Groq raised $350M at a $3.5B valuation to fund a shift away from designing its own inference silicon toward operating an Nvidia-powered inference cloud. The company plans to expand its data center footprint materially over the next two years. The pivot is notable because it concedes that merchant-silicon economics are harder than capacity resale in the current market.
