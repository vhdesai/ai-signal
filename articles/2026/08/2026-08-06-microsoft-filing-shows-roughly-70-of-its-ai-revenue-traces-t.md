# Microsoft filing shows roughly 70% of its AI revenue traces to OpenAI

A new Microsoft filing, reported by Bloomberg, disclosed that Microsoft booked $24.1 billion in revenue from OpenAI in the year to June — by Bloomberg’s estimate about 70% of its AI-unit sales, the clearest figure yet on how concentrated that business is. On the same day, CoreAI chief Jay Parikh told staff in a memo (first reported by 404 Media and CNBC) to default to OpenAI’s flagship GPT-5.6 Sol inside GitHub Copilot to extract more value from Microsoft’s token investment. The disclosure lands despite Microsoft’s own MAI models, 11,000+ hosted models, and a $5 billion bet on Anthropic. Against total company revenue the OpenAI figure is under 10%, but within the AI unit the dependence is stark. INDUSTRY IPO
