# 17. Stanford HAI: governing “world models” is AI’s next big policy challenge

In what HAI calls the first comprehensive policy brief on world models — AI that builds internal representations of physical environments to predict the consequences of actions — authors Fei-Fei Li, Amy Zegart, and Russell Wald argue these systems are harder to govern than LLMs because their errors can cause physical harm. They call for NIST-led measurement science, procurement rules requiring independent testing, and shared data infrastructure via the NAIRR. TrendingStanford HAI
