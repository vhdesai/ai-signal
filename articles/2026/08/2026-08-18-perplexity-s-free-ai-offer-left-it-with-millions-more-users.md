# Perplexity's free AI offer left it with millions more users in India

Perplexity's India revenue rose approximately 60% after its free Airtel-bundled offer ended for new users, even as download volumes declined. The result suggests the promotional cohort converted at a meaningful rate rather than churning entirely. It is an early data point that emerging-market AI distribution deals can produce durable paid users, not just vanity install counts. Model Releases MODELOPEN WEIGHTS
