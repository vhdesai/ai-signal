# Nvidia weighs Perplexity stake at $30B+ as the startup’s ARR tops $750M

Nvidia is in talks to invest in Perplexity through an equity round that would value the AI search company at more than $30 billion, up roughly 50% from last September’s $20 billion mark. Perplexity’s annualized revenue has climbed above $750 million from under $250 million at the start of 2026. Nvidia had previously weighed a technology-licensing arrangement and staff hires before pursuing the equity route; both companies declined to comment.
