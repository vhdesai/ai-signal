# Apple Surprise-Launches Mac mini on New M6 Chip and Mac Studio on M5 Max / M5 Ultra

Apple unexpectedly announced a Mac mini built on the all-new M6 chip starting at $899, alongside Mac Studio models on M5 Max and M5 Ultra at $2,499 and $5,499. Apple positioned the M6 — reportedly its first 2nm silicon — as a significant on-device AI step aimed at always-on agentic computing. The move pushes credible local inference further down the price curve for developers and knowledge workers.
