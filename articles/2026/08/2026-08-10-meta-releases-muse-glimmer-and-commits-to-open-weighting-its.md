# Meta releases Muse Glimmer and commits to open-weighting its most powerful model

Meta launched Muse Glimmer, a compact open-weight model distilled from its closed Muse Spark 1.2 system and small enough to run on a single consumer GPU. Mark Zuckerberg paired the release with a call for Washington to lower barriers on American open-source AI so it can compete with Chinese open-weight releases, and said an open-weight version of Muse Spark will follow. The move re-establishes Meta as the open-weight counterweight to OpenAI and Anthropic after a period of closed releases.
