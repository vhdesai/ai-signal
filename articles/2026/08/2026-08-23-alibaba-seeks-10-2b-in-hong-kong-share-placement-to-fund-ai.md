# Alibaba Seeks $10.2B in Hong Kong Share Placement to Fund AI Expansion

Alibaba announced plans to raise roughly HK$80 billion ($10.2 billion) through a new share placement, its largest such move, to fund AI infrastructure, chips, data centers and model development. The company has been increasing quarterly capital spending toward roughly $10 billion as it defends its position against domestic and global rivals. The equity raise signals that Chinese platform companies are willing to dilute shareholders to sustain the AI capex cycle rather than pace it to cash flow. POLICYINFRASTRUCTURE
