# Intel raises $20B in upsized common stock offering to fund AI plans

Intel priced an upsized $20B common stock offering at $95 per share — roughly a third larger than the $15B it targeted when the deal launched Monday morning. Proceeds are earmarked for its AI and foundry roadmap, and the upsizing indicates equity demand strengthened on the back of a turnaround rally in the stock. For enterprise buyers, the raise improves the odds that a credible second-source AI silicon and advanced packaging supplier stays funded through the next capex cycle. ________________________________ FUNDING
