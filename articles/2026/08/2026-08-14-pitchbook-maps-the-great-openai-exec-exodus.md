# PitchBook Maps the Great OpenAI Exec Exodus

PitchBook has published a formal tracker mapping OpenAI’s ongoing leadership departures: former COO Lightcap (Aug 12), CRO Dresser (Aug 14), head of ethics Bakalar (Aug 11), former CPO Kevin Weil, and multiple other senior figures. The cumulative pattern is now significant enough to warrant formal analytical tracking. For enterprise buyers and investors, organizational stability at the world’s highest-valued AI company is a material risk factor. The departures cluster around the transition from research-first culture to a commercially oriented, pre-IPO operating posture.
