# SoftBank in Talks to Buy Majority Stake in Humanoid Maker 1X at $6B Valuation

SoftBank is in talks to buy a majority stake in 1X Technologies, an OpenAI-backed humanoid robot developer, at ~$6 billion. The deal extends SoftBank’s aggressive push into physical AI alongside SB Energy and Nvidia partnerships.
