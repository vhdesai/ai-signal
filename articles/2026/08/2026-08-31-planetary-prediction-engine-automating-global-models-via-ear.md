# Planetary prediction engine: Automating global models via Earth AI

Google Research introduced a planetary prediction engine that automates geospatial model development from natural-language queries. The system is positioned to reduce planetary-scale modeling from weeks of manual data curation and feature engineering to minutes, with applications in food security, disaster risk, disease tracking, and socio-economic vulnerability mapping.
