# Gartner: AI Capabilities Outpacing Cost Savings — Enterprise Spending to Rise Exponentially

Gartner predicts enterprise AI costs will rise exponentially even as per-token prices fall, because organizations are deploying AI across far more use cases than unit-cost reductions can offset. Only 35% of leaders say AI consistently delivers business outcomes (HFS Research/TCS), and only 1 in 5 organizations are prepared for autonomous AI agents (Deloitte). CIO Dive's weekender rounds up the persistent gap between AI ambition and operational reality.
