# Foxconn Reports 35% Profit Rise on AI Server Demand

Foxconn reported Q2 net income of NT$59.97B ($1.86B), beating analyst estimates, as demand for AI servers powering data centers drives growth. The world's largest contract electronics maker — Nvidia's biggest server maker — stuck to its forecast of "strong" revenue growth for 2026.
