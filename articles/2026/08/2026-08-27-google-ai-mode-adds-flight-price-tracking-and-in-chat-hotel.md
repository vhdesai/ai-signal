# Google AI Mode Adds Flight Price Tracking and In-Chat Hotel Booking

Google added flight price tracking, rewards-points comparison, and in-chat hotel booking to AI Mode in Search. The update moves AI Mode from information retrieval toward transaction execution — a direct competitive response to assistant-led commerce and a template for how agentic search will capture booking intent.
