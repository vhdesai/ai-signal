# Alibaba Plunges After Announcing $10.2 Billion Share Placement to Fund AI Push

Alibaba priced a $10.2B (approx. HK$80B) Hong Kong placement of roughly 710 million shares at a steep discount to fund its AI buildout, sending the stock down about 10–11%. It is among the largest AI-related equity raises by a Chinese company and lands at the midpoint of Alibaba's roughly $56B three-year AI spending plan. The raise follows the company's fastest cloud and AI revenue growth in 22 quarters. Chairman Joe Tsai and CEO Eddie Wu subsequently bought about US$15M of shares with personal funds, per SCMP — a confidence signal after the drop.
