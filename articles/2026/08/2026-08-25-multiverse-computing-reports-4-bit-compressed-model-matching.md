# Multiverse Computing Reports 4-Bit Compressed Model Matching Full Precision

Multiverse Computing published a compression-plus-"healing" technique in which a large language model reduced to roughly half its parameters and quantized to 4 bits reportedly matches or exceeds its full-precision baseline. If independently replicated, the practical effect is materially lower inference cost and viable on-premises or edge deployment of larger models.
