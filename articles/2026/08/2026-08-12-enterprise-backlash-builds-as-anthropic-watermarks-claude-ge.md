# Enterprise backlash builds as Anthropic watermarks Claude-generated text

Anthropic began adding machine-detectable watermarks to Claude-generated text, and users are publicly objecting that the marks will expose undisclosed AI use in workplaces and classrooms. Anthropic rolled out the policy to meet the EU AI Act's Transparency Code, which requires labeling of AI-generated content.
