# When AI art has no author: generated images often can't be traced to training data

MIT CSAIL researchers developed a method for surgically removing training examples from a model and found that at large dataset scales, removing any single image, every image by a given artist, or every photograph of a given person often leaves the generated output substantially unchanged. This has direct bearing on attribution-based copyright claims and on the technical feasibility of per-work licensing regimes.
