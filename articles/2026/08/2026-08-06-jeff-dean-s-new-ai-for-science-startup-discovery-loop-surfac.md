# Jeff Dean’s new AI-for-science startup “Discovery Loop” surfaces with pitch deck

Outgoing Google chief scientist Jeff Dean shared a pitch deck for Discovery Loop, co-founded with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals, to “automate the experimental loop” of science. Khosla Ventures and Radical Ventures would co-lead the round, with Alphabet participating. The venture concentrates elite research talent departing Google into a single new lab.
