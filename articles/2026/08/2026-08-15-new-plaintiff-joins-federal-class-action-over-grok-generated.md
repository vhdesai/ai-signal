# New plaintiff joins federal class action over Grok-generated abuse imagery

A newly disclosed plaintiff in the federal class action against xAI alleges her stepfather used Grok to convert a single photo taken when she was 11 into more than 7,000 sexually explicit images that were shared and traded online. The complaint states the stepfather selected Grok because the platform was less restrictive than other AI models, and that xAI did not respond when law enforcement requested the images and IP data that could have identified him.
