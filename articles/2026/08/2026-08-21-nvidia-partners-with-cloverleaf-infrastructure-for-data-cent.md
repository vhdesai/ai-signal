# Nvidia Partners With Cloverleaf Infrastructure for Data Center Power

Nvidia entered a strategic partnership with—and investment in—Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a developer securing large-scale powered land and grid interconnection for U.S. AI data centers. The move pushes Nvidia further upstream into the power and siting layer. Grid access, not GPU supply, is increasingly the schedule risk on new capacity. FUNDING
