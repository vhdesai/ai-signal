# OpenAI Begins Showing Ads on ChatGPT in India — Its Largest Non-US Market

OpenAI started rolling out advertising to logged-in adult users on ChatGPT Free and Go tiers in India, its largest user market outside the U.S. with more than 100 million weekly active users. The move extends the advertising program launched in Europe this week into a market where subscription conversion is structurally low. It marks a meaningful shift toward consumer-internet economics rather than pure subscription and API revenue.
