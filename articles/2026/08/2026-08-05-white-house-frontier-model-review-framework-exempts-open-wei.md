# White House frontier-model review framework exempts open-weight models

The administration's frontier-model security review framework exempts open-weight models from pre-release review and applies primarily to closed-model developers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The design creates an explicit regulatory asymmetry based on release mode rather than measured capability.
