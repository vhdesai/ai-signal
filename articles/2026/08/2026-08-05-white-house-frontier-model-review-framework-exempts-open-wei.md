# White House frontier-model review framework exempts open-weight models

The administration’s frontier-model security review framework, mandated by a June executive order, exempts open-weight models from pre-release review and is expected to apply primarily to closed-model developers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. The design creates an explicit regulatory asymmetry based on release mode rather than measured capability. Enterprises standardizing on closed frontier APIs should anticipate that vendor release timelines are now partly a function of federal review cycles. POLICY
