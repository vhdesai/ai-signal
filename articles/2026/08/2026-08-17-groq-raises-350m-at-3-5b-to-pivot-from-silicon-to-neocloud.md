# Groq Raises $350M at $3.5B to Pivot From Silicon to Neocloud

Repositions from custom AI-chip vendor to capacity provider, expanding an Nvidia-powered data center footprint. The pivot concedes that differentiated inference silicon alone has struggled against the incumbent stack. Adds another mid-tier capacity supplier to the inference market while validating that custom silicon’s value may sit in cloud delivery rather than standalone chip sales.
