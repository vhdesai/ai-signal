# Unitree’s 460% IPO Stock Pop in Shanghai

Humanoid robot maker Unitree surged 460% on its Shanghai debut, the latest extraordinary first-day pop for a Chinese tech IPO. The listing reflects investor appetite for physical AI at a moment when Chinese markets are channeling capital into embodied systems. China’s robotics IPO pipeline is now producing public comparables at scale.
