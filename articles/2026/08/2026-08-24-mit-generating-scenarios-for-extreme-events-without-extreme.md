# MIT: Generating Scenarios for Extreme Events, Without Extreme Data

MIT engineers published an algorithm that generates plausible extreme-event and worst-case scenarios — such as a severe storm's likely duration, intensity, and area of impact — without requiring historical examples of those events. The method targets the sparse-tail-data problem that limits conventional risk models.
