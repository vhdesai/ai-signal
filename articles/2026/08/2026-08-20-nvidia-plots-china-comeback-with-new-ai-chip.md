# Nvidia Plots China Comeback With New AI Chip

Nvidia plans to begin small-batch shipments of an AI chip tailored for Chinese customers by year-end, according to two employees. The move would partially restore a market Nvidia has been largely excluded from under export controls. Volumes are described as small, suggesting a compliance-constrained product rather than a return to prior China revenue levels. M&A
