# Alibaba's Qwen passes 3 billion downloads, ahead of Meta and Google

Alibaba said its open Qwen family has surpassed 3 billion cumulative downloads across platforms, with 460+ open-source models spawning more than 300,000 derivatives. Hugging Face's August 14 report independently counted ~2.05B Qwen downloads in the first seven months of 2026, versus 418M for Google and 227M for Meta.
