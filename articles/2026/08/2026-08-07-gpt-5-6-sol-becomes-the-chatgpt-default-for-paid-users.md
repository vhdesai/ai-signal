# GPT-5.6 Sol Becomes the ChatGPT Default for Paid Users

OpenAI made GPT-5.6 Sol the default for Plus and Pro users across both instant chats and deep reasoning, adding a reasoning-effort slider that lets users trade latency for deliberation. Free and Go tiers move to GPT-5.6 Luna with unlimited text chats and a dedicated “Think” control. OpenAI reports Sol produces 68% fewer factual errors than the prior instant model on high-stakes finance, medicine, and law evaluations — the metric enterprises should validate independently before relying on it in regulated workflows.
