# OpenAI to Start Showing Ads on ChatGPT Free and Go Tiers in India

OpenAI will show ads to 100M+ weekly Indian ChatGPT users on Free/Go tiers, starting with 50 brands via WPP and Omnicom. An ad manager launching next month has a ∼$7.60 daily minimum. The move expands from U.S. (February) and Europe (August). Pre-IPO revenue diversification pressure: Q2 revenue was $6.7B, up from $5.7B, but OpenAI is reportedly still unprofitable while targeting 220M paying subscribers by 2030.
