# Anthropic will apply invisible watermarks to all Claude text and image output

Anthropic is embedding invisible, machine-detectable watermarks into Claude-generated text and images, with the marks designed to survive copy-and-paste and light editing. The change is driven substantially by EU transparency obligations and applies broadly across outputs. Enterprises should assess where watermark persistence intersects with internal document workflows — content that passes through Claude may remain attributable downstream, which is useful for governance and awkward for confidential drafting.
