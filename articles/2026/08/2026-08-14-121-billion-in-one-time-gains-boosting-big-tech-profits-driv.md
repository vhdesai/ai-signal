# $121 Billion in One-Time Gains Boosting Big Tech Profits — Driven by AI Investments

Gains on investments in companies like Anthropic are inflating earnings at some of the biggest technology companies, with WSJ tallying $121 billion in one-time gains across Big Tech's recent quarterly reports. The analysis highlights how the AI boom is creating paper wealth for strategic investors well before investees reach public markets, complicating the picture of how much these companies are actually earning from core operations versus investment windfalls. For analysts and board members, this is a reminder that reported earnings at companies with large AI portfolio positions now contain a meaningful non-operating component that distorts traditional profitability metrics. As Anthropic, Databricks, and other major AI companies approach public listings, these gains will either be realized or reversed — creating earnings volatility in either direction.
