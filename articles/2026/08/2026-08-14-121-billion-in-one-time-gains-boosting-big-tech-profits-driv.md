# $121 Billion in One-Time Gains Boosting Big Tech Profits — Driven by AI Investments

Gains on investments in companies like Anthropic are inflating earnings at some of the biggest tech companies, with WSJ tallying $121 billion in one-time gains across Big Tech’s recent quarterly reports. The analysis highlights how the AI boom is creating paper wealth for strategic investors well before investees reach public markets, complicating the picture of how much Big Tech is actually earning from operations vs. investment windfalls.
