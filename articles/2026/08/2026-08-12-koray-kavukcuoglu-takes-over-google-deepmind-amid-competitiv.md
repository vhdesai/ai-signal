# Koray Kavukcuoglu takes over Google DeepMind amid competitive pressure

Koray Kavukcuoglu is now leading Google DeepMind following the recent reorganization that saw Demis Hassabis step aside and several senior scientists depart. His mandate is to keep Gemini competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic on both capability and shipping cadence. The transition lands during reported roadmap changes in Google's Gemini release plans.
