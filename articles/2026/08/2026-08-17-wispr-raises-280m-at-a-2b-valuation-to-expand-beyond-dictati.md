# Wispr raises $280M at a $2B valuation to expand beyond dictation

Wispr, known for the Wispr Flow dictation tool, closed a $280M Series B led by existing investor Menlo Ventures at a $2B valuation. The company says the capital will fund meeting-capture tooling and its own speech models as it targets a broader "voice layer" position rather than a single-feature product. Competition in AI dictation has compressed pricing, making the platform expansion a defensive as much as an offensive move. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/wispr-raises-280m-at-2b-valuation-as-it-looks-beyond-dictation/
