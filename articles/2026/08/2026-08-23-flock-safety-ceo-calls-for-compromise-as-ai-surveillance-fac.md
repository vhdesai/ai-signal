# Flock Safety CEO Calls for "Compromise" as AI Surveillance Faces Bipartisan Backlash

The Washington Post identified 46 cases of officers misusing Flock cameras (including stalking). Three House Republicans introduced a bill banning federal purchase of automated surveillance. Flock reduced default data retention from 30 to 7 days. The backlash parallels the broader anti-AI-infrastructure sentiment now affecting both parties ahead of midterms.
