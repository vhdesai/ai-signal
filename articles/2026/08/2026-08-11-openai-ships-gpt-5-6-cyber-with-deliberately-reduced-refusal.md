# OpenAI Ships GPT-5.6-Cyber With Deliberately Reduced Refusals Behind Vetted Access

OpenAI released GPT-5.6-Cyber, a fine-tune of GPT-5.6 Sol trained for vulnerability research and exploit development, with deliberately reduced refusals on dual-use security requests. It completes 95.0% of exploit-chain prompts on OpenAI's internal benchmark versus 1.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol, and surfaced two previously unknown Chrome V8 flaws including CVE-2026-15903. Access is gated behind the new "Daybreak Red" tier requiring identity verification, SSO/MFA, logging, and SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification. The governance question for CISOs is no longer capability but who is authorized to run it.
