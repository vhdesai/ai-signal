# Nvidia Enlists Six Wall Street Firms to Mobilize Over $500B for AI Infrastructure

Nvidia is partnering with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to establish independent financing platforms aimed at directing more than $500 billion of third-party capital into AI compute infrastructure. The structures are designed to turn Nvidia compute and full-stack AI infrastructure into an investable asset class, letting customers finance data centers, chips and power systems without carrying the full upfront cost. The arrangements are still being finalized. The strategic read: Nvidia is moving from dominant supplier to financial orchestrator of the buildout — which also deepens the circular-financing concerns already attached to the sector. pehub.com — Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, KKR to mobilize over $500bn
