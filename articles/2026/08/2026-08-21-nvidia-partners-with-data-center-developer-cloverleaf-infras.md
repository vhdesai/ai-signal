# Nvidia Partners With Data Center Developer Cloverleaf Infrastructure

Nvidia entered a strategic partnership with — and investment in — Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a developer focused on securing large-scale powered land and grid interconnection for U.S. AI data centers. The move pushes Nvidia further upstream into the power and siting layer of the buildout. Grid access, not GPU supply, is increasingly the schedule risk on new capacity.
