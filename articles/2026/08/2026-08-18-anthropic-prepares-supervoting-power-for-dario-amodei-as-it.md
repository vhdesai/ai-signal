# Anthropic Prepares Supervoting Power for Dario Amodei as It Readies for Mega-IPO

Anthropic is preparing to give CEO Dario Amodei and co-founders supervoting stock to insulate them from shareholder pressure ahead of its expected IPO. The company also plans to maintain nonshareholder trustees with special stock to elect the majority of board members.
