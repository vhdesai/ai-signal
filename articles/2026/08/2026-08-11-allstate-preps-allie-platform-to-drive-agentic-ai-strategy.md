# Allstate Preps Allie Platform to Drive Agentic AI Strategy

Allstate is building out its Allie platform as the backbone of its agentic AI strategy, with CEO Tom Wilson declaring the insurer has “a technology-driven strategy, not a strategy supported by technology.” The platform represents one of the most prominent enterprise agentic AI deployments by a Fortune 100 company, signaling that agentic architectures are moving from pilot to production in regulated industries.
