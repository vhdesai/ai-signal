# Anthropic details how Claude's text watermarking works under the EU AI Act

Future Claude models will embed a text watermark based on Google DeepMind's SynthID-Text method, implemented to comply with EU AI Act transparency obligations that took effect August 2. Anthropic states the watermark adds no tokens or hidden characters, carries no identifying information about users or organizations, and has no measurable effect on output quality; detection weakens on short passages, code, and lightly edited human text. A watermark-detection API is planned but not yet released, and files carry C2PA content credentials instead. Around 190 signatories to the EU Code of Practice will implement comparable marking, so provenance checking is becoming a cross-provider default. https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark EXPORT CONTROLS
