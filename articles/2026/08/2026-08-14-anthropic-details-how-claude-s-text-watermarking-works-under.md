# Anthropic Details How Claude's Text Watermarking Works Under the EU AI Act

Future Claude models embed a text watermark based on Google DeepMind's SynthID-Text method, implemented to comply with EU AI Act transparency obligations effective August 2. Anthropic states the watermark adds no tokens or hidden characters, carries no identifying information about users or organizations, and has no measurable effect on output quality; detection weakens on short passages, code, and lightly edited text. A detection API is planned but not yet released, and files carry C2PA content credentials. Around 190 signatories to the EU Code of Practice will implement comparable marking — provenance checking is becoming a cross-provider default.
