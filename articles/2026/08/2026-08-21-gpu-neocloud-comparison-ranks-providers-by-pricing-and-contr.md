# GPU neocloud comparison ranks providers by pricing and contracted power

MarkTechPost published a buyer-oriented comparison of CoreWeave, Nebius, Lambda, Crusoe, and Groq using published pricing, contracted power, hardware roadmaps, contract structures, and independent quality ratings. The analysis highlights how neocloud competition is moving beyond hourly GPU pricing into power access, capacity commitments, deployment quality, and roadmap credibility. For AI infrastructure buyers, the implication is that GPU procurement should be evaluated as a capacity-and-risk decision, not just a spot-price comparison. MARKET RISKAI DEBT
