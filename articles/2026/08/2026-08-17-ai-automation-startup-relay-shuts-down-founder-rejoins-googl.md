# AI Automation Startup Relay Shuts Down — Founder Rejoins Google as VP of Chrome Product

Relay, the AI workflow automation tool backed by a16z that aimed to become "the new Zapier," is shutting down. Founder and CEO Jacob Bank will rejoin Google as VP of Product for Chrome, leading product and developer relations. Bank said Chrome is "a perfect place to collaborate with agents" and teased "really ambitious plans to help you work with AI in Chrome." The move follows Google's steady AI integration into Chrome via Gemini, which recently surpassed 1 billion users. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/ai-automation-startup-relay-shuts-down-staff-joins-googles-chrome-team/
