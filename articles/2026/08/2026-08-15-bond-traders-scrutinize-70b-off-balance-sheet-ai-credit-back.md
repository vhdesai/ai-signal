# Bond Traders Scrutinize ~$70B Off-Balance-Sheet AI Credit Backstops

~$70B in residual-value guarantees sit off-balance-sheet across Nvidia, Broadcom ($35B package for Anthropic), and Meta. Analysts describe them as nearly costless during expansion but strongly pro-cyclical in a downturn. Evaluating AI exposure now requires reading commitment footnotes, not just capex guidance. The guarantees concentrate tail risk in exactly the scenario where realization would hurt most. Yahoo Finance ANALYSIS
