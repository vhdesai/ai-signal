# Groq Raises $350M at $3.5B Valuation, Completes Pivot from Chipmaker to Neocloud

Groq raised $350M led by Disruptive (with planned Nvidia participation) as it formally pivots from AI chipmaker to neocloud provider. The $3.5B valuation is down from $6.9B last September, but Groq frames it as a new baseline for the "post-Nvidia-licensing-deal version" of the company. After losing its founder and top talent to Nvidia's $20B licensing deal in December, Groq now operates 13 data centers across four continents serving 6M+ developers and plans to scale from 54MW to 200MW+ by 2027. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/groq-raises-350m-to-fuel-its-pivot-from-ai-chips-to-neocloud/
