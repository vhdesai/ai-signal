# Jeff Dean’s Discovery Loop Startup Raising at 11-Figure Valuation

Legendary Google engineer Jeff Dean is raising a massive round for his new startup Discovery Loop at a billion-dollar-plus valuation. The company is barely a week old, underscoring the extraordinary premium investors place on elite AI talent. Dean spent over two decades at Google leading AI and infrastructure research and is among the most prominent researchers to launch a startup in the current cycle.
