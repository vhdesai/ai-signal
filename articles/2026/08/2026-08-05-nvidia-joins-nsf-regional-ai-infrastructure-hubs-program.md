# NVIDIA joins NSF regional AI infrastructure hubs program

NVIDIA said it is participating in the U.S. National Science Foundation's State and Regional AI Infrastructure Hubs program, which aims to expand access to AI compute, data, software, and technical support for research and education. The program is designed around state and multistate university consortia, with public-private partnerships and flexible infrastructure models. The effort ties AI infrastructure directly to workforce development, regional economic strategy, and scientific competitiveness.
