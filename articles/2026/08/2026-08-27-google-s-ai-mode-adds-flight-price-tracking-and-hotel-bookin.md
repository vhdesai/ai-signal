# Google's AI Mode Adds Flight Price Tracking and Hotel Booking

Google expanded AI Mode in Search with the ability to track flight prices, assist with hotel booking, and handle additional trip-planning steps. The update moves AI Mode from information retrieval toward transactional execution, effectively positioning it as an agentic travel agent. It is a concrete example of a search incumbent converting assistant traffic into booking intent — relevant to anyone modeling disintermediation risk in travel and e-commerce funnels. MONETIZATION
