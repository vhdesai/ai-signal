# Palantir CEO Alex Karp calls the AI industry "Marxist" after strong quarter

TechCrunch reports that Palantir posted a strong Q2 and that CEO Alex Karp used his shareholder letter to accuse major AI labs of effectively capturing enterprise intellectual property through model interactions. The rhetoric is extreme, but the underlying message is commercially relevant: Palantir is positioning model-agnostic enterprise AI as safer than direct dependency on frontier labs. The story reinforces a broader buying trend toward orchestration layers, private deployments, and multi-model strategies.
