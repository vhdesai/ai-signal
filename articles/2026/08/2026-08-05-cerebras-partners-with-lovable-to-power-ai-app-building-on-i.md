# Cerebras partners with Lovable to power AI app-building on its fast-inference platform

Cerebras announced a partnership bringing its wafer-scale inference performance to Lovable, a fast-growing AI software-creation platform, to enable more interactive software development experiences. The deal extends Cerebras's strategy of monetizing raw inference speed as the market's center of gravity shifts from training to inference. It also gives a vibe-coding platform a differentiated latency story against incumbents running on commodity GPU capacity.
