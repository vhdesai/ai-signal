# Zenity Raises $125M Series C to Secure Enterprise AI Agents

Tel Aviv-based Zenity closed a $125 million Series C led by Norwest, with participation from Qumra Capital, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Hitachi Ventures, LG Technology Ventures, Vertex Ventures, Third Point Ventures, DTCP and Intel Capital, bringing total funding to $180 million. The platform inspects agent intent and allows, modifies or blocks actions across Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, Gemini, Claude, Codex, Cursor and custom systems built on Bedrock, AgentCore, Foundry and Vertex AI. The round is a marker for how quickly agent runtime security has become a distinct budget line rather than an extension of existing DLP or CASB spend.
