# Koray Kavukcuoglu Takes Over Google DeepMind Amid Founder-Level Pressure on Gemini

Google elevated Koray Kavukcuoglu to lead DeepMind as Alphabet works to keep Gemini competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic. Separately reported executive moves indicate co-founder Sergey Brin has personally pressed key AI staff to concentrate effort on Gemini.
