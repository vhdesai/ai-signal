# Anthropic Signs a $35B Cloud Deal With Nvidia-Backed Lambda

Anthropic agreed to a roughly $35 billion computing arrangement with Lambda covering a ~350 MW data center in Nueces County, Texas, being developed by Hut 8. Nvidia supplies the chips and holds the lease. The circularity of chip vendor, cloud provider, and model lab underwriting one another remains the most important balance-sheet risk in the sector.
