# Teachers split on OpenAI's “ChatGPT for Teens”

OpenAI's teen-focused ChatGPT tier includes a “Study Mode” that offers guiding questions and step-by-step scaffolding instead of direct answers. This reaction piece surveys educators, who divide sharply: some treat it as a legitimate tutoring aid, while skeptics warn about cheating, low-quality generated content, and the absence of reporting or regulatory requirements. The underlying product rolled out earlier in the week; this analysis published August 23.
