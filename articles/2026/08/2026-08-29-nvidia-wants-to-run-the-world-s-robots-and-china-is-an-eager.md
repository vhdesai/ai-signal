# Nvidia wants to run the world's robots, and China is an eager customer

The Wall Street Journal highlighted Nvidia's push to provide the computing foundation for robotics, with China emerging as a major demand center. The story reinforces that Nvidia's AI infrastructure ambitions extend from data centers into physical-world automation. Robotics is becoming another arena where U.S. chip strategy, Chinese industrial demand, and embodied AI collide.
