# "Code-as-World": an agentic loop that rewrites real video into executable MuJoCo physics programs

MirroS's Code-as-World represents a physical scene not as pixels but as executable code — a scene definition runnable in the MuJoCo physics engine. An agentic loop recovers these programs from real video across up to five propose→execute→verify rounds, yielding training data with exact physical labels (mass, contact, gravity) that raw video cannot supply. The resulting Code-as-World-VL-9B scores 55.4 MRA on the QuantiPhy validation benchmark, edging Gemini-3.1 Flash (54.8) and sitting roughly 15 points above the strongest open-weight baseline. Two checkpoints (4B and 9B) were released under Apache 2.0.
