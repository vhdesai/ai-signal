# Anthropic publishes official Claude watermarking explainer

Anthropic's own explainer says future Claude outputs will contain a statistical text watermark and that other major AI providers serving the EU market are expected to implement their own approaches. The company emphasized that the watermark is not visible to readers, does not require extra tokens, cannot identify a specific user or chat, and is less reliable on small samples. The important nuance is that watermarking can estimate whether Claude was involved in producing text, but it cannot prove human authorship or identify text from other AI systems.
