# Harvey in Talks for $500M at a $15.5B Valuation

Legal AI startup Harvey is reportedly in discussions with Lightspeed Venture Partners and others to raise at least $500 million at a $15.5 billion valuation — a roughly 40% step-up from its $11B round in March. ARR has moved from ~$190M at end-2025 to roughly $300M, with total annualized revenue above $350M. At the proposed price, Harvey would trade near 44× revenue, well outside traditional software norms. A useful proxy for how vertical AI applications are being repriced relative to foundation-model labs. TALENT MARKETS
