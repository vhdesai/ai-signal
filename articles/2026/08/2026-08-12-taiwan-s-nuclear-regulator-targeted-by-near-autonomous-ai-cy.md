# Taiwan’s Nuclear Regulator Targeted by Near-Autonomous AI Cyberattack

Taiwan’s nuclear regulator was targeted by an AI-enabled intrusion in which agents conducted simultaneous reconnaissance and break-in attempts over four days, without continuous human direction. Attack chains are now being coordinated by software agents autonomously. Critical-infrastructure operators should assume attacker tempo exceeds human-in-the-loop defensive cycles. POLICY SECURITY
