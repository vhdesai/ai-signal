# Nvidia's $500 billion compute-financing platform draws private-credit scrutiny

Nvidia's financing partnership with six major financial institutions to fund AI infrastructure buildouts is now the dominant topic in private credit. CEO Jensen Huang said on X that Nvidia has the option to backstop up to $125 billion — about 25% of the potential deals — and characterized its chips as "revenue-generating assets." The structure effectively converts GPUs into a financeable asset class, extending buyer capacity beyond balance-sheet limits.
