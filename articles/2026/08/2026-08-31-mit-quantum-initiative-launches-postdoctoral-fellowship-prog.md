# MIT Quantum Initiative Launches Postdoctoral Fellowship Program

The MIT Quantum Initiative launched a postdoctoral fellowship program and will welcome its first cohort of QMIT Fellows this fall to advance interdisciplinary quantum research. Organizers describe the program as an investment in bringing new quantum capabilities toward transformative applications — part of a broader pattern of universities building dedicated talent pipelines adjacent to AI compute.
