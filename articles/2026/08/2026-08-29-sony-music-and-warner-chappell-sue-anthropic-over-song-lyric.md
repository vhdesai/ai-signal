# Sony Music and Warner Chappell sue Anthropic over song lyrics in Claude training data

Sony Music Publishing, Warner Chappell and other publishers filed suit against Anthropic and co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann, alleging a "brazen campaign" of intellectual property theft in assembling training data. The complaint, filed late Friday, is notable for naming founders individually rather than the corporate entity alone. Axios reported the same filing on August 29, characterizing it as one of the largest copyright actions yet brought against a frontier lab. The case sharpens an unresolved question for enterprises: whether downstream users of models trained on contested corpora carry any exposure. techcrunch.com/2026/08/29/sony-music-warner-sue-anthropic
