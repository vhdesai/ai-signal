# OpenAI begins showing ads on ChatGPT Free and Go tiers in India

OpenAI is rolling out advertising to logged-in adult users on ChatGPT's Free and Go tiers in India, its largest market by weekly active users at more than 100 million. Paid Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education tiers are excluded, and OpenAI states ads will not influence model answers and will exclude sensitive categories. This is the clearest signal yet that consumer monetization, not subscriptions alone, will fund inference costs at scale. INDUSTRIAL AI
