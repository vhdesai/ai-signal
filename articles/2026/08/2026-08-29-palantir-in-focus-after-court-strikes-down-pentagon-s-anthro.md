# Palantir in focus after court strikes down Pentagon's Anthropic blacklist

This analysis examines how a federal court ruling — finding the Pentagon illegally retaliated against Anthropic by designating it a "supply-chain risk" — could ripple into Palantir's defense programs ahead of a key September milestone. The underlying ruling by U.S. District Judge Rita Lin was issued August 28; this in-window piece is the market-reaction angle. It is a useful read on how federal procurement posture toward frontier labs is becoming a valuation input.
