# Google in talks for a $1.5B-plus hire-and-license deal with coding-agent startup Mechanize

Google is reportedly negotiating a deal worth more than $1.5 billion to license technology from and hire talent out of Mechanize, a roughly 35-person San Francisco startup that builds reinforcement-learning environments for software-engineering agents. The structure follows the now-familiar non-acquisition template of licensing plus talent transfer. The reported price implies extraordinary value per engineer and reflects scarcity in high-quality agentic training data. It also underlines that coding remains the most commercially proven agent workload.
