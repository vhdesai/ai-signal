# At TechBBQ, Europe's AI debate converges on sovereignty and control

TechCrunch's recap of TechBBQ in Copenhagen found European founders and investors repeatedly returning to one question: who actually controls the AI stack. Speakers including Signal's Meredith Whittaker pointed to a recent Anthropic outage that cut off European customers as evidence of dependency risk, and argued against embedding assistants deep into operating systems.
