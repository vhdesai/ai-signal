# AI-drafted legislation is overwhelming the House office that writes US laws

Eight current and former officials describe the House Office of Legislative Counsel now spending more time correcting AI-drafted bill text than it would take to draft from scratch, as congressional offices and outside groups paste model output into legislation. One June amendment reportedly reached draft form with residual chatbot phrasing intact.
