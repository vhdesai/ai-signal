# OpenAI launches AI Futures, a blog on power, governance, and human agency

OpenAI introduced AI Futures, the publication of a new Strategic Futures team, framed around a single question: how a free society preserves human agency if increasingly capable systems reduce the need for broad human cooperation or consent. The launch positions OpenAI in the governance debate on its own terms ahead of expected regulatory and public-market pressure. Executives should read it as a statement of intended policy posture rather than a research output.
