# Apple iCloud Policy Fueled Employee Leaks Ahead of OpenAI Trade Secrets Suit

The Information reveals that Apple's own iCloud data-sharing policies inadvertently enabled the employee data exfiltration that now forms the basis of Apple's trade-secrets lawsuit against OpenAI. Former Apple employees who joined OpenAI allegedly accessed confidential documents through iCloud sync configurations that Apple's security team failed to lock down before departures. The report is significant because it shifts the narrative from pure corporate espionage to systemic enterprise security failure — a cautionary tale for any organization whose employees use cloud-synced devices. The case is also notable as a proxy battle in the broader AI talent war, where non-competes are largely unenforceable in California.
