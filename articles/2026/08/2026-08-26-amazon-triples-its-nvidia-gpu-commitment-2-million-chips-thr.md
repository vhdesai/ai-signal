# Amazon Triples Its Nvidia GPU Commitment — 2 Million Chips Through 2028

Announced on Nvidia's earnings call, the expanded partnership adds roughly 2 million Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra GPUs to AWS data centers in 2027 and 2028 — triple a commitment made only five months ago. Nvidia networking, CPUs, open Nemotron models, and its robotics stack will be integrated across AWS. The tension worth noting: Amazon deepens Nvidia dependency at the same time its custom-chip business crossed a $25B annualized run rate.
