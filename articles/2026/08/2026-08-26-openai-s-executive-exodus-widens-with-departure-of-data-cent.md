# OpenAI's Executive Exodus Widens With Departure of Data Center Chief

Chris Malone, OpenAI's data center lead, has left the company, adding to a lengthening list of senior departures that includes revenue chief Denise Dresser and longtime executive Brad Lightcap. Before his exit, OpenAI had already reshuffled its infrastructure organization, moving Malone's reporting line away from President Greg Brockman and placing VP Sachin Katti in charge. TechCrunch notes the churn is striking given that OpenAI's compute investment is its clearest structural advantage over rivals, and it raises investor questions about leadership stability ahead of a potential IPO.
