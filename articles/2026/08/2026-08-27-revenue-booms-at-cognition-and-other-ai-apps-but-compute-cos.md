# Revenue Booms at Cognition and Other AI Apps, But Compute Costs Keep Cash Burn High

Previously unreported data show revenue surging at Cognition and several other AI application providers — offering reassurance to investors that startups can thrive despite custom-silicon competition. However, compute costs remain high enough to keep cash burn elevated even as top-line grows rapidly. The dynamic matters for anyone evaluating AI application-layer investments: revenue growth does not equal profitability when inference costs scale with usage.
