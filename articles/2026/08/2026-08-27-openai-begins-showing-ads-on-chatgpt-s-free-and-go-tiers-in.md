# OpenAI begins showing ads on ChatGPT's free and Go tiers in India

OpenAI has started rolling out advertising to logged-in adult users on the ChatGPT Free and Go tiers in India, its largest user market outside the U.S. with more than 100 million weekly active users. The move extends OpenAI's advertising program into a market where subscription conversion is structurally low. It marks a meaningful shift in the company's revenue mix toward consumer-internet economics rather than pure subscription and API revenue. TALENT
