# Google's Open Gemma Models Pass One Billion Downloads

Google DeepMind reported that the Gemma family of open models has surpassed one billion cumulative downloads, with developers publishing more than 100,000 derivative variants since launch roughly two years ago. Google is also creating an "Awesome Gemma" repository to catalog community tooling and applications. Open-weight distribution remains a primary competitive front against both proprietary frontier models and Chinese open releases.
