# Alibaba releases Qwen3.8-27B with FP8 weights for local deployment

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen3.8-27B, a long-context multimodal model distributed with FP8 weights. It is reported to run locally in roughly 17GB of memory, putting near-frontier multimodal capability within reach of a single high-end workstation. Confidence on specifics is moderate pending primary confirmation.
