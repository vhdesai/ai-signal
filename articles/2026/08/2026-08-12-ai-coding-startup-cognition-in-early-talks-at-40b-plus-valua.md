# AI Coding Startup Cognition in Early Talks at $40B-Plus Valuation

Cognition, maker of the Devin coding agent, is reportedly in preliminary discussions to raise at a valuation of at least $40 billion — more than a 50% step-up from its recent $26B mark — with annualized revenue said to be approaching $1 billion. Coding remains the clearest measurable commercial use case for agentic AI, which is drawing disproportionate capital.
