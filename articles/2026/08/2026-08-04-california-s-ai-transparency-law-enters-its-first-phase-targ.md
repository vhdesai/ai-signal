# California’s AI transparency law enters its first phase, targeting deepfakes and scams

California began enforcing the first phase of what backers call the nation’s strongest AI transparency law, with initial obligations aimed at deepfakes and AI-enabled scams and a path toward durable watermarking and disclosure for covered generative-AI providers. The rollout lands alongside the EU’s transparency enforcement, creating overlapping U.S.–EU obligations for large model providers. Companies operating in California should expect labeling, detection-tool, and disclosure requirements to tighten in subsequent phases.
