# OpenAI completes ~$7B employee tender offer at an $852B valuation

OpenAI wrapped a roughly $7B secondary share sale allowing current and former employees to sell into the round, setting a reported $852B valuation. The tender has been in progress since the company closed its $122B primary round in March, and is widely read as housekeeping ahead of a potential IPO. Providing liquidity without a new primary raise is a retention lever in a market where rivals are aggressively bidding for senior researchers.
