# AMD ships Instella-MoE-16B-A3B, a fully open Mixture-of-Experts LLM trained on its own GPUs

AMD released Instella-MoE-16B-A3B, a fully open Mixture-of-Experts language model trained from scratch on Instinct MI300X and MI325X accelerators. The model carries 16B total parameters but activates only ~2.8B per token, and ships under a research-oriented license — a notable signal that AMD’s silicon can host end-to-end frontier-style training, not just inference.
