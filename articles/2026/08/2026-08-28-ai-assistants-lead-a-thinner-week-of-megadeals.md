# AI Assistants Lead a Thinner Week of Megadeals

Most of the week's ten largest venture rounds went to AI-focused companies, led by Instinct, a developer of AI assistants, but the overall lineup of megadeals was sparser than in recent weeks. The composition — tooling and assistants rather than foundation models — suggests capital is moving down-market toward applied layers as frontier training costs concentrate among a handful of labs.
