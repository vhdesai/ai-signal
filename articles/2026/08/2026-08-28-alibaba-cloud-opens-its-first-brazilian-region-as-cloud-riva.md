# Alibaba Cloud Opens Its First Brazilian Region as Cloud Rivalry Reaches Latin America

Alibaba Cloud has launched two data centers in Brazil, its first major infrastructure footprint in South America, offering locally hosted cloud alongside a suite of agent-based AI services. The company already operates a Mexican region and now spans 106 availability zones across 31 regions. The expansion moves the U.S.–China technology contest beyond chips and models into cloud sovereignty in emerging markets, where data localization and latency increasingly drive procurement.
