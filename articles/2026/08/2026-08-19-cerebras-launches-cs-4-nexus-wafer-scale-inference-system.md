# Cerebras launches CS-4 "Nexus" wafer-scale inference system

Cerebras unveiled the CS-4 on its new Nexus architecture, built from three WSE-3 Turbo wafers on TSMC 5nm and claiming up to 30x faster inference than GPU systems, with 1,000+ tokens/sec on 10T-parameter models. The system uses roughly 50% fewer components than its predecessor. Cerebras is targeting 600 MW of deployed capacity by end-2027.
