# Anthropic signs ~$45B, six-year compute deal with Nscale

Anthropic agreed to spend roughly $45B over six years to rent about 460 MW at Nscale's Monarch data-center campus in West Virginia, expected to run on Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin systems from late 2027. Reports indicate the deal replaced Microsoft as anchor tenant and helps underpin Nscale's planned IPO. It is another datapoint in frontier labs locking up power and compute years in advance.
