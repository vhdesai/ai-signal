# Amazon tops $3 trillion as AWS growth eases AI-spending fears

Amazon crossed a $3 trillion market capitalization for the first time on Monday, with Microsoft climbing about 5%, as strong cloud earnings from both companies reassured investors worried about the pace of AI capital spending. AWS posted its fastest revenue growth in more than four years, and Amazon lifted its capital-expenditure outlook to fund additional AI capacity. For leaders, the move signals that public markets will, for now, reward hyperscaler AI buildouts when cloud demand visibly accelerates. FUNDING
