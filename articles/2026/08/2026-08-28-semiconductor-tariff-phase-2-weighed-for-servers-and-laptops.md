# Semiconductor Tariff "Phase 2" Weighed for Servers and Laptops, Threatening Data-Center Carve-Outs

The administration is weighing extending semiconductor duties beyond discrete chips to finished goods containing them — servers, laptops, and consoles — and eliminating the data-center and startup exemptions in place since January 2026. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has favored tying relief for foreign firms to U.S. fab investment, with a phase-in under discussion.
