# NVIDIA and Wall Street firms strike AI financing deal targeting $500 billion

NVIDIA and major Wall Street firms reportedly struck an AI financing deal targeting $500 billion. The deal highlights how the AI infrastructure buildout is increasingly being financed through large capital-market structures rather than conventional corporate capex alone. The strategic signal is that AI compute is becoming an investable infrastructure asset class, with financing capacity now a competitive differentiator alongside chips, power, and software.
