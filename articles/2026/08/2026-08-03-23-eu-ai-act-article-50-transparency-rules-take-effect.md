# 23. EU AI Act Article 50 transparency rules take effect

The EU AI Act’s Article 50 transparency obligations — requiring disclosure of chatbot interactions and AI-generated content — became effective (August 2), with reporting following August 3. Non-compliance carries fines up to €15M or 3% of global turnover, raising the compliance bar for consumer-facing AI in Europe. NewCalifornia
