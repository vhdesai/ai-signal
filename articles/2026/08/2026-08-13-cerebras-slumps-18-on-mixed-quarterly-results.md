# Cerebras Slumps 18% on Mixed Quarterly Results

Cerebras Systems fell over 18% premarket after missing key estimates despite soaring cloud revenue, raising doubts about its AI chips' ability to challenge Nvidia's dominance. The mixed results test the growth narrative for alternative AI chip makers at a time when hyperscalers continue to bet heavily on Nvidia's GPU ecosystem. URL behind paywall (Reuters).
