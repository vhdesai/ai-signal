# White House to Host AI Companies Tuesday to Review AI Oversight Framework

The Trump administration has invited staffers from major tech companies including OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic to the White House on Tuesday to review the completed version of an AI oversight framework. The meeting signals that the administration is moving from executive-order principles to operational governance — a shift that could define the regulatory environment for frontier AI development in the US for the next several years. For enterprise AI leaders, the framework's scope (voluntary vs. binding, which models are in scope, reporting requirements) will directly affect procurement, compliance, and deployment strategies. The timing is notable given the EU AI Act's enforcement powers taking effect this same week.
