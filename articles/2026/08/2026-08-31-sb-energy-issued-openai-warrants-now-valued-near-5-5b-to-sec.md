# SB Energy issued OpenAI warrants now valued near $5.5B to secure a 20-year data-center lease

Draft IPO documents reviewed by the Journal show SoftBank subsidiary SB Energy granted OpenAI warrants that have appreciated from about $3.6B at January issuance to roughly $5.5B, tied to a long-term lease at the PORTS-Pike campus in Ohio. SB Energy is preparing an IPO reportedly targeting $5-7B. The structure is another instance of circular financing across the AI buildout, where suppliers take equity-linked exposure to their anchor tenant. URL behind paywall. GEOPOLITICS
