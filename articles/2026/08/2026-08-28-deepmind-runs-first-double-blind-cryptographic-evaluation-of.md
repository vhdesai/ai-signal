# DeepMind Runs First Double-Blind Cryptographic Evaluation of a Frontier Model

Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite tested inside a confidential computing enclave — evaluators couldn’t access weights; Google couldn’t access prompts. Used Intel TDX and H100 Confidential GPU. Published methodology but not scores. Directionally important for procurement but not yet an independent standard.
