# OpenAI Moves to Dismiss Apple's Trade Secrets Lawsuit, Blames Apple's Own Security Practices

OpenAI filed a motion to dismiss Apple's trade secrets lawsuit, arguing Apple allowed employees to use personal iCloud accounts for work, failed to revoke access after departures, and that a manager continued accessing a departed engineer's iCloud. OpenAI contends Apple has not specified which trade secrets were taken and frames the suit as an anticompetitive effort to slow OpenAI's hardware and talent recruitment plans.
