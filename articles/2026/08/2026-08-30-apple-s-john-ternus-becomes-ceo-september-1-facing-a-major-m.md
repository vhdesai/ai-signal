# Apple's John Ternus becomes CEO September 1, facing a major management reshuffle

With Tim Cook moving to executive chairman, Gurman reports Ternus's most immediate challenge is revamping a decades-old senior team. The implications extend to Apple Intelligence direction, the hardware roadmap and AI executive alignment as Apple works to close its gap with rivals. Expect leadership churn to be an early signal of how aggressively Apple resets its AI strategy.
