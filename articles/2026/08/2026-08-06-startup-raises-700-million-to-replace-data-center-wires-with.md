# Startup raises $700 million to replace data-center wires with light

The Wall Street Journal reported that a startup raised $700 million to replace data-center wiring with optical links. The financing highlights how AI infrastructure bottlenecks are moving from GPUs alone to interconnect, memory, power, and data movement. Photonics is becoming a strategic infrastructure category because large AI clusters increasingly depend on faster, lower-power communication across racks and data centers.
