# Anthropic to give enterprise clients more control over data retention

Anthropic plans to let enterprise customers exercise greater control over their data when using its frontier models, reversing part of the retention policy it introduced in June. Business customers would still face a 30-day retention requirement, but would gain the option to hold data in their own cloud environment. The change follows sustained pushback from regulated-industry buyers whose zero-data-retention agreements were narrowed by the June covered-models policy. M&A
