# AT&T Plans to Curb Anthropic and OpenAI Spending by Shifting to Open-Source Models

AT&T plans to keep spending on Anthropic and OpenAI flat by shifting to open-source models like Nvidia’s Nemotron. Open-source now powers 40% of AT&T’s AI queries across 100,000 employees, with plans to reach 60–70%. VP Mark Austin called open-source models “just as good or better” for many tasks. If large enterprises follow suit, it could pressure IPO valuations at OpenAI and Anthropic.
