# NVIDIA releases Alpamayo 2 Super open VLA model for autonomous driving

MarkTechPost reports that NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34B-parameter vision-language-action model for autonomous driving, under the OpenMDW-1.1 commercial-use license. The model combines a Cosmos reasoning backbone with a diffusion-based action decoder and is positioned around trajectory generation, causal reasoning, auto-labeling, and visual QA. The strategic significance is NVIDIA extending its physical-AI stack from silicon and simulation into open commercial model layers for autonomous vehicles.
