# Taiwan charges Nvidia and Super Micro employees with AI-server smugglingBreaking

Taiwanese prosecutors charged nine people, including former Nvidia and Super Micro employees, with facilitating shipments of dozens of advanced AI servers to China in violation of U.S. export controls. Two defendants allegedly filed fraudulent paperwork to clear a 130-server purchase by claiming the hardware would remain in Taiwan. It is the latest escalation in Taiwan’s crackdown on restricted AI-chip diversion, running alongside a separate U.S. case involving Super Micro’s co-founder.
