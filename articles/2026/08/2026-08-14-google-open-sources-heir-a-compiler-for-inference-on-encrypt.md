# Google open-sources HEIR, a compiler for inference on encrypted data

Google released HEIR, a compiler toolchain that compiles models to run inference directly on encrypted data. Demonstrations covered private recommendations and fraud/anomaly detection. For regulated industries, homomorphic inference remains costly, but tooling maturity is the gating factor worth tracking.
