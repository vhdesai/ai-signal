# AMD releases Instella-MoE-16B-A3B, an open mixture-of-experts model trained on Instinct GPUs

MarkTechPost reported that AMD released Instella-MoE-16B-A3B, a fully open mixture-of-experts language model with 16 billion total parameters and 2.8 billion active parameters. The strategic point is less the model alone than the proof point: AMD is trying to show that competitive open models can be trained and served on its Instinct GPU stack. That matters as enterprises look for alternatives to single-vendor accelerator strategies.
