# OpenAI Slows Its Next Model After “Critical” Cyber Evaluations

OpenAI said preliminary evaluations could not rule out that its upcoming Astra model reaches the Critical threshold of its Preparedness Framework — meaning potential capability to discover functional zero-day exploits and execute novel attack chains with minimal human involvement. The company is deliberately reducing research velocity and imposing isolated testing environments, restricted network and tool access, hardened model-weight protection, and universal monitoring of high-risk actions and chain-of-thought. Notably, OpenAI framed the decision as inability to disprove the capability rather than confirmation of it. This is the first time a frontier lab has publicly throttled its own development pipeline on cyber grounds. O POLICY
