# Natural Gas Prices Could Triple Near AI Data Centers, Research Firm Warns

Energy research firm Noreva warns that natural gas prices could surge above $10/MMBTU in key U.S. hubs — up from today's $2–$4.50 range — as hyperscaler demand collides with declining supply growth and rising LNG exports. Meta, Microsoft, Google, and Amazon have all committed to multi-gigawatt gas power plants for AI data centers. Since fuel represents ~50% of electricity costs from large plants, a price tripling could materially increase token costs or push hyperscalers back to the grid, driving up consumer electricity prices. Noreva's CEO noted hyperscalers are "doing things that are not normal for an off-taker to do" in energy markets. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/hyperscalers-might-regret-embracing-natural-gas-if-new-forecast-proves-correct/
