# Washington Drafting Ban on New Chinese Data Center Components

The administration is drafting a ban on U.S. imports of new models of Chinese data center components, including optical transceivers, according to four people familiar with the deliberations. Shares of Chinese optical-module makers including Zhongji Innolight fell on the report.
