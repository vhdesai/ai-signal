# Huawei H1 profit drop quickens to 36% on rising costs, R&D spending

Reuters reports that Huawei’s first-half profit decline accelerated as rising costs and R&D spending weighed on results. The story is relevant to AI strategy because Huawei remains central to China’s domestic AI compute stack; margin pressure suggests the push for local chips, cloud infrastructure, and AI systems remains expensive even for national champions.
