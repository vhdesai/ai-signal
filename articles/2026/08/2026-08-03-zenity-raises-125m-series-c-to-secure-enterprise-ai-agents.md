# Zenity raises $125M Series C to secure enterprise AI agents

Agent-security startup Zenity closed a $125 million Series C led by Norwest to inventory, monitor, and govern autonomous AI agents across enterprise platforms such as Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT Enterprise. The raise underscores a fast-emerging category, agent observability and governance, as enterprises move from pilots to production. Zenity frames the opportunity around an anticipated era of roughly one billion AI agents operating inside corporate systems.
