# AutoFigure: agentic document pipelines for generating scientific figures

MarkTechPost published a technical walkthrough of AutoFigure, a toolkit that generates publication-style scientific figures directly from text descriptions and source papers. The piece covers environment setup, an API-backed generation workflow, and converting document-intelligence pipelines into diagrams with custom reference styling and gallery export. It is a practical example of agentic document intelligence moving into research workflows.
