# Enterprise AI agent success is tied to limiting autonomy

VentureBeat reported that enterprises seeing success with AI agents are limiting how much the agents can do alone. The pattern aligns with broader safety and reliability findings: agent deployments work best when scoped to bounded workflows, supported by high-quality documents, and constrained by permissions and escalation paths. For senior leaders, agent value should be measured by controlled delegation, not by maximal autonomy.
