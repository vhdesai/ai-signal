# Horizon3’s financing reflects strong investor conviction that autonomous security validation will become a durable…

Horizon3’s financing reflects strong investor conviction that autonomous security validation will become a durable category, not a temporary AI add-on. - Continuous pentesting aligns with board-level concerns because it translates abstract exposure into concrete evidence of exploitability. - The timing is important as recent agent incidents have raised awareness that AI can amplify offensive risk just as it can strengthen defense. - If budgets continue shifting in this direction, security assurance may become one of the earliest enterprise functions to fully normalize autonomous agents. - For CISOs, the message is that offensive simulation is moving from specialist consulting to always-on operational tooling.
