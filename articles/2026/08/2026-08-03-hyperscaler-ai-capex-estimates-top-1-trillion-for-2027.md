# Hyperscaler AI capex estimates top $1 trillion for 2027

Yahoo Finance reports Goldman Sachs analysis estimating 2027 hyperscaler capital expenditures above $1 trillion, more than $100 billion higher than pre-earnings expectations. The article notes rising capex guidance from Meta and Alphabet and pressure on free cash flow through 2028, with Microsoft presented as the relative outlier on near-term cash-flow resilience. This is the clearest infrastructure signal in the window: AI scaling is increasingly a balance-sheet and cash-conversion question, not just a model-performance race.
