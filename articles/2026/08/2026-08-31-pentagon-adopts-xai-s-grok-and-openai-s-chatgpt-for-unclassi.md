# Pentagon Adopts xAI’s Grok and OpenAI’s ChatGPT for Unclassified Military Use

The Pentagon announced it has adopted xAI’s “Grok for Government” and OpenAI’s ChatGPT for unclassified military use, expanding the roster of AI assistants available across the department. The decision continues the U.S. defense establishment’s rapid onboarding of commercial frontier models and raises the stakes on vendor concentration and model-provenance questions in national-security workflows.
