# Love in the Time of AI: Chatbots Are Taking Over Online Dating

The WSJ reports on the rapid proliferation of AI chatbots in online dating, where platforms are deploying AI to write messages, select photos, and manage conversations on behalf of users. The trend raises fundamental questions about authenticity, consent, and the value proposition of platforms built on human connection. For technology executives, the dating-app use case is a leading indicator of how AI intermediation will reshape consumer services more broadly — from financial advisory to healthcare to legal services — wherever trust and personal judgment are core to the user experience.
