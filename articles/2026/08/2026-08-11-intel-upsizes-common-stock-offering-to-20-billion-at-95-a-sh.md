# Intel Upsizes Common-Stock Offering to $20 Billion at $95 a Share

Intel priced 210.5 million shares at $95, raising roughly $19.7 billion and upsizing the deal from the $15 billion announced a day earlier on institutional demand. Underwriters hold a 30-day option for 31.6 million additional shares. Shares fell about 4.1% on the initial announcement as investors weighed dilution against Intel's rising capital needs; the company lifted its 2026 capex outlook from $18 billion to $20 billion in July and expects a further step up in 2027. The raise underlines how expensive simultaneous competition in AI processors, foundry and advanced packaging has become. https://www.ibtimes.sg/intel-raises-20-billion-stock-offering-why-intc-shares-fell-91958
