# MIT introduces GeoPT to help AI models simulate real-world physics

MIT researchers introduced GeoPT, a pretraining approach that helps AI simulation models learn physics from 3D mechanical interactions more efficiently. The system can train on up to 60% less data and reach peak performance twice as fast compared with leading models, with potential applications in robotics, vehicle design, collisions, fluid dynamics, and physical-world simulation. The work is strategically relevant because physical AI needs models that understand forces, not just pixels and text.
