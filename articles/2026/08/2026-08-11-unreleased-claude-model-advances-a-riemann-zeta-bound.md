# Unreleased Claude Model Advances a Riemann Zeta Bound

Anthropic published findings that an unreleased Claude research model improved a longstanding lower bound on Riemann zeta zeros from ~41.6% to 67.2%. Not a proof of the hypothesis — but frontier models producing novel, checkable results in research mathematics, not just reproducing known work.
