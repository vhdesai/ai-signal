# $121 Billion in One-Time Gains Boosting Big Tech Profits

Gains on investments in companies like Anthropic are inflating earnings, with WSJ tallying $121B in one-time gains across Big Tech’s recent quarterly reports. The AI boom is creating paper wealth for strategic investors before investees reach public markets, complicating the picture of how much these companies earn from operations vs. investment windfalls. As Anthropic, Databricks, and others approach listing, these gains will either be realized or reversed — creating earnings volatility in either direction. Boards should note the non-operating component now embedded in reported profitability.
