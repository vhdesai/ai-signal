# Evaluation harness finds AI models can be most confident when wrong

VentureBeat reported on an evaluation harness that found AI models were often most confident when wrong, surfacing a failure mode qualitative review had missed. The finding is relevant for enterprise adoption because confidence calibration directly affects agent approval flows, escalation policies, and human-in-the-loop design. The practical lesson is that model evaluation should measure uncertainty behavior and decision thresholds, not only answer quality.
