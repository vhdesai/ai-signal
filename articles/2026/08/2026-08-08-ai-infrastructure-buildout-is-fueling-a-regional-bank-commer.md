# AI Infrastructure Buildout Is Fueling a Regional Bank Commercial Lending Boom

The AI infrastructure build-out is generating a measurable "trickle-down" lending cycle at regional banks, with commercial and industrial loan demand from large and midsize companies rising sharply in Q2 — 16.1% of banks reported higher demand, up from 4.8% the prior quarter, per the Fed's senior loan officer survey. Fifth Third and PNC executives cited financing for concrete, aluminum, HVAC, and heavy-machinery suppliers serving AI data center construction, and regional bank stocks are trading near record highs.
