# Google Makes Visible AI Watermarks Optional, Keeps Invisible SynthID Intact

Google announced users can now remove visible watermarks from AI-generated images, videos, and songs created by its Nano Banana, Omni, and Lyria models. Invisible SynthID watermarks and C2PA metadata remain mandatory. VP Josh Woodward framed it as "striking a balance between creative control and safety." Google is also open-sourcing Credentio, a new library for developers to embed local C2PA validation. The move contrasts sharply with Anthropic's mandatory visible watermarking policy announced earlier this week, which drew user backlash. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/google-will-now-allow-users-to-remove-visible-watermark-from-its-ai-generations/
