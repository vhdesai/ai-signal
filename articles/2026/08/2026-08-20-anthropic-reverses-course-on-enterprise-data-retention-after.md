# Anthropic Reverses Course on Enterprise Data Retention After Customer Pushback

Anthropic plans to let enterprise customers exercise greater control over their data when using frontier models, reversing part of the retention policy introduced in June. Business customers would still face a 30-day retention requirement but would gain the option to hold data in their own cloud environment. The change follows sustained pushback from regulated-industry buyers—and lands one day after OpenAI’s competing zero-retention pledge.
