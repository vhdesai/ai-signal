# IBM Embeds OpenAI Frontier Models Across Consulting Delivery

IBM is placing GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting’s delivery platform, standing up a dedicated OpenAI practice and training tens of thousands of consultants. The deal gives OpenAI a channel into IBM’s regulated-industry client base while giving IBM a frontier-model story. It lands <1 year after IBM’s Anthropic alliance, signaling multi-model posture. Extends OpenAI’s SI distribution strategy alongside Infosys and TCS, pressuring Accenture and other global SIs to declare model alignments.
