# OpenAI Pauses Astra After It Nears First-Ever “Critical” Cyber Risk

OpenAI disclosed that its Astra model is the first system it can no longer rule out as “Critical” for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework — the tier reserved for models able to independently develop functional zero-day exploits against hardened real-world systems. All internal Astra activity that does not meet strengthened controls has been halted, and the model now runs under isolated testing, restricted network and tool access, encrypted weights, sandboxed execution, and real-time chain-of-thought monitoring. Government agencies and selected safety organizations get evaluation access before any public release. Every prior frontier model, including GPT-5.6 Sol, stayed in the High category.
