# Firebird opens the CIS region’s largest AI factory in Armenia, built on NVIDIA

U.S.-based AI cloud Firebird opened the CIS region’s largest AI factory in Hrazdan, Armenia, built on NVIDIA accelerated computing and Dell PowerEdge servers, with Armenia’s prime minister and Kazakhstan’s deputy prime minister attending. Firebird plans to deploy more than 70,000 NVIDIA Rubin and Blackwell GPUs and 300 MW of capacity by the end of 2027, and said NVIDIA intends to invest in the company following an earlier CoreWeave investment. Perplexity is an early customer. Coverage frames the U.S. export licence, rather than the GPU count, as the most consequential detail — compute as diplomacy.
