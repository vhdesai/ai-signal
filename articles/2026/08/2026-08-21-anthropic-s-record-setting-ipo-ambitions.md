# Anthropic's Record-Setting IPO Ambitions

DealBook highlights Anthropic's "record-setting I.P.O. ambitions" as the company prepares for what could be the largest tech IPO in history. The story comes amid a wave of hyperscaler debt issuance and growing investor concerns about whether the AI spending spree can sustain itself as bond yields rise and the U.S. federal debt approaches $40 trillion. Bessent acknowledged that debt issued by hyperscalers is creating "competition for capital" in the bond markets.
