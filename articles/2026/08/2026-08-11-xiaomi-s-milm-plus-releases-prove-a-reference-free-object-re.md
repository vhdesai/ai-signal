# Xiaomi's MiLM Plus Releases PROVE, a Reference-Free Object-Removal Benchmark

Xiaomi's MiLM Plus published PROVE, introducing two perception-aligned metrics — RC-S and RC-T — plus PROVE-Bench, a real-world video benchmark for evaluating object removal without paired ground truth. The absence of paired references has been a persistent obstacle to measuring inpainting and removal quality at scale. Evaluation infrastructure of this kind is unglamorous but load-bearing: it is what lets teams compare editing models on defensible criteria rather than curated demos. marktechpost.com — Xiaomi's MiLM Plus Releases PROVE
