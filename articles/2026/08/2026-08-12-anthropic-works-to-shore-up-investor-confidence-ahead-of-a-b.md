# Anthropic works to shore up investor confidence ahead of a blockbuster IPO

Anthropic is meeting prospective public-market investors as it races toward a listing this fall. Reporting indicates the company is fielding pointed questions on Chinese competition, the scale of AI infrastructure spending, Washington political friction, and public backlash to increasingly capable systems.
