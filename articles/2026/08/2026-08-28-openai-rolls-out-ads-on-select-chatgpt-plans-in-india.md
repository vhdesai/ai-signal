# OpenAI rolls out ads on select ChatGPT plans in India

OpenAI began introducing advertising on certain ChatGPT tiers in India as part of a broader monetization and growth push in one of its largest user markets. The move marks a meaningful shift toward ad-supported consumer assistants alongside subscription revenue. India has become a strategic proving ground for OpenAI's pricing and distribution experiments.
