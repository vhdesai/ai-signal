# VentureBeat reports that Thinking Machines, led by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, released Inkling Small, a…

VentureBeat reports that Thinking Machines, led by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, released Inkling Small, a 276-billion-parameter sparse MoE model under Apache 2.0. The model is roughly one-quarter the size of the company's larger Inkling system while approaching its benchmark performance, including strong coding results. The release reinforces a strategic trend: smaller, cheaper, and more permissively licensed models are becoming credible alternatives for enterprise workloads.
