# Mistral releases Shieldstral, a 3B open-weights policy-adaptive safety classifier

Mistral AI published Shieldstral, a 3-billion-parameter open-weights classifier that screens both text and images against moderation policies written in plain language and supplied at inference time. Unlike fixed-taxonomy safety models, it adapts to a customer's own policy wording without retraining. For enterprises, this is a practical path to running content governance in-house at low cost rather than depending on a vendor's built-in refusal behavior. AI Safety & Policy RESEARCHHOT
