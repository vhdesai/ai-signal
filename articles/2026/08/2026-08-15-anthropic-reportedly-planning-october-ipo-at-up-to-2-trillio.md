# Anthropic Reportedly Planning October IPO at up to $2 Trillion Valuation

Per an FT report, Anthropic is targeting an October listing at an estimated $2 trillion valuation, which would be the largest debut on record, though timing is not formally fixed. It follows a roughly $65B Series H at a $965B valuation and reported revenue above $47B, with bulls projecting $100–120B annualized by year-end. Single-outlet relay of the FT — unconfirmed.
