# Groq raises $350M at $3.5B to pivot from custom silicon to neocloud

Groq raised $350 million at a $3.5 billion valuation as it repositions from AI chip design toward an inference cloud business, expanding a data center footprint that now includes Nvidia-powered capacity. The pivot is a candid acknowledgment that custom inference silicon alone has struggled to win volume against the incumbent stack. It also adds another well-capitalized entrant to a neocloud segment whose long-term margin structure remains unproven. techcrunch.com – Groq raises $350M to fuel its pivot to neocloud Model Releases OPEN WEIGHTS
