# Google's open Gemma models pass one billion downloads

Google DeepMind said the Gemma family has surpassed one billion cumulative downloads, with developers publishing more than 100,000 fine-tuned variants since launch roughly two years ago, alongside a new "Awesome Gemma" repository cataloguing community work. Gemma runs across cloud, laptop, edge, and specialized hardware, letting enterprises customize without routing every query to a commercial API. The milestone is a reminder that open-weight ecosystems are a distinct competitive axis from frontier proprietary models — and increasingly a U.S.–China contest for developer default.
