# Google Says Its AI Can Automate Forward Deployed Engineers' Work

While OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, and Amazon invest billions hiring "forward deployed engineers" (Palantir's playbook), Google says AI can automate much of their work. Google Cloud VP Andi Gutmans: "If you want to activate 100% of your enterprise data, you're not going to be able to hire enough people."
