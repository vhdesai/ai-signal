While documentation updates are not product launches, they show continued investment in making advanced inference and post-training workflows easier to deploy outside hyperscaler-only environments. For engineering leaders, the practical takeaway is that AI infrastructure maturity increasingly depends on repeatable deployment recipes, not only faster hardware.
