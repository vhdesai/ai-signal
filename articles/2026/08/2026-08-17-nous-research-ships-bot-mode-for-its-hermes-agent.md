# Nous Research ships Bot Mode for its Hermes agent

Nous Research shipped Bot Mode for Hermes, replacing a single-agent session list with a roster of named bots, each carrying its own chat history, memory, skills, and pinned model. The design moves agent state from ephemeral sessions toward persistent, role-scoped identities. That pattern maps closely to how enterprises actually want agents organized — by function and permission boundary rather than by conversation. marktechpost.com – Nous Research ships Bot Mode for Hermes Products & Tools FUNDING CREATIVE AI
