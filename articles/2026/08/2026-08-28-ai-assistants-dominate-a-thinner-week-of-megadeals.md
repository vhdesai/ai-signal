# AI Assistants Dominate a Thinner Week of Megadeals

Crunchbase's weekly tally shows AI-focused startups taking most of the largest rounds, led by AI assistant developer Instinct. The overall lineup was sparser than recent weeks, suggesting selectivity rather than a broad pullback. Instinct's valuation trajectory — reported at $500M earlier in August before a new round at roughly $2.5B — illustrates how compressed the repricing cycle has become for agent-layer companies.
