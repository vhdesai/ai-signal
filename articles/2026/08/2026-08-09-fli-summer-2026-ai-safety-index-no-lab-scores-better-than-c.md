# FLI Summer 2026 AI Safety Index: No Lab Scores Better Than C+

The Future of Life Institute’s Summer 2026 index gave no frontier lab above C+. Anthropic led at 2.66, followed by OpenAI at 2.28 and Google DeepMind at 2.01. Landing alongside the containment reporting and congressional letter, it strengthens the case that safety practices remain materially inadequate relative to the capabilities being deployed.
