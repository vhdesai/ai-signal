# China allows ByteDance and Tencent to import ~10,000 Nvidia H200 chips each

ByteDance and Tencent have each received roughly 10,000 Nvidia H200 processors in recent weeks — the first sizeable shipments since Washington cleared each firm to buy up to 100,000 units. Beijing is routing the chips through Hong Kong.
