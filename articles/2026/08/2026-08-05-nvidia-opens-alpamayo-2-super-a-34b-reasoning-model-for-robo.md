# NVIDIA opens Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34B reasoning model for robotaxi and AV developers

NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34-billion-parameter open vision-language-action model for autonomous driving, published to Hugging Face under a license that explicitly permits commercial robotaxi and AV development. NVIDIA reports a 79.2 score on the LingoQA driving-comprehension benchmark, though that figure is vendor-reported and not yet independently replicated. The commercial-use license is the strategic move here: it lowers the barrier for AV startups to build on NVIDIA's stack rather than train in-house. Expect this to tighten NVIDIA's grip on the automotive AI toolchain ahead of the next robotaxi permitting cycle. Research Breakthroughs HotNewCursor
