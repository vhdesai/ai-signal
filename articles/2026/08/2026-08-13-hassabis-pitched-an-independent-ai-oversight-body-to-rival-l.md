# Hassabis Pitched an Independent AI Oversight Body to Rival Labs and U.S. Officials

Demis Hassabis proposed an independent industry body to codify shared AI safety practices, discussing it with heads of other frontier labs and Trump administration officials including Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and technology adviser Michael Kratsios. The concept is loosely analogous to the IAEA but faces a harder verification problem: assessing safety claims about privately developed software from direct competitors. The proposal landed days before Hassabis stepped aside from DeepMind’s day-to-day leadership. The fact that the conversation involved both rival CEOs and senior government officials suggests it has progressed beyond the whiteboard stage. WSJ REGULATION
