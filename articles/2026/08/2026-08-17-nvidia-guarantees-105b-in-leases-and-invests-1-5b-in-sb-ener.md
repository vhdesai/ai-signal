# Nvidia guarantees ~$105B in leases and invests $1.5B in SB Energy’s Ohio AI campus

Nvidia is backing roughly $105 billion of lease financing and taking a $1.5 billion equity position in SB Energy, the SoftBank-affiliated developer of the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio, which is expected to host OpenAI capacity. Nvidia’s own release confirms it will be the exclusive AI compute provider at the site and will provide credit support on land, power, and shell capacity.
