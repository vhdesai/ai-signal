# AMD Acquires Taalas to Hardwire Models into Silicon

AMD is acquiring Toronto-based Taalas, which etches trained AI models directly into silicon to cut the compute and memory overhead of inference. Terms were undisclosed; Taalas had raised roughly $219 million since its 2023 founding. AMD plans to fold the technology into its accelerator roadmap alongside Instinct GPUs, EPYC CPUs, and ROCm. The signal is that the next phase of the chip contest centers on specialized inference architectures and the memory wall, not on general-purpose GPU throughput alone.
