# Lovable confirms $13.3 billion valuation and $400 million Series C

Lovable said it raised $400 million in a Series C round led by Menlo Ventures and the Scaleup Europe Fund, confirming a $13.3 billion valuation. The company says it hosts 60 million projects with 900 million monthly visitors and had previously reported $500 million in annualized run-rate revenue. The funding underlines how quickly AI coding and app-generation platforms are being valued as application infrastructure, even as competitive and compute-cost pressures remain high.
