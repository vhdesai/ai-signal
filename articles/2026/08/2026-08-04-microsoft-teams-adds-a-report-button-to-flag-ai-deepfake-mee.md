# Microsoft Teams adds a “Report” button to flag AI deepfake meetings

Microsoft confirmed a new Teams capability, rolling out this month, that lets participants report a suspected security issue — including AI deepfake impersonation — during a live meeting. The move responds to rising real-time synthetic-identity threats in enterprise collaboration. It signals that deepfake defense is moving from research into shipped, user-facing controls.
