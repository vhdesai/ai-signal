# DeepSeek resumes funding talks and plans to hike model prices

DeepSeek has resumed fundraising discussions and plans to raise pricing on its models, signaling a pivot from the aggressive price-cutting that defined its market entry. Even the most cost-competitive Chinese AI labs face economic pressure to generate sustainable revenue as training and inference costs grow. For enterprises that adopted DeepSeek on low pricing, the planned hikes introduce vendor risk and reinforce the importance of multi-model procurement.
