# Uber faces fine of nearly $1B over automated driver suspensions

The Dutch Data Protection Authority is fining Uber EUR 825 million over its automated suspension of drivers, described as the second-largest penalty ever issued under GDPR. The case turns on algorithmic decision-making affecting workers rather than on data handling in the narrow sense. It is a direct precedent for any enterprise running automated adverse-action decisions on people in the EU. POLICY
