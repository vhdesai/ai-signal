# Anthropic IPO Filing Expected to Name AI Backlash as a Risk Factor

Anthropic is preparing registration documents for an IPO that people familiar with the process say could rival the largest ever, with a filing possible as soon as the end of August. Reporting indicates the filing will explicitly flag public hostility toward data centers and AI-driven job displacement among its risk factors. Anthropic's annualized revenue reportedly reached roughly $65B by end-July, making the offering the clearest public-market test yet of pure-play frontier AI economics. FUNDING
