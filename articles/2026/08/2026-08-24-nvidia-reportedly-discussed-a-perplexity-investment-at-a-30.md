# Nvidia reportedly discussed a Perplexity investment at a $30 billion-plus valuation

Reuters reported that Nvidia has discussed investing in Perplexity at a valuation above $30 billion, citing The Information. The potential investment fits Nvidia's broader strategy of supporting AI application and infrastructure companies that may drive demand for accelerated compute. For executives, the pattern is notable: Nvidia is increasingly using capital as an ecosystem-shaping tool, not merely as a financial investment.
