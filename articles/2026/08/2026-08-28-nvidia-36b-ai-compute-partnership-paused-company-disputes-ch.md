# Nvidia $36B AI Compute Partnership Paused — Company Disputes Characterization

Nvidia stepped back from its AI Compute Partnership less than two months after launching it, following internal antitrust concerns and pushback from cloud providers over customer controls. The program guaranteed rentals to smaller GPU cloud operators in exchange for 50% of revenue above a base hourly rate and had accumulated $36B in commitments. Nvidia publicly denied pausing the initiative, leaving the financing outlook for neocloud providers unresolved.
