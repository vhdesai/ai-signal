# Baidu Q2 revenue falls 4% as AI cloud surges 50%

Baidu reported Q2 revenue of RMB31.33B, down 4% year-over-year and below estimates, as online-marketing revenue fell 19%. AI Cloud Infrastructure revenue rose 50% to RMB7.3B, with GPU cloud revenue up 283%.
