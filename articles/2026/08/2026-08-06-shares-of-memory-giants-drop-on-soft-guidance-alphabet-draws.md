# Shares of Memory Giants Drop on Soft Guidance; Alphabet Draws Strong Demand for $25B Debt Sale

Memory chip stocks reversed sharply after major manufacturers issued softer-than-expected guidance, injecting uncertainty into the AI infrastructure narrative that had driven the sector higher for months. The selloff suggests that memory supply may be catching up to demand faster than expected, potentially easing one of the key constraints on AI hardware availability. Separately, Alphabet drew strong investor demand for a $25 billion debt sale with higher yields, demonstrating that even at elevated interest rates, fixed-income investors are eager to finance AI infrastructure buildouts. The dual signals create a complex picture for AI infrastructure planning: softening memory markets could reduce hardware costs, while robust debt demand ensures financing remains available for long-term buildout.
