# Google and Microsoft race to wire US schools with AI

The New York Times reports that Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and other large technology companies are investing billions to place their AI tools in US classrooms — from Copilot rollouts to Gemini for Education and grants routed through teacher unions. The piece frames the push as a competition to establish platform defaults for a generation of students. Researchers quoted question whether current systems are ready for K-12 deployment at all.
