# TechCrunch reports that Snap updated Spotlight monetization and amplification rules to exclude fully AI-generated…

TechCrunch reports that Snap updated Spotlight monetization and amplification rules to exclude fully AI-generated videos from rewards. The move is a platform-level response to low-quality generative content and an attempt to preserve human-created content value. It may become a reference point for other short-form video platforms as AI-generated media volume increases.
