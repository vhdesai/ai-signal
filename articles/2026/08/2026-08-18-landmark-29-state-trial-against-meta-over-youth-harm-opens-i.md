# Landmark 29-State Trial Against Meta Over Youth Harm Opens in Oakland

Opening arguments began in federal court in Oakland in a bipartisan action by 29 state attorneys general alleging Facebook and Instagram were designed to addict minors. The states seek product-level remedies — age restrictions and changes to features such as infinite scroll. POLICYHOT
