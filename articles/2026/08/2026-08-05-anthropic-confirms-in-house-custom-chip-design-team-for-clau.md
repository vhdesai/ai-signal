# Anthropic confirms in-house custom chip design team for Claude

Anthropic confirmed for the first time that it is building an internal chip-design team to co-design silicon and models for Claude. The company says it will continue running on AWS, Google, NVIDIA, and AMD hardware, positioning the effort as complementary. The move follows the vertical-integration logic Google and Amazon applied years earlier and signals that inference unit economics are the binding constraint on frontier-lab margins.
