# Adversarial Patterns Demonstrated to Defeat Surveillance Camera Detection at Def Con

Security researcher Bill Swearingen demonstrated at Def Con that reinforcement-learning-generated visual patterns printed on clothing and vehicles can defeat detection by 11 open-source surveillance algorithms — including software powering Flock license plate readers, Axon body cameras, and Clearview AI facial recognition. After 31 million training tests, the system ("noRecognition") generates improving patterns every minute. A live test on a Toyota Yaris successfully evaded a Flock camera. The patterns don't block recording — they prevent the algorithmic detection layer from triggering alerts. ________________________________ Infrastructure & Enterprise ENTERPRISE AGENTS
