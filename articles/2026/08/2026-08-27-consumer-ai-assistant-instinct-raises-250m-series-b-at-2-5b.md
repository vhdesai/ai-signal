# Consumer AI Assistant Instinct Raises $250M Series B at $2.5B Valuation

Instinct, a roughly one-year-old consumer AI assistant startup founded by 23-year-old Noah Shinn, closed a $250 million Series B co-led by Index Ventures and Benchmark at a $2.5 billion post-money valuation, bringing total funding to $350 million. Reporting notes the valuation is roughly five times its mark from only weeks earlier. The round is a datapoint on how quickly consumer-assistant assets are being repriced, and coverage also flags unresolved privacy concerns around the product's data collection. TALENT
