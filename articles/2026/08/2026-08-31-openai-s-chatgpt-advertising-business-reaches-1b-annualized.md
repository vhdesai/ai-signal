# OpenAI's ChatGPT Advertising Business Reaches $1B Annualized Run Rate

OpenAI disclosed that ChatGPT Ads has hit a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate less than 200 days after launch, with tens of thousands of advertisers across 40+ countries. Self-serve Ads Manager is expanding into India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa. The milestone establishes advertising as a credible second revenue engine alongside subscriptions and API.
