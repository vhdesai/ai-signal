# Alibaba raises $10.2B in Hong Kong’s biggest-ever follow-on to fund full-stack AI

Alibaba announced an HK$80 billion ($10.2 billion) share placement, selling 710 million shares at HK$112.70 — a 3.6% discount to Friday’s close. All net proceeds are earmarked for “full-stack AI capabilities” spanning chips, computing infrastructure and model development. It is the largest primary follow-on offering ever by a Hong Kong-listed company, and a clear signal that Chinese hyperscalers intend to fund the compute race from public equity markets.
