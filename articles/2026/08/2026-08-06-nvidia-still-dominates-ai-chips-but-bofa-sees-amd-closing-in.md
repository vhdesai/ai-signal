# Nvidia still dominates AI chips, but BofA sees AMD closing in

Nvidia continues to dominate AI accelerators, but Bank of America analysts see AMD closing the gap, drawing a parallel to AMD’s decade-long climb against Intel, Yahoo Finance reported. The note underscores intensifying competition in AI silicon even as Nvidia’s data-center revenue holds at record levels.
