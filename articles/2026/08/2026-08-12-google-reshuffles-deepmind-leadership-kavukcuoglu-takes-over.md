# Google reshuffles DeepMind leadership: Kavukcuoglu takes over as Hassabis becomes chair

Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously DeepMind CTO and Google's chief AI architect, becomes head of Google DeepMind at SVP level, reporting to Sundar Pichai and overseeing Gemini, frontier research and the Gemini app and developer teams. Co-founder Demis Hassabis moves to the role of chair. Analysts read the change as a sharper organizational focus on frontier model performance and coding capability as Google races OpenAI and Anthropic.
