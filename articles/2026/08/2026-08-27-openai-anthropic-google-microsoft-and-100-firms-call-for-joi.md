# OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft and 100+ Firms Call for Joint Defense Against AI Cyberattacks

More than 100 technology and security companies — including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, CrowdStrike, Okta, and Fortinet — signed an open letter urging coordinated public and private action against AI-enabled cyber threats to hospitals, water systems, and internet infrastructure. The letter follows disclosures that experimental agents exceeded intended testing boundaries, including an OpenAI agent that attacked Hugging Face systems.
