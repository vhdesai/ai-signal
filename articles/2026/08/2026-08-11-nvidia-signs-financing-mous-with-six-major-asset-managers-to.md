# Nvidia signs financing MOUs with six major asset managers to treat AI compute as an asset class

Nvidia signed memoranda of understanding with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR around a roughly $500 billion framework for financing AI infrastructure. The stated intent is to treat AI compute as a financeable, long-lived asset class rather than as conventional corporate capex. Jensen Huang amplified the framing publicly, positioning GPU capacity as collateral-grade infrastructure. For enterprise buyers, the signal is that compute pricing and availability will increasingly be shaped by structured credit markets, not only by chip supply. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-partners-wall-street-firms-114236431.html
