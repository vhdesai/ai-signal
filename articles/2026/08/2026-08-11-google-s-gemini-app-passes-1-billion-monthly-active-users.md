# Google's Gemini App Passes 1 Billion Monthly Active Users

Sundar Pichai confirmed the Gemini app surpassed 1 billion monthly active users, making it Alphabet's fastest product to the milestone. Google reported that 63% of users interact by voice, one in five Gemini Live sessions involve camera or screen sharing, and the system generates over 150 million images daily.
