# Anthropic's MHS preview reports early lab and quantum-computing automation results

Anthropic said early MHS partners used the standard to automate scientific and manufacturing workflows, including biotech assays, robotic-arm coordination, and quantum-computing laser calibration. Reported examples include QuEra improving laser relock success from 58% to 99.3% and Carnegie Mellon running dose-response experiments about three times faster. These are early partner results, but they point to a meaningful research-operations opportunity: agents that can safely operate instruments may accelerate experimental iteration.
