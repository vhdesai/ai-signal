# Federal Judge Strikes Down Pentagon’s Blacklisting of Anthropic on First Amendment Grounds

A federal judge in Northern California ruled the Defense Department’s designation of Anthropic as a security and supply-chain risk was unlawful and violated the company’s First Amendment rights. The dispute originated in Anthropic’s refusal of an open-ended Pentagon contract that would have overridden its usage limits, including bars on lethal autonomous weapons and domestic mass surveillance. Anthropic still faces separate litigation in Washington and the government may appeal. UPI →
