# Anthropic Investors Anticipate $2 Trillion IPO; Decart Acquisition Talks at $6B

Backers of Anthropic told the Financial Times they expect the company to double its current valuation when it goes public, potentially as soon as this fall — implying a roughly $2 trillion IPO. Separately, Anthropic is in talks to acquire Decart, an AI startup, for approximately $6 billion, according to Bloomberg. The dual moves suggest Anthropic is simultaneously preparing for public markets while continuing to acquire capabilities through M&A.
