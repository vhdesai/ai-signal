# xAI Faces Child-Safety Lawsuits Over Grok Imagery — and Countersues Its Own Users

Alleged victims are suing xAI over AI-generated child sexual abuse material and non-consensual deepfakes produced via Grok, and a new suit alleges Grok was trained on such material rather than merely capable of generating it. xAI has responded with an aggressive posture that includes taking legal action against its own users. The matter is likely to shape liability standards for generative image systems.
