# Nvidia Signs $500B+ Financing Alliance With Six Wall Street Firms

Nvidia signed MOUs with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to build compute financing platforms targeting $500B+ for AI infrastructure. The intent is to make GPU capacity a financeable, long-lived asset class. Jensen Huang positioned GPU capacity as collateral-grade infrastructure. The strategic read: Nvidia is moving from hardware supplier to financial orchestrator of the buildout — deepening circular-financing concerns.
