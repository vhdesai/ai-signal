# Microsoft and HUMAIN to bring ALLAM Arabic models into Foundry and Copilot

PIF-owned HUMAIN and Microsoft announced a long-term collaboration to make HUMAIN's ALLAM Arabic-language models available through Microsoft Foundry and to power specialized Microsoft 365 Copilot agents. The integrations are described as planned, with no launch date attached. Sovereign-language model partnerships continue to be a primary route into Gulf-region public sector demand.
