# MIT: AI-generated images often can't be traced back to training data

MIT CSAIL researchers describe "attribution decay": at large training scale, removing any single image or artist from the dataset does not measurably change model outputs, meaning generated images frequently cannot be attributed to specific training examples. Published in Nature Communications.
