# SaaS companies race to reinvent as AI agents replace software seats

The WSJ highlighted how formerly high-flying SaaS companies are scrambling to reinvent as AI threatens to obsolete traditional seat-based software interfaces. Agents that can navigate workflows autonomously undercut the value proposition of per-user licensing—the revenue model that powered a decade of SaaS multiples. The piece is the clearest signal yet that the "SaaS-pocalypse" thesis is moving from analyst conjecture to market reality, with enterprise buyers rethinking procurement strategies and vendors racing to pivot toward outcome-based or consumption pricing. For technology leaders, the article underscores the urgency of evaluating how AI agents will reshape vendor relationships and internal tooling strategies.
