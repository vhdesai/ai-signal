# OpenAI previews Astra to Washington officials before public release

OpenAI has begun previewing a new model family tentatively named Astra to policymakers and regulators in Washington, D.C. The model is described as better suited for long-running tasks and multi-agent coordination, and it appears tied to OpenAI's recent formal-reasoning claims around mathematics. The D.C.-first rollout suggests regulatory alignment and government adoption are becoming part of frontier-model launch strategy, not an afterthought.
