# Amazon Behind Massive Private Gas Plant for New Data Centers

Amazon confirmed it is financing GW Ranch, a 35-turbine, 7.65-gigawatt private gas plant in Pecos County, Texas, developed by Pacifico Energy — larger than any gas plant currently operating in the US and permitted to emit more than 30 million tonnes of greenhouse gases annually. Amazon argues self-generation avoids passing grid costs to consumers and says it is exploring on-site solar and storage while using brackish groundwater. Behind-the-meter power, once a niche xAI tactic, now has roughly 97 gigawatts of projects planned across every hyperscaler. Speed to compute is being purchased with carbon and permitting exposure.
