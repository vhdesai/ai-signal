# AI stock pullback barely dents broader market, but concentration risks persist

WSJ analysis says the latest AI-stock pullback has had limited impact on the broader market because AI capex benefits a wide ecosystem: cloud providers, chip designers, memory suppliers, data centers, and power providers. That resilience is encouraging, but it does not eliminate concentration risk if hyperscalers or Nvidia-linked supply chains correct together. The article keeps the AI bubble debate alive without treating every selloff as systemic. URL: Stock-market bubbles recovery
