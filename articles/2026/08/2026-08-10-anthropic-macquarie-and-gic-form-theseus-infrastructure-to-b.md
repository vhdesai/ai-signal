# Anthropic, Macquarie and GIC form Theseus Infrastructure to build dedicated data centers

Anthropic, Macquarie Asset Management and Singapore's GIC announced a strategic partnership establishing Theseus Infrastructure, a platform to develop, operate and lease AI data center capacity at scale, with an initial focus on the United States. The sites will be leased to Anthropic under long-term agreements. The structure gives Anthropic dedicated capacity without carrying the full construction balance sheet.
