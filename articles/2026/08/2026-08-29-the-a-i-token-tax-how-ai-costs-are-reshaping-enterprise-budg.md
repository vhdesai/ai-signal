# The A.I. Token Tax: How AI Costs Are Reshaping Enterprise Budgets

DealBook examines how AI usage costs are becoming a significant, often unpredictable line item — a new “tax” on digital operations. As companies move from pilots to production, cumulative token costs across the organization are forcing CIOs to grapple with cost governance, model selection, and the capability-vs-efficiency tension.
