# One in Five Enterprises Cannot Halt a Runaway AI Agent's Spending in Real Time

VentureBeat Pulse research finds that enterprises typically run three AI orchestration platforms concurrently, yet 21% report they cannot stop an agent's runaway spending in real time. The finding quantifies a control gap that most AI governance frameworks currently address only on paper. It also explains the commercial gravity behind the Stripe/OpenRouter transaction: spend observability is now a control requirement, not a reporting nicety. AI Safety & Policy SAFETYHOT
