# Rippling Ships AI Spend Console After Discovering 40% of R&D Budget Went to Tokens

HR platform Rippling revealed it was burning 40% of its R&D headcount budget on AI tokens — growing 80% month-over-month — before building an internal governance tool it now sells externally. The AI Spend Console tracks per-employee token spend against productivity output and routes prompts to cost-effective models. Rippling cut costs to 15% of headcount budget without reducing usage (600B tokens/month) by model-routing.
