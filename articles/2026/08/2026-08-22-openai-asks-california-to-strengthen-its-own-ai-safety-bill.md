# OpenAI Asks California to Strengthen Its Own AI Safety Bill

OpenAI's global affairs team said California's SB 53 "should be amended to expand safeguards," including monitoring of frontier models while still under training. The position is notable because it runs against the industry's general preference for lighter state-level obligations and for federal preemption. It suggests leading labs now see a defensible compliance floor as preferable to fragmented state rules. SECURITY
