# Google Releases Gemini 3.7 Flash at Half the Price of 3.6 Flash

Gemini 3.7 Flash shipped three weeks after 3.6 Flash, with a 1M-token context window at $0.75/1M input and $3.75/1M output — half the prior list rate, running through Dec 31. Reports DeepSWE v1.1 at 65.3% (vs. 49.0%) and WebDev Arena Elo of 1588 (vs. 1538). Now powers Gemini Spark for AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. The strategic emphasis is cost-per-agent-step, targeting high-volume agent traffic rather than frontier benchmark leadership. For enterprises running multi-step agent workflows, it competes directly with DeepSeek and Claude Haiku at the orchestration layer.
