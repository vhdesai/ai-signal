# Google Releases Gemini 3.7 Flash at Half the Price of 3.6 Flash

Google DeepMind shipped Gemini 3.7 Flash just three weeks after 3.6 Flash, describing it as its most capable workhorse model for coding and agentic workloads, with a 1M-token context window. Introductory pricing is $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output — half the prior list rate — running through December 31, 2026. Google reports DeepSWE v1.1 at 65.3% (vs. 49.0%) and WebDev Arena Elo of 1588 (vs. 1538). The release now powers Gemini Spark for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. The strategic emphasis is cost-per-agent-step: Flash is aimed at high-volume agent traffic rather than frontier benchmark leadership. For enterprises running multi-step agent workflows, Gemini 3.7 Flash's pricing makes it a direct competitor to DeepSeek and Claude Haiku at the orchestration layer.
