# Anthropic Researcher Shows Automated AI Can Improve Alignment Faster — and Cheaper — Than Humans

Anthropic published "Automated Researchers Can Reliably Mitigate Alignment Failures," demonstrating that AI systems can improve a model's performance across 10 alignment benchmarks without degrading overall capability. The automated alignment researchers (AARs) search literature, propose methods, train models in 30-minute cycles, and iterate — beating experienced human researchers "on average within six hours." The cost comparison is stark: $4/hour for an AAR vs. $150/hour for human researchers. The paper explicitly frames this as "early evidence that automated alignment post-training could become practical in the near term" — a step toward recursive self-improvement that could make human AI researchers "obsolete." 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/an-anthropic-researcher-just-gave-us-a-peek-at-self-improving-ai/
