# OpenAI describes building an AI-native finance function

OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar published lessons on building an AI-native finance function, including automated forecasting, stronger controls, and AI ROI measurement. The post matters because finance is becoming a proving ground for enterprise AI: high-value workflows are structured, measurable, and tied directly to planning and capital allocation. For senior leaders, AI adoption should increasingly be managed as an operating discipline, not a productivity side project.
