# NVIDIA frames AI factory compute as an investable infrastructure asset class

NVIDIA said it is partnering with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR on financing platforms intended to mobilize more than $500 billion of third-party capital for AI infrastructure over time. The company argued that AI factories can be underwritten like productive infrastructure because compute capacity generates revenue, can serve many workloads, and may retain residual value through software improvements and redeployment. The announcement directly addresses concerns about AI infrastructure circularity by emphasizing independent underwriting, though residual-value risk remains a core issue to watch.
