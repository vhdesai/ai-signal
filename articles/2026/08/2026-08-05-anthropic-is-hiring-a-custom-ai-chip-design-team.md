# Anthropic is hiring a custom AI chip design team

Anthropic confirmed it is building a custom silicon team to co-design hardware and models for faster, more efficient Claude workloads. The move follows the company's expanding compute deals with AWS, Google, NVIDIA, and AMD, and comes amid reports that Anthropic has explored Samsung as a manufacturing partner. Frontier labs are increasingly treating silicon as a strategic control point, not just a procurement category.
