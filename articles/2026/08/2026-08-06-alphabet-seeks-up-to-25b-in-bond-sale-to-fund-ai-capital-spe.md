# Alphabet Seeks Up to $25B in Bond Sale to Fund AI Capital Spending

Alphabet launched a multi-tranche U.S. bond offering seeking up to $25 billion, spanning maturities from two to 40 years, reportedly attracting roughly $115 billion in orders. The raise follows Alphabet's first negative free-cash-flow quarter and underscores how far AI capital intensity has pushed even the most cash-generative companies toward external financing. Debt-funded compute buildout is becoming the norm, which introduces refinancing sensitivity if accelerator depreciation accelerates. FUNDING
