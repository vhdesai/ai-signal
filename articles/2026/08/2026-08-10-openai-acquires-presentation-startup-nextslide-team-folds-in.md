# OpenAI acquires presentation startup NextSlide; team folds into ChatGPT

OpenAI acquired NextSlide, with the team joining the ChatGPT project. The move signals continued expansion of ChatGPT from conversation into document and deck generation — directly adjacent to Microsoft 365 productivity surfaces. Terms were not disclosed.
