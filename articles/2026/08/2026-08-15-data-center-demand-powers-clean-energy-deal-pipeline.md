# Data Center Demand Powers Clean Energy Deal Pipeline

PE investment in clean energy fell 62.3% quarter-over-quarter to $24.5 billion in Q2 2026, but the decline was almost entirely due to the absence of Q1's $40.6 billion AES take-private. Strip that out and the underlying pace is stable, with annual deal value climbing from $47.5 billion (2021) to a record $98 billion (2025). Median deal size reached $186.6 million in H1 2026. AI data center power demand continues to be a key driver of dealflow in the sector.
