# DealBook: SpaceX races for spectrum as satellite infrastructure becomes strategic real estate

DealBook examined SpaceX's push to secure radio spectrum, the invisible infrastructure that lets satellites communicate with Earth. The piece is not a model-release story, but it matters for AI-era infrastructure because low-latency satellite connectivity, sovereign control of spectrum, and global telecom regulation increasingly shape where compute, robotics, and autonomous systems can operate. The fight also shows how private first movers can create de facto rules before international governance catches up.
