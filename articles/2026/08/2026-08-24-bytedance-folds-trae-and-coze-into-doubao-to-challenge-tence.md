# ByteDance folds Trae and Coze into Doubao to challenge Tencent’s WorkBuddy

ByteDance is consolidating its Trae coding platform and Coze agent-building tool into Doubao, and plans to launch Doubao Work to compete directly with Tencent’s WorkBuddy productivity agent. The move follows a July 30 restructuring that merged Feishu’s product team into Doubao. WorkBuddy has quietly become China’s most popular AI productivity agent at roughly 21 million monthly PC visits in June, making this a consolidation play against a clear incumbent.
