# SK hynix and SanDisk publish the first HBF standard, expanding the AI-memory ecosystem

SK hynix and SanDisk unveiled the first High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) standard specifications at FMS 2026, within six months of forming the consortium, with participation from partners including Google and Tenstorrent. HBF targets the memory-capacity bottleneck for large-model inference by pairing flash economics with high bandwidth. Standardization this early signals a coordinated push to broaden AI-memory supply beyond HBM. INFRASTRUCTURECAPEXSTRATEGY
