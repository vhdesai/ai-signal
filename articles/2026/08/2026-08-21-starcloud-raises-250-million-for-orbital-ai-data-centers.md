# Starcloud raises $250 million for orbital AI data centers

Starcloud raised a $250 million extension to its Series A, valuing the orbital data-center startup at $2.3 billion. The company is developing satellites for AI inference in orbit and says it needs capital partly to secure launch capacity as rocket availability tightens. Orbital compute remains early and capital-intensive, but the raise shows continued investor interest in nontraditional AI infrastructure when terrestrial power, cooling, and siting become constraints.
