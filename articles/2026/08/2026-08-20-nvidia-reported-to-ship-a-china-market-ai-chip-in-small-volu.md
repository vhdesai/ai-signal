# Nvidia reported to ship a China-market AI chip in small volumes by year-end

Nvidia plans to begin shipping limited volumes of an AI accelerator designed for Chinese customers by the end of the year, according to reporting citing two employees. The move would partially restore a market Nvidia has been largely excluded from under export controls. Volumes are described as small, suggesting a compliance-constrained product rather than a return to prior China revenue levels. BUILD-OUT
