# Local bans and restrictions on AI data centers pass 500 nationwide

Reporting published today puts the number of local U.S. laws seeking to ban or restrict data center development above 500, as municipal and county officials respond to constituent pressure over power costs, water use and local pollution. The pushback is bipartisan and is now material to siting timelines rather than being purely reputational. Enterprises with committed capacity plans should treat local permitting risk as a distinct line item in AI infrastructure schedules.
