# Micron commits $10B to a Boise research hub targeting post-DRAM memory

Micron unveiled Micron Research Labs, a Boise long-horizon research hub backed by a planned $10B investment over the next decade covering post-DRAM architectures, advanced packaging, and future AI compute. It sits inside a wider ~$50B Boise buildout expected to add more than 17,000 jobs. CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said AI has "totally changed" memory's boom-and-bust cycle, with demand outstripping supply and 16 customers signing five-year strategic supply agreements — a structural signal that memory, not just logic, is the constrained input.
