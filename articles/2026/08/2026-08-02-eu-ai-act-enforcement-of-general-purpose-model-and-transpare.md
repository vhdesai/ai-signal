# EU AI Act: enforcement of general-purpose-model and transparency rules begins

From August 2, the Commission's AI Office and national authorities began enforcing the AI Act's obligations for general-purpose AI models: documentation, copyright policy, training-data summaries, and transparency duties requiring AI-generated or manipulated content to be labeled.
