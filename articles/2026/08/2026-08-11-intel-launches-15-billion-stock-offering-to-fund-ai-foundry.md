# Intel Launches $15 Billion Stock Offering to Fund AI Foundry Expansion

Intel is raising $15 billion in fresh capital targeting physical AI, purpose-built silicon, and advanced packaging amid the AI acceleration wave. The offering funds Intel's foundry expansion strategy as it aims to become a major contract chipmaker for AI workloads — an aggressive bet on capturing AI infrastructure demand beyond its traditional CPU business.
