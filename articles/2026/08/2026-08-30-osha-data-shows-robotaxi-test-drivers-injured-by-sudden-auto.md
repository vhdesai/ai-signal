# OSHA data shows robotaxi test drivers injured by sudden autonomous braking

A TechCrunch investigation of OSHA filings found Waymo and Zoox test drivers sustained more than two dozen injuries in 2024 and 2025 from hard braking and other abrupt autonomous vehicle movements, with some workers sidelined for months. Other developers may face similar issues but be exempt from OSHA reporting requirements, limiting visibility.
