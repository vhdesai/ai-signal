# Anthropic previews Model Hardware Standard for agents operating physical devices

Anthropic opened a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard, a shared specification for AI agents to discover and operate physical devices such as microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms, and quantum-computing lab equipment. The standard uses common drivers, discovery, safety limits, and MCP-compatible control paths to reduce hardware integration from weeks or months to hours or minutes. The important signal is that physical-world agents will require standardized interfaces and safety constraints before broad deployment in labs and manufacturing.
