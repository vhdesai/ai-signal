# Stripe reportedly moves to acquire OpenRouter as AI routing becomes a platform layer

IBM published analysis of Stripe's reported OpenRouter deal, framing it as evidence that money in AI is moving toward routing, gateway, billing, and model-abstraction layers. The strategic point is that as model choice fragments across providers, enterprises need a control plane for cost, latency, reliability, policy, and vendor portability. For executives, OpenRouter's reported value is less about any one model and more about owning the transaction layer between applications and model supply.
