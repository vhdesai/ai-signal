# Samsung's xMAE and HiMAE show foundation-model approaches for biosignal analysis

Samsung's xMAE and HiMAE research applies self-supervised learning to unlabeled wearable biosignals, aiming to support downstream tasks such as cardiovascular prediction, abnormal-result detection, sleep-stage classification, and health-data generation. xMAE reconstructs masked ECG signals from continuous PPG data, while HiMAE models short- and long-window wearable signals through a hierarchical masked autoencoder. For health AI leaders, the key takeaway is that foundation-model methods are being adapted to physiological time-series data, not just text, image, and video.
