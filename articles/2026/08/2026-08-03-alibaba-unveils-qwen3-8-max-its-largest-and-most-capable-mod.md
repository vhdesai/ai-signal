# Alibaba unveils Qwen3.8-Max, its “largest and most capable” model

Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Max, positioning it as the most capable model in its Qwen series and a direct challenger to leading U.S. and Chinese frontier systems. Independent third-party benchmarks were not available at launch, so the headline performance claims remain vendor-reported for now. The release continues the rapid cadence of Chinese frontier models and intensifies both competitive and policy scrutiny of Chinese systems.
