# EY Creates 'AI Value Realization Office' to Govern AI Spending and ROI

EY is setting up a dedicated "AI Value Realization Office" to centralize AI spending governance, drive returns, monitor usage, and decide which AI initiatives to scale. Dan Diasio, EY's global consulting AI leader, said 75% of potential enterprise AI value comes from horizontal value streams cutting across departments — not siloed projects. The office reflects a broader shift: an EY US AI Pulse survey found 98% of senior decision-makers said token spending had made them reconsider their approach. The firm has already reduced token consumption 60% through an AI router that directs employees to the best model per task. 🔗 https://www.businessinsider.com/big-four-ey-creates-ai-value-realization-office-2026-8
