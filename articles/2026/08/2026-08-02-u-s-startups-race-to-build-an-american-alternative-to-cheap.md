# U.S. startups race to build an American alternative to cheap Chinese AI

The Wall Street Journal reports that U.S. startups are racing to build open-weight models that can compete with lower-cost Chinese systems such as DeepSeek and Alibaba's Qwen. The challenge is not just technical; investors remain skeptical that open-weight startups can monetize effectively against subsidized or lower-cost Chinese competitors. The result is a national-security and market-structure issue: enterprises may increasingly choose Chinese-origin models for cost-sensitive workloads unless U.S. alternatives become both capable and economically viable.
