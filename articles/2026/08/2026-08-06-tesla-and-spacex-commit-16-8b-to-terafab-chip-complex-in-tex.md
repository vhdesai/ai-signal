# Tesla and SpaceX Commit $16.8B to "Terafab" Chip Complex in Texas

Tesla and SpaceX formally announced a jointly funded semiconductor complex called Terafab in Grimes County, Texas, with an initial $16.8 billion commitment. The site is intended to manufacture, package, and test memory and logic chips for Tesla vehicles, Optimus robots, and SpaceX computing systems, with a footprint that could eventually reach roughly 100 million square feet. The move takes two large chip buyers toward vertical integration and adds another non-traditional entrant to advanced packaging and fabrication capacity. FUNDING
