# Stanford Awarded Up to $27.2M by ARPA-H for AI-Powered Blood-Clot-Clearing Micro-Robot

ARPA-H awarded Stanford professor Renee Zhao's lab a multi-year grant of up to $27.2M to develop an AI-controlled spinning micro-robot that navigates the bloodstream and mechanically shrinks blood clots in place, without systemic thrombolytic drugs, aiming toward clinical use for stroke patients. The award is a notable example of large-scale U.S. government funding at the intersection of physical AI, medical robotics, and government-backed R&D. AI Safety & Policy POLICYLEGAL
