# LTX-2.5 Launches as Open-Weights Video Model — 10-Second Clips in 6.8s

An Nvidia-accelerated open-weights video model producing 10-second clips from an image in ~6.8 seconds, with day-one ComfyUI support. Open weights mean iteration runs on owned hardware with no per-generation billing — materially changing creative experimentation economics for media and marketing teams with IP constraints.
