# Zuckerberg Publishes 3,500-Word Manifesto on Open AI and 'Personal Superintelligence'

Mark Zuckerberg published an extensive manifesto arguing that superintelligence should be distributed widely rather than centralized, positioning individual empowerment over institutional control. The essay accompanied Meta's release of Muse Glimmer and its open-weight commitment for Muse Spark 1.2.
