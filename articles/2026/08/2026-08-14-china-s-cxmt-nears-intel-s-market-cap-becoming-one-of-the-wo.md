# China's CXMT Nears Intel's Market Cap, Becoming One of the World's Most Valuable Chipmakers

Chinese memory chipmaker ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) has reached a market capitalization of $540.5B, just behind Intel at $552.6B and ahead of Tencent at $505.8B. CXMT's rapid rise reflects surging demand for AI-related memory chips and China's push for semiconductor self-sufficiency. The development is notable for the shifting global power dynamics in the chip industry. 🔗
