# CoreWeave signs multi-year storage agreement with Solidigm

CoreWeave signed a multi-year strategic agreement with Solidigm for priority access to dedicated SSD capacity supporting its AI cloud platform. It is another reminder that AI capacity planning is not just GPUs and power; storage throughput and priority supply are becoming control points.
