# Higgsfield raises $400M Series B at a $5.4B valuation

Higgsfield, a generative video and image platform for professional creators, raised a $400 million Series B at a $5.4 billion valuation — roughly quadruple its January mark — with annualized revenue reported at about $700 million. Management framed the raise around deeper enterprise embedding of video AI in marketing and creative workflows. The revenue trajectory suggests creative-tool spend is converting faster than most enterprise AI categories. techcrunch.com – Higgsfield raises $400M Series B FUNDING VOICE AI
