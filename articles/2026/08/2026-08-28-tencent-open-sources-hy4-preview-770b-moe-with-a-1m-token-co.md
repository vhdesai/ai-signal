# Tencent Open-Sources Hy4 Preview: 770B MoE With a 1M-Token Context Window

Tencent's Hunyuan team released and open-sourced Hy4 preview — a MoE flagship with 770B total parameters, 49B activated per token, and a context window exceeding one million tokens. Positioned around long-horizon coding, office workflows, and research, with weights published alongside API access. Continued open-weight releases at this scale from Chinese labs keep downward pressure on inference pricing and complicate any assumption of durable capability leads at the frontier.
