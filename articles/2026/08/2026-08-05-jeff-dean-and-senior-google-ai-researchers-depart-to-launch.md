# Jeff Dean and Senior Google AI Researchers Depart to Launch Discovery Loop

Jeff Dean (Google Chief Scientist), Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals are leaving Google to co-found Discovery Loop, a public benefit corporation applying recursive self-improvement techniques to automate scientific discovery. Dean will serve as CEO; the seed round is co-led by Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures, with Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed, Doerr Capital, and Alphabet itself participating. This is one of the most significant simultaneous senior-researcher departures from Google's AI organization to date, and places Discovery Loop in direct competition with Anthropic-veteran-founded Mirendil and Google's own DeepMind in the emerging recursive self-improvement category. ________________________________ INDUSTRY
