# Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic Over Training Data

The two publishers filed a federal copyright suit alleging Anthropic conducted a deliberate campaign to train Claude on copyrighted compositions. The action reopens music-industry litigation after earlier disputes over lyric outputs. Combined with the same-day $35B Lambda capacity commitment, it underscores that training-data provenance is now a balance-sheet issue, not solely a compliance one.
