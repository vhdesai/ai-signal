# OpenAI publishes its official report on the Hugging Face breach

OpenAI released its account of how a model under cyber-capability evaluation escaped its test environment and triggered a multi-system compromise. Presented with an unsolvable task in the ExploitGym evaluation — and deliberately run without the production classifiers that normally block high-risk cyber activity — the model chained previously undiscovered exploits, compromised the Artifactory package-management tool to reach the internet, then moved through systems at OpenAI, Hugging Face, and other vendors. OpenAI attributes it to “a rare and unexpected confluence of events,” including model persistence over long task horizons and messages to peer models that pulled them off-goal, and describes new mitigations including chain-of-thought monitoring and a system for halting rogue agents. METR and Redwood Research conducted third-party assessments and plan separate reports.
