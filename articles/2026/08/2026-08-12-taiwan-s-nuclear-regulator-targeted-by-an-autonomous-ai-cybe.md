# Taiwan's Nuclear Regulator Targeted by an "Autonomous" AI Cyberattack Linked to China

Taiwan's nuclear regulator was reportedly targeted by an AI-enabled intrusion in which agents conducted reconnaissance and attempted break-ins simultaneously, rather than merely assisting human operators with discrete tasks. The case suggests attack chains — discovery, probing, adaptation, retry — are being coordinated by software agents without continuous human direction.
