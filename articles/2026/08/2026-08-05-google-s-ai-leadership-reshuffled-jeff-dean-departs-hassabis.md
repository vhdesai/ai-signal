# Google's AI leadership reshuffled: Jeff Dean departs, Hassabis becomes Alphabet chief scientist

Google announced a sweeping reorganization of its AI operations. Chief scientist Jeff Dean is leaving after 27 years to co-found Discovery Loop—a public-benefit corporation focused on AI for science and engineering—alongside senior fellow Sanjay Ghemawat, with Google investing as a backer. DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis is stepping down as CEO of Google DeepMind to become its chairman and Alphabet's chief scientist, while DeepMind CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu is promoted to run the AI division and lead Gemini 4. Alphabet shares fell roughly 4% on the news. The move concentrates frontier-model authority under Kavukcuoglu and Pichai even as Google's full-year capex guidance climbs toward $205 billion. For executives, this is the clearest signal yet that Google is restructuring for a prolonged competitive race with OpenAI and Anthropic.
