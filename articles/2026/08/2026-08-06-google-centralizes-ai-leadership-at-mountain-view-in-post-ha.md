# Google centralizes AI leadership at Mountain View in post-Hassabis reorg

Following the August 5 news that Demis Hassabis moves to DeepMind chairman and Alphabet chief scientist, Aug 6–7 reporting detailed a broader reorganization centralizing AI at Mountain View. Per Bloomberg, Koray Kavukcuoglu becomes operational research head and Sebastian Borgeaud’s coding team relocates from London to California. The moves concentrate research authority as Google races on Gemini.
