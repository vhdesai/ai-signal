# Code-testing startup Blacksmith's valuation jumps almost 10x in under a year

Blacksmith raised a $45 million Series B led by Peak XV Partners at a $550 million valuation, up from roughly $60 million at its Series A less than a year ago; GV and Y Combinator participated. The company reports more than 5,000 customers, up from about 700. The round underlines a second-order effect of AI coding: generation volume increases, and so does the testing and validation burden. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/blacksmiths-valuation-jumps-10x-to-550m-as-ai-coding-fuels-software-validation/
