# SB Energy Granted OpenAI Warrants Valued Near $5.5B to Secure a 20-Year Data-Center Lease

SoftBank's SB Energy issued OpenAI stock warrants now valued at roughly $5.5 billion in exchange for a 20-year lease on its Ohio AI campus, per draft IPO documents reviewed by the Journal. SB Energy is preparing an IPO targeting $5–7B. For executives evaluating counterparty risk: anchor tenancy is now purchased with equity dilution rather than priced into rent. WSJ → GEOPOLITICS
