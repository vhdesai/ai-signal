# OpenAI expands free ChatGPT text access with GPT-5.6 Luna

OpenAI is removing limits on text-based ChatGPT conversations for free users, with GPT-5.6 Luna becoming the default model for Free and Go tiers. The company is also adding a Think button for more complex questions and upgrading GPT-5.6 Sol for Plus and Pro users. This is both a product and distribution move: OpenAI is lowering usage friction at the same time ChatGPT has crossed the one-billion-weekly-user threshold.
