# IBM and OpenAI announce an enterprise AI partnership

IBM will bring OpenAI frontier models, including GPT-5.6, into its enterprise consulting practice under a joint go-to-market agreement covering secure deployment and cybersecurity ("OpenAI Daybreak"). The arrangement gives OpenAI deeper reach into regulated enterprise accounts where IBM already holds the relationship. The underlying announcement landed August 13, with coverage on the 14th.
