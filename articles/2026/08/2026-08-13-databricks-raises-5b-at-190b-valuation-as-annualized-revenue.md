# Databricks Raises $5B at $190B Valuation as Annualized Revenue Tops $7B

Databricks closed a $5 billion round at a $190 billion valuation led by Coatue, Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price, and Sixth Street Growth, after annualized revenue passed $7 billion growing at roughly 80% year over year. CEO Ali Ghodsi said the company initially targeted roughly $1 billion while investors pushed for up to $15 billion — the gap between ask and demand is itself a signal of how much late-stage capital is chasing AI data infrastructure. Databricks says it is cash-flow positive, with proceeds earmarked for AI research and acquisitions. The pricing establishes a new private-market benchmark for the data-platform layer beneath model deployment, and it is one of the largest private rounds ever raised in enterprise software. For enterprises, Databricks at this scale is a strategic vendor whose durability is now underwritten by institutional capital rather than dependent on a near-term IPO.
