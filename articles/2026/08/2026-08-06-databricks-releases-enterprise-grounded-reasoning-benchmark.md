# Databricks Releases Enterprise Grounded-Reasoning Benchmark, Adds Kimi K3 to Model Marketplace

Databricks released OfficeQA Pro V2, a benchmark developed with U.S. Treasury support that evaluates AI agents on questions grounded in roughly 120,000 pages of federal financial records; frontier models using default harnesses averaged 37.5% accuracy, while Databricks' own Genie agent framework raised the average to 52.8%. Separately, Databricks added Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, a lower-cost open-weight model, to its Unity AI Gateway alongside Claude, GPT, and Gemini — reinforcing its positioning as a model-agnostic enterprise AI layer.
