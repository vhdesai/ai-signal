# Brazil Commits ~$444 Million to Domestic AI Capacity With U.S. and Chinese Partners

Brazil announced ~$444M in AI investments, including ~$250M for a supercomputing project involving Huawei and iFlytek alongside U.S. participants. The deliberate multi-bloc sourcing illustrates how emerging markets are extracting leverage from U.S.–China competition. Expect more sovereign compute programs that decline to pick a single technology stack.
