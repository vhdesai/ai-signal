# DOJ Investigating Andreessen Horowitz Over Board Seats at Competing AI/Data Companies

The Justice Department has spent nearly a year examining whether a16z partners violated Section 8 of the Clayton Act by sitting simultaneously on the boards of competing companies, with Ben Horowitz's Databricks seat and Martin Casado's Fivetran seat reported as central. This is the first known interlocking-directorate action aimed at a venture firm. If the theory holds, it changes board-composition norms across the AI cap table — including for portfolio companies that are acquisition or partnership candidates. Technology executives on multiple boards should consult counsel about competitive overlaps.
