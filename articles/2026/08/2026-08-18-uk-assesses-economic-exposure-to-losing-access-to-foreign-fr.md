# UK Assesses Economic Exposure to Losing Access to Foreign Frontier Models

The UK government has begun an urgent assessment of the economic consequences of losing access to advanced foreign AI models, following US restrictions on foreign-national access to Anthropic's Fable 5. The review treats frontier-model availability as an economic-security dependency rather than a procurement question, given how deeply models are now embedded in coding, research, cybersecurity and professional services. Expect this to accelerate sovereign compute investment and strengthen the case for open-weight fallbacks in enterprise architecture.
