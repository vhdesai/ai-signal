# Patients and Clinicians Increasingly Use AI to Identify Rare Diseases

Patients, families, doctors, and nurses are turning to AI tools — including phenotype-matching systems such as Face2Gene — to shorten diagnostic odysseys for rare and undiagnosed conditions. The pattern is bottom-up adoption ahead of institutional governance, with clinicians using consumer-grade tools alongside sanctioned systems. Health systems face the familiar controls gap: real clinical value arriving faster than validation, documentation, and liability frameworks. For health-system CIOs and CMIOs, the story illustrates why shadow-AI policies need to account for clinical use cases where the tools are genuinely improving patient outcomes even without formal validation.
