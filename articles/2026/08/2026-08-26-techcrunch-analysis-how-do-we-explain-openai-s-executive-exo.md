# TechCrunch Analysis: How Do We Explain OpenAI's Executive Exodus?

TechCrunch's deep-dive argues the 14+ executive departures in 2026 reflect Greg Brockman's reassertion of leadership. "Everyone reports to Greg at the end of the day," OpenAI's head of product confirmed. The reorganization aligns with Altman cutting "side projects" and focusing on revenue, while Brockman — who built Stripe's business before OpenAI — fills the vacuum. The company's IPO is now pushed to 2027; Anthropic is reportedly already profitable while OpenAI's losses grow with revenue. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/how-do-we-explain-openais-executive-exodus/
