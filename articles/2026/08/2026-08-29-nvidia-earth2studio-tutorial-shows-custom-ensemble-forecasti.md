# NVIDIA Earth2Studio tutorial shows custom ensemble forecasting workflows

MarkTechPost published a technical workflow for building batched ensemble weather forecasting with NVIDIA Earth2Studio. The tutorial uses atmospheric initial conditions, ensemble perturbations, Zarr output, verification metrics, and diagnostic forecasting such as wind-power capacity factors.
