# Anthropic unveils Model Hardware Standard for agents to operate machines

Anthropic announced the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a model-agnostic interface — described as a USB-C for machines — that lets AI agents operate any device with a programmable interface, targeting scientific research and advanced manufacturing. It launches as a research preview with a selected group of science, robotics, and manufacturing organizations, with plans to open-source it later, mirroring the trajectory of Model Context Protocol. The move takes Anthropic into physical AI, where OpenAI, Amazon, Meta, and Apple have all invested heavily.
