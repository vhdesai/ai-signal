# Anthropic Signs a $9.1B, 20-Year, 191 MW Compute Lease With Riot Platforms

Anthropic contracted 191 megawatts of critical IT capacity at Riot Platforms' Rockdale, Texas campus under a 20-year co-location agreement generating roughly $9.1 billion in revenue through June 2048, with two five-year extension options worth up to about $16.1 billion. Delivery is staged: 96 MW by December 2027 and the full 191 MW by June 2028. Riot shares rose 17% intraday after a 25% after-hours move. The deal completes a pattern in which crypto miners' grid interconnects, substations and cooling — assets that take years to permit — become the scarce input for frontier AI rather than the chips themselves. https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-riot-9bn-data-centre-deal
