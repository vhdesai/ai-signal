# Big Tech turns to long-duration bonds for AI infrastructure

The Wall Street Journal reported that large technology companies are borrowing for decades to fund AI infrastructure buildouts. The trend reflects data-center asset lives, power needs, and server investment cycles that increasingly resemble industrial infrastructure rather than conventional software spend.
