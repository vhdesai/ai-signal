# IBM and Together AI sign $240M Nvidia-powered inference deal

IBM and Together AI signed a $240 million multiyear agreement to build an approximately 2,000-GPU Nvidia Blackwell cluster — HGX B300 with Spectrum-X networking — for inference on IBM Cloud. The deal is a clear marker that the infrastructure battle is shifting from training capacity to cost-efficient inference for open models.
