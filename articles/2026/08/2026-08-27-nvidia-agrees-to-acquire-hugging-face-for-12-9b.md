# Nvidia agrees to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9B

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to buy open-source model hub Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, pushing the chipmaker directly into the model-distribution layer used by hundreds of thousands of AI developers. The price is a sharp step up from Hugging Face's $4.5B valuation in its 2023 round — which included Nvidia, Google, and Salesforce — against reported annualized revenue near $150M. Strategically it gives Nvidia ownership of a neutral distribution point for open-weight models.
