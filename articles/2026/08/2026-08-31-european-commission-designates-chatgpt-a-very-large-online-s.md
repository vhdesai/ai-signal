# European Commission Designates ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine Under the DSA

ChatGPT crossed the 45-million average monthly EU user threshold and becomes the first AI assistant placed in the DSA's strictest tier, alongside new designations for Reddit and Roblox. The classification triggers systemic-risk assessment and mitigation obligations covering minors, elections, illegal content, and user well-being, plus independent audit and transparency duties. Enterprises embedding consumer-facing AI in the EU should expect these duties to cascade into vendor assurance requirements.
