# Lawsuit expands over alleged use of Grok to create explicit images from a childhood photo

A woman identified as Jane Doe 4 joined a lawsuit against xAI alleging that Grok was used to transform a childhood photo into thousands of explicit images. The case builds on earlier allegations from Tennessee teenagers that xAI failed to take basic precautions against misuse involving real people and minors. The story is a severe safety and legal signal for multimodal AI providers: image tools need robust abuse prevention, auditability, and rapid enforcement mechanisms, especially where real-person likenesses and minors are involved.
