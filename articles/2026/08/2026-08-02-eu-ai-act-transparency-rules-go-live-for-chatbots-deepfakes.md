# EU AI Act transparency rules go live for chatbots, deepfakes, and GPAI governance

A new phase of the EU AI Act took effect, activating transparency obligations such as chatbot disclosure and deepfake labeling, along with enforcement powers over general-purpose AI models. For companies serving EU users, this moves AI compliance from future planning to operational obligation. The near-term priority is to map where generative AI systems interact with users, produce synthetic media, or qualify as higher-risk deployments. URL: EU's AI Act goes live
