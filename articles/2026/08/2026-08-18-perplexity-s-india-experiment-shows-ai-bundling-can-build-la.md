# Perplexity's India Experiment Shows AI Bundling Can Build Lasting Revenue

Perplexity's 12-month free Perplexity Pro offer via Indian telecom Airtel (360M eligible customers) generated 56M downloads and peaked at 22M MAU — but the real signal is what happened after: India revenue rose ~60% after the offer closed, and daily IAP revenue climbed 9–27% above baselines as free subscriptions auto-renewed. Monthly net mobile revenue grew from $34K (Jan 2025) to $156K (Jul 2026). The experiment is being watched as a template for AI distribution in emerging markets, where OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic are all running similar plays. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/perplexitys-free-ai-offer-left-it-with-millions-more-users-in-india/
