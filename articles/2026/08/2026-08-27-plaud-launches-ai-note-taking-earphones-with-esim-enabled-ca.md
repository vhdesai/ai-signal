# Plaud Launches AI Note-Taking Earphones With eSIM-Enabled Case

Plaud, which reports over 2.5 million users of its hardware and software, introduced a note-taker in an earphone form factor paired with an eSIM-enabled charging case that lets users talk to AI agents without a phone. The design decouples the AI assistant from the smartphone as the connectivity anchor. It is a small but directionally relevant data point on ambient, always-available agent interfaces entering the enterprise productivity category.
