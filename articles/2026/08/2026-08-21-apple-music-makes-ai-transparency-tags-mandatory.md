# Apple Music makes AI transparency tags mandatory

Apple has told distribution partners that AI transparency tags are now required “in any instance where AI was used to create a material portion of the content,” with visible “Made With AI” labels arriving later this year. The move makes Apple the last major streaming service to adopt provenance labeling. It sets a de facto industry standard for AI disclosure in commercial content catalogs.
