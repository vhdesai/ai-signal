# OpenAI Special Projects Lead Brad Lightcap Departing

Brad Lightcap, who formerly served as OpenAI’s COO and most recently led its special projects division, told staff Tuesday that he is leaving. In an internal memo, he wrote that he sees “a few important new things the world will need to get right” in the next period of AI development. His departure continues a pattern of senior leadership turnover at OpenAI as the company prepares for an IPO.
