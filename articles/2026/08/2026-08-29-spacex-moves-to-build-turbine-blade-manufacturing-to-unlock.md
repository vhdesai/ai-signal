# SpaceX moves to build turbine-blade manufacturing to unlock AI data-center power

The Information reports that SpaceX is laying groundwork for a foundry in Bastrop, Texas to manufacture blades for large gas turbines, based on job listings and site indicators. Only a handful of foundries worldwide produce hot-section turbine blades, and existing order books are reportedly booked out for years — a bottleneck that constrains on-site generation for AI campuses. Vertical integration here would serve both xAI's gas-turbine-powered compute sites and SpaceX's own propulsion needs.
