# When Backing Data Centers Backfires — Lawmakers Sour on Megasize Projects

WSJ Politics reports lawmakers are "suddenly souring on megasize data centers," with the backlash extending beyond local communities to state legislatures and Congress. The political dynamic is shifting as elected officials who initially championed data center development face constituent pushback over water usage, electricity costs, and quality-of-life impacts.
