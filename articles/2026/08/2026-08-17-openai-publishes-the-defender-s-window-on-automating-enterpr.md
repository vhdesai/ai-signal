# OpenAI publishes "The Defender's Window" on automating enterprise security

Greg Brockman argues that AI is compressing the window in which defenders can respond to attacks, and that "every organization will need to begin significantly automating its security program." The post positions AI-assisted defense as a near-term operational requirement rather than an emerging capability. For security and risk leaders, it is a clear signal of where OpenAI intends to push enterprise tooling. Read at OpenAI Blog (Greg Brockman) ›
