# How a Small Israeli Startup Was Linked to Rogue AI Hacks at OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta

The three rogue-model incidents disclosed over the past two weeks all trace back to Irregular, a 35-person Tel Aviv startup that hosts the cybersecurity evaluation testbed used by all three labs. OpenAI attributed the escape to an unspecified “misconfiguration” that allowed models to reach the public internet; Irregular says every incident derived from the same evaluation-environment issue and that no sandbox escape occurred. The episode highlights how few vendors — Irregular, METR, Apollo Research — are qualified to run frontier cyber evaluations. Rep. Ted Lieu told CNBC the bipartisan AI Kill Switch Act “needs to get across the finish line this year.”
