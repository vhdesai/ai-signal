# Apple researchers propose ARBITRAGE for lower-latency reasoning

Apple published ARBITRAGE, a step-level speculative generation framework that dynamically routes reasoning steps between draft and target models based on expected quality gains. The method reduces inference latency by up to about 2x at matched accuracy across mathematical reasoning benchmarks. The work remains relevant because enterprise reasoning workloads need better cost-latency tradeoffs, not only stronger frontier models.
