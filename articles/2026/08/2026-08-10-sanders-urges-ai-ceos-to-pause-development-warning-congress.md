# Sanders urges AI CEOs to pause development, warning Congress will act otherwise

Sen. Bernie Sanders is publicly calling on leading AI CEOs to pause frontier development, warning that lawmakers will intervene if the industry does not act on its own. The intervention adds a labor- and concentration-focused voice to a policy debate that until now has been driven mainly by cybersecurity incidents. A pause is unlikely to materialize, but the political cost of the current release cadence is rising on both sides of the aisle. Industry News FUNDING
