# Google DeepMind ships SL2T sign-language translation on Android

DeepMind put SL2T, a sign-language-to-text model that translates directly from pose coordinates, inside two consumer Android products. It is reportedly the first time sign-language AI has shipped as a native phone feature rather than a research demonstration. The rollout was accompanied by a participatory development process with Deaf users and an advisory committee governing real-world deployment.
