# Databricks Raises $5B at $190B Valuation as Revenue Tops $7B

Closed $5B at $190B led by Coatue, Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price, and Sixth Street Growth. ARR passed $7B growing ~80% YoY. CEO Ghodsi targeted ~$1B; investors pushed for $15B. Databricks says it is cash-flow positive, with proceeds for AI research and acquisitions. One of the largest private rounds in enterprise software history, establishing a new benchmark for the data-platform layer beneath model deployment. At this scale, Databricks is a strategic vendor whose durability is underwritten by institutional capital rather than dependent on a near-term IPO.
