# Databricks Raises $5B at $190B Valuation as Revenue Tops $7B

Databricks closed a $5B round at $190B led by Coatue, Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price, and Sixth Street Growth, after annualized revenue passed $7B growing ~80% YoY. CEO Ali Ghodsi said the company targeted ~$1B while investors pushed for $15B — the gap is itself a signal of how much capital is chasing AI data infrastructure. Databricks says it is cash-flow positive, with proceeds earmarked for AI research and acquisitions. The pricing establishes a new private-market benchmark for the data-platform layer beneath model deployment, and it is one of the largest private rounds ever raised in enterprise software. For enterprises, Databricks at this scale is a strategic vendor whose durability is now underwritten by institutional capital.
