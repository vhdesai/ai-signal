# Google in advanced talks for a $1.5B+ deal with AI-coding startup Mechanize

Google is reportedly pursuing a $1.5 billion-plus talent-and-licensing arrangement with Mechanize, a roughly 35-person startup founded by Epoch AI's Tamay Besiroglu, combining a non-exclusive license with hires. The acquihire-style structure is the format Big Tech now favors to avoid conventional merger review. Yahoo Finance pairs the report with the same-day Jeff Dean departure under an AI brain-drain framing. It reinforces that coding agents are among the most fiercely contested categories in AI.
