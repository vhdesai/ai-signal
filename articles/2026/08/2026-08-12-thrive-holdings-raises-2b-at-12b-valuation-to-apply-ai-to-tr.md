# Thrive Holdings Raises $2B at $12B Valuation to Apply AI to Traditional Services

Thrive Holdings, created by Josh Kushner's Thrive Capital, is raising $2 billion at a $12 billion valuation with backing from SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The company acquires traditional service providers — currently spanning accounting (Current) and IT services (Shield Technology Partners) — and infuses them with AI, having completed nearly 70 acquisitions to date. Results include a one-third reduction in tax prep times and IT queries resolved 36x faster on average. A third platform focused on construction regulatory paperwork is being added.
