# Relativity integrates Google Cloud’s Gemini Enterprise for Legal

Relativity’s RelativityOne now integrates with Google Cloud’s Gemini Enterprise for Legal through the Model Context Protocol. The tie-up lets shared customers run AI-assisted legal workflows across both platforms. It is a vendor announcement complementing Google’s own legal-vertical expansion the same day, and a further signal that MCP is consolidating as the enterprise integration standard.
