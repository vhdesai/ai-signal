# WindBorne raises $37 million for AI weather forecasting

WindBorne Systems raised a $37 million Series B to expand its AI weather-forecasting platform, which combines a global balloon sensor network with proprietary forecasting models. Current customers include U.S. government weather and defense agencies, while the company is expanding toward commercial markets such as commodities and risk management. The story is a strong example of the physical-AI thesis: proprietary data acquisition plus model-driven prediction for high-value decision markets.
