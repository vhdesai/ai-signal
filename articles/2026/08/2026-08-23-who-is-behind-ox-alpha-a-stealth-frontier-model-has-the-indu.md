# Who is behind ‘Ox Alpha’? A stealth frontier model has the industry guessing

A free anonymous model called Ox Alpha, listed on OpenRouter as “a reasoning model designed for coding, sustained agentic work, and production workload,” has set off widespread speculation about its origin. Stripe CEO Patrick Collison — whose company is acquiring OpenRouter — called it “very impressive,” while independent fingerprinting analysis points toward Zhipu AI’s unreleased GLM line. Early independent tests put it at 80% on the DeepSWE coding benchmark, ahead of both Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol. Free access is reported to run through August 27.
