# OpenAI joins the PORTS–Pike project, targeting roughly 8 GW of Ohio data center capacity

OpenAI announced it will "secure approximately 8 gigawatts-IT at the PORTS–Pike Technology Campus" in Ohio, working alongside Nvidia, SB Energy and the Department of Energy. The scale places the campus among the largest single AI compute commitments announced to date and ties frontier model capacity directly to federal-site energy infrastructure. It follows reporting that Nvidia is in talks to invest up to $3 billion in SB Energy and to provide substantial credit support for the buildout.
