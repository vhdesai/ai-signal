# DiG-bench: a benchmark isolating scientific-discovery ability in LLMs

DiG-bench (Discovery in Games) comprises 70 interactive text games with undisclosed rules — 21 public and 49 private — played by humans and frontier models through an identical interface across seven difficulty tiers, where every game is human-beatable on first attempt. Reporting indicates frontier models improved with newer systems but the hardest tiers remain unsolved, and agentic harnesses did not outperform a basic harness. That isolates rule discovery, rather than tool use or planning, as the core bottleneck. Note: the project site is undated and the August 13 launch date rests on same-day secondary coverage.
