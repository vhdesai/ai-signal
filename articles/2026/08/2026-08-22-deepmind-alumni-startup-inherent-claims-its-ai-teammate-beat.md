# DeepMind-alumni startup Inherent claims its AI “teammate” beat Anthropic and OpenAI at replicating research

Inherent, founded by former DeepMind researchers, says its agent outperformed Anthropic's and OpenAI's systems on a research-replication benchmark. The company positions the product as an autonomous research collaborator rather than a chat assistant. The benchmark results are company-reported and have not been independently verified.
