# MIT: benefits of medical AI assistance vary sharply by user expertise

MIT researchers found that non-experts deferred to LLM-based diagnostic assistance even when the model was wrong, while trained clinicians reliably caught the same errors. The result complicates the assumption that AI assistance uniformly raises the floor for less-experienced users. It argues for deployment designs that calibrate model confidence display and require independent judgment before acceptance. The finding generalizes well beyond medicine to any high-stakes assisted-decision workflow. TrendingStanford HAI
