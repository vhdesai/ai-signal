# 4 of 5 Enterprises That Secured AI Agent Identities Still Cannot Contain a Rogue Agent

A July Pulse survey found that 80% of organizations that have implemented AI-agent identity and authentication controls never built corresponding isolation mechanisms to contain a compromised or misbehaving agent. The survey also revealed that 92% of enterprises default entirely to their hyperscaler or platform provider for AI security rather than building independent controls. The gap between authenticating agents and containing them is the operative governance risk for any organization deploying autonomous AI in production. Read alongside this week’s Taiwan nuclear intrusion and the reasoning-trace credential leak, the finding illustrates a systemic pattern: enterprises are deploying agents faster than they are building the containment infrastructure those agents require. Security teams should treat agent containment — not just identity — as a board-level risk item. VentureBeat POLICY
