# Taiwan indicts nine, including Nvidia and Super Micro staff, over B300 server smuggling

Taiwan's Keelung District Prosecutor's Office indicted nine people for falsifying paperwork claiming 130 Nvidia B300 servers would remain in Taiwan; prosecutors say 74 were rerouted to Chinese buyers, partly via Indonesia. Those charged include a partner manager in Nvidia's Taiwan distribution business and two former Super Micro Taiwan employees. Nvidia said it will work with Taiwanese authorities, and Super Micro said it is cooperating and is not a target of the investigation. These are the first indictments from a months-long probe that follows the March US charges against a Super Micro co-founder. FUNDINGPOWER
