# China's leading model developers remain dependent on Nvidia despite domestic alternatives

Reporting indicates China's top model developers continue to train primarily on Nvidia hardware because migrating to domestic accelerators, including Huawei's, carries substantial software-porting costs. The constraint is the CUDA-adjacent toolchain rather than raw silicon performance. This tempers assumptions that export controls translate quickly into hardware substitution.
