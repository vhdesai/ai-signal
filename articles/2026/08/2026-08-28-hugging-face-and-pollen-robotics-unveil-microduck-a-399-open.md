# Hugging Face and Pollen Robotics unveil Microduck, a $399 open-source RL biped

Pollen Robotics, part of Hugging Face, opened preorders for Microduck, a 25 cm bipedal robot priced at $399. The robot ships with neural policies trained through reinforcement learning in simulation, and the training environments, reward functions, domain-randomization settings, and sim-to-real recipe are public on GitHub. The launch is a useful signal for embodied AI: low-cost hardware plus open training loops may broaden experimentation beyond well-funded robotics labs. Products & Tools DEVELOPER TOOLSGPU
