# OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate tops $40 billion ahead of a potential IPO

OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate has reached $40 billion, roughly double its rate at the end of 2025, driven by coding tools, subscriptions and advertising. CFO Sarah Friar told investors that enterprise is now a bigger business than consumer — a material shift in the revenue mix. The figure was first reported by Bloomberg and lands as the company prepares for what could be a trillion-dollar listing. For enterprise buyers, the enterprise-over-consumer inflection signals where OpenAI's roadmap and support investment will concentrate. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/openai-cfo-friar-tells-investors-that-enterprise-bigger-than-consumer.html
