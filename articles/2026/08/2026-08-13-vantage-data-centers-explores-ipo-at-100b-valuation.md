# Vantage Data Centers Explores IPO at ~$100B Valuation

Silver Lake- and DigitalBridge-backed Vantage is exploring an IPO at ~$100B that could raise ~$10B, or an outright sale. Vantage has raised ~$11B since late 2023 and is involved in a Wisconsin campus tied to the OpenAI–Oracle Stargate buildout. A listing at this level would reprice hyperscale data centers as critical technology infrastructure rather than real estate. The timing aligns with Anthropic’s IPO preparation and Nvidia’s financing vehicle — signals that AI infrastructure is becoming a formal asset class.
