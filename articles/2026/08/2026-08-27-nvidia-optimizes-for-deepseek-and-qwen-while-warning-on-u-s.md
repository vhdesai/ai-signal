# Nvidia Optimizes for DeepSeek and Qwen While Warning on U.S. Restrictions

Nvidia is tuning its hardware and software stack for Chinese-origin open models including DeepSeek and Qwen, while warning that potential U.S. restrictions targeting models originating in China could damage its business. The position highlights a widening gap between where open-weight model momentum sits and where U.S. policy is heading. Enterprises standardizing on Chinese open weights should treat model provenance as a live compliance variable, not a settled one. SUPPLY CHAIN
