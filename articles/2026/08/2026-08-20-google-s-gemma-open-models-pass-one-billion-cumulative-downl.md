# Google’s Gemma Open Models Pass One Billion Cumulative Downloads

Google DeepMind’s Gemma family surpassed one billion cumulative downloads, with 100,000+ derivative variants. The figures quantify the scale of the open-weight fine-tuning ecosystem Google has built beneath Gemini. For enterprises, it strengthens the case for open-weight deployment where data residency or cost rules out API-only frontier models.
