# Instinct raises $350M at a $2.5B valuation before public launch

Instinct, a one-year-old AI assistant startup that has not yet launched publicly, closed a $350 million round at a $2.5 billion valuation. The raise reflects continued investor appetite for consumer-scale agentic assistants ahead of demonstrated revenue. Reporting also flags unresolved privacy and security questions around the product's data access model. CHINACLOUD
