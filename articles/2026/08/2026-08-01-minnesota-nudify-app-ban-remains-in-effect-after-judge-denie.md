# Minnesota nudify-app ban remains in effect after judge denies xAI request

In a high-importance 24-48 hour item, TechCrunch reported that a federal judge denied xAI's emergency request to block Minnesota's law banning AI nudify apps that generate non-consensual sexual imagery. The law remains in effect while litigation proceeds. The case is an early test of how courts will handle state-level regulation of harmful synthetic media and platform liability around generative tools.
