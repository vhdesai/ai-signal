# Made by Google 2026: Pixel 11 With Proactive Gemini; SL2T Sign-Language Ships

Google unveiled Pixel 11, Pro, Pro XL, a Pro Fold, and Pixel Watch 5, headlined by “proactive” Gemini features including HiLight ambient awareness, on-device sign-language transcription via SL2T (the first time sign-language AI has shipped as a native phone feature), more natural voice input, and AI-corrected GPS. SL2T deployment was accompanied by participatory development with Deaf users. Notably, the bundled AI Pro trial was cut from 12 months to 6 on the Pro, and dropped entirely on the base model — Google now expects Gemini subscriptions to stand on their own commercially. The event underscores Google’s vertical-integration strategy across custom silicon, Android, and Gemini as a consumer distribution moat. TechCrunch | Unite.AI (SL2T) NEW AGENTS
