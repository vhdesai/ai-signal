# DeepSeek Revenue Reaches $70 Million Through July — 10x Jump from 2025

DeepSeek generated ~475M yuan (~$70.7M) in the first seven months of 2026, roughly tenfold its full-year 2025 revenue. The Chinese lab’s commercial traction validates the low-cost model strategy and the thesis that inference-cost efficiency can drive meaningful revenue growth without US hyperscaler distribution.
