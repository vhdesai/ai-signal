# Alexander Rakhlin named director of MIT Statistics and Data Science Center

MIT named Alexander "Sasha" Rakhlin director of the MIT Statistics and Data Science Center. Rakhlin's work spans online learning, statistical theory, and machine-learning foundations, and he framed AI safety, robustness, and uncertainty as fundamentally statistical questions. The leadership change matters because MIT SDSC helps shape the research pipeline for rigorous, mathematically grounded AI reliability.
