# Databricks Closes $5B at a $190B Valuation as Annualized Revenue Tops $7B

Databricks raised $5B led by Coatue with Blackstone, MGX and T. Rowe Price participating, at a $190B valuation — its second multibillion-dollar round in roughly eight months. Reporting indicates the company sought about $1B and investor demand ran materially higher. The round lands against annualized revenue above $7B, giving it a multiple in line with the current private AI-infrastructure cohort rather than traditional data platforms. The signal for buyers: data-platform consolidation ahead of an eventual listing, with pricing power concentrated in fewer vendors. fortuneindia.com
