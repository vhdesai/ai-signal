# Anthropic's August 2026 Risk Report Rates Full-Automation Risk “Low” — For Now

Anthropic's new risk report rates the risk of fully-automated AI research as “low,” while cautioning that confidence declines as models improve, and notes ongoing CBRN misuse monitoring. On its internal CoBench evaluation, “Model 2” scored 62.8% and “Claude Mythos 5” scored 50.3%, both well below the 85% full-automation threshold.
