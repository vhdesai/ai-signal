# AI giants lean into health care to stall public backlash

Axios reports that major AI firms are leaning further into health care use cases as a way to show tangible public benefit amid broader scrutiny of generative AI. For executives, the strategic read is that health care remains one of the clearest reputational and adoption wedges for AI platforms, but also one of the highest-risk domains for accuracy, privacy, liability, and regulatory trust.
