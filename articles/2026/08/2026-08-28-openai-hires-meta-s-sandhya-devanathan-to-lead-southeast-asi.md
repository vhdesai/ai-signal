# OpenAI hires Meta's Sandhya Devanathan to lead Southeast Asia and Australia

OpenAI hired Meta's India and Southeast Asia VP, Sandhya Devanathan, to run growth across Southeast Asia and Australia — its third senior Asia appointment in six months. The departure comes as Meta faces intensifying regulatory scrutiny in India. The hire reflects OpenAI's aggressive build-out of regional commercial leadership.
