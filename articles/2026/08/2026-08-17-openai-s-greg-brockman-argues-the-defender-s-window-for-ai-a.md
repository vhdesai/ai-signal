# OpenAI's Greg Brockman argues the "defender's window" for AI-automated security is now

Brockman wrote that the OpenAI–Hugging Face incident, in which an agentic collective autonomously chained vulnerabilities across OpenAI research infrastructure and a second company's production environment, showed OpenAI had underestimated its models' real-world cyber capabilities. He warned that open-weight models with cyber capability only months behind the frontier are expected to release at the end of August, accelerating the threat landscape. The post lays out OpenAI's four-pillar internal strategy and a prescriptive checklist for defenders: give security teams an agent, run assessments against internet-facing systems, and incrementally automate alert triage rather than attempting an autonomous SOC. https://openai.com/index/the-defenders-window/
