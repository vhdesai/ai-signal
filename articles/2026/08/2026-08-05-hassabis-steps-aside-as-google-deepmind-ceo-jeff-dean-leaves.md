# Hassabis steps aside as Google DeepMind CEO; Jeff Dean leaves to found Discovery Loop

Alphabet reorganized the top of its AI division: Demis Hassabis moved from CEO to chairman and chief scientist, with Koray Kavukcuoglu taking operational leadership of models, research, and Gemini products. Simultaneously, Jeff Dean and three senior researchers (Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, Quoc Le) departed to co-found Discovery Loop, a public-benefit company targeting automated scientific research cycles. The departures remove decades of institutional knowledge and concentrate extraordinary research capital in a single new venture.
