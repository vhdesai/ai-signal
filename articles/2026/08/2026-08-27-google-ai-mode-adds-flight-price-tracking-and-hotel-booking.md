# Google AI Mode Adds Flight Price Tracking and Hotel Booking

Google added flight tracking, rewards-points comparison, and in-chat hotel booking to AI Mode in Search. Moves AI Mode from information retrieval toward transaction execution — a template for how agentic search captures booking intent.
