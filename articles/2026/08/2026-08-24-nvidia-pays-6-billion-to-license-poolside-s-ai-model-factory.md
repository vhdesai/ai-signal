# Nvidia pays $6 billion to license Poolside’s AI “model factory”BreakingHot

Nvidia is paying approximately $6B to license Poolside’s model-building software, alongside a reported $1B investment and the hiring of roughly 109 Poolside engineers to work on Nvidia’s open-weight Nemotron models. The deal deepens Nvidia’s move up the stack into open models and positions it more directly against OpenAI and DeepSeek. Notably it is structured as a licensing-plus-talent arrangement rather than an outright acquisition — a structure worth watching as an antitrust-aware deal template.
