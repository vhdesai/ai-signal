# WSJ reports DeepMind's Hassabis pitched an AI-oversight body before a governance shake-up

The Wall Street Journal reported that Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis pitched an AI-oversight body before an organizational shake-up. Even without the full article text, the timing is notable: leading labs are under increasing pressure to demonstrate credible internal and external governance as capabilities advance. For executives, the governance question is becoming operational rather than symbolic: who has authority to review frontier releases, and how independent is that oversight from commercial priorities?
