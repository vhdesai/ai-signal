# EU AI Act enforcement powers now apply to frontier AI providers

CNBC reported that the European Commission's AI Act enforcement powers are now active for general-purpose AI providers, including firms such as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. Regulators can demand model evaluations, restrict EU market access, and levy fines. The AI Act has moved from planning framework to active oversight, making EU compliance a near-term operational requirement for frontier labs and enterprise deployers serving European users.
