# OpenAI Q2 Sales Show Tepid Growth Compared With Anthropic

OpenAI's revenue grew much slower than Anthropic's last quarter, disappointing investors who hoped the ChatGPT maker would start to catch up to its rival. The disclosure raises questions about OpenAI's commercial trajectory ahead of its planned IPO.
