# Nvidia Weighs $3B Stake in SB Energy While Restructuring Its Ohio Backstop for OpenAI

Nvidia is in talks to invest up to $3 billion in SB Energy, the SoftBank subsidiary developing a large Ohio data center campus for OpenAI, per The Information. The investment sits inside a broader discussion of roughly $100 billion in credit support, with half the equity contributed at deal signing and half via SB Energy's planned IPO — a listing targeted as soon as next month at $5 billion or more. Separately, WSJ reported Friday that Nvidia has cut its expected initial guarantee to under $120 billion, down from $250 billion previously discussed. Read together, the two reports point to Nvidia trading balance-sheet guarantee exposure for direct ownership of the power and land layer. For enterprises modeling compute availability through 2027, the structure of vendor backstops — not headline capacity — is the load-bearing variable.
