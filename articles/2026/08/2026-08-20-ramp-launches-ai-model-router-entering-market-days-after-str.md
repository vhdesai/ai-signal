# Ramp Launches AI Model Router, Entering Market Days After Stripe’s $7.5B OpenRouter Deal

Ramp launched “Router”—its own model routing service supporting OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Nvidia, xAI, and Z.ai. Free through 2026. Features include benchmark-based routing, difficulty-based routing, and token spend dashboards. The launch confirms token expense management is becoming a contested fintech vertical. Notable: retains inputs/outputs for one year by default (opt-out).
