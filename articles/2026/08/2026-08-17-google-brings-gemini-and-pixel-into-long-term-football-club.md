# Google brings Gemini and Pixel into long-term football club partnerships

Google announced long-term Gemini and Pixel partnerships with Arsenal FC, FC Barcelona, FC Bayern Munchen, Liverpool FC, and Paris Saint-Germain. Gemini will be positioned around match and club insights, while Pixel will support behind-the-scenes content creation and club media production, including women's teams. The announcement is less a model story than a distribution story: Google is embedding AI assistants into high-frequency cultural and sports contexts to normalize everyday AI usage.
