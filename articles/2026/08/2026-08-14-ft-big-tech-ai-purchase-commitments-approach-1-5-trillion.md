# FT: Big Tech AI purchase commitments approach ~$1.5 trillion

The Financial Times reports that Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Nvidia, Oracle, and Meta have accumulated roughly $1.5 trillion in AI-related purchase commitments across chips, data centers, and energy — much of it off balance sheet. Goldman separately estimates a comparable figure in associated leases. The scale of forward obligations is becoming a central question for AI capex sustainability.
