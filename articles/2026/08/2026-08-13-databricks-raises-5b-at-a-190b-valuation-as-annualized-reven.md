# Databricks Raises $5B at a $190B Valuation as Annualized Revenue Tops $7B

Databricks closed a $5B round at a $190B valuation after annualized revenue crossed $7B. Reporting indicates the company initially sought roughly $1B while investors pushed toward $15B, settling in the middle — a demand signal about private AI-infrastructure capital rather than a capital requirement.
