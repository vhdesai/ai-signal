# Stripe Confirms $7.5B OpenRouter Acquisition — Real Motive Is AI Expense Management Power

Stripe officially confirmed its OpenRouter acquisition at $7.5B (founders received $1.5B, investors $6B). OpenRouter will continue operating independently. PitchBook analyst Franco Granda called it "Stripe's deliberate attempt to embed itself into the middle of capital flows in the AI era," gaining "some degree of power over suppliers such as the frontier labs themselves." The deal fits a growing market for token expense management alongside similar moves by Databricks, Rippling, and Ramp. Stripe's founders referenced "the singularity" in their investor letter — tongue-in-cheek, but noting 88% of Forbes AI 50 use Stripe products. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/stripe-didnt-really-buy-openrouter-because-of-the-singularity/
