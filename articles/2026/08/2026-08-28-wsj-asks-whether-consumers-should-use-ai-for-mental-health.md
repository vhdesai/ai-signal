# WSJ asks whether consumers should use AI for mental health

The Wall Street Journal examined whether consumers should use AI for mental-health support. The issue is increasingly important as general-purpose assistants are used for sensitive emotional advice outside clinical workflows. For leaders building or deploying AI assistants, mental-health use cases require clear boundaries, crisis escalation, privacy safeguards, and avoidance of exaggerated claims about therapeutic capability.
