# OpenAI's ChatGPT advertising business reaches a $1B annualized run rate

OpenAI said its ChatGPT ad product hit a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate roughly 200 days after launch, spanning tens of thousands of advertisers in more than 40 countries. Self-serve buying through Ads Manager expanded into India, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa. The company frames ads as the funding mechanism for free and low-cost tiers, which materially changes the competitive economics against subscription-only rivals. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/open-ai-chatgpt-ads-revenue.html INDUSTRY
