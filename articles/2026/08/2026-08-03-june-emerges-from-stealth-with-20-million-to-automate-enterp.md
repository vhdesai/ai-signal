# June emerges from stealth with $20 million to automate enterprise AI deployment

June, founded by former Salesforce AI executives, launched from stealth with a $20 million pre-seed round backed by Marc Benioff's Time Ventures and other enterprise-software leaders. The company aims to scan enterprise environments, identify AI-agent deployment blockers, and automate workflow design without relying on forward-deployed engineering teams. The strategic signal is clear: the enterprise market is starting to fund tooling for the unglamorous but critical last mile of AI adoption.
