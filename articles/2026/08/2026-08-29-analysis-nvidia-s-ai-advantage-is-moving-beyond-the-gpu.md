# Analysis: Nvidia's AI Advantage Is Moving Beyond the GPU

TechCrunch argues Nvidia's moat is shifting from GPUs to data orchestration — CPUs, networking, and storage systems that make gigawatt-scale centers work. The Vera CPU showed "upwards of 3x improvement" in data operations by eliminating bottlenecks. OpenAI's Jalapeño takes a similar approach (minimizing data movement entirely). Competition has moved to "a new layer, where building a rival GPU matters less than making the entire system work efficiently."
