# Alibaba sells $10.2B in stock to fund AI; shares plunge roughly 10%BreakingHot

Alibaba launched a roughly $10.2B share placement to non-U.S. investors to fund an aggressive AI and cloud buildout, offered at a discount that diluted existing holders. Hong Kong-listed shares fell about 10–11% on the news. The reaction signals that markets are now scrutinizing how AI capex gets funded, not merely whether AI demand exists.
