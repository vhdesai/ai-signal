# OpenAI pauses "Astra" after it nears the first-ever "Critical" cyber-risk rating

OpenAI said internal evaluations of its upcoming Astra model showed agentic-coding and cyber capability gains strong enough that it can no longer rule out a "Critical" rating under its Preparedness Framework — a tier no model has previously reached, with prior frontier systems including GPT‑5.6‑Sol holding at "High." The company paused non-compliant internal Astra work and imposed isolated testing, restricted access, encrypted weights, sandboxed execution, and real-time monitoring. Early access is being extended to government and safety evaluators. OpenAI's own disclosure landed Friday, Aug 7; the substantive coverage published Aug 8–9.
