# Google's Gemini app crosses 1 billion monthly active users

Sundar Pichai said the standalone Gemini app and web experience reached one billion monthly active users, making it Google's fastest-growing product ever. Roughly 63% of interactions are voice-driven, and the service generates more than 150 million images daily. The milestone follows ChatGPT reaching the same mark weeks earlier, confirming a genuine two-horse race at consumer scale.
