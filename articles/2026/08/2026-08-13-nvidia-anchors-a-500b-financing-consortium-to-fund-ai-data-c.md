# Nvidia Anchors a $500B+ Financing Consortium to Fund AI Data Centers

Nvidia is partnering with KKR, Goldman Sachs, Blackstone, BlackRock, and other large financial institutions in a structure intended to mobilize more than $500 billion for AI data center buildout. The effect is to convert GPU compute into a financeable, bankable asset class with debt-like funding rather than pure corporate capex.
