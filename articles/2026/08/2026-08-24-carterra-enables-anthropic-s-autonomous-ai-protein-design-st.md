# Carterra Enables Anthropic's Autonomous AI Protein Design Study

Carterra announced that its high-throughput surface plasmon resonance platforms supported an Anthropic study in autonomous AI-driven protein design, providing the binding measurements used to evaluate model-generated candidates. The work is notable for closing the loop between model output and physical assay at scale. It is a concrete example of frontier labs moving beyond software benchmarks into instrumented wet-lab validation. ________________________________ Academic Research RESEARCHMIT
