# Amazon Is Buying and Destroying Rare Books to Train AI Models

Amazon is buying rare books in bulk, cutting off their spines, and scanning them at a facility in Las Vegas (codenamed VGT3, identified by a dinosaur-holding-a-book logo) for LLM training data. 404 Media tracked a rare book to the facility using a tracking device. The practice targets out-of-print texts unavailable online, which are especially valuable because pre-2022 texts are guaranteed human-written — helping avoid "model collapse" from training on AI-generated content. Amazon confirmed it "purchases books through commercial channels to improve products." 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/amazon-once-an-online-bookseller-is-destroying-rare-books-to-train-ai-models/
