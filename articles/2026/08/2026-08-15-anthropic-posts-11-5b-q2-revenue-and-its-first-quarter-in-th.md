# Anthropic Posts $11.5B Q2 Revenue and Its First Quarter in the Black

Anthropic told investors that preliminary Q2 2026 revenue exceeded $11.5 billion — roughly 14× the year-ago quarter and more than double Q1 — with positive adjusted operating income. The disclosure lands as banks prepare a possible fall IPO, and follows last week's reporting of a $190–200 billion 2028 revenue forecast underpinning a potential ~$2 trillion valuation. For enterprise buyers, the numbers reduce the counterparty-risk argument that has historically favored larger-balance-sheet vendors (Microsoft, Google) in model procurement. Anthropic's transition from loss-making research lab to profitable frontier vendor changes its commercial positioning materially.
