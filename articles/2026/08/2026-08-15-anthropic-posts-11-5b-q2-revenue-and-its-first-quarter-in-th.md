# Anthropic posts $11.5B Q2 revenue and its first quarter in the black

Anthropic told investors that preliminary Q2 2026 revenue exceeded $11.5B — roughly 14x the year-ago quarter and more than double Q1 — with positive adjusted operating income. The disclosure lands as banks prepare a possible fall IPO.
