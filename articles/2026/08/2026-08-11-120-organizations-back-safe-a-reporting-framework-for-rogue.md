# 120+ Organizations Back SAFE, a Reporting Framework for Rogue AI Agents

More than 120 organizations — including Nvidia, Cisco, and CrowdStrike — are backing the Open Secure AI Alliance's Shared AI Findings Exchange (SAFE), a proposed standard for disclosing incidents involving autonomous agents. The draft requires confidential reporting within four business days and preserves prompts, agent traces, tool calls, and credentials as evidence. Notably, developer intent would not waive the reporting obligation.
