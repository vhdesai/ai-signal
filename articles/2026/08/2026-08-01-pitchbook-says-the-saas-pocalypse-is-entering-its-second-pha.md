# PitchBook says the SaaS-pocalypse is entering its second phase

PitchBook's Research Pitch flagged the next phase of SaaS disruption: AI agents moving from augmenting software users to replacing software seats outright. That shift pressures traditional per-seat pricing and pushes vendors toward usage- or outcome-based models. For investors and operators, the key question is whether incumbents can rebuild around agent-native workflows before agent-first entrants capture the value layer.
