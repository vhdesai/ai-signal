# Nvidia Warns Largest Customers of 15%+ Price Increases on AI Servers

Several of Nvidia's largest customers have been told that prices for servers containing its AI chips will rise by more than 15% in many cases, according to a Bloomberg News report. The increases are attributed to soaring memory costs and would apply to systems shipping early next year, including flagship Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell configurations. This is the clearest signal yet that the AI compute cost curve has stopped falling. Any 2027 infrastructure plan built on flat or declining per-rack pricing should be re-baselined. ANALYSIS
