# Berkeley Lab demonstrates AI modeling that accelerates advanced-materials discovery

A team at the U.S. Department of Energy's Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory demonstrated an AI modeling approach that rapidly and accurately predicts how advanced materials behave, compressing a traditionally slow design cycle. The work illustrates AI's expanding role in scientific discovery and materials engineering beyond language and code. Such tools could shorten development timelines for energy, semiconductor, and manufacturing materials.
