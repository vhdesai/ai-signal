# OpenAI Keeps Its Largest Frontier RL Run on Hold Over Cyber Capability

OpenAI paused reinforcement-learning training on its latest models for roughly two weeks and has kept its largest planned frontier RL run on hold after internal evaluations indicated critical-level offensive cyber capability. The company says it used the pause to harden defenses and tighten safeguards before resuming. This is the clearest instance to date of a frontier lab self-throttling on capability grounds, and it establishes a precedent regulators — including under California's SB 53 — are likely to cite. Executive Takeaways 1. The contested layer is orchestration, not models. Routing, spend control, and agent observability are consolidating fast — evaluate whether model access runs through a gateway you can meter and cap. 2. Agent spend is a live financial control gap. One in five enterprises cannot stop runaway agent spend in real time; treat this as a FinOps item, not an ML item. 3. Concentration risk is real. A single-vendor incident took down chat, API, and coding surfaces simultaneously — multi-model fallback is now an availability requirement. 4. Capability-based self-throttling has arrived. OpenAI's held frontier run will shape regulatory expectations and diligence questions on acquired model assets.
