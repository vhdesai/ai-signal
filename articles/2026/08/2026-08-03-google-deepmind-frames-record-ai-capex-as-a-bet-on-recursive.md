# Google DeepMind frames record AI capex as a bet on recursive self-improvement

The Information reports that Google DeepMind views its record AI infrastructure spending as a wager on recursive self-improvement, systems that can improve their own capabilities, with Google's annualized capital-spending path cited near $200 billion. The framing raises the stakes on whether a step-change in model capability arrives fast enough to justify the buildout. It also sharpens the industry-wide debate over whether current AI revenue can support hyperscaler capex. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
