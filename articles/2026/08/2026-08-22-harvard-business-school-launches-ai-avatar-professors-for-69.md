# Harvard Business School Launches AI Avatar Professors for $699 Entrepreneurship Bootcamp

Harvard Business School's "Foundry" bootcamp uses AI video avatars of professors to simulate pitch meetings, sales calls, and board sessions. The $699 program has been tested at 100+ universities across 50 states, aiming to scale HBS offerings far beyond its 900 annual MBA admits. PwC, law firms, and even a church are experimenting with similar AI-clone approaches. A Val Kilmer AI avatar recently appeared in a historical drama.
