# Zuckerberg's 6,500-word essay argues concentrated control is AI's biggest risk

Published alongside the Muse releases, Zuckerberg's essay contends that prevailing AI doom concerns are overblown and that the greater danger is a single government or company holding too much power over the technology. He calls on Washington to reduce training-data and distillation restrictions so American open models can compete globally. The piece is best read as a policy positioning document ahead of pending US rulemaking, not a technical roadmap.
