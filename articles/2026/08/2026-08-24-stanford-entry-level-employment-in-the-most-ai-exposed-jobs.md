# Stanford: entry-level employment in the most AI-exposed jobs is down 19%HotTrending

The August 2026 update of “Canaries in the Coal Mine?” by Brynjolfsson, Chandar and Chen uses ADP payroll data through June 2026 to find that workers aged 22–25 in AI-exposed occupations are now 19% below where they would be had they kept pace with less-exposed peers — widening from a 13% gap last year. The decline operates mainly through reduced hiring rather than layoffs, and concentrates where AI substitutes for rather than complements human tasks. Experienced workers show no comparable gap. This is the most rigorous labor-market evidence to date and has direct implications for early-career pipeline planning.
