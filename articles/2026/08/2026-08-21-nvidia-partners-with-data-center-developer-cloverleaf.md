# NVIDIA partners with data-center developer Cloverleaf

NVIDIA announced a partnership with Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a developer that helps secure power and site readiness for data centers. TechCrunch reported that Cloverleaf acts as a middle layer between utilities and data-center operators, and Reuters reported NVIDIA now owns a minority stake. The move continues NVIDIA's shift from selling AI systems into actively shaping the land, power, and infrastructure pipeline required to deploy them. INFRASTRUCTURESPACE COMPUTE
