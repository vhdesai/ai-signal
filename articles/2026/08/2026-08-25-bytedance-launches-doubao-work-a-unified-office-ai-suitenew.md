# ByteDance launches Doubao Work, a unified office AI suiteNew

ByteDance consolidated its office AI products — folding in TRAE and Coze — under a single brand, Doubao Work, with Feishu integration and a 30-day free-access offer. The launch is part of a broader Chinese-tech pivot from costly consumer AI toward enterprise and workplace AI. It positions ByteDance directly against Tencent and Alibaba in the office productivity layer.
