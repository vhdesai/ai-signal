# Applied Materials Posts Record Q3 Revenue of $9.12B on AI Chip Demand

Applied Materials reported record third-quarter revenue of $9.12B, up roughly 25% year over year, and guided to as much as $10.75B in the current quarter as AI-driven semiconductor equipment spending continues. Shares nonetheless slipped on the print, reflecting how much AI capex is already embedded in valuations. Equipment orders remain the earliest reliable indicator of 2027 fab capacity, and the guide implies continued expansion rather than digestion. Read alongside reported price increases at SMIC as a sign of tightening supply. finance.yahoo.com
