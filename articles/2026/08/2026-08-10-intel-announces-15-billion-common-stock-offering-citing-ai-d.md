# Intel Announces $15 Billion Common Stock Offering, Citing AI Demand

Intel announced a $15B underwritten public offering capitalizing on a ~400% share-price recovery driven by AI hardware demand. The company pointed to physical AI, purpose-built silicon, and advanced packaging as growth areas. One of the largest equity raises by a US semiconductor firm in the current cycle, signaling Intel intends to fund foundry capacity from equity rather than debt. HOT MARKETS
