# Nvidia Closes In on $12.9B Hugging Face Acquisition

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face, the dominant open-source model and dataset hub, for approximately $12.9 billion. The rationale reported is twofold: defend the CUDA ecosystem by owning the distribution point for open weights, and re-enter the cloud/hosted-inference business. If completed, the deal would concentrate a neutral community platform under the largest AI hardware vendor, which is likely to draw both antitrust attention and enterprise questions about model-hosting neutrality.
