# FSB chair warns G20 that frontier AI threatens financial stability

Financial Stability Board chair Andrew Bailey warned G20 finance ministers that frontier AI's growing autonomy and threat capabilities could destabilize the financial system through cross-jurisdictional cyber disruption. He also flagged that leverage tied to AI optimism could amplify a market correction. AI concentration risk is moving from a tech-policy topic into prudential supervision.
