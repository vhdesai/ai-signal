# OpenAI Ships GPT-5.6-Cyber Behind a Vetted "Daybreak Red" Tier

OpenAI released GPT-5.6-Cyber, a fine-tune of GPT-5.6 Sol trained for vulnerability research and exploit development, with deliberately reduced refusals on dual-use security requests. OpenAI reports 95% on its internal Advanced Cybersecurity Completion Rate benchmark versus 57.3% for its predecessor, and says the model helped surface two previously unknown Chrome V8 flaws, one fixed as CVE-2026-15903. Access is gated to the new Daybreak Red tier, requiring identity verification, SSO/MFA, logging and a recognized certification such as SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001. The governance question for CISOs is no longer the model's capability but who is authorized to run it and under what controls. thehackernews.com — OpenAI Launches GPT-5.6-Cyber with Reduced Safeguards OPEN WEIGHTS
