# TechCrunch reports that Google removed a newly launched Google Earth AI feature within 24 hours after criticism that it…

TechCrunch reports that Google removed a newly launched Google Earth AI feature within 24 hours after criticism that it could help create misleading synthetic geographic imagery. The tool reportedly allowed users to generate AI imagery and overlay it on satellite-map contexts. The reversal shows that generative-media risk is no longer limited to social platforms; geospatial products can create high-stakes misinformation vectors.
