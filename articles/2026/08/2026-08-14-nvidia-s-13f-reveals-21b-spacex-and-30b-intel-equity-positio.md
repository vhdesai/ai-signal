# Nvidia's 13F Reveals $21B SpaceX and $30B Intel Equity Positions

Nvidia's quarterly SEC 13F filing disclosed a $20.98 billion stake in SpaceX and $29.99 billion in Intel as of June 30 — together the large majority of its equity book. The SpaceX position traces back to Nvidia's participation in xAI's January round, later rolled into SpaceX stock. Both holdings are in companies that are also exclusive Nvidia chip customers, sharpening questions about circular financing in the accelerator market. For risk teams: Nvidia is now simultaneously a hardware supplier, financing guarantor, and equity owner of its largest customers — a concentration of roles that would draw scrutiny in any other industry.
