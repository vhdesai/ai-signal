# Nvidia's 13F reveals $21B SpaceX and $30B Intel equity positions

Nvidia's quarterly SEC 13F filing disclosed a $20.98B stake in SpaceX and $29.99B in Intel as of June 30 — together the large majority of its equity book. The SpaceX position traces back to Nvidia's participation in xAI's January round, later rolled into SpaceX.
