# MIT finds medical AI benefits vary sharply by user expertise

MIT researchers found that AI assistance improved skin-disease diagnosis accuracy, but explainability methods affected non-experts and clinicians differently. Non-experts were more likely to defer to AI explanations, even when those explanations were vague or wrong, while clinicians performed best with only the model prediction and no explanation. The finding is strategically important for clinical AI deployment: the same interface can help experts while misleading novices, so user-specific design is a safety requirement.
