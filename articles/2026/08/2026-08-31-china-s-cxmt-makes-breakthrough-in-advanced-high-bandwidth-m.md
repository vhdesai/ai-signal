# China's CXMT Makes Breakthrough in Advanced High-Bandwidth Memory Chips

China's leading memory-chip maker, ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT), has begun producing advanced high-bandwidth memory in small quantities — a milestone that could ease a key constraint on the country's domestic AI compute stack. HBM is a critical bottleneck for AI accelerator performance, and domestic production reduces dependency on Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron.
