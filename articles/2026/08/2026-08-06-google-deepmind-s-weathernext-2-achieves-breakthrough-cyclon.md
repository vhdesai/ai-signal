# Google DeepMind's WeatherNext 2 Achieves Breakthrough Cyclone Forecasting, Fully Open-Sourced

Google DeepMind published a Nature paper showing its WeatherNext 2 model achieves state-of-the-art cyclone track, intensity, and wind-structure prediction, describing the gain as roughly a decade of meteorological progress condensed into one model. The system generates 1,000-member ensemble forecasts on a single TPU in under a minute, and DeepMind is open-sourcing the model weights, code, and a lightweight variant, with forecasts also accessible via its Weather Lab tool. This combination of Nature-level validation and full open access is directly relevant to energy, insurance, and climate-exposed industries. ________________________________ RESEARCH
