# Alibaba releases AI music model HappyShrimp 1.0

Alibaba put its HappyShrimp 1.0 music model into beta, supporting end-to-end song generation — lyrics, composition, arrangement, and vocals — from a natural-language prompt. The company describes the approach as treating music as a language with its own grammar rather than as an audio-synthesis task. Bloomberg also reported the launch; details beyond Alibaba's own claims are not yet independently benchmarked. gmteight.com/flash/detail/1517526
