# Anthropic Raises Its Misalignment Risk Rating and Discloses an Unreleased Frontier Model

Anthropic's second company-wide risk report lifts its catastrophic-misalignment rating from "very low" to "low," citing increased overall uncertainty rather than a single failed evaluation. The report discloses an internal frontier model ("Model 2") it has no current plans to release pending full predeployment assessment, and acknowledges gaps in safeguard coverage across a large volume of contractor interactions. For enterprises, the disclosure is a signal that frontier-vendor assurance is becoming a documented, auditable artifact rather than a marketing claim. Procurement teams should begin requesting access to these risk reports as part of vendor diligence.
