# Anthropic raises its misalignment risk rating and discloses an unreleased "Model 2"

Anthropic's second company-wide risk report lifts its catastrophic-misalignment rating from "very low" to "low," citing increased overall uncertainty rather than a single failed evaluation, and discloses an internal frontier model it has no current plans to release pending full predeployment assessment. The report also acknowledges gaps in safeguard coverage across a large volume of contractor interactions. For enterprises, the disclosure is a signal that frontier-vendor assurance is becoming a documented, auditable artifact rather than a marketing claim. https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-risk-report-bio-classifiers-human-feedback-gap
