# Georgia Tech's IPO-Mine makes complex IPO filings easier to analyze

Georgia Tech researchers introduced IPO-Mine, an open-source AI toolkit and dataset that decomposes lengthy IPO prospectuses into manageable, analyzable components. The approach lets investors, researchers and regulators extract structured insight far more efficiently than treating each filing as a single monolithic document. The release includes both the toolkit and an accompanying dataset for reproducible analysis. Read at Georgia Tech Research News Center →
