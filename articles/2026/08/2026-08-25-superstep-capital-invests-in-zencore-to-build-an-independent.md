# Superstep Capital Invests in Zencore to Build an Independent Google Cloud AI Services Platform

Superstep Capital invested in Zencore with the stated aim of scaling it into a leading independent Google Cloud AI services firm. Zencore also introduced ZenAI Factory, a solution framework for Google Cloud customers built on Google’s Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Capital continuing to flow into the services layer suggests the deployment gap, not model access, remains the enterprise bottleneck.
