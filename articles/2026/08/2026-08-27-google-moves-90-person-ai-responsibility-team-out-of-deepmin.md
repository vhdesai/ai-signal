# Google Moves ~90-Person AI Responsibility Team Out of DeepMind

Google is relocating its AI responsibility unit into its global affairs division (lobbying and policy), completing in early September. The team evaluates models for CBRN hazards and studies psychological effects of chatbots. Staff raised concerns about reduced independence; other safety functions remain inside the lab.
