# Karnataka explores an AI-governance partnership with Anthropic

India's Karnataka state is exploring a partnership with Anthropic to apply AI to governance, skilling and deep-tech growth, with an early focus on fraud reduction. The discussions signal Anthropic's widening international public-sector engagement beyond U.S. federal work. Indian state governments are becoming meaningful early adopters for frontier-lab enterprise offerings.
