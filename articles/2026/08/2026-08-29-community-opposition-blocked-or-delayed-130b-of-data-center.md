# Community Opposition Blocked or Delayed $130B of Data-Center Projects in Q1

Roughly $130 billion in data-center projects were delayed or cancelled in Q1 2026 due to local opposition, per a Data Center Watch report — approaching the $156 billion disrupted across all of 2025. Public sentiment has shifted materially, with a majority of Americans now expressing more concern than excitement about AI. Siting risk and community consent are becoming schedule-determining inputs to capacity planning, not compliance afterthoughts. For executives with committed 2027–2028 capacity, this is now a first-order planning variable.
