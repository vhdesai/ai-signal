# The robotaxi market is entering a phase where federal approvals and local operating legitimacy are diverging rather…

The robotaxi market is entering a phase where federal approvals and local operating legitimacy are diverging rather than moving in lockstep. - Zoox’s federal progress shows national pathways can unlock commercial movement, but Waymo’s local scrutiny demonstrates deployment still depends on municipal politics and public tolerance. - International testing activity, including Baidu-Lyft in London, adds further complexity by broadening the regulatory comparison set. - This fragmented environment raises costs for operators because scale now requires multi-layer compliance and community-management capabilities. - For mobility investors, execution risk increasingly sits in governance and local trust, not only technology readiness.
