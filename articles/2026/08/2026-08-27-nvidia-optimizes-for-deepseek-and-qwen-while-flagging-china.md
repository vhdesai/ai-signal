# Nvidia Optimizes for DeepSeek and Qwen While Flagging China-Model Restriction Risk

Nvidia disclosed optimizations for DeepSeek V4 Flash and Alibaba’s Qwen 3.8. Simultaneously, an SEC filing warned U.S. restrictions on Chinese-origin models could be material. Nvidia argues optimization keeps developers on the American stack; lawmakers read it as amplifying Chinese model adoption.
