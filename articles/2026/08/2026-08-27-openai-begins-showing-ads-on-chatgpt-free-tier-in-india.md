# OpenAI Begins Showing Ads on ChatGPT Free Tier in India

OpenAI is rolling out advertising to logged-in adult users on ChatGPT’s Free and Go tiers in India (100M+ weekly actives). Paid tiers excluded. The clearest signal yet that consumer monetization via advertising, not subscriptions alone, will fund inference costs at scale.
