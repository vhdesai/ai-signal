# Saudi PIF-backed HUMAIN invests in MOZN for financial-services AI

AiThority republished a PR Newswire item announcing that HUMAIN, Saudi Arabia's PIF-backed full-stack AI company, made a strategic investment in MOZN and will co-build enterprise AI solutions for banking, financial crime prevention, KYC, GRC, and knowledge intelligence. The partnership is tied to HUMAIN's AI Agent Marketplace and planned commercial availability in H2 2026. The signal is sovereign AI moving from infrastructure ambition into regulated-industry application layers.
