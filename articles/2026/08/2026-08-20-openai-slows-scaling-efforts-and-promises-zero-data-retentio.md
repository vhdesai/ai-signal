# OpenAI slows scaling efforts and promises zero data retention for select frontier customers

OpenAI said it will offer zero data retention to certain frontier-model enterprise customers while simultaneously acknowledging it is "temporarily" slowing its scaling efforts. The scaling comment is the more strategically notable disclosure, implying compute or economics constraints at the frontier. COMPETITIVE
