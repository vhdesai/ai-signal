# Big Tech is borrowing its way through the AI boom

Yahoo Finance reported that major technology companies are increasingly using debt to fund AI infrastructure buildouts rather than relying only on operating cash flow. The pattern reflects the scale and duration of AI capex commitments: data centers, chips, power, and network capacity require financing structures closer to industrial infrastructure than software R&D. For executives, the key question is whether AI revenue growth can arrive quickly enough to support the larger balance-sheet commitments now being made.
