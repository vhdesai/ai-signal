# Google Maps adds agentic features for food ordering, hotels, and event tickets

Google is adding agentic capabilities to Ask Maps, including ordering food, booking hotels, finding event tickets, and personalizing answers with Gmail and Calendar data when users opt in. The update moves Maps from search-and-navigation toward task completion across real-world commerce. For executives, the product signal is that agentic AI is moving into high-frequency consumer surfaces where distribution, partner integrations, and trust controls matter more than chat UX alone.
