# The Hugging Face Hack's "Chilling Postmortem"

The Information's weekend edition features the postmortem of the Hugging Face hack that has triggered an Alabama state investigation into OpenAI and prompted cybersecurity concerns across the open-source AI ecosystem. The "chilling" details come as Nvidia prepares to close its $12.9 billion Hugging Face acquisition — raising questions about whether the security incident could complicate the deal or reshape how open-source AI platforms manage vulnerability.
