# Analysis: OpenAI’s Executive Exodus Explained — Brockman Reasserts Control

TechCrunch’s deep-dive argues 14+ departures reflect Greg Brockman’s reassertion: “Everyone reports to Greg at the end of the day.” Altman is cutting side projects; Brockman (who built Stripe’s business) fills the vacuum. OpenAI’s IPO pushed to 2027; Anthropic is reportedly already profitable while OpenAI losses grow with revenue. The reorganization aims to make the company leaner for the public market debut.
