# Demis Hassabis named Alphabet Chief Scientist; Koray Kavukcuoglu becomes Google DeepMind CEO

Alphabet announced Wednesday that Hassabis is ceding the DeepMind CEO role to become DeepMind chairman and Alphabet chief scientist, with longtime deputy Koray Kavukcuoglu stepping up as DeepMind CEO. Yahoo Finance characterizes it as the biggest AI executive overhaul at the company. The reshuffle concentrates Hassabis over frontier research while installing new operational leadership at DeepMind. Coming alongside senior departures, it is the most consequential governance change at Google's AI organization in years.
