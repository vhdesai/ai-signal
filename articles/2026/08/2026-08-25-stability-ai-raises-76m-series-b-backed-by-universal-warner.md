# Stability AI Raises $76M Series B Backed by Universal, Warner, Sony Music and EA

Stability AI raised $76 million in Series B funding, bringing total capital raised to $232 million. The investor roster is the signal: three major music groups and Electronic Arts backing a generative image company points to rights-holders buying a seat at the licensing table rather than litigating from outside it.
