# What happens when a kid's robot best friend dies?

A reported feature on AI companion toys for neurodivergent children, examining what happens when the vendor behind a device shuts down and the companion goes dark. The piece raises durability, duty-of-care and data questions for consumer AI products with emotional attachment. It is journalism rather than a peer-reviewed study, but it maps directly to product-lifecycle risk.
