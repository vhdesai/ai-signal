# Meta AI Research details the distillation and quantization stack behind Muse Glimmer

The primary research post explains how Meta compressed a frontier-class teacher model into a device-runnable agent. The combination of ~4-bit quantization (language model under 20GB) and DFlash speculative decoding is the core contribution, targeting practical latency for local tool-using agents. It is a notable data point that agentic capability, not just chat quality, is now being pushed down to consumer hardware. Expect fast community fine-tunes given the Apache 2.0 license.
