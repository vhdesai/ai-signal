# NVIDIA details PORTS-Pike infrastructure support for OpenAI compute in Ohio

NVIDIA said it is partnering with SB Energy to secure land, power, and shell capacity at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Portsmouth, Ohio, with OpenAI as tenant. The initial deployment is expected to provide 4.25 gigawatts of AI factory capacity, with the site able to support multiple generations of NVIDIA compute; NVIDIA also said OpenAI's existing and planned NVIDIA infrastructure commitments represent roughly 12 gigawatts through 2030. The announcement is a clear example of AI suppliers moving upstream into site and power constraints because frontier-lab demand is outpacing traditional balance-sheet and infrastructure capacity.
