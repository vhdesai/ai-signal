# Z.AI First-Half Revenue Rises Nearly Fivefold but Misses Targets

Hong Kong-listed Z.AI, maker of the GLM model family, reported first-half revenue of 954 million yuan (about $142 million), up roughly fivefold year over year on API and cloud demand, while still falling short of estimates by a reported 29% and sustaining heavy losses. Management cited sharply higher token volume and falling inference cost per token.
