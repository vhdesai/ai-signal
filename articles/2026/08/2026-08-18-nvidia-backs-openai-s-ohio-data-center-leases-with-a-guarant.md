# Nvidia backs OpenAI's Ohio data center leases with a guarantee of up to $105 billion

Nvidia has guaranteed up to $105 billion of OpenAI's lease obligations across approximately 4.25 gigawatts of Ohio data center capacity, part of a 20-year campus development in Pike County with SoftBank-backed SB Energy.
