# Marc Benioff-backed “June” exits stealth to tackle enterprise AI deployment

June emerged from stealth with a $20 million pre-seed round — backed by Salesforce’s Marc Benioff — to simplify how large enterprises actually deploy AI. The startup targets the “forward-deployed engineer” (FDE) bottleneck, the specialists companies increasingly rely on to get AI systems running reliably in production. It’s a bet that the hardest problem in enterprise AI is no longer model quality but operational adoption. Infrastructure FUNDINGHOT
