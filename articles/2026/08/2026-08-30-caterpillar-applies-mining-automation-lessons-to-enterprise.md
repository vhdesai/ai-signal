# Caterpillar applies mining-automation lessons to enterprise AI deployment

CTO Jaime Mineart described Caterpillar extending autonomy experience from mining into construction sites and quarries, with a Cat AI Assistant now used by technicians for voice-driven repair procedures and parts identification. The company draws on roughly 1.6 million connected assets and more than 16 petabytes of structured data, and uses AI agents internally to modernize legacy code and detect defects earlier. Caterpillar plans to spend $100M over five years training its 118,000 employees in AI, autonomy and robotics.
