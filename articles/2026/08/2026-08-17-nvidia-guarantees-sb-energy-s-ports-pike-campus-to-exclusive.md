# NVIDIA guarantees SB Energy's PORTS-Pike campus to exclusively host NVIDIA AI compute

NVIDIA confirmed it will be the exclusive AI compute provider at PORTS-Pike and will supply credit support tied to land, power, and shell construction. The structure — vendor equity plus credit backstop in exchange for exclusivity — extends the financing pattern NVIDIA has been building with asset managers and hyperscalers.
