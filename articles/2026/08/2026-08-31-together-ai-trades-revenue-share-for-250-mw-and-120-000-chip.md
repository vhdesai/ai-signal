# Together AI Trades Revenue Share for 250 MW and 120,000 Chips from Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN

Together AI secured 250 megawatts of capacity and roughly 120,000 accelerators from HUMAIN's Saudi data centers in exchange for a revenue share reported at approximately $5 billion annually. CEO Vipul Prakash pointed explicitly to U.S. community backlash, moratoriums, and project cancellations as the constraint driving the company offshore.
