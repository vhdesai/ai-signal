# Sony Music and Warner Sue Anthropic Over Lyrics in Training Data

Filed in N.D. Cal., naming Anthropic, CEO Amodei, and co-founder Mann. Alleges tens of thousands of copyright infringements from shadow-library sources and scraped lyric databases. Extends training-data litigation into music publishing where rights are well-documented and damages easier to quantify. Arrives as Anthropic prepares for IPO — litigation of this scale becomes a material disclosure item.
