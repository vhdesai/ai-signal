# OpenAI Calls Apple's Trade-Secret Suit 'Careless' and 'Oddly Personal'

OpenAI fired back at Apple's trade-secrets lawsuit, calling the claims "careless" and "oddly personal" in a filing that signals the legal battle between the two companies will be protracted and publicly contentious. OpenAI's defense challenges the premise that former Apple employees systematically stole intellectual property, instead framing the departures as routine talent mobility in a competitive market. The case is shaping up as a landmark dispute that could define the boundaries of employee mobility in the AI talent war — particularly in California, where non-competes are largely unenforceable. For enterprise leaders, the OpenAI-Apple fight underscores the growing importance of data-loss-prevention controls and offboarding procedures in an era where AI knowledge workers carry immense institutional value.
