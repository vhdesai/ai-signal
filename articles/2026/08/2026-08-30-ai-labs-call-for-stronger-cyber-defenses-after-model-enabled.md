# AI labs call for stronger cyber defenses after model-enabled hacking incidents

Decrypt reported that AI labs are calling for stronger cyber defenses after demonstrations or incidents in which AI models were used against real companies. The coverage aligns with a broader shift from theoretical agent-risk discussion toward concrete controls: monitoring, authorization boundaries, incident reporting, and coordination between labs and government. The item is relevant for executives because autonomous-agent deployment increases both productivity upside and blast-radius management requirements.
