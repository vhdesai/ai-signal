# OpenAI publishes enterprise research on moving from assistance to execution

OpenAI published research on how enterprises are adopting agentic AI, focusing on ChatGPT, Codex, and the shift from AI assistance toward execution of work. The piece frames leading adopters as organizations moving beyond experimentation into workflow-level deployment and measurable business process change. For senior leaders, the relevant question is increasingly not whether AI is being used, but where execution authority, controls, and ROI measurement are being redesigned around it.
