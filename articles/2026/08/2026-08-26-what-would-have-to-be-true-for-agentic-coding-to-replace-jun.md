# What would have to be true for agentic coding to replace junior engineers

A falsifiable-conditions analysis arguing that coding-benchmark gains are being over-read into labor-market conclusions. It draws on METR's time-horizon research distinguishing 50% and 80% success horizons on deliberately self-contained tasks, and on OpenAI's February 2026 move away from SWE-bench Verified over flawed test cases and training contamination. Of four stated conditions, the author concludes three still fail.
