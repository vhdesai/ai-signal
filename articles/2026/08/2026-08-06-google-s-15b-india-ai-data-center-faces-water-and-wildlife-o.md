# Google's $15B India AI data center faces water and wildlife opposition

Google's planned $15 billion AI and data-center hub in Visakhapatnam—developed with the Adani Group and billed as its largest India investment—is drawing legal challenges and protests over water use and proximity to a wildlife sanctuary. Google says advanced air cooling will limit water consumption, and the state government pledges no diversion of drinking water. The dispute shows how community, water, and permitting constraints—not just chip supply—are becoming gating factors for AI buildouts globally. Expect siting and social-license risk to feature more prominently in infrastructure planning as AI data center construction accelerates worldwide.
