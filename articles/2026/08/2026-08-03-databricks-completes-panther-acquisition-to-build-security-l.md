# Databricks completes Panther acquisition to build security lakehouse strategy

Databricks announced completion of its acquisition of Panther, an AI-native security operations platform with SIEM integrations, detection-as-code workflows, and AI agents for alert triage and response. The announcement was published seven minutes before the strict 24-hour window, so it is included under the 24-48 hour rule given the direct strategic relevance. Databricks is using Panther to position security operations as a data-management and AI problem, competing with legacy SIEM economics.
