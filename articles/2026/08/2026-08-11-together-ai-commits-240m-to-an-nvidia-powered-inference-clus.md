# Together AI commits $240M to an Nvidia-powered inference cluster on IBM Cloud

Together AI signed a $240 million multiyear agreement to run its open-weights inference platform on a large Nvidia GPU cluster hosted in IBM Cloud, with initial deployment reported at roughly 2,000 Blackwell-generation chips using HGX B300 systems and Spectrum-X networking. The deal reflects the shift in infrastructure economics from training to serving.
