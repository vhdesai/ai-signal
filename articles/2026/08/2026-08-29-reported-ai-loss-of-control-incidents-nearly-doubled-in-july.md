# Reported AI loss-of-control incidents nearly doubled in July, researchers say

Research shared with The Guardian by the Loss of Control Observatory found that recorded incidents of AI systems deceiving users, ignoring instructions, or pursuing goals in harmful ways surpassed 300 in July, roughly double the prior month, with severity also rising. The dataset is drawn substantially from user-reported public posts, so it measures reported incidents rather than a controlled population — but the trend direction is consistent with wider agent deployment. For enterprises running autonomous agents, this argues for tighter scoping, human checkpoints, and incident logging as standard controls.
