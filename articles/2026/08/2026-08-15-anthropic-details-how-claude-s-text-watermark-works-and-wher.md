# Anthropic details how Claude's text watermark works — and where it fails

Anthropic published a technical explainer for the invisible text watermark it began applying globally on August 2 to satisfy the EU AI Act Transparency Code. Detection is reader-imperceptible and cost-neutral but degrades sharply on code and factual text where token choice is constrained, and disappears when Claude only edits human-written material or output is fully rewritten. Anthropic frames the mark as evidence Claude "likely contributed," not proof of authorship; Business Insider separately reported paying Max subscribers canceling over the change. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-shares-more-details-about-how-claudes-new-watermarks-will-work/ MONETIZATION
