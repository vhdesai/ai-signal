# Chinese open models are reshaping the competitive math for Anthropic, OpenAI, and Nvidia

Analysts argue that cheaper, openly available Chinese models are compressing margins and pricing power across the U.S. frontier, while restricting those models would protect domestic developers but raise costs and slow adoption for U.S. users. The piece frames the strategic bind: openness accelerates diffusion and undercuts closed-model pricing, but restriction risks ceding the developer ecosystem. For Nvidia, sustained Chinese demand and an expanding open-model stack cut both ways. FUNDINGSECURITYAGENTS
