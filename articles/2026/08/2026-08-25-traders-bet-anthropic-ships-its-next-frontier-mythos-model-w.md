# Traders bet Anthropic ships its next frontier “Mythos” model within weeksTrending

Polymarket traders sharply raised the odds — to roughly 81% by September 15 — that Anthropic releases its next top-tier Mythos-class model, on more than $233K of volume. The market is notable because U.S. Commerce previously forced a three-day suspension of Anthropic’s Mythos models over export-control concerns before access was restored. Anthropic’s most recent generally available model was Claude Opus 5 in July 2026.
