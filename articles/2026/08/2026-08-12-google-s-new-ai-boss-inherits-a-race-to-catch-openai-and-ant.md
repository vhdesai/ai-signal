# Google's New AI Boss Inherits a Race to Catch OpenAI and Anthropic

Koray Kavukcuoglu is taking charge of Google DeepMind as it tries to keep Gemini competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic. The leadership transition comes at a critical juncture, as Google faces mounting pressure to demonstrate that its AI research prowess can translate into market-leading products. The appointment signals continuity with DeepMind's research-first culture but underscores the urgency of the competitive landscape.
