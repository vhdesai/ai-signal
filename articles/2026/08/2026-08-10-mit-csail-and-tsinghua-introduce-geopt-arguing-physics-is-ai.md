# MIT CSAIL and Tsinghua introduce GeoPT, arguing physics is AI's “third modality”

Researchers at MIT CSAIL with Tsinghua released GeoPT, which pre-trains models on physics before task training. The approach reaches peak performance roughly twice as fast and trains on up to 60 percent less data. The team frames physics as a third modality alongside text and pixels, with implications for simulation, robotics, and engineering workloads where labeled data is scarce.
