# Memory Prices Quadruple in a Year, Pushing Costs Into Consumer Hardware

AI-driven demand has quadrupled memory chip prices over the past year, and analysts expect them to stay elevated. TrendForce estimates the bill of materials for a 256GB iPhone 18 Pro could rise roughly 38% year over year, with memory's share of component cost climbing from about 10% to 34% or higher. Affected industries — including medical device makers — are lobbying Washington for relief through the CHIPS Act or Defense Production Act. This is the first clear case of AI infrastructure demand producing measurable consumer price inflation. MARKETSPOLICY
