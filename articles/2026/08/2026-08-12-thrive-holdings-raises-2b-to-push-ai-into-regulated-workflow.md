# Thrive Holdings Raises $2B to Push AI Into Regulated Workflows

OpenAI-backed Thrive raised $2B at $12B from SoftBank, D1, and Altimeter. The thesis: embed AI directly into accounting, IT, and regulated back-office ops rather than selling horizontal tooling. This roll-up model — acquire the workflow first, then automate it — is the clearest current template for capturing AI margin in services-heavy industries. The round is an early test of whether AI-native roll-ups can convert technical capability into durable operating margin at scale. CIOs should evaluate whether their current back-office vendors are acquisition targets — and plan accordingly for potential technology-stack transitions. TechCrunch BREAKING LEADERSHIP
