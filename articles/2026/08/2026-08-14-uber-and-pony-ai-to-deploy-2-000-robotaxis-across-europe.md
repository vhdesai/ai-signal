# Uber and Pony.ai to Deploy 2,000+ Robotaxis Across Europe

Pony.ai and Uber expanded their partnership to deploy over 2,000 autonomous vehicles across five European cities, extending beyond their initial Zagreb launch. The scale-up puts a Chinese autonomy stack onto European public roads at meaningful volume, ahead of most Western robotaxi operators outside the U.S. City-by-city regulatory approval remains the gating factor. Uber's positioning as the distribution layer for multiple AV stacks — rather than owning one — is the more durable strategic bet.
