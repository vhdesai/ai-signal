# IBM launches Apptio AI Value & ROI to connect AI spend to business results

IBM introduced Apptio AI Value & ROI (public preview), giving finance and technology leaders “a single view of AI spending, including token costs, and its connection to measurable business results.” IBM cites Gartner that 84% of finance leaders have not been able to measure the ROI of AI initiatives. General availability is planned for Q3 2026.
