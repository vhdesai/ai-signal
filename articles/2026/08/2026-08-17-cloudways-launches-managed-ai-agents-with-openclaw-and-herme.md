# Cloudways launches managed AI agents with OpenClaw and Hermes

Cloudways announced managed AI agents with the general availability of OpenClaw and Hermes, according to Business Wire coverage. The release appears aimed at packaging agent capabilities into a managed service for cloud-hosted businesses rather than requiring customers to assemble agent infrastructure themselves. The signal is that agent deployment is beginning to move into managed hosting and SMB-oriented platforms, not only hyperscaler and enterprise AI stacks.
