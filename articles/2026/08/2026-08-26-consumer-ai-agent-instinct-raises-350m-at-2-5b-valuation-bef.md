# Consumer AI Agent Instinct Raises $350M at $2.5B Valuation Before Public Launch

Instinct, a one-year-old AI assistant still in private beta, closed $350M co-led by Index Ventures and Benchmark at a $2.5B valuation. Reporting flags unresolved privacy and security questions around its data-access model — a live example of the consent surface enterprise buyers should expect to govern.
