# Meta Closes Smart-Glasses Recording Loophole, Launches Privacy Campaign

Meta is updating its AI smart glasses after users found that covering the capture-indicator LED bypassed bystander notification. The camera will now stop if the light is obscured mid-recording. The fix is small; the governing question is not. Always-available cameras create a consent problem phones do not, and AI wearables will scale only if bystanders trust the hardware.
