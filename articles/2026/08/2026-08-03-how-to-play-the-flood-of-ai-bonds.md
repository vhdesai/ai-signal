# How to Play the Flood of AI Bonds

Big Tech companies are rushing to issue long-duration bonds to finance AI infrastructure buildouts, but the WSJ cautions that investors may have better options than lending to tech giants for decades. The bond flood reflects the sheer capital intensity of the AI capex cycle — companies need to borrow for 20–30 year terms to match the expected useful life of data center assets. For CFOs and treasurers, the article highlights a shift in how AI infrastructure is being financed: rather than funding capex purely from operating cash flow or equity, the largest players are tapping fixed-income markets at scale, which introduces interest-rate risk and covenant complexity that didn't exist in previous technology investment cycles.
