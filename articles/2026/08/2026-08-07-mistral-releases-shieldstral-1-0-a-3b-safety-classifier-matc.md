# Mistral Releases Shieldstral 1.0, a 3B Safety Classifier Matching a 20B Model

Mistral AI released Shieldstral 1.0 3B, an Apache 2.0-licensed multimodal safety classifier that accepts a plain-language policy at inference time, removing the need to retrain for different deployment contexts. Built on Ministral-3B with a Pixtral vision encoder, it achieves 84.9% average F1 on text safety — matching GPT-OSS-Safeguard-20B at roughly a sixth of the parameter count — and fits within 16GB VRAM, giving multi-tenant SaaS companies a practical, commercially licensed content-moderation guardrail.
