# Tsinghua's PACMAN Group Releases Puro-2B, a Fully Open Recipe for Sub-$7K Pretraining

Tsinghua University's PACMAN group published Puro-2B, an open end-to-end recipe for pretraining a useful 2-billion-parameter language model on consumer RTX 5090 GPUs. The technical report claims a roughly $5,090 run outperforms Qwen2-1.5B and a $6,890 run approaches Qwen2.5-1.5B, with training code, checkpoints, and data released. The practical significance is the cost floor: credible small-model pretraining is moving within reach of individual research groups and corporate teams without frontier-scale clusters. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27370
