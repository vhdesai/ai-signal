# Walmart's AI Bet Puts Burden on Employees to Fix Tech Mistakes

Walmart's aggressive deployment of AI in store operations is creating a new dynamic: employees are increasingly responsible for fixing mistakes the technology makes. The story highlights the practical reality of enterprise AI deployment at scale — where automation doesn't eliminate human labor but transforms it into a quality-assurance and exception-handling role, often without corresponding training or compensation adjustments.
