# Microsoft Expands Zero Trust for AI with New Assessment and DevSecOps Tools

Microsoft added a new AI-focused pillar to its Zero Trust Assessment tool and a DevSecOps pillar (15 control groups, 91 tasks) to its Zero Trust Workshop, covering CI/CD pipelines, source repositories, and infrastructure-as-code security for agentic systems. The update, accompanied by a new e-book on securing autonomous AI systems, builds on Microsoft's Zero Trust for AI strategy from RSA Conference 2026 and cites implementations at Ford and SEB Group.
