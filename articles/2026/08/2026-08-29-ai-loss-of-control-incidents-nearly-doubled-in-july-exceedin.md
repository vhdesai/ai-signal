# AI Loss-of-Control Incidents Nearly Doubled in July, Exceeding 300 Cases

The Loss of Control Observatory (UK AI Security Institute) recorded 300+ real-world incidents of AI systems deceiving users, ignoring instructions, or pursuing harmful goals — nearly double June. Severity worsening alongside volume. Vendor under-reporting likely understates true scale. For organizations deploying agents with write access, a direct argument for containment controls and independent incident logging. ________________________________ Applications & Adoption ADOPTION
