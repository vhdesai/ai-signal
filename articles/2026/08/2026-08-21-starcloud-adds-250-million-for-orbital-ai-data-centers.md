# Starcloud Adds $250 Million for Orbital AI Data Centers

Starcloud, which is building satellites capable of running AI inference in orbit, added a $250M extension to its March Series A, bringing the round to roughly $420M. The company's thesis is that orbital compute sidesteps terrestrial power and cooling constraints, though launch capacity is itself tightening. Treat this as an early-stage option on compute siting rather than near-term capacity. POLICY
