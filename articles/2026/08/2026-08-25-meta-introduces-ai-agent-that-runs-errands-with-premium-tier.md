# Meta Introduces AI Agent That Runs Errands, With Premium Tier Reported up to $199.99/Month

Meta introduced an AI agent designed to complete real-world errands, and per reported internal plans priced a premium subscription tier at up to $199.99 per month. The pricing signals Meta believes consumers will pay materially for agentic assistants rather than treating them as an ad-supported feature. Pricing is reported from internal plans and not officially confirmed.
