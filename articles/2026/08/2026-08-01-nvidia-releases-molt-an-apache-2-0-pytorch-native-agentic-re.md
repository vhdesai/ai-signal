# NVIDIA releases “Molt,” an Apache-2.0 PyTorch-native agentic reinforcement-learning framework

NVIDIA’s NeMo team open-sourced Molt, a PyTorch-native framework for agentic reinforcement learning that packs its core RL logic into roughly 8.6K lines of code and ships under a permissive Apache 2.0 license. The lean, hackable design targets researchers and teams building RL-trained agents without the overhead of heavier orchestration stacks. Industry News Hot Trending Markets
