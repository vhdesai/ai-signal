# Together AI Takes 250 MW of Saudi Capacity From HUMAIN, Citing U.S. Data-Center Backlash

Together AI secured 250 megawatts and roughly 120,000 accelerators from Saudi state-backed HUMAIN under a revenue-share arrangement. CEO Vipul Prakash pointed directly to U.S. community opposition and project cancellations as the constraint driving capacity offshore. For enterprises: inference for Western customers increasingly runs in Gulf-hosted facilities, with attendant data-residency and export-control questions.
