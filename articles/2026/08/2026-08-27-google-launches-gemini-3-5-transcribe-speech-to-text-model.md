# Google launches Gemini 3.5 Transcribe speech-to-text model

Google introduced Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, a real-time speech-to-text model that strips filler words, handles self-corrections, formats output, and performs speaker diarization with word-level timestamps across 85+ languages. Google cited average word-error rates of 2.6% non-streaming and 4.0% streaming at sub-second latency. It is available now through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, positioning voice as a first-class interface for agents, call centers, and meeting automation.
