# Nvidia Takes a Minority Stake in Data Center Developer Cloverleaf Infrastructure

Nvidia invested in Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a developer that assembles large power-connected sites for data center construction. The investment extends Nvidia's pattern of putting capital upstream of its own demand — into power, land, and site development — rather than only selling accelerators. Grid interconnection timelines, not chip supply, are increasingly the binding constraint on AI capacity. FUNDING
