# Meta Closes Smart-Glasses Loophole That Allowed Recording With the Indicator Covered

Meta is changing software on its AI glasses after users found that covering the capture LED after a recording had already started bypassed the intended shutoff. The camera will now stop functioning if the indicator is obstructed mid-recording, and Meta is running a public-awareness campaign on how the indicator works.
