# Alibaba launches Qwen3.8-Max, its largest and most capable model yet

Alibaba unveiled Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 1-million-token context window, sending its shares up roughly 6%. The model ranks as the top Chinese text model on Arena.AI and second globally on multimodal benchmarks. Products & Tools New Enterprise AI India Cloud
