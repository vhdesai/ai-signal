# Pokee AI releases Pokee-Isaac 28B with a 10M-token context window

Pokee AI released Pokee-Isaac 28B, a 28-billion-parameter text-only foundation model with a 10M-token context window designed to run inside the customer security boundary. It reports 93.3% on RULER at 10M tokens — where comparison baselines return 0.0 beyond 2M — and leads BFCL v4 at 70.94 while placing second on Terminal-Bench 2.1. Prefill reaches 137,200 tokens/second at full context on a single B200. Weights are not published; deployment is licensed into VPC, on-premises or on-device at list pricing of $0.15/$1.00 per million tokens. Academic Research RESEARCH OPEN SOURCE
