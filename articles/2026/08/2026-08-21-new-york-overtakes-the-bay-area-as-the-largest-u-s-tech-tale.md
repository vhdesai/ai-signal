# New York Overtakes the Bay Area as the Largest U.S. Tech Talent Market

York's tech workforce reached approximately 394,300 jobs against the San Francisco Bay Area's 375,730, the first reversal in 13 years of the underlying CBRE analysis. AI-related roles now account for close to a third of U.S. tech job postings and grew about 45% year over year across the U.S. and Canada. Financial-sector AI demand plus Bay Area headcount reductions drove the shift, with implications for where firms site AI engineering capacity.
