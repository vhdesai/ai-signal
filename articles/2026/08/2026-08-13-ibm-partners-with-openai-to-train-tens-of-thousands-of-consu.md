# IBM Partners with OpenAI to Train Tens of Thousands of Consultants

IBM and OpenAI will jointly market AI offerings and develop industry-specific solutions for financial services, government, telecom, and retail. IBM will establish a dedicated OpenAI practice and certify tens of thousands of consultants on GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work.
