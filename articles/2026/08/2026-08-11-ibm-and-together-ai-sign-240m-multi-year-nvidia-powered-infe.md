# IBM and Together AI Sign $240M Multi-Year Nvidia-Powered Inference Deal

IBM and Together AI signed a $240 million multi-year agreement to build a large-scale AI inference cluster on IBM Cloud using Nvidia infrastructure, targeted at open-source model serving. The deal positions IBM Cloud as a credible neutral venue for open-weight inference at a moment when open models are gaining enterprise traction. It also illustrates the emerging pattern of AI-native startups anchoring capacity commitments with second-tier hyperscalers to escape queue contention at the top three. SUPPLY CHAINHOT
