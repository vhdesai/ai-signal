# Blue Owl Funds Lead $2.4B AI Factory Equipment Financing for IREN

Blue Owl–managed funds led a $2.4 billion equipment financing for IREN's AI factory buildout. The structure is notable because it routes private credit, rather than vendor-linked revenue sharing, into GPU and data-center equipment.
