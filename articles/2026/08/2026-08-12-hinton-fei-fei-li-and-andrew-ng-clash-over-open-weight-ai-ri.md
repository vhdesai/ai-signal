# Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng Clash Over Open-Weight AI Risks at Ai4

Three of AI's most respected voices debated open-weight AI at the Ai4 conference. Andrew Ng argued for openness to counter Chinese soft-power dominance: "I don't want there to be gatekeepers." Geoffrey Hinton acknowledged "that battle's been lost" but warned open weights make it cheap to train models for cyberattacks. Fei-Fei Li pushed for nuance: "This debate at the sweeping level of 'we can only tolerate one' is a false debate."
