# xAI Co-Founder Leaves to Build Open-Source AI Startup River AI

Igor Babuschkin, a key researcher who previously worked at OpenAI, Google, and xAI, has left to build River AI — a startup fundamentally geared toward the open-source philosophy. Babuschkin argues that the approach favored by Anthropic and OpenAI will stifle innovation and that the public does not want AI companies to "rule the world and control this superpowerful technology." https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/11/technology/igor-babuschkin-xai-river-ai.html Products & Tools PRODUCT NVIDIA
