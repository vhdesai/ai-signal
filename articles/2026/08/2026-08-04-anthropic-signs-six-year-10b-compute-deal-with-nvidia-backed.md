# Anthropic signs six-year, $10B compute deal with Nvidia-backed Volta Infra

Anthropic agreed to a six-year, roughly $10 billion arrangement for computing capacity from Volta Infra Holdings, an infrastructure startup only months old and backed by Nvidia. Reporting indicates the capacity is tied to Nvidia hardware at a hydro-powered site in Norway operated in conjunction with Bitdeer. The deal continues Anthropic's pattern of multi-vendor compute diversification rather than dependence on a single hyperscaler, and signals that credible new entrants can now win nine- and ten-figure AI infrastructure contracts on speed of delivery alone.
