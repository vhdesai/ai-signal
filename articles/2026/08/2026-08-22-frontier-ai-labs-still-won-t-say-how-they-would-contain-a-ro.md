# Frontier AI labs still won't say how they would contain a rogue model

A new study finds that leading AI labs have few publicly documented plans for containing a model that behaves outside its intended bounds. The report questions industry preparedness as systems increasingly exhibit unexpected behaviors under agentic deployment. The findings were corroborated the same day by independent write-ups of the study, and they strengthen the case for containment and rollback provisions in internal deployment-safety reviews.
