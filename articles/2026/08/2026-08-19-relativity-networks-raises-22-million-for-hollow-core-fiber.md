# Relativity Networks Raises $22 Million for Hollow-Core Fiber in AI Data Centers

Relativity Networks closed a $22 million round to commercialize hollow-core fiber, a rarely deployed technology that transmits data roughly 30% faster than conventional glass fiber by sending light through air. The target is interconnect inside and between AI data centers. Industry News FUNDING
