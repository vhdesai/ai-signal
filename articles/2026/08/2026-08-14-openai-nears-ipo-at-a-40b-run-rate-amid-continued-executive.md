# OpenAI nears IPO at a ~$40B run rate amid continued executive churn

OpenAI's annualized revenue has passed $40 billion as it moves toward a public listing, with Dali Rajic — formerly Wiz president and COO — installed as CRO. The revenue milestone lands against a sustained pattern of senior departures, and analysts caution that run-rate figures are not booked revenue. The leadership refresh signals cybersecurity moving to the center of OpenAI's enterprise go-to-market.
