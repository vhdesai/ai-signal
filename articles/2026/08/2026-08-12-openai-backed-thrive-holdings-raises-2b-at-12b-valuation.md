# OpenAI-Backed Thrive Holdings Raises $2B at $12B Valuation

Thrive Holdings, a Thrive Capital spinout that buys traditional businesses and implements AI into their workflows, raised $2B from SoftBank, D1 Capital, and Altimeter. OpenAI holds an equity stake and sends employees to work with Thrive's portfolio. The firm operates 70+ businesses across accounting and IT, where AI has cut tax prep times 30% and sped up help desk resolution 36x.
