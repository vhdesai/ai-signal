# OpenAI Asks Judge to Dismiss Apple Lawsuit

OpenAI has formally asked a judge to dismiss Apple's trade-secrets lawsuit, escalating the legal battle between the two companies from filing-stage posturing to a substantive motion for dismissal. The legal team argued that Apple's claims are insufficiently specific and that California's protections for employee mobility render the suit untenable. The motion's outcome will be closely watched because it could set precedent for how AI talent disputes are adjudicated — a question with implications for every major lab competing for a finite pool of frontier AI researchers.
