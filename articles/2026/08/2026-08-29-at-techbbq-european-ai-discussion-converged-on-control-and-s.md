# At TechBBQ, European AI discussion converged on control and sovereignty

Investors, founders and operators at the Nordic TechBBQ conference repeatedly returned to the question of who retains agency over AI systems — a framing distinct from the capability-and-scale conversation dominating U.S. discourse. The emphasis reflects Europe's regulatory posture as the AI Act moves into enforcement.
