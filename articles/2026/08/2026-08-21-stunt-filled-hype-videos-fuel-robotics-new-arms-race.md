# Stunt-Filled Hype Videos Fuel Robotics’ New “Arms Race”

Robotics companies are producing increasingly extreme stunt videos that exaggerate capabilities. A humanoid sent to Ecuador’s Mount Chimborazo required six humans carrying it most of the way. The “hype video arms race” raises concerns about inflated investor expectations in physical AI as VC funding surges.
