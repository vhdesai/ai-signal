# OpenAI makes GPT-5.6 Luna the default for free users and removes text-chat limits

OpenAI made GPT-5.6 Luna the default model for ChatGPT Free and Go users and removed limits on text-based chats, as the product crossed one billion weekly users. The move pushes a current-generation model into the free tier at a moment when cheaper open-weight rivals are eroding frontier pricing power. It raises baseline quality for hundreds of millions of users while sharpening the cost question behind free-tier inference at scale. Expect competitive pressure on Google's and Anthropic's consumer tiers—and a growing expectation among enterprise users that frontier-quality models should be table stakes, not premium add-ons.
