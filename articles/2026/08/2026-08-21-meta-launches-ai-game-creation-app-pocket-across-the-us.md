# Meta Launches AI Game-Creation App 'Pocket' Across the US

Meta rolled out its experimental AI-powered gaming app Pocket to all US users, letting people create small interactive games from simple text prompts and share them through a scrollable feed. The app is built on technology from Gizmo, a vibe-coding platform Meta acqui-hired earlier in the year, and Meta is shutting down the original Gizmo app. Pocket joins Meta's growing roster of standalone AI apps alongside Vibes, Instagram Instants and a Meta AI Mac app. The pattern suggests Meta is testing consumer AI surfaces outside its core social products.
