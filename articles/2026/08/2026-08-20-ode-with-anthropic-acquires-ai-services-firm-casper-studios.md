# 'Ode with Anthropic' Acquires AI Services Firm Casper Studios

Enterprise AI-transformation firm Ode with Anthropic acquired Casper Studios, an AI services firm that helps companies deploy Anthropic's Claude inside existing systems. The deal pairs Ode's custom AI engineering with Casper's implementation practice, with both firms building in the same Claude Code environment. Executives cited a shared client, Sphera, where an Ode-built internal tool reportedly cut operational bottlenecks by 70%. This is a vendor-distributed press release and deal terms were not disclosed.
