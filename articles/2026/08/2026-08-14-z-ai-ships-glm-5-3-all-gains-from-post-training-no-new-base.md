# Z.ai Ships GLM-5.3 — All Gains From Post-Training, No New Base Model

GLM-5.3 reuses the 743B-parameter GLM-5.2 base with every gain from scaled post-training. Terminal-Bench 3.0 rises from 4.6 to 28.3; CyberGym reaches 84.5%, edging GPT-5.6 Sol. Z.ai flagged the cybersecurity gain as unplanned and is withholding open weights for ~2 weeks for safety evaluation. Benchmarks are vendor-reported. The broader signal: post-training is now the primary performance lever for established base models, with cost-structure implications for organizations deciding whether to retrain or refine.
