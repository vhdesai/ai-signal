# Cognition reportedly seeks a new round at a $40 billion valuation

Cognition, the maker of the Devin AI coding agent, is reportedly in talks to raise again at a valuation of at least $40 billion after raising $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation in May. TechCrunch noted that the valuation discussion is tied to a reported $1 billion annualized revenue run rate, up from $492 million three months earlier. The signal for executives is that AI coding agents are moving from pilot budgets into enterprise-scale spend, but valuations now assume very rapid conversion of usage into durable revenue.
