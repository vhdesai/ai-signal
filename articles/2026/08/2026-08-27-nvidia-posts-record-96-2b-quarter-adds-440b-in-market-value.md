# Nvidia Posts Record $96.2B Quarter, Adds ~$440B in Market Value

Nvidia reported fiscal Q2 2027 revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% year over year, with data center revenue of $89.0 billion and Q3 guidance of $108.0 billion. Shares rose roughly 8.7%, adding about $440 billion in market capitalization — among the largest single-day gains on record — and breaking a four-quarter streak of post-earnings declines. CEO Jensen Huang argued AI has "reached its inflection point," citing an expanding base of customers requiring large GPU clusters. For planning purposes, the guidance implies the AI capex cycle has visible runway well into 2027 rather than a near-term plateau.
