# AWS confirms GPT-5.6 price cuts in Bedrock weekly roundup

AWS confirmed that GPT-5.6 Luna pricing in Amazon Bedrock was cut by 80%, with GPT-5.6 Terra also reduced by 20%. The roundup frames Luna as one of the most affordable frontier-class models available through Bedrock. This is a direct enterprise-procurement signal: model price compression is now showing up inside major cloud distribution channels, not only on model-provider websites. INFRASTRUCTUREAI CLOUDAPAC
