# Meta releases Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weight agentic model that runs on a single consumer GPU

Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter multimodal model distilled from its Muse Spark series and licensed under Apache 2.0. It fits within a 24 GB GPU and shows roughly a 3.1x speedup on an RTX 5090, aimed at always-on local agent workflows via llama.cpp and Ollama. Zuckerberg paired the launch with a push in Washington for lighter open-source AI rules, citing Chinese rivals, and added an independent-director safety sign-off.
