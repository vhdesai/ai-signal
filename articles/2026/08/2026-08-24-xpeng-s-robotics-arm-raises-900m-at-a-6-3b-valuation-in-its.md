# XPeng’s robotics arm raises $900M+ at a $6.3B valuation in its first outside round

XPeng’s robotics unit closed a first external round of more than $900 million at a $6.3 billion post-money valuation, led by IDG and Gaorong Ventures with strategic participation from Tencent and Alibaba. It is the largest single financing announced by a Chinese humanoid-robot spinout to date, following the unit’s $100 million Series A in 2022. Embodied AI is now attracting late-stage-scale capital on pre-revenue timelines.
