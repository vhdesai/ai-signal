# Anthropic's Text Watermark Faces Quality and Durability Questions

Commentary published overnight argues that Anthropic's watermarking approach — biasing token probabilities between semantically equivalent word choices to embed a detectable fingerprint — cannot be entirely quality-neutral, despite the company's claim of no impact on output. Anthropic has said the mark is sparse in code and factual text and disappears after a full rewrite. The unresolved question for enterprises is evidentiary weight: a probabilistic signal that degrades under editing is weak grounds for policy enforcement. For organizations building AI-usage governance policies, this analysis suggests watermark detection should be treated as an indicator rather than proof — and that compliance frameworks should not rely solely on watermark presence to determine whether content was AI-generated.
