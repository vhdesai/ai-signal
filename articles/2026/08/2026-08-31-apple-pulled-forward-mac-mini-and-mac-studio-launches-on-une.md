# Apple pulled forward Mac mini and Mac Studio launches on unexpected enterprise AI demand

Apple's off-cycle Mac mini (M6/M5 Pro) and Mac Studio refresh was driven by enterprise appetite for local AI compute that exhausted configurations earlier than planned, with AI companies reportedly ordering in the tens of thousands. Unified memory makes these machines viable hosts for local agentic workloads.
