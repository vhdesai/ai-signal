# Apple's camera-equipped AirPods surface with "Visual Intelligence"

A demo video found in the macOS 26.7 release candidate revealed Apple's long-rumored camera-equipped AirPods, which use Visual Intelligence to answer questions about the wearer's surroundings but cannot save photos or video.
