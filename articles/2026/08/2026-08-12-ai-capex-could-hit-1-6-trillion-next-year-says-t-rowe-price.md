# AI Capex Could Hit $1.6 Trillion Next Year, Says T. Rowe Price Fund Manager

A T. Rowe Price fund manager projects AI capital expenditure could reach $1.6 trillion in 2027, seeing more parallels to 1998's internet buildout than the dot-com bust. The analysis argues hyperscalers can comfortably fund their investment spending and derive high returns on invested capital with a short payback schedule.
