# Federal Judge Nullifies Pentagon's Supply-Chain-Risk Designation of Anthropic

A federal judge ruled the Defense Department unlawfully retaliated against Anthropic by designating it a supply-chain risk after the company refused an open-ended contract that would have overridden its restrictions on lethal autonomous weapons and domestic mass surveillance. The designation was blocked as illegal and unsupported. Separate litigation continues. The ruling gives frontier labs meaningful legal backing when resisting government demands that cross stated safety boundaries.
