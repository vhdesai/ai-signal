# Stripe Finalizes $7B+ Acquisition of AI Gateway OpenRouter

Stripe has agreed to acquire OpenRouter, the routing layer that gives developers a single access point across model providers, for more than $7 billion — a steep markup on the roughly $1.3 billion valuation reported in May. OpenRouter has positioned itself as "Stripe for AI," aggregating model access and reducing provider lock-in. The logic is metering and billing: whoever routes tokens can price, bundle, and reconcile them. It also places a payments company between enterprises and frontier labs at the exact point where switching costs are set. For enterprises building multi-model architectures, the acquisition creates a single vendor that handles both inference routing and financial settlement — a powerful lock-in position if it becomes the default integration point.
