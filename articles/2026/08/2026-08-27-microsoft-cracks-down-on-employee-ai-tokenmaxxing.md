# Microsoft cracks down on employee AI “tokenmaxxing”

Microsoft is reportedly tightening internal rules on AI compute spending after employees ran up large bills — one worker allegedly spent about $28,000 in 28 days — in a practice being called tokenmaxxing. The story highlights how surging internal AI usage creates novel cost-governance challenges even inside hyperscalers. Expect similar internal chargeback and quota policies to spread across large enterprises.
