# Navigating the AI era with a CMU focus on critical thinking

CMU instructors across disciplines have redesigned courses to incorporate AI as a teaching instrument rather than a shortcut, aiming to build curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking. The piece surveys how faculty are adapting pedagogy and assessment in an environment where generative AI is universally available to students. It is a useful read on how technical talent pipelines are being reshaped upstream.
