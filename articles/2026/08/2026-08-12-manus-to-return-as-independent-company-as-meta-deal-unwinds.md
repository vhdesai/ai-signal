# Manus to Return as Independent Company as Meta Deal Unwinds

AI agent startup Manus will soon return to operating as an independent company after its acquisition deal with Meta unwinds. The reversal is notable given Meta’s aggressive push into AI acquisitions and suggests that integrating autonomous agent startups into large platform companies remains challenging.
