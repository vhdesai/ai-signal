# U.S. labs cut model prices as low-cost Chinese competitors gain enterprise share

OpenAI and Anthropic are lowering prices on selected models as DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and other low-cost Chinese providers win workloads from companies managing large inference bills. OpenAI cut pricing on GPT-5.6 Luna substantially, and Anthropic positioned Claude Opus 5 at roughly half the price of its higher-end tier. FT data indicates prices paid for leading U.S. models have fallen materially since mid-July. The competitive question is shifting from benchmark leadership to useful work per dollar of inference — which compresses margins fastest for labs carrying the heaviest fixed costs.
