# Trump Administration's AI Self-Regulatory EO Has Stalled

The Trump administration internally circulated a draft executive order calling for a self-regulatory organization for frontier AI companies, but the effort has stalled amid interagency disagreements. No federal AI oversight framework is imminent. The gap leaves state-level regulation — California's SB 53, New York's RAISE Act — as the de facto compliance standard for frontier-model developers.
