# Alibaba releases HappyShrimp 1.0, an end-to-end AI music generation model

Alibaba introduced HappyShrimp 1.0, a model built for end-to-end song generation that the company describes as "understanding music as a unique language" rather than stitching together separate composition and vocal stages. The release extends Alibaba's push beyond text and vision models into generative audio. It lands as Chinese labs continue to broaden their open and commercial model portfolios across modalities.
