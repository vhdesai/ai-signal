# Hugging Face Draws M&A Interest

Business Insider reports that Hugging Face, the open-source AI model hosting platform and community hub, is drawing M&A interest from potential acquirers. The report comes as the AI infrastructure layer — from model marketplaces (OpenRouter/Stripe) to hosting platforms — has become a hot acquisition target. Hugging Face has become central to the open-source AI ecosystem and model distribution.
