# DeepSeek's V4-Flash is the cheapest well-known model to run

DeepSeek unveiled V4-Flash, which research firm Artificial Analysis measured as the cheapest well-known model to operate, at roughly $0.14 per million input tokens and $0.28 per million output tokens, while remaining competitive on intelligence benchmarks. That pricing sits far below premium frontier models and continues to reset expectations on inference cost. The launch intensifies price pressure on both U.S. labs and rival Chinese providers.
