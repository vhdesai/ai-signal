# Wall Street Counts on Nvidia to Keep the AI Party Going — Markets Brace Ahead of Earnings

Nvidia reports Q2 FY2027 results on Wednesday carrying an approximately $5T market capitalization. It has been the largest single contributor to S&P 500 earnings growth in seven of the past eleven quarters. US equity futures and Asian AI-linked names traded lower Monday as AI-related stocks came under pressure on rising-cost concerns. Korean retail and institutional investors net sold approximately $2.63B of Nvidia stock, reflecting cooling enthusiasm. Analyst attention centers on commentary about the Rubin ramp rather than the headline beat.
