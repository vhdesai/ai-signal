# LTX-2.5 Launches as an Open-Weights, Nvidia-Accelerated World Model

LTX released LTX-2.5, an open-weights video and world model that generates a 10-second clip from an image in roughly 6.8 seconds on Nvidia superchip hardware, with day-one ComfyUI support and weights on Hugging Face. Because the weights are open, iteration can run on a company's own hardware with no per-generation billing and no content leaving its systems.
