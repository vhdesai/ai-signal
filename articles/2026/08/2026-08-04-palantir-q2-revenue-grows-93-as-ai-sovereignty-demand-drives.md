# Palantir Q2 Revenue Grows 93% as "AI Sovereignty" Demand Drives 29% Stock Surge

Palantir reported second-quarter revenue of $1.94 billion, up 93% year over year and ahead of LSEG estimates of $1.8 billion, with commercial revenue up 149% to $764 million and government revenue up 90% to $809 million. The company raised full-year guidance to $8.15–$8.158 billion and shares rose 29.5%, narrowly missing their best single day ever. CEO Alex Karp attributed the quarter to enterprises seeking to keep proprietary data out of frontier labs — a positioning Citi analysts said "further weakens the bear case around rising AI competition." The read-through for buyers: data-sovereignty constraints are now a procurement driver, not a compliance footnote. ________________________________ EARNINGS
