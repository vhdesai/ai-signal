# Alibaba Plunges After $10.2B Placement to Fund AI Push

Alibaba priced HK$80B (~$10.2B) in the largest primary follow-on ever by a HK-listed company, stock fell ~10–11%. Earmarks AI buildout at the midpoint of a ~$56B three-year plan. Chairman Tsai and CEO Wu bought ~$15M personally post-drop. Fastest cloud/AI revenue growth in 22 quarters, but the market is now pricing dilution risk into AI capex narratives. CNBC FUNDING
