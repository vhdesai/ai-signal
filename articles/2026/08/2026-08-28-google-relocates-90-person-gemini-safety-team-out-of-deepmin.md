# Google Relocates ~90-Person Gemini Safety Team Out of DeepMind into Global Affairs

Google is moving the unit that vets Gemini for catastrophic risks — including chemical and biological misuse, with authority to veto releases — into its public policy and lobbying arm beginning in September. Reporting indicates some researchers were blocked from staying with the team. Critics argue the move weakens independent oversight; primary reporting broke Aug 27, with coverage continuing into the window.
