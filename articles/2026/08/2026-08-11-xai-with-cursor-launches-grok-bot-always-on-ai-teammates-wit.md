# xAI, with Cursor, launches Grok Bot: always-on AI teammates with their own cloud computers

xAI shipped Grok Bot in beta, introducing persistent AI agents that each receive their own cloud computer, sign into a user's existing applications, and complete multi-step jobs unsupervised, returning only for approvals. Bots learn workflows by observing a task once and can coordinate with one another. Access is bundled into SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra and Cursor Teams Premium, reflecting the xAI-Cursor partnership, and is available on desktop, including Linux, and iOS.
