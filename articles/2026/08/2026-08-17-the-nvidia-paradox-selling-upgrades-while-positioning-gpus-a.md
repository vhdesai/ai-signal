# The Nvidia Paradox: Selling Upgrades While Positioning GPUs as Long-Lived Assets

Nvidia faces a strategic tension: it wants customers to buy newest-generation chips every year while simultaneously pitching GPUs as an investable asset class with long-term value. The OpenAI/SB Energy deal will fill 8 GW of data center space exclusively with Nvidia's next-gen chips — CEO Jensen Huang said this could yield $150–$200 billion in revenue per hardware generation. Vera Rubin server racks ship this fall at ~$7M each, with demand concentrated among a handful of cloud giants, neoclouds (CoreWeave, Nebius, Nscale, Firmus), and AI labs. However, analysts warn of a potential GPU glut due to data center construction holdups, and some investors are concerned the spending spree could accumulate too much debt before profits materialize.
