# ByteDance Reportedly Pre-Training a 10-Trillion-Parameter Model

ByteDance is pre-training a model with as many as 10 trillion parameters — roughly three times the reported scale of Moonshot's Kimi K3 and approaching estimates for Anthropic's Mythos systems. The model is early in pre-training, a phase that typically runs three to six months before fine-tuning, with a possible year-end target. Reporting indicates founder Zhang Yiming directed teams to pursue genuine capability rather than short-term distillation of rival models. If completed on that timeline, it would be the most ambitious scale-up yet from a Chinese lab.
