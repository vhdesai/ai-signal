# Nvidia in talks with Korean inference-chip designer Rebellions

Nvidia has opened preliminary talks with Rebellions spanning a technical partnership, an equity investment, or an outright acquisition, with Jensen Huang meeting the company’s CEO in Santa Clara this week. Rebellions, valued near $2.3B, is itself preparing a Korean IPO. Any transaction would draw both DOJ and Korean regulatory scrutiny given Nvidia’s position in AI accelerators.
