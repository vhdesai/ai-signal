# EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Obligations Now Enforceable

The EU began enforcing Article 50 of the AI Act as of August 2, requiring providers to label realistic AI-generated or AI-manipulated images, audio and video, and to disclose when users are interacting with an AI system rather than a human. Obligations extend to biometric and emotion-inference systems, and general-purpose model providers must now maintain technical documentation, publish training-data summaries and adopt copyright compliance policies. Penalties reach €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover; private communications and clearly artistic or satirical content are exempt. Scope and enforcement consistency remain the open questions for multinationals. ________________________________ SECURITY
