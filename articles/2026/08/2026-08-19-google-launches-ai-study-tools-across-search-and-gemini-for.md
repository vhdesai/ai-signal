# Google Launches AI Study Tools Across Search and Gemini for Back-to-School

Google released new study features across Search and Gemini aimed at students, marking its latest push to make Gemini the go-to AI assistant for learning — competing directly with OpenAI's newly launched ChatGPT for Teens. The launch continues the education vertical battle between Google, OpenAI, and Apple (with Siri AI expected later this year). 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/google-launches-new-study-tools-for-students-across-search-and-gemini/
