# Anthropic relaxes Claude Fable 5's biology guardrails while warning of bioweapon risk

Anthropic retuned Claude Fable 5's biology safety classifier to cut unnecessary refusals—reportedly by roughly 85%—so the model will answer legitimate biology questions it previously blocked, while keeping virology, toxicology, and molecular-design queries restricted. The company simultaneously reiterated warnings about bioweapon uplift risk from frontier models. It is a pointed attempt to balance research usefulness against catastrophic-misuse concerns, and how Anthropic tunes that line will be watched closely by regulators and rivals alike.
