# Cerebras raises full-year guidance but shares fall ~14%

In only its second report since its May IPO, Cerebras lifted full-year guidance to roughly $880–890 million, yet shares dropped about 14% after hours. The disconnect reflects how much growth is already priced into AI-chip challengers competing against Nvidia's entrenched position.
