# Hugging Face is reportedly fielding M&A interest at a valuation of at least $13 billion

Business Insider reported that Hugging Face has been fielding acquisition interest for a deal worth at least $13 billion; Reuters also surfaced the report. The interest reflects Hugging Face's role as a neutral distribution, collaboration, and model-hosting layer across the AI ecosystem. Strategically, this is another signal that AI infrastructure value is shifting toward developer platforms and marketplaces, not only foundation-model labs.
