# OpenAI annualized revenue tops $40B ahead of IPO

OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate has passed $40B, roughly doubling since the end of 2025 on the strength of coding tools, consumer subscriptions, and advertising. The figure lands as the company prepares for public markets and reshuffles its go-to-market leadership, including the appointment of a new Chief Revenue Officer. Analysts have been quick to note that a run rate is not booked annual revenue, and that executive turnover plus an unresolved internal security incident complicate the IPO narrative. The signal for buyers: OpenAI is being priced as an operating company now, so procurement leverage increasingly rests on revenue quality and support depth, not model benchmarks. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-annualized-revenue-tops-40-111122841.html
