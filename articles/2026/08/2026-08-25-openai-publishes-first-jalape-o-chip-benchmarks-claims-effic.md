# OpenAI Publishes First "Jalapeño" Chip Benchmarks, Claims Efficiency Lead Over Nvidia Blackwell

OpenAI released the first performance results for Jalapeño, its custom LLM inference processor co-developed with Broadcom, claiming up to 1.9× more AI work per watt and materially lower latency than Nvidia's Blackwell-generation parts. Results were presented at Hot Chips using SemiAnalysis's InferenceX benchmark. The timing—hours before Nvidia's quarterly report—signals that OpenAI intends to shift a meaningful share of inference off merchant silicon. For infrastructure buyers, inference economics, not training capacity, is becoming the contested layer.
