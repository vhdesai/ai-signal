# Nvidia Denies Report It Will Ship a China-Specific AI Chip by Year-End

The Information reported that Nvidia planned small-volume shipments of an inference-oriented AI chip designed for Chinese customers by the end of 2026, citing two employees. Nvidia publicly rejected the account the same day, stating no China-specific part of that description is on its roadmap. The dispute sets expectations for whether Nvidia can re-enter a market it has largely been excluded from, and for how inference-class silicon is treated under export controls.
