# TSMC July Revenue Surges ~45% Year Over Year on Sustained AI Chip Demand

TSMC reported roughly 45% year-over-year growth in July revenue, extending a streak driven by AI accelerator and advanced-packaging demand from customers including Nvidia and Google. As the sole leading-edge foundry for most frontier AI silicon, TSMC's monthly figures are the cleanest near-real-time read on whether AI capex is converting into shipped hardware. The number undercuts the "AI demand is cooling" thesis that pressured semiconductor equities earlier this quarter.
