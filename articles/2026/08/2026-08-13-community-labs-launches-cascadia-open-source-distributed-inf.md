# Community Labs launches Cascadia, open-source distributed inference for Intel hardware

Cascadia is a new open-source runtime that pools the resources of Intel-powered machines, allowing them to run models larger than any single machine could serve. The project targets cost-efficient, hardware-agnostic large-model serving by aggregating commodity Intel CPUs and GPUs instead of relying on a single accelerator node. It is a direct attack on the inference cost curve at a moment when serving economics, not training, increasingly determine AI margins.
