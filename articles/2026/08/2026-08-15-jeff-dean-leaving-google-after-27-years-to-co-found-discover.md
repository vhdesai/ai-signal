# Jeff Dean Leaving Google After 27 Years to Co-Found “Discovery Loop”

Google chief scientist Jeff Dean is departing to co-found Discovery Loop, a public-benefit corporation focused on automating the machine-learning research cycle. The move accompanies a broader Alphabet AI leadership reshuffle, with Demis Hassabis stepping back from day-to-day DeepMind operations. Freshness caveat: the article is dated Aug 15 but recaps an announcement made Aug 5.
