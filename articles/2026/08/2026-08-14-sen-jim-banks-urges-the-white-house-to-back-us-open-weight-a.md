# Sen. Jim Banks urges the White House to back US open-weight AI models

Republican Senator Jim Banks released a letter pressing the administration to create incentives for US open-weight model development. The argument centers on ceding the open-weights ecosystem to Chinese labs. It lands the same week Chinese open models posted frontier-level benchmark results.
