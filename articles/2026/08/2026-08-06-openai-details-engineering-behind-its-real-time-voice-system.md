# OpenAI Details Engineering Behind Its Real-Time Voice System, GPT Live

OpenAI published an engineering account of how it built GPT Live, its low-latency, continuous-interaction real-time voice AI system, in roughly six months. The post focuses on systems-design tradeoffs for responsive voice interaction rather than new capability claims. Real-time voice remains one of the highest-stakes product battlegrounds among frontier labs, signaling continued OpenAI investment in multimodal infrastructure.
