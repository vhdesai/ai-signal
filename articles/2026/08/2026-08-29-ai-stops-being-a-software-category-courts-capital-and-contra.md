# AI Stops Being a Software Category: Courts, Capital, and Contracts Redraw the Map

The last 24 hours produced almost no new model weights and a great deal of new structure. A federal judge nullified the Pentagon's supply-chain-risk designation of Anthropic, giving frontier labs their first real legal footing to enforce use restrictions against a government customer. OpenAI moved to terminate Cursor's model access following SpaceX's acquisition, converting a distribution dispute into an explicit trust-and-counterparty test. Meanwhile Nvidia quietly stepped back from the revenue-sharing financing that underwrites second-tier AI clouds, and a16z closed $1.1B for hardware — two signals that the binding constraint has moved below the application layer.
