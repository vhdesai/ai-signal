# Silver Lake-Workday Talks Signal Potential End to AI-Driven SaaS Sell-off

Silver Lake's talks to acquire Workday sent the stock up 19% on Thursday, temporarily lifting the broader SaaS sector before most gains reversed on Friday. The Information notes the SaaS landscape remains murky: Atlassian's cloud growth is soaring and Palantir reports "scorching revenue gains," but IBM crashed on declining mainframe sales as customers shift spending to AI. UBS analyst Karl Keirstead warns that blue-chip executives want to cut traditional software spending "30% over the next three years" as AI alternatives improve.
