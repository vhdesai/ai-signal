# Dyna Robotics Unveils DYNA-2, Claiming the First Scaling Law in Robotics from Human Video

Dyna Robotics introduced DYNA-2, a world-action model pre-trained on more than one million hours of egocentric human video — roughly 170 years of continuous waking experience. The company reports that performance on unseen robot data scaled predictably across four orders of magnitude of human data, with cross-embodiment transfer and 87% zero-shot quality at new deployment sites. If the scaling claim holds under independent replication, it materially changes the data economics of robotics by decoupling progress from expensive teleoperation datasets. PRICING
