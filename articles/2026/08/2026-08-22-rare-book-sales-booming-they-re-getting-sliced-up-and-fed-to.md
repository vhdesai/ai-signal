# Rare-Book Sales Booming — They’re Getting Sliced Up and Fed to AI

WSJ reports on the booming rare-book market driven by AI companies purchasing physical books, cutting them apart, and scanning them for model training. The story extends earlier reporting on Anthropic’s scanning practices and shows how the AI training data pipeline is affecting even the antiquarian book market.
