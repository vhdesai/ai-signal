# Marvell Lands Google Custom AI Chip Deal With Up to $12.2 Billion Share Purchase Right

Marvell disclosed a commercial agreement with Google covering custom AI silicon, with terms allowing Google to purchase up to $12.2 billion in Marvell shares; the stock rose roughly 10%. Hyperscalers are structurally diversifying away from merchant GPUs and are willing to use equity to lock in supply. M&A
