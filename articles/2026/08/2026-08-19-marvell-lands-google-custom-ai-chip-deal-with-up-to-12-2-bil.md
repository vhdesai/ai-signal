# Marvell Lands Google Custom AI Chip Deal With Up to $12.2 Billion Share Purchase Right

Marvell disclosed a commercial agreement with Google covering custom AI silicon, with terms allowing Google to purchase up to $12.2 billion in Marvell shares; the stock rose roughly 10%. The deal gives Marvell custom-accelerator relationships across all three major US hyperscalers. It is another signal that hyperscalers are structurally diversifying away from merchant GPUs on cost and efficiency grounds, and are willing to use equity to lock in supply.
