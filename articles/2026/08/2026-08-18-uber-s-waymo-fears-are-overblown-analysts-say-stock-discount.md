# Uber's Waymo Fears Are Overblown — Analysts Say Stock Discount Makes No Sense

Uber stock has dropped 8% this year on fears of Waymo competition, but The Information's analysis argues sellers are overreacting. Uber trades at 2.6x next year's revenue vs. the S&P 500 average of 4x — a steep discount that "makes no sense" given the company can sustain rapid growth while staying competitive in autonomous driving. The analysis suggests the market is undervaluing Uber's food-delivery business, rising margins, and free cash flow generation.
