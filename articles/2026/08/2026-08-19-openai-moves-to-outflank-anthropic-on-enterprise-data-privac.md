# OpenAI Moves to Outflank Anthropic on Enterprise Data Privacy

OpenAI introduced "Private Safety Processing" — automated monitoring across multiple sessions that retains zero customer data — directly countering Anthropic's mandatory 30-day data retention for Mythos-class models. The move reframes privacy commitments as a primary differentiator in enterprise procurement rather than a compliance checkbox. Enterprises negotiating renewals in the next two quarters should expect materially better terms than 2025 baselines.
