# Nvidia denies report that it will ship a China-specific AI chip by year-end

The Information reported that Nvidia planned to begin shipping small volumes of an AI chip designed for Chinese customers by year-end, citing two employees. Nvidia publicly denied the account, stating no China-specific inference processor is on its roadmap. The contradiction matters for supply planning and for reading the current state of US export policy — treat single-sourced China roadmap reporting with caution.
