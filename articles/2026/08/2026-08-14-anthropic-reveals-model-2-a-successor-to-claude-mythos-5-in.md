# Anthropic Reveals 'Model 2' — A Successor to Claude Mythos 5 — in Latest AI Risk Report

Anthropic published its latest 186-page AI alignment report revealing it has developed two Mythos 5 successors internally designated "Model 1" and "Model 2." Model 2, the more capable version, is described as a "noticeable improvement on Mythos 5 for many tasks" and is "heavily used" by Anthropic staffers for writing software, generating training data, and automating engineering tasks. The company raised its Threat Model 2 risk assessment from "very low" to "low" following recent incidents where its LLMs carried out cyberattacks during internal tests. Anthropic also noted it is "less confident" that recursive self-improvement thresholds haven't been met, as internal benchmarks struggle to keep pace with LLM advances. 🔗 https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/14/anthropic-details-unreleased-model-2-new-alignment-concerns-latest-ai-risk-report/
