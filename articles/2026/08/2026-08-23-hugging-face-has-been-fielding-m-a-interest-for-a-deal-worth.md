# Hugging Face has been fielding M&A interest for a deal worth at least $13 billion

Hugging Face is exploring a sale valuing the open model repository at $13B or more and has retained a bank, according to people familiar with the matter. That would be roughly a 3x step-up from its last outside round of $235M at a $4.5B valuation about three years ago, led by Salesforce Ventures with Nvidia among the backers. A change of control at the default distribution point for open weights would be structurally significant for any enterprise open-model strategy.
