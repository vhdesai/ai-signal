# Stanford Runs 37,000 AI Agents as Virtual Biotech — Drug Design Confirmed by Merck

Stanford researchers operated a simulation of 37,000 coordinated AI agents functioning as a virtual biotechnology company. One of the system's drug candidate designs was independently confirmed as viable by Merck.
