# Alibaba plans to charge the largest users of its “open” Qwen model

Alibaba plans to charge its largest commercial users for its next open-source Qwen model through a revenue-sharing arrangement, borrowing a monetization tactic from rival Moonshot, The Next Web reported. The shift complicates the “open” positioning that made Qwen popular with developers. It signals Chinese labs increasingly looking to convert open-weight adoption into revenue.
