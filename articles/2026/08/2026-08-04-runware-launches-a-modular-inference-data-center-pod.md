# Runware launches a modular inference data-center pod

Runware unveiled Sonic Inference Pod, a self-contained modular data center designed to be deployed where power and cooling are available. Portable AI infrastructure is a direct response to the grid-interconnection bottlenecks now surfacing in Texas and other markets.
