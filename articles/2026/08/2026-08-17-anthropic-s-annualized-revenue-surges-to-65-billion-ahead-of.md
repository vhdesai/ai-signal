# Anthropic's Annualized Revenue Surges to $65 Billion Ahead of IPO

Anthropic's annualized revenue run-rate reached roughly $65 billion in July, with growth concentrated in enterprise API and coding workloads rather than consumer subscriptions. The trajectory reduces near-term vendor-viability risk in multi-year commitments but increases the likelihood of pricing and packaging changes as the company optimizes for public-market margins ahead of a potential IPO.
