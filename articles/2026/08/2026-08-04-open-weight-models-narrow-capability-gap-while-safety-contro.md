# Open-weight models narrow capability gap while safety controls lag

TechCrunch reported on SaferAI findings that China's Z.ai GLM-5.2 open-weight model is approaching frontier labs on cyber and bio capabilities while refusing none of the tested offensive cyber or dual-use biology tasks. The finding sharpens the policy debate around open weights, model access, and safety controls.
