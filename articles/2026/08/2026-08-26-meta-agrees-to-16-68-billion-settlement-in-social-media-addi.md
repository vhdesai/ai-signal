# Meta Agrees to $16.68 Billion Settlement in Social Media Addiction Case

Meta settled a landmark lawsuit brought by 29 U.S. states alleging its platforms were designed to addict children and that the company misled the public about associated harms. The settlement, disclosed in court filings, is among the largest consumer-protection resolutions in the sector. For technology leaders, it sets a materially higher liability benchmark for engagement-optimizing recommendation systems — the same class of algorithmic design now being extended by generative AI.
