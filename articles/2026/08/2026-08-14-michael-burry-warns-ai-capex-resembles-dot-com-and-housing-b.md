# Michael Burry Warns AI Capex Resembles Dot-Com and Housing Bubbles, Sets '2028 Clock'

"Big Short" investor Michael Burry escalated his AI bubble warnings, drawing parallels to early 2000 tech circular revenue, 2007 housing market denial, and Enron's off-balance-sheet structures. He noted that today's private AI companies carry "100–1000x the valuations" of dot-com era startups and that current capex levels are "orders of magnitude more capital flowing to the buildout than value of the use case." Burry set a base case of 2028 "when compute becomes too much," noting that "history says markets catch on well before that sort of turn." He has shorted several AI stocks this year. 🔗 https://www.businessinsider.com/michael-burry-ai-tech-stocks-euphoria-internet-housing-bubbles-enron-2026-8
