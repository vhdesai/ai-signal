# Nvidia invests $3.5 billion in MediaTek and deepens its AI infrastructure partnership

Nvidia and MediaTek expanded their collaboration across custom AI infrastructure, local AI computing, and automotive platforms, anchored by Nvidia's $3.5 billion investment in MediaTek convertible bonds. MediaTek will adopt Nvidia's NVLink Fusion platform to help hyperscalers build custom XPUs connected into Nvidia rack-scale AI factories. The move pushes Nvidia further from accelerator supplier toward chip-to-rack system architect.
