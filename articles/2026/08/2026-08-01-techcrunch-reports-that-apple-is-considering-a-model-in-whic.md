# TechCrunch reports that Apple is considering a model in which advanced Siri AI capabilities could be tied to paid…

TechCrunch reports that Apple is considering a model in which advanced Siri AI capabilities could be tied to paid iCloud+ tiers or incremental compute purchases. That would mark a shift from Apple's traditional bundled-software posture toward usage-tiered AI monetization. The implication is that consumer AI economics may increasingly resemble cloud-service metering, even inside tightly integrated device ecosystems.
