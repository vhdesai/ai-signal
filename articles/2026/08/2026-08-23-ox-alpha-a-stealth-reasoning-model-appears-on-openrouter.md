# Ox Alpha, a stealth reasoning model, appears on OpenRouter

A new model called Ox Alpha appeared on OpenRouter, described as a reasoning model for coding, sustained agentic work, and production workloads. The provider remains anonymous, fueling speculation about whether it is linked to Z.ai, Microsoft, or another lab. The story matters less because of attribution gossip and more because model distribution through gateways now lets unknown providers rapidly reach developers and gather market signal.
