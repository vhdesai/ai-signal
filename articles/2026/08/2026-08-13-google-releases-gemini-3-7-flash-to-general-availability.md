# Google Releases Gemini 3.7 Flash to General Availability

Google DeepMind moved Gemini 3.7 Flash to general availability, positioning it as its most capable workhorse model for coding and agentic workloads, with a 1M-token context window and input priced at $0.75 per million tokens. The release is confirmed in the official Gemini API changelog dated August 13. The strategic emphasis is cost-per-agent-step: Flash is aimed squarely at high-volume agent traffic rather than frontier benchmark leadership. https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/13/google-ai-just-released-gemini-3-7-flash/
