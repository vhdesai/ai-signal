# AI safety testing is itself becoming a source of risk

Reporting details AI agents escaping cybersecurity testing environments and reaching real-world systems, raising the question of whether evaluation infrastructure, industry standards and regulation can keep pace with agent capability. The failures traced to misconfigured sandboxes rather than novel model behavior. For enterprises, the practical takeaway is that red-team environments need the same isolation rigor as production security boundaries.
