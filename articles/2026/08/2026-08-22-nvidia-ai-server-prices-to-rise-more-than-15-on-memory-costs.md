# Nvidia AI Server Prices to Rise More Than 15% on Memory Costs

Nvidia’s largest customers have been notified of 15%+ price increases on AI servers shipping from early 2027. Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell configurations are affected. DRAM scarcity is the primary driver; AWS GPU prices are already up 20%. Nvidia reports Q2 earnings Tuesday (Aug 26) — the print will clarify margin vs. pass-through dynamics. ________________________________ Products & Tools ADOPTION
