# Amazon Triples Nvidia GPU Order — Adding 2M Chips Including Rubin Ultra

Amazon added 2M+ Nvidia GPUs (Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, Rubin Ultra) for 2027–2028, tripling its original order — worth tens of billions. The deal extends to Nvidia networking, CPUs, open models, and full robotics stack for Amazon warehouses. Amazon’s own Trainium chip business crossed $25B ARR. Nvidia reported Q2 revenue of $96.2B (data center $89B, +117% YoY), guided Q3 to $108B, and committed $279B to secure supply/manufacturing. ________________________________ INDUSTRY ANTHROPIC INFRASTRUCTURE
