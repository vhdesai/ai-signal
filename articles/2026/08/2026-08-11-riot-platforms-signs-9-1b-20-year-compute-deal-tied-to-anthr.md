# Riot Platforms Signs $9.1B, 20-Year Compute Deal Tied to Anthropic

Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms disclosed a 20-year agreement worth approximately $9.1 billion in total contract revenue to supply roughly 191 megawatts of capacity from its Rockdale, Texas campus to a frontier AI lab identified in reporting as Anthropic, with phased deployment through 2027 and 2028. Riot shares rose sharply on the disclosure. The deal reinforces a durable pattern: grid interconnects, substations and cooling at mining campuses are now the scarce asset, and converting them is faster than greenfield construction. thenextweb.com — Anthropic signs a $9.1bn, 20-year cloud deal with Riot Platforms
