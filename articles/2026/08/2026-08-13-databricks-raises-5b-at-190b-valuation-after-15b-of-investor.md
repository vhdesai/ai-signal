# Databricks Raises $5B at $190B Valuation After $15B of Investor Demand

Databricks closed a $5B round led by Coatue, Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price, and Sixth Street Growth — after fielding $15B in demand when CEO Ali Ghodsi originally sought only $1B. The company has hit $7B in annualized revenue growing at 80% YoY and is cash-flow positive. Its AI agent database Lakebase reached $100M ARR just over a year after launch.
