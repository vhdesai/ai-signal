# Caterpillar Applies Its Mine-Automation Playbook to Enterprise AI Rollout

Caterpillar is transferring decades of autonomous-mining experience into enterprise AI governance — constrained autonomy, heavy instrumentation, and staged trust expansion rather than broad agent latitude. CTO Jaime Mineart described a Cat AI Assistant used by technicians for voice-driven repair, drawing on 1.6 million connected assets and 16+ PB of structured data. The company plans $100M over five years on AI/robotics training for 118,000 employees. Its power-generation division, driven by data center demand, grew 72% to $3.10B in a record quarter.
