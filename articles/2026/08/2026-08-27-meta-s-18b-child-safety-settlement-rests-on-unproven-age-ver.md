# Meta's $18B child-safety settlement rests on unproven age-verification technology

Meta agreed to sweeping restrictions on minors' access to its apps as part of a multi-state settlement, but reporting notes the commitments depend on age-verification technology that does not yet work reliably at scale. The gap between the remedy on paper and the technical means to deliver it is the central issue. Expect this to become the template — and the test case — for enforcing platform obligations that outrun available tooling. SAFETY
