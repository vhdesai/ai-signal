# Model ML uses GPT-5.6 Sol for finance analysis and editable decks

OpenAI highlighted Model ML's use of GPT-5.6 Sol to carry finance work from research and analysis into editable, traceable PowerPoint decks and Excel workbooks. The case study shows where enterprise AI products are heading: not just chat outputs, but full workflow artifacts that remain auditable and editable in standard business tools. This aligns with the broader shift toward AI systems that generate business deliverables rather than just recommendations.
