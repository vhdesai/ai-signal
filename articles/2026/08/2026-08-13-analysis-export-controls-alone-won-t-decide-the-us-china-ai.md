# Analysis: export controls alone won't decide the US–China AI race

Harvard's Bruno Sergi and economist Kevin Chen argue export controls "do not constitute an innovation strategy," pointing to the CHIPS Act's $52.7B, more than $770B in announced US semiconductor investment since 2020, and 600-plus Chinese universities now offering AI degrees. Their conclusion is that talent pipelines, sustained R&D and allied coordination will determine leadership more than unilateral restriction.
