# OpenAI asks judge to dismiss Apple's trade-secrets lawsuit

OpenAI filed a motion to throw out Apple's suit accusing it of stealing trade secrets, calling the allegations meritless. The Verge reports OpenAI went further, describing the complaint as rotten to its core. The filing escalates a high-profile legal clash between two of the most important players in consumer AI distribution. The outcome could shape how aggressively large platforms police researcher and executive mobility.
