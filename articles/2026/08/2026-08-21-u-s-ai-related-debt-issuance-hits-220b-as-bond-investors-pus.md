# U.S. AI-related debt issuance hits ~$220B as bond investors push back

Reuters analysis puts 2026 U.S. corporate AI-related debt issuance near $220B, up from about $12.5B a year earlier, with investors demanding wider spreads and larger concessions — including on Amazon's recent $25B deal — as some large buyers approach internal exposure limits. Broadcom is separately reported in talks for a $60B–$80B financing package tied to AI chip commitments. The AI buildout is now large enough that its funding costs register in credit markets and Treasury yields, not just in capex lines. FUNDING
