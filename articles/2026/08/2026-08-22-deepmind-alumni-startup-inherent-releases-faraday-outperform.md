# DeepMind Alumni Startup Inherent Releases 'Faraday' — Outperforms Claude and GPT-5.5 at Replicating Research

London-based Inherent released Faraday, an AI agent that outperformed Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at independently reproducing scientific paper findings — despite running on Qwen 3.6 (27B parameters). The key: reinforcement learning that rewards “research taste” rather than rule-following. Faraday uses GPT-5.5 Codex for coding (like scientists using existing tools). The $50M-seeded company plans to grow to 25 employees, with London’s DeepMind talent exodus providing a hiring tailwind. Industry News INDUSTRY EDUCATION
