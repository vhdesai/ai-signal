# Rising Server Prices Shift Leverage from Nvidia to Samsung and SK hynix

The same memory shortage driving Nvidia's price increases is strengthening the negotiating position of its suppliers. Samsung and SK hynix are gaining pricing power as demand for HBM and server DRAM outpaces supply. The dynamic complicates the assumption that Nvidia captures the majority of AI hardware economics. For buyers, supply risk now sits with a second, more concentrated chokepoint upstream of the GPU.
