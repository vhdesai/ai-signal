# EU AI Act Moves from Rulebook to Real Enforcement

Europe’s landmark AI Act has shifted into active enforcement, and Brussels will now test whether the rulebook is workable for companies and capable of becoming a global standard. Direct implications land on OpenAI, Google, Meta, Mistral, and Microsoft. For enterprises deploying in the EU, the compliance question moves from drafting policy to evidencing it.
