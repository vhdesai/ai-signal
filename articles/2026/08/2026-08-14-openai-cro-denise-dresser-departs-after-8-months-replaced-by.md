# OpenAI CRO Denise Dresser Departs After 8 Months; Replaced by Former Wiz COO

OpenAI CRO Denise Dresser is leaving after just eight months. Her replacement is Dali Rajic, formerly president and COO of Wiz (acquired by Google for $32B). At Wiz, Rajic oversaw revenue growth from ~$200M to nearly $2B and is credited with closing eight-figure enterprise deals. The hire signals cybersecurity is becoming central to AI go-to-market strategy, as OpenAI races with Anthropic to address AI-introduced threats.
