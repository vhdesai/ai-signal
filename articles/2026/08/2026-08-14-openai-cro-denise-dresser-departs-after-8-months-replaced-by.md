# OpenAI CRO Denise Dresser Departs After 8 Months; Replaced by Former Wiz COO

OpenAI's Chief Revenue Officer Denise Dresser is leaving after just eight months in the role, continuing a pattern of rapid senior-leadership turnover at the company as it approaches a potential IPO. Her replacement is Dali Rajic, formerly president and COO of Wiz, the cybersecurity company acquired by Google for $32 billion. At Wiz, Rajic oversaw revenue growth from approximately $200 million to nearly $2 billion and is credited with closing eight-figure enterprise deals in heavily regulated industries. The hire signals that cybersecurity expertise is becoming central to AI go-to-market strategy, as OpenAI races with Anthropic to address the security threats that AI systems both create and face. For enterprise accounts, the CRO transition introduces near-term relationship risk — deal owners and escalation paths will shift — but the Wiz pedigree suggests OpenAI is orienting its sales organization around the security-first procurement posture that large regulated buyers now require.
