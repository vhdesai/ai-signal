# Nvidia Denies It Will Ship a China-Specific AI Chip by Year-End

Nvidia publicly denied a report — originating with The Information — that it plans to begin shipping a language processing unit (LPU) tailored for Chinese customers by year-end, stating it has no China-specific version on its roadmap. Shares moved on the report before the denial. The episode underscores how sensitive export-control-adjacent product decisions have become, and why China-market assumptions should be treated as unconfirmed until Nvidia states them directly.
