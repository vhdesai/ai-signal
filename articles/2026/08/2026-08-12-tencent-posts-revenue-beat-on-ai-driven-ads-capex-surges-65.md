# Tencent Posts Revenue Beat on AI-Driven Ads; Capex Surges 65%

Tencent reported Q2 revenue of 204.78B yuan ($30.36B), beating estimates, driven by 17% YoY domestic gaming growth and a 22% rise in AI-powered advertising revenue. However, core profit missed expectations. Capital expenditures surged 65% QoQ to 52.8B yuan as Tencent ramps AI compute infrastructure, resulting in negative free cash flow of 13.8B yuan. The company launched its Hy3 AI model globally and began testing Xiaowei, a WeChat-integrated AI assistant. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/china-tencent-earnings-q2-2026-gaming-ai-advertising.html ________________________________ INFRASTRUCTURE INTEL
