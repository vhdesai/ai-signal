# Olix raises Europe's largest chip round for optical AI silicon

PitchBook reported that London-based optical AI-chip startup Olix raised a $312 million Series B at a $3.3 billion valuation. The round, described as Europe's largest chip financing, reflects investor appetite for alternatives to GPU scarcity and sovereign-AI dependence on U.S. suppliers.
