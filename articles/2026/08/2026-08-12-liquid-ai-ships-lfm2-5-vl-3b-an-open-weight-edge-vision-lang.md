# Liquid AI ships LFM2.5-VL-3B, an open-weight edge vision-language model

Liquid AI released LFM2.5-VL-3B, a 3.1-billion-parameter open-weight vision-language model designed to run on phones, laptops, and single GPUs rather than in a data center. The low-latency, non-reasoning model adds visual grounding, screen understanding, and function calling, with reported gains over the prior LFM2.5-2.6B family. For enterprises, it signals that useful multimodal inference is moving to the device edge, changing the cost and privacy calculus for deployment.
