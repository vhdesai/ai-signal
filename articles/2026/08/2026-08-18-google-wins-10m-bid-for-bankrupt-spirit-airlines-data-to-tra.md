# Google wins $10M bid for bankrupt Spirit Airlines' data to train AI

Google won a bankruptcy-auction bid for a trove of Spirit Airlines internal data — roughly 100M emails, 500M Teams chats, and billions of pricing and transaction records — for $10M.
