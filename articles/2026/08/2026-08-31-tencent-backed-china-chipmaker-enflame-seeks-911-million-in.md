# Tencent-backed China chipmaker Enflame seeks $911 million in IPO

Bloomberg reports that Tencent-backed Enflame is seeking roughly $911 million in a Shanghai IPO. The financing points to continued capital formation around Chinese AI accelerators, where domestic alternatives to restricted U.S. GPUs remain a strategic priority for cloud providers and model developers.
