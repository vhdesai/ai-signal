# Nvidia invests $3.5B in MediaTek and extends NVLink Fusion to custom accelerators

Nvidia is investing $3.5 billion in MediaTek convertible bonds and deepening a partnership spanning data center, PC and automotive platforms. MediaTek will adopt NVLink Fusion, letting customers attach custom XPUs to Nvidia rack systems. The strategic read is defensive-offensive: as hyperscalers build their own accelerators, Nvidia positions its interconnect and rack architecture as the mandatory substrate, converting a competitive threat into a toll. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/nvidias-3-5b-mediatek-bet-reveals-its-plan-for-tackling-big-techs-ai-chip-buildout/
