# Big manufacturers find new demand equipping AI data centers

The Wall Street Journal reported that large manufacturers are finding new demand from AI data-center construction and equipment needs. The buildout is expanding the AI value chain beyond GPUs into electrical gear, cooling, industrial components, construction inputs, and power infrastructure. For executives, this is a reminder that AI capacity planning is now a multi-industry supply-chain problem, with bottlenecks likely to appear outside traditional cloud procurement categories.
