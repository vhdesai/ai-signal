# Anthropic will invisibly watermark text generated by Claude models

Anthropic will embed machine-readable invisible watermarks in text from Claude models launched on or after August 2, 2026, plus C2PA signed provenance for files. The move is part of its commitments under the EU AI Act's Article 50(2) Code of Practice. Marks are designed to survive copy-paste and some editing, and apply across Claude, the API, Claude Code, Cowork and Tag. Expect enterprise questions about detection in downstream content workflows.
