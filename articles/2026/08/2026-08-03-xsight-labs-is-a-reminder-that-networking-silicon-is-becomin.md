# Xsight Labs is a reminder that networking silicon is becoming a central bottleneck as AI clusters scale and east-west…

Xsight Labs is a reminder that networking silicon is becoming a central bottleneck as AI clusters scale and east-west traffic intensifies. - Investors are increasingly funding the full data-center stack rather than treating GPUs as the only scarce or strategic asset. - High-performance networking matters because large model training and inference performance can be constrained by interconnect efficiency as much as by raw compute. - The financing round suggests the market expects sustained demand for specialized infrastructure components that reduce cluster-level inefficiency. - For infrastructure buyers, network architecture is now a strategic AI decision rather than a routine plumbing consideration.
