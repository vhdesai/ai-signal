# Reuters reports ECB blog predicts an AI market correction

Reuters reported that an ECB blog predicts an AI market correction, adding to scrutiny of whether AI-linked valuations and infrastructure spending have run ahead of near-term returns. The warning aligns with broader concern that the market may be underpricing execution risk, financing complexity, power constraints, and uncertain monetization timelines. For senior leaders, the practical implication is to stress-test AI investment cases against slower adoption and higher infrastructure costs, rather than assuming linear demand growth.
