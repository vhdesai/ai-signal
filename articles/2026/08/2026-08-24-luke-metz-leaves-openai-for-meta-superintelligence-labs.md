# Luke Metz leaves OpenAI for Meta Superintelligence Labs

Researcher Luke Metz — who returned to OpenAI from Mira Murati’s Thinking Machines Lab in January — is joining Meta’s Superintelligence Labs, reporting to chief AI officer Alexandr Wang. Metz co-developed DCGAN and contributed to OpenAI’s o1 system card. It is the second departure from the OpenAI post-training team assembled around Barret Zoph earlier this year, and extends Meta’s sustained recruiting pressure on that group.
