# AI Startups Raised $407B in H1 2026 — OpenAI and Anthropic Took More Than Half

AI startups raised over $407 billion in VC funding in the first half of 2026, blowing past the $264 billion invested in all of 2025, per PitchBook's Q2 2026 AI Report. OpenAI and Anthropic alone accounted for roughly $217 billion of the total. While vertical AI application companies drove 62.9% of deal count, they captured only 12.9% of capital — horizontal platform companies (including frontier labs) absorbed 70.8% of deal value. PitchBook senior analyst Dimitri Zabelin expects capital concentration, not scarcity, to remain the defining trend through year-end.
