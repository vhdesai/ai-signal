# AI code-testing startup Blacksmith raises $45M at a ~$550M valuation

Blacksmith raised a $45 million round at roughly $550 million, close to a 10x valuation increase in under a year. The thesis is that as AI accelerates code generation, validation and testing become the binding constraint in software delivery. This is a second-order effect of coding-agent adoption that most enterprise toolchains have not yet budgeted for. Expect testing, verification and CI infrastructure to attract disproportionate investment. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/blacksmiths-valuation-jumps-10x-to-550m-as-ai-coding-fuels-software-validation/
