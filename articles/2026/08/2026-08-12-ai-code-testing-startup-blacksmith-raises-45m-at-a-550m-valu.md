# AI Code-Testing Startup Blacksmith Raises $45M at a $550M Valuation

Blacksmith raised a $45 million Series B led by Peak XV, with GV and Y Combinator participating — roughly a 10x valuation increase in under a year. The company provides CI infrastructure plus an agent that helps resolve failed checks, and says it now serves more than 5,000 customers, up from about 700. The round is a clean read on a second-order effect: AI-generated code increases, rather than reduces, validation workload. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/blacksmiths-valuation-jumps-10x-to-550m-as-ai-coding-fuels-software-validation/
