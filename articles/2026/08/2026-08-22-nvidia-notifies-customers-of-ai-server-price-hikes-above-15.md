# Nvidia notifies customers of AI server price hikes above 15%

Several of Nvidia's largest customers have been told that prices for servers containing its AI chips will rise by more than 15% in many configurations, driven by soaring memory chip costs. The increases apply to systems shipping early next year, affecting flagship Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell builds. Contract server manufacturers supplying Microsoft, Google, and Oracle have already passed notice to their own customers. Nvidia reports Q2 results on August 26, which should clarify how much of the increase flows to margin versus supplier cost.
