# TechCrunch Deep-Dive: Who's Behind the Stealth Model 'Ox Alpha'?

TechCrunch investigated the anonymous “Ox Alpha” model on OpenRouter, which Stripe CEO Patrick Collison called “very impressive.” Speculation remains unresolved: initial evidence pointed to Z.ai (which previously tested GLM-5 anonymously as “Pony Alpha”), but an update suggested Microsoft’s unreleased MAI instead. Reddit remains split. OpenRouter describes it as “developed by a third-party provider who has chosen to remain anonymous during this preview.” The episode highlights the growing phenomenon of anonymous model drops as market signals — and the geopolitical dimension of unattributed frontier AI.
