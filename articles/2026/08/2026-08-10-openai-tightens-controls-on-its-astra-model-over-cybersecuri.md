# OpenAI tightens controls on its Astra model over cybersecurity risk

OpenAI said it slowed development of and imposed additional controls on Astra, its next frontier model, after it could not rule out critical cyber capabilities. It is one of the first instances of a leading lab publicly delaying a flagship release on capability-threshold grounds. The disclosure lands amid an intensifying debate about whether internal evaluation regimes are sufficient without external verification. ________________________________ POLICY
