# MiniMax releases H3, an omni-modal video model for 15-second 2K clips with stereo audio

MarkTechPost reported that MiniMax released MiniMax H3, an omni-modal video model that generates 15-second 2K clips with native stereo audio. The release shows media-generation competition moving beyond text-to-video demos toward richer outputs with synchronized audio and higher production quality. The likely enterprise relevance is in marketing, training, entertainment, and synthetic-content workflows where controllability and provenance will determine adoption.
