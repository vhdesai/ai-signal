# Kog aims to squeeze more inference throughput from conventional GPUs

French startup Kog is developing a low-level inference engine intended to accelerate LLM decoding on standard data-center GPUs such as Nvidia H200s and AMD MI300Xs. Its initial demo showed 3,000 tokens per second per request on a purpose-built small model, and the company is now trying to extend the approach to larger LLMs. The strategic signal is that software optimization for existing GPU fleets may become a significant cost lever before enterprises shift to specialized inference silicon.
