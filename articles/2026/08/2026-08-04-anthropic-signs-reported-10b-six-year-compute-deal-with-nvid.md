# Anthropic Signs Reported $10B, Six-Year Compute Deal With Nvidia-Backed Startup Volta

Anthropic will source cloud compute from Volta — a startup founded earlier this year and a member of Nvidia's Cloud Partner program — under a reported $10 billion, six-year arrangement first reported by Bloomberg. Volta is partnering with crypto-mining firm Bitdeer to build a 133-megawatt facility in Norway running Nvidia's Vera Rubin architecture. The deal follows recent Anthropic compute agreements with SpaceX and Amazon. The pattern worth tracking is that frontier labs are now underwriting greenfield, single-tenant capacity from unproven counterparties, which concentrates delivery and credit risk across the buyer side of the market.
