# OpenAI to cut off Cursor's model access following SpaceX's $60B acquisition

OpenAI notified SpaceX that it will wind down the contract supplying its models to Cursor (Anysphere) effective November 12, invoking a change-of-control provision triggered by SpaceX's roughly $60 billion acquisition of the coding startup. OpenAI's own statement cites prior contract-compliance concerns with Musk-owned entities and competitive-use risk. Cursor already ships Grok models, and OpenAI reportedly served a minority of Cursor traffic, so the near-term product impact is limited — but the precedent matters. Model supply is now an explicit strategic lever, and enterprises with single-vendor coding-agent dependencies should assume similar terminations are possible. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html
