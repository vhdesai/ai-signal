# Anthropic reportedly weighing ~$2 trillion valuation for an October IPO

Citing Financial Times reporting, Anthropic may seek a valuation above $2T in an autumn listing, with backers modeling $100B–$120B in revenue by the end of 2026 against IDC estimates of $40–50B annualized today. The figure is investor-modeled and has not been confirmed by Anthropic. It is framed against OpenAI's recent $7B buyback at an $852B valuation.
