# Meta’s AI agent push puts focus on new consumer business

Seeking Alpha reports that Meta’s AI agent strategy is drawing investor focus as a potential consumer revenue engine. The strategic question is whether Meta can convert its distribution in social and messaging products into monetizable agent behavior without creating new trust, safety, or platform-control issues.
