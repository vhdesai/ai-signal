# House Democrats call on AI companies to testify before Congress on recent hacking incidents

A group of House Democrats is asking leaders of Anthropic, OpenAI and other AI companies to testify about the recent security incidents, describing them as a clear risk to safety. The request runs in parallel with the "AI Kill Switch Act" introduced in July, which would require AI companies to maintain the ability to shut down, throttle or suspend their models. Together they mark the shift from voluntary lab self-regulation toward statutory obligation. POLICYBREAKING
