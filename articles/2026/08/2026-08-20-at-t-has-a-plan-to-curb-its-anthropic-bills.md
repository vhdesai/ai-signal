# AT&T Has a Plan to Curb Its Anthropic Bills

AT&T plans to keep employee spending on Anthropic and OpenAI models flat by routing simpler tasks to cheaper models and reserving frontier capacity for high-value use cases. If more large enterprises follow the same playbook, it compresses the per-seat revenue that frontier labs are counting on for IPO-grade growth. Cost optimization is becoming the next chapter of enterprise AI procurement.
