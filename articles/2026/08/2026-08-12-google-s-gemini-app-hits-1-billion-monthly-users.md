# Google’s Gemini App Hits 1 Billion Monthly Users

Google’s Gemini reached 1 billion monthly users, making it the fastest-growing product in the company’s history, per CEO Sundar Pichai. The milestone came less than a month after reporting 950 million MAUs at earnings. Google said 63% of users interact with Gemini directly, while 100 million Apple users via the Apple Intelligence/Siri integration contribute to the total. OpenAI’s ChatGPT hit 1 billion users on July 31, making this a neck-and-neck race.
