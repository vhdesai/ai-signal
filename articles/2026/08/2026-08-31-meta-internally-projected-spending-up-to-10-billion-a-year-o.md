# Meta internally projected spending up to $10 billion a year on Anthropic models

Meta reportedly modeled annual spend of up to $10 billion on Anthropic models even as it publicly argued against frontier-lab concentration. Heavy internal usage of Claude Code appears to have driven the surge before costs forced a pullback. The story highlights how rivals increasingly depend on each other's models while trying to preserve strategic independence.
