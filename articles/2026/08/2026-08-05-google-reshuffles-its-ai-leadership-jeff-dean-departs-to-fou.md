# Google reshuffles its AI leadership: Jeff Dean departs to found Discovery Loop, Hassabis moves to chairman

Google announced a sweeping reorganization of its AI operations. Chief scientist Jeff Dean is leaving after 27 years to co-found Discovery Loop — a public-benefit corporation focused on AI for science and engineering — alongside senior fellow Sanjay Ghemawat, with Google investing as a backer. DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis is stepping down as CEO of Google DeepMind to become its chairman and Alphabet’s chief scientist, while DeepMind CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu is promoted to run the AI division and lead Gemini 4; Alphabet shares fell roughly 4%, and TIME followed with reorganization detail on Aug 6. For executives, the move consolidates frontier-model authority under Kavukcuoglu and Pichai even as Google’s full-year capex guidance climbs toward $205 billion.
