# 'Near-autonomous' AI agents attack Taiwan's nuclear safety agency

Taiwan's nuclear regulator was targeted in what is described as a largely autonomous, AI-driven intrusion campaign linked to China, with agents conducting reconnaissance and intrusion attempts across government and energy targets. Reporting indicates attackers used freely available agent frameworks and bypassed built-in safety guardrails.
