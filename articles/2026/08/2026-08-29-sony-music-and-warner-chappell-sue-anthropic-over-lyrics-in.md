# Sony Music and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic Over Lyrics in Training Data

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell Music filed suit in the Northern District of California, naming Anthropic, CEO Dario Amodei, and co-founder Benjamin Mann, alleging tens of thousands of instances of copyright infringement. The complaint asserts copyrighted compositions were obtained from shadow-library sources and scraped lyric databases. The case extends training-data litigation from text and images into music publishing, where rights are unusually well documented and damages are easier to quantify. It arrives just as Anthropic prepares for an IPO — litigation of this scale becomes a material disclosure item.
