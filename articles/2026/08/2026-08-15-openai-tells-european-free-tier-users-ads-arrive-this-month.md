# OpenAI tells European free-tier users ads arrive this month

OpenAI Ireland notified Free and Go plan users across the EEA and Switzerland that advertising will begin appearing in ChatGPT later this month; Plus, Pro, Enterprise, Business and Education tiers remain ad-free. Initial targeting is contextual only — current-conversation topic, general location, device type — with explicit opt-in required before personalized ads draw on chat history or memory. Advertisers receive aggregate views and clicks only.
