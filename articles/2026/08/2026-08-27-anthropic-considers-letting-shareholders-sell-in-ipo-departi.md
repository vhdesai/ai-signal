# Anthropic Considers Letting Shareholders Sell in IPO, Departing from SpaceX Playbook

Anthropic is planning to let existing shareholders sell stock in its blockbuster IPO while considering longer-than-usual lockup periods. The approach departs from the SpaceX model and could set a new template for mega-IPOs in AI.
