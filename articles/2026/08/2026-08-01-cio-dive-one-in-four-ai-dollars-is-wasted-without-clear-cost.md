# CIO Dive: one in four AI dollars is wasted without clear cost ownership

CIO Dive highlighted a Harness report finding that roughly 25% of AI spending is wasted and that more than half of businesses lack a dedicated owner for AI costs. As enterprises move from pilots into production, unmanaged inference, redundant tooling, and poorly governed infrastructure are becoming material budget leaks. The finding reinforces that AI FinOps is becoming an operating discipline, not a back-office optimization. URL: 1 in 4 dollars spent on AI goes to waste, report finds
