# Sam Altman says he's worried about AI being controlled by a few powerful players

The OpenAI CEO said he worries AI will end up controlled by a handful of companies, models or people, leaving most consumers without a say. He argued that fear of AI "going off the rails" could lead society to trade significant liberty for safety, inviting heavy regulatory centralization. The remarks come days after OpenAI publicly urged California to strengthen SB 53.
