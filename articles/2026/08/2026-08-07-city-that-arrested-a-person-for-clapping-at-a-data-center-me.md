# City That Arrested a Person for Clapping at a Data Center Meeting Moves to Virtual Meetings

Emporia, Kansas held its city commission meeting virtually with no public comment period, citing “public safety” after commissioners received death threats over the proposed gigawatt-scale Flint Hills Digital Campus on 1,000 acres of prairie. The commission voted 5-0 to send a citizen petition for a high-impact data center ban to a judge rather than decide it locally. A resident arrested in July for clapping during a hearing said the threats appear to originate largely from non-locals. Local permitting opposition is becoming a material schedule risk for hyperscale siting.
