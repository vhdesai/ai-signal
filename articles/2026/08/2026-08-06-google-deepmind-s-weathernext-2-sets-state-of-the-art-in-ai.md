# Google DeepMind’s WeatherNext 2 sets state of the art in AI cyclone forecasting — and is open-sourced

In a paper published in Nature, Google researchers showed WeatherNext 2 achieving state-of-the-art accuracy in predicting a cyclone’s track, intensity, and wind structure — what the team frames as roughly a decade of meteorological progress in a single model. Google is open-sourcing WeatherNext 2, making the advance broadly available to forecasters and researchers. The result was corroborated the same day by New Scientist and Unite.AI.
