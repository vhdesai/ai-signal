# Nvidia's $500B Compute-Financing Platform Draws Private-Credit Scrutiny

Nvidia's financing partnership with six major financial institutions is now the dominant topic in private credit. CEO Jensen Huang said Nvidia has the option to backstop up to $125 billion — about 25% of the potential deals — and characterized its chips as "revenue-generating assets." The structure converts GPUs into a financeable asset class, extending buyer capacity beyond balance-sheet limits while concentrating residual-value risk on a single vendor's depreciation assumptions. Separately, FT analysis indicates Big Tech purchase commitments tied to compute, chips, data centers, and energy have accumulated close to $1.5 trillion — future cash obligations that do not sit on the balance sheet as debt. Evaluating AI exposure now requires reading commitment footnotes, not just capex guidance.
