# Xiaomi MiLM Plus releases PROVE benchmark and metrics for object removal evaluation

Xiaomi's MiLM Plus released PROVE, a set of perception-aligned object-removal metrics and a real-world video benchmark accepted at ACM MM 2026. The work targets a practical evaluation gap: traditional full-reference metrics often mis-rank object-removal outputs because the task has many plausible ground truths. For teams building image and video editing systems, PROVE is less a consumer feature than an evaluation harness for model bake-offs, CI gates, and reward-model development.
