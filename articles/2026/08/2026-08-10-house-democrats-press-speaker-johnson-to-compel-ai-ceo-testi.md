# House Democrats Press Speaker Johnson to Compel AI CEO Testimony

House Democrats sent a letter Monday urging Speaker Mike Johnson to compel the chief executives of OpenAI, Anthropic, and other major AI firms to testify before Congress about the recent hacking incidents, which lawmakers characterized as a “clear risk to safety.” This moves the agent-safety story from lab disclosure into congressional oversight and marks a shift from voluntary commitments toward compelled accountability.
