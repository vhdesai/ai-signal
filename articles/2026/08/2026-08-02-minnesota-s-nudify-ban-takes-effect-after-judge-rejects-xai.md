# Minnesota’s “nudify” ban takes effect after judge rejects xAI’s bid to pause it

Minnesota’s first-in-the-nation ban on AI “nudification” tools is now in force after a federal judge declined xAI’s request to block it. The court set an August 19 hearing on a preliminary injunction, leaving the law active in the interim. The case is an early test of state-level limits on generative-AI misuse and the liability exposure for model providers.
