# Apple trains a China-specific AI model with Alibaba

Apple is reported to be training a custom China-specific large language model with Alibaba to power Apple Intelligence in that market. The approach reflects the regulatory reality that foreign frontier models cannot be deployed directly in China. It also puts Apple in more direct competition with Huawei's on-device AI stack.
