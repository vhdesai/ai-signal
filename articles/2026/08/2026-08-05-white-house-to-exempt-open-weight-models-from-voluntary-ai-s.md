# White House to exempt open-weight models from voluntary AI safety testing

The administration told developers including Meta, Anthropic, Google, Nvidia and OpenAI that the voluntary safety framework ordered by June's executive order will exclude open-weight models such as Llama and Nemotron, targeting only closed frontier systems. Critics warn that downloadable models with high cyber capability are precisely the hardest to safeguard after release. Five Democratic senators pushed for mandatory testing instead. The policy was briefed August 4 with fresh primary coverage August 5.
