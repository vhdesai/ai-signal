# DeepSeek reportedly plans a gigawatt-scale data center in Inner Mongolia

Memeburn reported that DeepSeek is planning a data center of at least one gigawatt in Inner Mongolia, signaling a possible escalation from low-cost model releases into major domestic compute buildout. The claim is single-source and capacity figures should be treated as preliminary, but the strategic direction is plausible: Chinese frontier labs need power, land, and local compute capacity to sustain model competition. If realized, the project would reinforce that AI competition is shifting from model cards to infrastructure control. URL: DeepSeek data center plan
