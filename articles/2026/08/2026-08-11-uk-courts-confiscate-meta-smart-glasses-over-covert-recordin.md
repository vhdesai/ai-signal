# UK Courts Confiscate Meta Smart Glasses Over Covert Recording Risk

His Majesty's Courts & Tribunals Service will seize camera-equipped smart glasses at court entrances across England and Wales and return them on exit, citing the difficulty of enforcing existing filming bans against devices that record without the visible gesture of raising a phone. Meta points to its recording indicator and anti-tampering measures. New York courts have taken comparable action, and UK hospitality and theater venues have introduced their own restrictions. The pattern signals that always-on wearable AI will be regulated at the venue and institution level before national rules catch up. URL not verified.
