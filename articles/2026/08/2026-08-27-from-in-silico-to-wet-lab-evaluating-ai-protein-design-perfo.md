# From in-silico to wet-lab: evaluating AI protein design performance

A detailed benchmark walkthrough uses Anthropic's claude-protein-binder-design dataset — 1,440 AI-designed miniprotein binders tested against 16 targets — to compare 10 leading structure predictors. The analysis examines how target identity, expression titers, and consensus scoring affect real wet-lab success rates. It closes with cross-validation practices for teams operationalizing protein-design workflows.
