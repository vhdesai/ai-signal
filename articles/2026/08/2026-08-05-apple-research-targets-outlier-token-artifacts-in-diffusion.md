# Apple research targets outlier-token artifacts in diffusion transformers

Apple published research on outlier tokens in diffusion transformers for image generation, showing that high-norm tokens can distort local patch semantics in modern RAE-DiT pipelines. The paper introduces Dual-Stage Registers to reduce artifacts and improve image generation quality across ImageNet and large-scale text-to-image settings. The work is relevant for production media-generation systems because quality improvements increasingly come from understanding model internals, not simply scaling model size.
