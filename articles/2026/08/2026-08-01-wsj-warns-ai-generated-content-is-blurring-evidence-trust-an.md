# WSJ warns AI-generated content is blurring evidence, trust, and reality

The Wall Street Journal examined how deepfakes, synthetic media, and AI-augmented recordings are making verification harder for institutions, journalists, courts, and companies. The concern is that detection tools are structurally behind generation capabilities, making trust asymmetrical and expensive. For enterprises, this moves synthetic-media risk from reputational concern into legal, regulatory, and evidentiary domains. URL: AI and the Blurring of Reality
