# Anthropic Posts $11.5B Q2 Revenue — First Profitable Quarter

Preliminary Q2 revenue exceeded $11.5B — ~14× year-ago, >2× Q1 — with positive adjusted operating income. Lands as banks prepare a fall IPO backed by a $190–200B 2028 revenue forecast. For enterprise buyers, the numbers reduce the counterparty-risk argument that historically favored larger-balance-sheet vendors. Anthropic’s transition from loss-making lab to profitable frontier vendor changes its commercial positioning materially. CNBC FUNDING
