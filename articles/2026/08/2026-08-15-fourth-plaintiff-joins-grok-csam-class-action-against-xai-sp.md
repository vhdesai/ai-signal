# Fourth Plaintiff Joins Grok CSAM Class Action Against xAI/SpaceX

A woman alleges a family member used Grok to convert a single childhood photo into 7,000+ explicit images. Plaintiffs argue the platform was chosen because it was less restrictive, and that xAI did not respond to law-enforcement requests. The most advanced U.S. test of platform liability for image-model misuse. Seeking class action status. The case is establishing the template for downstream indemnification terms in AI vendor contracts — procurement and legal teams should track closely.
