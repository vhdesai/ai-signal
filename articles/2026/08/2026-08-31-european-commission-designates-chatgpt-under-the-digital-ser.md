# European Commission designates ChatGPT under the Digital Services Act

The Commission designated ChatGPT as a Very Large Online Search Engine, alongside Reddit and Roblox as Very Large Online Platforms, after the services declared at least 45 million average monthly EU users. The designated services have four months — until January 2027 — to meet additional DSA obligations, including systemic risk assessment and mitigation covering illegal content, effects on minors, user well-being, fundamental rights, electoral processes and public security.
