# OpenAI and Anthropic Buy Tens of Thousands of Macs for Agent Training; Apple Pulls Forward Refreshes

OpenAI has acquired tens of thousands of Apple desktop systems for reinforcement-learning workloads on computer-use agents, with Anthropic renting similar Mac capacity through AWS. Unified-memory desktops suit memory-bound, trial-and-error desktop-task training. The demand pulled forward Apple's Mac mini and Mac Studio refreshes ahead of the iPhone cycle — a signal that inference is migrating toward owned, on-premises hardware for latency, cost, and data-control reasons.
