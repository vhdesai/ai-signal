# IBM and Together AI Sign $240M Nvidia-Powered Inference Deal

IBM and Together AI signed a $240M multiyear agreement to build a large inference cluster on IBM Cloud, starting with ~2,000 Nvidia Blackwell-generation chips using HGX B300 systems and Spectrum-X networking. The deal reflects a decisive shift from training capacity to cost-efficient inference for open-weight models that enterprises want to control directly. For IBM, it is a strategic route into the AI buildout without competing head-on with hyperscalers on raw training capacity — positioning IBM Cloud as a credible venue for third-party model serving. Together AI brings its open-weights inference platform and model-routing technology. The deal also validates Spectrum-X as the standard interconnect for inference clusters, not just training supercomputers. The Register EARNINGS
