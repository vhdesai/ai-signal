# Discovered Materials applies AI to the search for cooler-running chip materials

Startup Discovered Materials is using AI-driven materials search to find substrates and compounds that reduce heat generation in AI workloads, targeting the thermal load that drives data-center electricity and cooling costs. The approach treats materials discovery as a large combinatorial search problem rather than a lab-throughput problem. If it works at scale, thermal efficiency gains translate directly into lower siting and power requirements — the same constraint now driving local data-center bans.
