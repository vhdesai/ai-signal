# AI Code-Testing Startup Blacksmith Jumps to $550M Valuation

$45M raised at ~$550M — nearly 10× in under a year. The thesis: as AI accelerates code generation, testing becomes the binding constraint. 5,000+ customers, tens of millions in ARR with ~30 employees. Expect testing, verification, and CI infrastructure to attract disproportionate investment as a second-order effect of coding-agent adoption.
