# OpenAI Cuts Off Cursor After SpaceX's $60B Takeover of Anysphere

OpenAI will end Cursor's direct model access on November 12, citing an inability to be confident SpaceX will operate within its terms of service and referencing Elon Musk's admission that xAI distilled OpenAI outputs. Cursor CEO Michael Truell said OpenAI models serve roughly 5% of Cursor user traffic, a figure that reflects Cursor's own Composer 2.5 model post-trained from Moonshot's open Kimi K2.5 checkpoint. Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown publicly committed additional compute and raised Claude usage limits inside Cursor within hours. The November 12 deadline turns an abstract vendor lock-in argument into a measurable test of whether frontier model access is a dependency or a purchasing decision.
