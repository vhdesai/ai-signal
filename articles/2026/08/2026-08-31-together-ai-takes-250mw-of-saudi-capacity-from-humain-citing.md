# Together AI takes 250MW of Saudi capacity from HUMAIN, citing US data-center backlash

Together AI announced a partnership with Saudi state-backed HUMAIN giving it access to roughly 250MW and a large chip footprint, under a revenue-share arrangement. Together's leadership pointed directly to US community opposition, moratoriums, and cancellations as a domestic capacity constraint.
