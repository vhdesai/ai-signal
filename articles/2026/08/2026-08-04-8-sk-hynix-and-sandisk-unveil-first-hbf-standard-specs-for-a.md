# 8. SK hynix and Sandisk unveil first HBF standard specs for AI memory

At FMS 2026, SK hynix and Sandisk introduced the first High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) standard specifications — a memory class aimed at AI infrastructure — alongside a Google DeepMind panel and participation from Google and Tenstorrent. HBF targets the bandwidth and capacity bottlenecks constraining large-model training and inference at scale. Industry News BreakingAmazon
