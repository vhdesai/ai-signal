# Nvidia to invest $1.5B in SB Energy, the SoftBank developer behind OpenAI's Ohio campus

Nvidia said it will invest $1.5B in SB Energy, the SoftBank-owned developer building the PORTS-Pike campus in Pike County, Ohio, joining SoftBank Group and OpenAI as an investor. The arrangement makes Nvidia the exclusive supplier of AI compute infrastructure at the site, and Nvidia is separately providing credit support tied to the initial 4.25 IT-GW of land, power, and shell buildout. This is vendor financing at industrial scale and deepens the circularity between Nvidia's balance sheet and its customers' capacity commitments. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-investing-1-5b-softbank-151624715.html
