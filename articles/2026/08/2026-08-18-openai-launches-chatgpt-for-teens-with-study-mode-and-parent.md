# OpenAI Launches ChatGPT for Teens with Study Mode and Parental Controls

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens with age-appropriate safety measures and homework anti-cheating features — years after teens became core users (900M weekly actives total). Study Mode provides guiding questions instead of direct answers, and "homework reminders" intervene when cheating is detected. Parents can set Quiet Hours and receive safety notifications. The launch follows multiple lawsuits over AI chatbots and teen mental health outcomes. PRODUCT WARP DEV TOOLS
