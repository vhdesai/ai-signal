# CoreWeave and Super Micro surge on evidence of sustained AI buildout

CoreWeave reported Q2 revenue of $2.6B, up 112% year over year, alongside a contracted backlog reported at roughly $104B. Shares of CoreWeave and Super Micro Computer jumped to their highest levels since June on the strength of the forecasts.
