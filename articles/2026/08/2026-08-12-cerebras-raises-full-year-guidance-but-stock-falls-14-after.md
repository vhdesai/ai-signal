# Cerebras raises full-year guidance but stock falls 14% after hours

In its second earnings report since May's IPO, Cerebras raised full-year guidance to roughly $880M–$890M on core quarterly revenue of about $210M, and said it expects revenue to triple next year. The stock nonetheless fell about 14% in extended trading. The divergence between guidance and price reflects how tightly AI-hardware valuations are now indexed to expectations rather than delivery. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/cerebras-cbrs-q2-earnings-report-2026.html EARNINGS
