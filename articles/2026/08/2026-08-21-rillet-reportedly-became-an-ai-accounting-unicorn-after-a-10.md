# Rillet reportedly became an AI accounting unicorn after a $100 million raise

TechCrunch reported that AI accounting startup Rillet raised $100 million and became a unicorn in roughly 48 hours. The story is a signal that investors continue to fund vertical AI applications where domain-specific workflows, data, and compliance needs create defensible product wedges. Accounting is especially attractive because the work is repeatable, document-heavy, and measurable, but buyers will still expect auditability and integration with existing financial systems.
