# Marvell sells off ~8% as Google custom-silicon revenue slips to fiscal 2029

Marvell beat on the quarter and raised its outlook, but shares fell sharply after management indicated its large custom-chip agreement with Alphabet will not become a material revenue engine until fiscal 2029. Google also received warrants for a mid-single-digit percentage of Marvell shares as part of the arrangement.
