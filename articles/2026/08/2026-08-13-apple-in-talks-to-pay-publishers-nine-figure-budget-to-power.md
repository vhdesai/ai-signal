# Apple in Talks to Pay Publishers Nine-Figure Budget to Power Siri AI with News

Apple has reached out to publishers about licensing content to provide Siri with current news and information, with a proposed nine-figure budget. Unlike industry-standard fixed licensing fees, Apple is proposing a variable pay-per-use compensation model.
