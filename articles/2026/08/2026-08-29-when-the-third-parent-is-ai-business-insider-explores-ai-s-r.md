Business Insider examines the growing phenomenon of AI chatbots becoming a de facto "third parent" for children, with kids turning to AI for homework help, emotional support, and companionship. The piece raises concerns about the developmental implications of children forming relationships with AI systems and the uncharted territory of AI-mediated child development.
