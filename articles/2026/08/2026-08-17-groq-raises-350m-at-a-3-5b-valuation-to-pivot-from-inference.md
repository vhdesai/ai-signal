# Groq raises $350M at a $3.5B valuation to pivot from inference silicon to neocloud

Groq closed $350 million as it repositions from a custom AI-chip vendor to a capacity provider, expanding an Nvidia-powered data center footprint. The pivot is notable because it concedes that differentiated inference silicon alone has been insufficient to hold share against incumbent accelerators.
