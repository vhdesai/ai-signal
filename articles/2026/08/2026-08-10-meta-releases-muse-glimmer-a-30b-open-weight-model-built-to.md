# Meta releases Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weight model built to run on one consumer GPU

Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter model under an Apache 2.0 license, distilled from the larger Muse Spark model and targeted at local agentic workloads — long-horizon execution, tool calling, multimodal input and long-context memory. Quantization brings the footprint from over 55 GB at full precision to under 20 GB at roughly 4-bit, fitting a 24–32 GB memory envelope, with reported decode speedups of 3.1x on an RTX 5090 and 1.8x on an M5 Max. Distribution is through Hugging Face with llama.cpp, MLX, ExecuTorch, Ollama, vLLM and SGLang support, and optimization partnerships with AMD, Arm, Dell, Intel and Nvidia. The release lands alongside Mark Zuckerberg's renewed public argument for open weights against closed systems from OpenAI and Anthropic.
