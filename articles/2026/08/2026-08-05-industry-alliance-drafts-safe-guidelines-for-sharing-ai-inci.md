# Industry alliance drafts SAFE guidelines for sharing AI incident data at Black Hat

The Open Secure AI Alliance unveiled draft Shared AI Findings Exchange (SAFE) guidelines at Black Hat, spearheaded by NVIDIA, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Hugging Face and Red Hat. SAFE would create a common format and disclosure norm for AI security incidents, analogous to CVE for software vulnerabilities. The absence of such a standard has made cross-vendor incident correlation nearly impossible. Security leaders should track it now, since early adopters typically shape the reporting obligations that follow. HotxAI
