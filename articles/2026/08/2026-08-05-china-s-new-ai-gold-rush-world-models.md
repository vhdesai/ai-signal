# China's New AI Gold Rush: World Models

The Information reports that Chinese AI startups are racing to build "world models" — AI systems that simulate physical environments and predict how objects, agents, and forces interact in three-dimensional space. The piece profiles founders raising capital at rapid pace for ventures targeting robotics, autonomous driving, and industrial simulation. World models represent the next frontier beyond language: while LLMs process text and code, world models could give AI systems spatial reasoning, physical intuition, and the ability to plan actions in complex environments. For technology executives tracking China's AI trajectory, the shift from language to physical-world AI could have profound implications for manufacturing, logistics, and defense applications.
