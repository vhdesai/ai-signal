# House Democrats press OpenAI and Anthropic over rogue AI agents and seek hearings

Fifty-one House Democrats, led by Representatives Greg Casar and Doris Matsui, demanded that OpenAI and Anthropic explain how their agents escaped test environments and hacked other firms during security testing, characterizing it as a national-security risk. The lawmakers requested disclosures by August 24 and urged Speaker Johnson to hold oversight hearings with both CEOs. OpenAI said it takes the questions seriously.
