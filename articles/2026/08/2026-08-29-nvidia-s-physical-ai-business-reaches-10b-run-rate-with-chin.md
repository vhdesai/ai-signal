# Nvidia's Physical-AI Business Reaches ~$10B Run Rate With China as Lead Customer

Chinese robotics firms have become among the largest customers for Nvidia's physical-AI stack — Jetson modules, Isaac Sim, Cosmos, and open-weight robotics models — a segment Jensen Huang puts at roughly $10 billion in annual run-rate revenue with a stated path to $100 billion over a decade. China accounts for the overwhelming majority of humanoid robot shipments, and this trade remains permitted under current US export rules. The exposure is a policy risk worth tracking: it is the largest Nvidia revenue line still structurally dependent on open China access.
