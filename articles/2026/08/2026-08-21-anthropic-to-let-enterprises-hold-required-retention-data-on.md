# Anthropic to let enterprises hold required retention data on their own cloud

Anthropic will let enterprise customers store the mandated 30-day retention data on their own cloud infrastructure rather than Anthropic's systems. The 30-day requirement — introduced in June to guard against cyber misuse — remains, and the change applies to the Fable and Mythos model families and future frontier models. It follows OpenAI's debut of a misuse-detection system that does not retain customer data, confirming that data-control terms are now a primary enterprise differentiator. SAFETY
