# Axios: “Nvidia Almighty” — Chip Profits Recycled Across the AI Ecosystem

Axios frames Nvidia as simultaneously the AI industry’s supplier, banker, and kingmaker, plowing chip profits back into an ecosystem whose buildout demands ever more of its compute. The analysis is the narrative counterpart to Nvidia’s pause on revenue-sharing deals. It is the clearest articulation yet of the concentration risk sitting under current AI capex.
