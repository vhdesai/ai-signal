# Zuckerberg publishes a 6,500-word case for broadly distributed superintelligence

Mark Zuckerberg argued that superintelligence should be shared with as many people and businesses as possible, framing concentration of control in a single government, company or person as the primary risk. Proposals include free access for billions of users, paid auctions for scarce compute, private agent modes, and a $1B "Future is for Everyone Fund" for communities hosting Meta data centers. Coverage consistently framed this as Meta positioning open access against the more restricted postures associated with Anthropic and OpenAI. Critics countered that the argument understates bioweapon-offense asymmetries and normalizes recursive self-improvement. POLICY
