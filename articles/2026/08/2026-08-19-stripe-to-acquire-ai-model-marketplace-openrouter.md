# Stripe to acquire AI model marketplace OpenRouter

Stripe agreed to acquire OpenRouter, the routing marketplace that lets developers select and switch among frontier models behind a single interface. Reported valuations cluster around $7–7.5B, several times OpenRouter's valuation at its $113M Series B earlier this year. The deal extends a payments company into AI inference infrastructure and signals that the model-abstraction layer is becoming a strategically contested position. M&A
