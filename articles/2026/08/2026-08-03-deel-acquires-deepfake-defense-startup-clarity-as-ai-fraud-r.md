# Deel acquires deepfake-defense startup Clarity as AI fraud rises

Payroll and HR unicorn Deel said Monday it has acquired Clarity, an Israeli AI-security startup specializing in identity verification, deepfake detection, and synthetic-identity fraud prevention. Terms were undisclosed, but the deal is estimated at $40–50 million. The purchase reflects rising enterprise concern about AI-generated fraud in hiring and workforce systems. EXPANSION
