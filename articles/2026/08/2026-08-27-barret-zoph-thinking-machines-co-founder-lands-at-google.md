# Barret Zoph, Thinking Machines Co-Founder, Lands at Google

Barret Zoph — who co-founded Thinking Machines Lab with Mira Murati and served as its CTO before a brief stint at OpenAI — has joined Google. The move continues an unusually fluid period of senior researcher movement across the frontier labs, following a documented stream of OpenAI executive departures earlier in the month. Talent concentration at Google is a competitive signal worth tracking alongside model releases.
