# OpenAI’s “Jalapeño” Chip Posts Inference Benchmarks Beating NVIDIA’s Blackwell

OpenAI’s first custom inference chip, built with Broadcom, posted early results on SemiAnalysis’s InferenceX benchmark showing more tokens per user and more throughput per kilowatt than current state-of-the-art parts. The Register estimated roughly 1.5x–1.9x more AI work at peak throughput versus Blackwell. The results landed hours before NVIDIA’s own quarterly print, sharpening the custom-silicon narrative at exactly the wrong moment for the incumbent.
