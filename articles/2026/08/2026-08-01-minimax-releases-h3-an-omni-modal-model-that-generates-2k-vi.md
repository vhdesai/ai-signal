# MiniMax releases H3, an omni-modal model that generates 2K video with native stereo audio

MiniMax launched H3, an omni-modal video model that produces 15-second 2K clips with native stereo audio from a unified mix of text, image, video, and audio context. Bundling synchronized audio-visual generation in a single model pushes further into territory that has typically required stitching separate video and audio systems together.
