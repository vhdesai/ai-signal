# Google Maps Adds Agentic Commerce: Food Ordering, Hotel Booking, Event Tickets

Google expanded its "Ask Maps" feature with agentic task-completion: users can order food via Square, Toast, and Uber Eats, book hotels, and buy event tickets directly in the app, alongside a new "Personal Intelligence" layer that draws on Gmail and Calendar for personalized responses. The update transforms Maps from a navigation utility into a transaction layer, directly challenging Yelp, OpenTable, and Uber Eats as the discovery-to-purchase interface. ________________________________ PRODUCT
