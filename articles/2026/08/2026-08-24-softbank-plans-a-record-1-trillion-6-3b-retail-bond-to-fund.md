# SoftBank plans a record ¥1 trillion ($6.3B) retail bond to fund its AI push

SoftBank is preparing what would be Japan’s largest retail bond ever — roughly ¥1 trillion with a seven-year maturity — to help repay a bridge loan backing its OpenAI stake and fund further physical-AI acquisitions. It is the group’s third bond sale this year, on top of a $10 billion credit facility secured earlier in August against its OpenAI holding. Terms are expected in early September at yields above 4%.
