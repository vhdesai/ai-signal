# Hassabis Pitched Independent AI Oversight Body to Labs and U.S. Officials

Demis Hassabis proposed an independent body to codify shared AI safety practices, discussing it with rival lab heads and Trump administration officials including Treasury Secretary Bessent and tech adviser Kratsios. The IAEA analogy faces a harder problem: verifying safety claims about privately developed software from direct competitors. The proposal landed days before Hassabis stepped aside from DeepMind. The fact that conversations involved both rival CEOs and senior government officials suggests progression beyond whiteboard stage. WSJ REGULATION
