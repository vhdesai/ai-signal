# Stanford study finds data brokers are not following California's strict privacy laws

Stanford HAI highlighted an empirical audit, "Privacy Without Remedy," of data-broker compliance with California's CCPA and 2023 Delete Act. It finds only 9% of 522 registered data brokers were fully compliant with transparency requirements, and in an audit of 250 brokers, 43% make it impossible for consumers to exercise all privacy rights while 64% introduce at least one design feature creating substantial friction. The authors attribute the gaps to decentralized self-compliance, weak enforcement and regulatory ambiguity.
