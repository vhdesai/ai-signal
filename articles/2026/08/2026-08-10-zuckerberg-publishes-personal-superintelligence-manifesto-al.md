# Zuckerberg Publishes "Personal Superintelligence" Manifesto Alongside Glimmer Launch

Mark Zuckerberg published a 6,500-word letter arguing Meta will distribute superintelligence widely through free or affordable personal AI agents that work "24/7 on your behalf." Meta distinguishes between openly released models (Glimmer, 30B) and its more powerful closed models (Muse Spark). Analysis notes the framing recycles patterns from earlier social media defenses and proposes dynamic compute auctions — a concept that raises surge-pricing concerns for enterprise deployments.
