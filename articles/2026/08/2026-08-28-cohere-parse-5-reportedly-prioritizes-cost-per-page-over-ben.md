# Cohere Parse 5 reportedly prioritizes cost per page over benchmark wins

VentureBeat reported that Cohere Parse 5 loses a document parsing benchmark on points but wins on cost per page. The framing matters because enterprise document AI often scales by pages processed, not by leaderboard position. Buyers should evaluate extraction accuracy, latency, and unit economics together, especially when parsing becomes a front door for RAG, compliance review, and agent workflows.
