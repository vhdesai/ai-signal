# Amazon to shut Mechanical Turk on September 30 after 21 years

AWS will close Mechanical Turk, launched in 2005, on September 30, 2026, having stopped new sign-ups on July 30 alongside SageMaker Ground Truth and Augmented AI. The shutdown marks the end of the early human-data-labeling era as models increasingly generate and validate their own training data. For enterprises, it removes a long-standing default option for cheap human-in-the-loop annotation.
