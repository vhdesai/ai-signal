# OpenAI updates GPT-Live page after SynthID audio-watermarking rollout

OpenAI's GPT-Live release page was sitemap-updated inside the reporting window and includes a dated July 31 update that supported GPT-Live audio generated through ChatGPT Voice and the OpenAI API now carries SynthID watermarking. GPT-Live is OpenAI's full-duplex voice architecture, designed to listen and speak simultaneously while delegating harder reasoning to frontier models in the background. The watermarking component is strategically important because real-time voice AI raises fraud, impersonation, and provenance risks beyond those of text interfaces.
