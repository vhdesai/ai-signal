# OpenAI President Greg Brockman to Publish Essay on AI Cybersecurity Tipping Point

OpenAI president Greg Brockman is expected to publish an essay today warning of increasing cybersecurity threats from AI and describing a tipping point that "may be underappreciated." He says OpenAI is improving its cyberdefenses as models get smarter and more dangerous, advising: "Over the coming months, every organization will need to begin significantly automating its security program to stay secure." The announcement comes amid accelerating concern about AI-generated attack vectors.
