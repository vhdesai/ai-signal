# Nvidia to pay Poolside $6B for a non-exclusive license and hire ~109 of its staff

Nvidia agreed to pay roughly $6 billion under a non-exclusive technology license with AI coding startup Poolside and to invest a further $1 billion at a reported $12 billion pre-money valuation, while extending offers to about 109 Poolside employees. The structure leaves the original company intact rather than acquiring it. It extends Nvidia beyond silicon into the model and coding-agent layer, and offers a template that sidesteps conventional merger review.
