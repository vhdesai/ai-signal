# Big Manufacturers Find New Demand in Equipping AI Data Centers

The WSJ's daily digest highlighted that traditional big manufacturers are finding significant new demand by pivoting to supply AI data center construction — from electrical switchgear to cooling systems to backup generators. The trend represents a second-order economic effect of the AI buildout, creating unexpected growth for industrial companies far removed from the AI software stack.
