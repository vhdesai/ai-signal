# A16Z Faces DOJ Antitrust Scrutiny Over Board Seats at Databricks, Fivetran

The Justice Department is examining Andreessen Horowitz over board seats it holds at competing data companies Databricks ($190B valuation) and Fivetran. Ben Horowitz sits on Databricks' board; partner Martin Casado is on Fivetran's. Federal prosecutors are investigating potential violations of the Clayton Act's Section 8 prohibition on interlocking directorates — a probe that could have wide repercussions for how VC firms govern their portfolio companies across the AI data infrastructure landscape.
