# OpenAI partners with the American Psychological Association on youth mental health

OpenAI announced a collaboration with the American Psychological Association to “bring psychological science into how we think about responsible AI development and use among young people.” Planned outputs include family-facing resources, guidance for clinicians and school psychologists, and youth convenings. The move responds to intensifying scrutiny of AI’s effects on adolescents.
