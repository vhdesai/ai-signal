# UK’s Olix raises $312M Series B at a $3.3B valuation for optical AI chips

London-based Olix, a two-year-old optical AI-chip startup, closed a $312 million Series B — described as Europe’s largest chip round — tripling its valuation to $3.3 billion in under six months. Founded by 25-year-old James Dacombe and backed in part by a UK government sovereign-AI fund, the company is positioning its photonics-based silicon as an alternative to Nvidia amid the GPU shortage. First products are expected to reach customers next year. FUNDING
