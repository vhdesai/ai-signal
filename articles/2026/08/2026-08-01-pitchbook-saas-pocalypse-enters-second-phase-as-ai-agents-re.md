# PitchBook: "SaaS-pocalypse" Enters Second Phase as AI Agents Replace Software Seats

PitchBook's latest research brief declares that the SaaS disruption cycle has entered its second phase, moving from "AI copilots augmenting SaaS users" to "AI agents eliminating SaaS seats entirely." The thesis builds on Gartner's $234B agentic AI projection from earlier this week, arguing that traditional per-seat SaaS pricing models are structurally misaligned with agentic workflows that require outcomes-based or consumption-based pricing. For enterprise software investors, the implication is clear: incumbents that cannot pivot to agent-native architectures face compression, while a new generation of "agent-first" startups is attracting capital at premium valuations.
