# Nvidia Backs Firebird's New AI Factory in Armenia, Plans 70,000+ GPU Buildout by 2027

AI cloud startup Firebird opened the CIS region's largest AI data center in Hrazdan, Armenia, built on Nvidia Blackwell and Rubin GPUs via the Nvidia DSX platform, with attendance from Armenia's prime minister and senior Kazakhstani and U.S. officials. Firebird plans over 70,000 Nvidia Rubin and Blackwell GPUs and 300 MW of capacity by end-2027, with a roughly 2 GW roadmap across Armenia and Kazakhstan, and Nvidia announced a direct equity investment in the company alongside an earlier CoreWeave stake.
