# Apple in talks to pay publishers for news to power an AI Siri

Apple has approached publishers about multiyear licensing deals, reportedly with a nine-figure budget, to let a revamped AI Siri draw on current news content. The strategy grounds answers in licensed material rather than scraped data as Apple plays catch-up in generative AI. It also sets a market price signal for editorial content at a moment when data-rights disputes are intensifying across the industry.
