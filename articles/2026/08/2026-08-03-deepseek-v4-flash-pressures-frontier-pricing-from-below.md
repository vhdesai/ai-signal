# DeepSeek V4-Flash pressures frontier pricing from below

DeepSeek released V4-Flash, a smaller version of its flagship open-source model, and early testing suggests it performs competitively against larger open and proprietary systems at much lower cost. The Information reported that V4-Flash can rival OpenAI GPT-5.6 Luna and Zhipu GLM-5.2 on several capability measures, with cost per task potentially 60% lower than Luna. The signal for enterprise buyers is that “good enough” low-cost models are becoming a serious default for agentic workloads. URL: The Information search: DeepSeek V4 Flash
