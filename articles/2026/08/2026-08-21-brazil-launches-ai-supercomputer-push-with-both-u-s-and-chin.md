# Brazil Launches AI Supercomputer Push With Both U.S. and Chinese Partners

Brazil announced roughly $444M in AI investments, including approximately $250M for a supercomputing project involving Huawei and iFlytek alongside U.S. participation. The deliberate two-bloc sourcing reflects a sovereign-compute strategy that avoids exclusive alignment with either Washington or Beijing. For multinationals, this raises practical export-control and partner-screening questions in emerging markets.
