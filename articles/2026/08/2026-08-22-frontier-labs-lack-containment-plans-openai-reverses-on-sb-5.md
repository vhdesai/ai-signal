# Frontier Labs Lack Containment Plans; OpenAI Reverses on SB 53

Guidelight AI Standards found leading labs have “few containment protocols ready for an emergency.” Anthropic and Meta scored lowest; OpenAI highest (3/5) but still lacks a formal plan. Separately, OpenAI now calls for strengthening the SB 53 bill it previously opposed. The convergence suggests rapid industry movement toward supporting enforceable safety regulation — at least publicly. ________________________________ RESEARCH STARTUP
