# OpenAI Settles with DOJ Over Green-Card Hiring Practices, Accepts 3 Years of Oversight

The DOJ's Civil Rights Division settled with OpenAI and its divested subsidiary Statsig over alleged Immigration and Nationality Act violations, requiring $3.2M in fines and restitution plus three years of DOJ oversight over PERM-role hiring, including semiannual reporting on foreign-worker petitions and U.S. citizen interviews. The DOJ alleged OpenAI avoided public job postings and used discouraging application methods for U.S. citizens. This is the first major DOJ regulatory action targeting OpenAI's employment practices. ________________________________ POLICYGOVERNANCE
