# Federal judge strikes down the Pentagon's supply-chain-risk designation of Anthropic

US District Judge Rita Lin ruled that the Department of Defense's designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk was unlawful, finding it constituted First Amendment retaliation against a government critic rather than a national-security judgment. The ruling sets aside the designation and rejects the government's invocation of national security as sufficient justification on its own.
