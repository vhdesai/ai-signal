# 2. DeepSeek V4-Flash rated the cheapest well-known model to run

Independent evaluator Artificial Analysis clocked DeepSeek V4-Flash at roughly 3¢ per benchmark suite — against Kimi K3 at 86¢, GPT-5.6 Sol at $1.86, and Claude Fable 5 at $3.15 — with list pricing of $0.14 / $0.28 per million tokens. The result intensifies downward pressure on frontier pricing umbrellas as “good enough” low-cost models capture a growing share of production workloads. NewCybersecurity
