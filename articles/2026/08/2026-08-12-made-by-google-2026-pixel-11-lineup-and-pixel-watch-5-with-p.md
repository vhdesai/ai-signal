# Made by Google 2026: Pixel 11 lineup and Pixel Watch 5 with proactive Gemini

Google unveiled the Pixel 11, 11 Pro, and Pro XL, a Pixel Pro Fold, and Pixel Watch 5, headlined by "proactive" Gemini features including HiLight, sign-language transcription, and more natural voice input, plus AI-corrected GPS. Notably, the bundled AI Pro trial on the 11 Pro was cut from twelve months to six. The event underscores Google's use of vertical integration across hardware, Android, and Gemini as a consumer distribution advantage.
