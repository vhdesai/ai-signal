# Amazon triples AI chip orders as its robotaxi supplier network expands

Amazon is reported to have roughly tripled its AI chip orders while broadening the supplier base underpinning its autonomous-vehicle programs. The move extends the pattern of hyperscalers securing multi-year silicon capacity well ahead of realized demand. It also reinforces Amazon's dual-track strategy of buying merchant accelerators while scaling in-house Trainium capacity. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/amazon-com-amzn-triples-ai-180950072.html MARKETS
