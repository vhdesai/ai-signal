# CoreWeave Q2: Revenue Doubles to $2.575B While Cash Burn Hits $5.7B

CoreWeave reported Q2 revenue of $2.575 billion, up 112% year-over-year, but cash burn rose by roughly the same percentage to $5.7 billion — highlighting the capital-intensive reality of the neocloud model. Despite the red ink, investors cheered: CoreWeave stock jumped 13% in after-hours trading, with Wall Street focused on forward profitability metrics rather than current cash flow. The company remains the best-known challenger to established cloud providers in the AI infrastructure space.
