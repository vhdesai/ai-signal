# Nvidia Weighs Reducing Memory on Next-Gen Rubin Ultra GPU Due to HBM Shortage

Nvidia is considering reducing the amount of high-bandwidth memory on its next-generation Rubin Ultra GPU. The company has been testing at least three versions of the chip — some with less memory than originally announced — partly due to advanced HBM shortages.
