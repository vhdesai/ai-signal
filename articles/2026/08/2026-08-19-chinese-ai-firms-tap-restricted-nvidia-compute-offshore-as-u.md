# Chinese AI Firms Tap Restricted Nvidia Compute Offshore as U.S. Weighs Cloud Crackdown

Chinese labs including Moonshot AI have accessed restricted Nvidia GB300-class compute through data centers in Thailand, Malaysia, and Japan — legal because U.S. export controls govern physical chip ownership, not remote access. The Remote Access Security Act passed the House in January but remains stalled in the Senate. Analysts link this offshore compute access directly to recent capability gains in Chinese models, exposing a material gap in the current export-control framework.
