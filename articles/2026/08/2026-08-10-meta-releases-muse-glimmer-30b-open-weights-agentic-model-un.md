# Meta Releases Muse Glimmer — 30B Open-Weights Agentic Model Under Apache 2.0

Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30B-parameter multimodal model distilled from its closed Muse Spark, designed to run always-on AI agents locally on consumer hardware. At 4-bit quantization it fits in 24GB VRAM (1.0% benchmark degradation). A DFlash block-diffusion drafter achieves 3.1× decode speedup — 233 tok/s on RTX 5090, 50 tok/s on M5 Max. Beats Gemma4-31B and Qwen3.6-27B on MCP Atlas (75.5), DeepSearch QA (74.6), and SWE-Bench Pro (51.2); trails on OSWorld and TerminalBench. Apache 2.0 with HuggingFace weights, GGUF quantizations, and ExecuTorch builds. RELEASE OPENAI CYBER
