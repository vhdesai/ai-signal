# China’s Zhipu (Z.ai) Posts ~5x Revenue Growth on API and Coding-Plan Demand

Hong Kong-listed Zhipu (Z.ai) reported first-half 2026 revenue up nearly fivefold year-on-year to 954 million yuan (about $142 million), with open-platform and API revenue surging more than 28-fold to 825 million yuan. Losses narrowed 12% to 2.07 billion yuan. Growth was driven by its GLM-5.1 and GLM-5.2 models; the newly released GLM-5.3 Flash charges $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, intensifying a Chinese price war that may compress second-half margins.
