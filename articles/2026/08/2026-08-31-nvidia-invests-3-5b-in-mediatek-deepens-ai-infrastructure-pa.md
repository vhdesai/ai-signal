# Nvidia Invests $3.5B in MediaTek, Deepens AI Infrastructure Partnership

Nvidia and MediaTek announced an expanded collaboration spanning custom AI infrastructure, local AI computing, and automotive platforms, anchored by Nvidia’s $3.5B investment in MediaTek convertible bonds. MediaTek will adopt Nvidia’s NVLink Fusion platform to help hyperscalers build custom XPUs that connect into Nvidia rack-scale AI factories. The move extends Nvidia’s role from accelerator supplier toward chip-to-rack system architecture.
