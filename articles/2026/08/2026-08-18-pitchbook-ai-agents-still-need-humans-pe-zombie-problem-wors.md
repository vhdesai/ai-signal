# PitchBook: AI Agents Still Need Humans; PE Zombie Problem Worsens

PitchBook's daily roundup highlights that "agents need humans, too" — reflecting growing industry recognition that agentic AI deployments require substantial human oversight and exception handling in production. Separately, PE's zombie fund problem is worsening: roughly 40% of PE-backed US companies (representing $860B+ in NAV) have been held for more than seven years, the highest level since 2016 and the third consecutive annual increase. Private market returns, however, just had their best quarter since 2020.
