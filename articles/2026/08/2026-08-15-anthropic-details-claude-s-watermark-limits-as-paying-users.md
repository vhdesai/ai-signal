# Anthropic details Claude's watermark limits as paying users churn

Anthropic published a technical explainer of the invisible text watermark now shipped with Claude outputs, describing it as reader-imperceptible and cost-neutral but narrowly bounded: detection weakens sharply on factual passages and code, and disappears when Claude proofreads human-written text or when output is fully rewritten. Anthropic frames the mark as evidence Claude "likely contributed," not proof of authorship. The rollout — driven by the EU AI Act's transparency requirements — has produced visible cancellations among Claude Max subscribers, and a secondary market of watermark-removal apps has already drawn scam warnings. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-shares-more-details-about-how-claudes-new-watermarks-will-work/
