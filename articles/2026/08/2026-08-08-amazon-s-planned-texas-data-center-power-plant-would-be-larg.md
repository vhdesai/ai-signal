# Amazon's Planned Texas Data Center Power Plant Would Be Largest U.S. Climate Polluter

Amazon is investing in an on-site natural gas power plant for a Pecos County, Texas data center permitted to release 33 million tons of CO₂ per year — more than any existing U.S. power plant. Amazon's carbon emissions rose 16% last year. The company acknowledged "the world looks different now than when we co-founded the Climate Pledge."
