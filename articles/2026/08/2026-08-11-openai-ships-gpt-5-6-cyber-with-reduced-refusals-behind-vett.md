# OpenAI Ships GPT-5.6-Cyber With Reduced Refusals Behind Vetted Access Tier

A fine-tune of GPT-5.6 Sol for vulnerability research with deliberately reduced refusals. Completes 95% of exploit-chain prompts (vs. 1.5% for Sol) and surfaced Chrome V8 CVE-2026-15903. Access gated behind “Daybreak Red” tier requiring identity verification, SSO/MFA, logging, and SOC 2/ISO 27001 certification. The governance question is no longer capability but authorization and controls. The Hacker News | VentureBeat LAUNCH OPEN WEIGHTS
