# OpenAI Completes $7B Employee Tender Offer at $852B Valuation

OpenAI completed a secondary sale allowing employees to sell roughly $7 billion in shares, according to Bloomberg. The transaction was priced at an $852 billion valuation — flat to its March primary round, which added $122 billion in fresh capital. A flat secondary at this scale is a retention instrument rather than a repricing event, and the absence of a markup is itself a data point on where private AI valuations have settled. FUNDING
