# Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max Claims Frontier-Level Agentic Computer Use

Alibaba moved Qwen3.8-Max to general availability — a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model accepting text, image and video input across a 1M-token context — and claims it outperforms leading Western frontier models on agentic computer-use tasks, including autonomously completing software projects spanning more than 10 days. Open weights are indicated to follow, and Alibaba shares rose on the announcement. The claims are vendor-reported and lack an independent benchmark table, so treat the capability assertions as unverified. The pricing implication is the more actionable point: a credible 2.4T-parameter competitor at published per-token rates puts sustained pressure on frontier-tier contract pricing.
