# Sony Music and Warner sue Anthropic over alleged music IP theft

Sony Music Publishing, Warner Chappell, and other music publishers sued Anthropic and co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann, alleging a "brazen campaign" of torrenting, scraping, and downloading copyrighted works to train Claude. Anthropic told TechCrunch it disagrees with the claims and will defend itself. The case expands AI copyright pressure beyond books and web text into lyrics and sheet music, increasing the importance of training-data provenance and licensing strategy. AI BUDGETSENTERPRISE IT
