# AI Stock Pullback Barely Dents Broader Market — But Bubble Risks Persist

WSJ columnist James Mackintosh observes that the latest pullback in AI-related stocks has scarcely affected the broader market, arguing that America's boom-and-bust cycles are becoming easier to absorb — for now. The analysis notes that while individual AI names have experienced sharp corrections (Situational Awareness's 67% drawdown, Apple's 7% post-earnings drop), the S&P 500 remains resilient because AI capex is flowing through a wide ecosystem of beneficiaries: cloud providers, chip designers, memory manufacturers, data center builders, and energy companies. The warning, however, is that concentration risk remains elevated, and a synchronized correction across hyperscalers could propagate differently.
