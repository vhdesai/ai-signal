# PitchBook: Olix raises Europe's largest chip round for optical AI silicon

PitchBook reported that UK optical AI-chip startup Olix raised a $312 million Series B at a $3.3 billion valuation, described as Europe's largest chip round. The company is positioning photonics-based silicon as a potential alternative amid GPU scarcity and sovereign-AI priorities.
