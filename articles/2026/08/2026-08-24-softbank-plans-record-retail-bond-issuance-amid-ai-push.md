# SoftBank plans record retail bond issuance amid AI push

The Wall Street Journal reported that SoftBank plans a record retail bond issuance as it continues its AI investment push. The item highlights how AI infrastructure and platform bets are spilling into broader financing markets, including retail-accessible debt.
