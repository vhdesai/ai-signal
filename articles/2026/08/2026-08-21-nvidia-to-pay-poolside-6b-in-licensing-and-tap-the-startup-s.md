# Nvidia to pay Poolside $6B in licensing and tap the startup's staff

Nvidia agreed to pay roughly $6B under a non-exclusive technology licence with AI coding startup Poolside and to invest a further $1B at a $12B pre-money valuation, with about 109 Poolside employees receiving Nvidia offers. The company is not acquiring Poolside, which continues to operate independently. The structure — licence plus acqui-hire plus minority stake — gives Nvidia model-layer IP and scarce engineering talent while sidestepping conventional merger review, and is likely to be copied by other cash-rich acquirers.
