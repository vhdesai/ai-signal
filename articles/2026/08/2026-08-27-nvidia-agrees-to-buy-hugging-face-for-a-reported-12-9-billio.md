# Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for a reported $12.9 billion

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face, the repository hosting more than one million open-source AI models, for roughly $12.9 billion. The deal would push Nvidia well beyond silicon into the distribution layer of the open-model ecosystem, deepening its integration with the developer community that consumes its hardware. Follow-on coverage from Ars Technica and Forbes on August 27–28 framed the central enterprise question as dependency risk: a single vendor would own both the dominant accelerator and the default model registry.
