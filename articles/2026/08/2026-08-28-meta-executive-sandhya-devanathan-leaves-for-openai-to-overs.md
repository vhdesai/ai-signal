# Meta Executive Sandhya Devanathan Leaves for OpenAI to Oversee Southeast Asia

Meta's Sandhya Devanathan is joining OpenAI to oversee operations across Southeast Asia and Australia, as Meta faces growing regulatory scrutiny in India. The hire reflects OpenAI's aggressive expansion in emerging markets (where it recently launched ChatGPT ads and continues to scale its user base beyond the U.S.). 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/meta-executive-leaves-for-openai-as-the-social-media-giant-faces-growing-scrutiny-in-india/
