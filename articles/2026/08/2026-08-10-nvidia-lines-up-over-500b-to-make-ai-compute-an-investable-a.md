# Nvidia lines up over $500B to make AI compute an investable asset class

Nvidia signed memoranda of understanding with Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to mobilize more than $500 billion, enabling customers to finance data centers and GPUs off balance sheet. Jensen Huang framed it as the first time technology chips have become an investable asset class, calling them revenue-generating assets. It is a direct response to July's AI-capex anxiety and reshapes how buyers fund compute.
