# Anthropic signs a $9.1B, 20-year, 191 MW compute deal with Riot Platforms

Anthropic locked in a 20-year co-location agreement worth roughly $9.1 billion for 191 MW of capacity at Riot Platforms' Rockdale, Texas campus, running through 2048 and worth up to approximately $16.1 billion with extensions. Bitcoin miner Riot's stock jumped between 17% and 25% on the news. Capacity phases in with 96 MW by December 2027 and the full 191 MW by June 2028, deepening Anthropic's run of long-term compute and power commitments. Read at The Next Web / 24/7 Wall St. →
