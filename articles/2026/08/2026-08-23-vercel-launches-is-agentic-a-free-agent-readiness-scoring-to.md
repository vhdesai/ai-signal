# Vercel launches ‘Is Agentic’, a free agent-readiness scoring tool for public websites

Vercel released a free tool that audits public websites against more than 100 checks from Ora and returns an agent-readiness score. The premise is that sites now have two audiences — humans and autonomous agents — and most are optimized only for the first. Expect “agent readiness” to become a recurring line item in web and commerce roadmaps.
