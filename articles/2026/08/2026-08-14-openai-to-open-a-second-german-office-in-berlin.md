# OpenAI to open a second German office in Berlin

Sam Altman told Handelsblatt that OpenAI will add a Berlin site alongside its Munich office, and said the company favors building its own data centers in Germany. Altman characterized Germany as a top-three global market for ChatGPT. The move reflects the sovereign-compute positioning increasingly required to sell into European public sector and regulated industries. finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-plans-open-office-berlin-101838563.html
