# OpenAI Bans Russia-Origin Accounts Running a Fake Israeli Think Tank Influence Operation

OpenAI disclosed that it banned a cluster of ChatGPT accounts it assesses as very likely Russian in origin, which used VPNs to generate content for a fabricated Israel-based think tank and a "sovereignty index" ranking Russia above the countries criticizing its war. The operation published AI-generated research misattributed to real academics and pushed material across major social platforms. The case illustrates that platform-level abuse detection, not model refusal behavior, is where influence operations are actually caught. ________________________________ PRIVACY
