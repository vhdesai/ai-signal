# Meta Removed More Than 50 Ads Containing AI-Generated Child Abuse Imagery After Press Inquiry

Meta's advertising systems distributed more than 50 ads containing AI-generated child sexual abuse imagery across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and Threads from late 2025 into this week, per the company's own ad library, and removed them only after a press inquiry. The failure is in automated ad review at scale, not in content generation alone. It is the most concrete near-term argument yet for the synthetic-media provenance and ad-screening obligations now moving through multiple regulatory tracks.
