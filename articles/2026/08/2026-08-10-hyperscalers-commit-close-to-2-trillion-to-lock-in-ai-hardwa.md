# Hyperscalers commit close to $2 trillion to lock in AI hardware and memory supply

Reported hyperscaler commitments to secure AI compute and memory supply now approach $2 trillion in aggregate, with Alphabet accounting for the largest single share and Apple far behind its peers. The concentration of forward purchase commitments in a handful of buyers gives them structural pricing leverage over memory and accelerator suppliers. It also raises the stakes on utilization: these are multi-year obligations underwritten by demand that has not yet been fully realized in revenue.
