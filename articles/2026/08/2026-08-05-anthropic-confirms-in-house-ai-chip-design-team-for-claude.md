# Anthropic confirms in-house AI chip design team for Claude

Anthropic confirmed it is building an internal team to design custom AI chips, with the stated goal of co-designing hardware and models so Claude runs faster and more efficiently. The company said it will continue to rely on AWS, Google, NVIDIA, and AMD hardware, positioning the effort as complementary rather than a supplier replacement. Related reporting notes the roles are senior, with compensation advertised up to roughly $485K for engineers who have shipped silicon. The move follows the same vertical-integration logic Google and Amazon applied years earlier, and signals that inference unit economics remain the binding constraint on frontier-lab margins.
