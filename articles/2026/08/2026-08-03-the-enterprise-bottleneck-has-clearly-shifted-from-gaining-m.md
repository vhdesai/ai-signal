# The enterprise bottleneck has clearly shifted from gaining model access to embedding AI into actual business workflows…

The enterprise bottleneck has clearly shifted from gaining model access to embedding AI into actual business workflows and operating processes. - Forward-deployed engineers are becoming scarce because successful deployments require deep context on systems, approvals, handoffs, and measurable business outcomes. - This makes AI adoption look more like enterprise transformation consulting than commodity software procurement. - The result is that process redesign, change management, and systems integration are now as valuable as the model itself. - For executives, durable advantage will come from operationalizing AI in priority workflows, not from simply standardizing on a preferred model vendor.
