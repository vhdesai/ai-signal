# Texas pauses new data center approvals pending statewide grid audit

Governor Greg Abbott directed the Public Utility Commission of Texas and ERCOT to audit all data centers seeking grid interconnection, effectively halting new project approvals. Regulators are collecting on- and off-site electricity and water demand, noise mitigation plans, and related data across a pending interconnection queue reported at hundreds of gigawatts. Texas has been the default low-friction siting choice for US AI buildouts, so the pause removes the industry's most permissive large market from the near-term pipeline and raises siting risk premiums nationally.
