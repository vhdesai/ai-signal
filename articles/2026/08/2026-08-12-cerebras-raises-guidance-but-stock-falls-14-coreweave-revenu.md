# Cerebras Raises Guidance but Stock Falls 14%; CoreWeave Revenue Doubles

Two infrastructure earnings in one day paint a nuanced picture. Cerebras raised full-year guidance to ~$880–890M and expects revenue to triple next year, yet shares fell ~14% — illustrating how much growth is already priced into AI-chip challengers competing against Nvidia. CoreWeave reported Q2 revenue of $2.6B (+112% YoY) with backlog near $104B; shares surged to their highest since June. The divergence shows markets treating alternative silicon differently from GPU-native infrastructure: CoreWeave benefits from being the default Nvidia execution layer, while Cerebras must prove its wafer-scale architecture can take share. For buyers, alternative-silicon economics remain unproven even when demand is strong. Cerebras (CNBC) | CoreWeave (CNA) EARNINGS
