# IBM partners with OpenAI to accelerate its enterprise AI push

IBM will stand up a dedicated OpenAI practice inside IBM Consulting, train and certify tens of thousands of consultants, and embed GPT-5.6, Codex and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting Advantage. Financial terms were not disclosed. The deal lands less than a year after IBM's Anthropic alliance, signaling a multi-model posture rather than exclusivity.
