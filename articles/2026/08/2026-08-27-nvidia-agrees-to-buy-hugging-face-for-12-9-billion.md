# Nvidia Agrees to Buy Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion

Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face, the GitHub-like repository of open-source AI models, for $12.9 billion. The deal gives Nvidia control of the central hub for the open-source AI ecosystem, creating a powerful distribution platform that could drive demand for Nvidia hardware. Hugging Face's annualized revenue recently jumped 50% to $150 million. The acquisition follows M&A interest first reported by Business Insider.
