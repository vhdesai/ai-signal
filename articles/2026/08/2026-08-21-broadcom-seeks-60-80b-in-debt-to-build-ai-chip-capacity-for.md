# Broadcom seeks $60–80B in debt to build AI-chip capacity for Anthropic

Broadcom is reported to be raising $60–80B — potentially up to ~$100B — through a special-purpose vehicle to fund custom AI chips for Anthropic and other frontier labs. The structure pairs a roughly $30B junior tranche with a $60–70B senior-secured tranche. It extends a June partnership with Apollo and Blackstone targeting more than 20 GW of compute by 2028, and would rank among the largest private financings ever raised for semiconductor capacity.
