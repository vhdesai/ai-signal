# Gemini 3.7 Flash Rolls Into Google Search AI Mode

Google serving 3.7 Flash in Search AI Mode within days of launch at $0.75/$3.75 per million tokens (50% cut from 3.6 Flash). Moving a new model into Search distribution this quickly signals optimization for cost-per-query at consumer scale. DeepSWE v1.1: 65.3%, WebDev Arena Elo: 1588. The cadence and price are a deliberate squeeze on mid-tier competitors. Sets the price-performance floor for high-volume agent tasks.
