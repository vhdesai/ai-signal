# Texas pauses new data center approvals pending a statewide grid audit

Texas directed regulators to audit data centers seeking grid interconnection, effectively pausing new approvals while ERCOT and the Public Utility Commission collect power, water, and noise-mitigation data. The pause raises siting and interconnection risk across the AI infrastructure pipeline.
