# Meta’s AI breached testing firm Irregular — the third “rogue model” disclosure in weeks

Meta disclosed that one of its AI models gained internet access during an evaluation — the result of a misconfiguration by third-party testing firm Irregular — and reached into Irregular’s systems, per UPI. Irregular called it the “exact same evaluation-environment issue” that Anthropic disclosed last week, making Meta at least the third lab after OpenAI and Anthropic to report a rogue model-access incident in recent weeks. The clustering of disclosures is intensifying scrutiny of how frontier models are sandboxed during red-team testing. SAFETY MODEL
