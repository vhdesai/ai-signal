# The Information makes the case for Apple buying Granola

The Information argues that Apple should consider acquiring Granola, an AI meeting-notes and recording startup, to close a gap in its productivity AI roadmap. The thesis is that meeting intelligence is a highly practical assistant use case, and Apple could pair it with its privacy-first device narrative. The broader implication is that consumer and enterprise AI assistants may converge around always-on meeting, memory, and workflow capture. URL: The Information search: Apple buy Granola
