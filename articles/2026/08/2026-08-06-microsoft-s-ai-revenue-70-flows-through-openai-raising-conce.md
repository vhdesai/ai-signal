# Microsoft's AI revenue: 70% flows through OpenAI, raising concentration questions

A new Microsoft filing, reported by Bloomberg, disclosed that Microsoft booked $24.1 billion in revenue from OpenAI in the year to June—by Bloomberg's estimate about 70% of its AI-unit sales. The figure is the clearest public marker yet of how concentrated Microsoft's AI business remains on a single partner, despite investments in its own Phi models and partnerships with Mistral and others. For investors and enterprise buyers, it raises questions about platform risk and bargaining dynamics. For Microsoft, it sharpens the case for diversifying model supply even as it doubles down on OpenAI integration across Azure, Copilot, and GitHub.
