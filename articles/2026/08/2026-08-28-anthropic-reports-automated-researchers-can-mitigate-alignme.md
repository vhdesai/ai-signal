# Anthropic reports automated researchers can mitigate alignment failures

Anthropic published research showing that Claude-based automated researchers improved models across 10 categories of alignment failures without degrading general capabilities. The system searched literature, proposed methods, trained models, and tested results; Anthropic says the best automated methods outperformed proposals from human safety researchers in some settings and generalized to withheld benchmarks and larger models. The work is an early signal that AI may accelerate alignment research, but it also depends heavily on benchmark quality and monitorability. RESEARCHAGENTS
