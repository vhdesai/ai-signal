# OpenAI materially updates GPT-Live with audio watermarking context

OpenAI's GPT-Live release page was materially updated in the current window according to sitemap evidence, following a dated July 31 update adding SynthID watermarking to supported GPT-Live audio. GPT-Live is OpenAI's full-duplex voice architecture, designed to listen and speak simultaneously while delegating complex reasoning in the background. The watermarking element is strategically important because real-time voice AI raises higher fraud, impersonation, and provenance risks than text chat.
