# OpenAI's "Jalapeño" Chip Beats Nvidia Blackwell on Inference-Efficiency Tests

OpenAI's custom Jalapeño accelerator outperformed Nvidia Blackwell systems on key inference-efficiency benchmarks, according to CNBC, intensifying the shift toward in-house silicon among the largest AI buyers. CNBC frames the result as a direct threat to Nvidia's margin structure, arriving the same day as Nvidia's guidance reset. If custom inference silicon holds a durable efficiency edge, the economics of large-scale serving — not training — become the strategic decision point for buyers. ENERGY
