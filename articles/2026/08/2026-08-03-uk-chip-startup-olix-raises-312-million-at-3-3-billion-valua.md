# UK chip startup Olix raises $312 million at $3.3 billion valuation

Data Center Dynamics reported that UK chip startup Olix raised $312 million at a $3.3 billion valuation, backed by the U.K. government's Sovereign AI venture fund. The financing highlights how governments are using industrial policy and direct investment to build domestic AI compute capacity. Sovereign AI is increasingly moving from policy slogan to capital allocation across chips, data centers, and model infrastructure.
