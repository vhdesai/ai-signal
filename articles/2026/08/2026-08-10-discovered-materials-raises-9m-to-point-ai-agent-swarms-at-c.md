# Discovered Materials raises $9M to point AI agent swarms at cooler chip materials

The Y Combinator–backed startup closed a $9 million seed round led by Lightspeed India Partners, with Peak XV Partners and angel investors including Paul Graham and Gokul Rajaram. Its pipeline uses Anthropic models in a custom harness to generate candidate materials, then runs trained physics models to simulate and filter them — moving from roughly 20 human guesses a day to thousands of agent-generated candidates. Alongside the funding the company published hundreds of candidate materials and a "Material Discovery Bench" to track how frontier models perform on the task. The sober framing matters: the investor leading the round says candidate generation is not the bottleneck — filtering and synthesis are — and no AI-discovered material has yet reached commercial scale.
