# Nvidia to Pay Poolside $6 Billion for Non-Exclusive License and Hire ~109 Staff

Nvidia agreed to pay roughly $6 billion under a non-exclusive technology license with AI coding startup Poolside and to invest a further $1 billion at a $12B pre-money valuation, while extending offers to ~109 employees. The structure leaves the company intact—extending Nvidia beyond silicon into the model layer and offering a template that sidesteps conventional merger review.
