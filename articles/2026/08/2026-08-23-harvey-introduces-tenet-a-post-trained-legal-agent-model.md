# Harvey Introduces Tenet, a Post-Trained Legal Agent Model

Harvey released Harvey Tenet as a research preview, its first post-trained proprietary model, built on Moonshot AI's open-weight Kimi K3 base and post-trained with Fireworks AI using asynchronous reinforcement learning. Harvey reports the model nearly doubles completion on its legal agent benchmark. The notable governance fact: a Chinese-origin base model sitting underneath privileged legal work.
