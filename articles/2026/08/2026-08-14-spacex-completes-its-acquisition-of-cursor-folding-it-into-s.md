# SpaceX completes its acquisition of Cursor, folding it into SpaceXAI

SpaceX has closed its acquisition of Anysphere's Cursor, which will operate inside SpaceXAI alongside the Grok product line following the recent Grok Bot and Grok 4.6 releases. The deal consolidates one of the most widely deployed AI coding environments under an owner whose primary strategic asset is compute and launch infrastructure rather than developer tooling. Enterprises standardized on Cursor should expect roadmap convergence with Grok agents and should revisit data-handling terms under the new parent. Reported transaction values circulating in secondary coverage vary widely and are not independently confirmed. https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/14/spacex-lands-deal-to-likely-purchase-claude-code-and-openai-codex-competitor/
