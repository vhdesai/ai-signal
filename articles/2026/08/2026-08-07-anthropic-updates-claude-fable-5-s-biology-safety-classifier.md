# Anthropic Updates Claude Fable 5's Biology Safety Classifiers, Cuts False Positives ~85%

Anthropic updated the safety classifiers on Claude Fable 5, its frontier production model, reducing false-positive routing on biology-related queries by roughly 85%. Everyday health, educational, and clinical biology queries are now handled directly by Fable 5 rather than automatically escalating to Opus 5, while requests classified as dual-use — virology, toxicology, molecular design — still route to the more restricted model. The update affects all Fable 5 users and enterprise Claude deployments.
