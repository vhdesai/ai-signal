# Stripe Acquires AI Marketplace OpenRouter for $7.5 Billion

Stripe confirmed its acquisition of OpenRouter, the AI model marketplace, for ~$7.5B — a massive jump from its $1.3B valuation in May. OpenRouter aggregates access to hundreds of AI models through a single provider and billing system. Monthly revenue has tripled to ~$13M since April.
