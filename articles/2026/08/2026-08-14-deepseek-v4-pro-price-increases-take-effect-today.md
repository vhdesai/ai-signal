# DeepSeek V4-Pro Price Increases Take Effect Today

DeepSeek’s steep API repricing is now live (August 16) with peak/off-peak tiers replacing flat-rate pricing. Increases reported at 50%–1,100% depending on workload and time of day. V4-Pro at several multiples of V4 Flash. Still cheaper than most Western alternatives, but the cost gap that drove Chinese-model adoption is narrowing materially. The company that proved cheap inference is testing willingness to pay for premium performance. Cost-driven buyers should re-baseline immediately.
