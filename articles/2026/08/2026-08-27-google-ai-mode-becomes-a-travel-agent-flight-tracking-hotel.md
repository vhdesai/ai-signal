# Google AI Mode Becomes a Travel Agent — Flight Tracking, Hotel Booking, Points/Miles

Google expanded AI Mode from search to transactions: flight price tracking (300+ airlines, 180+ countries), hotel booking through Booking.com, Marriott, Hilton, Expedia, and others, plus cost-in-points/miles display. Users can build itineraries and book with Google Pay. The shift from search to commerce positions AI Mode as a full travel agent — a meaningful competitive threat to online travel agencies.
