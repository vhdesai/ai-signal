# The Case That a Cheap Flash Model Should Absorb ~45% of Enterprise AI Volume

VentureBeat examined GLM-5.3-Flash — the low-cost model that surged on OpenRouter — and the argument that it is cheap enough to handle roughly 45% of enterprise AI task volume. The underlying dynamic is that inference costs are collapsing at the low end while capability saturates at the frontier, making routing rather than model choice the primary cost lever. Practical implication: organizations without a tiered routing strategy are likely overpaying for a large share of their workloads. ANALYSISGOVERNANCE
