# AI vendors test outcome-based pricing as Salesforce-style monetization gets harder

OpenAI has begun letting selected large customers pay only when its AI completes a task, starting in customer service, while Salesforce is already grappling with the complexity of usage and outcome-based AI pricing. Vendors such as Intercom, Zendesk, and Salesforce have pushed resolution-based models, but attribution remains operationally messy. If it scales, outcome pricing could reshape how enterprises budget, audit, and govern AI spend.
