# Cisco AI Product Orders Hit $4B in a Single Quarter

Cisco reported $4 billion in AI product orders in fiscal Q4, after $5.3 billion in the prior three quarters combined. Cloud providers are buying more AI networking chips and switches to maximize GPU utilization. AI products generated $4 billion in FY revenue, expected to rise to $7.5 billion next year. Q4 revenue was $17.3 billion (+18% YoY), roughly $500 million above forecast.
