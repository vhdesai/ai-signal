# TechCrunch: Is It Legal to Train AI on Copyrighted Books? It's Complicated

A comprehensive legal analysis finds the AI training copyright landscape remains "all over the place." Key rulings: Judge Alsup's Anthropic decision ($1.5B fine) actually ruled that AI training itself is lawful — penalizing only the piracy method. Judge Bibas in Thomson Reuters v. Ross ruled against fair use when AI directly competes with the source material. Attorney Cathy Gellis notes the $1.5B fine is "generally good news for AI training" since it distinguishes "reading" from "copying." The article also notes Thaler v. Perlmutter: 100% AI-generated works are not copyrightable. Copyright law hasn't been updated since 1976. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/23/is-it-legal-to-train-ai-models-on-copyrighted-books-its-complicated/
