# OpenAI Moves to Cut Cursor's Access to Its Models Following SpaceX Acquisition

OpenAI is proceeding with termination of Cursor's model access after SpaceX completed its reported $60 billion acquisition of the AI coding platform on August 14. Coverage on August 31 puts the cutoff date at November 12, 2026, following OpenAI's own August 28 post explaining its decision.
