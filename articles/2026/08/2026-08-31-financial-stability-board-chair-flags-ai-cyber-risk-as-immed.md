# Financial Stability Board chair flags AI cyber risk as immediate stability concern

Nairametrics reported that the Financial Stability Board chair identified advanced AI's effect on cyber risk as the most immediate AI-related concern for global financial stability. The framing matters because it moves AI governance from broad model-safety debates into operational resilience, incident response, and systemic-risk supervision for financial institutions. For regulated enterprises, the likely pressure point is demonstrable control over AI-enabled cyber exposure and third-party AI dependencies. SAFETYAGENT SECURITY
