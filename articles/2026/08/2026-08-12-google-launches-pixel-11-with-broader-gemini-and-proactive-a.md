# Google launches Pixel 11 with broader Gemini and proactive AI features

Google used its Made by Google 2026 event to unveil the Pixel 11 lineup, Pixel Watch 5, Pixel Tag, and new Gemini-powered device features. The AI updates include American Sign Language support in Live Transcribe via Pixel Camera, a voice-input feature called Rambler for less-structured speech, more direct Circle to Search access from the camera, and proactive contextual suggestions across apps. The launch shows Google continuing to use vertical integration across hardware, Android, Gemini, and on-device AI as a consumer distribution advantage. ACCESSIBILITYMULTIMODAL AI
