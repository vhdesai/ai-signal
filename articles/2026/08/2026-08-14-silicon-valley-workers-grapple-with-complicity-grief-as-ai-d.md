# Silicon Valley Workers Grapple with "Complicity Grief" as AI Disrupts Their Own Jobs

A combination of layoffs, AI advancement, and massive paydays has Silicon Valley workers questioning everything. Bay Area therapists report a phenomenon called "complicity grief" — tech workers watching their own inventions displace colleagues. The WSJ daily digest flagged the trend alongside the broader narrative that AI models released late last year fundamentally changed what many believed was possible, compressing years of expected disruption into weeks.
