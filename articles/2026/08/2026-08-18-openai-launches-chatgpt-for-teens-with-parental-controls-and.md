# OpenAI Launches ChatGPT for Teens With Parental Controls and Study Mode

OpenAI rolled out a dedicated ChatGPT experience for users aged 13–17, with tighter restrictions on sexual or romantic roleplay, graphic violence and self-harm content. Study Mode provides guiding questions instead of direct answers, and parents can set quiet hours and receive safety notifications. Users identified as minors are placed into the experience automatically. The launch — years after teens became a core user segment — is a pre-emptive regulatory move as much as a product one.
