# AI Accounting Startup Rillet Reaches Unicorn Status in a 48-Hour Round

Rillet closed a $100M round that took it to unicorn valuation in roughly two days, an unusually compressed process even by current standards. The company applies AI to general-ledger and close workflows, a back-office category where measurable cycle-time reduction is easier to underwrite than in open-ended assistant products. The speed of the raise is a useful indicator of how investors are pricing applied-AI software with clear operational metrics. ANALYSIS
