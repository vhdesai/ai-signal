# Pre-IPO Stock Trading Enters Scaling Era as Wall Street Partners With Private Marketplaces

Morgan Stanley, Charles Schwab, and other established firms are acquiring pre-IPO trading platforms or striking fintech partnerships to bring retail investors into private market deals. "The established firms are realizing this is a market that's here to stay and not just a five-year fad," said Monark Markets CEO Ben Haber. The trend signals deepening conviction about a convergence of public and private markets, with AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI among the most sought-after pre-IPO names.
