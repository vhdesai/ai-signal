# Black Hat USA 2026 Vendors Converge on Agentic SOC and AI Defense Tooling

The second wave of Black Hat USA vendor announcements centered almost entirely on AI: agentic security operations, automated red-teaming and AI-native detection from vendors including Palo Alto Networks, SentinelOne and Abnormal AI. The concentration of launches around agent monitoring rather than model guardrails signals where practitioners believe the exploitable surface now sits. Buyers should expect significant feature overlap and short-lived differentiation across this cohort.
