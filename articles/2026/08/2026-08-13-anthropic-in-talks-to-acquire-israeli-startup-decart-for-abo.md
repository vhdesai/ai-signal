# Anthropic in Talks to Acquire Israeli Startup Decart for About $6 Billion

Anthropic is in early-stage talks to acquire Decart AI, which builds GPU optimization, real-time generative video, and world-model technology, for roughly $6 billion. It would be by far Anthropic's largest acquisition, and reporting indicates Decart's team would fold into Anthropic's inference organization to lower serving costs ahead of a potential IPO. Decart previously raised approximately $300M with Nvidia among its investors.
