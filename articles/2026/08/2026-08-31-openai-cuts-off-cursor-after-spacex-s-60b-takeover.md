# OpenAI cuts off Cursor after SpaceX's $60B takeover

Forbes reported that OpenAI plans to end Cursor's direct model access on November 12 following SpaceX's acquisition of Anysphere, Cursor's parent company. Cursor's CEO said OpenAI models account for about 5% of Cursor traffic, suggesting the company has reduced dependency through Anthropic access and its own coding models. The story is strategically important because it tests whether AI application companies can commoditize frontier model supply rather than remain captive to one lab. Infrastructure INFRASTRUCTUREHOT
