# The Custom Silicon Behind Waymo's Robotaxi Economics

TechCrunch Mobility detailed the custom inference chip underpinning Waymo's robotaxi scaling plans, alongside news that Bedrock Robotics now has excavators running fully autonomously at three commercial sites. Autonomy economics increasingly hinge on purpose-built inference silicon rather than general-purpose GPUs. Custom accelerators at the edge remain the most credible route to unit-cost viability in physical AI.
