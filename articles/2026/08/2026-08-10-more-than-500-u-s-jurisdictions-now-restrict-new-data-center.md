# More Than 500 U.S. Jurisdictions Now Restrict New Data Centers

More than 500 U.S. towns and counties have restricted new data center development, with over 150 jurisdictions adding limits in July alone. New York imposed a one-year statewide moratorium on large facilities and Texas paused approvals. Combined with the financing announcements above, the picture is a widening gap between capital availability and permitted land — siting risk, not capital, is now the binding constraint on compute expansion.
