# SemiAnalysis' Dylan Patel Targets $400 Million Venture Capital Fund

Dylan Patel, the semiconductor analyst who built SemiAnalysis into one of the most influential voices in AI infrastructure research, is raising a $400 million venture capital fund focused on AI hardware and infrastructure startups. The move from analyst to investor reflects a broader pattern: the AI infrastructure boom is creating a new class of domain-expert investors who can leverage deep technical knowledge to identify opportunities that generalist VCs miss. For the AI chip and infrastructure ecosystem, Patel's fund could accelerate investment in the custom silicon, networking, and cooling startups that sit between hyperscalers and commodity hardware.
