# OpenAI acquires presentation startup NextSlide; team joins ChatGPT

OpenAI has acquired NextSlide, a startup that turns prompts, notes, and documents into editable presentations, with the team now working on ChatGPT. Terms were not disclosed. Founder Ahmed Beshry noted the deal actually closed earlier this year and the announcement was several months late. The move extends ChatGPT further into document and productivity surfaces long held by incumbent office suites.
