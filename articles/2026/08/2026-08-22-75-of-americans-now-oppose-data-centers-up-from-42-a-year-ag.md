# 75% of Americans Now Oppose Data Centers — Up From 42% a Year Ago

A full 75% of Americans now oppose data centers, up from 42% a year ago (Embold Research/HeatMap Pro). Opposition outweighs support by 43 pts among Republicans, 65 among independents, and 75 among Democrats. Texas halted 1,800 DC constructions; Pennsylvania restricted expansion via executive order. The researcher compared the anti-DC sentiment to the fervor that led to Prohibition.
