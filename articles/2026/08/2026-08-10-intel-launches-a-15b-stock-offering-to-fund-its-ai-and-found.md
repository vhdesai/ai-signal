# Intel launches a $15B stock offering to fund its AI and foundry push

Intel disclosed a $15 billion underwritten common-stock offering, with a $2.25 billion option, to fund physical AI, purpose-built silicon, advanced packaging and external wafer capacity. Shares fell roughly 4% on dilution concerns. Combined with the Nvidia financing news, it underlines how much fresh capital the compute buildout is now absorbing.
