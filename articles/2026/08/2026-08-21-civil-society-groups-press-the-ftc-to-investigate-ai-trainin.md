# Civil-society groups press the FTC to investigate AI training-data book acquisition

Advocacy groups are urging the FTC to investigate AI firms that buy, scan and destroy physical books to build training corpora. The same digest flags Micron Research Labs’ $10B post-DRAM memory research effort and a study linking generative-AI exposure to lower wages. Reported via aggregator, so treat the details as preliminary.
