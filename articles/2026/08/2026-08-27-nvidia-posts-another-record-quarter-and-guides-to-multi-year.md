# Nvidia posts another record quarter and guides to multi-year AI growth

Nvidia reported quarterly revenue of $96.22 billion for the period ending July 2026, above the roughly $91.90 billion consensus, and issued forward guidance above estimates. Management framed AI-driven demand as durable into fiscal 2028, with commentary pointing to approximately 70% revenue growth over that horizon — a materially more aggressive stance than sell-side models assumed. Shares rose on the print. For planning purposes, the guidance implies continued tight supply and sustained pricing power in accelerated compute through at least the next two budget cycles.
