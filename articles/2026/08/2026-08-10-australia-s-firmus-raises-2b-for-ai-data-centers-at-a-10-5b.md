# Australia's Firmus raises $2B for AI data centers at a $10.5B valuation

Firmus, an Australian AI infrastructure operator, raised US$2 billion at a valuation above US$10.5 billion to expand data center capacity across Asia-Pacific. The round reflects investor appetite for sovereign and regional compute outside the U.S. hyperscaler footprint. Asia-Pacific capacity remains structurally short relative to demand. Enterprises with data-residency constraints in the region should track this class of operator.
