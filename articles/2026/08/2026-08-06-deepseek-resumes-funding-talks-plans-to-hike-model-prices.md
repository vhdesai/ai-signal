# DeepSeek Resumes Funding Talks, Plans to Hike Model Prices

DeepSeek has resumed fundraising discussions and plans to increase pricing on its models, signaling a pivot from the aggressive price-cutting strategy that defined its market entry. The move suggests that even the most cost-competitive Chinese AI labs face economic pressure to generate sustainable revenue as model training and inference costs grow. For enterprise buyers who adopted DeepSeek models based on low pricing, the planned hikes introduce vendor risk and underscore the importance of multi-model procurement strategies that avoid dependency on any single provider's pricing.
