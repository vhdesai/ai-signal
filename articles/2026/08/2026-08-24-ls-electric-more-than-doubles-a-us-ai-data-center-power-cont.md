# LS Electric more than doubles a US AI data center power contract to $166M

South Korea's LS Electric said a June contract to supply a 38-kV high-voltage distribution system to a North American Big Tech customer's AI data center was expanded by about $90.3M to $165.72M, a 117% increase, running through January 30, 2027. The expansion exceeded the company's mandatory disclosure threshold of roughly 2.5% of 2025 consolidated revenue. LS Electric attributed the follow-on order to delivery performance on the initial project and operates plants in Utah and Bastrop, Texas.
