# Brazil commits ~$444M to AI supercomputing, split between Chinese and U.S. partners

Brazil announced roughly $444M in AI infrastructure investment, including about $250M for a supercomputing project involving Huawei and iFlytek, with a separate U.S.-led project expected. The deliberate split reflects a strategy of building domestic compute capacity without committing exclusively to either technology bloc. For multinationals, Brazil is an early template for how large emerging markets will extract investment from both sides of the U.S.–China divide — and a reminder that export-control exposure is becoming a country-by-country question.
