# Sony Music and Warner Sue Anthropic, Alleging 'Brazen Campaign' of IP Theft

Sony Music Publishing, Warner Chappell, and other publishers sued Anthropic and co-founders Dario and Benjamin Mann, accusing the lab of "blatant theft" and "flagrant piracy" through illegal torrenting to train Claude. The suit — filed in N.D. California — is "particularly broad," specifically targeting lyrics and sheet music in torrented books. Anthropic said it will "defend robustly."
