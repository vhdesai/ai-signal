# OpenAI Received ~$5.5B in SB Energy Warrants Ahead of SoftBank Data-Center IPO

Draft IPO documents show SB Energy — the SoftBank venture powering OpenAI's Stargate build-out — granted OpenAI roughly $5.5 billion in warrants, up from $3.6 billion in January, to anchor the Cincinnati-area Ports-Pike data-center site. SB Energy is targeting a U.S. listing as soon as this week, seeking $5–7 billion. It is the clearest example yet of AI-lab equity being used as currency to lock in compute capacity.
