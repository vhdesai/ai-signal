# CoreWeave expands AI cloud platform to Indonesia

AiThority reports that CoreWeave announced three Indonesian data-center facilities totaling 360MW of contracted IT power, marking its first Asia-Pacific data-center footprint. The facilities are expected online in 2028 and expand CoreWeave's global AI infrastructure platform beyond its current U.S. and European concentration.
