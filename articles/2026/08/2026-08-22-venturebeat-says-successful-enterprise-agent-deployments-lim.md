# VentureBeat says successful enterprise agent deployments limit autonomy

VentureBeat reported that enterprises seeing success with AI agents are limiting how much those agents can do alone. The takeaway aligns with other operational signals: agents create value when scoped to bounded workflows, routed through permissions, and paired with escalation paths rather than granted broad authority. For executives, agent strategy should focus less on full autonomy and more on constrained delegation with measurable controls.
