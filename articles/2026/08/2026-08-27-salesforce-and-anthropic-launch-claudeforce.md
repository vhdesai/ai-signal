# Salesforce and Anthropic launch “Claudeforce”

Salesforce and Anthropic expanded their partnership with Claudeforce, embedding Salesforce data, workflows, and actions directly inside Claude. The first product, Salesforce in Claude, ships to pilot customers with 37 prebuilt sales skills covering meeting prep, pipeline analysis, and deal health, with an open beta targeted for September. Analysts read it as a step away from model-agnostic enterprise AI toward deeper single-vendor coupling.
