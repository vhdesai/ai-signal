# OpenAI Locks In 10-Gigawatt Ohio Data Center Lease With Nvidia Backing

OpenAI has signed a 10-gigawatt data-center deal in Ohio with SoftBank's SB Energy, partly backed by Nvidia. The project is expected to become one of the largest artificial intelligence hubs to date. This follows The Information's exclusive report last week that Nvidia is negotiating a $3 billion equity investment in SB Energy alongside discussions over ~$100 billion in credit support for the campus. SB Energy plans to IPO as soon as next month.
