# Google DeepMind Leadership Overhaul: Hassabis Steps Back as CEO, Koray Kavukcuoglu Elevated, Dean's Startup Pitch Deck Goes Public

Demis Hassabis is ceding day-to-day CEO duties at Google DeepMind to become Alphabet's chief scientist and DeepMind's non-executive chairman, with Koray Kavukcuoglu elevated to SVP overseeing Gemini model development and reporting directly to Sundar Pichai. Separately, Jeff Dean publicly shared the pitch deck for Discovery Loop, the AI-for-scientific-discovery startup he is co-founding with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals after departing Google; the deck highlights the founding team's role building Google Search, Ads, Gmail, Gemini, and TPUs, and lists Khosla Ventures and Radical Ventures as co-leads with Alphabet itself participating. Together these are the most consequential Google AI leadership events in years, following Gemini delivery delays and a wave of senior researcher exits. ________________________________ INDUSTRYLEGAL
