# AI Coding Valuations Accelerate: Cognition $40B, Lovable $13.3B, Blacksmith $550M

Three funding events in one day paint a clear picture. Cognition (Devin) is negotiating at ~$40B, months after $26B, tied to ~$1B ARR with customers including Mercedes-Benz, NASA, and Goldman Sachs. Lovable closed $400M at $13.3B after ~$500M ARR, with 60M hosted projects and 900M monthly visitors. Blacksmith raised $45M at $550M — nearly 10× in under a year — as AI-generated code increases validation workload. Taken together, investors are underwriting agentic coding as a durable, enterprise-scale revenue category, and testing infrastructure is emerging as a critical second-order investment theme.
