# Google Wins Spirit Airlines’ Bankrupt Data Estate for $10M

Google outbid rivals for ~100M emails, 500M Teams messages, 30M lines of code, and 175K employee records dating to 1986 (PII to be scrubbed). Google says data supports AI model development. Establishes a live market price for distressed enterprise data estates — a consideration that belongs in M&A and wind-down planning. Any data-rich organization should note its digital estate has acquisition value for AI training. TechSpot CONSOLIDATION
