# OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads reached a $1B annualized run rate in under 200 days

OpenAI disclosed that its advertising business, launched earlier this year, has reached a $1B annualized revenue run rate with tens of thousands of advertisers across 40-plus countries. Self-serve Ads Manager access is expanding into India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa. The milestone establishes a consumer-scale, non-subscription revenue line that partially decouples OpenAI's economics from seat-based enterprise licensing. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/open-ai-chatgpt-ads-revenue.html
