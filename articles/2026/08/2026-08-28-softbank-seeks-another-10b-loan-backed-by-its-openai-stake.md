# SoftBank seeks another $10B loan backed by its OpenAI stake

SoftBank is seeking a further ~$10B margin loan tied to its OpenAI stake, only weeks after closing a similarly sized facility, to fund and refinance its position. The borrowing underscores rising leverage across the AI-infrastructure financing chain. Credit analysts have begun flagging the concentration risk of AI-collateralized debt.
