# Hyperscalers reported to have committed nearly $2 trillion to AI hardware and memory

Reporting compiled Monday puts cumulative hyperscaler commitments for AI hardware and memory supply at close to $2 trillion, with Google described as the largest single committer and Apple the smallest of the major buyers. The figures reflect multi-year supply agreements rather than single-year spend, so they should not be read as annual capex. The concentration of memory pre-buys is the more consequential detail: it prices out smaller buyers and lengthens lead times across the market.
