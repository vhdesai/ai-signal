# Google advances AMIE toward expert-level audio-visual clinical consultations

Google introduced AMIE (Video), a real-time video medical-consultation AI built on Gemini and Project Astra using an asynchronous three-agent design spanning Talker, Planner and Perception roles. In a randomized OSCE-style study covering 100 scenarios, 300 live consultations and 30 board-certified primary care physicians, evaluators rated AMIE (Video) on par with physicians for diagnosis, history-taking, management and communication, and higher at eliciting physical signs. The team notes the study used trained patient actors rather than real patients, so real-world clinical validation remains outstanding. Read at Google Research / Google DeepMind Blog →
