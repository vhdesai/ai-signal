# Anthropic in Talks to Acquire Decart for ~$6B to Cut Inference Costs

Anthropic is in early-stage talks to acquire Decart AI, which builds GPU optimization, real-time generative video, and world-model technology, for roughly $6 billion. It would be Anthropic's largest acquisition by far, and reporting indicates Decart's team would fold into Anthropic's inference organization to lower serving costs ahead of the potential IPO. Decart previously raised approximately $300M with Nvidia among its investors. The strategic logic is clear: inference cost is the margin lever that determines whether Anthropic's revenue growth converts to a sustainable public-company P&L. Talks are unconfirmed and could collapse, but the reported price signals how seriously Anthropic is investing in inference economics as a competitive moat.
