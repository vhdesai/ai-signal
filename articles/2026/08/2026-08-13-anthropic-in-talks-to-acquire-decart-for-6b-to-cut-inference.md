# Anthropic in Talks to Acquire Decart for ~$6B to Cut Inference Costs

Anthropic is in early-stage talks to acquire Decart AI, which builds GPU optimization, real-time generative video, and world-model technology, for roughly $6B. It would be Anthropic’s largest acquisition by far. Reporting indicates Decart’s team would fold into Anthropic’s inference organization to lower serving costs ahead of the potential IPO. Decart previously raised ~$300M with Nvidia among its investors. The strategic logic is clear: inference cost is the margin lever that determines whether Anthropic’s revenue growth converts to a sustainable public-company P&L. Talks are unconfirmed and could collapse. Yahoo Finance PARTNERSHIPS
