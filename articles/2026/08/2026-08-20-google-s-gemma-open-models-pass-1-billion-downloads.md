# Google's Gemma Open Models Pass 1 Billion Downloads

Google reported that its Gemma family of open-weight models has surpassed one billion cumulative downloads, with more than 100,000 community-derived variants. Distribution at that scale gives Google a defensible position in the on-premises and edge tiers where API-based frontier models cannot compete on cost or data residency. For enterprises, it strengthens the case for a two-tier strategy: open weights for high-volume routine work, frontier APIs for hard reasoning. PRICING
