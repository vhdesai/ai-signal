# Marvell's $120B Google Custom-Silicon Deal Gets a Timing Reality Check

Marvell shares fell roughly 8% despite raised forecasts after CEO Matt Murphy said revenue from the Google custom AI-chip agreement — worth up to $120 billion through fiscal 2033 — becomes materially meaningful only in fiscal 2029. The reaction is a marker of investor discipline returning to AI infrastructure headlines. Custom accelerators remain a credible second pillar to Nvidia GPUs, but design-to-deployment cycles are multi-year and should be modeled as such. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/marvell-announced-google-ai-deal-180959027.html
