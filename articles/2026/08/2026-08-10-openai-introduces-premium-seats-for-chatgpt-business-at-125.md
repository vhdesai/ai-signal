# OpenAI introduces Premium seats for ChatGPT Business at $125/user/month

OpenAI added a Premium seat tier giving heavy users 5x more usage and removing the five-hour limit. Premium seats cost $125/user/month ($100 billed annually) and can be mixed with $25 Standard seats. Early sign-ups through August 20 receive up to $500 in credits. Signals a move toward usage-segmented enterprise pricing. URL: OpenAI Blog: Premium seats
