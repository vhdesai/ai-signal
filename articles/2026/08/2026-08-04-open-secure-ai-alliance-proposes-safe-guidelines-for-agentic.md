# Open Secure AI Alliance proposes SAFE guidelines for agentic cybersecurity incidents

The Open Secure AI Alliance, with participation from NVIDIA, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Hugging Face, Red Hat, and the Linux Foundation, proposed Shared AI Findings Exchange guidelines for reporting and learning from agentic AI cybersecurity incidents. SAFE aims to confidentially collect incidents and near misses, notify impacted parties, identify recurring control failures, and publish operating recommendations. The framework reflects a shift toward shared AI incident intelligence as agents become part of critical enterprise workflows.
