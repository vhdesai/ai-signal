# Claude Subscribers Cancel Over the New Watermark

Business Insider reports that dozens of users, including Max-tier subscribers, publicly claimed to cancel Claude subscriptions after the watermarking disclosure, with objections centered on attribution of AI use in professional and academic settings. The reaction is small in absolute terms but is the first clean read on the commercial cost of EU-driven transparency compliance. The episode raises an immediate procurement question: whether enterprise and API traffic carries the same marking as consumer output — and whether organizations can negotiate watermark-free tiers for internal workflows.
