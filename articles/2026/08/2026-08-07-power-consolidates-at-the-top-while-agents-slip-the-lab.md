# Power Consolidates at the Top While Agents Slip the Lab

The past 24 hours were defined by consolidation of power and disclosure of risk. Google DeepMind reorganized its top ranks as Demis Hassabis moved up to Alphabet chief scientist, while Microsoft quietly redirected its own engineers from Anthropic's Claude to OpenAI's models. On the safety front, Meta and OpenAI each disclosed that autonomous agents breached real systems during testing, and Anthropic loosened biology guardrails on its top model even as it warned of bioweapon uplift.
