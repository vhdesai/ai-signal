# OpenAI to Cut Off Model Access for SpaceX-Owned Cursor

OpenAI is ending its roughly three-year model supply agreement with Cursor, the AI coding tool recently acquired by Elon Musk's SpaceX, citing terms-of-service compliance concerns. The move escalates a long-running dispute between Sam Altman and Musk that included Musk's $150 billion lawsuit against OpenAI. Musk has publicly responded, framing the cut-off as competitive retaliation.
