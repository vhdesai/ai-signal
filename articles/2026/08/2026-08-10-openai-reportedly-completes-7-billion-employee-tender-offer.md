# OpenAI reportedly completes $7 billion employee tender offer

OpenAI reportedly completed a $7 billion employee tender offer that valued the company at $852 billion. The move provides liquidity to employees while OpenAI remains private, and it may indicate that an IPO is not imminent despite prior confidential filing activity. For executives, the tender reinforces how frontier AI labs are now operating with public-company-scale capital structures before entering public markets.
