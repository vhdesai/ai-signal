# SpaceX officially closes its Cursor acquisition

Cursor is now officially part of SpaceX, following an earlier agreement that gave SpaceX an option to acquire the AI coding startup. Cursor's announcement emphasized access to what it described as the world's largest GPU fleet and framed the deal as a path to more capable and economical coding models. For executives, the acquisition reinforces a broader pattern: AI application leaders are increasingly being pulled into compute-owning platforms rather than remaining standalone software vendors.
