# IBM and Together AI Sign $240M Deal for an Nvidia-Powered Inference Cluster

IBM and Together AI signed a $240 million multiyear agreement to build a large inference cluster on IBM Cloud, starting with roughly 2,000 Nvidia Blackwell-generation chips using HGX B300 systems and Spectrum-X networking. The deal reflects the shift in infrastructure competition from training frontier models to serving them economically, particularly for open models enterprises want to control directly. For IBM it is a route into the AI buildout without competing head-on with hyperscalers on raw capacity. URL not verified. ________________________________ Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH
