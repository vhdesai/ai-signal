# Nvidia Q2 Revenue Doubles to $96.2B, Guides $108B — Shares Slip After Hours

Net income $59.69B ($2.46/share), revenue $96.22B vs. $92.27B consensus. Adjusted EPS $2.22 beat $2.09. Guidance ~$108B implies 89% YoY growth assuming no China data-center revenue. Operating expenses rose 55% to $8.41B. CEO Huang: “compute is revenue.” Despite the beat, shares slipped — the bar is now so high that blowout prints are priced in. Analysts focused on Rubin ramp and customer concentration.
