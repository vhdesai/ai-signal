# Cloudflare Introduces Adaptive Intelligence for Bot Management

Cloudflare announced Adaptive Intelligence, a continuous detection engine built directly into Cloudflare Bot Management. The product reverses the economics of automated cyberattacks by continuously adapting detection against AI-enabled traffic patterns. AI security is moving toward always-on, behavior-adaptive defenses rather than static bot rules.
