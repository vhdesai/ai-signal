# "I Let Google's Personal Intelligence Access My Life — It's a Huge Help"

WSJ's first-person review of Google's Personal Intelligence product describes it as "a huge help," integrating deeply across email, calendar, files, and communications. The review suggests personal AI assistants are maturing from novelty to genuine productivity tools — though the level of data access required raises significant privacy considerations for enterprise and consumer users alike.
