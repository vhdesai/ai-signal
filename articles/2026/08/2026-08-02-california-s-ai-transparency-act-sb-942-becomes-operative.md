# California's AI Transparency Act (SB 942) becomes operative

California's AI Transparency Act, SB 942, became operative on August 2, requiring large generative-AI providers to embed durable latent watermarks, carrying details such as system, version, and creation date, in synthetic images, video, and audio, and to offer a free public detection tool. Early coverage flagged that some major image generators were not yet compliant as penalties began. The law is one of the first U.S. state watermarking mandates, creating near-term compliance pressure for providers serving California users.
