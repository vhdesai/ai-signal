# Apple Music Will Label AI-Generated Songs, With Disclosure Tags Now Mandatory

Apple notified music-industry partners that recordings identified as materially generated using AI will carry visible labels on Apple Music, extending its AI Transparency Tags program. Reporting indicates the underlying disclosure tags move from optional to mandatory for distributors and rights holders. This shifts classification burden onto the supply chain and is a plausible template for provenance labeling in other media categories. POLICY
