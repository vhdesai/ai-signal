# Microsoft Adds Copilot AI Capabilities Across Excel in August Update

Microsoft’s August 2026 Excel update centers on Copilot, adding AI-assisted change tracking, data visualization, chat history and Python-based analysis. The additions continue the pattern of embedding generative AI into the daily surfaces of Microsoft 365 rather than shipping it as a separate destination.
