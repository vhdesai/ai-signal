# Federal Judge Rules Pentagon Illegally Labeled Anthropic a Supply-Chain Risk

A federal judge ruled the Trump administration was wrong to name Anthropic a supply-chain risk, calling it “unlawful retaliation.” The decision is a significant check on executive-branch power to use supply-chain designations as leverage over AI vendors in defense procurement. For enterprises selling to government, regulatory designations now require substantive national-security justification.
