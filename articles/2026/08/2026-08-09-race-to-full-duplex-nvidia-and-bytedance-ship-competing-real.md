# Race to Full-Duplex: NVIDIA and ByteDance Ship Competing Real-Time Voice Architectures

Within 24 hours, both NVIDIA (NemotronLabs VoiceChat 11B) and ByteDance (SeedRealtime) released full-duplex voice models collapsing cascaded speech pipelines into end-to-end architectures. NVIDIA's approach is open-weights with explicit tool-calling support; ByteDance's adds native video understanding but remains closed. The simultaneous releases signal that real-time, interruptible voice interaction with sub-500ms latency is the next competitive frontier beyond text LLMs. Neither is production-ready: NVIDIA is research-labeled, ByteDance offers no external API.
