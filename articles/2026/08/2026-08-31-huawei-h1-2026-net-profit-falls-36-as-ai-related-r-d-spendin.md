# Huawei H1 2026 net profit falls 36% as AI-related R&D spending surges

Revenue rose 9.6% year over year to 467.8B yuan, but net profit fell 36% to 23.8B yuan (~$3.5B) — a second consecutive first-half decline — driven by AI research spending and rising memory-chip costs. R&D climbed roughly 25% to 121.4B yuan, more than a quarter of revenue, as Huawei pushes AI-chip self-reliance. Margin compression is the visible cost of decoupling.
