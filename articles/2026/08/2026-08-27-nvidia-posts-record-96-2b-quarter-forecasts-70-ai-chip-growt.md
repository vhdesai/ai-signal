# Nvidia posts record $96.2B quarter, forecasts ~70% AI-chip growth

Nvidia reported roughly $96.2B in quarterly revenue with data-center sales near $89B, up about 117% year over year, and issued a first year-ahead outlook projecting revenue growth close to 70%. The company has committed as much as $160B toward memory supply and is increasingly using its balance sheet to finance customers' AI infrastructure. Jensen Huang reiterated that demand remains supply-constrained rather than appetite-constrained.
