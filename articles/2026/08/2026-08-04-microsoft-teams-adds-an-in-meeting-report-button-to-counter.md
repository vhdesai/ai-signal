# Microsoft Teams adds an in-meeting Report button to counter AI deepfake participants

Microsoft began rolling out a Teams control that lets participants report a suspected security issue — including a suspected deepfake attendee — during a live meeting. The feature responds to a rising class of social-engineering attacks in which synthetic video or voice impersonates an executive to authorize payments or data access. Making reporting in-the-moment rather than post-hoc is the meaningful change, since these attacks resolve within a single call. Security teams should pair the rollout with updated incident runbooks for meeting-based impersonation. TrendingCloudflare
