# Stanford's Evo 2 Model Designs a Functional Bacteriophage That Kills E. Coli

A Stanford team used Evo 2, its in-house genomic foundation model capable of writing entire genomes, to design a novel bacteriophage that effectively kills E. coli — the first demonstrated use of a whole-genome AI model to engineer a functional virus against a bacterial pathogen. Researchers frame the result as a potential entry point into a new generation of AI-designed antibiotics, a landmark data point for AI-for-science investment theses. Academic Research ACADEMICAWARD
