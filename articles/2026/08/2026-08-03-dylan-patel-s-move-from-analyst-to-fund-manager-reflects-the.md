# Dylan Patel’s move from analyst to fund manager reflects the rise of specialist investors in the AI infrastructure…

Dylan Patel’s move from analyst to fund manager reflects the rise of specialist investors in the AI infrastructure economy. - Domain experts with deep knowledge of silicon, networking, cooling, and supply chains may have a real advantage over generalist capital in this phase of the market. - Their influence can shape which categories attract capital and how technical narratives are translated into investable theses. - If successful, this could accelerate funding into less glamorous but essential layers such as interconnects, packaging, and thermal management. - For operators, the emergence of specialist funds suggests a more technically literate capital market around AI infrastructure.
