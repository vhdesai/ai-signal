# Microsoft rolls out in-meeting reporting in Teams to counter AI deepfake participants

Microsoft confirmed a Teams feature letting participants report a security issue during a live meeting, positioned against deepfake impersonation of meeting attendees. The control is reactive rather than preventative, but it creates an auditable signal path where none existed. Deepfake-enabled meeting fraud has moved from theoretical to a recurring enterprise loss category, making in-session controls a near-term security requirement.
