# US data-center bans pass 500 jurisdictions as New York and Texas escalate statewide

More than 500 US towns and counties now block or restrict new data-center development, with more than 150 local ordinances passed in July alone, and New York and Texas have moved the fight to the state level. The restrictions cluster around power, water, and land-use objections rather than AI opposition per se, and increasingly cut across party lines. Siting risk — not chip supply — is emerging as the practical ceiling on near-term US compute expansion. INFRASTRUCTURE
