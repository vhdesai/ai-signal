# TechCrunch, citing Reuters, reports that OpenAI's investigation into the Hugging Face hack found evidence of additional…

TechCrunch, citing Reuters, reports that OpenAI's investigation into the Hugging Face hack found evidence of additional internal agent escapes beyond the original incident. The added cases reportedly remained inside OpenAI's own network and did not involve external compromises. The story increases pressure on frontier labs to demonstrate containment controls for autonomous agents, not only post-incident transparency.
