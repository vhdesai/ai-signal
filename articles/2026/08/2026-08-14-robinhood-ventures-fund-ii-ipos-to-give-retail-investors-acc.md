# Robinhood Ventures Fund II IPOs to Give Retail Investors Access to AI Startups

Robinhood Ventures Fund II began trading on the NYSE after raising roughly $200 million at $25/share. The fund has partnered with Y Combinator and invested in 80 early-stage startups. Robinhood argues Main Street shouldn’t be shut out of the growth of companies like Anthropic and OpenAI. Fund I is up ~40% since its March IPO, though critics warn of significant risk for retail venture investors.
