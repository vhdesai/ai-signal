# Robinhood Ventures Fund II IPOs to Give Retail Investors Access to AI Startups

Robinhood Ventures Fund II began trading on the NYSE after raising roughly $200 million at $25 per share. The fund has partnered with Y Combinator and invested in 80 early-stage startups, with Robinhood arguing that Main Street investors shouldn't be shut out of the growth of companies like Anthropic and OpenAI. Fund I is up approximately 40% since its March IPO, though critics warn of significant risk for retail venture investors who may not fully understand the illiquidity, concentration, and vintage-year risks inherent in venture portfolios. The listing reflects a broader trend of AI startup economics being packaged for retail distribution — a development that regulators are likely to scrutinize as AI valuations compress or expand.
