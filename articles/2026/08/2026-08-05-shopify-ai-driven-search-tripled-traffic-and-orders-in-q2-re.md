# Shopify: AI-Driven Search Tripled Traffic and Orders in Q2, Revenue Up 36%

On Shopify's Q2 earnings call, President Harley Finkelstein reported that AI-driven traffic and orders to Shopify stores tripled year-over-year, while traditional search traffic also continued growing. Revenue reached $3.6B (+36% YoY), beating consensus by roughly $200M, with 75% of AI-attributed purchases occurring in long-tail categories outside the top 100. Shopify has built native connectors to Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Manus, Replit, and Vercel. This is among the first major public-company earnings disclosures to quantify a positive commercial impact from AI-native search.
