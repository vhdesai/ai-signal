# NVIDIA details serving Alibaba's 2.4T-parameter Qwen3.8 model on GB300 NVL72

NVIDIA published deployment guidance for Alibaba's open-weight Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B model, a 2.4 trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 95 billion active parameters per token. NVIDIA said the model reaches more than 4,000 tokens per second per GPU and over 350 tokens per second per user on GB300 NVL72 in FP8 precision on day zero, with additional NVFP4 optimizations expected. The item is important because open-weight frontier-scale models are becoming data-center-scale infrastructure workloads, not just downloadable research artifacts.
