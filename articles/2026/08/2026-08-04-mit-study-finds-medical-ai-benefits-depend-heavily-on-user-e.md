# MIT study finds medical AI benefits depend heavily on user expertise

MIT News reported on a Nature Medicine study examining how clinicians and non-experts use medical AI explanations while diagnosing skin disease. The study challenges the assumption that more explanation is always safer, finding that clinicians and non-experts benefit from different interface designs.
