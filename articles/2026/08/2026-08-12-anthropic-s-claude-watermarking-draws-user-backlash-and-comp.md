# Anthropic's Claude watermarking draws user backlash and compliance attention

TechCrunch reported that some Claude users are objecting to Anthropic's new invisible watermarking of AI-generated text. Anthropic rolled out the policy in connection with the EU AI Act's Transparency Code, which requires AI-generated or AI-edited content to be identifiable to computer systems. The controversy shows how provenance requirements are moving from abstract policy into user-facing product decisions that affect education, workplaces, journalism, and software development.
