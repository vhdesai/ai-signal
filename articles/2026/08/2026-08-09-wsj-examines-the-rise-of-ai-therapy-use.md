# WSJ examines the rise of AI therapy use

The Wall Street Journal examined people turning to chatbots for mental-health support, a rapidly growing use case that sits at the intersection of consumer AI, clinical risk, and platform responsibility. The issue is high-stakes because AI systems can feel always available and emotionally responsive while lacking the safeguards and accountability of licensed care. The story reinforces why youth safety, crisis handling, and domain-specific guardrails are becoming central to consumer AI governance.
