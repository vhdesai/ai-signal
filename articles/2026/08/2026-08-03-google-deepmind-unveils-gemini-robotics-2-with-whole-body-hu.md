# Google DeepMind unveils Gemini Robotics 2 with whole-body humanoid control

Google DeepMind detailed Gemini Robotics 2, showing improved dexterous manipulation, whole-body humanoid control, and the ability to adapt to new robot embodiments from fewer than 200 examples. The update pushes the company's embodied-AI stack toward more general, transferable control across hardware platforms. It positions Gemini as a foundation layer for robotics developers, not only software agents.
