# Alibaba Qwen3.8-Max raises the bar in China's model race

Alibaba unveiled Qwen3.8-Max, a roughly 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model positioned as its most capable system to date. Coverage framed the release as another escalation in the U.S.-China frontier race, with Qwen3.8-Max landing close to Moonshot's larger Kimi K3 while reportedly competing aggressively on price.
