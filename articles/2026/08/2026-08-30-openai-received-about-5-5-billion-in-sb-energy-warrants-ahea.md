# OpenAI received about $5.5 billion in SB Energy warrants ahead of SoftBank data-center IPO

Draft IPO documents show SB Energy, the SoftBank venture powering OpenAI's Stargate build-out, granted OpenAI roughly $5.5 billion in warrants to anchor a Cincinnati-area data-center site. SB Energy is reportedly targeting a U.S. listing as soon as this week. The structure makes AI-lab equity a visible currency for locking in compute capacity.
