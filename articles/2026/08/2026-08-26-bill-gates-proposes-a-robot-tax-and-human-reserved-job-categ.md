# Bill Gates Proposes a Robot Tax and “Human Reserved” Job Category

In a ~6,000-word Gates Notes essay, Gates argued the tax code structurally favors automation and proposed levies on both AI tokens and physical robots. His second proposal, “Human Reserved,” would designate roles society keeps in human hands regardless of AI capability.
