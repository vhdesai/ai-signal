# The Information: Why Apple Should Buy Granola

The Information makes a strategic case for Apple to acquire Granola, an AI-powered meeting note-taking and recording startup, arguing that the deal would address a critical gap in Apple's AI product suite. The column opens with a striking observation: there is now a near-certainty that any professional was unknowingly recorded by AI at least once in the past week, and that frequency is accelerating. For Apple, whose privacy-first positioning has left it without a competitive AI assistant for productivity workflows, an acquisition like Granola would provide a Siri-adjacent capability that users actually want — real-time meeting intelligence — while reinforcing Apple's trusted-device narrative rather than competing head-to-head with cloud-first AI providers.
