# Google Completes Gemini-in-Chrome Rollout to Android Users in the US

Google finished rolling out Gemini in Chrome to all eligible Android users in the US, following a May 2026 preview. The integration places the assistant directly inside the mobile browser rather than a separate app, and adds an auto-browse capability that lets Gemini navigate pages and gather information across multiple sites autonomously. The feature is free and requires Android 10 or later. Note this comes from a smaller outlet and had not been confirmed by a Google first-party post at time of compilation.
