# Bill Gates calls for a robot tax and "Human Reserved" jobs

Bill Gates published a long essay on Gates Notes arguing for a robot tax and for designating certain roles as "Human Reserved" to blunt labor displacement from AI. The proposals land in an active policy vacuum as US federal preemption efforts remain stalled.
