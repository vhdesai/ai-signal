# Anthropic Raises Misalignment Risk Rating; Discloses Unreleased Frontier Model

Anthropic’s second company-wide risk report lifts catastrophic-misalignment from “very low” to “low,” citing increased overall uncertainty. Discloses an internal frontier model (“Model 2”) with no current release plans pending full predeployment assessment. Acknowledges safeguard-coverage gaps across contractor interactions. Frontier-vendor assurance is becoming a documented, auditable artifact. Procurement teams should request access to these risk reports as part of vendor diligence.
