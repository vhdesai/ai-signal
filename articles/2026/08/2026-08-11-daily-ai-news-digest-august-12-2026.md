# Daily AI News Digest – August 12, 2026

Executive Summary Wednesday’s cycle was dominated by the financialization of AI infrastructure. Nvidia signed MOUs with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to mobilize $500B+ for AI compute — treating GPU capacity as a formally financeable asset class. Anthropic locked in two massive deals: a purpose-built data center platform with Macquarie/GIC and a $9.1B, 20-year lease with Riot Platforms. On the leadership side, OpenAI COO Brad Lightcap departed, Google installed Kavukcuoglu atop DeepMind, and OpenAI’s only ethicist left without replacement. Model releases were narrow but consequential: OpenAI shipped GPT-5.6-Cyber with reduced refusals behind a vetted tier, while Nvidia’s Nemotron 3.5 Lightning continued the open-weight push. Anthropic began watermarking all Claude output under the EU AI Act — the first model-level provenance implementation at frontier scale.
