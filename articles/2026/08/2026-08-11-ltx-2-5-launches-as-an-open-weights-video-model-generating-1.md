# LTX-2.5 launches as an open-weights video model generating 10-second clips in 6.8 seconds

LTX-2.5 shipped as an NVIDIA-accelerated open-weights video generation model, producing a 10-second clip from an image in about 6.8 seconds on Nvidia superchips, with day-one ComfyUI support. Because the weights are open, iteration can run on owned hardware with no per-generation billing and no content leaving the enterprise boundary. That materially changes the economics of high-volume creative experimentation.
