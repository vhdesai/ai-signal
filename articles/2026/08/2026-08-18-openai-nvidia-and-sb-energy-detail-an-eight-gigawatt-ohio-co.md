# OpenAI, Nvidia and SB Energy detail an eight-gigawatt Ohio compute campus

The Pike County, Ohio project is structured as a 20-year site commitment with a stated ambition of roughly eight gigawatts of compute capacity, making it one of the largest single-site AI buildouts announced to date.
