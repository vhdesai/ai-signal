# One in Five Enterprises Cannot Stop a Runaway AI Agent’s Spending in Real Time

VentureBeat Pulse Research finds enterprises run three AI orchestration platforms concurrently, yet 21% report no real-time ability to halt runaway agent spending. A majority permit production agent deployment without human review. The gap is one of controls and observability, not model quality—the most defensible reason to slow agent rollouts.
