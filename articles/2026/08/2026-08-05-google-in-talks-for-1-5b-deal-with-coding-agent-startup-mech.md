# Google in talks for $1.5B+ deal with coding-agent startup Mechanize

Google is reportedly negotiating a deal worth more than $1.5 billion to license technology from and hire talent out of Mechanize, a roughly 35-person San Francisco startup building RL environments for software-engineering agents. The price implies extraordinary value per engineer and reflects scarcity in high-quality agentic training data. Coding remains the most commercially proven agent workload.
