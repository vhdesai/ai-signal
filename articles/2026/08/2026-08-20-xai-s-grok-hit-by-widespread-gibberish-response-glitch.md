# xAI's Grok Hit by Widespread 'Gibberish' Response Glitch

A glitch caused xAI's Grok to return paragraphs of nonsensical word-salad to many users, primarily those on Grok Lite querying directly on Grok.com; the X.com Grok account was unaffected. xAI acknowledged the issue on X as a rare temporary generation glitch and said refreshing usually clears it. The incident follows heavy staff turnover at xAI, which has reportedly lost most of its founding team and more than 50 researchers. For enterprises evaluating Grok, it is a reliability data point worth logging.
