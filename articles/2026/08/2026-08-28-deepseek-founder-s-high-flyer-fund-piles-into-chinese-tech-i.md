# DeepSeek founder's High-Flyer fund piles into Chinese tech IPOs

High-Flyer Quant, the hedge fund founded by DeepSeek's Liang Wenfeng that bankrolled the AI lab, is moving aggressively into China's active IPO market in pursuit of returns. The piece ties DeepSeek's financial backer to a broader surge in Chinese technology listings. It is a reminder that DeepSeek's funding model remains unusual among frontier labs.
