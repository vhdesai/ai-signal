# SpaceX Officially Closes $60B Acquisition of Cursor

AI coding startup Cursor is now officially part of SpaceX. The deal, first announced in April as a partnership with an acquisition option, values Cursor at $60B. Cursor said the move gives it "access to the largest fleet of GPUs in the world" via SpaceX's computing infrastructure, which already serves Anthropic and Google as compute customers. All four Cursor co-founders — including Swedish co-founder Arvid Lunnemark, now worth an estimated $2.4B — remain with the company. Cursor's integration into SpaceX/xAI creates a vertically integrated coding AI + compute stack under Musk's portfolio. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/spacex-officially-closes-its-cursor-acquisition/
