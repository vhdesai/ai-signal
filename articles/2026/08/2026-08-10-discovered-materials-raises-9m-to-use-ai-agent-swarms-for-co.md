# Discovered Materials Raises $9M to Use AI Agent Swarms for Cooler Chip Materials

Y Combinator-backed Discovered Materials closed a $9M seed from Lightspeed India Partners to use swarms of AI agents for discovering new semiconductor thermal materials. The system uses Anthropic models for material-lead generation and custom physics models for simulation verification, performing thousands of guesses per day versus ~20 during a PhD. Founded by a Stanford materials science PhD, the startup released a "Material Discovery Bench" and plans to patent new materials for GPU applications. ________________________________ Research Breakthroughs RESEARCH ADVERSARIAL
