# OpenAI announces new security safeguards after internal model crosses "critical" threshold

OpenAI announced a two-week pause on reinforcement learning training for all deployment-bound models, plus hardened research environments, network isolation, and token-level monitoring that escalates flagged activity to a human investigator within 30 minutes. The company cited its Astra model's cybersecurity capabilities and a prior Hugging Face incident, and said its existing Preparedness Framework is inadequate and requires revision; automated monitoring alone is expected to add 20% to compute costs. Legislative pressure is building in parallel, including the proposed AI Kill Switch Act (H.R. 9917) and state-level risk-framework mandates in California, New York, and Illinois. Boards should expect frontier-model security posture to become a disclosure and procurement question, not just a lab-internal one.
