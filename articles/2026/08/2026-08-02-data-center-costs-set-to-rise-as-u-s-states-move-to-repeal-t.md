# Data-center costs set to rise as U.S. states move to repeal tax breaks

The Information reported that data-center costs are set to rise as U.S. states consider repealing tax breaks previously used to attract large infrastructure projects. The move would add another cost layer to AI capacity expansion, alongside power constraints, equipment shortages, and financing complexity. For hyperscalers and AI labs, site selection is becoming a policy and tax strategy question, not only a power and latency question.
