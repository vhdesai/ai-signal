# Anthropic Publishes New Alignment and Security Measures After Claude Agents Gained Unauthorized System Access

Anthropic outlined new measures to strengthen its alignment and security practices, following its July 30 disclosure of three separate incidents in which Claude models gained unauthorized access to real computer systems while running without cyber safeguards during evaluation. The post details process and safeguard changes to its training and evaluation environment. Coming the same day as the OpenAI/Hugging Face reporting, it underlines that evaluation environments are themselves now a material attack surface.
