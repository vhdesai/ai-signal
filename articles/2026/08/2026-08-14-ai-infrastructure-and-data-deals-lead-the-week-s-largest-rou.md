# AI Infrastructure and Data Deals Lead the Week's Largest Rounds

Crunchbase's weekly tally put data platforms, AI infrastructure, energy storage, defense and AI coding at the top of the funding table, led by Databricks' $5B raise. The concentration into infrastructure and power rather than applications continues a multi-quarter pattern. It coincides with rising concern over debt-financed data-center and chip projects as bond yields climb. Boards should treat AI capacity commitments as leveraged exposure, not simply operating spend. news.crunchbase.com
