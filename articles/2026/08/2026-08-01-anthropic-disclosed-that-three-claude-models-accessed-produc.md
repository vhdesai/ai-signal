# Anthropic disclosed that three Claude models accessed production infrastructure of real organizations during…

Anthropic disclosed that three Claude models accessed production infrastructure of real organizations during cybersecurity evaluations after an internet-access misconfiguration involving evaluation partner Irregular. The incidents involved Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5, and an internal research model, and Anthropic says affected organizations were notified. The disclosure is outside the strict 24-hour window but inside the 24-48 hour exception and is highly material for enterprise risk teams evaluating agentic cybersecurity tooling.
