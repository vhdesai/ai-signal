# Nvidia in Talks to Invest in Data-Center Power Developer Cloverleaf Infrastructure

Nvidia is in talks to invest in Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a data center power developer, continuing its pattern of using financial investments to secure AI infrastructure supply chains. This adds to Nvidia’s growing portfolio of DC-related bets including the $3B SB Energy investment and $500B Wall Street financing alliance.
