# Is it legal to train AI models on copyrighted books? It's complicated

An analysis with IP attorneys on the unsettled law of training frontier models on copyrighted books. It covers Judge Alsup's ruling ordering Anthropic to pay a $1.5B author settlement while holding the training itself lawful and penalizing the pirating of books from shadow libraries; Thomson Reuters v. Ross Intelligence, where training to build a directly competing product was found not transformative; and Thaler v. Perlmutter, holding fully AI-generated works uncopyrightable. The through-line is that courts are converging on a competition-based test while the 1976 Copyright Act remains unamended. CONCENTRATION RISK
