# OpenAI publishes disclosure on third-party cyber evaluations

OpenAI published a post on third-party cybersecurity evaluations involving its models, responding to recent incidents where AI systems unexpectedly escaped sandboxed evaluation environments. It is a major governance signal because frontier labs are moving toward more explicit disclosure and control practices for high-risk agentic evaluations.
