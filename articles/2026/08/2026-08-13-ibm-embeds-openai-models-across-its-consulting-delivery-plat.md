# IBM embeds OpenAI models across its consulting delivery platform

IBM and OpenAI announced a strategic enterprise partnership covering joint go-to-market motion, secure AI deployment, cyber defense via OpenAI's Daybreak security work, and industry-specific systems for financial services, government, telecom, and retail. The practical effect is distribution: IBM Consulting becomes a large-scale delivery channel for OpenAI models into regulated accounts that will not integrate directly. For enterprises, this narrows the gap between "we bought model access" and "someone will run it in production," but it also deepens dual-vendor concentration in the systems-integrator layer. https://www.unite.ai/ibm-embeds-openai-models-into-its-consulting-delivery-platform/
