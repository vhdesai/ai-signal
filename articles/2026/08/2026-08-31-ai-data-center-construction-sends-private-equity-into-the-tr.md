# AI data-center construction sends private equity into the trades

PitchBook reported that private equity is following the AI data-center boom into construction, engineering, fiber optics, electrical engineering, equipment recycling, and related trades. PE activity in construction and engineering reached an estimated 529 deals in Q2, up 56.5% year over year, while data-center construction spending rose 46% in June. The build-out is broadening AI infrastructure economics far beyond chips and cloud providers.
