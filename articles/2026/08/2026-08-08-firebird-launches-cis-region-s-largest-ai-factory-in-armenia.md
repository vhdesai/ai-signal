# Firebird Launches CIS Region’s Largest AI Factory in Armenia

Firebird opened the CIS region’s largest AI factory in Hrazdan, Armenia, built on the NVIDIA DSX platform with Dell PowerEdge servers and delivered in just over six months. The company plans to deploy more than 70,000 NVIDIA Rubin and Blackwell GPUs and 300 megawatts of capacity in Armenia by the end of 2027, part of an approximately 2-gigawatt roadmap spanning Armenia, Kazakhstan, and other frontier markets. NVIDIA said it intends to invest in Firebird, following an earlier CoreWeave investment; Perplexity is an early tenant. Armenia’s prime minister and the US chargé d’affaires attended the opening — compute access as statecraft. ENERGY
