# Trump Memo Lets Private Companies Hack Foreign Cybercriminals; California Mandates AI Security Officers

President Trump's August 12 memo gives federal agencies 60 days to create a program allowing private companies to conduct "cyber effects operations" against foreign hackers — including "manipulation, disruption, denial, degradation, or destruction" of criminal tech infrastructure. The memo lacks detail on target identification and company risk. Separately, California is requiring every state agency to appoint an AI security officer, establishing one of the most comprehensive government AI governance frameworks in the U.S.
