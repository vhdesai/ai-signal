# Consumer AI use cases face backlash in parenting and creator workflows

TechCrunch reported two separate trust flashpoints: Sam Altman promoting ChatGPT as a family and parenting assistant, and Hank Green pausing production after saying his AI use in creative workflows had become unhealthy. Together, the stories show that consumer AI backlash is increasingly about context, disclosure, and emotional appropriateness—not just whether the tool works. For product leaders, the key risk is overextending AI into intimate or voice-sensitive workflows before norms and disclosure practices are settled.
