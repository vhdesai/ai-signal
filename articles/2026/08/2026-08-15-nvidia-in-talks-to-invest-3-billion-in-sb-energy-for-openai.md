# Nvidia in Talks to Invest $3 Billion in SB Energy for OpenAI Ohio Data Center

Nvidia is in talks to invest as much as $3 billion in SB Energy, the SoftBank-backed developer of a massive planned Ohio data center campus for OpenAI. The investment is being discussed as part of Nvidia's negotiations to provide around $100 billion in credit support for the project. The deal adds to a growing pattern of Nvidia using its financial heft to support AI-related companies and projects — making it easier for firms to buy and use Nvidia hardware. SoftBank-backed SB Energy plans to IPO as soon as next month.
