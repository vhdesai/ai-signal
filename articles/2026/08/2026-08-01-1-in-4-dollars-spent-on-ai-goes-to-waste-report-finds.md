# 1 in 4 Dollars Spent on AI Goes to Waste, Report Finds

A new report from Harness reveals that enterprises are wasting roughly 25% of their AI budgets due to lack of cost governance, with more than half of businesses lacking a dedicated owner for AI expenditures. The finding lands at a critical inflection point: organizations are moving from experimentation to production, where inference costs, redundant tool subscriptions, and unmonitored GPU provisioning become material budget line items. The report underscores the growing importance of FinOps-style discipline for AI workloads — cost visibility, chargeback models, and automated right-sizing — as CFOs increasingly demand accountability from AI investments.
