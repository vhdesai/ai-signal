# Heavy AI adopters grew headcount 10.2%, contradicting the layoff narrative

Fortune surveyed contradictory AI employment data, centered on a Ramp/Revelio Labs study of more than 21,000 US firms finding that high-intensity AI adopters expanded headcount 10.2% over two years and entry-level hiring 12%. That sits against a Stanford finding of a 16% relative employment decline for workers aged 22–25 in AI-exposed roles, and a July 2026 BLS report showing employers cut 23,000 jobs. The honest read is that AI adoption correlates with growth at the firm level while displacing specific early-career functions. The underlying dataset is published by Ramp. PLATFORM
