# Heavy AI adopters grew headcount 10.2%, contradicting the layoff narrative

A Ramp/Revelio Labs study of 21,000+ US firms found that high-intensity AI adopters expanded headcount 10.2% over two years and entry-level hiring 12%. That sits against a Stanford finding of a 16% relative employment decline for workers aged 22–25 in AI-exposed roles.
