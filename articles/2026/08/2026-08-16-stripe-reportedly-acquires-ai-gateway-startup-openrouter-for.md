# Stripe Reportedly Acquires AI Gateway Startup OpenRouter for $7B+

Stripe has finalized a deal to acquire OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, according to Bloomberg. OpenRouter provides a single API access point for 400+ AI models, helping customers select different models for different tasks — its CEO previously described it as "Stripe for AI." The startup raised a $113M Series B at $1.3B valuation just three months ago from Sequoia, a16z, Menlo Ventures, and Alphabet's CapitalG. The acquisition signals Stripe's strategic expansion from payments infrastructure into AI model routing and orchestration. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/
