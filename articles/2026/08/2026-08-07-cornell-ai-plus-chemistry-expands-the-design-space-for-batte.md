# Cornell: AI plus chemistry expands the design space for battery electrolytes

Cornell researchers published IonNet in Science Advances on August 7, a model that predicts ion mobility directly from chemical composition, even without precise crystal structures — removing a long-standing bottleneck in solid-state electrolyte discovery. Screening roughly 4,500 stable compounds, the system flagged 87 candidate fast-ion conductors. Thirteen of the top 20 were subsequently confirmed in simulation. The work is a concrete example of AI compressing materials-discovery cycles in a domain with direct energy-storage implications.
