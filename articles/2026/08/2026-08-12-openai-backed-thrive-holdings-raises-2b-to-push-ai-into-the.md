# OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings raises $2B to push AI into the enterprise

Thrive Holdings raised $2 billion at a $12 billion valuation from investors including SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The vehicle's thesis is applying AI inside operating businesses rather than selling models or tooling. It is an early test of whether AI-native roll-ups can convert capability into margin at scale. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/openai-backed-thrive-holdings-raises-2b-to-bring-ai-to-the-enterprise/ FUNDING
