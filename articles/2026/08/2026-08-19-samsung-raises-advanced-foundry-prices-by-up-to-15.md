# Samsung Raises Advanced Foundry Prices by Up to 15%

Samsung lifted prices on new 4nm, 5nm and 8nm foundry orders placed in July, with increases reaching 15% and Chinese customers absorbing the steepest hikes. Its 4nm Pyeongtaek lines are reported at full capacity as AI demand spills past TSMC's constrained allocation. Expect cost pass-through into accelerators, networking silicon and devices over the next two to three quarters.
