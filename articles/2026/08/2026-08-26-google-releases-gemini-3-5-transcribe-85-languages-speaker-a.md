# Google Releases Gemini 3.5 Transcribe — 85+ Languages, Speaker Attribution

Auto-detects 85+ languages, learns custom vocabulary, captures alphanumerics, attributes to 3 speakers with word-level timestamps. WER: 4.0% streaming, 2.6% non-streaming. Removes filler words and resolves self-corrections — compliance/legal workflows should retain original audio as system of record.
