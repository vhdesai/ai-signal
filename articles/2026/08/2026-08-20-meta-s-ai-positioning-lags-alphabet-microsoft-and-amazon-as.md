# Meta's AI positioning lags Alphabet, Microsoft and Amazon as capex pressures cash flow

Coverage this cycle highlights Meta trailing its hyperscaler peers on AI-attributed market value despite comparable infrastructure commitments, with its market capitalization near $1.39T. Separate reporting notes the stock trading near 52-week lows as data-center spending compresses free cash flow even against stronger second-quarter revenue. The divergence matters to enterprise buyers because it shapes which platforms sustain aggressive pricing and capacity expansion into 2027.
