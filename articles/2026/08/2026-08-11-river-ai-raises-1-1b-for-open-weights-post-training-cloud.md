# River AI Raises $1.1B for Open-Weights Post-Training Cloud

River AI, founded by xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin, announced $1.1 billion raised across seed and Series A, co-led by General Catalyst and AMP PBC, with strategic investment from both Nvidia and AMD Ventures. Its shipped product is a training API offering LoRA fine-tuning and reinforcement learning on open models from 35B to 1T parameters, metered per million tokens rather than per GPU hour, with customer-owned checkpoints. For enterprises, the near-term relevance is a credible route to custom models without standing up an infrastructure team.
