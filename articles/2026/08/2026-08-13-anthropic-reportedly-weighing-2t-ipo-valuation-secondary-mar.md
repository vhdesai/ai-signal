# Anthropic Reportedly Weighing ~$2T IPO Valuation; Secondary-Market Frenzy Intensifies

Anthropic may seek a valuation above $2 trillion in an autumn listing, with backers modeling $100B–$120B in revenue by end-2026 against IDC estimates of $40–50B annualized today. The figure is investor-modeled and unconfirmed by Anthropic — framed against OpenAI's recent $7B buyback at $852B. WSJ separately reports active investor meetings fielding questions on Chinese competition, infrastructure spend, and regulatory friction. Business Insider reports that buying Anthropic shares on secondary markets has become extraordinarily competitive, with demand far outstripping supply ahead of the potential listing. The dynamics underscore investor conviction in Anthropic's position as one of two dominant frontier AI labs. A successful offering would force public markets to price frontier-lab economics — extraordinary revenue growth against compute, talent, and data-center costs — for the first time.
