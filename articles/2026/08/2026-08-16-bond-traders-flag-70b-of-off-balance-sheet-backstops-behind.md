# Bond traders flag ~$70B of off-balance-sheet backstops behind the AI buildout

Roughly $70 billion in residual value guarantees tied to AI data-center projects sit off the balance sheets of major AI and chip companies, on top of Nvidia's newly announced $500B financing partnership with BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, Apollo, Blackstone, Brookfield and KKR, under which Nvidia guarantees up to 25% of certain projects. Broadcom is backstopping a $35B debt package tied to Anthropic, and Meta structured roughly $27B and $13B data-center deals with similar mechanisms. Analysts describe the guarantees as nearly costless during expansion but sharply pro-cyclical in a downturn. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/bond-traders-agonizing-over-70-190000845.html
