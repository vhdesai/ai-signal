# OpenAI slows Astra model development after cyber capability threshold review

OpenAI said it paused or slowed some internal work on its upcoming Astra model after preliminary evaluations suggested it may have reached a critical cybersecurity capability threshold. Under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, that means the model may be capable of independently identifying and carrying out cyberattacks against traditionally well-protected real-world systems. The disclosure is significant because a frontier lab is publicly delaying an unreleased model over safety controls rather than only discussing risk after launch.
