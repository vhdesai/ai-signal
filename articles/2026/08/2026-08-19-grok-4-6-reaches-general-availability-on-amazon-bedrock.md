# Grok 4.6 Reaches General Availability on Amazon Bedrock

xAI's flagship Grok 4.6 is now generally available on Amazon Bedrock, one week after initial release, giving the model a second major cloud distribution channel. Bedrock availability materially lowers the procurement barrier for AWS-committed enterprises. It continues the pattern of frontier labs treating multi-cloud distribution as table stakes rather than exclusivity.
