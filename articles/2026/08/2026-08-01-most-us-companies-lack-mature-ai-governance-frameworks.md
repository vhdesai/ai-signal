# Most US Companies Lack Mature AI Governance Frameworks

A Schellman report finds that while US companies are increasing spending on AI governance, the vast majority are unprepared for the risks posed by agentic AI systems now entering production. The gap between governance intent and execution is widening as autonomous agents introduce novel risk vectors — including unmonitored tool use, cascading failures across multi-agent systems, and data exfiltration through prompt injection. The finding arrives just days after the EU delayed its own AI Act high-risk deadlines, suggesting that neither regulatory frameworks nor enterprise governance programs have kept pace with the speed of agentic deployment.
