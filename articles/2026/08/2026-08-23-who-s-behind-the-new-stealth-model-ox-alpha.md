# Who's behind the new 'stealth model' Ox Alpha?

A free anonymous model called Ox Alpha appeared on OpenRouter on Thursday, August 20, billed as a reasoning model built for coding, sustained agentic work and production workloads, with its provider unnamed during the preview. Stripe CEO Patrick Collison, whose company is acquiring OpenRouter, called it "very impressive." Attribution speculation has moved between Z.ai's GLM family and an unreleased Microsoft MAI model, with no confirmation from any lab.
