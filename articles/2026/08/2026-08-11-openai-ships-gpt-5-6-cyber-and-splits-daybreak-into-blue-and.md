# OpenAI ships GPT-5.6-Cyber and splits Daybreak into Blue and Red access tiers

OpenAI released GPT-5.6-Cyber, a model trained for advanced defensive security work, alongside a two-tier Daybreak program: Blue for defenders and Red for approved researchers doing vulnerability research, zero-day discovery and exploit validation. OpenAI reports the model completes roughly 95% of advanced dual-use security requests versus about 2% for the base model. The company says it uses the model internally for red-teaming. The release lands after OpenAI warned that its upcoming Astra model may reach a "Critical" cyber capability threshold. Products & Tools PRICING
