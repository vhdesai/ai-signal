# Apple Music will label songs "materially generated using AI"

Apple told music-industry partners it will apply visible "Made With AI" labels to tracks that content providers identify as materially generated using AI, extending its existing AI Transparency Tags program. The approach discloses rather than bans, shifting classification responsibility onto distributors and rights holders. If it holds, it becomes a de facto disclosure standard for synthetic media on major platforms — and a precedent worth watching for enterprises publishing AI-assisted content of any kind. GOVERNANCE
