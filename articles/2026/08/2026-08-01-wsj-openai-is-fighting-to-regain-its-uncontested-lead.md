# WSJ: OpenAI is fighting to regain its uncontested lead

The Wall Street Journal argues that OpenAI no longer has the uncontested lead it enjoyed after ChatGPT, with Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, and open-weight alternatives eroding its advantage across benchmarks, developer mindshare, and enterprise adoption. The analysis frames OpenAI's next moat as agentic AI, but notes that the company is stretched across AGI research, consumer products, enterprise sales, infrastructure dependencies, and corporate restructuring. The strategic question is whether OpenAI can execute fast enough while better-capitalized or more focused rivals compress the gap. URL: How OpenAI Lost Its AI Crown—and the Fight to Win It Back
