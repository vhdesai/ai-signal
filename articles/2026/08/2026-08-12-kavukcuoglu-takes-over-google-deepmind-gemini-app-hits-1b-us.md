# Kavukcuoglu Takes Over Google DeepMind; Gemini App Hits 1B Users

Koray Kavukcuoglu becomes SVP and head of Google DeepMind reporting directly to Sundar Pichai, replacing Demis Hassabis (now chair). His remit: Gemini model development, frontier research, and the Gemini app/developer teams. Google confirmed the Gemini app crossed 1B monthly active users — its fastest-growing product ever, with 63% engaging via voice. Distribution, not model leadership, remains Google’s strongest card.
