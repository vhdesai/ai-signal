# Claude Suffers Elevated Error Rates in a Multi-Hour Monday Outage

Multiple Anthropic models began returning high error volumes starting at 07:06 Paris time on Monday. Anthropic identified the cause but had not published a restoration timeline at the time of reporting. Coming days before a potential IPO filing, the incident is a reminder that frontier-model availability remains a live operational dependency.
