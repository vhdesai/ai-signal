# Amazon is reported to be destroying rare books to digitize them for AI training

Reporting traced a shipment of roughly 1,000 rare books to an Amazon facility in Las Vegas where, according to employees, spines are cut off so pages can be scanned faster, destroying the physical copies. Amazon confirmed it purchases books through commercial channels to develop its products and services but declined to discuss methods or scale. The story adds physical-provenance questions to an AI training-data debate that has so far focused almost entirely on digital licensing.
