# Judge denies xAI’s bid to block Minnesota’s “nudify” ban; law takes effect

A federal judge refused xAI’s request to halt a Minnesota law banning “nudify” apps that generate non-consensual sexual imagery, allowing the statute to take effect. The ruling is an early test of how state-level restrictions on abusive synthetic-media tools will hold up against First Amendment challenges from AI companies.
