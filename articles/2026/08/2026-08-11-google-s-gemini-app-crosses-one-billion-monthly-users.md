# Google's Gemini app crosses one billion monthly users

Sundar Pichai announced that the Gemini app surpassed one billion monthly active users, making it Google's fourteenth product to reach that milestone and putting it roughly level with ChatGPT. Google cited 63% voice usage, more than 150 million images generated per day and over 100 million iOS users. The milestone coincides with continued rollout of Gemini 3.5 Flash for coding and agentic tasks.
