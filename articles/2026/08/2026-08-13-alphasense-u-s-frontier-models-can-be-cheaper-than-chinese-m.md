# AlphaSense: U.S. Frontier Models Can Be Cheaper Than Chinese Models in Practice

An AlphaSense study challenges the assumption Chinese AI is always cheaper. Testing financial data analysis, GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 4.8 generated better answers at lower total cost than Moonshot’s Kimi K3 and Z.ai’s GLM-5.2 despite higher per-token pricing. The key insight: smarter models use fewer tokens per task — fewer retries, more concise outputs, fewer rounds. OpenAI board chair Bret Taylor echoed this, calling frontier models “cheaper and more efficient” in practice. For procurement, this argues for total-cost-per-task benchmarking rather than per-token price comparisons.
