# Z.ai unveils GLM-5.3, an open-weights model with near-frontier cyber performance

Zhipu's Z.ai announced GLM-5.3, reporting 84.5% on the CyberGym benchmark versus 83.8% for Anthropic's model. The company says it will release weights publicly after security testing, with high-risk capabilities gated. If the results hold, it is the first open-weights model to edge a US frontier lab on a major cyber benchmark.
