# Anthropic Shows Automated AI Researchers Improve Alignment Faster — and Cheaper — Than Humans

Anthropic published “Automated Researchers Can Reliably Mitigate Alignment Failures,” demonstrating AI systems can improve a model across 10 alignment benchmarks without degrading overall capability. The automated researchers beat experienced humans “on average within six hours” at $4/hour vs. $150/hour. “Human-guided research directions do not lead to stronger performance.” The paper calls this “early evidence that automated alignment post-training could become practical in the near term” — a clear step toward recursive self-improvement. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/an-anthropic-researcher-just-gave-us-a-peek-at-self-improving-ai/ Industry News INDUSTRY M&A OPEN-SOURCE
