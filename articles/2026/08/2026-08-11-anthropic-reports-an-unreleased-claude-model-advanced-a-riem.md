# Anthropic Reports an Unreleased Claude Model Advanced a Riemann Zeta Bound

Anthropic published findings that an unreleased research version of Claude improved a longstanding lower bound on the fraction of Riemann zeta function zeros satisfying the hypothesis, reportedly moving it from ~41.6% to 67.2%. The result is not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis and remains subject to independent mathematical verification. Its significance is directional: frontier models producing novel, checkable results in research mathematics rather than reproducing known work.
