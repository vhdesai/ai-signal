# EY Transforms Internships into Yearlong AI-Era Residencies

EY is launching a paid "Career Residency" that extends beyond the traditional 8-week internship to 8–12 months, giving assurance interns ongoing part-time work, personalized coaching, and AI skills development. The shift reflects how AI is displacing traditional "assembly work" (presentations, proposals, synthesis) and requiring earlier development of judgment and critical thinking. EY received 24,000+ applications from CPA-track students last year and extended 2,400 offers — applications grew 33% YoY. Successful residents will enter the firm at analyst level rather than staff. 🔗 https://www.businessinsider.com/ey-internship-residency-career-ai-skills-2026-8
