# Once-Hot Software Companies Race to Reinvent Themselves as AI Closes In

The WSJ 10-Point newsletter highlights how formerly high-flying SaaS companies are scrambling to reinvent their product lines as AI threatens to obsolete traditional software interfaces. The piece names specific companies whose seat-based business models are under pressure from agentic AI systems that can perform the same workflows without human operators. This is the clearest articulation yet of the "SaaS-pocalypse" thesis that PitchBook and Gartner flagged earlier this month — moving from theoretical projection to observable market behavior. For enterprise software buyers, the implication is accelerating vendor risk: incumbents that cannot pivot to AI-native architectures face margin compression and potential irrelevance.
