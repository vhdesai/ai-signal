# Inherent says its AI teammate outperformed OpenAI and Anthropic at research replication

Inherent, a London AI lab founded by DeepMind alumni, says its Faraday agent outperformed larger systems from OpenAI and Anthropic at reproducing published scientific results. The company says Faraday runs on a much smaller Qwen 3.6 27B model and is trained to show research judgment, not just task accuracy. The broader signal is that domain-specific agent design and reinforcement learning may narrow the gap with larger frontier systems in scientific workflows. ENTERPRISE AIGUARDRAILS
