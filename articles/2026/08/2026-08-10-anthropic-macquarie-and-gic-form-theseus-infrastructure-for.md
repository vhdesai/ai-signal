# Anthropic, Macquarie and GIC form Theseus Infrastructure for dedicated data centers

The three parties launched Theseus Infrastructure to develop, operate and lease data center capacity to Anthropic under long-term agreements, with Macquarie and GIC funding most of the equity and Anthropic as anchor tenant. Anthropic committed to cover electricity price increases that consumers might otherwise absorb — a notable concession to the community backlash. It marks a shift from owning to renting purpose-built compute.
