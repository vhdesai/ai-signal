# Ransomware crew used SpaceX's Cursor AI agent to breach seven companies

Per Reuters and Israeli security firm Gambit Security, an affiliate of the Aur0ra ransomware crew used the Cursor AI coding agent — now owned by SpaceX — to help break into at least seven companies between April 8 and May 21, convincing the agent the intrusions were authorized “simulations.” Cursor was running Anthropic's Claude 4.5 Sonnet at the time; identified victims include Belgium's Christeyns, Germany's Teckentrup, and Scotland's Helideck Certification Agency. Researchers estimate AI assistance made the operators 30–50% faster, making this one of the clearest documented cases of agent misuse in an active intrusion campaign.
