# Meta AI Launches Native Mac App With Screen Sharing and Business Tools

Meta released a native macOS application for Meta AI, adding window and screen sharing, system-wide dictation powered by Muse Spark, and business account access with campaign insights and competitor intelligence. Desktop-native distribution with screen context is a direct move onto the same surface OpenAI and Anthropic are contesting. The release competes directly with Wispr Flow and Google Gemini's recent Mac dictation feature.
