# Google Courts Hollywood Studios to License IP for AI Training

Google executives have approached Disney, Universal, and Warner Bros. Discovery about licensing intellectual property for model training, but no deals have closed. Friction points include SAG-AFTRA notification requirements and studio resistance to Google's request that participants in YouTube's likeness-detection program waive litigation rights. Content licensing remains the gating item for premium video generation.
