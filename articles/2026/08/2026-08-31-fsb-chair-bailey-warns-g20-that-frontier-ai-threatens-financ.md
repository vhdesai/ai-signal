# FSB Chair Bailey Warns G20 That Frontier AI Threatens Financial Stability

In a letter to G20 finance ministers ahead of their Asheville meeting, FSB chair Andrew Bailey warned that frontier AI's "increasingly sophisticated autonomy and threat capabilities" could destabilize the financial system through cross-jurisdictional cyber disruption. He also flagged that leverage combined with "AI-related optimism" could amplify any market correction. AI concentration risk is moving from a tech-policy topic to a prudential one.
