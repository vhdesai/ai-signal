# Chinese AI labs still train on Nvidia; switching to Huawei silicon carries a reported ~50% cost premium

Despite export controls and domestic-silicon mandates, Chinese labs continue to train on Nvidia hardware because CUDA lock-in makes migration expensive — reportedly around a 50% cost increase to move to Huawei. The finding tempers assumptions about how quickly domestic accelerators displace Nvidia in Chinese training workloads. Accessed via an AI Weekly reproduction; the SCMP original is paywalled.
