# Anthropic reports an AI-derived improvement to a Riemann zeta-function bound

Anthropic reported that an unreleased Claude model raised a bound on the proportion of Riemann zeta zeros lying on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2%. The result consumed roughly 31 million output tokens and was subsequently human-validated and formalized in Lean. It is one of the most concrete claims yet of frontier models contributing original results in pure mathematics rather than reproducing known proofs.
