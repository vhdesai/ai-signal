# Apple's Camera-Equipped AirPods May Avoid 'Pervert Pods' Problem via Recording Restrictions

Apple's leaked camera-equipped AirPods reportedly won't allow users to record photos or videos — a privacy-by-design approach that could avoid the backlash that hit other AI wearables (like Meta Ray-Ban and Humane).
