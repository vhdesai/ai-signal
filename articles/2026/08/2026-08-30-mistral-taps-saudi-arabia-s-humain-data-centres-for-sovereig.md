# Mistral taps Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN data centres for sovereign AI push

Paris-based Mistral AI and PIF-backed HUMAIN have entered a strategic collaboration reported as worth "hundreds of millions of euros," centered on sovereign AI: running on HUMAIN data centres, jointly developing Arabic-capable frontier models, and targeting regulated industries with an initial focus on cybersecurity and voice. The article notes the agreement itself was announced on August 24, so the underlying deal predates this window — only the coverage is in-window.
