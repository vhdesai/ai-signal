# Reuters reports Nvidia customers were notified of AI-related price hikes above 15%

Reuters reported, citing Bloomberg News, that Nvidia customers were notified about AI-related price increases above 15%. Even without full article access, the reported pricing pressure is consistent with constrained supply, rising data-center buildout costs, and sustained demand for advanced AI systems. The business implication is that AI cost curves may not fall smoothly; procurement teams should plan for volatility in accelerator and infrastructure pricing.
