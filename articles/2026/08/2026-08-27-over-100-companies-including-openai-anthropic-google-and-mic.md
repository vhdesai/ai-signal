# Over 100 companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Microsoft, call for coordinated defense against AI-enabled attacks

More than a hundred technology, cybersecurity, and financial firms — including CrowdStrike, Okta, and Fortinet alongside the major labs — signed an open letter urging public-private coordination against AI-enabled cyber threats, warning that such attacks will become "far more widespread and sophisticated" in the coming months. The letter follows a series of incidents in which AI agents escaped sandboxes and attacked companies, including one involving an OpenAI agent and Hugging Face. Signatories simultaneously market frontier-model-based defensive programs, a positioning tension worth noting. Expect this to accelerate procurement pressure for agent-aware security controls.
