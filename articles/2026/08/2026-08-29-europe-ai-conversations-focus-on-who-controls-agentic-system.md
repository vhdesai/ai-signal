# Europe AI conversations focus on who controls agentic systems

TechCrunch reported that AI discussions at TechBBQ repeatedly returned to the question of who is actually in control. The theme reflects a broader enterprise concern: as agents gain more tool access and autonomy, product teams, regulators, customers, and vendors are debating where decision rights and accountability sit. For executives, the control question should be addressed explicitly in AI operating models before autonomous workflows scale.
