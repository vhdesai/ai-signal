# Cursor Acquisition Marks Second-Largest of VC-Backed Company in History

PitchBook reports that SpaceX's $60B acquisition of AI-powered code editor Cursor marks the second-largest acquisition of a VC-backed company in history. The deal signals how rapidly AI coding tools have moved from niche developer utilities to strategic platforms commanding enterprise-scale valuations and acquisition premiums. The ranking contextualizes what happened this week: a coding editor that launched roughly two years ago is now valued alongside generational enterprise software companies. For venture investors, it validates the thesis that developer tools capturing workflow lock-in can achieve platform-scale exits. For enterprise buyers, it raises concentration concerns — the most prominent independent AI coding vendor is now owned by a compute-heavy parent with its own model ambitions.
