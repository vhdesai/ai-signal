# Fields Medal Mathematician Joins OpenAI, Citing AI Existential Risk

Jacob Tsimerman, a Fields Medal-caliber mathematician widely regarded as one of the most brilliant number theorists of his generation, has joined OpenAI's safety team — driven not by excitement about AI's potential but by genuine terror about its risks. The hire is notable because Tsimerman represents a new archetype in AI safety: elite academics from outside machine learning who view frontier AI as a civilizational threat serious enough to abandon tenured positions. His decision to work inside a lab rather than criticize from the outside reflects a growing belief among safety-minded researchers that influencing AI development from within may be more effective than external advocacy.
