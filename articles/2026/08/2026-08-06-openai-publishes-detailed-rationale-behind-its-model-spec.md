# OpenAI Publishes Detailed Rationale Behind Its Model Spec

OpenAI published an explainer on the design philosophy behind its Model Spec, the governing document defining how its models are trained to behave, what values they embody, and how instruction conflicts are resolved. The post provides added transparency into OpenAI's alignment approach at a moment of intensifying global regulatory scrutiny of frontier model behavior, relevant to enterprise AI governance teams evaluating vendor alignment practices.
