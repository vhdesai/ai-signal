# Trane and Eaton Partner on Electrical and Thermal Systems for AI Data Centers

Trane Technologies announced a collaboration with Eaton to integrate electrical and thermal systems purpose-built for AI-focused data centers. Power delivery and heat rejection — not compute procurement — have become the pacing items in high-density buildouts. Expect continued consolidation of the power-and-cooling supply chain around a small number of integrated vendors.
