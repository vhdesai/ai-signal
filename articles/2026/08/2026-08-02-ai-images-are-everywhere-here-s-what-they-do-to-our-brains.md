# AI Images Are Everywhere — Here's What They Do to Our Brains

The WSJ examines the cognitive and societal effects of ubiquitous AI-generated imagery, exploring how synthetic visual content affects perception, trust, and decision-making at both individual and institutional levels. The piece draws on neuroscience research suggesting that repeated exposure to AI-generated images erodes the brain's ability to distinguish authentic from synthetic content, creating what researchers call "reality fatigue." For enterprises, the implications extend to brand protection, misinformation defense, and the growing need for provenance verification in visual communications — a market that startups like C2PA-affiliated companies are racing to address.
