# Nvidia's AI advantage is moving beyond the GPU

TechCrunch argues Nvidia's competitive edge is shifting from raw GPU horsepower toward whole-system data-center design — using smarter network traffic control and interconnects to raise effective efficiency rather than simply adding processor cycles. The piece positions networking and systems integration as Nvidia's emerging moat as rivals bring their own accelerators to market. For buyers, it reframes the comparison from chip-versus-chip to rack- and cluster-level throughput.
