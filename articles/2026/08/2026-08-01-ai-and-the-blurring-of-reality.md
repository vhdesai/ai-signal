# AI and the Blurring of Reality

The WSJ explores how AI-generated content — deepfakes, synthetic media, and AI-augmented recordings — is fundamentally blurring the line between real and fabricated, creating unprecedented challenges for trust, journalism, and institutional credibility. The piece argues that the technology has crossed a threshold where detection tools consistently lag behind generation capabilities, making verification asymmetric and expensive. For enterprises, the implications extend beyond reputational risk: AI-generated evidence is beginning to surface in litigation, regulatory proceedings, and corporate espionage scenarios, raising questions about the evidentiary standards that courts and boards will need to adopt.
