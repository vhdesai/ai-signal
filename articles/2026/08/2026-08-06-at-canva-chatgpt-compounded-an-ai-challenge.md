# At Canva, ChatGPT compounded an AI challenge

Canva's AI product rollout was undercut by ChatGPT and similar tools that replicate many of its core design capabilities for free—illustrating how frontier models commoditize capabilities even in creative, UX-heavy verticals that seemed defensible. The story highlights a broader pattern: as general-purpose AI tools absorb specialized functionality, vertical SaaS providers must find defensibility in data, workflow integration, or proprietary capabilities that models cannot easily replicate. For executives assessing AI's competitive impact, Canva serves as a cautionary example of how quickly a frontier model release can reshape market dynamics in adjacent categories.
