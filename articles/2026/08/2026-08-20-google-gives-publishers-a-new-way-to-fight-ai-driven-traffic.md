# Google gives publishers a new way to fight AI-driven traffic losses

Google introduced a "preferred source" button that lets readers pin publishers across Search, Discover, and Google News, partially offsetting click losses from AI-generated summaries. The move is defensive — it addresses publisher grievances without changing how AI Overviews consume content. For content-dependent businesses, it shifts some traffic recovery onto direct audience relationships rather than ranking.
