# Google DeepMind ships Gemini 3.5 Transcribe with 2.6% word error rate

Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is a speech-to-text model with built-in self-correction handling, filler-word removal and automatic formatting. Google reports an average word error rate of 4.0% streaming and 2.6% non-streaming, across 85+ languages with speaker attribution. The model targets the enterprise transcription tier that has been a persistent weak point for general-purpose multimodal models.
