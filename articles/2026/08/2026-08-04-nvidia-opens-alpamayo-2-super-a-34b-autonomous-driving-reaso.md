# Nvidia opens Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34B autonomous-driving reasoning model, to commercial use

Nvidia released Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34-billion-parameter reasoning model for autonomous driving, under terms permitting commercial deployment. The move lowers the entry cost for robotaxi and ADAS developers who would otherwise fund in-house driving-policy models from scratch. Strategically it extends Nvidia's pattern of seeding demand for its silicon by giving away the model layer above it.
