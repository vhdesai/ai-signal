# Hugging Face Opens Orders for Microduck, a $399 Open-Source Robot

Hugging Face began taking orders for Microduck, a $399 open-source desktop robot that developers can train at home out of the box. The device is aimed at lowering the entry cost for embodied-AI experimentation and building a developer community around open robotics stacks. Timing is notable given the same-day report of Nvidia's pending acquisition of the company. Infrastructure GEOPOLITICS
