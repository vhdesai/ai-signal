# Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sue Anthropic over lyrics in Claude's training data

Two of the largest music publishers filed suit against Anthropic late Friday, alleging what they describe as one of the largest ongoing thefts of intellectual property, centered on song lyrics used to train Claude. The complaint names company leadership and seeks statutory damages that could reach the maximum per-work amount across a very large corpus, implying multi-billion-dollar exposure. Coming alongside prior European rulings that memorized lyrics constitute reproduction, this raises the practical bar for training-data provenance.
