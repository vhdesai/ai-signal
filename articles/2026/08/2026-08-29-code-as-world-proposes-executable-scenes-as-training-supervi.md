# Code-as-World proposes executable scenes as training supervision for physical reasoning

Code-as-World is also a research signal for physical reasoning: verified executable scenes provide labels for mass, contact, gravity, geometry, and trajectories that raw video does not contain. MarkTechPost reported that Code-as-World-VL-9B outperformed Gemini-3.1 Flash on QuantiPhy validation in the reported setup, while remaining limited to rigid-body settings. The broader implication is that world-model progress may come from better intermediate representations, not only larger video generators. RESEARCHLAB AUTOMATION
