# Purdue Study Finds On-Policy Distillation Works Largely Without the Teacher — and Proposes a Supervision-Free Alternative

Purdue researchers Yi Ding and Ruqi Zhang show that teacher supervision in on-policy distillation is substantially noisy — and that noise increases with teacher scale — yet the student policy is largely insensitive to it. They find the technique’s gains come mostly from suppressing low-log-probability tokens, which requires no teacher at all. Their proposed method, On-Policy Self-Adaptation (OPSA), uses entropy-adaptive negative advantages and improves Avg@32 on AIME24 by 35.41 points over base Qwen3-1.7B, a 263% relative gain. arXiv
