# Situational Awareness invests $400M in chip startup Source Foundry despite portfolio losses

Leopold Aschenbrenner's AI-focused hedge fund put $400 million into Source Foundry — a startup founded by Stanford researchers working to make chip manufacturing faster and cheaper — bringing its total stake to $500 million, per Wall Street Journal reporting. The bet comes after a difficult stretch: the fund sold the majority of its public portfolio to Citadel at the end of July amid the decline in AI infrastructure stocks, with reported assets under management falling from $20 billion to $10 billion, though it retained its Anthropic shares. The move signals continued conviction in the supply-side manufacturing layer even as public AI infrastructure valuations correct. ANALYSIS
