# Shopify says AI search is driving traffic and sales, not replacing Google

Shopify said AI-driven traffic and orders to merchant stores tripled year over year in Q2, while traditional search remained one of its largest and still-growing traffic sources. Management framed AI as complementary to search because agents can match richer product constraints and send buyers directly to product pages. This is an important counterpoint to publisher concerns about AI search: structured commerce data may benefit from AI referral flows in ways advertising-supported content does not.
