# Unreleased Claude research model raises the proven Riemann zeta lower bound from 41.6% to 67.2%

Anthropic reported that an unreleased Claude research model, run over multi-day sessions with roughly 60 coordinated subagents, raised the proven lower bound for the share of nontrivial Riemann-zeta zeros known to lie on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2%. The result does not resolve the Riemann hypothesis but materially advances a long-standing bound, and the argument was formalized in Lean for mechanical verification. Anthropic reports roughly 650 failed approaches before the successful line of attack. It is one of the more credible demonstrations to date of autonomous multi-agent research producing verifiable novel mathematics. RESEARCH
