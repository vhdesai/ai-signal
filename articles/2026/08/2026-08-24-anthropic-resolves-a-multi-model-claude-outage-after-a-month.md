# Anthropic resolves a multi-model Claude outage after a month of incidents

Anthropic reported elevated errors beginning at 04:50 UTC on August 24 affecting Claude Mythos 5, Fable 5, Opus 5 and Opus 4.8, identified a cause at 05:27 UTC and marked the incident resolved at 08:30 UTC — roughly three hours of degradation. Anthropic has not publicly stated the root cause, and its status history records multiple separate incidents across models, API, Claude Code and Cowork through mid-August. Neither the frequency nor the causes have been formally explained. For teams with production dependencies on a single frontier provider, the month's pattern is a concrete argument for multi-model failover.
