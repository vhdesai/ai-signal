# IBM Partners With OpenAI to Embed Frontier Models Across Consulting Delivery

IBM announced a strategic partnership placing OpenAI's frontier models — including GPT-5.6 — plus Codex and ChatGPT Work directly into IBM Consulting's delivery platform and enterprise offerings. The deal gives OpenAI a channel into IBM's large regulated-industry client base while giving IBM a frontier-model story it could not build organically.
