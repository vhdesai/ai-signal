# AI Chip Startup Etched Doubles Valuation to $21 Billion in Under a Month

Etched raised $700M at a $21 billion valuation—roughly double its mark from one month prior—led by Jane Street, which installed Etched's first shipped inference cluster and moved from customer to lead investor.
