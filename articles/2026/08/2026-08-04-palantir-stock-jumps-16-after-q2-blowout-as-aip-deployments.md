# Palantir stock jumps 16% after Q2 blowout as AIP deployments scale

Palantir shares rose roughly 16% Tuesday after a Q2 2026 beat and a raised full-year outlook, with management crediting accelerating U.S. commercial growth to production deployments of its Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP). The print reinforces the thesis that enterprise AI spend is converting from pilots into expanding, revenue-bearing contracts. Notably, peers C3.ai and UiPath sat out the rally, signaling that the market is rewarding demonstrated AI monetization over positioning.
