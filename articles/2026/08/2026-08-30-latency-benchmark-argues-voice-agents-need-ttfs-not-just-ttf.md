# Latency benchmark argues voice agents need TTFS, not just TTFT

MarkTechPost published a benchmark-driven analysis of inference APIs for voice and realtime agents, arguing that time to first token is insufficient for conversational systems because text-to-speech often needs a complete clause before it can speak. The piece compares LLM first-token latency, output speed, streaming speech-to-text, and text-to-speech layers, framing practical end-to-end voice latency targets around roughly 700ms to 1.2 seconds. For product teams, the takeaway is that model/provider selection should optimize the full turn pipeline rather than a single inference metric. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCHAGENTS
