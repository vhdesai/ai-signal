# Musk's faster path to gas turbines for AI power carries a pollution problem

Musk says casting turbine blades in-house at SpaceX's Bastrop foundry could bring gas turbines online up to 18 months sooner, a meaningful compute-power advantage for his AI build-out. TechCrunch pairs the claim with environmental pushback: the NAACP alleges xAI has run Colossus turbines in Memphis without permits. Energy permitting is emerging as a hard constraint on frontier compute expansion.
