# Sam Altman calls on the industry to “pace” AI development

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has publicly suggested the AI industry should “pace the rate of AI development,” reviving the accelerate-versus-decelerate (“decel”) debate among leaders. The comments — dissected on TechCrunch’s Equity podcast — come amid mounting scrutiny of safety incidents and competitive pressure. Coming from the field’s most prominent accelerationist, the shift in tone is notable for boards weighing AI risk posture.
