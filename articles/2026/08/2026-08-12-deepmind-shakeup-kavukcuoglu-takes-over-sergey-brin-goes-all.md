# DeepMind Shakeup: Kavukcuoglu Takes Over; Sergey Brin Goes “All-In” on Gemini

Koray Kavukcuoglu now leads Google DeepMind as SVP reporting directly to Sundar Pichai, while Demis Hassabis moves to a chair role. The reorganization follows weeks of senior talent departures and analysts framed it as prioritizing execution and release cadence over open-ended research, with closing the coding gap against OpenAI and Anthropic as the immediate test. Reuters reported Sergey Brin has personally pressed staff to go “all-in” on Gemini, elevating frontier model leadership to a founder-level mandate. Google confirmed the Gemini app crossed 1B MAU — its fastest-growing product ever — with 63% of interactions voice-driven and 150M+ images generated daily.
