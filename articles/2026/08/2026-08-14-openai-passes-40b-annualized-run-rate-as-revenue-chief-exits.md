# OpenAI Passes $40B Annualized Run Rate as Revenue Chief Exits Ahead of Expected IPO

OpenAI is reported to have crossed a $40B annualized revenue run rate, a figure that would place it among the fastest-scaling enterprise software businesses ever — while its Chief Revenue Officer departed after roughly eight months, the latest in a run of senior exits. The company named Dali Rajic as incoming CRO as it reshapes go-to-market leadership. Analysts have cautioned that a run rate is not booked annual revenue, a distinction that will matter considerably under public-market disclosure. The combination of accelerating revenue and an unstable executive bench is the central diligence question for anyone underwriting an OpenAI listing. finance.yahoo.com
