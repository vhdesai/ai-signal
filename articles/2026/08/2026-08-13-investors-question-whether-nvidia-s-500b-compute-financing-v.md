# Investors Question Whether Nvidia's $500B Compute Financing Vehicle Is Large Enough

Analysis of Nvidia's $500B third-party financing platform — built with Apollo, BlackRock, Goldman Sachs and others — argues the structure is both risky and strategically sound, particularly for extending the revenue life of prior-generation GPUs. Separately, reporting indicates investors view the facility as necessary but insufficient, covering roughly the 10 GW of infrastructure needed next year alone. The mechanism matters more than the number: Nvidia is moving demand financing off hyperscaler balance sheets and into third-party capital. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/nvidias-new-500b-plan-is-risky-but-brilliant-especially-for-aging-gpus/
