# MIT's CrysVCD designs AI-generated materials that are actually synthesizable

MIT's "crystal generator with valence-constrained design" enforces electron and valence chemistry rules before the expensive generation step rather than after it. The team reports high lattice-dynamics stability in roughly 70% of generated candidates while still hitting target properties such as high thermal conductivity. Because it plugs into existing diffusion and LLM-based material generators as a pre-filter, it removes most of the ~90% of compute typically spent screening out unstable candidates.
