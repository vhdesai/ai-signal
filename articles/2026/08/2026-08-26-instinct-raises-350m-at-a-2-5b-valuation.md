# Instinct raises $350M at a $2.5B valuation

Instinct, a consumer AI startup roughly a year old, has raised $350 million at a $2.5 billion valuation. The company has generated substantial consumer traction alongside privacy concerns tied to its product model. The round is another data point that late-stage capital continues to concentrate in a narrow set of viral consumer AI assets.
