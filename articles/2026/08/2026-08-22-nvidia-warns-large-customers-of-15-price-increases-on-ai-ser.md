# Nvidia Warns Large Customers of 15%+ Price Increases on AI Servers

Nvidia has told its largest customers that AI server prices will rise by more than 15%, driven by surging DRAM and HBM memory costs. Increases hit flagship Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell configurations shipping early 2027. Contract manufacturers supplying Microsoft, Google, and Oracle have already passed notice. The disclosure lands days before Nvidia’s Q2 earnings on August 26. INFRA POLICY
