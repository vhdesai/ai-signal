# Robotics Startup Generalist Hits $3B Valuation with $200M Extension

Generalist (ex-DeepMind + Boston Dynamics founders) raised $200M extension led by 8VC at $3B — up from $2B in June. Its Gen 1.5 model lets robots learn tasks from 3–12 second video demos. Backed by Nvidia, Bezos Expeditions, Fei-Fei Li. Competitors include Physical Intelligence ($11B) and Skild AI ($14B). Investors bet robotics may soon reach its “ChatGPT moment.” ________________________________ INDUSTRY FUNDING
