# Google moves its ~90-person AI responsibility team out of DeepMind into global affairs

Google is relocating its roughly 90-person AI responsibility unit from Google DeepMind into its global affairs division, which handles lobbying and public policy, with the transition completing in early September, according to The Wall Street Journal. The team's work includes evaluating models for chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear hazards and studying the psychological effects of chatbots. Some staff raised concerns about reduced independence and diminished access to Gemini researchers; leadership says compute, headcount, and DeepMind access are preserved. Other safety functions covering model behavior, privacy, and security remain inside the lab. Items were verified against original publication pages where accessible.
