# Shopify reports AI-driven traffic and orders tripled year over year

Shopify said AI-referred traffic and orders to its merchants’ stores tripled year over year in Q2, and argued that AI search is additive rather than cannibalistic — a notably different outcome than publishers have experienced. The data point matters because it is one of the first vendor-reported, transaction-level measures of AI search monetization at scale. Executives evaluating commerce channel strategy should treat it as directional, not audited.
