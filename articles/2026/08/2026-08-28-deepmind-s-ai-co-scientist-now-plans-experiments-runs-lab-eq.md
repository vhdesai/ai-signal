# DeepMind’s AI Co-Scientist Now Plans Experiments, Runs Lab Equipment, and Writes Papers

Co-Scientist has moved from suggesting hypotheses to executing them, running closed-loop workflows in materials science, biology, and computer science. It drove a chemical-vapor-deposition reactor to grow monolayer MoS₂, MoSe₂ and WS₂ in single attempts, predicted E. coli swarming phenotypes matching unpublished wet-lab data, and found an inference-time architecture that beat six frontier models on HealthBench. Verification modules cross-check manuscript figures against execution logs; a double-blind study of 30 experts across 450 reviews reported reduced hallucination and plagiarism. arXiv paper →
