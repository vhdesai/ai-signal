# Business Insider: The Hottest AI Models Aren't the Ones Developers Actually Use

data from Hugging Face shows that developers gravitate toward different models than those generating the most buzz in Silicon Valley. While frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google dominate headlines, actual production usage patterns reveal preference for smaller, faster, and cheaper models optimized for specific tasks — reinforcing the trend toward model routing and specialization over one-size-fits-all frontier systems. 🔗
