# OpenAI Publishes Its Containment Posture for Critical Cyber Capability

OpenAI's own announcement argues the defensive upside of advanced cyber capability should reach security teams, while additional controls wrap continued development. The post details the operational changes — tighter tooling boundaries, expanded safety testing, and pausing internal work that does not meet the new control standard. For enterprise security leaders, the practical implication is that the threat model shifts from filtering malicious prompts to securing the model, its tools, its training environment, and every downstream system it can reach.
