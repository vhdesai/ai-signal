# Most US companies still lack mature AI governance as agentic systems spread

CIO Dive cited a Schellman report finding that companies are spending on governance but remain underprepared as agentic AI moves into production. The gap is important because agents add new risk vectors: unmonitored tool use, cascading multi-agent failures, data exposure, and unclear accountability. Governance programs now need technical controls, ownership models, and incident processes that match the autonomy of the systems being deployed.
