# Nvidia Q2 Revenue Doubles to $96.2B, Guides to $108B — Shares Slip After Hours

Nvidia reported net income of $59.69B ($2.46/share) with revenue more than doubling to $96.22B versus a $92.27B consensus. Adjusted EPS of $2.22 beat the $2.09 FactSet consensus. Guidance of ~$108B for the current quarter implies about 89% year-over-year growth, with the company noting its outlook assumes no data center compute revenue from China. CEO Jensen Huang framed the quarter as an inflection: "compute is revenue." Despite the beat, shares slipped in after-hours trading — the bar is now so high that blowout prints are priced in.
