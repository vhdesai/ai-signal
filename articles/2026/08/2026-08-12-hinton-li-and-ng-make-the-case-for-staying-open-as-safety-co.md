# Hinton, Li, and Ng make the case for staying open as safety concerns mount

At the Ai4 conference, Geoffrey Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng debated regulation, open access, and U.S. competitiveness as Chinese labs advance. The disagreement centered on whether openness accelerates safety research or proliferation risk.
