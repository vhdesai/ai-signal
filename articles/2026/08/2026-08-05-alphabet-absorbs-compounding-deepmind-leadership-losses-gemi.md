# Alphabet Absorbs Compounding DeepMind Leadership Losses; Gemini 4 Confirmed

Demis Hassabis is moving from Google DeepMind CEO to unit chairman, with CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu assuming day-to-day leadership. Chief scientist Jeff Dean departed after 27 years alongside Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le to co-found Discovery Loop. Both Gemini co-technical leads exited within weeks of each other; GOOG fell ~4%. Separately, Google DeepMind confirmed Gemini 4 development, retiring the delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro. Enterprises with Gemini 3.5-dependent roadmaps should assume a version-skip migration. ANALYSIS
