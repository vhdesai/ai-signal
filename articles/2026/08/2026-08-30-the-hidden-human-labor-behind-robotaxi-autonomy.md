# The Hidden Human Labor Behind Robotaxi Autonomy

TechCrunch's mobility briefing examined OSHA filings showing Waymo and Zoox test drivers sustained two dozen+ injuries from hard braking and abrupt autonomous movements in 2024–2025. The reporting is a useful corrective to autonomy claims in adjacent agentic domains: headline automation rates often obscure a persistent human-in-the-loop tail, and unit economics remain sensitive to residual labor costs.
