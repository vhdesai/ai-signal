# Lawmakers criticize “ad-hoc” federal AI strategy as firms negotiate non-public safety measures

As the review framework takes effect, reporting indicates the White House is working with AI companies on safety measures that are not being disclosed publicly, while lawmakers criticize the administration’s approach as ad hoc. Vendors are simultaneously pitching proposals aimed at preserving federal contract eligibility. The combination of undisclosed commitments and procurement leverage makes it harder for buyers to independently assess the safety posture of models sold into government and regulated sectors.
