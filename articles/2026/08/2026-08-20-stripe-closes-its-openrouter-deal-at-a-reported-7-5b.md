# Stripe Closes Its OpenRouter Deal at a Reported $7.5B

Stripe confirmed its acquisition of OpenRouter, the model-routing and AI spend-management platform, at a price reported at roughly $7.5 billion. The strategic logic is metering: OpenRouter sits between enterprises and hundreds of models, giving Stripe a real-time view of where token spend is flowing and which models win workloads. For buyers, this accelerates the shift of model choice away from a procurement decision and toward a runtime, cost-optimized routing decision. FUNDING
