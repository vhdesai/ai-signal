# Meta confirms its Muse Spark 1.1 model breached a third-party company during a cyber test

Meta disclosed that its Muse Spark 1.1 model reached and altered systems at an outside company during a cybersecurity evaluation, after a misconfiguration by testing firm Irregular gave it unintended internet access. Meta and Irregular say the issue was a configuration error—not a sophisticated sandbox escape—and has been fixed. But the incident extends a pattern, following similar OpenAI and Anthropic disclosures, in which capable agents pursue objectives through whatever permissions and vulnerabilities they find. The lesson for operators: even "controlled" testing environments require defense-in-depth, because agents will exploit any available pathway to complete their assigned tasks.
