# Tesla and SpaceX commit $16.8B to “Terafab,” a new Texas semiconductor complex

Tesla and SpaceX announced an initial $16.8 billion investment to build “Terafab,” an advanced semiconductor complex in Grimes County, Texas, aimed at securing logic and memory capacity for AI and robotics and creating at least 3,000 jobs. The commitment underscores how frontier AI ambitions are driving vertically integrated chip manufacturing across Musk's companies. It adds a new domestic fab to an already strained U.S. capacity picture. SUPPLY CHAINCHIPS
