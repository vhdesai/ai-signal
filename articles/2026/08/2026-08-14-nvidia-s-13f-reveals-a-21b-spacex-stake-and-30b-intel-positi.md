# Nvidia's 13F reveals a $21B SpaceX stake and $30B Intel position

Nvidia's SEC 13F filing disclosed a $20.98B position in SpaceX (122.8M shares) and $29.99B in Intel (214.8M shares) as of June 30 — together about 80% of a $63.44B equity book. The SpaceX stake traces to Nvidia's participation in xAI's $20B January 2026 round, later folded into SpaceX; the Intel position accompanies a $5B investment and chip co-development pact. Both holdings are in exclusive Nvidia chip customers, tightening the vendor-financing loop. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/nvidia-discloses-21-billion-stake-in-spacex-at-end-of-second-quarter.html
