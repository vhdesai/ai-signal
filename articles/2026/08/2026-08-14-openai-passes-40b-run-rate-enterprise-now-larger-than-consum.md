# OpenAI Passes $40B Run Rate; Enterprise Now Larger Than Consumer

OpenAI’s ARR hit $40B (~2× end-2025), with CFO Friar telling investors enterprise is now bigger than consumer. The CRO departed after 8 months; Dali Rajic (ex-Wiz COO) was named replacement. Analysts caution a run rate isn’t booked revenue — a distinction that matters for IPO disclosure. The enterprise-over-consumer inflection signals where OpenAI’s roadmap investment concentrates. The combination of accelerating revenue and unstable executive bench is the central diligence question.
