# Inherent Says Its "Faraday" Agent Beat Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at Replicating Published Research

Inherent, a London lab founded by DeepMind alumni, released Faraday, an agent designed to reproduce published scientific findings. On the company's own Replica benchmark — 310 tasks drawn from 100 machine-learning and AI-for-science papers — Faraday outscored Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 and OpenAI's GPT-5.5. The benchmark is vendor-authored and not independently validated, but reproduction-as-a-task is a credible early proxy for autonomous R&D capability.
