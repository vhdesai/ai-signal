# Legal AI Firm Harvey Reportedly Raising $500M at a $15.5B Valuation

Harvey, which builds AI software for law firms and in-house legal teams, is reported to be raising at least $500 million at a valuation of roughly $15.5 billion. The round would place it among the highest-valued vertical AI application companies, well ahead of most horizontal tooling startups. It reflects a broader repricing of domain-specific AI where regulated workflow depth, not model access, is the defensible moat.
