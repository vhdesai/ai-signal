# Bain Joins Anthropic's Claude Partner Network at Top Tier

Bain & Company and Anthropic announced a global partnership placing Bain at the Global Premier tier of the Claude Partner Network. The arrangement pairs Anthropic's enterprise model access with Bain's deployment and change-management capacity. It continues a pattern in which frontier labs use the large consultancies as their enterprise distribution channel rather than building comparable services organizations.
