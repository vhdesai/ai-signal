# Nvidia and Wall Street Launch $500B AI Financing Alliance

Nvidia announced a landmark partnership with BlackRock, Apollo, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to deploy $500 billion in lending to finance the AI buildout. Larry Fink called it "a next future for financial engineering," with funds flowing via loans, credit, and securitized "yield-based products." The move shifts systemic risk from Nvidia's balance sheet to Wall Street investors, though critics have flagged concerns about using GPU chips as collateral. Morgan Stanley analysts project hyperscalers will invest $3.5 trillion into AI infrastructure through 2028.
