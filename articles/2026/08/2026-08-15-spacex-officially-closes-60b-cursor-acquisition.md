# SpaceX Officially Closes $60B Cursor Acquisition

Cursor confirmed that the AI coding company is now formally part of SpaceX, completing an option first structured in April. Cursor's announcement leaned heavily on infrastructure access, citing "the largest fleet of GPUs in the world" — the same SpaceX compute currently rented to Anthropic and Google. Combined with SpaceX's earlier absorption of xAI, this consolidates model training, compute, and a leading developer surface under one owner. The deal removes the most prominent independent AI-coding vendor from the market. Buyers of AI coding tools should reassess vendor concentration, neutrality, and data-governance assumptions.
