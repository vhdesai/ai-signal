# Anthropic to add invisible text-native watermarks to new Claude model outputs

Anthropic is reported to be adding statistically encoded, text-native watermarks to output from Claude models launched on or after August 2, 2026. Because the signal is embedded in token selection rather than stored as metadata, it is designed to survive copy-paste and light editing. The policy is reported to apply worldwide under the EU AI Act code of practice Anthropic signed, extending to existing models later. This makes Anthropic the second major lab after Google to deploy token-bias text watermarking.
