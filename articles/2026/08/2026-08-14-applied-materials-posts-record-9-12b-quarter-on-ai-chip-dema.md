# Applied Materials Posts Record $9.12B Quarter on AI Chip Demand

Applied Materials reported record third-quarter revenue of $9.12 billion, up roughly 25% year over year, and guided to as much as $10.75 billion in the current quarter as AI-driven semiconductor equipment spending continues. Shares slipped on the print, reflecting how much AI capex is already embedded in valuations. Equipment orders remain the earliest reliable indicator of 2027 fab capacity. Read alongside SMIC's reported price increases as a sign of tightening supply across the semiconductor value chain.
