# OpenAI-Backed Thrive Holdings Raises $2B at $12B Valuation

Thrive Holdings, which applies AI as an operating layer inside traditional service businesses rather than selling models, raised $2 billion at a $12 billion valuation with participation from SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. The thesis is roll-up economics: acquire labor-heavy service firms in accounting, IT, and compliance, then compress cost with AI. Execution risk sits in integration and reliability, not in model access.
