# Apple tells court its OpenAI trade secrets probe has widened to more ex-employees

In a new court filing, Apple said additional former employees may have retained or accessed confidential information before moving to OpenAI, expanding the scope of its trade secrets case. The claim escalates a dispute that already sits at the intersection of talent mobility and IP in an unusually fluid AI labor market. Expect tightened offboarding controls and more aggressive non-solicit enforcement across large AI employers.
