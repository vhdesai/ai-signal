# Liquid AI Open-Sources Pipette, a Reproducible On-Device Benchmarking Suite

Liquid AI released Pipette, an open-source platform for benchmarking foundation models on edge devices, built with Artificial Analysis as an independent evaluator. It measures model quality, quantization, runtime and hardware together, addressing the well-documented gap where model-card numbers rarely predict behavior on a phone. An evaluation and methodology contribution rather than a product launch.
