# Google Restructures AI Leadership; Hassabis Steps Back, Jeff Dean Departs

Google is consolidating AI leadership with Demis Hassabis moving from day-to-day DeepMind operations into a broader scientific and chairman-style role, while Koray Kavukcuoglu assumes greater operational responsibility. Longtime Google engineer Jeff Dean is leaving to co-found a startup focused on automating scientific research. Separate reporting indicates primary control of AI efforts is shifting from London toward Silicon Valley amid board concerns about coding and enterprise performance relative to Anthropic and OpenAI. The restructuring signals Alphabet prioritizing shipping cadence over research autonomy. BUSINESS MODEL
