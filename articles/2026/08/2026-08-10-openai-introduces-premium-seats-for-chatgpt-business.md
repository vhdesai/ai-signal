# OpenAI introduces Premium seats for ChatGPT Business

OpenAI added a Premium seat tier giving heavy users 5x more usage and removing the five-hour limit. Premium seats cost $125 per user per month, or $100 billed annually, and can be mixed with $25 Standard seats. Early sign-ups through August 20 receive up to $500 in credits. The tiering signals a move toward usage-segmented enterprise pricing.
