# Nvidia Reportedly Agrees to Acquire Hugging Face for $12.9B

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to buy Hugging Face for ~$12.9B. The deal would give Nvidia ownership of the primary open-model distribution layer at a moment when rivals are all building in-house silicon — a thriving open ecosystem keeps more of the market on Nvidia hardware. Treat as reported, not closed: terms, regulatory path, and timing are all unstated.
