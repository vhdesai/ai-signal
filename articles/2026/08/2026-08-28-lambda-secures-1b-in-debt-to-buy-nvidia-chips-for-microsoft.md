# Lambda Secures $1B in Debt to Buy Nvidia Chips for Microsoft; AI Debt Crosses $400B

Lambda raised $1B in private short-dated debt (JP Morgan) to buy Nvidia chips and lease to Microsoft — atop a $1B credit facility (May) and $926M GB300 GPU loan (this week). Lambda is in talks for a $3B pre-IPO round. Broader context: global AI-related debt has crossed $400B in 2026 (Bloomberg).
