# How an MIT Research Project Became a Global Programming Language

MIT News profiles Julia, the programming language that began as an MIT research project and now has a global following among scientists, engineers and mathematicians. The piece traces its use in frontier research and in designing new drugs, jet engines and heat pumps — a useful case study in how university-originated technical infrastructure compounds over a decade.
