# Anthropic names Tino Cuéllar Chief Global Affairs Officer

Anthropic announced that Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar, former California Supreme Court justice and Carnegie Endowment president, will join as Chief Global Affairs Officer. The hire deepens the company's policy bench at a moment when frontier-model governance is being written in several jurisdictions simultaneously. It follows a broader pattern of labs recruiting senior legal and diplomatic figures rather than conventional government-relations staff. Read it as preparation for a heavier regulatory engagement cycle. NewZenity
