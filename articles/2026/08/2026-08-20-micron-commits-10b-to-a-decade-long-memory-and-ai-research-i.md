# Micron commits $10B to a decade-long memory and AI research institute

Micron announced Micron Research Labs, a U.S.-based long-horizon research institution headquartered in Boise, Idaho, backed by a planned $10B commitment over ten years. The scope centers on memory architecture and AI systems research rather than near-term product engineering. The announcement lands amid volatility in memory pricing expectations and is positioned as a signal of confidence in sustained AI-driven demand.
