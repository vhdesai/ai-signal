# Anthropic raises its misalignment risk rating and shelves an internal frontier model

Anthropic's August 2026 risk report raises its catastrophic-misalignment rating from "very low" to "low," attributing the change to increased overall uncertainty rather than a single failed evaluation. The report discloses an unreleased internal model — "Model 2," materially more capable than Claude Mythos 5 — that Anthropic says it has no current plans to release because predeployment safety assessment is incomplete.
