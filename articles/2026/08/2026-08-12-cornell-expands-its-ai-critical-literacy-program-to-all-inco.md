# Cornell expands its AI Critical Literacy Program to all incoming students

Following a spring pilot, Cornell will offer its AI Critical Literacy Program in the fall to all incoming students as well as interested faculty and staff. Universities are moving from prohibition toward structured competency-building. Enterprises hiring from these pipelines should expect incoming talent with formal, not improvised, AI-usage training.
