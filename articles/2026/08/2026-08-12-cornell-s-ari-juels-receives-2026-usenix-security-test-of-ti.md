# Cornell's Ari Juels receives 2026 USENIX Security Test of Time Award for model-extraction research

The award, announced August 12 at the 35th USENIX Security Symposium in Baltimore, recognizes the 2016 paper "Stealing Machine Learning Models via Prediction APIs," co-authored with researchers now at ETH Zurich, Yale, Duke, and the University of Toronto. The work showed that models exposed through prediction APIs could be closely reconstructed through crafted queries.
