# Andreessen Horowitz Adds $1.75 Billion to Its Fifth Growth Fund, Bringing It to $8.5 Billion

Andreessen Horowitz said it raised an additional $1.75 billion for its fifth growth fund, taking the total to $8.5 billion after an initial $6.75 billion close in January. Partners David George and Raghu Raghuram wrote that “there has never been an investing environment like what we’re living through right now,” citing enterprise AI, defense tech and manufacturing. The raise comes days after the firm closed $1.1 billion for its first dedicated AI hardware fund targeting chips, memory, networking and storage.
