# Google says AI agents helped fix more than 1,000 Chrome security bugs

Google reported that AI-driven tooling identified and helped remediate 1,072 Chrome security vulnerabilities across two recent releases. The disclosure is one of the clearest production-scale examples of AI improving defensive software security workflows.
