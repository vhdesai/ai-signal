# Google DeepMind frames sign-language AI as a governed deployment, not just a model release

Google DeepMind's SL2T work is also notable as a research-to-product transition: the team says Deaf users shaped concept design, data collection, evaluation, and impact assessment. The group established the AI Sign Language Advisory Committee to guide real-world deployment and plans to extend the work to additional sign languages and sign-language generation.
