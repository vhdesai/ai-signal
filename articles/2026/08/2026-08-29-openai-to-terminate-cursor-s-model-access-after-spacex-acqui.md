# OpenAI to Terminate Cursor's Model Access After SpaceX Acquisition

OpenAI notified SpaceX that it will wind down the agreement supplying its models to Cursor on November 12, invoking a change-of-control clause triggered by SpaceX's $60 billion acquisition of Anysphere. OpenAI cited a lack of confidence that the acquirer would adhere to its terms of service, referencing prior contract disputes with Musk-owned entities. For enterprises, the episode is a concrete demonstration that model access can be withdrawn on corporate-control grounds, independent of technical performance or contract term. cnbc.com
