# Palantir lifts 2026 guidance as AI-driven commercial demand accelerates

Palantir raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance following a quarter in which U.S. commercial revenue grew sharply and cash flow accelerated, with the stock reacting strongly. Management attributed the momentum to enterprise and sovereign AI deployments moving from pilots into production contracts. The result is one of the cleaner public datapoints that applied AI spending is converting into recognized software revenue, not just infrastructure capex.
