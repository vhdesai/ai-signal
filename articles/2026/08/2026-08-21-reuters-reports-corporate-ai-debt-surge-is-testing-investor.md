# Reuters reports corporate AI debt surge is testing investor limits

Reuters reported that rising corporate debt tied to AI investment is testing investor appetite as fatigue emerges. The story fits a broader pattern of AI infrastructure commitments moving into leases, guarantees, structured financing, and balance-sheet exposure across the ecosystem. Senior leaders should expect investors and boards to press harder on utilization, payback periods, customer concentration, and residual-value assumptions behind AI capacity expansion.
