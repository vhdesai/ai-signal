# xAI Ships Grok 4.6 With 500K Context, Reaching Parity With GPT-5.6 Sol

xAI released Grok 4.6, a post-training upgrade focused on long-running agents, coding, and visual work, with a 500K-token context window and $2/$6 per-million pricing. Independent composite benchmarking put it level with GPT-5.6 Sol, notable because the gain came from post-training rather than a new pretraining run. Distribution is through the xAI API, Grok Build, and Cursor — an agent-tooling channel strategy rather than a consumer one. https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6
