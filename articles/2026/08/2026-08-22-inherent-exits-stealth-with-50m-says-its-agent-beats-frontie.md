# Inherent exits stealth with $50M, says its agent beats frontier labs at reproducing papers

London-based Inherent, founded by Google DeepMind alumni including chief scientist Edward Hughes, emerged from stealth with a $50M seed round. The company says its Faraday agent — built on a 27B open-weight base model and using a third-party coding model for implementation — outperformed Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at independently reproducing findings from published scientific papers. The team is roughly a dozen people and plans to reach 20–25 by year-end. The claim is vendor-reported and not yet independently replicated, but reproduction benchmarks are an increasingly credible proxy for real research capability. RESEARCH
