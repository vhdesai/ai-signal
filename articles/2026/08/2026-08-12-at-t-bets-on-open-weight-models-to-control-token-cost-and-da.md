# AT&T bets on open-weight models to control token cost and data exposure

AT&T is leaning heavily on open-weight models — reportedly around a quarter of its AI usage — to contain token costs and keep proprietary data in-house. The move reflects a maturing enterprise cost discipline: routing high-volume, low-complexity workloads to self-hosted open weights while reserving frontier APIs for hard tasks. Expect this hybrid routing pattern to become standard procurement practice.
