# Nvidia Falls 3.1% as Washington Reviews Offshore Routes to China AI Chip Access

Nvidia shares dropped 3.1% to $217 as Washington signaled a review of how Chinese firms access Nvidia silicon through offshore data centers. Separate reporting notes that Chinese labs remain heavily dependent on Nvidia because migrating CUDA training pipelines to Huawei Ascend and its CANN stack can add at least 50% more engineering time and cost, even as domestic hardware improves for inference. The offshore-access loophole is now the live policy question, not export licensing of the chips themselves. Research Breakthroughs RESEARCHHOT
