# Airbnb says AI is accelerating feature development and search testing

Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky said AI helped reduce the time from concept to shipped feature by as much as 60%, while the company shipped nearly 80% more features and improvements than in the same period last year. Airbnb is also testing AI search with natural-language queries and personalized, AI-generated listing highlights. The update is a concrete operating-model example of AI increasing product velocity before becoming the primary consumer interface.
