# SpaceX closes $60B all-stock acquisition of Cursor

SpaceX completed its purchase of AI coding company Cursor, exercising an option established in an April technology-development agreement. The transaction follows SpaceX's earlier absorption of xAI, consolidating Musk's AI assets under a single balance sheet. Cursor cited access to SpaceX's GPU fleet as the strategic rationale; the deal removes the most prominent independent AI-coding vendor from the market. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/spacex-officially-closes-its-cursor-acquisition/
