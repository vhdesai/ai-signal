# AI boom creates jobs beyond Silicon Valley

Yahoo Finance reported that AI-driven investment is creating jobs far from Silicon Valley, reflecting the geographic spread of data centers, energy projects, manufacturing, and infrastructure services. The shift broadens AI's economic footprint beyond software hubs into regions with power, land, industrial labor, and logistics capacity. That matters for policy and workforce planning because AI growth increasingly depends on physical infrastructure and regional development.
