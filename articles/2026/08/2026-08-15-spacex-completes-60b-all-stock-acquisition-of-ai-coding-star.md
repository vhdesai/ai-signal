# SpaceX Completes $60B All-Stock Acquisition of AI Coding Startup Cursor

SpaceX closed its $60B all-stock acquisition of Cursor, built by Anysphere, with the transaction finalizing Aug 14 and confirmed publicly Aug 15. The deal follows an April 2026 partnership that granted SpaceX a buyout option. It marks one of the largest developer-tooling acquisitions on record and advances SpaceX's push into AI tooling and large-scale compute.
