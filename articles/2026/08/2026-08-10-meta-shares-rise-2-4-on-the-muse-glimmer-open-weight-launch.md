# Meta shares rise ~2.4% on the Muse Glimmer open-weight launch

Meta stock rose roughly 2.4% following the Muse Glimmer announcement. The reaction suggests investors are reading the open-weight strategy as an ecosystem-capture play rather than a giveaway of frontier value. It is an early signal that markets may reward open distribution when paired with on-device reach.
