# Alibaba Qwen3.8-Max intensifies frontier and price competition

Alibaba's Qwen team introduced Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter MoE multimodal model that it says outperforms GPT-5.6 Sol Max and Claude/Fable 5 on an agentic desktop-computer-use benchmark. If independently validated, it would strengthen China's position in agentic systems and self-hosted frontier deployments.
