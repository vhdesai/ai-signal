# U.S. builds barriers around drones and robots — but China has the scale to route around them

TechCrunch argues Washington's 100% tariffs on Chinese drones and its ban on Chinese humanoid robots for military use will not offset China's manufacturing scale across physical-AI supply chains. The likely outcome is redirection of that capacity to other markets rather than displacement. Relevant to any diligence involving hardware-dependent AI supply chains.
