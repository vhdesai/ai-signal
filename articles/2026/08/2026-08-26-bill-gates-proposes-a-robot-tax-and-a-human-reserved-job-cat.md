# Bill Gates Proposes a Robot Tax and a "Human Reserved" Job Category

In a ~6,000-word essay, Gates argued the tax code structurally favors automation and proposed levies on both AI tokens and physical robots, with revenue funding retraining and safety nets. His second proposal, "Human Reserved," would designate roles society keeps in human hands regardless of AI capability — some permanently on ethical grounds, some as temporary transition buffers. He identifies customer service, sales, software engineering, paralegal work, construction, hospitality, and entry-level roles as exposed. Both proposals would materially compress frontier-lab economics.
