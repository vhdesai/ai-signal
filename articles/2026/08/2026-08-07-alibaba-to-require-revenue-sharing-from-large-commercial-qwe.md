# Alibaba to require revenue sharing from large commercial Qwen users

Alibaba plans to require revenue-sharing from companies generating more than $20 million in annual sales by offering its Qwen 3.8-Max model as a service. The weights remain downloadable, but the largest resellers would no longer get unlimited free commercial use. This is the first significant attempt by a major Chinese lab to monetize deployment of an open-weight model. URL: Yahoo Finance: Alibaba revenue sharing Qwen
