# SoftBank Explores Buying Majority Stake in 1X Humanoid Maker at $6B

SoftBank is in talks for a majority stake in 1X Technologies, an OpenAI-backed humanoid robot developer, at ~$6B. Physical AI M&A accelerates — following XPeng at $6.3B and Generalist at $3B in the same week. SoftBank’s interest signals the humanoid robotics thesis has moved from venture bets to strategic control positions. FUNDING
