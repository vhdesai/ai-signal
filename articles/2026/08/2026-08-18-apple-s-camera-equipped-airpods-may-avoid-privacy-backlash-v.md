# Apple's Camera-Equipped AirPods May Avoid Privacy Backlash via Recording Restrictions

Apple's leaked camera-equipped AirPods reportedly won't allow users to record photos or videos — a privacy-by-design approach avoiding backlash that hit other AI wearables. Cameras would only enable real-time AI vision features for Siri without persistent capture, positioning Apple to differentiate on privacy in the AI wearables race.
