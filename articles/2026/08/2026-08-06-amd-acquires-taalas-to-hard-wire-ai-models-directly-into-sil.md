# AMD acquires Taalas to hard-wire AI models directly into silicon

AMD agreed to acquire Taalas, a Toronto startup that builds custom chips around individual AI models, and plans to integrate the technology with its Instinct GPU roadmap for inference. The deal pushes AMD deeper into model-specific accelerators as it seeks differentiation against Nvidia. URL: SiliconANGLE: AMD acquires Taalas
