# AMD acquires Taalas to hard-wire AI models directly into silicon

AMD has agreed to acquire Taalas, a Toronto startup that builds custom chips around individual AI models, and plans to integrate the technology with its Instinct GPU roadmap for inference. The deal pushes AMD deeper into model-specific accelerators as it seeks differentiation against Nvidia in inference. It reflects a broader bet that fixed-function, model-in-silicon designs can sidestep the GPU memory-bandwidth wall. INFRASTRUCTURECHIPS
