# Tencent posts RMB 204.8B quarter with AI-boosted marketing and 65% higher AI capex

Tencent reported Q2 revenue of RMB 204.8B, up 11% year over year, with non-IFRS net profit up 9% to RMB 68.4B. Marketing services grew on AI-driven targeting and domestic gaming rose 17%, while AI infrastructure spending climbed roughly 65%. It is the cleanest evidence yet that Chinese platform incumbents are funding AI capex out of improving core-business economics rather than at the expense of them. https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TCEHY/earnings/TCEHY-Q2-2026-earnings_call-667897.html
