# China's CXMT Nears Intel's Market Cap, Rivaling World's Most Valuable Chipmakers

Chinese memory chipmaker ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) has reached a market cap of $540.5B, just behind Intel at $552.6B and ahead of Tencent at $505.8B. The rapid rise reflects surging demand for AI-related memory chips and China’s accelerating push for semiconductor self-sufficiency, reshaping the global power dynamics of the chip industry.
