# Cerebras Runs OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol at 750 Tokens Per Second in New Ultrafast Tier

Cerebras is serving OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol at roughly 750 tokens per second in a new ultrafast inference tier, corresponding to OpenAI's preview of "Ultrafast mode" at up to 14x baseline speed. Latency at this level changes what is architecturally feasible for interactive agents — multi-step reasoning chains that previously read as batch jobs become conversational. The commercial question is whether specialized silicon holds this margin as Nvidia's rack-scale systems close the inference-latency gap. https://www.unite.ai/cerebras-runs-openais-gpt-5-6-sol-at-750-tokens-per-second-in-new-ultrafast-tier/
