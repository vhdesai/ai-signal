# SoftBank secures a $10B margin loan backed by its OpenAI stake

SoftBank Group arranged a $10 billion margin loan collateralized by its ownership position in OpenAI, adding to borrowing used to fund its artificial intelligence investments. The structure shows how aggressively SoftBank is leveraging existing AI positions to finance further bets rather than raising fresh equity. It is a notable signal about how private AI stakes are now being treated as financeable collateral. Rising leverage against illiquid AI positions is worth watching as a systemic risk indicator.
