# Wispr raises $280M at a $2B valuation, expanding beyond AI dictation

Wispr, known for its AI dictation product, raised a $280 million Series B led by Menlo Ventures at a $2 billion valuation. The company signaled ambitions beyond transcription into broader voice-driven interaction. The round is a useful signal that the input-layer of enterprise productivity — how users address agents — is being priced as a defensible category rather than a feature. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/wispr-raises-280m-at-2b-valuation-as-it-looks-beyond-dictation/
