# Anthropic signs ~$45B compute deal with Nscale

Anthropic has agreed to rent approximately $45 billion of AI compute from British infrastructure provider Nscale, covering around 460 megawatts at a West Virginia data center development. It is among the largest publicly reported compute commitments by any AI lab and continues Anthropic's pattern of locking in multi-vendor capacity. The commitment implies substantial forward revenue assumptions and reinforces power availability as the binding constraint. COMPUTEHYPERSCALERS
