# Vantage Data Centers Explores IPO at ~$100B

Silver Lake- and DigitalBridge-backed Vantage explores an IPO at ~$100B that could raise ~$10B, or an outright sale. Vantage raised ~$11B since late 2023 and is involved in a Wisconsin campus tied to the OpenAI–Oracle Stargate buildout. A listing at this level reprices hyperscale data centers as critical technology infrastructure rather than real estate. Aligns with Anthropic’s IPO and Nvidia’s financing vehicle — AI infrastructure is becoming a formal asset class.
