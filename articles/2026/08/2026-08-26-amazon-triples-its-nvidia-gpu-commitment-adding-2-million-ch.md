# Amazon Triples Its Nvidia GPU Commitment, Adding 2 Million Chips Through 2028

Announced on Nvidia’s earnings call, the expanded partnership adds ~2 million Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra GPUs to AWS data centers in 2027–2028 — triple a commitment from five months ago. The deal extends past silicon into Nvidia networking, CPUs, Nemotron models, and robotics across AWS. The tension: Amazon deepens Nvidia dependency while its custom-chip business crossed $25B ARR.
