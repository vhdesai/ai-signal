# shuts down creator revenue sharing to curb AI-generated spam

X is winding down its Creator Revenue Sharing program on September 7, 2026 and replacing it with an Original Content Rewards Program that pays for verified Premium impressions on original work. The company said the prior program’s “incentives were misaligned,” rewarding aggregators, clickbait accounts and low-effort AI-generated reposts. Aggregator payouts have already been cut 60%, with a further 20% reduction planned. This is one of the first large-platform monetization redesigns explicitly built around AI slop economics.
