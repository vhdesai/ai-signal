# Rippling launches AI Spend Console after internal token costs spiked

Rippling launched AI Spend Console after discovering that AI token usage was on track to equal 40% of its R&D headcount budget, with one engineer reportedly spending $50,000 per month. The tool maps AI spend by employee, team, and role and tries to connect usage to productivity outcomes rather than raw consumption. This is a high-signal enterprise pattern: after initial AI enthusiasm, companies are moving into cost governance, model routing, and ROI measurement.
