# Cognition Reportedly in Talks to Raise at $40B Valuation

AI coding startup Cognition, maker of the Devin coding agent, is reportedly in talks to raise at a valuation of at least $40B — up from $26B just three months ago. The leap is predicated on achieving $1B in annualized revenue run rate.
