# Anthropic appoints Tino Cuellar as first Chief Global Affairs Officer

Anthropic appointed Mariano-Florentino "Tino" Cuellar as its first Chief Global Affairs Officer, a new C-suite role focused on policy, international engagement, and government relations. Cuellar previously led the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, served on the California Supreme Court, and was a trustee of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust. The appointment signals that Anthropic sees geopolitics, regulation, and government engagement as core operating issues rather than support functions.
