# Pew: One-Third of Web Pages Published Since ChatGPT’s Launch Show Signs of AI Authorship

35% of web pages published after ChatGPT’s launch show “significant signs of AI authorship” (Pew analysis on Common Crawl data). Pages on .com domains showed AI authorship at ~10x the rate of .edu/.gov domains. The finding arrives after Cloudflare reported bot traffic overtook human traffic—creating a web where “bots read web pages written by other bots.” Data provenance is now a first-order quality concern.
