# AWS and Nvidia to deploy two million additional GPUs for AI workloads

AWS and Nvidia plan to deploy two million more GPUs for AI workloads, extending their partnership into CPUs, networking and robotics. The additional capacity augments Nvidia hardware already running on AWS, which continues to balance Nvidia supply against its in-house Trainium and Inferentia silicon. The report positions this as another escalation in the hyperscaler race to expand AI compute capacity.
