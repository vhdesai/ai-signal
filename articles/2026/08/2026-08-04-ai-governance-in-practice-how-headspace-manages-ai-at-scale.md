# AI Governance in Practice: How Headspace Manages AI at Scale

CIO Dive profiles how Headspace is tackling AI governance as employees across the organization build apps, agents, and automations faster than traditional IT oversight can track. The case study illustrates a challenge facing every enterprise: when AI development is democratized, governance must evolve from centralized approval gates to distributed guardrails. Headspace's approach includes code review frameworks, usage monitoring, and risk-tiering systems that allow rapid experimentation while maintaining safety standards. For CIOs, the practical lesson is that AI governance cannot be bolted on retroactively — it must be embedded in the development workflow from the start.
