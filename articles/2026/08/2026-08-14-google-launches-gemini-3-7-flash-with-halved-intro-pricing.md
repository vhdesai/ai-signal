# Google launches Gemini 3.7 Flash with halved intro pricing

Google shipped Gemini 3.7 Flash just three weeks after 3.6 Flash, positioning it as the workhorse model for coding and agentic workloads. Reported benchmarks jump materially — FrontierCode from 34.4% to 43.6% and DeepSWE from 48.6% to 65.3%. Introductory pricing is cut roughly in half to $0.75/$3.75 per million tokens through the end of 2026. It is live across the Gemini API, AI Studio, Antigravity, and Spark.
