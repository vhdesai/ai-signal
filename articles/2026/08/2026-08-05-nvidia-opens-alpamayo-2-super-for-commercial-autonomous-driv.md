# NVIDIA opens Alpamayo 2 Super for commercial autonomous-driving development

NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34B-parameter open vision-language-action model for autonomous driving, under a permissive commercial license. The model pairs a 32B reasoning backbone with a diffusion-based action decoder and targets long-tail driving events where conventional planners fail. The commercial license lowers the entry cost for robotaxi developers and extends NVIDIA's stack into open model layers above the silicon. URL: MarkTechPost: NVIDIA Alpamayo 2 Super
