# NVIDIA opens Alpamayo 2 Super for commercial autonomous-driving development

NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34B-parameter vision-language-action model for autonomous driving, under a commercial-use license. The model targets trajectory generation, causal reasoning, auto-labeling, and visual question answering for robotaxi and ADAS developers.
