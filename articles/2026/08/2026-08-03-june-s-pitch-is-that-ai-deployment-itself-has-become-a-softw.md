# June’s pitch is that AI deployment itself has become a software category, not just a services problem handled by…

June’s pitch is that AI deployment itself has become a software category, not just a services problem handled by expensive implementation teams. - By scanning systems like Salesforce, ServiceNow, Databricks, and Workday, it aims to map process friction and recommend where agents can be inserted with highest leverage. - The caliber of investors indicates broad conviction that the last mile of enterprise AI integration is now a venture-scale opportunity. - If successful, tools like this could productize work that is currently bespoke, slow, and heavily dependent on scarce forward-deployed talent. - For CIOs, the emergence of this category suggests the next control plane in enterprise AI may be workflow discovery and deployment orchestration.
