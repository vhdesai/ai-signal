# Planned Amazon data center could become the largest U.S. climate polluter

TechCrunch reported that Amazon is investing in an on-site natural-gas power plant for a planned Texas data center that could be permitted to emit 33 million tons of carbon dioxide per year, citing The New York Times. Amazon said the facility would use new on-site generation that would not raise electricity costs for Texas families, but the plan adds to concerns about AI's emissions footprint.
