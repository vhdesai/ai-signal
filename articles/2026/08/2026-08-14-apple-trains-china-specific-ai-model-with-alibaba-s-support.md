# Apple Trains China-Specific AI Model With Alibaba’s Support

Apple trained its own China-specific model with Alibaba’s help and secured regulatory clearance to ship it — a departure from relying on a third-party model. Gives Apple more control in a market with both regulatory constraints and Huawei competition. Shifts Apple–Alibaba from supplier to training partner. For multinationals: regulatory fragmentation now forces separate model stacks per market, not just separate data residency.
