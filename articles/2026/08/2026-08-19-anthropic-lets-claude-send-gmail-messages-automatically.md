# Anthropic Lets Claude Send Gmail Messages Automatically

Anthropic enabled Claude to send emails on a user's behalf through its Gmail integration without requiring per-message approval, though approval-first remains the default setting. The change is small in surface area and significant in principle: it moves an assistant from drafting to executing an irreversible external action. Organizations deploying Claude with mailbox access should confirm which approval posture is enforced by policy rather than left to individual user preference.
