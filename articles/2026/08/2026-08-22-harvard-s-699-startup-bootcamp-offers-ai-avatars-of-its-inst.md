# Harvard's $699 startup bootcamp offers AI avatars of its instructors

Harvard Business School's eight-week “HBS Foundry” bootcamp for entrepreneurs includes AI avatars of its instructors, built by startup HeyGen, paired with weekly live instructor sessions. The $699 program illustrates how quickly synthetic-presenter tooling is moving into elite executive education. Expect similar hybrid human/avatar formats to surface in corporate training procurement conversations.
