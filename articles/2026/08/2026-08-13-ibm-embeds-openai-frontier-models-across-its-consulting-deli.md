# IBM Embeds OpenAI Frontier Models Across Its Consulting Delivery Platform

IBM announced a strategic partnership placing OpenAI's frontier models — including GPT-5.6 — plus Codex and ChatGPT Work directly into IBM Consulting's delivery platform and enterprise offerings. IBM will stand up a dedicated OpenAI practice, train and certify tens of thousands of consultants, and embed the models into IBM Consulting Advantage. The deal gives OpenAI a channel into IBM's large regulated-industry client base while giving IBM a frontier-model story it could not build organically with Granite alone. It lands less than a year after IBM's Anthropic alliance, signaling a multi-model posture rather than exclusivity. The move extends OpenAI's systems-integrator distribution strategy alongside Infosys and TCS, and puts further pressure on Accenture and other global SIs to declare model alignments.
