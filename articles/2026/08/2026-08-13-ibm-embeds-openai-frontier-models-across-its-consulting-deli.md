# IBM Embeds OpenAI Frontier Models Across Its Consulting Delivery Platform

IBM announced a strategic partnership placing GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work directly into IBM Consulting’s delivery platform. IBM will stand up a dedicated OpenAI practice, train tens of thousands of consultants, and embed the models into Consulting Advantage. The deal gives OpenAI a channel into IBM’s regulated-industry client base while giving IBM a frontier-model story. It lands less than a year after IBM’s Anthropic alliance, signaling multi-model posture. Extends OpenAI’s SI distribution strategy alongside Infosys and TCS, pressuring Accenture to declare model alignments. TechCrunch GEOPOLITICS
