# Microsoft Closes Up for 2026, Finally

The Information reports that Microsoft's stock has finally closed positive for 2026, a milestone that reflects the market's growing confidence in the company's AI monetization trajectory through Azure, Copilot, and its Anthropic partnership. The recovery comes after a rough first half when investors questioned whether massive AI infrastructure spending would translate into revenue growth. For enterprise technology leaders, Microsoft's performance validates the "AI platform company" thesis — that companies embedding AI across their product portfolio (cloud, productivity, security, developer tools) can command premium valuations even amid broader market turbulence.
