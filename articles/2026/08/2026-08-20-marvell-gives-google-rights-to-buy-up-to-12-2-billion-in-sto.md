# Marvell Gives Google Rights to Buy Up to $12.2 Billion in Stock as Part of Chip Deal

Marvell Technology has given Google the right to acquire a stake of up to $12.2 billion as part of an expanded chip partnership. The deal deepens the financial interweaving of hyperscalers and their semiconductor suppliers — with chipmakers increasingly offering equity stakes to secure long-term supply commitments. The arrangement reflects how custom chip partnerships are evolving from simple vendor-customer relationships into strategic alliances with significant financial entanglements.
