# Sony Music and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic Over Alleged Copyright Infringement

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell Music filed suit against Anthropic alleging a "brazen campaign" to train Claude on copyrighted music, with potential damages reaching $150,000 per song. The lawsuit adds another major copyright front at a moment when Anthropic is preparing a large public listing. Model supply chains and training-data governance remain material legal and reputational risks.
