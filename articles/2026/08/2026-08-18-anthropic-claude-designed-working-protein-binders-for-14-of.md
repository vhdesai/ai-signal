# Anthropic: Claude designed working protein binders for 14 of 15 targets

Anthropic published lab-validated results in which Claude models designed protein binders that succeeded against 14 of 15 targets, with 22–35% of designs binding versus a typical industry rate of 10–15%. In a separate test, Claude Opus 5 analyzed raw NMR and LC-MS data in 23 and 19 minutes and matched a lab's purity reading (96.4% vs 96.33%).
