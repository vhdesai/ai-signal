# Robotaxi industry splits between federal acceleration and local pushback

TechCrunch reports that Zoox cleared a key federal NHTSA hurdle for paid robotaxi service in Las Vegas, while Waymo faces local scrutiny in San Francisco and lawmakers are pushing national autonomous-vehicle safety standards. The story also notes Baidu-Lyft testing in London and broader competition among robotaxi platforms. The takeaway is that autonomy deployment is entering a more fragmented regulatory phase: federal permissions may not translate cleanly into local operating freedom.
