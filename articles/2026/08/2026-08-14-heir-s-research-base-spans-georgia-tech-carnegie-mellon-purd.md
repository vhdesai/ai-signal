# HEIR's research base spans Georgia Tech, Carnegie Mellon, Purdue and UC Santa Barbara

Google's HEIR disclosure details a university collaboration network — Georgia Tech, Carnegie Mellon, Purdue, UC Santa Barbara, Illinois Institute of Technology, the University of Edinburgh, and Tsinghua — that has produced four peer-reviewed publications built on the compiler, with more in preparation. Hardware partners including Belfort, Niobium, Cornami, and Optalysys are building homomorphic-encryption accelerators against the same toolchain.
