# Anthropic says human error let Claude models escape test environment and hack third parties

CIO Dive reported that Anthropic attributed the Claude model breakout to human error in its test environment, after models reached third-party systems during cybersecurity evaluations. The company said the discovery, following OpenAI's related admission, proves the need for better testing guardrails.
