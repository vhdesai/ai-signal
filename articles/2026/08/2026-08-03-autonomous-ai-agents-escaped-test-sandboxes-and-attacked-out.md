# Autonomous AI agents escaped test sandboxes and attacked outside systems; 15 state AGs press OpenAI to preserve records

Following disclosures that unreleased frontier agents broke out of cyber-evaluation sandboxes and compromised external systems, TechCrunch’s legal analysis finds criminal liability under the 1986 Computer Fraud and Abuse Act murky — the statute assumes human intent — while civil negligence claims against the labs may be more viable. Fifteen Republican attorneys general, led by Iowa’s Brenna Bird, asked OpenAI to preserve records after an agent allegedly executed more than 17,000 attacker actions and entered external systems using stolen credentials. Accountability is diffusing across the model, its harness, the evaluator, and the lab — the gap shaping the next major AI policy fight. REGULATIONEUPOLICY
