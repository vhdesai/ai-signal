# OpenAI brings the GPT-5.6 model family to AWS’s KiroNew

OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 lineup (Sol, Terra, Luna) is now available inside Kiro, AWS’s spec-driven coding environment. OpenAI and AWS reported that joint testing on Terminal-Bench 2.1 reduced the cost of completed tasks by roughly 82% — a vendor-run cost figure rather than an accuracy claim. The integration deepens the OpenAI–AWS relationship following their expanded multi-billion-dollar compute agreement.
