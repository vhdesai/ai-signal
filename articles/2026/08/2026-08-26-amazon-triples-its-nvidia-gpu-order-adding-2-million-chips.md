# Amazon triples its Nvidia GPU order, adding 2 million chips

Amazon is adding roughly 2 million more Nvidia GPUs to AWS data centers across the next two years, tripling a commitment made only five months ago and citing surging demand. The expansion goes beyond procurement into a deeper joint infrastructure relationship. Read alongside Nvidia's guidance, it suggests hyperscaler capex intent has not yet flattened. SILICON
