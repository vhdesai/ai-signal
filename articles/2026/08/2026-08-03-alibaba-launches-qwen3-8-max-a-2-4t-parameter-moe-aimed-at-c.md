# Alibaba launches Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4T-parameter MoE aimed at coding and long-horizon work

Alibaba launched what it called its “most capable” model yet — Qwen3.8-Max, a Mixture-of-Experts system (reported at ~2.4T parameters with ~95B active) targeting coding and multi-step professional tasks, claiming parity with or advantage over leading U.S. models on internal benchmarks. Reported API pricing of roughly $2 per million input / $6 per million output tokens undercuts several rivals, with Qwen Chat availability and downloadable weights expected shortly. The release continues China’s pattern of pairing frontier-scale capability with aggressive price positioning. Products & Tools PRODUCTVOICEOPENAI
