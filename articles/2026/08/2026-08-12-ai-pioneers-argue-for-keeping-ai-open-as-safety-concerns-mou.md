# AI pioneers argue for keeping AI open as safety concerns mount

At the Ai4 conference, Geoffrey Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, and Andrew Ng made different but overlapping arguments for preserving openness in AI. Hinton distinguished open-source software from open-weight models and warned that open weights can lower the barrier to misuse, while Ng argued that openness prevents a small number of labs from becoming gatekeepers and is also a source of geopolitical soft power. The debate captures the policy tradeoff now facing governments and enterprises: openness supports competition and innovation, but model access controls remain a core safety lever.
