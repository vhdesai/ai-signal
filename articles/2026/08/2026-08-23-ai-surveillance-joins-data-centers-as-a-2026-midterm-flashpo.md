# AI Surveillance Joins Data Centers as a 2026 Midterm Flashpoint

Members of Congress and congressional candidates are moving to harness grassroots anger over AI surveillance, with Flock's camera network joining data centers as a target in midterm campaigning. The development extends a backlash that has already produced hundreds of local restrictions on data center construction and is now surfacing as national-level political positioning. For companies with siting, permitting, or public-sector exposure, the practical implication is that community consent and surveillance optics are becoming schedule risks on par with power and supply.
