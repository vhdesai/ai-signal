# Salesforce and Anthropic Launch "Claudeforce" CRM Plugin

Salesforce and Anthropic expanded their partnership with a joint plugin that gives sales teams 37 prebuilt skills for querying live CRM data and automating tasks directly inside Claude. The integration extends Claude into Salesforce workflows and Slack, positioning it as an operating layer over system-of-record data rather than a standalone assistant. It is one of the clearest examples yet of a frontier lab embedding agentic capability inside an incumbent enterprise suite.
