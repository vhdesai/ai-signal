# DeepSeek V4-Flash sharpens price pressure on frontier labs

DeepSeek released V4-Flash, a smaller version of its flagship open-source model that early tests suggest can compete with larger open and proprietary systems at much lower cost. The Information reported that V4-Flash appears competitive with OpenAI GPT-5.6 Luna and Zhipu GLM-5.2 on several benchmarks, with cost per task potentially 60% lower than Luna.
