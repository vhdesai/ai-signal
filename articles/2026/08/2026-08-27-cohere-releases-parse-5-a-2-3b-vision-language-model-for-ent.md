# Cohere releases Parse 5, a 2.3B vision-language model for enterprise documents

Cohere released Parse 5, a 2.3B-parameter vision-language model that converts PDFs, slides, and images into Markdown with HTML tables, bounding boxes, and image descriptions. MarkTechPost reports a ParseBench score of 79.2, ahead of Mistral OCR 4, Azure Document Intelligence, and Databricks AI Parse. The write-up cautions that the headline figure averages only three of the benchmark's five dimensions, omitting charts and visual grounding.
