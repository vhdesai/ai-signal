# DeepMind strategy chief frames record AI capex as a bet on recursive self-improvement

Google DeepMind Chief Strategy Officer Jasjeet Sekhon, speaking at UC Berkeley’s Agentic AI Summit, characterized the industry’s unprecedented capital spending as a wager on recursive self-improvement — systems that improve their own capabilities — while acknowledging current revenue does not yet justify the outlay. The remarks put an explicit thesis under the hyperscaler build-out and the “is $1T enough” debate. The risk it crystallizes: the return depends on a capability step-change that has not yet arrived.
