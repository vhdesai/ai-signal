# OpenAI's First Custom Chip Named "Jalapeño"

The Information's Briefing reveals that OpenAI's first internally designed chip has been named "Jalapeño," marking the company's push into custom silicon to reduce its dependence on Nvidia. The chip development underscores how frontier AI labs are increasingly investing in proprietary hardware to control costs and optimize inference performance.
