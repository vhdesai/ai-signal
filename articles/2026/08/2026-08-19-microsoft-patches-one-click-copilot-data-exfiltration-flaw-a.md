# Microsoft Patches One-Click Copilot Data-Exfiltration Flaw After Eight Months

Microsoft shipped a fix for "CoSnitch," a vulnerability chain that let a single click exfiltrate enterprise email and calendar data through Copilot's connected accounts, with no confirmation prompt and the option to plant persistent instructions. The flaw was reported nearly eight months before the patch landed. The governance takeaway: AI assistants with connector access need the same patch SLAs, monitoring and exposure tracking as core productivity infrastructure.
