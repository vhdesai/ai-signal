# Apple briefly posts — then pulls — a guide for connecting Siri to Alibaba’s Qwen in China

Apple published and then quickly withdrew documentation describing how Siri connects to Alibaba’s Qwen models for the China market. The brief exposure is the clearest confirmation yet of the localization architecture behind Apple’s China AI strategy. The retraction suggests the partnership is not yet cleared for public announcement.
