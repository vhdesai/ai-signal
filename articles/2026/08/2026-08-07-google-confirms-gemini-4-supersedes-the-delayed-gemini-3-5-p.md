# Google Confirms Gemini 4 Supersedes the Delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro

Google DeepMind confirmed development of Gemini 4 and effectively retired the repeatedly delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro, with Kavukcuoglu owning the program following the leadership restructuring. The move consolidates a fragmented release cadence — Gemini 3.6 Flash shipped in July without matching Pro or Ultra variants — into a single next-generation line. Enterprises with Gemini 3.5-dependent roadmaps should assume a version-skip migration rather than an incremental upgrade.
