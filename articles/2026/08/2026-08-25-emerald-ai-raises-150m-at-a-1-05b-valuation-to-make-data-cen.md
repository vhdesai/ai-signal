# Emerald AI raises $150M at a $1.05B valuation to make data center load flexible

Emerald AI closed a $150M round led by DCVC and Energize Capital, with Nvidia, Samsung Ventures, GE Vernova and Salesforce Ventures participating, valuing the two-year-old company at $1.05B. Its Conductor platform lets utilities signal data centers to throttle, shift or reschedule workloads in real time rather than waiting on grid build-outs. Silicon Valley Power engaged Emerald because roughly 55 data centers on its 20-square-mile grid are exhausting spare capacity as AI customers request more power immediately. The round signals that grid flexibility software is becoming an investable layer between compute clusters and utilities.
