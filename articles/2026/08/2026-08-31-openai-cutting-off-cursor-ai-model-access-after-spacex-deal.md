# OpenAI cutting off Cursor AI model access after SpaceX deal

Quartz reports that OpenAI is cutting off Cursor’s access to its models following Cursor’s acquisition by SpaceX, consistent with OpenAI’s own earlier statement that it would wind down the contract. The story underscores how strategic control of developer tooling, model distribution, and competitive adjacency can override near-term API revenue.
