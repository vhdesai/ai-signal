# AI Was Supposed to Win People Over by Now — It Hasn't

Pew finds 52% of Americans are "more concerned than excited" about AI, up from 37% in 2021. CNBC poll shows a majority of 18–34 year-olds don't trust AI leaders. The Republican NRSC warned AI companies that data centers are hurting Ohio elections. Airbnb CEO Chesky acknowledged the industry "isn't shipping products regular people like." Souring consumer sentiment is becoming a business problem — a headwind for adoption and a tailwind for regulation.
