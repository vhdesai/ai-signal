# Etched's valuation doubles to $21B in a month

Etched raised a new round at a $21 billion valuation, roughly double its mark from a month earlier. The round was led by Jane Street, which had installed Etched's first shipped AI cluster system and moved from customer to lead investor. The compression of the funding cycle is a signal that inference-specialized silicon is being validated on deployed performance rather than roadmap promises. ANALYSIS
