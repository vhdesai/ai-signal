# Anthropic: Worker-Retraining Programs May Not Scale to AI Displacement

A meta-analysis of 56 randomized U.S. studies plus European evidence found typical job-training programs lift employment by only 2–3 percentage points and earnings by ~$1,000/year against ~$13,000/participant cost, leaving most programs close to fiscally break-even. A handful of high-performing “sector programs” showed larger gains but replication attempts have repeatedly failed. The authors conclude that if AI displaces workers at the scale current projections suggest, existing retraining infrastructure would likely fall far short. The most-cited policy remedy — “we’ll retrain workers” — should be treated as an unproven assumption rather than a viable plan. Technology executives should invest in internal workforce transition programs rather than relying on government retraining infrastructure. Anthropic NEW INFERENCE
