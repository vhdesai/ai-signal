# Z.ai Ships GLM-5.3 With No New Base Model — All Gains From Post-Training

GLM-5.3 reuses the 743B-parameter GLM-5.2 base, with every reported gain attributed to scaled post-training rather than a new pretraining run. Terminal-Bench 3.0 rises from 4.6 to 28.3, and CyberGym reaches 84.5%, edging GPT-5.6 Sol on that benchmark. Z.ai flagged the cybersecurity gain as unplanned and is withholding open weights for roughly two weeks pending safety evaluation and hardening. Benchmarks are vendor-reported and await independent verification. The broader signal: post-training is now the primary performance lever for established base models, with cost-structure implications for organizations deciding whether to retrain or refine.
