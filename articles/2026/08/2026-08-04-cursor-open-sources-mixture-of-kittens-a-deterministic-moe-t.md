# Cursor open-sources Mixture-of-Kittens, a deterministic MoE training megakernel

Cursor released MoK, a deterministic mixture-of-experts training megakernel targeted at GB300 NVL72 racks, and reports it runs up to 2.37x faster than the strongest public baseline. Determinism matters as much as the speedup: reproducible MoE training runs make debugging and compliance auditing of large training jobs tractable. The release is notable coming from an applications company rather than a frontier lab, signaling that infrastructure differentiation is moving down the stack. Benchmarks are vendor-reported and warrant independent validation. TrendingNVIDIA / Research
