# Weekly Funding Concentrates in Data, AI Infrastructure, Defense, and AI Coding

Crunchbase's weekly tally shows Databricks returning for another $5 billion just eight months after a raise of the same size, alongside large rounds for River AI ($1.1B), data center and electricity storage companies, defense startups, and AI coding platforms. The composition matters more than the totals: capital is flowing to the physical and data substrate rather than to application-layer differentiation. That pattern is consistent with an investment thesis that compute, power, and proprietary data — not model access — are the durable moats.
