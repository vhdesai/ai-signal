# Nvidia beats on fiscal Q2 with $96.22B revenue — shares still slip after hours

Nvidia posted fiscal Q2 2027 revenue of $96.22B against roughly $92.17B expected, with EPS of $2.22 versus $2.10. Despite the beat, shares fell after hours to about $206 from a $209.91 close, with the Nasdaq climbing on August 27 as markets digested the print. The reaction suggests expectations, not results, are now the binding constraint on AI-infrastructure sentiment.
