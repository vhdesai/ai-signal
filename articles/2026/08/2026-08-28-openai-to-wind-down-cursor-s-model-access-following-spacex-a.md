# OpenAI to Wind Down Cursor’s Model Access Following SpaceX Acquisition

OpenAI notified SpaceX it will terminate the contract supplying OpenAI models to the Cursor coding tool, proposing a Nov 12, 2026 shutoff. OpenAI said it cannot be confident that SpaceX — which also holds X and xAI — will honor its terms of service, citing prior contract violations by Musk companies, and referenced added accountability requirements around its upcoming model, Astra. For enterprises, this is a live demonstration that model supply is a competitive lever, not a utility.
