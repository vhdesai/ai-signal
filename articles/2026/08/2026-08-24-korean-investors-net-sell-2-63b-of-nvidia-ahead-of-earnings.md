# Korean Investors Net-Sell ~$2.63B of Nvidia Ahead of Earnings

Korean retail and institutional investors net sold approximately ₩3.64 trillion (about $2.63 billion) of Nvidia stock, according to data from the Korea Securities Depository's SEIBRO portal. The selling reflects cooling enthusiasm for the AI trade among a retail base that had been among the most aggressive overseas buyers of US AI names. Flow data of this kind is an early indicator of sentiment shifts that typically precede broader repositioning. FUNDING
