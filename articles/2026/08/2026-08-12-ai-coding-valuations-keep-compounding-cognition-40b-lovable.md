# AI Coding Valuations Keep Compounding: Cognition $40B, Lovable $13.3B

Cognition is negotiating at ~$40B (months after closing $1B at $26B), tied to a reported $1B ARR. Lovable closed $400M at $13.3B after hitting ~$500M ARR in June.
