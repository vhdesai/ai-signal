# Robinhood Ventures Fund II IPOs for Retail AI Startup Access

Robinhood Ventures Fund II began trading on NYSE after raising ~$200M at $25/share. Partnered with Y Combinator, invested in 80 early-stage startups. Fund I is up ~40% since March. Robinhood argues Main Street shouldn’t be shut out from companies like Anthropic and OpenAI, though critics warn of significant risk for retail venture investors. The listing reflects a broader trend of AI startup economics being packaged for retail distribution — a development regulators are likely to scrutinize. GEOPOLITICS
