# The Information reports that America increasingly dislikes data centers

The Information reported on rising U.S. opposition to data centers, a trend increasingly tied to AI infrastructure buildout. The operational risk is straightforward: land, power, water, grid interconnection, and permitting are becoming public-policy constraints, not just facilities decisions. AI infrastructure leaders should expect community acceptance and utility impact to become gating factors for capacity expansion. EDUCATIONAI AVATARS
