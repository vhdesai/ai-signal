# Nvidia’s planned ~$750B AI outlay draws “circular financing” and bubble scrutiny

NPR reported that Nvidia is set to spend on the order of $750 billion across the AI supply chain, prompting critics to warn of “circular financing” — where chipmakers, clouds, and model labs fund each other’s demand. The scale is fueling a broader debate about whether AI infrastructure investment has outrun near-term returns.
