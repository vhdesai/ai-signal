# OpenAI expands ChatGPT advertising to five new international markets

In an August 11 update to its advertising post, OpenAI said ChatGPT Ads has launched in the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and South Korea, extending the pilot beyond the US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. Ads appear only for logged-in adult Free and Go users and are labeled as sponsored. OpenAI states that ads do not influence model answers and that chat content is not shared with advertisers.
