# Tesla sets September 3 Cybercab launch event in Austin

Tesla emailed invitations for a September 3 Cybercab launch event in Austin, promising “exclusive access” to the two-passenger driverless vehicle built without a steering wheel or pedals. It is the first purpose-built Tesla robotaxi to appear publicly since the October 2024 reveal. The event follows Nevada’s recent approval for Tesla to operate commercial robotaxis in Clark County.
