# Hugging Face Fielding M&A Interest at $13 Billion or More

Hugging Face is exploring a sale valuing the open model repository at $13B+ and has retained a bank. That would be roughly a 3x step-up from its last outside round of $235M at a $4.5B valuation about three years ago. A change of control at the default distribution point for open weights would be structurally significant for any enterprise open-model strategy.
