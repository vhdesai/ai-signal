# Adversarial Patterns Defeat 11 Surveillance Camera Systems at Def Con

Researcher Bill Swearingen demonstrated that RL-generated visual patterns printed on clothing and vehicles defeat detection by 11 open-source surveillance algorithms — including Flock license plate readers, Axon body cameras, and Clearview AI facial recognition. After 31M training tests, the system generates improving patterns every minute. A live test on a Toyota Yaris evaded a Flock camera. The patterns don’t block recording — they prevent the algorithmic detection layer from triggering alerts. What to Watch * Microsoft’s Maia 300 reveal timeline and whether Anthropic or other large customers commit publicly. * Anthropic’s August 14 auto-mode flip — watch for enterprise opt-out patterns and any incidents in the first week.
