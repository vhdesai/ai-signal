# NVIDIA Reports Fiscal Q2 Results Today Amid a Sharp Pre-Print Selloff

NVIDIA reports fiscal Q2 after today’s close in the sector’s most-watched print, with attention on AI-accelerator demand and forward guidance. Shares fell sharply through the week despite broadly positive beat expectations. Analysts consistently frame guidance versus expectations — not the headline numbers — as the variable that moves the stock and, by extension, AI capex sentiment.
