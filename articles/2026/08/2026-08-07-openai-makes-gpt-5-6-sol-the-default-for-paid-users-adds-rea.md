# OpenAI makes GPT-5.6 Sol the default for paid users, adds reasoning-effort slider

OpenAI promoted GPT-5.6 Sol to the default model for Plus and Pro users with a new reasoning-effort slider that lets users trade latency against depth. Sol produced 68% fewer factual errors than the prior model on high-stakes finance, medicine, and law evaluations. Inference budget is now a product surface, not just an infrastructure setting—signaling that cost-per-quality-level will increasingly shape enterprise model procurement.
