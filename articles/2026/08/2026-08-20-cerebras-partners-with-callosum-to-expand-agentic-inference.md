# Cerebras Partners With Callosum to Expand Agentic Inference in Europe

Cerebras announced a partnership with London-based frontier-AI and compute-software company Callosum to deliver ultra-low-latency heterogeneous agentic inference. Callosum's software orchestrates multi-agent workloads across Cerebras' Wafer-Scale Engine silicon. The deal expands Cerebras' European footprint, following its recent announcement of 200 MW of European data-center capacity. This is a vendor-distributed press release, so the commercial terms are unverified independently.
