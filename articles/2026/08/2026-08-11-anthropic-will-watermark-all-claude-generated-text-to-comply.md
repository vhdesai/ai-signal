# Anthropic Will Watermark All Claude-Generated Text to Comply with EU AI Act

Anthropic announced it will watermark text generated by all Claude models released after August 2 to comply with the EU AI Act's Transparency Code. The watermark is embedded at the model level and persists through copy-paste and some editing. Applies across all Claude surfaces — API, Claude Code, Cowork, and Tag. Uses C2PA for files. Other companies including Google, Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI have also committed to the code. SAFETY INCIDENT
