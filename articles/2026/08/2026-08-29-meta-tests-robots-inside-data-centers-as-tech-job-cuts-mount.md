# Meta Tests Robots Inside Data Centers as Tech Job Cuts Mount

Meta piloting robots in data center operations. ~140,000 US tech job eliminations in 2026, with Amazon, Oracle, Meta, and Microsoft accounting for nearly 50,000. AI-driven headcount effects extending from knowledge work into facilities and logistics — automation now reaches the physical infrastructure that supports it. 24/7 Wall St.
