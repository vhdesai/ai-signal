# DeepSeek ships V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, an experimental multimodal model at Flash pricing

DeepSeek released V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, which preserves V4-Flash text, reasoning, and agent performance while adding a substantial jump on visual-agent tasks, with images billed at up to 384 tokens at standard Flash rates. OpenRouter, which made it immediately available, reported it beating Opus 4.8 on Agents' Last Exam and ZeroBench. Open weights remain unconfirmed and the "experimental" label is meaningful, but the release continues the pattern of Chinese labs compressing frontier multimodal capability into low-cost tiers.
