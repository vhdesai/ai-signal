# Apple downgraded as AI-driven memory costs squeeze iPhone economics

Jefferies analyst Edison Lee downgraded Apple to underperform, citing rising memory costs as supply is diverted to AI data centers, along with a reportedly abandoned all-glass 2027 iPhone. An early example of AI infrastructure demand pricing through into consumer hardware margins via the memory supply chain.
