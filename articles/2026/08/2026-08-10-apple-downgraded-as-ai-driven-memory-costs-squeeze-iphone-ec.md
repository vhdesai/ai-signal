# Apple downgraded as AI-driven memory costs squeeze iPhone economics

Jefferies analyst Edison Lee downgraded Apple to underperform, citing rising memory costs as supply is diverted to AI data centers, along with a reportedly abandoned all-glass 2027 iPhone. It is an early example of AI infrastructure demand pricing through into consumer hardware margins. Note: single-source roundup — worth verifying against the original Jefferies note.
