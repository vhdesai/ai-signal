# Marc Benioff-backed June raises $20 million to automate AI deployment work

June emerged from stealth with a $20 million pre-seed round led by Marc Benioff's Time Ventures, aiming to help enterprises deploy AI into messy legacy systems. The company argues that building an agent template is easy, but connecting it to fragmented data, duplicate fields, legacy workflows, and enterprise platforms is the hard part. The funding is a signal that AI implementation services and tooling may become as valuable as model access itself.
