# Gemini App Crosses 1 Billion Monthly Active Users

Sundar Pichai said the Gemini app has surpassed one billion monthly active users, making it the 14th Google product to reach that mark and, per Google, its fastest-growing ever. Google disclosed that 63% of Gemini users engage via voice, the app generates more than 150 million images per day, and it has over 100 million active iOS users. The figure excludes AI Mode in Search, which separately reports more than a billion monthly users. Distribution, not model leadership, remains Google's strongest card while its frontier releases lag. techcrunch.com — Google's Gemini app surges to 1 billion users FUNDING
