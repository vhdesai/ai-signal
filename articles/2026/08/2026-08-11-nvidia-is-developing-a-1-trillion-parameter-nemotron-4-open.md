# Nvidia is developing a ~1-trillion-parameter Nemotron 4 open model family

Nvidia is reported to be building a new open model generation under the Nemotron 4 program, including a model targeting roughly one trillion parameters, aimed at competing with leading open-weight releases. The strategic logic is demand generation rather than model monetization: stronger open models optimized for Nvidia hardware pull through GPUs, networking, and software. It also positions Nvidia against fast-moving Chinese open-weight releases. https://www.theinformation.com/search?query=Nvidia%20Nemotron%204
