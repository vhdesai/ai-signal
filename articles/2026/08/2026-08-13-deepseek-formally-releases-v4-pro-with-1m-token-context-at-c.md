# DeepSeek Formally Releases V4 Pro With 1M-Token Context at Commodity Pricing

DeepSeek formally released its production V4 Pro model, ending a roughly four-month preview period and aiming to regain ground against fast-moving domestic rivals. The mixture-of-experts architecture carries a one-million-token context window and is priced at roughly $0.435 and $0.87 per million input and output tokens — significantly undercutting every major U.S. frontier lab on per-token cost. Reuters reported DeepSeek is also introducing peak and off-peak API pricing across V4 Pro and V4 Flash, a structure common in cloud infrastructure but new to frontier model APIs. Benchmark claims remain vendor-reported and await independent verification, but the pricing alone is disruptive: if performance holds at these levels, it reprices the inference cost assumptions underlying most enterprise AI budgets. Western enterprises evaluating multi-vendor strategies should include DeepSeek in cost modeling even if regulatory constraints limit direct deployment. HOT AGENTS
