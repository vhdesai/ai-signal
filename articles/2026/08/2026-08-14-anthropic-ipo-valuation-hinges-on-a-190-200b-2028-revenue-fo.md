# Anthropic IPO Valuation Hinges on a $190–200B 2028 Revenue Forecast

Anthropic is projecting roughly $190–200 billion in annual revenue by 2028, a previously unreported figure that bankers and investors are using to underwrite one of the largest IPOs on record. Wall Street is pricing the company on a forecast three years out — an unusually long horizon that concentrates valuation risk in assumptions rather than results. Commentary the same day questioned whether a ~$2 trillion implied value is defensible given rising compute costs and open-weight competition. Treat any Anthropic-dependent commercial terms as being negotiated against a pre-IPO backdrop where the company has strong incentives to maintain growth metrics.
