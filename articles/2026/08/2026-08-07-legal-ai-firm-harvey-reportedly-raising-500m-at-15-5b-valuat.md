# Legal AI firm Harvey reportedly raising $500M at $15.5B valuation

Harvey, which builds AI software for law firms and in-house legal teams, is reported to be raising at least $500 million at roughly $15.5 billion. The round places it among the highest-valued vertical AI application companies. It reflects a broader repricing where regulated workflow depth, not model access, is the defensible moat.
