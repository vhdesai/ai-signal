# Lovable Raises $400M Series C and Opens 450 Roles

Vibe-coding platform Lovable raised a $400M Series C and is scaling hiring to roughly 450 roles this year, part of a cluster of AI startups that collectively raised about $2.4B in the period. The hiring scale is the operational tell — these companies are staffing enterprise sales, support, and compliance functions, not just research. That shift marks the app layer's transition from product-led experimentation to enterprise contracting. https://www.forbes.com/sites/rachelwells/2026/08/13/lovable-is-hiring-after-a-400-million-raise-here-are-the-68-jobs/
