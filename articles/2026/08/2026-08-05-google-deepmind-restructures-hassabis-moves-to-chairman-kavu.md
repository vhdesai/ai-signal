# Google DeepMind Restructures: Hassabis Moves to Chairman, Kavukcuoglu Takes Operational Control

Sundar Pichai moved Demis Hassabis into the roles of Google DeepMind chairman and Alphabet chief scientist, where he will focus on long-term AGI strategy and Isomorphic Labs. Koray Kavukcuoglu assumes operational leadership of Google's models, research, and Gemini product teams. The split separates the frontier-science mandate from the increasingly urgent job of shipping competitive products, and follows reporting of internal morale pressure and Gemini release slippage.
