# Rep. Ro Khanna to introduce a “Data Center Bill of Rights”

Rep. Ro Khanna is introducing a data center bill of rights as voters nationwide recoil from potential utility rate hikes tied to the facilities powering artificial intelligence. The proposal signals intensifying political friction over AI's energy and grid footprint. Siting, power procurement and local rate impact are becoming material constraints on data center expansion plans.
