# South Korea’s DeepX valuation surges to $2.2B in fresh funding

South Korean AI-chip designer DeepX raised new capital at roughly four times its previous valuation, reaching about $2.2 billion. The round signals sustained investor appetite for AI silicon outside the U.S. and China, particularly for edge and on-device inference.
