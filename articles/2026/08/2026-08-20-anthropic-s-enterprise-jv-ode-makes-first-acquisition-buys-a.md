# Anthropic's Enterprise JV "Ode" Makes First Acquisition — Buys AI Consultancy Casper Studios

Ode, the joint venture established by Anthropic and Wall Street firms including Blackstone, has made its first acquisition since its July launch — buying AI consultancy Casper Studios, which helps businesses develop applications using AI. The acquisition aims to accelerate Claude chatbot adoption among enterprises at a time when corporate executives are trying to figure out how to get the most from AI while managing rising technology bills. Ode secured $1.5 billion from Wall Street firms and Anthropic.
