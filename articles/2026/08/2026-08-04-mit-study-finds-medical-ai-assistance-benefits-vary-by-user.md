# MIT study finds medical AI assistance benefits vary by user expertise

MIT News reports on a Nature Medicine study by MIT, Columbia, and Stanford researchers examining how clinicians and non-experts use medical AI explanations while diagnosing skin disease. Non-experts improved with AI but often by deferring to it, including when LLM explanations were wrong; clinicians were better at catching errors and performed best with a plain model prediction and no explanation. The study challenges the assumption that more explainability is always safer and suggests AI interface design should vary by user expertise. Academic Research ACADEMICAUTONOMOUS SCIENCENSF
