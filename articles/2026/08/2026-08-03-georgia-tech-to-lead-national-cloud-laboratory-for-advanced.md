# Georgia Tech to lead national cloud laboratory for advanced manufacturing and materials

Georgia Tech announced an $18.1 million NSF award to build a programmable cloud laboratory for advanced manufacturing and materials as part of NSF's broader Genesis Mission. The system will use AI agents to translate experimental recipes into robotic workflows, coordinate autonomous experimentation, and learn iteratively through digital twins. Although published in the 24-48 hour window, it is included because it is a high-signal federal investment in AI-enabled autonomous science.
