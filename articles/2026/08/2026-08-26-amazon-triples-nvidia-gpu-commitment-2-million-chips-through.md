Announced on earnings call. ~2M Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra GPUs for AWS 2027–2028 — triple a commitment made five months ago. Nvidia networking, CPUs, Nemotron models, and robotics stack integrated across AWS. Amazon deepens Nvidia dependency while its custom-chip business crossed $25B ARR.
