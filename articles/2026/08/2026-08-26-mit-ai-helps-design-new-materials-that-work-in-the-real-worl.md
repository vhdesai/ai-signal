# MIT: AI Helps Design New Materials That Work in the Real World

MIT researchers introduced CrysVCD, a crystal generator with valence-constrained design that encodes chemistry rules about valence electrons into the generation step itself, so models output stable, usable crystals rather than candidates that must be screened out downstream. Published in Nature Computational Science, the method reports high lattice-dynamics stability in nearly 70% of generations, 68% mechanical stability and 85% metastability. Target properties include high thermal conductivity for data-center cooling — a direct tie-in to AI infrastructure constraints.
