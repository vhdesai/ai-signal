# OpenAI Pauses Parts of Astra Model Development After Crossing "Critical Cybersecurity Threshold"

OpenAI publicly disclosed that its upcoming Astra model has been partially suspended after preliminary evaluations found it may meet the "critical cybersecurity capability" threshold defined in the company's Preparedness Framework — meaning it could potentially identify and execute attacks against well-protected real-world systems. OpenAI stated it "cannot rule out Critical capability level at this time," has enacted stricter internal security controls, and is coordinating with government agencies and AI safety organizations. This is the first time a frontier lab has voluntarily announced a development pause tied to autonomous cyber-offense capability. ________________________________ POLICYSAFETY
