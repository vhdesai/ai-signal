# Tesla and SpaceX commit $16.8B to Terafab semiconductor complex in Texas

Tesla and SpaceX announced an initial $16.8 billion investment to build an advanced semiconductor complex—dubbed Terafab—for AI and robotics chips in Texas. The move underscores how frontier AI ambitions are driving vertically integrated chip manufacturing strategies, with hyperscalers and major tech players building captive capacity rather than relying solely on TSMC and other foundries. For the semiconductor industry, it adds another massive demand signal for fab capacity and equipment. For enterprise technology leaders, it signals that the companies building AI applications are increasingly unwilling to leave their silicon supply chains to chance.
