# Unitree's 460% IPO Stock Pop in Shanghai Wasn't Unusual in China

Humanoid robot maker Unitree surged 460% on its Shanghai debut, the latest in a series of extraordinary first-day pops for Chinese tech IPOs. The listing reflects investor appetite for physical AI and robotics at a moment when Chinese markets are channeling capital into embodied systems. For global investors tracking the AI-to-hardware transition, China's robotics IPO pipeline is now producing public comparables at scale.
