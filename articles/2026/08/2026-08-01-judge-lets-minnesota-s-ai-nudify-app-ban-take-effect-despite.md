# Judge lets Minnesota's AI “nudify” app ban take effect despite xAI challenge

A federal judge denied xAI's request to temporarily block Minnesota's first-of-its-kind ban on AI tools that generate non-consensual sexualized imagery. The ruling focused partly on timing, but it allows the law to take effect while xAI's broader challenge proceeds. The case is an early test of whether states can regulate high-risk generative-AI tools directly, rather than only punishing end users after harm occurs.
