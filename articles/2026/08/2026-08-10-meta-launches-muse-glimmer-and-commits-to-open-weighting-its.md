# Meta Launches Muse Glimmer and Commits to Open-Weighting Its Most Powerful Model

Meta launched Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weight agentic model under Apache 2.0, distilled from its closed Muse Spark 1.2 teacher and designed to run on a single consumer GPU. Zuckerberg paired the release with a 6,500-word essay arguing concentrated control — not AI capability — is the technology’s biggest risk, and called on Washington to lower barriers on American open-source AI. Meta is also creating a $1B fund for first-responders in data-center towns. META shares rose ~2.4%. NEW MODEL RELEASE
