# Z.ai GLM-5.3: Near-Frontier Cyber Capability Under Gated Access

Z.ai released GLM-5.3 with gains from post-training only (no new base). CyberGym: 84.5% (above Anthropic on detection, below on exploitation at 54.4% vs. 78%). Open weights planned after security testing, with higher-risk capabilities behind a trusted-access tier. The dual-use implication: a near-frontier offensive-security capability is on track to become freely downloadable. The clearest test yet of whether open models can ship dual-use cyber with meaningful guardrails.
