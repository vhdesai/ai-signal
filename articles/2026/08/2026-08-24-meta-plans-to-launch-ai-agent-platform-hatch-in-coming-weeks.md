# Meta Plans to Launch AI Agent Platform "Hatch" in Coming Weeks; New "Watermark" Model in October

Meta plans to launch its consumer AI agent platform, dubbed "Hatch" internally, as soon as the next several weeks, and is targeting October for its latest AI model called "Watermark." The agent platform represents Meta's push to bring AI agents directly to consumers through its social platforms, competing with OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Gemini in the consumer AI race.
