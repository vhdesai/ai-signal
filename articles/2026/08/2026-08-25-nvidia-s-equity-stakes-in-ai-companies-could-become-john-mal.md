# Nvidia's Equity Stakes in AI Companies Could Become "John Malone-Style" Platform

The Information's Briefing argues Nvidia's accumulating equity stakes in AI companies — including the expanded Perplexity investment — could become more useful in ways "other than just financial returns," likening the strategy to cable mogul John Malone's empire-building through cross-ownership. The analysis suggests Nvidia is building a conglomerate-style platform via strategic investments that ensure long-term hardware demand.
