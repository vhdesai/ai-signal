# Google Quietly Courts Hollywood Studios for AI Training and Character Licensing

Google has approached Disney, Universal, and Warner Bros. Discovery about licensing studio IP for its AI models, even as copyright litigation from Disney remains active. Individual character licenses are reportedly valued near $40 million. SAG-AFTRA says no major studio has notified it of a completed AI licensing agreement. The pattern — litigate, then license — is becoming the default path to legitimate training data. Yahoo Finance → EXCLUSIVE
