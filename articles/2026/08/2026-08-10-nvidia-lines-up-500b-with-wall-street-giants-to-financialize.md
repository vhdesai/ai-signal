# Nvidia lines up $500B with Wall Street giants to financialize AI compute

MOUs with Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, KKR. Nvidia may backstop up to 25% of any project. BlackRock's Fink: "We need to raise this money as fast as possible." Shifts systemic risk from Nvidia's balance sheet to Wall Street investors. DealBook: either the inflection point in the AI boom or start of a credit bubble.
