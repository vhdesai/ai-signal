# SpaceX reportedly lays groundwork for turbine-blade factory to address data-center power crunch

The Information reported that SpaceX is laying groundwork for a turbine-blade factory to address data-center power constraints. The item fits the broader pattern of AI companies moving further into physical infrastructure as power availability becomes a bottleneck for model training and inference. If hyperscale AI demand continues rising, vertical integration into power equipment and energy supply chains may become a competitive differentiator. INFRASTRUCTUREWEATHER AI
