# Anthropic's New Text Watermarks Trigger User Backlash

Anthropic's rollout of watermarking for Claude-generated text has drawn objections from users who relied on the tool undetected in workplace and academic settings. The move aligns with tightening transparency and disclosure expectations, including EU AI Act Article 50 obligations that took effect earlier this month.
