# OpenAI rolls out "Computer History" for the ChatGPT Mac app

OpenAI's Mac desktop app can now turn recent local computer activity into a searchable timeline that ChatGPT and Codex can draw on as memory. It is a meaningful step toward context-aware desktop agents that understand what a user has actually been working on. Expect enterprise questions on data residency and endpoint policy to follow quickly.
