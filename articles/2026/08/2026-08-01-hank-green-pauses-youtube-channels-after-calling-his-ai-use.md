# Hank Green pauses YouTube channels after calling his AI use "not healthy"

Science creator Hank Green said his use of ChatGPT in production workflows had become unhealthy after an LLM-generated phrase slipped into a published video. He apologized publicly and said he would reduce output to rebuild a non-AI-dependent creative process. The episode is a useful signal for enterprise leaders: productivity gains from generative AI can be offset by trust erosion when audiences or stakeholders perceive overreliance or hidden automation.
