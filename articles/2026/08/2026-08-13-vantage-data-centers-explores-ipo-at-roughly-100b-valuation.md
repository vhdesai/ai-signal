# Vantage Data Centers Explores IPO at Roughly $100B Valuation

Vantage Data Centers, backed by Silver Lake and DigitalBridge, is exploring options including an IPO at roughly a $100 billion valuation that could raise about $10 billion, or an outright sale. Vantage has raised approximately $11 billion since late 2023 and is involved in a Wisconsin campus tied to the OpenAI–Oracle Stargate buildout.
