# Situational Awareness Puts $400M Into Chip Startup Source Foundry

Leopold Aschenbrenner’s AI-focused hedge fund invested $400M in Source Foundry, a Stanford-founded chip manufacturing startup, bringing total exposure to ~$500M. The bet came despite steep recent losses — the fund sold its public portfolio to Citadel and saw AUM fall from $20B to $10B.
