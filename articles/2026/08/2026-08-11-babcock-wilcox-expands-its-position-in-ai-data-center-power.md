# Babcock & Wilcox expands its position in AI data center power generation

Power-generation company Babcock & Wilcox is deepening its commitments to supplying AI data centers, another instance of industrial energy firms repositioning around AI load growth. Power availability, not chip supply, is increasingly the gating factor on new capacity.
