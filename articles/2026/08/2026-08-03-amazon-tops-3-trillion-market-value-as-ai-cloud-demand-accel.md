# Amazon Tops $3 Trillion Market Value as AI Cloud Demand Accelerates

Amazon crossed the $3 trillion market capitalization threshold for the first time, driven by accelerating AI cloud demand through AWS and a broader rally in technology stocks. The milestone comes on the heels of Amazon's 37% AWS revenue growth and $220 billion capex plan, making it the latest hyperscaler to be rewarded by investors for demonstrating clear AI revenue pathways. The $3T valuation places Amazon alongside Apple and Microsoft in the most exclusive market-cap tier, reinforcing the narrative that cloud infrastructure providers are the primary financial beneficiaries of the AI buildout. For enterprise buyers, Amazon's market strength also implies sustained pricing power and continued aggressive investment in AI infrastructure.
