# MIT’s CrysVCD Raises AI-Generated Materials Stability to ~70%

Published in Nature Computational Science, CrysVCD reverses the standard generate-then-screen workflow. A language model first produces chemically valid formulas; only then does a diffusion model construct the crystal structure. ~70% lattice-dynamics stability and roughly an order-of-magnitude efficiency gain. The framework targets properties relevant to chips: high thermal conductivity and high dielectric constant.
