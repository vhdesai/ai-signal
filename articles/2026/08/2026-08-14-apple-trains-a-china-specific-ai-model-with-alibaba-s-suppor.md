# Apple Trains a China-Specific AI Model With Alibaba's Support

Apple is reported to have trained its own China-specific model with Alibaba's assistance rather than relying entirely on a local partner's model, and to have secured regulatory clearance to ship it. The approach gives Apple materially more control over AI features in a market where it faces both regulatory constraints and intensifying competition from Huawei. It also shifts the Apple–Alibaba relationship from model supplier toward training and compliance partner. For multinationals, this is a template for region-specific model strategy under data-localization regimes. finance.yahoo.com
