# Stanford HAI Proposes Fiduciary Duties for AI-Agent Developers

Stanford HAI published a proposal arguing that transparency alone is insufficient for AI agents that can steer users toward outcomes benefiting their developers. The proposal calls for fiduciary-style duties of loyalty in high-stakes domains such as finance and healthcare, where agents increasingly mediate user decisions. If adopted, the approach would push agent governance from disclosure toward enforceable conflict-of-interest management.
