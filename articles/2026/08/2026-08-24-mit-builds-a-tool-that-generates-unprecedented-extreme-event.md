# MIT builds a tool that generates “unprecedented” extreme events without extreme dataNew

MIT researchers Kai Chang and Themis Sapsis introduced an “Extreme Event Aware” (η-learning) method that generates plausible worst-case scenarios — an extreme storm’s likely duration, intensity and footprint — without requiring past extreme events in its training data. The model learns statistically from ordinary records such as daily weather to project events expected only once a century. The team reports the approach transfers to robotic navigation and financial-market crashes. The underlying open-access paper appeared in Nature Communications, with MIT’s writeup published August 24.
