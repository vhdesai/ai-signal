# Oracle Drawing Up Layoff Plans as AI Infrastructure Debt Mounts

Oracle is preparing a new round of layoffs this month as part of an effort to reduce payroll while the company racks up billions in debt to fund AI infrastructure. The cuts could reach double-digit percentages on some teams, underscoring the financial strain the AI buildout imposes even on major enterprise tech incumbents racing to compete with cloud hyperscalers.
