# Nvidia Pauses Revenue-Sharing Deals With AI Cloud Providers

AI Compute Partnership program frozen <2 months after launch amid antitrust concerns. Key financing mechanism for neoclouds without hyperscaler balance sheets. If it doesn’t restart, capacity plans and pricing at second-tier GPU providers become materially less certain.
