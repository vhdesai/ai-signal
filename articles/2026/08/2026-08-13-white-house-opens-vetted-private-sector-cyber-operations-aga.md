# White House opens vetted private-sector cyber operations against foreign criminal networks

The White House directed the National Coordination Center to permit vetted U.S. firms to conduct cyber-surveillance and "cyber-effects" operations against foreign criminal groups under Department of Justice and Homeland Security oversight. The framework includes vetting requirements, $1 million bonds, and explicit exclusions on the most severe outcomes.
