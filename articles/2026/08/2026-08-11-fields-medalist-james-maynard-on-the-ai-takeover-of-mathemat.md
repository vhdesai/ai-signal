# Fields Medalist James Maynard on “the AI takeover of mathematics”

Oxford's James Maynard describes a year of soul searching in the mathematics community as models begin producing results that require expert validation rather than mere checking. The interview captures the shift from skepticism to structural questions about authorship, verification, and what mathematical training is for.
