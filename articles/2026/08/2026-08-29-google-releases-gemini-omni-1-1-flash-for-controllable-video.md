# Google releases Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash for controllable video generation

Google released Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, a production update to its native multimodal video generation and editing model. The update adds scene extension using up to 10 seconds of prior context, first/last-frame control, 360p drafting at lower cost, 4K upscaling, and video references for character consistency. The release is strategically relevant because professional video workflows are moving toward controllability, iterative editing, and cost-managed production rather than one-shot generation.
