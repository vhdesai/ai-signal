# Alphabet Absorbs Compounding DeepMind Leadership Losses

Coverage continued to develop on Alphabet's August 5 announcement that Demis Hassabis is moving from Google DeepMind CEO to unit chairman and Alphabet chief scientist, with CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu assuming day-to-day leadership as senior vice president reporting to Sundar Pichai. Chief scientist Jeff Dean departed after 27 years alongside Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le to co-found Discovery Loop, a research-automation venture in which Alphabet is a founding investor and compute supplier. Both Gemini co-technical leads have now exited within weeks of each other, and GOOG fell roughly 4% on the announcement. The structural read is that Alphabet is trading DeepMind's semi-independence for centralized execution speed at Mountain View. MARKETS
