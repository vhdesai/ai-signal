# Anthropic ~$2T IPO + ~$6B Decart Acquisition + Secondary-Market Frenzy

Anthropic may seek a valuation above $2T in an autumn listing, with backers modeling $100B–$120B revenue by end-2026. Business Insider reports secondary-market demand for Anthropic shares has become extraordinarily competitive. Simultaneously, Anthropic is in early talks to acquire Decart AI (~$6B) for GPU optimization and inference cost reduction ahead of the IPO — Decart’s team would fold into the inference organization. The strategic logic: inference cost is the margin lever that determines whether revenue growth converts to a sustainable public-company P&L. A successful offering would force public markets to price frontier-lab economics for the first time. Yahoo Finance (IPO) | Yahoo Finance (Decart) PARTNERSHIPS
