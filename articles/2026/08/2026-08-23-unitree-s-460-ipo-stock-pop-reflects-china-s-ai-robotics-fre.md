# Unitree's 460% IPO Stock Pop Reflects China's AI Robotics Frenzy

Humanoid robot maker Unitree surged 460% in its Shanghai stock market debut, the latest in a series of eye-catching first-day pops for Chinese tech IPOs. The Information reports the pop wasn't unusual for China's current market dynamics, where AI and robotics companies command extraordinary premiums. The valuation enthusiasm reflects investor conviction about physical AI's long-term potential, even as many humanoid companies have yet to deploy commercially at scale.
