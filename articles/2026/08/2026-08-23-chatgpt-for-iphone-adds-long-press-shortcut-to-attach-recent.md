# ChatGPT for iPhone adds long-press shortcut to attach recent photos

OpenAI's ChatGPT iOS app now lets users attach recent photos through a long-press gesture, reducing friction for image-based prompts. Coverage also notes Siri and Shortcuts integration details, along with a delayed rollout in the European Union. A small change, but consistent with OpenAI's push to make multimodal input the default interaction mode on mobile.
