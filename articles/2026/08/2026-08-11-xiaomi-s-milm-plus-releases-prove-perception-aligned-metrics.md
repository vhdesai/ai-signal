# Xiaomi's MiLM Plus releases PROVE, perception-aligned metrics for video object removal

Xiaomi's MiLM Plus published PROVE, a benchmark and metric pair (RC-S and RC-T) for evaluating object-removal quality in real-world video, aligned to human perception rather than pixel-level similarity. The work addresses a persistent gap where conventional metrics reward outputs humans judge as visibly flawed. Better evaluation is a prerequisite for production use of generative video editing.
