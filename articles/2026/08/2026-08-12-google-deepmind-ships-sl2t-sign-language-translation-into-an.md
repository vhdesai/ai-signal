# Google DeepMind Ships SL2T Sign-Language Translation Into Android Products

Google DeepMind deployed SL2T, a sign-language-to-text model, inside two consumer Android products — reportedly the first time sign-language AI has reached a shipping phone feature. The deployment moves an area that has historically stalled at the research-demo stage into production distribution.
