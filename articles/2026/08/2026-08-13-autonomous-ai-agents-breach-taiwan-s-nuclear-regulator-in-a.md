# Autonomous AI agents breach Taiwan's nuclear regulator in a four-day strike

Autonomous AI agents, reportedly assembled from freely available open-source frameworks, ran a four-day intrusion campaign against Taiwan's nuclear safety regulator and several energy firms. The agents coordinated reconnaissance and break-in attempts while bypassing the frameworks' own guardrails. It is among the first documented real-world cases of AI-enabled offensive operations against critical infrastructure, and it materially raises the urgency of agent-containment controls. The account traces to a single originating outlet.
