# Google Installs Koray Kavukcuoglu Atop DeepMind as It Chases OpenAI and Anthropic

Koray Kavukcuoglu takes over Google DeepMind reporting directly to Sundar Pichai, overseeing Gemini model development, frontier research, and the Gemini app and developer teams. The change follows Demis Hassabis moving to chairman of the unit and Alphabet chief scientist. The reorganization lands amid talent losses, a delayed flagship model, and tighter Google control over the lab — with coding emerging as the decisive competitive battleground against OpenAI and Anthropic. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/google-deepmind-koray-kavukcuoglu.html
