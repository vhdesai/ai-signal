# Tencent posts AI capex surge while defending returns

Tencent reported a surge in AI capital expenditure alongside marketing services up roughly 22% and domestic gaming up roughly 17%, with management defending what it called superior returns on AI investment. The stock is down about 26% year to date amid fierce competition among Chinese AI players. The result illustrates how even profitable incumbents are being asked to justify AI spend against near-term margin pressure.
