# Hugging Face Launches $399 Open-Source "Microduck" Robot for Physical AI

Hugging Face, with Pollen Robotics, opened preorders for Microduck, a 25-cm bipedal robot at $399 with 15 motors, camera, lidar, and inertial sensors, shipping before Christmas in North America, Europe, and the UK. The SDK, simulation environment, and reinforcement-learning stack are published on GitHub, with sim-to-real deployment supported. Strategically this is distribution: a cheap physical target that pulls the open-weights ecosystem into embodied AI. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/hugging-face-is-selling-a-cute-399-open-source-duck-robot-microduck/
