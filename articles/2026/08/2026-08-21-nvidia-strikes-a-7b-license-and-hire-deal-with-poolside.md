# Nvidia strikes a ~$7B license-and-hire deal with Poolside

Nvidia will reportedly pay $6B for a non-exclusive license to Poolside’s “Model Factory” and its Laguna coding models, plus a $1B investment at a $12B pre-money valuation, and extend offers to roughly 109 employees. The license-plus-hire structure avoids a formal acquisition and its antitrust exposure. Strategically it pushes Nvidia further up the stack from silicon into the model and software layer.
