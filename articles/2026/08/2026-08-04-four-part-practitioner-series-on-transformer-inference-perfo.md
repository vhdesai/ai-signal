# Four-part practitioner series on transformer inference performance

Machine Learning Mastery published four in-window tutorials covering transformer inference measurement, static versus dynamic versus continuous batching, decoding strategies and output control, and the training-to-inference path. Together they form a practical reference for teams tuning serving cost and latency rather than model quality. Continuous batching in particular is the single highest-leverage lever most teams have not yet pulled. Useful as onboarding material for platform engineers newly assigned to LLM serving.
