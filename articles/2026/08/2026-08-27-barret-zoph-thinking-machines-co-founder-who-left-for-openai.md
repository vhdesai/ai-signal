# Barret Zoph — Thinking Machines Co-Founder Who Left for OpenAI — Now at Google

Barret Zoph, who co-founded Thinking Machines Lab alongside Mira Murati and served as its CTO before being ousted, briefly went to OpenAI and has now landed at Google. The move highlights the intense talent churn among frontier labs and the gravitational pull of Google's research infrastructure. Zoph's journey from Murati's startup to OpenAI to Google in less than a year illustrates how quickly allegiances shift in the competitive AI talent market. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/barret-zoph-the-thinking-machines-co-founder-who-defected-to-openai-is-now-at-google/
