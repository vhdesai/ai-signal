# California AI Transparency Act (SB 942) becomes operative

California's AI Transparency Act, as broadened by AB 853, took effect August 2. It requires large generative-AI providers to embed C2PA-compatible provenance in AI-generated images, video, and audio; offer a public detection tool; and let users apply visible AI labels.
