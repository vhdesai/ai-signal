# Nvidia pauses its $36B AI cloud revenue-share financing program over antitrust concerns

Nvidia stepped back from its AI Compute Partnership — a credit-support and revenue-sharing program for smaller "neocloud" providers — less than two months after launching it, amid internal antitrust concerns and partner pushback over customer-control terms. The program had reportedly accumulated roughly $36 billion in commitments. Nvidia has publicly disputed characterizations that the initiative is on hold. Either way, the episode highlights how vendor financing has become structurally load-bearing for AI capacity growth. https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-pauses-ai-cloud-revenue-120700044.html
