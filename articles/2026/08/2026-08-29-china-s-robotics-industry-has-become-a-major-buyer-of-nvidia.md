# China's robotics industry has become a major buyer of Nvidia's "physical AI" stack

The WSJ reports that Chinese robotics companies are among the largest customers for Nvidia's physical-AI portfolio — edge modules, simulation, and world-model tooling — a category where trade remains permitted under current US rules. Nvidia has described physical AI as an approximately $10 billion annual run-rate business with substantially larger long-term ambitions. The piece frames a policy tension: export controls target training GPUs while the robotics stack flows largely unimpeded. https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/nvidia-wants-to-run-the-worlds-robots-china-is-an-eager-customer-bdf46169
