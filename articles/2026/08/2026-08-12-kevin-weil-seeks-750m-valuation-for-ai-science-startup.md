# Kevin Weil Seeks $750M Valuation for AI Science Startup

Kevin Weil, OpenAI's former Chief Product Officer, has sought a valuation of at least $750 million for a new AI science startup, according to three people with knowledge of the discussions. The fundraising effort is the latest signal that AI's next frontier is scientific research, as former top executives from leading AI labs launch ventures aimed at applying foundation models to drug discovery, materials science, and other research domains.
