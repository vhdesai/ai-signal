# Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate reaches $65B, up roughly 7x year over year

Anthropic told investors over the weekend that its annualized run rate hit $65 billion at the end of July, up from $47 billion in May and roughly $9 billion at the close of 2025. The disclosure follows a confidential IPO filing and preliminary Q2 revenue reported above $11.5 billion. The trajectory suggests enterprise API and coding workloads are converting to committed spend faster than most 2025 forecasts assumed — a relevant input for anyone modeling multi-year vendor pricing leverage. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/anthropic-says-annualized-revenue-climbed-to-65-billion-in-july.html REGULATORY
