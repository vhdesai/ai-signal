# Bill Gates Calls for "Human Reserved" Jobs and a Tax on AI Tokens and Robots

In a long essay published this week, Bill Gates argued that governments should designate categories of work as "Human Reserved" — protected from automation in the way nature reserves are protected from development — and floated taxing AI tokens and robots to fund the transition. He suggested a scope as broad as up to 40% of current jobs and warned that world leaders have no credible plan for displacement, child development effects, or criminal misuse. The shift in tone from one of the sector's most consistent optimists is itself the news, and it will be cited in labor and tax policy debates. ________________________________ SECURITY
