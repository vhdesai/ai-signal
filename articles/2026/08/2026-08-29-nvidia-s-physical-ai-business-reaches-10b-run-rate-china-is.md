# Nvidia's Physical-AI Business Reaches ~$10B Run Rate — China Is Lead Customer

Chinese robotics firms are among the largest customers for Jetson, Isaac Sim, Cosmos, and open-weight robotics models. Jensen Huang cites $10B ARR with a path to $100B over a decade. China accounts for the majority of humanoid robot shipments; this trade remains permitted under current export rules. Largest Nvidia revenue line still structurally dependent on open China access.
