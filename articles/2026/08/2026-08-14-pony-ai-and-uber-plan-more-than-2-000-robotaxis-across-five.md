# Pony.ai and Uber plan more than 2,000 robotaxis across five European cities

Pony.ai and Uber are expanding their autonomous-driving partnership to deploy over 2,000 robotaxis across five European cities, building on existing operations in Zagreb, with stated intent to extend into the Middle East. Additional cities and a full timetable have not been disclosed. Deployments in the thousands begin testing autonomy as transportation infrastructure rather than a pilot program. Uber's positioning as the distribution layer for multiple AV stacks — rather than owning one — is the more durable strategic bet here.
