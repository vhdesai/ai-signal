# Tencent open-sources Hy4 preview: 770B parameters, 49B active, 1M-token context

Tencent's Hunyuan team released Hy4 preview under a permissive license, a Mixture-of-Experts model with 770B total parameters, 49B activated per token, and a context window exceeding one million tokens. The release includes FP8 weights and serving recipes for common inference stacks, with the company positioning it toward coding, office work, and research tasks. It continues the Chinese labs' strategy of competing on open weights and cost-per-token rather than closed frontier parity.
