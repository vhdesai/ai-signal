# Memory-chip crunch driven by AI demand pushes costs into consumer hardware

AI-driven demand has reportedly quadrupled memory prices over the past year, squeezing Apple, medical-device makers and other buyers, and prompting lobbying in Washington for relief through mechanisms such as the CHIPS Act or Defense Production Act. TrendForce estimates the bill of materials for a 256GB iPhone 18 Pro could rise roughly 38% year over year, with memory's share climbing from about 10% to over a third. Component inflation is now a first-order margin issue outside the AI sector itself. Hardware-dependent product roadmaps should be re-costed.
