# SK hynix commits roughly $38B to two new fabs for AI memory

SK hynix committed 54 trillion won (~$38.1B) to build new fabrication plants at Yongin and Cheongju, targeting cleanroom completion in 2028–2029. The plants expand HBM, DRAM, and NAND capacity.
