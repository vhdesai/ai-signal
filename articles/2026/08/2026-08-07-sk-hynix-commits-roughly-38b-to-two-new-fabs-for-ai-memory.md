# SK hynix Commits Roughly $38B to Two New Fabs for AI Memory

SK hynix committed 54 trillion won — about $38.1 billion — to build new fabrication plants at Yongin (Y2) and Cheongju (M17), targeting cleanroom completion in 2029 and 2028 respectively. The plants are intended to expand HBM, DRAM, and NAND capacity, with the company framing AI demand as a structural shift rather than a cycle. Memory, not logic, is the tighter constraint on large-cluster deployment, so this capacity timeline matters for 2028–2030 compute availability. INFRASTRUCTURE
