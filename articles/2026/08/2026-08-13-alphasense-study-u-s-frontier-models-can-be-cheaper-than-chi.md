# AlphaSense Study: U.S. Frontier Models Can Be Cheaper Than Chinese Open-Weight Models

An AlphaSense study challenges the assumption that Chinese AI is always cheaper. Testing models for financial data analysis, OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 Sol and Anthropic’s Opus 4.8 generated better answers at lower total cost than Moonshot’s Kimi K3 and Z.ai’s GLM-5.2, despite higher per-token pricing. The key insight: smarter models use fewer tokens per task, making sticker-price comparisons misleading. OpenAI board chair Bret Taylor echoed this, calling frontier models “cheaper and more efficient” in practice. The Information: When Anthropic and OpenAI Are Cheaper
