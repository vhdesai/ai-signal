# Grok Sends Gibberish Responses Amid xAI Staff Exodus

xAI’s Grok chatbot sent extended nonsense responses to Grok Lite users, with Reddit “overwhelmed with complaints.” The Grok account confirmed a “rare temporary generation glitch.” Separately, The Information reports xAI has lost most founding team members and 50+ researchers since the SpaceX merger. The reliability incident, compounded by talent attrition, adds risk for enterprises evaluating Grok on commercial channels.
