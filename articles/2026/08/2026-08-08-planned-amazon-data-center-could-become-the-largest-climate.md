# Planned Amazon data center could become the largest climate polluter in the U.S.

For a planned data center in Pecos County, Texas, Amazon is backing an on-site gas plant permitted to emit up to 33 million tons of CO₂ per year — reportedly more than any existing U.S. power plant. Amazon said the project will not raise electricity costs for Texas families. The company's emissions rose 16% last year, putting visible strain on its 2040 net-zero commitment. The story sharpens the emerging tension between AI capacity buildout and hyperscaler climate pledges.
