# IBM Embeds OpenAI Frontier Models Into Its Consulting Delivery Platform

IBM announced a partnership embedding OpenAI's frontier models — including GPT-5.6 — plus Codex and ChatGPT Work directly into IBM Consulting's delivery platform. The deal converts a services organization into a distribution channel for OpenAI in regulated enterprise accounts where procurement, not capability, is the binding constraint. It also signals that IBM has chosen partnership over Granite-first differentiation for its highest-value delivery work. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/ibm-partners-with-openai-to-bolster-enterprise-ai-push/
