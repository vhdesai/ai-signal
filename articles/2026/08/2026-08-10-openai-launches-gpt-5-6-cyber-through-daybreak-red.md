# OpenAI launches GPT-5.6-Cyber through Daybreak Red

OpenAI expanded Daybreak, its cyber-defense offering, with a new restricted-access cybersecurity model called GPT-5.6-Cyber. The model is available through Daybreak Red for approved partners doing authorized vulnerability research, exploit validation, and security testing, while Daybreak Blue focuses on incident response, malware analysis, and patch validation. The move reflects a delicate balance: frontier cyber models are becoming useful defensive tools, but access must be tightly governed because the same capabilities can be dual-use.
