# OpenAI cuts off Cursor after SpaceX's $60 billion takeover

OpenAI will end Cursor's direct model access on November 12, citing an inability to be confident SpaceX will operate within its terms of service and referencing Musk's admission that xAI distilled OpenAI outputs. Cursor CEO Michael Truell said OpenAI models serve roughly 5% of Cursor user traffic, a figure that reflects Cursor's own Composer 2.5 model, post-trained from Moonshot's open Kimi K2.5 checkpoint. Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown publicly committed additional compute and raised Claude usage limits inside Cursor within about two hours of the announcement. The November 12 date turns an abstract argument about vendor lock-in into a measurable test of whether frontier access is a dependency or a purchasing decision. https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/31/openai-cuts-off-cursor-after-spacexs-60-billion-takeover/
