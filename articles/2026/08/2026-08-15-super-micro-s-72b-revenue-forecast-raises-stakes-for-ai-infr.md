# Super Micro's $72B Revenue Forecast Raises Stakes for AI Infrastructure Trade

Super Micro Computer's forward revenue forecast of $72B has intensified discussion about whether the AI infrastructure boom justifies current valuations. The forecast represents a dramatic ramp in AI server shipments and validates continued hyperscaler capex commitments, even as investors debate whether AI spending is sustainable at current levels. 🔗
