# Nvidia Guarantees SB Energy's 8-GW Ohio Campus; OpenAI Signs a 20-Year Lease

Nvidia will provide credit support for land, power, and shell buildout on the initial 4.25 IT-GW of SB Energy's PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio, and will invest $1.5 billion directly into SB Energy; the site will exclusively host Nvidia AI compute. OpenAI separately confirmed it has contracted for roughly 8 gigawatts-IT under a 20-year lease, with the first 800 MW expected in 2028 and a six-year buildout through 2032. OpenAI is adding $40 million in community grants plus up to $84 million in Codex credits for Ohio students, and says it will pay only as capacity becomes available. The structure matters more than the headline number: Nvidia is now simultaneously hardware supplier, credit guarantor, and equity investor in the power substrate — a concentration of roles that changes the risk profile of the entire deal and sharpens circular-financing questions.
