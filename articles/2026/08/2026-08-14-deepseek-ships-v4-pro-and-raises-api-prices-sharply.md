# DeepSeek ships V4 Pro and raises API prices sharply

DeepSeek moved its flagship V4 Pro out of preview and simultaneously raised API pricing across the V4 family, with increases effective 16:00 UTC on August 16 and peak/off-peak billing introduced at roughly a 2x spread. Published pricing puts V4 Pro at up to $1.32 per million cache-miss input tokens and $3.96 per million output tokens at peak; Caixin reported some workloads rising by as much as 1,100%. Even after the increase, DeepSeek remains cheaper than most frontier Western alternatives.
