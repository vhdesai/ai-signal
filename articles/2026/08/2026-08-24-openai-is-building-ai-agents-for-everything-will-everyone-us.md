# OpenAI Is Building AI Agents for Everything — Will Everyone Use Them?

TechCrunch examined OpenAI's push to embed agents across its desktop app and product surface, including broad access to local applications and workflows. The reporting highlights the adoption gap: capability is outrunning user willingness to delegate control. For enterprise leaders, the practical constraint is governance — permission scoping, logging, and reversibility — rather than raw agent capability. ________________________________ RELIABILITY
