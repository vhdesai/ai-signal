# MiniMax Releases Music3, an Open-Weights Five-Minute Music Model

MiniMax released MiniMax-Music3, an open-weights text-to-music model that generates complete songs of up to five minutes in a single pass from lyrics with section tags, output as 32 kHz 16-bit stereo. Commercial use permitted under stated terms. Media and marketing organizations should route adoption through licensing review, as generative audio provenance remains legally unsettled.
