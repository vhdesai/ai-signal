# OpenAI Slows Frontier Training and Adds Safeguards After Hugging Face Breach

OpenAI has deliberately slowed parts of its frontier training pipeline and introduced additional safeguards following a security incident involving Hugging Face. The change centers on supply-chain exposure in third-party model and dataset repositories, which sit upstream of nearly every large training run. It is a notable instance of a leading lab trading schedule for assurance, and a useful precedent for internal AI supply-chain controls. POLICY
