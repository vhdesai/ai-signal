# MiniMax releases MiniMax-Music3, an open-weights five-minute music model

MiniMax released MiniMax-Music3, an open-weights text-to-music model that generates complete songs of up to five minutes in a single pass from lyrics with section tags plus a structured caption, output as 32 kHz 16-bit stereo. The release includes multiple serving paths and license conditions that permit commercial use under stated terms. Media and marketing organizations should route any adoption through licensing review, as generative audio provenance remains legally unsettled. marktechpost.com – MiniMax releases MiniMax-Music3 AGENTS
