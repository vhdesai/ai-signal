# OpenAI expands its Daybreak Cyber Partner Program to major defenders

OpenAI is embedding frontier cyber models with partners including Accenture, IBM, Capgemini, Cognizant, EY, KPMG, PwC, NCC Group and SpecterOps, alongside Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike, Cisco, Sophos, Akamai, Fortinet and Cloudflare. The stated goal is helping defenders find serious vulnerabilities, validate which ones matter, and remediate faster. This is the commercial complement to the GPT-5.6-Cyber release.
