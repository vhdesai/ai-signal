# XPENG Robotics Raises Over $900M at a $6.3B+ Valuation

XPENG's robotics business announced share purchase agreements to raise more than US$900 million at a post-money valuation above US$6.3 billion, in what the company frames as its first external round. The raise continues the pattern of Chinese mobility platforms spinning out embodied-AI units and funding them independently of the automotive parent. Valuation at this level, pre-commercial scale, signals investor willingness to underwrite humanoid and general-purpose robotics on a long horizon. ANALYSIS
