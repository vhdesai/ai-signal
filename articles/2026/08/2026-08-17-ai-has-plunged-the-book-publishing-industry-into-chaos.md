# AI Has Plunged the Book Publishing Industry Into Chaos

The WSJ Daily Digest highlighted a major feature on how AI is disrupting book publishing — covering both the training data acquisition practices (like Anthropic's book-scanning) and the flood of AI-generated content threatening traditional publishers. The story follows the "fair use" ruling for Anthropic's scanning practices and represents the latest front in AI's transformation of creative industries.
