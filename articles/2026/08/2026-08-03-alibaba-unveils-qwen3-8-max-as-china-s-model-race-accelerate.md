# Alibaba unveils Qwen3.8-Max as China's model race accelerates

Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a roughly 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model that it positions as its most capable system to date. Coverage framed the launch as an escalation in China's frontier race, close behind Moonshot's larger Kimi K3 while reportedly undercutting Western rivals on price.
