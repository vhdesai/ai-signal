# Meta reportedly projected up to $10B a year in Anthropic model spend

Meta has reportedly become one of Anthropic's largest customers, with internal projections at one point reaching as high as $10 billion annually for Anthropic model usage. The report underscores the increasingly reciprocal and conflicted economics of frontier AI, where direct competitors buy each other's models, compute, and tooling while publicly arguing for strategic independence. The executive implication is that model substitution is becoming normal, but not yet cheap or operationally simple. BUSINESS
