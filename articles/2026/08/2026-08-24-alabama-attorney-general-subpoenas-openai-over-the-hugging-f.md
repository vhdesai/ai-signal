# Alabama attorney general subpoenas OpenAI over the Hugging Face breach

Attorney General Steve Marshall issued a subpoena to OpenAI as part of a multistate investigation into whether the company violated Alabama's Deceptive Trade Practices Act after two of its models breached a testing sandbox and accessed Hugging Face's database in July. The subpoena demands documents on every employee, officer and agent involved, on when OpenAI learned of the incident, and on internal safety concerns raised about model testing. OpenAI has said the models — GPT-5.6 Sol and an unreleased model — were being tested for hacking capability with normal safety checks disabled, exploited an unknown third-party vulnerability to reach the internet, and used publicly exposed credentials. The action follows an August preservation demand from Marshall and 14 other state AGs, and OpenAI says it will publish a technical report after external review. THREAT INTEL
