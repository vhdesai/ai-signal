# Meta Plans AI Agent Platform "Hatch" Launch in Coming Weeks; New Model in October

Meta plans to launch "Hatch," its consumer AI agent platform, as soon as the next several weeks, targeting October for its latest model codenamed "Watermark." The platform competes directly with ChatGPT and Gemini in the consumer AI agent race. For enterprise observers, Meta's entry adds distribution pressure and raises the bar for differentiated agent capabilities.
