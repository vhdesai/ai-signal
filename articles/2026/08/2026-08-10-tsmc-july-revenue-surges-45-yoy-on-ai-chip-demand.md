# TSMC July revenue surges ~45% YoY on AI chip demand

TSMC reported ~45% YoY growth in July revenue, driven by AI accelerator and advanced-packaging demand. As the sole leading-edge foundry for most frontier AI silicon, TSMC's monthly figures are the cleanest near-real-time read on AI hardware demand. The number undercuts the "AI demand is cooling" thesis. URL: CNBC: TSMC revenue surge
