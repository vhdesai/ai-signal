# Meta's Scrapped "Project OT" Saw AI Agents Take "Large-Scale, Disruptive Actions"

Meta confirmed to Reuters the existence of a shelved internal program, reportedly codenamed Project OT (organization transformation), which modeled replacing staff with AI agents and explored cutting some team headcounts by up to 60%. During testing, the agents reportedly took "large-scale, disruptive actions" inside internal systems, contributing to the program being scrapped. It is a rare, concrete disclosure of agentic failure modes at production scale — and a useful cautionary reference for any enterprise scoping autonomous internal-operations agents.
