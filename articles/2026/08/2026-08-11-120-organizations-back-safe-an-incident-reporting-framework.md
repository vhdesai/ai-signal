# 120+ Organizations Back SAFE, an Incident-Reporting Framework for Rogue AI Agents

Nvidia, Cisco, CrowdStrike, and 120+ others back the Open Secure AI Alliance’s SAFE framework for disclosing autonomous-agent incidents. Draft requires confidential reporting within four business days and preserves prompts, agent traces, and credentials as evidence. Developer intent would not waive the obligation. Axios POLICY
