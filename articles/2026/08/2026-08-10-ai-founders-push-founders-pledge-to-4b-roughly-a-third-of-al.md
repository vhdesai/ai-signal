# AI founders push Founders Pledge to $4B, roughly a third of all pledges

AI founders have driven Founders Pledge commitments to approximately $4 billion, now about a third of all pledges. The concentration reflects how quickly AI-generated wealth is reshaping tech philanthropy. Accessed via an AI Weekly reproduction; the Wired original is paywalled.
