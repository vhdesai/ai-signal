# LTX 2.5: Open-Weights Video Generation Model — 10-Second Clips in 6.8 Seconds

LTX 2.5, an open-weights video generation model, can generate a 10-second AI video from a single image in just 6.8 seconds on NVIDIA superchips. The model represents a significant speed improvement in the open-source video generation space, potentially making real-time creative video workflows feasible for production use. * Sources: TechCrunch, MarkTechPost, VentureBeat, Bloomberg, ABC News Australia, Anthropic, Meta AI, OpenAI.*
