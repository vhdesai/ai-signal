# Google DeepMind Reorganization Exposes a London–Mountain View Split

Google's AI reorganization — with Demis Hassabis moving away from day-to-day operations, Koray Kavukcuoglu assuming greater operational control, and chief scientist Jeff Dean departing after 27 years — followed long-running tension between DeepMind's London research culture and Mountain View's product organization. Subsequent reporting indicates primary control of AI efforts is shifting toward Silicon Valley amid board concerns about coding and enterprise performance relative to Anthropic and OpenAI.
