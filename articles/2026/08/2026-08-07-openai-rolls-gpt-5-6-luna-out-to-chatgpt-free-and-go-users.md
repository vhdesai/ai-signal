# OpenAI rolls GPT-5.6 “Luna” out to ChatGPT Free and Go users

OpenAI began rolling GPT-5.6 “Luna” out to ChatGPT Free and Go users, with unlimited text chats slated to follow, according to Lowyat.NET. Luna becomes the default model for lower tiers, distinct from the flagship GPT-5.6 Sol that powers paid and enterprise use. The move widens access to OpenAI’s latest generation while steering heavier workloads toward premium tiers. MODEL CHINA
