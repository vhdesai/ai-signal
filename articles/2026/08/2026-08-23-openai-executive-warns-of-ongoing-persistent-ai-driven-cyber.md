# OpenAI Executive Warns of "Ongoing, Persistent" AI-Driven Cyber Attacks

OpenAI's Chris Lehane told The Guardian that organizations should prepare to defend against continuous, AI-executed cyber attacks as frontier models gain the ability to plan and carry out multi-stage intrusions, and called for new safety standards. Critics quoted in the piece argue AI firms are moving recklessly relative to their own warnings. Security leaders should assume adversary tooling improves on the same curve as commercial agents and plan detection accordingly.
