# Nvidia Says Groq Racks Will Be Online This Year Following $20B Purchase

Systems built on Groq’s inference silicon will reach customers before year-end, moving quickly to productize the $20B acquisition. Nvidia framed the effort around low-latency inference — an increasingly distinct workload from training. Signals Nvidia intends to defend the inference tier rather than cede it to specialized challengers or hyperscaler silicon.
