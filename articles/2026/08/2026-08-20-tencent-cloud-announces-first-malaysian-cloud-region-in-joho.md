# Tencent Cloud Announces First Malaysian Cloud Region in Johor

Tencent Cloud unveiled plans for its first Malaysian cloud region, with up to three availability zones in Johor, to support enterprise AI workloads. The announcement came at its C-Suite Dialogue 2026 in Kuala Lumpur, where it also showcased WorkBuddy, an Agent Development Platform, and the TokenHub model-as-a-service platform. Tencent made its Hy3 model, formerly Hunyuan, free through Aug 31. It also partnered with Universiti Teknologi Malaysia to train more than 1,000 AI practitioners.
