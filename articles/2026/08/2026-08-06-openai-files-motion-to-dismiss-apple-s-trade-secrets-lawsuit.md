# OpenAI files motion to dismiss Apple’s trade-secrets lawsuit

OpenAI filed a 31-page motion in the Northern District of California to dismiss Apple’s July trade-secrets suit, calling it “baseless and pretextual” and arguing Apple “fails to sufficiently describe” the trade secrets at issue — asserting OpenAI has “no use, need or desire” for them. Axios notes the word “fail” appears roughly 50 times. A hearing is set for October 1, and the ChatGPT–Siri partnership continues in the interim.
