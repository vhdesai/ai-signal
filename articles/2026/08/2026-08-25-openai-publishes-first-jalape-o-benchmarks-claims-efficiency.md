# OpenAI Publishes First Jalapeño Benchmarks, Claims Efficiency Lead Over Nvidia Blackwell

OpenAI released performance results for Jalapeño, its custom LLM inference processor co-developed with Broadcom, claiming up to 1.9x more AI work per watt and materially lower latency than Nvidia’s Blackwell parts. Presented at Hot Chips on SemiAnalysis’s InferenceX benchmark. Head of hardware Richard Ho said deployment begins in “very small volumes” by end of 2026. The chip minimizes data movement by keeping model state local. The timing — hours before Nvidia’s quarterly report — underscores that OpenAI intends to shift a meaningful share of inference off merchant silicon.
