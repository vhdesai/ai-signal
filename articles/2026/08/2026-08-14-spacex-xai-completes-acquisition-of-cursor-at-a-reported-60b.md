# SpaceX/xAI completes acquisition of Cursor at a reported ~$60B

SpaceX/xAI has closed its acquisition of Anysphere, maker of Cursor, in a deal reported at roughly $60 billion. Cursor's team is expected to work across both Grok and Cursor products under the combined SpaceXAI entity. It is the largest consolidation to date in AI coding tools and places a frontier lab in direct control of a leading developer surface.
