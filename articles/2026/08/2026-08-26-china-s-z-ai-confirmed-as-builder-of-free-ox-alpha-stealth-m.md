# China's Z.AI Confirmed as Builder of Free "Ox Alpha" Stealth Model, Weights to Follow

Z.AI (Zhipu) confirmed that Ox Alpha — the free, high-performing stealth model that has topped online usage charts for roughly a week — is a new iteration of its GLM series, and said it would release the weights. The model reportedly handles a million-token context and accepts video input while remaining free to use. Zero-cost frontier-adjacent Chinese open weights continue to compress the pricing floor for Western API providers.
