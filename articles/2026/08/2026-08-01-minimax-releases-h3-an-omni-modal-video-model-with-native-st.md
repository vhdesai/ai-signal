# MiniMax releases H3, an omni-modal video model with native stereo audio

MiniMax released H3, an omni-modal model that can generate 15-second 2K video clips with synchronized native stereo audio from mixed text, image, video, and audio context. The release pushes media generation beyond isolated text-to-video demos toward richer outputs that previously required stitching separate audio and video systems together. Enterprise relevance is likely in marketing, training, entertainment, and synthetic-content workflows where controllability and provenance will determine adoption.
