# OpenAI warns the window to strengthen cyber defenses is closing

OpenAI published a warning that AI is making cyber attacks materially more sophisticated and more widespread, and that defensive capability is not keeping pace. Security practitioners quoted in the coverage pointed to conventional but under-executed controls — patching discipline, identity hardening, and phishing resistance — as the highest-yield near-term responses. For CISOs, the practical takeaway is that threat escalation is arriving faster than AI-native defensive tooling is maturing.
