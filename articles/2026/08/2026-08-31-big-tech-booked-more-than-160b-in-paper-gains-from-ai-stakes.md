# Big Tech Booked More Than $160B in Paper Gains From AI Stakes Last Quarter

Alphabet, Amazon, Nvidia, and Microsoft collectively recorded over $160 billion in "other income" in Q2 2026 from mark-to-market gains on private AI holdings — more than double the prior quarter, with Alphabet at $97.9 billion and Amazon at $53.4 billion. Analysts caution that these unrealized gains inflate headline earnings independent of operating performance — a material quality-of-earnings issue across the AI complex.
