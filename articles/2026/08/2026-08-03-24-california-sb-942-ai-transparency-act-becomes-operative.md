# 24. California SB 942 AI Transparency Act becomes operative

California’s SB 942 took effect, requiring C2PA content provenance and free AI-detection tools from providers with 1M+ monthly active users, with penalties of $5,000 per day for violations. It adds a significant U.S. state-level provenance mandate alongside the EU’s transparency rules. TrendingSuperintelligence
