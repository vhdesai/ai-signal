# xAI's Grok Imagine faces new lawsuits as Minnesota nudification ban takes effect

Three new lawsuits were filed against xAI over Grok Imagine as Minnesota's nudification ban took effect August 1 following an unsuccessful court challenge. The combination of state statute and private litigation creates compounding exposure for image-generation products without robust consent controls. Other states are expected to follow Minnesota's statutory template. Any enterprise embedding third-party image generation should review indemnification terms. TrendingSaferAI / Z.ai
