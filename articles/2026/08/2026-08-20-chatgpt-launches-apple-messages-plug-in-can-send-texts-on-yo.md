# ChatGPT Launches Apple Messages Plug-In — Can Send Texts on Your Behalf

OpenAI released an Apple Messages plug-in for ChatGPT that can sort, search, draft, and send messages. OpenAI warns persistent approval “removes your final chance to review a message before ChatGPT sends it as you.” This is the second agentic messaging integration this week, after Anthropic’s autonomous Gmail capability—accelerating the shift from drafting to executing irreversible communications.
