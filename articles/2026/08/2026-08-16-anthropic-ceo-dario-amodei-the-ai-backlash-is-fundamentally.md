# Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei: the AI backlash is "fundamentally a crisis of trust"

Amodei argued that public resistance to AI is less about the technology's capabilities than about institutional credibility, noting that "ordinary people don't trust companies, governments, or the tech industry." The framing shifts the burden from persuasion to demonstrable accountability. For enterprises, it reinforces that deployment friction increasingly originates with employees and customers rather than model performance.
