# Anthropic signs a $10B compute deal with AI cloud startup Volta

Anthropic committed roughly $10 billion over six years to Volta, an AI cloud startup whose capacity is built on NVIDIA's Vera Rubin systems. The deal continues the pattern of frontier labs locking multi-year capacity outside the three hyperscalers to hedge concentration risk and secure the newest silicon. For enterprise buyers it is another signal that frontier-model pricing is underwritten by long-dated fixed obligations. It also strengthens NVIDIA's position as the substrate beneath both hyperscaler and neocloud supply. HotOracle
