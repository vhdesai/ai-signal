# OpenAI enterprise revenue passes consumer for the first time

CFO Sarah Friar told investors that enterprise revenue now exceeds ChatGPT consumer revenue, a crossover the company had guided to year-end. The year opened at roughly a 60-40 consumer-enterprise split.
