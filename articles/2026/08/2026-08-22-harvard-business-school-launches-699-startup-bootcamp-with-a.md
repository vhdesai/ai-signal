# Harvard Business School Launches $699 Startup Bootcamp with AI Avatar Instructors

HBS Foundry, a new $699 startup bootcamp, uses AI avatars of its instructors for feedback during practice pitches and simulated board meetings. The program demonstrates how elite institutions are deploying AI to scale premium educational experiences at a fraction of MBA tuition — a model that could reshape executive education and professional development.
