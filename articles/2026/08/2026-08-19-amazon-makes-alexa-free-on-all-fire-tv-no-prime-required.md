# Amazon Makes Alexa+ Free on All Fire TV — No Prime Required

Amazon is making its AI-powered Alexa+ free on all compatible Fire TV devices, auto-upgrading users regardless of Prime status. Removing both the subscription and the Prime gate signals Amazon is optimizing for assistant reach over near-term monetization. The installed-base advantage on living-room hardware is the strategic asset being pressed in the consumer AI distribution race.
