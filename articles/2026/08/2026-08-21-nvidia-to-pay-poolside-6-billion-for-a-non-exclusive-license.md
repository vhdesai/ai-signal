# Nvidia to Pay Poolside $6 Billion for a Non-Exclusive License and Hire 109 Staff

Nvidia agreed to pay roughly $6B under a non-exclusive licensing agreement with AI coding startup Poolside and invest a further $1B at a $12B pre-money valuation, with about 109 Poolside employees receiving Nvidia offers. The company continues to operate independently rather than being acquired. The structure gives Nvidia access to model-building IP and scarce engineering talent while sidestepping much of the review that a conventional acquisition would attract.
