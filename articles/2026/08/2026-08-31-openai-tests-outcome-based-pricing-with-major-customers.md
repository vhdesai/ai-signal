# OpenAI Tests Outcome-Based Pricing With Major Customers

OpenAI has begun letting selected large customers pay only when its AI completes a task, starting in customer service — following resolution-based models already offered by Intercom, Zendesk, and Salesforce. Skeptics, including Stripe, warn that attributing an outcome to a single AI system is operationally messy. A shift toward outcome pricing would materially change how AI spend is budgeted and audited.
