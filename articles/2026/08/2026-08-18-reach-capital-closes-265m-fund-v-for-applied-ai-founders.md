# Reach Capital closes $265M Fund V for applied-AI founders

Reach Capital announced the close of a $265 million Fund V, continuing the 11-year-old San Francisco firm’s thesis of backing AI applications in education, health, and workforce categories. The raise is notable for its application-layer focus at a moment when the majority of AI capital is concentrated in frontier labs and infrastructure. It is a modest but useful signal that LP appetite still extends beyond compute. techcrunch.com – Reach Capital raises $265M Fund V
