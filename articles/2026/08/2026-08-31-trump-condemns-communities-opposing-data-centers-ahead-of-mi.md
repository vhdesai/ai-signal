# Trump Condemns Communities Opposing Data Centers Ahead of Midterms

President Trump said communities that reject data centers will end up “backwards and poor,” adding that “if we kill the Golden Goose, you will only have yourselves to blame.” The comments follow a National Republican Senatorial Committee warning that Democrats are successfully campaigning on data-center opposition. The Information’s analysis counted more than 500 active data center bans as of early August — siting risk is now a first-order constraint on AI capacity plans.
