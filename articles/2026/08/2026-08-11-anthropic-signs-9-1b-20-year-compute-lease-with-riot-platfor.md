# Anthropic Signs $9.1B, 20-Year Compute Lease With Riot Platforms

Anthropic contracted 191 MW of capacity at Riot’s Rockdale, Texas campus under a 20-year deal generating ~$9.1B through 2048, with extension options to ~$16.1B. Riot shares rose 17% intraday. Crypto miners’ grid interconnects and cooling — assets that take years to permit — are now the scarce input for frontier AI, not the chips themselves.
