# DOJ Investigating a16z Over Board Seats at Competing AI/Data Companies

DOJ examining whether a16z partners violated Section 8 of the Clayton Act via interlocking directorates (Horowitz at Databricks, Casado at Fivetran). First known action against a VC firm. If the theory holds, it changes board-composition norms across the AI cap table. Technology executives on multiple boards should consult counsel about competitive overlaps. Forbes FUNDING
