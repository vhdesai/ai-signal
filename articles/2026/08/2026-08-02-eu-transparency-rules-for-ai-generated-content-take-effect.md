# EU transparency rules for AI-generated content take effect

EU transparency obligations became enforceable on August 2, requiring clear labels and machine-readable marks for deepfakes and certain AI-generated content, plus disclosure when people interact with chatbots or agents. The European Commission can inspect general-purpose models, restrict market access, and levy fines up to 15 million euros or 3% of global turnover. Providers serving EU users face immediate labeling and compliance requirements. POLICY
