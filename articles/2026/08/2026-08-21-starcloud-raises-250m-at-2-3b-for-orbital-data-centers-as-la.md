# Starcloud Raises $250M at $2.3B for Orbital Data Centers as Launch Options Dry Up

Starcloud extended its Series A by $250M (total: $420M) at $2.3B to build satellites for AI inference in orbit. The company has requested FCC permission for 88,000 spacecraft and is racing to lock Starship launch capacity before Falcon 9 ends in 2028. Nvidia invested $25M — Starcloud is the only known company running an H100 in orbit and is sharing learnings as Nvidia develops its space GPU (Vera Rubin Space-1).
