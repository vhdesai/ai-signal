# Musk's faster path to gas turbines for AI compute carries environmental risk

Elon Musk says a new SpaceX foundry will cast turbine blades in-house and bring gas generation online roughly 18 months faster than competitors, addressing a power bottleneck for large AI training clusters. Environmental groups have alleged xAI ran turbines in Memphis without permits. Energy permitting is becoming a hard constraint on frontier compute expansion.
