# OpenAI Leader Warns of “Ongoing, Persistent” AI-Driven Cyberattacks

OpenAI’s Chris Lehane told The Guardian that organizations should prepare for continuous, autonomous cyberattacks as frontier models acquire multi-step offensive capability. He said the industry is “hitting a different chapter.” The comments follow OpenAI’s decision to slow frontier training after a security incident. POLICY
