# Klaviyo Acquires Elias Torres' AI Customer-Success Startup, Names Him CPO

Publicly traded Klaviyo (NYSE: KVYO) acquired Agency, the AI-powered customer success startup founded by serial entrepreneur Elias Torres (previously co-founder of Drift and Performable), which had raised $32M from Sequoia, Menlo Ventures, and Felicis. Torres joins as Chief Product Officer to lead Klaviyo's AI agents for marketing campaigns and post-sale support. Terms were not disclosed. The move signals Klaviyo is betting its product roadmap on agentic AI to compete with Salesforce, Decagon, and Sierra. ________________________________ EARNINGSINDUSTRY
