# EU AI Act Moves From Statute to Enforcement

Core obligations now in force after August compliance date. Transparency, documentation, and GPAI duties are examinable — not aspirational. Many compliance teams read the delay as blanket relief; it was not. Enterprises deploying models in the EU should assume documentation requests are a live risk. Axios POLICY
