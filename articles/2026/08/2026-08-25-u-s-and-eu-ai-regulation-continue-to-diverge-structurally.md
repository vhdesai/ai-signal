# U.S. and EU AI Regulation Continue to Diverge Structurally

Analysis contrasts the EU's category-based prohibitions and general-purpose model obligations with the U.S. approach of sector-specific enforcement and litigation. The practical consequence for multinationals: EU obligations set the effective global compliance floor while U.S. exposure arrives through enforcement actions. Dual-track governance remains the pragmatic posture for any company operating in both markets.
