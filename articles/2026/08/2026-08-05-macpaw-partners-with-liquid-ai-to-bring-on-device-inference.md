# MacPaw partners with Liquid AI to bring on-device inference to third-party developers

Ukraine-based MacPaw is partnering with Liquid AI to power its products with locally hosted models, and plans to expose that stack to developers building for its app store. The arrangement is an example of edge inference being packaged as a platform service rather than a per-app engineering effort. For regulated or privacy-sensitive workloads, on-device distribution channels of this kind are becoming a viable deployment path.
