# Discovered Materials raises seed funding to use AI for cooler chips

Discovered Materials raised a $9 million seed round to use swarms of AI agents and physics models to find materials that could make AI chips run cooler. The startup is focusing on semiconductor thermal constraints, using agents to generate candidate materials and simulations to validate whether they are viable. This is a direct example of AI being applied to the bottlenecks AI itself is creating: thermal efficiency, chip materials, and data-center power consumption.
