# Pokee AI releases Pokee‑Isaac 28B, a 10M-token-context agentic model built to run inside the customer boundary

Pokee AI released a 28B text-only foundation model with a 10M-token context window, licensed for VPC, on-premises, and on-device deployment rather than published as open weights. The company claims 93.3% on RULER at 10M tokens, where cited baselines degrade beyond roughly 1–2M, plus a lead on BFCL v4 (70.94) and τ³-bench (0.662 avg), and second place on Terminal-Bench 2.1. Benchmarks were measured on a single B200-class GPU; consumer-GPU claims are vendor guidance. Provisional pricing is $0.15/$1.00 per million input/output tokens. MarkTechPost Note: no monitored company (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, Alibaba, Baidu, Tencent) shipped a confirmed model release inside the 24-hour window.
