# The A.I. Token Tax: How AI Usage Costs Are Reshaping Enterprise Budgets

DealBook leads with "The A.I. Token Tax" — examining how AI usage costs are becoming a significant and often unpredictable line item in enterprise budgets. As companies move from pilots to production, the cumulative cost of AI tokens across the organization is creating what amounts to a new "tax" on digital operations. The story explores how CIOs are grappling with cost governance, model selection, and the tension between capability and efficiency.
