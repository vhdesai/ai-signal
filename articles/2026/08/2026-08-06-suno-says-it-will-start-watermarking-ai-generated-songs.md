# Suno says it will start watermarking AI-generated songs

Suno announced audio watermarking, fingerprinting, tighter download controls, and updated community guidelines as it faces legal pressure from music labels and artist groups. The company says the tools are intended to improve transparency, reduce copycat misuse, and prevent mass distribution abuse on streaming platforms. The move is a concrete example of provenance becoming a platform requirement for generative media, especially where rights holders and distribution channels are involved.
