# Wispr raises $280M at a $2B valuation, expanding beyond dictation

Wispr, known for its AI dictation product, raised $280 million in Series B funding led by Menlo Ventures at a $2 billion valuation. The company signaled ambitions well beyond transcription toward broader voice-driven interaction.
