# Google Research introduces GlucoFM, a 0.72M-parameter foundation model for glucose monitoring

GlucoFM separates a slow physiological "state" stream from a transient "event" stream and pretrains with two JEPA-style objectives. It scored 58.8 task-averaged PR-AUC across 14 cohort-task evaluations against 54.7 for the strongest CGM-specific baseline, pretrained on 109,066 hours of unlabeled data from 477 subjects using a single H100. The efficiency point matters more than the domain: sub-million-parameter models are beating specialist baselines. The authors note it is a research prototype, not regulatory-cleared.
