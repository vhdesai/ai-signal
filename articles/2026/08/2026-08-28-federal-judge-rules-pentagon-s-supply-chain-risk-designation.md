# Federal judge rules Pentagon's "supply-chain risk" designation of Anthropic was unlawful

A federal judge in California found that the Department of Defense violated the First Amendment when it labeled Anthropic a supply-chain risk, ruling the designation was retaliation tied to the company's AI safety limits. It is Anthropic's first court win against the administration; a second Pentagon lawsuit continues in Washington. The ruling matters beyond one vendor — it constrains the use of procurement designations as leverage over model providers' safety policies. techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/anthropic-court-win-pentagon FUNDING
