# OpenAI’s unreleased “Astra” model reports advances on 10 long-standing math and theoretical CS problems

OpenAI said an internal, unreleased model — “Astra” — produced new results across ten longstanding problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science, including improved bounds for sphere packing and error-correcting codes and a disproof of a rigidity conjecture, with the next-generation model reveal embedded in the accompanying blog post. The company released research write-ups, machine-checkable proofs, and reasoning walkthroughs for each result. If the results survive peer scrutiny, it is an early data point for AI contributing original, verifiable research rather than only reproducing known work.
