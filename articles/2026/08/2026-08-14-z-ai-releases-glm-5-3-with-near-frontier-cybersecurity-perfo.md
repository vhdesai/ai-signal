# Z.ai releases GLM-5.3 with near-frontier cybersecurity performance and gated access

Z.ai released GLM-5.3 on August 14, reusing the GLM-5.2 base model and delivering its gains entirely through post-training — a notable efficiency claim for complex coding and long-horizon tasks. The company reports 84.5% on the CyberGym vulnerability-detection benchmark, marginally above the 83.8% it cites for Anthropic's comparable model, though it trails badly on exploit development (54.4% vs. 78%). Z.ai plans an open-weights release after further security testing while restricting higher-risk capabilities behind a trusted-access tier.
