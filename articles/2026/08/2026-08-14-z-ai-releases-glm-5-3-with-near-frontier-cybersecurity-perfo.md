# Z.ai Releases GLM-5.3 With Near-Frontier Cybersecurity Performance

Z.ai released GLM-5.3, reporting significant gains in long-horizon coding and cybersecurity achieved entirely through post-training on the same GLM-5.2 base model. CyberGym reaches 84.5%, marginally above Anthropic's comparable model on vulnerability detection, though it trails on exploit development (54.4% vs. 78%). Z.ai plans an open-weights release after further security testing while restricting higher-risk capabilities behind a trusted-access tier. The dual-use implication is direct: a near-frontier offensive-security capability is on track to become freely downloadable — the clearest test yet of whether open models can ship dual-use cyber capability with meaningful guardrails.
