# Intel announces a $15 billion common stock offering, citing AI demand

Intel announced a $15 billion underwritten public offering of common stock on Monday, capitalizing on a share-price recovery driven by AI hardware demand. The company pointed to physical AI, purpose-built silicon, and advanced packaging as the growth areas the proceeds will support, alongside general capital expenditure and working capital. The raise is one of the largest equity offerings by a US semiconductor firm and signals Intel intends to fund foundry and packaging capacity from equity rather than debt.
