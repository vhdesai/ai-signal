# AI Data Center Demand Is Squeezing Memory for Android Phones

Google is imposing new memory-use limits on Android apps as AI data center buildouts absorb memory supply and contribute to hardware shortages, with lower-cost handsets most exposed. It is one of the clearest consumer-visible spillovers of AI infrastructure demand into adjacent hardware markets. Product organizations with mobile footprints should expect tighter device-side resource budgets through the next cycle. AI Safety & Policy POLICYHOT
