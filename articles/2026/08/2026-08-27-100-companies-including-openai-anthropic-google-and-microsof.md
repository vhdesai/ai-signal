# 100+ companies including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft call for coordinated defense against rogue AI

More than a hundred technology, cybersecurity, and financial firms — including CrowdStrike, Okta, and Fortinet alongside the major labs — signed an open letter warning that AI-enabled cyberattacks will become far more widespread and sophisticated in the coming months, naming hospitals, water treatment plants, and internet infrastructure as exposed. The letter calls for new public-private partnerships and coordinated security standards at local, national, and international levels. The signatories occupy a conflicted position: the same firms are shipping ever more capable models while marketing defensive programs — OpenAI's Daybreak, Anthropic's Mythos, and Microsoft's Perception platform.
