# UC San Diego to test novel power technology for AI data centers in $8.48M partnershipNew

The San Diego Supercomputer Center will pilot a new power-delivery technology intended to change how AI data centers draw electricity while preventing them from overwhelming California’s grid. The $8.48M partnership targets energy demand from compute-intensive workloads — one of AI infrastructure’s fastest-growing bottlenecks. Grid constraint is increasingly the binding limit on capacity expansion, making pilots like this strategically relevant to siting decisions.
