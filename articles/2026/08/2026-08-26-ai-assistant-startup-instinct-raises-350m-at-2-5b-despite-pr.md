# AI Assistant Startup Instinct Raises $350M at $2.5B Despite Privacy Concerns

One-year-old Instinct raised $250M Series B (co-led by Index Ventures and Benchmark), bringing total funding to $350M at $2.5B valuation. Founded by 23-year-old Noah Shinn, the AI assistant connects to users' apps and devices for life management tasks (grocery orders, travel planning, subscription cancellation). Still in private beta, it has already sparked privacy controversy over invasive permissions and sweeping terms of use.
