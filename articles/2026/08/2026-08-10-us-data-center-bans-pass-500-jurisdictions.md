# US data-center bans pass 500 jurisdictions

More than 500 US towns and counties now block or restrict new data-center development, with 150+ ordinances passed in July alone. New York and Texas have escalated to the state level. Siting risk—not chip supply—is emerging as the practical ceiling on near-term US compute expansion. URL: The Information search: data center bans
