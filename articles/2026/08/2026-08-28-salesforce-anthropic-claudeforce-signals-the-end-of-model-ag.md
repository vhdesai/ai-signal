# Salesforce–Anthropic "Claudeforce" Signals the End of Model-Agnostic Enterprise AI

Salesforce and Anthropic announced Claudeforce on August 26, establishing Claude as the default reasoning engine across the Salesforce ecosystem rather than one option among several. Analysis published Friday argues this moves the pair past simple API integration into a structural distribution partnership.
