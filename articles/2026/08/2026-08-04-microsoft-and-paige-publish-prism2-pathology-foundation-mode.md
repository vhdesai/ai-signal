# Microsoft and Paige publish PRISM2 pathology foundation model

Microsoft Research and Paige published PRISM2, a pathology foundation model trained on tissue images paired with language derived from pathology reports, with results appearing in Nature Medicine. Microsoft says the unified model matches or exceeds specialized cancer-detection models across multiple cancer tasks without task-specific retraining.
