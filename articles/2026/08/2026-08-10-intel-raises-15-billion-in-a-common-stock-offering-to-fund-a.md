# Intel raises $15 billion in a common stock offering to fund AI chip growth

Intel announced a $15 billion underwritten public offering of common stock, moving to capitalize on a share-price surge driven by AI compute demand. The raise is one of the largest equity issuances in the sector this cycle and signals that Intel intends to self-fund capacity expansion rather than rely solely on partner or government capital. For buyers, the relevant question is whether the proceeds translate into competitive foundry and accelerator supply on a timeline that matters, given TSMC's continued lead in AI-related output.
