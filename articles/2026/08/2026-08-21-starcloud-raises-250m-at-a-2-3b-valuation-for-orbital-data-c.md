# Starcloud raises $250M at a $2.3B valuation for orbital data centers

Starcloud closed a $250M Series A extension at a $2.3B post-money valuation to build data centers in orbit, at a moment when terrestrial power and launch capacity are both constrained. The pitch is continuous solar power and passive radiative cooling outside the grid and permitting constraints now slowing ground buildouts. Execution risk remains high — launch availability is the binding constraint — but the round shows investors are funding structurally unconventional answers to the compute-energy bottleneck.
