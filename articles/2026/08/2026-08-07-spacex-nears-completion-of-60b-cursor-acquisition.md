# SpaceX nears completion of $60B Cursor acquisition

Cursor told staff that SpaceX could complete its $60 billion acquisition as soon as the end of next week, with leadership flagging potential brand-name changes. The deal would give SpaceX a proprietary AI-native development platform, consolidating coding-agent talent in a way that reshapes the competitive landscape for developer tooling.
