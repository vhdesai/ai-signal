# OpenAI cuts off Cursor after SpaceX's takeover of Anysphere

OpenAI plans to end Cursor's direct model access on November 12 after SpaceX's reported takeover of Anysphere, citing ownership and concerns tied to xAI's admitted use of OpenAI outputs. Cursor says OpenAI models represent roughly 5% of traffic, while Anthropic committed additional compute within hours. The cutoff turns model access from an abstract platform risk into a live vendor-dependency test.
