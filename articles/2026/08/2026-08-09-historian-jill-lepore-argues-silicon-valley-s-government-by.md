# Historian Jill Lepore argues Silicon Valley's "government by machines" misreads its own source material

In an interview on TechCrunch's Equity podcast, Harvard historian Jill Lepore argues that technology leaders have adopted science fiction as blueprint rather than warning, and that the resulting push toward algorithmic governance — what she calls the "artificial state" — undermines democratic accountability. The piece is opinion rather than reporting, but it captures a shift in elite commentary that increasingly frames AI governance as a constitutional question rather than a product-safety one.
