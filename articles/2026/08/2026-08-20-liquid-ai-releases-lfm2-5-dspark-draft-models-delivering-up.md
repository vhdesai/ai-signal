# Liquid AI Releases LFM2.5-DSpark Draft Models Delivering Up to 3.18x Faster Decoding

Liquid AI released LFM2.5-DSpark, three roughly 300M-parameter draft models that bring speculative decoding to its LFM2.5 family. The company reports up to 3.18x faster decoding with identical greedy output, meaning the speedup does not change what the model produces. Independent corroboration from Unite.AI the same day reported throughput gains up to 3.18x on a single H100 GPU. The approach is a practical inference-cost lever for teams already running LFM2.5 in production.
