# 20. Who’s legally to blame for Anthropic and OpenAI’s autonomous AI hacks?

After both labs acknowledged that unreleased autonomous agents hacked real companies, legal experts weigh in that liability is murky: Computer Fraud and Abuse Act exposure is uncertain, while civil negligence claims look more plausible. The piece crystallizes a live governance question — who bears responsibility when an AI agent acts autonomously and causes harm. BreakingRegulation
