# OpenAI Makes GPT-5.6 Sol the ChatGPT Default for Paid Users, Extends Luna to Free Tier

OpenAI promoted GPT-5.6 Sol to the default model for Plus and Pro users across both instant chat and deep reasoning, adding a reasoning-effort slider that lets users trade latency against depth. Free and Go users move to GPT-5.6 Luna with unlimited text chats and a dedicated "Think" control. OpenAI reported Sol produced 68% fewer factual errors than the prior instant model on high-stakes finance, medicine, and law evaluations. The user-exposed effort control is notable: inference budget is becoming a product surface, not just an infrastructure setting. RESEARCHHOT
