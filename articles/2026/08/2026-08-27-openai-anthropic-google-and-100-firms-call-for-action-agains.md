# OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and 100+ Firms Call for Action Against Rogue AI

More than 100 technology companies — including OpenAI, Anthropic and Google — published a joint open letter warning that AI-powered cyberattacks are poised to become significantly more common, and urging governments and industry to act. Politico's coverage frames it as an unusually broad coalition asking for urgent, coordinated defensive investment rather than new restrictions on model capability. The signal for enterprises: the labs themselves now treat AI-enabled offensive capability as a near-term operational threat, not a speculative one. SECURITY
