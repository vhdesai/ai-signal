# Nebius Q2: Revenue Surges 454% to $582M as AI Compute Demand Explodes

Nebius, the Nvidia-backed neocloud, reported a 454% expansion in Q2 revenue to $582 million. Cash burn rose to $3.4 billion as capex hit $5.657 billion. CEO Arkady Volozh said Nebius could “sell today our entire 2027 capacity if we wanted.” Shares jumped 17%. The results reinforce the neocloud thesis but highlight massive capital requirements.
