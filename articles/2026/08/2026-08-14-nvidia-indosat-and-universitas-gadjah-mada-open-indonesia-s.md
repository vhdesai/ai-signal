# NVIDIA, Indosat, and Universitas Gadjah Mada open Indonesia's first university AI technology center

NVIDIA, Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison, and Universitas Gadjah Mada launched the UGM Indosat NVIDIA AI Technology Center in Yogyakarta, described as Indonesia's first university-based AI technology center. The center will provide access to NVIDIA's AI platform and Indosat's sovereign GPU-as-a-service infrastructure, with initial work focused on healthcare, agriculture, and disaster response. The announcement reflects a broader sovereign-AI pattern: governments and universities are pairing local talent programs with domestic compute access to reduce dependence on external AI capacity.
