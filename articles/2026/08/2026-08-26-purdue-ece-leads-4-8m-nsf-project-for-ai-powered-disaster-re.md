# Purdue ECE leads $4.8M NSF project for AI-powered disaster-response networks

Purdue won a $4.8M NSF grant for HEIDRA, a distributed on-device AI system that links phones, drones, vehicles and sensors into a temporary intelligent network when cellular and internet infrastructure fails. The design emphasizes responder-in-the-loop decision-making so emergency personnel validate or reject AI recommendations. Field testing is planned at the Indiana National Guard's Muscatatuck center.
