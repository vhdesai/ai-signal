# Microsoft and HUMAIN Expand Strategic Collaboration at LEAP 2026

Microsoft and Saudi state-owned AI firm HUMAIN announced an expanded strategic collaboration at LEAP 2026, unveiling new enterprise AI offerings. The partnership deepens Microsoft’s Gulf-region cloud and AI footprint for enterprise and government customers, in a market where sovereign-AI capacity is being contracted years ahead of demand.
