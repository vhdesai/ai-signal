# Inherent Exits Stealth With $50M — Claims Agent Beats Frontier Labs at Reproducing Papers

Founded by DeepMind alumni, Inherent’s Faraday agent (27B open-weight base) claims to outperform Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 at independently reproducing scientific paper findings. Results are vendor-reported and not yet replicated, but reproduction benchmarks are an increasingly credible proxy for real research capability.
