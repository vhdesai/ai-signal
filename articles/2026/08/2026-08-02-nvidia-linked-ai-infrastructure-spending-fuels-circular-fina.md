# Nvidia-linked AI infrastructure spending fuels circular-financing concerns

NPR reported that Nvidia is expected to spend on the order of $750 billion across the AI supply chain, prompting critics to warn about circular financing among chipmakers, clouds, model labs, and data-center developers. The concern is that AI demand may be partially self-reinforcing when suppliers finance customers who then buy more infrastructure. Even if demand is real, the scale and opacity of the funding loops make balance-sheet quality and cash-flow conversion central to the AI infrastructure thesis.
