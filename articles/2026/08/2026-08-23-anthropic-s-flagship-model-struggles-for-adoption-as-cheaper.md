# Anthropic’s Flagship Model Struggles for Adoption as Cheaper Rivals Gain

The FT reports Anthropic’s most capable model is failing to convert capability leadership into usage share, as buyers migrate to materially cheaper alternatives. Enterprises adopt model-routing strategies, sending routine work to low-cost models. Intelligence-per-dollar, not raw benchmark position, is now the governing procurement metric. INDUSTRY
