# AI is putting pressure on corporate IT budgets

The Wall Street Journal reported that AI is increasing pressure on corporate IT budgets. The likely drivers are familiar to CIOs: higher cloud spend, GPU-backed services, data-platform upgrades, security controls, and demand from business units experimenting with AI tools. The executive implication is that AI funding needs to move from ad hoc innovation budgets to governed portfolio management with clear ROI, risk, and cost-allocation discipline. URL behind paywall.
