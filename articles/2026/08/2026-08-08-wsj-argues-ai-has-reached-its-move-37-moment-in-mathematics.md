# WSJ argues AI has reached its “Move 37” moment in mathematics

Ben Cohen’s column contends that AI has crossed an AlphaGo-style watershed in mathematics, citing a wave of machine-generated proofs and counter-proofs that mainstream mathematicians did not anticipate. The piece situates Demis Hassabis’s DeepMind trajectory alongside OpenAI’s mathematics work and treats surprising machine discoveries as an expected output of frontier models rather than an anomaly. It is opinion-adjacent analysis, but a useful barometer of how quickly the “AI as discovery engine” framing is moving into mainstream business press. AI Safety & Policy POLICY
