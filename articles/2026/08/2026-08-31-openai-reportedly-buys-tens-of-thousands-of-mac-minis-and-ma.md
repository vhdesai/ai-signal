# OpenAI reportedly buys tens of thousands of Mac minis and Mac Studios to train computer-use agents

OpenAI has acquired tens of thousands of Apple desktop systems to run reinforcement-learning workloads for computer-use agents, with Anthropic reported to be renting similar Mac capacity through AWS. The machines are not substituting for GPU clusters; unified-memory desktops suit memory-bound, trial-and-error desktop-task training. Reported side effects include constrained availability of high-memory configurations and pull-forward of Apple's Mac mini and Mac Studio refreshes. https://www.theinformation.com/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=OpenAI%20Mac%20mini%20agents
