# Google says Gemini app surpassed 1 billion monthly users

Google said the Gemini app has passed 1 billion monthly users, calling it the fastest-growing product in the company's history. The usage mix is strategically important: Google said 63% of users speak directly to Gemini, one in five Gemini Live interactions use camera feeds or screen sharing, and iOS has more than 100 million active users. For executives, the signal is that multimodal assistants are moving from novelty usage to daily utility, with mobile and cross-platform reach becoming a distribution advantage.
