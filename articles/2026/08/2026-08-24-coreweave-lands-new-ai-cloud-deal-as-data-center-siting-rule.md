# CoreWeave Lands New AI Cloud Deal as Data Center Siting Rules Tighten

CoreWeave secured an additional AI cloud commitment even as local permitting and siting requirements for data centers become more restrictive. The combination illustrates the sector's core tension: demand for GPU capacity is contracted well ahead of the community and grid approvals required to deliver it. Siting risk is becoming a material factor in AI capacity forecasts.
