# Brad Lightcap, OpenAI's longtime COO, is leaving

Brad Lightcap, one of OpenAI's longest-serving executives and formerly its COO and CFO, said he is leaving to "start something new." The departure follows other senior leadership changes at OpenAI and comes as the company is reportedly preparing for a potential IPO. The near-term impact is less about day-to-day product execution and more about operational continuity, investor confidence, and how OpenAI retains institutional knowledge as it scales.
