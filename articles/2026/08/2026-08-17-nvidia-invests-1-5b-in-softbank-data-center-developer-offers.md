# Nvidia Invests $1.5B in SoftBank Data Center Developer, Offers $105B Credit for OpenAI Facility

Nvidia will invest $1.5B in SB Energy and provide up to $105B in credit to build OpenAI's Ports-Pike data center near Cincinnati, which could scale from 4.25GW to 8GW. The investment ensures Nvidia is the sole compute infrastructure supplier. The site — former U.S. nuclear enrichment land owned by the DOE — will include a 9.2GW natural gas power plant costing $33B. The deal extends Nvidia's strategy of investing in customers and infrastructure developers to lock in GPU demand. 🔗 https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-investing-1-5b-in-softbank-data-center-developer-behind-openai-project/
