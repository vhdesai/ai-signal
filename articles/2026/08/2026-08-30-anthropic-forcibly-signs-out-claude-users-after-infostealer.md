# Anthropic Forcibly Signs Out Claude Users After Infostealer Malware Hijacks Sessions

Anthropic is force-signing-out affected users, wiping saved payment methods, and refunding charges after infostealer families — Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine, and Acreed on Windows; AMOS on some Macs — stole active Claude session cookies to burn subscriber usage limits. Infections trace to pirated downloads and malicious apps rather than any Claude-side breach. This is endpoint compromise propagating into AI service accounts — a direct concern for any organization where employees hold personal or shadow-IT AI subscriptions.
