# AI data startup Micro1 reaches $500M gross run rate amid AI training boom

Micro1 disclosed a $500M gross run rate, up sharply year over year, driven by demand for expert-labeled training and evaluation data. It still trails Mercor (~$2B gross annualized) and Handshake (~$1B), but the trajectory underscores how much frontier-lab spend is flowing to human data supply rather than compute alone. Data-vendor concentration is becoming a real diligence item for anyone dependent on fine-tuned or evaluated models. INDUSTRY
