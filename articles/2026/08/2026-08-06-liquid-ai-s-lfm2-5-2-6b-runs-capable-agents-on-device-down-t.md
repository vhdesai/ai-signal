# Liquid AI's LFM2.5-2.6B runs capable agents on-device—down to a Raspberry Pi

Liquid AI released LFM2.5-2.6B, a compact model it says delivers strong agentic performance on edge hardware with no cloud or GPUs—running on devices as small as a Raspberry Pi. The launch advances the on-device thesis that not every workload needs a frontier data center. For enterprises, small high-quality models point toward cheaper, private, latency-sensitive deployments where data never leaves the device. It is another data point that capability-per-parameter is improving quickly at the small end of the model spectrum, opening agentic AI to IoT, manufacturing, and regulated environments that cannot tolerate cloud dependency.
