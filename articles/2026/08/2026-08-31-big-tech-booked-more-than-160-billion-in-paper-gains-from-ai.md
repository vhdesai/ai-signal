# Big Tech booked more than $160 billion in paper gains from AI stakes last quarter

Alphabet, Amazon, Nvidia, and Microsoft collectively recorded more than $160 billion in other income from mark-to-market gains on private AI holdings in Q2 2026. Analysts warned that unrealized gains are inflating headline earnings independent of operating performance. For investors and operators, AI exposure is now a quality-of-earnings issue as well as a growth narrative.
