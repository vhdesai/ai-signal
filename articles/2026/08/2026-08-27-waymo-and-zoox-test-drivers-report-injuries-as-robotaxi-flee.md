# Waymo and Zoox test drivers report injuries as robotaxi fleets scale

A TechCrunch review of injury records found test drivers for Waymo and Zoox sustained more than two dozen injuries across 2024 and 2025 from hard braking and other sudden autonomous-vehicle maneuvers. The findings surface an occupational-safety dimension of autonomy validation that has largely escaped scrutiny. As fleets scale, the human cost of the testing pipeline becomes a regulatory and reputational exposure in its own right.
