# PORTS-Pike advances as an 8-GW AI infrastructure template anchored by an OpenAI lease

The PORTS-Pike project in Ohio is taking shape as a combined federal-site redevelopment, on-site generation and hyperscale construction program supporting an 8-GW OpenAI lease. The structure pairs power generation with compute capacity on the same footprint, addressing the grid-interconnection bottleneck that now gates most large AI campuses. It is emerging as a reference model for gigawatt-class projects rather than a one-off.
