# EuroHPC awards Bull a €387.8M contract for the LUMI-AI supercomputer in Finland

The EU's EuroHPC joint undertaking selected Bull to build LUMI-AI alongside the existing LUMI system in Kajaani, with AMD processors, IBM storage, and Nokia networking, funded jointly by EuroHPC and a six-country consortium. The system is expected operational in the second half of 2027. It adds to a European AI-factory network now spanning 19 centers, reinforcing sovereign-compute procurement as a durable, non-hyperscaler demand channel. https://www.domain-b.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/europe-lumi-ai-supercomputer-bull-2026
