# Meta Internally Projected Spending Up to $10B a Year on Anthropic's Models

Meta at one point modeled annual spend of as much as $10 billion on Anthropic models while publicly criticizing frontier labs for consolidating power. Engineers drove the surge through heavy Claude Code usage before costs forced a pullback. Meta's consumer agent, internally called Hatch, was built and tested on Claude but is expected to launch on Meta's own model. The story underscores how direct competitors buy each other's models while arguing for strategic independence.
