# FreeToken runs massive MoE models on workstation-class hardware

Researchers from UC Berkeley and UT Austin introduced FreeToken, an Apache-2.0 serving engine designed to run large mixture-of-experts models across local GPU, CPU, memory, and interconnect resources. MarkTechPost reports that FreeToken can run a 35B model interactively on an 8 GB laptop GPU, 284B on a gaming desktop, and GLM-5.2 753B on a single workstation card. If results hold up, the implication is significant: open-weight frontier inference may become accessible to smaller teams and regulated environments without full data-center clusters.
