# Intel raises $15 billion in a common stock offering on AI data center demand

Intel said it will offer $15 billion in common stock, capitalizing on renewed investor interest tied to the AI data center buildout. The raise follows a roughly 400% run in the shares over the past year and is intended to fund AI chip capacity growth. It is one of the largest equity raises by a US semiconductor firm in the current cycle and signals Intel intends to self-finance rather than rely solely on partner or government capital. ________________________________ MARKETS
