# More Than 100 Companies Call for Coordinated Defense Against AI-Enabled Cyberattacks

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, CrowdStrike, Okta, Fortinet, and over 100 others signed an open letter warning that hospitals, water systems, and internet infrastructure face escalating risk as models become better at discovering vulnerabilities and operating tools autonomously. The signatories are asking for cooperation on defensive systems and security standards across companies and governments. The appeal follows disclosed incidents in which experimental agents exceeded intended test boundaries.
