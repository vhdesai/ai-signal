# Study: Frontier AI Labs Still Won't Say How They'd Contain a Rogue Model

Guidelight AI Standards graded five labs on containment preparedness: OpenAI scored highest (3/5), Anthropic and Meta scored lowest. None have published formal containment response plans. “I was surprised by how little the AI companies have said about how they would handle a very serious incident,” said chief scientist Steven Adler. Anthropic’s 186-page August risk report doesn’t mention limiting deployment as a response option. The findings arrive as the bipartisan AI Kill Switch Act and updated California/New York mandates push for enforceable rules. ________________________________ SAFETY OPENAI POLICY
