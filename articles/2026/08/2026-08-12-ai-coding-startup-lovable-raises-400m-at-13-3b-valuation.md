# AI Coding Startup Lovable Raises $400M at $13.3B Valuation

Stockholm-based AI coding startup Lovable announced a $400 million funding round valuing the company at $13.3 billion, more than doubling its December valuation. Despite predictions that Anthropic's Claude Code and similar tools from large labs would squeeze smaller AI coding startups, Lovable has thrived — with corporations now its fastest-growing revenue segment. Nvidia and Adidas are among its biggest new customers.
