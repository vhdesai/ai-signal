# ChatGPT Ads Arriving This Month for European Free-Tier Users

OpenAI Ireland notified Free and Go plan users across the EEA that ads will appear in ChatGPT later in August. Paid tiers (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, Education) remain ad-free. Targeting is contextual only — topic, coarse location, device — with explicit opt-in before chat history informs personalization. The ad-free guarantee for paid tiers establishes a clear data boundary enterprise procurement can contract against.
