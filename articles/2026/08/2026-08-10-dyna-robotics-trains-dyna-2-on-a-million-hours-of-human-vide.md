# Dyna Robotics trains DYNA-2 on a million hours of human video with no robot data

Dyna Robotics introduced DYNA-2, described as the first robot foundation model pre-trained on over one million hours of egocentric human video — roughly 170 years of footage — with no robot-collected training data. The model reports approximately 90% task success. If it holds up, it materially lowers the data-collection barrier that has constrained robotics foundation models.
