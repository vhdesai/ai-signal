# OpenAI Pauses Model Training for Two Weeks Over Safety Concerns

OpenAI took a two-week break from training new AI models, saying it needed time to rethink security measures for risky trial runs. The pause follows Greg Brockman's recent essay warning of an underappreciated cybersecurity tipping point from advancing AI. The decision to halt training — a significant operational and financial commitment — signals that safety concerns are becoming serious enough to override the pressure to ship faster than competitors.
