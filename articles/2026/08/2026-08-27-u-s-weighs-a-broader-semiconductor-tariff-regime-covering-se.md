# U.S. weighs a broader semiconductor tariff regime covering servers and laptops

The administration is considering new tariffs on semiconductors used in the U.S., extended to an expanded range of adjacent hardware including data center servers, laptops, and gaming systems, according to a Politico report relayed by CNBC. The measures are described as early-stage, subject to change, and likely to arrive via a staggered rollout. The White House reiterated that reshoring semiconductor manufacturing is a priority. If enacted, the scope would move tariff exposure beyond chips into the full AI infrastructure bill of materials.
