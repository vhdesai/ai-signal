# OpenAI's Head of Ethics Departs Under a Year After Joining

Chloé Bakalar, OpenAI's head of ethics and reportedly its only dedicated ethicist, has left the company less than a year after joining from Meta, per Financial Times reporting. OpenAI says responsibility for ethical considerations is distributed across multiple teams. The exit lands during heightened scrutiny of frontier safety governance, including the fallout from July's autonomous-agent security incident and the concurrent launch of reduced-refusal cyber models.
