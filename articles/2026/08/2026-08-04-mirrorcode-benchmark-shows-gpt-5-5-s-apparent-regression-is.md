# MirrorCode benchmark shows GPT-5.5's apparent “regression” is an evaluation artifact

The August 2026 MirrorCode leaderboard places Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 first among frontier models at 64%, while GPT-5.5's apparent drop from 44% to 10% turns out to reflect a harder evaluation configuration rather than a real capability loss. The finding is a reminder that headline benchmark scores are highly sensitive to test design and easily misread. For decision-makers, it underscores the need to scrutinize methodology before drawing conclusions from model leaderboards. Academic Research RESEARCH
