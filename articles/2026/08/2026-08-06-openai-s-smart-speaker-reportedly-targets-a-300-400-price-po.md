# OpenAI's smart speaker reportedly targets a $300-$400 price point

TechCrunch reported that OpenAI's first hardware device, developed with Jony Ive's LoveFrom, is expected to be a portable, screenless smart speaker priced between $300 and $400. The device would extend ChatGPT from software into an always-available household interface, but the economics are challenging: smart speakers have historically been difficult to monetize, and a premium price raises adoption risk. The move signals OpenAI's ambition to own more of the AI user interface stack.
