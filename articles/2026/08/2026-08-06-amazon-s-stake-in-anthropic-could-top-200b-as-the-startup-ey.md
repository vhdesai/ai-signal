# Amazon’s stake in Anthropic could top $200B as the startup eyes an October IPO

Anthropic plans to go public in October and is reportedly targeting a valuation above the $965 billion mark set in its May funding round, according to Yahoo Finance. Amazon, an early strategic investor, could see its stake valued at more than $200 billion if the reported figures hold. A listing of that size would be one of the largest AI IPOs to date and a significant liquidity event for its cloud backers. EARNINGS
