# Apple researchers study alignment methods for multimodal LLMs

Apple Machine Learning Research published a study on preference alignment in multimodal large language models, comparing offline and online approaches and introducing Bias-Driven Hallucination Sampling, a preference-data construction method that does not require external annotation or additional models. The item falls in the 24-48 hour window by PDT timing but has strong date evidence and direct relevance to multimodal hallucination control. It is relevant to Apple's broader strategy around safe on-device and multimodal AI. Academic Research ACADEMICAI FOUNDATIONSMIT
