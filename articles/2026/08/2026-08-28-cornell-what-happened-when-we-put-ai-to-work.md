# Cornell: What Happened When We Put AI to Work

Cornell recapped a workshop in which roughly 30 Cornell and Weill Cornell Medicine faculty and staff tested an AI-enabled software development methodology. The recurring finding was that the hardest problems were organizational — goals, standards, governance, and cross-unit collaboration — rather than technical. The team concluded AI’s greatest value may be freeing people for problem-solving rather than replacing expertise.
