# Consumer AI agent Instinct raises $250M Series B at a $2.5B valuation

Instinct, operated by Spear Street Technology and led by 23-year-old founder Noah Shinn, told the Wall Street Journal it closed a $250M Series B co-led by Index Ventures and Benchmark, bringing total funding to $350M at a $2.5B valuation. The product is a personal agent users connect to their apps and devices and interact with by text and phone call. The company is barely a year old and still in private beta. Notably, it is already drawing scrutiny over the breadth of permissions it requests and terms-of-use language that privacy observers have flagged — a live example of the consent surface enterprise buyers should expect to have to govern.
