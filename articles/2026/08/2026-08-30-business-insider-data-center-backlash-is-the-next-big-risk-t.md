# Business Insider: Data Center Backlash Is the Next Big Risk to the AI Trade

Investment professionals say community opposition to data centers — now bipartisan and spreading from local zoning to state politics — could delay or block projects critical to the AI buildout, directly impacting chip demand, hyperscaler capex, and neocloud growth. West Virginia offers a counterpoint: Gov. Morrisey proposed using 50% of data center revenue to eliminate state income tax.
