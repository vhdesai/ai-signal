# Altman warns AI could concentrate in the hands of a few powerful players

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman warned that AI could end up concentrated among a small number of powerful companies, partly because safety fears may push society to trade “a lot of liberty for safety.” He framed the moment as a choice between “AI authoritarianism or liberty” and argued such trade-offs have historically been long-term net losses. Business Insider notes the tension with OpenAI’s own almost entirely closed-weight model strategy.
