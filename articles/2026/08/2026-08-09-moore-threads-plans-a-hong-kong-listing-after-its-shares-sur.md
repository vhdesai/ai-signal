# Moore Threads Plans a Hong Kong Listing After Its Shares Surged 420%

Moore Threads, the Beijing AI chipmaker founded by former Nvidia China executive Zhang Jianzhong, said in a Sunday filing it will pursue a Hong Kong listing at an “appropriate time.” First-half revenue rose 147% to 1.74 billion yuan and net loss narrowed to 11.6 million yuan from 270.9 million, putting the company near break-even. Shares are up more than 420% since its Shanghai STAR debut. Unlike peers shifting to custom ASICs under US export controls, Moore Threads has stayed close to the GPU model as the domestic substitute for hardware Chinese labs can no longer buy.
