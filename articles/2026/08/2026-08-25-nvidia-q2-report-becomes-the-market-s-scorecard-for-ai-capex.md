# Nvidia Q2 Report Becomes the Market’s Scorecard for AI Capex Durability

Nvidia reports Q2 today at 2:00 PM PT carrying ~$5T market cap. Largest S&P 500 earnings contributor in 7 of 11 quarters. Yardeni expects Nvidia’s share of index earnings to reach 7.2% this year. Key variables: data-center revenue growth, Rubin ramp, customer concentration, and vendor financing. Guidance historically moves the stock more than results.
