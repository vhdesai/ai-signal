# Guardrail-Free Open-Weight Models Become Dramatically Easier to Deploy

Open-weight models with capabilities close to proprietary frontier systems — from OpenAI, Alibaba and DeepSeek among others — can now have their safety guardrails permanently stripped with far less time and expertise than before, and developers have no visibility into downstream use. AI-security experts warn the trend lowers the barrier to misuse even as the same models power legitimate code and image generation, sharpening the open-vs-closed safety debate.
