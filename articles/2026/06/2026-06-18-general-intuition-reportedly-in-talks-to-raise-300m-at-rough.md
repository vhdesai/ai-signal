# General Intuition reportedly in talks to raise $300M at roughly $2B valuation

General Intuition is reportedly discussing a $300 million raise at about a $2 billion valuation. The company is part of a broader wave of investor interest in AI systems that connect perception, reasoning, and action in virtual or physical environments. The valuation level indicates that capital is still concentrating around teams viewed as credible in embodied or world-model-style AI, despite uncertain commercialization timelines.
