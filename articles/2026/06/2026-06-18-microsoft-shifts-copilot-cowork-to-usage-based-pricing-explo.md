# Microsoft Shifts Copilot Cowork to Usage-Based Pricing; Explores DeepSeek V4

Microsoft confirmed two significant changes: usage-based billing (citing unsustainable costs from power users) and active exploration of a fine-tuned DeepSeek V4 as a lower-cost alternative. Copilot Cowork reached GA on June 16 with 50%+ of the Fortune 500 already using it.
