# Alibaba Opens Qwen to Third-Party Apps as China’s AI Agent Race Intensifies

Alibaba Cloud unveiled an expanded agentic AI ecosystem built on its Qwen model, opening it to external brands and applications. The move parallels Tencent’s recent WeChat agent plans and signals that China’s consumer AI competition is shifting from model benchmarks to agent-platform integration.
