# Microsoft and Nvidia Unveil Surface RTX Spark Dev Box; Maia 200 Reaches Production

Jensen Huang joined Satya Nadella to announce the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box — a compact desktop delivering up to 1 petaflop with 128 GB of unified memory, capable of running 120B-parameter models locally. Microsoft also confirmed its second-generation Maia 200 accelerator is live in Iowa and Arizona data centers, with Italy, Australia, and South Korea next. The dual push underscores Microsoft's drive to control cost and capacity across both edge and cloud AI compute.
