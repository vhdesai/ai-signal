# OpenAI adds enterprise usage analytics and spend controls

OpenAI introduced usage analytics and updated spend controls for ChatGPT Enterprise. The update is a practical governance move: large customers increasingly need budget guardrails, chargeback visibility, and adoption telemetry before expanding AI usage across functions. For executives, the signal is that enterprise AI purchasing is shifting from experimentation to operational cost management.
