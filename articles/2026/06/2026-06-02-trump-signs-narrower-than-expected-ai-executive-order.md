# Trump Signs Narrower-Than-Expected AI Executive Order

President Trump signed an executive order ("Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security"), with a same-day CFR assessment calling it significantly narrower than earlier drafts. It calls for voluntary pre-release review of frontier models rather than mandatory approvals. Politico characterized it as "tiptoeing toward real AI oversight." Executives with government-facing deployments should track implementing details; OpenAI published a parallel governance blueprint positioning against mandatory model approvals.
