# Noam Shazeer, co-author of the foundational 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper and VP of Engineering co-leading…

Noam Shazeer, co-author of the foundational 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper and VP of Engineering co-leading Google's Gemini models, announced his departure to join OpenAI. Google paid $2.7 billion just two years ago to bring Shazeer back from Character.AI. The move represents one of the largest individual talent shifts in the AI industry's history and strengthens OpenAI's research bench ahead of its planned IPO.
