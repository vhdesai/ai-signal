# OpenAI hired Dean Ball, the former lead author of the Trump White House's AI Action Plan, to head a newly created…

OpenAI hired Dean Ball, the former lead author of the Trump White House's AI Action Plan, to head a newly created "Strategic Futures" team starting July 6. Ball's appointment signals OpenAI's intent to build deep government-relations capacity as it prepares for both its IPO and intensifying regulatory scrutiny. He previously served as a senior fellow at the Foundation for American Innovation.
