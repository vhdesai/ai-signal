# Google Borrows Nvidia's Playbook to Build a Rival AI-Chip Business

Google is backing the Lake Mariner data-center project with a $3.2B guarantee, and the facility will lease TPU capacity to Anthropic. This repositions TPUs as a direct alternative to Nvidia GPUs and a new external revenue stream — the most explicit sign Alphabet intends to monetize custom silicon beyond its own products.
