# Axios: Congress Wants In on the Data Center Backlash

Axios reported that Congress is preparing legislation to address the growing data center backlash, following New York's moratorium and community resistance nationwide. A new bill from Representative Bresnahan would set federal standards for data center siting. The move signals that AI infrastructure permitting is transitioning from a local zoning issue to a federal legislative priority—with implications for buildout timelines across the industry.
