# Tim Cook Warns AI Chip Demand Making Apple Price Increases "Unavoidable"

Surging prices for memory and storage chips, driven by high demand from AI companies, will mean higher prices for Apple devices, Cook told the WSJ: "The situation has become unsustainable." Separately, Intel shares jumped after Trump announced the chipmaker would partner with Apple on U.S.-made chips.
