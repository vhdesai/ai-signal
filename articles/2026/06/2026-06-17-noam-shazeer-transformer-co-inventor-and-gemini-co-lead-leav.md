# Noam Shazeer, Transformer Co-Inventor and Gemini Co-Lead, Leaves Google for OpenAI

Noam Shazeer, VP of Engineering at Google DeepMind and co-lead of the Gemini model line, announced he is joining IPO-bound OpenAI — the highest-profile Google-to-OpenAI talent move of the AI era. Google paid roughly $2.7 billion in 2024 to bring Shazeer back from Character.AI; he departs less than 22 months later. Shazeer co-authored the foundational 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper and will lead architecture research at OpenAI. Gemini 4, targeted for late 2026, now faces a mid-cycle co-lead departure.
