# OpenAI: Small Doses of "Beneficial-Trait" Training Make Models Broadly Safer

Allocating ~5% of RL training to truthfulness, corrigibility, and transparency produced broad alignment gains — beating baselines on 44 of 53 benchmarks with 9.1-point average improvement. Gains generalized out of domain and persisted under adversarial prompting. Suggests RL can be a tool for durable safety, not just an alignment risk.
