# In the Weights launches AI-centric vanity search

TechCrunch covered In the Weights, a consumer-facing tool that scores or surfaces a person's presence in AI systems. The product is early and more cultural than enterprise-critical, but it reflects growing user concern over how AI models represent people, brands, and reputations. This could become relevant for executives tracking AI-era brand visibility, provenance, and reputational monitoring.
