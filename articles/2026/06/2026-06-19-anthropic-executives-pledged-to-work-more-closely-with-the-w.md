# Anthropic executives pledged to work more closely with the White House in a formal proposal to Commerce Secretary…

Anthropic executives pledged to work more closely with the White House in a formal proposal to Commerce Secretary Lutnick, offering enhanced security protocols and government oversight mechanisms in exchange for lifting the ban on its most powerful models. The proposal comes after week-long negotiations between Anthropic senior staff and Commerce officials failed to produce a resolution.
