# DuckDuckGo's "No-AI" Search Engine Sees Traffic Boom

DuckDuckGo made its AI-free search experience easier to set as a default, reporting a surge in traffic—U.S. installs up 18% post-Google I/O. The trend underscores a growing counter-movement among users who prefer traditional search results without AI-generated summaries or answers. For platform strategists, it's a data point on the limits of AI-first search: a non-trivial user segment actively opts out.
