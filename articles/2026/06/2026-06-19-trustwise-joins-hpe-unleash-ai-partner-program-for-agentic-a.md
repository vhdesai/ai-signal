# Trustwise joins HPE Unleash AI partner program for agentic AI runtime control

Trustwise announced it has joined HPE’s Unleash AI partner program to bring runtime control capabilities for agentic AI to enterprise customers. The announcement is vendor-driven, but the theme is strategically relevant: enterprises are looking for controls that operate during AI execution, not only during model selection or prompt design. Runtime governance is likely to become a buying criterion as agents gain access to tools, data, and workflows.
