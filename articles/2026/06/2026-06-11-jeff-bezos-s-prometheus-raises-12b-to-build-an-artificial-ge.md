# Jeff Bezos's Prometheus Raises $12B to Build an "Artificial General Engineer"

Bezos-backed Prometheus raised $12 billion — the largest AI startup round ever — to build autonomous systems for designing, building, and managing physical infrastructure. The raise surpasses DeepSeek's $7.4B and positions Prometheus at the intersection of AI and physical engineering, a category distinct from language models but potentially larger in economic impact.
