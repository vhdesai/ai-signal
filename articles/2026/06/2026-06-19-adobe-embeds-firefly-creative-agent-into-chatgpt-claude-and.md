# Adobe Embeds Firefly Creative Agent into ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini

Adobe's AI "Creative Agent" now lets users invoke it from third-party assistants including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Adobe stresses that Firefly-native output remains commercially safe, but content routed through third-party models may not carry the same indemnification. Positions Adobe as an interoperable creative layer rather than a closed suite.
