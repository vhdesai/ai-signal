# Baseten reportedly raising $1.5B as inference demand remains strong

Baseten is reportedly close to a $1.5 billion round at a $13 billion valuation. The report underscores how investor attention has moved from model training alone to inference infrastructure, where production workloads, latency, and cost optimization determine AI economics. The valuation also indicates that the market continues to price scarce inference capacity and tooling aggressively.
