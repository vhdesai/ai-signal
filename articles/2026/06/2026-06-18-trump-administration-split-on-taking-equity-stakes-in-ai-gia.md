# Trump Administration Split on Taking Equity Stakes in AI Giants

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has reportedly favored distributing equity stakes in leading AI companies to Trump Accounts, while Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick prefers creating a sovereign wealth fund. Senator Bernie Sanders has drafted legislation for a sovereign fund approach. The debate reflects Washington's growing recognition that frontier AI companies represent strategic national assets — and uncertainty about how to structure the government's relationship with them.
