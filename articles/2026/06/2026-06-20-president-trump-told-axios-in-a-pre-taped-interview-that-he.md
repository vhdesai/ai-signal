# President Trump told Axios in a pre-taped interview that he no longer considers Anthropic a national security threat,…

President Trump told Axios in a pre-taped interview that he no longer considers Anthropic a national security threat, two days after meeting CEO Dario Amodei at a G7 lunch. Trump said Anthropic has "behaved very responsibly," signaling a potential path to restoring access to the disabled Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models. The shift comes after a week of intense lobbying by Anthropic and bipartisan Congressional pressure on Commerce Secretary Lutnick.
