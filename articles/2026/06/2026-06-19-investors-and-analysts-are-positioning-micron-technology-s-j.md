# Investors and analysts are positioning Micron Technology's June 24 earnings report as a key indicator of whether…

Investors and analysts are positioning Micron Technology's June 24 earnings report as a key indicator of whether AI-driven chip demand can sustain the broader market rally. Deutsche Bank raised its price target to $1,500 (47% upside), citing an unusually long DRAM upcycle now at 12 quarters. Micron stock has surged approximately 250% in 2026 on the back of insatiable AI memory demand from hyperscalers.
