# OpenAI and Anthropic Jointly Urge Congress to Regulate Synthetic DNA

In a rare joint action, OpenAI and Anthropic CEOs signed a letter to Congress urging legislation to regulate synthetic DNA purchases, warning that advancing AI capabilities are lowering the technical barriers to biological weapons development. The WSJ reported the letter as an exclusive — the first time the two leading frontier labs have coordinated on a specific legislative ask. The initiative shifts the AI safety conversation from model behavior toward downstream misuse in the physical world.
