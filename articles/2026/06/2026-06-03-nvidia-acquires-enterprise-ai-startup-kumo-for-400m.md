# Nvidia Acquires Enterprise AI Startup Kumo for $400M+

Nvidia acquired Kumo AI, a five-year-old startup that sells predictive AI software to enterprises, for more than $400 million. The acquisition deepens Nvidia's push beyond chips into enterprise software, positioning it to offer integrated hardware-software AI solutions.
