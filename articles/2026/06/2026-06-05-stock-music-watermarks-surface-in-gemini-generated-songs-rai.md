# Stock-Music Watermarks Surface in Gemini-Generated Songs, Raising Training-Data Questions

Testers reported that songs generated by Google's Gemini app (via DeepMind's Lyria model) included the spoken phrase "purchase your track today" — an audio watermark that stock-music marketplaces embed in free preview tracks. The appearance suggests unlicensed preview audio may have entered Lyria's training data. The finding adds to mounting IP and provenance scrutiny of generative-audio systems.
