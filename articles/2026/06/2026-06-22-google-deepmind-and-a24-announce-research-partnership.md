# Google DeepMind and A24 announce research partnership

Google said Google DeepMind and A24 are forming a research partnership focused on AI and creative production. The significance is that model labs are moving from generic content-generation demos into domain-specific collaborations where workflow, rights, quality control, and production economics can be studied in context.
