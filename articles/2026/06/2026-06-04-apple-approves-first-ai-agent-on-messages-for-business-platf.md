# Apple Approves First AI Agent on Messages for Business Platform

Apple approved Poke as the first AI agent on its Messages for Business platform, enabling businesses to deploy conversational AI agents within iMessage. The approval signals a strategic shift — Apple is opening its walled garden to third-party AI agents, directly competing with Meta's WhatsApp Business Agent launched earlier this week.
