# U.S. Bank Regulators Ramp Up Scrutiny of AI Use at Financial Companies

Reuters reported exclusively that U.S. bank regulators are ramping up scrutiny of AI use at financial institutions, following last week's global financial watchdog call for tighter controls on agentic AI. The increased regulatory attention adds compliance overhead for financial firms deploying AI agents in trading, lending, and customer service—and could slow enterprise AI adoption in the sector where agent deployment is most advanced.
