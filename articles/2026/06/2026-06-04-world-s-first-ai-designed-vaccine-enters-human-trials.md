# World's First AI-Designed Vaccine Enters Human Trials

The BBC reported that a "world-first" vaccine designed entirely by artificial intelligence has entered human clinical trials. The AI-designed candidate targets a universal approach to respiratory pathogens. If successful, it would validate AI-driven drug design as a practical path to clinical deployment—not just a research tool—and could significantly compress the timeline from pathogen identification to vaccine candidate.
