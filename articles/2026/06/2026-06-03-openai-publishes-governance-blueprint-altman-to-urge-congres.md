# OpenAI Publishes Governance Blueprint, Altman to Urge Congress Against Mandatory Model Approvals

OpenAI published "A Blueprint for Democratic Governance of Frontier AI," proposing a federal regulatory framework while positioning against mandatory pre-release model approvals. Politico noted the blueprint diverges from the Trump administration's recently signed executive order. The paper advocates for a single federal AI regime rather than a patchwork of state laws.
