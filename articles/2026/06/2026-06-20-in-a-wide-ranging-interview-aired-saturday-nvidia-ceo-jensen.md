# In a wide-ranging interview aired Saturday, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang compared AI's impact on the workforce to the…

In a wide-ranging interview aired Saturday, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang compared AI's impact on the workforce to the Industrial Revolution, arguing it will create new categories of jobs while transforming existing ones. He emphasized the US "should absolutely lead" in AI development and reiterated his VivaTech thesis that physical AI--robots and autonomous systems--represents the next major compute wave.
