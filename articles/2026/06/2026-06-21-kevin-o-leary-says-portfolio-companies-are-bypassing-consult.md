# Kevin O'Leary says portfolio companies are bypassing consultants for AI

Business Insider reported comments from investor Kevin O'Leary that companies he backs are using AI directly instead of traditional consulting firms. The quote should be treated as anecdotal, but it reflects a broader pressure on advisory and services models as executives test AI for analysis, operations, and decision support. The likely near-term impact is not wholesale replacement but margin pressure and demand for consulting firms to bundle AI-enabled delivery.
