# Meta Launches Business Agent for WhatsApp Globally

Meta launched its AI-powered Business Agent for WhatsApp globally, enabling businesses to automate customer interactions — from answering questions to closing sales — via conversational AI. More than one million businesses already use a Business Agent across WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram. With 2+ billion WhatsApp users, Meta can onboard enterprises into agentic AI without requiring new platform adoption.
