# Google invests in A24 to develop AI-powered filmmaking tools

Variety reported that Google is investing $75 million in A24 to develop AI-powered filmmaking tools, adding a prominent media partner to Google’s broader push around creative AI. The deal is notable less for near-term revenue than for distribution and legitimacy: premium content partners can help normalize AI-assisted production while testing where studios, creators, and audiences draw boundaries.
