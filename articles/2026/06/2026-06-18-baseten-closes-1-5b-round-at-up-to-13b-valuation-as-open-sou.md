# Baseten Closes $1.5B Round at Up to $13B Valuation as Open-Source Inference Demand Surges

AI inference infrastructure startup Baseten is finalizing a $1.5 billion dual-tiered raise at $11B–$13B valuation — more than doubling its $5B valuation from three months ago. Co-led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction, Spark Capital, Sands Capital, and Wellington Management; customers include Cursor, Mercor, and OpenEvidence. One customer reportedly completed a specific task at 30% of the cost of a proprietary alternative. The raise is a direct structural consequence of the Fable 5 crisis and broader enterprise closed-model anxiety. Tags: BENCHMARK
