# Microsoft confirmed two significant Copilot Cowork changes in the same week: a shift from flat-rate to usage-based…

Microsoft confirmed two significant Copilot Cowork changes in the same week: a shift from flat-rate to usage-based billing (citing unsustainable compute costs from power users), and active exploration of a fine-tuned, self-hosted DeepSeek V4 as a lower-cost alternative to OpenAI and Anthropic models. The disclosure signals that enterprise AI token economics are becoming a binding constraint even for the largest platform companies. Copilot Cowork reached general availability on June 16 with over half the Fortune 500 already using it.
