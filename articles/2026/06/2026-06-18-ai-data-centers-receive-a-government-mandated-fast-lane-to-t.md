# AI data centers receive a government-mandated fast lane to the grid

A new government-mandated process is expected to accelerate grid connections for AI data centers. The change addresses one of the industry’s biggest non-chip constraints: power availability and interconnection delays. For infrastructure planning, this suggests policy is beginning to adapt to AI-scale energy demand, but it may also intensify local and regional debates over power allocation.
