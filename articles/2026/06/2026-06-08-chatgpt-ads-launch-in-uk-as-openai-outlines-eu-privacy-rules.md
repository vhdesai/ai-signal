# ChatGPT Ads Launch in UK as OpenAI Outlines EU Privacy Rules

OpenAI began serving ads in ChatGPT in the UK — its first major international ad expansion — while publishing EU-specific privacy rules. The move signals OpenAI is rapidly building its ad business ahead of IPO, creating a third monetization pathway beyond subscriptions and API revenue.
