# IBM and Google Cloud Announce Strategic AI Partnership

IBM and Google Cloud announced a strategic partnership, with Google tapping thousands of IBM consultants to bring Gemini AI to enterprise customers. The deal pairs Google's model capabilities with IBM's delivery infrastructure—targeting organizations that need hands-on implementation support rather than self-service AI tools.
