# Deepfake expert says AI is making authenticity harder for ordinary users

Business Insider interviewed digital forensics expert Hany Farid, who said AI-generated images and video are making it increasingly difficult for ordinary people to know what is real. The strategic risk is not only fraud; it is erosion of trust in digital evidence, communications, and public information. Organizations should assume deepfake readiness is now part of communications, security, and incident-response planning.
