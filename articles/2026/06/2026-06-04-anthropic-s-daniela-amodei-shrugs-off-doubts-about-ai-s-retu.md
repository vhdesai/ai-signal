# Anthropic's Daniela Amodei Shrugs Off Doubts About AI's Returns Ahead of IPO

Anthropic president Daniela Amodei pushed back on growing skepticism about AI investment returns, arguing the industry is in early innings and enterprise adoption is accelerating. Her remarks directly counter the "AI sticker shock" narrative that gained traction as companies like Uber cap AI budgets — an important frame as Anthropic prepares its landmark IPO.
