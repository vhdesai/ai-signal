# Snap spins off AI video team into Dotmo due to costs

Snap is spinning off an AI video team into a new company, Dotmo, with cost pressure cited as a driver. The move highlights a recurring issue for consumer AI features: compelling generation capabilities can carry infrastructure costs that are difficult to absorb inside ad-supported products. The strategic read is that companies may separate AI-heavy product bets from core operations until unit economics become clearer.
