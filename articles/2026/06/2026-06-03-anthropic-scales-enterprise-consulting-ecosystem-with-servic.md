# Anthropic Scales Enterprise Consulting Ecosystem with Services Track and Partner Hub

Anthropic added a tiered Services Track and public Partner Hub to its Claude Partner Network, which has drawn 40,000+ firm applications and 10,000+ certified consultants since March. It cited large-scale rollouts at Accenture (30,000 trained), Cognizant (~350,000), Deloitte (470,000), KPMG (276,000+), and PwC. The structure aims to convert successful pilots into production deployments through vetted integration partners—the stickier layer of enterprise AI adoption.
