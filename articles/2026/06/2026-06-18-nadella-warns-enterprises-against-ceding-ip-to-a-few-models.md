# Nadella Warns Enterprises Against Ceding IP to "a Few Models That Eat Everything They See"

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella posted on X that "the last thing any of us want is a world where every company across every sector is ceding value to a few models that eat everything they see," calling on enterprises to build agentic systems that retain their own IP. Delivered one week after the Fable 5 shutdown, the statement reads as a direct warning about closed-model dependency — and a push toward Microsoft's Azure-hosted open-model alternatives.
