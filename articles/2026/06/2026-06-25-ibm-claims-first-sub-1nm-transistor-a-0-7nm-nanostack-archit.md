# IBM claims first sub-1nm transistor — a 0.7nm "nanostack" architecture

IBM said its Albany research lab produced the first chip technology to operate below 1 nanometer — a 0.7nm (7-angstrom) transistor built in three dimensions via a "nanostack" architecture, packing ~100 billion transistors onto a fingernail-sized area. Though IBM exited chip manufacturing years ago, the result positions its research ahead of TSMC, Samsung and Intel on transistor scaling and points to a longer runway for compute density as AI demand surges.
