# SpaceX Officially Prices $75B IPO at $135/Share; Market Debut Today

SpaceX priced shares at $135 in the largest IPO in history, raising $75B at $1.75T. Early crypto-traded contracts initially fell before recovering toward $2.4T. Fortune reports some analysts see the stock worth half Musk's price. The xAI AI division remains central to the valuation thesis despite trailing frontier labs.
