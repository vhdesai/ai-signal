# The Trump administration's Commerce Department order forcing Anthropic to disable Fable 5 and Mythos 5 relies on an…

The Trump administration's Commerce Department order forcing Anthropic to disable Fable 5 and Mythos 5 relies on an unprecedented interpretation of export control law applied to AI model weights. Bloomberg reports the order asserts federal authority to restrict who can access frontier models--even domestically--raising legal questions about whether software can be treated like physical munitions under existing frameworks. The case could set binding precedent for how all frontier labs operate.
