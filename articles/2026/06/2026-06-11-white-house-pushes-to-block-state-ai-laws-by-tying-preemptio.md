# White House Pushes to Block State AI Laws by Tying Preemption to Kids' Safety

Politico reported that the White House is framing its push to preempt state AI laws around children's safety—a politically popular angle that could overcome Republican resistance to federal preemption. Axios covered a parallel congressional push, with a new bill specifically targeting data center regulation. For enterprises, the strategy creates a possible pathway to federal uniformity, but the kids' safety framing may introduce its own compliance obligations.
