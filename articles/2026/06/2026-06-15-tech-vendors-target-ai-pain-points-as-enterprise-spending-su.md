# Tech Vendors Target AI Pain Points as Enterprise Spending Surges

CIO Dive reported that tech vendors are increasingly targeting specific AI deployment pain points — observability, cost management, governance, and agent reliability — rather than competing on model capability. The shift validates that the enterprise AI stack is maturing beyond model selection into operational infrastructure.
