# Google to Appeal German Ruling Holding It Liable for AI Overviews

Google will appeal a Munich court ruling holding it directly liable for content in AI-generated search overviews, after the system falsely linked publishers to fraud. An early test of who bears liability for AI-synthesized answers in the EU.
