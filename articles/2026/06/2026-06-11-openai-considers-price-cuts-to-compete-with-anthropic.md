# OpenAI Considers Price Cuts to Compete with Anthropic

The WSJ reported that OpenAI is considering price cuts to compete with Anthropic, whose Claude Fable 5 release has intensified the model capability race. The report adds pricing pressure from above (Google's subscription cuts) and below (DeepSeek) to the competitive dynamics. For enterprise buyers, the pricing convergence could finally provide the leverage they've been seeking against usage-based cost escalation.
