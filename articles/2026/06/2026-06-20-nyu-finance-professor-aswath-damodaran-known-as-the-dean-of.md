# NYU finance professor Aswath Damodaran, known as the "Dean of Valuation," warned that the AI investment boom carries…

NYU finance professor Aswath Damodaran, known as the "Dean of Valuation," warned that the AI investment boom carries greater systemic risk than the dot-com crash because it is fueled by massive debt-funded infrastructure spending rather than the dot-com era's light-asset model. When the correction comes, he argues, the pain will extend beyond equity holders to lenders and the broader economy. He also cautioned that the rush to include SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic in the S&P 500 could reshape passive investing.
