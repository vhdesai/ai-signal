# Warner Music Acquires AI Attribution Startup Sureel AI

Warner Music acquired Sureel AI, a startup focused on AI-generated content attribution. The deal positions Warner to track and monetize AI-generated music using its copyrighted material — moving rights holders from litigation to infrastructure as their primary AI strategy.
