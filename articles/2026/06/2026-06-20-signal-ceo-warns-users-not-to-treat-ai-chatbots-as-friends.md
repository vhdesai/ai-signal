# Signal CEO warns users not to treat AI chatbots as friends

TechCrunch reported Signal president Meredith Whittaker's warning that users should not treat AI chatbots as friends or sentient counterparts. The comments are part of a broader safety and product-design debate about anthropomorphism, emotional dependency, and privacy in consumer AI systems. For executives, the issue is relevant to trust and liability: assistant UX choices can create user expectations that exceed technical reality.
