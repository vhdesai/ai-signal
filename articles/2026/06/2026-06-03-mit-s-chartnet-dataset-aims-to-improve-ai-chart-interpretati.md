# MIT's ChartNet Dataset Aims to Improve AI Chart Interpretation

MIT researchers introduced ChartNet, a training dataset designed to improve vision-language model accuracy on charts and scientific figures—a persistent weakness for multimodal models analyzing business and technical visuals. Increasingly relevant for enterprise analytics and research workflows.
