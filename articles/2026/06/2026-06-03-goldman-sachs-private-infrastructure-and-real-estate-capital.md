# Goldman Sachs: Private Infrastructure and Real Estate Capital to Finance AI Data Center Boom

Goldman Sachs published analysis projecting that private infrastructure and real estate capital will play an increasingly central role in financing the AI data center buildout, as traditional tech balance sheets prove insufficient. The report aligns with Alphabet's $80B capital raise and the broader trend of sovereign wealth funds, pension funds, and private equity entering the AI infrastructure stack—fundamentally changing who owns and controls AI compute.
