# Bipartisan "Great American AI Act" Draft Proposes Federal Governance Framework, Would Preempt State Laws

The House released a bipartisan draft of the "Great American AI Act," proposing a new federal AI governance framework that would preempt state AI laws for three years. Politico noted the bill reflects growing congressional consensus that a patchwork of state regulations is unworkable for the industry. The draft creates federal standards for high-risk AI systems while establishing safe harbors for compliant companies. For enterprises, it could provide regulatory clarity—but the three-year preemption clock means states could reimpose their own rules if Congress doesn't pass permanent legislation.
