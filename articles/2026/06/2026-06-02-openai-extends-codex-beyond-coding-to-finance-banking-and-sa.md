# OpenAI Extends Codex Beyond Coding to Finance, Banking, and Sales

OpenAI unveiled six new Codex agent plugins targeting finance, banking, and sales — with legal and corporate-finance capabilities planned — and said Codex will merge into ChatGPT "in the next few weeks." The expansion converts Codex from a developer-only tool into a general enterprise productivity surface. For enterprises standardized on ChatGPT, this lowers the adoption barrier to agentic workflows.
