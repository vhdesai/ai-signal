# OpenAI Says China Launched Influence Campaign to Shape U.S. Attitudes on AI Data Centers

OpenAI publicly accused China of launching an influence campaign to shape American attitudes toward AI data centers, according to E&E News. The claim follows earlier reports from Axios about Chinese-linked opposition to U.S. data center construction. OpenAI's direct public accusation—naming China specifically—escalates the narrative from industry lobbying to a national-security framing that could accelerate permitting reform.
