# Tencent Shares Jump 10% on AI Agent Plans for WeChat

Tencent shares rose over 10% after the company disclosed plans to test AI agent prototypes within WeChat, its messaging super-app with over a billion users. The announcement positions Tencent to embed agentic AI into one of the world's largest mobile ecosystems. For enterprise observers, the move is significant: WeChat's integration of AI agents could set the template for how Asian super-apps deploy autonomous AI at scale.
