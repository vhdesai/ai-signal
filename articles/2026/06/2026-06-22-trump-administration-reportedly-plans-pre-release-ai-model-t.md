# Trump administration reportedly plans pre-release AI model testing under cybersecurity order

MSN reported that the Trump administration is preparing to test AI models before public release under a new cybersecurity order. If implemented, mandatory pre-release testing would materially change launch timelines and compliance burdens for frontier labs, especially if government access, benchmark selection, and remediation requirements become conditions for deployment.
