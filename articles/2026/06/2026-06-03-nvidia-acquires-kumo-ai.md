# Nvidia Acquires Kumo AI

Nvidia acquired Kumo AI, a graph neural network and predictive analytics startup. The deal extends Nvidia's AI software portfolio beyond training and inference into enterprise predictive analytics, complementing its hardware dominance with application-layer capabilities.
