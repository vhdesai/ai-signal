# xAI Fired Engineer Who Raised Grok Safety Alarms, New Lawsuit Claims

A former xAI engineer filed a lawsuit claiming he was fired for raising safety concerns about Grok's outputs. TechCrunch reported the case adds to the growing pattern of safety-related whistleblower actions at AI companies—following similar incidents at OpenAI and Google. The lawsuit lands as SpaceX (which owns xAI) begins trading publicly, exposing the company to reputational and legal risk at maximum visibility.
