# Transformer Co-Inventor Noam Shazeer Leaves Google Gemini for OpenAI

Shazeer, co-author of "Attention Is All You Need" and VP Engineering co-leading Gemini, is joining OpenAI. Google paid $2.7B in 2024 to bring him back from Character.AI; he departs 22 months later. The highest-profile Google-to-OpenAI talent move of the AI era, strengthening OpenAI's research bench ahead of IPO.
