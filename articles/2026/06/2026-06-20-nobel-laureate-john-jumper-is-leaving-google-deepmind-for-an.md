# Nobel laureate John Jumper is leaving Google DeepMind for Anthropic

TechCrunch reported that Nobel laureate John Jumper, known for his work on AlphaFold, is leaving Google DeepMind for Anthropic. The move is strategically meaningful because it transfers one of DeepMind's most visible scientific AI leaders to a direct frontier-lab competitor. For executives, it underscores that AI leadership is increasingly defined by scarce research talent, not only compute access or distribution.
