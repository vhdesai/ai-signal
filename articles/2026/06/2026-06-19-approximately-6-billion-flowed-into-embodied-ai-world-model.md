# Approximately $6 billion flowed into embodied AI world-model companies in Q1 2026 alone, fueled by NVIDIA's Cosmos 3…

Approximately $6 billion flowed into embodied AI world-model companies in Q1 2026 alone, fueled by NVIDIA's Cosmos 3 omnimodel release and the "Great Parallel" thesis from NVIDIA's Jim Fan. However, a new analysis from Fusion Fund argues the LLM scaling analogy breaks down for physical AI because the real world lacks a universal training unit equivalent to text tokens--suggesting billions in investment may be chasing architectures that will not converge as transformers did for language.
