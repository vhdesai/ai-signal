# DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta Expand Research into Machine Consciousness and AI Welfare

The Financial Times reported that Google DeepMind, Anthropic, and Meta are expanding formal research programs into machine consciousness and AI welfare—investigating whether frontier AI systems could develop states that warrant moral consideration. While the research remains preliminary, the fact that the three leading labs are investing in this area signals a shift from purely capability-focused R&D toward foundational questions about the nature of AI cognition.
