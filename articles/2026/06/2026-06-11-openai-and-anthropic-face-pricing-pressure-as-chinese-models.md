# OpenAI and Anthropic Face Pricing Pressure as Chinese Models Undercut Costs by Up to 9x

Multiple outlets reported that Chinese AI models—led by DeepSeek and Kimi—are undercutting OpenAI and Anthropic pricing by up to 9x, with Decrypt asking "Is OpenAI proving DeepSeek right?" by pursuing a price war with Anthropic. Euronews questioned whether Anthropic's Fable 5 is worth the price if OpenAI becomes cheaper. The pricing convergence validates the "Tokenpocalypse" thesis: frontier model economics are being repriced downward from both competitive and cost-structure pressures.
