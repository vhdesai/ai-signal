# U.S. says ASML’s top chip tool may be in China; ASML disputes it

U.S. officials reportedly suggested that ASML’s most advanced chipmaking tool may be in China, while ASML says it is not. The story reflects continuing uncertainty and enforcement complexity around advanced semiconductor export controls. For AI supply chains, the issue matters because lithography access remains one of the hardest chokepoints in leading-edge accelerator production.
