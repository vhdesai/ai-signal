# Trump Administration at War with Itself Over AI Regulation

Wired published an investigation revealing deep internal divisions within the Trump administration over AI regulation. The piece details competing factions—pro-innovation deregulators versus national-security hawks favoring guardrails—creating policy paralysis at a critical moment for the industry. For enterprises planning compliance strategy, the takeaway is that federal AI regulation remains unpredictable, reinforcing the importance of state-level and voluntary frameworks.
