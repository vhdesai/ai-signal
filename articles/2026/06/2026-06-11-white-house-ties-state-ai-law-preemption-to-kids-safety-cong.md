# White House Ties State AI Law Preemption to Kids' Safety; Congress Targets Data Centers

The White House is framing its push to preempt state AI laws around children's safety — a politically popular angle to overcome Republican resistance. Separately, Congress is preparing a bill setting federal standards for data center siting, transitioning AI infrastructure permitting from local zoning to federal legislative priority.
