# OpenAI Hires Former White House AI Strategist Dean Ball

Dean Ball, former lead author of the Trump White House AI Action Plan, will head a newly created "Strategic Futures" team starting July 6. Signals OpenAI's intent to build deep government-relations capacity as it prepares for IPO and regulatory scrutiny.
