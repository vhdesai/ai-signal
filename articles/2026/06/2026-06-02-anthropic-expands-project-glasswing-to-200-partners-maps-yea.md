# Anthropic Expands Project Glasswing to ~200 Partners; Maps Year of AI Cyberattacks onto MITRE ATT&CK

Anthropic widened Project Glasswing—its restricted defensive-security program powered by Claude Mythos—to roughly 200 partner organizations across 15+ countries, including Okta, Samsung, SK Hynix, NATO, and ENISA. Partners have surfaced 10,000+ high- or critical-severity flaws. Separately, Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published an analysis of 832 banned accounts mapped onto MITRE ATT&CK, concluding that AI-enabled attacks are becoming more autonomous as models chain multiple operational steps. Portions appeared in Verizon's 2026 DBIR.
