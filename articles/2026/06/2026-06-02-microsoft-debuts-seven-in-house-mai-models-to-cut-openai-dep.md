# Microsoft Debuts Seven In-House MAI Models to Cut OpenAI Dependence

At Build 2026, Microsoft unveiled a family of seven first-party "MAI" models, led by MAI-Thinking-1 — its first reasoning model (a 35B active-parameter mixture-of-experts with a 256K context window) — and MAI-Code-1-Flash, a 5B coding model rolling out in GitHub Copilot and VS Code. Microsoft says MAI-Thinking-1 reaches human-preference parity with Claude Sonnet 4.6, matches Opus 4.6 on SWE-Bench Pro, and undercuts GPT-5.5 on cost. MAI models tuned for McKinsey delivered ~10× better cost efficiency than GPT-5.5. The move marks Microsoft's strategic pivot from reselling OpenAI to "participating at the frontier."
