# Elastic reportedly agrees to buy Deductive AI for up to $85M

Elastic has reportedly agreed to acquire Deductive AI, a startup applying AI to structured and unstructured enterprise data. The deal fits the pattern of search, observability, and data-platform vendors buying AI-native capabilities rather than building every layer internally. For buyers, it reinforces that AI search and data reasoning features are becoming core platform differentiators, not standalone add-ons.
