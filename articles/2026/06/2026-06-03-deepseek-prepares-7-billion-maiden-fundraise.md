# DeepSeek Prepares $7 Billion Maiden Fundraise

Reuters reported that DeepSeek is preparing to raise approximately $7 billion in its first external funding round. The Chinese AI lab—which gained attention earlier this year for training competitive models at a fraction of Western costs—would use the capital to scale infrastructure and model development. The round, if completed, would make DeepSeek one of the best-funded AI startups globally and intensify the U.S.–China frontier model competition.
