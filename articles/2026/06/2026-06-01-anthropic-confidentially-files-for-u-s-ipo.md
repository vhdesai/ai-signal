# Anthropic Confidentially Files for U.S. IPO

Anthropic submitted a confidential draft S-1 to the SEC, setting the stage for one of the most consequential AI listings to date and edging ahead of rival OpenAI, which is preparing its own filing. The move follows a $65 billion raise that lifted the company's valuation to roughly $965 billion, with an annualized revenue run rate reported at $4.7 billion. A confidential submission lets Anthropic advance preparations while shielding financials, with no fixed timeline to trade. The filing cements the transition of frontier AI labs from private capital to public accountability.
