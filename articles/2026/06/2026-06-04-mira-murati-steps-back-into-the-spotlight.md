# Mira Murati Steps Back into the Spotlight

Former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati made her first significant public appearances since departing OpenAI, with TechCrunch noting she is proceeding "carefully." While details of her next venture remain limited, Murati's re-emergence is closely watched given her central role in OpenAI's technical direction and the expectation she will launch a competing AI venture.
