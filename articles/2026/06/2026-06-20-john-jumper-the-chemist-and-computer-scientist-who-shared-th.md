# John Jumper, the chemist and computer scientist who shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for developing AlphaFold,…

John Jumper, the chemist and computer scientist who shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for developing AlphaFold, announced he is leaving Google DeepMind after nearly nine years to join Anthropic. He is the third senior DeepMind researcher to depart in as many months, underscoring the escalating AI talent war. DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis thanked Jumper publicly, calling his AlphaFold work world-changing. The move follows Noam Shazeer's departure to OpenAI just two days earlier.
