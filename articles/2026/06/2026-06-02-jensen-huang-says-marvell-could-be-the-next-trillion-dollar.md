# Jensen Huang Says Marvell Could Be the Next Trillion-Dollar Company; Stock Surges 25%

During his COMPUTEX keynote, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang singled out Marvell Technology as a potential next trillion-dollar company, citing its custom silicon capabilities for AI infrastructure. Marvell shares surged approximately 25% on the remarks. The endorsement highlights the strategic value of custom ASIC design as hyperscalers seek alternatives to general-purpose GPUs for specific AI workloads.
