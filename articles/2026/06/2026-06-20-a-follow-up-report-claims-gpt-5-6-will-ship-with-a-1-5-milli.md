# A follow-up report claims GPT-5.6 will ship with a 1.5 million token context window (up from GPT-5.5's 500K), stronger…

A follow-up report claims GPT-5.6 will ship with a 1.5 million token context window (up from GPT-5.5's 500K), stronger reasoning capabilities, and improved coding performance. OpenAI's chief scientist has reportedly described the upgrade as a "meaningful leap." The timing would coincide with Anthropic's Fable 5 still being offline, giving OpenAI a window of reduced competition at the frontier.
