# Anthropic Maps a Year of AI-Enabled Cyberattacks onto MITRE ATT&CK

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team analyzed 832 accounts banned for malicious cyber activity between March 2025 and March 2026, mapping them against the MITRE ATT&CK framework. Key finding: threat actors increasingly deploy AI in the later, more complex stages of operations, and attacks are becoming more autonomous as models chain multiple steps together. Portions appeared in Verizon's 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report.
