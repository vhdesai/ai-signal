# Open-Source AI Stocks Surge as Fable 5 Ban Spotlights Closed-Model Risk

Chinese open-source AI companies MiniMax and Zhipu (Z.ai) surged as enterprises globally reassessed single-vendor AI strategies following the Anthropic Fable 5 shutdown. Zhipu simultaneously launched GLM-5.2, a 1M-token context window frontier model with MIT-licensed open weights targeting the gap Fable 5 left. The episode is accelerating a structural shift toward hybrid open/closed model portfolios. Tags: INDUSTRY
