# xAI Asks Court to Strip Alleged Grok Deepfake Nudes Victims of Anonymity

xAI filed a motion requiring alleged victims of Grok-generated deepfake nude images to use their real names in legal proceedings. The request is unusual in intimate-image-abuse cases and has drawn criticism from digital-rights advocates. The case has implications for how AI companies approach litigation involving generative model harms.
