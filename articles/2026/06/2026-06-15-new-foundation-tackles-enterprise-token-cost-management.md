# New Foundation Tackles Enterprise Token Cost Management

CIO Dive reported on a new industry foundation formed to address the "Tokenpocalypse" — enterprise AI token costs that have consistently exceeded budgets. The initiative aims to create standards for token consumption measurement, benchmarking, and cost optimization across multi-model deployments.
