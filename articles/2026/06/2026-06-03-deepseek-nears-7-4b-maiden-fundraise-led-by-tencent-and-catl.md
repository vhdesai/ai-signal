# DeepSeek Nears ~$7.4B Maiden Fundraise Led by Tencent and CATL

DeepSeek is close to finalizing ~50 billion yuan (~$7.4B) in one of China's largest-ever startup financings, with Tencent and battery maker CATL as the two largest investors and the state-backed National AI fund participating. CATL's involvement is notable — suggesting Chinese industrial conglomerates see AI as strategically adjacent to their core businesses. The round arms the model lab with capital to compete with U.S. frontier labs on compute.
