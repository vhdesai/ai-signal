# Market analysts continue to assess SpaceX's $60 billion all-stock acquisition of Anysphere (Cursor), announced June 16…

Market analysts continue to assess SpaceX's $60 billion all-stock acquisition of Anysphere (Cursor), announced June 16 shortly after SpaceX's record $75B IPO. MarketWatch reports the deal positions SpaceX to compete directly with Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex in the AI-assisted software development market. Seeking Alpha issued a rating downgrade on SpaceX stock citing integration risks and premium valuation.
