# Anthropic Confidentially Files S-1 for Landmark IPO

Anthropic filed a confidential IPO prospectus with the SEC, setting the stage for what could be the largest technology IPO in history. The company is eyeing an October listing at a valuation approaching $1 trillion. CFO Krishna Rao is leading the preparation, with Axios reporting that enterprise spending scrutiny and "AI sticker shock" may test investor appetite. The filing cements the transition of frontier AI labs from private capital markets to public accountability.
