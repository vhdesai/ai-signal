# OpenAI Expands Codex Agent Beyond Software to Finance, Banking, and Sales

OpenAI unveiled plugins extending its Codex agent to non-engineering roles—including public-equity investment, banking, and sales—with legal and corporate-finance capabilities planned. The company also intends to integrate Codex into ChatGPT broadly "in the next few weeks." The expansion directly targets Anthropic for enterprise customers as both firms move toward IPOs. For enterprises already on ChatGPT, this lowers the barrier to agentic workflows across business functions.
