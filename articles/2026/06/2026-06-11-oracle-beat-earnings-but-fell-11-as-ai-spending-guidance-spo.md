# Oracle Beat Earnings but Fell 11% as AI Spending Guidance Spooked Investors

Oracle reported record Q4 with OCI revenue +93% YoY but dropped 11% after AI capex guidance raised cash-flow concerns. CIO.com noted Oracle is experimenting with outcome-based AI billing. The disconnect between strong demand and investor anxiety about returns defines this earnings cycle — visible at Oracle, Microsoft, and Meta alike.
